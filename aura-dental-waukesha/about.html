About AURA Dental

Compassionate Family Dentistry in Waukesha, Wisconsin, where compassionate care meets advanced technology.

Our Mission

We focus on creating a stress-free experience while helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile. From routine cleanings to advanced treatments, our goal is to make every appointment easy, comfortable, and personalized for you.

At AURA Dental, we believe that quality dental care should be accessible and comfortable for everyone. Our practice combines state-of-the-art technology with a warm, welcoming environment to ensure you receive the best possible care.

Meet Our Dentist

Led by an experienced and compassionate dental professional

Dr. Aqil Valika, D.M.D.

"My goal is to help every patient achieve optimal oral health in a comfortable, welcoming environment. I believe in taking the time to understand each patient&#x27;s unique needs and concerns."

Dr. Aqil Valika, D.M.D., is dedicated to providing exceptional, personalized dental care to patients and families in Waukesha, Brookfield, Milwaukee, and surrounding communities. With extensive experience in general, cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry, Dr. Valika combines advanced clinical expertise with a compassionate, patient-centered approach.

Dr. Valika earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) from the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) College of Dentistry in 2020, following his undergraduate studies at UIC. His areas of expertise include cosmetic dentistry, smile makeovers, restorative dentistry, dental implants, Invisalign, preventive care, and family dentistry.

As a Waukesha dentist, Dr. Valika believes exceptional dental care starts with listening to his patients. He takes the time to understand each patient&#x27;s concerns and goals before creating a personalized treatment plan focused on their oral health, comfort, and long-term results. Whether you need routine preventive care, restorative treatment, a smile transformation, or dental implants, his goal is to make every patient feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.

At Aura Dental, Dr. Valika combines modern dental technology with a gentle, hands-on approach to deliver precise, comfortable, and comprehensive care. His commitment to continuing education and advanced digital dentistry allows him to provide modern treatment options while maintaining the personalized experience his patients value.

Dr. Valika is proud to serve the Waukesha community and surrounding areas, helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful, and confident smiles through comprehensive, personalized dental care.
