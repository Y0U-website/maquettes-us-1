Dental Implants

  • How long does it take to get a dental implant? Getting a dental implant typically involves a multi-step process. After the initial consultation, the implant is placed into the jawbone, which needs time to fuse with the bone. This healing phase usually takes several months, after which the final crown is attached.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene are key to maintaining their longevity. Once fully healed and integrated, implants act just like natural teeth, providing a durable and reliable solution for tooth replacement.
