Meet our DENTIST

Compassionate, Trustworthy, Caring

X-twitter Linkedin-in Dr. Indu Kosuri, DDS , is a dedicated and compassionate family dentist in Mechanicsburg, Pennsylvania , proudly serving patients throughout Central Pennsylvania and Cumberland County . She completed her advanced dental training at Howard University in Washington, D.C., in 2013 .

Dr. Kosuri is passionate about continuing education and stays at the forefront of modern dentistry through continuing education, including dental implants , braces , clear aligners, Myofunctional Therapy and Dental Sleep Medicine

With over a decade of experience in general, preventive, cosmetic, and pediatric dentistry , Dr. Kosuri is known for her gentle approach and commitment to patient comfort. She strongly believes that prevention is the foundation of lifelong oral health and emphasizes patient education to help individuals and families make informed decisions about their dental care.
