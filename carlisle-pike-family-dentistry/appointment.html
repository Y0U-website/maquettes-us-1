CPFD Wellness Plan - Affordable Dental Care Without Insurance

At Carlisle Pike Family Dentistry , we believe quality dental care should be accessible to everyone—even without dental insurance. That’s why we’re excited to offer the CPFD Wellness Plan , an affordable in-house dental membership designed to help individuals and families maintain excellent oral health at a predictable, low cost.

Our dental wellness plan eliminates the hassles of traditional insurance while providing essential preventive dental care and valuable savings on additional treatments.

https://carlisle-pike-family-dentistry.illumitrac.com/

If you’re looking for affordable dental care in Mechanicsburg, PA , the CPFD Wellness Plan is a simple and stress-free solution.

  • No yearly maximums
  • No deductibles
  • No claim forms
  • No pre-authorization requirements
  • No waiting periods (immediate eligibility)
  • Up to two comprehensive dental exams
  • Two routine cleanings and all necessary X-rays
  • Emergency dental exam with necessary X-rays
  • Oral cancer screening
  • 20 % off all additional dental treatments (Some procedures may require referral to a specialist and are not covered under this plan)
