Andrea Blythe, DDS

Dr. Andrea Blythe is a dedicated General Dentist committed to providing exceptional, patient-centered dental care at BLVD Dentistry & Orthodontics on Riverside Dr. Her passion lies in helping patients achieve healthy and beautiful smiles through comprehensive treatment and a focus on individualized needs.

Dr. Blythe earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2007. She further honed her skills through a rigorous General Practice Residency in a VA hospital’s surgical setting, gaining invaluable experience in advanced procedures. This was followed by serving as a full-time staff doctor at the VA in Oklahoma City, Ok. In 2012, Dr. Blythe and her family relocated to Austin. She joined BLVD Dentistry & Orthodontics on Riverside Dr. in 2016 as a dentist, where she continues to provide quality care to the community.

Dr. Blythe prioritizes staying at the forefront of dental advancements. She actively pursues continuing education courses to maintain and enhance her clinical skills. She is a proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Texas Dental Association (TDA), and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), reflecting her dedication to the profession and its standards.

Dr. Blythe values being an active member of the Austin community. She enjoys the city’s vibrant outdoor scene and diverse culinary offerings. She and her husband have three daughters and, in her free time, she enjoys cooking, gardening, hiking, and quality time with family and friends.
