Opt-in Disclosure

Patients can designate to receive appointment reminders or opt-in via text messages from Chris Hickerson, D.D.S. when completing the new patient paperwork either online or on paper before their first visit. Additionally, patients can provide verbal consent, either in person or over the phone.

Please be aware that message and data rates may apply for any messages sent to you from us and to us from you. Also carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If you have any questions about your text plan or data plan, it is best to contact your wireless provider.

Patients may change this preference or opt-out at any time by contacting our office by phone, text, mail, email, or in person. You can also text “HELP” for support or more information, or text “STOP” to unsubscribe at any time. The types of messages sent include appointment reminders, typically sent one week prior to the appointment and another the day before. Message frequency may vary, with an average of 1-2 messages per month. You can review our privacy policy below or at:
