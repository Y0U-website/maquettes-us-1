0 Our Office Meet the Doctor Scott M Behnan DDS, MS

Dr. Scott Behnan practices orthodontics in our Fort Gratiot and Sandusky offices. He was born in Detroit, MI and raised in Rochester Hills, MI. He is a graduate of Birmingham Brother Rice High School. He attended the University of Michigan for 11 years, where he earned his B.A. in economics in 2002, his D.D.S from dental school in 2006, and finally his M.S. in orthodontics in 2009.

Dr. Scott Behnan has presented research papers at the American Association of Dental Research and at the University of Michigan, and has been published in the American Journal of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics. His research interests include perspectives on the need for optimum oral hygiene during orthodontic treatment.

His professional memberships include the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, the Great Lakes Association or Orthodontists, the Michigan Association or Orthodontists, and the Macomb Dental Society. In 2010, he became board-certified by the American Board of Orthodontists. Dr. Behnan is also a member of the Thumb Area Dental Clinics (TADC).

Dr. Scott Behnan spends much of his time with his wife and daughters. They are excited about having a long-term future in Michigan. Dr. Scott Behnan is an avid musician and has years of experience playing the piano, guitar, and drums. He also enjoys travel, reading, soccer, jogging, and spending time with his family.
