Meet the Team at Curtis Endodontics

It is common to experience apprehension about having endodontic treatment like root canals. At Curtis Endodontics, we strive to provide you a comfortable, stress-free experience.

As endodontists, Drs. Darrell and Shane Curtis have two basic goals: relieve pain and save teeth. Their advanced training allows them to provide expert treatment with skill, precision, and meticulous attention to detail. The same dedication is apparent with procedures that ensure a comfortable experience and results that keep your smile healthy and problem-free long into the future.

You can expect the same commitment from our compassionate professional team, who cares deeply about patient comfort and the overall quality of your experience. At Curtis Endodontics, we go above and beyond to put you at ease and ensure that your appointments are stress-free from start to finish.

Please take a few minutes to get to know Drs. Darrell and Shane Curtis and learn about their commitment to your health and comfort.

Dr. Darrell M. Curtis

Dr. Curtis grew up in Central Pennsylvania and graduated from Cumberland Valley High School. He received his dental degree from the University of Maryland in 2011. Following dental school, he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland and served a three-year tour of duty as a general dentist at Spangdahlem Air Base in Germany.

Dr. Curtis was accepted into the Air Force Post-Graduate Dental School Endodontics Residency and received his specialty certification in 2017. During that time, he received extensive clinical and didactic training in non-surgical root canal therapy, apical microsurgery, and trauma management. He also earned a master’s degree from the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences for research that was ultimately published in the Journal of Endodontics.

Dr. Curtis is married and has five boys. He spends much of his free time coaching and playing sports with his kids.

Dr. Shane J. Curtis

Dr. Curtis grew up in Central Pennsylvania and graduated from Cumberland Valley High School. He received his dental degree from the University of Maryland in 2014. Following dental school, he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Ohio and then went on to complete a three-year tour of duty at McGuire Air Force Base in New Jersey. In 2017, he was named the Air Mobility Command Clinical Dentist of the Year.

The next year, Dr. Curtis was accepted into the Albert Einstein Medical Center endodontics residency program and received his specialty certification in 2020. During that time, he received extensive training in leading-edge techniques for root canal therapy, endodontic microsurgery, and trauma management. Dr. Curtis believes all patients should be treated like family and combines this approach with clinical expertise to provide personalized and comfortable endodontic care.

When he s not in the office, Dr. Curtis can usually be found at Hersheypark, Paulus Farm, or Adventure Park with his wife and three young children.
