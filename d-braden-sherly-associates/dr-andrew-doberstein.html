Dr. Andrew Doberstein

Dr. Doberstein was born and raised in the suburbs of Philadelphia. He graduated from Penn State, and went on to complete his dental training at The University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Doberstein has extensive experience in areas including fillings, root canals, extractions, crowns and bridges, periodontics, implants, and Invisalign.

Dr. Doberstein’s favorite aspect of dentistry is interacting and building relationships with his patients and giving them the gift of a smile. In his free time Dr. Doberstein enjoys being active, traveling, rooting for Penn State and Philadelphia sports, and eating all of the good food that the area has to offer.
