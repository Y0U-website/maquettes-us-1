Dr. Waseem Faraj

Dr. Waseem Faraj is a passionate and dedicated dentist who is committed to delivering comprehensive and personalized oral care to patients of all ages. With a strong emphasis on preventative dentistry and patient comfort, Dr. Faraj takes pride in cultivating lasting relationships with his patients while providing exceptional dental services.

A proud graduate from Iraq, he has further honed his expertise through advanced residency training in the United States, accumulating over 15 years of valuable experience in the field. Dr. Faraj is dedicated to lifelong learning and actively engages in continuing education to remain at the forefront of the latest dental advancements. He is not only committed to enhancing individual smiles but also fervently advocates for optimal oral health within the community.
