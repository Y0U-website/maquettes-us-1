In more complex cases, Dr. Du may collaborate directly with your general dentist to ensure a coordinated treatment plan. If you do not currently have a general dentist, we are happy to provide trusted referrals.

Most patients who are in good overall health and have sufficient bone are candidates for dental implants. If there are any medical considerations, we will coordinate with your physician to ensure safe treatment.

If additional bone support is needed, advanced imaging and planning technology allow us to precisely evaluate and prepare the area for implant placement.

Bone grafting procedures can often restore lost bone and create a stable foundation for implants, even in cases where bone loss has occurred due to age or periodontal disease.

Young patients should wait until jaw growth is complete (typically around ages 18 20). For adults, there is no upper age limit, overall health is more important than age. Dental implants have been successfully placed in patients well into their later years.

Treatment timelines vary depending on your specific needs and can range from a few months to up to a year. If bone grafting is required, additional healing time may be needed before implant placement.

While same-day implant options may be available in select cases, our priority is long-term success and stability. Our focus is on achieving results that last for years to come.

In straightforward cases, we can provide a close estimate for implant placement. For more complex cases, costs may vary depending on the need for additional procedures such as bone grafting.
