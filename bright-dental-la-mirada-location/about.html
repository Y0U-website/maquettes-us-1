Emma Kim, D.M.D.

Dr. Emma with over 20 years of experience has been providing personalized attention and outstanding dental care for La Mirada and surrounding areas since she and her partner Dr. Gaminchi, a friend from dental school decided to continue the practice of Dr. Israel, a trusted dentist for La Mirada for multi generations.

A graduate of the prestigious Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, and Boston University, Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Emma combines gentle dental care with an extensive focus on advanced and experienced clinical skills. She is also a faculty member at UCLA School of Dentistry which keeps her up to date on the advancement and education of dentistry. Her passion for life is her family and educating and advocating of prevention of dental diseases in part of the world that may not have the benefit of good dental care.

Farinoush Gaminchi, D.M.D.

Dr. Gaminchi has been practicing dentistry for over 24 years. Dr. Gaminchi attended U.C.L.A. as an undergrad and graduated in 1988 with a bachelors degree in Biology. She then attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine where she got a D.M.D. in 1992. Dr. Gaminchi went on to finish a one year hospital internship residency in Connecticut at Yale - New Haven Hospital and then completed a 3 year specialty in periodontics (gum surgery) and implant dentistry in 1997. Dr. Gaminchi is a wife and mother of 3 children. She enjoy’s yoga, skiing and reading.

Brian Ly, D.M.D.

Dr. Brian Ly was born and raised in California. He received his undergraduate degree and dental degree from UCLA School of Dentistry in 2015. Taught to strive for clinical excellence, he makes it a top priority to provide gentle and compassionate care. Dr. Ly has been a Bright Dental of La Mirada provider since 2016 and is an integral contibutor to the care provided to this community. He is married and enjoys spending time with his family.

Jane Riew, R.D.A, R.D.H., H.A.P.

Jane has been a licensed dental professional since 2013 working in several different aspects of dentistry. Away from the dental office, Jane can be found as a volunteer for her children’s school or neighborhood church. She enjoys sports, as well as traveling. Jane is married and has two children.

Maria, R.D.A.

Maria has been dedicated to caring for our patients for more than 15 years. She began her career as a dental assistant in 2004 and earned her RDA in 2007. Maria is an exceptional healthcare provider, and our patients absolutely adore her. Outside the office, she enjoys a full and happy life with her husband and their two children. She loves gardening, taking long walks, and spending her Sundays at church.

Letty, R.D.A.
