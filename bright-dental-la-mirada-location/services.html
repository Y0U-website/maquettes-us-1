General Dentistry Services in La Mirada

Our dentists provide general dental services in the diagnosis, treatment and prevention of disorders and diseases affecting the teeth and gums. Their goal is to help you maintain a healthy smile for the entire family. General dentistry services at Bright Dental of La Mirada include crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, periodontal therapy, and fillings.

Root Canal Therapy

Root Canal therapy is a restorative technique that involves the cleaning of the infected nerve area and filling the root canal space. When a tooth is fractured, the health of the pulp (the living tissue inside the tooth) can be compromised. Any decay in the pulp can lead to further decline in the overall health of the damaged tooth.

Persistent problems including sensitivity to hot or cold is usually an indication of declining tooth health. Root canal treatment usually lasts a lifetime, although from time to time, a tooth may have to be retreated due to new infections.

White Mercury-Free Fillings

Dental fillings are used to repair a tooth damaged by decay or injury. Cavity treatment includes removal of the decayed portion of the tooth and then filling the area with composite. Fillings protect your teeth from further decay as well as alleviate any pain associated with decay. At Bright Dental of La Mirada , we use only tooth colored (white fillings) made of composite or porcelain that restore and mimic the natural appearance of the tooth structure.

Dental Crowns

Crowns are synthetic tooth-shaped caps made of resin, porcelain, metal or porcelain fused to metal, that are placed on the top of a tooth. Crowns are used to restore a tooth’s function and appearance when portions have deteriorated and must be removed.

Dental Bridges

Dental Bridges are used to replace sections of damaged or missing teeth in order to provide a natural-looking appearance and proper bite. Bridges can be permanent or removable. Custom made to fit and function properly, bridges are barely visible and can bring back the natural shape of the mouth as well as the proper bite alignment between upper and lower teeth.

Dentures

Dentures are removable artificial dental appliances that replace missing teeth and surrounding tissue. Dentures created by the team at Bright Dental of La Mirada are made to look like your natural teeth. There are two types of dentures: complete dentures and partial dentures. Complete dentures are used to replace all of the teeth and partial dentures are used when some of the natural teeth remain.

Tooth Extractions

A tooth extraction can become necessary for many reasons. The team at Bright Dental of La Mirada will be able to help you with the tooth extraction while making you as relaxed and as comfortable as possible during the procedure.
