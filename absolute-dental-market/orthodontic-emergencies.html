Orthodontic Emergencies FAQs

Is A Broken Wire An Orthodontic Emergency?

Yes! A broken wire would be considered an orthodontic emergency due to its aversive effects. It can cause an incredible amount of discomfort, as well as sores, cuts, and infections.

What Is Considered An Orthodontic Emergency?

Orthodontic emergencies are situations that occur where you cannot wait for your next scheduled appointment. These situations include but are not limited to:

  • Severe trauma
  • Bleeding
  • Atypical discomfort
  • Signs of infection
  • Cuts or sores
