What Are The Pros And Cons Of Dental Braces?

There are pros and cons to any orthodontic treatment. Depending on what types of braces your dentist recommends, here are some things to consider:

  • Metal braces While metal braces are affordable and effective, they can be hard to clean and cause irritation. Also, teeth with braces are the most noticeable form of treatment.
  • Ceramic braces Ceramic or clear braces are less obvious than metal braces and work faster than plastic aligners. However, they are more expensive than metal braces and tend to stain easily.
  • Invisible braces Clear plastic trays are virtually invisible, making them the most discreet option. You also remove them when eating, so there are no dietary restrictions. Unfortunately, that also means you can easily lose the aligners or derail your treatment if you forget to reinsert them.

What Should One Know Before Getting Braces?

Before getting braces, you should understand the plan and what you can do to make your treatment successful. You may be instructed to avoid certain foods and drinks, use special oral hygiene products, or see your orthodontist regularly for adjustments. It’s also important that you have realistic expectations related to the length and cost of treatment.

Are Dental Braces’ Sole Purpose To Straighten Teeth?

While the most common reason for dental teeth braces is to improve the appearance of a person’s smile, there are other benefits to orthodontic treatment. Teeth that are aligned are easier to clean, reducing the risk of tooth decay, cavities, and gum disease. Braces may also help with jaw and bite issues that can become painful.

Can You Still Get Braces Even If You Have A Dental Implant?

Because dental implants are permanently fused to the jaw bone, braces won’t cause them to shift the way natural teeth do. However, you can still get braces if you have a dental implant because they will shift the other teeth around the implant for better alignment. You should always speak to your dentist to see if you’re a good candidate for orthodontic treatment if you have dental implants.

Is It Possible To Reverse The Effects Of Dental Braces?

Yes, teeth can shift back to their original positions if you’re not careful. While small shifts are normal and expected, you don’t want your teeth to completely revert to how they were. If this happens, you will need to undergo treatment again to realign your teeth. To prevent your teeth from moving after orthodontic treatment, you will most likely need to wear a removable retainer at night.
