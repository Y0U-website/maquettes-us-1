What Are The Benefits Of An Implant-Supported Dental Bridge?

If you’re considering a dental implant bridge, here’s why many patients are choosing this type of dental restoration.

Prevents Bone Loss

One of the biggest advantages of an implant-supported bridge is its ability to support healthy bone. Without teeth to support it, the jaw bone loses density, which can cause serious dental issues. The implants preserve the bone’s integrity by replacing tooth roots and their function.

Longevity And Durability

Dental implants and the bridges they support are incredibly durable and can last a lifetime with the right care. While you can expect to have your dental restoration for at least 10 years, routine check-ups and regular maintenance will prolong its life.

Improve The Appearance Of Your Smile

Because an implant-supported bridge replaces at least three missing teeth, it can significantly improve the look of your smile. You can smile confidently, knowing there are no visible gaps in your teeth and changes to your face due to bone density loss.

Better Functionality

Another benefit of an implant dental bridge is improved dental function, including biting and chewing. Patients often notice that these bridges also help with speaking and communicating clearly.

Does Not Alter Natural Teeth

Some patients are hesitant about dental bridges because the supporting teeth need altering to accommodate the abutments and bridge. However, implant-supported bridges rest on the titanium implants, leaving the surrounding natural teeth unaltered.
