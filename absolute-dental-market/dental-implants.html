What’s Involved With a Dental Implant Procedure?

At Absolute Dental, we make the dental implant procedure as easy and straightforward as possible.

Consultation and Treatment Planning

First, you’ll come in for an initial consultation, where the dentist will inspect your gums, teeth, and jaw to determine the best course of action.

Surgical Implant Placement

After your consultation, you’ll have the titanium posts surgically installed into your jawbone. We offer many different anesthesia and sedation options so you’re as comfortable as possible.

Osseointegration Healing Phase

Next, you’ll enter the healing phase when your body will begin to accept the new artificial tooth root . During this phase, your body will heal around the posts we have implanted in your jawbone to prepare for the final phase, where you’ll receive your new dental crowns.

Abutment and Final Crown Placement

Finally, you’ll receive your dental crowns that look and act like natural teeth. The post will first get an abutment, or connector , then the final crown will be placed. The final result is custom designed to blend in naturally with your other teeth. You may even forget your new teeth are there.

Potential Risks and Complications of Dental Implants

Dental implants have a very high success rate . This means it is unlikely that there will be complications with your procedure. Overall, they are considered a very safe and effective.

However, as with any surgery, there may be complications. There is a small risk of infection at the surgery site, or the body rejecting the implants. Choosing an experienced dental provider like Absolute Dental is essential for the best outcomes.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

In comparison with other options for replacing missing teeth, caring for your dental implants is easy — you’ll care for them in basically the same way as you care for your natural teeth.

You should brush twice per day, floss in the evening before bed, and see a dentist every six months or as directed. You can also use antibacterial mouth wash, if you wish, but it should never replace brushing, flossing, or seeing your dentist.

Otherwise, quitting smoking and avoiding other tobacco and nicotine products will help add to their longevity. You should also be careful about using your teeth as tools (never open packages or rip tape with your teeth), and limit very hard or very sticky foods, such as popcorn kernels, ice, taffy, and caramel.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most people are good candidates for dental implants, but, in general, you should have relatively healthy gums, sufficient bone density in your jaw, and good overall health. Even if you have experienced bone loss, additional procedures, such as bone grafts, may help.

You can speak with your doctor to understand more, but dental implants are one of the most widely available procedures to fix tooth loss. A consultation with our team at Absolute Dental is the best way to tell if you’re an appropriate candidate. At your consultation, the dental implants cost may also be discussed with your dentist.
