WHO WE ARE

Dental Services Company

We are a dental services company focused on leveling the playing field for independent dental practices

WHAT WE DO

Negotiate, Advocate & Support

We level the playing field and ensure our members receive benefits typically reserved for corporate dentistry.

WHY WE DO IT

Passionate about communities and small business

We care about our members and want to ensure they are able to maintain clinical authority. Our practices are passionate about their communities just as we are.
