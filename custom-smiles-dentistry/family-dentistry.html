Your dental needs grow and evolve with you!

Your dental needs change as you grow, mature, and go through life. Here at Custom Smiles Dentistry , we will help you take the best possible care of your teeth and gums, to ensure that your dental health is the best it can be for many years to come.

Let’s face it: your dental needs change, depending on your age, stage of life, and numerous other factors that need to be taken into account. A young child has different dental needs from a pregnant woman, as does an older person or someone with a history of complex dental issues.

For maximum peace of mind, a comprehensive dental clinic can help you take the best care of your teeth at whatever age or stage of life you’re in. so that you can focus on living your life without having to worry about your teeth at all!

What we offer

Children require specialized dental care to maximize their comfort at the dental office from early on, and to instill good oral hygiene practices to last their entire lifetime. Family dental offices offer child-friendly exams, preventive treatments like sealants and fluoride applications, and early orthodontic evaluations to address concerns before they escalate.

Regular checkups and cleanings ensure that potential issues are caught early and treated effectively before they evolve into much bigger problems. Extra preventative options are sometimes recommended where appropriate, such as dental sealants to protect vulnerable areas from decay, or fluoride treatments to strengthen your teeth against cavities.

Even with regular brushing and flossing, restorative dental care is sometimes necessary to restore any damage that occurs to your teeth from tooth decay, erosion, or injury. Restorative treatments include any dental treatments that restore full dental health to your teeth, such as fillings, root canals, crowns or bridges. Restorative dental work ensures that you’re able to talk and eat normally, and saves you from needing much more complex dental work later on.

In addition to ensuring the health of your teeth and gums, cosmetic dentistry can give you the confidence boost you need to live your best life! Treatments such as veneers to transform chipped or misshapen teeth is just one example of the services we offer to help you have the most beautiful smile possible!

Periodontal care including treating gum disease and teaching you how to take good care of your gums is a critical part of quality dental care. Taking care of your teeth AND gums is essential to your long-term oral health, ensuring that your teeth continue to serve you for many enjoyable years ahead.

Unfortunately, dental emergencies can and do happen quite unexpectedly. As much as possible, we try to accommodate any unexpected dental emergencies that arise, such as a sudden and severe toothache or broken teeth due to sudden trauma or injury.
