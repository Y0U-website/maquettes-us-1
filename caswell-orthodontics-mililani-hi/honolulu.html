Our Orthodontic Services

We believe orthodontic care should be personalized, preventive, and focused on helping you feel your best. That’s why we offer a wide range of services tailored to each patient’s needs.

Preventive Orthodontics

This service helps us catch and address small problems before they grow into bigger ones. It’s ideal for children and allows us to guide their tooth and jaw development early, often avoiding the need for more intense treatment later.

Interceptive Orthodontics

Also known as initial treatment, this is about spotting and treating issues while a child’s mouth is still developing. Early action can stop bigger problems down the road and make future orthodontic care easier and more effective.

Corrective Orthodontics

If you or your child already has bite issues or crooked teeth, our corrective treatments like braces or clear aligners can help fix them. These solutions are designed to improve both how your smile looks and how your teeth work.

Clear Aligners

If you want to make your teeth aligned invisibly? Our clear aligners are custom-made, removable, and a great option for teens and adults who want to improve their smile without the look of traditional braces.

Retention Therapy

It is not the end of your treatment journey when your braces are taken off. Retainers keep your teeth in their new positions and help protect your results. We provide custom-made retainers and check in regularly to make sure your smile stays put.

Braces

We offer a variety of braces, including classic metal and low-profile ceramic options. Whether it’s for a child, teen, or adult, braces are a dependable way to fix crooked teeth, crowding, or bite issues with care and comfort throughout the process.
