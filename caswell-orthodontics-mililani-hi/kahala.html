Our Orthodontic Services in Kahala, HI

Your smile is distinctive, just like you are, and as such should be treated with special regard. Therefore, we provide a broad spectrum of services for all children, young adults, and adults.

Preventive Orthodontics

Preventive care is all about identifying the smaller dental issues to avert a larger challenge that can come in the future. For elementary school-aged children, this entails observing their dental development and ensuring their jaw alignment is progressing properly.

Interceptive Orthodontics

At times, children may exhibit the first signs of incisors and canines, or even anterior and posterior dental occlusion and bite issues. Interceptive orthodontics works while the jaw is still developing and can therefore guide it and the teeth into more favorable positions.

Corrective Orthodontics

When dental alignment is already maloccluded and not in proper relation, corrective orthodontics is the answer. Braces and clear aligners are effective means of moving teeth to their correct anatomical positions. Not only for the facial aesthetics that help you in improving your smile, but also makes it comfortable when you are chewing, speaking and other oral hygienic works.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a discrete approach to orthodontic treatment and a more recent innovation. Clear aligners are not made of metal brackets and therefore do not create unsightly and bulky appliances.

Retention Therapy

Retention is equally as crucial as the other steps in orthodontic treatment, as the teeth are at risk of migrating into undesired positions. Retention therapy employs bespoke retainers to secure the smile optimally. The teeth, if the retainer is not used, may continuously migrate to their former positions.

Braces

Installation of braces is one of the first and most effective orthodontic treatments, focusing on correcting crooked teeth, excessive and insufficient gaps, and other occlusion and bite issues.
