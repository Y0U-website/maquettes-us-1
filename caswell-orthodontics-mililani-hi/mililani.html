Our Orthodontic Services

Preventive Orthodontics

Preventive care helps stop dental issues before they start. For young kids, this means keeping an eye on how their teeth are coming in and making sure everything is on track. It’s a smart way to set up a healthy smile early on.

Interceptive Orthodontics

If your child shows early signs of bite problems or misaligned teeth, interceptive care helps guide their teeth and jaw as they grow. It can make future treatment more easier, or sometimes make it unnecessary.

Corrective Orthodontics

If your teeth are already misaligned or you have a bite issue, we use braces or clear aligners to gently move them into place. Corrective care helps your teeth look and work the way they should.

Clear Aligners

Want to straighten your teeth without the obvious braces? Clear aligners are a great option. They’re nearly invisible, easy to take out, and custom-made for your smile. A great choice for teens and adults who want a simple and low-key solution.

Retention Therapy

Once your treatment is done, we help you keep your smile just the way it is. Retainers are custom-made to hold your teeth in place and prevent them from shifting. It’s a key part of making sure your results last.

Braces

We offer braces for kids, teens, and adults. Whether you choose traditional metal braces or something more modern, braces are a reliable way to fix crooked teeth, gaps, or bite issues. We make sure the process is smooth, comfortable, and tailored to you.
