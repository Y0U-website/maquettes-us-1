The practice

We offer a dental membership plan for patients without dental insurance. For one low payment, you’ll get access to the basic dental care you need to maintain a healthy smile and discounts on other services.

Dental implants are a permanent, natural-looking solution to missing teeth whether you need a single tooth replaced or an entire arch, our expert team can restore your smile with dental implants.

Sedation dentistry offers a safe and effective solution for managing dental anxiety, making treatments more comfortable for children, adults and those with complex procedures. We offer a range of sedation options, including nitrous, oral and IV sedation.

Clear aligners deliver an advanced orthodontic solution using nearly invisible trays to align teeth with precision and comfort—no brackets, wires or hassle. They are a discreet and convenient way to straighten teeth for both teens and adults.

Les soins

Abutment Placement: Once healing is complete, we attach a small connector called an abutment to the implant. This piece will hold your final crown, bridge or denture securely in place.

Treatment Planning: We develop a personalized treatment plan, which may include single implants, bridges or full-arch solutions. If needed, preparatory procedures like bone grafting or tooth extraction are scheduled.

Dental implant pricing varies depending on the number of implants. We offer a membership plan and flexible financing.
