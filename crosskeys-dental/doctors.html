Dr. Badali is a native of Rochester and is happy to return home after several years away for training. He graduated from Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine on Long Island after which he completed a General Practice Residency program at Upstate University Hospital in Syracuse.

A lover of Chicago sports, he believes that Walter Payton is still super-human and always thinks that this is the Cubs’ year (and we’re ready to get back to the WS!)… He is married to Megan, the most supportive woman on Earth. They have a rambunctious and overly sweet little girl named Lila and an even more rambunctious little man named Joe.

Dr. Gergelis was born and raised in East Amherst, New York. He obtained his bachelor’s degree in Biology at SUNY Fredonia and attended the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, graduating in May 2020.

After dental school, Dr. G moved to Syracuse to complete a one-year general practice residency at Upstate Medical University and the Syracuse VA Dental Clinic, further strengthening his restorative, surgical, and prosthodontic skills. He then spent three years in Pittsburgh practicing general dentistry.

In the summer of 2024, Dr. G and his partner Rosalie moved back to Rochester to be closer to his Western NY home. Outside of the office, he enjoys going to concerts, playing music, spending time in nature, attempting to cook, rooting for the Bills, and hanging out with his friends, family, and two cats.

The staff
