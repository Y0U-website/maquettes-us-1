Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. These small titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they act as sturdy anchors for prosthetic teeth. The procedure is fairly simple and can truly benefit patients. Dental implants offer several advantages, including:

  • Natural Appearance
  • Durability
  • Improved Oral Health
  • Enhanced Functionality

Invisalign/Clear Correct

Also known as clear braces. Any adult with an issue with the alignment of their teeth or bite can be a good candidate for invisible braces. Clear braces can correct problems with the bite or alignment of the teeth. A few of the particular problems that invisible braces can help correct include:

  • Overbite
  • Crowding
  • Gaps and spaces
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite
