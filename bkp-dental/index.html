The practice

Dr. Le. graduated from the University of Utah with a degree in electrical engineering and completed his D.D.S. In Buffalo, New York, in 2004. He has over twenty years of experience in dentistry and is well known for his gentle approach and patient care.

Root canals treat infected or damaged tooth pulp, saving the natural tooth. The procedure removes the infection, fills the space, and seals the tooth. Root canals relieve pain, prevent further damage, and restore the tooth’s function and appearance.

Dentures replace missing teeth, providing support for facial muscles and improving speech and chewing. Custom-made for a comfortable fit, they restore your smile’s appearance and function. Dentures can be full or partial, depending on your needs.

X-rays and exams are essential for diagnosing dental issues early. They help in detecting cavities, bone loss, and other oral problems. Our comprehensive exams ensure your teeth and gums are healthy, providing a strong foundation for long-term dental care.

Fillings restore teeth damaged by decay, returning them to their normal function and shape. We use high-quality materials to ensure durability and natural appearance. Fillings prevent further decay and help maintain the structural integrity of your teeth.

Bridges replace one or more missing teeth, filling gaps to restore your smile. They are anchored to adjacent teeth or implants, providing stability and support. Bridges improve chewing, speaking, and prevent remaining teeth from shifting out of position.

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth, offering a natural look and feel. They provide strong support for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Implants improve chewing and speaking, and they help maintain facial structure by preventing bone loss.
