Orthodontics for Kids and Adults

Dr. Othman not only completed her Doctor of Dentistry degree, but went on to receive her Masters in Orthodontics. Shee spent an additional three years in school studying smiles of children and adults and ways to improve them using braces and orthodontic appliances.

Dr. Othman not only treats children in our practice but teens and adults, too.

You can have the smile you’ve always dreamed of. There are several benefits to having your smile improved as an adult including having better self esteem, more confidence and easier dental hygiene.

Our team at Concord Orthodontics is devoted to beautiful smiles and has orthodontic solutions to fit every budget and lifestyle. Our fees vary with the complexity of each unique smile but easy payment plans can be arranged to suit. During your first orthodontic visit we will carefully review your unique smile and show you similar cases Dr. Othman has worked on so you can understand what is achievable.
