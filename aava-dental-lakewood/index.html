The practice

Orthodontics comes from the Greek words orthos, meaning “straight,” and dontia, meaning “teeth.” But the work of an orthodontist goes far beyond aesthetics. These dental specialists focus on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting dental malocclusions—conditions where teeth and jaws are misaligned.

Think of orthodontics as the behind-the-scenes architecture of your smile. Whether it’s guiding a child’s jaw into better balance or helping an adult reclaim comfort after years of crowding or bite issues, orthodontics lays the foundation for healthier teeth and a more functional bite.
