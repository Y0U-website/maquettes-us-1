Invisalign in Belmont, MA

  • Convenience – Invisalign lets you continue to eat your favorite foods and brush and floss like you normally would because you can remove your aligners whenever you need to.
  • Predictability – Invisalign offers a more controlled method of orthodontic treatment because we carefully plan the movements of each individual tooth for predictable results. We can even generate a preview of what your smile will look like when your treatment is complete!
  • Invisibility – Invisalign is virtually invisible, so your coworkers and classmates won’t even know you’re wearing aligners.

The Invisalign Process

When you come to our office for your complimentary Invisalign consultation, we will examine your teeth and jaw, go over your dental history, and determine whether Invisalign is the right orthodontic solution for you. Should you decide to move forward with Invisalign, we will take photographs and impressions of your teeth, which lets us create a 3D model of your smile on our computer to plan your treatment. Our treatment plan is sent to Invisalign, where your custom-made aligners are fabricated.

Every two weeks (or as directed), you’ll switch to the next set of aligners in your treatment plan. Each set brings you a little bit closer to your final results: a beautiful smile! To ensure that your treatment is on the right track, we’ll have you visit our office every four to six weeks to monitor your progress. Once you’re done with your final set of aligners, we’ll provide you with custom retainers to maintain your results for decades to come.

Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

Invisalign is less expensive than ceramic braces (sometimes also known as clear braces) and lingual braces, but the price is comparable to that of metal braces—sometimes slightly more and other times slightly less, depending on your orthodontic case.

Can your teeth move back after Invisalign?

Yes, as with any orthodontic treatment, your teeth can move back after Invisalign if you don’t wear your retainers as directed.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

The average treatment time for Invisalign is between nine and 15 months, with more severe orthodontic cases taking longer to correct than minor misalignments.

Dental insurance plans that cover orthodontia often cover Invisalign up to the cost of metal braces, but not always. Our team can help you understand your benefits and out-of-pocket expenses.
