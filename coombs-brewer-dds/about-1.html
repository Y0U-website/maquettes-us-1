About Us

The Best Practices of Modern Dentistry For The Greater Carson City Area

Dr. Ryan Coombs and Dr. Shannon Brewer have built their family dental practice upon the pillars of modern dentistry’s most advanced techniques and applications. Each is an experienced practitioner, and doctors and staff hone their expertise through the tireless pursuit of leading-edge practices in dental health by completing hours of annual continuing education courses.

Start your journey to better health and well being. Call the offices of Drs. Coombs & Brewer today.

During the last 12 years Dr. Coombs has been fortunate to practice dentistry in small rural communities as well as larger metropolitan cities. He has volunteered in community health clinics and as a dental provider for the Mission of Mercy service weekends. This diverse exposure has helped Dr.Coombs refine his skills as a dentist who provides expert, comprehensive and considerate care.

Dr. Coombs enjoys many outdoor activities available in and around Carson City. He will venture into the mountains for hikes, fishing and snow skiing. Dr. Coombs has a passion for cooking. He hopes to add a little western flare to the down south Oklahoma barbecue that he learned while living away.

Dr. Shannon Brewer is from Oklahoma. She attended St. Gregory’s University where she graduated with a Bachelors of Science in 1998. From there, Dr. Brewer moved to Omaha, Nebraska. It’s here that she received her Doctorate in Dentistry from Creighton University in 2002. While attending Creighton University Dr. Brewer met Dr. Ryan Coombs.

Dr. Brewer has a diverse background in her practice of dentistry. Upon graduation, she remained in Omaha for one more year and remained at Creighton University Dental School as a part-time instructor. She also served as the Clinic Director at One World Community Health Center in Omaha during this time. Dr. Brewer was able to help educate future dentists and provide dental care to an underserved dental population in need of help.

Dr Brewer moved to Carson City in the summer of 2015. When not at work Shannon is busy with her children and their activities. Despite growing up in Oklahoma and spending most of her time in the plains of the midwest, Dr. Brewer enjoys many of the various experiences that can be had from living this close to the Sierra Nevada Mountain range.
