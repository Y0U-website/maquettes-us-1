Although they’re below the gumline, your teeth’s roots form a solid structure that is fundamental to maintaining healthy teeth. Damaged roots cause tooth decay, and in extreme cases, total tooth loss.

Dental implants, however, can replace the root of a tooth allowing additional hardware to be attached to restore your smile to a full and bright position. Often crowns, bridges or even a ball socket can be used to connect a denture.

There are a number of implants options available. Call the Carson City office of Drs. Coombs & Brewer today for a personal consultation about the best for your smile.
