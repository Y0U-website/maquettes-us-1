Signs Your Child May Need to See an Orthodontist

Every child grows differently, but a few signs are worth a closer look. If you notice any of these, it may be time to schedule an evaluation.

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Trouble biting or chewing food
  • Frequent mouth breathing
  • Ongoing finger or thumb sucking
  • Crowded, crooked, or misplaced teeth
  • Teeth that stick out or don’t meet properly
  • Jaws and teeth that look out of proportion with the face
  • Crowded front teeth by age seven or eight
