How Clear Aligners Work

Clear aligner treatment starts with detailed 3D imaging of your teeth. This technology maps out each small movement and lets you see your projected results before your first tray ever goes in.

From there, you receive a series of custom-made aligners. You wear each set for about two weeks, removing them only to eat, brush, and floss.

Every few weeks, you’ll visit our office so Dr. Catherine can check your progress and keep your treatment on track. Each new set gently shifts your teeth a little closer to their final position.

Most patients complete treatment in about 9 to 15 months, using somewhere between 18 and 30 aligners. Your exact plan depends on your goals and the condition of your teeth.

Everyday Benefits of Clear Aligners

  • Removable: Take them out to eat your favorite foods and clean your teeth with ease.
  • Virtually invisible: The clear trays blend in, so treatment stays discreet.
  • Comfortable: Smooth, custom trays mean no metal brackets or wires.
  • Predictable: 3D imaging lets you preview your new smile before you begin.
