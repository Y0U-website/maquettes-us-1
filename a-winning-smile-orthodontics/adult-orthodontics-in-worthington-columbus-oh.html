Discreet and Effective Treatment Options for Adults

We understand that our adult patients value treatments that fit seamlessly into their busy lives. That’s why we offer several aesthetic options that deliver outstanding results without compromising your professional appearance.

Invisalign® for Adults

Invisalign® is a highly popular choice for adults due to its nearly invisible design. This system uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into place. The aligners are removable, allowing you to eat, drink, brush, and floss with ease.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces provide the same reliable strength as traditional metal braces but with a more subtle appearance. The brackets are made from a clear, translucent material that blends with the natural color of your teeth, making them much less noticeable.

Metal Braces

Today’s metal braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever. Using advanced self-ligating brackets, they offer a time-tested, effective, and often more affordable solution for correcting even the most complex orthodontic cases.
