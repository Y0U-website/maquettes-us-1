How Ceramic Braces Work

The process for ceramic braces closely mirrors traditional braces. Dr. Catherine bonds clear ceramic brackets to your teeth, then connects them with a thin archwire.

That wire applies gentle, steady pressure to guide your teeth into proper alignment. Over time, this moves your smile toward a healthier, straighter position.

The biggest difference is appearance. You get the same reliable mechanics, just with a far more subtle look on your teeth.

Advanced Self-Ligating Ceramic Brackets

To make your treatment even more comfortable and discreet, we use self-ligating ceramic brackets.

These modern brackets hold the archwire in place with a small, built-in clip, so there’s no need for traditional elastic ties. The clips are whitened to blend with the clear brackets, keeping your braces nearly invisible.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Ceramic Braces?

Ceramic braces are a versatile option for many patients who want a less visible way to straighten their teeth.

They can effectively correct a range of orthodontic issues, including:

  • Crowded or crooked teeth
  • Gaps and spacing between teeth
  • Overbites, underbites, and other bite concerns
