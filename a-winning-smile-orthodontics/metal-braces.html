How Metal Braces Work

Metal braces are made up of small brackets and a thin archwire. Dr. Catherine bonds the brackets to the front of each tooth, then connects them with the wire.

That wire applies gentle, steady pressure that gradually shifts your teeth into their ideal position. When needed, she can add springs or small elastics to fine-tune your bite and jaw alignment.

The process is straightforward and proven. Over time, those small, consistent adjustments add up to a straighter, healthier smile.

Modern Self-Ligating Brackets for Greater Comfort

Today’s metal braces are smaller and more comfortable than older styles. To make treatment even easier, we use self-ligating brackets.

Instead of relying on elastic ties to hold the archwire, these brackets use a small built-in clip. That simple change brings real, everyday benefits.

Benefits of Self-Ligating Brackets

Dr. Catherine carefully selects the right bracket system for each patient, delivering precise results and a smooth experience from start to finish.

  • Greater comfort: Reduced friction often means a gentler treatment experience.
  • Easier cleaning: Without elastic ties, brushing and flossing are simpler and more effective.
  • A more discreet look: Smaller brackets keep your braces subtle.
  • Room for personality: You can still add colorful elastics for a fun, custom style if you’d like.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Metal Braces?

Metal braces are an excellent option for patients of all ages, and they can treat even the most complex orthodontic cases. That’s part of what makes them so versatile.

  • Crowded or crooked teeth
  • Gaps and spacing between teeth
  • Overbites, underbites, and crossbites
  • General misalignment and bite concerns
