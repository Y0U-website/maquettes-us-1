Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term option for replacing missing teeth.

Unlike dentures or partials, you won’t experience problems with slipping or sliding, and they don’t require a specialized daily care routine beyond regular brushing and flossing. Unlike bridges, dental implants do not require alteration to your existing, healthy teeth. In fact, bridges can be supported by implants for fewer alterations during a more complex restoration.

Do I Need Dental Implants?

A missing tooth can affect appearance, chewing, and the way the surrounding teeth function. Over time, changes can also occur in the bone where a tooth is missing.

Dental implants are one option for replacing a missing tooth, but they are not the only option. Depending on your individual situation, a bridge, partial denture, complete denture, or implant-supported restoration may also be considered.

Dr. Hannahan will evaluate the area, review your X-rays with you, and explain the advantages and limitations of the available options so you can make an informed decision about what is right for you.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants can be a good option for many adults, but several factors need to be considered. Your overall health, gum health, available bone, medical history, and the location of the missing tooth all play a role in determining whether an implant is appropriate.

Dr. Hannahan will review your health history, examine your mouth, and evaluate your X-rays. If additional treatment or evaluation is needed before implant placement, she will explain that as part of the treatment-planning process.

Healthy gums and adequate bone support are important considerations in implant treatment, and implants still require regular home care and professional follow-up.

How Are Dental Implants Placed?

Before implant treatment begins, Dr. Hannahan will perform an examination and review the necessary X-rays to evaluate the area and the available bone.

An oral surgeon places the implant into the jawbone. The bone then heals around the implant and provides a stable foundation for the replacement tooth. Once healing is complete and the implant is ready to be restored, Dr. Hannahan creates the crown or other restoration that attaches to the implant.

Dr. Hannahan also restores existing dental implants when a patient already has an implant in place and needs a new or replacement restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a dental implant is right for me?

Dental implants can be an excellent way to replace missing teeth, but they are not the only option and may not be right for every patient. Your overall health, the health of your gums, the amount of available bone, the location of the missing tooth, and your individual goals all help determine whether an implant is a good choice.

Dr. Hannahan can evaluate your mouth and discuss the different ways a missing tooth may be replaced. If an implant is appropriate, she will explain the process and help you understand how it compares with other options such as a bridge or removable partial denture.
