Beyond the Practice

Service Beyond the Dental Office

Beyond her clinical practice, Dr. Katie is involved in education and community service. She serves as the Director of Community Dentistry and a Clinical Assistant Professor at Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine.

Additionally, she contributes to charitable initiatives such as Give Kids a Smile and Veterans Care Day and has previously served on the ADA’s Council on Advocacy and Access for Care.

She also shares her insights on dental health through her personal blog, morethanteeth.org .

Dr. Katie is a wife, mom to three boys, and enjoys all the craziness and wonder that comes with it. In her free time, she loves fitness, officiating basketball, and anything that gets her out into nature.

Whether exploring nature or serving her community, Dr. Katie brings passion and dedication to every aspect of her life.
