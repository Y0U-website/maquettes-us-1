Meet Dr. Lisa Baines: Your Smile…

Meet Dr. Lisa Baines , a dentist committed to continued growth and learning, always seeking the best for her patients. I’m also a founding member of the Airway, Sleep & Pediatrics Pathway group and a Breathe Ambassador trained in a functional approach to Frenuloplasty courtesy of the Breathe Institute.

One of my most important accomplishments was finishing the Pediatric Tongue and Lip Ties and Laser Frenectomies educational course through Global Laser Oral Health Education. Meet Dr. Lisa Baines , an expert in pediatric dental care, and learn how advanced techniques can help your child thrive.

I am a member of the Amplified Dynamics Leadership Team, focusing on building relationships and awareness of health both within the office and with patients. Lastly, my family participates in the Central Illinois Type 1 Diabetes Support Group. We can share information to help manage this disease and help our loved ones thrive instead of just survive.

We have a great relationship with our neighbors. They are a hard-working couple who loves (tolerates) our kids as much as we do! We often take turns making dinner for each other, as we all enjoy cooking, grilling, and experimenting with recipes. When doing yard projects, they gladly let the kids join in to move dirt, dig a hole, or other things. They are also known for their endless supply of stories. When the weather is right, we will sit by the fire in the evening for nights in a row. In this picture, the fire was so hot that we escaped the heat by laying down to toast our marshmallows.

We love outdoor activities, hunting, fishing, playing in the creek, riding bikes, and doing projects. One of those outdoor activities is having a garden. We try growing different items every year, but asparagus, tomatoes, and cucumbers are a staple. Lettuce varieties are fun because they grow so quickly and we can measure them with a ruler and track them within weeks. We are always amazed by how quickly asparagus grows. It can grow 3 inches or more overnight it seems. The kids love the asparagus and cucumbers, but I still can’t get them to like tomatoes.

I like llamas so I am imparting that love to my daughter. In the past, we could see llamas at the state fair, but recently we keep our eyes open for “Llamas Days” at different farms. They are so fluffy, have super visible teeth and I just think they are interesting and unique.

We have a bearded dragon! I am a cat lover but my sinuses and allergies don’t agree so when my 8-year old asked for a reptile I was ok with it! Little did we know that bearded dragons are kind of lazy and have sluggish metabolisms. Warm baths are incorporated in its care to keep it “regulated.”

I’ve always loved art and being creative. When my kids ask me to paint their faces on a random Wednesday, I usually agree. Correct, this was not Halloween. Just a normal Wednesday filled with fun and laughter!

My husband Steve is co-owner / operator of a roofing company, B & H Roofing . He has a biology medical science degree but enjoys being outdoors and found his way to roofing during his college summer job. One day our kids will convince him to put Christmas lights on our house. He refuses to get on a roof and do that in his off time!

Our kids love fishing. My oldest son would love to be a fisherman that “Gets paid” when he is older.
