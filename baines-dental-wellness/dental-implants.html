What Are Implant Retained Dentures

Did you know nearly 40 million Americans are missing all of their teeth? If you’re one of them or are interested in learning more about your oral health options, you need to know about implant-retained dentures and All-on-4 treatment. Both of these durable, dependable Springfield Dental Implants solutions are proven to give you back the freedom to eat and do what you like and take years off of your appearance.

Implant-retained dentures are affixed permanently in your mouth to secure posts. As the name suggests, All-on-4 is a type of implant-retained denture held together using four posts. No matter what type of Springfield Dental Implants you’re looking for, we can help.
