The practice

Every child should have access to high-quality dental care and at Bumble Bee Smiles we treat our patients like family.

Our caring team is dedicated to taking care of your family. We understand the importance of good oral care and will help your children develop good habits that will last for a lifetime.

We believe in proactive measures to safeguard your child’s oral health. That’s where dental sealants come in! These thin, protective coatings are applied to the chewing surfaces of your child’s back teeth, helping to prevent cavities and maintain their smile.

If you’re looking to give your child the best start in oral health, schedule an appointment with us to learn more about sealants!

Regular dental check-ups are essential to ensure that your child’s teeth and gums are developing properly and staying healthy.

Our experienced pediatric dentists perform thorough examinations to detect any potential issues early on. From checking for cavities and assessing tooth development to evaluating oral hygiene habits, our goal is to keep your child’s smile in its best shape.

Combined with gentle and effective cleanings, these visits empower your child with proper oral care knowledge and techniques, setting them on a path of lifelong dental health.

We work with you to maximize your insurance reimbursement for covered procedures. We accept Medicaid, SoonerCare, and take all fees of service for all other insurances.
