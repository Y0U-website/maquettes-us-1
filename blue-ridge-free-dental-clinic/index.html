Mission The Blue Ridge Free Dental Clinic’s mission is to deliver high-quality emergency, restorative, and preventative dental care to people who cannot otherwise afford these critically needed services. We restore lives—one tooth at a time.

Since its beginning in 2005, the Clinic has performed over 76,000 procedures and over 24,000 patient visits with a value exceeding 12 million dollars. Patients seeking care with our Clinic live in poverty (250 % FDL) with the financial burden of oral health services resulting in episodic and incomplete care.
