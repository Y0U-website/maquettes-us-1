Board of Directors 2025 Dr. Paul DeCarlo: Restoring Smiles, One Visit at a Time

Since 2015, Dr. Paul DeCarlo has generously volunteered his time each week at the Blue Ridge Free Dental Clinic, bringing his expertise in restorative dentistry—especially fillings and crowns—to patients in need. Beyond the treatment chair, Dr. DeCarlo has also helped guide the Clinic’s mission as a dedicated member of our Board of Directors for the past six years.

Help Us Restore Smiles—And Lives The Blue Ridge Free Dental Clinic provides life-changing emergency, restorative, and preventative dental care to those in Western North Carolina who simply can’t afford it. For many, we are their only option. We don’t just treat teeth—we restore confidence, dignity, and the ability to live without pain.

Since opening in 2005, the Clinic has provided over 76,000 procedures and welcomed more than 24,000 patient visits, delivering over $12 million in dental care—free of charge.

Our biggest challenge isn’t the need—it’s having enough staff to meet it. The cost of maintaining our dedicated team of professionals is our greatest ongoing expense. Without unrestricted donations for personnel, we simply cannot continue to provide the level of care our patients deserve—or expand to reach those still suffering in silence.

Your gift helps us do more than fix teeth. It helps someone smile without pain, eat without fear, and face the world with hope.

Please consider making a donation today. Your support truly changes lives—one smile at a time.
