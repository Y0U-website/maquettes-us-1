Accessibility Statement

  • A “Skip to main content” link and a visible keyboard focus indicator throughout
  • Full keyboard operability, with no keyboard traps
  • Labels, required-field indicators, and clear, text-based error messages on all forms (we never rely on color alone)
  • Sufficient color contrast and readable, resizable text (supports browser zoom to 200 %)
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences and large touch targets on mobile
  • An education-video section with titles and a plain-language summary; a clear disclaimer accompanies each

Documents (PDF)

Patient packet PDFs generated by this site use selectable text, a document title, a logical reading order, clear headings, and readable fonts. If you need a form in an alternative format, please contact the office nearest you and we will assist you.

Known limitations

Some third-party content, such as embedded maps and any future embedded videos, is provided by external services whose accessibility we do not fully control. We provide accessible alternatives (address text, phone numbers, and directions links) wherever possible.
