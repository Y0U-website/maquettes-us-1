Dental Checkups & Teeth Cleanings

Regular exams and cleanings are the bedrock for any long-term oral care routine. Without them, you can never know for sure if you’re doing everything right to keep plaque and dental disease at bay. Our exams are thorough, yet comfortable, so you can feel confident about your smile day-to-day. Our cleanings not only remove tartar (which you can’t remove at home), but they keep plaque to an absolute minimum. These visits accompanied with at-home brushing and flossing should keep your smile healthy for many, many years.

Nightguards for Bruxism

Did you know that stress doesn’t only affect your overall health? It also has the ability to damage your tooth enamel! Teeth grinding, or bruxism, is a common condition where you grind or clench your teeth together for long periods of time. It most often occurs while you sleep, which is why we prescribe nightguards! These oral appliances are designed to fit your smile exactly and give it the protection it needs throughout the night.

Dental Crowns

When teeth are too damaged to receive a filling or you need to cover a tooth following a dental bridge , dental crowns are the restorations we use. Not only are they customized to fit over your tooth securely, but they can be made from high-quality, translucent ceramic porcelain for a truly natural appearance. With proper care, you can expect them to last well over a decade and make chewing your favorite foods comfortable and easy.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

In the past, you may have received a dental filling made from silver amalgam material, a metal alloy that is durable, yet noticeable in your smile. You may even notice some sensitivity from your treated tooth if you’ve had the filling for a long time. The good news is we can replace your silver filling with one made from composite resin, a material that can be easily molded and shaded to match your existing enamel. Most fillings only take one visit to complete, yet they offer 10 years of protection for your natural tooth.

Veneers

Like many other types of cosmetic dentistry, Dental veneers are one treatment that you don’t want others to notice. Our dental office is happy to provide highly personalized veneers to hide chips, cracks, permanent stains, and even small gaps in between teeth. With our in-house custom staining process (both for crowns and veneers) and a 4-day veneer turnaround time, you can easily and quickly transform your smile. Even patients who are just visiting for a few days can get the smile of their dreams sooner than ever.
