The Dental Implant Process in Columbia, MO

Getting dental implants is a process that requires precision and planning, but the long-term results are well worth it. Here’s what you can expect:

1. Comprehensive Consultation & Planning

Dr. Foote will conduct a thorough examination of your mouth, jawbone, and overall health. Using advanced 3D imaging, we’ll create a precise treatment plan tailored to your unique needs.

2. Implant Placement Surgery

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This is a precise procedure performed with comfort in mind.

3. Osseointegration & Healing

This is the most critical phase. Over the next 3-6 months, your jawbone naturally fuses with the implant in a process called osseointegration, creating a rock-solid foundation.

4. Abutment Placement

Once healed, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. The gum tissue is allowed to heal around it.

5. Your Custom Restoration

Finally, we attach your permanent, custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture. The restoration is designed to match your natural teeth in size, shape, and color, completing your smile.

The Life-Changing Benefits of Dental Implants

Why choose dental implants over other tooth replacement options? The advantages are profound:

  • Natural Look & Feel: Implants function and appear like your own teeth.
  • Preserve Jawbone Health: They stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically follows tooth loss.
  • Restored Chewing Power: Eat all your favorite foods with confidence and without pain or slippage.
  • Improved Speech: Say goodbye to mumbling or slipping that can happen with ill-fitting dentures.
  • Long-Lasting & Durable: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a wise long-term investment.
  • Enhanced Oral Hygiene: Unlike bridges, implants allow for easy flossing and cleaning between teeth.
