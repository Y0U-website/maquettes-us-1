Meet Dr. Lauren Frisch

Our clinical practice combines fundamental treatment techniques with new technologies for predictable, excellent results.

The practice

Dr. Frisch is originally from Michigan and studied Neuroscience at the University of Michigan. She also speaks Spanish, having studied at the University of Salamanca in Salamanca, Spain. Dr. Frisch then trained at the University of California, San Francisco to earn her Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) and Masters in Orthodontics.

Dr. Frisch is Board Certified by the American Board of Orthodontics and is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, San Diego County Dental Society, San Diego Association of Orthodontists, Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists, and American Association of Orthodontists.
