2. Website Analytics

We want to understand how visitors use our site without spying on them. We use Fathom Analytics , a privacy-centric tool that is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR.

  • No Cookies: Fathom does not use cookies to track you.
  • Anonymity: All data is processed anonymously. We can see which pages are popular, but we cannot identify you personally, nor do we track your behavior across other websites.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide a better experience, we use a small number of third-party tools. We have configured them to be as private as possible:

  • Local Fonts: Unlike many websites, we do not use Google Fonts servers. Our fonts are hosted on our own secure server. This prevents Google from seeing your IP address when you load our pages.
  • YouTube (Video): We use YouTube’s Privacy-Enhanced Mode. Additionally, we use a click-to-play system. No data is shared with YouTube or Google until you explicitly click the Play button on a video.
  • Google Maps: Our contact page features an embedded Google Map to help you find our office. By interacting with this map, Google may collect your IP address and set cookies in accordance with their own privacy policy.

4. No Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to ask what information we hold about you or to request that we delete your contact information from our digital records.
