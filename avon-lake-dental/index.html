The practice

Combining advanced dental technology with years of clinical expertise, we provide precise, conservative treatments designed to protect your natural smile while achieving beautiful, lasting results. We take the time to understand your unique goals, explain every step of your care, and ensure you feel confident in every decision.

Our commitment to excellence extends beyond clinical care, we strive to create an environment where comfort, trust and attention to detail come together seamlessly. Whether your visiting for preventive care, restorative treatment or a complete smile transformation, our goal is to help you enjoy a lifetime of healthy, confident smiles.

We believe in treating every patient with dignity and respect, whether you’re coming in for a routine cleaning or a major restoration.

We stay current with the latest dental technologies and techniques to provide precise, high-quality care.

We offer customized dental plans that address your specific oral health needs — whether you need preventive cleaning, cosmetic enhancement, or full-scale restorative work.

Our office is conveniently located on the corner of Moore Walker Road. We strive to make your time spent with us as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Routine dental cleanings and exams help prevent oral health issues and detect problems early. During your visit, Dr. Pyke will perform a comprehensive exam, including digital X-rays and intraoral photos, evaluate your oral health, address any concerns, and recommend personalized care to keep your smile healthy and beautiful.

Professional teeth whitening is a simple and effective way to brighten your smile and reduce staining caused by everyday food and beverages including but not limited to, coffee, tea, wine tobacco. We create customized whitening trays designed for a comfortable, precise fit. We will provide professional-strength whitening gel for safe and effective at-home treatment.
