Dental Implants in Briarcliff…

  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution and can last 25-30 years or even a lifetime with proper care. The titanium implant post fuses with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation that won’t shift or decay like natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings are essential for implant longevity. Most patients find that implants feel and function just like their natural teeth.
  • Am I a candidate for dental implants in Briarcliff Manor? Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for this treatment, provided they have adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your oral health, bone structure, and medical history to determine if implants are right for you. Age is rarely a limiting factor, and even patients with some bone loss may be candidates with bone grafting procedures.
  • What’s the dental implant procedure like? The dental implant process typically involves three phases over 3-6 months. First, we surgically place the titanium implant post into your jawbone. After healing and osseointegration (bone fusion), we attach an abutment to the implant. Finally, we place your custom crown. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and can return to work the next day. We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure proper healing.
  • How do dental implants compare to dentures or bridges? This treatment offers superior stability, comfort, and longevity compared to traditional dentures or bridges. Unlike dentures, implants don’t slip or require adhesives. Unlike bridges, implants don’t require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth. They also help preserve jawbone structure and provide the most natural chewing and speaking experience. While the initial investment is higher, implants often prove more cost-effective long-term.
