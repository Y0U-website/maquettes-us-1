The Bone Grafting Process at Briarcliff Dental Care

Understanding what to expect can go a long way toward easing any anxiety you may have about the procedure. Bone grafting is performed with local anesthesia, and sedation options are available for patients who need additional comfort. The appointment typically takes under an hour, depending on the extent of the augmentation needed.

During the procedure, your provider will gently open the gum tissue at the treatment site and place the grafting material directly into the area of bone loss. A membrane is often placed over the graft to protect it and encourage proper healing. The site is then sutured closed, and the healing process begins. Over the following weeks and months, your body gradually incorporates the new bone material and builds new tissue around it, creating the density needed for implant placement.

Healing and Timeline

Healing from a bone graft takes time, and patience is genuinely part of the process. Most patients will need to allow three to six months for the graft to fully integrate before the implant can be placed. During this period, our team closely monitors your progress and uses advanced imaging to confirm when the site is ready for the next stage of treatment.

Post-procedure care is straightforward. Patients are typically advised to stick to softer foods, maintain gentle oral hygiene habits, and avoid any pressure or trauma to the treated area. Discomfort is manageable and usually subsides within a week. Our team will provide thorough, personalized aftercare instructions to make your recovery as smooth as possible.

Why Bone Grafting Is Worth the Wait

It is natural to feel impatient when a multi-step treatment plan stands between you and the smile you are working toward. But understanding why bone grafting matters puts the timeline in a different light. Attempting to place an implant in a jaw without adequate bone density is not only ineffective; it also risks implant failure and can lead to more severe complications down the road. Bone grafting is the step that sets every subsequent step up for success.
