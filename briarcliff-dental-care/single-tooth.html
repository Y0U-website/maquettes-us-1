Why Choose a Dental Implant Over Other Options?

When a tooth is missing, patients are typically presented with three options: an implant, a fixed bridge, or a removable partial denture. Each approach has its place, but single tooth implants carry distinct advantages that make them the preferred choice for many patients. A bridge requires grinding down the healthy teeth on either side to serve as anchors, a permanent alteration to teeth that were otherwise intact. A partial denture, while removable and more affordable upfront, can shift over time and does not prevent the bone loss that occurs when a root is absent.

An implant, by contrast, actually stimulates the jawbone just as a natural tooth root does. This prevents the bone resorption that commonly leads to the sunken facial appearance associated with long-term tooth loss. It also eliminates the need to compromise any neighboring teeth. When you visit our restorative dentistry team in Briarcliff Manor, we take the time to review all options with you and help you understand which path aligns with your health, goals, and budget.

One of the most common questions patients have is how long the implant process takes.
