All on 4 All on 6 Dental Implants in Jersey City & North Bergen

Many people facing tooth loss find themselves at a crossroads, uncertain whether to opt for All-on-4 or All-on-6 dental implants. Both methods are effective full-mouth restoration options, but they cater to different needs and conditions.

At Ararat Dental, we offer both All-on-4 and All-on-6 dental implants, along with comprehensive consultations to determine which option is best suited for your individual needs.

All-on-4 dental implant

The All-on-4 dental implant technique utilizes four implants to support a new set of fixed teeth, either in the upper or lower jaw. This approach doesn’t require bone grafting and involves placing two vertical implants at the front and two angled implants at the back of the mouth. This setup supports a hybrid type of denture, which is fixed and offers a substantial improvement without needing to replace each tooth individually.

All-on-6 dental implant

Conversely, the All-on-6 dental implant method uses six implants to secure a dental bridge. This option might necessitate bone grafting due to the greater number of implants, which provides increased stability and support, particularly beneficial for those with significant bone loss in the jaw. The additional implants in the All-on-6 method also enhance the overall function and longevity of the dental prosthesis.

Both implant methods share the primary benefit of replacing missing teeth effectively, providing a stable foundation for false teeth that do not slip or move. The implants allow for the attachment of fixed dentures, giving patients a more natural feel and efficient chewing function compared to traditional removable dentures.

Dental implants offer effective solutions for tooth replacement, they serve different needs based on the patient’s oral health status and bone density.

It is crucial to consult with a dental specialist to determine which system best suits your specific conditions and goals. Visiting Ararat Dental for a personalized consultation will provide clarity and direction for your dental restoration needs.
