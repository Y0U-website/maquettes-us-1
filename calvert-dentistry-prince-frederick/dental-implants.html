Dental Implants in Prince Frederick, MD

Dental implants are a durable, natural-looking solution to replace missing teeth. The titanium posts are surgically placed in the jawbone and topped with custom restorations, offering comfort and stability unmatched by traditional options. Whether you need a single implant, a full-arch restoration or implant-retained dentures, our expert team will create a personalized treatment plan to restore your smile.

Process of Dental Implants

Initial Consultation We begin with a comprehensive exam, including digital X-rays, and discuss your goals and medical history. This helps us determine the best implant option for you—whether it’s a single implant, implant-retained denture or All-on-4.

Treatment Planning We create a customized treatment plan and timeline based on your needs. If bone density is a concern, we may recommend a bone graft before implant placement.

Implant Placement The implant—a small titanium post—is surgically placed into your jawbone. It acts as a sturdy root for your new tooth or restoration.

Next Steps After Getting Dental Implants

Healing and Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with your jawbone in a critical process called osseointegration.

Abutment and Impression Once healed, we place a small connector called an abutment on the implant and take impressions of your mouth to design your custom crown, bridge or denture.

Final Restoration Your custom restoration is secured to the implant, completing the process. We ensure that it looks natural, fits comfortably and restores full function.

FAQ

  • Are dental implants a long-term solution for missing teeth? Yes, dental implants are designed to last decades with proper care, making them one of the most durable tooth-replacement options available.
  • Do dental implants require special cleaning or care? No, just brush, floss and schedule regular dental checkups like you would for natural teeth.
  • Can dental implants support dentures or bridges? Absolutely. Implants can secure full dentures, partials or bridges.

Related Services

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Dental crowns and bridges are custom-made restorations designed to repair damaged teeth or replace missing ones. Crowns cap and protect compromised teeth, while bridges fill the gaps left by one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth for support. These durable solutions restore function and aesthetics.

Dentures

Dentures are a reliable solution for replacing missing teeth, whether you need a full set or just a few replacements. We offer complete and partial dentures, custom-made to fit comfortably and restore natural function. With proper care and regular dental visits, dentures can help you smile confidently and eat with ease.
