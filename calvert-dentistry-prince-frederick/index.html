Dental crowns and bridges are custom-made restorations designed to repair damaged teeth or replace missing ones. Crowns cap and protect compromised teeth, while bridges fill the gaps left by one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth for support. These durable solutions restore function and aesthetics.
