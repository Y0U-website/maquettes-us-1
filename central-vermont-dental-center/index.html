0 Restorative Dentistry Involves the repair of a tooth damaged by cavities, tooth wear, chips or fractures

Typically covered in part or in whole by insurances like Delta Dental, CBA Blue, Green Mountain Care.

Wisdom teeth commonly don’t have enough room to fully erupt.

Ideal age for removal is teenage years to early twenties in order to minimize risks and easy recovery

Many patients seek treatment in our office because of Dr. Adlers training in sedation for dentistry at Duquesne University 2018.

This is a greater level of sedation than “laughing gas” and combines two medications, one for discomfort, and one to help you relax.
