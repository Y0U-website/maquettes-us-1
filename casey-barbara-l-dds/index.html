The practice

At Chesapeake Health Care, we proudly serve the healthcare needs of the communities of the three lower counties of the Eastern Shore of Maryland. Since our founding, we’ve been committed to providing accessible, compassionate, and comprehensive healthcare to every member of our community.

With over 170 providers across 22 locations in Somerset, Wicomico, and Worcester counties, we offer accessible, comprehensive care to our community. Our team includes board-certified physicians, nurse practitioners, dentists, behavioral health specialists, and support staff dedicated to your wellbeing. Our Mission : To improve the health and wellness of the communities we serve by providing comprehensive, integrated, patient-centered healthcare services that are accessible, affordable, and of the highest quality. We believe that quality healthcare is a right, not a privilege, and we work tirelessly to remove barriers to care for all Eastern Shore residents.

As a Community Health Center, we offer financial and insurance assistance (including a sliding-fee scale, based on your household income and family size), interpretation services in multiple languages, transportation assistance for medical appointments, case management to help navigate healthcare systems, prescription discounts through the 340B program, and laboratory service discounts. Standard Chesapeake Health Care office hours are from 8:30 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Friday . However, certain offices have extended hours including early morning and evening appointments to accommodate your work schedule. Please visit our locations page for specific hours, phone numbers, services offered, and directions for each individual health center.

If you are unable to keep your appointment, please give us at least 24 hours’ notice. This allows us to schedule other patients. Also, please report any changes in your name, address, phone number, or insurance information when you phone or visit the health center.
