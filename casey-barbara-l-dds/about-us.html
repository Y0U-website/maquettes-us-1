About Us

Who We Are

Our Mission

Chesapeake Health Care’s mission is to provide affordable, culturally competent patient and family-centered health care, leading to individual and community wellness to residents of the three lower counties in Maryland, regardless of their ability to pay.

Our Vision

The vision of Chesapeake Health Care is to have a community that comes together in health, to eliminate the suffering of disease and social conditions of our neighbors by expanding service and decreasing disparities.

Values

The values of Chesapeake Health Care are the value of Life, Affordability and Availability, Listening and Learning Skills, Unity, and Empathy.

Chesapeake Health Care is a Health Center Program grantee under 42 U.S.C. 254b, and a deemed Public Health Services employee under 42 U.S.C. 233 (g)-(n) covered by FTCA.

This health center receives HHS funding and has Federal Public Health Service (PHS) deemed status with respect to certain health or health-related claims, including medical malpractice claims, for itself and its covered individuals.

Our History

Three Lower Counties Community Services, Inc. was incorporated in 1994 to provide primary medical and dental care to patients in Somerset, Wicomico, and Worcester counties in Maryland. Since then, our services have expanded to include care for migrant/seasonal farm workers, mental health, pharmacy, obstetrics and gynecology, maternal-fetal medicine, dental, and pediatrics.

In 2016, we changed our name from Three Lower Counties Community Services to Chesapeake Health Care to more accurately reflect our core mission, which is to provide affordable, culturally competent patient and family-centered health care. As a Community Health Center, we serve the healthcare needs of the three lower counties of the Eastern Shore of Maryland.

In 2017, we opened a new OB/GYN facility, which is located in the Woodbrooke Medical Campus in Salisbury. This 26,700 sq. ft. facility has 32 exam rooms. We have providers who specialize in obstetrics, gynecology, and maternal fetal medicine.

Our employee workforce has grown to more than 650 people. In 2025, more than 65,000 patients received healthcare services. We continue to grow and provide exemplary service to our patients.
