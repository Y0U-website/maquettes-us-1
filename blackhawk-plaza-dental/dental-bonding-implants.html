Dental Bonding Implants

In Dental Bonding, Dr. Cai uses tooth-shaded composite resin to rejuvenate worn-down teeth, correct cracks and chips, and reshape the appearance of dental enamel.

For dental implants, titanium posts are permanently anchored into the jaw to replace missing teeth. They are then capped with natural-looking restorations so they look and feel like natural teeth.
