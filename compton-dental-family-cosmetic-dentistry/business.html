About

Formerly known as Albertville Cosmetic Dentistry, Compton Dental is set in the heart of Albertville. Our mission as a dental practice is to serve patients of all ages to the best of our ability, with excellent service and care.

Our mission as a dental team in Albertville, AL is to be an integral part of the community. We strive to help the families in this area become educated about their dental health and maintain and repair healthy, beautiful teeth.
