Brenham Orthodontics is locally owned and has four convenient locations. Our dedicated and compassionate team cares for families from Brenham, TX, and the surrounding communities of Bellville, Columbus, and Giddings.

Patient comfort is our top priority. From traditional braces to clear aligners, retainers, and self-ligating braces, our office, under the guidance of Dr. Troy Gor, handles the orthodontic needs of your whole family under one roof.
