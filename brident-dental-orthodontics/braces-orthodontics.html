Braces orthodontics FAQs What does an orthodontist do? Orthodontists specialize in reshaping smiles. They complete advanced training to understand how teeth, jaws, and facial structure work together. They’re experts in diagnosing alignment issues and designing personalized treatment plans using braces or aligners.

  • 1. Planning: Your treatment starts with a free exam. A board-eligible orthodontist will review your dental history and perform a full clinical evaluation. We’ll take X-rays and photos of your teeth to create a personalized treatment plan.
  • 2. Active Treatment: This is when your braces or aligners are placed. You will visit us periodically for adjustments to ensure your teeth are moving correctly and comfortably toward their new positions.
  • 2. Retention: Once your teeth are straight, we provide a custom retainer. This prevents your teeth from shifting back to ensure your new smile stays perfectly in place.
