The practice

Achieving a beautiful smile is sometimes as simple as understanding how to use dental insurance to your advantage. Our staff will help explain what insurance we accept and how you can best use it .

Dental insurance plans typically cover the cost of routine dental care, such as cleanings, check-ups, and x-rays. It also often covers a portion of the cost of necessary dental procedures, such as fillings, extractions, root canals, crowns, and potentially other cosmetic dentistry services.

We offer cleanings, check-ups, fillings due to cavities, Invisalign and whitening. Not to mention, extractions, root canals, crowns, and dental implants.

This is your one-stop shop for everything dentistry. All of these services are offered in clean, comfortable chairs, with your choice of Netflix, HBO Max, & Apple TV+ . Need to zone out? We’ve got you covered (headphones are available upon request).

Brushery was founded with the intent to create a modern dental office in a great location, with an experience people would want to tell someone about .
