About Us

At Chatham Dental Boutique, we strive to offer high quality dentistry in a caring and nurturing way.

Location: 466 Southern Boulevard, Chatham, NJ 07928 .

Dr. Singh has been providing exceptional dental care in NJ since 2011 with Chatham Dental Boutique opening in 2022.

As soon as you walk in our doors, you will know you are in excellent hands at Chatham Dental Boutique. You will be welcomed with a smile and seen on time for your appointment. Dr. Ramnik Singh and her team only use the latest techniques and finest materials in dentistry to deliver comfortable, long-lasting results. Our office provides a relaxing environment, equipped with modern amenities, including a coffee bar with coffee, water bottles, ceiling-mounted TVs with wireless headphones, and blankets.

Dr. Ramnik Singh’s intent is to serve the neighborhood and bring her experience to the community. She wants her patients to leave Chatham Dental Boutique wowed by the quality of care and with a sound understanding of what treatment choice they made and why. Dr. Ramnik Singh wants to educate her patients so they can be partners in their dental health.

Our office is located in picturesque Chatham Township in a building named after our second president, John Adams! Our waiting room overlooks a beautiful courtyard with trees that blossom during the spring time, and our operatories have the views of the lush Fairmont golf course. We welcome you to enjoy these serene surroundings from our open, well-lit office.
