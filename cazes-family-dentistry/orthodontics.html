Orthodontic Options for All Ages, from Teens to Adults

Cazes Family Dentistry - Orthodontics & TMJ A wide range of orthodontic solutions is available to enhance smiles for every family member, whether they’re young adults or more seasoned individuals. While we take pride in perfecting your smile, orthodontic care also plays a crucial role in improving your dental health. Misaligned teeth and an uneven bite not only make cleaning difficult but can also cause discomfort and even pain. Fortunately, orthodontic treatments, such as braces can address these problems, sometimes in as little as six months.

How to Determine If Orthodontics Is Right for You

Consider orthodontic treatment if you encounter any of the following issues:

  • Overbite: Your upper teeth protrude significantly.
  • Spacing: Noticeable gaps between your teeth.
  • Underbite: Your lower teeth extend past your upper teeth.
  • Crowding: Teeth overlap or are too close to floss effectively.
  • Crossbite: Your upper and lower teeth don’t align properly.
  • Pain: Persistent headaches, jaw discomfort, or pain in the upper back and neck.

Orthodontic Treatment Options

A variety of orthodontic treatment options are available to suit different personal requirements and budgets. Here are some common choices:

Traditional Braces

Often the first image that comes to mind with braces is bulky metal brackets and wires. However, today’s brackets are much smaller, less obtrusive, yet equally effective. They are simpler to maintain and more comfortable to wear. We provide braces right here in Washington Township. Our dental clinic is a one-stop-shop for you dentist and orthodontist needs.

Clear Braces

For those who prefer a less noticeable option, clear braces are a popular choice, especially among adults. These braces offer the effectiveness of traditional orthodontics with a stealthier appearance, making it hard for anyone to notice at a glance that you’re wearing them.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners, like Invisalign , have become increasingly popular. These are custom-made, transparent trays that gradually move your teeth to their optimal position. They fit tightly yet comfortably and can be removed for eating, oral hygiene, or important social and professional events.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age to get braces?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by the age of seven. This early assessment allows orthodontists to detect and evaluate any issues that may need early intervention, and even prep for braces. However, orthodontic treatment isn’t just for children; adults can also benefit from orthodontics, such as braces, as teeth can be adjusted at any age.

How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?

The duration of orthodontic treatment varies widely depending on the individual’s dental issues, the type of treatment selected, and how well the patient complies with care guidelines. On average, treatment with braces or aligners typically lasts between 18 to 36 months, but simpler cases may require less time, and more complex situations might need longer.

Are there any risks associated with orthodontic treatment?

While orthodontic treatments are generally safe, they do carry some risks. These can include minor tooth decay due to inadequate brushing and flossing around brackets and aligners, potential root resorption (shortening of tooth roots), or loss of correction if retainers are not worn as advised post-treatment. It’s important to follow all care instructions and maintain regular check-ups to mitigate these risks.

What are the differences between metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners?

Metal braces consist of traditional metal brackets attached to the teeth, connected by wires, and periodically tightened to align the teeth. Ceramic braces work similarly but are made with tooth-colored or clear materials, making them less visible. Clear aligners are nearly invisible trays that fit over the teeth, which can be removed for eating and cleaning. Each type has its advantages, depending on the patient’s needs and aesthetic preferences.

How to take care of your teeth with braces?

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is crucial during any orthodontic treatment. This involves regular brushing and flossing to remove food particles and plaque from around braces or with aligners. Using a fluoride toothpaste and mouthwash can help prevent tooth decay and gum problems. Additionally, it’s important to avoid hard and sticky foods that can damage orthodontic devices. Regular visits to your orthodontist or dentist for adjustments and cleanings are also vital!
