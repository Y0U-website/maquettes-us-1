Frequently Asked Questions

What Does Cosmetic Dentistry Include?

Cosmetic dentistry encompasses various treatments and procedures aimed at enhancing the appearance of your teeth, gums, and bite. The primary focus is on improving the color, shape, position, size, and overall look of your teeth and mouth. While cosmetic treatments often enhance comfort and function, their main goal is aesthetic improvement.

How Can I Correct My Smile?

The term correct can mean different things depending on your goals. If you want a brighter smile, professional teeth whitening might be enough. For more significant changes, you might consider options like dental bonding , composite fillings , orthodontics , veneers , crowns, implants , or bridges. We’ll create a personalized treatment plan based on your desired results, whether you want a healthy look or a glamorous transformation.

Can I Get a Smile Makeover Without Braces?

Yes, there are alternatives to braces for fixing crooked or misshapen teeth and gaps. Dental bonding or veneers can address these issues. Additionally, Invisalign clear aligners offer a discreet orthodontic solution for straightening teeth.

How Do I Fix My Missing Tooth/Teeth?

To replace missing teeth, options include dental implants, bridges , and other prosthetics like partial or full dentures . The best solution for you depends on the number of missing teeth, their location, your preferences, and your budget.

How Long Does a Cosmetic Dentistry Procedure Take?

The duration of cosmetic dentistry procedures varies based on the treatment. Teeth whitening can be completed in a single visit, while veneers or crowns might require a couple of appointments. More extensive procedures like implants could take several months from start to finish, allowing for healing time between steps.
