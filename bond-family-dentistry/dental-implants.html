FAQs About Dental Implants in Everett

  • How long does the process take? Treatment can take several months. Healing time is important for the implant post to bond with the jawbone and create a strong base.
  • What is the cost of dental implants? The cost of dental implants can vary depending on factors like bone grafting, number of implants, and restorations. We offer flexible payment options to help patients move forward.
  • Is there discomfort after surgery? Most patients feel mild discomfort after the procedure. This improves within a few days. Cold compresses and rest can help you recover quickly.
  • Can implants fail? Implant failure is rare but possible. Good oral health, regular dental checkups, and proper care lower your risk and support long-term success.
  • Are implants better than dentures or bridges? Implants offer a stronger and more natural tooth replacement option. Unlike traditional dentures or dental bridges, implants support the jawbone and feel more secure when you eat.
