Our Dental Practice

Our mission

Dentistry that puts your family first

Avondale Dental has been helping the Hayden Lake community smile brighter since 1976. Our building was originally Aunt Urma’s convenience store! Our practice strives to lead with purpose, setting standards through community stewardship, integrated healthcare, tech-driven efficiency, and memorable experiences that leave lasting impressions on all we serve.

Whether it’s been a while since your last dental check-up or your family is searching for a new dental home, Avondale Dental is your go-to dental provider in Hayden Lake. We see patients of all dental backgrounds and never judge or lecture patients. Our team focuses on finding personalized solutions to put each patient on a path to optimal oral health.

Our office offers a comfortable atmosphere with modern technology and amenities like blankets, headphones, water, coffee, and sedation options. Give us a call or book your first appointment online to start your path toward your best smile.

Dr. Jon Goddard, DMD

Dr. Jon Goddard (pronounced God-erd) is joining the Avondale Dental family! Dr. Goddard graduated summa cum laude from Oregon State University before completing his Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Oregon Health & Science University. Dr. Goddard has been practicing for years in Texas while his wife finished her orthodontia residency, and they’re both excited to return home to the Pacific Northwest.

Dr. Goddard is passionate about using his expertise and keen eye for detail to improve people’s lives. He treats each patient as he would a family member, which is what makes dentistry so meaningful to him. Dr. Goddard keeps his skills sharp through his involvement in several top tier organizations, such as the ADA, ISDA, and OKU Dental Honor Society.

When he’s not busy helping patients, Dr. Goddard can usually be found fishing, hunting, snowboarding, working on his (self-proclaimed) mediocre golf game, and enjoying time with his wife, family, and friends.
