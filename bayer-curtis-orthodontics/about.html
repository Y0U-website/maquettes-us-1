About Us | Orthodontist Fort Walton Beach

We want you to smile with joy!

Let’s get to know each other! Bayer & Curtis Orthodontics is proud to serve the Fort Walton Beach area, offering advanced orthodontics that deliver radiant smiles. Combining experience, warmth, and the latest technology, we’re ready to cater to the needs of your entire family.

Dr. Curtis understands that no one succeeds on their own, and as such, she has always made an effort to give back to her community. Some of her fondest memories include assisting with the “Special Smiles” program during the Special Olympics and receiving prestigious awards such as the Award of Excellence given by the ASDA (American Student Dental Association) and the Alumni Scholarship award. She continues to participate in community outreach dentistry programs such as “Give Kids a Smile”, “Mission of Mercy”, and “Smiles Change Lives”.

Dr. Curtis enjoys spending time with her husband, Damian Curtis, on the Emerald Coast basking in the sun or in the Alabama woods to enjoy hunting, nature, and relaxation. Dr. Curtis and her husband have three beautiful children, Hayden, Martha Rylee, and Clark. The kids keep their lives interesting, busy, and exciting.

Meet the Team

Office Supporting the Community Dr. Leigh Curtis encourages everyone to smile! She is involved with Smiles Change Lives, a non-profit organization that assists children and adolescents who need braces and other orthodontic treatment but cannot afford it. Dr. Curtis also supports the community by sponsoring Girl and Boy Scout projects, local teams, and making contributions to patients’ activities.
