About Cole Family Dental in Athens

At Cole Family Dental, we provide patients of all ages with exceptional and compassionate dental care in a warm and comfortable atmosphere. From routine cleanings and check-ups to life-changing smile makeovers, Dr. Cole and our team can help you achieve the healthy, attractive smile and lifestyle you have always imagined.

Complete Family Dental Care

We want to help all patients achieve and maintain healthy smiles so they can live life to the fullest. Come to us for comprehensive family dental care. Our general dental services include everything from children’s dentistry to senior dental care and everything in between. Complete dental care also includes education. We also empower you to make wise, well-informed decisions for your oral health through patient education and individual consultation.

Modern Dental Equipment

We have equipped our office with advanced technology and family-friendly amenities, such as massaging dental chairs and TVs in our treatment rooms. We use industry-leading dental technology to provide comfortable, efficient dental care. Count our local dentists to deliver high-quality, modern care.

Warm, Comfortable Environment

We treat all patients with care, doing everything we can to make kids, adults, and seniors have a positive dental experience. Our friendly team strives to offer a comfortable, enjoyable visit for all.

We go above and beyond the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA-recommended standards for cleanliness and safety. Before your visit, we disinfect all of our equipment in a designated area with state-of-the-art sterilization technology. We also thoroughly sanitize each room between patients. We worry about the cleanliness of our office so you’ll never have to.

Dentist Dr. Adam Cole

Cole Family Dental is led by Dr. Adam Cole. As a local dentist, Dr. Cole was born and raised in Athens. He entered the field of dentistry to help people in his community live happier, healthier lives.
