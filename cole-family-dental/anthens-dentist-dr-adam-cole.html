Dr. Adam Cole

Why Did You Become a Dentist?

Dr. Adam Cole was born and raised in Athens, and had always planned on coming back home when he finished school.

In college, he decided to pursue a career in dentistry so that he could help people live happier, healthier lives. For Dr. Cole, dentistry is a powerful vessel for helping people improve their quality of life. He has a passion for helping people with their dental needs and he strives to make every visit to the dentist an enjoyable one.

It means the world to him to return to his hometown as a local dentist in Athens. He focuses on family-oriented dental care to help patients throughout the community improve their oral health and overall wellness.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Cole had an interesting path to dentistry. He graduated from Auburn University Summa Cum Laude with a bachelor’s degree in Zoology. He then earned his DMD in 2009 from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry, where he was the vice president of his class.

Since graduating, he’s continued to foster his knowledge and skillset in dentistry through continued education and dental organization involvement. He is a member of several dental organizations, including the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.
