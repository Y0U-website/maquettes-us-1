Services

Dental Implants From Single tooth replacement to full arch replacement, we offer it all at Alani Dental & Implant Center.

Full Mouth Reconstruction We can restore your smile, bite, and overall mouth health using multiple crowns, veneers, or implants to achieve a full mouth reconstruction

Preventive Dentistry Our skilled team provide thorough check-ups, Cleanings, Periodontal care and maintenance, Fluoride treatment, Oral Cancer Screening, and more to keep your teeth healthy and strong.

Sedation Dentistry If you have Dental fear and Anxiety, we offer options to help you relax for your treatment. We offer Nitrous Oxide (laughing gas) as well and Oral Conscious Sedation for more extensive procedures

Restorative Dentistry We offer composite tooth colored fillings for treatment of cavities.

Same Day Crowns We offer same day crowns that are strong and sturdy to save teeth

Dentures Get the smile of your dreams with our custom-made Full Dentures and Partial Dentures. We also offer Over Dentures that snaps onto dental implants for anchorage

Root Canals Our gentle and friendly dentist eliminates pain and infection by performing root canal therapy for anterior and premolar teeth
