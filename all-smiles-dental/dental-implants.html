Our Dental Implant Services

Implant Options at All Smiles Dental

Every mouth is different, so we offer several implant solutions depending on what you need:

Single Tooth Implants Best for replacing one missing tooth. We place one implant topped with a custom crown. It’s a standalone solution that doesn’t affect your other teeth at all.

Implant-Supported Bridges Best for replacing multiple teeth in a row. Instead of grinding down healthy teeth (like a traditional bridge requires), we anchor the bridge to implants on either end. Stronger, longer-lasting, and better for your oral health.

All-on-4® Full Arch Restoration Best for replacing all teeth on the top or bottom (or both). Using just four strategically placed implants per arch, we can secure a full set of beautiful, permanent teeth. Many patients call this new teeth in a day —and it truly can be life-changing.

Mini Dental Implants Best for patients with lower bone density who may not be candidates for traditional implants. Mini implants are smaller in diameter, require less bone, and involve a less invasive procedure. They’re also great for stabilizing loose dentures.

Bone Grafting & Sinus Lifts If you’ve been missing teeth for a while, your jawbone may have thinned out. No worries—bone grafting can rebuild the foundation you need before we place your implants. We handle it all right here in our office.

Why Bismarck Patients Love Dental Implants

  • They last. With proper care, dental implants can last 25 years—often a lifetime. Compare that to dentures (5-10 years) or bridges (10-15 years) that need replacing.
  • They look completely natural. Your crown is custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. Most people can’t tell the difference.
  • They protect your jawbone. When you lose a tooth, the bone underneath starts to shrink (it’s called resorption). Implants are the only replacement option that stimulates the bone and prevents that sunken face look over time.
  • They stay put. No slipping, no clicking, no embarrassing moments. Implants are anchored in your jaw, so they’re as stable as your natural teeth.
  • You can eat anything. Steak, apples, corn on the cob, crusty bread —nothing is off-limits. That’s freedom denture wearers simply don’t have.

Step 4: Healing Period (Osseointegration)

Here’s where the magic happens. Over the next 3-6 months, your jawbone grows around the implant and fuses with it—a process called osseointegration. Don’t worry, you won’t be walking around with a gap. We’ll give you a temporary tooth so you can eat, talk, and smile normally while you heal.

Once your implant is fully integrated, we attach the abutment and take impressions for your permanent crown. Your crown is custom-crafted to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. When it’s placed, you’re done—walk out with a complete, beautiful smile that’s built to last.
