Sawtooth Dental has been proudly serving the Twin Falls, Hagerman, and Shoshone areas for 46 years. Dr. Gary Dixon started the practice in 1972. Then in the mid-1970s, he opened an office in Shoshone to help better and serve everyone there. Dr. Eric Thomas joined Sawtooth Dental in 1997. After three years of being a dentist with Dr. Dixon, Dr. Thomas wanted to take his services and, thus, opened…

In 2012 Sawtooth built the building that they are in now to serve their patients better. After Dr. Gary Dixon retired, Dr. Brent Sorenson joined the practice in 2015. Having three dentists at Sawtooth will provide extensive care to the Twin Falls, Hagerman, and Shoshone areas. We love the opportunity to be around everyone in the Magic Valley.
