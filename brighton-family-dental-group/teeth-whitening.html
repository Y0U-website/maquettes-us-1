Which teeth whitening service is right for me?

Crest White Strips-Professional Strength Brighton Family Dental can provide our patients with Crest White Strips in a Professional Strength format! This is NOT available in your neighborhood supermarket or pharmacy. This is an inexpensive, convenient solution for patients looking for a teeth whitening they can do at home.

Dr. Scheff will take an impression of your teeth so that a special, customized tray can be made for your teeth. Once the custom tray is made a special gel containing carbamide peroxideis is used. This solution allows oxygen to enter the enamel and dentin. This process will lighten and whiten your teeth!

Want a better smile in less than 90 minutes? Pola Office Teeth Whitening takes place in one trip to Brighton Family Dental and allows you to quickly get your teeth much whiter!
