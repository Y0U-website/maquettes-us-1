The practice

We, Doctors Kelly and Matt Bateman made Nacogdoches our home in 2011. We have had to opportunity to serve many East Texans mainly in Nacogdoches, Sabine, Panola, Angelina, and Rusk Counties. We currently work full-time at our own private practice here in the city of Nacogdoches.

Through our combined services, we offer all aspects of dentistry! From toddlers to teens, implants to root canals; we do it all!

We met and were trained at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. We have spent the better part of the last decade learning about how we can best treat your dental needs by practicing and refining the skills that will make your smile shine.

Have a teen with crooked teeth??...we can straighten them out for you! ;)

Tooth pain? Need a bad tooth taken out?...come in and we ll get it out!

Your mother-in-law getting on your nerves??..bring her in for a root canal, we do that too!

Top quality care is our top priority. We practice evidence based dentistry and use only the highest quality of dental materials.

Our office offers a more relaxed atmosphere, from the reception area to the treatment rooms you ll feel the difference; contemporary and refreshing.
