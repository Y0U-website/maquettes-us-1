Why Should I Get Implant Retained Dentures?

Implant retained dentures offer unparalleled stability and comfort, allowing your dentures to stay securely in place without slipping or irritation. They also contribute to improved oral health by preventing bone loss and maintaining the integrity of your bone’s structure. With this innovative solution, you can enjoy the freedom to eat your favorite foods, speak naturally, and smile confidently.

Here’s How It Works

Consultation and Assessment

Before we proceed with implant-retained dentures, we will schedule a consultation with our team to evaluate your dental and overall health. This assessment will help us determine if this option is suitable for you. As part of the consultation, we will take x-rays and impressions of your mouth to develop a personalized treatment plan.

Placement & Healing

The next step in the dental implant process is to have the implants surgically placed into the jawbone by either Dr. Smith or Dr. Browning. Following the placement, there&#x27;s a healing period of several months during which the implants fuse with the jawbone. During our Fixed Hybrid treatment, the teeth are fitted to the implants on the same day.

Denture Attachment

After your dental implants have fully healed and fused with your jawbone, the last step is to attach your denture to the implants. This involves a small attachment that securely snaps your denture onto the implants. With your denture attached, you can enjoy the comfort and confidence of having a stable and secure fitting denture that won&#x27;t slip or move around in your mouth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between an implant retained denture and a conventional denture?

Implant retained dentures are anchored securely to dental implants, offering superior stability and eliminating issues like slipping or discomfort. Unlike conventional dentures, they allow you to enjoy a natural bite, speak confidently, and eat without restrictions.

What is home care like for an implant retained denture?

Proper home care varies based upon the type of denture, but generally, regular brushing and rinsing with an antibacterial mouthwash helps to maintain oral hygiene. Additionally, scheduling regular check-ups with our team will keep your implant retained dentures in great condition. By properly caring for your implant retained dentures, you can enjoy a confident and carefree smile for years to come.
