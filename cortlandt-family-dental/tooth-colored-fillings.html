What to Expect During Treatment

  • Numbing: The area around the tooth is gently numbed to keep you comfortable.
  • Decay Removal: Dr. Izaguirre gently removes the decayed or weak material.
  • Conditioning: The tooth surface is prepared so the resin bonds securely.
  • Placement and Curing: The composite is placed in thin layers, shaped, and hardened instantly with a special light.
  • Shaping and Polishing: The filling is shaped and polished for a smooth surface and an accurate, comfortable bite.

How Long Do Tooth-Colored Fillings Last?

With good home care and regular cleanings, composite fillings hold up well for many years. Their strong bond helps seal the edges and reduces the risk of new decay or leakage. We check the condition of every filling at your exams and will recommend refreshing or replacing them when wear appears.

Care Tips After a New Filling

  • Caring for your new filling helps ensure its longevity.
  • Avoid very hard or sticky foods for the rest of the day.
  • Brush twice daily with a fluoride toothpaste.
  • Clean between your teeth every day with floss or an interdental cleaner.
  • Use a nightguard if you clench or grind your teeth.
  • Call us right away if your bite feels high or if you notice lingering pain.

Are Tooth-Colored Fillings Right for Every Cavity?

Small to medium-sized cavities are ideal for composite. Very large areas of decay or extensive fractures may require a stronger solution like an inlay, onlay, or a full crown for long-term strength and stability. We design treatment based on the size, location, and forces applied to the tooth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Tooth-Colored Fillings hurt? You should feel pressure and vibration, but not pain. We numb the area completely and can add more anesthetic if needed to ensure your comfort.

Will the filling stain? Composite resists everyday staining. However, frequent consumption of coffee, tea, or wine can slightly discolor the edges over time. Routine cleanings and touch-up polishing help keep them looking natural.

How long does a filling visit take? Most single-tooth fillings take between 30 to 60 minutes. Larger or multiple fillings will take longer. We will give you a clear time estimate when you schedule.
