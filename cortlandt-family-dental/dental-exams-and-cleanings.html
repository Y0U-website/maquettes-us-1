What Happens During a Dental Cleaning?

  • Our hygienist performs a gentle yet thorough cleaning to remove harmful buildup.
  • Plaque and tartar removal above and below the gumline
  • Polishing to smooth tooth surfaces and reduce new buildup
  • Flossing and targeted tips for hard-to-reach areas
  • Fluoride treatment when appropriate for extra enamel protection
  • Home care coaching that fits your daily routine

Why Dental Exams & Cleaning Matter

  • Consistent preventive care is the easiest way to maintain your smile and save money.
  • Prevent cavities and gum disease
  • Reduce bad breath and surface staining
  • Protect existing restorations like fillings, crowns, and bridges
  • Avoid urgent visits by catching minor issues early
  • Support whole-body health, as healthier gums improve overall wellness

Targeted Care for Every Age

For Children and Teens

  • We provide specialized care to protect young smiles during development.
  • Gentle cleanings and easy-to-understand guidance
  • Sealants for molars at the right time
  • Fluoride to strengthen enamel
  • Mouthguard advice for sports
  • Nutrition and snack tips that reduce cavity risk

For Adults and Seniors

  • Our focus shifts to maintenance, managing restoration wear, and addressing systemic issues.
  • Screening for gum disease and recession
  • Evaluation of cracked or worn teeth from clenching or grinding
  • Maintenance of crowns, bridges, dentures, and partials
  • Medication and dry mouth guidance to protect enamel
  • Options for whitening and small cosmetic fixes

Signs You Should Book Now

  • If you notice any of these symptoms, please call us to schedule an appointment.
  • Bleeding gums when brushing or flossing
  • Tooth sensitivity to cold or sweets
  • Bad breath that does not improve
  • Soreness when chewing
  • New spaces between teeth or teeth that feel loose
  • It has been more than six months since your last visit

Home Care Between Visits

Are dental x-rays safe? Yes. We follow the ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) principle and use digital sensors that use far less radiation than older film. Will a cleaning whiten my teeth? Cleanings remove surface stains and make teeth look brighter. For a bigger change, ask about professional teeth whitening.

How long is an appointment? Most routine visits take about one hour. First visits can run a little longer so we can learn your history and goals.

  • Your habits at home are critical to maintaining the results of your professional cleaning.
  • Brush twice daily for two minutes with a fluoride toothpaste
  • Clean between teeth once a day with floss or interdental brushes
  • Aim for a soft-bristle brush and a light touch
  • Sip water often and limit frequent snacking
  • Wear a nightguard if you clench or grind
  • Do not ignore bleeding or swelling—call us right away
