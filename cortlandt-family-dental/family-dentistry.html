Care for Adults

Routine Family Dentistry helps you avoid larger procedures. We screen for cavities, gum disease, clenching (bruxism), and oral cancer signs. We discuss issues like dry mouth, sensitivity, and grinding. If a tooth needs repair, we act early with a filling or crown to protect its structure and comfort.

Care for Seniors

As dental needs change, we adjust your care plan. We help manage issues related to medications that affect saliva flow. We maintain existing crowns and bridges and can fit or refresh dentures and partials for better chewing and speech. We review at-home tools that make daily care easier.

How Often Should Families Visit?

Most patients do best with exams and cleanings every six months. If you have gum inflammation or specific risk factors, we may recommend three- or four-month gum care. We personalize your recall intervals to keep your smile stable between visits.

Home Care Tips Between Visits

What if I haven’t seen a dentist in years? You are welcome here. We start with a simple, non-judgmental exam, address any urgent needs, and build a manageable long-term plan you can follow comfortably.

Are tooth-colored fillings durable? Modern composites bond well to the tooth structure and hold up to daily chewing when placed correctly and cared for. Can Family Dentistry improve my smile’s appearance? Yes. Healthy gums and well-shaped, tooth-colored fillings support a better smile. Ask us about professional teeth whitening, bonding, or crowns when appropriate for a full aesthetic upgrade.

  • Brush twice daily for two minutes.
  • Floss or use interdental cleaners once daily.
  • Use a fluoride toothpaste.
  • Wear a nightguard if you clench or grind.
  • Sip water often and limit frequent snacking.
  • Call us at the first sign of pain or swelling.
