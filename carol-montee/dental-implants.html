What to Expect During Treatment

Dental implant treatment is completed in several stages and begins with a comprehensive evaluation. During your consultation, we will determine whether implants are the right option for your needs and develop a personalized treatment plan.

The process typically involves placing the implant into the jawbone and allowing it to heal and integrate with the bone. Once healing is complete, a custom restoration is attached to create a natural-looking result.

Treatment timelines vary from patient to patient, but our team will guide you through every step of the process and ensure you understand what to expect.
