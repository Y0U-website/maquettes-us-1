Dr. Catherine Foote

meet the team

Dr. Catherine Foote is a native of Ardmore, PA. Following graduation from Lower Merion High School, she dedicated ten years to higher education at the University of Pennsylvania where she received a Bachelor’s degree in Biology, followed by a D.M.D. degree and Certificate of Orthodontics from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. She is also a certified injector with the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE).

A third generation dentist, Dr. Foote is an active member of the local community, supporting school and community programs. She is a member of a number of local and national organizations including: American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontics, Women in Orthodontics, Academy of Stomatology, and the Main Line Dental Society.

Dr. Foote resides in Wayne with her husband Neil, daughters Elsie and Piper, and son Graysen.

Dr. Foote and her team understand that there are varying levels of tolerance for dentistry, especially among children and teens. Foote Orthodontics staff members have experience with patients who have special emotional, physical, cognitive and sensory needs. We look forward to working with you and your family to develop a pace and style which suits you and your child during orthodontic treatment.
