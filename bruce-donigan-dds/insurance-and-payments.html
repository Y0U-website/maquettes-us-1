Insurance and Payment Plans

Insurance

We accept most insurance plans. We will file your claims and send them to the insurance company electronically. You are responsible for the portion of treatment not covered by your insurance plan.

Most of the major insurance plans are accepted by our office. We have information on several hundred plans in our computer. If you have questions about your insurance plan, call us. At this time, we are not accepting new patients with "straight Medicaid" (green plastic card) . We do accept Blue Cross Complete dental plans associated with the Healthy Michigan Plan. Visit the website of the Tri-County Dental Health Council for information on obtaining dental care for low-income or Medicaid coverage.

We participate in several network plans -- Dentemax, Dental Connections, GEHA Federal Employees, Ameriplan and others.

We make every effort to keep fees reasonable for patients who are not covered by insurance. Our payment plans can help to make it easier for those on a budget.

If you are in doubt about insurance coverage or have questions about the costs of dental care, contact us. If your employer will be changing plans soon, contact us before the enrollment deadline!

Payment Plans

We appreciate payment at the time of treatment, but if necessary we can make arrangements for manageable monthly payments.

We accept credit and debit cards, cash and personal checks.

Care Credit

We also accept CareCredit, a nationally-known provider of flexible payment plan options for dental treatment. For more information about CareCredit and how it can help you to afford the dentistry you need, call us or visit the CareCredit website .
