Gentle Caring Dentistry in Detroit&#x27;s New Center Our mission is to provide you with personalized, high-quality care. We are a team dedicated to improving and maintaining your oral health. We’re proud of the work we do, and we value our patients!

We offer broad array of services, such as preventative care, cosmetic dentistry, crowns, implants, bridges, dentures and much more. Our team has the professional experience to realize that there is no such thing as one-size-fits-all treatment, so we never use a one-size-fits-all approach to your dental plan.

We are located in Detroit&#x27;s historic Fisher Building Since 1981, we have been providing quality and personalized oral health care while keeping your individuality in mind. Our goal is to improve and maintain your oral health so you can smile with confidence.

Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry Whether your teeth are the wrong shape, crooked, stained, or chipped, we can treat your teeth with minimal methods using the latest techniques and the finest materials.

Preventative Care Efficient preventative care is key to having a healthy smile. Research has shown a connection between periodontal (gum) disease and general health. Consistent checkups will allow our team to diagnose oral cancer, gum disease, and tooth decay before they spread and become a problem.
