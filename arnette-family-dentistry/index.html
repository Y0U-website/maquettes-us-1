The practice

Doctor Arnette is an experienced and caring dentist who keeps up with the latest developments in dentistry. He will listen to your needs and preferences and help you choose the best dental options for you.

We want you to enjoy your visit to our office. That’s why we offer Wi-Fi throughout the office, convenient hours, and affordable fees. Contact us today to schedule an appointment. We can’t wait to see you!

Whether you need a routine checkup, a filling, a crown, or a smile makeover, Arnette Family Dentistry has you covered with over 12 dental services to meet your needs.

Do you want to maintain your oral health and prevent dental problems? If so, you should visit your dentist regularly for a routine dental cleaning and exam.

Do you have a tooth that is decayed, fractured, or cracked? If so, you may need a dental filling to restore your tooth and prevent further damage.

Do you want to keep your teeth healthy and prevent any dental problems before they become serious? If so, you may benefit from digital x-rays, a modern and advanced technology that allows us to take high-quality images of your teeth and gums.

Do you have missing teeth that affect your smile, your confidence, or your quality of life? If so, you may benefit from dentures, a type of removable dental prosthesis that can replace your natural teeth and restore your oral health and function.

If you need any restorative procedures, such as fillings, crowns, bridges, implants, dentures, or root canals, you can also enjoy a 20 % discount on these services when you pay in cash at the time of service.

Les soins

At Arnette Family Dentistry, we take your dental health seriously. We practice comprehensive dentistry, which involves the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of conditions affecting your teeth, gums, and jaw.

Do you have a tooth that is severely damaged or missing? If so, you may need a dental crown or bridge to restore your tooth and improve your smile.

If you have lost one or more teeth due to injury, disease, or decay, you may be wondering what your options are to restore your smile and function. One of the most advanced and effective solutions is implant restoration.
