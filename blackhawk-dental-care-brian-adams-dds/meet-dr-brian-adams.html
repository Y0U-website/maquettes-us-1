Meet Dr. Brian Adams Meet Danville Dentist Dr. Brian Adams: Where Expertise Meets Innovation A Commitment to Excellence For Danville

One of our expert Danville dentists , Dr. Brian Adams worked as a management consultant until age 30, but he just didn’t have the passion for it. There was always something about dentistry that piqued his interest, and the idea of improving the quality of life for a patient was a mission Dr. Adams was ready to stand behind. He has always enjoyed helping others, and dentistry offered him the chance to make a difference in the lives of his patients and to watch their smiles grow.

Dr. Adams earned his dental degree from the University of Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco. Since graduation, he has taken well over 1,000 hours of continuing education, far beyond what is required as a Danville dentist .

In 2012, he was accepted as a clinical mentor at the prestigious Kois Center of Dental Excellence . This rigorous five-year curriculum in Seattle, Washington includes complex restorative casework and a series of written and oral examinations. This puts Dr. Adams into an elite group of Danville dentists that have shown proficiency in all aspects of restorative dentistry .

The Kois Center is the pre-eminent continuing education center that draws practicing dentists from over 30 different countries.

In addition, Dr. Adams is a board member of the Contra Costa Dental Peer Review Committee, where he was nominated by his dental peers to help maintain the standard of care for practicing dentists throughout California. This committee is composed of local Danville dentists that have demonstrated clinical excellence in their field and choose to give back to their professional community.

Another facet of Dr. Adams is his commitment to bringing the best possible care to his patients

As a dedicated Danville dentist , Dr. Adams invests in continuing education and specialized training because he believes his dental knowledge will pay dividends in his patient’s care. He believes that by investing additional time outside of the practice into expanding his dental knowledge, his patients will see obvious dividends in their care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Adams loves spending time with his wife, son, and daughter. He enjoys golfing, hiking, skiing, and watching the 49ers.

Visit Dr. Adams and his team for advanced, wellness-focused dentistry in Danville, Alamo, and the surrounding communities.
