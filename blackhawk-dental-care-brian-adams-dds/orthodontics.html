Invisalign Orthodontics in Danville, CA

Every two weeks, you’ll swap your current aligners with the next in the series. Each aligner applies the appropriate amount of force to comfortably coerce your teeth into the proper position. Our Invisalign orthodontics in Danville offers a gradual change, however over the course of treatment you’ll be amazed at the results, which some patients achieve in as little as 12-15 months.

Throughout your treatment, you’ll wear between 18 and 30 different sets of aligners, which you keep in your mouth for a minimum of 20-22 hours each day. After approximately six weeks, Dr. Adams will evaluate your progress and guarantee you’re on track to your anticipated results with clear braces in Danville .

Invisalign orthodontics correct misaligned, gapped, or crooked teeth, and since the clear aligners are virtually undetectable when worn, they’re also discreet. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign doesn’t require multiple visits to the orthodontist to tighten wires or adjust brackets. And because they are removable, you can eat and drink whatever you like, and brushing and flossing your teeth is as easy as ever.

Invisalign is less invasive than traditional orthodontics, however it’s not right for everyone. If we determine you need more advanced orthodontic therapy, we’re happy to refer you to a highly recommended orthodontist, conveniently located in the same building as our office. We’re here to get you the straightest smile possible, so don’t hesitate to ask us if Invisalign is right for you!
