Caring for the Whole Person: Making Mental Health Care Accessible

At Bluestem Health, we know mental health care is essential to caring for the whole person. As a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC), Bluestem Health believes everyone deserves access to compassionate, high-quality behavioral health services . By integrating behavioral health with primary medical care , we help patients receive timely support in a familiar healthcare setting, making it easier to care for both their physical and emotional well-being while removing challenges to getting these services.

Why Mental Health Matters

Mental health affects every aspect of our daily lives. It influences how we think, feel, work, build relationships, manage stress, and care for our families. Conditions such as anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and substance use disorders are common, but many people never seek treatment because of the fear of judgment or inability to find affordable care.

In Lincoln’s underserved communities, these challenges are often compounded by shortages of behavioral health providers, financial hardship, and limited transportation options. This results in individuals waiting until symptoms become overwhelming before reaching out for help.

Be encouraged that early intervention can make a significant difference. Speaking with a behavioral health professional can provide practical coping strategies, emotional support, and treatment plans to improve overall quality of life.

Whole-Person Care

Mental health does not exist in isolation. At Bluestem Health, we use an integrated healthcare model. Instead of treating mental health separately from physical health, behavioral health providers work closely with primary care providers to coordinate your care.

This team-based approach means your healthcare providers work with one another to better understand your overall health needs, so you don’t have to be the middle man. If you’re managing diabetes while coping with depression or experiencing anxiety that affects your sleep and blood pressure, your care team works together to develop a comprehensive treatment plan, made especially for you.

Rather than focusing only on symptoms, your integrated care team will help you build a healthier life through prevention, education, and compassionate treatment that supports the whole person.

A Trauma-Informed, Compassionate Philosophy

Seeking help for mental health concerns can be difficult. Many people have experienced trauma, loss, discrimination, or other life challenges that make healthcare settings feel intimidating.

Bluestem Health uses a trauma-informed approach that recognizes these experiences and focuses on creating a safe, respectful environment where patients feel heard and supported. Providers work in partnership with patients and their families whenever appropriate, helping individuals participate actively in decisions about their care.

This compassionate philosophy helps reduce stigma while encouraging patients to seek support before mental health concerns become crises.

Accessible and Affordable Care for Everyone

Access to healthcare is one of the biggest challenges for Lincoln’s underserved community members. Bluestem Health addresses this by offering behavioral health services across multiple clinic locations throughout Lincoln.

Our behavioral health providers serve patients at the Main Clinic , Kreshel Clinic , Thompson Clinic , and Pediatric Clinic at Health 360 . When same-day support is needed, phone and telehealth appointments may also be available, helping patients receive timely care, even when transportation or scheduling is difficult. Interpreter services are also available to improve patient-to-provider communication and access for diverse patient populations.

Cost should never be the reason someone doesn’t get the help they need.

As a FQHC , Bluestem Health offers affordable healthcare options, including our Sliding Fee Discount Program for eligible patients. We also accept Medicaid , Medicare , and many insurance plans. For many families facing financial hardship, these resources make counseling and behavioral healthcare significantly more accessible than they might expect. Combined with medical, dental , pharmacy , and community outreach services, you can receive comprehensive care under one organization at Bluestem Health.

Taking the First Step

Reaching out for help is often the hardest part of improving your mental health. Whether you’re feeling overwhelmed by stress, experiencing anxiety or depression, coping with grief, or facing challenges related to trauma or substance use, you don’t have to navigate those challenges alone. Finding your own behavioral health provider can be cumbersome and feel daunting.

Bluestem Health is committed to making behavioral healthcare more accessible, affordable, and welcoming for every member of our community. By integrating mental health services with primary care, reducing financial barriers, and providing compassionate, team-based treatment, Bluestem Health is helping build a healthier community—one person, one family, and one conversation at a time.

If you are seeking behavioral health services, consider scheduling an appointment with a Bluestem Health primary care provider to get started. Together, we can help you determine the right path toward better mental and physical health because caring for your mind is essential to caring for your whole self.
