  • Invisalign | Appleton Dental Center top of page Invisalign ® Achieve a straighter, more confident smile with Invisalign What is Invisalign? Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually move your teeth into their ideal position. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are nearly invisible, removable, and more comfortable, making them a popular choice for both teens and adults. The Procedure Consultation & Digital Scan – We assess your smile and take 3D digital scans to create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Custom Aligners – Your custom aligners are created to gently shift your teeth into place over time.
  • Wearing Your Aligners – Wear each set of aligners for 20-22 hours a day, switching to a new set approximately every 1-2 weeks.
  • Progress Checkups – Visit our office every 6-8 weeks to monitor your progress and receive your next set of aligners.
  • Enjoy Your New Smile! – Once treatment is complete, we’ll provide you with retainers to maintain your beautifully straightened teeth.
