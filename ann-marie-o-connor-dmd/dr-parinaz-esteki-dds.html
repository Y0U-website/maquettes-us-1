Dr. Parinaz Esteki DDS

Dr. Parinaz Esteki DDS General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Parinaz Esteki is a General Dentist at Dental Associates of Arlington. With a gentle and caring approach, Dr. Esteki strives to make every patient feel comfortable and valued throughout their dental experience. She believes that empathy, patience, and clear communication are essential in helping patients overcome dental anxiety and maintain lifelong oral health. Her focus is always on providing the highest quality of care while treating each patient as she would her own family.

After several years of international clinical experience, Dr. Esteki earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from UT Health San Antonio School of Dentistry, where she graduated with highest honors. She practices all aspects of general dentistry and has a special interest in smile design and cosmetic dentistry. With careful attention to detail and a passion for artistry, she is devoted to helping patients achieve natural, healthy smiles that reflect their confidence and individuality.

Committed to professional growth, Dr. Esteki regularly attends workshops and continuing education courses. This dedication allows her to provide patients with the latest and most effective treatment options in modern dentistry. She is committed to patient education and comprehensive treatment, ensuring that each individual receives thoughtful, personalized care tailored to their needs.

Dr. Esteki is a member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.
