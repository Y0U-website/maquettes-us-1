Dr. Nick Katsavochristos DMD General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Nikolaos Katsavochristos DMD (Dr. Nick) is a General Dentist at Dental Associates of Arlington. Dr. Nick has been in private practice since 2002. Upon earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery diploma from Aristotle University in 2000, he worked in his private practice in Athens, Greece, where he earned a reputation for delivering high-quality care and offering first-class treatments to his patients. In the US, he attended the Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, graduating with honors.
