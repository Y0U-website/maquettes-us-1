Services

Restorative Dentistry When dental problems arise, we restore more than your teeth we restore comfort, confidence, and quality of life. Our restorative solutions are designed to blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

  • Professional teeth cleanings
  • Digital X-rays & intraoral imaging
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Dental sealants for children and adults
  • Oral cancer screenings
  • Personalized oral hygiene education
  • Tooth-colored fillings (composite resin)
  • Porcelain crowns and bridges
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Dental inlays and onlays
  • Root canal therapy
  • Tooth extractions
  • Dental implant restorations

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhancing your smile can transform your entire appearance. Our cosmetic treatments are customized to highlight your natural beauty and give you the smile you’ve always wanted.

Sleep Apnea & Bruxism Management Your oral health is connected to your overall well-being. We offer effective, non-invasive solutions for patients struggling with nighttime teeth grinding or mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea.

  • Professional teeth whitening (in-office and take-home)
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Composite bonding
  • Full smile makeovers
  • Invisalign clear aligners for discreet orthodontic care
  • Custom nightguards for bruxism (teeth grinding)
  • Oral appliance therapy for sleep apnea
  • TMJ evaluations and treatment plans

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can be stressful we’re here when you need us most. Our office provides same-day emergency appointments to relieve pain and protect your long-term dental health.

  • Toothaches and dental pain relief
  • Chipped, cracked, or broken teeth
  • Lost fillings or crowns
  • Abscesses or swollen gums
  • Trauma and dental injuries
