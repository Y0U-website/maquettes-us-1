What are Dental Implants?

Implant-Supported Prosthesis Options

Single Tooth Implants

Ideal for replacing a single missing tooth, this involves placing one dental implant and attaching a crown. It looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Used when multiple teeth are missing. Unlike traditional bridges, these are supported by implants rather than adjacent teeth, providing more stability and preserving natural teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

A secure alternative to traditional dentures, these are anchored by implants. They can be removable or fixed, offering improved comfort, stability, and chewing efficiency.

All-on-4/All-on-6

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, this solution uses 4 or 6 implants to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth. It provides a stable and permanent set of teeth with fewer implants.

The Dental Implant Process at Campus Walk Dental Care Practice

  • Consultation: During your initial consultation, our experienced dental team will assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
  • Implant Placement: The dental implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia for your comfort. Our skilled dentist will precisely place the implants into your bone.
  • Healing and Integration: Over the following weeks, the implants will fuse with your bone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable foundation for your new teeth which acts similar to your tooth roots.
  • Custom Restoration: Once the implants have fully integrated, custom-made restorations will be securely attached to the implants, completing your smile transformation.
  • Follow-Up Care: We will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to ensure the longevity of your dental implants. Regular check-ups and cleanings at Campus Walk Dental Care Practice will help maintain your oral health and the integrity of your implants.
