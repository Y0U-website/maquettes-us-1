The practice

At Alldent Dental Center, we are passionate about your entire family’s oral health. We welcome patients of all ages, from one year up, and have served the Chicago area with quality family dentistry for decades. Whether you need baby’s first visit, wisdom teeth extractions, or dental implants, we’re here for you.

Our team proudly serves the communities of Lyons, Riverside, Berwyn, Elmhurst, Oak Park, and Cicero. For your convenience, Alldent Dental Center offers all our services in English, Spanish, and Arabic. We hope you’ll visit, and let us help maintain your healthy, natural smile for as long as possible. Call us today and make an appointment.

Dr. Hegazy and our experienced staff offer a wide range of dental services and are focused on keeping you relaxed and comfortable throughout your time with us. If you suffer from anxiety at the dentist or are undergoing a complex procedure, we offer nitrous oxide sedation to help you feel calm.

You can always count on the expertise of our team to keep your procedure as efficient as possible. Our Lyons dentist has extensive experience with wisdom teeth extraction and has received special training in many areas of dentistry. He is also a member of the Chicago Implant Institute and is a Platinum Invisalign Provider.

Our Lyons dental office maintains the latest technology to ensure you’re getting the modern dentistry you deserve. Our iTero® digital scanner allows us to render images much faster and more accurately than with traditional impressions. We also have a cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) 3D scanner, which captures images of your teeth, gums, and jaws in one scan.
