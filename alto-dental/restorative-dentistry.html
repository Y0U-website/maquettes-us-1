Same-Day Crowns

White Zirconia Crowns

White zirconia crowns offer durable protection with a more natural-looking appearance. Made from a strong tooth-colored material, they can be a great option for patients who need lasting coverage while still wanting their restoration to blend in with their smile.

Stainless Steel Crowns

Stainless steel crowns are a durable, protective option often used for children’s teeth that need more support than a filling can provide. At Alto Dental, we may recommend a stainless steel crown when a tooth has significant decay, has been treated with a pulpotomy, or needs reliable coverage to stay functional until it naturally falls out.

Dental Bridges

Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

If you&#x27;ve suffered from severe dental trauma, have lost many teeth, or are dealing with a variety of dental difficulties, full mouth-rehabilitation may be right for you. Throughout a variety of treatments and procedures planned out by your dentist, full mouth-rehabilitation can completely restore your overall health and boost your confidence.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth with strength, stability, and a natural look. By replacing both the tooth root and the visible tooth, implants help restore comfortable chewing, clear speech, and confidence in your smile. They can also support your long-term oral health by helping preserve the jawbone after tooth loss. Whether you are missing one tooth or several, dental implants can help you feel more like yourself again.

Dentures

Dentures are a removable, affordable solution that can complete your smile after missing one or all of your natural teeth. To ensure a natural look and feel, most dentures are made of a combination of acrylic and metal and are custom-crafted for a comfortable fit. Looking to see if dentures are right for you? Schedule a consultation to find out if a set of full or partial dentures can restore your bite. It&#x27;s about time you got back to living your life with a smile.

Partial Dentures

Missing just a few teeth? Partial dentures may be the right choice for you. Partial dentures look natural, are removable, and can be a great solution to restoring the function of your teeth and jaw. Make the first step in restoring your smile by booking a consultation.

Emergencies

Our dental practice proudly offers emergency dentistry to ensure patients receive fast, reliable care when unexpected dental problems arise. Whether you’re experiencing severe tooth pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, swelling, or another urgent dental issue, our team is committed to providing prompt treatment to relieve discomfort and protect your oral health. We understand that dental emergencies can happen at any time, which is why we prioritize same-day appointments whenever possible and deliver compassionate, efficient care in a comfortable environment.

By choosing this treatment, we can restore your little one&#x27;s oral health and bring back their bright smile, allowing them to continue enjoying all the laughter and carefree moments of childhood.
