The practice

Today, he also serves as a clinical assistant professor in restorative dentistry at the University of Illinois Chicago, where he teaches the next generation of dentists. That academic mindset shapes the way he cares for patients at Alto Dental: with precision, patience, and clear explanations that help you feel confident about your options.

His philosophy is simple: tell, show, do. He explains what he sees, shows you the details through scans, X-rays, and visuals, then walks you through treatment step by step.

From routine cleanings to same-day crowns and urgent dental visits, our services are designed to meet the needs of busy families, working professionals, and patients who want thorough answers before moving forward.

Stay ahead of dental issues with thorough cleanings, exams, digital X-rays, and oral cancer screenings. We focus on early detection, clear education, and a strong hygiene foundation for long-term oral health.

Same-Day Dental Crowns With digital scanning and Sprintray technology, we can design and deliver natural-looking crowns, inlays, and onlays in a single visit. No messy impressions. No temporary crown. No weeks of waiting.

Root Canal Therapy Root canal treatment should be precise, comfortable, and affordable. Using a dental microscope for enhanced visibility, Dr. Gharib provides thorough care designed to relieve pain and help save your natural tooth.

Emergency Dentistry Tooth pain can’t always wait. If you’re dealing with a dental emergency, call our office and we’ll work to see you as soon as possible, often the same day.

Why Alto Dental The details matter here At Alto Dental, we believe patients make better decisions when they can actually see and understand what’s happening. That’s why we’ve built our practice around advanced diagnostics, thoughtful education, and treatment plans that are clearly explained before anything begins.
