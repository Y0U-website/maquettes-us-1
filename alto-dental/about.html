Meet your dentist

Dr. Hisham Gharib, DDS

Dr. Hisham Gharib brings 19 years of experience and a deeply educational approach to patient care. He earned his dental degree from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, holds postdoctoral diplomas from the United Kingdom, and pursued advanced training in operative, esthetic, and digital dentistry.

He also serves as a clinical assistant professor in restorative dentistry at the University of Illinois Chicago, where he teaches future dentists modern restorative and digital techniques. That teaching background shapes the way he practices at Alto Dental: explain clearly, show what’s happening, and make sure patients feel informed before treatment begins.

His “tell, show, do” approach means you’ll understand what he sees, why it matters, and what your options are before anything moves forward.

Why patients trust his approach

No rushing. No guesswork.

Dr. Gharib’s care is meticulous, but never overwhelming. He uses advanced imaging, digital scans, and clear visuals to help patients see what he sees and make informed decisions about their oral health.

Many surrounding offices refer root canal patients to Alto Dental because of Dr. Gharib’s experience, microscope-assisted approach, and commitment to keeping care affordable and precise.

Our team

Focused care, familiar faces

Alto Dental is a small, organized office by design. Our team works together to keep visits welcoming, efficient, and easy to navigate from the first phone call to the treatment chair.

We welcome questions, explain next steps clearly, and help you understand everything from your treatment plan to your insurance estimate.

Your first visit

What to expect

Your first visit is designed to give us a complete picture of your oral health while helping you feel informed from the beginning.

After reviewing your medical history and main concerns, we’ll complete a thorough exam with any necessary diagnostic imaging. Dr. Gharib will then walk you through what he sees, explain your options, and help you understand the recommended next steps, including timing, cost, and insurance details.

Advanced tech that makes dental care clear

Our technology helps us diagnose more accurately, plan more precisely, and explain your care in a way that makes sense.

CBCT 3D imaging

Detailed 3D imaging helps us evaluate teeth, roots, bone, and surrounding structures with greater accuracy.

Dental microscope

Enhanced magnification supports precise root canal therapy, retreatment, and detailed restorative care.

iTero intraoral scanner

Digital scans replace messy impressions and support Invisalign, crowns, and other restorative treatment.

Sprintray same-day crowns

Our digital restorative workflow allows us to design and place many crowns, inlays, and onlays in a single visit.

3D printing

In-office 3D printing helps us create precise components efficiently and keep treatment moving smoothly.
