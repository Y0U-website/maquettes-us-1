Our Location in Bothell

Welcome to our location in Bothell. Conveniently located on Beardslee Boulevard, our office is really easy to find. You will find plenty of designated patient parking behind the building.

Upon your arrival, you are greeted by an accessible, no stairs entrance; simply walk through the main door, and our office awaits you straight ahead.

Welcoming Reception Area

Our friendly expert staff makes the check-in process quick and easy. They take the time to answer any of your questions and create a welcoming atmosphere.

Our reception area is designed with children in mind. Bright colors, comfortable seating, and engaging activities help everyone feel at ease from the moment they arrive.

Kid-Friendly Waiting Area

Our waiting area features comfortable seating and a cozy fireplace to keep children comfortable before their appointments.

We understand that a positive waiting experience contributes significantly to a child’s overall perception of dental visits. Our waiting area fosters a relaxing start to your visit.

Our Beautiful Aquarium

One of our most popular features is our colorful fish tank. Children love watching the fish swim, which serves as both a distraction and a calming element in our office environment.

Many children look forward to visits just to see what’s new in our aquarium, creating positive associations with their dental appointments.

Modern Treatment Rooms

Our treatment rooms feature state-of-the-art equipment in a comfortable, child-friendly environment. Each room is designed to make dental care as efficient and pleasant as possible.

With large windows, natural light, and a bright, airy atmosphere, we’ve created treatment spaces that help children feel relaxed during their dental procedures.

Open Treatment Area

Our open treatment area is designed with multiple chairs allowing us to treat several patients at once. This layout creates a bright, spacious environment with plenty of natural light.

Children find comfort in seeing other patients receiving care nearby, and our open design allows us to efficiently provide excellent dental care in a comfortable setting.
