Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Yea

Dr. Yea is a dedicated pediatric dentist with decades of experience helping children maintain healthy, happy smiles. She has focused her practice on providing exceptional dental care to children of all ages, with prevention and minimally invasive procedures.

After earning her dental degree from the University of Michigan, Dr. Yea returned to Seattle, her hometown, to complete her Pediatric Dental Residency at the University of Washington, where she also earned a Master of Public Health. She is passionate and committed to staying at the forefront of her field and serves as an attending at Seattle Children’s Hospital, helping train future pediatric dentists while continuing to care for her patients.

Dr. Yea lives in Seattle with her husband and two boys. She is currently baking sourdough, trying pilates classes and loves to read.

Dr. McCoy

After finishing dental school at the University of Washington, Dr. McCoy followed his passion for children’s dentistry by completing two years of specialty training in Dallas, Texas at Baylor College of Dentistry, earning a Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry and a Masters in Oral Biology. He also gained extensive clinical and emergency experience at Dallas’ Children’s Medical Center and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital for Children.

Dr. McCoy cherishes time with his wife, Shannon, and their four children—Whitney, Duncan, Warren and Isaac—often participating in church activities, visiting local parks and playing/watching sports.
