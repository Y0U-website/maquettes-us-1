Let Us Introduce Ourselves

The dental practice team at Purtuc Dental, LLC. is proud to provide you with expert consultation and treatment.

Dr. Cynthia Y.H. Purtuc D.M.D.

Dr. Cynthia Purtuc graduated cum laude from Temple University School of Dentistry. Dr. Purtuc practiced in Philadelphia before moving to Pennington, New Jersey. Dr. Purtuc is fluent in Japanese, Chinese, she is also holding a Master’s Degree in Computer Science. Dr. Purtuc provides generalized family dentistry including cavity restorations, crowns and bridges, dentures, implant restorations, and extractions.

She is an active member of the New Jersey Dental Association, Mercer Dental Society and American Dental Association.

Dr. Purtuc and her husband Dr. Sorin Purtuc are actively involved in the community and support local sports teams and children’s activities.

Sorin M. Purtuc, D.M.D. Endodontist

Dr. Sorin Purtuc graduated magna cum laude from Temple University School of Dentistry. After finishing dental school he went on to complete his specialty at Temple’s Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy) program.

Dr. Purtuc is an active memember of American Association of Endodontics, American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and Mercer Dental Society.

In his spare time Dr. Purtuc enjoys biking, jogging, taking care of his garden and fish, and most of all, spending time with his family.

Patricia McSorley RDH

Pat has been working at this office for over 20 years. She is dedicated to provide the highest level of oral health care to our patients.

From the initial visit and throughout a patient’s dental experience in our office, Pat pays close attention to many areas of importance which affect a person’s health.

Our goal is to catch the disease in the earliset stage in order to get it under control and maintain it at a healthy level.

Michael D’Agostino CDT, TE

Mike is our full-time in-house Certified Lab Technician. He has provided beautifully crafted life-like dental restorations such as crowns, veneers, bridges and dentures for over 35 years.

Working together in the lab, Mike and Dr. Purtuc analyze each case and customize the restorations using the highest quality materials and advanced technologies.
