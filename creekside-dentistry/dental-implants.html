FAQs About Dental Implants

  • Am I too old for dental implants? Probably not! Most of our older patients can sustain dental implants. The deciding factor is not age, but the condition of your oral health . Dental implants need healthy bone and gums to support them. We would check these areas first and see if they can be restored with proper treatment. Once your oral health is in a good state, we could proceed with your implants procedure.
  • Are dental implants painful? Many of our patients have been pleasantly surprised at how painless their procedure was. You are numbed throughout your treatment so you don’t feel anything and often our patients have only needed over-the-counter pain medication to remain comfortable post-surgery. Most people can go back to work and their daily activities the day after surgery.
  • Do dental implants need special care? Not really. Caring for your dental implants is just like caring for your natural teeth. Following an excellent oral hygiene routine of brushing and flossing and seeing your dentist for regularly scheduled visits will keep your gums, teeth, and dental implants healthy .
