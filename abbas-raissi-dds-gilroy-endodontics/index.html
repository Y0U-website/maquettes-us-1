With over 22 years of clinical experience, Dr. Raissi has dedicated his career exclusively to endodontics, providing advanced root canal and specialty care to patients in Gilroy and surrounding communities. Our staff combines extensive experience, modern technology, and a compassionate approach to help preserve natural teeth and deliver predictable, long-lasting results.

Endodontics is a dental specialty focused on diagnosing and treating problems inside the tooth. As an endodontic specialized practice, we concentrate on procedures such as root canal treatment, endodontic retreatment, and other complex cases that require advanced training and precision that save teeth and alleviate pain.
