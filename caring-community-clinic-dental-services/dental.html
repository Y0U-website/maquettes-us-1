The Dental Clinic operates out of the Morgan Building which was donated to the organization in November of 2016. The building was dedicated to Dr. Ken & Mary Morgan in May 2017. The first dental patient was helped in September 2017.

Caring Community Dental Clinic The Caring Community Dental Clinic in Jacksonville is part of the healthcare safety net in Onslow County. The clinic provides tooth extractions to low-income, uninsured persons, ages 18 and over. Services are funded by private contributions and grants.
  • Clients are not required to pay but are encouraged to make a donation if possible.
  • Volunteer dentists and a small staff give patient-centered care that increases quality of life for individuals and improves population health among vulnerable populations.
