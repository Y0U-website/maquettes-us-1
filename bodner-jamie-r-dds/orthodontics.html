Orthodontics

Invisalign is used to correct crooked, jumbled, overly-spaced teeth and malpositioning of the teeth that can later cause problems such as gum conditions, overcrowding , grinding and clenching of the teeth and teeth that break or chip due to misalignment.

What is the process of getting your Invisalign ?

The dentist will take an x-ray and do an oral evaluation to look at the placement of the teeth. This step is instrumental in determining if you are a candidate for Invisalign based upon your teeth positioning, bone and gum structure, and gum condition. Then, a full mouth scan will be done via a simulation to determine the length of the treatment, and how many aligners are required to shift the teeth. Before pictures are taken and submitted to Invisalign, and the comparison of the before and after appearance becomes available.

Once the aligners are ordered, the Invisaligncompany fabricates the custom aligner trays, specifically designed for your mouth. Aligners must be worn for a minimum of 22 hours per day, each aligner lasting for several weeks. Each sequential aligner slowly moves the teeth to the ideal location; helping you get the beautiful smile you deserve.

  • When you are ready for meals, the aligners must be removed prior to eating.
  • Teeth should be brushed before replacing the aligners to ensure that all food particles are removed, to hinder the progression of cavities.
  • There are no restrictions on what types of food can be eaten, like with traditional metal braces. You can have it all!
  • These are less painful than metal braces, and can be removed for the purposes of photos.
  • Most patients are finished with their treatment in a year!
  • Invisalign also provides the retainers for after your aligner treatment, with no additional charge!
