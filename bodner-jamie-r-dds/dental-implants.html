Dental Implants

Implants are an excellent and permanent solution for replacing missing teeth, in a natural way, without having to shave down the neighboring teeth. Dental implants are advantageous because of their appearance and that they are not removable like dentures.

What is it like to get implants at our office ?

After a tooth has been removed, and the gum, bone and surrounding tissue has healed, an implant can be placed to begin the replacement process. The implant is essentially a screw, which is drilled beneath the gum, into the bone as the support for the remainder of the implant parts. After the implant placement, the gum, bone and supporting tissue needs to heal and regrow around the implant. Once the healing process is complete, an impression of the implant area is taken.

This model is sent out to a dental laboratory and is worked on by the dental laboratory technicians. After a few days, the permanent implant crown returns to the office and is cemented and attached via another piece called an abutment, completing the treatment for that tooth.

Dental Crowns

What are Crowns ?

When a cavity has become too enlarged, a regular composite filling may not suffice to completely seal the tooth and treat the symptomatic effects of the tooth being exposed. Thus, dentists use crowns to completely treat the tooth.

What are the indications for a dental crown?

Crowns can be done after root canal treatment, or without root canal therapy; depending on the condition of the tooth and the decay sustained. A cracked tooth, large previous restorations, an esthetic upgrade are some other indications. Veneers

What is a veneer ?

A veneer is very similar to a crown, in the functions of esthetics and is most often done to patients’ front teeth. Veneers are essentially shells of ceramic on the surface of the teeth to cover worn out or eroded enamel, uneven teeth and spacing and chips or cracks in the structure of the teeth.

Bridges

What is a Dental bridge?

A bridge is a fixed prosthetic device that can be done to cap the teeth and to bridge a gap that is present due to missing teeth. This is not a removable appliance; instead it is cemented onto existing teeth and can only be removed by a dental professional.

When do we recommend bridges?

Bridges are recommended if one or more teeth are missing, and are done to replace the empty space, where the teeth once were. In addition, implant bridges can also be done to replace missing teeth and can also have implants built into them, as well. The appliance attaches to the teeth on both ends and are made of crown like material to mimic the natural teeth that are now missing.

What happens when you don’t replace missing teeth?

Failure to replace the missing teeth can cause the remaining teeth to shift into the empty space, creating malocclusion and a bad bite, and may lead to gum disease and temporomandibular joint complications.

Teeth Whitening

Why is teeth whitening done ?

Teeth whitenings are done when stains and discoloration are present on the surface of the teeth. It is a cosmetic treatment that is done to create and correct color changes in the teeth.

What causes stains in your teeth?

Consuming food or beverages with heavy artificial colors, natural food colors like turmeric, drinking excessive black coffee, red wine, lack of oral hygiene, trauma, smoking and natural aging are some reasons why teeth may stain overtime.

Are you the right candidate for teeth whitening ?

The first step to finding out if a whitening is right for you, is to schedule an appointment with our dentists to evaluate the strength of your teeth and gum condition. A dental screening and evaluation is essential before starting your whitening.

What are the different ways to perform teeth whitening?

In-Office Whitening

The use of peroxide gel in high concentrations is efficient in changing the shade of your teeth by brightening them. The gums are protected from being bleached, and the gel is placed on the teeth surfaces carefully and in fixed time intervals. In no time at all, your smile can be bright and white!

Prescription Strength Take-Home Whitening Kits

These take home kits are excellent options for right before a party or a performance. They are usually a week long treatment of pre-filled trays done from the comfort of your own home. These use hydrogen peroxide as the active ingredient, in a lower dose than in-office whitenings.

Do it Yourself Whitenings

You can find teeth whitening products such as whitening toothpastes and mouthwashes and whitening strips at your local pharmacy!
