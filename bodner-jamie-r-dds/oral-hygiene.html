Oral Hygiene

What does your oral hygiene tell you about your general health?

The mouth is the port of entry to your digestive and respiratory tracts, and the bacteria present in your mouth can cause diseases.

Our body’s natural defenses and good oral care can keep bacteria under control, thus preventing diseases.

Good overall oral hygiene includes pink and healthy gum tissue, teeth that are clean and plaque free, and an absence of bleeding on brushing and flossing.

What do we do to improve your oral health?

We do professional dental cleanings with handheld instruments to remove substances that you cannot reach through regular brushing and flossing.

These substances include plaque, tartar, calculus and buildup.

Here at NYC dental Pro, we are happy to guide you with oral hygiene techniques and help you stay on the lookout for possible problem areas inside your mouth.

What can you do at home to maintain your oral hygiene?

Brush your teeth twice daily, everyday with toothpaste that has been accepted by the American Dental Association (ADA)

Floss your teeth at least once a day, to ensure removal of food particles, not captured by brushing.

Change your toothbrush every three to four months, or sooner if the bristles are frayed.

Eat right! A healthy diet and minimal snacking will keep your smile bright and healthy.

Visit us regularly for your professional dental cleanings, evaluations and x-rays. Consistent dental appointments can help us catch a problem in its beginning stage.

ADA-accepted toothpastes and antimicrobial mouth rinses can limit bacterial growth in the oral cavity, preventing various dental conditions such as gingivitis and other forms of gum disease and tooth decay.
