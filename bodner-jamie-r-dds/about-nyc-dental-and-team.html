Our Team Members

Dr. Venkatesh Swaminathan obtained his DDS degree from University of Detroit Mercy. He majored in periodontics with an oral pathology minor from Indiana University School of Dentistry. He graduated specializing in periodontics and implantology.

Dr. Venkatesh also practiced in India specializing in prosthodontics for over 9 years before he migrated to the United States.

Dr. V, as we all refer to him, is a Periodontist and Prosthodontist who performs various dental procedures to provide the most suitable treatment for his patients.
