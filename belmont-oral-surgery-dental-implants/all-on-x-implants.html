What is the All-On-X Concept?

The All-On-X procedure is a modern approach to full-mouth reconstruction. The "X" represents the variable number of implants used—often four or six—to permanently anchor a full bridge of prosthetic teeth. By placing the implants in areas with the greatest bone density, this technique often avoids the need for extensive bone grafting and provides a secure, stable foundation for your new smile, typically in a single day.

Benefits of All-On-X Implants

Immediate Function

In most cases, you will leave your surgical appointment with a beautiful, fixed temporary bridge, allowing you to eat and smile with confidence on the same day.

Unmatched Stability

Because your new teeth are anchored directly to your jaw bone, they will never slip or move like traditional dentures. This eliminates discomfort and allows you to enjoy all your favorite foods.

Preserves Jaw Health

The dental implants stimulate the jaw bone just like natural tooth roots, which halts the bone deterioration process that occurs after tooth loss and helps maintain your natural facial contours.

A Permanent Solution

With proper care and regular checkups, your All-On-X restoration is designed to last a lifetime, making it a lasting investment in your health and well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "All-On-X" mean?

"All-On-X" is a term for a full-arch tooth replacement solution where a complete bridge of teeth is permanently supported by a specific number of dental implants. The "X" can be 4, 6, or another number depending on your unique anatomy and needs.

Am I a good candidate for this procedure?

Ideal candidates are those missing most or all of their teeth in an arch, current denture wearers seeking a more stable solution, or those with multiple failing teeth that require extraction. A consultation with 3D imaging is needed to assess your jaw bone and determine your candidacy.

How do I care for my All-On-X bridge?

You will care for your new teeth much like you would natural teeth, with regular brushing and the use of a water flosser to clean underneath the bridge. Routine professional cleanings and checkups are also essential to ensure the long-term health of your implants.

Is the procedure really done in one day?

Yes, the surgical procedure to remove any remaining failing teeth, place the dental implants, and attach a beautiful, fixed temporary set of teeth is typically completed in a single day. The final, permanent bridge is placed a few months later after the implants have fully healed.
