Our standard The practice of Amisha V. Patel, DMD is committed to making this website accessible to all visitors, including people who use assistive technologies. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we test against those guidelines as the site evolves. Measures in place ✦ Sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements
  • ✦ Full keyboard navigability with visible focus indicators
  • ✦ A skip-to-content link on every page
  • ✦ Descriptive alternative text on images
  • ✦ Clearly labeled form fields with accessible error messages
  • ✦ A logical heading structure on every page
  • ✦ Touch targets sized for easy use on phones and tablets
  • ✦ Support for reduced-motion preferences
  • ✦ A mobile-first responsive layout tested at small screen sizes

If you encounter a barrier

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some content may not yet be fully accessible to every person or technology. If a part of this site presents a barrier for you, you can reach the practice by phone for any information on it, and reported barriers are reviewed as part of maintaining the site.
