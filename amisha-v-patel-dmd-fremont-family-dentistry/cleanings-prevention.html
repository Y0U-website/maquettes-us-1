Frequently asked questions

How long does a teeth cleaning take?

A routine cleaning at our office usually takes 45 to 60 minutes, including time for your hygienist to check in on how your gums are doing. We never rush cleanings — thorough and gentle beats fast.

How long after a teeth cleaning can I eat?

After a routine cleaning you can eat right away. If your visit includes a fluoride treatment, we recommend waiting about 30 minutes so the fluoride has time to do its job.

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

For most healthy mouths, every six months. If you have a history of gum disease, Dr. Patel may recommend periodontal maintenance visits every three to four months instead — a different type of cleaning that protects treated gums.
