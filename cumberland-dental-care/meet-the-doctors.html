Meet Dr. Greer and Dr. Prince

0 MEET OUR DOCTORS Ben Greer DMD A native of St. Marys, Dr. Greer graduated from Camden County High School and attended Valdosta State University to earn his Bachelors Degree in Biology. His career path towards dentistry was set into motion by a boating accident, which resulted in dental trauma and reconstruction. He was amazed at how impactful dentistry could be in a person’s life, and put all his efforts into gaining acceptance to dental school.

After completing dental school at The Dental College of Georgia in 2018, Dr. Greer accepted an optional two-year position at DCG in the General Practice ResidencyProgram. With the idea of complete dentistry, he gained skills in implant surgery, IV and oral sedation, esthetics, crown and bridge, full mouth reconstruction and even some root canal procedures. Dr. Greer prides himself on staying up to date with modern dentistry and taking continuing education courses regularly.

His professional memberships include the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and is a fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

Dr. Greer is happily married to his wife, Leanne who are proud parents their beautiful girl, Elizabeth, and a labrador retriever, Abilene. Outside of the office, Dr. Greer enjoys golf, fishing, and hunting. His most unique hobby is beekeeping, from which he jars his own honey!

Stanley Prince DMD

Dr. Prince is a graduate of Camden County High School, the University of Georgia (Go DAWGS!!) with a BS in Biology and graduated from the Medical College of Georgia where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 1991.

Dr. Prince enjoys giving back to his community –both to his home of Camden County and to the medical community as well. He is an active member of many dental societies including the Academy of General Dentistry , Georgia Dental Association , American Orthodontic Society , and the American Dental Association .

Dr. Prince and his staff participate annually in “Project Smile”, a mission that delivers much needed dental care to hundreds of children in Belize, C.A... Dr. Prince gives back to his community through his work with the local Kiwanis club of St. Mary’s. He is also a member of the First Baptist Church of Kingsland. He enjoys spending time with his wife, Julie, and their children, and in his spare time he enjoys hunting, fishing, and riding his motorcycle.
