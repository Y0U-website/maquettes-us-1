About Our Practice

Founded in 1981 by Dr. Samuel LaValley, Polzin & LaValley Family Dentistry offers complete dental care to families from Menominee, MI, and surrounding communities. From the beginning, our practice has nurtured a reputation for kind, patient-focused services. We are passionate about helping our patients achieve their best oral health and partner with them to bring their dream smiles to life.

Dr. Polzin and our staff genuinely care about you, your smile, and your family. As a locally owned practice, we believe in building community and giving back to the area we live and work in. Throughout the years, we have worked hard to create an environment where patients from three to 103 years old feel comfortable and cared for. Our practice is newly remodeled to incorporate the latest technology and treatments, including dental implants and laser dentistry. You and your loved ones can count on us for all of your general , cosmetic , and restorative dental needs.
