Dr. Patrice A. Barber A native of Silver Spring, Maryland, Dr. Patrice Barber received a full academic scholarship to Florida A&M University upon graduation from high school. After graduating summa cum laude with a degree in biology/pre-dentistry, she went on to continue her education at the University of Maryland Dental School. Here she was a recipient of the U.S. Army’s Health Professions…

Upon entering the Army, Dr. Barber was commissioned as a Captain and attended a year-long residency in general dentistry (AEGD-1) at Fort Sill, OK before completing a tour at Camp Casey, Korea. She completed her military obligation at Fort Bragg and transitioned to civilian dentistry in 2004. She then spent 5 years as an associate at a group practice in the Fayetteville area before striking out…

Outside of the office, Dr. Barber regularly makes time to speak at various events within the community about the dental field and oral health issues. She is active at her church, serving as a mentor to youths, and is a member of the choir. She is an active member of several service organizations within the community.
