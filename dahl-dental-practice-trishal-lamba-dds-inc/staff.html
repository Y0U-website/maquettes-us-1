Welcome to our page! Just a little bit about me:

I grew up in Southern California and graduated with a degree in Biology from UCLA. During my gap year before dental school, I worked with the head of Cardiology in the Department of Medicine at UCLA on atherosclerosis research, eventually publishing a paper.

Attending Columbia College of Dental Medicine in New York, fulfilled two dreams in one stroke: living in New York and becoming a dentist. My tenure in New York helped me mature into the person I am today: someone who loves Broadway musicals, great food, and exploring. During my time in dental school I worked in student government as a Yearbook editor and Social Chair.I also served as President of the American Association of Women Dentists Organization.

I continued my dentistry training at the UCLA VA hospital with a General Practice Residency.

My love of volleyball led me to my career in dentistry. Watching my dentist restore my chipped front teeth after an accident in a game, I saw how important excellent dentistry can be to a person’s health, sense of well being and self confidence. I realized I wanted to be part of that process.

I am a dental hygienist with more than 30 years experience in local practices. I am passionate about welcoming patients into our dental practice and providing gentle care in response to their needs.
