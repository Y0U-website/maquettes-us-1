The Smile Restoration Project

Each year, we restore one deserving neighbor’s smile — a complete, fixed full-arch restoration provided at no cost, with implants generously donated by our partner BioHorizons. Because sometimes, restoring a smile restores a life.

Apply for yourself, nominate someone you love, or — if you’re a dentist — refer a patient.

What the Recipient Receives

One selected recipient will receive the following complimentary care, provided at our Southaven office by our doctors:

Three Ways to Open the Door

Choose the path that fits you. Applications are accepted online only — each takes about ten minutes, and every story is read with care.

For Myself

Missing or failing teeth have changed how you eat, speak, or smile — and the cost of treatment has kept it out of reach.

For Someone I Love

A parent, friend, neighbor, or coworker who deserves this — nominate them and tell their story on their behalf.

I’m a Dentist

Refer a patient from your practice. Your office remains their dental home — we provide the specialty care they can’t otherwise access.

Your Information

The person filling out this form. If you re nominating someone else, their details come next.

How the Recipient Is Chosen

This is not a drawing, and no one is chosen by luck. Every application is read by our team and weighed on five things:

Financial need

The story

Clinical suitability

Impact

Commitment

Treatment begins in January 2027, and — with the recipient’s blessing — the journey will be shared through the spring. Every application receives an acknowledgment when it arrives, and every applicant hears from us once the recipient is selected.

The Care Behind the Gift

The awarded treatment is provided by the doctors of Advanced Dental Implant & TMJ Center in Southaven, Mississippi.

Dr. Pradeep Adatrow, DDS, MSD

One of fewer than 100 clinicians in the United States board-certified in both the surgical and restorative specialties of implant dentistry — which means the recipient’s entire treatment, from surgery to final smile, is guided by a specialist certified in every phase of it.

Dr. Jay Patel, DDS, MPH

Trained at the University of Southern California and the University of Pittsburgh, Dr. Patel shares the practice’s belief that advanced care should change lives — not just smiles.
