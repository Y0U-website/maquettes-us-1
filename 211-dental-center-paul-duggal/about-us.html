About us

211 Dental Center Paul Duggal DDS PC has cared for families in Middletown, NY, since it opened over 40 years ago. Dr. Paul Duggal and his team offer comprehensive dental care at affordable prices.

Our office is located at 121 Wickham Avenue in Middletown, NY .

If you would like to make an appointment, please call (845) 343-9919 or send an email to info@paulduggaldds.com .

Dr. Paul Duggal

With over 37 years of experience in the Middletown area, Dr. Paul Duggal is a compassionate dentist who offers comprehensive, high quality gentle dental care in his state-of-the-art practice.

Dr. Duggal completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at NYU Dental School in 1980. He is a member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association and 9th District Dental Society.
