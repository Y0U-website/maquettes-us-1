AShfak uddin, dds General, Cosmetic and Implant Dentist

University of Buffalo - School of Dental Medicine

Growing up, Dr. Uddin always had a fascination with science and a passion for helping others. It was this combination that led him to pursue a career in dentistry.

After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the State University of New York at Buffalo (2013) Dr. Uddin completed a General Practice Residency at Faxton St. Luke’s Healthcare in Utica, NY allowing him to offer comprehensive services to patients of all ages. With a passion for dentistry and a commitment to ongoing education, he stays at the forefront of the latest advancements in dental techniques and technology, providing exceptional dental care with a gentle touch.

Dr. Uddin believes in the importance of patient education and takes the time to thoroughly explain treatment options and answer any questions to ensure each patient feels empowered to make informed decisions about their oral health. From digital smile design to minimally invasive procedures, he employs modern techniques to deliver superior results while prioritizing patient comfort and satisfaction.

Beyond his technical expertise, Dr. Uddin is known for his friendly demeanor and knack for putting even the most anxious patients at ease. He understands that dental visits can be daunting for some, which is why he and his team have created a sleek, modern office environment designed to enhance relaxation and minimize stress.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Uddin enjoys spending time with his family, exploring the outdoors, hiking, traveling and working out.

Dr. Grover began his career as a dentist in Middletown NY and New York City. He gained his clinical and administrative experience working for Dr. Duggal. Using the experience he gained early in his career he established his practice in Stamford CT.

Dr. Grover has been serving the local Middletown community for the past 8 years. With his team of doctors he is able to provide the highest quality of dental care to patients of all ages.

Dr. Grover joined 211 Dental Center again in 2022 providing Dental Implants and Invisalign.

He studied at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine where he earned his dental degree with honors. He regularly attends educational seminars and workshops to stay up to date with the latest advancement in dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association, AAID and the Connecticut Dental Association.

Dr. Grover is a dad to his beautiful 3 years old daughter. He enjoys being a dad and spending his weekends with his family.
