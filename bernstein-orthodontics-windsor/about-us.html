Meet Dr. Rael Bernstein

You deserve the very best from start to finish, and at Bernstein Orthodontics we don’t take that for granted. After struggling with a five-year journey in orthodontics as a child, Dr. Bernstein decided to become an orthodontist to help make orthodontic treatment a more rewarding and engaging adventure for patients.

You’ll Love Our Team

Dr. Bernstein and our team promise to work hard to give you the treatment in your best interest, will listen attentively to you, and warmly welcome you to each of your visits. Our friendly, professional team walks with you every step of the way—from your first visit to the exciting day you get your braces off.
