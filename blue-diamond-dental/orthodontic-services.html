The Long-Term Benefits of Straight Teeth

Choosing to straighten your teeth offers incredible benefits that go far beyond aesthetics. Properly aligned teeth create a much healthier environment in your mouth.

When your teeth overlap or crowd together, they create tight spaces where plaque and bacteria easily hide. Straight teeth are significantly easier to brush and floss, which drastically reduces your risk of developing cavities and gum disease. Furthermore, correcting a misaligned bite prevents uneven wear on your tooth enamel and relieves unnecessary stress on your jaw joints.
