Our Financing Philosophy

We know that financial matters can be sticky at times, and we’re here to help. We want our premium services to be accessible to all patients and we work with you to take any stress out of the treatment process. If you’re seeking a specific service that feels out of reach financially, we offer a complete payment plan with a flexible payment schedule.

Insurance and Billing

Anderson Dental Professionals accepts most traditional insurance plans. As long as you’re free to choose your own dentist, we’re happy to file insurance for your reimbursement. If you’re not sure about your plan, give us a call and our office will verify its acceptance. Although we don’t participate in Health Management Organizations, we will gladly file your insurance claims for you.

Payment Options

Our administrative experts are happy to submit all insurance forms for patients, making sure to recover the maximum benefits. We accept cash, check, MasterCard, Visa, Discover, and American Express. We also offer flexible payment plans and accept CareCredit.
