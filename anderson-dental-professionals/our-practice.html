Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays allow the dentist to capture an image of a tooth or several teeth and put it into an imaging program. The imaging program contains various tools that give the dentist a holistic and accurate view of the area in question as well as surrounding structures. Not only are they more precise, they’re also safer for patient health. These digital x-rays reduce radiation by nearly 80 % when compared to a standard x-ray, making them much better for the patient’s well-being.

Intraoral Camera

This incredible diagnostic tool allows dentists to view the entire mouth on a monitor and examine different angles that weren’t accessible even a few years ago. The intraoral camera permits closer examinations of any potential issues or problems so that your dentist can take effective action. Additionally, the patient has the ability to see the same images as the dentist firsthand. These images can be useful in gaining procedural acceptance from insurance companies as well.

Electrical Handpieces

Electric handpieces are a stronger alternative to air-driven handpieces, which are both used to eliminate decay. They allow dentists to remove decay from the tooth at a faster pace, cutting down on the time it takes to complete a procedure while maintaining precision accuracy.

Rotary Endodontics

Endodontics, more commonly known as root canal, is a necessary procedure for removing infected tooth roots and restoring oral health. Rotary Endodontics involves using a specific electrical hand piece to perform the root canal procedure, making it faster and giving the dentist increased ease and agility throughout the process.
