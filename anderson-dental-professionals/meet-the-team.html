About Us

Building lasting relationships with our patients over time is one of our staff’s favorite aspects of the dentistry business. Your comfort and health are our main priorities, and when you walk into our Crown Point office, you’re treated like family.

In order to provide premium quality treatment, each member of the Anderson Dental Professionals staff maintains the highest levels of accreditation and pursues ongoing education to stay ahead of the curve.

Read on to learn more about the friendly and knowledgeable faces you’ll encounter in our Crown Point office.

Meet Dr. William S. Anderson, D.D.S.

Dr. Bill Anderson has been practicing dentistry for 28-years. His father, Dr. Ned Anderson, started the Anderson Dental Professionals practice over 30 years ago. After completing his undergraduate degree at DePauw University in Greencastle, Indiana, he went on to the Indiana University School of Dentistry, where he graduated in 1994.

Dr. Bill enjoys staying current on industry news and advancements in dentistry. He continues to take educational classes, ensuring that his patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced dental care possible. Above all, Dr. Bill prides himself on his gentle approach and the building of genuine relationships with each of his patients.

Meet Dr. Anderson

Dr. Natalie Anderson is a dedicated and passionate dental professional based in Valparaiso, IN. With a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine and a Bachelor of Science in Neuroscience from Indiana University’s Hutton Honors College, Natalie brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to our practice.

Dr. Natalie’s dedication to excellence has been recognized through honors such as the Hutton Honors College Degree, Magna Cum Laude distinction from Indiana University, Phi Beta Kappa society member and the James W. and Betty Dye Scholarship.

Committed to providing exceptional care, Dr. Natalie has developed comprehensive treatment plans, utilizing advanced dental technologies and collaborating with specialists. She is devoted to continuing education which ensures she stays at the forefront of advancements in dentistry. Passion for oral health extends to her volunteer work, where she provided dental care to underserved communities in Nicaragua and Panama. Dr. Natalie’s commitment to excellence is evident through her academic achievements and certifications in CPR and laser technology.

In her free time, Dr. Natalie enjoys cooking, reading, and exploring the great outdoors, having visited over 20 national parks and aiming to visit all 63 of them! Outside of work Dr. Natalie is a passionate animal lover who adores her black lab Benji and orange tabby Nala.

Dr. Natalie’s unwavering commitment to patient care, coupled with her diverse skills and experiences, make her a valuable asset to any dental practice. We are thrilled for you to meet her!
