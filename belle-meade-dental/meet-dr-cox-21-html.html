Meet Dr. Cox

Dr. Cox has been married to his wife, Leah, for 23 years and has three sons - Jonas, Fletcher and Asher. His family attends Pinelake Church, where Dr. Cox leads men’s bible study and has participated in yearly dental missions. An avid cyclist and runner, Dr. Cox is a 4x Ironman Finisher. He enjoys coaching his son’s baseball team and hunting in his spare time. Dr. Cox says that "dentistry is a calling, not only to help my patients with their dental needs, but I’m also there as an encourager of overall health and spiritual well-being."
