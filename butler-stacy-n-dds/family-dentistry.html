The Best Care With State-Of-The-Art Digital X-Rays

Our state-of-the-art digital X-rays allow our dentist to take an image of your tooth or teeth and put it into an imaging program. These X-rays emit nearly 80 % less radiation than a standard X-ray. Other benefits include:

  • Amazing diagnostic tool for viewing different angles in your mouth.
  • Ability to view the entire mouth.
  • Get a closer look at any potential issue or problem.
  • See for yourself, firsthand, the same images the doctor is seeing.
  • Excellent for gaining procedure acceptance from insurance companies.
