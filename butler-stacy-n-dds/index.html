Dental implants can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or all the teeth in your mouth. Best of all, they look and feel like natural teeth.

The entire implant process may be performed over a period of months based on the individual. Your smile will look just like it used to. After a short period of getting used to the implant, it will feel just like one of your own teeth. The process goes as follows:

An incision is made in the gum so that the implant can be inserted.

Installation of the implant itself, where a screw is placed into the jaw bone.

Temporary restorations may be installed so that you can eat and speak normally and maintain a proper aesthetic appearance for your smile.

After healing an abutment on the implant, serving as the base for your new tooth.

An impression of the abutment is taken and used to create your restoration.
