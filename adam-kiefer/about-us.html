Welcome to Kiefer Family Dental

An Evansville native, Dr. Adam Kiefer is excited to have the opportunity to bring his community the services and skills that he has developed over the years. Dr. Max Lingo established a well-respected dental practice in his centrally located practice for over 40 years, and with his passing, Dr. Kiefer has the ability to continue in his legacy to provide high quality, patient focused, comfortable dental care. Kiefer Family Dental was officially established in December of 2020, and we are excited to welcome you to our practice for all of your dental needs.

Dr. Kiefer attended Indiana University for both his undergraduate and dental degrees and has worked locally in Evansville since 2015. It is his mission to provide you his honest recommendations while working with your treatment desires as a patient. No matter how minimal or extensive your dental needs may be, Dr. Kiefer will work with you to address your oral health concerns. Kiefer Family Dental provides comprehensive dental services including oral surgery , implants , cosmetics , emergency appointments, clear aligner therapy, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and routine check-ups.

Your oral health is our biggest priority. Our commitment is to treat you and your family with compassionate, honest and professional dental care. Kiefer Family Dental will be expanding insurance participation to include most insurance plans so you can receive the care you deserve. Call us today to schedule a visit !
