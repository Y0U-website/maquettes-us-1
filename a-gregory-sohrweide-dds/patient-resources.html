Dental FAQs

Do we accept dental insurance?

Yes, but we are an out-of-network dental office. I will submit it to your insurance and whatever they don’t cover, I will send you a bill for the remaining. We cannot accept Medicaid which includes Fidelis and Healthplex, they strictly only want in-network dentists.

Do we do emergency visits?

It depends on the situation. If you need an extraction or root canal treatment, we would refer you to a specialist, as we do not extract teeth or do root canals. If you broke a tooth or lost a filling, we can normally get you in fairly soon.
