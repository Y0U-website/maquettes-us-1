Caring Dental Services for the Entire Family

Preventive Dentistry

Each member of our team is dedicated to helping our patients achieve healthy smiles. Our general and preventive dentistry services focus on keeping your teeth, gums, and mouth healthy. To complement our in-office teeth cleanings and exams, our hygienists and doctor will work with you to establish great at-home dental hygiene habits.

Routine exams and cleanings are important for helping us create a personalized treatment plan for every patient. During these appointments, we will clean and polish your teeth, check for any signs of gum disease or oral cancer, review your dental history, and take any necessary x-rays. To help our younger patients avoid cavities, we may apply dental sealants to provide an additional layer of protection to the teeth.

Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

Our cosmetic and restorative dentistry treatments are designed to help repair your teeth and restore luminescence to your smile. For your smile to stay healthy and beautiful for a lifetime, it needs to retain strength and structure. We utilize state-of-the-art technology to ensure you are receiving modern treatments and thorough results.

• Complete Dentures • Partial Dentures • Crowns • Bridges • Dental Fillings • Root Canal Treatment • Bondings

Emergency Dental Service

At A. Gregory Sohrweide, DDS, we understand that dental emergencies can happen at any time. If you or a loved one is facing a dental crisis, don’t hesitate to call our office at 315-638-0265 for immediate assistance. We prioritize urgent care and will strive to accommodate you as quickly as possible, especially if you are in pain.

Common Emergency Dental Issues Contact us if you are experiencing any of the following dental emergencies:

For any after-hours dental emergencies, please call 315-472-2679 . Our team is ready to assist you with your emergency dentistry needs, ensuring you receive the care you deserve.

  • Knocked-out (Avulsed) Teeth: Quick action can save your tooth. Rinse it gently and keep it moist.
  • Severe Tooth Pain: This can indicate various issues; prompt evaluation is crucial.
  • Broken, Chipped, or Cracked Teeth: We can restore your smile and alleviate discomfort.
  • Objects Lodged Between Teeth: Don’t attempt to remove them yourself; we can help safely.
  • Cuts or Injuries to the Tongue, Gums, Cheeks, or Lips: These may require immediate care to prevent further complications.
  • Jaw or Facial Pain: This can be a sign of serious conditions that need urgent attention.
  • Lost Dental Restorations: Crowns, fillings, and bridges can be replaced swiftly.
