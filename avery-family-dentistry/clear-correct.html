The Clear Way To Straighten Your Teeth

Frequently Asked Questions

If crooked or crowded teeth are affecting your confidence or standing in the way of your best oral health, ClearCorrect may be the ideal solution. To find out if you’re a candidate, schedule a consultation with Dr. Avery by calling (847) 662-6080 .

During your visit, you’ll have the opportunity to share your smile goals, and Dr. Avery will assess your oral health to create a personalized treatment plan tailored specifically to your needs.

You’ll wear your ClearCorrect aligners for most of the day—typically around 22 hours—removing them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. While treatment plans vary from patient to patient, most cases are completed in about twelve months.

Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit the dental office periodically to check your progress and receive your next set of aligners, ensuring everything stays on track.

Although ClearCorrect aligners are much easier to maintain than traditional braces, it’s still important to follow a few simple guidelines to keep them clean and in great shape between sets.

Along with removing your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water, be sure to:

  • Rinse before reinserting: Always rinse your aligners with water before placing them back in your mouth to remove saliva and debris.
  • Soak regularly: Clean your aligners by soaking them in a clear, anti-bacterial soap solution to prevent cloudiness and odor.
  • Maintain oral hygiene: Continue brushing your teeth twice a day and flossing daily to keep your mouth healthy throughout treatment.
  • Brush aligners gently: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and water—without toothpaste—to gently clean your aligners when you brush your teeth.
  • Store properly: When not in use, keep your aligners in their protective case to avoid loss or damage.
