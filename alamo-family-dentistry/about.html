About Alamo Family Dentistry

Aesthetic and Implant Dentist & Family Dentistry located in Alamo, CA

At Alamo Family Dentistry, patients of all ages receive compassionate, high-quality dental care in a welcoming, family-friendly environment. Located in Alamo, Danville, San Ramon, Walnut Creek, California, experienced dental expert Sushma Balakrishna, DDS, and the team are committed to helping every patient achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.

The experienced team at Alamo Family Dentistry offers a full range of services from routine cleanings and preventive care to cosmetic treatments , dental implants , Invisalign , and emergency dentistry. They take the time to listen, answer questions, and create personalized treatment plans that reflect each patient’s unique needs and goals.

At the heart of the practice is a focus on comfort and education. Whether you’re visiting for your child’s first checkup or exploring options to restore your smile, the Alamo Family Dentistry team is here to support you every step of the way. With a gentle approach and the latest technology, they strive to make every visit as smooth and stress-free as possible.

Alamo Family Dentistry proudly accepts most major PPO insurance plans and makes it easy to schedule care that fits your lifestyle. Patients return year after year for the warm, attentive service and the lasting relationships they build with the team.

Experience friendly, modern dental care that puts your needs first schedule your visit with Alamo Family Dentistry by phone or online.
