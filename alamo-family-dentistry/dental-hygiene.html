Dental Hygiene

Dental Hygiene services offered in Alamo, CA

Good dental hygiene is the foundation of a healthy, confident smile. At Alamo Family Dentistry in San Ramon, California, Sushma Balakrishna, DDS, and her team provide thorough dental cleanings and hygiene support to help patients prevent cavities, gum disease, and other common issues. Whether you’re coming in for a routine checkup or getting back on track with your oral health, the team offers gentle, judgment-free care tailored to your needs. What does a dental hygiene visit include?

A professional hygiene visit is one of the most effective ways to keep your teeth and gums healthy. It’s more than just a polish your appointment includes a full cleaning by a skilled dental hygienist who gently removes plaque and tartar from spots that are easy to miss at home. Your teeth will also be polished to remove surface stains and leave your smile feeling fresh and clean.

After your cleaning, Dr. Balakrishna will review any concerns and answer questions about your oral health or treatment options.

  • Digital X-rays to check for cavities or bone loss
  • A gum health screening to catch early signs of gingivitis or periodontal disease
  • Fluoride treatment , if needed, to strengthen enamel and reduce sensitivity
  • One-on-one hygiene tips , customized to your needs and daily routine

Why are dental cleanings important?

Even with great brushing and flossing habits, plaque can build up in hard-to-reach places. Over time, it hardens into tartar, which can’t be removed without professional tools. If left untreated, that buildup can lead to gum inflammation, tooth decay, and other issues.

Routine care is one of the easiest ways to avoid complex, costly procedures later on.

  • Prevent cavities and gum disease
  • Maintain a bright, stain-free smile
  • Freshen breath by removing odor-causing bacteria
  • Identify problems early, before they become serious
  • Support your overall health, since poor oral health is linked to heart disease, diabetes, and more

How often should you get a cleaning?

Most people benefit from a dental cleaning every six months, but your ideal schedule may vary based on your needs. Patients with a history of gum disease, dry mouth, or medical conditions like diabetes may require more frequent care. The team at Alamo Family Dentistry will help you determine what’s best for you.
