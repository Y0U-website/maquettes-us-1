Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear Aligner Therapy is a modern alternative to straighten your teeth without wires or metal. Clear Aligner Therapy involves a series of custom trays designed specifically for you and the plan that Dr. Akhrass creates to create to move your teeth. The trays are made from a smooth plastic that covers your teeth and gradually moves them into place.

Retainers

Retainers are used to maintain the position of your teeth, after they have been moved orthodontically. Here at Akhrass Dental Implant & Cosmetic Center, retainers are made for our patients who have completed clear aligner therapy, as well as for existing patients who have been treated with traditional braces by an orthodontist.
