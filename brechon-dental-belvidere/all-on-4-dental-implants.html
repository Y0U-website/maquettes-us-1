The All On 4 Dental Implant Procedure

Prior to the innovation of All-on-4 dental implants, if you wanted a dental implant you underwent almost a year of treatment when Brechon Dental would extract the teeth, allow for sufficient healing and denture wearing, bone grafting if needed and more healing, place the dental implants, more healing time with more denture wearing and then finally placing the false tooth on the healed implant. Now, with the new All-on-4 dental implant procedure, the entire process, from extraction to implant placement to fixed teeth, is achievable in just one visit.

All on Four Dental Implant Aftercare

Research has shown that placing the dental implants immediately after tooth extraction can improve the outcome of the procedure. Brechon Dental has seen that patients can resume a normal diet in just six weeks after the implants are placed. The more closely you follow your specific after-care instructions from us, the better the results you will experience.

During the days following the more invasive procedure, eating softer foods will keep you comfortable. Once the healing process begins, you can gradually include harder and more abrasive foods. Once Brechon Dental has completed your All-on-4 dental implant procedure, and you are completely healed, you will need to take care of your new teeth and your jaw to keep your overall oral health strong.
