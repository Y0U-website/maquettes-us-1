Dental Implants

Dental implants are a popular and effective dental service used to replace missing teeth. They are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth, providing a long-lasting solution for those who have lost one or more teeth.

At Bay Area Dental , Dr. Theresa Kujala specializes in the custom restoration phase of your treatment. To ensure the highest level of care, she works in close collaboration with premier local oral surgeons who expertly handle the surgical placement. Once your implant has safely healed, Dr. Kujala beautifully crafts and attaches your permanent, custom restoration right here in our office. These restorations can be used to support a variety of options, including single implants, full-mouth implants, and implant-supported bridges.

Single Implants

Single implants are used to replace one missing tooth. Following the surgical placement of the implant post by a trusted local specialist, the area is allowed to fuse with the bone over a period of several months. Once this healing process is complete, Dr. Kujala takes over to custom-craft your restoration. An abutment is attached to the implant, and she places a custom-made crown on top. This creates a natural-looking, functional replacement tooth that is designed to last for many years.

Full-Mouth Implants

Full-mouth implants are used to replace all of the teeth in the upper or lower jaw. This is a more extensive procedure that typically involves placing multiple implants throughout the jawbone by our trusted surgical partners. After the implants have successfully fused with the bone, Dr. Kujala masterfully designs and secures a full, custom-tailored arch of replacement teeth to complete your smile. Full-mouth implants are a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for those who have lost all of their teeth.

Implant-supported bridges are used to replace several missing teeth in a row. Supported by two or more dental implants placed by a local specialist, these implants provide a highly stable anchor. Dr. Kujala then designs and attaches a durable bridge that is crafted to look and function just like your natural teeth. Implant-supported bridges are a long-lasting solution for those who have lost multiple teeth in a row.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Improved Appearance

Dental implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth, providing a natural-looking, aesthetically pleasing solution for missing teeth.

Convenience

Dental implants are a convenient solution for missing teeth, offering a seamless, coordinated treatment process between Dr. Kujala and your specialist.

Durability

Dental implants are a durable and long-lasting solution. With the proper care and maintenance, they can last a lifetime.

Restored Function

Dental implants are designed to function like natural teeth, allowing patients to eat, speak, and smile with full confidence.

Preservation of Jawbone

Dental implants can help to preserve the jawbone and prevent bone loss, which can occur when teeth are missing.

Secure, Permanent Fit

Dental implants are permanently anchored into your jawbone, meaning they will never slip, click, shift, or cause embarrassing moments for you.

Transform Your Smile with Advanced Implant Technology

At Bay Area Dental, Dr. Theresa Kujala offers dental implants as a long-term solution for tooth loss. They are also used to secure partial or complete dentures as well as support for dental bridges.

While the foundational artificial tooth root is surgically anchored into your jaw by a skilled local surgeon, Dr. Kujala meticulously designs the visible replacement tooth that sits on top. Since these implants are held by the jawbone, they have the same dependable foundation as natural tooth roots. With permanently restored implants, you will no longer have to experience discomfort in chewing, laughing or speaking.

Our team will discuss all dental solutions with you to help you make the most informed decisions about your dental health. Dr. Kujala is highly skilled in the custom restoration of dental implants, managing your entire treatment plan from start to finish alongside our surgical partners, and she will help you determine the best implant pathway for your smile.
