6 Benefits of Clear Aligners to Straighten Teeth

Do you hide your teeth when you smile? It can be difficult to feel confident or attractive when you have crooked teeth. But the thought of spending years in metal braces? Nothing could be worse. Thankfully, our dentist in Bay Area Dental offers an alternative to conventional treatment with clear aligners! Clear aligners have helped countless individuals achieve beautiful, straight smiles without the hassle and they can help you, too! Choosing clear aligners comes with several benefits, including:

Discrete

Clear aligners are custom-made dental trays that fit over your teeth and are virtually invisible while being worn. You won’t have to feel self-conscious about metal brackets and wires taking over your smile.

Oral Hygiene

Clear aligners allow you to maintain optimal dental hygiene during treatment. You won’t have to carefully maneuver your toothbrush around brackets or floss between wires.

Convenient

Clear aligners can easily be removed whenever you eat, drink, brush, and floss. You won’t have to limit the types of food you eat or alter your original oral hygiene routine and habits.

Comfortable

Clear aligners are made from flexible plastic that comfortably fit along your gum line and over your teeth. No poking wires or sharp metal brackets that irritate your gums, tongue, or the soft tissue inside your mouth.

Treatment Length

Clear aligner treatment is often shorter. In some cases, patients complete clear aligner treatment in half the time as conventional braces. Your treatment time will depend on the condition of your teeth.

Fewer Appointments

Unlike traditional braces, you are sent home with several weeks’ worth of trays at a time, meaning fewer interruptions to your busy schedule and shorter, simpler check-ins with Dr. Kujala.
