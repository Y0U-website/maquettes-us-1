Appointments

Please call today, 631-581-5121, to arrange a personal appointment time. Your call will be received by one of our professional team members. They will become your personal ambassador, answer any questions you may have, explain how you can get started and arrange a convenient appointment.

Location

The office is centrally located on the South Shore of Long Island at 375 East Main Street, East Islip, NY 11730. We are situated just seconds off Exit 45 of the Southern Parkway in a free standing building. We are approximately 15 minutes from MacArthur/Islip Airport.

Transportation

We provide complimentary private car service to and from local train stations, hotels and MacArthur Airport.

We can arrange hotel accommodations at the Hilton Garden Inn 3485 Veterans Memorial Highway, Ronkonkoma, NY 11779 631-738-7800, the Marriott Courtyard 5000 Express Drive South, Ronkonkoma, NY 11779 631-612-5000, or the Residence Inn 7 Court House Dr, Central Islip, NY 11722 631-231-0025... or you can contact them directly. Lounge

Although there will be minimal waiting time during your visit, we can provide beverages in our reception area. For those requiring privacy, our private lounge is available and equipped with WiFi, television, full size refridge, microwave, private lavatory, desk/conference table and couch.

Local Attractions

East Islip Public Library, Hecksher State Park, and Bayard Cutting Arboretum are relaxing attractions very close by. For excitement, Bethpage Ballpark, home of the Long Island Ducks Minor League Baseball team is just 15 minutes away. Smith Haven Mall and Tanger Deer Park Outlets are nearby and sure to fulfill shoppers’ desires. Delicious meals and drinks can be had at Vinnie’s Mulberry Street, Lily Flanagans, and Verace,... with possible celebrity sightings at nearby Teller’s Steakhouse.
