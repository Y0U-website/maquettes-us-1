Orthodontics for Adults

It’s never too late...

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids anymore…

A large percentage of our patients are adults, and they agree that it’s never too late to improve their greatest asset – their smile.

Tooth alignment can be changed at any age if your tooth, gum, and bone structures are healthy. We offer a variety of treatments that are designed with adults in mind. We work closely with your general dentist to give you an amazing smile and influence your overall dental health in a positive way! Your new smile can begin today!

FAQs

  • Am I past the age to receive orthodontic treatment? A beautiful smile can be achieved through orthodontic treatment at any age as long as your tooth, gum, and bone structures are healthy! It’s never too late to get the smile you’ve always wanted. With advances in technology, it is now an easier and an better experience for adults to go through orthodontic treatment. Even if you are looking at restorative options with your dentist, we will work side-by-side to provide a collaborative approach for the best possible outcome!
  • How long will treatment last? Since everyone’s biology and dental situations vary, treatment times will be different for each patient. We will be able give you an estimated treatment time prescribed specifically for you during the initial screening appointment .
  • What are common orthodontic problems? Some of the common orthodontic problems that we are able to treat include: Underbite
  • Spacing
  • Upper Front Teeth Protrusion
  • Crowding
  • Overbite
  • Openbite
  • Skewed Dental Midlines
  • How can we accelerate my treatment? Some patients will qualify to use our AcceleDent Aura technology. With this device, tooth movement may be accelerated by approximately 40 % without causing any underlying issues! AcceleDent Aura uses SoftPulse Technology™ to generate small vibrations, or micropulses through a mouthpiece used nightly to enhance the movements directed by the orthodontics, allowing for faster tooth movement.
  • Do you accept my insurance? We are not a direct participator with insurance companies, though we do accept assignment of benefits if they are available through your plan. We offer many financial options in addition to any insurance assignment benefit, including lower down payments and customizable payment options. All financial options will be discussed and given to you at the initial consult.
