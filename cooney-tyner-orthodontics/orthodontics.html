Orthodontics

We strive to time the start of orthodontic treatment at the most ideal window of opportunity of growth and development for each individual patient. This helps to ensure that each patient receives the best possible results in the most efficient and predictable manner.

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just for kids anymore! Tooth and bite alignment can be changed at any age if your tooth, gum, and bone structure is healthy. We offer a variety of treatment options that are designed for different situations and all age groups.

  • Children Give your child a lifetime of healthy smiles by evaluating their need for treatment early. Though an orthodontist can enhance a smile at any age, there is an optimal time period to begin treatment. Beginning treatment at the right time ensures the greatest result with a more efficient use of your time. Learn More
  • Teen Take advantage of your child’s growth by seeking orthodontic treatment to help align their bite and teeth in a proper position. The most common period for treatment is between the ages of 11 and 15, where confidence and positive self-esteem can help your teen be successful. Learn More
  • Adult It is never too late to improve your greatest asset…your smile! With new techniques, appliances, and technology advances, we can greatly reduce discomfort levels, decrease the frequency of visits, shorten treatment time, and allow you to choose from a variety of options that fit your lifestyle while dramatically boosting your dental health and overall wellness. Invisalign

Invisalign® will help you get the beautiful straight teeth you’ve always wanted — without wires and brackets.

Hardly anyone will know that you’re straightening your teeth with these virtually undetectable, removable aligners. These clear aligner trays are virtually undetectable and removable. Invisalign treatment is clinically proven to be effective for a broad range of teeth straightening cases, from mild to complex. With 20 years of clinical research, Invisalign is backed by the data and experience of 4 million cases. At Cooney & Tyner Orthodontics, we are named the Top Provider in the Capital Region with many years of experience with the Invisalign system.

AcceleDent

We believe that staying on top of the latest technology advances allows us to better treat our patients. One way we utilize today’s technology is offering you to complement your orthodontic treatment with AcceleDent Optima.

With this device, you may accelerate your tooth movement by approximately 40 %! AcceleDent Optima uses SoftPulse Technology™ to generate small vibrations, or micropulses, through a mouthpiece that is used nightly to enhance the movements directed by your orthodontics, allowing for faster tooth movement. This technology can be used on all patients that have a healthy tooth and bone structure. It is completely safe, easy, and has also been said to reduce any discomfort that may occur with orthodontics!
