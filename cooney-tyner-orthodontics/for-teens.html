Orthodontics for Teens

The time is right...

Children around age 12 are growing rapidly, and orthodontists can usually take advantage of these growth spurts to help shape the bite and teeth correctly. Any issues with crowding, spaces, and bite position can be easily identified at this time which is why many children in this age range are undergoing orthodontic treatment. There are numerous treatment options for teens to seamlessly fit orthodontics into their busy lifestyles.

FAQs

  • How long will treatment last? Since everyone’s biology and dental situations vary, treatment times will be different for each patient. We will be able give you an estimated treatment time prescribed specifically for you during the initial screening appointment .
  • What are common orthodontic problems? Some of the common orthodontic problems that we are able to treat include: Underbite
  • Spacing
  • Upper Front Teeth Protrusion
  • Crowding
  • Overbite
  • Openbite
  • Skewed Dental Midlines
  • Should my teen have conventional braces or Invisalign? Both conventional braces and Invisalign will achieve the desired result. Many teens like the discreet look of the Invisalign system while some prefer to have conventional braces with colors. The decision lies within you and your teen determining which option will work best with their lifestyle. At the initial screening, we will thoroughly discuss both options to be sure you can make an informed decision.
  • How can we accelerate my Teen’s treatment? Some patients will qualify to use our AcceleDent Optima technology . This device may be accelerate tooth movement by approximately 40 % without causing any underlying issues! AcceleDent Aura uses SoftPulse Technology™ to generate small vibrations, or micropulses, through a mouthpiece to enhance the movements directed by the orthodontics.
  • What types of braces are there for teens? We offer a variety of options when it comes to getting a more ideal smile. Many times, different types of appliances may be necessary prior to or in conjunction with braces systems. For our bracket systems, we offer conventional metal brackets as well as less noticeable clear ceramic brackets. Cooney Orthodontics is also proud to be the only VIP Platinum Plus Invisalign Provider in the Albany, NY region. We will thoroughly discuss which options will be most advantageous for you at your initial screening.
  • What happens when my teen is done with treatment? All orthodontic patients are instructed to wear their clear retainers at night indefinitely. Sleeping with your retainers in at night will ensure a healthy bite and maintain the new position of your teeth as time goes on.
  • Do you accept my insurance? We are not a direct participator with insurance companies, though we do accept assignment of benefits if they are available through your plan. We offer many financial options in addition to any insurance assignment benefit, including lower down payments and customizable payment options. All financial options will be discussed and given to you at the initial consult.
