Dental Procedures Provided

Cleanings & Prevention

A preventive program is a cooperative effort by the patient, dentist, and dental staff to preserve the natural dentition and supporting structures by preventing the onset, progress, and recurrence of dental diseases and conditions.

Restorations

Through improved techniques and modern technology, we are now able to offer more options for restoring a tooth back to its normal shape, appearance and function.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Emergency Care

We are happy to offer emergency appointments so that you can be seen as soon as possible for immediate dental needs like a broken tooth, dental infection, swelling and pain.

Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry specializing in the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of jaw, face and bite irregularities.

Endodontics

Endodontic therapy, more commonly known as a root canal, is needed when the nerve of a tooth has become irreversibly irritated or infected as a result for tooth decay or other damage.

Pediatric Dental Care

A lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles starts at a young age, we recommend kids begin receiving regular dental check ups starting at 6 months.

Implants and Oral Surgery

The dentists at Cudahy Dental are highly trained in the planning and restoration of dental implants and are proud to offer in-house implant placement.

Dentures

Dentures are custom made, removable dental appliances that’s are designed to replace missing teeth. Cudahy Dental are pleased to offer this affordable replacement option for our patients.
