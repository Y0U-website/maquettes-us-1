Pediatric Dental Care

Cudahy Dental has been a proud member of SE Wisconsin for 60+ years with services for family members of all ages. That’s why we are excited to announce our newly renovated, kid friendly dental clinic, Cudahy Dental Kids! This updated space allows us to continue creating positive dental experiences and high quality, comfortable dental care to our youngest patients.

A lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles starts at a young age, we recommend kids begin receiving regular dental check ups starting at 6 months of age or after the eruption of their first primary (aka baby) tooth.
