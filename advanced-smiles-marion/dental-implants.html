What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial teeth and roots. The roots, or posts, are typically made from titanium and inserted into the jawbone by your dentist. The teeth then get attached to the artificial roots.

Over time, your jaw bone will fuse with the implant to create a strong foundation for the artificial tooth. As they fuse, it&#x27;ll improve the implant&#x27;s stability and prevent your jawbone from deteriorating.

Dental implants can be a lengthy process that spans several months. You need to allow time for your jawbone to heal after the post is inserted and before the tooth is placed.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Prevent future bone loss with dental implants.
  • Coming in a wide variety of sizes and shapes, detail implants can match the color and shape of your existing teeth.
  • Dental implants function and feel like natural teeth, allowing you to speak naturally.
