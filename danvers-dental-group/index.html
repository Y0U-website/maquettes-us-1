At our practice, we are committed to providing the highest quality care using the most advanced technology available. That’s why we use the state-of-the-art 3Shape Scanner , a revolutionary tool designed to make your dental experience more efficient, comfortable, and precise.

With the 3Shape Scanner, we capture highly accurate 3D digital impressions of your teeth instantly, eliminating the need for traditional, messy molds. This technology ensures a faster, more comfortable process for you, reducing any discomfort from lengthy or invasive impression methods - you can see an image of your teeth in real time to make collaborative decisions about care.
