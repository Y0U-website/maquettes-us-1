The Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment comes with several benefits. We’ve listed a few of the most popular below.

Smile Aesthetics

Orthodontics can correct crooked and overlapping teeth, providing an aesthetically pleasing smile.

Smile Function

An aligned bite supports the jaw joints (TMJs) for superior function and protection from jaw joint issues.

Smile Longevity

An aligned smile puts less wear and tear on the teeth, contributing to long-term smile health and integrity.

Smile Hygiene

Aligned teeth are easier to brush and floss effectively, providing fresher breath and fewer oral health issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are teeth permanently straight after braces?

Braces are highly effective at correcting misalignment and creating a beautiful, balanced smile. A common question among both parents and patients is whether those results last forever. The truth is that maintaining optimal orthodontic results requires ongoing care. While braces straighten teeth, without proper follow-up, teeth can gradually shift back toward their original positions over time.

Why Teeth Can Shift After Braces

Without preventive measures, teeth shifting after braces is, unfortunately, common. Why?

After braces are removed, the surrounding bone and tissues need time to stabilize. Everyday factors such as chewing forces, jaw growth in children and teens, grinding or clenching (bruxism), and natural aging can influence tooth movement. This is why braces aftercare importance cannot be overstated.

Maintaining Kids Orthodontic Results

Wearing retainers as prescribed is the most effective way of keeping teeth straight after braces. Retainers help hold teeth in place while the mouth adjusts to the new alignment. Skipping or shortening retainer use increases the risk of relapse and can compromise braces results maintenance. Regular dental checkups also help monitor alignment and catch small changes before they become bigger issues.

Parents play an essential role by encouraging consistent retainer wear and helping children establish responsible habits. With the right care, the results achieved with braces can remain stable for many years.

The Benefits of Interceptive Orthodontics

Our orthodontist in Northborough, MA, uses Interceptive orthodontics to help guide your child’s oral development should the need arise. With interceptive orthodontics, your child may spend less time in braces later or forgo them altogether.

Our children’s dentist in Northborough or orthodontist will discuss findings with you following all dental exams or orthodontic evaluations. We’ll lay out the treatment options and help you make an informed choice for your child’s smile.

  • Space Maintainers: Baby teeth hold space for your child’s adult teeth. When these first molars are lost too early, adult teeth may erupt incorrectly on the jaw due to tooth shifting. Fortunately, a space maintainer can hold the space until the adult tooth erupts.
  • Palatal Expanders : Sometimes a child’s jaw is too narrow to hold a full arch of adult teeth in aligned positions. They end up with overcrowded, overlapping, and crooked teeth. Expanders gently widen the jaw over time to better accommodate adult teeth.

Orthodontic Options for Patients of All Ages

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces consist of tooth brackets threaded with guidewires. Your child’s orthodontist adjusts the guidewires periodically to bring their teeth into straighter positions, correcting bite misalignment and improving smile aesthetics.

Therapy with braces can last for several months or longer than two years, depending on the complexity of your child’s orthodontic needs.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign is a system of clear aligners worn over the teeth. These aligners help move teeth into straighter positions over the treatment timeline, which can last several months or more than 2 years. Invisalign is ideal for older teens and adults.

Unlike traditional braces, you can remove Invisalign during meals, so there are no eating restrictions.
