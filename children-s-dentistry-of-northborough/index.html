Our dental practice cares for children, teens, and adults in the communities of Northborough, Grafton, Shrewsbury, Marlborough, Westborough, and Southborough.

Starting kids off with positive feelings about their dentist is the best way to protect their oral health for a lifetime. Every child responds differently to dental appointments, and our team is trained to understand and cater to their unique needs. Your pediatric dentist in Northborough spends all the time necessary before completing a procedure to ensure your child feels safe and comfortable…

We make it a point to involve both you and your child in the dental experience . The best chance we have for helping them achieve excellent oral health is when you both understand the importance of excellent oral health habits.
