Meet the Team at Children’s Dentistry of Northborough

Our outstanding team is dedicated to providing compassionate care and helping your child achieve the best possible oral health. Each of us brings our unique knowledge and expertise, and we work closely with each other to make sure your child has the best possible dental experience in a warm and friendly environment.

Jolanta Macdonald, DMD

In December 2004, Dr. Macdonald built and opened Children s Dentistry of Northborough. She is the sole owner and works with her general dentist husband, Craig and orthodontist friend, Anita. Children s Dentistry of Northborough is a family practice made up of clinicians that take pride in the excellent dental care they provide.

Dr. Macdonald is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Massachusetts Dentists Alliance, and Mommy Dentist in Business group. She was a courtesy staff member at Children’s Hospital in Boston from 2004 until 2019 where she was responsible for overseeing dental residents in the clinic.

In her leisure time, Dr. Macdonald enjoys scuba diving, spending time with her family, skiing, and taking photographs of her children, Kaitlyn, Alex, and Madi Rose. She has two goldendoodles named Teddy and Cali and a senior Korat cat name Violet.

Craig Macdonald, DMD

Dr. Craig Macdonald joined our practice in 2011 and works with us on some Fridays and Saturdays to offer general dentistry services to our teen and adult patients. Dr. Macdonald graduated from Colgate University with majors in biology and economics.

He attended the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 1999. Upon graduating he continued his education with a one-year General Practice residency at Danbury Hospital, Danbury, CT. In July 2010, he received the Fellowship award from the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Macdonald first entered private practice in Rockville, Maryland, before returning to New England in 2002. He worked as an associate dentist in Hopkinton for four years before arriving at his current location in Natick. Dr. Macdonald is a member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. In 2013, he completed an Associate Fellowship through the World Clinical Laser Institute.

Dr. Macdonald is married to Dr. Jolanta Macdonald, and they have three children. When not at work, he enjoys spending time with his three children, skiing, swimming, and golfing.

Anita Bhatt, DMD, MDS

Dr. Bhatt has been practicing in Westboro since 2000. She received her Doctor of Medical Dentistry degree from the Tufts University of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts, before earning both a Certificate of Graduate Training and a Master of Dental Science degree in Orthodontics at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.
