Explore Patient Resources General Dentistry Routine Dental Exams and Cleanings During routine dental exams, we will assess the health of your teeth, gums, and overall oral cavity. Regular cleanings involve the removal of plaque and tartar buildup, as well as polishing to help prevent cavities and gum disease. These visits also provide an opportunity to discuss any concerns or preventive measures to maintain optimal oral health.

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, can range from mild gingivitis to severe periodontitis. Treatment may involve deep cleaning procedures such as scaling and root planing to remove plaque and tartar below the gumline.

Temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders can cause jaw pain, clicking or popping sounds, headaches, and difficulty chewing. Treatment options may include lifestyle modifications, exercises, dental appliances, or medications to alleviate symptoms and improve jaw function.

Cosmetic Dentistry Veneers Veneers are thin shells made of porcelain or composite resin that are custom-designed to fit over the front surface of the teeth. They can improve the appearance of teeth that are discolored, chipped, misaligned, or irregularly shaped. Veneers provide a durable and natural-looking solution to enhance the aesthetics of your smile, creating a uniform and radiant appearance.

Dentures are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues. Full dentures are used when all natural teeth are missing in the upper or lower arch, while partial dentures are used when some natural teeth remain. Dentures restore the ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently, providing a comfortable and functional solution for tooth loss.
