Understanding sleep apnea and its treatments

The silent disruptor of restful sleep

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) is a common but often undiagnosed health condition that causes the body to repeatedly stop breathing while sleeping and can significantly impact your health, relationships, and quality of life. Obstructive Sleep Apnea patients may experience excessive daytime fatigue, headaches, irritability, difficulty concentrating, and depression, and if left untreated can lead to serious health complications including hypertension, diabetes, obesity, arrhythmia, sexual dysfunction, and stroke.

If you’re struggling with sleep, we can help

  • Reduced serious health risks including heart attack, stroke, high blood pressure
  • Improved mental health
  • Stronger immune system
  • Improved relationship with bed partner
  • Improved sexual health

What to expect during sleep apnea treatment

Here’s what you can expect from our sleep apnea screening and treatment process:

  • Screening for sleep apnea events: We’ll perform an initial screening to identify signs of sleep apnea and, if necessary, refer you for a diagnostic test.
  • Referral for diagnosis: If sleep apnea is detected, we’ll refer you to your primary care provider or one of our trusted telehealth care providers for a full sleep study. Most patients can complete a home sleep study in the comfort of their bed.
  • Custom appliances: We provide custom-fabricated oral appliances to help manage obstructive sleep apnea and improve your sleep.
