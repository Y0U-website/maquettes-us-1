Orthodontics

Our orthodontic solutions are designed to effectively correct these issues, enhancing both dental function and aesthetics.

  • Spacing: Unwanted gaps between teeth may occur due to irregular jaw development, habits like thumb sucking, or frequent pacifier use.
  • Overbite: In this condition, the upper teeth protrude significantly and hide a major portion of the lower teeth.
  • Underbite: The lower teeth close in front of the upper teeth, typically due to a typical positioning of the lower jaw.
  • Crossbite: A misalignment where one or more lower teeth bite ahead of the upper teeth.
  • Open bite: A noticeable gap remains between the upper and lower teeth when they are in occlusion.

What Is The Best Time To Get Orthodontic Treatment?

Most orthodontists recommend scheduling a clinical exam when your child’s permanent teeth start to emerge in the oral cavity. This period marks the transition from primary to permanent teeth, and any abnormalities during this phase can have long-term effects on a child’s dental alignment. For most children, this transition phase occurs around the age of 6 or 7.

However, the best time to initiate orthodontic treatment is between the ages of 11 and 14. This is because during this phase, the jaw is still growing and teeth are more responsive to the treatment process. At our Dickinson, TX, practice, we offer advanced orthodontics treatments for patients of all ages. These treatments are safe, comfortable and can help you gain a straight, confident smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What issues can orthodontic treatment correct?

Orthodontics addresses a range of concerns affecting both appearance and function, including crowding, spacing, overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite. Our solutions are designed to correct these issues while improving dental function and facial aesthetics.

What is the best age to begin orthodontic treatment?

Most orthodontists recommend an initial exam around age 6 or 7, when permanent teeth start to emerge. The ideal time to begin treatment is usually between ages 11 and 14, when the jaw is still growing and teeth respond well. That said, we offer orthodontic treatment for patients of all ages.

How do you decide which orthodontic treatment is right for me?

Depending on the condition of your teeth and jaws, we create a personalized treatment plan focused on correcting misalignment, improving oral function, and enhancing your smile. Our practice uses advanced technology to ensure a smooth, comfortable experience.

Can adults benefit from orthodontics?

Yes. Although the ideal window for treatment is during the pre-teen years, our advanced orthodontic treatments are safe and comfortable for patients of all ages and can help you achieve a straight, confident smile at any stage of life.
