Benefits Of Dental Implants with Us in Dickinson, TX

With high-quality dental implants at our Dickinson, TX, facility, you can enjoy many benefits, including:

Procedure Of Dental Implants In Dickinson, TX

If you are eligible for implant treatment, we will provide a comprehensive overview of the process and create a personalized treatment plan. The initial phase involves the surgical insertion of titanium metal posts within the jawbone. We typically perform this procedure under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. The duration of this surgery is typically around one hour for a single implant and may extend accordingly for multiple implants.

The post-surgical healing period may last for about 3 to 6 months. This allows the implant posts to merge with the surrounding bone through the process of osseointegration. Following the healing period, we will affix the dental prosthesis—be it a crown, bridge, or denture—to the implant posts. After this process, you will be able to eat, speak, and smile with greater confidence and ease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Several factors determine your eligibility, including sufficient jawbone density to anchor the implant posts, healthy gums free of periodontal disease, and good oral hygiene. Chronic smoking and certain medical conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, hypertension, or heart disease can affect the outcome, so we assess each of these during your consultation.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Implants act as a durable, long-term replacement for missing or damaged teeth. They are firmly secured in the jawbone, restore the functions of natural teeth, blend seamlessly for a natural look, support your facial muscles, help minimize bone loss, and are simple to clean and care for.

What does the dental implant procedure involve?

The process begins with the surgical insertion of titanium posts into the jawbone under local anesthesia, which takes about an hour for a single implant. After a healing period of 3 to 6 months, during which the posts fuse with the bone through osseointegration, we attach your final prosthesis such as a crown, bridge, or denture.

How long is the healing period after implant surgery?

The post-surgical healing period usually lasts about 3 to 6 months. This allows the implant posts to merge with the surrounding bone before we affix the final restoration, after which you can eat, speak, and smile with greater confidence.

Can implants replace more than one missing tooth?

Yes. We offer multiple types of dental implants for long-term replacement of missing or severely damaged teeth. Depending on your needs, implants can support a single crown, a bridge, or a denture, and surgery time extends accordingly for multiple implants.
