Recognizing the Need for Endodontic Treatment

Endodontic issues can cause significant discomfort and potentially lead to more serious dental problems if left untreated. Be aware of these common signs that may indicate the need for endodontic care:

If you’re experiencing any of these symptoms, don’t hesitate to reach out to Dental Designs for a professional evaluation and personalized treatment plan.

  • Persistent tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Swelling or tenderness in the gums
  • Discoloration of a specific tooth
  • Pain when biting or chewing
  • Prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures
