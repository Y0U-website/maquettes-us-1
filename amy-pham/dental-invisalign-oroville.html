What is Invisalign dental treatment?

Invisalign treatment is the process of wearing a series of clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth through advanced digital 3 D image technology base on your specific needs by your doctor Invisalign treatment make your teeth straight, closes gaps or spaces, corrects bites move your dream smile teeth to the final desired position.

OVER BITE

An overbite is when your upper front teeth overlap with your lower front teeth. Most people have at least a little overbite.

UNDER BITE

An under bite is when you close your mouth and your lower front teeth are in front of your upper teeth

CROSS BITE

You close your mouth and some of your upper teeth to rest on top of the lower teeth rather than on the outside, like they normally would

GAPS IN TEETH

When you have empty spaces between two or more of your teeth, this spacing issues or gap teeth.

OPEN BITE

The mouth is closed, your upper and lower teeth don’t touch this is referred to as an open bite.

CROWDED TEETH

Teeth is not enough space in your jaw for your teeth to fit normally, your teeth can bunch up, overlap and twist, imperfect smile

The Treatment

The journey begins with a scan or impressions. Their orthodontist works closely with Invisalign treatment specialists to develop a complete digital treatment plan, carefully plotting your teen’s best smile from start to finish.

The Aligners

Your teen will receive their Invisalign clear aligners created with our proprietary SmartTrack9 material. Throughout their treatment, you can monitor their compliance through an indicator on the aligner. They’ll wear them for 22 hours a day, but can easily remove them so there are fewer interruptions to their lifestyle.

The Transformation

As they progress through their treatment, they’ll see their smile and life transform through a series of small, expertly designed shifts. And with the Invisalign Teen Guarantee, you have the option to switch to wires and brackets if your teen decides Invisalign treatment isn’t for them in the first six months.

Q & A

The average time needed for Invisalign to straighten your teeth is about 12 months. The length of treatment depends on the severity of your case and can only be determined by your doctor

Invisalign doesn’t use metal brackets or wires to move your teeth. Instead, a series of clear aligners apply constant pressure to your teeth. During each stage, only certain teeth are allowed to move. Many people start seeing a difference in their teeth within two to three months of starting to use the aligners.

Depending on the nature of your treatment, your dentist may have specific recommendations about how often you should come in for treatment. Typically, adult Invisalign patients will need to visit the dentist at least once every six weeks.
