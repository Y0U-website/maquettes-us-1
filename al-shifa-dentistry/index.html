We accept most Insurances, including some Minnesota state insurance plans, and offer competitive pricing for those without dental insurance. Feel free to call with any questions!

Dr. Nasir Latif received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1995. He completed his residency in general dentistry in 1996. In 2002, he attended Duggan Study Institute (DSI) in Colton, California. At DSI, he extensively studied enhanced restorative techniques and cosmetic restorations.

He finished his National Dental Board Exam in early 2006 at Loma Linda University, Loma Linda, California. The same year, he joined a multi-doctor practice in Minnesota. Here, he worked with dedication, and in early 2009 he established Al-Shifa Dentistry.

Now with over 14 years of experience, he enjoys practicing in all aspects of dentistry, especially Dental Implants, Cosmetic Dentistry, Oral Surgery, Restorative Dentistry, Root Canals, Crowns, Bridges and Partial Dentures.
