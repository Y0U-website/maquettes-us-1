Meet the Team

Each member of our team brings a wealth of experience and a deep commitment to patient care, ensuring a seamless and comforting experience for every visit. Our team’s dedication to excellence and patient well-being is at the heart of our practice’s reputation in the Roaring Fork Valley.

What to expect

Patient Experience

When you walk through our doors you’ll be welcomed by our friendly team in our cozy and comforting office. After checking in, we’ll lead you back to your treatment room and get a chance to see the rest of the office. You’ll get to know Dr. Gordon, and she’ll take the time to get to know you and understand your smile goals. She’ll perform a comprehensive exam and make treatment recommendations based on your unique smile.

What to Bring

To help streamline your visit, we ask that you please bring a valid ID, your insurance card (if using), and any previous dental records to help us serve you better.
