Porcelain Veneers

We partner with one of the most well-known aesthetic dental labs in the country. They are the masters of the veneer and have helped to establish its popularity among celebrities and personalities. Each case starts with a wax-up of your teeth so you can see what the veneers are going to look like. Once you sign off on the design, we put the lab to work fabricating custom-made veneers with the size, shape, and color you desire to give you that celebrity bright smile. If necessary, we can laser contour your gums to create a beautiful scalloped presentation you will love.

Aesthetic Composite Bonding

Small to moderate fractures of the front teeth caused by trauma can often be restored with strong and resilient aesthetic composite bonding. These "fillings" are suitable for children and adults and are hand-crafted by the dentist. The dentist will match your existing tooth color (or take it a shade lighter if veneering several teeth), shape, and polish the tooth or teeth to give you a smooth, clean look and feel.

Peg Lateral Restoration

Did you know that peg teeth are present in approximately 5 % of people? The most common peg shaped tooth is the upper (maxillary) lateral incisor. Depending on your specific situation, we can restore your peg lateral(s) using orthodontics, crowns, porcelain veneers or aesthetic composite veneers (or some combination) to give you back the smile and confidence you desire.

Missing teeth

For those with gaps between their teeth, we offer the best treatment possible. Dental implants help restore the function and aesthetics of missing teeth in the front and back. For missing front teeth, we use highly aesthetic porcelain crowns that match the rest of your teeth. For missing teeth in the back, we use more durable, but still aesthetic, zirconia or ceramic crowns to restore your missing teeth. We offer many implant options for single tooth replacement, multiple tooth replacement, or even complete arch replacement. See our page on implants, or call us today to learn more!
