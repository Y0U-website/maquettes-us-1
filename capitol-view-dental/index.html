The practice

Dr. Smith’s journey to dentistry is anything but typical. A classical guitarist turned Nashville-based dentist, he brings a unique blend of precision, artistry, and empathy to every appointment. With a sincere, down-to-earth approach, Dr. Smith is here to help you feel confident about your smile.

At Capitol View Dental, it’s not just what we do, it’s how we do it. From curated amenities to thoughtful communication, we’ve built a practice that feels more like a boutique experience than a traditional dental visit.

Honest, Transparent Care You deserve to know exactly what’s going on with your oral health and what your options are. We take the time to explain everything in real, human terms. No upselling. No pressure. Just honest guidance, open conversations, and care that always keeps you in control.

Advanced Technology Your smile deserves the best tools available. That’s why we’ve invested in leading-edge technology like digital X-rays, 3D printing, an intraoral scanner, and same-day smile design services. These innovations help us diagnose more accurately, plan more precisely, and treat more comfortably—with results you can see and feel faster.

Minimally Invasive Dentistry Less is more when it comes to preserving your natural teeth. We focus on conservative treatment methods that are gentle on your enamel and tissues, designed to prevent problems before they escalate. It’s dentistry that respects your time, your health, and your peace of mind.

Comprehensive Services From everyday cleanings to implants, cosmetic treatments, and emergency care, we offer a full range of services in one convenient location. That means fewer referrals, more continuity of care, and a team that truly understands your smile from start to finish.
