Clear Aligners takes a modern approach to straightening teeth, using a custom-made series of aligners created just for you. These aligner trays are made of smooth, comfortable and virtually invisible plastic that you wear over your teeth.

You’ll achieve a great smile with little interference in your daily life. The best part about the whole process is that most people won’t even know you’re straightening your teeth.

Our office was one of the very first in the country to offer Clear Aligners treatment. We have a great deal of experience with Clear Aligners. We have done more cases than any other practice in the St. Louis area. To make this procedure even easier we now have an intra-oral scanner that eliminates the need for gooey impressions.
