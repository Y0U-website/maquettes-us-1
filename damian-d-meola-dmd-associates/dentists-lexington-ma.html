An Atmosphere of Comfort and Care

Smile & Skin of Lexington is a state-of-the-art dental office with highly trained and experienced dentists and staff who provide outstanding treatment in an atmosphere of comfort and care. With the most advance dental technologies available, our multi-specialty office is designed to treat patients of all ages, which enables families to have all their dental needs met in one convenient location.

Section

Dr. Damian D. Meola

Dr. Meola is the Owner and Director of Smile and Skin Aesthetics. He has been practicing dentistry since 1989, and is a second-generation dental care provider. In addition to general dentistry services, Dr. Meola offers Invisalign and is certified to perform Botox © and Juvederm © Dr. Meola is also a Clinical Instructor at Tufts University.

Dr. Caitlin Esteves

Dr. Esteves has been practicing at Smile and Skin Aesthetics since 2016. Dr. Esteves received her Bachelor’s degree at Binghamton University. She completed her dental education through Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, earning a degree as a DMD. In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Esteves offers Invisalign and is certified to perform Botox© and Juvederm©.

Dr. Chris Salzillo

Dr. Salzillo received his Bachelor’s degree from Providence College in 2008 and his Master’s degree from Boston University in 2010. He received his DMD from Tufts University School of Dentistry in 2015. Dr. Salzillo completed his Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency in the US Army at Fort Carson, Colorado.

Dr. Christian Wesolowski

Dr. Christian Wesolowski is a General Dentist who joined our practice in 2023 and is a graduate of the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.
