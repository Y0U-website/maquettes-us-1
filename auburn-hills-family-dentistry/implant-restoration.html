Comprehensive Examination and Assessment

Dr. Haycock begins with a thorough oral examination to evaluate your implant’s current condition and identify any underlying issues. He carefully inspects your restoration for visible damage like chips or cracks while checking for signs of loosening or movement around the implant site. This detailed assessment also includes screening for peri-implantitis and other complications that could affect your restoration’s success.

Personalized Restoration Planning

Based on your examination results, we create a customized treatment plan that addresses your specific restoration needs and concerns. Dr. Haycock explains whether your restoration requires repair or complete replacement, discusses material options, and provides realistic timelines for completion. We ensure you understand every aspect of your treatment before proceeding with any restoration work.

Professional Restoration Placement

The restoration procedure involves carefully removing your damaged prosthetic while preserving the integrity of your underlying implant. Dr. Haycock takes precise dental impressions of your mouth and sends them to our trusted laboratory, where technicians craft your new restoration to exact specifications. You’ll experience minimal discomfort during this non-invasive process since no surgical work is required on the implant itself.

Post-Treatment Care Instructions

Following your restoration placement, Dr. Haycock provides specific care instructions tailored to your type of implant restoration. He demonstrates proper cleaning techniques for your particular restoration, whether it’s a single crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture. These personalized guidelines help ensure your new restoration maintains its appearance and function for years to come.

Ongoing Monitoring and Maintenance

Regular follow-up appointments allow us to track your restoration’s performance and address any concerns before they become serious problems. Dr. Haycock examines the fit and stability of your restoration while providing professional cleaning around the implant area. These scheduled visits help protect your investment and ensure your restored implant continues to serve you well long-term.

FAQs

What is dental implant restoration?

Dental implant restoration is the process of repairing or replacing the crown, bridge, or denture portion that attaches to your dental implant post. This procedure addresses damage, wear, or loosening of the visible tooth portion while preserving the underlying titanium implant. The restoration process typically involves non-invasive techniques that focus solely on the prosthetic component.

How do dental implants affect your appearance after dental implant restorations?

Dental implant restorations are custom-designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size, creating a seamless and attractive smile. When properly restored, implants look completely natural and often improve your overall facial appearance by maintaining proper jaw structure. The restoration process ensures your replacement teeth blend perfectly with your existing smile.

How to restore deep dental implants?

Deep dental implant restoration requires careful assessment of the implant’s position and surrounding tissue health before determining the appropriate restoration approach. Dr. Haycock works with specialized techniques and custom abutments to properly connect the restoration to deeply placed implants. The process may involve additional steps to ensure optimal fit and function while maintaining the implant’s stability.

What is the difference between dental implants and dental implant restorations?

The implant is the titanium post that’s surgically placed into your jawbone and serves as the root replacement for your missing tooth. The restoration is the visible crown, bridge, or denture that attaches to the implant and functions as your replacement tooth. While implants can last decades, restorations typically need replacement or repair sooner due to normal wear from daily use.
