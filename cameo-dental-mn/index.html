Les soins

A Dental Bridge is used to literally bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth. It consists of an artificial tooth (or teeth) held in place by Dental Crowns cemented onto the adjacent natural teeth or supported by dental implants.

Dental Implants represent the most advanced option for replacing a missing tooth. An implant is a surgical fixture placed into the jawbone that acts as a tooth root, providing a sturdy, long-lasting foundation for a replacement tooth or crown.
