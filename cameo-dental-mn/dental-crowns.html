Dental Crowns, Bridges & Implants

A Dental Bridge is used to literally bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth. It consists of an artificial tooth (or teeth) held in place by Dental Crowns cemented onto the adjacent natural teeth or supported by dental implants.

  • To restore a tooth that has been severely worn down or broken, especially after a large Filling.
  • To cover a dental implant.
  • To cover a discolored or poorly shaped tooth as part of Cosmetic Dentistry.
  • Restores your ability to properly chew and speak.
  • Prevents remaining teeth from shifting out of position, which can happen after a Tooth Extraction.
  • Maintains the natural shape of your face and jawline.

Crowns, Bridges, and Implants FAQ

  • Which is better: a dental bridge or a dental implant? Dental Implants are often considered the best long-term replacement because they function as a tooth root and do not require altering adjacent teeth, though a Dental Bridge is a successful alternative for many patients.
  • How do I care for my new bridge or crown? You should care for a Dental Crown or bridge just like a natural tooth, with regular brushing, flossing, and scheduled Checkups.
  • Will a crown look like my natural tooth? Yes, Dental Crowns are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth, providing an effective Cosmetic Dentistry result.
