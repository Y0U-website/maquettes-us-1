Our History and Commitment to the Rosemount Community

Cameo Dental is deeply rooted in Rosemount, MN, with a history spanning over 75 years . Our practice was founded in 1946 by Dr. Raymond Mardell after he returned from World War II, establishing a lasting legacy of dental excellence. We are not just a dental office; we are a multi-generational community institution dedicated to the health and happiness of our neighbors.

A Legacy of Local Care

The Cameo Dental Difference: Family Feel

We’re a small, private practice with a dedicated team of twelve. Longevity and genuine friendliness set us apart only two of our staff members have been with us for less than twenty-five years. This remarkable retention means you receive consistent, experienced care from people who truly know you. Patients often say our team feels like family, and we’re proud to now serve third and even fourth generations of local families.

Our Mission and Philosophy

Our philosophy is simple: to provide comprehensive, family-focused dental care with quality and compassion. We’re committed to helping you maintain and enhance the natural beauty of your smile, treating every patient with respect and integrity. From routine checkups to specialized procedures, your dental health is our priority delivered with a comforting, community-centered approach that ensures every patient feels valued and supported.
