If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, please call our office right away to see if we can accommodate you. For routine appointments, contact us during business hours we’re happy to answer your questions and find a time that works for you and your family. We’re committed to providing efficient, professional service. Let us help you take the next step toward a healthier, more beautiful smile.

Your dental health is a priority, and so is your personal information. While we do not have a custom online privacy policy, we are committed to patient confidentiality and safety. We have a comprehensive patient privacy policy that we send directly to our patients for their review.

Our office team, managed by Kim Rohr, is dedicated to making your experience seamless and comfortable from the moment you call. We look forward to meeting you and having your family join the long tradition of satisfied patients at Cameo Dental. We use an English-speaking staff to ensure clear communication about your dental care and treatment options.
