In-Office Teeth Whitening

In-office teeth whitening is the fastest way to achieve a brighter smile. During your appointment, our San Antonio dentist will apply a powerful whitening gel to your teeth, which is then activated by a special light. This process can lighten your teeth by several shades in just one visit, providing you with immediate and dramatic results.

Take-Home Whitening Trays

For those who prefer to whiten their teeth in the comfort of their own home, we offer custom-made take-home whitening trays. Our dentist in San Antonio, TX will take impressions of your teeth to create personalized trays that fit perfectly over your teeth. You’ll be provided with a professional-grade whitening gel and instructions on how to use the trays at home. With consistent use, you can achieve a brighter smile in just a few weeks.
