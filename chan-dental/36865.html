Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last compared to other tooth replacement?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care and regular dental checkups. Made of titanium, the implant fuses with the jawbone and provides a stable foundation for a replacement tooth.

Implants Longevity Compared to Other Tooth Replacements

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge typically lasts 5 to 15 years. While a bridge is a functional solution, it relies on healthy adjacent teeth for support, which can weaken or damage them over time.

Full or Partial Dentures

Removable dentures usually have a lifespan of 5 to 10 years. Over time, dentures will require relining or replacement due to wear and tear or natural changes in the mouth.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Enhanced Stability and Function

Implants are designed to mimic the root structure of natural teeth. Unlike dentures, which can slip or cause discomfort, implants stay firmly in place so you can eat and speak confidently.

Preserved Jawbone Health

One of the unique benefits of dental implants is their ability to stimulate the jawbone, just like a natural tooth root. This stimulation prevents bone loss, a common issue with other tooth replacement options.

Dental Implant After Care

More good news about dental implants they re easy to care for because they closely mimic a natural tooth!

You can brush and floss your implant just like a natural tooth. Keeping your implant tooth clean and bacteria-free is the key to its success.

Routine visits with your Torrance dentist every six months for exams and teeth cleanings will also protect the health of your dental implant. Dr. Chan will take x-rays once a year to ensure that your implant is healthy and properly functioning.

Avoid Certain Foods

Just as you would with a natural tooth, you should avoid biting directly into hard foods with your implant tooth, as this could damage the porcelain crown.

Cut hard foods like apples, crusty bread, and carrots into bite-size pieces to avoid damage to your natural teeth and implants.

Your Path to a Restored Smile

We understand that committing to a dental implant is a big decision. Dr. Chan and our team in Torrance, CA will provide the support you need and keep you informed at every step of your journey.

Initial Implant Evaluation

The first step is an appointment for an evaluation to ensure your jawbone is healthy enough to stabilize the implant post. We do this with leading-edge technology like 3-D cone beam scans that show your jawbone and all supporting structures.

Implant Placement and Healing

Contrary to what you might think, implant surgery is not complex or painful. Dr. Chan uses powerful anesthetics to numb the area so you won’t feel any pain, and we also offer nitrous oxide for your comfort. Our caring team will support you throughout your procedure.

As the implant heals, bone and tissue in your jaw grow around it; this is called osseointegration. Healing time differs for everyone but typically takes three to six months.

Restoring Implant Function and Appearance

After healing, we place the abutment on the implant post, then attach a tooth restoration to restore function and appearance. The type of restoration depends on the number of teeth we are replacing; a single tooth would get a dental crown, multiple teeth would require an implant bridge, and an implant denture would replace an entire arch of teeth.

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)

CBCT, or Cone Beam Computed Tomography, is a leading-edge dental imaging technique that produces 3-D images of your teeth, soft tissues, nerve pathways, and bone in a single scan. At Chan Dental, we use this technology for complex procedures such as dental implant placement, orthodontic planning, and diagnosis of dental conditions that might not be visible with regular x-rays.

As our patient, your treatment benefits from CBCT’s precision, clarity, and enhanced care. This technology enables precise treatment planning to ensure optimal results and minimal complications.
