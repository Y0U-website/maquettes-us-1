What to Expect From the Dental Implant Procedure at Our Leander Practice?

During your initial consultation at our facility, Dr. Prameela Vennapusala starts by discussing your oral health issues and assessing your teeth and gums. After a detailed evaluation, including digital X-rays, we finalize your customized treatment plan. The main part of the process is implant surgery, during which we secure one or more implant posts in your jawbone.

This is followed by a recovery phase that typically lasts 3 to 6 months.

Once your implants have healed and fused well with the jawbone, we place a custom-designed crown , bridge, or full-arch denture to restore your smile and oral functions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do dental implants look like natural teeth?

Yes, dental implants look and function very similar to your natural teeth, as they replace the full tooth (crown and root parts).

How many implants will I need?

It depends on different factors, including the number of teeth you have lost and your jawbone thickness. We will examine your oral health to determine the best option.

How long do implants last?

Dental implants provide long-term tooth replacement, and with proper care, they can last 20 years or more.
