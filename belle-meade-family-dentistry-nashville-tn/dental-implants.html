Our Implant Services

We provide a full range of implant options for patients across Nashville, Belle Meade, and surrounding areas:

● Single Tooth Implants

Perfect for replacing a single missing tooth without altering neighboring teeth.

● Implant Restorations

Have an existing implant that needs a new crown or repair? We restore damaged or outdated restorations placed elsewhere.

Replace a full arch of teeth with just four implants. A stable, fixed alternative to traditional dentures that restores aesthetics and function.

A hybrid option that snaps into place using dental implants for added security — no more slipping.

Low bone density? We offer bone grafting to rebuild jaw structure and prepare for future implants.

FAQs

How long do dental implants last? With proper care, implants can last decades — many for life. The restoration may need replacing over time, but the implant post itself is highly durable.

Are dental implants painful? Placement is done under local anesthesia and can be combined with oral sedation. Post-op discomfort is typically manageable with medication and resolves in a few days.

Can I get an implant if I’ve had dentures for years? Possibly. We’ll check your bone density and may recommend bone grafting before implant placement if there’s bone loss.

How do I care for my implants? Treat them like natural teeth — brush, floss, and visit us for regular checkups and cleanings.
