Preventive Dentistry Services

We offer a full range of preventive dentistry services to help you maintain good oral health:

Brushing at home doesn’t remove all plaque. Plaque builds up between your teeth and under your gums, becoming tartar. Using specialized tools, our hygienists will remove this plaque and tartar for you.

We have digital X-rays, a panoramic machine, and a CBCT. We use digital imaging to benchmark your progress, spot trouble signs early, and can even create cosmetic smile designs virtually. We take HD intraoral photos, which allow us to show your dental issues to you first hand and the treatment that may be needed.

Left untreated, gum disease can lead to tooth loss, mouth pain, and worse. During your checkup, we will examine your mouth for early signs of gum disease and we will provide a deep cleaning if needed.

Dental sealants are a thin coating placed on your teeth that can help to prevent cavities from forming. According to the ADA , sealants can reduce the risk of decay in molars by 80 %!

You may be grinding your teeth at night without realizing it. Severe grinding can lead to tooth damage, jaw pain, and more. A custom night guard can help to cushion and protect your teeth from damage.
