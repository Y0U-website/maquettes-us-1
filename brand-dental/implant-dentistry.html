Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process usually requires several visits with multiple dental specialists—but at Brand Dental, we can perform all aspects of your dental implant treatment in-house. From initial surgical placement to fitting your custom crown, we can provide single tooth implants, implant bridges, and even full mouth implants at our Kingston, PA location.

Dental Implant Services

We are excited to offer our patients these dental implant services:

Dental implants resemble the look, feel, and function of a natural tooth. To reconstruct a functional tooth requires three parts: the implant post, an abutment, and a crown. We perform all aspects of implant dentistry, including the surgical and restorative phases, through our office.

When replacing multiple teeth at once, an implant-supported bridge is an excellent option. Dental implants are placed on either end, and a dental bridge (tooth-like restoration) is placed over them. Besides maintaining the structure of surrounding teeth, an implant-supported bridge is a long-lasting solution.

With fixed implant dentures, you won’t need to worry about your dentures slipping or falling out throughout the day. We offer implant-supported dentures custom designed to fit comfortably in your mouth and create a natural looking, slip-free and worry-free smile.

We can use dental implants to replace all missing teeth. We will create a treatment plan you ’ re comfortable with and explain all the steps along the way. Full mouth reconstruction can restore the function, appearance, and comfort that your smile once had.

Implant Technology
