Dr. Michel was raised in Salina and graduated from Salina Central High School. After high school, she attended Kansas State University, where she earned a dual degree in Life Science and Gerontology with a minor in Business. After her undergraduate education, she attended the University of Missouri Kansas-City, where she received her Doctorate of Dental Sciences Degree. Ashley will be returning home with her high school sweetheart, James Michel, whom she recently married in September 2023.

As someone who was born and raised in Salina, Ashley represents the values and comradery this community relies on. Therefore, when you choose Dr. Ashley Michel as your dentist, you can expect individualized dental care and a familiar smile.

When you choose Ahlers Family Dentistry, you’ll enjoy the very best in dental care. Dr. Ahlers and Dr. Michel are so excited to be providing great care to the Salina community.
