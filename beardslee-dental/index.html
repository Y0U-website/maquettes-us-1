The practice

At Beardslee Dental, we are committed to providing that comfortable, neighborhood feel where the team actually knows YOU! We get to know our patients personally because we honestly care.

There is no judgement and lectures here. Instead, we prefer to have a two-way discussion about your teeth, your health, and your dental goals and we’ll achieve them together. We love seeing patients of all ages and take pride in our role of caring for their smile.

The Beardslee Dental team looks forward to providing exceptional dental care to the Bothell, Juanita, Mill Creek, Kenmore, Kirkland, and Woodinville, WA communities for many years to come.

Dr. Ara Carmody is a Bothell family dentist with a conservative treatment philosophy. She has the unique ability to put you at ease, treat your dental needs with a gentle touch, and send you off with a smile. You’ll have her complete attention the moment you sit in the dental chair. We’re certain you’ll quickly notice and feel Dr. Carmody’s compassion and commitment to her patients. She is…

Dr. Carmody has hand selected a talented team of dentists, dental hygienists, and dental assistants to join her in providing the upmost quality care that Beardslee Dental has come to be known for.

Beardslee Dental’s experienced and knowledgeable team provide a wide range of dental services to keep your mouth healthy for life. From dental cleanings, dental fillings and dental crowns to advanced dental treatments such as dental implants and dental veneers, Beardslee Dental has you covered. We are here to serve the Bothell, WA and surrounding communities for many years to come.
