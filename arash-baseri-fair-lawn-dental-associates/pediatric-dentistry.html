Pediatric Dentistry

Restorative Dentistry

When it comes to ensuring your little one&#x27;s smile feels and functions at its absolute best, we&#x27;ve got you covered. From tackling cavities with dental fillings to addressing more complex issues like pediatric frenectomies and pulpotomies, our goal is to bring back the sparkle to your child&#x27;s decayed or damaged teeth. With our comprehensive pediatric restorative care, you can reclaim your child&#x27;s overall oral health, empowering them to eat, laugh, and grow with unwavering confidence. Pediatric Dentistry

Cleanings & Exams

Just as for adults, dental check-ups and cleanings for kids are important. Our friendly dental team will ensure your child&#x27;s teeth and gums are clean and healthy. We also examine their mouth and any necessary x-rays to catch any potential toothy troubles early. These visits are a great time to chat about what to expect as your child&#x27;s smile grows, including tips for oral care at home and food choices that can help keep their teeth strong and bright.

Pediatric Dentistry

Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. Typically, x-rays are recommended every few years, though children with a higher risk of oral disease may need more frequent scans.

Sedation

Is your child having trouble staying still at the dentist? Do they get anxious about dental visits? We have a solution: sedation options. Our sedation methods can help calm restlessness and anxiety, making sure your child gets the dental care they need without fear or stress. This helps your child stay calm and comfortable and starts building a positive attitude towards dental care and oral health. If you think sedation might help your child, let us know. We&#x27;ll help you find the right option for them.

Pediatric Dentistry

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.

Dental Crowns

We know that your child being happy and healthy is the most important, and when they get little bumps in the road, it can be scary! If your little one is in need of a solution for damaged or decayed teeth, pediatric dental crowns are here to save the day. Our meticulously crafted white zirconia crowns are specifically designed to restore those tiny teeth to their former function and appearance. With dental crowns, your child can say goodbye to discomfort and enjoy a durable solution that will keep their smile shining bright.

Pulpotomies

Is your child experiencing a toothache that just won&#x27;t go away? It might be a sign of infection, but fear not! Our pulpotomy procedure can alleviate their discomfort in a single visit. Think of a pulpotomy as a "baby root canal" that stops the infection in its tracks. By choosing this treatment, we can restore your little one&#x27;s oral health and bring back their bright smile, allowing them to continue enjoying all the laughter and carefree moments of childhood.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Pediatric Dentistry

Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child&#x27;s mouth healthy and happy!
