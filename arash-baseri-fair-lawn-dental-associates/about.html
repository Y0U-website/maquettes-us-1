A thoughtful approach to care

Meet Dr. Arash Baseri

Dr. Arash Baseri, DMD, brings over 12 years of experience to Fair Lawn Dental Associates. With a strong foundation in comprehensive and restorative dentistry, Dr. Baseri is known for his warm demeanor, attention to detail, and commitment to delivering personalized care that puts patients at ease. Though his full educational background and journey into dentistry are not detailed here, his passion for patient-centered treatment shines through in every aspect of his practice.

Dr. Baseri’s dental philosophy is rooted in continuous learning and a drive to provide the highest standard of care using advanced technology. He frequently utilizes tools like iTero intraoral scanning and 3D CBCT imaging to ensure clear diagnostics and precise treatment planning—especially in complex procedures like implant placement and restorative cases. From everyday cleanings to dental implants and Invisalign, he believes in tailoring care to meet the unique needs of each patient.

Patients especially appreciate Dr. Baseri’s straightforward and transparent approach. He makes it a point to clearly explain all treatment options and helps patients make informed decisions with confidence and peace of mind.

At the heart of Dr. Baseri’s practice is a desire to be a trusted provider for families and individuals in Fair Lawn and its surrounding communities. He strives to make Fair Lawn Dental Associates a place where patients genuinely feel heard and cared for in an environment where high standards of care meet comfort and reassurance.

Modern comforts and smart technology, built for you

At Fair Lawn Dental Associates, we understand that details matter. While we keep our space bright and relaxing, it’s our advanced technology and thoughtful experience that truly elevate your care. Every visit is designed with calm, clarity, and quality in mind.

Innovative technology that enhances your care

We invest in the tools that make your dental experience more informed, more precise, and more comfortable. Advanced technology allows us to diagnose with accuracy, reduce uncertainty, and create tailored treatment plans that feel seamless from start to finish.

iTero Intraoral Scanner Our iTero scanner creates quick, precise 3D digital impressions without the mess and discomfort of traditional impressions, making procedures like Invisalign or crowns far more comfortable and efficient. It’s just one of the ways we work to improve patient experience every day.

3D CBCT Our in-house CBCT imaging system gives us 360° views of your oral anatomy, which is critical for planning dental implant placement and other complex treatments. This means more precise outcomes and fewer surprises during treatment.

Helping our local community

At Fair Lawn Dental Associates, we’re always exploring opportunities to give back. We believe in building trust not only with our patients, but within the entire Bergen County community, and are open to future initiatives that connect, support, and care for the families around us.
