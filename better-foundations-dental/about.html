Meet Our Dentists

Experienced, compassionate, and dedicated to building lasting relationships with every patient.

Dr. Richa Bhatt, D.M.D.

Dr. Richa Bhatt is a caring and experienced general dentist who believes that a healthy smile begins with a healthy foundation. She graduated with High Honors from the Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree.

Before starting Better Foundations Dental, Dr. Bhatt spent several years at Harvard Vanguard Medical Associates Group, where she collaborated with a team of specialists on comprehensive cases that deepened her understanding of the connection between oral health and overall wellness.

Dr. Bhatt is known for her gentle touch, compassionate care, and commitment to patient education. Outside of dentistry, she enjoys biking, hiking, yoga, and spending time with her family and two young children.

Dr. Xiaoping (Cheri) Li, D.M.D.

Dr. Xiaoping (Cheri) Li has been practicing dentistry in the Boston and Greater Boston area for more than a decade. She earned her dental degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Li is highly skilled in oral surgery and tooth extractions and is dedicated to making even the most complex surgical procedures as comfortable and stress-free as possible for her patients.

Dr. Li is a member of the American Dental Association, the Tufts Asian Dental Organization, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. She is currently specializing in periodontal and implant surgery at Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine.
