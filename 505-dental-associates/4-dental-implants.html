Implant Surgical Procedure

As with a single implant, the procedure for All-on-4 dental implants begins with a consultation with our qualified dental experts . Following this:

  • You and your dentist formulate a treatment plan together that addresses your concerns and budget.
  • If applicable, your dentist (open on Saturday) removes any remaining roots of your teeth.
  • Your New York City dentist inserts four titanium screws at the designated positions in a surgical procedure performed in the clinic.
  • Attachments are placed on the implant screws.
  • You recover from the local or general anesthesia in a waiting area until you feel well enough to go home.
  • Your dentist takes impressions of your mouth to construct your permanent arch.
  • You’re fitted with a temporary dental prosthesis to wear while your mouth heals and the implants fuse with the bone.
  • After four to six months, you return for your permanent arch prosthesis, which is made of zirconia or acrylic on a titanium framework.

Regaining Your Smile

As an ideal candidate for All-on-4 dental implants, you’re missing most or all of your teeth on the lower or upper jaw. You may be wearing full dentures currently. Your gums are healthy and you have enough bone in your upper and lower jaw to hold the titanium implants.

Your dentist’s goal is to help you regain a functional, healthy mouth for life. It’s never too late to replace a tooth, whether you lost it recently or not. With a high long-term success rate, All-on-4 dental implants represent an option for safe, precise, aesthetic and reliable results.
