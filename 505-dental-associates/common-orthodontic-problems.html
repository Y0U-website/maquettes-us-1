But Isn’t Orthodontic Treatment Awkward and Lengthy?

The potential benefits of orthodontic treatment are undeniable, but understandably many people don’t enjoy the thought of having braces for years, imagining they will need the heavy metal train track braces we can all remember from school days.

While metal braces are still widely used and are frequently a cost-effective solution for kids’ orthodontic treatment, there are some far more discreet options for adults. Unlike children who will often make a feature of having braces by using brightly colored elastics, most adults will prefer virtually invisible braces.

The technologies used in orthodontics have improved significantly over the past few years, and nowadays, some options can straighten teeth more quickly, and especially if only the front teeth require treatment. Adult braces can be fixed or removable, depending on the problems requiring correction.

Fixed or Removable Braces?

Fixed braces are still a popular solution, but instead of metal brackets they have clear ceramic brackets and which are powered by thin discrete wires.

Removable braces include solutions like Invisalign which uses clear BPA free plastic aligners that fit tightly over the teeth. Once in the mouth they are virtually invisible, but there may be a slight halo effect created by the aligners.

Although removable braces may seem a tempting solution, clear fixed braces can sometimes provide better and more precise results and especially if the tooth movements required are a little trickier.

With clear fixed braces, you could get outstanding results in as little as a year.

Solutions like Invisalign tend to work best on more straightforward orthodontic problems, but they do need excellent compliance as you must remember to wear them all the time, ideally only taking them out for meals and for brushing and flossing.

Some solutions are cosmetically oriented, and which will only straighten the front teeth that are visible when you smile. These cosmetic braces can work significantly more quickly and sometimes treatment is completed in a matter of weeks or just a few months.

Some braces can move the tooth and tooth root at the same time, speeding up treatment. However, it’s best to be guided by our orthodontist as he has spent years completing additional training in this field and can provide you with the most appropriate and effective treatment.

Retainers

Even adults will still need to wear retainers after orthodontic treatment.

When your braces are removed, your teeth need time to settle into their new locations. The bone around them also needs time to remodel, as the tooth roots will have moved through the bone. As it gradually remodels, the bone will help to hold your teeth in their new positions, but while this is happening, it is essential to wear your retainers as directed by your orthodontist. Otherwise, all your hard work could be for nothing as you risk your teeth shifting back into their old positions.
