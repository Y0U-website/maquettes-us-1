Orthodontics at Bay Area Dental, Airway & Sleep More Then Straight Teeth: Orthodontics That Shapes Lives Orthodontics should do more than straighten teeth—it should support proper function, breathing, and long-term oral health. At Bay Area Dental, Airway & Sleep, we focus on treatments that address the root causes of misalignment, ensuring every smile we align is healthier, stronger, and built to last.

Smart Solutions for Better Alignment Airway- Friendly Clear Aligners These advanced aligners guide teeth into position without compromising airway space. By encouraging forward movement of teeth instead of retraction, they help improve breathing while achieving precise alignment.

Palatal Expansion For growing patients, palatal expanders gently widen the upper jaw, creating space for proper development and supporting better airway health. This proactive approach reduces the need for braces later.

Myofunctional Therapy A crucial companion to orthodontic care, myofunctional therapy retrains oral muscles to ensure proper tongue posture, enhance breathing, and maintain alignment. This therapy addresses habits like mouth breathing, which can lead to misalignment or relapse.

Early Intervention Starting orthodontic care early allows us to guide jaw and airway growth during crucial development phases. Early intervention can prevent complications like poor jaw alignment, mouth breathing, and crowding down the road.

Advanced Technology Using tools like 3D imaging, we create precise, customized treatment plans that address the root causes of misalignment and ensure effective, lasting results.
