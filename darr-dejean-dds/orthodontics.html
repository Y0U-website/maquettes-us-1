Orthodontics

Traditional Braces

Our office prides itself on offering our patients a full spectrum of services. This is why we offer orthodontic treatment. There are many reasons people straighten crooked teeth: for cosmetic purposes, to correct a misaligned bite, or to make oral hygiene easier and more efficient. If you would like to learn more about braces, please contact our office at (337) 942-9233.

Invisalign

Are you interested in straighter teeth but hesitant about traditional braces? Invisalign may be for you. Invisalign treatment is the process of wearing a series of clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth. No brackets and wires, and none of the restrictions that come with metal braces. If you would like to find out if Invisalign is right for you, please contact us at (337) 942-9233.
