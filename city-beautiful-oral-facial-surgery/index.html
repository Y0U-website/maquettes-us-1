The practice

In a world of corporate chains, we offer something different—care that’s truly personal. As a local, family-run practice, we’re committed to treating you with warmth, respect, and genuine compassion. We take the time to know you, not just your chart, and every aspect of our care is tailored to your comfort and unique needs. Here, you’re not just a patient—you’re a part of our practice family.

We provide broad-scope oral and maxillofacial surgical care, from dental implants and wisdom tooth removal to jaw surgery and facial cosmetic surgery. Every treatment is personalized to your unique needs, guided by open, honest communication and a commitment to clinical integrity. With same-day treatments, emergency care, and phone consultations available , we’re here when you need us!
