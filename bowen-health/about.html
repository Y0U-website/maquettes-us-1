ABOUT

0 About Bowen Health Healthcare that focuses on the whole person. At Bowen Health we provide high-quality healthcare for the whole person, mind and body, in northeast and north central Indiana. We aim to be your healthcare home by offering judgement-free, patient-centered care. Services include wellness exams, sports physicals, sick visits, mental and emotional health care, dentistry, addiction recovery, life skills coaching and more. Bowen Health serves all people – we accept Medicaid and no one is turned away for an inability to pay. You belong at Bowen.

Our mission is to provide compassionate, patient-centered care for mind and body that improves the quality of life for our communities.
