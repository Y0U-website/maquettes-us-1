Les soins

Composite fillings blend in with natural enamel colors. Many patients prefer the look of composite fillings, especially in the front of the mouth.

If the dentists can’t save the tooth, they can replace it with a dental bridge. A bridge is a permanently attached replacement tooth that sits between two healthy teeth or crowns.

Dental implants are titanium posts that fuse with the jawbone. The dentist surgically installs the post in the jawbone and lets it heal for several months. On top of the post, the dentist then places a crown that replaces a natural tooth.

Cosmetic dentistry is important because many people feel they can’t smile or express themselves comfortably.
