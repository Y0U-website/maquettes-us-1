The Bullis Pond Dental team is composed of caring, friendly individuals who are committed to working with you and your family to create a positive dental experience every time you visit our practice. What’s in a name?

Bullis Pond was the gathering spot for family celebrations and recreation for the historic Bullis Family Farm. The natural beauty of the pond compliments the comfortable atmosphere at Bullis Pond Dental all in a very convenient location. Pictures of the Bullis Farm are the focus of the decor in our reception area. There is reading material based on the history of the area also available. Including Before Oakwood: The Bullis Edgewood Stock Farm, written by Jack Bullis.
