Fillings

Composite fillings act as a dental solution for problems such as tooth decay or cracks. A composite filling is a tooth-colored plastic and glass mixture used to restore decayed teeth. This procedure is also utilized for cosmetic improvements of the smile by fixing any discoloration or reshaping any disfigured teeth.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-lasting and natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. An implant consists of a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it functions as an artificial tooth root. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

Dental implants help restore proper chewing function, improve speech, and prevent bone loss that can occur after tooth loss. They also support surrounding teeth and maintain the natural structure of your face. With proper care, implants can last for many years and feel just like your natural teeth.

Our team carefully evaluates your oral health, bone structure, and overall needs to determine if dental implants are the right solution for you.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Full mouth rehabilitation is a comprehensive treatment approach designed for patients who have multiple dental concerns affecting the health, function, and appearance of their smile. This personalized treatment plan may involve a combination of restorative, cosmetic, and surgical procedures to rebuild and restore the entire mouth.

Full mouth rehabilitation is often recommended for patients with extensive tooth wear, missing teeth, bite problems, chronic dental pain, or damage caused by decay, trauma, or long-term oral health issues. Treatments may include crowns, bridges, dental implants, fillings, gum therapy, or bite correction, depending on your specific needs.

Our doctors take a thorough and strategic approach, carefully evaluating your oral health, bite alignment, and aesthetic goals to create a customized plan that restores comfort, function, and confidence in your smile.

Extractions

An extraction is performed to remove a tooth, whether because of disease, crowding, or damage. When extractions are required, the area around the tooth will be numbed and your dentist will remove the tooth. A small amount of bleeding is normal, as your mouth will replace the removed tooth root by forming a blood clot in the area.

Preventative Dentistry

Good dental preventative care is really important to ensure a healthy mouth, including your gums and teeth.

It is important to brush your teeth at least twice daily to help remove plaque and food particles that get stuck to your teeth. It is recommended that you brush your teeth for two minutes (or longer), though most people rush through this time. You need to make sure that you reach every side of each tooth, including the inside and outside. To help keep your breath fresh, you should also brush your tongue. Then, you can begin flossing.

Teeth Whitening

A common request we receive is how to make a smile whiter and brighter. This is a relatively simple and quick treatment option that can be performed periodically. Certain foods and beverages, such as wine and coffee, stain the teeth and contribute to discoloration. The teeth whitening process provides a solution that lightens the shade of the teeth.
