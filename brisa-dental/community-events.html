Give Kids A Smile Event Call us to make an appointment at (804) 716-3480

At Brisa Dental, we believe every child deserves access to quality dental care, regardless of their circumstances. That’s why we’re proud to host our Give Kids A Smile event, providing free dental cleanings and education to children in our community. This special event is designed to promote oral health awareness and ensure kids receive the care they need in a fun, welcoming environment. Our team of compassionate dental professionals will be on hand to offer gentle cleanings, hygiene tips, and guidance to help parents and children establish lifelong healthy habits.

Beyond just a check-up, Give Kids A Smile is about building confidence and reducing dental anxiety for young patients. We want every child to feel comfortable visiting the dentist and excited about taking care of their smile. Stay tuned for the official date and details on how to participate—we can’t wait to give back to the community and brighten smiles together!
