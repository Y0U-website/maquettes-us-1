General Dentistry

Brisa Dental provides comprehensive dental care for children and families. Our services include thorough exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, and treatments for cavities and gum health. We focus on patient education, ensuring parents and kids understand the best ways to maintain strong, healthy teeth through proper oral hygiene habits.

Children’s Dentistry

At Brisa Dental, we provide gentle and specialized care to ensure your child’s smile stays healthy and bright. Our kid-friendly approach helps ease dental anxiety, making visits stress-free and enjoyable. We offer regular check-ups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and cavity prevention, focusing on early detection and education to build lifelong healthy habits.

Infant & Toddler Dentistry

Early dental care is crucial for lifelong oral health. We offer specialized services for infants and toddlers, starting as early as six months old. Our team provides guidance on teething, nutrition, and early tooth development while ensuring your little one’s first dental visits are positive and comforting. We work closely with parents to establish strong oral hygiene routines from the start.

Preventive Care & Education

Prevention is key to maintaining a healthy smile. Our preventive care services include dental sealants to protect against cavities, fluoride treatments for stronger enamel, and hands-on brushing and flossing lessons. We also offer nutritional counseling to promote better oral health and reduce the risk of decay, ensuring children develop habits that last a lifetime.

Sedation & Comfort Options

Some children may feel anxious during dental visits, which is why we offer safe nitrous oxide—commonly known as laughing gas—to help them stay relaxed and comfortable. For those who require deeper sedation, we partner with many trusted pediatric clinics to provide deeper sedation options when needed, ensuring a calm, reassuring experience for every child.

Notary Service

At Brisa Dental, we care for our community beyond just dental health. That’s why we offer professional notary services to help families with important documents. Whether you need affidavits, contracts, or other official paperwork notarized, our team provides a quick, reliable, and convenient service right in our office. We’re here to make life easier—because Brisa Dental is about more than just smiles!
