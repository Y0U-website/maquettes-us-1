What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is like an artificial tooth root made from titanium, securely placed into your jaw. Once the implant has settled and fused with the bone, it provides a sturdy base for a custom-made restoration, such as a dental crown, bridge, or denture. This restoration is what completes your smile and restores the appearance and function of the missing tooth or teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process can often be split up into four distinct steps. Depending on your individual case, it can look something like this:

  • Consultation and Planning: Your first step is a personalized consultation with Dr. Q. During this visit, we’ll discuss your goals, evaluate your oral health, and determine if dental implants are right for you. If they are, we’ll create a customized plan tailored to your needs.
  • Implant Placement: We work closely with a local oral surgeon or periodontist for this step. Once you have your dental implant, it needs time to heal and integrate fully with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Restoration Placement: After the healing phase, it’s time for the exciting part! We’ll design and place your custom restoration, whether it’s a dental crown, bridge, or denture. Our team uses top-quality materials to ensure your restoration is durable and perfectly matches your smile.
  • Follow-Up Care: Your dental implant restoration can last many years, but regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential to keeping it in great shape. We’ll provide guidance on how to care for your new smile and maintain your oral health.

Benefits of Dental Implants

If you’re missing one or more teeth, you know how it can impact your daily life. From difficulty chewing your favorite foods to feeling self-conscious about your smile, missing teeth can affect both your oral health and your confidence. That’s where implant restorations come in:

  • Natural Look and Feel: Dental implant restorations are crafted to blend seamlessly with natural teeth. Once your restoration is in place, it will feel like your real tooth and look so natural that no one will know the difference.
  • Durability and Strength: Dental implants can last for decades with proper care. Unlike other tooth replacement methods, they provide a permanent solution that can withstand the wear and tear of daily life.
  • Improved Oral Health: Implants help to preserve your jawbone by simulating natural tooth roots, preventing bone loss over time. Additionally, they don’t require the alteration of neighboring teeth, which means they stay intact.
  • Enhanced Quality of Life: With dental implants, chewing, speaking, and smiling feel effortless. Say goodbye to slipping dentures and restricted diets.
