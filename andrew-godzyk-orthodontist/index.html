At Dr. Andrew Godzyk’s Milwaukie, Oregon orthodontic office , your smile is our top priority! We are delighted to provide you the compassionate, personalized care you deserve at each visit. Thank you for choosing us.

Being an orthodontist is incredible: the profession allows me to improve people’s smiles, which increases their self-esteem and comfort around friends, peers, and colleagues. It is immensely rewarding to welcome someone new to the practice, often a person who feels uncomfortable with the appearance of their teeth, knowing I will be able to make a lasting change in their life.

Whether I am transforming a child’s, teen’s, or adult’s smile, I design treatment specific to the individual. Focusing on the person’s oral health and aesthetic concerns, I determine the ideal approach that ensures the best treatment experience and predictable, stable, and beautiful results.

Throughout the treatment journey, I have the opportunity to get to know each patient and their family. We share stories and develop a bond of trust and respect. By the end of their treatment, they have a beautifully healthy, confident smile and we’ve become friends!

I have practiced orthodontics since 1993, drawn to the oral healthcare field after interactions with my childhood dentist. Experiencing kind and compassionate dental care was inspiring: I wanted to share that experience with others.

I attended Eastern Oregon University, graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 1985. I then attended the Oregon Health Sciences University School of Dentistry, completing my Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) in 1990 and Orthodontics Specialization in 1993.

While dental school and then an orthodontics residency gave me an excellent foundational understanding of my specialty, the field is constantly advancing. Throughout the year I complete many hours of continuing education through meetings, lectures, seminars, and online processes in order to stay current on the latest developments.
