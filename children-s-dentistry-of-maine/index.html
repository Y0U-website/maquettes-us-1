0 Meet the Team Aaron T. Schmick DMD, MsD Dr. S was born and raised in Montoursville, PA. He has called southern Maine home since July 2017, where he lives with his wife Kara, two sons Hans and Ross, and mini schnauzer Daisy. Dr. S is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He completed his residency training at VCU in Richmond, Virginia.

Dr. S’s passion for working with and treating children started at a young age. He was very fortunate to have a fantastic medical team treat him as a child. His goal is to give back to kids in the same manner his doctors provided for him. One way he achieves this outside the office is through the MMC Cleft Lip and Palate team, which he has been a member of since 2018.

In his free time, Dr. S enjoys all the outdoor activities Maine has to offer. You may see him out on a trail hiking in the summer or skiing in the winter. At home, he enjoys landscaping and cooking new dinner recipes.

There are many aspects to prevention in addition to your child’s six-month cleaning appointments. Dr. S and his team will review proper techniques for brushing and flossing, discuss helpful dietary tips, as well as different products that may allow you to more easily accomplish the daily routine with your little ones.

Other preventative services offered include Fluoride treatments at your child’s cleaning appointment to help strengthen and remineralize their teeth. Another option is sealants, which is a material that sticks to the grooves of the molars to help protect those highly susceptible areas from decay.
