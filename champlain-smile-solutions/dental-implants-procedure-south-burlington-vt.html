What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial titanium tooth roots that are surgically placed into the jawbone. They provide a strong, stable base for different types of replacement teeth, including dental crowns, bridges, and dentures. Dental implants are extremely popular because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Who is a candidate for dental implants?

Most of our patients who are in good overall health and have adequate jawbone density may be suitable for dental implants. However, factors such as gum health, chronic conditions, and smoking habits influence eligibility. Our team will perform a thorough evaluation to decide if dental implants are right for you.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Many patients at Champlain Smile Solutions are surprised to learn that the dental implants procedure is relatively comfortable. The placement is typically completed under local anesthesia, and patients report minimal pain during and after the surgery. Over-the-counter pain relief and following the post-care instructions given by our team keep discomfort to a minimum and facilitate faster healing.

How long does the entire process take?

The timeline for getting dental implants differs based on individual treatment plans. Generally, the process usually takes about 3-9 months from start to finish. This includes healing time for the implant to fuse with your jawbone (a process called osseointegration) before the final restoration is placed.

How long do dental implants last?

With the right care and regular dental examinations at Champlain Smile Solutions, dental implants can last for many years (even decades). Good oral hygiene, including brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings, is essential for maintaining your implants and the surrounding gum tissue. As with all medical treatments, prognosis is dependent on individual patient risk factors.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes! One of the more significant benefits of dental implants is how natural they look. The crowns or restorations attached to the implants are custom-made to match characteristics such as the color, size, and shape of your existing teeth, providing a seamless and natural-looking smile.

Are dental implants worth the investment?

Dental implants are considered an excellent long-term investment in one’s oral health and quality of life. Although the initial cost may be higher than that of other solutions, their durability and natural function often make them a more cost-effective choice over time. They allow you to eat, speak, and also smile confidently without worrying about slipping or shifting.
