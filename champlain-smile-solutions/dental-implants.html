How are dental implants placed?

We have refined the dental implant procedure to achieve the best possible outcomes while maximizing your comfort and time.

  • Consultation: The dentist will check your teeth, gums, and overall oral health to assess your eligibility for dental implants. This examination will involve taking digital X-rays and 3D scans to develop a tailored treatment plan. Additionally, the dentist will inform you if any preliminary procedures, such as bone grafting or tooth extractions, are required before the surgery.
  • Implant surgery: We always prioritize your comfort during your implant procedure. We use powerful anesthetics to ensure a painless experience. Additionally, we offer multiple sedation options, including IV sedation, to provide next-level comfort. Once you are comfortable, the dentist will surgically place a titanium post at a pre-determined location in the jawbone.
  • Healing: The implants should be left to heal and fuse with the jawbone for 4-6 months. Following the dentist’s instructions during this period is important to ensure a successful procedure.
  • Abutment placement: The dentist will uncover the implant to attach a small metal connector. This minor surgery requires two weeks of healing.
  • Restoration: This is the most exciting step of the dental implant journey. The dentist will restore your implants with a customized, life-looking prosthesis. After restoration, you’ll regain instant function and aesthetic boost.
