What are implant-supported dentures?

Conventional dentures are a simple and effective solution for missing teeth, but they become unstable over time as gums shrink. Our team overcomes this flaw by integrating dental implants into your dentures.

The dentist anchors a full set of prosthetic teeth on 2-6 dental implants strategically placed in the jawbone. Implant-supported dentures are firmly secured in the jawbone, rivaling the stability of your natural teeth.

Many patients find implant dentures the perfect replacement for extensive tooth loss. They provide root stabilization, allowing you to eat all your favorite foods without instability or discomfort. Besides, implant-supported dentures look like natural teeth and last many years with proper care. Moreover, dental implants prevent bone loss by providing functional stimulation when chewing and grinding.
