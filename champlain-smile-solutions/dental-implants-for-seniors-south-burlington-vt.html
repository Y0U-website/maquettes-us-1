Fact #1: Implants are a Common Dental Treatment

Contrary to what people may think, dental implants are not a novel treatment. They’ve been around for some time, with millions of seniors enjoying the amazing results of implant surgery. Implants offer a modern approach to replacing lost teeth, providing a more durable, stable option than traditional dentures or bridges.

Fact #2: Getting Implants is a Process

The dental implants procedure requires oral surgery and a lengthy recovery. Dr. Giulianelli will place the titanium posts (implants) inside your jawbone surgically, where they will fuse with your bone system and heal. This fusing process can take 4-6 months to complete. Once healed, the implants are used as a platform to support your new teeth.

Fact #3: Implant Surgery Isn’t Painful

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel a thing. We also offer dental sedation to relieve any anxiety or fear you may have about undergoing oral surgery. Any gum swelling or discomfort you feel after your surgery is temporary and can generally be managed with ice packs and OTC or prescription pain medication.

Fact #4: Implant Smiles Are Natural and Pleasing

Implant restorations look 100 % real as they’re designed to resemble your natural teeth. They’re custom-made for a precise fit and blend in seamlessly with your real teeth to give your smile an authentic appearance. Good oral hygiene and routine cleanings will keep your new smile going strong for years.

Fact #5: Implants Restore Full Chewing Power

Implants restore your ability to chew, so you can eat your favorite foods comfortably and confidently. Your new teeth will be stable and secure, even when you bite and chew. You’ll have the biting force of natural teeth to eat what you want and get full nutritional value from your food.

Fact #6: Implants Prevent Bone Loss

When teeth are lost, the jawbone around them starts to deteriorate due to a lack of stimulation from chewing. This causes you to lose facial support, which creates a sunken-in look. By integrating with your jaw, implants prevent bone loss and facial collapse, helping you retain a younger-looking appearance.

Fact #7: Implants Can Potentially Last a Lifetime

Titanium implants never decay and can potentially last a lifetime, with good oral care. Porcelain restorations are also enduring, lasting 10, 20 years or more. You can enjoy your beautiful new smile for some time to come.
