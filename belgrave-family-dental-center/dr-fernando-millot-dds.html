Personal Touch :

Dr. Millot chose to become a dentist because he has always been captivated by the power of a smile. He noticed how a genuine smile can express confidence, warmth, and kindness often without saying a single word. He believes that every smile tells a unique story, and that helping patients achieve one can truly change how they feel about themselves and how they connect with others.

For Dr. Millot, dentistry is more than restoring teeth, it’s about restoring confidence and improving lives. He takes great pride in creating a welcoming environment where patients feel heard, cared for, and comfortable throughout their visits. His gentle approach and attention to detail reflect his belief that excellent dental care should combine both precision and compassion.

Outside the office, Dr. Millot enjoys spending quality time with his family, cheering for the Los Angeles Dodgers, and watching soccer games. Whether at the ballpark or relaxing at home, he values every opportunity to stay active and share moments with the people who matter most to him.

Professional Experience :

Dr. Fernando Millot has over 31 years of experience in dentistry. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Juarez University in 1994, and later obtained another Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from La Salle University in 2010.

He has completed advanced continuing education programs, including Cosmetic Dentistry Hands-On Seminars with Sulzer Calcitek, and Advanced Hands-On Dental Implant Placement and Cadaver Seminars through the Canadian Dental Implant Institute and Mexican Oral Implant Institute (1999).

Dr. Millot practiced dentistry in Mexico from 1994 to 2010, and has been serving patients in California, USA, since 2011, offering comprehensive care in general dentistry.
