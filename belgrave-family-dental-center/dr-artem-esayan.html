Professional Experience :

Dr. Artem Esayan is a dedicated and detail-oriented dentist committed to providing exceptional care through precision, compassion, and advanced clinical skills.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Yerevan Haybusak University in 2017, where he developed a strong foundation in general and restorative dentistry. Driven by a passion for continuous growth, he continued his education through the International Dental Program at Nicolae Testemit and State University of Medicine and Pharmacy between 2021 and 2023, further refining his clinical expertise and expanding his international perspective on dental care.

Dr. Esayan also completed advanced implant training in Brazil, one of the most respected and intensive hands-on programs in the field. This specialized experience significantly strengthened his skills in implantology and broadened his approach to restorative and cosmetic dentistry.

With over 8 years of experience, Dr. Esayan provides comprehensive dental care, focusing on preventive, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. His friendly and approachable personality helps patients feel at ease from the very first visit, reflecting his genuine care for people and their wellbeing.
