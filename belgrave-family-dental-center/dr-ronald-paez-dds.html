Personal Touch :

Dr. Paez chose a career in dentistry because he finds deep satisfaction in helping people improve not only their oral health but also their confidence and overall wellbeing. He believes that a healthy smile can have a powerful impact on a person’s life boosting selfesteem, comfort, and happiness.

Patients appreciate Dr. Paez’s caring nature and calm approach. He takes the time to listen, explain each procedure clearly, and ensure every patient feels comfortable and informed. His goal is to make every visit as positive and stress free as possible.

Outside of the office, Dr. Paez enjoys spending quality time with his family and friends. He values balance, kindness, and lifelong learning qualities that guide both his personal life and his approach to patient care.

Professional Experience :

Dr. Ronald Paez is a dedicated and experienced general dentist with over 32 years of experience providing high-quality dental care to patients of all ages. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of the East College of Dentistry in Manila, Philippines, in 1993.

Throughout his career, Dr. Paez has been committed to continuous learning, completing numerous advanced education seminars focused on cosmetic dentistry, restorative procedures, root canal therapy, and oral surgery.

Before joining Belgrave Family Dental, Dr. Paez practiced with Western Dental, where he gained extensive experience in comprehensive dental care. For the past 25 years, he has been a trusted member of the Belgrave Family Dental team, known for his gentle touch, patience, and dedication to helping patients achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles.
