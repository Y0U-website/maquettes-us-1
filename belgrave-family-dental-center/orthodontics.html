What a Patient Can Expect

Orthodontic treatment begins with an evaluation to assess tooth alignment, bite, and overall oral health. From there, a personalized treatment plan is created to help achieve the best possible results.

During treatment, teeth are gradually guided into their ideal position through gentle adjustments over time. Regular visits allow your dental team to monitor progress and ensure everything stays on track.

By the end of treatment, patients can enjoy a straighter, healthier smile that supports both confidence and long-term oral health.

1. Consultation Complete assessment and personalized treatment plan.

2. Treatment Plan Custom plan using braces or clear aligners.

3. Alignment Process Teeth gradually shift into proper position.

4. Retention Retainers help maintain your new smile.
