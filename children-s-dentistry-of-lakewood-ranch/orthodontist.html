Benefits of Early Orthodontic Treatment for Your Child

  • Prevents More Serious Issues. Early orthodontic treatment can spot and address possible oral health issues before they worsen in the long run, saving time, expense, and discomfort in the long run.
  • Improves Oral Function. Correcting misalignments or bite issues early on can improve a child’s ability to chew and speak effectively.
  • Enhances Aesthetics and Self-Esteem. Straight teeth and a well-aligned jaw can boost a child’s confidence, contributing to their social and psychological well-being.
  • Promotes Healthy Jaw Growth. Early intervention can guide jaw growth, ensuring there is enough space for permanent teeth to emerge correctly.
  • Facilitates Easier Care of Teeth. Aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Shortens Overall Treatment Time. Addressing early signs of orthodontic issues can often mean a shorter total time wearing braces or other corrective devices during the precious developmental years.

What Age Is Best for Braces?

The ideal age for a child to begin wearing braces depends on their unique dental development needs and the specific issues at hand. On average, most children can wear braces between 10 and 14. This is when the mouth and jaws are still growing, and the permanent teeth have mostly come in, making it an optimal time for correction.

However, the starting age can vary significantly based on the individual’s orthodontic needs. Some children might benefit from earlier intervention with what’s known as Phase One orthodontic treatment, possibly around age 7 to 9, to address severe bite or space issues.

Misconceptions About Orthodontic Treatments

  • Orthodontic Treatment is Purely Cosmetic. Despite popular belief, orthodontic treatment goes beyond improving the appearance of a smile. It also addresses functional issues, such as bite alignment and difficulties with eating or speaking. Furthermore, a correctly aligned jaw and teeth can prevent long-term dental health issues.
  • Braces are Only for Kids or Young Teens. There is no age limit to orthodontic treatment. While early intervention can have optimal results during adolescence due to growing bones, teenagers and adults can benefit from braces.
  • Orthodontic Treatments Are Expensive. Many believe orthodontic treatments are financially out of reach. That’s why many people may decide to forgo treatment altogether. However, most dental practices offer flexible payment plans, insurance options, and sometimes scholarships to make treatment more accessible.
