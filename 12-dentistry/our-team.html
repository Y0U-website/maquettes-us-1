Our Team

0 Who We Are We go beyond treating teeth—we build trust through honest, personalized care. From the moment you arrive, you’re welcomed like family with genuine concern for your well‑being.

Because dental anxiety is common, we create a safe, calming environment with gentle care and sedation options for stress‑free visits. Dr. Heydari’s advanced training and modern technology make appointments efficient and comfortable.

We’re excited to be your new dental home—explore who we are and the care we proudly provide.

Dr. Pedraum Heydari DMD, DICOI Born in Seattle and raised in Kent, Dr. Pedraum Heydari is proud to care for the South Puget Sound communities that shaped him. He discovered his passion for dentistry at the University of Washington while assisting and volunteering in clinics serving underserved patients.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) and a Certificate in Public Health from the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health before returning to the Pacific Northwest to begin his career.

A graduate of the prestigious Kois Center and a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), Dr. Heydari is committed to advanced training and clinical excellence. He remains active in leading dental organizations and continually pursues education to provide the highest standard of care.

Outside the office, he enjoys hiking, golfing, exploring the Pacific Northwest, and spending time with his service dog, Toby. A proud foodie, he’s always happy to trade restaurant recommendations.
