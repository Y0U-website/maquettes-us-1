Why Choose Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely considered the most effective tooth replacement option for patients who qualify. They restore both the function and appearance of your smile and offer important oral health benefits.

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone begins to deteriorate because it no longer receives stimulation from the tooth root. A dental implant replaces that root and helps maintain bone strength and density.

Placing an implant in the empty space prevents neighboring teeth from shifting. Because implants help preserve the jawbone, they also reduce the risk of future tooth loss.

How We Place Your Dental Implant

A dental implant has two main parts: a small titanium post placed in the jawbone and a custom crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

Dr. Heydari performs the entire implant process in our South Puyallup office, so you do not need to visit a specialist. Using advanced 3‑D cone beam imaging, he identifies the ideal placement site and avoids sensitive anatomical structures.

Once the implant is placed, it needs time to heal and integrate with the surrounding bone. This process typically takes six to eight months. Our patients consistently tell us the results are well worth the wait.

Caring for Your Dental Implant

A dental implant is an investment in your long‑term oral health. With proper care, it can last for many years.

• Maintain regular professional cleanings

• Protect your gums from inflammation and gum disease

Gum disease is the biggest threat to implant stability. Combining good home care with routine visits helps ensure your implant stays healthy and secure.
