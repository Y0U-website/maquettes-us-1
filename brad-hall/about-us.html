About Us

Dr. Brad Hall and his team serve patients in Athens, Watkinsville, Bogart, Gainesville and the northeast Georgia area. We provide the highest quality comprehensive dental care to all our patients and recognize the importance of treating every patient as an individual. We will provide you with the information you need in order to make informed decisions about your oral health.

We Are Committed to Your Dental and Overall Health We are an evidence-based practice that emphasizes the importance of the patient’s active role in their dental treatment choices and overall health. The oral tissues can often serve as a barometer of systemic illness. A thorough initial exam and regular follow up visits are important to your overall wellness. If you are looking for a dentist, we are accepting new patients and look forward to helping you maintain or restore your oral health.

Dr. Hall and his team are committed to providing the highest standard of care for our patients, we participate in continuing education classes throughout the year in order to review current concepts in dental care and also keep abreast of the latest research, newest technology and innovations which are shaping the future of dentistry.
