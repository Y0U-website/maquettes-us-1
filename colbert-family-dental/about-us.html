Meet the Team at Colbert Family Dental

Our team members at Colbert Family Dental share a commitment to providing you and yours with exceptional patient care. From our skilled dental hygienists and assistants to our supportive administrative staff, we prioritize a seamless experience for your family.

Compassion Is the Heart of Our Practice

Understanding and minimizing dental anxiety is central to ensuring that every visit with us is comfortable and stress-free. Our team excels at creating a friendly and calming environment by listening actively to your concerns and providing gentle reassurance.

We thrive on teamwork and coordinate closely to ensure that each step of your dental experience is smooth and stress-free. Our desire to be the best at what we do motivates us to continuously expand our knowledge and skills to ensure you receive the most up-to-date care.

Dr. Mackenzie Colbert

Dr. Mackenzie Colbert grew up with a family tradition of dentistry, and her interest was sparked by observing her father and two uncles as dentists. It was not until high school that she found a passion for ceramics in addition to science and realized pursuing dentistry would merge her two interests.

Dr. Mackenzie earned her undergraduate degree in human physiology at the University of Iowa and graduated from The University of Iowa College of Dentistry. She subsequently completed a General Practice Residency at the Minneapolis VA Hospital. Here, she gained advanced experience in oral surgery, implant placement, and periodontal surgery. She completed complex, comprehensive restorative treatment plans that positioned her to better serve her future patients and build a practice that would meet all their oral health needs.

In her free time, Dr. Mackenzie enjoys all the great things Minnesota has to offer with her husband and son. She enjoys biking, cheering on the Hawkeyes (and the Gophers when not playing the Hawkeyes), spending time on the water in any capacity, and checking out new restaurants around the Twin Cities.

Dr. Elizabeth Harting, DDS

Dr. Ellie Harting grew up in Brainerd, MN, where both sides of her large family have deep roots. With her father in healthcare and her mother in banking, Ellie quickly realized she was drawn to the people-centered and dynamic nature of healthcare. She developed a passion for ceramics in high school and continued taking ceramics courses throughout college, fostering her appreciation for creativity and working with her hands. Dentistry ultimately felt like the perfect combination of her interests in science, creativity, and connecting with people.

Dr. Ellie especially enjoys getting to know her patients and building lasting relationships with them. She strives to create a friendly, comfortable environment where patients can feel at ease during their visits even if it is in a dental chair.

When she’s not practicing dentistry, Dr. Ellie enjoys spending time with her family and friends, training and spending time with her dogs, tackling DIY projects, and staying active through water sports, snowboarding, golf, and working out.

Connie

Connie grew up in the Anoka area and attended Anoka High School before earning her dental assisting degree in 1980 from Hennepin Technical College. She has been a core member of Dr. Colbert’s team for many years, serving patients, leading the team, and running the office from day to day. In 2025, Connie celebrated her 45th year with Colbert Family Dental! She even got to celebrate it along side Dr. Mike Colbert when he got pulled out of retirement for a few months to help cover Dr. Mackenzie s maternity leave.

Connie is a member of the Dental Assisting National Board and is registered with the Minnesota Dental Board. She also continues her education through the OMS Study Group and attends several continuing education courses each year.

When she is not at the dental office, Connie enjoys spending time with her family, kids, and grandchildren. She loves to camp and go four-wheeling in the summer.

Addie

Addie received her dental hygiene training at the University of Iowa/Kirkwood Community College in 2023. She joined Colbert Family Dental shortly after, bringing the latest training in dental hygiene to our practice.

In her short time with us, Addie has become known for her ability to ease patients’ anxieties, making their dental experience a positive one. Educating patients about their oral health and witnessing their growth in confidence are the most rewarding aspects of her job.

When she s not with her patients at the clinic, Addie enjoys reading, movies, games, and cheering on her favorite sports teams the Chicago Cubs and Iowa Hawkeyes. Although she’s new to the area, Addie appreciates the change of scenery from cornfields everywhere to our many beautiful lakes and recreational activities.

Whether you re here for a routine cleaning or more complex dental needs, Addie s gentle touch and friendly demeanor will make your visit a pleasant and calming experience.
