Dental Implants Champlin MN

Since a dental implant is made of titanium, which is biocompatible, it can naturally heal and integrate with your bone over a period of time through a process called osseointegration. This typically takes three to six months; our dentists will monitor your healing process.

  • The abutment This attaches the implant to its custom restoration, which is a porcelain crown for a single tooth replacement or a bridge or denture to restore multiple missing teeth.
  • The restoration After the healing and integration process, our dentists will place your final restoration.

The Outstanding Advantages of Dental Implants in Maple Grove

You may not have realized that dental implants offer several advantages that other tooth replacement options do not.

Longevity

Dental implants can potentially last a lifetime with the right home care and routine dental checkups.

Aesthetics

Implants offer the look, feel, and function of natural teeth and blend seamlessly with your smile.

Reliability

Dental implants boast a 95 percent success rate, making them a highly reliable procedure for your smile.

Bone Health

Implants protect the health of your jawbone, replacing your root to keep it healthy and stimulated.

Wise Investment

Because of their longevity, dental implants can be considered a wise long-term smile investment.
