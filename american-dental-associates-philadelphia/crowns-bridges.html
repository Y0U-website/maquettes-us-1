What Is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is used to replace one or more missing teeth in a row. It typically consists of prosthetic teeth supported by neighboring teeth or, in some cases, dental implants. Bridges help close gaps left by missing teeth, which can otherwise affect chewing efficiency, speech clarity, and bite alignment.

By filling the space, a bridge can help distribute bite forces more evenly and limit unwanted movement of surrounding teeth. While bridges do not replace tooth roots, they can play an important role in restoring daily function and appearance.
