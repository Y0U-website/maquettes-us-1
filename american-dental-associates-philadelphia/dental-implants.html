If you are missing one or more teeth, dental implants in Philadelphia, PA, may be a reliable option to restore function, comfort, and appearance. Dental implants are designed to replace missing tooth roots and support crowns, bridges, or full-arch restorations. For many patients, implants offer improved stability compared to traditional removable options.

At American Dental Associates, implant care is approached with careful planning, clear communication, and patient comfort in mind.

What to Expect at American Dental Associates

Patients seeking dental implants in Philadelphia, PA, can expect a structured, step-by-step process focused on education and comfort.

Your first visit includes a review of your dental and medical history, digital imaging, and a discussion of your goals. This appointment helps determine whether implants are appropriate or if preparatory procedures, such as bone grafting, should be considered.

Using diagnostic imaging and planning tools, our dentist designs a treatment plan specific to your anatomy and needs.

Implant placement is typically performed using local anesthesia. Many people compare the experience to a routine dental extraction. Post-procedure instructions are provided to support healing and comfort.

Over several months, the implant integrates with the jawbone. During this phase, temporary restorations may be used, and progress is monitored through follow-up visits.

Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthetic is attached. The final restoration is designed to blend naturally with your smile and support everyday function.

All-on-4 Dental Implants in Philadelphia, PA

For patients missing most or all teeth in an arch, All-on-4 dental implants may be an option. This approach uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth. In certain cases, this method may reduce the number of implants required and streamline treatment.

All-on-4 is not appropriate for everyone. Bone structure, bite forces, and health history must be carefully evaluated to determine whether this approach is suitable.
