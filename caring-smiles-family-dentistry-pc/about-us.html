Get to Know Dr. Warren

Dr. Warren is a strong believer in mentorship and dental outreach. He has volunteered at dental clinics and career days at various local schools and health fairs. He also mentors underrepresented minorities with aspirations of entering the dental profession.

Dr. Warren’s calming chairside manner makes him able to connect with his patients on a personal level. He takes time to explain his recommended treatment options and treats all of his patients like they are his family.

Get to Know Dr. May

Dr. May earned her DDS degree from the University of Michigan. This was after completing her Bachelor of Science degree in Psychology, while completing her pre-dental studies, also at the University of Michigan. During her third and fourth years of dental school she was selected by faculty to serve as a pre-clinic and dental anatomy tutor for the first and second year students.

During her third year of dental school, Dr. May traveled to Jamaica to participate in an outreach program through Health Care International where she provided free dental care for one week to hundreds of people in need, mostly children. Upon graduation, she received the Russell W. Bunting Periodontal Study Club Award for outstanding proficiency in clinical periodontics.

Dr. May participates in several local mentorship, volunteer, and outreach programs. She enjoys taking continuing education courses to stay up to date with the latest technology and advances in the field of dentistry. In addition, Dr. May has many interests outside of dentistry, including acting. So if you see someone on TV who looks like her, it indeed may be her! She is also a member of Empowerment Church in Southfield, Michigan. Most of all she loves spending time with her husband (Dr. Warren), their daughter (Wesli), family, friends, and goldendoodle (Quincy).

Dr. May prides herself on her chairside manner and gentle touch. She truly enjoys practicing dentistry as it perfectly blends her love of science, art, and psychology.
