Dental Cone Beam CT 3D Imaging

A Dental Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is a specialized type of X-ray technology that provides three-dimensional images of dental structures, including the teeth, jawbone, and surrounding tissues.

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas, is a form of conscious sedation. It makes you completely relaxed, yet alert enough to follow the instructions of the dentist. It is a great reducer of anxiety and is very safe. Once the nitrous oxide is turned off, you are completely back to normal and it is safe for you to drive yourself home.
