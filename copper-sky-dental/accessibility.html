Accessibility Statement

  • Skip navigation link: Allows users to skip to main content
  • Alternative text: Images include descriptive alternative text
  • Heading structure: Proper heading hierarchy for screen readers
  • Color contrast: Sufficient contrast between text and backgrounds
  • Readable fonts: Larger font sizes for improved readability
  • Responsive design: Content adapts to different screen sizes
  • Form labels: All form fields have associated labels
  • Focus indicators: Visible focus states for interactive elements

Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with assistive technologies, including:

  • Screen readers
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation

Browser Compatibility

Our website is designed to work with current versions of major web browsers, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

Our Office Accessibility

Beyond our website, the physical Copper Sky Dental office is designed with accessibility in mind. We are located on a single floor at 9431 W Thunderbird Rd Ste 2 in Peoria. Parking is at-grade and within a short walk of the entrance, the path from the lot to the front door is paved and step-free, and the entrance opens wide enough for wheelchairs, walkers, and other mobility aids. Our reception desk has a lower section for patients who prefer to be seated while checking in.

Our operatories accommodate wheelchairs and mobility aids, and our clinical team is trained to assist patients with transfers when needed. We extend appointment times for patients who need more time to settle into the chair or who benefit from a slower pace during treatment. Anyone who would like to discuss accessibility needs before an appointment is welcome to call ahead so we can prepare the room and the schedule in advance.

Ongoing Efforts

We treat accessibility as a continuing responsibility, not a one-time project. The website is reviewed against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria on a regular cadence, and any issues that surface, whether from internal testing, automated tools, or patient feedback, are tracked and resolved. Our ongoing work includes:

  • Regular manual and automated accessibility testing on every new page and template
  • Training the team on accessibility best practices for both the website and the patient experience in the office
  • Building accessibility checks into our design and development process so issues are caught before they ship
  • Reviewing third-party widgets such as the appointment form, the Google Map embed, and any analytics scripts for their accessibility implications
  • Responding to accessibility feedback from patients, family members, and visitors promptly and following up on each report
