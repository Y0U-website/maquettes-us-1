Honest answers about cost — face to face, not fine print.

You won’t find a price list on our website, and that’s intentional. Here’s why — and what you can expect instead.

Why we don’t post prices

No two treatment plans are alike — your anatomy, your health, and your goals shape your plan. A one-size-fits-all price on a website wouldn’t be honest. Instead, you receive complete, exact pricing at your consultation, so there are no surprises.

What treatment costs

Your investment depends on your anatomy and your plan — extractions, bone, anesthesia, and the final teeth you choose. At your complimentary consultation, we walk you through the fee for your specific plan — clearly, in plain language, itemized and in writing.

Monthly payment plans

We utilize multiple third-party financing companies, with monthly payment plans available with approved credit — so you can find the monthly payment that fits your budget.

Dental insurance

We do not accept dental insurance — but we complete and submit the claim forms for our patients. Some patients have told us they received a partial reimbursement from their insurance toward their treatment — every plan is different.

The price is only half the question. The provider is the other half.

The challenge for patients is finding the right provider — one with real experience in these complex treatments, who will still be there when there are issues. With the influx of corporate dentistry, patients have faced practices closing, or the dentist who treated them no longer being there.

And with discount implant centers on billboards offering the lowest pricing, there are always stipulations behind the "low-cost" number. Is general anesthesia included? What is the skill set of the surgical and restorative dentists? When centers charge the lowest prices, they are unable to pay the surgeon or restorative dentist at a rate suitable for the experience this type of treatment requires.

Always ask the right questions before you make an investment that will impact you for the rest of your life.

What patients ask us about full-mouth restoration.

Full-mouth dental implants replace an entire arch — or both arches — of missing or failing teeth with fixed replacement teeth anchored to dental implants placed in the jaw. Unlike removable dentures, they stay in permanently and let you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

All-on-4® is one proven technique for full-mouth restoration, supporting a full arch of fixed teeth on four implants. Depending on your anatomy and goals, your plan may use the All-on-4® treatment concept or a different implant configuration. Your 3D CT scan determines the right approach. Learn about All-on-4® →
