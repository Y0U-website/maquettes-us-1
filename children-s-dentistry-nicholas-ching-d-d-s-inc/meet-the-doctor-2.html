0 Meet the Doctor Nicholas Y. Ching, D.D.S. Born and raised in the Bay Area, Dr. Ching went to the University of California, Los Angeles, where he graduated Summa Cum Laude in Microbiology, Immunology, and Molecular Genetics. He completed his dental education at the University of California, San Francisco. Dr. Ching then completed his specialty training in pediatric dentistry at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where he was also appointed Chief Resident at Boston Children’s Hospital. Dr. Ching is board certified and a diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry!

Dr. Ching’s enthusiasm and passion for oral health extends beyond the Children’s Dentistry office. He has been appointed as an Assistant Clinical Professor at the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry. There, he helps to oversee and mentor predoctoral students. He also serves on the Board of Directors for the Berkeley Dental Society.

In addition to his volunteer activities, Dr. Ching loves spending time with his wonderful wife, Yeun Joo, and his sons, Kentaro and Kojiro. He also enjoys running/hiking, cheering on the Golden State Warriors, watching Disney and Pixar movies, and exploring all the great eats in the Bay Area!
