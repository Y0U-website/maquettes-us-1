Replacing Missing Teeth With Implants

Long Term Tooth Replacement Designed to Restore Function and Confidence

Missing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and feel about your smile. Dental implants offer a long term solution for replacing one or more missing teeth while supporting jawbone health and facial structure. At Creekview Dental, implants are used to restore function and appearance without relying on surrounding teeth for support. A dental implant is made from biocompatible titanium and is placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, the implant supports a crown, bridge, or denture.

What to Expect from Dental Implant Treatment

Healing, Bone Support, and Long Term Stability

Dental implant treatment is typically completed over multiple appointments. After implant placement, the bone gradually integrates with the titanium post during the healing phase. Once healing is complete, the protective cap is removed and a permanent restoration is placed.

Replacing a missing tooth promptly can help prevent jawbone loss and facial collapse. When a tooth is not replaced, the bone in that area may shrink over time. In some cases, bone grafting may be recommended to provide adequate support for an implant. During your consultation, we will evaluate bone health and discuss whether preparatory procedures are necessary.

Dental Implant Treatment Options

Choosing the right dental practice is an important decision. At Creekview Dental, patients receive personalized care in a welcoming environment where comfort, quality, and long term oral health are the priority.

Single Tooth Implants Single tooth implants are used to replace one missing tooth with a natural looking crown supported by a single implant.

Teeth-in-a-Day Implant Teeth-in-a-Day allows patients to receive dental implants and a temporary restoration in a single visit.

All-on-X Implant Designed to replace a full upper or lower arch of teeth using a strategically placed number of implants.

Dental Implant Surgery Dental implant surgery involves placing implant posts into the jawbone during a surgical appointment.

Considering Dental Implants?

Learn if Implant Treatment is Right for You

Dental implants are considered the preferred method for replacing missing teeth, but not every patient is an ideal candidate. Factors such as bone health, overall oral health, and treatment goals play an important role in planning care. A personalized evaluation allows us to recommend the most appropriate tooth replacement option based on your needs and budget.

During your consultation, imaging and a comprehensive exam help determine whether implant treatment is appropriate and whether any preparatory procedures may be needed to support long term success.
