One Hour Crowns

SUPERIOR QUALITY

We prepare the tooth and take an optical impression. A special camera makes a digital image of your tooth. A 3D virtual model is then converted to the computer screen by using the CEREC software. We then design your restoration to produce your customize tooth using state-of-the-art ceramic milling technology. In approximately 30 minutes your tooth is ready!

Root Canals

SAVE YOUR REAL TEETH

Our technology allows us to quickly sterilize and restore teeth that need root canal therapy. We are able to complete your treatment in most cases in half the time than conventional methods.

Dental Implants

LOOK, FEEL AND FUNCTION LIKE NATURAL TEETH

Dental implants are used to replace missing teeth. An implant is placed in the bone during a minor surgical procedure as a root substitute to hold a permanent tooth or denture. They are the standard of care to replace missing teeth because they look, feel and function like natural teeth. We employ state of the art technologies to ensure your dental implants are planned, placed and restored properly.
