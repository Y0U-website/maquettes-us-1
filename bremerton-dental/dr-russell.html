About Dr. Russell

Nathan G. Russell, DDS, MBA Bremerton Dentist

Dr. Russell grew up in the farm country of Southern California and moved to the Seattle area in 1995 after marrying his sweetheart, Brooke. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Washington in Cell and Molecular Biology and Finnish. He also earned his dental degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry. Upon graduating from dental school, Dr. Russell enlisted in the United States Air Force as a medical officer in the Dental Corps.

While in the military, he attended specialty residency training at Keesler Air Force Base Medical Center. This advanced training included: Oral Medicine, Oral Surgery, Endodontics, Prosthodontics, and Pedodontics. He has been a dentist in Bremerton and Bainbridge Island since 2006. Recently, Dr. Russell went back to the University of Washington Foster School of Business to obtain his M.B.A. degree.

Together with his wife and five children, they are proud and involved members of their community. Additionally, Dr. Russell is a veteran and maintains his service in the Air Force as an active reservist at McChord AB (JBLM). He enjoys serving his country and proudly wears the uniform. Dr. Russell looks forward to working with a wonderful staff and meeting with patients.

Dr. Russell Nathan is now the appointed president of the Washington State Dental Association. Read more about it here: https://issuu.com/wsda/docs/wsda_news_issue_3_2023
