Adult braces options

  • Traditional metal braces This is the more traditional orthodontic treatment approach. Metal braces are cost-effective, and they work to straighten teeth and correct similar issues.
  • Clear braces You can smile confidently with clear braces knowing these blend in with your natural teeth and are straightening your smile discreetly.
  • Lingual braces Also known as invisible braces , no one will know you’re wearing these as they sit behind the teeth as opposed to the front of your teeth.
  • Clear aligners Clear aligners , like Invisalign® , give adults a way to straighten their teeth discreetly without changing their lifestyle as the aligners are removable.
  • Self-ligating braces Self-ligating braces use a specialized clip that attaches to the archwire rather than elastics, which can make the teeth straightening process more comfortable than traditional methods.

The difference between adult orthodontics and orthodontics for children and teens:

The main difference between treating adults and children or teens is that the jawbones of younger patients are still developing. For adults, these bones have stopped growing, which may make orthognathic surgery possible to align the jawbones. Other differences include:

  • Gum or bone loss (periodontal disease) Adults are more likely than children to experience gum recession or even bone loss due to gingivitis or advanced periodontal disease. Patients with straighter teeth are less likely to get gum disease.
  • Worn or missing teeth Over time teeth can become worn down and shift into different positions that can only be corrected with orthodontic care. Missing teeth can cause other teeth to shift and tilt, creating a bad bite and increasing the possibility of gum disease.
  • Incomplete orthodontic treatment as a teen Many adults received some orthodontic treatment as a child or teen, but never completed their treatment. As an adult, they choose to complete their orthodontic treatment to achieve the healthy, beautiful smile they always wanted.

Schedule consultation Adult Orthodontics FAQs Considering orthodontic treatment as an adult?

You’re not alone. More adults than ever are choosing to improve their smiles — for confidence, health, or simply because it’s something they’ve always wanted to do. Below are some of the most common questions we hear from adult patients about braces, Invisalign®, and what to expect during treatment.

  • Straight teeth are healthy teeth — Teeth that are properly aligned are easier to keep clean with flossing and brushing, and may help prevent other health problems.
  • A beautiful, straight smile builds confidence — Orthodontic treatment can help boost your self-confidence, giving you a better quality of life and the freedom to smile without holding back!
  • Mouths left untreated can get worse — Not receiving orthodontic treatment when it’s needed can create long-term health issues costing you more in the end.
  • Can adults get braces? Yes. Many adults choose orthodontic treatment to improve both function and appearance. We offer options tailored to adult needs, including clear braces, metal braces, and Invisalign®.
  • How long does adult orthodontic treatment take? The length of treatment depends on your specific case, but most adults finish in about 12 to 24 months with regular checkups and consistent care.
  • Do adult braces cause pain? You may feel mild soreness for a few days after getting braces or adjustments, but modern orthodontic technology makes treatment far more comfortable than in the past.
  • Will braces affect my lifestyle? Not much at all. Most adults continue their normal routines. If you play sports or a musical instrument, we can recommend protective gear or adjustments to make treatment easier.
  • Is clear aligner treatment an option for adults? Yes. Invisalign® and other clear aligners are great for adults who want a discreet, flexible option for straightening their teeth — depending on your case, they may work as well as traditional braces.
