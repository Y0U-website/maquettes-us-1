Every child’s smile develops differently, and early orthodontic evaluations can make a big difference in long-term dental health. At Cordoba Orthodontics, we help parents understand when and why to begin treatment — and what to expect along the way. Below are answers to some of the most common questions about early orthodontic care and Phase One treatment.

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • A hard time chewing or biting food
  • Mouth breathing
  • Finger or thumb sucking
  • Crowded, misplaced, or blocked teeth
  • Jaws that pop or make sounds when opening and closing
  • Teeth that come together abnormally, or do not come together at all
  • Jaws and teeth that are not proportionate to the rest of the face
  • Crowded front teeth around age seven or eight
  • What age should my child see an orthodontist? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an orthodontic check-up by age 7. At Cordoba Orthodontics, early evaluations help us guide jaw growth and detect issues before they become more complex.
  • What is Phase One treatment? Phase One, or early orthodontic treatment, begins while your child still has baby teeth. It focuses on guiding jaw development, making space for permanent teeth, and improving overall bite alignment.
  • Does every child need early orthodontic treatment? No. Some children benefit from early intervention, while others may not need treatment until later. After your child’s initial evaluation, Dr. Cordoba will recommend what’s best for their specific needs.
  • How long does Phase One treatment take? Most Phase One treatments last 12 to 18 months, depending on your child’s growth and treatment goals. Regular monitoring helps ensure the best long-term results.
  • What happens after Phase One treatment? After Phase One, your child enters a resting period to allow permanent teeth to come in naturally. Dr. Cordoba monitors their progress until it’s time to start Phase Two, if needed, for full alignment.
