The teen years are the perfect time for orthodontic treatment — teeth and jaws are still developing, making it easier to achieve great results. At Cordoba Orthodontics, we offer braces, clear aligners, and other options that fit your teen’s lifestyle. Here are answers to questions we often hear from parents and teens.

  • Clear ceramic braces Ceramic braces can discreetly fix many of the same issues as metal braces and are great braces for teens as they blend in with the teeth.
  • Traditional metal braces Metal braces are the most common type of braces and are more comfortable today than ever before. Teens can make braces more fun with colorful rubber bands that show off their style.
  • Lingual braces Lingual braces , also known as “invisible braces cannot be seen when you smile. These behind-the-teeth braces are a great orthodontic treatment option for busy teens.
  • What types of braces are available for teens? We offer traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, lingual braces, and Invisalign Teen. Each option is designed to give effective results with comfort and style in mind.
  • How long will my teen need braces? Treatment time varies depending on the case, but most teens wear braces or aligners for 18 to 24 months. Regular checkups help ensure the best progress and outcome.
  • Can my teen play sports or instruments with braces? Yes. Braces shouldn’t stop your teen from doing what they love. We recommend protective mouthguards for contact sports, and most instrument players adjust easily.
  • What if my teen forgets to wear aligners? Consistency matters with Invisalign Teen. If aligners aren’t worn as directed, treatment can take longer. We help teens stay on track with guidance and check-ins.
  • Is orthodontic treatment just for looks? No — straight teeth are easier to clean and can improve bite function, speech, and long-term dental health, in addition to boosting confidence.
