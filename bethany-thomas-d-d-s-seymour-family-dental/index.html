The practice

At your first appointment, Dr. Thomas will complete a thorough dental examination with x-rays taken by our state of the art, low radiation panoramic x-ray machine. This serves as the foundation for all successive treatment and allows Dr. Thomas to have the time and opportunity to discuss your priorities and interests.

Once the examination has been completed, we can schedule you for treatment and cleanings as needed. Every patient has unique needs; we prefer to treat everyone as an individual. Creating and maintaining your oral health is our primary goal!
