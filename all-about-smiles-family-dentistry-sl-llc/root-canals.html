Root Canals

A root canal is a highly effective dental procedure used to treat infected or damaged teeth and relieve persistent tooth pain. During treatment, the infected tissue inside the tooth is carefully removed, the root canals are cleaned and disinfected, and the tooth is sealed to help prevent future infection. Root canal therapy can often save a natural tooth that might otherwise need to be extracted, helping patients maintain a healthy, functional smile.
