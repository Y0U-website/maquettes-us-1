Our Office

Your next great escape will leave you with a smile on your face!

We know that if you haven’t been to the dentist in several years, you’d probably rather be somewhere else. Like the beach, your back deck, your office. Tahiti.

So we designed our space to be soothing and comfortable. Our furniture, from the waiting room seats to the exam room recliners, is cushy. We chose soothing, spa-like colors for the walls. We found original artwork that complements the open and airy layout of our rooms.

To bring some of that calming "beach feeling" to you, we’ve installed a waterfall in the waiting room. While you won’t be there long - we pride ourselves in staying on schedule - we hope the water helps you to relax and brings pleasant thoughts about the ocean. Or Tahiti.
