OUR MISSION

At 1Smile, we believe that everyone deserves a confident and radiant smile. Our mission is to provide safe, convenient, and effective teeth whitening services that leave our customers secure and proud of their smiles.

A LITTLE HISTORY

Our company, 1Smile , was founded in January 2021 with the goal of providing top-quality teeth whitening services to individuals both in the comfort of their own homes and in a professional office setting.

We understand the importance of a bright, confident smile and strive to make the teeth whitening process easy, convenient, and affordable for everyone. Our experienced Teeth whitening professionals use only the best teeth-whitening products available on the market to ensure our clients receive the best results possible.

In addition to offering in-office teeth whitening services, we also provide a convenient mobile option for those who prefer the comfort of their own home.

Our mobile services are designed to fit into even the busiest of schedules and provide the same high-quality results as our in-office services.

At 1Smile, we are dedicated to providing a personalized, friendly, and professional experience to each and every one of our clients.
