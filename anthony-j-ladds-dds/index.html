The practice

Recognizing that dentistry is constantly evolving, Dr. Dan Haley is devoted to continuing education and dedicated to providing patients with the best possible balance of cutting-edge technology and dependable, modern techniques. Our entire team is passionate about serving your dental needs and looks forward to helping you achieve and maintain a beautiful, healthy smile.

Our service is at the core of everything we do. Our team works together to provide a warm, comfortable, and relaxing environment. Our goal is not only to help you achieve optimal oral health but also to provide you with treatments that are both effective and comfortable.

With advances in science and technology, new materials and techniques are being developed to make treatments more convenient, comfortable, and effective. We offer new services and technologies to provide more biomimetic and conservative ways to provide optimal dental care for every patient.
