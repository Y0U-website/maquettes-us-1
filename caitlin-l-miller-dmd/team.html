Scott Z. Pugh

DMD

Dr. Scott Pugh is from the mountains of North Carolina near the town of Sparta. He completed his undergraduate degree at UNC-Chapel Hill with a Bachelor of Science in Biology and a Minor in Chemistry in 2009.

He spent 4 years working at DSM Pharmaceuticals in Greenville, NC as a Quality Control Lab Analyst. Dr. Pugh completed his graduate degree at East Carolina University with a Doctor of Dental Medicine, DMD. His hobbies include fishing, golfing, and traveling. He is a member of the American Dental Association, North Carolina Dental Society, and Davidson County Dental Society.
