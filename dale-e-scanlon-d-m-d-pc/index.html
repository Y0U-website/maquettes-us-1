The practice

The Dental Office of Dale E. Scanlon, D.M.D. is conveniently located in the Oaklands Corporate Center in Exton, PA specializing in comprehensive dentistry for the entire family. Our practice places a premium on excellent service, quality care and patient convenience.

We are committed to delivering quality dental care services to all our patients. Get in touch with us today!

Dr. Scanlon attended Wilkes University in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania as an undergraduate. After graduating in 1983 with a Bachelor of Art degree in Physics,

he attended Temple University School of Dental Medicine in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and graduated in 1987 with a degree in Dental Medicine. In 2000, Dr. Scanlon completed additional studies at the University

of Pennsylvania and earned a Bachelor of Arts degree, cum laude.

He became the director of the Dental Care Center at University City located at 34th and Market Streets in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Dr. Scanlon has been on

the faculty at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine.

In January 2005, Dr. Scanlon opened his office in the Oaklands Corporate Center in Exton, Pennsylvania.
