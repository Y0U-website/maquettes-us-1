What the dental implant process involves

The consultation

Dr. Chin reviews bone volume and gum health using in-office CT and panoramic imaging, compares an implant honestly against your other options, and gives you a full estimate and timeline before anything is planned.

Placement

The post goes in under local anesthesia, with nitrous oxide or oral sedation available if you want it.

Integration

Over 3 to 6 months, the bone fuses to the titanium. You go about your life.

The crown

Once integration is confirmed, an iTero scan captures the site and your custom porcelain crown is fabricated and fitted.

The follow-through

Implants do not decay, but the gum and bone around them still need watching. That happens at your regular hygiene visits.

Placed and restored by one dentist Dr. Chin completed treatment planning and functional occlusion training at the Kois Center, which is what allows him to plan an implant around your whole bite instead of one empty socket.

When a case calls for a specialist, like significant bone grafting, he’ll refer you to someone he trusts and stays involved in your care.
