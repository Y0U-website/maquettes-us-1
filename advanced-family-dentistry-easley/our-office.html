Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Theresa Trapp, DDS

Dr. Trapp received her Bachelor of Science from the University of Illinois in 1989. She received her dental degree in 1993 from Indiana University School of Dentistry. She has a General and Hospital Dentistry certificate from Mount Sinai Hospital Medical Center in Chicago, IL.

She is a member of the American Dental Association , South Carolina Dental Association , Academy of General Dentistry , and the Piedmont Dental Society . She has been practicing in Pickens County since July 1994.

Dr. Trapp and her husband, Dr. Peter Schriver, have two children and reside in Pickens.
