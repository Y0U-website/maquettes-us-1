Common Orthodontic Issues We Treat

Our team provides comprehensive orthodontic dental care for a variety of alignment and bite issues, including:

  • Crowding: Crowded teeth occur when there is not enough room in the jaw for all teeth to fit properly. This can cause teeth to overlap, twist, or become difficult to clean.
  • Spacing: Spacing issues may occur due to missing teeth, jaw development concerns, or naturally smaller teeth. Orthodontic treatment can help close gaps and create a more balanced smile.
  • Overbites: An overbite occurs when the upper front teeth extend too far over the lower front teeth. Severe overbites can contribute to tooth wear, jaw discomfort, and difficulty biting or chewing properly.
  • Underbites: An underbite happens when the lower teeth protrude beyond the upper teeth. This condition can affect speech, chewing, and facial appearance.
  • Crossbites: A crossbite occurs when some upper teeth sit behind the lower teeth rather than in front of them. Left untreated, crossbites can lead to uneven tooth wear, gum recession, and jaw alignment issues.

Our Orthodontic Treatment Options

At Center City Emergency Dentist, we understand that every patient has different goals and needs. That is why we offer personalized treatment recommendations designed to achieve the best possible results.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces remain one of the most effective methods for correcting a wide range of orthodontic concerns. Using brackets and wires, braces gradually move teeth into their ideal positions over time. Today’s braces are more comfortable and efficient than ever, making them an excellent option for both teenagers and adults.

Clear Aligners

For patients seeking a more discreet alternative to braces, clear aligners from Invisalign are an excellent solution. These custom-made trays gently shift teeth into alignment while remaining virtually invisible. Because they are removable, clear aligners also make eating, brushing, and flossing more convenient.

Emergency Orthodontic Care in Philadelphia

Has a bracket broken free from your teeth, or is a loose wire poking the inside of your cheek? Our office provides emergency orthodontics to help patients address urgent issues quickly and effectively.

  • Broken Brackets: Brackets can become loose or break due to hard foods, accidents, or normal wear. A broken bracket may affect treatment progress and should be repaired as soon as possible.
  • Loose or Poking Wires: Orthodontic wires can shift or become loose, causing irritation to the cheeks, lips, or gums. If a wire is causing discomfort, an orthodontist can provide adjustments to restore comfort and keep treatment on track.
  • Damaged Orthodontic Appliances: Retainers, aligners, and other orthodontic appliances can become damaged or lost. Prompt replacement helps maintain treatment progress and prevents setbacks.
  • Orthodontic Discomfort: Some discomfort is normal during orthodontic treatment, particularly after adjustments. However, severe pain or unusual symptoms may require professional evaluation.

When to Seek Immediate Care

You should contact an emergency orthodontist in Philadelphia if you experience significant pain, damaged appliances, severe irritation, or any issue that interferes with your ability to eat, speak, or maintain treatment. Prompt care helps prevent complications and ensures successful treatment outcomes.
