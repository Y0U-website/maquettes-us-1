Pediatric Dental Treatments

Finding a pediatric dentist you can trust is an important part of helping your child build healthy habits and a positive relationship with dental care. At Eastside Pediatric Dental , we’re proud to provide comprehensive pediatric dental treatments for infants, children, teens, and individuals with special needs throughout Fairport, Rochester, Pittsford, Penfield, Victor, and the surrounding communities.

Whether your child is due for a routine dental cleaning or needs restorative treatment, our experienced team is here to provide personalized care in a welcoming, kid-friendly environment. Every treatment plan is tailored to your child’s age, oral health needs, and comfort level, helping families feel confident every step of the way.

Comprehensive Pediatric Dental Services

We offer a full range of pediatric dental services designed to protect growing smiles, restore damaged teeth, and support healthy oral development. Explore our treatment categories below to learn more about the care we provide.

Preventive Pediatric Dentistry

Keep your child’s smile healthy with routine cleanings, dental exams, and follow-up visits.

Restorative & Cosmetic Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings and esthetic restorations designed to protect growing smiles.

Advanced Restorative & Pulp Therapy

Specialized treatments that help preserve primary teeth and support healthy oral development.

Tooth Extractions & Space Maintenance

Gentle extractions and space maintainers that support proper dental development.

Specialized Pediatric Dentistry

Personalized dental care for children with unique healthcare and developmental needs.

Compassionate Care Designed Around Your Child

We know a visit to the dentist can feel overwhelming for some children. That’s why we’ve created an office where kids can feel comfortable from the moment they walk through our doors. Our friendly team takes the time to explain each step, answer questions, and help every patient feel relaxed. For your child’s first visit, we’ll walk you through what to expect and help create a positive experience from the start.

From preventive dental care to more advanced pediatric dental treatments, we use modern technology and gentle techniques to make appointments as comfortable as possible. We also offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for children who may benefit from additional relaxation during treatment.

Our goal is simple: help every child enjoy a positive dental experience while building healthy habits that last a lifetime.

Serving Families Throughout Fairport and Greater Rochester

Eastside Pediatric Dental is proud to care for children and families throughout Fairport, Rochester, Penfield, Pittsford, Victor, Perinton, Brighton, and nearby Monroe County communities. Whether you’re bringing your child in for a routine dental exam or restorative treatment, you’ll receive personalized care from a team that’s committed to your family’s oral health.
