What is Pinhole Gum Rejuvenation®?

Dr. John Chao, DDS invented the Pinhole Surgical Technique® — a revolutionary, minimally invasive treatment for gum recession now adopted by over 4,000 dentists worldwide.

The practice

Pinhole Gum Rejuvenation, also know as The Pinhole Surgical Technique® (PST®), invented and patented by Dr. John Chao, is a scalpel-free, suture-free procedure for treating gum recession. Using a tiny pinhole made with a needle, specially designed instruments gently reposition the gum tissue over the receded area — no cutting, no grafting, no stitches.

Most patients experience minimal discomfort and see immediate cosmetic improvement. Multiple teeth can be treated in a single visit.

Gum disease is the most common cause of tooth loss in adults. If you have been told your teeth are "hopeless" or that you need surgery, Dr. Chao offers alternatives that can save your teeth without painful procedures.
