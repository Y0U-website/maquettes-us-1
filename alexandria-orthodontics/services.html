Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMD)

At Alexandria Orthodontics, we understand that jaw discomfort, tension headaches, muscle pain and difficulty chewing can be signs of a bigger issue — Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMD) . TMD affects the joint that connects your jaw to your skull, often leading to discomfort, pain, and functional issues that impact your daily life.

We provide a comprehensive, non-surgical approach designed to treat the root cause of your symptoms, not just mask them.

Lasting relief is most often achieved through a combination of the following treatments:

Gentle, targeted sound wave technology is used to relax the jaw muscles, reduce inflammation, and increase circulation — all without pain or downtime.

Custom-made oral appliances (also called splints or nightguards) help reposition the jaw, reduce clenching or grinding, and relieve pressure on the TMJ.

Misaligned teeth and bite issues often contribute to TMD. Through precise orthodontic treatment, we can improve jaw alignment and reduce long-term strain on the joint.

Most patients experience the greatest relief when all three steps are combined as part of a customized treatment plan. Each phase works together to reduce pain, restore function, and protect your jaw joint over time.

Advanced Self-Ligating Braces for Faster, Smoother Results

Our braces represent the latest evolution in orthodontic treatment. Unlike traditional braces that rely on elastic ties to hold the wire in place, we use a self-ligating system, which means the brackets have a built-in mechanism to hold the wire. This allows for greater control, smoother movement, and less friction, helping teeth shift more efficiently and comfortably.

The bracket system is designed with low-friction technology that enables lighter forces, which can mean shorter treatment times, fewer appointments, and greater patient comfort compared to traditional systems. The brackets also provide 3D control earlier in treatment, which enhances precision and improves smile aesthetics from the very beginning.

In addition to excellent performance, our brackets and systems are known for their ability to create a beautiful smile arc and fuller smile, aligning with the natural contour of your lips. The system is engineered for better facial balance, improved bite alignment, and optimized smile aesthetics.

3D Intraoral Scanner Technology

Advances in orthodontic technology have allowed us to more precisely diagnose and treat our patients. We have committed ourselves to staying in a leadership position in all aspects of orthodontics…allowing us to better serve you and your family.

Part of this state-of-the-art technology at Alexandria Orthodontics is our scanning technology, which does away with having to take impressions of your teeth. Clear Aligners

Just like braces, you will need to complete an initial consultation at our office so that Dr. Heather can evaluate your smile and overall oral health to ensure that aligners are a good treatment option for you! At this consultation, we will take “before” photos and records, as well as a digital impression of your teeth that will allow us to create the custom aligners.

About every eight weeks or so, as scheduled by Dr. Heather, you’ll pick up a new set of aligners to keep moving your teeth. During this time, she will monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your aligners to make sure that they are working as efficiently and effectively as possible.

Aligner treatment can typically be completed in an average of 12-18 months. However, much like any form of orthodontic treatment, the treatment time will vary based on each person’s needs, and how long the aligners are worn each day. Optimal results occur when the aligners are worn for 20-22 hours per day, and are generally removed only for eating and during regular brushing and flossing. During your consultation, Dr. Heather will provide you with an estimate for the length of your treatment time.
