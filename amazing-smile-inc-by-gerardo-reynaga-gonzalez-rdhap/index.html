You or your loved one’s oral and dental health is an important part of overall health and wellbeing. For the elderly or disabled, it can become challenging to get to the dentist. Seniors or disabled individuals may not be able to go to the dental office due to issues with mobility, transportation, and time.

Amazing Smile provides mobile dental hygiene services to patients who have difficulties accessing a brick-and-mortar dental office. We bring our mobile dental hygiene practice to homebound patients or people in need of at-home dental hygiene care.

Amazing Smile provides onsite mobile dental hygiene services in San Francisco, Marin, Contra Costa, Alameda, and San Mateo county.

We provide routine cleanings, deep cleanings, maintenance cleanings and more. Our services are provided gently, respectfully and with integrity for each and every one of older adult and special needs populations. Our services include:

New Patient Experience On your first visit we will conduct a health history assessment, intra-oral examination, oral cancer screening and more.

Regular Dental Cleaning We provide dental cleaning above the gums performed on a healthy periodontium with removal of extrinsic stains, polish, and floss.
