Dr. Ash Estafan – Expert Prosthodontist in Port Washington, NY

Dr. Ash Estafan, a highly skilled prosthodontist in Port Washington, NY , earned his DDS degree from NYU College of Dentistry . After graduation, he completed a General Practice Residency at the Department of Veterans Affairs Hospital in Manhattan, NY , where he gained advanced clinical experience. In 2001, Dr. Estafan joined Dr. Denise Estafan’s dental practice in Port Washington, NY , providing top-quality dental care to the local community.

Following his general residency, Dr. Estafan pursued specialized training in Prosthodontics at NYU , completing an intensive three-year program focused on dental crowns, porcelain veneers, dental implants, bridges, and complex restorative treatments . As a Board-Certified Prosthodontist , he has dedicated his career to delivering high-quality cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

Dr. Estafan is also a respected educator, having lectured at NYU College of Dentistry , the American College of Prosthodontists , the Greater New York Academy of Prosthodontists , and the International Association of Dental Research . He continues to teach NYU’s DDS students several times per year.

Passionate about modern dental technology, Dr. Estafan integrates digital X-rays, laser dentistry, optical scanning, and digital photography into his practice to enhance patient care. A proud Port Washington native , he graduated from Schreiber High School before earning his B.A. from Connecticut College in New London, CT .

For expert cosmetic and restorative dentistry in Port Washington, NY , Dr. Ash Estafan offers personalized, high-quality dental care. Contact our office today to schedule an appointment!
