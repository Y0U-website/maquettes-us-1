Dr Nick Baylon

Dr. Nick is a board certified pediatric dentist who grew up in the Stillwater area with his parents Drs. Richard and Christine Baylon. He received his dental degree from the University of Minnesota in 2010. He practiced as a general dentist in a community clinic for several years and then completed a one-year general practice residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in The Bronx, NY. Dr. Nick completed a two-year pediatric dentistry residency program at the University of Minnesota in 2015 and received his diplomate status with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2016.

He married Dr. Teo Baylon in 2013. Dr. Nick and Dr. Teo enjoy spending time with their young daughter.
