Undiagnosed headaches? Ear pain, neck, shoulder and back pain? Dizziness? Temporomandibular joint ( TMJ ) disorders and craniofacial problems are difficult to diagnose because the symptoms can mimic dozens of other disorders. The pain you are experiencing is very real. In fact, over 10 million people in the U.S. suffer from TMJ disorders.

Les soins

Many factors can lead to or aggravate a TMJ disorder. Trauma (such as a car accident, fall or blow), muscular imbalances, malocclusions, clenching or grinding your teeth, inherited facial structure or stress can all be factors in your pain.
