Healthy gums are the foundation of a lasting smile. Our periodontal treatments address gum disease and inflammation, helping to prevent tooth loss and maintain overall oral health. We also offer deep cleanings and maintenance programs for ongoing care.

Whether you’re a teen looking to align your bite or an adult ready to perfect your smile, our orthodontic services offer modern solutions for straighter teeth. We provide traditional braces as well as discreet alignment options to suit your lifestyle and comfort—helping you achieve a confident, balanced smile.

We remove plaque, tartar, and bacteria from deep periodontal pockets, helping to stop the progression of gum disease and restore a healthier foundation for your teeth. By smoothing the tooth roots, this procedure encourages the gums to reattach and heal properly, reducing inflammation, bleeding, and sensitivity over time.

Regular cleanings are the foundation of a healthy smile. The Dr. provides gentle yet thorough cleanings to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains—keeping your gums healthy and your teeth bright. Preventive care visits also include a detailed exam to catch early signs of decay or gum disease before they become bigger issues.

Crowns and bridges restore damaged or missing teeth by covering or replacing them with strong, natural-looking restorations. They improve function, protect your teeth, and bring back a complete, confident smile.

Les soins

Dental fillings repair cavities by removing decay and restoring the tooth with a durable material. They protect against further damage while bringing back normal function and a natural look.

Dentures are custom-made removable replacements for missing teeth that restore your smile and ability to eat and speak comfortably. They’re designed to look natural while providing support for facial structure and everyday function.

ClearCorrect braces are clear, removable aligners that gradually straighten teeth without metal wires or brackets. They’re comfortable, discreet, and fit easily into your daily routine.
