The practice

We pride ourselves on utilizing cutting-edge dental technologies to offer precise and effective treatment plans. Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art digital X-rays, advanced digital scanners, and intraoral cameras. These tools enable us to gain an in-depth understanding of your oral health, allowing for accurate diagnosis and tailored treatment plans.

Our dedication to technology extends to treatments like our same-day dental crowns. Thanks to our CEREC® system, we can complete your crown in a single appointment, eliminating the need for multiple visits. This is just one of the many ways we streamline your dental experience, respecting your time and comfort.

At 19th Street Dental, our team is led by dentists with advanced training from Boston University, the University of Michigan, and the Dental College of Georgia. We hold memberships with the AACD, AGD, AAID, and Georgia Dental Association, and we’re committed to lifelong learning, regularly pursuing continuing education to stay ahead of the latest advancements in dentistry.
