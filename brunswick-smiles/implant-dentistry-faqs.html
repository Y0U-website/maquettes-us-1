How can I replace a missing tooth?

Dental bridges, dentures, and tooth implants are all good options for tooth replacement in modern dentistry. When you come in for an evaluation, we can help you figure out which choice is best for your needs.

Two crowns are used to permanently secure one or more replacement teeth in your mouth with a dental bridge. Because a bridge is constructed of ceramic porcelain, it blends in with your smile. It’s also a stationary piece of equipment that doesn’t move when you talk or eat.

Multiple lost teeth, or even an entire arch, can be restored with a full or partial denture. Complete dentures are generally detachable and designed to replace all of your teeth, whereas partial dentures are affixed to artificial teeth that fill in the gaps between your natural teeth.

A dental implant replaces both the root and the crown of your tooth, restoring both your functional capacity and your attractive smile. Dental implants keep your jawbone stimulated, which protects your dental health. Choosing a dental implant is virtually the same as obtaining a new tooth because it will look, function, and feel the same as your original tooth. Dental implants can be used to support bridges, partials, and dentures, which can be utilized to replace numerous lost teeth.

Because there is no movement or sliding while you talk or eat, tooth implants provide denture users with more stability and support than they have ever had before.

How long do dental implants last?

One of the most significant benefits of using dental implants to rebuild your smile is their long-term durability. With proper maintenance, dental implants can survive for many years, even a lifetime in some cases.

Even though your porcelain implant crown will never get cavities, everyday brushing and flossing are still essential for maintaining the health of your implant. Plaque and bacteria build-up can irritate your gums and lead to gum disease if you don’t brush and floss regularly. Dental implants are vulnerable to infection, which can lead to bone loss and implant failure.

A good home care routine helps safeguard your dental implant and prevents complications like tooth infection. Your implant will remain healthy if you have regular examinations every six months, as your dentist will monitor it with x-rays.

Your tooth implant can last a lifetime if you follow a consistent home care routine and visit your dentist for preventive exams and teeth cleanings.

Which is better Dental implants or dental bridge?

Dental implants are a great way to replace one or more lost teeth, but they aren’t right for everyone.

You’ll need enough healthy bone structure to support a dental implant, which can be diminished if you’ve had extensive gum disease or worn a denture for a long time. A tooth implant may not be possible for some people due to insufficient healthy bone.

Dental implants also necessitate a surgical procedure to insert the implant into your jawbone, which some patients may not want or be able to tolerate.

Furthermore, the expense of a dental implant may be expensive for some people.

A dental bridge could be a possibility for replacing a tooth and restoring your smile in any of these situations. Scheduling an evaluation with us is the best way to find the best option for you.

Why are dental implants so expensive?

Dental implants are the best option for tooth replacement because they rebuild your teeth down to the root, giving you the closest alternative to your natural tooth.

The procedure necessitates implantation surgery, which is usually performed by a specialist or oral surgeon. After that, you’ll have to wait a few months for your restoration, which could be a crown for a single tooth or a bridge or denture for many lost teeth.

The cost of the treatment process includes implant placement surgery and restoration, and it varies based on the circumstances of each patient.

While the cost of one or more dental implants may be more than other tooth-replacement choices, it’s vital to consider all of the benefits they provide, including:

They look, feel, and function like a natural tooth

They protect your jawbone against the deterioration that occurs with the loss of a tooth root

They offer excellent longevity over other replacement options

Dental implants may be a more cost-effective alternative for some patients because they can last a lifetime with proper home care and periodic dental exams.
