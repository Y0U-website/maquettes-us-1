Meet Your Doctor

CHRISTOPHER H. MARTONE, D.M.D., P.C. IN NATRONA HEIGHTS, PA

Dr. Martone is board certified with the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and is a member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology. Dr. Martone has also been on staff at UPMC-St. Margaret’s Hospital for over 20 years. A native of Aspinwall, PA., Dr. Martone spends much of leisure time with his family and watching sporting events.

I always believed that I had innate abilities needed to be successful in this field. I enjoy helping my patients achieve improved dental health. I am a gentle person with a calming manner. I treat every patient with dignity and respect. My goal is to provide each person who seeks my help with a positive dental experience. ~ Dr. Martone
