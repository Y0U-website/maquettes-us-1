Repairing the Health of Your Gums

Periodontal disease, also known as gum disease, develops in your teeth when plaque and tartar build-up along and under the gum line. At Access Dental Group, our team will diagnose if you are experiencing the effects of periodontal disease. If this is the case, we’ll help to repair any damage that has already occurred.

Multiple Stages of Gum Disease

Gum disease starts with gingivitis and then progresses to more serious periodontal disease. If plaque is not correctly removed from your teeth, it hardens and becomes tartar. At this stage, the tartar releases toxins that cause inflammation within your gums and can destroy their attachment to your tooth.

Advanced stages of gum disease can happen when there is bone loss due to tartar and its toxins. If you are noticing any of these symptoms, schedule an appointment at Access Dental Group to diagnose and offer treatment. The answer can be anything from a simple cleaning to periodontal surgery.
