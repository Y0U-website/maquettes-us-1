What are Implant Supported Restorations?

Implant supported restorations are revolutionary advancements in restorative dentistry that involve dental prosthetics such as crowns, bridges, or dentures anchored securely to dental implants titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone acting as artificial tooth roots. This approach offers a permanent and natural-feeling replacement for lost teeth or compromised dental structures, vastly improving chewing ability, speech, and facial aesthetics while preserving the health of your jawbone.

  • Implant-Supported Dentures: These dentures are anchored firmly to implants, preventing the slipping or discomfort common with traditional dentures .

Are You a Candidate for Implant Supported Restorations?

Understanding if you’re a suitable candidate for implant supported restorations is crucial for achieving the best dental outcomes. Here are the essential factors that will influence your candidacy:

  • Overall Health Status: Maintaining good overall health, free from uncontrolled medical conditions like diabetes, is vital for healing and recovery after the surgical procedure.
  • Jawbone Density: Adequate bone density in the jaw is necessary to support the dental implant. Bone grafting might be required for those with bone loss.
  • Gum Health: Healthy gums are essential; any signs of gum disease may need treatment before considering implants.
  • Lifestyle Choices: Avoiding tobacco use and maintaining a healthy diet can significantly enhance healing and integration of the implant.
  • Commitment to Oral Hygiene: An ability to maintain excellent oral hygiene practices post-procedure is critical for the longevity of the restoration.

Comparing Implant Supported Dentures with Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can slip, causing discomfort and limiting food choices. On the other hand, implant supported dentures anchor directly to implants embedded in your jawbone, providing unmatched stability. This security helps preserve jawbone health and allows you to enjoy a more varied diet and clearer speech, greatly enhancing your quality of life.
