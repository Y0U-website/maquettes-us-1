What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root made of titanium, which serves as a sturdy foundation for a replacement tooth, such as a crown. Implants not only restore the appearance of your smile but also help preserve your oral health by preventing bone loss and maintaining the integrity of your jaw.

Types of Dental Implants

Dental implants present a versatile solution, addressing diverse needs from replacing a solitary tooth to supporting a complete arch of teeth.

Single tooth implants

For those missing one or more teeth, single tooth implants make for an optimal choice. They involve the placement of a dental implant and a crown to replace a missing tooth, with the implant acting as a substitute for the natural tooth root and the crown substituting the visible portion of the tooth.

Implant-supported bridges

Implant-supported dentures are a game-changer for individuals who require a full arch of teeth replacement. These removable dentures are secured to two or more implants, offering a fixed or removable solution that replicates the appearance and feel of natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

The advantages offered by dental implants renders them a compelling choice for individuals aiming to boost their oral health and aesthetics. These benefits include:

  • Natural Appearance and Functionality : Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, providing a seamless and comfortable fit.
  • Improved Oral Health : Unlike bridges, dental implants do not require the alteration of adjacent teeth, preserving overall oral health and integrity.
  • Long-lasting Solution : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, offering a durable and cost-effective tooth replacement option.
  • Enhanced Confidence : Dental implants restore a confident smile, boosting self-esteem and allowing you to engage in social activities without worry.
  • Improved Eating Experience : Dental implants allow for normal eating habits, enabling you to enjoy a wide variety of foods without restrictions.

Healing and osseointegration

Healing and osseointegration is the process of the jawbone growing around the implant post, creating a strong foundation for the artificial tooth. This process is vital for the long-term success of the dental implant, as it ensures the implant is securely anchored within the jawbone.

The healing and osseointegration period can span a few months, during which maintaining good oral hygiene and adhering to your dental provider’s guidance becomes crucial for successful implant integration.

Abutment and crown placement

Following sufficient healing of the jawbone, the concluding stage of the dental implant procedure entails affixing a connector, or abutment, to the implant post and situating a custom-made crown on top. The crown is designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental implants can be a lengthy process, as there needs to be enough bone mass in your jaw for the implantation and if not, a bone graft surgery must take place first. Therefore, one of the downsides of dental implants is the time involved in the healing process.

Dental implants typically last for 10-30 years, with the implant itself possibly lasting a lifetime with proper care. The crown of the implant is more prone to wear and tear and will need replacing in 10-15 years.

Dental implants are constructed with titanium, a material that is compatible with the jawbone for strong and long-lasting dental solutions.
