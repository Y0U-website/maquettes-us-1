What Is Facial Development in Dentistry?

Facial development involves guiding the growth of bones and soft tissues in the face to achieve balance, symmetry, and improved function. At Cohen Dentistry, we use oral appliances designed to increase the transverse width of the maxilla through palatal expansion and 3D-printed spring signals . These methods encourage natural bone growth, which can positively impact facial aesthetics, breathing, and overall health.
