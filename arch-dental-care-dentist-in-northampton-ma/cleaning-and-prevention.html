Periodontal Therapy

What is Periodontal Therapy and Why is it Needed?

Gum disease is a severe oral health problem; if left untreated, it can lead to tooth loss and other serious health problems. At Arch Dental Care in Northampton, MA, our team of experienced dental professionals offers Periodontal Therapy as part of our comprehensive dental services.

Why Periodontal Therapy is Important

Gum disease is caused by the buildup of plaque and tartar, which can irritate and inflame the gums. If left untreated, gum disease can progress to a more advanced stage, known as periodontitis, which can cause severe damage to the supporting structures of the teeth, leading to tooth loss. Periodontal Therapy is a treatment designed to address the underlying causes of gum disease and restore oral health.
