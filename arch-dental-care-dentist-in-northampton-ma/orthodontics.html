Invisalign

What is the process for getting Invisalign at Arch Dental Care in Northampton, MA?

We understand that traditional metal braces may not fit everyone best. That’s why we offer Invisalign, the clear alternative to metal braces. Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to gently and effectively straighten your teeth. The aligners are virtually invisible, so you can straighten your teeth without anyone knowing.

Invisalign is not only an excellent option for those who are self-conscious about the appearance of metal braces, but it is also a comfortable option for those who have had negative experiences with them in the past. The aligners are smooth and do not have any metal brackets or wires that can irritate your cheeks, lips, or gums. They are also removable, so you can eat and drink what you want without worrying about food getting stuck in your braces.

Another advantage of Invisalign is that you will have fewer office visits compared to traditional metal braces. With Invisalign, you will receive a new set of aligners approximately every two weeks, and your progress will be monitored through regular check-ups with your orthodontist.

At Arch Dental Care, we are proud to offer Invisalign to our patients. Our orthodontist is a certified Invisalign provider with years of experience helping patients achieve the straight, beautiful smile they’ve always wanted. Our staff is dedicated to ensuring you have the best possible experience with Invisalign from start to finish.
