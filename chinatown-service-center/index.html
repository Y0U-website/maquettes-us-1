The practice

WHO WE ARE We started off as a small group of community volunteers who recognized the need to address the problems of Chinese immigrants. In 1971, we became an established nonprofit organization.

WHAT WE DO We assist over 30,000 unique clients each year through 150,000 touch points, providing services such as healthcare, financial planning, and even care for the youth! We strive to help our community thrive and live a better quality of life.

WHERE WE WORK Our main office resides in the Alpine Tower located in Los Angeles, California. On top of our main office, we currently have six different locations, each serving multiple purposes. Come visit us!
