Our team is dedicated to providing personalized, exceptional care for every patient. We build trust-based relationships, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood. THE DOCTOR

Meet The Doctor

James Oh , DDS, MICOI

Dr. Oh, a Loma Linda University graduate, combines his passion for science and the arts in dentistry. With experience owning practices in Carmel, Salinas, and Monterey, he specializes in minimally invasive treatments and uses advanced technologies like CAD/CAM. Dr. Oh has extensive post-graduate training and is active in several dental organizations.

In his free time, he enjoys golf, tennis, skiing, playing piano, and composing music. He is married to Sandy and has two daughters, Jessica and Tiffany.
