Our Team

Dr. Jason Berry Dr. Jason Berry has proudly provided exceptional dental care to the Hood County community for nearly 30 years. With a focus on restorative and implant dentistry, he is committed to helping patients restore both the function and appearance of their smiles through personalized, comprehensive care.

Dr. Berry earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Abilene Christian University before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Baylor College of Dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Berry enjoys spending time with his four children. In his free time, you’ll often find him on the golf course or working on his ranch.

Dr. Elizabeth Berry

Dr. Elizabeth Berry graduated Cum Laude from Texas A&M University in 1994 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with honors from Baylor College of Dentistry in 1998.

After gaining valuable experience as an associate, Dr. Berry and her husband, Dr. Jason Berry, established their own private practice in Granbury, Texas. Since then, she has dedicated herself to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care to the community.

In 2009, Dr. Berry earned her Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD), a distinction held by less than 6 % of dentists in the U.S. and Canada. This prestigious honor required more than 500 hours of continuing education and passing a comprehensive exam covering all dental specialties, including implant dentistry.

Dr. Berry has also completed advanced cosmetic and restorative training at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. Over the past decade, she has expanded her expertise by becoming certified in laser dentistry and is an active member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry. She is also a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and is currently pursuing her fellowship with the organization.

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Berry is passionate about service and education. She has participated in dental mission trips to Brazil and Mexico and regularly volunteers at local outreach clinics. As adjunct faculty at Texas A&M School of Dentistry, she mentors dental students during their rotations at Hope Clinic, a nonprofit dental clinic in Cleburne, Texas.

At home, Dr. Berry and her husband have raised four children—two sons and twin daughters—who have brought endless joy and adventure to their lives. Her oldest son, Seaton, is now in dental school and preparing to join the Berry family legacy in dentistry. Dr. Berry enjoys spending time with her family, staying active, and contributing to her church and community.

Dr. Adrienne Montgomery Dr. Adrienne Montgomery has proudly served the Hood County community for the past eight years. She is especially passionate about caring for patients who experience dental anxiety, taking the time to build trust and help them feel comfortable and confident throughout their dental visits.

Dr. Montgomery provides comprehensive dental care, including crowns, fillings, root canal therapy, extractions, and sedation dentistry. She has also completed extensive training in laser dentistry, allowing her to offer advanced treatment options that promote precision, comfort, and improved clinical outcomes.

A Texas native, Dr. Montgomery earned her Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology from Texas Christian University before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas School of Dentistry at Houston.

Outside the office, Dr. Montgomery enjoys spending time with her husband and their young daughter. She recently relocated from Cleburne to Pecan Plantation and is excited to call Granbury home while continuing to care for the community she has proudly served for years.
