Preventative Dentistry in Granbury

  • Cavity Prevention: Plaque, a sticky film of bacteria, is the primary cause of cavities. During preventative visits, our dental hygienists remove plaque from hard-to-reach areas that daily brushing and flossing may miss.
  • Early Detection of Issues: Preventative dentistry in Grandbury allows dentists to detect dental problems in their earliest stages. This includes identifying cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, and other potential concerns before they cause significant damage.
  • Better Overall Health: Oral health is strongly connected to overall health. Poor oral hygiene can lead to conditions such as heart disease, diabetes, and even pregnancy complications. By maintaining regular preventative dental care, you’re also supporting your general health.

Preventative Dentistry Services at Berry & Berry Dental Associates

We offer a comprehensive range of preventative dentistry in Granbury to meet the needs of every family member. Whether you’re a parent looking to protect your child’s teeth or an adult seeking to maintain a healthy smile, we have the services you need.

Routine dental exams in Granbury are the cornerstone of preventative care. During your exam, Dr. Jason Berry, Dr. Elizabeth Berry, or Dr. Adrienne Montgomery will thoroughly inspect your teeth and gums for signs of decay, gum disease, and other oral health issues. We’ll also perform oral cancer screenings and take digital X-rays if necessary to closely examine areas below the surface.

Even with excellent at-home care, it’s impossible to remove all plaque and tartar from your teeth. Professional cleanings in Granbury, performed by our skilled dental hygienists, help to eliminate buildup and keep your teeth and gums healthy. These cleanings also polish your teeth, leaving them smooth and bright.

Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars and premolars. These teeth have deep grooves that can trap food particles and bacteria, making them more susceptible to cavities. Sealants act as a barrier, preventing decay and making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene.

At Berry & Berry Dental Associates in Granbury , education is a key component of preventative dentistry. We take the time to teach our patients the best practices for brushing, flossing, and caring for their teeth at home. By providing personalized advice and tips, we empower our patients to take control of their oral health between visits.

Early detection of oral cancer can significantly improve treatment outcomes. During your exam, our team will perform a non-invasive oral cancer screening to look for any abnormalities in the mouth, gums, or throat.
