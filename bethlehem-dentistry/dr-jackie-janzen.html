Dr. Jackie Janzen, DMD

Associate Dentist

Dr. Jackie Janzen was born and raised in Suwanee, Georgia, her path to professional excellence began at Collins Hill High School, where she cultivated her passion for achievement. She went on to earn her bachelor’s degree from Florida Southern College, excelling both academically and as a standout member of the women’s golf team, demonstrating discipline, drive, and precision—qualities she brings to her dental practice today.

Beyond the office, Dr. Janzen is deeply rooted in her community and personal life. She resides in Duluth, Georgia, with her husband, Bruce, embracing an active and fulfilling lifestyle. She enjoys golfing, running, working out, and spending quality time with friends and family, finding balance and inspiration in the relationships and activities that fuel her passion for life and dentistry.
