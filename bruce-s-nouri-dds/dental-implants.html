How the Process Works

Before surgery begins, a dentist must review your medical history and provide a thorough exam. From there, they will design a treatment plan focused on your needs.

This treatment plan may include the following steps:

As you have to wait for your bone to grow and heal, the entire process can take several months. Bone grafting can also extend the treatment timeline. Bone grafting is when a dentist strengthens the jawbone to better support the implant.

  • Removing the damaged tooth
  • Bone grafting (if needed)
  • Placing the dental implant
  • Waiting for the bone to grow and heal
  • Placing the abutment
  • Securing the artificial tooth
