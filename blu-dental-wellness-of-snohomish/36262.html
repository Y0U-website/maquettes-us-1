Benefits of All-on-4 Implants

All-on-4 and dental implants , in general, offer numerous benefits, making them a popular choice for tooth replacement:

  • Improved chewing function You can chew properly and enjoy your favorite foods again without discomfort.
  • Enhanced facial appearance Implants help support facial structure after tooth loss to avoid the sunken appearance associated with missing teeth.
  • Long-term tooth replacement All-on-4 implants become part of your dental anatomy and can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Secure and stable Forget the slipping, shifting, and soreness associated with traditional dentures. All-on-4 implants stay securely in place without the need for messy adhesives.
  • Teeth-in-a-day In many cases, we can perform tooth extraction (if necessary), place your implants, and attach replacement teeth all in one day.

The Procedure for Getting All-on-4 Dental Implants

Evaluation and Treatment Planning

Dr. Timmerman examines your mouth using advanced technology like 3-D cone beam CT scans to ensure you are a good candidate for the procedure. Then he uses the information to create an individualized treatment plan for optimal results.

Implant Placement

Our team wants you to have a great experience, so your comfort is our priority. The strong local anesthetic we use ensures your mouth is numb and you won’t feel any pain during your procedure. After placing the four implants, Dr. Timmerman attaches temporary replacement teeth so you can start smiling confidently right away.

Recovery and Final Dentures

Over the next few months, your mouth will heal, and the implants will fuse with your jawbone. Once fully integrated, we will securely attach the final replacement teeth to the implants.
