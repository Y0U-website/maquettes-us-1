Understanding How Dental Implants Work

A dental implant replaces the missing tooth root with a sturdy titanium post. The implant is placed surgically in the jawbone, and bone and tissue grow around it as it heals. After healing, the implant acts as a stable foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth, such as a crown, bridge, or denture.

The Life-Changing Benefits of Dental Implants

Patients love the benefits that dental implants offer. From improved health and function to greater confidence and feeling great about your smile, most of our patients tell us they wish they had chosen to do dental implants sooner.

Improved Bone Health

Dental implants help prevent bone loss by replacing your natural tooth from root to crown. Replacing the entire tooth keeps the jaw bone stimulated, slowing down bone loss due to a missing tooth.

Greater Dietary Choices

Dentures and bridges limit the crunchy or chewy foods you can eat. Dental implants only require some common-sense precautions, similar to your natural teeth, allowing you to eat the foods you love most.

Improved Self-Confidence

What are implant-supported dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are an excellent solution to replace missing teeth because they offer a blend of stability, functionality, and aesthetics. This tooth replacement works by securing a full or partial denture onto dental implants surgically placed in your jawbone.

Implant-Supported Dentures in Marysville, WA

Implant-supported dentures are secured by several dental implants placed directly in your jawbone. The implants act like the roots of your natural teeth and create a stable foundation for natural-looking customized dentures.

Over several months, a healing process called osseointegration occurs as the implants heal and fuse with your jawbone. The implants become a permanent part of your dental anatomy and provide unparalleled stability for your dentures.

The Benefits of Implant Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer numerous benefits, including:

  • Improved stability and comfort compared to traditional dentures
  • Enhanced chewing and speaking ability
  • Prevention of jawbone loss that occurs after tooth loss
  • A more natural fit and feel than removable dentures
  • A fuller, more youthful facial appearance

Implant Placement

The next step is placing the dental implant at our comfortable dental office in Snohomish, WA. The procedure is not painful because we numb the treatment area with a potent local anesthetic and prioritize your comfort throughout.

Healing and Osseointegration

The implant post is designed like a tiny screw with individual threads that allow for more contact with the bone tissue in your jaw. As your jawbone heals after placement, it fuses with the implant. In 3-6 months, the implant post fully heals and becomes a permanent part of your dental anatomy.
