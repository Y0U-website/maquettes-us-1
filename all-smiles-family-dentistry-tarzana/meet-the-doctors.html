Dr. Mansouri , Endodontist

Dr. Mansouri, our esteemed dentist at All Smiles Family Dentistry, specializes in root canal therapy and oral microsurgery. Renowned for his dedication to delivering cutting-edge care, Dr. Mansouri combines advanced technology with a compassionate approach to prioritize both treatment excellence and patient comfort.

His academic journey began with distinction at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), where he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology. Driven by his passion for dentistry, he continued at UCLA School of Dentistry, achieving his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Furthering his expertise, Dr. Mansouri completed his residency at Saint Louis University, obtaining a Certificate in Endodontics and a Master of Science in Dental Research.

A dynamic professional, Dr. Mansouri maintains active membership in prominent organizations including the American Association of Endodontists, California Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the Alpha Omega Dental Fraternity. With nearly two decades in private practice, he possesses a wealth of knowledge and hands-on experience crucial for achieving successful outcomes in endodontic care.

Dr. Mansouri’s commitment to excellence is reflected not only in his clinical proficiency but also in his personalized approach to patient care. He strives to ensure every individual receives the highest standard of treatment tailored to their unique needs, fostering trust and comfort throughout their dental journey.

At All Smiles Family Dentistry, Dr. Mansouri continues to uphold his reputation as a leader in endodontics, dedicated to advancing the field while prioritizing the well-being of his patients. His unwavering dedication and expertise make him a trusted choice for those seeking specialized root canal therapy and oral microsurgery in the region.

Dr. Pedram Fakheri , Periodontist

Dr. Pedram Fakheri’s journey in dentistry is marked by a commitment to excellence and comprehensive patient-centered care. He earned his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from Yeshiva University in New York, laying a strong foundation for his academic pursuits. Driven by a passion for oral health, he went on to achieve his DMD degree from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey.

Continuing his pursuit of knowledge and specialization, Dr. Fakheri pursued advanced surgical training in Advanced Education in Periodontics and Implant Surgery at Loma Linda University. There, he not only obtained a Specialty certificate but also earned his MSD degree, focusing on the intricacies of periodontal care and dental implants.

Dr. Fakheri is dedicated to offering his patients a comprehensive range of treatment options, ensuring they receive personalized care tailored to their specific needs. His philosophy emphasizes patient education and empowerment, enabling individuals to make informed decisions about their oral health.

A firm believer in continuing education and staying abreast of the latest advancements in dentistry, Dr. Fakheri remains actively engaged in professional associations and ongoing training opportunities. His expertise and compassionate approach make him a trusted provider in periodontal and implant surgery, dedicated to enhancing the smiles and oral health of his patients.
