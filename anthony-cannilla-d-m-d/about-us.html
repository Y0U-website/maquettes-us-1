Meet Dr. Anthony Cannilla, D.M.D.

Dr. Anthony Cannilla has dedicated his career to delivering high-quality, patient-centered dentistry to families throughout Montville, Pine Brook, and the surrounding communities. His path to dentistry began with a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Penn State University in 1995, followed by work as a Quality Control Microbiologist in Long Island.

Driven by a passion for healthcare and hands-on patient care, he earned his D.M.D. from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine in 2003 after completing graduate studies at Hofstra University.

Advanced Training & Professional Excellence

After dental school, Dr. Cannilla completed a year-long general dentistry residency at Mountainside Hospital, focusing on:

  • Cosmetic dentistry
  • Pediatric dentistry
  • Root canal therapy
  • Implant procedures

ICOI Diplomate in Implant Dentistry

Dr. Anthony Cannilla is proud to be a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), the highest level of distinction awarded by the organization and a recognition held by only a select group of implant dentists worldwide.

This prestigious credential reflects Dr. Cannilla’s advanced training, clinical excellence, and ongoing leadership in dental implantology. To earn Diplomate status, he successfully completed 120 diverse implant cases, fulfilled 250 hours of advanced continuing education, passed a rigorous examination by the ICOI Advanced Credentialing Committee, and contributed to the field through professional presentations.

Dr. Cannilla was formally recognized as an ICOI Diplomate in January 2024 in San Diego, following extensive coursework completed through Vizstara in Englewood, New Jersey. This achievement represents not just a milestone, but an ongoing commitment to staying at the forefront of implant dentistry, thus ensuring patients receive the highest standards of precision, safety, and long-term success in their dental care.

A Commitment to Community

Since 2010, Dr. Cannilla has served as the School Dentist for the Montville Township Board of Education, visiting kindergarten classrooms and helping students understand the importance of good oral hygiene in fun, engaging ways.

He also participates in preschool dental health programs, the Order of the Sons of Italy, and Vizstara’s Mentorship Program, which focuses on advanced implant case training.
