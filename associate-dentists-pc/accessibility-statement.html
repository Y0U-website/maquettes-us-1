Accessibility Statement

We continue to monitor and improve accessibility to better serve all users.

  • Provided text alternatives for images and interactive elements
  • Ensured sufficient color contrast for readability
  • Enabled keyboard access to forms and menus
  • Reviewed site structure for compatibility with screen readers

Declaration of partial compliance with the standard due to third-party content

Some pages on this website include content or functionality provided by third-party vendors (such as scheduling tools, maps, or embedded media). While we make every effort to select accessible solutions, we do not control the accessibility of third-party components and cannot guarantee full compliance on those sections.

As a result, we declare partial compliance with accessibility standards for these portions of the site. We are continuously reviewing and updating our tools to improve accessibility wherever possible.

Accessibility arrangements in the organization

At Associate Dentists PC in Council Bluffs, IA, we are committed to making our office accessible and welcoming to all patients, including those with disabilities. Our current accessibility accommodations include:

We are continually working to improve accessibility. If you have any questions or require further accommodations, please contact our office at (712) 328-0708.

  • Parking: Accessible parking spaces are available directly in front of our office entrance, with a level, unobstructed path from the lot to the building.
  • Entrance: Our entrance is at ground level with no steps, allowing easy wheelchair and walker access.
  • Reception and Waiting Area: The front desk and waiting area are wheelchair-accessible, with clear pathways and seating options available for patients with mobility needs.
  • Operatories: Our treatment rooms are designed to accommodate patients with mobility limitations, and staff are available to assist as needed.
  • Communication Assistance: We are happy to provide printed instructions or forms in large font upon request. Please notify our staff in advance if additional communication accommodations are needed.
