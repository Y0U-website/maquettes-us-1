About Cornerstone Dental Group

Cornerstone Dental Group in Westford, MA offers complete dental services for the whole family. With state-of-the-art dental equipment, we are able to offer exceptional oral and dental health treatment. Our services also include cosmetic dentistry with several options to grant you a stunning new smile.

We additionally offer emergency dental procedures to help those who have suffered from a traumatic dental experience. Whatever the reason for your visit, we want to foster an environment of trust with our patients, so we work hard to make our office a welcoming and relaxed environment.

He subsequently graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2010, where he earned both Merit and Alberti Scholarships. In 2011, he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency, mentored and taught by board-certified specialists in all facets of dentistry.

In 2021, Dr. Kharin achieved Fellowship status with the Academy of General Dentistry, joining an exclusive group of dentists committed to clinical excellence through ongoing education. He is the sole AGD Fellow in Bedford, MA.

Throughout his career, Dr. Kharin has dedicated numerous hours to perfecting the art of dentistry through seminars, lectures, hands-on courses, and participation in various study clubs. His objective has always been to possess the knowledge and skills to provide exceptional dental care to his patients.

Dr. Kharin’s behavioral background, erudite personality, and profound sense of compassion, coupled with a comprehensive understanding of his profession, have shaped a distinctive style of patient interaction. From the initial meeting, he endeavors to learn about his patients’ desires, wishes, past experiences, and fears, aiming to comprehend the individuals for whom he will be providing care. Following a meticulous dental examination, Dr. Kharin guides patients in formulating a comprehensive treatment plan to achieve the optimal health that modern dentistry can offer.

Residing in Concord, MA, with his wife and two young sons, Dr. Kharin is not only a dedicated dental professional but also an avid reader, traveler, art and music connoisseur, gardener, nature enthusiast, and a published author. Additionally, he proudly serves his country as a Major in the U.S Army Reserve.
