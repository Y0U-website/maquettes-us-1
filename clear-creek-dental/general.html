General Services

Preventative Checkups

Preventive checkups are essential for maintaining optimal oral health and preventing potential dental issues. During these appointments, our skilled team conducts a thorough examination, reviewing both your teeth and gums, while also taking the opportunity to provide a professional cleaning.

This proactive approach allows for the early detection of any concerns, enabling timely interventions to keep your smile healthy. Dental checkups are a key component in supporting good oral hygiene.

Cleanings

Dental Cleanings are a vital service designed to maintain oral and overall dental health. During these appointments, our experienced hygienists perform a thorough cleaning to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains, which helps prevent cavities and gum disease.

In addition to cleanings, we offer dental deep cleaning, also known as scaling and root planing. This is a non-surgical procedure to treat gum disease by removing plaque and tartar from below the gum line. We typically recommended when regular cleanings aren’t enough to manage periodontal disease

Additionally, cleanings provide an opportunity for early detection of potential issues, allowing for appropriate care and treatment when needed. Committing to regular cleanings not only enhances your smile’s appearance but also contributes significantly to your long-term oral health.

X-Rays

Utilizing the latest digital imaging technology, X-rays provide precise and detailed views of your teeth, gums, and surrounding structures, allowing us to accurately diagnose any potential issues. Our experienced team prioritizes your comfort throughout the process, focusing on safety with minimal radiation exposure.
