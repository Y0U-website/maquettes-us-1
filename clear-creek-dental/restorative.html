Restorative Services

CEREC Crowns

CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics) crowns are a cutting-edge solution in restorative dentistry, allowing for the day-of creation of custom crowns using our in-house CEREC machine.

This advanced CAD/CAM (Computer-Aided Design/Computer-Aided Manufacturing) technology designs and fabricates crowns with precision and efficiency, guaranteeing a perfect fit and natural appearance. By eliminating the need for multiple appointments and temporary crowns, our same-day CEREC crowns provide a swift and seamless restoration experience.

Implants

Dental implants help restore your smile and function after tooth loss, providing exceptional support for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Utilizing the latest technology and individualized care, we make your implant procedure comfortable and efficient.

Missing teeth can disrupt your bite’s balance, making replacement essential. Dental implants, which act as artificial roots, offer outstanding stability. When saving a tooth isn’t feasible, choosing dental implants can be the best option for long-term oral health.

Dentures

Full and partial dentures can be an effective and affordable solution for replacing missing teeth. Not only do dentures restore function, but they also offer essential support for your cheeks and lips, enhancing your appearance. We use advanced dental technology to create personalized, comfortable dentures that boost your confidence.

Upper dentures are typically acrylic and flesh-colored, covering the roof of your mouth, while lower dentures are horseshoe-shaped for tongue space. We also offer options for implant-supported dentures, with customizable materials including high-quality plastic or porcelain teeth to suit your needs.

Partials/Bridges

Restore your smile with confidence through our diverse bridge options. Traditional bridges offer robust support, filling gaps with a secure and stable solution. Cantilever bridges cater to specific needs, providing flexibility and efficiency in restoring your smile.

Clear Creek Dental provide a budget-friendly alternative that combines durability with a natural appearance. For those seeking enhanced durability and stability, Implant-Supported bridges offer a secure and long-term solution to complete your smile with strength and comfort.

Composite Resin Fillings

Composite resin fillings are a tooth-colored blend of glass and plastic designed to effectively restore decaying teeth while providing a natural appearance. These versatile fillings not only address cavities, but also elevate your smile by allowing for color modification and reshaping of disfigured teeth.

Their strong adhesion reinforces the remaining tooth structure and helps prevent further decay, while their resistance to fractures makes them ideal for both front and back teeth. With their aesthetic appeal and durability, composite fillings are an excellent choice for restorative procedures.

Sealants

Dental sealants are a highly effective preventive treatment aimed at safeguarding the teeth from cavities, particularly in children and young adults.
