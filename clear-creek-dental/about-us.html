Our Dentists

Dr. Gregory B. Carman, DMD

Dr. Gregory B. Carman, DMD, was born in California but spent nearly his entire childhood in Northern Nevada. He graduated with honors from Earl Wooster High School and went on to the University of Nevada, Reno, where he earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology with a GPA exceeding 3.9 and graduated with High Distinction. Dr. Carman was then accepted into the esteemed Oregon Health and Sciences University Dental School, graduating four years later with high marks.

In 2009, he and his wife, Stacy, returned to Northern Nevada to enjoy the beautiful sunshine and a variety of outdoor activities.

In January 2013, Dr. Carman took over the practice from Dr. Harrington and has since enjoyed working closely with both the staff and patients to maintain the tradition of delivering exceptional dental care. He is deeply invested in each patient’s dental health, striving for the highest level of perfection in every treatment, whether he’s creating custom-stained CEREC-fabricated EMAX crowns or ensuring careful implant placements. Dr. Carman believes in treating others as he would want himself or his family to be treated, though he may occasionally fall short in his delivery of good jokes.

Dr. Carman lives with his wife, Stacy, and their three children. When he’s not discussing his new CEREC crowns or dental practices, he is likely to share amusing stories from his experiences with fatherhood. In his free time, he enjoys downhill skiing, golfing, hunting, hiking, running, and off-roading with his Jeep.

Dr. Jack L. Harrington, DDS

Dr. Jack L. Harrington, DDS, graduated from the University of Southern California School of Dentistry in 1974. During his time in dental school, he served as Class President and was elected to the honorary dental fraternity, Alpha Tau Epsilon. In 1970, Dr. Harrington earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Psychology from the University of Southern California, graduating with academic honors, Cum Laude.

While at USC, he was also a proud member of the 1970 National Championship Baseball Team and participated in the Kappa Sigma fraternity.

Dr. Harrington holds dental licenses in Nevada, California, and Hawaii, and he is an active member of both the Nevada Dental Association and the American Dental Association.

He currently resides in Glenbrook, Nevada, with his wife, Kit, and their three children, Cody, Austyn, and Brette.

Dr. Nicole Rahming, DMD

Dr. Nicole (Niki) M. Rahming, DMD, was born and raised in Reno, Nevada. She graduated from Reno High School, where she excelled both academically and in softball. Dr. Rahming went on to attend the University of Evansville in Indiana on a full Division 1 softball scholarship, earning a Bachelor of Science in Exercise Science with Cum Laude honors.

After completing her undergraduate studies, Dr. Rahming returned to Nevada and was accepted into the University of Nevada Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated with high distinction. She then took a position at a corporate group practice near her alma mater in Evansville, Indiana.

Living in southern Indiana provided Dr. Rahming with the opportunity to explore nearby historic towns and cities, including Chicago, St. Louis, Nashville, and Louisville, as well as several locations on the east coast. After three years, she moved back to Reno to be closer to family and to serve the dental needs of fellow Nevadans.

Dr. Rahming is dedicated to delivering quality dental care to every patient as if they are part of her own family. She aims to improve the oral health of the community through a comprehensive approach that positively impacts each patient’s overall health and well-being. Committed to staying updated on the latest advancements in the field, she ensures that her patients receive the best treatment options available.

Dr. Rahming is certified in laser therapy and Invisalign clear aligners, and she is a member of both the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

In her free time, she enjoys skiing, hiking, watching movies, playing board games, and traveling, often spending quality time with her friends and family.
