Welcome to Ace Dental Office

Your trusted choice for general dentistry and implants.

Since our humble beginnings in 2011 as a single-operatory practice in Borough Park, Ace Dental Office has grown to serve the Crown Heights community in a modern, three-operatory facility. Our commitment to your oral health and comfort has remained steadfast throughout the years.

Explore our website to learn more about the services we offer or contact us directly at (845) 638-4880 .

Dr. Efraim Fuzailov, DDS

Dr. Efraim Fuzailov is a graduate of Stony Brook University of Dental Medicine. Following his graduation, he completed a one-year residency at Kingsbrook Jewish Medical Center in 2011. In June 2013, Dr. Fuzailov received a certificate of academic achievement in oral implantology from the New York Implant Institute.

With a passion for precision and patient care, Dr. Fuzailov has dedicated his career to creating beautiful, healthy smiles for all his patients.
