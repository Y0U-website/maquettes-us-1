The practice

Aesthetic Dental Care of Kentlands is proud to be your local, family-friendly dental office. We care for families from Gaithersburg, MD, and the surrounding communities of Kentlands, North Potomac, Darnestown, and beyond.

Our skilled, compassionate team, under the guidance of Dr. Chitra Ghafari, manages everything from regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to cosmetic dentistry, implants, and sleep apnea therapy.

In today’s busy world, we take our time with each patient. We enjoy developing personal rapports with our patients so we can help them create a treatment plan that best suits their individual needs.

Rest assured that regardless of your dental needs, we will treat you with the most compassionate, respectful care available.
