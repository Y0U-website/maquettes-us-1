Teeth that are cracked or have large cavities sometimes require a crown to ensure the structural integrity of the tooth. Bridges are similar to crowns and can be permanently placed to replace missing or failing teeth.

When cavities become very large, they can reach the center of the tooth where the nerve lives. If the nerve becomes infected or abscessed, endodontic treatment, or a root canal, may be necessary to resolve the infection and pain.
