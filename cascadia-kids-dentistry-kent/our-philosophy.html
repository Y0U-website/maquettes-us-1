The Importance of Building Relationships Early

We love kids!

Here’s how we ensure every child feels comfortable and safe at the dentist!

Communication is imperative. Dr. Nick, Dr. Kathie, Dr. Hayley and Dr. Peter will get to know you and your family with one-on-one communication.

Did your child have a bad experience in the past? We drop down to their level and discuss those past experiences with them to gain trust.

We won’t lean that chair back right away. Instead, we let your child feel empowered to love the dentist and give them control in their dental care.

Every appointment is tailored to your child. From finding the perfect kid-friendly shows and using the same team every time, your child will love the dentist.

If these options aren’t enough to ease your child’s fears or anxiety, we offer in-office general anesthesia.

What Makes Our Team Different

Our team works hard to create positive dental experiences for kiddos. And the best way to do that is to be energetic, upbeat, and to speak fluent kid. We know how to communicate with children of all ages in ways that make them feel respected. When they understand what’s going on with their oral health, they’re more likely to take care of their smiles. By instilling a little responsibility, we open up the door for more adventure!
