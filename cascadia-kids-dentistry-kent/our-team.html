Our Team

We know that pediatric dental care is more than just cleanings, fillings, and other restorative work . Our team is the heart of our office, and they are here to make every child feel welcomed, comfortable, and safe. We take a communicative approach to kids’ dental care, which means that your child will understand every step of the process. Our goal is to empower children to take control of their dental health from a young age and foster positive hygiene habits that will last a lifetime!

Meet the Cascadia Kids Team

Cascadia Kids Dentistry was built around quality, integrity and compassion. So we’ve recruited some of the most qualified, honest and compassionate dental professionals in the greater Seattle area. Our team of tiny-tooth experts understands that enthusiasm and friendship go a long way. And nothing makes us happier than maintaining a child’s oral health while catching up on all their adventures during every visit!
