How to Get Here

Our office is located in the shopping center at the corner of SE Kent Kangley Road and Maple Valley Black Diamond Road SE, not far from Tahoma High School. If you’re coming to our office from the North, just take the 169 southbound and turn left on SE Kent Kangley Road. Make a quick right into the shopping center. Our office is just south of the Texaco gas station.

Visiting from south of Maple Valley? Take the 169 north and make a right at SE Kent Kangley Road. Make another right into the shopping center. Our office is just south of the Texaco gas station.

Is your teenager due for a dental visit? Bring them in before or after school! Head south on 164th Ave SE, then turn left onto SE 272nd Street. This street will turn into SE Kent Kangley Road. Stay on it until you reach the shopping center at Maple Valley Black Diamond Road SE. Make a right into the shopping center. Our office is just south of the Texaco gas station.
