Our Philosophy

Non Judgmental

It may have been a while since your last exam. A year...3 yrs...5 yrs. We don’t care. We have a zero shame policy in our office. We’re here to help you achieve your oral health goals.

Quality

My Dad taught me it’s not what you do, but how you do it. Craftsmanship was a core principle of my childhood that I’ve carried over into my profession as a dentist. I care about every exam, every filling, every crown, every procedure.

You matter

You matter. Period. We don’t care what insurance you’re on, your socioeconomic background, etc. I value the trust you put in me as your provider. Everyone deserves care. Everyone deserves respect. Everyone deserves empathy.

Affordable

We understand dentistry may not fit into your budget when up against other priorities. We scrutinize every dollar spent and strive to pass those savings on to you. We also take care credit to allow another way to cover those expenses.
