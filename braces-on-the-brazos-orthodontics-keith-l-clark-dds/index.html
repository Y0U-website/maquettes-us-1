The practice

Dr. Keith L. Clark and his team makes it naturally easy for patients to feel welcomed, inspired and rewarded on a daily basis. It is our mission to give patients a joyful experience of upfront honesty, overflowing kindness, and driven expertise.

We offer a welcoming, family-friendly orthodontic experience at our Granbury or Cleburne orthodontic office , tailoring care to your unique needs. Your individual treatment plan will be efficient, affordable, and deliver the natural-looking, radiant smile you desire.

In addition to traditional metal and clear (ceramic) braces for all ages, Dr. Clark offers early orthodontic treatment for children. He also provides Invisalign® clear aligners, the cosmetic choice for many teens and adults.

Combining advanced orthodontic techniques with truly personalized care, we hope to celebrate life with you and all it’s special moments!

Most teens and adults are reluctant to get the orthodontic care they need due to the appearance of orthodontic appliances.

Invisalign® is a wonderful, modern alternative treatment to braces. You can straighten your teeth without installing metal in your mouth.

Iconix stainless steel champagne colored brackets truly offer the best of both worlds – the durability of stainless steel and the aesthetics of “white” gold.

Even though there are many types of braces, traditional braces are still the most popular option. Great strides in technology have been made since they were first used, so they are becoming more comfortable and effective.
