Dental Services in Decatur

We offer everything your family needs for happy and healthy smiles. Whether you are due for a dental cleaning, are thinking about a straighter smile, or in need of a full smile makeover, we’ve got you covered with expert dental services.

Preventive Dentistry

Good dental health is an important factor in overall health, which is why the American Dental Association recommends you see your dentist every six months. Building a relationship with a dental practice can help you maintain and improve your smile, and prevent problems before they become serious. Our preventive services include Comprehensive Exams, Sealants, Fluoride Treatments, Space Maintainers, Mouthguards, and Oral Cancer Screenings.

Night Guards Night guards are custom-fit appliances worn during sleep to protect teeth from grinding and clenching (bruxism). They create a barrier that reduces enamel wear, helps prevent chips and fractures, and can protect crowns, fillings, and veneers from damage. Night guards may also reduce jaw muscle strain and morning headaches for some patients. A custom fit is typically more comfortable and stable than store-bought options, making it easier to wear consistently.

Teeth Cleaning Preventative teeth cleanings are a cornerstone of lasting oral health. Professional cleanings are important to helping reduce the risk of tooth decay and gum disease.

Dental X-Rays X-ray imaging is a cornerstone of modern dentistry. It gives a dental professional a comprehensive view of the patient’s oral health and reveals what the naked eye cannot see.

Dental Sealants Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings that adhere to the chewing surface of back teeth. Research has shown they reduce the risk of tooth decay by nearly 80 % in molars. This is especially important for your child’s dental health.

Restorative Dentistry

We’re passionate about restorative care. We offer the latest in restorative treatments and technologies to help restore your smile. We’ll give you the information you need to make the best choice for your dental health. We offer complete restorative solutions that help you build confidence, increase functionality, and improve your dental health.

Dental Bridge A bridge is a dental appliance that replaces one or more natural missing teeth, thereby "bridging" the space between two teeth. Fixed bridges are cemented onto teeth on either side of the space. Unlike removable partial dentures, fixed bridges cannot be taken out of the mouth by the patient. If you are missing any teeth and are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene practices, you may be a good candidate for a bridge.

Dental Crowns A crown, also known as a cap, covers a tooth to restore it to its normal shape and size, thereby strengthening and improving its overall appearance.

Dental Filling To treat a cavity, your dentist will remove the decayed portion of the tooth and then "fill" the area on the tooth where the decay was. Fillings are also used to repair cracked, broken or injured teeth and teeth that have been worn down from nail-biting, tooth grinding or other misuse.

Dental Implants Dental implants are replacement tooth roots. Implants provide a strong foundation for fixed (permanent) or removable replacement teeth that are made to match your natural teeth.

Dentures Replace your missing teeth with cost-effective dentures to restore your smile, feel confident and enjoy your favorite foods again.

Root Canal A root canal is a treatment used to save a tooth that is badly decayed and/or infected.

Tooth Extraction When tooth decay or traumatic injuries compromise the restorability of your tooth, it’s typically best to remove the entire tooth to prevent pain or recurring infections that put your other teeth at risk.

Emergency Dental

If you’re experiencing extreme pain or other dental emergencies its important to receive care right away. Call our office for an emergency appointment, and in many instances we will be able to see you the same day.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Our cosmetic dentistry services can give you the smile you’ve always wanted. We offer a variety of services including whitening, veneers and crowns.

Dental Bonding Dental bonding is a cosmetic treatment that uses a tooth-colored material to improve the shape and appearance of a tooth. It’s commonly used to repair small chips, smooth rough or uneven edges, close minor gaps, and cover certain discoloration. Bonding is typically a conservative option that can often be completed in one visit with little to no drilling. With good home care and regular dental visits, bonding can last for years and blend naturally with your smile.

Dental Veneers Transform your smile with custom dental veneers designed to enhance the appearance of your teeth. Our veneers look natural while covering chips, stains, or gaps for a flawless finish. Whether you want a subtle improvement or a complete smile makeover, our skilled dental team provides personalized veneer treatments that deliver long-lasting, beautiful results.

Teeth Whitening A white, bright smile can help create a youthful appearance. We offer a number of different whitening options, depending upon your needs. We recommend that you consult with your dentist to determine which option is best for you. Your dentist may need to perform an exam to determine if your teeth are healthy enough for whitening.

Children’s Dentistry

Establishing a relationship with a dentist for your child within six months of the first tooth eruption, or by his/her first birthday is an important step to ensure a lifetime of healthy smiles. Additionally, children often have different needs than adults, making it important to find a dentist who understands children.

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, affects millions of people worldwide. This common yet serious dental issue can lead to significant oral health problems if left untreated.
