The practice

We love seeing kids be such great little patients. Having good experiences with the dentist early on will set them up for a lifetime of trust in their healthcare providers.

Each child’s situation will be different than the next, and there is no one size fits all approach. That’s why we at Core Pediatric Dentistry treat each patient with personalized care to ensure great short and long term dental health.

Dr. Kaur’s goal is to provide evidence-based care tailored to each child’s specific needs. She is motivated to impact the community by improving children’s oral health and creating big smiles for every family. Ultimately, seeing a child in a better place after we care for them is the best feeling in the world.

Dr. Kaur now lives in the North Hills with her husband and is excited to call Pittsburgh her new home. In her free time, Dr. Kaur enjoys exploring new coffee shops, trying new restaurants, traveling and spending time with her family and friends.
