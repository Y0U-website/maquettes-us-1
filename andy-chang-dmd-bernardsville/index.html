The practice saw continued growth under the direction of Dr. James Fox and later Dr. Paul Condouris. Dr. Chang is proud to continue the tradition of service to the Bernardsville and surrounding community.

Laser dentistry can be a precise and effective way to perform many dental procedures. We use a soft tissue diode lasers in our office for minor surgical procedures, to reshape tissue, improve clotting, and enhance the quality of dental work. When performed with a laser, many of these procedures often result in quicker recovery and less discomfort.
