What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

To receive dental implants, you will first come into our dental office for a consultation. Our esteemed black dentist in Bowie will evaluate the condition of your oral health, the number of teeth you’re missing, and the density of your jawbone. If we find anything that might cause us to recommend additional treatment (i.e., gum disease, tooth decay, bone resorption), we will schedule you for these before suggesting the next step in the dental implant process.

However, once we have determined that you’re a good candidate for this option, we will schedule your implant surgery with a local specialist.

Dental Implant Surgery

We partner with the best local implant dentists in the area, so when it’s time to receive your dental implants, you can rest assured that you will be in good hands. During surgery, you will receive local anesthesia to numb the various portions of your mouth undergoing treatment. Small incisions will be made in your gums to access your bone before each dental implant is put in place.

Ensuring they are positioned correctly, the dentist will close the gum tissue and cover the areas with protective healing caps.

Dental Implant Osseointegration/Abutment

Once your implants are placed, we must let them heal for about 3 to 6 months. This is to allow osseointegration to occur. Your titanium posts must fuse with the surrounding bone tissue if they are to remain stable and long-lasting. After you are healed and your implants are firmly attached to the bone, you will receive your metal abutments, which help to connect your custom restoration to the various posts along your jawbone.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

The final step in the dental implant process is what everyone looks forward to the final placement of the custom restoration. Earlier on, we will capture impressions of your mouth so that your newly crafted crown, bridge, or denture can be fabricated by skilled lab technicians. When ready, our black woman dentist in Bowie will secure it to your implants, ensuring it looks natural and feels comfortable.

As long as you are happy with the results, you will be free to leave and begin enjoying the benefits of your healthier smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Traditional dentures and dental bridges may look realistic, but they just don’t provide the same stability and function as your natural teeth. When it comes to tooth replacements, dental implants are currently the only solution that delivers flawless, reliable results with the ability to last a lifetime. It’s no wonder they’re considered the gold standard of tooth replacement! Here are a few of the benefits of dental implants in Bowie that you can look forward to.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • The freedom to eat and drink anything A recent survey found that more than one-third of denture-wearers felt that their prosthetic limited their ability to enjoy life to the fullest. After all, dentures tend to warp when exposed to hot beverages, and they don’t restore much chewing power. Dental implant restorations maintain their shape no matter what you drink, and because they recreate your tooth roots, they also bring back almost all of your original chewing force. You’ll be able to eat and drink virtually anything you want!
  • Teeth that stay in place With dental implants, you won’t need to worry about your teeth shifting around or falling out; your new pearly whites are firmly anchored in place.
  • A confident smile Your implant restorations are designed to mimic your natural teeth and complement your facial features. Once they’re in place, others shouldn’t even be able to tell that your teeth are not real!

Health Benefits

  • Prevention of bone loss After a tooth falls out, the jawbone begins to deteriorate in that area. Eventually, this bone shrinkage can lead to an increased risk of further tooth loss and other oral health problems. Dental implants are the only tooth replacement that stimulates the jawbone like real teeth, keeping it strong.
  • No gum soreness Unlike dentures, implant restorations don’t slide around on the gums and cause irritation.
  • Preservation of natural teeth To place a dental bridge, the two adjacent teeth need to be altered, leaving them weaker and more sensitive over time. Since dental implants are self-supporting, we can preserve your natural tooth structure.
  • Improved overall health Studies show that people who have lost five or more teeth have a higher risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and other systemic illnesses. Dental implants function just like real teeth, so it’s reasonable to assume that they do not increase such risks.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Younger appearance By stimulating your jawbone, dental implants prevent your face from taking on a sunken, wrinkled appearance. As a result, they help you maintain a youthful glow for years, even decades, to come!
  • Saved money Dental implants can easily last for 30+ years, compared to the five to ten years that traditional prosthetics last. With dental implants, you’ll end up saving money that you would have spent on frequent repairs and replacements.
  • Easy maintenance Making dental implants last is simple; no complicated oral hygiene routine is required. All you have to do is brush, floss, and visit your implant dentist in Bowie for checkups on a regular basis.

Missing One Tooth

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you’ve lost two or more teeth in a row, an implant bridge will seamlessly fill the gap in your smile. A row of fused replacement teeth will be anchored on both sides by a dental implant. For more advanced loss of both consecutive and nonconsecutive teeth, an implant partial denture may be a better option. A gum colored base material is molded to fill the gaps in the smile and support several replacement teeth. Depending on the number and location of the missing teeth, a partial denture may be supported with two to four implant posts.

Missing All Teeth

Following complete tooth loss, we can create an implant full denture. Full dentures, like partials, are crafted using a gum colored base material to support an entire row of replacement teeth. Depending on the natural size and shape of the patient’s jawline and the materials used to craft the denture, four to six dental implant posts may be used to support the prosthetic.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

What Factors Determine the Cost of Dental Implants?

  • The Number of Implant Posts Because dental implants are a versatile treatment option, they can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, and even a full arch. This means if you only need one implant, the cost will be significantly less than if you were replacing all your teeth.
  • Pre-Implant Dental Work If you don’t have enough bone to support the implant, Dr. Hightower might suggest a bone graft. This is a separate procedure that will add expense. The same can be said if you need gum disease therapy or any other dental work before receiving your implants.
  • Anesthesia or Sedation Since dental implant placement is a surgical procedure, either local anesthesia or sedation will be administered. It’s important that you are comfortable throughout the process, and depending on your dental or medical insurance, it may cover certain types of sedation.
  • Customized Crowns Similar to the number of implant posts, the number of crowns needed can affect cost. Most dental insurance plans offer coverage for at least a percentage of these tooth replacement prosthetics.
  • Materials Used The materials used to craft your custom restorations, as well as those that make up the implant, are factored into the overall cost of your procedure.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Your newly placed dental implants can potentially keep your smile whole, healthy, and strong for a lifetime, but that’s only if you take excellent care of them. Luckily, it only takes excellent oral hygiene, a little help from the team at Ambiance Dental Spa, and some common sense to keep your new teeth in great shape. Here are a few stupendous guidelines for keeping your implants beautiful, functional, and infection-free for as long as possible.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

While dental implants are not vulnerable to cavities, they can still be compromised by infections of the gum tissue and jawbone structure supporting them. After getting implants, keep your mouth in excellent health by brushing twice and flossing once daily and regularly using antibacterial mouthwash.

Eat a Healthy Diet

While you can eat anything you like with dental implants, it’s important to ensure that your diet contains plenty of protein, calcium, and vitamin C to ensure that your jaws and gums stay healthy. It’s also great to avoid overindulging on items that are especially hard, sticky, or chewy to prevent oral injuries and infections. Excessive sugar consumption will provide harmful bacteria with the fuel they need to cause destructive oral infections that can compromise your implants, so enjoy your sweets in moderation.

Break Bad Habits

Certain bad habits can set your dental implants up for destruction. Smoking or chewing tobacco is completely horrible for your oral health and a significant factor in many cases of implant failure, so kicking these habits is one of the best things you can do for your smile. It’s also important to avoid excessive alcohol consumption as well as using your teeth to open packages or bottles.

Protect Your Dental Implants

While dental implants are crafted to be incredibly strong and durable, they are not invincible. Protect them from injury by wearing an athletic mouthguard when playing sports and a nightguard to bed if you grind your teeth at night. We’ll be happy to provide you with customized dental protection appliances that offer superior protection at our office.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Even if you have no remaining natural teeth, it’s still crucial to come to our office in Bowie every six months for routine dental exams and cleanings. This way, we can monitor your gums and dental implants for any issues and address them in their early stages.

Dental Implant FAQs

Does getting dental implants placed hurt?

For many reasons, no. The jawbone does not have many nerve endings, plus a patient will be numbed with local anesthetic before the placement procedure begins. Most people describe feeling a little pressure while in the chair, but nothing that resembles pain. A little soreness is normal for a few days afterward, but this can usually be easily mitigated by taking OTC medication or applying a cold compress to the area.

How long does it take to recover from dental implant surgery?

Most patients can resume their normal routine the day after surgery. It’s recommended that someone avoid strenuous physical activity for the first week, as this could delay healing. A little bleeding is normal and should stop after the first couple of days, and any swelling should go away after three to four days. After this initial period, it may take up to six months for the implants to fuse with the jawbone, but a patient should feel perfectly fine throughout the process.

How long does dental implant surgery take?

The length of the procedure varies depending on how many implants a patient is having placed at once. On average, a single implant requires about one to two hours, and this accounts for administering anesthesia, sterilizing the area, and actually positioning the implant within the jawbone.

How long do dental implants last?

The average lifespan of a dental implant is 30+ years, which is multiple times longer compared to traditional bridges and dentures. Like any dental restoration, implants’ durability is directly influenced by a patient’s health and lifestyle. Brushing and flossing every day, getting regular checkups, and avoiding chewing hard items that could wear down the teeth are all ways a patient can ensure the longevity of their new teeth.

Can someone be too old to get dental implants?

Thankfully, there is no upper age limit when it comes to being a good candidate for dental implants. All the patient needs to bring to the table is decent oral health, stable overall health, and an adequately strong jawbone to support the new teeth. While seniors tend to have more health issues that can complicate the dental implant process, these can usually be accounted for to guarantee a smooth and successful treatment.
