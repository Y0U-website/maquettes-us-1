About

Where Little Smiles Get Big Care.

Astro Pediatric Dentistry is a family-owned pediatric dental practice in Fairfield, New Jersey, founded by a husband-and-wife team who share one mission: To make every child feel safe, seen, and genuinely cared for from the very first dental visit.

Built by two people who truly love kids.

Dr. Samantha You

General Dentist

Dr. Samantha You holds a degree from Touro College of Dental Medicine and completed her General Practice Residency at New York Medical College. She focuses on older pediatric patients — tweens, teens, and young adults — and brings 15 years of experience working with adolescents both in and out of the dental chair.

Dr. Minsuk Cha

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Trained at Touro College of Dental Medicine and Stony Brook Hospital, Dr. Minsuk Cha completed a pediatric dentistry residency and earned his board certification. He has a particular gift for connecting with young and anxious children — the kind of calm presence that makes even the most reluctant patient feel at ease.

Dentistry that starts with trust.

We believe that a child’s earliest dental experiences shape their relationship with oral health for life. That’s why we slow down, listen, and meet every patient exactly where they are — emotionally and developmentally. A successful dental visit at Astro isn’t just a clean bill of dental health. It’s a child walking out with a little more confidence than when they walked in.

Our practice is guided by four core values:

Empathy first

We see fear and crying as communication, not inconvenience. Every response is thoughtful and patient.

Honest, no-pressure care

We explain every option clearly — including risks and benefits — so families feel confident, never rushed.

Long-term relationships

We welcome babies, toddlers, kids, and teens. Families never need to switch practices as children grow.

Transparent education

We use intraoral cameras, 3D scanners, and AI radiograph tools to show families exactly what we see.

We try to treat every child the way we would want our own to be treated — with empathy, honesty, and the kind of care you actually remember.

— Dr. Minsuk Cha & Dr. Samantha You, Founders
