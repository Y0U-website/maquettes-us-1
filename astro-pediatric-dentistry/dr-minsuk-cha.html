A Pediatric Specialist Who Sees The Whole Child

Astro Pediatric Dentistry was born from a simple idea: that a dental practice can feel warm, calm, and even fun — especially for the youngest patients. Dr. Minsuk Cha, a board-certified pediatric dentist, and Dr. Samantha You , a general dentist with a lifelong passion for working with adolescents and teens, joined their strengths to create a practice that grows with every family it serves.

Together, they bring a full spectrum of pediatric dental care under one roof — from infant frenectomies and first-tooth visits to complex restorative dental care and dental treatment for teens . Families in Fairfield don’t have to look elsewhere as their children grow. Astro is designed to be their long-term dental home.
