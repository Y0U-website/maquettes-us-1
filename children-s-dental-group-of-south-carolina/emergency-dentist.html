Emergency Children’s Dental Services in Columbia, SC Dental emergencies can be stressful and frightening, especially for children. Children’s Dental Group of SC provides prompt, compassionate, and expert care to address any dental emergencies your child may encounter.

When to Seek Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. It’s crucial to seek immediate care if your child experiences any of the following:

Severe Toothache Persistent or severe pain that doesn’t subside with over-the-counter pain relief.

Broken or Chipped Tooth Any trauma to the teeth that results in a fractured, chipped, or cracked tooth.

Knocked-Out Tooth A tooth completely knocked out requires immediate attention to increase the chances of saving it.

Loose Tooth A permanent tooth that has become loose due to an injury.

Soft Tissue Injuries Any cuts, lacerations, or injuries to the gums, cheeks, lips, or tongue.

Swelling or Abscess Swelling, pus, or abscess formation in the mouth can indicate an infection that needs urgent care.

Our Emergency Dental Services

We are equipped to handle various dental emergencies at the Children’s Dental Group of SC. Our emergency services include:

Immediate Pain Relief We prioritize alleviating your child’s pain and discomfort.

Emergency Examinations A thorough examination to assess the extent of the injury or issue.

Tooth Repair and Restoration Treatment for broken, chipped, or knocked-out teeth to restore your child’s smile.

Infection Treatment Prompt infection care, including abscess drainage and antibiotic therapy, if necessary.

Soft Tissue Care Treatment for injuries to the gums, cheeks, lips, and tongue.
