Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants

Most healthy adults missing one or more teeth are good candidates for dental implants. The most important factors are having adequate jawbone density to support the implant and healthy gum tissue free of active periodontal disease. Patients with insufficient bone volume are not automatically excluded. In many cases, a bone graft can rebuild the necessary foundation before implant placement. Certain health conditions, smoking habits, or unmanaged periodontal disease may affect candidacy or require additional preparation before treatment can move forward.

The only way to know for certain whether implants are right for you is through a thorough evaluation at our Pace, FL office, where we assess your bone structure, gum health, and overall goals for replacing your missing teeth.
