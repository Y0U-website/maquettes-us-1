Meet Dr. J S.A. DeMeo

My passion for orthodontics comes from all of the big smiles that I see at the end of treatment. I love the physics and vectors that help determine the best path for tooth eruption and how that meshes with the biology of each individual.

My favorite part about being an orthodontist are the lasting relationships I make throughout treatment. I love getting to know my patients over the course of treatment, hearing about their sports, school, hobbies and vacations.I enjoy serving my community through work with the local volunteer organizations and activities like Give Kids A Smile. I also volunteer my time on the Albany Med Craniofacial Team to help treat children with cleft palates. I previously served on the Board of the Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists and continue to help them as a section editor of their journal.

I’m proud to be a professional member of the following associations:

The Northeastern Society of Orthodontists (NESO) is a regional constituent of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) representing orthodontists across the northeastern United States and eastern Canada.

The New York State Dental Association (NYSDA) is the professional organization representing dentists in New York State, including specialists like orthodontists.

Diplomate, American Board of Orthodontics

Upon successful completion of both the written and clinical exams, the orthodontist is recognized as a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. This is the highest level of certification an orthodontist can achieve in the specialty.

Only dentists who successfully complete an accredited orthodontic residency program after dental school are accepted for membership in the American Association of Orthodontists.

The American Dental Association (ADA) is the national professional organization for dentists in the United States, including orthodontists.
