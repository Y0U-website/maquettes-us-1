The practice

At Darien Orthodontics, every patient works directly with Dr. Dan from the complimentary first consultation through the final appointment. No handoffs. No rotating providers.

We offer braces and Invisalign for children, teens, and adults, using the latest technology in a comfortable, boutique setting.

Conveniently located in downtown Darien’s Corbin District, Darien Orthodontics is proud to serve families across Darien and Fairfield County.

The practice is now open 3–4 days per week, with expanded appointment availability for new and existing patients.
