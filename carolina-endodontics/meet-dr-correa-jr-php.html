Dr. Correa Jr.

Endodontist

Dr Mauro Correa Jr. was born and raised in Northern California. He joined the army right after high school and continues to serve in the army reserves. He attended Rider University, where he received his Bachelor of Science degree in natural science, and then attended the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine where he earned his DMD degree.

He completed an advanced education in general dentistry one-year program in the army at Fort Sill, OK, and did his residency training in endodontics at the Eisenhower Army Medical Center at Forth Eisenhower, GA. Notably, during his endodontics residency, he undertook and completed a research project, leading to a publication in the esteemed Journal of Endodontics. He practiced in Columbia since 2019.

Dr Correa lives in Blythewood with his wife. They have three children.

Serving Columbia, Irmo, Chapin, Lexington, Newberry, Elgin SC and Surrounding Areas
