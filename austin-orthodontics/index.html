Welcome To Austin Orthodontics

At Austin Orthodontics, our mission goes beyond creating beautiful smiles- we focus on developing healthy, functional bites that support overall wellness.

The practice

As an airway-specific orthodontist, Dr. Austin takes a comprehensive approach to treatment, evaluating how jaw growth, facial development, and breathing patterns work together. By addressing the root causes of orthodontic concerns rather than just the symptoms, our team helps patients achieve long-term stability, improving function, and creating confident smiles that last a lifetime.

Introducing PASS™ — Dr. Austin’s new philosophy designed to create healthier smiles and healthier airways. Because great orthodontics isn’t just about straight teeth… it’s about supporting proper breathing, growth, and long-term wellness. We’re excited to bring this whole-health approach to every patient we serve.

Healthy breathing is essential to healthy living and orthodontic treatment plays a vital role. As an airway-focused orthodontist, Dr. Austin designs treatment plans that not only align teeth but also promote proper jaw development and airway function. This approach is ideal for adults seeking comprehensive correction of dental, skeletal, and airway issues.
