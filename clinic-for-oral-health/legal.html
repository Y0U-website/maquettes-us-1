Care Philosophy

My approach to oral health involves a comprehensive evaluation prior to a thorough discussion of all treatment options available for each patient. Once a patient has an understanding of the options, they are then able to decide which option best addresses their concerns and goals to obtain or maintain oral health and a beautiful, confident smile.

- Dr. Judith K. Schmidt, DDS, P.C.

Meet The Founder

Dr. Schmidt attended Minot State University for biology, chemistry, and economics. She then entered the University of Minnesota School Of Dentistry and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

In pursuit of additional education and experience treating patients with more complex medical histories, to provide a full range of extraction services, the surgical placement of implants, and to provide moderate sedation, she applied and was accepted as the intern for a one year Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Internship at the VA Medical Center in Minneapolis, MN.

The additional education and training she received during that year enabled her to provide additional services to the residents of the Wahpeton, Breckenridge and the surrounding communities.

Dr. Schmidt is a Member of Several Distinguished Organizations

Dr. Schmidt is also passionate about providing her services to various dental mission organizations in our region. When not in the clinic, she enjoys her family, friends, and many outdoor activities.

Advanced dental technology

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)

This low-radiation scan is especially useful for dental implants, root canals, TMJ evaluation, and complex cases, giving us unparalleled insight that traditional X-rays simply can’t provide.

Smile Cloud

This innovative digital platform allows us to design you dream smile by combining facial analysis, dental proportions, and aesthetic principles. You’ll be able to preview your new smile before treatment even begins!

Trios Scanner

A state-of-the-art digital tool that captures highly accurate 3D images of your teeth and bite. The Trios Scanner is faster, more comfortable, and more precise than traditional molds.

Solea Laser

Our state-of-the-art clinic is equipped with 3 general operatories, 1 surgical suite, and 3 hygiene rooms. We utilize medical-grade sterilization, digital radiography, 3D imaging, advanced digital software & more.
