Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are prosthetic structures that function as artificial tooth roots to replace missing teeth. Most implants are made of titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gums where a tooth is missing.

The nature of your implant procedure depends on the condition of your jawbone and the type of implant being placed. Dental implants provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth and can restore both the appearance and function of your smile. Our Anaheim dental office is committed to helping patients enjoy the long-term benefits of implant dentistry.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry involves dental procedures focused on repairing or restoring damaged oral structures. This may include treatments such as tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants, and other restorative solutions. As a trusted dentist in Anaheim, CA, Dr. Christine creates personalized treatment plans to help patients regain the health, function, and appearance of their smiles.

Whitening

Professional teeth whitening can be used to improve tooth discoloration caused by staining, aging, or chemical damage. Using advanced whitening technology, we offer a safe and effective way to create a brighter, more confident smile.

In cases of severe discoloration, crowns or veneers may be recommended. However, because whitening treatments are both affordable and effective, professional teeth whitening is often an excellent first step for enhancing your smile.

Gum Disease / Periodontics

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, is a bacterial infection that affects the gums and supporting structures around the teeth. It is most often caused by plaque and tartar buildup when proper brushing and flossing habits are not maintained.

To help treat gum disease, we offer scaling and root planing, a non-surgical deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque and bacteria below the gum line where regular brushing and flossing cannot reach. This treatment is often recommended when gum disease progresses from gingivitis to periodontitis and helps protect the health of your gums and teeth.

Our team provides emergency dental care in Anaheim, CA and offers same-day emergency appointments whenever possible. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, contact our office right away for immediate assistance.
