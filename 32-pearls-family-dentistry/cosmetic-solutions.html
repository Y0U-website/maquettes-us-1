Cosmetic Solutions

Whether you’re looking to restore, enhance, or personalize your smile, we offer cosmetic solutions tailored to your goals.

From porcelain veneers and cosmetic bonding to tooth gems and custom grills, we’ll help you achieve a smile that reflects your confidence and personal style.

Porcelain veneers Porcelain veneers are custom-crafted, thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your smile.

They can correct discoloration, worn teeth, chips, small gaps, and minor irregularities while providing a natural-looking result.

Cosmetic bonding

Cosmetic bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair chips, close small spaces, reshape teeth, and enhance the appearance of your smile. It is a conservative treatment that can often be completed in a single visit.

Tooth Gems Express your style with professionally placed tooth gems.

Whether you’re looking for a subtle sparkle or a bold statement, tooth gems offer a fun, non-invasive way to personalize your smile without permanently altering your natural teeth.

Ready to Add Some Sparkle? Professionally placed tooth gems are a fun, non-invasive way to personalize your smile. Available as a stand-alone appointment or an add-on to your dental cleaning.
