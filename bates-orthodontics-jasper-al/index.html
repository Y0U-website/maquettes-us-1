The practice

Ages 7-10 : Better known as “Phase I” - to address skeletal and dental issues in children before all permanent teeth are in to guide jaw growth and tooth alignment, potentially simplifying future treatment

Ages 11+ : To correct malocclusions like spacing, crowding, overbites, & underbites to improve both function and aesthetics. Often results in better chewing, speech, and oral health, along with a more aesthetically pleasing and confident smile

Ages 18+ : Working with an oral surgeon, to address severe malocclusion (improper bite) and skeletal jaw deformities that cannot be corrected with orthodontic treatment alone

After graduating from high school, a general dentist completes 4 years of college and 4 years of dental school.

An orthodontist completes those same 4 years of college and 4 years of dental school, but they continue after dental school for another 2-3 years to specifically study the specialty of orthodontics.
