Comprehensive Evaluation and Bone Assessment

We begin with a thorough examination using advanced imaging to evaluate your jawbone density and determine the best placement strategy for your specific situation. Our team of general dentists reviews your medical history and discusses whether you’re a candidate for single tooth replacement with a crown, multiple teeth restoration with a bridge, or full arch reconstruction with implant-supported dentures. This detailed assessment ensures we select the most appropriate implant solution for your unique needs.

Customized Implant Placement with Comfort Options

During your implant surgery, we use local anesthesia to keep you comfortable throughout the straightforward procedure that places the titanium post directly into your jawbone. We also offer nitrous oxide sedation to help you stay calm and relaxed during the process, especially if you’re receiving multiple implants for bridge support or full arch replacement. Our minimally invasive technique promotes faster healing while ensuring precise placement for optimal long-term success.

Monitored Healing and Bone Integration Period

After your implant placement, you’ll need to wait several months for the titanium post to fuse with your bone tissue through osseointegration, creating an exceptionally sturdy foundation. Our team of general dentists schedules regular follow-up appointments to monitor your healing progress and ensure the implant is integrating properly with your jawbone. This healing phase is crucial for developing the strong connection that makes your implant feel secure and comfortable like a natural tooth root.

Final Restoration Attachment and Function Testing

Once your implant has fully integrated, we attach your custom-made restoration, whether it’s a single crown, fixed bridge for multiple teeth, or implant-supported denture for full arch replacement. We carefully test your bite and chewing function to ensure your new teeth work harmoniously with your existing teeth and feel completely natural. This final phase transforms your implant from a simple post into a fully functional tooth replacement.

Ongoing Maintenance and Long-Term Care Guidance

Your completed dental implants require the same regular maintenance as your natural teeth, simply brush twice daily, floss regularly, and maintain your routine cleanings and checkups with our practice. We provide specific instructions for caring for your particular restoration type, whether it’s a single implant crown or multiple implants supporting a bridge or denture. This straightforward care routine ensures your implants remain healthy and functional for many years to come.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that we surgically place into your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots, creating a permanent foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. The titanium material bonds with your natural bone through osseointegration, making the implant as stable as your original teeth. This tooth replacement method restores both the visible portion and root structure of your missing teeth, unlike other options that only address what you see above the gum line.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, your dental implants can function for decades or even a lifetime since the titanium post becomes permanently integrated with your jawbone. The crown or restoration attached to your implant may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear, but the implant foundation typically remains intact. Following our care instructions and maintaining excellent oral health significantly extends the lifespan of your entire tooth replacement system.

Why avoid dairy after a dental implant?

Avoiding dairy products temporarily after your implant surgery helps promote optimal healing and reduces the risk of complications at the surgical site. Dairy can potentially interfere with blood clot formation and may increase bacterial activity in your mouth during the critical initial healing period. Following our post-operative dietary guidelines, including the temporary dairy restriction, helps ensure successful osseointegration and protects your investment in tooth replacement.
