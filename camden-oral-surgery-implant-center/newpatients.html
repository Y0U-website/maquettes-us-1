New Patients

  • Obtain basic vitals including Blood Pressure, Pulse, and Breathing rates and gather any additional medical information required.
  • Review your referral form sent from your regular dentist.
  • Review any existing x-rays sent from your dentist, or any x-rays you have with you.
  • Obtain any new x-rays that may be required.
  • Examine you and diagnose your condition.
  • Explain the treatment options that are available to you.
  • Discuss the risks and benefits of those options with you.
  • Prepare a cost estimate with your insurance.
  • Determine with your personal costs may be.
  • Schedule your next appointment for your procedure.

Procedures…

All our scheduled procedures are performed from 8:00am to 12:00pm Monday-Friday due to the requirements for an empty stomach. All I.V. sedation procedures require an adult escort to drive home.

PLEASE HAVE NOTHING ELSE SCHEDULED BEFORE OR AFTER YOUR PROCEDURE THAT DAY.

YOU SHOULD COME DIRECTLY TO THE OFFICE THAT MORNING AND GO DIRECTLY HOME TO RECOVER AFTER YOUR PROCEDURE.

  • Greet you and welcome you when you arrive.
  • Review your planned procedure and collect any payments up-front based on your estimate and financial agreement signed at your consultation
  • Seat you in the treatment room
  • Verify you by name and date of birth
  • Review your medical history to ensure no changes and nothing to eat status
  • Review your signed informed consent with you
  • Attach all the necessary heart monitors and apply gentle oxygen support.
  • The doctor will then anesthetize you with dental injections (if your procedure is to be performed awake) or initiate an I.V. for delivery of sedation medications (if your procedure is to be performed asleep.)
  • Perform a final time out safety check to verify you by name, date of birth, and the procedure.
  • The doctor will safely perform your procedure.
  • Recover you in the treatment room for a few minutes after your procedure.
  • If you underwent I.V. sedation, we will then transfer you to a recovery room with monitors for a minimum of 30 minutes of post-operative observation.
  • Once you are aware and awake and comfortable, we will discuss any post-operative instructions with you and provide with written hand-outs, icer packs, gauze, and any necessary items for you and provide you either paper written prescriptions or send your digital prescriptions directly to your pharmacy of choice.
  • We will provide you with a follow-up appointment if necessary (not all procedures require a follow-up appointment for check-up)
