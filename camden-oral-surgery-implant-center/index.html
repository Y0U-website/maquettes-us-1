All patients are required to undergo a full consultation with our team first before any care is rendered.

We do not routinely perform walk-in surgery extractions and procedures for most patients. This is intended to protect all our patients from the risk of inadequate informed consent where the patient does not fully understand the medical and financial considerations involved, and they are not prepared medically or financially for the care they require. If you wish to have the procedure performed the same day of your consultation, you must inform the office when you call to set up your initial appointment. We will not expect to perform your procedure at the same time of your consultation unless you tell us in advance and we are prepared.
