What is All-On-X? A procedure that involves placing multiple implants into your jaw bone in order to support a full arch prosthesis. Four or more dental implants are utilized to replace multiple missing and damaged teeth. The prosthesis is usually screwed in to the implants and helps restore and rehabilitate normal oral function and aesthetics.
