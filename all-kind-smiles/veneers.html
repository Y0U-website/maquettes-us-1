Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a popular cosmetic dentistry option for patients who want to improve the appearance of their smiles. Veneers can address concerns such as discoloration, chips, small gaps, uneven teeth, and certain issues with tooth shape or alignment while creating a natural-looking result.

If you’re considering porcelain veneers in West Allis, the All Kind Smiles team can help you understand how veneers work and whether they’re the right cosmetic treatment for your smile.

What Are Porcelain Veneers?

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells typically made from porcelain or another high-quality dental ceramic. They are designed to cover the front surface of a tooth and improve its color, shape, size, or overall appearance.

Because each veneer is customized for the individual patient, the shape and shade can be selected to complement your surrounding teeth and create a balanced, natural-looking smile.

What Can Veneers Correct?

Porcelain veneers are versatile and may be used to address several cosmetic concerns, including:

Veneers aren’t the right solution for every cosmetic concern. Depending on your teeth and your goals, treatments such as dental bonding, Invisalign, teeth whitening, or dental crowns may be more appropriate.

  • Stained or discolored teeth that don’t respond well to whitening.
  • Chipped or worn teeth.
  • Small gaps between teeth.
  • Uneven or irregularly shaped teeth.
  • Minor alignment concerns.
  • Teeth that appear too small or disproportionate to your smile.

Benefits of Porcelain Veneers

  • Natural appearance – Porcelain closely resembles the translucency and appearance of natural tooth enamel.
  • Customized for your smile – Each veneer is designed to complement the size, shape, and color of your surrounding teeth.
  • Stain resistant – Porcelain is highly resistant to staining from common foods and beverages.
  • Durable – With good oral hygiene and regular dental care, porcelain veneers can provide beautiful results for many years.
  • Addresses multiple concerns – Veneers can improve several aspects of a tooth’s appearance with a single restoration.

What Is the Process for Getting Veneers?

The process begins with a cosmetic consultation. Our team will evaluate your teeth and gums, discuss what you’d like to change about your smile, and determine whether veneers are an appropriate treatment option.

If you decide to move forward, a small amount of enamel may be removed from the front surface of the teeth being treated. This creates room for the veneers and helps them fit naturally within your smile.

Impressions or digital scans are then taken so your custom veneers can be created. Temporary veneers may be placed while your final restorations are being made.

Once your veneers are ready, you’ll return to All Kind Smiles so we can evaluate their shape, shade, fit, and appearance. After any necessary adjustments are made, the veneers are securely bonded to your teeth.

How Do I Care for Porcelain Veneers?

Veneers don’t require complicated maintenance. Good everyday oral hygiene is one of the best ways to protect both your veneers and the natural teeth underneath them.

If you grind or clench your teeth, our team may also recommend a protective nightguard to help reduce stress on your veneers and natural teeth.

  • Brush your teeth twice each day.
  • Floss daily around your veneers and natural teeth.
  • Visit All Kind Smiles regularly for dental exams and professional cleanings.
  • Avoid biting directly into extremely hard foods or objects.
  • Don’t use your teeth to open packages, tear materials, or perform other tasks they weren’t designed to do.

Are Porcelain Veneers Permanent?

Porcelain veneers are considered a long-term cosmetic treatment. Because placing traditional veneers typically requires removing a small amount of natural enamel, the process is generally not reversible.

Veneers themselves will not necessarily last forever. Like other dental restorations, they can eventually wear, chip, or require replacement. With proper care, however, porcelain veneers can provide long-lasting cosmetic results.

If you’re interested in changing your smile but aren’t comfortable with an irreversible treatment, dental bonding or another cosmetic option may be worth considering. Our team can explain the advantages and limitations of each treatment during your consultation.

Do I Need Veneers on All of My Teeth?

No. The number of veneers you need depends entirely on your smile and what you’d like to change.

Some patients may need only one veneer to improve the appearance of a chipped, discolored, or irregularly shaped tooth. Others may choose several veneers to create a more consistent appearance across the teeth that show when they smile.

During your cosmetic consultation, we’ll discuss your goals and recommend a treatment plan based on your individual smile rather than a predetermined number of veneers.
