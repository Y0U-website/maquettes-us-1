Cosmetic Dentistry

If you’re unhappy with the color, shape, spacing, or overall appearance of your teeth, cosmetic dentistry can help you feel more confident about your smile. At All Kind Smiles, we offer a variety of cosmetic dental treatments designed to enhance your natural smile while keeping your individual goals in mind.

Whether you’re interested in a brighter smile, straighter teeth, or changing the appearance of individual teeth, our team can help you explore your options and create a treatment plan that’s right for you.

Free Cosmetic Dentistry Consultation

Want to improve your smile but aren’t sure where to start? All Kind Smiles offers complimentary cosmetic dentistry consultations to help you understand your options.

During your consultation, we’ll talk about what you’d like to change about your smile, evaluate your teeth and oral health, and discuss treatments that may help you achieve your goals. There’s no one-size-fits-all approach to cosmetic dentistry, so we’ll work with you to develop a plan based on your smile, priorities, and budget.

What Is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth and smile. Depending on your needs, treatment can address concerns such as discoloration, uneven or chipped teeth, gaps, worn teeth, and tooth alignment.

At All Kind Smiles, cosmetic treatment options may include professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, Invisalign clear aligners, tooth-colored fillings, dental bonding, and complete smile makeovers.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Food, beverages, aging, and everyday life can gradually cause teeth to become stained or discolored. Professional teeth whitening offers a convenient way to brighten your smile and achieve more predictable results than many over-the-counter products.

All Kind Smiles offers both in-office teeth whitening and custom take-home whitening options, allowing you to choose the approach that works best for your smile and lifestyle.

Porcelain Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, custom-made restorations placed over the front surfaces of teeth to improve their appearance. Veneers can address several cosmetic concerns at once, including discoloration, small gaps, chips, worn teeth, and irregular tooth shape.

Each veneer is designed to complement your surrounding teeth and create a natural-looking result that fits your smile.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign offers a discreet alternative to traditional braces for many patients who want a straighter smile. Instead of metal brackets and wires, Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into their desired positions.

The aligners are removable and nearly invisible when worn, making Invisalign a popular option for adults and teens who want to straighten their teeth without traditional braces.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings restore teeth affected by cavities or minor damage while blending naturally with your smile. Composite material can be matched to the color of your surrounding teeth, making the restoration much less noticeable than traditional silver-colored fillings.

Composite fillings also bond directly to the tooth, allowing our team to preserve as much healthy tooth structure as possible during treatment.

Dental Bonding, Shaping, and Contouring

Dental bonding is a simple and versatile cosmetic treatment that uses tooth-colored composite resin to improve the appearance of a tooth. It may be used to repair small chips, close minor gaps, improve tooth shape, or conceal certain imperfections.

Shaping and contouring may also be used to make subtle adjustments to the edges or shape of teeth, helping create a more balanced and natural-looking smile.

Complete Smile Makeovers

If you have several things you’d like to change about your smile, a smile makeover may combine multiple cosmetic and restorative treatments into one personalized plan.

Our team will evaluate your oral health, listen to what you want to accomplish, and discuss the treatment options available to you. From subtle improvements to a more comprehensive transformation, we’ll help you create a plan that fits your goals and budget.
