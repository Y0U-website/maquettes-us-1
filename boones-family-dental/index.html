The practice

Dr. Brown worked at the office in 2015 before she purchased the office and renamed it Boones Family Dental. She loved the area and the relationships she established with her patients. She married her high school sweet heart and has two beautiful children. The family enjoys camping and hiking. Their favorite place is Lost Lake in the Mt. Hood National Forest.

Dr. Zenger (AKA Dr. Z) is Oregon-raised. She attended Grant High School, where she discovered her interest in dentistry during a weekend elective. She spent 4 years in Salem at Willamette University before coming back to Portland to complete her dental degree at OHSU. Her love for treating and caring for patients has only grown.

Outside the office, Dr. Z spends time with her husband, twin baby boys and her dog. The family likes to get outside to enjoy what the PNW has to offer, from Mt. Hood to the coast and in between. Their favorite activities are hiking, fishing and sipping a good cup of coffee on a rainy beach day.

We provide a variety of oral appliances for obstructive sleep apnea also called mandibular repositioner device. Our dentist is qualified through American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine.

Our goal is to provide a positive experience for all our patients through compassion, listening, understanding and education. We treat our patients how we would want to be treated.
