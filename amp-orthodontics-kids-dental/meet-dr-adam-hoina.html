Founder, ABO-Board Certified Orthodontist, Family Dentist Dr. Adam Hoina, the heart and smile behind Amp Orthodontics & Kids Dental, has dedicated his career to crafting healthy, confident smiles with a focus on holistic, minimally invasive techniques and personalized care. Educational Background: Bachelor of Science in Biology from SUNY Stony Brook University
  • Master of Science in Physiology and Biophysics from SUNY Stony Brook University
  • Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine
  • Residency in General Practice at the Northport Veterans Administration Medical Center
  • Residency in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at Tufts University

Professional Experience:

  • ABO-Board Certified in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics
  • Extensive experience in orthodontics for all ages and family dentistry for babies, children, and teens
  • Devoted to crafting personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s unique needs

Practice Philosophy:

While his credentials highlight his ABO-Board certified expertise in orthodontics, Dr. Adam’s heart reserves a special place for general dentistry for children. His broad-spectrum practice encompasses not only detailed orthodontic treatments for both adults and children, but also emphasizes quality general dentistry for the young ones, ensuring our littlest patients feel safe, seen, and smiley!

  • Focuses on a preventive , conservative, holistic approach to oral health
  • Passionate about modern advancements in biomimetic and minimally invasive dental techniques
  • Emphasizes a curated, convenient, and customized approach to orthodontic care and general dentistry for kids
  • Strives to create a relaxing, serene environment for all patients

Professional Affiliations:

  • Member of the American Association of Orthodontists
  • Actively engaged in continuing education to stay at the forefront of orthodontics and dental care advancements
