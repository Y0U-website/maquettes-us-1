The practice

Since its founding in 2006, we have remained dedicated to providing exceptional, high-quality, patient-centered dental care to North Port, Florida, and the surrounding communities. Our team provides a full spectrum of dental services from routine cleanings, preventive care, restorative treatments, cosmetic enhancements, and beyond, all tailored to meet the unique needs of patients of every age.

For nearly two decades, our passion has been perfecting smiles with expertise, compassion, and personalized attention. By integrating the latest technologies and advanced treatment methods, we ensure every patient experience receives the most effective, personalized dental care that is gentle, welcoming, and stress-free at every visit.

We value honesty, transparency, compassion, and precision, making Aaron Memon, DMD one of the most highly rated dental offices in the North Port area. We put our hearts into our work every day and consider it an honor and privilege to take care of you.

Dr. Aaron Memon has been helping patients smile with confidence since graduating from Nova Southeastern College of Dental Medicine in 2005. Just a year later, he opened his own practice with one goal in mind: to create a warm, welcoming space where patients feel comfortable, cared for, and informed every step of the way.

Known for his friendly personality and gentle approach, Dr. Memon takes pride in making every visit as stress-free as possible. He offers a full range of dental services—from routine cleanings and preventive care to restorative treatments and cosmetic enhancements—always tailored to meet each patient’s unique needs.

Over the years, Dr. Memon has built strong, lasting relationships with his patients by listening closely, answering questions, and creating personalized treatment plans. His focus on open communication and trust has earned him a loyal following and a reputation for compassionate care.

Beyond the office, Dr. Memon is actively involved in professional dental organizations and community outreach programs. When he’s not caring for smiles, he enjoys spending time with his family, golfing, and hiking!
