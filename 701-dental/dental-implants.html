Dental Implants in Bismarck, ND

Dental implants are a revolutionary treatment in the field of dentistry, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth. These implants are designed to mimic the structure of natural teeth, with a titanium post acting as the tooth root and a custom-made crown functioning as the visible part of the tooth. Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, offering a sturdy and durable base for the crown. This treatment is highly effective and offers a plethora of benefits, making it the go-to solution for many patients in Bismarck, ND, who are looking to restore their smile.

Why Should I Get Dental Implants?

If you&#x27;re missing one or more teeth, dental implants offer a long-term solution that closely resembles your natural teeth. Unlike dentures , which can be uncomfortable and require daily removal and cleaning, dental implants are permanent and blend seamlessly with your existing teeth. They not only restore the aesthetic appeal of your smile but also improve your oral health by preventing bone loss in the jaw. Moreover, dental implants allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence, enhancing your overall quality of life.

The Benefits Of Dental Implants

Improved Oral Health

Unlike other tooth replacement options, dental implants don&#x27;t require altering other teeth, leaving more of your natural teeth intact, which is beneficial for your long-term oral health. Additionally, they prevent bone loss in the jaw, which can occur when you have missing teeth.

Enhanced Appearance

Dental implants look and feel like your own teeth. Because they are designed to fuse with bone, they become permanent. This offers a natural look and enhances your smile, boosting your confidence and self-esteem.

Easier Eating

Sliding dentures can make chewing difficult. Dental implants function like your own teeth, allowing you to eat your favorite foods with confidence and without pain.

The Dental Implant Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

In the first step, Dr. Alexa Carlson will conduct a comprehensive oral examination, which may include X-rays, to determine if you&#x27;re a suitable candidate for dental implants. She will discuss your dental health history, your needs, and your treatment goals. Dr. Carlson will also assess your bone density and gum health, as these are crucial factors for successful implant placement. She&#x27;ll create a personalized treatment plan and discuss timeline expectations, costs, and any necessary preparatory procedures like bone grafting.

Implant Placement

During this step, the dental implant, a small titanium post, is surgically placed into the bone socket of the missing tooth. As the jawbone heals, it grows around the implanted metal post, securing it in the jaw. This healing process, called osseointegration, typically takes 3-6 months and forms the strong foundation needed for your new tooth. Local anesthesia ensures your comfort during the procedure, and most patients can return to work the next day.

Abutment and Crown Placement

Once the implant has bonded to the jawbone, an abutment, or a small connector post, is attached to the post to securely hold the new tooth. Then, a crown, which is made to match your natural teeth, is attached to the abutment. Before placing the permanent crown, Dr. Carlson will ensure proper fit, bite alignment, and color matching with your surrounding teeth. With proper care, your dental implant can last a lifetime while looking and functioning just like a natural tooth.
