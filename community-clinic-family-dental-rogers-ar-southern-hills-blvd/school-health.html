Back-to-school resources

Arkansas required vaccines by grade level. Make sure your student is covered before the first day.

Kindergarten – 1st Grade

  • DTAP: at least 4 doses, one on or after 4th birthday
  • Polio (IPV): at least 3 doses, one on or after 4th birthday
  • MMR: 2 doses
  • Hepatitis B: 3 doses
  • Hepatitis A: at least 1 dose, on or after 1st birthday
  • Varicella (Chickenpox): 2 doses or documented proof of disease

2nd – 12th Grade

  • DTAP: 4 doses (one on or after 4th birthday); 3 doses if unvaccinated and age 7 or older
  • Polio (IPV): 3 doses, one on or after 4th birthday
  • MMR: 2 doses
  • Hepatitis B: 3 doses
  • Varicella: 2 doses or documented proof of disease
  • Tdap: 1 dose required at age 11
  • Meningococcal: 1 dose at age 11, 2nd dose at age 16 (if 1st dose given after 16, only 1 dose needed)
