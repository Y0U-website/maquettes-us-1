Dental Anxiety

Many people put off or avoid going to the dentist altogether. For three out of four patients, this can be due to dental anxiety. A poor prior experience at the dental office can have negative effects on the mind and body and deferred dental care can be tough to fix. At Auburn Dental Group, we work hard to educate the patient and talk you through each procedure. We are happy to work with you to set a comfortable pace for treatment.
