The practice

Children’s Dentistry of Athens focuses on preventative care to help your child grow a healthy smile that will last a lifetime. We use proven, state-of-the-art technology to treat dental problems with a conservative approach whenever possible. Our goal is to ensure that every child we serve achieves a healthy and beautiful smile.

Serving the children of Athens and McMinn County, Sweetwater, Madisonville, Decatur, Cleveland, and the nearby counties, we are excited to meet you and care for your child’s dental needs! For more information or to address any questions you may have, be sure to check out our FAQs .

Dr. Adam loves watching his patients grow up and being a part of special milestones in their lives!
