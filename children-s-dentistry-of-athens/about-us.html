What Makes Us Unique

At Children’s Dentistry of Athens, you can be confident in the dental office you have chosen for your child. From your first visit, we want you to feel welcomed and well cared for.

Our dentist and staff promote preventative dental care including a healthy diet and great oral hygiene at every visit. Because of this, we encourage parents to be active participants in each and every visit with their child, so we reinforce this education with all members of the family. This gives us the opportunity to educate you as well so you can help reinforce these healthy habits at home.

For Your Child

We know that each individual child will require unique care and circumstances. We are excited to get to know your child and learn what works for their unique personalities. This way, we are able to tailor appointments and procedures in a way that works for your child so that they can’t wait to visit our office for their dental cleanings and checkups!

We want to provide your children with healthy, and cavity-free childhoods, join our family and schedule an appointment with us.
