Implant Supported Bridges

An implant supported bridge may be the ideal treatment if you are missing three or more teeth in a row. As part of a restorative dentistry treatment plan, we do not need to remove healthy tooth structure from the adjacent teeth as we would with a traditional bridge. Instead, we can anchor the bridge on strong, durable dental implants for maximum functionality, aesthetics, dental health, and longevity.

Implant Supported Dentures

Dentures have long been the most popular option for replacing most or all of the teeth. However, they can be bulky and prone to slipping. Just two dental implants can stabilize a removable “snap on” denture, while just a handful can support a complete, fixed set of replacement teeth on the upper or lower arch.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Can you get dental implants years after extraction?

Yes, you can get dental implants even if it has been several years—or even decades—since your teeth were extracted. Many patients choose to get dental implants after years of discomfort with traditional dentures. If your teeth have been missing for some time, we will need to ensure that you have sufficient bone mass in your jaw and healthy gums to support your dental implants; if not, supplementary procedures like bone grafting may be needed. Other than this, there is no reason that waiting years to get dental implants would impact your eligibility for the dental implant restorations.

What happens if you don’t have enough bone for dental implants?

When there is not enough bone in the jaw to support dental implants, there are a few possible options. There are some dental implant restorations that require less support than traditional implants—you may be a candidate for one of these solutions. Otherwise, we can use bone grafting and/or a sinus lift to augment your jawbone before dental implant surgery.

How much do dental implants cost in Pomona, NY?

The cost of dental implants in Pomona, NY depends on the type of dental implant you’re getting, whether you need a dental crown or other final restoration, and any supplementary procedures, if needed. With so many factors at play, there simply isn’t a one-size-fits-all answer when it comes to cost. During your consultation with us, we will provide you detailed pricing for your specific situation.

How painful is the dental implant procedure?

Getting dental implants is not painful at all—you will be given local anesthetic injections to ensure that you’re comfortable throughout the procedure. You may feel some pressure and movement, but not pain. Our experienced team uses advanced techniques to make the process as stress free as possible. In the hours and days after the implant surgery, you’re likely to experience some tenderness at the surgical site, but this can usually be alleviated with cold compresses, over-the-counter pain relievers, and a soft food diet.

How long does it take for dental implants to heal?

Your soft tissue (gums) should heal within two weeks of your dental implant surgery, but the healing that takes place beneath your gums requires more time. Osseointegration, or the process in which your bone and your teeth implants fuse together, can take up to six months. Once this occurs, your dental implants will have the same stability as your natural teeth, providing a stable foundation for your final restoration.

Is dental implant surgery worth it?

Yes, dental implants are worth it—just ask our patients! Dental implants are the most natural solution for missing teeth. They’re more comfortable and stable than dentures and they’re easy to care for. You’ll never have to worry about them slipping, impacting your speech, or irritating your gums. In fact, after a while, you’ll probably forget that you even have teeth implants at all. Our implant dentistry approach, combined with personalized care from our experienced dentists, ensures you receive the best solution for your new smile.
