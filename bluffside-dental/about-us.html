Meet Doctor Berger Bluffside Dental

Dr. Jeremy Berger

Dr. Berger s decision to pursue dentistry stemmed from his love of science and desire to build meaningful, personal connections with patients. Since beginning his dental career in 2016, he has enjoyed the dynamic and creative nature of the field, where each day presents unique challenges and opportunities to positively impact lives. Practicing in a small-town setting has allowed him to form lasting relationships with patients, fostering a warm, supportive environment at Bluffside Dental.

One of Dr. Berger’s favorite aspects of his work is collaborating with his dedicated team. He takes pride in the professionalism, passion, and skill each staff member brings to the practice, making every day both rewarding and inspiring.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Berger enjoys an active lifestyle. He is an avid fan of Minnesota sports, cheering on his favorite teams despite the inevitable ups and downs. He loves playing golf, hiking in the great outdoors, and diving into a good book. Above all, he treasures time spent with his wife, Danielle, and their two children, Noah and Charlotte.

A unique highlight of Dr. Berger’s life is his musical background. For three years, he was a member of the Limestones, an a cappella group at St. Olaf College. The experience fostered his love of music, collaboration, and performance, and the group still reunites for a concert every five years.

For those who may not know, Dr. Berger is also a proud Disney fanatic, always looking forward to his next magical adventure. Whether revisiting classic movies or planning trips to the theme parks, he finds joy in the wonder and nostalgia that Disney creates.

With his blend of clinical expertise, compassion, and community spirit, Dr. Jeremy Berger is committed to making every patient’s experience at Bluffside Dental comfortable and positive.
