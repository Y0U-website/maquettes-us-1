He took time to make her feel comfortable and even entertained her throughout the procedure. She is excited to go to the dentist every time!"

Dr. Jeremy Berger began his professional career as a chemist for a pharmaceutical company, but after a few years, he realized he wanted more human connection. This led him to explore dentistry because he could utilize his skills while interacting with people.

Dentistry also allowed Dr. Berger to combine the art and science of dentistry to help his patients achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Together with his caring dental team, Dr. Jeremy Berger would like to welcome you to Bluffside Dental, which he co-founded in 2018 after purchasing the practice of Dr. Gary Erickson, a long-time dentist in Red Wing.

At Bluffside Dental, we’re proud to be part of the Red Wing community; we embrace our small-town values and work hard to get to know you and your family. As soon as you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted with a friendly smile and a warm welcome.

Any stress you may have been feeling will melt away when we seat you comfortably in your treatment room and answer your questions. If you’re still feeling anxious, we offer nitrous oxide to keep you calm and help you get the treatment you deserve.

We’re dedicated to ensuring you have a positive dental experience, and we’ll do whatever we can to make that happen.
