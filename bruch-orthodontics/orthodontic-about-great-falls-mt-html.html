About Bruch Orthodontics in Great Falls, MT

At the office of Dr. Christopher Bruch, we offer a range of affordable orthodontic services to help our patients achieve the smile they have always wanted. No matter your age or your budget, we have an option that will work for you.

We work hard to provide a warm and welcoming environment for every patient. Dr. Bruch takes the time to discuss one-on-one with every patient about their options, and what would be best for their overall individual situation. We schedule as much time as needed to discuss these options, so our patients never feel rushed or pressured to accept treatment.

Our patients leave happy, knowing they are receiving the best orthodontic care from a team that truly cares about them. We work to fit in appointments that are convenient for our patients and works around their schedule, as much as possible.

Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Christopher G. Bruch and his wife Laura were born in Utah. They have two daughters who grew up in Great Falls, and two granddaughters. Dr. Bruch did his undergraduate study at Weber State University, graduating with honors. He attended Creighton University School of Dentistry, graduating with high honors, and awards for both academic and clinical excellence.

He then completed a residency at Indiana University, becoming a certified orthodontic specialist. Clinical faculty gave Dr. Bruch the Farnsworth Award for Excellence in patient care. He stays current by attending continuing orthodontic education every year.

Dr. Bruch and his family came to Great Falls and began practicing in 1992. They moved into their current office at Broadwater Station in 2006. Dr. Bruch has an outstanding staff with an incredible amount of experience. We are very proud of the fact that we have virtually no staff turnover, allowing patients to develop a relationship with those assisting in their care. Orthodontics is what we do and what we love.

Outside the office Dr. Bruch likes to hunt with his three labs, fish, float, play basketball, and do things with Laura, his girls, and granddaughters.
