Braces that Reflect Your Personality & Lifestyle

Everyone is different. We all have unique faces, jaws, teeth and smiles. One size does not fit all. At Bruch Orthodontics , we offer a wide variety of services and treatments to fit each person’s individual needs and desires. No referral is needed to make an appointment to come in for an initial evaluation. Our best referrals come from our patients.

We use the clinical examination, digital radiographs, and patient input to determine the areas that need to be addressed, and develop a treatment plan to achieve the optimum result. It may include braces to align teeth, an appliance to bring jaws into balance, or even the option of orthognathic jaw surgery.
