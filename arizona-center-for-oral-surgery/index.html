Arizona Center for Oral Surgery is located in Glendale, Arizona and proudly serves patients from across the Valley.

The practice

Our board-certified oral surgeons provide personalized, whole-patient care evaluating each patient’s overall health, comfort, and long-term goals, not just their teeth. Every treatment plan is thoughtfully customized to deliver safe, effective, and predictable outcomes.

We offer advanced healing solutions such as PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) to support faster recovery and help reduce complications like dry socket following extractions. Our doctors are recognized leaders in this field and have published clinical research on the use of PRF in oral surgery.

Arizona Center for Oral Surgery was also the first practice in the Valley to offer Teeth-in-A-Day, providing patients with immediate, life-changing smile restoration.
