Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4® Dental Implants

All-on-4® is a full-arch tooth replacement treatment concept that uses four strategically placed dental implants to support a complete arch of replacement teeth. The implants provide a stable foundation for the restoration and can offer an alternative to traditional removable dentures.

Traditional dentures are removable and rest directly on the gums. All-on-4® restorations are supported by dental implants, which provide a more secure foundation and help reduce the movement that can occur with conventional removable dentures.

Many patients who are missing most or all of their teeth may be candidates for All-on-4®. However, candidacy depends on several factors, including your oral health, jawbone structure, existing teeth, and overall health. An examination and appropriate imaging are needed to determine whether this treatment is right for you.

In appropriate cases, a temporary full-arch restoration may be attached to the implants on the same day as surgery. This is sometimes referred to as a same-day smile or Teeth-in-a-Day. Your treatment timeline will depend on your individual situation and implant stability.

Some swelling, discomfort, and dietary restrictions are normal during the initial healing period. You will receive detailed post-operative instructions from our team to help support your recovery. Follow-up appointments will allow us to monitor your healing and implant integration.

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term tooth replacement solution. Their longevity depends on factors such as oral hygiene, regular professional care, overall health, and proper maintenance of the restoration. The prosthetic teeth may require adjustment, repair, or replacement over time.

The cost of All-on-4® treatment varies depending on your individual treatment plan, the type of restoration selected, and whether additional procedures are necessary. During your consultation, our team can discuss your recommended treatment and provide information about the associated costs and payment options.
