What are dental implants?

Dental implants are small, sturdy, titanium posts that act like a natural tooth’s root structure. Titanium metal is used because it&#x27;s naturally compatible with bone and oral tissues. The implant will be placed into your upper or lower jaw bone and over time the bone will naturally grow around the implant, allowing for crowns, bridges, or overdentures to be placed if necessary.

Why dental implants?

Not only do they restore the equilibrium of your mouth structure, but dental implants also make chewing and speaking easier. Implants are natural looking, giving you a complete smile that yields confidence and leaves an impression. They’re also durable as long as they’re given the same maintenance as your natural teeth with proper brushing, flossing and regular checkups.
