Meet The Doctors

Dr. Helsley

Dr. Zackery Helsley is compassionate about providing excellent dental care in the most comfortable and kind atmosphere possible. He is dedicated to lifelong learning and continued education to give the best diagnosis and treatment he can provide. He is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA)and Indiana State Dental Association (IDA).

Training:

Dr. Helsley obtained his Indiana Dental License and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2016. He is trained inCEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramic) technology allowing an option to provide the same day full coverage crown restoration. He began his dental career as an Active Duty commissioned officer in the UnitedStates Army learning from doctors from across the country until he honorably discharged in 2021.

At The Office:

Dr. Zack Helsley loves being a dentist as the profession of dentistry uniquely offers a chance to change people&#x27;s lives through a beautiful, healthy and comfortable smile. He believes eating comfortably and effectively improves your overall health and happiness. He also believes that your smile leaves a lasting impression almost as memorable as what you say when you first meet someone.Improving your appearance increases your self-confidence and improves your quality of life. Dr. Zack looks forward to helping you and your family continue to enjoy life as much as possible!

Outside Of The Office:

Dr.Zack loves to enjoy nature with his family when he is away from the office. He is supported at home by his wife, son and daughter that all love the peace and beauty provided by spending time in the great outdoors! If he is not on the water or out in the woods you can find Zack working with Boy Scouts of America, volunteering to pull parade floats for sports teams, or helping at a community church event. Growing up in 4-H and FFA he also desires to spend more time supporting these organizations as his kids grow.
