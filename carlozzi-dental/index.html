0 A Family Practice. The History 1935: Twin brothers, John and Louis Carlozzi were born in Cleveland and 25 years later graduated from Case Western Reserve Dental School.

John and Lou served 2 years as officers in the Navy as dentists.

1962: The twins returned to Cleveland and established a dental practice on the first floor of their family home at 2022 Noble Road.

1970: The office moved to a new location at 5001 Mayfield Road.

1998: Kevin M. Carlozzi joined the dental practice and worked with his brother John until 2010.

2022: Dr. John and his team are excited about moving into the next chapter of Carlozzi Dental at our new location 5231 Mayfield Road.
