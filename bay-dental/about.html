About Bay Dental

Dentists located in St. George and Great Kills, Staten Island, NY

With two convenient locations in Staten Island, New York, Bay Dental is one of the area’s largest dental facilities. Vincent Circosta, DDS, deliver the highest quality of care to their patients by taking advantage of state-of-the-art technology, such as digital impressions with an intraoral scanner and cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans.

From routine teeth cleanings to All-on-X dental implants, the Bay Dental team treats each patient with the individualized care they deserve. The entire team has specialized training in general, restorative , and cosmetic dentistry to ensure their patients feel confident and secure in their smiles.

Advanced technology and dental equipment allow Bay Dental to provide comprehensive treatment for all types of dental concerns, including periodontal disease and tooth decay. The practice is home to top-notch dental restorations, like crowns , bridges , and dental implants .

Convenient hours and a knowledgeable staff mean patients can always ask the Bay Dental team questions regarding their dental care and oral hygiene. The staff keeps every patient updated on their upcoming visits and works with them to ensure they accommodate emergency treatment appointments.

Bay Dental welcomes new and returning patients to schedule a consultation with the team. Call the location nearest you or book an appointment online to learn more.
