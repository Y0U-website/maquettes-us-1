Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Ronald Olszewski, DDS

Dr. Ronald Olszewski, DDS obtained his degree in 1984 from the University of Michigan and is proud to serve as a dentist in Grand Rapids, MI . He’s also a member of the American Dental Association , Michigan Dental Association , Donated Dental Services, Trade Network, and the International Association for Orthodontics.

Outside of the fulfillment of being a husband and a busy father of three beautiful children, Dr. Olszewski is enthusiastic about seeing his patients leave his office with teeth restored to sound condition and, in the case of cosmetic dentistry , with an attractive smile. In his spare time, he is an avid Michigan football fan.
