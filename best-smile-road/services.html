GENERAL DENTISTRY

We provide general dental services that are a part of preventative care. General dentistry can help prevent future dental problems:

  • Pediatric Dentistry: We offer pediatric care for children in addition to our treatments for adults.
  • Root Canals: A root canal can preserve the tooth structure after an infection.
  • Tooth Extraction: If tooth decay has destroyed the structure of one’s tooth to a point where restoration is impossible, the tooth may need to be extracted. Teeth may also be extracted prior to the placement of a dental implant or to remove a wisdom tooth that is not breaking through the gums properly or threatens to crowd surrounding teeth.

RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

We can restore missing teeth, damaged teeth, and even infected gums with restorative dental care. Our restorative dental treatments include:

Dental Exams

During your routine dental exam, we will examine your teeth, gums and soft tissue for dental decay, gum disease or early signs of oral cancer to keep your smile and mouth healthy and beautiful.

Dental Bridges

A bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth in one area of your mouth allowing you to once again speak, chew and smile naturally. our patients can enjoy the oral health that they deserve. To learn more about dental bridges, or to schedule an appointment with our friendly dentist and we invite you to call or visit Best Smile Road.

We will help you determine whether a custom dental bridge is the right choice for restoring your smile after tooth loss. For more information, please call or visit us today.

  • A bridge is a fixed dental restoration, meaning it is non removable.

Tooth Crown

Your smile may benefit from a crown if a tooth is damaged or decayed to the point that a dental filling will not provide a successful restoration. Our crowns are made best quality , giving them better strength and endurance than even the original tooth. Crowns can also be used for a number of other restorative and aesthetic purposes, including to:

Support a large filling when little natural tooth structure remains to find out whether a dental crown is right for you, we welcome you to contact us today!

  • Dental crowns cover and protect teeth like caps.
  • Protect a tooth following root canal treatment
  • Anchor and attach a dental bridge
  • Cover and complete a dental implant
  • Enhance the beauty of your smile
  • Improve a misshapen tooth
  • Strengthen a tooth that is fractured or weak

Root Canal Therapy

The nerve tissue inside the tooth is housed in a hollow tube called the “root canal system,” which consists of a pulp chamber and nerve canals. There are a number of reasons a nerve can become infected:

Extensive decay, trauma, or cyst formation. Infection in this area is often associated with toothaches and the formation of puss (abscess). Experiencing severe pain? Don’t wait call us now to determine if a root canal is necessary.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are lasting, stable tooth replacements that can be used to replace one missing tooth, several missing teeth or complete arches of teeth. The dental implant post, which is a screw made of titanium, is surgically placed in the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. It replaces the missing tooth root and ensures that your dental restoration is stable. A top choice for replacing missing teeth with confidence and precision.

Dentures & Partials

Our dentists may recommend complete or partial dentures to restore your smile if you are missing multiple teeth; dentures can improve your ability to properly speak and eat, restore your natural facial volume, and enhance the overall beauty of your smile. Your dentures will be custom made to fit your smile comfortably, aesthetically and securely. We invite you to call or visit us to learn more about how dentures can improve your smile!

Periodontal Disease Treatment

Periodontal therapy covers a range of treatments aimed at combating gum disease and promoting oral health. It addresses conditions such as gingivitis and periodontitis, which can lead to gum inflammation, bleeding, and tooth loss if untreated. Common procedures include scaling and root planing, laser therapy, and surgical interventions to remove infected tissue and regenerate healthy gum tissue.

Emergency Treatment

We understand that dental emergencies can arise unexpectedly. A dental emergency is known to cause immense pain and worry, and can often restrain the patients from performing day to day activities, like walking and speaking. When you need dental treatment fast, we’ll do everything we can to accommodate your schedule and get you in as soon as possible. Simply give us a call if you have suffered from a dental injury or are in need of immediate attention.
