About

If you have been looking for a professional dental team who will take care of your needs and will listen to your dental concerns, you have found the right place at Eagle Dental Center.

The team at Eagle Dental offers all phases of dentistry, and we’re dedicated to providing you with the comfort and well-being that you deserve. Our team wants to make certain that your visits and experiences are pleasant. We understand that your smile is one of your biggest assets. At Eagle Dental Center, we proudly serve all patients in the entire four-state area!

Meet Our Dentists

Dr. Todd Hegmann

Dr. Hegmann is from Texarkana and graduated from Pleasant Grove High School. He got his DDS and MS degrees from West Virginia University in 2006 and has completed Advanced Education Programs in General Practice Residency, Prosthodontics, and Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Internship including implant placement and implant-supported dentures. On his weekends he loves to spend time with his two sons and playing golf.

Dr. Chad Patterson

Dr. Chad Patterson grew up in Texarkana, Texas. He attended Pleasant Grove from elementary to high school graduating in 1998 from Pleasant Grove High School. After high school, he continued his education at the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville, Arkansas graduating in 2002. Following college, Dr. Patterson began dental school at the University of Tennessee in Memphis, Tennessee. He is following in the footsteps of his uncle, Rowan Patterson, and grandfather, Bill Patterson, who were both dentists that served the Texarkana area.

Dr. Punit Patel

Dr. Patel is from College Station, Texas, where he attended Texas A&M University prior to dental school. He obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Southern California Dental School in Los Angeles. Dr. Patel and his wife are newly married. and have spent the last few years in Dallas and Corpus Christi where he has spent time in general practice. He is excited to be a part of the Eagle Dental family and get to know all of the great patients!
