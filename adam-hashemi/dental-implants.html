Are You An Ideal Candidate for Our Dental Implant Services?

At SparQ Family Dental, deciding if someone is an ideal patient for dental implants involves a complete evaluation process. Here are the key factors that we consider:

By assessing these factors, our team ensures that each patient is well-suited for a successful dental implant procedure.

  • Oral Health: Healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density are essential. Active gum disease or untreated dental issues must be addressed before implant treatment.
  • Overall Health: Patients should be in good health overall. They should have no uncontrolled chronic conditions like diabetes or heart disease.
  • Bone Density: An adequate jawbone is necessary for implant success. Bone grafting may be recommended if bone density is insufficient.
  • Lifestyle Factors: Smoking can increase the risk of implant failure, so reducing or quitting smoking is often advised.
  • Age Considerations: Jawbone growth should be complete, typically in late adolescence or early adulthood.
