Dentist in Erie CO

Dr. Tara Carroll

Dr. Carroll knows firsthand what a transformed smile can do. At just seven years old, she began orthodontic treatment and spent more than five years in braces. The confidence she gained inspired a career dedicated to helping others feel that same joy.

Dr Carroll s passion for dental knowledge and skills are forever evolving through dental continuing education, taking many courses in implants, cosmetic dentistry, clear aligner therapy, digital dentistry, practice management to name a few.

With more than 20 years of experience, Dr. Carroll is passionate about prevention-focused care and treating the whole patient, believing oral health is deeply connected to overall wellness. She and her team proudly serve families across Erie, Broomfield, Lafayette, Westminster, Thornton, Brighton, Firestone, Frederick, Dacono, Northglenn, and Longmont, and are active supporters of local schools, sports teams, and community events.

Outside the office, Dr. Carroll is a fitness enthusiast who runs, walks, lifts, and trains her dogs, who are frequent office visitors. She loves photography, exploring new cities and countries, and has recently discovered a talent for designing custom cabinetry and home remodeling. A fun fact: she played rugby in college.

Wonderful staff and fantastic caring treatment from everyone on the team: hygienist, dentist, and front office team.
