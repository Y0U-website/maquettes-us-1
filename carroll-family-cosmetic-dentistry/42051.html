Dental Implants Erie CO

  • Specialist Referral: Implant placement is a surgical procedure performed by an oral surgeon or periodontist. Dr. Carroll refers patients to a trusted specialist for this step and communicates with them throughout the process.
  • Implant Placement: The specialist places the titanium post into the jawbone under local anesthesia.
  • Healing Period: Osseointegration typically takes three to six months. Dr. Carroll monitors your progress, staying in contact with the specialist until confirming the implant is ready for restoration.
  • Implant Restoration: Dr. Carroll takes impressions or digital scans and places the final restoration a crown, bridge, or denture on top of the implant.
