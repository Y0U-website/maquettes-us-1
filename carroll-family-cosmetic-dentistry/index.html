An implant bridge replaces two or more missing teeth in a row, typically using two implant posts as anchors, with the replacement teeth spanning the gap between them. Unlike a traditional bridge, we do not need to alter the neighboring natural teeth.

A dental implant is the one replacement that looks, feels, and functions like a real tooth.
