Children should be evaluated by an orthodontist by the age of 7. Some children are referred to an orthodontist at a younger age because the general dentist detects a more severe problem. Most 7 year-olds do not require braces, but sometimes early skeletal or growth-modification needs to be addressed. Cross bites also require correction at a young age.

Adults are seeking orthodontic treatment now more than ever before. The benefits include, but are not limited to: increased cleansibility of the teeth, better tooth stability in the bone, spacing preparation for dental implants, crowns or veneers, improved speech, eating and obviously straight teeth!
