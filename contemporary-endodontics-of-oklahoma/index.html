We offer a full range of endodontic treatments in Edmond, OK, aided by the latest in dental technologies. Our endodontist, Dr. Ball, works closely with your general dentistry provider to ensure comprehensive care.

Cracked Teeth Repair – If you have a cracked tooth, you may experience sensitivity when you bite, chew, or eat hot or cold foods. This is because the pulp tissue is exposed. We treat damaged teeth to prevent infections, alleviate dental pain, and help you keep your natural teeth.

Les soins

Root Canals – When a cavity has progressed beyond the exterior structures of a tooth and reaches the pulp at the center, root canal treatment is needed to remove infected tissue, clean the affected tooth, and reseal the tooth with a dental filling or crown…

Dental Trauma – Dental trauma can leave teeth cracked, fractured, displaced, or even missing. We can work with other dental professionals to restore your smile and eliminate pain from exposed or irritated pulp.
