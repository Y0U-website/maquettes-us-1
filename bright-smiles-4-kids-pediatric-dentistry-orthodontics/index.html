Welcome to Bright Smiles 4 Kids

Providing great patient care is the primary goal of our doctors. From the moment you enter our practice, we want you and your child to feel like a member of the family.

The practice

We specialize in providing dental care for children and focus on creating a positive dental experience for every child. Our goal is to instill lifelong dental habits to maintain a healthy smile.

We always want our patients and their parents to feel informed and confident they are getting the best treatment possible. Please click on the links below to learn more about our practice and your child’s dental health.

During this visit, we will get to know your child and show them the tools we use to help keep their smile healthy.

We will evaluate your orthodontic needs and work with you to develop a treatment plan for your new smile.

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your appointment if you have not completed patient registration.

Help your child carefully follow any home instructions and practice good oral hygiene.
