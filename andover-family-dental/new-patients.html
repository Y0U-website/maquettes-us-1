Patient Resources

  • When should I Schedule My Child&#x27;s First Dental Appointment? Your child should see a dentist as soon as their first tooth appears or no later than their first birthday whichever comes first! This will give us the chance to make sure that their teeth and jaws are developing correctly, and when they turn 3, we’ll begin giving them regular checkups and cleanings similar to your own.
  • How Long Does It Take To Repair a Decayed/Broken Tooth? This answer will be different for every patient depending on their specific situation, but in most cases, we can repair a tooth in just one appointment. However, if a tooth requires another treatment like root canal therapy before it can safely receive a restoration, then the process may require two appointments.
  • Can You Help With a Dental Emergency? Yes! If you’re experiencing a severe toothache or your teeth have been badly damaged in an accident, go ahead and give us a call. We’ll schedule a same-day appointment so we can get you out of pain and repair your smile right away.
  • When Should I Visit the Dentist? We recommend getting at least two checkups and cleanings each year to help prevent the most common dental problems. And, if you’re experiencing any kind of dental issue, whether it be a cavity or swollen gums, we suggest giving us a call right away. The earlier we’re able to treat something, the easier, faster, and less expensive it will probably be.

  • Is There an Option Other Than Metal Braces For Straightening My Teeth? Yes, and it’s called Invisalign . Instead of using brackets and wires to move the teeth, a patient just has to wear a series of custom-made clear plastic aligner trays. No one will notice you’re wearing them because they are so discreet looking, and you can even take them out whenever it’s time to eat or brush your teeth.
