Dr. Sharma brings more than 20 years of experience in dentistry and is proud to serve the Andover community. She eagerly anticipates the opportunity to share her extensive clinical knowledge and expertise with you and your family soon!

What led her to choose the rewarding profession of dentistry? This dynamic field perfectly blends Dr. Sharma’s deepest passions an appreciation for science, meaningful interactions with individuals, and hands-on work that creates a tangible impact on people’s lives! After earning her undergraduate degree from the esteemed Adelphi University, she pursued her Doctor of Dental Medicine at the prestigious Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Subsequently, she further honed her skills by completing a rigorous General Practice Residency at Waterbury Hospital in Connecticut, and she is currently an active member of the American Dental Association. Dr. Sharma’s dedication to continuous education is truly commendable, as evidenced by her pursuit of advanced knowledge through respected institutions like SPEAR Education and the renowned KOIS Center.

At home, Dr. Sharma and her husband are proud parents to their delightful son, who brings them endless joy. Outside of her dedicated work at Andover Family Dental, she enjoys exploring new places through traveling and the exhilarating sport of rock climbing. In fact, she is beginning to introduce these exciting interests to her son and finds immense joy in sharing these adventures with him, fostering a love for exploration and active living in their family.

Charles Braga,

DMD, periodontist.

Dr. Braga has been a dedicated periodontist for over two decades, serving patients at Andover Family Dental by not only helping them preserve their dental health but also enhancing their overall well-being and quality of life through his specialized care. From an early age, Dr. Braga showed a strong interest in combining scientific knowledge with his manual skills for healing. The dental profession unites these talents and passions into a fulfilling career.

In 1989, Dr. Braga earned his Bachelor of Arts in Biology and French from Boston University, graduating Magna Cum Laude. He then pursued his education at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where he obtained his Doctor of Dental Medicine (also graduating Magna Cum Laude), followed by a Master of Medical Sciences and his C.A.G.S in Periodontology.

Since the early 2000s, Dr. Braga has been a recognized expert in laser-mediated periodontology. He teaches monthly at the Institute for Advanced Laser Dentistry in Cerritos and has lectured worldwide on Laser Assisted New Attachment technologies. A member of the American Academy of Periodontology, he has extensive experience with various medical lasers and is a fellow of the Institute for Advanced Laser Dentistry, skilled in implant dentistry.

In his spare time, Dr. Braga enjoys traveling, following political developments, cooking, and exploring information technology. He is also passionate about home improvement, renovating a 20th-century home since 2002, with ambitions to transform it into an Art Deco masterpiece.
