The Dental Implant Advantage

When you choose dental implants at Infinity Sedation Dentistry , you’re investing in your long-term oral health and quality of life. All aspects of the dental implant process—surgery, placement, and restoration—are completed in one location, ensuring convenience and efficiency for our patients.

Implants offer advantages that no other tooth replacement option can match:

  • Unmatched Stability and Function : Dental implants function just like natural teeth, allowing you to eat your favorite foods, speak clearly, and smile with confidence.
  • Preservation of Jawbone : Implants stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, preventing bone loss and maintaining facial structure.
  • Protection of Adjacent Teeth : Unlike traditional bridges that require modification of neighboring teeth, implants stand independently, preserving the integrity of your remaining natural teeth.
  • Long-Term Value : With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective solution in the long run.
  • Natural Appearance : Our custom-crafted restorations are designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. Most people won’t be able to distinguish between your implants and natural teeth.

Dental Implants vs. Other Tooth Replacement Options

When considering options for replacing missing teeth in Sioux Falls, Dakota Dunes, and Watertown, it’s important to understand the differences between dental implants and alternatives.

Dental Implants

  • Permanent solution that integrates with your jawbone
  • Prevents bone loss and maintains facial structure
  • Functions and looks like natural teeth
  • No impact on adjacent healthy teeth
  • Long-term investment with 95 %+ success rate

Dental Bridges

  • Requires modification of adjacent healthy teeth
  • Typically lasts 7-10 years before replacement
  • Does not prevent bone loss in the jaw
  • Lower initial cost than implants

Removable Dentures

  • Non-surgical solution
  • Lowest initial cost option
  • May slip or cause discomfort
  • Requires special cleaning and maintenance
  • Does not prevent bone loss

Caring for Your Dental Implants

With proper care, your dental implants can last a lifetime. Here’s how to maintain your investment:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Floss daily around each implant
  • Use antimicrobial mouth rinse as recommended
  • Attend regular dental checkups every 6 months
  • Avoid chewing extremely hard foods with implant crowns
  • If you grind your teeth, ask about a nightguard to protect your implants

Types of Implant Restorations We Offer

Single-Tooth Replacement

For a single missing tooth, we place one implant with a custom crown. This solution preserves adjacent teeth while providing a permanent replacement that functions like your natural tooth.

Multiple-Teeth Replacement

If you’re missing several teeth, strategic placement of two or more implants can support a fixed bridge, replacing multiple teeth without affecting healthy adjacent teeth.

Full-Arch Replacement

For patients missing all teeth in an arch, our innovative full-arch solutions use 4-6 implants to support a complete set of new teeth. Options include:

  • Implant-Supported Bridges : Fixed, non-removable restorations that feel like natural teeth.
  • Implant-Retained Dentures : Snap-on dentures that offer improved stability while remaining removable for cleaning.
  • Fixed Hybrid Prostheses : Permanent solutions that combine the best aspects of bridges and dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after implant placement. Our advanced techniques, combined with appropriate sedation, make the process remarkably comfortable. Many patients return to work or normal activities the day after surgery.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health are candidates for dental implants. Even if you’ve been told elsewhere that you’re not a candidate, our advanced techniques may offer solutions for complex cases, including those requiring bone grafting.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, the implant itself can last a lifetime.

How much do dental implants cost at Infinity Sedation Dentistry?

The investment for a single dental implant typically ranges from $2,500–$5,000, including the implant, abutment, and crown. We offer financing options to make treatment more affordable with monthly payments.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Many dental insurance plans provide coverage for implants. Our knowledgeable team will help maximize your benefits and explain all costs before treatment begins.

How do I care for dental implants?

Care for implants much like natural teeth—regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings. While implants can’t develop cavities, the surrounding gum tissue requires proper care to ensure long-term success.
