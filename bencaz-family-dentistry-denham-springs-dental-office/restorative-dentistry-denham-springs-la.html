To place the crown, we will remove any damaged material from the visible part of the tooth. We’ll then take an impression of your tooth to send to the lab where your crown will be made. After about a week or two, your permanent crown will be ready for fitting.

Dental Bridges

A dental bridge is a prosthetic device that replaces one or more missing teeth. Bridges are typically made from a combination of materials, including porcelain and gold alloys. The bridge is cemented to healthy teeth on either side of the gap created by the missing tooth. This allows the bridge to restore your smile and your ability to bite and chew properly.

Implant-Supported Restorations

If you are looking to replace one tooth or an entire arch of teeth, implant-supported restorations may be the best option for you. Dental implants are surgically placed below the gum line, where they fuse with the jaw in a process called osseointegration. Once the implants have healed into the jawbone, our dentists can design and place a custom crown or denture to restore your smile.

These restorations supported by dental implants offer superior strength and stability. Implants also prevent the deterioration of the jaw bone that can occur when teeth are missing.
