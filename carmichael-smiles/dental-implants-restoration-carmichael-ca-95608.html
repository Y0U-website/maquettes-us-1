Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • What Is a Dental Implant? A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium post that replaces a missing tooth root. It supports a crown, bridge, or denture, restoring both function and appearance. The dental implant procedure offers a permanent solution for missing teeth that also protects surrounding healthy teeth and bone structure.
  • Am I a Candidate for Dental Implant Treatment? Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for dental implant treatment. Ideal candidates have good gum health, adequate jawbone density, and no uncontrolled medical conditions that could interfere with healing. Dr. Sandhu will conduct a thorough evaluation to determine your suitability.
  • How Long Do Dental Implants Last? With proper care, dental implant treatment can last decades, often a lifetime. Good oral health habits, such as brushing twice daily, flossing once a day, and attending regular checkups, are essential for maintaining both implants and healthy teeth.
  • How Do Dental Implants Benefit Oral Health? When a tooth is lost, the jawbone can shrink, and nearby healthy teeth may shift. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone like a natural tooth root, preventing bone loss and keeping surrounding teeth in proper alignment. They also make it easier to maintain oral health compared to bridges or removable dentures.
  • Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the dental implant procedure, thanks to local anesthesia and optional sedation. Any post-surgical soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. Our team takes extra care to ensure you remain comfortable throughout your dental implant treatment.
  • How Much Does Dental Implant Treatment Cost? The cost depends on the number of missing teeth, the type of restoration, and whether additional procedures, such as bone grafting, are required. During your consultation, we’ll provide a detailed estimate and discuss financing options.
  • How Do I Care for My Dental Implants? Caring for implants is much like caring for healthy teeth: brush twice daily, floss once daily, and attend regular dental visits. Proper maintenance will extend the life of your dental implant treatment and keep your smile healthy.
  • Can Smokers Get Dental Implants? Smoking can slow healing and increase the risk of implant failure. While smokers can still receive dental implant treatment, quitting before and after the dental implant procedure greatly improves the likelihood of success.
  • How Soon After Tooth Extraction Can I Get a Dental Implant? In some cases, a dental implant procedure can be performed immediately after extraction. In other cases, healing time is necessary before placement. Dr. Sandhu will recommend the best timing for your dental implant treatment to protect your oral health.
  • Are Dental Implants Better Than Bridges or Dentures? For many patients, yes. Dental implants offer superior stability, durability, and function compared to bridges or dentures . They don’t require altering healthy teeth, and they eliminate the slipping and discomfort sometimes associated with traditional dentures.
