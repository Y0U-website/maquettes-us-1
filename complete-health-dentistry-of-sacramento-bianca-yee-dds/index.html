Dr. Bianca Yee is a proud native of Sacramento. After graduating from John F. Kennedy High School in 2000, she proceeded to earn a Bachelor of Science degree in Psychology at the University of California, Davis in 2004. Directly thereafter, she achieved a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree at the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco in 2007.

Dr. Yee values family and serving the community that is so instrumental in inspiring her goals, passion, and vision for life and dentistry. She focuses on the importance of preventative care and the impact of oral health on overall health.

Learning is one of Dr. Yee’s passions and she believes that it is an essential part of growing both personally and professionally. She is a member of multiple study clubs, the Pankey Institute, and various symposiums presented by some of the most renowned teachers in her field.
