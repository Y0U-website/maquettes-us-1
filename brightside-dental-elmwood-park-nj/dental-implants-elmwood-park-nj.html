Dental Implants in Elmwood Park, NJ

Replace one or more missing teeth with dental implants planned for comfort, function, and a natural-looking result. Brightside Dental evaluates your bone support, gum health, bite, and smile goals before recommending an implant option.

Implants can support a single crown, bridge, or denture depending on how many teeth are missing. During your consultation, we explain the timeline, alternatives, maintenance needs, and whether additional steps such as an extraction or grafting discussion may be needed.

Every implant plan is personalized. Final recommendations depend on your oral health, bone support, medical history, and treatment goals.

Dental implants may be right for patients who want to

Replace missing teeth

Implants can replace one tooth, multiple teeth, or support larger restorations.

Improve chewing stability

A properly planned implant restoration can feel more secure than removable options.

Protect long-term function

Implants help restore bite support and reduce strain from missing teeth.

How the implant process starts

Implant consultation We review your goals, medical history, missing teeth, and current oral health.

Digital imaging X-rays or cone-beam CT imaging may be used to evaluate bone support and anatomy.

Personalized treatment plan We explain options, timeline, costs, and whether grafting or additional care may be needed.

Restoration planning The final tooth, bridge, or denture is planned for function, appearance, and long-term maintenance.

Implant options we can discuss

Single tooth implant A common option when one tooth is missing and surrounding teeth are otherwise healthy.

Implant bridge Can replace multiple missing teeth without using every missing tooth space for an implant.

Implant-supported denture Can improve stability for patients struggling with loose removable dentures.

Alternative options Bridges, partials, or dentures may be appropriate depending on your situation.
