Accessibility Statement Statement

Commitment to Accessibility

Brightside Dental is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying relevant accessibility standards to enhance usability for all patients and visitors.

Conformance Status

Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines help make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

Measures to Support Accessibility

Brightside Dental takes the following measures to ensure accessibility of our website:

  • Providing text alternatives for non-text content, such as images
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast for readability
  • Structuring content with clear headings and navigation
  • Supporting keyboard navigation across the site
  • Regularly reviewing and improving accessibility features

Ongoing Efforts

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing effort and are continuously working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website. We periodically review our site using accessibility tools and user feedback to identify and address any issues.

Third-Party Content

Some pages on our website may include links to third-party platforms, such as online appointment scheduling systems. While we strive to work with accessible providers, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content. If you experience difficulty, please contact us and we will assist you.

Feedback and Assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this website, or if you need assistance with any part of our site, please contact us:

We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within 1–2 business days. Accessibility-related requests are given priority, and we will work with you to provide the information or services you need through an accessible communication method.

Alternative Access

If you are unable to access any content on this website, please contact us and we will provide the information or assist you through an alternative method, such as by phone or email.

Compatibility

This website is designed to be compatible with modern web browsers and assistive technologies, including screen readers (such as NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver) and keyboard navigation.

Limitations

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and correct these issues.
