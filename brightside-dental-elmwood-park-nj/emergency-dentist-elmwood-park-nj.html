Emergency Dentist in Elmwood Park, NJ

Tooth pain, a broken tooth, swelling, or a lost crown can’t wait. Brightside Dental provides calm, prompt urgent dental care for Elmwood Park and nearby Bergen County communities.

If you have facial swelling, trouble breathing/swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or serious trauma, seek emergency medical care first.

Dental emergencies we can help with

Tooth Pain

Lingering pain, pain when biting, sensitivity, or suspected infection.

Broken Teeth

Cracked, chipped, fractured, or traumatized teeth that need evaluation.

Lost Crown or Filling

Loose crowns, lost fillings, or broken dental restorations.

What to do now

Call Brightside Dental Tell us your symptoms so we can guide you on timing and next steps.

Save any crown, filling, or tooth fragment Place it in a clean container and bring it with you.

Use a cold compress This can help with swelling or soreness after trauma.

Avoid chewing on the painful side Wait until we evaluate the tooth and restoration.

Do not place aspirin on the gums It can burn the tissue around the painful tooth.

How we evaluate urgent tooth pain

Focused emergency exam We listen to your symptoms and check the tooth, gums, bite, and surrounding area.

Digital imaging when needed X-rays or cone-beam CT imaging may help us locate infection, cracks, or deeper damage.

Clear treatment options We explain whether a filling, crown, root canal, extraction, or medication is appropriate.

Comfort-first next step Our goal is to relieve discomfort when possible and help you protect your smile.

Common emergency dental treatments

Tooth-colored fillings

For conservative repairs when decay or a small fracture can be restored.

Dental crowns

When a tooth needs more protection after cracking, large decay, or root canal treatment.

Root canal therapy

When infection or nerve inflammation is causing significant pain and the tooth can be saved.

Extractions

When a tooth cannot be predictably restored and removal is the safest option.
