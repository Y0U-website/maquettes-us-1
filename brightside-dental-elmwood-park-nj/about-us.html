Meet our Doctors

Every member of our team is focused on providing a personalized and transparent treatment and ensuring you meet your dental needs

Dr. Young Jun (Kevin) Cho

Dr. Young Jun (Kevin) Cho is a general dentist who emphasizes conservative, preventive care for patients of all ages. He is a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, with advanced training in contemporary rotary endodontics, fixed prosthetics, implants, and clear aligner orthodontics. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his dog and two cats, as well as playing tennis, bouldering and swimming.

Dr. Antony Zeng

Dr. Antony Zeng focuses his practice on providing comprehensive dental care with an emphasis on prioritizing the patient’s needs. As a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, Dr. Zeng prides himself on continuing education and has training in fixed and removable prosthetics, implant dentistry, and clear aligner orthodontics. Outside of the dental office, Dr. Zeng is an avid sports fan and enjoys spending time with his two cats.

Dr. Diane Mears

Dr. Diane Mears has been practicing general family dentistry for 35 years. She is proficient in restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, and is certified in Invisalign orthodontics. Her extensive experience commitment to continuing education allow her to provide high-quality care to patients of all ages. Outside of work, she enjoys hiking, gardening, and spending time with her family and her black Lab, Moose.

Our Values

From day one, Brightside Dental has been committed to delivering the highest standard of care. We prioritize transparency in everything we do and aim to build lasting, trust-based relationships with every patient we serve.

Transparency

We believe that informed patients feel empowered. That’s why we carefully explain every treatment—what’s involved, why it’s recommended, and exactly what it will cost—before you proceed. We want you to feel confident and clear about your care at every step.

Excellence

You deserve exceptional dental care. Our team continually invests in advanced training and state-of-the-art equipment to deliver precise, efficient, and comfortable treatments. From diagnostics to execution, we focus on delivering results that meet the highest standards.

Integrity

At Brightside Dental, ethical care is non-negotiable. Treatment recommendations are always based solely on what’s best for your oral health—not influenced by commissions or insurance incentives. We stand by our commitment to honesty and trust in every interaction.

Empathy

We approach dental care from a place of understanding and compassion. Our goal is to treat each person as an individual—with respect, patience, and kindness—taking the time to listen, alleviate concerns, and provide the support you deserve
