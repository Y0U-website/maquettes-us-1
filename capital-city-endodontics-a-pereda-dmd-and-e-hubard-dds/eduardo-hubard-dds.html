Ed Hubard, DDS

Dr. Hubard, a native of Mexico City, attended boarding school in Michigan at the age of 9, where his parents hoped he could learn the English language. It was at this early stage that he fell in love with the United States. After attending Summer Camp that same year, he promised himself that someday he would become part of this amazing country. Dr. Hubard completed his initial dental training in the Latin-American University of Mexico City and in June of 1981 he was accepted at Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, Texas where he completed his training in Endodontics.

Dr. Hubard has been married to his lovely wife, Luz Maria, for over 40 years. They have 3 wonderful, grown children and 3 lovely grandchildren. Dr. Hubard, is an avid woodworker as well as a machinist. He loves spending time in his well-equipped workshop and traveling with his entire family whenever possible.
