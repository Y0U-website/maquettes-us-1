Meet Our Staff

Megan Megan has worked as a dental hygienist for the past 9 years, after detouring from a civil service job. Patients greatly appreciate both her excellent listening skills and her teaching abilities in the finer points of brushing, flossing and Listerine. A “born and raised here” ‘Bama girl, she married her high school sweetheart, Tim. They share a home with their dog, “Abby.” Megan’s quiet hobbies include crochet and cross stitch. She also has an adventurous spirit, “I love a good road trip!” -- and she manages to squeeze in the occasional concert along the way.

Gena Gena has 20 years of experience in the dental field – 17 of them as a hygienist (so she’s polished a few teeth!) She is a lifelong Birmingham-area resident, and rather than sports – is a big “fan” of family. She loves most spending time with her husband and 2 very active daughters. Gena is a member of Hope Church in Gardendale and volunteers in Kids Kingdom Consignment. When not wielding a toothbrush, she may instead be found holding a mic, singing with the praise band at church.

Allison Allison has faithfully spent the past 17 years right here, assisting Dr. Cunningham. Her knowledgeable, calming spirit is greatly appreciated by our patients (as well as all of her fellow team members)! She loves riding “ANYWHERE” on the Harley with her husband Matthew. They are proud parents of one sassy toddler, who already loves motorcycle riding with her dad! Completing their happy family is a fluffy Maltese, who answers to “Douglas.”

Jennifer Jennifer is our scheduling coordinator and has 20 years of pediatric dental experience under her toothbrush. Her desire is to daily show the love of Jesus in all areas of her life. She and husband Timothy enjoy time with family and friends, live music, and being “anywhere there is sunshine and water.” Their family includes 2 adult daughters and a busy grandson. Jennifer enjoys Alabama football, “ROLL TIDE!”

Karah Karah has been a hygienist for 4 years. She has a very positive attitude and loves meeting new people. Her goal is to help people not only feel great about their smile, but help them feel comfortable and confident as well! She currently resides in Dora with her cat. In her spare time she enjoys traveling and spending time with her friends and family.
