Preventative Care

Comprehensive Dental Exams

Cleanings and X-Rays

We recommend coming every 6 months to let one of our highly trained hygienists clean your teeth. Once a year, they will also take x-rays to ensure that your teeth and surrounding bone are healthy. At that appointment, your hygienists will provide oral hygiene instruction so you know best how to care for your smile!

Nightguards

An additional service we provide that helps keep your teeth strong and protected are our nightguards. Our nightguards are custom made and help stabilize your jaw, which in turn helps your muscles relax. This helps with the soreness associated with clenching and grinding your teeth.
