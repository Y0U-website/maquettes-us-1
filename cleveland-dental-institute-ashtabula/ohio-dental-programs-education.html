Cleveland Dental Institute Ohio Dental Programs

Cleveland Dental Institute is proud to be a teaching facility, offering continuing education courses, dental assisting programs, General Practice Residencies (GPR), and Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) programs.

General Practice Residency Program

The CDI Ohio General Practice Residency Program is a one-year residency program for recent dental school graduates, working in Cleveland Dental Institute’s private practice locations in both Cleveland, Ohio and our Miami Valley Hospital location.

Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program

The CDI Ohio Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program is a two-year program for dental residents, designed to further develop techniques learned in their undergraduate programs. The AEGD program, based in both Columbus and Cleveland, Ohio, provides invaluable experience of dental procedures, purposely focused on complex, real-patient cases.

CDI Ohio Dental Assisting Program

Cleveland Dental Institute offers a comprehensive dental assisting program that spans eleven weeks. Students develop skills inside of our high-tech, private practice training center, working side-by-side with our doctors and instructors.

CDI Ohio Continuing Education Courses

Cleveland Dental Institute’s continuing dental education courses are updated each year and include opportunities for further study abroad. Past courses include Bench Test Prep, Implant Continuum, Endodontics, Smile Design, Lab, Local Anesthesia, Oral Surgery Continuums, and more.

The Mission of CDI Ohio

The mission of The Cleveland Dental Institute is to educate and train general dentists and dental auxiliary personnel to serve their community and to improve access to care to a diverse patient population. This is accomplished in a holistic environment that fosters diversity, collegiality, and professionalism.

Cleveland Dental Institute Standards & Certifications

The CDI administration selection committee upholds the highest standards in who cares for you. Our residents are all top-tier students that come to CDI from all over the globe to practice under our world-class faculty.

CDI Dental Assisting Program is a fully ADA CERP- and PACE-Recognized Provider.

Cleveland Dental Institute is THE ONLY CDCA Exam Training Facility in Ohio, and our AEGD program is fully accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA).
