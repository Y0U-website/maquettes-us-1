Dental Implants | Dental Implants Ohio

The process of the dental implants procedure begins with your doctor placing the implant.

Next is the process of Osseointegration (meaning: combines with bone ). This is part of the healing process in which the bone grows around the implant, making the implant strong and secure.

Finally, once the implant is in place and your doctor feels it is strong enough, a custom crown will be placed. CDI crowns are custom-fitted and custom-colored so that your smile looks perfectly.

  • STEP 3: Custom Crown 100 % 100 %
