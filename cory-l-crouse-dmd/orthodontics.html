What Is the Importance of Orthodontics in Aiea HI?

Orthodontic treatment involves using braces or other orthodontic appliances to reposition the teeth to improve their appearance, function, and dental health. Crooked or misaligned teeth can be a big problem because they can lead to oral health issues, including tooth decay, gum disease, and even tooth loss.

For patients whose bite is misaligned, orthodontic treatment can help correct this issue. When the teeth are not aligned properly, this can cause the jaw to be out of alignment. This can result in painful symptoms like headaches and migraines. Additionally, when the teeth are crooked, it can be difficult for patients to clean them properly, increasing their risk of developing cavities and tooth decay. This is why it’s important to have orthodontic treatment to correct the alignment of the teeth and ensure that the teeth are straight and healthy.

Orthodontic Treatment Methods

There are several different types of orthodontic braces that are currently available to patients, including traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, and invisible aligners like Invisalign. The type of brace you choose will depend on your personal preferences and our dentist’s recommendations. Generally, patients with moderate-to-severe misalignment are good candidates for orthodontic treatment. To find out which type of orthodontic braces is right for you, schedule an appointment with your dentist today!

Braces can treat a wide range of dental issues, including overcrowding, spacing between teeth, malocclusion, overbite, underbite, and crossbite. Spacing between the teeth is one of the most common issues that patients seek treatment for when they visit their orthodontists. This is because when there’s space between your teeth, it can lead to aesthetic problems as well as issues with chewing and biting food.

If a patient has spaces between their teeth that are too large, they may need braces to prevent future problems with other teeth moving out of place due to the space created. The alignment of teeth is also essential in chewing food properly and effectively without experiencing pain or discomfort in their jaws or facial muscles.

Traditional braces use metal brackets and wires to guide the movement of teeth, which limits the kinds of foods patients can eat during treatment. The brackets can also irritate the gums and cause discomfort. Thankfully, there are alternatives to traditional braces without sacrificing quality treatment. Patients who want to straighten their teeth discreetly can consider Invisalign.

These clear aligners are removable for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. They are worn over the teeth and can be easily removed when it’s time to brush or floss. Patients who choose Invisalign also don’t have to worry about damaging their appliances while chewing on ice or other hard items. The trays can easily be popped in and out as needed. During your consultation, your dentist will decide if this treatment is a good fit for you.
