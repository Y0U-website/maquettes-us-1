In-Office Treatment in Aiea HI

An in-office treatment produces the fastest results because it uses the most potent whitening gel. Two main types of professional teeth whitening treatments can brighten your smile in just one trip to your dentist’s office. These treatments are:

Whichever method you choose, consult your dentist before getting started to make sure the treatment is a good fit for your unique smile. If you’re considering professional teeth whitening, contact your dentist to schedule a consultation today.

  • Conventional whitening: This treatment involves wearing a special mouthpiece that contains a bleaching agent, which breaks up stains on your teeth and removes them immediately. It’s a quick procedure that takes about an hour to complete. Your dentist can also provide you with a touch-up kit to use at home to keep your smile bright for months to come!
  • Laser whitening: Laser whitening offers long-lasting results because the light from the laser penetrates your tooth enamel to whiten below your surface and remove stubborn discolorations. It works by activating a hydrogen peroxide-based gel called carbamide peroxide that’s applied to the teeth. While the process feels similar to traditional whitening methods, patients may need multiple sessions to achieve optimal results.

At-Home Treatment

In-office teeth whitening provides the fastest and strongest results for patients, helping to improve the lightness of teeth by up to ten shades in just one visit. However, at-home whitening products provide an incredible way to enhance and maintain the results of in-office treatment. Patients can conveniently use take-home trays to whiten their smiles in the comfort of their homes while maintaining excellent oral health.

For patients who want to see the dramatic results of professional-grade treatments in the convenience of their own homes, professional take-home kits are an excellent option. These kits include custom-made trays and professional-grade bleaching gels that provide consistently positive results in almost all patients. The custom trays fit comfortably over the teeth and keep the gums from being affected by the peroxide-based bleach. This ensures that the bleaching gel only affects the surfaces of the teeth, leaving them whiter and brighter without causing unnecessary discomfort.
