When Is a Crown Needed in Aiea HI?

Your dentist may recommend a crown for several reasons. If you have cracked or chipped teeth, a dental crown can be used to restore the strength and appearance of your teeth while also protecting them from further damage. You may also need a dental crown after a root canal treatment to protect the tooth from future damage due to biting forces.

Once in place, a dental crown is permanently cemented to ensure the restoration cannot be removed without a dentist’s help. The permanent nature of a dental crown allows it to provide comfort, strength, and stability for the affected tooth for several years to come.

Advantages of Crowns

Dental crowns are beneficial for a number of reasons. In addition to improving the appearance of your smile, they can also help strengthen and protect your teeth. They can also be used to anchor a bridge or cover a tooth after root canal therapy. Crowns are also often used in conjunction with dental implant restorations.

Another advantage of using dental crowns is that they can be placed during a single appointment with your dentist. This means you can avoid multiple trips to the dental office and the costs associated with these visits. In addition, they do not require any special care beyond regular brushing and flossing. However, it is vital to maintain good oral hygiene while wearing a crown.

Crowns are cemented onto your teeth and cannot be removed on your own. For this reason, it is crucial that you only receive treatment from a dentist you trust. Although many patients are satisfied with their dental crown treatment, some experience irritation or infection. To avoid these risks, you should work with an experienced and knowledgeable dentist. Your dentist can discuss your treatment options and goals to develop a treatment plan that meets your specific needs.

Types of Crowns

Dental crowns are typically made from porcelain but can also be made from other materials like gold. Gold is durable and can last many years, while porcelain can match the color of your natural teeth for a more natural-looking appearance. Porcelain can also be tinted to closely match the natural shade of your tooth enamel. Your dentist can help you decide which material is suitable for your needs.

Both types of crowns are very durable and can last long if you take good care of them and regularly visit your dentist.
