  • Gaps – Space in your bite caused by missing teeth or alignment issues
  • Underbite – Lower teeth protruding farther than your upper teeth
  • Crossbite – A lateral misalignment in the teeth in which the upper tooth does not overlap its corresponding lower tooth
  • Overbite – Upper teeth that protrude too far over the lower teeth
  • Open bite – The upper and lower teeth do not come together, leaving a space between the biting surfaces
  • Misplaced midline – The centerline of the upper and lower ridges do not directly overlap

  • Braces – Braces are the most commonly used orthodontic devices. The system consists of brackets, wires, and bands. The brackets are cemented onto the surface of your teeth, while a U-shaped wire is threaded through them. Your orthodontist will regularly adjust these wires and bands to gradually move your teeth over time and give you the smile you’ve always wanted. Kids can also request colored braces from their dentist as well.
  • Invisalign – Invisalign uses clear aligners that allow you to gradually straighten your teeth without anyone ever knowing you’re wearing them!
