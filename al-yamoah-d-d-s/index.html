The practice

Dr. Al is the compassionate specialist who truly cares for all patients and every family. He is driven to provide an exceptional, kid-centric and family-friendly dental home for every child and every family, one that fosters growth and centers on well being.

“At Lake Cities Pediatric Dentistry, we know that smiles have the power to improve the world, so we stand for smile power and we stand for helping everyone’s smile make the world a better place.”

As trained pediatric dental professionals, we carry a wealth of knowledge, experience, and expertise. Our team is equipped with the latest technologies, and we constantly stay up-to-date on advancements in pediatric dentistry. What really sets us apart is our compassionate approach. We see beyond teeth - we see the kids behind the smiles and guide them towards a life of excellent dental health.

Let us help you navigate this journey because at Lake Cities Pediatric Dentistry, we’re not just dentists, we’re partners in your child’s bright and healthy future.
