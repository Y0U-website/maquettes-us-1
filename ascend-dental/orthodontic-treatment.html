Overview and Benefits

Orthodontic treatment does more than create a stunning smile. When you choose orthodontics Eagle residents trust, you’re investing in better oral health, improved confidence, and long-term dental wellness. Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing your risk of cavities and gum disease. Plus, correcting bite issues can eliminate jaw pain and prevent excessive tooth wear.

Our orthodontic solutions address various concerns including crowded teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. Whether you’re an adult seeking discreet treatment or a parent exploring options for your child, we customize every treatment plan to match your lifestyle and goals.

Treatment Expectations

Your orthodontic journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. We’ll take digital X-rays, 3D scans, and photographs to create a detailed treatment roadmap. You’ll see a preview of your future smile and understand exactly how we’ll achieve your goals.

Regular check-ins keep your treatment on track. For traditional braces, expect visits every 4-6 weeks for adjustments. Invisalign patients typically visit every 6-8 weeks to receive new aligner sets. Each appointment takes about 30 minutes, fitting easily into your busy schedule.

Once active treatment ends, we’ll provide custom retainers to maintain your beautiful new smile. Follow-up visits ensure your teeth stay perfectly aligned for years to come.
