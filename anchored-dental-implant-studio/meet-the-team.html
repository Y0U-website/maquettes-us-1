MEET THE TEAM Experts you can trust At Anchored Dental Implants, our team is dedicated to delivering exceptional care with precision, compassion, and expertise. From your first consultation to your final results, we’re here to guide you every step of the way. Get to know the professionals who make your comfort and confidence their top priority.

Dr. Henley Dr. Aaron Henley is passionate about full mouth implant dentistry because he’s seen firsthand the life-changing impact it can have. For him, there’s nothing more rewarding than watching a patient walk out with a brand-new smile—and a new sense of health, confidence, and joy.

Originally from Michigan, Dr. Henley earned his dental degree from The Ohio State University, where he focused on oral surgery and dental implants. He has since pursued advanced, international training in both surgical and prosthetic techniques, allowing him to guide his patients through every step of their transformation—from the first scan to the final smile.

Outside the office, Dr. Henley lives an active and adventurous life. He’s an avid snowboarder, and all-around sports enthusiast, with a love for volleyball and pickleball. He and his wife, Brooke, enjoy traveling the world together and spending time with their golden retriever, Hank.
