Bellingham Dental Group was founded in 2013 with a simple belief: that exceptional dental care should feel personal, not clinical. What started as a small practice has grown into a full-service dental home for thousands of families across Bellingham and the surrounding communities.

We&#x27;ve invested in the latest technology, including digital X-rays, 3D imaging, and same-day restorations, but our greatest investment has always been in our people. Every member of our team is chosen for their skill, their warmth, and their genuine commitment to your wellbeing.

Parker Haley, DDS

Dr. Haley is a compassionate dentist with over 15 years of experience, starting his career in California before relocating to Bellingham. A proud graduate of the University of Washington, he is an affiliate faculty of the UW and actively mentors future dental professionals. He specializes in helping patients manage dental anxiety, and outside the office enjoys travel, family time, and his French bulldog Lucy.

Dr. Kate Hall, DDS

Dr. Hall graduated from the University of Washington School of Dentistry. She specializes in helping patients overcome dental anxiety with her gentle demeanor and reassuring presence. Outside of work she enjoys home projects, painting, and drawing. She also has an Axolotl named Igor!

Dr. Chris Tataryn, DDS

Originally from Washington State, Dr. Tataryn earned his DDS from Loma Linda University. He brings enthusiasm and energy to his work, always striving to help patients feel comfortable and confident. Outside the office he enjoys the gym, snowboarding, motorcycles, and skydiving.

Dr. Akash Patel, DDS

Originally from Vancouver, BC, Dr. Patel earned his DMD from the University of Illinois in Chicago. He loves connecting with patients and getting to know them. Outside of work he enjoys sports, hiking, the beach, and traveling. He recently celebrated his 40th birthday with a trip to Tokyo!
