Meet Dr. Sarah

We Are Ready To Help YOU

Sarah Bihm, DDS

Dr. Sarah was born and raised in the Acadiana area. She grew up in Rayne and attended Notre Dame High School in Crowley (right across the street from her office now). There she met her husband, Travis Webb, and they have two children, Eleonore and Cora.

Dr. Sarah graduated with a degree in Biological Sciences from LSU in 2009 and later graduated from the LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans in 2013. She is a current member of the American Dental Association, The Academy of General Dentistry and the Louisiana Dental Association. Dr. Sarah attends multiple continuing education courses every year so that she can bring the latest technology and techniques to her patients.

Outside of the office, Dr. Sarah enjoys spending time with her family, decorating, organizing, playing Mahjong, and traveling. She also loves anything cats and Disney!

When asked what Dr. Sarah loves about dentistry, she replied:

"As an obsessive compulsive perfectionist, I truly try my very best to give each patient exactly what they want, which is a more confident form of themselves, their very own masterpiece. A beautiful smile can give one the feeling of a new and improved persona, and the fact that I can do that for others makes me feel so privileged that I have been allowed to be a part of this profession."

Dr Sarah and her team are here for all of your dental needs, and we can’t wait to show you how it feels to actually LIKE coming to the dentist!
