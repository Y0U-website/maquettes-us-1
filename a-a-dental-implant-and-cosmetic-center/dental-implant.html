What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process at A & A Dental Implant and Cosmetic Center is carefully planned to ensure safe, predictable, and long-lasting results. Here’s a step-by-step overview:

1. Consultation and Comprehensive Evaluation

During your first visit, our specialist implant dentists perform a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and bone. We need to take 3D imaging and X-rays to accurately assess bone density and structure, review your medical history, and determine if dental implants are the right solution for you. A personalized treatment plan, timeline, and cost estimate are created based on your unique needs.

2. Implant Placement:

The implant, typically made of a titanium alloy , and is surgically placed into the bone to serve as a strong and stable artificial root. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia. For patients with insufficient bone, additional procedures such as bone grafting or sinus lifts may be recommended.

3. Healing and Integration:

After placement, the implant undergoes a natural process called osseointegration , where it fuses with the bone. This phase usually takes 3–4 months , during which the implant becomes firmly anchored, providing a durable foundation for your final restoration. During this time, patients are given detailed instructions for oral care and diet to support optimal healing.

4. Final Restoration:

Once healing is complete, a custom-made crown, bridge, or full arch prosthesis is attached to the implant. These restorations are designed to match the color, shape, and function of your natural teeth, providing a fully restored smile. For patients using dentures, there is also the option to create implant-supported fixed dentures , giving added stability and making them essentially permanent.

5. Follow-Up and Maintenance:

Regular check-ups are essential to ensure the long-term success of your implants. With proper oral hygiene, routine dental visits, and good care, dental implants are a durable and reliable solution that can maintain a natural-looking, fully functional smile for many years.

6. Contact Us

Contact us today to schedule your appointment or ask any questions you may have. Our friendly team is ready to assist you with care, guidance, and support, ensuring a smooth and comfortable experience while helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile for years to come.

Single Tooth Implants

When a single tooth is lost due to injury or decay, a dental implant is the most natural and lasting replacement. Our single tooth implants are anchored directly into the jawbone and topped with a custom crown for seamless function and appearance. They protect bone structure, prevent shifting, and restore your smile with confidence and comfort.

Implant-Supported Bridges If you’re missing multiple teeth, implant-supported bridges offer a strong and stable alternative to traditional bridges. Rather than relying on adjacent teeth for support, these bridges are anchored by dental implants, ensuring long-term durability and improved jawbone preservation. At A&A Dental , we design each bridge to look completely natural and fit comfortably.

Full Arch Implants (All-on-4 / All-on-6)

Our All-on-4 and All-on-6 full arch implants are ideal for patients missing all upper or lower teeth. With as few as four or six implants, we can support a full set of prosthetic teeth that restore your bite and appearance in one streamlined solution. These advanced procedures deliver exceptional function, aesthetics, and immediate results.

Implant-Supported Dentures Unlike traditional dentures, implant-supported dentures offer a secure, non-slip fit that stays in place without adhesives. Anchored to dental implants, these dentures allow you to chew, speak, and smile with complete confidence. Our team customizes each set to your facial structure, ensuring natural aesthetics and enhanced comfort.

Bone Grafting for Implants

For patients with insufficient jawbone volume, bone grafting is a vital step before implant placement. This procedure rebuilds and strengthens the jaw to support implants effectively. At A&A Dental , we offer a range of bone graft techniques including sinus lifts to help patients become eligible for successful, long-lasting dental implants.

Implant Maintenance & Repair Ongoing care is essential to preserving the function and health of your dental implants. We provide implant maintenance and repair services to address issues like loose crowns, wear, or inflammation. Our team performs thorough cleanings, assessments, and repairs to extend the life of your implants and keep your smile looking and feeling its best.
