The practice

Dr. Ashley Stein Araiza graduated from Fallbrook Union High School prior to attending and earning her Bachelors of Science in Biology at San Diego State University. After graduating from the prestigious USC School of Dentistry in 2009, Dr. Araiza began practicing dentistry in her home town of Bonsall alongside Dr. Joseph Perri.

In 2019, Dr. Araiza moved her dental practice to a beautiful new facility in Fallbrook. Since 2011, Dr. Araiza has been a member of the Pala Mesa Collaborative Care Group for continuing education. Additionally, Dr. Araiza has been a community volunteer at the Bonsall and Fallbrook elementary schools via Fallbrook Smiles Project (FSP) since 2012. Currently, she serves as a board member for FSP.

Aside from dentistry, Dr. Araiza served on the board of directors for the Fallbrook Land Conservancy for 7 years. Moreover, having a passion for California native plants, Dr. Araiza participated as a docent guide for the seasonal hikes on the Santa Margarita Ecological Reserve. Above all else, she enjoys spending time outdoors with her husband, Sergio, and daughter, Zoe, and pet pug, Ninja. ​ ​ ​…
