The orthodontic treatments offered by Dr. Bell’s office can straighten teeth gently to give you the smile you’ve always wanted. Contact our office today to schedule your next Care without Scare appointment.

  • Fastbraces®: The Fastbraces® system straightens teeth faster than traditional braces, hence the name. Fastbraces® work for correcting teeth that have grown in sideways, tilted or overlapping; it also corrects spaces between teeth. Fastbraces® takes about six months to straighten teeth, depending on how much correction is needed, so they require fewer appointments with Dr. Bell.
  • Invisalign: Invisalign is one of the most popular alternatives to traditional wire braces, and there are good reasons for that. The clear plastic aligners used with Invisalign are made of thin plastic, they are removable, comfortable, and the system is nearly invisible. The aligners are custom made and push the patient’s teeth in the desired direction. One aligner is worn for about two weeks and then the patient receives the next one in the mail. The aligners can be removed for meals, and for brushing and flossing.
