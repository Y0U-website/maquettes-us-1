What Is the Dental Implant Procedure?

A dental implant placement is a surgical procedure. It will involve Dr. Carroll and the team inserting the implant in the bone below the surface of the gumline.

Patients considering dental implant placement should be aware that a certain amount of bone density in the jaw or skull is required for an implant to be placed. The implant needs support and structure from the surrounding bone to do its job properly. In time, the implant itself will fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration; once this happens, the implant will promote bone growth and density as a new part of your jawbone.
