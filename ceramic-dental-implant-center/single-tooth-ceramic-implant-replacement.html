How is a single implant maintained?

A ceramic implant crown requires daily plaque removal and professional monitoring. The team checks the gum tissue, implant stability, crown, contact with neighboring teeth, bite, and bone levels when imaging is clinically indicated.

New bleeding, swelling, drainage, pain, mobility, food trapping, bite changes, or a loose crown should be assessed. Zirconia does not prevent plaque-related inflammation or mechanical complications.
