The practice

To restore smiles with biologic, metal-free precision using SDS ceramic implants.

From one missing tooth to a complete fixed smile, treatment is planned around your health, function, and esthetic goals.

Single-Tooth Implant Single-tooth ceramic dental implants are available at both our New York City (Central Park South) and Scarsdale (Westchester) locations. A metal-free SDS zirconia implant and ceramic crown can replace a missing tooth with a biologic, esthetic solution for patients seeking ceramic dental implants in NYC, Westchester, and the greater New York area.

Immediate Placement When clinically appropriate, a failing tooth can be gently removed and replaced with a metal-free ceramic implant during the same visit. This advanced approach preserves natural bone and soft tissue, reduces total treatment time, and allows for faster, more comfortable healing, often with fewer appointments.

Laser Therapy Laser technology supports minimally invasive dental surgery by preserving healthy tissue and enhancing natural healing. State-of-the-art laser therapy elevates dental surgery through unparalleled precision, comfort, and accelerated healing.

Multiple-Implant Solutions For patients who need more extensive reconstruction, we offer full-arch and multi-implant zirconia solutions. Our protocols provide a stable, metal-free foundation that are customized for each patients function, health and esthetics.

Biologic bone regeneration rebuilds lost bone to create a strong, stable foundation for ceramic implants. When needed, sinus elevation techniques restore bone height in the upper jaw—supporting natural healing, facial structure, and long-term implant success.
