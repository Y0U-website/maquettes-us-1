Dale Goldschlag, DDS, DICOI

Dr. Dale D. Goldschlag is a Board-Certified Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (DICOI) and a recognized pioneer in metal-free, all-ceramic zirconia implantology. With more than 30 years of advanced surgical experience and over 35,000 implant procedures completed, he is one of the most experienced ceramic implant surgeons in the United States, serving patients from New York, the Tri-State area, across the U.S., and internationally.

As Founder and Clinical Director of the Ceramic Dental Implant Centre, located at 120 Central Park South, Suite 1G, New York City, Dr. Goldschlag established the region’s first boutique practice devoted exclusively to biologic, metal-free implantology and regenerative dentistry. His biologic philosophy and precision-guided microsurgical techniques define a new level of excellence in esthetics, function, and systemic compatibility.
