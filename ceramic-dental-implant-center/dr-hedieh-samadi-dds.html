Hedieh Samadi, DDS

Dr. Hedieh Samadi is a highly accomplished dentist with more than 20 years of clinical experience in airway-centered, biologic, and restorative dentistry. As Director of the Ceramic Dental Implant Centre in New York City, Dr. Samadi is known for her patient-centered approach, combining artistry, advanced technology, and biologic precision to achieve total oral and systemic wellness.

Her expertise spans sleep apnea therapy, TMJ and craniofacial pain management, and biologic restorative care, with a special focus on metal-free, all-ceramic zirconia implant systems. Dr. Samadi is extensively trained in airway and sleep medicine and has earned national recognition for her success in treating patients with complex airway, occlusal, and restorative needs using non-invasive, biologic methods.

She integrates advanced SDS Swiss Dental Solutions zirconia implant systems into her treatment philosophy — providing patients with biocompatible, esthetic, and long-lasting alternatives to traditional metal implants.

Philosophy & Vision

Dr. Samadi’s philosophy is rooted in balance, biology, and patient well-being. She believes that healthy function begins with proper breathing and airway support, and that biologic materials can transform both oral and systemic health. Her patient-centered approach combines precision, compassion, and innovation — helping patients restore natural beauty, confidence, and vitality through biologically sound dentistry.

At the Ceramic Dental Implant Centre, Dr. Samadi provides a comprehensive and personalized experience for every patient, offering biologic excellence and esthetic precision through her commitment to metal-free, ceramic implant dentistry.
