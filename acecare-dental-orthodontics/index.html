Our office features state-of-the-art technology to help you achieve a beautiful smile. We provide a comprehensive dental care for all your family’s dental needs.

Dr. Cho graduated with magna cum laude, from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. His concern for his patients and his respect for his colleagues earned him the Department of General Dentistry’s prestigious Joseph P. Speranza Memorial Award .

Dr. Cho resides in Orlando, Florida, with his wife, Lisa, and their daughter, Ellie. Outside the office, he enjoys hiking, spending time outdoors, and making memories with his family.
