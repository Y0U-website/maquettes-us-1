Meet Our Welcoming & Experienced Team

From the moment you connect with us, you’ll discover that we’re committed to making every visit feel welcoming and relaxed. From scheduling and answering questions to our customized and efficient services, we work hard to help you feel seen, heard, respected, and well-cared for throughout every visit.

Dina

With us since 2019, Dina’s focus is on ensuring our office runs smoothly, our team is supported, and our patients receive the top-notch dental experiences they deserve. She loves leading our wonderful staff each day and does all she can to make Belmont Dental Care the standout dental practice in Chicago.

When she’s away from the office. Dina loves being with her two children and their dog. Getting outside, going on long runs, and enjoying the culture and vibe of the city are some of her favorite pastimes.

Nubia

A key member of our team since 2004, Nubia is committed to creating a positive environment for patients, ensuring they feel at ease while receiving the highest-quality dental care possible. Hearing “I was so comfortable I could have fallen asleep” never gets old for Nubia!

In her free time, you can find Nubia with her husband and grown children. Some of her favorite activities include baking, visiting flea markets, traveling, and being with her loved ones.

Danielle

With us since 2017, Danielle holds an associate degree in applied science from Harper College. Her focus is on improving her patients’ overall health by providing the highest standard of hygiene care, and she loves it when patients tell her that visits are easier than they expected!

When she isn’t caring for smiles, Danielle is usually spending time with her husband and children. She is a fan of DIY projects, working out, going to the park with her family, and traveling as much as possible.

Mark

An important member of our team since 2019, Mark holds a bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene from the University of South Dakota and is committed to helping patients prevent oral health issues with education and customized care. He prides himself on changing the way people feel about dentistry, and believes that an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure.

In his free time, Mark loves spending time with his husband, dog, and two cats. His favorite pastimes include riding his bike, traveling, and exploring the city.

Lisbeth

Part of our team since 2024, Lisbeth is committed to providing efficient and welcoming support to our doctors and patients alike. She has an innately soothing approach that helps put people at ease, and her healthcare philosophy is centered on helping patients understand the importance of ongoing preventive oral healthcare. In her free time, Lisbeth enjoys time with her family, staying active, and contributing to our community.

Marlene

Known for her positive attitude and impressive work ethic, Marlene loves helping and supporting patients and colleagues alike. She’s passionate about expanding her knowledge and taking on new challenges, and believes that dental care should be comfortable, supportive, and stress-free for all. In her free time, Marlene loves being with her son, Jacob, and enjoys going to the gym, enjoying nature, and traveling.
