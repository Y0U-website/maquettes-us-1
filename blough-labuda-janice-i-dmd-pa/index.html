The practice

Dr. Blough-LaBuda’s skill and confidence will put you at ease and bring you closer to perfect dental health. Our dental services include teeth cleaning, teeth whitening, cavity treatment, root canals, digital x-rays, capping, crowning, nitrous oxide sedation, implant crowns, and denture repair.

Contact us today for an appointment, or to obtain important information on diet, lifestyle, environmental and many other factors that contribute to dental health. Dr. Janice Blough-LaBuda welcomes you as a new dental patient and as a friend. To find out more about our services, or to schedule your appointment, contact us Monday through Friday.
