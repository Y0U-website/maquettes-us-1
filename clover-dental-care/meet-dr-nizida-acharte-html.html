Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Acharte recently married in August 2025 and is happily settling into life in the New Haven area with her husband and their beloved Goldendoodle puppy. She is excited to become an active member of the community she now calls home, both personally and professionally.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Acharte enjoys family ski trips in the winter and playing tennis in the summer. Traveling has become a cherished passion she shares with her husband—they’ve compiled a list of favorite destinations and dream locations they hope to visit together. A fun fact: Dr. Acharte has visited Machu Picchu twice in her life! While staying closer to home, she loves playing classic games like dominoes, backgammon, and mancala with family and friends.
