Dentist

Dr Jana McCall, DDS

Dr. McCall is proud to be recognized as a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology. This status is only granted to periodontists who have completed significant achievements beyond the mandatory educational requirements of the specialty. Dr. McCall provides a wide range of periodontal treatments for periodontal disease, dental implant placement, and cosmetic periodontal procedures. Dr. McCall’s patients and colleagues find her to be a gentle, easy-going person which translates well with patient care. Dr. McCall currently resides in Maryland with her husband and two daughters.
