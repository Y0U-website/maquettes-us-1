Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials, such as titanium, which are surgically placed into your jawbone. These implants serve as a stable foundation for replacement teeth, such as dental crowns, dental bridges or dentures. Dental implants are highly versatile and can be used to replace a single tooth, several teeth or even a full arch of missing teeth. They integrate with your jawbone, which helps maintain bone density and prevent the jaw from shrinking over time.
