About Us

At American Dental, we are proud to have a team of highly trained dentists and professionals to tend to your dental needs.

FAQs

What dental services do you offer? We offer a wide range of dental services including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, oral surgery, periodontics, endodontics, and emergency dentistry.

Do you provide orthodontic treatment for both adults and children? Yes, we do; we offer orthodontic treatment for adults and adolescents and early treatment for children.

What types of dental implant options do you have? We have single tooth dental implants, multiple teeth dental implants, and All-On-4 dental implants.

Do you offer Invisalign or any other clear aligner treatments? Yes, we do offer Invisalign and invisible braces as an alternative to traditional braces.

What cosmetic dentistry procedures do you offer? Our cosmetic dentistry services include whitening of teeth, veneers, and crowns.

Do you have more than one location? Yes, we have multiple locations, including but not limited to Belmont-Cragin, Downtown Chicago, Evergreen Park, Lincoln Park, Lincoln Square, Midway Archer, Palos Heights, and River West.

What can I expect on my first visit? On your first visit, you will have a comprehensive exam and cleaning, including digital X-rays.

Do you offer emergency dentistry? Yes, our office does offer emergency dentistry.

What payment options do I have? We have several options for payment methods and also accept many different insurance plans. Please view our links on Payment Options and Insurance for additional information.
