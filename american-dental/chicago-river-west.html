FAQs

Do you accept City of Chicago and Guardian HMO dental plans? Yes! We proudly accept both City of Chicago DNOA HMO and First Commonwealth / Guardian HMO, along with other major plans.

What services are available at this location? General dentistry (exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, extractions, etc.) and orthodontics (braces and clear aligners).

How do I switch to your office under my HMO plan? Call your insurance provider, assign yourself to American Dental – River West Office, and have them fax your eligibility to us. Once received, we’ll schedule you right away at our brand-new, state-of-the-art facility.

Can I book online or talk to someone right away? Absolutely! Use our easy online booking tool or chat with us live on the website for fast help anytime.
