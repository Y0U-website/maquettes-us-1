Meet your dentist

Dr. Azita Ebrahimzadeh

Dr. Azita Ebrahimzadeh has been passionate about dentistry since childhood. Inspired at the age of six by a visit to the dentist, she knew from an early age that she wanted to help people smile with confidence. That early dream became a lifelong mission to deliver caring, conservative dental care with empathy and precision.

She earned her undergraduate degree in Biology from William Paterson University and went on to receive a Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences from UMDNJ. She completed her dental training at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine and continued her education with a residency at Hackensack University Medical Center. She is an active member of the New Jersey State Dental Association, the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and local county dental societies.

Dr. Azita enjoys all aspects of general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Her approach emphasizes prevention and patient education, creating a foundation for lasting oral health. Patients often comment on her gentle touch and the time she takes to make them feel comfortable and informed about their oral health.

Outside of the office, Dr. Azita enjoys spending time with her husband and their children, hiking, and exploring new places through travel.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Arash Baseri

Dr. Arash Baseri brings advanced training and a deep commitment to patient care to A&A Dental. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Biology from Rutgers University, followed by a Master’s in Biomedical Sciences at UMDNJ — where he also met his wife, Dr. Azita. He went on to complete his dental degree at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (formerly UMDNJ).

After dental school, Dr. Baseri pursued a General Practice Residency at Hackensack University Medical Center, gaining advanced experience in all aspects of dentistry. He then continued his training with an implant fellowship at DentalXP. Dedicated to staying at the forefront of his field, Dr. Baseri regularly attends continuing education courses and believes ongoing learning is essential to delivering the highest quality care and respect his patients deserve.

Outside of the office, Dr. Baseri enjoys playing and watching soccer, reading, and spending time with his wife and their son, Kian.

Compassionate care from a dedicated team

At A&A Dental, our team is the heartbeat of the practice, many of whom have been with us for years and are deeply committed to making each patient feel welcomed and respected. When you visit us, you’ll experience personalized attention from people who truly care — it’s just part of who we are.

What to expect at your first appointment From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted with a kind smile and a warm atmosphere that puts you at ease. We believe in attentive, unrushed care — your first visit includes a comprehensive overview of your oral health and time to discuss your goals, concerns, and questions. You’ll never feel like “just another patient” here.

Patient Experience Your comfort is our priority. We take our time during each appointment and ensure every patient receives thorough care tailored to their unique needs. You’ll meet our friendly team, review your medical history, and receive a full evaluation so we can guide you toward the healthiest and most confident smile possible.
