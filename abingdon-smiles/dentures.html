How do I care for my dentures?

Although our dentures are created from high-quality materials, they need optimal maintenance to keep looking their best. Observe the following tips to ensure your dentures remain in great shape:

  • Rinse your dentures with warm water after removing them from the mouth
  • Clean your dentures with a soft brush and non-abrasive soap
  • Don’t use toothpaste as it may be abrasive to your restoration
  • Handle your dentures carefully to avoid chips and damage
  • Visit your dentist every six months for routine check-ups and cleanings
  • Take great care of your gums and remaining teeth

How long do dentures typically last, and when should they be replaced?

With proper care, dentures typically last between 5 and 10 years. Over time, natural changes in your mouth—like bone resorption or gum reshaping—can affect how well they fit. Regular checkups at our office allow us to monitor your dentures for any needed adjustments or relining. If your dentures feel loose, uncomfortable, or show visible wear, it may be time to replace them to maintain optimal comfort and function.
