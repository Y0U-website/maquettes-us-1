Frequently Asked Questions about Immediate Implants

Who can get immediate dental implants?

Same-day dental implants are ideal for patients with good overall health and adequate jawbone support. If your bone is strong enough to stabilize an implant right away, you may qualify. Furthermore, patients without active gum disease and those who don’t smoke heavily tend to have the best outcomes. A consultation with a qualified dentist will help determine whether you are a suitable candidate for same-day dental implants.

How long does the procedure take?

Same-day dental implants mean exactly that. We can complete the procedure in a single visit, and the surgery lasts a few hours. After extracting bad/failing teeth, the dentist surgically places a titanium post in the same bone that once supported your tooth. In many cases, we restore your implant with a temporary prosthetic tooth to maintain oral function and appearance as you heal.

How do I care for dental implants as they heal?

We’ll give you tailored instructions to ensure a seamless recovery. Typically, we’ll provide guidelines on your diet, oral hygiene, and symptom management. We’ll also advise you to avoid tobacco products as they slow down healing, increasing the risk of implant failure. During healing, we’ll schedule follow-up appointments to monitor progress.

While everyone is different, it typically takes 2-3 weeks to recover from implant surgery. However, several months are required for the implants to integrate with the jawbone.

Are same-day implants permanent?

Yes! Just like delayed implants, same-day dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. Once the implant integrates with the jawbone, it creates a solid foundation that lasts several decades, even a lifetime, with proper care. The final restoration may need repair or replacement over time due to natural wear, but the implant itself is long-lasting.

Can dental implants fail?

Dental implants have an over 95 % success rate, making them one of the most reliable dental procedures. Unfortunately, dental implants can fail, but it’s very rare. Possible causes of implant failure include infection (peri-implantitis), smoking, implant rejection, and poor oral hygiene. The good news? Following aftercare instructions and working with an experienced dentist helps minimize the risk of failure.

Are there any risks with same-day dental implants?

As with any surgical procedure, there are risks. These may include infection, nerve damage, excessive bleeding, or implant instability if proper healing does not occur. However, with careful planning and experienced care, serious complications are uncommon.

Does every dentist offer same-day implants?

While many dentists offer dental implants, not everyone provides same-day implants. If you want to restore your smile with immediate dental implants, find an experienced dentist with a proven track record of successful cases.
