What is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant is used to take the place of a lost tooth. If you’re missing many teeth, you may opt for an option such as a bridge or multiple implants to get the job done. As a substitute for the missing tooth’s root, our dentist at Claremont Dental Group inserts a titanium post into the jawbone. After that, a dental crown is placed on top of the titanium post to act as a chewing surface.

What Patients Can Expect

If you’re visiting our office for a tooth implant in Claremont, NH, here’s what you can expect. Our friendly dentist will evaluate your overall oral health during the initial session to see if your jawbone is ready for a single tooth implant.

Our dentist may want to perform a bone graft before suggesting dental implants to see if you have enough bone to support the procedure. Our dentist will start by cutting into the gum tissue and drilling a hole into your jawbone to place a titanium post that acts as roots.

Once your gums have accepted the post, which takes three to six months, you’ll come in for another visit for the abutment and a crown to be placed. Please call our office with any questions about the process of a tooth implant in Claremont, NH, or to book an appointment!
