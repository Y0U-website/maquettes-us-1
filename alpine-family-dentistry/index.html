Our log cabin dental office in Eagle River, AK, is of truly Alaskan quality, making our patients feel at home in our dental practice. Inside our cozy log walls, you will be treated by skilled dental professionals who use only the best dental technologies.

Our caring dental staff will go out of their way to make you comfortable during dental procedures, providing blankets and neck pillows if needed.
