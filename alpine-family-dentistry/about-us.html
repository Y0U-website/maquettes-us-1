Meet the Team at Alpine Family Dentistry

At Alpine Family Dentistry, we pride ourselves on offering modern dental technologies in a cozy and inviting dental office. From the moment you walk into our log cabin dental office, our friendly dental team, including our skilled dentist, Dr. Brett Kocherhans, will go out of their way to make you feel comfortable. We are an understanding team of dental professionals who realize that visits to the dentist aren t always fun. For some people, dental appointments and procedures even cause anxiety. However, we try our best to make every visit to our office as pleasant as we can!

We love working with people of all ages, and we especially love serving the dental needs of families. Dr. Brett provides comprehensive dental care in our Eagle River office to better serve every member of your household s dental needs, which makes your dental appointments more convenient and easier to schedule . During your dental appointments, we will be eager to get to know you as a person, not just as a patient. Your smile is important to us, but we know you are more than a smile.

When you visit Alpine Family Dentistry, you will receive personalized and personable dental care. We look forward to meeting you!

Dr. Brett Kocherhans

I graduated as a member of the inaugural class of the University of Utah School of Dentistry after earning my bachelor s degree, summa cum laude, from Utah Valley University. I chose dentistry because it combines science, artistry, and the opportunity to build meaningful relationships with people. Helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles is one of the most rewarding parts of my profession.

My wife, Amie, was born and raised in Alaska, and together we re raising our three children, Claire, Parker, and Tanner, here in Eagle River. Alaska is truly home for our family. We love the incredible outdoors, the strong sense of community, and being close to family. When I m not in the office, I m spending time with my family, exploring Alaska, attending local events, or playing the guitar.

At Alpine Family Dentistry, my goal is for every patient to feel like part of our family. I believe every person deserves to be treated with kindness, respect, and honesty while receiving exceptional dental care. Whether you re here for a routine cleaning or more complex treatment, I strive to create a comfortable, stress-free experience where you feel informed and confident every step of the way.

There s nothing more rewarding than seeing a patient leave with a healthy smile and a positive experience. I look forward to welcoming you and your family to Alpine Family Dentistry!
