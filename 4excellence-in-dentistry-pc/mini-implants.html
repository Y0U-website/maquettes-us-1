As with any surgery, there are some risks involved in placing mini implants, including infection. The surest way to minimize these risks is to choose an experienced oral surgeon.

In comparison to traditional implants, mini implants provide a lesser degree of support because of their smaller size. In some cases, mini implants can secure dental crowns and bridges, but they are utilized most effectively for dentures or larger bridges. For patients who have a tendency to clench their jaw or grind their teeth, there is a greater risk of malfunction because of the increased pressure on the implant.

Depending upon the individual patient, standard implants may be a better choice, and Dr. Albanese at 4Excellence in Dentistry can help make this determination during a consultation.

  • Mini implants can be placed in as little as two hours, and are immediately ready to support restorations. Patients can leave the dentist’s office with a fully restored smile, and even eat a meal the same day.
  • Standard implants require ample jawbone density. Patients may require bone grafting prior to traditional implant surgery. Patients who have been disqualified for traditional implants because of jawbone atrophy are often good candidates for mini implants.
  • Because the surgery is less invasive and the time investment is minimal, mini implants are more affordable than traditional dental implants.
  • Like traditional implants, mini implants offer the benefit of improved jawbone health. Implants stimulate the jawbone similarly to natural tooth roots, and halt the process of bone atrophy that occurs when teeth are missing.

Mini Implants: A Worthwhile Investment

Dental implant posts function like artificial tooth roots, holding restorations securely in place so they will not slip or become dislodged. In addition to restored function and aesthetics, dental implants also provide stimulation to the jawbone, preventing the atrophy that occurs in the prolonged absence of tooth roots.

Mini implants are smaller in diameter than traditional dental implants, and the placement process is much less invasive. Once they have been placed, mini implants can immediately support a dental appliance. The entire mini implant placement process can be completed in less than two hours, allowing patients to leave our office prepared to eat a meal with their new teeth.

Calculating Treatment Costs

Mini implants are much more affordable than standard dental implants. The benefits provided by implants as well as the expenses involved in wearing traditional non-implant dentures must be considered.

When patients with missing teeth wear a traditional dental appliance, the jawbone will atrophy. This condition affects the structure of the face, contributing to the sunken look that occurs when tooth loss is not addressed for many years. It also explains why traditional dentures and bridges frequently start to come loose and fit differently over time, requiring adjustments or replacement. During the course of a patient’s lifetime, this can add up to a need for many dental appointments and replacement appliances.

Mini implant-supported dentures will not become loose or need to be altered because of a shrinking jawbone. Mini implants actually halt the atrophy process, preserving bone density. When they are placed by a dentist skilled in implant dentistry, implant-supported dentures can last for years. Benefits include increased comfort, unparalleled denture stability, and a beautiful, natural-looking smile.

Mini Dental Implants Candidacy: Are Mini Implants Right for You?

Benefits of Mini Dental Implants

Like standard dental implants, mini implants consist of titanium posts that are surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as artificial roots for dental restorations. Implant-supported dentures and bridges will not come loose like traditional dental appliances can, and also help prevent the atrophy of the jaw that occurs when teeth are missing.

Because mini implants are smaller in diameter than traditional implants, the placement process is much less complex. Once mini implants have been placed, they can immediately support a restoration. The entire process typically takes less than two hours, and patients can expect to leave our office with a fully functional, natural-looking smile. Mini implant patients also experience less discomfort and a shorter recovery time compared with traditional implant surgery.

Candidacy and Considerations

Because mini dental implants are smaller, they do not require the same amount of jawbone density that is needed to successfully support traditional implants. Although some patients with compromised jawbone density can undergo bone grafting prior to dental implant surgery, mini implants may be a more favorable option for some of these patients.

Despite their many benefits, mini implants are not right for everyone. This type of implant may not be the best choice for patients who are chronic teeth grinders, because the pressure can damage the implant over time. We will make absolutely certain that mini implants will benefit you before recommending this treatment.
