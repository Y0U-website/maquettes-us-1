What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once integrated with your bone, they provide a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike removable dentures or bridges that rely on adjacent teeth, implants are independent restorations that preserve your remaining natural teeth. Components of a Dental Implant: 1. Titanium Implant Post Replaces the tooth root 2. Abutment Connects the implant to the crown 3. Custom Crown The visible tooth replacement

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have:

Good General Health Able to undergo minor surgery Adequate Bone Density Sufficient jawbone to support implants Healthy Gums Free from active periodontal disease Commitment to Oral Hygiene Willing to maintain proper care Non-Smoker Or willing to quit during treatment

What if I Don’t Qualify Initially?

Implant Care and Maintenance

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth:

Daily Care:

  • Brush twice daily with soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Floss daily around implants
  • Use antimicrobial mouthwash
  • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol

Professional Care:

  • Regular cleanings every 6 months
  • Annual implant check-ups
  • Professional X-rays to monitor bone levels
  • Prompt treatment of any issues

Lifestyle Considerations:

  • Avoid chewing ice or hard objects
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth
  • Use a mouthguard during sports
  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene

Frequently Asked Questions - Dental Implants Clifton

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear.

Is implant surgery painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. We use local anesthesia during surgery and provide pain medication for recovery. Many patients return to work the next day.

How long does the implant process take?

Traditional implants take 3-6 months for complete healing. However, we offer same-day options for immediate results in appropriate cases.

Will my insurance cover dental implants?

Many insurance plans now cover a portion of implant treatment. We’ll verify your benefits and help maximize your coverage.

Can I get implants if I have gum disease?

We must treat gum disease before implant placement. Dr. Doktorman offers comprehensive periodontal therapy to prepare you for successful implants.

What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?

We offer bone grafting procedures to build up insufficient bone. Many patients who were previously told they couldn’t have implants are now candidates.
