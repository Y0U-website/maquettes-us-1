What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants - what is the process ?

At All Smiles Dental Care in Hazlet, we understand that getting dental implants is a significant decision. We are committed to guiding our patients through each stage of the process with care and expertise.

  • Initial Consultation: Your dental implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Charanjit Sandhu, DDS. During this visit, we assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and answer any questions you may have. This is also an opportunity to review your medical history to ensure you are a suitable candidate for dental implants.
  • Treatment Planning: Once we determine that dental implants are the right solution for you, we create a personalized treatment plan. This plan outlines the number of implants needed, the type of restoration (crown, bridge, or denture), and the overall timeline for your procedure.
  • Implant Placement: The next step involves surgically placing the titanium implant posts into your jawbone. This procedure is done under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Over several months, the implants naturally fuse with your bone through osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.
  • Healing and Recovery: Your implants are integrating with the bone, so you’ll need time to heal. We provide post-operative care instructions to minimize discomfort and promote proper healing.
  • Abutment Placement: After fully integrating your implants, we attach abutments to the implant posts. These abutments act as connectors between the implants and your dental restorations.
  • Custom Restoration: We create a custom dental crown, bridge, or denture precisely designed to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. This restoration is securely attached to the abutments, completing your smile.
  • Follow-Up Care: Regular follow-up appointments are essential to monitor your implant’s stability and ensure the long-term success of your new smile. We guide proper oral hygiene and maintenance to keep your implants in optimal condition.
