All On 4 Dental Implants services offered in Hazlet, NJ

The All-on-4® treatment is a full-arch implant system, supported on four implants without the need for a bone graft. Charanjit Sandhu, DDS, and her dental team at All Smiles Dental Care offer this innovative dental implant solution to patients with little bone volume. Call the office in Hazlet, New Jersey, today or book an appointment online to learn more about All-On-4 implants.
