I have found dentistry to be a very rewarding profession. I enjoy helping patients obtain and maintain optimum oral health with the highest level of care, all in a comfortable setting. It is gratifying to be able to help others and form a trusting partnership that lasts for years.

I earned my Bachelor of Science in 1989 and my Doctorate of Dental Medicine in 1993 from the University of Kentucky. I joined the team in 2001 and became a partner of Braelinn Village Family Dentistry the following year.

Since completing my formal education, I have continued my studies of new and emerging dental treatments and technology through local and national organizations and study clubs. I believe it is my duty to make patients confident that they are receiving the best possible care.

I grew up in Campbellsville, Kentucky, and moved to the Peachtree City area in 1995. My wife Kristin and I have been married for more than two decades. We have a son, Ben, a rescue swimmer for the U.S. Navy, and daughter, Kaitie, a dental student at the University of Kentucky.
