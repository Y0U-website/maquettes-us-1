Meet the Team at Century Dental

Dr. Fernando Archuleta

Early in his career he became committed to using his skills to change people’s lives for the better, and that shows in his focus to obtain the advanced training necessary to address your complicated dental problems. Dr. Archuleta has helped hundreds of people just like you, who have avoided the dentist for years, achieve the smile they’ve always wanted.

Growing up in Colorado and Arizona, Dr. Archuleta moved to Maryland at the turn of the century. He studied Biochemistry at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County and after attending dental school at the University of Michigan he is a proud Wolverine, cheering on the football team every season. Dr. Archuleta resides in Arlington with his wife, Erika, and in their spare time they enjoy travel and discovering new food.

Annika

Outside of the dental office, Annika is a licensed real estate agent and serves as captain of a USTA tennis team. She enjoys traveling, hiking local trails, and spending quality time with her granddaughters.

Kathy

Kathy attended New York City Technical College and started working in the dental hygiene field in 1990. She has been a valued member of the Century Dental team since 2019.

Kathy loves getting to know patients and their families and establishing personal connections with them. In her role as a dental hygienist, Kathy provides oral exams, dental cleanings, scaling and polishing, dental x-rays, fluoride varnish, sealants, and patient education. She enjoys helping patients make improvements to their oral health that in turn help improve their overall health.

When she’s not at work helping patients, Kathy is busy with her family, including her husband, their two teenage boys, her 89-year-old mother, and their collie, Taz.

Anteja

Anteja received her dental assistant training at Kaplan University Central Florida and has been part of the team at Century Dental since 2010. Some of her duties include assisting the dentist chairside, taking x-rays and impressions, and performing fluoride applications and Zoom! teeth whitening procedures.

Outside of dentistry, Anteja enjoys spending time with her family and friends.

Tyler

Tyler, A Montgomery County native, is a compassionate and detail oriented Treatment Coordinator who helps ensure every patient feels welcome and cared for. She works closely with families to coordinate treatment plans, manage scheduling, and support the flow of the office with positive energy and a team-first attitude.

Outside the office, Tyler enjoys community outreach, real estate, and spending time outdoors with her son and her two furry pup sons, Kash and Bentley.
