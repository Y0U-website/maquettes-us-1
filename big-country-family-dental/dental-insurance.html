About Us

Dental Insurance & Your Care: What You Need to Know

At our Ellensburg dental office, we accept many insurances and understand that you want to get the most from your dental insurance benefits. We have many patients who choose to come to us with no insurance. At the end of the day, dental care that is done well, the first time, is the most affordable.

Many patients expect their plans to fully cover the care they need—but often find that’s not the case. That’s why we believe it’s important to clearly explain how dental insurance works and how we help you get the excellent, reliable, and comfortable dental care you deserve.

Dental Plans Are Based on Budgets, Not Personal Needs

At our Ellensburg dental practice, we provide personalized care based on your unique dental health, not just what your policy allows. Insurance companies don’t evaluate your teeth or ask about your treatment preferences. That’s our job—and we take it seriously.

We Recommend What’s Best for You—Not Your Insurance Company

Our goal is to help you achieve and maintain a healthy smile for life. We will always recommend the treatment we believe is best for your long-term oral health, regardless of insurance limitations.

You’ll receive clear explanations about your current dental condition, how it developed, and the treatment options available to you. We’ll guide you through every step so you can make informed choices that align with your goals and comfort.

Quality Dental Care in Ellensburg That’s Built to Last

Most of our patients tell us they want their dental work done right the first time—with results that are durable, natural-looking, and comfortable. That’s exactly what we strive for every day. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning, a filling, or advanced restorative care, you can expect the highest standards of dental care.

Use Dental Insurance as a Tool, Not a Barrier

It’s important to remember that dental insurance is designed to reduce the cost of care—not to cover everything. Think of it as a helpful tool that contributes toward your treatment, rather than a comprehensive solution.

We’ll help you understand what your plan covers, work with your insurance provider, and advocate on your behalf to maximize your benefits.

Payment for all appointments is due at time of service. If your insurance does not cover what it says it will, we encourage you to reach out directly to them. We must emphasize that, as dental care providers, our relation is with you, not your insurance company. While the filing of insurance claims is a courtesy we extend to our patients, all charges are your responsibility from the day the services are rendered.

Our office accepts cash, check, credit card, debit card, online payments and CareCredit.

Your comfort, health, and satisfaction are our top priorities.
