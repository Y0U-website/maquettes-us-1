Providing Compassionate, Comprehensive Dental Care in Ellensburg, WA

Dr. Sarah brings a unique blend of advanced training, clinical excellence, and heartfelt compassion to every patient she treats. A proud graduate of the University of Washington School of Dentistry , Dr. Sarah is deeply committed to helping her patients achieve long-lasting oral health through a personalized, minimally invasive approach.

Advanced Training & Expertise

As a general dentist, Dr. Sarah has pursued advanced coursework in periodontology, prosthodontics, oral surgery, and dental photography, building a strong foundation in both the health of the gums and the restoration of teeth. Her ongoing education reflects her dedication to excellence and her deep understanding of the connection between oral health and overall wellness.

In addition to her dental degree, Dr. Sarah has completed numerous continuing education courses in:

Oral surgery Dental implant placement Full-mouth reconstruction Invisalign clear aligner therapy

She has trained under some of the leading cosmetic dentists in Washington State , refining her skills in esthetic dentistry to provide patients with smiles that are as beautiful as they are healthy.

Her clinical training also includes moderate oral sedation certification , allowing her to offer safe, comfortable care for patients who experience anxiety or require more complex procedures.

Her Path to Dentistry

Early in her career, Dr. Sarah considered pursuing oral and maxillofacial surgery through a combined MD path and completed Step 1 of the U.S. Medical Licensing Examination before deciding to focus her career on family and restorative dentistry.

A Philosophy Rooted in Prevention and Purpose

Prevent cavities before they start Manage and treat gum disease Understand the link between oral and overall health Build sustainable habits for lifelong wellness

Whether she’s placing implants, restoring a full smile, or simply teaching a patient how to floss more effectively, Dr. Sarah puts her heart and soul into her work. Her approach is always gentle, thorough, and rooted in your long-term health—not just a quick fix.

A Partner in Your Health Journey

Dr. Sarah sees every patient as a partner in care. She takes time to listen, educate, and involve you in every decision about your treatment. Her goal is not only to treat dental problems—but to empower you to prevent them .

If you’re looking for a dentist in Ellensburg who combines clinical excellence with genuine care , Dr. Sarah would be honored to welcome you to her practice.
