Dental implants represent the most impressive tooth replacement option in dentistry. They are permanent and come just about as close to a real tooth as, well, real teeth. Our periodontists place dental implants in the jawbone to replace one tooth, multiple teeth, or a full set of missing teeth. They are an extremely popular option among most of our patients.

Dental implants are a good option for most people with healthy gums and fully developed jawbones. You should have enough bone to anchor and support the implants.
