Admissions The future of dentistry begins here. Admissions As a student at the Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine – one of the world’s leading dental schools – you will learn the art and science of dental medicine in a state-of-the-art facility under the mentorship of innovative and experienced faculty.

DMD Program

The four-year DMD program combines rigorous coursework in biomedical, behavioral, and clinical sciences with intensive pre-clinical and clin i cal experiences , preparing students from day one to become confident, innovative providers .

DMD Advanced Standing Program

This two-year program prepares internationally-trained dentists with the education they’ll need to practice dentistry in the U.S. The program follows a condensed version of the DMD curriculum, providing students with extensive coursework and clinical experiences.

Postdoctoral Programs

We offer advanced degree and certificate programs in 10+ dental specialties , giving students a wide range of opportunities to advance their education in their chosen specialty. Our postdoctoral programs include a rigorous mix of coursework, clinical experiences, and research.
