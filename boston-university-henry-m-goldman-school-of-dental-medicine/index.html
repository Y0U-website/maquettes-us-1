The practice

Our faculty engage in innovative research and scholarship that grows knowledge within the oral healthcare field.

We foster mutual respect and cultural humility to ensure that faculty, staff, and students reach their full potential.

We engage in outreach activities to support and enhance our local and global communities while reducing health inequities.

Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine offers state-of-the-art dental care through our teaching clinic and faculty practice. Emphasizing preventive and restorative dentistry, our experienced dentists, hygienists, and students provide a range of patient services at our Patient Treatment Centers.

It’s our goal to provide a supportive, rewarding work environment for every member of our faculty and staff. With each individual accomplishment, GSDM takes another step toward achieving its mission in education, research, oral health care, and community service.
