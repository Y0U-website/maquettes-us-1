Welcome to Our Dental Family

At our dental office in Mt Vernon, NY, we strive to create a warm and welcoming environment where patients feel at ease from the moment they walk through our doors. Our goal as a family-oriented practice is to make dental care a positive experience for people of all ages , particularly those who have gone through problems at the dentist in the past.

Our bilingual practice serves the diverse needs of our community, and the way we provide dental care ensures that every patient feels valued and heard. Using the latest dental technology, Anthony DeBenedictis, D.D.S. provides informative and professional services that puts our patients in control.

About Our Practice

Our team brings over 30 years of comprehensive dental experience, offering a range of services , including periodontal, orthodontic, and oral surgery, for patients of all ages. Our bilingual staff speaks English and Spanish, ensuring you feel comfortable and understood. To us, dentistry is not just about filling cavities – it is about building trust, understanding, and a genuine connection with each patient, making you feel like a part of our family.
