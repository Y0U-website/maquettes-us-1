The practice

Walk-ins Are A vailable On A First C ome-First Served Basis.

Our staff is ready to help you with making appointments, scheduling lab work, assisting in finding low cost medications or with outside referral services.

Referral to our hygienist for cleaning is available upon dentist s discretion. Appointments always advised.

Complete vision care is offered at least one time per month. Please make sure to call for appointments and service dates. Appointments are advised.

Counseling services are always available on an appointment basis and held in the East wing of the clinic. The warm and relaxing environment goes to creating a comfortable and safe place to talk with grief counselors, family counseling and support for care givers.

The clinic offers a variety of medical services, from women s health, Spanish diabetes education programs, well visits and access to outside procedures provided at no charge to the patient. Walk-in services are available, however, appointments are recommended as we do often get overwhelmed and the wait time can be quite lengthy.

Target Group: Over 18 years old, low income, non-insured, under-insured, un-sheltered. Children over 8 can be seen on a case by case basis.

Fees: This is a free clinic solely supported by donations and grants.

Les soins

We offer full x-ray services, along with extractions, fillings and simple tooth repairs.

Honoraires
