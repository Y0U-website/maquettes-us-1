About Dr. Steven Chan, DDS

Meet Dr. Steven Chan, D.D.S.

Complete Family and Cosmetic Dental Care with Your Comfort in Mind

29+ Years of Trusted Dental Care in Alameda

Dr. Steven Chan graduated from the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry and has been serving the Alameda community for over 29 years. His practice, Alameda Smiles, is built on a simple principle: every patient deserves gentle, thorough care in a comfortable environment.

Dr. Chan provides a full range of dental services, including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and Invisalign orthodontics. Whether you need a routine cleaning, a smile makeover, or dental implants, Dr. Chan combines clinical expertise with a calm, patient-first approach.

Multilingual Care: English, Cantonese, Mandarin & Japanese

Dr. Chan is fluent in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, and Japanese. For Alameda’s diverse community, this means you can discuss your dental health, ask questions, and understand your treatment plan in the language you’re most comfortable with. Many patients travel from Oakland, San Francisco, and across the East Bay specifically for this multilingual care.

Community & Personal Interests

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Chan is an active community volunteer, participating in dental outreach programs both locally in Alameda and internationally. He’s a world traveler with a passion for learning about different cultures. When he’s not caring for patients, you’ll find him biking around the island, hiking Bay Area trails, or playing badminton and basketball.

Dr. Kruti Desai

Dr. Kruti Desai is a valued member of the Alameda Smiles team, bringing additional expertise and a warm, compassionate approach to patient care. Together, Dr. Chan and Dr. Desai ensure that every patient receives personalized attention and high-quality dental treatment.
