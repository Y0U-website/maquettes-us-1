Oral Hygiene Consults

We can teach you how to brush, floss, and clean your teeth properly to remove plaque and bacteria.

WHAT? Proper oral hygiene is essential for maintaining good oral health and preventing dental problems.

WHY? Your dentist or dental hygienist can teach you how to brush, floss, and clean your teeth properly to remove plaque and bacteria.

WHEN? Regular brushing and flossing can help prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath.

Mastering the Art of Effective Brushing and Flossing

Proper oral hygiene is the most important thing you can do to maintain good oral health. Brushing your teeth twice daily and flossing once daily can help remove plaque and bacteria, which are the leading causes of cavities and gum disease. Using fluoride toothpaste and seeing your dentist for regular checkups and cleanings is also essential.
