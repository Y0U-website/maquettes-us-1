Dr. Jones was fortunate to discover a profession he was passionate about at an early age. He was first introduced to the field of oral and maxillofacial surgery after needing jaw surgery in high school. His surgeon noticed his potential and enthusiasm, and was able to guide him in a direction to obtain a career in a profession that he continues to be just as passionate about today!

He was born and raised in rural Alabama. He received his undergraduate degree from The University of Alabama and received his doctorate from University of Alabama School of Dentistry in 1995. Dr. Jones enjoys all aspects of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery with a strong emphasis on implant restoration surgery. He attributes his successful practice to approaching every patient, whether it be a simple extraction or a complex facial fracture,

He has been a partner for greater than 23 years and is proud of the role Bay Center for Oral Implant Surgery has played in our community. Dr. Jones enjoys spending time with his fantastic daughters Anna and Sophia. He enjoys boating and considers himself an avid snow skier, even though both of his daughters can easily beat him down a black diamond slope.

Dr. Jakubowski joined Bay Center for Oral Implant Surgery in 2013. He then decided to follow his father’s footsteps into the career of dentistry, moving to Fort Lauderdale to complete his dental training at Nova Southeastern University.

While at dental school he served in multiple leadership positions, including Student Government President. He still is involved in leadership and community service to this day serving on the Pinellas County Dental Association board and will soon serve as its President.
