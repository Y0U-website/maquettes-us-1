The Dental Implant Process

Although every case is unique, the typical process includes:

1. Consultation & 3D Imaging

We evaluate your oral anatomy and review your goals.

2. Implant Placement

A precise surgical procedure where the implant is inserted into the jawbone.

3. Healing & Integration

The implant bonds with the bone, forming a strong foundation.

4. Restoration Placement

Your general dentist places a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis.

5. Long-Term Care & Maintenance

Routine visits ensure your implants remain healthy for years to come.
