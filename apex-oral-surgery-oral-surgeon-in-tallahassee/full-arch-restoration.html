Full Arch Restoration…

This treatment uses 4–6 dental implants to anchor a full arch of new teeth. Because the implants support the restoration, the result is secure, functional, and long-lasting—unlike traditional dentures that sit on the gums.

  • Have failing or severely damaged teeth
  • Wear dentures but want a permanent alternative
  • Want a stronger, more natural bite
  • Want to replace their entire smile with fixed, attractive teeth

Why Choose Full Arch Restoration?

Full Arch Restoration offers dramatic benefits that extend far beyond appearance:

Permanent, Non-Removable Teeth

Your new teeth are fixed in place—they do not come out, and they do not move.

Restore Your Bite Strength

Full arch implant prosthetics restore up to 90 % of natural chewing power, allowing you to enjoy foods you may have had to avoid.

Prevent Bone Loss in the Jaw

Dental implants stimulate bone and prevent the bone deterioration caused by missing teeth or denture wear.

Immediate Aesthetic Improvement

Many patients leave surgery with a temporary set of functional, beautiful teeth—often the very same day.

No Messy Adhesives or Slippage

Unlike dentures, these teeth stay put. No clicking, no slipping, no discomfort.

Long-Term Durability

Full arch implant bridges can last decades with proper care.

Life-Changing Confidence

Patients report feeling younger, healthier, and more confident in their smile and daily life.

Types of Full Arch Restoration at Apex Oral Surgery

At Apex Oral Surgery, we offer a full range of advanced full arch solutions, each tailored to your anatomy, goals, and long-term health.

All-on-4® Dental Implants

The All-on-4® system uses four implants placed at specific angles to maximize bone support, even for patients with bone loss.

  • Fewer implants required
  • Often avoids the need for bone grafting
  • Immediate placement of temporary teeth
  • Lower treatment cost compared to traditional full arch implant options

All-on-6 Implant System

Using six implants, this approach provides even greater stability and is ideal for patients with moderate bone density.

  • Increased structural support
  • Enhanced long-term durability
  • More secure full arch restoration

Custom Full Arch Implant Bridges

For patients seeking the highest level of aesthetics and durability, we offer premium zirconia or hybrid implant bridges designed for superior longevity and beauty.

Who Is a Candidate for Full Arch Restoration?

Most patients qualify, and even those told they “don’t have enough bone” can often receive implants with advanced grafting or All-on-4® solutions.

  • Have multiple missing or failing teeth
  • Wear removable dentures
  • Have advanced decay or gum disease
  • Want a permanent, fixed alternative to dentures
  • Have bone loss (in many cases, grafting can restore bone)
  • Want a stronger bite and more attractive smile

The Full Arch Restoration Process
