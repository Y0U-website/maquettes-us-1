MEET JORDAN WEIS, DMD

Dentist/Owner

Dr. Jordan Weis is a family dentist here to continue a great history of patient care in Jefferson City and the nearby communities. He grew up in Effingham, Illinois, where he found his passion for dentistry early. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree in biology from Southern Illinois University Edwardsville. Soon after, he was accepted into Midwestern University’s inaugural class of Dental Medicine in Downers Grove, Illinois.

After graduation, Dr. Weis worked as an associate dentist in Vandalia, Illinois, before moving to Missouri in 2021 to become the new and proud owner of Central Dental Care with his wife, Beth Weis. Dr. Weis takes pride in understanding the latest treatments available for his patients and using that knowledge to provide the best care in a comforting way!
