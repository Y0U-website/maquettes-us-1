Clear Aligners & Braces in Jefferson City, MO

Crafting Confident Smiles with Professional Orthodontics!

Here at Central Dental Care we offer traditional braces and Clear Aligners. Clear Aligners are an orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth without the use of metal braces. The Clear Aligners are a series of custom-made, clear aligners made from a simple mouth scan that cover your teeth and pull them into the proper position over time. Clear Aligners can be taken on and off and are less noticeable than metal braces.
