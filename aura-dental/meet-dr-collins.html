Meet Shana Vasquez,

Registered Dental Hygienist

Shana Vasquez brings more than 31 years of dental experience to Aura Dental Group Columbus. Her career began as a Certified Dental Assistant, followed by advanced training as an Expanded Functions Dental Assistant, before she continued her education to become a Registered Dental Hygienist. She holds a Bachelor of Science in General Science and has completed specialized training in orofacial myology and systemic oral health education.

Ms. Vasquez leads Aura Dental Group’s dental hygiene services for long-term care communities. Her work is centered on expanding access to preventive oral healthcare for older adults, medically complex patients, and individuals who may have difficulty traveling to a traditional dental office.

Ms. Vasquez believes that every resident deserves to be treated with dignity, patience, compassion, and respect. Her mission is to remove barriers to care and help long-term care facilities provide their residents with consistent access to high-quality preventive dental services.
