Travis Hampton

Dr. Travis Hampton attended Valdosta State University in Valdosta, Georgia, where he majored in biology. He was accepted into the Medical College of Georgia, School of Dentistry at the age of 19, making him one of the youngest applicants ever accepted in the program. He graduated from the Medical of Georgia in 1991 with a doctorate degree in dental medicine. While in dental school, Dr. Hampton was selected by his fellow classmates for the Dorothy C. Hearn award, which recognizes personal attributes advantageous to dentistry.

Dr. Hampton has practiced dentistry in his family practice in Covington, Georgia for over 20 years. Dr. Hampton firmly believes in continuing education and is a Master in the Academy of General Dentistry, which requires over 1,100 hours of continuing education and to pass the rigorous Mastership examination. He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry, and the American Academy of Facial Esthetics. While not practicing dentistry, Dr. Hampton enjoys golfing and other sports and playing the guitar.

Dr. Barbara L. Verdell, DDS, FICOI, FAAIP is a dedicated dentist focused on helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. A proud graduate of the Howard University College of Dentistry, she brings over 22 years of clinical experience and a warm, compassionate approach to everyday patient care.

Outside the Office, when she is not helping patients love their smiles, Dr. Verdell enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, and working out.
