The Implant Process at Countryside Dental

We won’t sugarcoat it: getting an implant isn’t a one-appointment process. But it is a very manageable one , and we’re with you at every step. Here’s generally how it goes:

The Consultation

We start by getting to know you and your goals. We’ll review your imaging, talk through your health history, and make sure implants are the right path. This is also a great time to ask every question you’ve been sitting on.

Placement

The implant post is inserted during a minor oral surgery procedure . If dental anxiety is a worry, we offer sedation options to keep you comfortable and relaxed throughout the procedure. Most patients are relieved by how manageable the experience is.

Healing

After placement, the implant needs time to fuse with the bone. This phase typically takes a few months, though the timeline varies by patient. We check in with you regularly during this period to make sure everything is healing well.

The Crown

Once the implant has integrated fully, we attach your custom crown. This is the part you see and use every day. We match the shape and shade to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth so the final result feels seamless.
