About Countryside Dental

Modern Dentistry. Countryside Comfort.

Countryside Dental was built on a simple belief: you shouldn’t have to leave the country to receive exceptional care.

Our practice blends the best of both worlds big-city technology and true Southern hospitality, to create a dental experience that feels modern, warm, and deeply personal.

We proudly serve families across LaGrange and surrounding rural communities, offering everything from cleanings to implants to advanced surgical care under one roof. Patients looking for cosmetic dental care in LaGrange will find our full range of aesthetic services right here. But more than that, we offer something harder to find these days: a place where people feel seen, valued, and treated like family.

MESSAGE FROM THE DOCTORS

Dentistry has changed drastically over the years. Some changes for the better, and some…well, you know. Countryside is not just a regular dental practice. This is the practice where we create your denture for your granddaughters’ wedding, where we give you your smile back after being laid off for years, where we fix a front tooth before your big job interview. A dental practice where you don’t just feel like family, you ARE family.

People can say what they want about “living rural” or “living in the country”. But as country girls ourselves, we know folks out here have the biggest hearts. Our doors are open to everyone, and it is time for our big ‘ole hearts to match our patient’s future big ‘ole smiles.

So, c’mon. Lace up your boots and help us kick up some dust… It’s Countryside.

MEET YOUR DOCTORS

Dr. Jenai Bostic, DMD

Dentist & Practice Owner

A proud Spelman graduate and UAB School of Dentistry alumna, Dr. Bostic blends advanced clinical expertise with a warm, down-to-earth approach. Known for her gentle touch and calming personality, she makes every patient feel like they’re catching up with a trusted friend.

Her specialized training in implants and restorative dentistry helps patients achieve confident, lasting smiles.

Dr. Yokeca Watts, DMD

Dentist & Practice Owner

With advanced residency training focused on IV sedation, bone grafting, and complex implant placement, Dr. Watts brings precision and comfort to every procedure. Her calm presence helps even anxious patients feel at ease, while her surgical expertise allows Countryside Dental to offer a full range of advanced treatments right here in LaGrange, GA. Dr. Watts is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, the American Academy of General Dentistry, and the Georgia Dental Society, where she currently serves as Treasurer.

Together, Dr. Bostic and Dr. Watts share one mission:

To bring modern, meaningful dental care to the countryside, and to treat every person who walks through their doors like family.

WHAT MAKES COUNTRYSIDE DIFFERENT?

With Yomi robotic-assisted surgery, CBCT imaging, digital scanning, and 3D printing, we provide cutting-edge care without the big-city commute.

Our patients drive from LaGrange, Hogansville, Franklin, West Point, Carrollton, Columbus, Valley (AL), Roanoke (AL), Opelika (AL), and beyond. We proudly serve communities that deserve high-quality care close to home.

We take time to listen, really listen to your concerns, goals, and questions. No pressure. No judgment. No rushed care.

From preventive care to implants, cosmetics to emergencies, we offer complete care so you can feel confident in one trusted place.
