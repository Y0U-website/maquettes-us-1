financing

At Alford Family Dentistry, we make dental care more affordable by accepting CareCredit, a health and wellness credit card that allows you to get the care you need today and pay over time. With CareCredit, you can easily manage out-of-pocket expenses by breaking them into convenient monthly payments. Simply apply online for an instant credit decision, and start receiving the dental treatment you deserve without the stress of immediate costs.

Insurance Information

At Alford Family Dentistry, we strive to make your dental experience as pleasant as possible. We offer flexible payment options, accepting VISA, MasterCard, debit cards, cash, and checks.

Although we do not participate in any insurance networks, we accept most dental insurance programs, including all PPO in-network plans. This includes providers such as Aetna, Ameritas, Assurant, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Delta Dental, Humana, MetLife, United Healthcare, and many more. Our team will assist with all necessary forms for your dental benefits, but please remember that your policy is an agreement between you and your insurance company.

It’s important to note that you are responsible for any remaining balance if your insurance does not cover the full amount. We can provide a pre-treatment estimate to give you an idea of what your benefits will cover. Our fees are consistent with what we charge all patients for similar services. Since insurance policies and coverage can vary significantly, we encourage you to review your policy details to understand your specific benefits.
