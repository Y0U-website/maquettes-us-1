A Commitment to Comprehensive Dental Care

At Alford Family Dentistry, we believe that a healthy, confident smile is a vital part of overall well-being. Whether you’re visiting us for routine dental care or more specialized treatments, our approach is always centered on communication, education, and patient comfort. We understand that every patient is unique, so we take the time to tailor each treatment plan to your individual needs, ensuring that you receive the best possible care. Our dedicated team is here to answer all your questions, discuss your concerns, and guide you through every step of your dental journey.

Our Office: Designed with Your Comfort in Mind

We know that a positive dental experience goes beyond the treatments we provide. From the moment you walk through our doors, we want you to feel at ease. That’s why our office is thoughtfully designed with modern amenities that create a warm and inviting atmosphere. Whether you’re waiting for your appointment or receiving treatment, every aspect of our space is intended to make you feel comfortable and cared for. At Alford Family Dentistry, we’ve created an environment where you can relax, knowing you’re in good hands.

Meet Our Team

Blaire Alford, D.M.D. Dr. Alford knew from an early age she wanted to make people smile and, while in the seventh grade, felt God’s calling to become a dentist. She graduated from Terry High School and went on to study all four years as a chemistry major at Mississippi College before attending the University of Mississippi Medical Center’s School of Dentistry. Dr. Alford is a current member of the American Dental Association, Mississippi Dental Association, Academy of General Dentists, and the Mississippi Association of Women Dentists.

Dr. Alford is dedicated to staying current in dental concepts and technologies. She is committed to continuing education in order to provide her patients with great dental care.

Born and raised in Terry, Mississippi, Dr. Alford currently resides there with her husband, Brandon, and their two daughters, Julia Pate and Massey. They attend First Baptist Church of Terry and enjoy a busy, fulfilling life together. Dr. Alford loves getting to know each patient personally and strives to create lasting relationships as both a dental provider and friend!

Chantel Everett, D.M.D. Dr. Everett has been practicing dentistry for 30 years. She graduated with numerous awards from the University of Mississippi Medical Center and holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Nursing and a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. Dr. Everett worked as a solo practitioner in Clinton for 24 years and, in 2020, transferred ownership to Dr. Blaire Alford. Dr. Everett continues to be part of the outstanding team at AFD, where she considers our patients as family.

Dr. Everett is married to her high school sweetheart, Joel, and they have one son, Reece, who is also a dentist.

In her leisure time, Dr. Everett enjoys reading, fantasy football, volunteering at her church, and spending time with friends and family at beautiful Smith Lake, Alabama.

Dr. Everett is a lifetime member of the American Dental Association and is also a member of Dynamic Mobile Dentistry, a team that offers on-site dental care to residents of nursing home facilities in Alabama.

Dr. Everett has a tremendous appreciation for the dental profession and gives God all the glory for the opportunity to change lives by helping patients achieve and maintain healthy, beautiful smiles.
