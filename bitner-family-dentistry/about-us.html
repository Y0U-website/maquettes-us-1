Dr. Baha Qablawi

Dr. Baha Qablawi, DMD, earned his dental degree from NOVA Southeastern University after completing his Bachelor of Science in Chemistry at Florida State University, where he was also a member of the Seminoles football team. He holds advanced certifications in Invisalign® and mini dental implant dentistry, and is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-focused care. Dr. Qablawi emphasizes clear communication and education, helping patients make confident, informed decisions about their oral health.
