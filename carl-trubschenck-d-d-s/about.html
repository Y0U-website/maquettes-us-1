ABOUT our citrus heights dental team Dedicated to Excellence in Dental Care for Over 30 Years Dr. Carl Trubschenck Dr. Trubschenck studied dentistry at renowned UOP School of Dentistry in San Francisco. There he graduated with honors and went on to receive post-graduate training in periodontics (gum surgery), and cosmetic dentistry. He now maintains a busy practice, enjoying all phases of restorative and cosmetic services in Citrus Heights, CA.

Dr. Trubschenck has been an instructor for UOP School of Dentistry. Also, throughout his 30+ years of dental practice, Dr. Trubschenck has served as a national consultant on new dental products and materials, and has been a contributing editor for the Journal of the American Dental Association.

In addition, he is currently the hospital staff dentist & consultant for a hospital in Sacramento.

Dr. Trubschenck is also proud to have been the “Dentist to the Dentists,” — serving dozens of other dentists, who recognize him to be not only gentle and caring, but technically excellent.

Because of his exceptional dental care, he has helped over 10,000 patients in his career, that have traveled from many cities around California, and even many from out of state and country!
