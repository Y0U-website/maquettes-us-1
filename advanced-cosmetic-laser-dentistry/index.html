Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. Once integrated with the bone a process called osseointegration a custom-crafted crown is attached on top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural one.

Dr. Lee holds the "DICOI designation" (Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists) one of the highest credentials available in implant dentistry. He is also a member of the "American Dental Implant Association", reflecting his deep commitment to implant excellence.

With an in-house laboratory and state-of-the-art technology, our Dover, NJ practice offers precision implant placement with a focus on natural aesthetics and long-term success.
