The Dental Implant Process

Crown Placement

A custom porcelain crown is attached, completing your new smile.

Healing Period

Over 3 6 months, the implant fuses with the bone (osseointegration).

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients return to work the next day.

Consultation & Planning

We take digital X-rays and 3D scans to evaluate your bone density and design your implant placement.

Candidates for Dental Implants

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have a fully developed jawbone
  • Have adequate bone density (or can receive a bone graft)
  • Don t smoke heavily
  • Have good overall oral and systemic health
