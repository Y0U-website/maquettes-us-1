The practice

Dr. Lee grew up in Mill Creek. She attended the University of Washington for her undergraduate degree, and then was accepted to Harvard School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts, where she graduated as Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry. Following dental school, Dr. Lee returned home to the Northwest to complete her advanced degree at the University of Washington School of Orthodontics.

After working closely with Dr. Robert Bendzak, Dr. Lee took over his practice in August, 2016.

City Grin Orthodontics generally serves the Kenmore, Bothell, Lake Forest Park, Brier, Mountlake Terrace, Lake City, Shoreline, Juanita, and Kirkland areas.​

The same core principles that are associated with this practice since Dr. Robert Bendzak opened the doors in 1963 still guides us today. We continue to strive towards excellence by providing quality care in a fun and comfortable environment. Not only will you have a personal experience in the office, we hope to see you in the surrounding area as we are active in the communities we serve!
