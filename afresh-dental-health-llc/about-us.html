Dr. Yvonne

Dr. Yvonne s favorite aspect of her work is forming relationships with patients at all stages of life and getting to know them as people. She believes understanding a patient s dental needs and desires often stems from recognizing their core values and what s most important to them.

After exploring various options, she found her passion in dentistry despite fainting the first time she watched a root canal.
