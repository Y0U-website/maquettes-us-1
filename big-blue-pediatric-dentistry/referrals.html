Frenectomy Referral for providers

Immediate Appointments

Specific appointment times are set aside for Frenectomy and Functional Frenuloplasty consultations. In most cases, a frenectomy for infants and young children can be done same day. Functional Frenuloplasties, which require sutures, will be scheduled after the consultation is completed

Transparent Costs

We are in network with multiple dental insurance plans. For patients who do not have dental insurance or have and out of network plan, a medical superbill will be given. All cost’s are presented to the patient up front and there are no additional costs for follow up appointments

Detailed Evaluation and consultation

In addition to the information given by the referring provider, our doctors will complete an in depth history. A thorough oral assessment is done which includes anatomical and functional findings. If a procedure is recommended, the provider will discuss all options and recommendations for a successful release
