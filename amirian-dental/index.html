If you are considering restoring your smile with dental implants, our very own Dr. Inna Amirian is a trusted expert in creating beautiful dental implant restorations in Hudson, MA, that look and feel like your natural teeth.

Dental implants are a permanent solution to replace damaged or decayed teeth, and function exactly as your regular teeth would. In fact, most people can’t even distinguish between your new natural-looking implants and your original teeth!

Implants are composed of titanium rods that are surgically inserted into your jaw bone and eventually completely fuse with the bone. Amirian Dental partners with a trusted local specialist who performs the initial implant procedure by setting the rods in the jawbone.
