Our Services

At Coastal Dental Hygiene Studio, we offer a wide range of preventative hygiene services. Our team of experienced and friendly dental professionals is dedicated to providing you with the best possible care.

Do we take insurance?

We are now able to bill Northeast Delta Dental . Please note that we are an out-of-network provider with Delta Dental.

If you have insurance other than Northeast Delta Dental , payment will be due in full at the time of service . Upon request, we can provide you with a detailed “Superbill” that you may submit to your insurance company for possible reimbursement.

New Patients

We welcome new patients to our practice and look forward to providing you with the best possible care. Our team will work with you to ensure that your first visit is comfortable and stress-free.

Patient Education

At Coastal Dental Hygiene Studio Dental Practice, we believe that patient education is an important part of maintaining good oral health. We provide our patients with the information they need to make informed decisions about their dental care.
