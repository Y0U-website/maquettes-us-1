Meet the Faces Behind the Smiles Christina Tibbetts, IPDH

With over 20 years of experience in dental hygiene, Christina Tibbetts is a highly skilled and compassionate Independent Practice Dental Hygienist (IPDH) dedicated to delivering exceptional preventive care. Born and raised along the coast of Maine, Christina brings both professional expertise and a deep sense of community to her practice.

Known for her gentle touch and patient-centered approach, Christina takes the time to understand each patient’s needs, helping them feel informed, comfortable, and cared for at every visit. She is especially passionate about making dental care a positive and empowering experience for people of all ages.

Outside of the office, Christina enjoys life in coastal Maine with her husband, two children, and their two beloved dogs. Whether she’s walking the beach, spending time with her family, or connecting with patients, Christina brings warmth, attentiveness, and dedication to everything she does.

“I love helping people feel confident in their smiles and supported in their oral health—it’s truly the most rewarding part of what I do.”

Amanda Catalano is a licensed Independent Practice Dental Hygienist (IPDH) with over 13 years of experience providing compassionate, thorough, and professional dental hygiene care. Born and raised in Maine, Amanda is deeply committed to serving her local community with a personalized, patient-first approach.

She is passionate about preventive oral health and takes pride in delivering high-quality care that is both comfortable and effective. Amanda believes in educating patients, building trust, and creating a positive experience for every visit—no matter how long it’s been since your last cleaning.

When she’s not caring for patients, Amanda enjoys spending time outdoors with her husband and their three energetic boys. Whether it’s hiking, boating, or just enjoying Maine’s natural beauty, she brings the same warmth and energy to her family life as she does to her practice.

“My goal is to create a space where patients feel heard, respected, and empowered to take charge of their oral health—one smile at a time.”
