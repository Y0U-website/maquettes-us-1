Periodontal Maintenance

This appointment is for patients who have been previously diagnosed with periodontal (gum) disease and have completed active periodontal therapy, such as scaling and root planing. Periodontal maintenance is essential to help manage gum disease and prevent further progression.

Review of medical and dental history Comprehensive periodontal charting and gum health evaluation Removal of plaque, tartar, and biofilm above and below the gumline Irrigation or localized antimicrobial treatment (as needed) Polishing of teeth and restorative surfaces Oral cancer screening Personalized home care guidance and updates Annual bite-wing x-ray, does NOT include FMX

Teen Maintenance

Our Teen Oral Maintenance Appointment is designed specifically for adolescents, typically ages 12–18, to support their oral health during a time of rapid growth and change. We focus on education and prevention—helping teens build healthy routines that last a lifetime, while identifying early signs of gum issues or decay. Our calm, non-judgmental approach helps teens feel comfortable and confident in caring for their smile.

Review of dental and medical history Assessment of gum and tooth health Thorough cleaning to remove plaque, tartar, and stains Fluoride or hydroxyapatite treatment to strengthen enamel Oral hygiene coaching tailored to teen habits (braces, sports, diet, etc.) Optional discussion of sealants, mouthguards, or ortho referrals if needed

Child Maintenance

Our Child Maintenance Appointment is designed to help kids develop strong, healthy smiles from the start. We provide gentle, age-appropriate care in a fun and welcoming environment that helps children feel safe and relaxed.

A review of your child’s dental and medical history Gentle cleaning to remove plaque and tartar Polishing to remove surface stains Fluoride treatment to protect developing teeth Oral hygiene education for kids and parents Monitoring of growth, development, and eruption patterns Guidance on habits like thumb-sucking, diet, brushing, and flossing Annual bite-wing x-ray, does NOT include FMX

Scaling and Root Planing

Periodontal therapy is often completed over one or more appointments, depending on the severity of the condition. Regular maintenance afterward is critical to keeping the disease under control. Our goal is to stop the progression of gum disease, reduce inflammation, and help you maintain a healthier, more comfortable smile.
