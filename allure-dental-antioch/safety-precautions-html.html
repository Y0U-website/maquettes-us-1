What’s New???

SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

AIR PURIFICATION

We have installed two forms of air purification units. The first unit purifies the air from viruses, bacteria, mold, and allergens in the entire building. The second units are setup in each of our procedure rooms with HEPA filters & UV-C Light to help purify the air of bacteria & viruses. Investing in and utilizing a dual unit approach, we are very confident that the air you breath in our office will be free of any virus, bacteria, allergens, mold, and other pathogens.

UV-C WATER STERILIZATION

We have installed UV-C water sterilization devices to our dental chairs. This will help make our water supply even safer. We already had a water filtration system, but wanted to take you safety to the next level to insure no bacteria, viruses or chemicals is transferred in our water supply.

PPE

We will be wearing facial masks and in certain cases a N95 mask. Also we will be wearing fluid resistant gowns, goggles, and face shields to help keep everyone safe.

BARRIERS

We have installed clear barriers in our front office to prevent the spread of any airborne particles. This will help keep everyone safe.

CABINETS

We have undergone construction in our procedure rooms to make sure supplies & material in our procedure rooms are closed in cabinets & safe from any aerosols which may get pass the air purifiers.
