Dr. Nazia Zaidi General Dentist

Dr. Nazia Zaidi grew up on the east coast, in the New York/New Jersey area. She attended Rutgers University where she received both her Bachelors and Masters before going on to New York University for her Doctorate in Dental Surgery. While at NYU, Dr. Zaidi actively contributed to her school community as a board member in the American Student Dental Association and Student Council.

Dr. Zaidi has been serving the pediatric population in the greater Indianapolis area for the last several years. She loves what she does and is excited to bring her passion for pediatric dentistry to Westfield, her current home town.

Outside of her professional endeavors, Dr. Zaidi loves exploring the world with her husband and two sons. They recently took a memorable trip to Italy where they were able to learn about its rich history and eat some delicious food! Additionally, her husband works as an Emergency Physician serving the Hamilton County area. Together, they find joy in serving the communities they live in.
