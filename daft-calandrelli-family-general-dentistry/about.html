Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Gian Calandrelli, DMD

Born and raised in Rehoboth, Dr. Gian Calandrelli brings a unique blend of local roots, technical training, and personal warmth to every patient relationship. A graduate of Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Calandrelli blends his passions for art, psychology, and hands-on design to offer care that’s as thoughtful as it is technically precise.

With advanced training through The Pankey Institute, Dr. Calandrelli focuses on comprehensive, relationship-driven dental care—moving beyond “quick fixes” to help patients regain long-term oral health and enjoy true healing. He is especially skilled in diagnosing and treating TMJ/TMD disorders, using therapeutic modalities such as custom splints, appliance therapy, and integrative approaches that bring lasting relief to those who need it most.

Dr. Calandrelli believes good dentistry is human-centered—and transformative. His priority is creating a calming, collaborative experience that helps patients feel in control of their own health journey. Whether you’re here for cosmetic work, implants, or routine care, he customizes each treatment plan to reflect your goals, your story, and your smile.

Outside the office, Dr. Calandrelli devotes time to woodworking, programming, and spending quiet moments with his wife and their dog—reflecting the same meticulous attention to craft and harmony he brings to each dental case. His vision for the practice is simple but powerful: To redefine what it means to “go to the dentist” through deeper trust, modern tools, and authentic care.

Where Comfort Meets Cutting-Edge Care

Our investment in innovative technology is a reflection of our commitment to your long-term wellness. At Daft & Calandrelli, patients benefit from tools and systems designed for accuracy, comfort, and quicker healing—all while receiving care in a setting focused on calm, connection, and personalized service.

CEREC & CAD/CAM Technology Same-day crowns and restorations are possible thanks to our in-house design and milling capabilities. These tools allow us to produce precise, custom restorations without multiple appointments—and without relying on third-party labs.

CBCT 3D Imaging Our Cone Beam Computed Tomography scanner lets us analyze jaw structure, nerve pathways, and sinus spaces in exacting detail. It ensures accurate diagnosis and surgical planning, especially for implant placement or TMD treatment.

Guided Implant Placement Using surgical guides designed with CAD/CAM software and 3D-printed in-house, our doctors can place implants with higher accuracy and lower risk. This results in better healing, function, and peace of mind for patients undergoing restorative solutions.
