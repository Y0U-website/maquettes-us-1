Why This Practice Exists — And Who It&#x27;s For

Dr. Deshpande didn&#x27;t build a practice for people who love going to the dentist. She built it for everyone else.

Most dental offices are designed around efficiency — get patients in, get them out. Dr. Deshpande designed hers around something different: the patient who almost didn&#x27;t come in at all.

She noticed early in her career that the patients who needed care the most were the ones least likely to show up — not because they didn&#x27;t care about their teeth, but because a past experience had left them dreading the chair. So she made a decision to slow down. To explain every step before doing it. To never rush through a procedure just to hit the next appointment.

That philosophy is still the backbone of Brilliant Smiles today. Every new patient gets time — time to ask questions, time to understand what&#x27;s happening in their mouth, and time to feel comfortable before anything begins. Nobody is made to feel judged for how long it&#x27;s been since their last visit.

The Diamond Invisalign credential didn&#x27;t come from adding a trendy service to a menu. It came from years of Dr. Deshpande focusing deeply on orthodontic cases and refining her approach with hundreds of patients. Align Technology awards Diamond status to the top 1 % of providers nationally — a benchmark earned through volume, complexity, and outcomes, not just enrollment in the program.

Brilliant Smiles is intentionally a small practice. That&#x27;s not a limitation — it&#x27;s a choice. It means Dr. Deshpande knows her patients by name, remembers their history, and is never handing you off to someone you&#x27;ve never met.
