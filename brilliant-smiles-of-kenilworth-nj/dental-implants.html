Essential information about this procedure

What many people don&#x27;t realize is that a missing tooth also affects the jawbone underneath it. Without a tooth root stimulating the bone, the jaw slowly begins to shrink in that area — which can eventually change the shape of your face and affect the teeth around the gap.

A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that addresses all of this at once. A small titanium post — about the size of a tooth root — is placed into the jawbone, where it fuses naturally over several weeks. That process, called osseointegration, creates a permanent foundation. Once healed, Dr. Deshpande places a custom porcelain crown on top that&#x27;s matched to the shape and shade of your surrounding teeth.

The result isn&#x27;t just cosmetic. You get back the full chewing function of a natural tooth, the bone underneath stays healthy, and nothing in your mouth has to shift or compensate. For most of our patients, the biggest regret is that they didn&#x27;t do it sooner.

Questions We Hear About Dental Implants Are dental implants as permanent as they sound? They really are — implants are anchored directly into your jawbone and function just like a natural tooth root. With proper care, they can last decades, and many patients have them for life. They don&#x27;t slip, they don&#x27;t need to be removed, and they look and feel completely like your own teeth.

Is the implant procedure painful?

The placement is done under local anesthesia, so you won&#x27;t feel the procedure itself. Afterward, most patients experience mild soreness for a few days — similar to how your mouth feels after a tooth extraction — which over-the-counter pain relief manages well for most people. Dr. Deshpande has placed many implants and takes a careful, unhurried approach that patients consistently describe as much more manageable than they expected.

How long does the whole implant process take?

From implant placement to final crown, the process typically takes 3 to 6 months. That timeline allows the implant to fully integrate with the bone — a process called osseointegration — before the permanent crown is attached. Dr. Deshpande will give you a personalized timeline based on your specific situation at your consultation.

Are implants covered by dental insurance?

Coverage varies widely — some plans cover a portion of the implant cost, while others don&#x27;t include it at all. We accept most major dental plans and will check your specific benefits so you know what to expect before committing to treatment. Even where insurance coverage is limited, many patients find that the durability and quality of life improvement make implants worth the investment compared to alternatives that need to be replaced over time.

Who is a good candidate for a dental implant?

Most healthy adults with sufficient jawbone density are good candidates. Factors like gum disease, bone loss, or certain health conditions can affect eligibility, but many of these can be addressed before implant placement. The best way to find out is to come in for a consultation — Dr. Deshpande will review your X-rays and health history and give you an honest assessment of whether implants are the right fit for you.
