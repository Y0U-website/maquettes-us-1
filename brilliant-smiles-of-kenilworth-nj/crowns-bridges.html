A crown fits over the entire visible portion of the tooth, protecting what&#x27;s underneath and restoring full function. You can chew normally, speak normally, and smile without thinking twice about it. And because every crown Dr. Deshpande places is individually designed using digital imaging, it&#x27;s matched precisely to the shade, shape, and contour of the teeth around it. Most people can&#x27;t tell the difference — and that&#x27;s exactly the point.

Bridges work on the same principle, but for a tooth that&#x27;s already gone. Instead of leaving a gap that shifts surrounding teeth over time, a bridge anchors to the teeth on either side and fills the space with a lifelike replacement. It&#x27;s a reliable, fixed solution — no removal, no adhesive, no daily hassle.

How many appointments will I need, and how long do they take?

A crown or bridge typically requires two visits. The first appointment — usually about an hour — involves preparing the tooth and taking impressions for your custom restoration. You&#x27;ll leave with a temporary crown while your permanent one is being crafted. At the second visit, we fit and cement the final restoration, which usually takes about 30 to 45 minutes.

Will my crown or bridge look like a real tooth?

Yes — today&#x27;s porcelain crowns and bridges are matched to the color and shape of your natural teeth, so they blend in seamlessly. Most people won&#x27;t be able to tell which tooth was restored, including you. Dr. Deshpande takes care with the fit and appearance so the result looks and feels completely natural.

Does my insurance cover crowns and bridges?

Crowns and bridges are considered restorative procedures, and most major dental plans cover a significant portion of the cost. We accept most major insurance plans and are happy to walk you through your benefits before treatment so there are no surprises. If you have questions about out-of-pocket costs, just ask — we&#x27;ll give you a clear picture upfront.

How long will a crown or bridge actually last?

With good home care and regular checkups, crowns and bridges routinely last 10 to 15 years or longer — many last much longer. The key is keeping up with cleanings and catching any small issues early. Dr. Deshpande will check the fit and condition of your restoration at every visit to make sure it stays in great shape.

How do I know if I need a crown?

You may need a crown if a tooth is significantly cracked, weakened by a large filling, or has undergone root canal therapy. During your exam, we&#x27;ll explain whether a crown is the best option and show you exactly why on your X-rays.
