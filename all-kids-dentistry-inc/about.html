About All Kids Dentistry

All Kids Dentistry is a pediatric dental practice located in beautiful Eau Claire, WI. The clinic specializes in comprehensive dentistry for children ages 12 months - 16 years old.

Dr. Wallace is a Board Certified pediatric specialist. She has over 20 years of dental experience and enjoys applying her specialty pediatric training to make each child’s visit to the dentist a pleasant one. The staff at All Kids Dentistry is committed to your dental needs by providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, friendly atmosphere. We provide comprehensive dental services for your children, from routine teeth cleanings to preventive care as well as restorative services.We are focused on making each patient and parents experience a pleasurable one!!
