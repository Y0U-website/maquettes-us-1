Primary Care Providers

DR. ANDREW WILLIAMS, MD FACP

Dr. Williams is a Harvard trained physician, board certified in Internal Medicine. In addition to working as a Physician and Chief Medical Officer for Community Health Center of the North Country, he is also the Associate Chief Medical Officer at St. Lawrence Health Systems and an Assistant Clinical Professor at the University of Rochester School of Medicine.

Dr. Williams has received numerous awards and honors for his academic achievement, most recently the 2020 Dr. Gary Ogden Rural Health Practitioner of the Year Award and the Fort Drum Regional Health Planning Organization’s 2021 Health Hero Award. He is an active member of 3 different medical societies: American College of Physicians, Massachusetts Medical Society, and American Society of Echocardiography.

DR. JOHN DUFFY, MD

Dr. Duffy received his Doctor of Medicine Degree from the Ross University School of Medicine in New York City in 2000 followed by a Chief Residency in Family Practice at Ohio State University from 2000-2003.

Prior to joining our team Dr. Duffy spent time at Advanced Integrated Medical Center, Carolina East Internal Medicine, and Capital Family Medicine in North Carolina before relocating to New York where he joined the team at Canton-Potsdam Hospital from 2016-2020 followed by six years at Claxton Hepburn Medical Center where he also served as Medical Director from 2024-2026.

DR. ROBERT T. ROGERS, II, MD

Dr. Rogers is an Internist with degrees from both Dartmouth College and McGill University. He completed his internship and residency at Albany Medical Center. He is a Fellow with the American College of Physicians and is certified by the American Board of Internal Medicine. Prior to joining our team Dr. Rogers practiced internal medicine for 46 years here in the North Country with Canton-Potsdam Hospital. He has also been Medical Director of the Clarkson Physician Assistant Program since its inception in 2010.

Robert Stickles, LCSW-R

Bob joins our Canton Behavioral Health team with over 20 years of experience under his belt. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Sociology at SUNY Potsdam college in 1994 before earning his Master of Science degree in Social Work from Syracuse University in 2004. He has provided social services throughout the county since 2000 and held positions at St. Lawrence County Department of Social Services, Claxton Hepburn Medical Center, and the St. Lawrence Psychiatric Center.

Sara Elliott, LCSW

Sara received her Bachelor of Science in Human Development and Family Relations from SUNY Plattsburg in 2016, followed by her Master of Science degree in Social Work from SUNY Albany in 2020. Since the completion of her degree, she has spent the past four and a half years as a Chemical Dependency Counselor at St. Lawrence Health Inpatient Rehabilitation. She has expertise in addiction assessment and motivational interviewing, and demonstrates a strong commitment to client advocacy and holistic recovery. When appropriate, she is joined by her Certified Canine Good Citizen, Goosey Girl.

Zoel Houle, LMSW

Zoel is the newest member of our Behavioral Health team. He received his Bachelor of Arts Degree in Psychology from the University of North Florida in May of 2008, and then went on to receive his Master of Social Work, Magna Cum Laude from Syracuse University in August of 2024. Prior to joining us, Zoel spent time helping clients at THRIVE Wellness and Recovery, Citizen Advocates Behavioral Health and Urgent Care, and Family Counseling Services of Northern New York.

Goosey Girl, Animal-Assisted Therapy

Goosey Girl is a Registered Canine Good Citizen who brings comfort, support, and healing to those in need. Trained and certified through The American Kennel Club, Goosey Girl specializes in providing emotional support during therapy sessions, helping clients feel at ease and fostering a safe, welcoming environment. With an intuitive sense of comfort and frivolous demeanor, Goosey Girl excels at offering unconditional companionship, reducing stress, and promoting joy and relaxation during appropriate visits with Sara Elliott, LCSW.

Noelle Polniak, Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner

Noelle has spent the past 10 years providing women’s health services at Canton-Potsdam OBGYN/Rochester Regional Health, bringing along a wealth of experience to a much needed service at our health center. She has previous work experience at Massena Hospital, Claxton-Hepburn Medical Center, and Planned Parenthood of the North Country. She received her Bachelors of Science in Nursing in 2008 from the University of Central Florida, followed by her Women’s Health Nurse Practitioner degree in 2012 at the University of Cincinnati in Ohio.
