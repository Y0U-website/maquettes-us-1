Meet Dr. Hunt

A native Mississippian, Dr. Kyle Hunt finished summa cum laude and received his Bachelor of Science from Mississippi College in 2002. Dr. Hunt finished first in his class and received his Doctor of Dental Medicine in 2006 from the University Of Mississippi School Of Dentistry.

Dr. Hunt completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery training at the Medical College of Georgia in Augusta, Georgia. During this time, he received extensive training in all aspects of oral and maxillofacial surgery, serving as chief resident from 2009-2010.

Dr. Hunt joined Central Mississippi Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery in July 2010. His practice covers broad scope oral and maxillofacial surgery, including dental implant surgery, traumatic facial injuries, dentoalveolar surgery, orthognathic surgery, soft and hard tissue pathology, and reconstruction. He is on staff at St. Dominic Jackson Memorial Hospital and Mississippi Baptist Medical Center. He is committed to providing excellent care to the medical and dental community.

He is a diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, a fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, and a member of the American Dental Association and the Mississippi Dental Association.

Dr. Hunt is a past president of the Mississippi Dental Association for District IV and the past president of the Mississippi Society Of Maxillofacial Oral Surgeons.

Dr. Hunt is an active member of Brandon Baptist Church. He enjoys spending time with his wife and five children, deer hunting, coaching youth sports, church activities, and outdoor activities.
