Dr. Allison lives in Duluth with her husband, Travis, and their five children. In the summers she enjoys swimming in local lakes while in the winter you can find her skiing, either downhill or cross country. She enjoys NPR, crafts, festivals and yoga. Dr. Allison is excited to become more active in the Twin Ports community.

Dr. Allison originally from Poughkeepsie NY, finished her undergraduate work at Colgate University in Hamilton, NY and received her degree in Dentistry from the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Allison is a member of the National Dental Honor Society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon, founded in 1914 to recognize scholarship and character in the dental profession. Since its inception membership in Omicron Kappa Upsilon has come to represent one of the highest honors attainable in the dental profession.

Dr. Allison is also an active member of the American Dental Association , Minnesota Dental Association , and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry as well as several other local and professional organizations.
