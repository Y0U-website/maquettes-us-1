  • Invisalign Straighten your smile discreetly and comfortably with Invisalign clear aligners. This modern orthodontic treatment uses a series of nearly invisible, removable trays to gently shift your teeth into alignment—without the need for traditional metal braces. Invisalign can: Correct mild to moderate crowding or spacing

  • Improve bite alignment

  • Teeth Whitening (Gloscience) We’re proud to now offer GloScience Professional Whitening , a cutting-edge treatment that delivers rapid, long-lasting results with minimal sensitivity. Using a combination of warming heat and LED light technology, GloScience safely accelerates whitening for a noticeably brighter smile in just one visit. For those who prefer to whiten gradually, our take-home whitening system allows you to achieve professional results in the comfort of your own home. Whichever option you choose, you’ll enjoy a whiter, more confident smile in no time.

  • Close gaps between teeth
  • Correct slightly crooked or misshapen teeth
  • Repair chips, cracks, or worn edges

Other Services
