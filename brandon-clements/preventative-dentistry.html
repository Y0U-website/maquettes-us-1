Preventative Dentistry

  • Dental Fillings When tooth decay strikes, our tooth-colored fillings made from porcelain or composite resin restore both function and appearance. Fillings eliminate discomfort, stop decay from spreading, and blend naturally with your smile.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Early detection saves lives. Our simple, painless oral cancer screening examines your entire mouth for signs of abnormal cell growth. With regular dental visits, oral cancer can often be caught early—when it’s most treatable. In 90 % of cases, if followed by proper treatment, oral cancer is generally curable.
  • Root Canal Treatments When a tooth becomes infected or severely decayed, root canal therapy can save it. During treatment, our dentists gently remove infection, clean the root canal, and seal the tooth to prevent further damage. Common Symptoms: - Extreme sensitivity to cold and hot temperatures - Severe toothache - Tender, swollen gums - Tooth discoloration
