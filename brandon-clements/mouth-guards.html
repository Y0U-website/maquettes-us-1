  • TMJ/Bruxism Therapy & Splints If you experience jaw pain, frequent headaches, or a clicking sound when you chew, you may be suffering from a TMJ disorder—also known as TMD. The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) connects your jawbone to your skull and plays an essential role in talking, chewing, and yawning. When this joint becomes strained or misaligned, it can cause discomfort that affects your daily life. Learn More About TMJ
  • Snore Guard Appliances Snoring can affect your sleep, your health, and your partner’s rest. At St. Simons Island Dental Associates, we offer a simple, comfortable solution with custom-fit Snore Guard appliances. These small oral devices gently reposition your jaw to keep your airway open, helping reduce or eliminate snoring—without surgery, medication, or discomfort. Most patients notice improvement after the very first night. Learn More

TMJ symptoms:

You don’t have to live with ongoing jaw pain or tension. Our team will work with you to identify the cause of your discomfort and design a treatment plan that fits your needs.

  • Jaw pain or stiffness
  • Popping or clicking sounds when opening your mouth
  • Headaches or earaches
  • Facial soreness or tenderness
  • Difficulty opening or closing your mouth fully
