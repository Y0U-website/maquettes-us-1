Dr. Steen

Training Through Dedication and Service After earning his dental degree, Dr. Steen moved to New Orleans to complete an internship in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at Louisiana State University (LSU)/Charity Hospital. During this time, he remained at Charity Hospital through Hurricane Katrina, caring for stranded patients and becoming the last oral surgeon to leave the hospital—on an airboat.

Dr. Steen continued his six-year residency at LSU, where his comprehensive training included:

  • Undergraduate: Gonzaga University, Spokane, WA — B.S. in Biology
  • Dental School: University of Washington School of Dentistry — Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.)
  • Medical education at Louisiana State University School of Medicine, earning his M.D.
  • A general surgery/neurosurgery residency year
  • Completion of his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency

Surgical Expertise and Special Interests

Dr. Steen’s advanced training allows him to perform a wide range of procedures with precision and artistry.
