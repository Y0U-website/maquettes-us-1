Types of Dental Implants

Finding the Best Fit for Your Smile

Dental implants come in several types and styles, each designed to meet different needs—from replacing a single missing tooth to restoring a full set of teeth. Understanding the various options helps you make an informed decision and feel confident in your treatment plan. Whether you need one implant or a complete restoration, your oral surgeon will tailor the approach to your bone structure, smile goals, and overall health—ensuring a secure, natural-looking, and long-lasting result.
