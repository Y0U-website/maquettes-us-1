Dr. Steen is passionate about all aspects of oral and maxillofacial surgery. He places great value on compassionate patient care and strives to deliver exceptional clinical outcomes through precise and skillful surgical techniques.

Dr. Rubens, a distinguished Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon, retired in 2016 to focus on his family before returning to clinical practice in 2018. He has published and presented research in corrective jaw surgery, trauma, and reconstructive jaw surgery.

Our practice specializes in dental implants and oral maxillofacial procedures, combining advanced technology with expert care to restore both function and aesthetics. From replacing missing teeth with precision implants to addressing complex jaw and facial concerns, we are committed to providing personalized solutions that enhance your health, comfort, and confidence.

Every procedure is performed with skill, compassion, and attention to detail, ensuring results that last a lifetime.
