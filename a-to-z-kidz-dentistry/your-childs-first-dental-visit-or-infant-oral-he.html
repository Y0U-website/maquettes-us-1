For Patients

  • The Position: You will hold your child in your lap facing you, and then gently recline them backward so their head rests safely in the doctor’s lap.
  • The Care: While in this position, the doctor will perform a gentle cleaning, apply a protective fluoride treatment, and easily examine their mouth.

Tiny Teeth, Tiny Toothpaste

Because infants haven’t mastered the skill of spitting yet, we recommend using just a rice-grain amount of fluoride toothpaste. It’s the perfect, safe amount to protect their developing enamel!

What We Will Discuss

We are here to answer all your questions. During your visit, we’ll chat about:

  • Proper brushing and flossing techniques for tiny mouths.
  • Healthy bottle and sippy cup habits.
  • Comfort habits (like thumb-sucking or pacifiers).
  • Baby-friendly nutrition for strong teeth.

A Quick Note on Tears (It’s Completely Normal!)

If your little one cries during their first exam, please don’t worry! Crying is a completely normal, age-appropriate response to a new environment and new faces. It does not mean they are in pain.

By bringing your child in every six months, they will quickly get used to the routine. Before you know it, they’ll be happily hopping into the big dental chair all by themselves!

Your child’s visit, is not only about our team getting to know them, but also about you getting to know our team at A to Z Kidz Dentistry. We encourage you and your child to ask questions, express any concerns, and share with us anything you want us to know to help make your little one’s care as individual as they are.

Our Facilities

Keeping your child’s comfort in mind, services are completed in treatment rooms with TVs on the ceiling. Private treatment rooms are available for optimal patient experience. A parent can be present during their children’s visits, even restorative appointments, so they can offer support and comfort to their child. We love that they can see how gentle and caring we are taking care of their little ones.
