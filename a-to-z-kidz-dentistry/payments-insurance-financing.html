Financial Policy

In-House Financing is an option where we keep your credit card information stored securely and automatically process your payment on a designated date for up to 3 months. This allows you to pre-pay for the proposed dental treatment. This is only valid for treatment over $500.00. A 1.5 % finance charge will be added to any account over 60 days without prior arrangement. In the event your account is turned over to a collection agency for non-payment or other delinquency, you will be responsible for payment of any collection costs and / or attorney fees, in addition to the balance owed.
