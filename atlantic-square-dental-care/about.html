0 About Us Continuing a Legacy of Care Atlantic Square Dental was founded in 1991 by Drs Gorretti and Miranda Li. After decades of providing quality dental care to the local residents of Monterey Park, Dr Fan took over the practice when the sisters jointly decided to retire and has been continuing their tradition of delivering only the best dental treatment for all of our patients.

Our office specializes in general dentistry and is able to address any dental concern that you may have. We see patients of all ages and work with other nearby specialists to coordinate your treatment so that all your dental needs are taken care of properly.

Our staff works hard to ensure all treatment in this clinic is performed with utmost care and compassion.

Most of all, we listen to our patients and work with them to acheive realistic goals that suit their needs. Our staff is dedicated to helping our patients improve their oral health and overall quality of life.

We accept most PPO insurances and Denti-Cal (Medicaid for Dental).
