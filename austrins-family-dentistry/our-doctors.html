Dr. Emilija Austrins (b.1914 - d.1972), graduated from University of Latvia Dental School in Riga, Latvia in 1934. She was a dentist in Latvia until her family immigrated to the United States in the 1950’s. In order to practice dentistry in the USA, she attended and graduated the University of Pittsburg Dental School. In 1957, she established Austrins Family Dentistry. Emilija was a hard working and loving person. She had 5 children and welcomed everyone into her life. A lucky few of the Austrins Family Dentistry patients have been part of all three generations of dentists.
