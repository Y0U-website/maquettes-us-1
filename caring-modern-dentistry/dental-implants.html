What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have an impressive success rate of over 95 %. However, this depends on oral hygiene, overall health, and lifestyle choices. With proper care and regular check-ups, your implants can last a lifetime.

How long does the dental implant procedure take?

The entire process typically takes 3–6 months, including healing time. This timeline may vary based on individual circumstances, such as the need for bone grafting or additional procedures.

Are dental implant procedures painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure, thanks to local anesthesia and sedation options. Post-surgery, mild soreness can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team ensures your comfort throughout the process.

How should I maintain my implants?

Proper maintenance includes brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and attending biannual dental check-ups. Avoiding habits like smoking or teeth grinding also contributes to the longevity of your implants.

How much do dental implant procedures cost?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of implants, preparatory procedures, and individual needs. While the upfront cost may seem high, implants are a long-term investment that can save money on future dental work. Contact us for a detailed consultation and cost breakdown.
