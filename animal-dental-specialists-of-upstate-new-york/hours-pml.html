If you need veterinary care outside of these hours, please seek care from your regular veterinarian or an emergency clinic!

Animal Dental Specialists operates Monday through Thursday , between the hours of 7:00 am and 6:00 pm.

Dental Referral Consultation Appointments are made Monday through Thursday . A Dental Referral Consultation is an hour long appointment with you, your pet, and our doctor.

Dental Surgery Procedures are performed Monday through Thursday . Admission for your pet’s dental procedure is at 7:00 am , and pets will go home the same day.
