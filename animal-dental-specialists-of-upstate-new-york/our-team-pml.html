Our Doctors

Eric M. Davis, DVM, Dipl. AVDC

A Syracuse native, Dr. Davis graduated with Honors and Distinction from Cornell University in 1975, and received his DVM degree from the Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine in 1979. He then worked as an associate veterinarian at two different veterinary practices in the Syracuse area before establishing his own veterinary hospital, Lyndon Veterinary Clinic in 1985.

During his residency, Davis satisfied the credential requirements and passed the examination to become a Fellow of the Academy of Veterinary Dentistry in 2001. That same year, Dr. Davis joined the medical staff of the Cornell University Hospital for Animals, traveling to Ithaca twice a week in order to teach students and perform dental procedures on a wide variety of animal patients.

Dr. Davis has been married to his wife, Hannah, since 1991, and in addition to two lovely daughters, Briana and Lauren, and five grandchildren. Their family also includes one dog, two cats, one horse, one pot-bellied pig, two goats, and one reef tank aquarium. He used to be a long-distance bicyclist and brew his own beer. Now he drinks craft beer made by others, and cycles between the easy chair and the sofa. New hobbies include making wooden toys and furniture for his grandchildren and writing articles for peer-reviewed journals on a variety of oral health topics in animals and humans.

Emily Tetlow, DVM

Resident, Dentistry and Oral Surgery

Dr. Tetlow is an upstate New York native who completed her undergraduate studies at SUNY Binghamton and earned her DVM degree with honors from The Ohio State University. She has been practicing small animal veterinary medicine since 2020. Before joining ADS, she spent a year shadowing Dr. Davis, while at the same time, practicing dentistry at two different veterinary hospitals and pursuing continued dental education across the country. Dr. Tetlow joined our team in June 2024 as a resident of the American Veterinary Dental College to pursue becoming a Board Certified Veterinary Dentist .

Dr. Tetlow has had a passion for dentistry since veterinary school, recognizing the huge impact it has on a pet’s quality of life. She aspires to follow in Dr. Davis’ footsteps by providing a high level of dental care to patients while educating owners on the importance of it. As part of her residency training, Dr. Tetlow participates in regular didactic learning with Cornell University and has trained with specialists in additional veterinary fields, including radiology, surgery, and anesthesia.

Dr. Tetlow has two cats of her own, Delilah and Goliath, and is a cat person at heart. She shares her home with her husband and their puppy, Fitzpatrick (Fitz), named after a player on her favorite team, the Buffalo Bills. Outside of the office, you can find her cheering them on every football Sunday, practicing ballroom dancing, or thrift shopping. She looks forward to helping your furry family members!

Our Consultants

Stephanie Hon, DVM, DACVAA

Board Certified Veterinary Anesthesiologist Consultant (Remote)

For certain patients and procedures, we partner with Anesthesia Black , a team of highly trained anesthesia professionals who provide expert support when additional layers of care are needed.

Through remote teleconsultation, their team works closely with our doctors and technicians to support anesthesia planning and monitoring when indicated. This collaboration allows us to extend specialized expertise to our patients while keeping care close to home.

We are proud to have Anesthesia Black available as a resource, adding confidence and peace of mind whenever a case calls for their involvement.

  • Board-certified veterinary anesthesiologists (ACVAA Diplomates) bring advanced training in anesthesia and pain management, offering consultation, case oversight, and guidance for complex situations.
  • Veterinary Technician Specialists in Anesthesia & Analgesia (VTS-A&A) are specialty-credentialed technicians with proven expertise in the care and monitoring of anesthesia cases.
  • Anesthesia Black’s support staff help ensure smooth logistics, efficient processes, and an added focus on patient safety.
