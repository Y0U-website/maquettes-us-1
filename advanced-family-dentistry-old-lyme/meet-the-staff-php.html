Hygienist

Kathi graduated in 1997 with an Associate’s Degree in Hygiene from the University of New Haven. She continued her education while working full-time as a hygienist and completed her Bachelor’s degree in Science. Kathi was the recipient of the "Hu-Friedy Clinical Excellence" award and graduated Magna Cum Laude. She is great with patients and is always striving to learn more about the field.

Kathi is a Connecticut native and she and her husband have two grown boys. She enjoys spending time at their cottage and hanging out on the boat. During Kathi’s vacation you will always find her at a warm destination with white sandy beaches.

Hygienist

Laura graduated in 2005 from the University of Connecticut with a Marketing and Psychology degree. She then earned her Degree in Hygiene from the University of New Haven in 2011. Following graduation she completed an internship where she volunteered at a Romanian orphanage teaching children about oral health and hygiene. Laura has a very gentle touch and always has a good story to tell.

Outside of work she enjoys spending time with friends, working out, hiking and she is always up for experiencing new things.
