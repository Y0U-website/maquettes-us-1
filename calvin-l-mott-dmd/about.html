Dr. Calvin L. Mott

DMD, FAGD

Everyone deserves a healthy smile, and I am excited to have to opportunity to provide the highest quality of dental care to the Rolla community. Each day provides a unique challenge, and it is my goal to meet the needs of my patients with the same care, respect, and concern that I would expect to receive.

I attended the University of Arkansas for my undergraduate education, graduating with a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry. I continued on to dental school, earning my Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Louisville. Keeping abreast of the newest, most effective treatments in the dental industry is vital to providing my patients with the quality of care they deserve. I continue my education regularly, focusing on areas such as practice management, lasers, and cosmetic treatments.
