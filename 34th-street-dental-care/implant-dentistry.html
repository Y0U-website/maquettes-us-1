Implant Dentistry in Berwyn, IL

Implant dentistry is a type of restorative dentistry that replaces missing teeth with artificial tooth roots or implants. The implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they then become fused to the bone. Once in place, the implants provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

An implant dentist is a specialist who places dental implants. Some general dentists have placed implants, but they were not trained to place them. Thus, these dentists cannot place implants independently.

Implant dentists have completed either an additional two years of training beyond dental school or an additional one year of training beyond dental school and earned a certificate in implant dentistry. This training allows implant dentists to not only place implants but also diagnose and make a treatment plan for their patients.

Benefits of visiting an implant dentist

Those who are missing teeth often visit an implant dentist for treatment. An implant dentist is a restorative dental specialist who offers several treatments to replace missing teeth. Dental implants are a popular tooth replacement option because they are the next best thing to your natural teeth. They look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

Dental implants – What are they?

Dental implants are titanium screws that serve as tooth roots. The screws are surgically placed within the jawbone. The titanium screws fuse with the jawbone and serve as stable roots for crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Dental implants are a great solution for restoring one tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire row of teeth. They are considered permanent restorations because they become fused with the jawbone.

Benefits of dental implants

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that restore complete dental function. Dental implants are secured in the jawbone like natural teeth, and they allow patients to eat, speak, and chew normally. Dental implants also prevent bone recession and gum recession, preserving patients’ facial appearance.

How can implant dentistry help?

Implant dentistry can help people overcome tooth loss. In fact, implants are the most successful method of tooth replacement, according to the American College of Prosthodontists. The success rate for implants is over 95 %, which is significantly higher than other options like dentures.

Factors to consider when determining if implants are right for you

Although implants are some of the most reliable and long-lasting tooth replacement options, they aren’t right for everyone. Factors such as your general health and the health of your jawbones can all affect whether or not implants are right for you.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that provide the stimulation that your jawbone needs to remain healthy. If you don’t have enough jawbone tissue to support implants, you can undergo a procedure known as a bone graft. A bone graft involves adding extra bone tissue to your jawbone, which will allow it to support implants.
