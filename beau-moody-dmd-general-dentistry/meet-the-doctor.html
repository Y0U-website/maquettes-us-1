Get to know Dr. Jake Scheiter

Dr. Jake Scheiter, a proud native of Maryville, Illinois, is a dedicated dentist focused on crafting confident smiles for his patients. After completing his undergraduate studies in Biological Sciences with excellence at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, Dr. Scheiter followed his passion for dentistry and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine.

Outside the dental chair, Dr. Scheiter finds joy in immersing himself in the local sports culture, frequently attending St. Louis sports games to root for his favorite teams. Additionally, he finds joy in exploring new places, immersing himself in diverse cultures, and creating lasting memories through his travels. Dr. Scheiter’s passion for dentistry, paired with his engaging personality, makes him a trusted and reliable dentist that his patients can depend on for compassionate care and outstanding outcomes.
