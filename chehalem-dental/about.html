About

0 Meet Dr. Ogle I grew up in western Washington and moved around a lot while growing up. I moved to Newberg in 2001 to go to George Fox University where I earned a degree in computer science. I worked as a software developer for almost 10 years before deciding to head back to school and change directions.

After graduating from dental school at Oregon Health & Science University, I worked in several different clinics around the more rural parts of Oregon serving low income patients.

I am married to my beautiful wife Nicole, my college girlfriend from GFU. We were married in 2006 and have 3 great kids, who keep us very busy. We have a daughter (Reese, 10), and two boys (Wyatt, 5 and Bennett, 3). In my “free time”, I enjoy playing the guitar, hiking, watching movies, and drinking tea.

Toothbrush: I’ve used the Oral B Genius electric toothbrush since I was in dental school and I really like it.

Toothpaste: I really just use whatever I can get my hands on. People always ask if I have a preference between Crest and Colgate and I always tell them it’s like the difference between Coke and Pepsi, just personal preference. Side note: I do not recommend drinking Coke, Pepsi, or any other soda.

Meet Dr. Strom

Dr. Kirstin Strom, originally from Wisconsin, moved out to Oregon in 2016 to pursue a career in dentistry. She received her dental degree from Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry. During her studies she found a love for the pacific northwest outdoors and decided to make it her home. While not in the office, Dr. Strom enjoys hiking, camping, and traveling. She’s always looking for her next adventure. When not outdoors, she enjoys spending her time with her husband Ronald, and dog Lola.

Lonna

I have worked in the dental field for over 20 years and I love the sense of purpose and tight community dentistry represents. I also enjoy helping others and contributing to their smiles with compassion and understanding.

Toothbrush: I began using an electric toothbrush after my hygienist recommended my doing so several years ago. Although Oral B and Sonicare are both excellent brands, I prefer the Oral B.

Toothpaste: I switch between Crest brands and Tom’s fluoridated toothpaste. My flossing habits are at least twice per day and I add the Waterpik into the mix at least once a week.

Dominique

I have been a dental hygienist since 2007. I love being a dental hygienist because I get to help make you healthier and work with you to prevent cavities and gum disease. I enjoy meeting new people and learning your story. I love my job!

I have worked at several great dental offices over the years but this is the best team I have ever had the privilege of working with. I work just a couple of days a week and the rest of the time I am home with my two young daughters. I love to cook, play board games, and visit all of the amazing parks and attractions the Pacific Northwest has to offer.

Toothpaste: I am not too picky as long as it has fluoride in it!

Vanessa

I became a dental hygienist in 2004, inspired by my desire to promote healthy, happy smiles. I always try to bring a gentle touch and warm smile to my work.

I switched to an electric toothbrush during dental hygiene school, and have been using a Sonicare or Oral B brush ever since. The clean I feel after using an electric brush does not compare to what I feel after using a manual toothbrush.

Away from the office I stay busy with my husband, and two boys, and their dog, a puggle named Ramsey. As a family we enjoy exploring the beautiful Pacific Northwest. We like to spend quality time with family and friends, and check out new restaurants and breweries.

Toothbrush: Oral B or Sonicare (an electric)

Toothpaste: I use Sensodyne and Tom’s of Maine Rapid Relief to manage sensitivity and minimize acid wear.
