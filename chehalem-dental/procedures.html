Fillings

When your tooth has a small to medium size cavity, we can repair it with a filling.

Crowns & Bridges

When you have a large cavity or fractured tooth, a crown is typically required to restore the tooth.

Root Canals

If your pulp becomes infected through a deep cavity or exposed through a fracture, you will need a root canal.

Kids

Every staff member in our office is a parent and we treat your children as gently and compassionately as if they were our own kids.

Implants

A dental implant is the best solution to replace missing teeth.

Pulling a Tooth

Sometimes teeth need to be removed. There are a variety of reasons for this including advanced decay or fractures.

Dental Exams

This is where we identify issues and develop multiple solutions to restore you to optimum oral health.

Teeth Cleaning

This is the most important procedure that we perform. The importance of having your teeth professionally cleaned and regularly checked by a dentist cannot be overstated.

Mouth Guards

At Chehalem Dental we make two different appliances for your mouth.

Teeth Whitening

We can make you “bleach trays” that you take home. We also offer in office whitening.
