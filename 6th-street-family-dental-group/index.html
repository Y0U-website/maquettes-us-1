The practice

Your West LA Dentist will look after your dental care to craft a plan to put you on track for lasting and high quality overall dental health.

We are dedicated to providing the highest level of dental medicine along with friendly, compassionate service.

When you are new to our office, we put in the effort to provide you with a positive and comforting experience.

During each visit, you will be treated by trained professionals who will provide top quality care to prevent dental problems and treat current conditions.

West LA Dental is proud to feature some of the most advanced dental imaging technology available today, including 16-bit CT scan X-ray sensors, high-resolution 3D imaging, and comprehensive cephalometric analysis of the skull. These cutting-edge tools allow us to capture incredibly detailed images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures—right here in our office.

Our digital panoramic and cephalometric imaging systems give us unparalleled diagnostic clarity, enabling precise treatment planning and a higher standard of care. Whether evaluating orthodontic needs, TMJ conditions, or surgical planning, our advanced 3D technology ensures you receive care that’s accurate, efficient, and tailored to you.

This superior level of imaging is not commonly found in many dental practices, and we’re excited to offer it to our patients. Call West LA Dental today to schedule your appointment and experience the difference.
