Dr. Caitlin Kompanowski

DDS

Dr. Caitlin has proudly served as a dentist at Advanced Solutions Family Dental since 2020. Known for her friendly and compassionate approach, she is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable and confident while achieving healthy, beautiful smiles.

An active member of both the Dental Arts Club and the Chicago Dental Society, Dr. Caitlin is committed to continuing education and staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry. Her passion for the profession began at a young age, inspired by her father, who is also a dentist.

Whether providing routine hygiene care, restorative treatments, or cosmetic enhancements, Dr. Caitlin works closely with each patient to help them achieve their smile goals. She is especially excited to offer Invisalign® clear aligner therapy and has been practicing clear aligner treatment since 2020, helping patients straighten their smiles with comfort and confidence.

Outside the office, Dr. Caitlin enjoys spending time with friends and family — and of course her perfect pooch, Nugget! She loves traveling, crafting, and baking, and enjoys sharing recipes with patients. She’s also always happy to talk all things Disney.
