We’re proud to have the best team in town!

At Alpine Periodontics, Dr. Shin and his team of qualified dental professionals take great pride in providing comfort, quality, and optimum care to all patients. We pride ourselves on using the most state-of-the-art equipment, such as digital x-ray imaging systems, dual implant machinery, computer-aided diagnosis, and laser operation machines.

Such state-of-the-art technology has enabled us to achieve the best post-operative results and highest surgical implant quality. We strive for total satisfaction from our patients. Our services are affordable and we offer payment plans and care credit to our patients who may need them.
