The practice

Dr. Nicholas was born and raised on the Oregon coast in a small town near Tillamook. While growing up and watching his dad change so many peoples lives in the dental chair, he knew from an early age that he wanted to do the same.

Dr. Nicholas attended Oregon State University for his undergrad and Oregon Health & Science University to obtain his doctorates.

Our team has the skills and training to give you more treatment options all in one spot. This means less referrals and more convenience for you and your family.

Dental implants, dentures, extractions, and fillings are just a few examples of the services we offer. We also have IV sedation, root canal therapy, and pinhole gum grafting.

Please see our services page for a full overview of general, cosmetic, and restorative solutions.

What’s the one thing that’s always associated with healthcare? Hidden pricing. We find that very frustrating and are changing all of that at Above Dental.

Dentistry can be expensive; that’s why we offer multiple financing options and have a membership plan. For your convenience, we accept and bill most insurances, with one notable exception, OHP (Oregon Health Plan). OHP will not provide coverage because we aren’t a contracted OHP provider.

Given this information, please don’t let your insurance status dictate your oral health. We welcome anyone to our North Bend dental practice and promise to help you the best we can.
