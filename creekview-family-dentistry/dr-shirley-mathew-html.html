Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

From the time she was a young child, Dr. Mathew loved drawing, painting, and doing other sorts of crafts directly with her hands. Additionally, she was fascinated by biology and human physiology as she grew up but wanted to make sure that her chosen career would leave enough time for family. Dentistry turned out to be the perfect way to apply science in a practical way to people’s lives while still maintaining normal hours for a healthy work/life balance.
