The practice

Dr. David Herald strives to provide the highest level of patient care through a lifelong commitment to learning and investing in modern, comfortable, dental techniques. We are committed to improving the lives of those we serve by providing high-quality dentistry in a patient-centered, fun environment, with a gentle touch.

Our purpose is to involve our patients in their treatment choices through exceptional listening and communication. We strive to utilize the best resources, stay abreast of current tools and techniques through a commitment to lifelong learning, and provide the information necessary to tailor treatment for each individual so that together we can arrive at the best, most comfortable care.

Our goal is to build lasting relationships with our patients, their families, and our dental team. This will be done as we continually and consistently act with full honesty and integrity. We appreciate that our patients have a choice in providers; we treat our patients as we would expect to be treated.

Please feel free to contact our Cottonwood, AZ office to schedule an appointment with Dr. Herald. Our office is a Delta Dental Premiere provider and will work with all dental insurance companies. We gladly accept new patients and try to find the most convenient times to fit our patient’s busy schedules.
