Restore Your Smile with Dental Implants in Norwell, MA

Dental implants in Norwell, MA, are a sturdy and long-lasting solution to replace missing teeth. They are designed to function and appear similar to natural teeth. Caring for them involves regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits for preventative care and they may last many years with proper care and maintenance! Individual treatment outcomes and implant success may vary based on factors including bone density, oral health, medical history, and patient compliance with care instructions. Not all patients are candidates for dental implants. Implant longevity depends on proper oral hygiene, regular dental visits, and avoiding habits that may damage the restoration. A comprehensive examination is required to determine if you are a suitable candidate for dental implant treatment and to discuss realistic expectations for your specific situation.
