  • All-on-6 Dental Implants: The All-on-6 approach is similar but uses six implants for added stability and even distribution of biting forces. The additional implants can be beneficial for patients with more bone or those wanting extra support for their prosthetic teeth.

Benefits of All-on-4 and All-on-6 Treatment

  • Potentially Faster Results: These procedures may be completed in a single day in appropriate cases, though individual treatment timelines vary based on healing and specific patient needs.
  • Stable and Secure: Using fewer implants than traditional implant-supported dentures may reduce the need for bone grafts while providing a stable base for the prosthetics when properly integrated.
  • Natural Look: Custom-made prosthetic teeth are designed to complement your natural teeth.
  • Improved Functionality: You may be able to eat, speak, and smile more comfortably, as these implants are designed to function similarly to natural teeth.
  • Long-Lasting: With proper care, and regular maintenance, All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants may last many years, offering a durable solution for tooth replacement though individual results vary.
