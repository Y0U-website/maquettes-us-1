The practice

A comprehensive range of pediatric dentistry treatments is available, including preventive dentistry, to keep your child’s teeth healthy.

Sedation dentistry is available for anxious or very young children so they can relax and remain calm during treatment.

Strengthening and restoring your child’s natural teeth to protect against further damage and decay.

Fissure sealants are a useful technique to prevent cavities. They help to reduce the risk of tooth decay in children.

Tooth-colored fillings are natural looking and blend in seamlessly with your child’s other teeth, so your child can keep smiling brightly.

We use stainless steel crowns to restore and protect damaged teeth. They are both strong and long-lasting.

Regular dentist visits and professional dental cleanings are an important part of any child’s dental routine.

Dental bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to restore, repair, and improve a damaged smile.

Les soins

A range of services to treat malocclusion, mouth and jaw problems, including interceptive care, for early intervention and prevention.

Invisalign clear aligners are a more comfortable and aesthetically pleasing alternative to traditional braces.
