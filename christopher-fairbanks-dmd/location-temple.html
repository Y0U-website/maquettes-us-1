Dentist in Temple, TX

Fairbanks Dental Associates

Our Temple dental office is dedicated to providing quality dentistry in a relaxing environment for the temple community and surrounding communities. At our local dental practice, we serve patients with personalized care and featured dental services ranging from preventive care and routine cleanings to cosmetic dentistry, restorative dentistry, and emergency dental care.

Whether you need a dental checkup, help with gum disease, a tooth extraction, root canals, or dental implants for missing teeth, our friendly team delivers comprehensive care using advanced technology and state of the art techniques. For those with dental anxiety, we provide sedation options like nitrous oxide to ensure a stress free dental experience.

With same day appointment availability, flexible payment plans, and financing options, we welcome new valued patients seeking a trusted dental home for healthy smiles and a beautiful smile for the whole family.

Fairbanks Dental Associates is conveniently located in Temple, TX and proudly provides dental services to patients in Bell County , Harker Heights , Belton , Little River-Academy , Salado , and Troy , TX.
