About Belton, TX

Nestled in the heart of Bell County, Belton serves as the county seat and offers a perfect blend of historic charm and modern amenities. The city’s rich heritage, combined with its strategic location along I-35 between Austin and Waco, makes it an ideal place for families and professionals alike. The University of Mary Hardin-Baylor adds a vibrant educational atmosphere to this growing community.

  • Belton Lake - A 12,300-acre reservoir offering exceptional recreational opportunities including fishing, boating, and waterfront parks
  • Bell County Museum - Historic museum showcasing the region’s rich heritage through interactive exhibits and preserved artifacts from Central Texas
  • Miller Springs Nature Center - A 260-acre natural area below Belton Dam offering hiking trails, wildlife viewing, and scenic overlooks of limestone bluffs and diverse ecosystems
