Getting To Know You

Your first visit at Archer Dental begins with a brief meeting. We want to get to know you in a relaxed, face to face atmosphere, so we can better understand your personal needs and desires. Our goal is to improve not only your dental health, but your whole body health and overall well being.

Your First Exam

We will begin with a thorough evaluation of your oral health. Using the mot up-to-date digital technology, we will complete a comprehensive oral exam to check your teeth, gums, jaw joint and function as well as perform an oral cancer screening.

Before Any Treatment Begins

we put together a complete plan to reach your goals and make sure all of your questions are answered, so that you are able to make a confident, informed decision regarding your personal care. We offer a variety of payment options to fit within your budget and will work to maximize your dental benefits.
