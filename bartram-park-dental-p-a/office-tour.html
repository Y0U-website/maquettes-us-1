About Bartram Park Dental

Dr.George A. Varghese has been practicing dentistry for over 30 years. He received his Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) from the prestigious New York University College Of Dentistry(NYU). He is a graduate of the mini-residency Implant program at the University of Buffalo, NY. Due to his academic excellence, he was selected for a one year mini residency in TMD and orofacial pain disorders which he completed with honors.
