BridgeMill Dentistry-Canton, GA: All-on-Four Dental Implants

Once healed, the implants will be able to support an entire arch of teeth! Just four implant screws on the bottom and four on top for a brand-new smile! Our dentists will then place your permanent crowns specially crafted for your mouth to look exactly like natural teeth. Your dental implants will last a lifetime with proper care, and the crowns should only need replacement every ten to fifteen years.

Benefit of All-on-Four Dental Implants

The best benefit of all-on-four dental implants is that the procedure allows our BridgeMill Dentistry team to give you a complete mouth restoration with the placement of only a few dental appliances. Best of all, your implants look and function just like natural teeth. You care for them through simple brushing and flossing. All-on-four dental implants will also allow you to eat the foods you love with comfort and ease and may even cause drastic improvements in speech. The implants also protect the jawbone from further decay.

In addition to health benefits, all-on-four dental implants will restore your confidence, preserve the youthful appearance of your lower face, and eliminate the need for costly procedures down the road. At BridgeMill Dentistry, we understand that undergoing a dental implant procedure is an important decision. Our team of compassionate professionals will work with you every step of the way to ensure your smile goals are met while you remain cared for. From the time of your first consultation to the day you leave with your new smile, we’ll strive for every step to be as comfortable as possible.
