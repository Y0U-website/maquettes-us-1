Dr. Heidi Pantazis earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine at the Dental College of Georgia (formerly known as the Medical College of Georgia). Growing up in Buffalo, New York, she completed her undergraduate degree in Philosophy and Biology at the University of Buffalo and after moving to Georgia, completed her master’s degree in molecular Cellular Biology and Physiology at Georgia State University.

During her undergraduate studies, Dr. Heidi was actively involved in programs providing access to dental care for underserved communities, and in her professional career continues to remain involved in opportunities to bridge the gap in dental health disparities, especially to those who are unable to easily access essential services.

Dr. Heidi strives to provide exceptional care to her patients and enjoys lifelong learning, keeping current with continuing education. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association and has completed additional training with the American Academy of Facial Esthetics.

Dr. Heidi lives in Marietta and enjoys spending time with her husband and two children, family, and friends.
