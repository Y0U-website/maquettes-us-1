What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first visit to our office is designed to get you better acquainted with all we offer as well as introduce you to our caring staff. On your first visit, we will review your medical and dental histories and evaluate your oral health. If necessary, we will take digital x-rays of your teeth and explain to you whether or not you will require any dental treatment.
