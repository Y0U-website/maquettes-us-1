Our Services

Teeth Whitening

It is possible to have a brighter smile fast with our effective and unique teeth whitening treatments. Our doctors use patented technology that can brighten your entire tooth by up six shades brighter. We customize each patient’s care based on their sensitivity so we’re sure they get what works best for them.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is the art of keeping your smile naturally beautiful through treatments that erase or correct esthetic problems. If you have teeth issues like stained, chipped, misaligned, oddly spaced or malformed teeth, we can design a cosmetic treatment specifically to address them.

Same-day Crowns and Bridges

With CEREC technology, you can get a beautiful crown or bridge in just one appointment. O ur dentists do everything in-house instead of using an outside lab and take digital impressions on your mouth while designing a custom crown or bridge using specialized computer software. You’ll be able to chew on your new tooth crown comfortably and securely after just one visit!

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a popular and effective long-term solution for people who suffer from missing teeth, failing ones, or chronic dental problems. With our state-of-the-art technology, Careswell and Anderson Dental can rebuild your smile with a nearly perfect replica of natural teeth with the same look, feel, and functions.

Root Canals

Root canals may be needed for patients who experience pain and sensitivity in their teeth. This is one of the most common restorative treatments, saving an infected tooth from needing to undergo a full surgical procedure such as root canal therapy or extraction.

Extractions

Our dentists will use every measure possible to prevent this, however, there are certain occasions when a tooth needs to be extracted. If you are experiencing severe tooth decay, infection, orthodontic correction, or fractured teeth or roots, we may advise you to have a tooth extracted.ents.

Implant Crowns

Dental implants are a great option for replacing missing teeth, as they require little maintenance and look similar to natural tooth structures. The technology involved in dental implants has advanced over the last ten or so years. The risk of failure from rejection or functionality issues is extremely low.

Pediatric Dentistry

We’re here to take care of your child’s oral health needs. We offer a wide range of child and adolescent oral care, tooth brushing tips, cavity prevention, emergency care, and more!

SureSmile Clear Aligners

SureSmile is here to help you straighten your teeth. The process begins with digital impressions sent directly from our lab, which produces a custom-made set of aligners for each patient. No metal or wires are required!
