Holli Careswell, D.D.S.

Owner/Dentist

In addition to running her practice, Dr. Careswell is also on the Forbes Health Advisory Board, a group of board-certified clinicians who review articles, advise on content and help people make simpler, smarter and less stressful decisions regarding their health. And she is a busy wife and mom of two boys (and two dogs)—spending her off time watching sporting events, boating, working out, skiing and honing her photography skills.

You can see Dr. Careswell’s before and after gallery on her Instagram page (@dr.holli).

Jacob Anderson, D.D.S.

Owner/Dentist

What does Dr. Anderson’s job entail? Anything to do with teeth and smiles, from performing routine exams to addressing dental emergencies. A native Kansan, Dr. Anderson grew up right here in Lee’s Summit, and graduated from Lee’s Summit West in 2006.

He went on to the University of Kansas (go, Jayhawks!), where he graduated with highest distinction with a Human Biology degree, then continued his education at the UMKC School of Dentistry with a scholarship from the United States Army. He graduated cum laude in 2014 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Dr. Anderson received advanced education in general dentistry with a residency at Fort Sill, Oklahoma, where he acquired new skills and gained valuable experience in root canals, crowns, oral surgery and esthetic dentistry. He completed his military commitment there, and considers it an honor to have served soldiers and helped with their complex dental needs.

Dr. Anderson has been married to his wife (our very own Dr. Teegarden) since 2017. They have three young sons—and two equally energetic dogs—who they enjoy spending time with. He also loves being outdoors, playing golf, watching the Jayhawks, and cooking. He’s excited about being back home and helping the community he grew up him.

Dr. Tiffany Teegarden

Associate Dentist

As an associate dentist in our office, Dr. Teegarden practices dentistry for the whole family—from pediatric fillings to complete dentures. (And all the extractions, crowns, bridges and more in between.) She particularly loves seeing children—watching them grow up and keeping their mouths healthy in the process!

Dr. Teegarden has a passion for serving others, and is proud to use her scientific knowledge and artistic eye and abilities to help her patients achieve a smile that not only looks, but functions and feels, great. When she’s not seeing her own patients—or filling in for other dentists, hygienists, receptionists and even the IT and fix-it guys), you’ll find her busy at home, quilting, crafting, and taking care of her three boys (who—fun fact—she shares with her husband, our very own Dr. Anderson).

Denise
