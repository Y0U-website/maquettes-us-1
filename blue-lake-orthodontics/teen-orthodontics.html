Teen orthodontic care, personally guided by Dr. Z

Orthodontic care that fits a teenager’s life

Metal braces

Metal braces remain the workhorse of teen orthodontics for a reason: they handle severe crowding, spacing, and complex bite issues with precise, predictable tooth movement. Blue Lake Orthodontics uses high-grade stainless steel brackets with advanced, light-force wires for a gentler experience than the braces parents remember. Teens can personalize their look with colored bands and change the colors at each visit.

Clear braces

Clear braces deliver the same reliable results as metal braces with transparent, low-profile brackets that blend into the smile. They’re a popular middle ground for teens who need the control of braces and want a subtler look in photos and on video calls.

Clear aligners

Clear aligners straighten teeth with a series of custom-made, removable transparent trays — no wires, no brackets, and no food restrictions. Aligners suit responsible teens who will wear their trays as directed, and they make brushing, flossing, and school lunches simpler. Dr. Z has advanced training in clear aligner therapy and will confirm whether your teen is a candidate during the consultation.

When should teens start orthodontic treatment?

Most teens start comprehensive orthodontic treatment between the ages of 11 and 14, once most of their permanent teeth have come in.

Starting during these growth years lets Dr. Z guide jaw development and tooth movement at the same time, which can shorten treatment and reduce the chance of more involved correction later. If your child is younger, Blue Lake Orthodontics also offers Phase 1 early treatment ; if your teen is older, treatment is still very effective.

Treatment length varies with each case. Many teens complete treatment in roughly one to two years, and Dr. Z will give you a specific estimate for your teen after her exam, 3D digital x-rays, and scan.

Braces vs. clear aligners for teens: how to choose

With Blue Lake’s 3D scanning and Digital Smile Design, your teen can preview a simulation of their expected result before committing to a plan, which tends to be the moment reluctant teens get on board.

  • Complexity: Braces give Dr. Z precise control for severe crowding and complex bite problems. Aligners work well for many cases, and candidacy is confirmed at the consult.
  • Responsibility: Braces work around the clock with no effort from your teen. Aligners only work when they’re worn, so they fit teens who will keep trays in as directed.
  • Lifestyle: Aligners come out for eating, brushing, and photos. Braces stay put through sports seasons, band practice, and busy weeks — no lost trays.
  • Look: Clear aligners are nearly invisible, clear braces are subtle, and metal braces with colored bands let teens make a statement.
