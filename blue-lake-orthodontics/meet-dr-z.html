Meet Dr. Zehra Syed

Board-Certified Birmingham Orthodontist

Zehra Syed, MBA, DMD, MSD is a board-certified orthodontist and the founder of Blue Lake Orthodontics. A Birmingham native, she returned home in 2021 to open the boutique, patient-first practice she always envisioned.

Get to Know Dr. Z

Hey everyone! I’m Dr. Zehra Syed (most patients call me Dr. Z!), and I’m so excited to welcome you to Blue Lake Orthodontics. I am genuinely passionate about helping my patients achieve the beautiful, confident smiles they desire all while providing a comfortable and easy orthodontic experience. Whether it’s in day-to-day life or in providing orthodontic care, my greatest happiness comes from the ability to help those around me laugh more, smile often, express joy, and exude confidence.

As a Birmingham, AL native, I grew up in the Oak Mountain community, where I was recruited to Mississippi State University to play soccer for the MSU Bulldogs (Hail State!). There, I pursued Bachelor’s degrees in Biology and Business and later attained an MBA before returning to Birmingham to embark on my dental education.

While at UAB School of Dentistry, I discovered the life-changing impact orthodontic treatment could have for patients in all walks of life. I loved the genuine connections and strong doctor-patient relationships that could be fostered throughout the course of treatment.

This profound interest in orthodontic care led me to the Medical University of South Carolina, where I completed a 3-year residency and served as Chief Resident. With a strong education in advanced surgical orthodontics , clear aligner therapy , growth modification treatments, and traditional braces, I completed my certification process to become a Board Certified Orthodontist.

Following my education, I returned home to Birmingham, AL, to live out my dream of being an orthodontist. I can’t wait to welcome you to our Blue Lake Orthodontics Family!

One orthodontist, from start to finish

Many of the families who find us have already experienced the frustration of seeing three or four different orthodontists at a larger practice. They often tell me how much that inconsistency affected their trust and made treatment feel less personal.

I understand why continuity matters. At Blue Lake Orthodontics, you and your child see me, a board-certified orthodontist, from the first consultation through the final retainer check. There is no rotating cast of providers and no need to explain your case to someone new at every visit. Just one orthodontist who knows your family, understands your treatment plan, and stays with you every step of the way.
