FAQs about same-day orthodontics

Are same-day braces different from traditional braces?

Same-day braces are the same as traditional braces. The difference is the expedited process where the braces are applied on the day of your consultation, thereby starting your treatment journey without delay.

K L How quickly will I receive my same-day aligners?

After the initial scan on your consultation day, the aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth perfectly. While they won’t be ready to take home the same day, this process significantly shortens the waiting period, and you’ll receive your aligners as soon as they are available.

K L Is everyone eligible for same-day orthodontics?

Candidates for our same-day orthodontic treatment must have good oral health, a recent dental cleaning, and no untreated cavities or gum disease. Many patients seeking orthodontic treatment can benefit from our same-day services. However, eligibility may depend on your specific orthodontic issues. A comprehensive evaluation during your consultation will help determine if same-day orthodontics is the best option for you.

K L How do I care for my teeth after receiving same-day braces or aligners?

The care for same-day braces or aligners is the same as braces and aligner treatments that are not started on the same day. It includes maintaining good oral hygiene through regular brushing and flossing, attending follow-up appointments, and adhering to dietary recommendations to ensure the success of your treatment.
