Am I a candidate for surgical orthodontics?

Surgical orthodontics is ideal for adults with skeletal discrepancies that affect their oral function and aesthetic appearance. Here’s a quick guide:

  • Structural concerns: Ideal for addressing jaw misalignment and severe bite problems not solvable by braces alone.
  • Readiness for surgery: Candidates should have completed jaw growth, typically by age 16 for girls and 18 for boys.
  • Commitment to comprehensive treatment: This journey involves pre-surgical orthodontic treatment, the surgical procedure, and post-surgical orthodontics for fine-tuning.

The importance of regular dental visits for orthodontic success

At Blue Lake Orthodontics, good oral health is a must—candidates for braces, clear aligners, or any orthodontic procedure must not have untreated cavities or gum disease. Patients must visit their dentist regularly to ensure optimal oral health and the best results for their orthodontic journey.

FAQs about surgical orthodontics

How do I prepare for orthodontic surgery?

Recovery times vary, but most patients can expect to return to normal activities within 2-3 weeks, with a full recovery taking several months. Swelling, discomfort, and a modified diet are common post-surgery. Follow-up appointments are crucial for monitoring healing and progress.

K L How long will I need to wear braces after surgery?

Most patients will need to wear braces before and after surgery. The post-surgery orthodontic treatment may last for 6 to 12 months, depending on the individual’s case, to fine-tune the bite and ensure optimal alignment.

K L Can orthodontic surgery improve my appearance?

Yes, in addition to improving oral function, orthodontic surgery can significantly enhance facial aesthetics by bringing the jaw into proper alignment, resulting in a more balanced and harmonious facial appearance.
