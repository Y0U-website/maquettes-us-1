Bringing artistry and warmth to every smile

Get to know Dr. Lauren Patrick

Dr. Lauren Patrick brings nearly a decade of experience, compassion, and deep clinical expertise to Blanc Dental Aesthetics. A native of Chicago’s south side and a long-time Iowa resident, Dr. Patrick is a proud wife, mother of two, and a passionate connector who genuinely loves getting to know each of her patients. She creates an elevated dental experience grounded in trust, education, and beautiful results.

Dr. Patrick earned her BA in Biochemistry, Cell and Molecular Biology from Drake University in 2012 and went on to earn her DDS from Creighton School of Dentistry in 2016. She is a member of the American Dental Association and a Qualified Dentist with the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. She is also certified in Invisalign and botulinum toxin, allowing her to combine form and function to help patients achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Much of Dr. Patrick’s vision for Blanc Dental Aesthetics is focused on the details: the warm greetings, the personal attention, the celebratory moment when treatment ends, because she knows that those small moments of connection can transform a patient’s entire outlook on dentistry. Whether someone is seeking maintenance, relief, or a cosmetic smile transformation, she believes in clear communication, goal-oriented care, and creating a plan that patients feel excited about.

Her favorite procedures: like clear aligners, oral sleep appliances, Botox, and smile rehabilitations, allow her to improve patients&#x27; health and confidence in one seamless experience. She also values her ability to help nervous or hesitant patients feel comfortable, empowered, and excited about their oral health journey.

Beyond the office, Dr. Patrick finds joy in family, food, and outdoor adventures. She&#x27;s happiest when hosting a dinner for loved ones, cooking together with her little girls, or biking Iowa’s beautiful trails.

Compassionate care from a team that feels like family

Our team is your dental dream team

From the moment you step into our office, you’ll be welcomed with genuine smiles, warmth, and intention. Our team is carefully chosen to provide a boutique dental experience that reflects kindness, enjoys connection, and delivers high-quality care with heart.

Your visit should feel like a relaxing reset

Here’s what you can expect on your first visit

From the start, we want to make your visit feel like more than just a checkup. Enjoy a warm welcome, personalized care, and a calm, beautifully designed space that puts you at ease. We take time to understand your goals and tailor every detail to give you a dental experience you’ll actually look forward to.

Patient Experience Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital x-rays, and a discussion of your dental goals: all in a calming, spa-like environment. Our team will walk you through your care plan with transparency and answer any questions you might have along the way. And if all goes well, you’ll leave with a clean bill of dental health, and maybe even a warm eucalyptus towel.

What to Bring For a smooth first visit, please bring the following:

Your dental insurance card (if applicable)

Any previous dental records, if available

Spa-inspired tech and amenities to elevate your care

We offer a suite of premium amenities and technology designed to make your appointment as seamless and comfortable as possible. From advanced imaging to cozy comforts, every detail is here to support your peace of mind, and peace of body.

Intraoral scanner

Say goodbye to goopy impressions. Our intraoral scanner captures highly accurate images of your teeth in seconds, making your experience faster, more comfortable, and more precise, especially for procedures like Invisalign or smile makeovers.

Comfort menu

We believe your dental visit should feel like an experience, not a chore. With TVs, headphones, iPads for kids, hot towels, and a touch-up sink, we’ve designed our space to feel calming, lux, and refreshingly thoughtful, all without sacrificing top-level clinical care.

Online scheduling

We know your time is valuable. That’s why we make it easy to book your appointments online, anytime, from any device. Choose times that work with your schedule, and arrive confident knowing we’ve got the rest covered.

Giving back locally to nourish smiles and minds

Where smiles and nutrition connect: our community giveback

At Blanc Dental Aesthetics, our community roots run deep. That’s why we’re partnering with local organizations to support nutrition education and food literacy in schools. From gardening tips to the science of recipes, we want to empower families right here at home to make informed choices, because what you eat directly affects how your smile feels. Together, we’re planting seeds for healthier futures.
