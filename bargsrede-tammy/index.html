The practice

Cosmetic dentistry can improve the appearance of your smile, and it can also improve the health of your teeth. Cosmetic dentistry is focused on improving the appearance of a person’s smile. From teeth whitening to porcelain veneers, cosmetic procedures are focused on enhancing a smile’s aesthetics and helping patients feel better about how their teeth look.

Sedation dentistry uses medication to relax and keep you comfortable while the dentist performs the necessary treatment. It’s often recommended for patients with anxiety or who typically avoid dental services because of their fear or anxiety.

At Tier One Dental, we perform teeth whitening procedures to remove discoloration and stains from your teeth. Many factors cause teeth to become discolored and stained, including consuming dark-colored foods and beverages like coffee, tea, soda, wine, dark berries, and tobacco.

Family dentistry is like a dental hub that caters to patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors. Instead of hopping between different dentists for various family members, you can streamline your dental visits by opting for one trusted family dentist who can treat everyone under the same roof.

Les soins

Dental crowns and bridges are great solutions for several cosmetic and functional issues. A crown is a tooth cap that replaces the entire visible portion of a tooth, while a bridge is a prosthetic tooth replacement held in place by a crown on either side.

A wisdom tooth extraction is the removal of one or more of the wisdom teeth from the mouth. The teeth become impacted or unable to fully erupt into the mouth when there is not enough room in the jaw to accommodate them.
