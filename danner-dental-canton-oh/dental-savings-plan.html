Smile Savers Club Terms & Conditions

  • The plan is valid for a 12 month period from the date a patient enrolls.
  • The plan is only available to patients at Danner Dental in Canton, OH.
  • The next preventive or periodontal appointment must be scheduled before you leave our office. If you need to cancel or reschedule, you must call the office 36 hours prior to the appointment.
  • It is your responsibility to schedule your appointments and keep them within the 12 month time period. If you don’t make your appointments in that period, you forfeit all the benefits of the plan.
  • To obtain 15 % Smile Savers treatment savings benefits, treatment must be prepaid at time of scheduling with one of the following methods: HSA account, MasterCard, Visa, Amex, Discover, cash or check. Outside financing (Care Credit, Proceed, ScratchPay, Alphaeaon, Dental Finance, etc) is excluded from the Smile Savers discount program, although Smile Savers members may still use these programs if they choose, without the 15 % discount on treatment.
  • Smile Savers is strictly for patients WITHOUT dental insurance, or those with dental insurance that choose NOT to use it. This savings plan cannot be combined with any other insurance plan.
