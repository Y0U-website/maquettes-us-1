Cleaning & Prevention

TEETH CLEANING Teeth cleaning is now recommended twice per year. You can trust the team at Broadway Dental Care to remove all plaque and tartar, then floss, polish and brush your teeth. Then we will apply a fluoride treatment and inspect for any decay, cavities and/or other signs of disease.

MOUTHGUARDS & SLEEP APPLIANCES There are a variety of mouthpieces and mouthguards used to treat sleep issues. If you are grinding your teeth (also known as Bruxism) or snoring to the point sleep deprivation, call the team of experts at Broadway Dental Care. We are constantly staying abreast of the latest technologies available to ensure both you and your partner get a good night’s sleep.
