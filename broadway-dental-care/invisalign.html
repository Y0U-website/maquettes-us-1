BENEFITS

Invisalign is not only beneficial cosmetically, but it also aids in the overall health of your mouth. When comparing to other treatments, it is easy to see why Invisalign is becoming one of the most popular Orthodontic treatments available! Here are just some of the many benefits you will find when going through your journey with us:

  • Removable One of the most popular features about Invisalign is the freedom to remove them at any time. Although, we recommend that you keep them in as much as you can in order to get faster, better results!
  • Easy cleaning Simple to clean! There are no extra flossers or tools like metal braces have in order to properly clean them. All you have to do is pop them out, brush your teeth, brush your trays, and then put them back in and you are good to go!
  • Clear aligners The clear trays are hardly noticeable by other people, which is also a popular feature! When comparing to chunky metal braces, Invisalign is something you won’t feel embarrassed by or worried about.
  • No diet changes The first question that is usually asked is, What do I need to avoid? and our answer is almost nothing! You can continue to eat and drink the same things you were consuming prior to your journey with us, including sticky and crunchy things!
  • Sooner results Instead of taking 2-3 years like traditional braces, Invisalign can provide you with the desired results in just 6-18 months!
  • Metal-free Invisalign is only made out of a smooth, plastic material, perfect for those who don’t want the tangy metal taste, or those with metal allergies or sensitivities!
  • No irritation Being metal free and made out of plastic, these aligners cause no irritation to your cheeks or gums. Compared to the poking and prodding of metal wires of braces, this is a very favored aspect!

FAQ

Invisalign’s plastic trays are meant to move your teeth to the position that benefits you. This gentle pressure can cause some soreness in the beginning of your journey, or when switching your trays. Definitely not as intense as traditional braces, this can be uncomfortable at first, but will subside over a few days as you get used to it. We recommend Acetaminophen (Tylenol), Ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin) or other over-the-counter pain medications to bring you the most comfort and soothe the soreness.

If you are suffering from overbite, underbite, crossbite or overcrowding of your teeth, it is not just a cosmetic issue. These issues can cause tremendous pressure on your mouth, creating overall pain and discomfort or even wear, fractures, chipping and damage to your teeth. Luckily, mild to moderate cases are what Invisalign is made for! Our team will work hard to correct the pain and discomfort you are in, so you can achieve comfort with your smile.
