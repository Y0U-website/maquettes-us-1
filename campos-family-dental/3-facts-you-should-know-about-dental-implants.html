3 Facts You Should Know About Dental Implants November 11, 2025 By Update First Last in Content - Business Text - First Last field

Dental implants have transformed the landscape of oral health and cosmetic dentistry as a revolutionary solution for tooth replacement. Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants provide a long-lasting and natural-looking alternative that significantly improves the quality of life for individuals with missing teeth. If you’re considering dental implants, there are three important facts you should know to make an informed decision.

1. They’re Durable and Long-Lasting

One of the most compelling reasons people opt for dental implants is their durability and the fact that they function like natural teeth. Made from biocompatible materials like titanium, dental implants offer a strong foundation as they fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This fusion not only provides stability but also helps in preserving the integrity of your jawbone, thereby preventing further deterioration that often occurs with missing teeth.

2. They’re Popular for a Reason

The advanced technology and high success rate of dental implants have made them increasingly popular worldwide. According to Mordor Intelligence, the global dental implant market was valued at $4.6 billion in 2021 and is expected to grow to $6.8 billion by 2026. This growth reflects the increasing awareness and demand for dental implants as more people recognize their advantages over traditional dentures and bridges. High success rates, typically around 95 % and above per the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, further boost their appeal among patients and dentists alike.

3. They Need to Be Approved by a Dentist

While dental implants offer numerous benefits, the procedure may not be suitable for everyone. Candidates must have healthy gums and adequate bone structure to support the implant. Additionally, lifestyle habits such as smoking can negatively affect the healing process and the longevity of the implants. It’s critical to consult with a dental professional to evaluate your specific situation and determine whether dental implants are the right choice for you.
