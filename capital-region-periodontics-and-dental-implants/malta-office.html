About Malta, NY

Malta is a welcoming town in Saratoga County, ideally situated between Saratoga Springs and Clifton Park along the shores of Saratoga Lake. Known for its blend of quiet, upscale neighborhoods, outdoor recreation, and a growing business district, Malta offers small-town charm with easy access to the amenities of the greater Capital Region. It’s the kind of community where families put down roots — and exactly the kind of place we’re glad to serve.

  • Malta Drive-In Theatre — A beloved seasonal outdoor cinema that has offered classic double features under the stars for generations, preserving a nostalgic mid-20th-century entertainment tradition.
  • Round Lake Auditorium — A historic glass-enclosed performance venue in the village of Round Lake, dating back to 19th-century camp meetings and still hosting concerts, music festivals, and community events today.
  • Saratoga Lake — A scenic recreational lake bordering Malta, popular for boating, fishing, and kayaking, with local marinas and boat launches providing easy access to the water.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Malta Area Periodontist Practice

What kinds of conditions does a periodontist treat?

A periodontist specializes in the structures that support your teeth — primarily the gums and jawbone. We diagnose and treat gum disease at every stage, from early gingivitis to advanced periodontitis, and we address related issues such as gum recession, bone loss, and tooth loss.

In addition to treating disease, periodontists are the specialists who place dental implants and perform procedures like grafting and regeneration. If you’re noticing symptoms such as bleeding gums, our Malta team can identify the cause and recommend the right treatment.

What should I expect during my first appointment?

Your first visit typically begins with a consultation that includes an exam and dental X-rays. For patients considering implants, we use advanced three-dimensional imaging to evaluate the health of your jawbone and determine whether any preparatory steps, such as bone grafting, may be needed.

This planning process helps us design a treatment tailored to your specific situation and goals. Our team will walk you through every step with clear communication so you feel informed and comfortable throughout.

Can dental implants replace more than one missing tooth?

Absolutely. Dental implants are a versatile solution that can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch. For patients missing most or all of their teeth in an arch, we offer efficient full-arch options like All-on-Four, which uses just four implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth, and Teeth-in-an-Hour, a computer-guided approach that can provide functional teeth in a single surgery. During your consultation, we’ll help determine which option best matches your needs and health.

If you’re transitioning from another provider, our team handles the details, coordinating your insurance and gathering your dental X-ray images to ensure seamless continuity of care.

To get started, simply call our Malta office at 518-450-6995 and our front desk team will help you schedule a visit.
