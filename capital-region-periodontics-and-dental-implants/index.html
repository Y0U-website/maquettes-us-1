Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically made from titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone. They provide a sturdy foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth designed to match your natural teeth. Dental implants are a long-term tooth replacement option, enhancing both the function and aesthetics of your smile.
