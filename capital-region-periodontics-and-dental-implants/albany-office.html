About Albany, NY

Albany is the capital of New York State and one of the oldest surviving settlements in the country, sitting on the west bank of the Hudson River in the heart of the Capital Region. As a center of state government, education, and healthcare, the city blends centuries of history with a vibrant, modern downtown. Its walkable neighborhoods, cultural institutions, and proximity to the SUNY Albany campus make it a welcoming home for the many families and professionals our practice serves.

  • New York State Capitol — This grand 19th-century seat of state government took more than 30 years to build and is celebrated for its ornate architecture, including the famous hand-carved "Million Dollar Staircase."
  • Empire State Plaza — A striking modern complex of government and cultural buildings, home to The Egg performing arts center, an observation deck, and a rotating collection of contemporary art.
  • Washington Park — A historic 24-acre green space near downtown that hosts Albany’s beloved annual Tulip Festival each spring and offers scenic walking paths, gardens, and a picturesque lake.

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Albany, NY Periodontist Practice

What is a periodontist, and how is one different from a general dentist?

A periodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the gums, bone, and other support structures that hold your teeth in place. While a general dentist handles routine care such as cleanings and fillings, periodontists complete additional years of specialized training in the field of periodontology – building on the advanced knowledge and skills needed to handle the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of gum disease – placing dental implants, and perform procedures like grafting and regeneration.

Our board-certified periodontists at Capital Region Periodontics & Dental Implants bring that advanced expertise to every patient we see in Albany.

Do I need a referral to become a patient?

No referral is required, though we happily accept patients who are referred by their general dentist. We accept patients both with and without a referral, and our team works to make the transition to our practice as smooth as possible. If you are coming to us from another provider, we coordinate the transfer of your insurance information and dental X-ray images so we can provide seamless continuity of care.

What are dental implants, and are they right for me?

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. Over several months, the bone naturally bonds with the implant through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth that look, feel, and function like your natural smile.

Whether you’re a good candidate depends on factors such as your overall health, jawbone density, and treatment goals. During a consultation, we use advanced 3D imaging to evaluate your jawbone and determine the best option for you, which may include single implants, All-on-Four, or same-day solutions.

What treatments do you offer for gum disease?

Periodontal disease is a common condition caused by harmful bacteria in the mouth. Left untreated, it can develop into severe periodontal disease, leading to deep periodontal pockets, gum recession, and bone loss. The good news is that it can be treated.

We offer a full spectrum of periodontal care tailored to the severity of your condition. For early to moderate gum disease, treatments like scaling and root planing and our periodontal maintenance program remove harmful plaque and tartar below the gum line.

For more advanced cases, we provide specialized therapies including gum and bone grafting, bone regeneration, ridge preservation, crown lengthening, periodontal splinting, LANAP laser therapy, and the minimally invasive Pinhole Surgical Technique. Our goal is always to save your natural teeth whenever possible.
