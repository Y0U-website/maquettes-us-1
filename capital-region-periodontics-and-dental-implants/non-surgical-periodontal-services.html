Procedures Offered

  • Scaling and Root Planing : This is a comprehensive deep cleaning procedure that targets the removal of plaque and tartar accumulation below the gum line. By thoroughly cleaning these areas, the procedure helps to smooth the surfaces of the tooth roots, which not only aids in the removal of harmful bacteria but also plays a vital role in preventing future buildup and promoting healthier gums.
  • Antimicrobial Therapy : This involves the careful application of antimicrobial agents specifically designed to reduce harmful bacteria and inflammation in the gum tissue. By targeting these issues, the therapy promotes healing and helps to prevent infections, ultimately leading to improved gum health and overall oral hygiene.
  • Periodontal Maintenance (Professional Teeth Cleaning) : These regular cleaning sessions are essential for maintaining optimal gum health and preventing the recurrence of periodontal disease. During these visits, dental professionals perform thorough cleanings that go beyond typical brushing and flossing, ensuring that any plaque or tartar is removed. The gums are monitored for any signs of deterioration, thus supporting long-term oral health.
