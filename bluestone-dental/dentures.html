Schedule a consultation with Dr. David S. Bluestone, D.D.S. at Bluestone Dental for full and partial dentures. Dentures are possibly the best choice for tooth replacement for many dental patients.

Immediate Dentures

These are designed before tooth extraction and placed over the gums to wear as tissues heal. Because there is a great deal of swelling which subsides after the extraction of several teeth, the immediate denture may need to be adjusted. A full reline once healing has finished will be included in the treatment plan. The immediate denture offers the advantage of a full set of teeth during the healing process.

Partial Denture Process

Partial denture appliance - This may be used when several teeth have been lost or extracted (some natural teeth remain). The procedure is designed to bring back beauty plus functionality.

During your dental consultation, we will discuss different types of partial dentures.
