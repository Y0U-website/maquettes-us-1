Complete Family Dentistry Services in Reading, PA

Dr. David S. Bluestone D.D.S. encourage dental health and awareness to ALL patients. We are here to provide care and answer questions to everyone 0 and up. There is no wrong age for a first visit!

We are happy to perform procedures on all ages and are excellent at putting patients’ needs and anxieties first and foremost. We offer the following services for the whole family.

Cleanings

One of the key components to good oral hygiene are routine dental cleanings from your dental office. Professional cleanings can help to minimize plaque and tartar build-up which can affect the health of a tooth in the long run if left untreated. Once all plaque and tartar have been removed during your cleaning, we’ll then polish your teeth for a dazzling clean smile.

X-Rays

Dental X-Rays are a great way to get a comprehensive look at a patient’s entire mouth full of teeth to check for any issues and take preventative care. X-Rays are important in helping us to identify problems that may be going on below the gum line and in the very back of patient’s mouths. We can get a good look and check for things like tooth decay, cracked or chipped teeth, and examine the bone in and around the teeth and gums in your mouth that could be related to other underlying medical issues.

Fluoride

Regular fluoride treatments are great in preventing and reducing cavities in both children and adults. They are also a great way to prevent and even help repair existing tooth decay before it becomes visible. Bluestone Dental offers fluoride treatments to the entire family, ensuring you are protecting those smiles and have a healthy set of teeth.

Children’s Dentistry

As a children friendly dentist, we adore your kids! We specialize in complete dental care for the entire family. It’s never too early to start routine pediatric dental care and preventative maintenance. Even if your child only has one or a couple teeth! Bring your children in as soon as you’d like, from zero in age to adults, we take care of everyone.
