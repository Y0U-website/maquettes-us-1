Accessibility arrangements in the organization

Arcadia Medi-Dental Group is committed to providing accessible services to all individuals. The following accessibility features are available at our office:

Patients with specific accessibility needs are encouraged to contact us prior to their visit. We are committed to making our services accessible to all.

  • Parking and Entry: ADA-compliant accessible parking is available near the main entrance. The entrance is wheelchair-accessible via a ramp, although automatic doors are not provided. Staff are available to assist with entry as needed.
  • Interior Access: The office is on a single floor with hallways and doorways wide enough for wheelchairs and mobility devices. Seating in the reception area accommodates various mobility needs. While the front desk is not lowered, staff can assist as necessary.
  • Restrooms and Treatment Areas: An accessible restroom with grab bars is available. All dental operatories are accessible to patients using mobility aids.
  • Additional Services: Service animals are welcome.
  • Written materials and text communication are available for hearing-impaired patients.
  • Large print forms are available for patients with low vision.
  • Patients may request extra time or special accommodations when scheduling.
  • Staff are trained to assist individuals with disabilities.
  • Signage: Braille signage is not currently available; however, staff assistance is provided for navigation.
