Step 2: Implant Surgery (Teeth in a Day)

  • Once healed, we replace the temporary teeth with your custom-made, permanent smile

Who’s a Candidate?

Even if you’ve been told in the past that you don’t qualify for implants, our advanced techniques and diagnostics may open up new possibilities for you.

  • Have multiple missing or failing teeth
  • Struggle with loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Want a long-term, non-removable solution
  • Have adequate jawbone (or are open to bone grafting if needed)
  • Are in overall good health (we’ll review your history to be sure)

FAQs

Does the procedure hurt? We offer multiple sedation options to ensure you’re comfortable. Most patients report little to no pain during the procedure and mild soreness afterward.

How long does the procedure take? The implant placement and temporary teeth are typically completed in one day.

How long do All-on-4 implants last? With good care, the implants can last a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth may need updating every 10 15 years.

Can I eat normally after getting implants? After the initial healing phase, yes! You’ll enjoy a wide range of foods with confidence again.
