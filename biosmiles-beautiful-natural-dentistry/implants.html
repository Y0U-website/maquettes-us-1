Ceramic Zirconia Implants

Zirconia dental implants are a type of ceramic called zirconium oxide. Unlike traditional metal implants, zirconia dental implants are hypoallergenic and non-corrosive, making them an excellent option for patients with allergies or sensitivities to other dental materials. For those considering dental implants in Boise, ID, studies show that the conductivity of metal implants promotes more bacterial and plaque growth than metal-free zirconia dental implants.

Zirconia dental implants are better-looking because zirconia looks a lot like enamel. Metal dental implants, over time, can corrode and darken sometimes this is visible through a worn crown or the soft gum tissue. This is not a concern with metal free zirconia dental implants because they are non-corrosive, less allergenic, and will stay bright and attractive for many decades or even a lifetime.
