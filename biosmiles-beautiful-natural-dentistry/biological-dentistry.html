Dental Allergy Testing

One of our services is Biocompatibility Testing in Boise, ID. This is a simple test where we take a blood sample and test it against various dental materials to observe an immune response. By doing this, we can see if you may have an allergy to any dental materials. This helps us find the best and safest options for you regarding dental treatments.
