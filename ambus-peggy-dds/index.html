Dr. Peggy Ambus was born and raised in San Francisco, having attended Lowell High School, the University of San Francisco and the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry.

The practice

Dr. Ambus is a 1987 graduate of the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco.

She has been successfully serving Burlingame as a private practice dentist since she established her dental office in 1993.

Dr. Ambus is the proud mother of two children, both having been born and raised in Burlingame. In her free time, Dr. Ambus enjoys spending time with her family, hunting for antiques and following fashions.

Dr. Michael Bahlinger is originally from Orange County, California, having attended Long Beach State University before studying dentistry at the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry.
