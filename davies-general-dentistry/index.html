The practice

Dr. Kristi Davies and Dr. Kent Davies are siblings who proudly continue the tradition set by their father, grandmother, and great grandfather by providing dental care in the Waukesha, WI community. Both dentists are committed to offering a range of beneficial oral health services in an office that makes every part of patient care comfortable.

Davies General Dentistry provides valuable services for Waukesha, WI residents and members of our neighboring communities. Whether you are looking for a place that provides dependable general care, need help with specific smile issues or have a dental emergency, we are ready to help!
