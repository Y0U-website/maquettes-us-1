A dental bridge is a fixed, non-removable appliance that replaces one or more missing teeth, restoring your smile, chewing ability, and facial shape. The most common type, a traditional bridge, uses crowns on adjacent teeth to anchor artificial teeth (pontics) in the gap.

Bridges typically require two or more visits. Teeth are prepared and impressions taken for a custom bridge. A temporary bridge is worn until your permanent bridge is fitted, adjusted, and cemented for a long-lasting, natural result.

A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength.

Porcelain crowns are popular for their durability and natural appearance, blending seamlessly with your smile. The procedure takes two visits—one to shape the tooth and another to place the final cover.

A porcelain Onlay is a custom-made restoration that resembles your natural tooth, often called a partial crown. Made in a dental laboratory and permanently cemented by Dr. Moglianesi. Onlays repair teeth with large fillings, decay, or damage while preserving more natural tooth structure than a full crown.

Onlay treatment is completed in two visits—first to prepare the tooth and take impressions, then to cement the custom onlay for a precise, comfortable fit.

Veneers are thin, custom-made porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to create a beautiful, natural-looking smile. They can reshape teeth and often serve as an alternative to crowns for cosmetic or restorative purposes.

Veneers are completed in two visits—first for shaping and impressions, then for precise bonding and final polishing.

Orthodontics

Straight teeth improve chewing, biting, and speaking, while enhancing appearance and boosting confidence. Properly aligned teeth also reduce the risk of dental problems associated with malocclusion, such as overbite, underbite, crossbite, and overcrowding.

Invisalign® offers a nearly invisible, removable alternative to traditional braces for straightening teeth. Custom-made aligning trays are changed every few weeks to gradually move teeth into the desired position, providing both comfort and aesthetic appeal.

Invisalign® is effective, discreet, and comfortable, making it a popular choice for adults and teens seeking orthodontic correction. Contact our office to learn if Invisalign® is right for you.

  • Overcrowding: Too little space for teeth to align properly.
  • Large gaps: Caused by missing teeth or jaw growth.
  • Crossbite: Upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth.
  • Overbite: Upper teeth extend beyond the lower teeth.
  • Underbite: Lower teeth extend beyond the upper teeth.
  • Nearly invisible: Ideal for adults or those concerned about appearance.
  • Removable: Trays can be taken out for eating, drinking, or special occasions.
  • Straightens teeth naturally: Unlike veneers, Invisalign® moves teeth rather than covering them.
  • Comfortable: Smooth trays reduce irritation compared to metal braces.
  • Planning: 3D digital images of the jaw are taken to design the treatment plan and predict results.
  • Custom Aligners: Typically 20–29 sets per arch arecreated to gradually reposition teeth.
  • Wearing Trays: Trays should be worn nearly all the time, except while eating or drinking. They are replaced every two weeks, with visible progress throughout treatment.

TMJ

TMJ disorder affects the jaw joint and surrounding muscles, causing headaches, jaw pain, teeth grinding, and sometimes ear ringing. Many sufferers do not realize a dentist can effectively treat the underlying cause.

Dr Moglianesi will perform a thorough examination, review your bite and medical history, and take X-rays to determine the best treatment. Early intervention can relieve pain, prevent further damage, and restore proper function.

If you are experiencing persistent jaw pain or clicking, schedule a TMJ evaluation to learn how our custom treatment plans can relieve tension and restore comfort.

  • Symptoms often worsen during stress and can lead to neck pain or dizziness.
  • Misaligned teeth (“bad bite”) are the most common cause.
  • Untreated TMJ can result in tooth erosion, jawbone damage, and loose teeth.
  • Bite Guards: Custom night guards protect teeth from grinding.
  • Bite Analysis G Realignment: Adjusting the bite can relieve pain and improve aesthetics.
  • Relaxation Techniques G Muscle Relaxants: Reduce stress and alleviate symptoms.
  • Restorative Adjustments: In some cases, reshaping or restoring teeth can eliminate a bad bite.

Sleep Apnea

Sleep apnea is a serious sleep disorder characterized by repeated pauses in breathing during sleep. These pauses reduce oxygen intake, disrupt sleep, and can strain the heart. Many individuals with sleep apnea are unaware of their condition.

Untreated sleep apnea increases the risk of heart problems, high blood pressure, and other serious health issues. The repeated lack of oxygen forces the heart to work harder, which can lead to life-threatening consequences over time.

Dental appliances are an effective treatment option for sleep apnea, particularly for patients who are not significantly overweight. These devices reposition the lower jaw slightly forward to open the airway, reduce snoring, and improve sleep quality. They can serve as an alternative to CPAP therapy in many cases.

Tired of restless nights? Ask about our comfortable, custom oral appliances designed to improve sleep quality and breathing.
