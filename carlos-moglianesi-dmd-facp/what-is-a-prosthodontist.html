Board Certification and Expertise

Dr. Carlos Moglianesi is a Board-Certified Prosthodontist, one of the highest levels of achievement in the field. Becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics requires:

  • Passing a comprehensive written examination.
  • Presenting and defending multiple patient cases before a board of experienced prosthodontists.
  • Ongoing recertification every eight years, including attending and presenting scientific lectures or publishing research.
