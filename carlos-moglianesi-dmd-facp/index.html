The practice

At Carlos Moglianesi, DMD FACP, we are dedicated to helping individuals and families achieve brighter, more confident smiles through expert cosmetic dentistry. We serve individuals and families in Chatham and throughout Morris County with compassionate, comprehensive dental care.

A board-certified prosthodontist, Dr. Moglianesi specializes in cosmetic and restorative treatments that renew and enhance smiles. With meticulous attention to detail and a passion for patient care, he is committed to providing natural-looking, lasting results.

Our general dentist, Dr. Buniak, brings years of experience in dental and hygiene care. She is dedicated to helping patients of all ages maintain optimal oral health and achieve the best version of their smile.
