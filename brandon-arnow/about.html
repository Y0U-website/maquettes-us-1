Our Values Your Comfort .

Dr. Brandon Arnow graduated from Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine in 2009. After returning home to Arizona, he established one of the most successful denture practices for the last 10 years in the Greater Phoenix Area.

He loves joking with his patients, which puts even the most apprehensive patients at ease. Dr. Arnow loves seeing how a new smiles brings out so much joy in his patients and enjoys serving in the White Mountains where he now calls home.

Tino

For the past 25 years, Tino has thrived as a dental lab technician, honing his skills and expertise in a field he is deeply passionate about. His journey has taken him from the vibrant energy of New York to the diverse landscapes of Arizona. Tino’s professional relationship with Dr. Arnow started with his time in the Phoenix area.

When the opportunity arose to join the team at ComfortFit Dentures in Show Low, he readily embraced the chance to contribute his skills and experience in a new setting. He truly values the rewarding work he undertakes and the supportive team environment at ComfortFit Dentures.
