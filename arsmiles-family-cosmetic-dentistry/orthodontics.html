Clear Aligners for Achieving Normal Occlusion

Achieving normal occlusion—a balanced, functional bite—is a key goal in orthodontics. Our clear aligners straighten teeth, including the visible front teeth, while guiding them into their ideal position for lasting stability and comfort.

Unlike traditional braces with wires and brackets, clear aligners use custom trays that apply gentle, controlled forces for precise tooth movement. They address spacing issues, align both front and lower teeth, and promote a balanced bite.

This modern, removable alternative supports space closure, improves bite alignment, and helps prevent future dental complications—delivering optimal results for both function and aesthetics.

Progressive Aligner Changes

With clear aligner therapy, you’ll switch to a new set of aligners every few weeks, making small, precise adjustments that gradually move your teeth while keeping discomfort minimal. The treatment process is designed to stay on schedule, but timing can vary depending on case complexity and patient compliance.

Innovative tools now allow for accurate progress tracking and timely adjustments, helping ensure predictable, effective results. Whether your case is straightforward or more complex, these advancements make clear aligner therapy a reliable option for achieving a healthier, well-aligned smile.

Retainers After Clear Aligners

Once your treatment is complete, fixed or removable appliances (retainers) keep your teeth from shifting back. Removable retainers are custom-made devices, typically crafted from clear plastic, that can be conveniently taken out for eating and cleaning. They play a crucial role in maintaining teeth alignment after clear aligner therapy by holding your teeth in their new positions.

Consistent retainer wear protects your results, supports long-term oral health, and helps prevent future orthodontic issues. Our team will guide you on the right retainer type and schedule to maintain your beautiful, healthy smile for years to come.

Pre-Orthodontic Care for Kids and Adolescents

Pre-orthodontic treatment in children and adolescents can prevent crowding, misalignment, and impaction of permanent teeth, reducing the need for complex treatments later. Various dental appliances can address malocclusion, and the appropriate timing varies per child. Orthodontists recommend a first evaluation by age 7 to ensure early intervention and optimal dental health.
