Benefits of the All-on-4 System

Patients choose All-on-4 for its convenience, durability, and life-changing impact. Key advantages include:

Unlike traditional full dentures , the All-on-4 system gives you the freedom to eat, smile, and speak without worry.

  • Fixed teeth in one day —many patients leave with a temporary full-arch prosthetic the same day as surgery
  • No adhesives or removal —the restoration stays securely in place at all times
  • Preservation of jawbone —implants stimulate the bone, reducing resorption
  • Improved diet and speech —enjoy greater biting power and clarity in communication
  • Cost-effective —fewer implants reduce surgery time and overall expense compared to individual implants

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4

Is All-on-4 right for me if I’ve worn dentures for years? Yes. Many patients who’ve experienced jawbone loss due to long-term denture wear still qualify for All-on-4 due to the angled implant placement.

How long does the procedure take? The surgical portion of All-on-4 typically takes a few hours, and many patients receive their temporary prosthetic the same day.

Is the All-on-4 system removable? No. The restoration is fixed in place and only removable by your dentist. It feels and functions much like natural teeth.

What’s the difference between All-on-4 and traditional implants? Traditional implants often require one post per tooth and can involve bone grafting. All-on-4 uses only four implants per arch and is designed to maximize available bone.

Does insurance cover All-on-4? Coverage varies, but we’ll help you navigate your insurance benefits. Financing options are also available—see our patient info page for details.
