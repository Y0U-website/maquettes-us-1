Signs Your Implant Restoration May Need Repair or Replacement

If your current implant-supported restoration shows any of the following issues, it may be time to consult with our team:

We offer thorough evaluations to determine whether a repair or full restoration is the best course of action.

  • Loose or shifting crown or denture
  • Cracks, chips, or visible damage
  • Discomfort when biting or chewing
  • Gum irritation around the implant site
  • Changes in your bite alignment or function

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restorations

How long do dental implant restorations last? On average, implant crowns and bridges can last 10–15 years with proper care, while the titanium post can last much longer. We recommend regular checkups to extend their lifespan.

Can you restore implants placed by another dentist? Yes. Our team is experienced in working with implant systems from a variety of providers and can help repair or restore existing dental implants regardless of where they were placed.

Is the restoration process painful? Most restoration work is minimally invasive and does not involve the implant post or bone. Many patients require only local anesthesia or none at all.

What if my implant feels loose? A loose implant crown or denture is usually repairable, but it’s important to schedule a visit promptly to avoid complications.

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients? Yes. We provide IV sedation and other comfort options for patients who prefer a relaxed, stress-free experience.
