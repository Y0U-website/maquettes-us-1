Pay Now What Are Hybridge Dental Implants? If you are missing most or all of your teeth and want a permanent, natural-looking replacement, Hybridge dental implants offer one of the most advanced full-arch restoration systems available. At Chips Dental Associates , we provide Hybridge implant solutions at our Gibsonia and Pittsburgh offices, helping patients reclaim their confidence, comfort, and ability to eat the foods they love.

Hybridge is a fixed, full-arch implant system that replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth using as few as five to six strategically placed dental implants. A custom-engineered prosthetic is permanently attached to the implants, creating a restoration that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth. Unlike removable dentures, a Hybridge restoration stays in place 24/7. There is no need for adhesives, clasps, or nightly removal.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Hybridge Implants?

Hybridge dental implants are ideal for patients who are missing all or most of their teeth in one or both arches, those currently wearing dentures who want a permanent upgrade, and patients with enough jawbone to support implant placement. If bone loss is a concern, our team can evaluate whether bone grafting can create the foundation needed for successful implant placement. Even patients who have been told they are not candidates for implants may qualify after a consultation at our office.

The Hybridge Treatment Process

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation at our Gibsonia or Pittsburgh office. We use 3D CT scanning and digital impressions (iTero) to create a detailed map of your jaw and plan the optimal placement for each implant.

On the day of surgery, your implants are placed under IV sedation or general anesthesia for maximum comfort. In many cases, a temporary set of teeth is attached the same day so you leave the office with a functional smile. After a healing period of several months, your final Hybridge prosthetic is fabricated and permanently secured.

Frequently Asked Questions About Hybridge Dental Implants

How long do Hybridge implants last?

With proper care and regular dental checkups, Hybridge dental implants can last 20 years or longer. The implants themselves are designed to be a lifetime solution, and the prosthetic can be maintained or replaced if needed.

Is the Hybridge procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort after the procedure. We use IV sedation during surgery and provide detailed aftercare instructions and pain management guidance to support a smooth recovery.

How much do Hybridge dental implants cost?

The cost varies depending on the number of implants needed, whether bone grafting is required, and other factors. We offer free consultations to discuss pricing, insurance coverage, and financing options.

Can I eat normally with Hybridge implants?

Yes. One of the biggest advantages of Hybridge over traditional dentures is the ability to eat virtually anything, including foods that require strong biting and chewing force.
