What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial roots for missing teeth. Once placed into the jawbone, they fuse naturally with the bone through a process called osseointegration. After healing, a custom crown is attached to the implant, giving you a secure, natural-looking replacement that looks and feels just like a real tooth.

Why Choose Dental Implants? Natural Look & Feel Mimics your natural teeth in appearance and function. Durability With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. Bone Preservation Prevents jawbone loss caused by missing teeth. Protects Healthy Teeth No need to grind down adjacent teeth, like bridges. Stability Provides a secure foundation that functions like a natural tooth root. Improved Chewing & Speaking No slipping or discomfort like dentures. Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?
