About us

At Canby Dental, we’re committed to providing high quality, patient focused dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Led by Dr. Angel Harounian, our team helps patients throughout Reseda achieve healthy, confident smiles through modern, compassionate dentistry.

Compassionate Dental Care for the Entire Family

At Canby Dental, we believe every patient deserves personalized, high quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Located in Reseda, California, our practice is dedicated to helping individuals and families achieve healthy, confident smiles through comprehensive dental services and a patient first approach.

Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning, cosmetic enhancement, dental implants, or TMJ treatment, our goal is to make every appointment a positive experience. We take the time to understand your needs, answer your questions, and create customized treatment plans designed to support your long term oral health.

Meet Dr. Angel Harounian

Dr. Angel Harounian is a dedicated general dentist known for providing comprehensive, patient centered care with a friendly and professional approach. A graduate of the University of Southern California in 2016, Dr. Harounian combines advanced clinical training with a genuine passion for helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

With experience across all areas of dentistry, she offers a full range of services including cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, veneers, clear aligner therapy, TMJ pain management, and family dentistry. Her commitment to continuing education allows her to stay current with the latest advancements in dental care, ensuring patients receive modern, effective treatment options.

Dr. Harounian is an active member of the Los Angeles Dental Society, the San Fernando Valley Dental Association, and Alpha Omega, reflecting her dedication to professional excellence and lifelong learning.

Known for her gentle demeanor and attention to detail, Dr. Harounian takes pride in building lasting relationships with patients and helping them feel comfortable and confident throughout their dental journey.

Our Philosophy

At Canby Dental, we focus on more than just treating teeth we focus on caring for people. We believe that trust, communication, and compassion are the foundation of exceptional dental care. Every treatment recommendation is made with your best interests in mind, and we strive to ensure that every patient feels informed, respected, and comfortable.

Our mission is simple: to provide exceptional dental care that improves oral health, enhances confidence, and supports overall well being.

Serving Reseda and the San Fernando Valley

Canby Dental proudly serves patients throughout Reseda, Northridge, Tarzana, Encino, Winnetka, and the surrounding San Fernando Valley communities. We are committed to providing convenient, high quality dental care close to home.
