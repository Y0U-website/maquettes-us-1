When Do You Need Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you have:

  • A single missing tooth
  • Multiple missing teeth
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Difficulty chewing due to tooth loss
  • Bone loss caused by missing teeth
  • A desire for a permanent tooth replacement solution

Types of Dental Implants We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

  • Replaces one missing tooth
  • Restores function and appearance
  • Preserves surrounding healthy teeth
  • Natural looking results

Implant-Supported Bridges

  • Replace multiple missing teeth
  • Anchored securely by implants
  • More stable than traditional bridges
  • Helps preserve jawbone health

Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Secure alternative to traditional dentures
  • Improved comfort and stability
  • Enhanced chewing ability
  • Reduced slipping and movement

Our Dental Implant Process

Consultation & Evaluation We perform a comprehensive examination, including digital imaging, to determine if dental implants are right for you.

Implant Placement A titanium implant is precisely placed into the jawbone where it will serve as the new tooth root.

Healing & Integration Over the next several months, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone to create a strong foundation.

Final Restoration A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, completing your smile restoration.
