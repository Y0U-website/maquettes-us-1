When Do You Need a Dental Cleaning?

You should schedule regular cleanings if you:

  • Haven’t had a cleaning in six months
  • Notice plaque buildup on your teeth
  • Experience bad breath
  • Have bleeding or swollen gums
  • Want to maintain a healthy smile
  • Are prone to cavities or gum disease
