Meet Dr. Jay Kanegawa in Aiea, HI

Dr. Kanegawa has served on the boards of the Association of Hanahauoli Alumni (former President), Punahou Alumni Association, Hawaii Dental Service (Delta Dental) (former Chairman of the Board), The New Dentist Committee of Hawaii Dental Association (former Chair), The Health Committee of Hawaii Dental Association (former Chair), and Kick Start Hawaii.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Kanegawa is an avid foodie who spent over 14 years with Williams Sonoma. He rides his Peloton religiously, is a devoted husband and father, and a proud owner of a very demanding (but cute) Tibetan Terrier.

  • Dr. Gilbert T. Kanegawa established Aiea’s first dental practice.
  • Dr. Ray K. Maesaka taught at Indiana Dental School for over 40 years and received an excellence-in-teaching award from Indiana University.
  • Hawaii Dental Service Board of Directors, 1995–2004
  • Temple University School of Dentistry Graduate
  • Second-generation dentist serving Aiea families
