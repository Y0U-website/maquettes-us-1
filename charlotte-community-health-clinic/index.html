The practice

We believe that health involves the entire person. That’s why we provide services to keep your whole body and mind healthy, all in the same convenient location.

We provide preventative care and annual physical exams for children and adults. We also treat acute and chronic illnesses, including prescriptions, laboratory services, and imaging services.

We offer a range of preventative, restorative, and urgent dental services for children and adults. Our team provides gentle teeth cleaning and restoration to keep you smiling.

Our Licensed Clinical Social Workers let you express your feelings in a safe environment and learn to cope with stressful situations. Every counseling session is different, but all sessions prepare you to better take control of your life.

COVID-19 Update: CCHC is committed to continue providing high-quality care to all of our patients throughout the coronavirus pandemic. To keep everyone safe, we cannot accept walk-in patients right now. Patients who do not have an appointment will not be seen. If you are a CCHC patient, please call ahead or use your MyChart patient portal to make an appointment.
