COVID

See more CDC recommendations about what to do if you feel sick.

  • Do NOT come to the clinic without calling ahead if you have a fever, cough, or other symptoms of a cold.
  • Use a thermometer to measure your temperature before calling a hotline or doctor’s office.
  • If you are a CCHC patient, please dial the main number at (704) 316-6561 and select the option to speak to a nurse.
  • If you are not a CCHC patient, please call one of the testing resources at the top of this page.
  • Wash hands often with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. Use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer if soap and water are not available.
  • Clean surfaces that are touched often, like doorknobs and cell phones.

How should I stay healthy?

The best way to keep yourself and your family safe is to prevent the disease from spreading. Don’t wait until someone in your family gets sick to think about what you plan to do.

See more CDC recommendations on how to prepare your family for COVID-19.

  • Wash hands often with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. Use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer if soap and water are not available.
  • Avoid crowded places and stay home when possible, especially if you are older than 60 or have a chronic medical condition.
  • Wear something over your nose and mouth (a cloth mask, bandana, or t-shirt) when you have to leave home
  • Avoid close contact with other people, especially if they’re coughing or don’t feel well.
  • Try not to touch your face, including your eyes, nose, and mouth.
  • Clean surfaces that are touched often, like doorknobs and cell phones.
  • If you have questions about COVID-19, call one of the hotlines above.
  • Talk with your family and friends about what you will do if someone gets sick.
