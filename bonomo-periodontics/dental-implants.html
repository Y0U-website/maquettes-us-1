Dental Implants

What is Our Dental Implant Procedure?

Our dental implant, or teeth-in-a-day, procedure is a minimally invasive dental four-on-four implant that provides support for your replacement teeth without the need for multiple surgeries. This eliminates the need for removable dentures and allows you to enjoy a natural-looking and functional smile.

This procedure involves the placement of four dental implants in your jawbone, specifically in areas where there is the highest bone density. These implants serve as anchors for a fixed bridge, which supports the replacement teeth.

Unlike other options such as dentures or bridges, our teeth-in-a-day procedure helps to prevent bone atrophy and gum recession. This means that your surrounding bone and gum tissue are better preserved, maintaining the natural structure of your face and preventing that sunken look that can occur with traditional tooth replacement options.

We recommend this procedure for people looking for a long-term, reliable solution for missing teeth.

The Dental Implant Procedure Like other dental implants, teeth-in-a-day implants are placed during an outpatient surgical procedure while the patient is under sedation.

The patient will be fitted with a prosthetic arch, then after the bone has fused with the arch, the patient will be fitted with a second stronger prosthetic.

A Permanent Solution Made for You

We offer you a permanent solution for your missing teeth, boasting a long-lasting nature that can potentially last a lifetime with proper care.

With proper oral hygiene and regular check-ups, your implants can last for many years, providing a stable and reliable solution.

Whether you need a single tooth, or multiple teeth replaced, implants can offer you a permanent and effective option to restore not only the function of your teeth but also the confidence to smile and eat without reservation.
