Teeth cleaning is a dental procedure that removes plaque, tartar, and stains from the surfaces of teeth to prevent cavities and gum disease. It helps maintain oral hygiene and keeps the mouth healthy and fresh.

A dental filling is a restorative procedure used to repair a tooth damaged by decay, cracks, or wear. It restores the tooth’s normal shape, function, and strength while preventing further decay.

A dental sealant is a thin, protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent cavities. It creates a smooth barrier that keeps out food particles and bacteria.

A porcelain veneer is a thin shell of ceramic material bonded to the front surface of a tooth to improve its color, shape, or alignment. A porcelain crown is a full-coverage restoration that encases the entire tooth to restore its strength, function, and natural appearance.

Dental aesthetic bonding is a cosmetic procedure in which tooth-colored resin is applied and shaped to improve the appearance of teeth. It can correct issues like chips, gaps, discoloration, or irregular shapes for a more natural-looking smile.

Les soins

A dental crown is placed over a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, strength, and appearance. A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to the surrounding natural teeth or implants.

Oral surgery involves surgical procedures performed in the mouth to treat conditions affecting the teeth, gums, and jaw. Tooth extraction is a common oral surgery procedure that removes a damaged, decayed, or problematic tooth to maintain oral health.

Implant-supported dentures are removable or fixed dentures that are anchored securely to dental implants in the jawbone. They provide improved stability, chewing function, and comfort compared to traditional dentures.
