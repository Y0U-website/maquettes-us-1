What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants near you is a way of replacing all of your upper or lower teeth supported solely by four implants, which are titanium posts. Implants are surgical posts that can be placed in your jawbone and serve as roots for teeth. After the implants heal and biologically bond to your bone, the prosthetic teeth are attached.

All-on-4 simply means that all of your teeth are attached to four implants. With traditional implants, you might need eight or more implants to have a full mouth of teeth. These four implants are placed in the ideal positions where the bone is strongest in your jaw.

The first two implants are placed straight down in the front of your jaw. These are the two strongest areas. The other two implants are placed at an angle in the back of your mouth, which allows us to utilize more of what bone you have left if there has been bone loss with your missing teeth.

The All-on-4 Treatment Process

Your First Visit

At your first appointment, we will take X-rays and do an evaluation of your mouth. We check your bone level for implants and your overall oral health. We will also discuss what you are looking for in your new smile.

If you have some teeth that need to come out, in many cases we are able to remove them on the same day as your implant surgery. That means fewer appointments for you.

Surgery Day

On surgery day, our dentist in Sun City West , AZ, will numb your mouth so you feel no pain. For patients who are nervous, we provide sedation to help relax them during treatment.

We place 4 implants into your jaw. The front two go straight down, and the back two are placed in at a 45-degree angle. The angle allows us to avoid important nerves and use the strongest part of your jawbone.

Temporary Teeth

In many cases, we are able to provide temporary teeth the same day as surgery. The temporary teeth let you eat soft foods and smile immediately, while implants are healing.

Your temporary teeth will be attached to your implants so they won’t slip like regular dentures. While your bone bonds to the implants, you will need to eat softer foods for a period of time.
