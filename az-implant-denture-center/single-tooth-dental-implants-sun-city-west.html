What is a Single-Tooth Dental Implant?

A single-tooth dental implant is a full replacement tooth made of three components. The first component is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the gum tissue and then into the jawbone, at the site of the natural tooth, where the tooth root was previously present, and will act in place of the natural tooth root. The second component is called an abutment, which will properly coordinate the titanium post to the new tooth. Finally, the third component is called a crown, which is a natural-looking replacement tooth that goes on top of the abutment.

The titanium post is a very important part because it will eventually fuse with the jawbone, called osseointegration, stabilizing the implant system. Once the titanium post has properly osseointegrated, it will support the replacement tooth for many years.

Caring for Your Single-Tooth Implant

Taking care of your single-tooth dental implants near you is easy. Just brush and floss, like a natural tooth! Visiting the dentist near you , for routine appointments (every six months) will help you maintain a healthy implant. These implants can last a lifetime with proper care.

Avoid chewing on hard objects (such as ice) or using your teeth as a tool. If you grind your teeth at night, than our dentist may recommend a nightguard to protect your implant and teeth.
