What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots created from titanium metal. The implants are inserted into your jawbone in the area of your previously lost teeth. After it has healed and integrated into your jawbone, we attach a crown, bridge , or denture on top, creating a new tooth which is aesthetically pleasing, functional and feels just like your real teeth.

The titanium implant creates a solid base for your new tooth. Unlike their removable counterparts, implants are not resting on your gums. They become integrated with your jawbone and will not shift or move while eating or talking. Most patients describe their implants as feeling just like their natural teeth.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Caring for dental implants near you is easy. Just brush at least twice a day and floss once daily as you would with natural teeth. The key to successful long-term use of your implants is routine dental visits and professional cleanings.

Do not chew on hard objects such as ice or pen caps. Also, avoid using your teeth as tools to open packages. These habits can harm both natural teeth and implants.

FAQ

Properly cared for, dental implants can last for 10-30 years, and even a lifetime. Your crown restoration on top may need to be replaced every 10-15 years, but the implant itself should not fail. Does getting dental implants hurt? Most patients report the implant surgery is less painful than having a tooth extracted. Our dentist near you provides local anesthesia while placing the dental implant, and usually any soreness after the placement can be handled with over-the-counter pain medication.
