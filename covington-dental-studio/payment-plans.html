Affordable Dental Payment Plans at Covington Dental Studio

Our practice is committed to providing affordable dental care to Covington and surrounding Alcovy, Social Circle and Oxford communities. We believe everyone deserves accessible, best in class care, which is why we offer dental payment plans and financing resources alongside our accepted dental insurances.

With dental insurance, our office accepts most major dental insurance providers. We also handle insurance billing for patients as a courtesy. You are welcome to check and see if your insurance is listed as one of our in-network providers or reach out to your insurance company directly with further questions.

If you find yourself out-of-network or don’t have insurance, we have a number of options available so you can receive the care you need, whether that’s general, cosmetic or restorative dentistry services.

Dental Care Payment Plans

Covington Dental Studio offers both in-house dental payment plans and third-party financing partnerships for patients. These are great options for anyone looking for help with budgeting or planning for oral health treatments, especially because of the zero-interest benefits. Our financial coordinators are available during extended office hours to talk through your options with you, so please don’t hesitate to reach out with questions.

Visit Covington Dental Studio Today

Our practice accepts most major credit cards, cash and checks. Patients are responsible for any payments at time of service.
