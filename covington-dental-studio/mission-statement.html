Mission Statement

  • Treat each patient with dignity, respect, kindness and understanding.
  • Demonstrate that the patient and their health is valued by every member of our team.
  • Serve our community through preventive dentistry, educational programs and other outreach projects.
  • Maintain highly qualified and motivated personnel who show initiative in their work, excellent patient service, and concern for our patients.
  • Build a practice that runs smoothly and efficiently, pacing our days to be sure of the timeliness of patient appointments.
  • Retain employees that work together in a spirit of harmony and cooperation, with each individual using their time wisely.
  • Meet our patients’ needs alongside allowing time for continued professional and personal fulfillment.
  • Earn a fair monetary return for our efforts and investment, while being sure services are affordable for patients.
  • Hold ourselves to a high ethical standard and always keep our patients’ best interest in mind.
  • Provide a healthy and clean facility above ADA and OSHA standards.
  • Respect our patients’ privacy and be compliant with all federal and state regulations.
  • Deserve the respect of each and every one of our patients, while striving to become leaders in our chosen field.

Core Values

The core values of our practice follow the same underlying theme found in our dentist office mission statement: honesty, integrity, character, dedication, education and respect.

The simplest way to put it is that Covington Dental Studio gives great care. The entire team works together with a dedication to treat you with the consideration you deserve, while providing what you need for your continued health. This is not only because of the value we place on building relationships within our community, but also because of the professional and moral principles we hold ourselves to.

Our patients come first, always. You are the center of our practice. When you come in to visit, we’re certain you’ll see why you can trust us with care and advice.
