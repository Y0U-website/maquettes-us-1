About Us

General dentistry keeps your mouth healthy and prevents future problems from occurring by practicing good oral hygiene habits. This includes biannual exams, routine cleanings, educational consultations and digital X-rays.

Cosmetic dentistry combines looks and function. We help you achieve a whiter, brighter smile.This includes veneers, teeth whitening, laser gum reshaping and composite fillings. See for yourself how treatments can improve confidence by altering the color, shape and size of your teeth and gums.

Restorative dentistry replaces or rehabilitates missing or damaged teeth. This includes dental crowns, inlays, onlays and implants. Root canal therapy and oral surgery fall under this area as well. The goal is to prevent future issues from recurring while restoring stability to the structure of the mouth.

Technology and Amenities

Comprehensive dental care means we prioritize all aspects of patient health. You’ll find the latest in technology at our family dental center, as well as amenities to help you feel comfortable during your appointment.

Whether its flat-screen HD TVs, a comfortable waiting room with a beverage station, or digital panoramic X-rays for safer, more accurate imaging, this practice ensures patients are receiving best in class care from the moment you walk through our doors.

Safety Standards

Our office is clean and safe for your family. We follow all guidelines set by the American Dental Association and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration . The Covington Dental Studio then goes beyond those recommended standards with a practice policy to disinfect equipment prior to your visit, sterilize bays between patients, and ensure no cross contamination occurs.

If you have any further questions about what procedures are in place for cleanliness and safety, please don’t hesitate to ask!

Emergency Dental Services

When there’s a dental emergency, we work with patients to deliver care as quickly as possible. This is one of the reasons we offer same-day appointments, so we can treat patients immediately and effectively. Our team is available throughout the day, if you’re experiencing a dental emergency, give us a call at 678-567-4999.

Our dentists are trained oral surgeons and sedation technicians. For patients who require sedation for anxiety or procedures, the treatments are administered with your care and comfort in mind.
