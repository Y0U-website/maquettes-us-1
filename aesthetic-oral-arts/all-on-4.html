The All-on-4 Technique

What makes the All-on-4 dental implant technique unique? This treatment provides stability for a permanent prosthetic. Patients who have lost all the teeth in their upper or lower arch have a reliable alternative to traditional dentures.

With proper maintenance and regular check-ups, All-on-4 dental implants can remain functional for decades.

The All-on-4 protocol involves the strategic positioning of four dental implants into the jawbone. Two implants are vertically oriented and two are placed at an angle to maximize the available bone. Where traditional implants require several months of healing before a restoration can be attached, All-on-4 patients do not have to wait to enjoy full function. Immediately after placing the All-on-4 implants, Dr. Pfister will attach a prosthetic denture to the posts. Patients walk out of surgery with a complete set of brand new teeth.

With proper maintenance and regular check-ups, All-on-4 dental implants can remain functional for decades.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants All-on-4 dental implants offer many advantages to patients over traditional removable dentures, including:

  • Immediate function: Rather than wait several months for the implants to fuse with the jawbone, patients can leave All-on-4 dental implant surgery with a fully functioning set of prosthetic teeth.
  • Aesthetics: Every denture is customized to look and feel as natural as possible.
  • Improved stability: The denture prosthesis is fixed, offering stability and eliminating the risk of problems such as clicking, falling out, and slipping that traditional denture wearers often deal with.
  • Lowered risk of bone loss: Because dental implants stimulate surrounding bone, they reduce the likelihood of both the bone loss related to missing teeth and the associated oral health problems.
  • Longevity: If you care for your implants appropriately, you can expect them to function anywhere from 10 to 20 years.
  • Dependability: When placed by a credentialed dental professional, All-on-4 dental implants have a very high rate of long-term success.
