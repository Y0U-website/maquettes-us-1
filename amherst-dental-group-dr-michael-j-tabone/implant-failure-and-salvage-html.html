How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If we catch the problem early enough, we may be able to address it without needing to remove the implants. This could be as simple as performing a deep cleaning and treating the infection. Or it could require more extensive work. However, in some situations, we may need to remove the implant entirely, treat the infection or cause of failure, and then possibly consider starting the implant process over again.
