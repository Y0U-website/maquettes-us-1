Our Hygienists

Jen

Jen has been a Dental Hygienist since 2009. Jen received her Dental Hygiene degree through Lake Superior College in Duluth, MN. Jen then went on to receive her Bachelors Degree in Dental Hygiene through Mankato State University. Jen joined the Winegar Dental team in November of 2016. Jen enjoys working with patients to develop or improve methods that will meet each individual’s oral health needs.

Jen grew up in the Brainerd Lakes Area and enjoys spending time with family and friends. She also has a love for the great outdoors.

Jill

Jill graduated from Mankato State University in Dental Hygiene. Much of Jill’s working life was spent in Grand Rapids. She is originally from Brainerd and in 2013, had the opportunity to move back to this town she loves. When she joined the Winegar Dental family, she was welcomed with open arms from staff and patients alike.

Jill loves hanging out with her high school sweetheart, turned husband in 2019. Jill’s hobbies include: hunting, fishing, gardening and everything Minnesota outdoors. She loves to sew, quilt and do stained glass. She is detailed oriented and enjoys working with her hands, which may be why dental hygiene suits her.

Abigail

Abigail became part of the Winegar Dental team in October of 2018, after graduating from the St. Cloud Technical and Community College Hygiene program. She then went on to further her education and received her Bachelors Degree in Dental Hygiene through Mankato State University. She enjoys the career she has chosen because of the relationships she has made with patients, and co-workers.

Abigail grew up in Browerville, MN and now lives in Pequot Lakes with her husband Benjamin, their son Ryder and their 3 dogs. When not working at the office, she enjoys hiking, traveling, working out and spending time with friends and family.
