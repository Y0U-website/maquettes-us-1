New Patient Visit

We are delighted to welcome you to our practice. During your first visit, we will review your medical history as well as any concerns you may have. We understand that great long-term relationships are built upon solid foundations and we will start building one here. Our goal is to provide you with great customer service and care. Please feel free to explore our website and let us know how we can assist you in any way. Below is a typical outline for a new patient initial visit:

  • Discuss medical history
  • Take digital x-rays as needed
  • Record blood pressure
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Tooth charting
  • Visual examination
  • TMJ exam
  • Professional cleaning
  • Create a treatment plan for any necessary and optional procedures
