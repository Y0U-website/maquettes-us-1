Common Questions About Family Dentistry

  • What is the main difference between a family dentist and a pediatric dentist? A pediatric dentist focuses exclusively on children. Our family dentist center is trained to treat all ages, from children through seniors, providing lifelong care.
  • When should my child first visit the dentist? We generally recommend the first visit by their first birthday, or within 6 months of their first tooth coming in. At this time, it is normal to expect a review of brushing and homecare habits, and an oral exam. The most important part is to get your child used to being in the dental chair to build a familiarity as they grow.
  • Do you offer same-day emergency dental care? Yes, we usually maintain openings in our schedule daily for emergencies.
