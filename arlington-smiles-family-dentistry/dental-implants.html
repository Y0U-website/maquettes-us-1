What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a permanent solution for a missing tooth. There are many reasons such as periodontal disease, tooth decay or injury, which can cause permanent loss of a tooth. Our team of expert dentists in Arlington helps you restore a natural looking, fully functional tooth and regain your confidence with a pleasant smile.
