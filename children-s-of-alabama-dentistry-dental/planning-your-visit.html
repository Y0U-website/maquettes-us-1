Planning Your Visit

We extend you a warm welcome. Children’s is a private, not-for-profit hospital that serves as the primary site of the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) pediatric medicine, surgery, research and residency programs. The partnership of internationally-recognized doctors and specialty care teams produces excellent care for every step of helping children heal — from well checkups to conferences with specialists.

Select one of the links below to take you to the appropriate registration page. There you can preregister as well as find helpful information you might need to know prior to your visit.
