The practice

At Big Sky Dentures & Implants, we help people every day who are frustrated with loose dentures, missing teeth, poor chewing ability, and the loss of confidence that comes with it. Our entire clinic is built around solving these problems with reliable, long-term solutions.

If your dentures don’t stay in place, your bite feels weak, or missing teeth are affecting how you eat and live, you are exactly who we treat. Adhesives, slipping plates, and constant adjustments shouldn’t be your “normal.”

At Big Sky Dentures & Implants , we focus exclusively on predictable, long-term solutions for patients who want to restore function, not just manage symptoms. Our clinic provides mini dental implants, implant-supported dentures, snap-in dentures, complete dentures, partials, and same-day repairs, all delivered in one location by a team that treats denture and implant challenges every day.

Mini dental implants allow many patients, even those with limited bone, to stabilize their dentures or replace missing teeth with less surgery, less cost, and faster recovery than traditional implants. These small, durable implants give you a secure bite, improved speech, and a smile that stays in place.

We serve Missoula and the surrounding communities with efficient, patient-focused care designed to get you eating, smiling, and living comfortably again.

If you’re ready for dentures that actually stay in place, or you want to replace missing teeth with secure, lasting implants, schedule your consultation today.

We accept several major dental insurance plans and handle the verification for you. Contact our office and our team will confirm your coverage, explain your benefits clearly, and outline any expected out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins.

We provide a complimentary benefits analysis so there are no surprises. We also accept Medicaid, ensuring high-quality dental care remains accessible and inclusive for all patients.
