What Is the Process for Same-Day Implant Dentures?

We’ve designed the same-day implant denture process at Big Sky Dentures & Implants to be as efficient and patient-friendly as possible. The process begins with a consultation and a 3D CBCT scan. This scan gives our team a detailed look at your jawbone and helps us plan the exact position of each implant.

For snap-in dentures, we’ll use the scan to design your custom denture. At your next visit, we will place the implants and fit your new snap-in denture! You’ll walk out with a secure, functional smile without a long wait.

For a Fix-on-Six roundhouse bridge , we place the implants and attach a temporary bridge the same day. This lets you heal with teeth in place while your final zirconia restoration is being fabricated. Once you’ve fully healed, we’ll swap the temporary for your permanent bridge.

Patients who want quicker results might choose snap-in dentures, but those who prefer the look of roundhouse bridges still benefit from a streamlined treatment!

How Mini Dental Implants Make Same-Day Implant Dentures Possible

Mini dental implants are the key to delivering stable, secure dentures in a single visit. Their slim, one-piece design allows us to place them with a faster, less invasive technique compared to traditional implants. This streamlined process means fewer steps, less discomfort, and a much quicker recovery.

To place a mini implant, we first create a small pilot hole through the gum tissue and into the bone. He’ll then gently position the implant into the bone, repeating the process wherever an implant is needed to support the denture. Since mini implants cause much less trauma to the mouth, no stitches or complex surgical steps are needed. This also means that healing is much faster, too.

And because mini implants exhibit excellent stability right after placement, we can attach a denture the same day. For snap-in dentures, this means you can walk in with missing or failing teeth and leave with a full set of secure, functional teeth. The procedure is efficient, comfortable, and designed to get you smiling again without the wait.
