Family Dentist Office in Bartlesville, OK

Dedicated Family Dental Care for Healthy, Beautiful Smiles

We opened in 2021 and are in Bartlesville, OK. We proudly serve Bartlesville and the surrounding areas. As proud Bartlesville Chamber of Commerce members, we’re deeply committed to supporting our local community while providing exceptional dental care.

Our practice offers a full range of dental services, including preventative, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry, to meet all your oral health needs. From routine cleanings to wisdom teeth removal , we deliver personalized care with your comfort as our priority. We’ve got you covered if you’re looking for a new dentist!

We feature wheelchair accessibility throughout our entrance, parking lot, and seating. Our family-friendly dental clinic offers a relaxed, fun atmosphere with amenities like TVs, a coffee bar, and blankets to ensure your visit is as enjoyable as possible. Plus, we proudly speak Spanish to serve our diverse community better.

At Bartlesville Family Dental, we take pride in knowing all our patients by name. Let us help you achieve your dental goals with personalized, family-oriented dental care.
