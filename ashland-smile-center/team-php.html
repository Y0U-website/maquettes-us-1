We are committed to providing you and your family with the highest quality dentistry possible. Starting with a friendly smile when you enter, our staff will guide you to the patient waiting area where you can relax before you are called back.

MEET OUR DENTIST

Dr. Javauny K. Hyde

Dr. Javauny K. Hyde is a dedicated and compassionate dentist with a commitment to exceptional care and a patient-centered approach. A proud graduate of Howard University College of Dentistry, Dr. Hyde furthered his training through the General Practice Residency at Louisiana State University, where he honed his skills in advanced dental procedures.

As a Fellow of the International Dental Implant Association, Dr. Hyde is passionate about the latest advancements in dental technology and implantology. He believes in individualized, holistic care and ensures every patient receives a personalized experience tailored to their unique needs.

Originally from Kingston, Jamaica, Dr. Hyde brings a diverse cultural perspective to his practice and strives to create a welcoming environment for patients from all backgrounds. With a genuine interest in all aspects of dentistry, he stays current with emerging trends to provide each patient with the best possible care.

SPECIAL DENTISTRY

We’re thrilled to offer advanced procedures from our expert oral surgeon

We perform many oral and maxillofacial surgery procedures at our Ashland office. Our doctors have extensive training in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of oral health conditions. Our Oral Surgeon is board certified in his specialty and is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is highly skilled in implants, wisdom teeth removal, full arch rehabilitation, bone grafting, sinus augmentations, impacted canines and facial trauma.
