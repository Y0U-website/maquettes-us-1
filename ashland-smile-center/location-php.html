Our Services

We offer a wide range of services to meet our patients’ needs. Click on the services below to learn more and contact us to schedule your appointment today.

  • Digital Dentistry Through digital scanning and state-of-the-art x-ray technology Ashland Smile Center offers an improved patient experience.
  • Dental Implants Implants can offer a long-lasting and comfortable solution for a natural looking and beautiful smile.
  • Endodontics Our dentists can remove the infected pulp around a tooth and the nerve if necessary to prevent future infection.
  • Oral Surgery We offer wisdom teeth removal, TMJ treatment, sinus augmentation, and more from our highly trained oral surgeons.
  • Emergency Dentistry When dental emergencies occur, give our office a call and we will see you as soon as possible.
  • Laser Technology We use laser technology for soft tissue surgery because it helps to decrease swelling and discomfort as well as improve recovery time.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Bring your entire family to Ashland Smile Center! We offer services for every age. It is important to start healthy oral habits at a young age.
  • Invisalign Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that gently corrects your teeth in an effective and easy way.
  • Teeth Whitening Get the bright white smile you are looking for with our teeth whitening options. We offer in-office whitening and at-home whitening services.
  • Dentures and Partials Restorative dentistry includes replacing teeth when necessary to improve your smile and preserve your other teeth.
  • Dental Veneers Veneers are custom made coverings for the front of your teeth that can correct chipped, cracked, decayed, or crooked teeth.
  • Tongue and Lip-tie Treatment Dr. Butterworth utilizes laser technology to perform tongue and lip-tie releases (also known as a frenectomy) in children and adults.
