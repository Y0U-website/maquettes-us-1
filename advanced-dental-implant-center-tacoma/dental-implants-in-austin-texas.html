Zygomatic Dental Implants

Zygomatic dental implants are an innovative solution for patients with significant bone loss who cannot undergo conventional implant procedures. These implants are anchored into the zygomatic (cheek) bone, offering a solid foundation for upper arch dental prosthetics. This technique bypasses the need for complex bone grafting and sinus lift procedures, streamlining the restoration process. As a result, zygomatic implants offer a faster, more reliable option for restoring functionality and aesthetics to even the most challenging dental cases.
