Flexible Financing for Your Dental Care

Everyone&#x27;s financial situation is different, which is why we provide multiple payment options designed to make dental treatment easier to manage. During your visit, our knowledgeable team will explain your options, answer your questions, and help you choose the solution that works best for your needs.

Why Patients Choose Us

Cherry offers a simple way to spread the cost of dental treatment into manageable monthly payments. With a quick application process and fast approval decisions, many patients can begin treatment without paying the full amount upfront.

CareCredit is one of the most widely accepted healthcare financing programs, helping patients pay for dental care over time. Qualified applicants may have access to promotional financing options that make treatment more affordable.

For qualifying treatments, we also offer in-house payment plans designed to provide additional flexibility. Our goal is to help you receive recommended dental care while keeping payments manageable.

If you have dental insurance, we&#x27;ll help you maximize your benefits whenever possible. Our administrative team will verify your coverage, explain your estimated out-of-pocket costs, and submit claims on your behalf to simplify the process.

Even if you don&#x27;t have dental insurance, our financing and payment plan options can help make quality dental care more affordable.
