About – Aesthetic Family Dental

Dental Excellence Since 1994

Aesthetic Family Dental is a trusted, family-owned dental practice proudly serving the Gaithersburg, Maryland community for over three decades. Founded in 1994 by Dr. Imtiaz Noor, and joined in 2021 by his son, Dr. Haseeb Noor, our practice is built on a legacy of compassionate care, clinical excellence, and long-term patient relationships.
