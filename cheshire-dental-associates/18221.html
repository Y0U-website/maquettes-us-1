Are dental x-rays safe?

Absolutely. Dental x-rays emit low rates of radiation, especially when compared to other types of medical radiography. Additionally, at Cheshire Dental Associates, we take it a step further by using digital x-rays and digital photography to get the information we need. This further reduces the amount of radiation you are exposed to while allowing for excellent diagnostic capability. We also use lead aprons and other shielding techniques to make sure that you are exposed to the absolute minimum amount of radiation necessary.

I Brush and Floss Every Day. Do I Still Need a Dental Cleaning?

We hear this question a lot. While we’re thrilled to hear that you are doing a great job keeping up with your home-care routine, it’s still important to get your regular cleaning and comprehensive exam.

For one thing, keep in mind that when you brush and floss, you’re really only spending a few minutes, and you’re using tools that are pretty effective but still fairly limited.

Your teeth are full of tiny nooks and crannies where bacteria can hide and plaque can build. Over time, this build-up can cause some real damage, even if your teeth look clean and healthy.

When you get a cleaning here at our Cheshire dental office , your hygienist will spend up to an hour carefully going over your teeth with specially designed instruments, removing every last trace of plaque and tartar from your teeth. Tartar is plaque that has hardened, and it can’t be removed with a toothbrush or dental floss. When tartar builds up beneath the gumline, the stage is set for periodontal disease, which is another important reason to keep up with your cleanings.

One way to think of this is the difference between vacuuming your living room rug and giving it a good cleaning with a rug shampooer. Regular vacuuming is important; it keeps mess to a minimum and keeps your carpet (and home) looking nice. But dust and dirt can become lodged in between the fibers, and you don’t even realize it until you give your rug a shampooing and see how much dirt is in the water you are sucking back up. Yuck!
