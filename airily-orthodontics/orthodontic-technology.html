Frequently Asked Questions

Why is orthodontic technology important?

Modern technology allows us to diagnose more accurately, plan more effectively, and deliver better results. It also reduces discomfort, speeds up appointments, and improves overall patient experience.

Are digital X-rays safer than traditional ones?

Yes. Digital X-rays use significantly less radiation and provide clearer images instantly, allowing for faster diagnosis and safer care.

Do I need to prepare for a CT scan or digital X-ray?

No special preparation is needed. These scans are quick, non-invasive, and typically completed right in the treatment chair during your visit.

Can kids benefit from these technologies too?

Absolutely. Early imaging helps us catch developmental issues before they progress. Tools like Cone Beam CT and panoramic X-rays allow us to identify early signs of crowding, jaw growth irregularities, and bite alignment problems.

Does this technology add to the cost of treatment?

Our diagnostic tools are included in your overall care plan. They help us work more efficiently, reducing the need for extended treatment or unexpected corrections—saving time and cost in the long run.
