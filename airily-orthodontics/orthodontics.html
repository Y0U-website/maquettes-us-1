Our Growth-Focused Approach

We focus on correcting the root cause of misalignment using a non-extraction, non-retractive philosophy whenever possible. By guiding jaw development and preserving natural structure, we help patients achieve stable, functional results.

Orthodontic Treatment Options at Airily Orthodontics

We offer customized treatment options based on your needs, age, and goals.

Early Orthodontic Treatment

Early care helps guide jaw growth and prevent more complex issues later, especially in children with crowding, bite concerns, or airway issues.

Braces

Braces provide precise, effective correction for mild to complex alignment and bite problems.

Invisalign

Invisalign offers a discreet, removable option for teens and adults seeking flexibility.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are hidden behind the teeth for a less visible treatment option.

Maxillary Skeletal Expansion

A maxillary skeletal expander can widen the upper jaw and improve airway function, especially in teens and adults.

Tissue-Supported TADs Expander

A tissue-supported TADs expander helps create space and support proper jaw development.

TMJ and TMD Treatment

TMJ/TMD treatment addresses jaw pain, bite issues, and functional concerns related to jaw alignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between clear aligners and Invisalign?

Invisalign is a specific, highly customizable brand of clear aligners. It offers more control and tracking capabilities, making it suitable for a wider range of cases.

Do adults need different treatment options?

Adults may have more mature bone structure and lifestyle needs. We offer discreet braces, aligners, and even non-surgical palatal expansion to meet those needs comfortably and effectively.

Can orthodontic treatment really help with breathing or sleep issues?

Yes. By expanding the jaws, improving tongue posture, and creating more room for nasal airflow, orthodontic treatment can help reduce snoring, mouth breathing, and even sleep apnea in some cases.
