The Invisalign Treatment Process

  • Consultation & Digital Scan We begin with a full exam and 3D digital scan of your teeth and jaw. This allows Dr. Demergis to assess your case and determine if Invisalign is right for you.
  • Custom Treatment Plan Your digital scan is used to create a precise treatment map, showing how your teeth will shift with each set of aligners.
  • Wearing Your Aligners Aligners are worn 20–22 hours a day and replaced every one to two weeks. Each new set brings you closer to your final results.
  • Progress Monitoring You’ll visit our office for regular check-ins to ensure your treatment is on track. We’ll make any necessary adjustments to optimize your progress.
  • Final Results & Retention Once your treatment is complete, we’ll fit you with a retainer to preserve your new smile and prevent any unwanted movement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Invisalign different from regular clear aligners?

Invisalign uses proprietary SmartTrack material and advanced digital mapping, offering greater control and precision compared to generic aligner systems. It’s particularly effective for more complex movements.

Am I a candidate for Invisalign?

Most teens and adults with mild to moderate alignment issues are good candidates. During your consultation, we’ll assess your bite and determine the best course of treatment.

How long does Invisalign take?

Treatment time varies based on your case but typically ranges from 6 to 18 months. You&#x27;ll receive a clear estimate during your consultation.

Do I need to avoid certain foods?

No. Since Invisalign trays are removable, there are no food restrictions. Just remember to take your aligners out while eating and brush before putting them back in.

Is Invisalign covered by insurance?

Many dental insurance plans offer coverage for Invisalign. Our team will verify your benefits and help you navigate your options. Visit our Patient Info page for a list of accepted insurance providers.
