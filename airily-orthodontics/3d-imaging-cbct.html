Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 3D CT scan safe?

Yes. We use low-radiation technology that’s safe for both children and adults when clinically necessary.

Do I need to prepare for the scan?

No preparation is needed. You’ll simply stand or sit still for a few seconds while the scan completes.

How is this different from a standard dental X-ray?

Unlike 2D dental X-rays, 3D CT scans offer a complete view of bone, teeth, and joints—helping us see depth and angles with greater precision.

Can I get a copy of my scan?

Yes. We can provide digital copies for your records or to share with other healthcare providers if needed.

Does insurance cover 3D scans?

It depends on your plan. Many orthodontic diagnostics are covered—our team will help you check your benefits. Visit our Patient Info page to learn more.
