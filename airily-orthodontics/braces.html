Our Braces Treatment Process

Every braces case at Airily Orthodontics begins with a comprehensive consultation. Here&#x27;s what you can expect:

  • Orthodontic Evaluation We begin with a clinical exam and digital imaging using our in-house and Intraoral Scanner to assess your dental and skeletal structures.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan Dr. Demergis creates a detailed, step-by-step treatment strategy based on your age, condition, and overall goals.
  • Bracket Placement and Adjustments Brackets are placed on the teeth and connected with archwires to apply gentle, consistent pressure. You’ll return for periodic adjustments to keep progress moving steadily.
  • Monitoring Growth and Function We regularly monitor facial and jaw development, especially in younger patients, to make sure your treatment is supporting overall oral health and airway wellness.
  • Retention Phase Once active treatment is complete, we’ll fit you for a retainer to maintain your new smile and protect your results.

Braces for Children and Teens

Early orthodontic treatment with braces can guide proper jaw development and prevent more serious complications later. Dr. Demergis specializes in evaluating the airway and facial structure, allowing us to intervene at the right time—when growth can still be directed. Braces are an essential part of many of our airway dentistry strategies for children and teens.

Braces for Adults

It’s never too late to benefit from orthodontic treatment. Adult patients often choose braces to correct long-standing bite issues or crowding that has shifted over time. With today&#x27;s technology, adult braces are more comfortable, efficient, and discreet than ever.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will I need to wear braces?

Treatment times vary based on the complexity of your case. Most patients wear braces for 18 to 24 months, though some may require shorter or longer treatment depending on their individual needs.

Are braces painful?

You may experience mild discomfort or pressure after adjustments, but it’s temporary. We take steps to ensure every phase of your treatment is as comfortable as possible.

Can I eat normally with braces?

You’ll need to avoid very hard or sticky foods, but you’ll still be able to enjoy most meals with minor modifications. Our team will provide a full list of dietary tips to help you adjust.

Will I need to wear a retainer after braces?

Yes, retainers help keep your teeth in their new positions and prevent relapse. You’ll be fitted for a retainer once your braces come off.

Do you accept insurance for braces?

Yes. We work with a wide range of insurance providers and can help you review your benefits. Visit our Patient Info page for a full list of accepted plans.
