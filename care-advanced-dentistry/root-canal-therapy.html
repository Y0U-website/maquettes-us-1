Root Canal Therapy for Milford, CT & Beyond Endodontic Treatment (Root Canal Treatment)

A root canal is a safe, effective procedure that can relieve pain, eliminate infection, and save your natural tooth. Most root canal treatments can be completed in just one or two comfortable visits with little to no discomfort, allowing you to restore your oral health and protect your smile without the need for an extraction.

During a root canal at Care Advanced Dentistry, we carefully remove the infected nerve and pulp, clean and disinfect the inside of the tooth, and seal it to prevent future infection. Left untreated, an infected tooth can lead to severe pain, abscesses, and additional dental complications. About Root Canal Therapy

When a tooth’s nerve tissue or pulp is damaged, it breaks down and bacteria begin to multiply within the pulp chamber. The bacteria and other decayed debris can cause an infection or abscessed tooth. An abscess is a pus-filled pocket that forms at the end of the roots of the tooth. An abscess occurs when the infection spreads all the way past the ends of the roots of the tooth. What Are the Signs That a Root Canal Is Needed?

Sometimes, no symptoms are present; however, signs you may need a root canal include:

  • Swelling that may spread to other areas of the face, neck, or head.
  • Bone loss around the tip of the root.
  • Drainage problems extending outward from the root. A hole can occur through the side of the tooth with drainage into the gums or through the cheek with drainage into the skin.
  • A tooth’s nerve and pulp can become irritated, inflamed, and infected due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures on a tooth, and/or large fillings, a crack or chip in the tooth, or trauma to the face.
  • Discoloration (a darkening) of the tooth
  • Swelling and tenderness in the nearby gums
  • Prolonged sensitivity/pain to heat or cold temperatures (after the hot or cold has been removed)
  • A persistent or recurring pimple on the gums.
  • Severe tooth pain.
