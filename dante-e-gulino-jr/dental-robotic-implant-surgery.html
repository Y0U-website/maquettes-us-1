Dental Robotic Implant Surgery in Groton, CT, and Westerly, RI

Advancements in dental technology have transformed the way oral care is delivered, with robotics playing a significant role in improving precision, efficiency, and patient outcomes. Among the most innovative developments in the field is dental robotic implant surgery, which enhances the accuracy of dental implant placements while reducing risks and recovery times.

Understanding Dental Robotics

Robotic systems in oral surgery have been developed to enhance the skills of oral surgeons rather than replace them. These systems work by offering computer-guided precision, real-time feedback, and automated assistance to ensure optimal implant placement. The use of artificial intelligence (AI) and haptic feedback allows for improved treatment planning and execution, leading to more predictable results.

One of the most recognized robotic-assisted dental systems is Yomi® by Neocis, an FDA-approved robot that assists Dante Gulino in performing minimally invasive and highly precise implant surgeries. The system allows for dynamic guidance, where our surgeon remains in control while the robotic arm ensures accuracy and stability.

How Robotic Implant Surgery Works

Robotic-assisted implant surgery involves several key steps:

Pre-Surgical Planning

Before the procedure, advanced 3D imaging techniques such as cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans are used to capture detailed images of the patient’s jawbone. These scans allow the robotic system to create a digital surgical plan, mapping out the optimal implant placement based on the patient’s bone structure and dental needs.

Intraoperative Guidance

During the surgery , the robotic system provides real-time guidance to the surgeon, ensuring that the implant is placed at the exact pre-planned location, angle, and depth. The robotic arm assists in maintaining steady hand movements, preventing deviations from the planned trajectory.

Enhanced Precision and Accuracy

Robotic guidance allows for micromillimeter precision, reducing the risk of errors, nerve damage, or improper implant placement. The system also accounts for any minor adjustments needed during surgery, ensuring a seamless process.

Minimally Invasive Approach

Unlike traditional implant surgeries that may require larger incisions, robotic-assisted techniques enable a minimally invasive approach, reducing trauma to the surrounding tissues. This results in less bleeding, swelling, and post-operative discomfort, leading to a faster healing process.

Post-Surgical Monitoring

Following the surgery, the robotic system helps track the patient’s progress and allows for digital follow-up assessments. This ensures that the implant integrates properly with the jawbone and that no complications arise.

The Benefits of Robotic Implant Surgery

Unmatched Precision

One of the primary advantages of using robotics in implant surgery is the level of precision it offers. The system ensures that each implant is placed with pinpoint accuracy, reducing the likelihood of misalignment, implant failure, or post-surgical complications.

Minimized Surgical Risks

Since robotic guidance eliminates human error, the risk of damaging surrounding nerves, blood vessels, or sinuses is significantly reduced. Patients can undergo implant surgery with greater confidence and minimal risks.

Faster Healing and Reduced Discomfort

With robotic-assisted techniques requiring smaller incisions, patients experience less pain, swelling, and discomfort compared to traditional implant surgery. This also translates to quicker recovery times, allowing patients to return to normal activities sooner.

Shorter Surgery Time

Robotic systems help optimize workflow, reducing the overall surgery duration. Since the implant placement is pre-planned and guided in real time, procedures are more efficient and predictable.

Greater Implant Longevity

Proper placement of a dental implant is critical for its long-term success. By ensuring optimal positioning and angulation, robotic-assisted surgery enhances the longevity and durability of the implant, reducing the likelihood of future complications.

Ideal for Complex Cases

Patients with limited bone density, challenging anatomical structures, or previous implant failures can greatly benefit from robotic-guided surgery. The system allows for precise navigation around complex dental structures, increasing the success rate for difficult cases.Contact us to learn more.

Robotic Implant Surgery vs. Traditional Implant Surgery: A Comprehensive Comparison

Precision and Accuracy

Advantage: Robotic-assisted surgery significantly reduces human error and ensures optimal angulation and depth of the implant.

  • Robotic Implant Surgery: Utilizes AI-driven guidance and haptic feedback to ensure precise implant placement with micromillimeter accuracy.
  • Traditional Implant Surgery: Relies on the surgeon’s manual dexterity and experience, which can sometimes lead to slight variations in implant positioning.

Surgical Invasiveness and Trauma

Advantage: The minimally invasive approach of robotics results in less bleeding, swelling, and pain after surgery.

  • Robotic Implant Surgery: Minimally invasive due to smaller incisions, leading to less trauma to surrounding tissues.
  • Traditional Implant Surgery: Often requires larger incisions and more tissue manipulation, increasing post-surgical discomfort.

Risk of Surgical Complications

  • Robotic Implant Surgery: Reduces risks of nerve damage, sinus perforation, and implant misalignment by providing real-time guidance during surgery.
  • Traditional Implant Surgery: Since it is manually performed, there is a higher risk of minor deviations that could lead to complications like implant failure or nerve injury.

Efficiency and Surgery Duration

Advantage: Robotic implant surgery is typically faster, making it ideal for patients who want a quicker, more efficient procedure.

  • Robotic Implant Surgery: Pre-planned digitally, leading to a streamlined surgical process with fewer intraoperative adjustments.
  • Traditional Implant Surgery: Requires manual measurements and intraoperative decision-making, which may extend the procedure’s duration.

Healing Time and Recovery

  • Robotic Implant Surgery: Patients experience faster healing due to smaller incisions and reduced trauma. Most can resume normal activities within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Traditional Implant Surgery: Longer recovery due to larger incisions and potential for swelling or bruising.

Who Is a Candidate for Robotic-Assisted Implant Surgery?

Most patients who require dental implants can benefit from robotic-assisted surgery. However, ideal candidates include:

  • Patients needing single, multiple, or full-arch implants
  • Those with sufficient bone density to support implants
  • Patients with previous implant failures due to misalignment
  • Individuals looking for a minimally invasive procedure with reduced recovery time
  • Patients with dental anxiety, as robotic-guided surgery offers a more predictable and controlled experience

What to Expect During and After the Procedure

During the Procedure

Dr. Dante Gulino will administer local anesthesia or sedation to ensure patient comfort. The robotic system will guide the implant placement while allowing the surgeon to remain in full control. The procedure is typically completed within an hour, depending on the number of implants needed.

After the Procedure

Patients may experience mild swelling and discomfort, which subsides within a few days. Post-operative care includes prescribed pain medications, a soft-food diet, and proper oral hygiene practices. Most patients return to normal activities within 24 to 48 hours. Follow-up visits ensure proper healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone.
