Advancements in dental technology have transformed the way oral care is delivered, with robotics playing a significant role in improving precision, efficiency, and patient outcomes. Among the most innovative developments in the field is dental robotic implant surgery, which enhances the accuracy of dental implant placements while reducing risks and recovery times.

At Dante E Gulino Jr PC in Groton, CT, and Westerly, RI, we integrate robotic-assisted implant surgery to ensure that patients receive cutting-edge treatment with greater precision than traditional techniques.

Robotic systems in oral surgery have been developed to enhance the skills of oral surgeons rather than replace them. These systems work by offering computer-guided precision, real-time feedback, and automated assistance to ensure optimal implant placement. The use of artificial intelligence (AI) and haptic feedback allows for improved treatment planning and execution, leading to more predictable…

One of the most recognized robotic-assisted dental systems is Yomi® by Neocis, an FDA-approved robot that assists Dante Gulino in performing minimally invasive and highly precise implant surgeries. The system allows for dynamic guidance, where our surgeon remains in control while the robotic arm ensures accuracy and stability.

Before the procedure, advanced 3D imaging techniques such as cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans are used to capture detailed images of the patient’s jawbone. These scans allow the robotic system to create a digital surgical plan, mapping out the optimal implant placement based on the patient’s bone structure and dental needs.
