What Sets Our Black River Falls Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. Meet Your Dentist

Skilled and Compassionate Professional Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Elizabeth Herzberg

Background

Dr. Herzberg is proud to call the Black River Falls area “home” for her entire life. Born and raised here, she was fortunate to return to Black River Falls, to begin her dental career, following dental school at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. For over 20 years, she has been providing for the oral health needs of the community and plans to continue to do so for many more. She is honored and privileged to treat her patients, a responsibility she does not take lightly.

Dental Style

Dr. Herzberg believes in treating patients the way she would like her family to be treated.

Fun Facts

Outside of work, Dr. Herzberg and her husband have three children that they are very proud of. They have been and continue to be a part of the area, participating on multiple sports teams and in other community events. They live on their family farm and enjoy all things country, especially hunting.
