Meet your Team

Debbie

Debbie has been a dedicated dental hygienist for many years and truly loves what she does. She is gentle, caring, and enjoys helping patients with their home care so they can keep their smiles healthy between visits. Outside the office, she loves spending time with her two dogs and her family, and she has a strong interest in holistic approaches to health and wellness.

That same patience and care she puts into her garden shines through in the way she looks after her patients’ oral health.

Victoria
