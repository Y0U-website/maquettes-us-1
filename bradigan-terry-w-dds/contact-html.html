Driving Directions

From Interstate 89 - Northbound

From North bound get off exit 18. Take a right off of exit on to Route 120. Continue on Route 120 after 4th set of lights. We will be on the left-hand side as you start down the hill. There will be a sign the says Hanover Road Professional Center. Turn left into business complex. We are the last building on the left.

From Interstate 89 - Southbound

From South bound get off exit 18. Take a left off of exit on to Route 120. Continue on Route 120 after 4th set of lights. We will be on left hand side as you start down the hill. There will be a sign that says Hanover Road Professional Center. Turn left into business complex. We are the last building on the left.

From Hanover, NH

Coming out of the town of Hanover, head on Route 120 towards Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center and Lebanon. Continue on 120 through 2 sets of lights. After second set of lights, take the first right (Not at the stop light, after the stoplight). Directly across the road from the 2nd entrance to Jesses Restaurant. Look for a sign that says Hanover Road Professional Center. We are the last building on your left.
