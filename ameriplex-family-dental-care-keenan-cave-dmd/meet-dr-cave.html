Meet Dr. Cave

General, Cosmetic, and Restorative Dentistry

You’re In Good Hands

Keenan Cave, DMD welcomes you to AmeriPlex Family Dental Care and is looking forward to helping you with all of your oral health needs.

Dr. Cave has been around dentistry his entire life. His father Stephen Cave, DDS has owned and operated a private practice in Southern Indiana for 40 years. His older sister Ashlee Wheat, DDS also practices dentistry. Dr. Cave began his career path in construction management before realizing he had a passion for dentistry as well.

He received his master’s degree from Indiana University–Purdue University Indianapolis. He then went on to get his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine Illinois . Since then, he has been practicing dentistry in central Indiana.

Dr. Cave is Invisalign® certified and can provide orthodontic treatment using clear, removable Invisalign trays.

His interests include guitars (playing, buying and selling), dogs and other animals, home repair, and, of course, his family and raising his son.
