The practice

Dr. Edalatpour’s clinic provides a fun environment for young children and is designed to provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience for her young patients as they learn about the importance of dental care. Dr. B’s clinic provides dental care for children from all different countries as well as those with special needs.

Dr. Edalatpour currently serves as Clinical Associate of Pediatric Dentistry at Boston University

Dr. Edalatpour has volunteered her time to numerous community and international projects. In 2002 and 2017 she traveled to the Dominican Republic where she volunteered her services to hundreds of patients. She also traveled to Haiti in 2017 to volunteer with Dentists for Humanity. Along with 30 others dental professionals, Children’s Dental Care has participated in the Boston Jimmy Fund Walk.…

She served as a Chair for the East Middlesex district of MDS until June of 2014. She also served as a Guest Board Member of Massachusetts Dental Society offering input on a wide range of issues relating to MDS and the dental profession. She is an active member of other professional organizations, such as American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Massachusetts Academy of Pediatric Dentistry…

Dr. Edalatpour speaks fluent Turkish, Persian, and English and is also learning Portuguese. She is an avid traveler, having visited more than 30 different countries, including Brazil. Traveling is her favorite hobby along with yoga, martial arts and table tennis.
