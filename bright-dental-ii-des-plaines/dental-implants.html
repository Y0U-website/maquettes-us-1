Full-Arch Restoration

(All-on-4 / All-on-6)

  • Fixed, non-removable full arch of teeth
  • Often completed with a temporary set same-day
  • Ideal for patients with extensive tooth loss or failing dentition

Implant-Retained Dentures

(Overdentures)

  • More affordable than fixed full-arch options
  • Snaps in/out for easy cleaning
  • Eliminates slipping and adhesive creams

Mini Dental Implants

  • Less invasive placement procedure
  • Faster healing time
  • Often stabilizes lower dentures

Consultation & 3D Imaging

Comprehensive exam, CBCT scan, and digital treatment plan mapped to your bone structure and bite.

Implant Placement

Implant post is surgically placed under anesthesia/sedation. Most patients return to normal activity within 24–48 hours.

Osseointegration (Healing)

Over 2–4 months, the implant fuses with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation.

Abutment & Restoration

Once healed, a custom abutment and crown, bridge, or denture is attached — completing your smile.

Long-Term Maintenance

Routine checkups and cleanings keep your implant functioning like a natural tooth for 20+ years.
