Why Choose Metal Braces?

At Bright Dental, we ensure that your metal braces are customized to fit your smile. Patients can even personalize their braces with colored bands, making orthodontic treatment a fun experience—especially for kids and teens.

For those who prefer a virtually invisible orthodontic solution , Invisalign and other clear aligner systems provide a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. Using a series of custom-made, removable aligners, Invisalign gradually moves your teeth into alignment—without wires, brackets, or dietary restrictions.

  • Strong and durable for complex alignment issues
  • Cost-effective compared to newer technologies
  • Continuous advancements make them smaller and more comfortable than ever before
  • Ideal for patients with severe misalignments or bite issues

Advantages of Invisalign:

Bright Dental is a certified Invisalign provider. We use advanced digital scans and treatment planning tools to design your aligners with precision. The aligners are custom-fitted, ensuring a comfortable fit and optimal movement for each stage of treatment.

After your orthodontic treatment is complete, retainers play a critical role in preserving your results. Teeth naturally shift over time, and retainers help ensure your newly aligned smile stays in place.

  • Virtually invisible —perfect for working professionals or self-conscious teens
  • Removable aligners —eat, drink, brush, and floss with ease
  • Fewer office visits —typically requires less frequent adjustments
  • Digital planning —see your expected results before you begin

Types of Retainers We Offer:

Removable Retainers:

  • Hawley Retainers – a combination of metal wires and acrylic, durable and adjustable
  • Clear Retainers – transparent and snug-fitting, similar in appearance to clear aligners
  • Easy to clean and wear
  • Ideal for nighttime use

Fixed (Permanent) Retainers:

At Bright Dental, we evaluate your specific case and lifestyle to recommend the best type of retainer. We also provide guidance on how to clean, maintain, and monitor your retainer to ensure your results last.

  • A thin, custom wire bonded to the back of your teeth
  • Provides constant support without the need for daily wear
  • Especially useful for lower front teeth, which are more prone to shifting
