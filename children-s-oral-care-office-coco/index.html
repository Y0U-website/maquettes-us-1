The practice

Routine Dental Exams and Cleanings at our office take care of your child’s regular dental needs and help them to have a plaque-free healthy smile for a given time.

The dental sealants are plastic coatings placed on the chewing surface of the back teeth so that the molars and premolars can stay protected from decay.

Fluoride treatment is one of the best ways to protect your child’s teeth from cavities. It is done when your child comes for the the regular dental cleaning session.

Dental crowns are tooth caps that are worn to strengthen the natural teeth and protect them from damage. It also helps to restore the shape of the teeth.

Tooth-colored fillings protect the teeth from the damage caused cavities and maintains their appearance. They are safe, effective, and match the natural colors of your kid’s teeth.
