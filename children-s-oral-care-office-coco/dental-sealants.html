Who is eligible for dental sealants?

COCO offers high-quality dental sealants to children, as they are more prone to cavities. It is a preventive technique that protects your back teeth and saves them from cavities. Dr. Castillo, our experienced pediatric dentist, will thoroughly check the condition of your child’s teeth and apply the sealant to ensure that the teeth remain fully protected.

Getting dental sealants in Nashville, TN

Dr. Castillo, a renowned pediatric dentist at COCO children’s oral care office, recommends dental sealants for every child. He says that it is one of the best ways to protect your child from cavities and tooth decay.
