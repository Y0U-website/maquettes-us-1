Dental Implant Surgery

Every oral surgery begins with a consultation. Your oral surgeon will carefully review your medical and dental history, conduct an oral evaluation, and discuss treatment options with you. 3D scans may be taken during this visit.

Depending on your specific case, immediate implants may be placed on the same day as the consultation, while other options may require a separate surgical appointment.

The surgical procedure begins with the administration of anesthesia. Once you are comfortable and the anesthesia has taken effect, your surgeon will skillfully place the dental implant post into the jaw bone. After the implants have been placed, the site will be sutured closed.

Over the next few weeks, the titanium implant post will gradually fuse with the jaw bone, creating a sturdy foundation to support the implant. After the healing period, your restorative dentist will place the custom-made crown.

To ensure a safe procedure and a smooth recovery, it is important to follow all the instructions provided by your surgeon. You can find general instructions on our website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I eat with dental implants?

After the healing period, you can enjoy eating a wide variety of foods, including steak, nuts, jerky, popcorn, and more.

Do dental implants hurt?

Some discomfort is expected following oral surgery. Anesthesia is used during the procedure to ensure your comfort. After surgery, we will provide detailed instructions for pain management. Fully healed dental implants do not cause any pain.

How long does the dental implant procedure take?

A typical surgery for one implant takes about one hour. However, the duration of the procedure may vary depending on the patient and treatment plan. A more accurate time estimate can be provided during your initial visit.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many patients are eligible for dental implants. However, they may not be recommended for individuals with certain medical conditions or severe bone loss or periodontal disease.
