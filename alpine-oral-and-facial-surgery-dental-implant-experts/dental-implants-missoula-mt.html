Dental Implants in Missoula, MT

  • Improved Aesthetics : Dental implants closely resemble natural teeth, providing a seamless and natural-looking smile. They are custom-designed to match the shape, size, and color of your existing teeth, ensuring a harmonious and aesthetically pleasing result.
  • Enhanced Functionality : Dental implants restore full chewing and biting capabilities, allowing you to enjoy a wide range of foods without any discomfort or restrictions. Unlike removable dentures, implants eliminate the worry of slippage or movement, providing a stable and secure bite.
  • Preservation of Jaw Bone : When a tooth is lost, the underlying jaw bone can begin to deteriorate over time. Dental implants act as artificial tooth roots, stimulating the jaw bone and preventing bone loss. This helps to maintain the natural shape of your face and prevent the sunken appearance that can occur with missing teeth.

Types of Dental Implants

At Alpine Oral & Facial Surgery, we offer a comprehensive range of treatments to address various tooth replacement needs:

  • Single Tooth Replacement : A single implant can be placed in the mouth with sufficient jaw bone support. In cases where there is insufficient bone, a bone graft may be recommended to ensure successful implant placement.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge : For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can be an ideal solution. A single implant can support multiple crowns, eliminating the need for adjacent teeth support.
  • Same-Day Implants : In certain cases, implants can be placed immediately after a tooth extraction, saving time and additional procedures. This option is suitable for patients with healthy jaw bone structure.
  • Full-Arch Restoration : For patients who have lost all or most of their teeth, a full-arch restoration can provide a complete smile transformation. This comprehensive treatment option involves restoring an entire upper or lower arch of teeth, providing a fully functional and aesthetically pleasing result.

The Dental Implant Surgery

Before undergoing any oral surgery, a thorough consultation with Dr. Holtzen is necessary to create a personalized treatment plan. Advanced CBCT scanning ensures accurate surgical guides and precise implant placement.

The surgical procedure involves placing the implant post into the jaw bone, which will fuse with the surrounding bone tissue over time. This process, known as osseointegration, provides a permanent and stable foundation for the replacement tooth or teeth.

Once healed, the custom-made crown is placed on top with an abutment to connect the implant to the crown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful?

Although some discomfort is to be expected with any surgical procedure, patients typically experience minimal pain or discomfort during and after dental implant placement. Local anesthesia and sedation options are available to ensure a comfortable experience. After the procedure, any discomfort can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups are essential to ensure the longevity of dental implants. It is important to follow good oral hygiene practices and avoid habits such as smoking, which can negatively impact the success and lifespan of dental implants.

Can dental implants replace any tooth in the mouth?

Yes, dental implants can replace any tooth in the mouth, whether it is a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even a full arch of teeth. Dental implants offer versatility and can be customized to meet individual tooth replacement needs. Whether you are missing one tooth or several, dental implants can provide a natural-looking and functional solution to restore your smile and oral health.
