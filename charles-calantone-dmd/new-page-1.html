0 What We Offer Cleanings/X-rays/Comprehensive Exams Professional dental cleanings remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains that brushing alone cannot reach. They help prevent gum disease, cavities, and bad breath, and keep your smile healthy. Routine dental X-rays and comprehensive exams help detect cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues early. These visits are essential for maintaining long-term dental health and creating personalized treatment plans.

Dental Fillings

Fillings are used to repair teeth damaged by cavities, cracks, or wear from grinding or acid erosion. They restore tooth structure, stop further decay, and help you bite and chew comfortably again.

Crowns

Crowns are needed when a tooth is badly decayed, cracked, worn down, or has had a root canal, and no longer has enough strength to function on its own. The tooth is prepared by trimming its structure to make space for a custom crown, which fits over it to restore strength, function, and appearance.

Bridge

A dental bridge is used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to the natural teeth or crowns on either side of the gap. It restores your ability to chew and speak properly, maintains your facial shape, and prevents surrounding teeth from shifting out of place.

Implant Crown/ Implant Supported Bridge

An implant crown is placed on top of a dental implant—a small titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth. This option is ideal for patients who have lost a tooth due to decay, injury, or gum disease, and it restores natural function and appearance with a long-term, stable solution.

Dentures (removable oral appliances)

Dentures are used to replace several or all missing teeth due to decay, gum disease, or injury. They help restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal is needed when the inside of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay, trauma, or cracks. The procedure involves removing the nerve tissue (pulp) that lives in the root canal inside the tooth, relieving pain, eliminating infection, and preserving your natural tooth. After root canal treatment, the tooth is no longer as strong as it once was and can be missing a significant amount of structure. A post and core is often needed to rebuild the tooth and provide enough support for a secure, long-lasting crown as the final restoration

Extractions

A dental extraction may be needed when a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, infected, or causing crowding that affects your oral health. Missing a tooth can impact chewing, alignment, and appearance, making it important to consider replacement options to maintain function and a healthy smile.

Veneers

Veneers are thin porcelain shells placed over the front of teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps, or slightly crooked teeth. They provide a long-lasting, natural-looking way to enhance your smile

Teeth whitening

Our at-home whitening kits offer a convenient and customizable way to brighten your smile with minimal sensitivity. By using professional-grade products, you can control the level of whitening, adjusting the treatment to suit your comfort and gradually achieving your desired results without the risk of irritation.

Night guard

A nightguard is recommended for patients who grind or clench their teeth during sleep, a condition known as bruxism, which can lead to tooth wear, jaw pain, headaches, and even cracked teeth. It can also help alleviate discomfort caused by temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. Over time, bruxism can result in serious dental issues, such as worn-down enamel and increased tooth sensitivity, making a nightguard an important preventive measure to protect your teeth in the long run.

Sleep apnea oral appliance

A sleep apnea appliance is designed for patients who have been diagnosed with mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea. These appliances help to keep the airway open by repositioning the jaw during sleep, improving airflow and reducing snoring. It is an effective alternative to CPAP therapy for those who prefer a more comfortable, portable solution.
