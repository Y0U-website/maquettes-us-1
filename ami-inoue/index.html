The American Academy of Orthodontics suggests children are seen at age 7 for an orthodontic consultation. Some of these children, who have crossbites or severe crowding for example, will need Phase 1 Orthodontic Treatment.

Traditional Braces (Phase 2) If your child does not need Phase 1 Orthodontic Treatment, most children start Traditional Orthodontic Treatment between the ages 11-14.

Clear braces offer a more discreet way to straighten teeth, using tooth-colored or transparent materials that blend naturally with your smile.

It’s never too late to get the smile you’ve always wanted. Sometimes retreatment or traditional treatment is necessary for adults whose teeth have shifted overtime. Adult orthodontic treatment can help improve your smile, bite, and overall oral health at any age.
