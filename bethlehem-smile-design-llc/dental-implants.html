Dental Implants

Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults who are in good general health and have sufficient bone density in the jaw are ideal candidates for dental implants. However, certain medical conditions or habits (like smoking) may impact your suitability. During your consultation, your dentist will assess your overall oral health and discuss any potential risks or limitations.

The cost of dental implants varies depending on factors like the type of implant, the number of implants needed, the location, and any additional procedures (such as bone grafting). While dental implants can be more expensive than dentures or bridges, they are a long-term investment in your oral health, offering durable and natural-looking results.

  • Dental Implants Dental implants are modern, long-lasting solutions for replacing missing or severely damaged teeth. They are titanium posts or frames that are surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gums. Once placed, these implants act as a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth, designed to match the natural teeth. Benefits of Dental Implants Improved Aesthetics: Dental implants look and feel like your own teeth. They are designed to match the natural color and contour of your teeth, providing a seamless look.
  • Enhanced Comfort: Since implants are permanently fixed in place, they become part of your mouth. This offers comfort and eliminates the pain or discomfort associated with removable dentures.
  • Better Oral Health: Unlike bridges, dental implants do not require adjacent teeth to be altered. This preserves the integrity of your surrounding teeth, contributing to better long-term oral health.
  • Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. They are highly durable, and the titanium used is resistant to decay.
  • Improved Speech and Function: Unlike ill-fitting dentures, dental implants stay in place, eliminating the risk of slurring or mumbling. They also improve your ability to chew and eat comfortably.
  • Boosted Confidence: Restoring missing teeth with implants helps enhance self-esteem, as they provide a natural, youthful appearance.
  • Consultation and Planning: Your dentist will first perform a thorough exam, which includes X-rays and potentially 3D imaging, to assess the health of your jawbone. If needed, a bone graft may be performed to ensure the jaw has enough bone density to support the implant.
  • Surgical Placement: The dental implant is placed into the jawbone through a minor surgical procedure. The implant is covered by gum tissue and left to heal for several months, allowing it to fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Abutment and Restoration: Once the implant has integrated with the bone, an abutment is attached to the implant. This serves as a connector for the artificial tooth. After the abutment is placed, your custom crown or bridge is fitted on top.
  • Final Adjustment: Once the restoration is in place, your dentist will make any necessary adjustments to ensure that your bite is correct and comfortable.
  • Endosteal Implants: These are the most common type of implants and are placed directly into the jawbone. They may consist of a screw, cylinder, or blade-shaped design.
  • Subperiosteal Implants: These are placed on or above the jawbone, typically for patients who don’t have enough healthy jawbone to support endosteal implants. They are less commonly used today due to advances in bone grafting techniques.
  • All-on-4 Implants: A popular choice for patients who are missing most or all of their teeth. Four dental implants are placed to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth, offering a quicker and more affordable solution compared to individual implants.

Everything you need to know

How often should I visit the dentist for a check-up?

It’s recommended to visit your dentist at least twice a year for regular check-ups and cleanings. This helps prevent dental issues and catch potential problems early.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

In case of a dental emergency, such as a knocked-out tooth, severe pain, or a broken tooth, contact our office immediately. We provide emergency care and will schedule you as soon as possible.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes, dental X-rays are safe and a critical part of your dental care. Modern technology uses minimal radiation, and we take precautions to protect your health.

What is the best way to prevent cavities?

Brushing your teeth twice a day, flossing daily, eating a balanced diet, and avoiding sugary foods are key ways to prevent cavities. Regular dental check-ups are also important for early detection.

Do you offer teeth whitening treatments?

Yes, we offer both in-office and take-home teeth whitening options. Schedule a consultation to learn more about the best whitening solution for your needs.
