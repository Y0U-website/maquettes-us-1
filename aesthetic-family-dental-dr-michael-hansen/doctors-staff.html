Dr. Jeffrey Donatelli

Dr. Donatelli graduated from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2019. Upon graduation he was inducted into the dental honor society Omicron Kappa Upsilon. Dr. Donatelli is originally from Monroeville, PA. He attended Gateway High School and graduated in 2011. After high school, Dr. Donatelli then went to Saint Vincent College where he played ice hockey, and graduated in 2015 with a B.S. degree and a major in biology.

After dental school, he went on and performed a one-year surgical internship in Oral and Maxillofacial surgery at St. Christopher’s Hospital for Children, where he further developed his clinical and procedural skills.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Donatelli loves to stay active. He also enjoys going to the gym, playing sports, biking, and spending time with fiancé, his nieces and nephew. He looks forward to providing excellent dental care and serving his community.

Dr. Kaylie Kozen

Dr. Kozen is a 2023 graduate from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. She is an inducted member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society. Following graduation, she completed a general practice residency through UPMC and the VA Medical Center to advance her training to provide the highest quality of care to her patients.

Dr. Kozen grew up in the South Hills of Pittsburgh and graduated with distinction from Penn State University in 2016. Prior to dental school, Dr. Kozen worked in early education and has years of experience working with children.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kozen enjoys spending time outdoors whether it be playing tennis, biking, or hiking. She enjoys throwing pottery, volunteering at her local animal shelter, and spending time with her family and friends.
