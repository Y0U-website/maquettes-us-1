The practice

Banana Smiles is dedicated to making your child’s visit to the dentist a smile-filled event! We provide every child with advanced pediatric dental solutions and keep them comfortable and smiling throughout their visit.

Whether your little one is here for an exam and cleaning, preventive care, or a filling, they will be treated with the greatest kindness and compassion: as we would our own child. First time visitors and returning patients will always receive a warm welcome and quickly feel like they re part of the banana Smiles family.

At our pediatric dental practice, we take a conservative approach and focus strongly on preventative care. We’ll let your child set the pace and tailor treatment to his/her individualized needs. Our kids’ dentist and upbeat support team strive to make visits relaxing, fun, and informative, in order to foster a strong foundation for lifelong oral health.

Dr. Fawaz was born and raised in Boca Raton, Florida and graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a B.S. in neuroscience. He then attended NYU’s College of Dentistry, where he received a DDS and a certificate in Special Patient Care. He completed both his General Practice Residency and his Pediatric Dental Residency program at Montefiore Medical Center in New York City.
