Doyoung Choi, DDS, MS

Dr. Choi is always excited to learn more and adopt new ideas to maintain and improve her clinical skills. The field of orthodontics sees new development every year, and she understands how crucial it is to keep up with all the available clinical information and technology.

Choi Orthodontics offers the newest and most efficient treatment options, including 3D scanning, mini implants, and aesthetic options, such as clear braces and aligners. The team is equipped to deliver the best possible orthodontic care to create happy smiles.

  • "When I first considered my career in orthodontics, I didn’t realize how truly amazing it is to change and affect my patients’ lives every day. I’ve seen so many patients become more confident with better self-esteem. Not everyone in this world has the privilege to work with so many wonderful people with positive attitudes and goals. That’s why I wake up in the morning looking forward to working with new lives every day." Outside the Office

Dr. Choi is originally from South Korea. She came to West Virginia to attend Wheeling Jesuit University for her undergraduate education. That s where she met her husband, Benedict, and they got married at the campus chapel after finishing their schooling.

Benedict is a general surgeon who works at Heritage Valley Health System in Beaver, Pennsylvania. They have three little dogs, Jax, Opie, and Coco.

She thrives on getting new experiences, going to new places, facing new challenges, and trying new dishes. She embraces the differences that make life more interesting. The main hobbies of hers are traveling and gardening.
