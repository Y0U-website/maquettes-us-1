April 2026 Tooth Fairy Event

We invited families in our community to come meet the Tooth Fairy, learn about oral hygiene, and have fun in a comfortable, kid-friendly environment. The goal of the event was to help children feel more confident at the dentist and ease any dental anxiety.

2025 Needham Baseball and Softball Opening Day

We are proud sponsors of Needham Baseball and Softball! We had a great time celebrating Opening Day and handing out dental goodies to players, families, and community members.

2025 YMCA Healthy Kids Day

YMCA Healthy Kids Day is a community event focused on promoting healthy habits and wellness for kids and families. We were excited to be part of an event that encourages children to build healthy routines from a young age.
