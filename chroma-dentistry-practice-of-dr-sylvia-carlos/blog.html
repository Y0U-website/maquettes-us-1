What to Do If You Have Dental Pain: Advice from Your Dentist

Dental pain can strike at the most unexpected times, and it’s not only uncomfortable—it can also be a sign that something more serious is going on. If you’re experiencing tooth pain, here are some steps you can take to get relief and protect your oral health until you can see a dentist:

1. Rinse with Warm Saltwater A saltwater rinse is a simple yet effective way to clean the affected area and reduce inflammation. Mix half a teaspoon of salt into a glass of warm water and swish it around your mouth for about 30 seconds before spitting it out.

2. Take Over-the-Counter Pain Relievers Medications like ibuprofen (Advil) or acetaminophen (Tylenol) can help manage pain and reduce swelling. Always follow the instructions on the package and consult your healthcare provider if you’re unsure which is best for you.

3. Apply a Cold Compress If your face is swollen, applying a cold compress to the outside of your cheek can help reduce swelling and numb the pain. Use a cold pack or wrap ice in a cloth and apply it for 15 minutes at a time.

4. Try Clove Oil Clove oil has natural pain-relieving properties. You can apply a small amount to the painful area using a cotton ball. This can provide temporary relief, but it’s not a substitute for professional care.

5. Watch What You Eat Avoid hard, crunchy, or very hot foods that can aggravate the pain. Stick to soft foods, and try to chew on the opposite side of your mouth to prevent further irritation.

6. Elevate Your Head While Resting If the pain is keeping you awake at night, try propping your head up with an extra pillow. This can reduce blood flow to the affected area and ease discomfort.

7. See a Dentist as Soon as Possible While these tips may help you manage the pain temporarily, dental pain is often a sign of an underlying issue that requires treatment. Whether it’s a cavity, infection, or other problem, it’s important to schedule an appointment so we can address the cause and get you back to feeling your best.
