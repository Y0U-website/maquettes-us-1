About

The Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Center, P.A.

Dr. Scott Thames established The Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Center, P.A., in Laurel upon completion of his training in 1984. Dr. Clay Thames joined his brother in practice by opening the Hattiesburg clinic in 1995. Following the retirement of Dr. Scott Thames, Dr. Darren Alexander joined the practice in 2018.

Dr. Clay Thames and Dr. Darren Alexander both now serve patients in the Hattiesburg and Laurel, MS, locations.
