Dental Implant Surgery

If you recently had a tooth extraction or have been missing teeth. This option is for you.Dental implant surgery offers a tooth replacement solution that is permanent. They can replace a single tooth or an entire dentition.

The first step is initial consultation and understanding the current condition of your dentition. 3D images will be taken to carefully examine the quality and quantity of bone surrounding your jaw bone. Once we determined that you are a candidate for a dental implant. The surgery will be scheduled typically under intravenous sedation.

We utilize high quality dental implant system called OsseoLink. This system is fully FDA approved and the best part is that it is proudly developed and made in the United States.

Unfortunately, dental implants are typically NOT covered by insurance companies. However, our office has very competitive pricing in the market and our goal is to make this option as much affordable as possible.

If you are looking for a permanent solution for your missing teeth, this is a great option for you. Please give us a call to schedule an appointment for initial consultation.
