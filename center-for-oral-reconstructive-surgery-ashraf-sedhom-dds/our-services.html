Orthognathic Surgery

Orthognathic surgery corrects misalignments of the jawbones (maxilla and mandible). While orthodontics aligns the teeth, this surgery moves the jawbones into the proper position for improved function and appearance. The procedure involves cutting and repositioning bone segments, often following a period of orthodontic treatment. After surgery, a short period of orthodontic treatment is usually needed to finalize the teeth alignment. Although risks exist, they are generally minor and easily managed. Positive thinking and family support throughout recovery can greatly enhance healing.

Jaw Pathology

Oral and maxillofacial pathology deals with the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the mouth, jaw, and surrounding areas. One of the most serious conditions is oral cancer, which can manifest in the jaw. If you notice any unusual changes in the appearance of the inside of your mouth, it’s important to seek professional evaluation.

Jaw Tumors

Jaw tumors, which can occur in either the upper jaw (maxilla) or lower jaw (mandible), may cause symptoms like persistent jaw pain or difficulty eating. Oral cancer is one of the most common causes of jaw tumors, and it’s crucial to identify and treat it early. If you experience any concerning symptoms, seek an evaluation as soon as possible.

Cyst Removal

A cyst is a pocket that forms in the body, often filled with air, fluid, or other substances. These can develop in the bones, organs, or tissues, including the jaw and mouth. Cysts often form when pores become clogged, trapping skin cells inside. While many cysts are benign, it’s important to have them evaluated and removed if necessary to prevent complications. We specialize in safely removing cysts to restore your oral health.

TMJ Therapy

TMJ disorders can cause pain and discomfort in the jaw, face, and neck. Our TMJ therapy focuses on relieving this pain through specialized treatments that target muscle tension and jaw misalignment. Whether you experience headaches, jaw clicking, or facial pain, we offer effective solutions to reduce discomfort and restore jaw function.
