The practice

Good oral health is vital for a child’s development. We take pride in creating and maintaining beautiful and healthy smiles for our younger patients in an environment that is lighthearted and fun.

Our dental office uses state-of-the-art equipment, and we strive to make your child’s visit with us as pain and anxiety free as possible. Here are a few of the reasons people travel from Central to Downeast Maine to see us.

We have video games, TV’s and Kid Friendly murals on the wall to make all feel welcome.

Parents are encouraged to accompany their children during appointments.
