The practice

At Columbia Oral & Facial Surgery Center, Dr. Kenneth Wu, DDS, and Dr. Mathew Woodward, DDS, provide expert oral and maxillofacial surgical care, focusing on your comfort and well-being. We specialize in dental implants, wisdom teeth extractions, and tooth extractions to restore oral health and enhance your smile.

Our goal is to deliver personalized, high-quality care using state-of-the-art technology, helping you achieve the results you deserve.
