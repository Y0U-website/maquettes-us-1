Dental implants are a transformative solution for replacing missing teeth, offering both functionality and natural aesthetics. This advanced procedure involves placing artificial tooth roots into the jawbone, providing a strong foundation for crowns, bridges, or even full-arch restorations. Implants improve oral health, restore smiles, and enhance confidence.

This procedure involves removing damaged or non-restorable teeth and preparing the site for a future implant. Bone graft is placed into the extraction site to prevent your body from resorbing the bone so that there will be adequate height and width of bone for an implant after healing is complete.

A single dental implant is a titanium screw that is placed into your bone to essentially replace the root’ of a missing tooth. Through a healing process called osseointegration the bone fuses to the metal fixating the screw in place. Once the implant is fully healed, your dentist can use it to place a crown, replacing your missing tooth.

Advanced solutions for replacing and entire arch of lost teeth using implant-supported bridges or dentures for full-mouth restoration.

This sinus is an air-filled cavity in your upper jaw above your upper back teeth that often is too low to allow for dental implants to be placed. A sinus augmentation is a type of procedure that adds bone to the floor of that air cavity increasing the overall height of bone allowing for placement of the appropriate length dental implant.
