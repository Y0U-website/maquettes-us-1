How to Get Here

Starting from the southbound WA-202, head southeast on Railroad Ave. Then, in about a quarter-mile, turn right onto Snoqualmie Pkwy. Stay on the road for 2 miles before turning right onto Center Blvd SE, followed by a quick right on SE Kinsey St to turn into the parking lot.

From I-90, take the exit for WA-18 E, and continue onto Echo Glen Rd. Continue onto Snoqualmie Pkwy before taking a left onto Center Blvd SE. Then, make a quick right onto SE Kinsey St, and our office will be on the right-hand side.

Starting from Mount Si High School, head southwest on Meadowbrook Way SE toward Railroad Ave. Turn right on Railroad Ave. Then, turn left onto Snoqualmie Pkwy. In about 2 miles, turn right onto Center Blvd SE before making a quick right onto SE Kinsey St, and our office will be on the right-hand side.
