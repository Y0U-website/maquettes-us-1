About Shi

Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) — Gies Scholarship recipient · Member — the only student in his class awarded OKU membership · International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) · Academy of General Dentistry (AGD)

He kept training on implants specifically — an implant diploma from NYU, a fellowship in the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, and implant conferences every year. His implant work is 3D-guided: every case is planned in 3D from a CBCT scan, and a custom 3D-printed surgical guide maps the depth, angle, and position of each implant. He describes the guide the way a driver describes GPS — it holds the route while the judgment stays with the surgeon. Dr. Shi places every implant by hand.

For qualifying patients he does same-day implants, pairing 3D planning, the surgical guide, and CEREC milling so that extraction, placement, and a provisional restoration happen in a single visit. And because he performs the work himself from surgery through the final restoration, the treatment stays consistent and the cost stays lower.

Beyond implants, Dr. Shi handles the complex restorative cases at Centre Dental, including full-mouth rehabilitation and cases other offices have turned away. He is fluent in English and Mandarin, explains each option until a patient understands not just the what but the why, and answers the clinic phone himself.
