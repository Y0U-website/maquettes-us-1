The practice

Dr. John Shi founded Centre Dental in 2004 with a simple philosophy: every patient deserves compassionate care, honest recommendations, and exceptional dentistry. For more than two decades, he has helped patients throughout Lower Manhattan restore healthy smiles with dental implants, Invisalign®, cosmetic dentistry, and preventive care.

Every smile is unique, which is why we take the time to listen to your concerns, review your medical and dental history, answer your questions, and explain every treatment option clearly. Together, we create a personalized treatment plan designed to support your oral health, restore your smile, and help you achieve long-term success.

Our team proudly provides dental care in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese, making it easier for patients throughout Chinatown and Lower Manhattan to feel comfortable, informed, and confident throughout every visit.
