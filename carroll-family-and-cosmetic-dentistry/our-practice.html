Our mission

Bringing smiles to your neighborhood

Dr. Joel Carroll founded Carroll Family and Cosmetic Dentistry in 1988. In 2015, Dr. Jocelyn Davis (Carroll) joined the practice to work alongside her father. In 2023, Dr. Joel Carroll retired, passing the legacy to Dr. Jocelyn Davis (Carroll), who is dedicated to upholding Carroll Family and Cosmetic Dentistry’s esteemed reputation in the Grand Rapids community.

Our modern office creates a comfortable atmosphere, complete with contemporary amenities, including warm blankets, eye masks, headphones, TV entertainment, guest Wi-Fi, phone charges, and a play area and prizes for kids.

We proudly welcome new and familiar smiles from Grand Rapids and the surrounding communities such as Walker, Allendale, Standale, and Comstock Park to our practice. Give us a call or book your first appointment online to start smiling brighter with Carroll Family and Cosmetic Dentistry.

Meet your new dentist

Jocelyn R. Davis (Carroll), DDS

Jocelyn R. Davis (Carroll), DDS, grew up in Grand Rapids, Michigan, received her bachelor’s degree at Michigan State University, and her dental degree at the University of Michigan. Formerly as Jocelyn Carroll DDS, daughter of Dr. Carroll, she joined the practice as a father-daughter team from 2015 throughout 2022, and today carries on her father’s legacy.

My goal is to have my patients comfortable and fully understand their dental needs and treatment," states Dr. Davis (Carroll). "Patients of all ages and comfort levels receive my same focus, for a positive dental experience and full confidence in their visit and treatment."
