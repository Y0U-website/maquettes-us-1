Founder & Instructor

Dr. Sery received a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from the University of Benin in Nigeria. She received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California, Los Angeles, and continued her education at the Bronx Lebanon where she earned her specialty degree in Pediatric Dentistry. She is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She currently has hospital privileges at Caromont Hospital, Gastonia, Kings Mountain Hospital, and Carolinas Medical Center - Main and University.
