Looking to start a career in dentistry? Our 12-week program is taught in a dental office by experienced dentists and dental assistants. Gain hands-on experience and learn the skills you need to succeed in this rewarding field. Enroll now and take the first step towards your new career!

Coronal Polishing for the Dental Assistant II The coronal polishing program for Dental Assistant II is designed to provide lecture and hands-on training in polishing teeth to remove stains and plaque. Upon completion, students will be able perform coronal polishing procedures under the supervision of a licensed dentist. Our program has been approved by North Carolina Board of Dental Examiners.

Intraoral Radiography for the Office-Trained Dental Assistant This course is designed for the office trained dental assistant needing dental radiography certification. Out of state dental assistants and foreign trained dentists are also eligible for this course. It has been approved by the NC Board of Dental Examiner.

Nitrous Oxide Certification This course is designed to provide dental assistants or dental hygienists a certification to monitor the administration of nitrous oxide. This course has been approved by the NC Board of Dental Examiner.
