Dr. Clifford Sneed

Dr. Clifford “Randy” Sneed is a lifelong resident of Fairmount, Indiana, with deep roots in the Grant County community. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Ball State University before pursuing his dental education at the prestigious Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry in Nashville, Tennessee, graduating in 1992.

Inspired by his uncle, George Bruner, Dr. Sneed returned home after dental school to join Bruner Dental Center, where he has proudly served patients ever since. For more than three decades, he has been committed to providing exceptional dental care while continually advancing his education and training to bring the latest techniques and technology to his patients.

Believing that everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile, Dr. Sneed offers a comprehensive and holistic approach to dental care. His expertise includes orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, endodontics, esthetic treatments, dental implants, and a wide range of restorative and preventive services. His dedication to innovation and patient-centered care has made him a trusted provider for generations of families throughout the region.

Beyond his professional accomplishments, Dr. Sneed and his wife, Dr. Katti Sneed, have devoted their lives to serving Grant County and investing in their community. Together, they have raised three sons while building a legacy of service, compassion, and commitment to improving the lives of others.

Today, Dr. Sneed continues to help patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles through personalized care, advanced treatment options, and a passion for excellence that has defined his career since 1992.
