Quick and Easy Referral Process

We strive to make the referral process as smooth as possible for healthcare professionals. Our admissions team works closely with you to ensure timely and seamless transitions for your patients.

  • Submit a Referral Form: Complete our secure online referral form, or contact our admissions team directly to provide patient information and care requirements.
  • Consultation and Assessment: Once we receive the referral, our admissions and clinical teams will review the patient’s care needs, discuss insurance coverage, and schedule an initial consultation if needed.
  • Care Coordination: We coordinate directly with your team to ensure all medical records, medications, and therapy plans are transferred accurately, enabling us to deliver the highest quality of care from day one.
  • Admission Confirmation: Our admissions team will keep you updated throughout the process and notify you once the patient has been successfully admitted to our facility.
