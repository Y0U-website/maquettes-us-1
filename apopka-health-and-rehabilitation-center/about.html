ABOUT

  • Compassionate Care: Our team goes beyond just meeting physical needs, offering emotional and social support to create a nurturing environment.
  • Individualized Treatment: Every resident is unique, and we believe their care plan should be too. We tailor our services to meet each individual’s health goals and preferences.
  • Excellence in Service: We hold ourselves to the highest standards, ensuring that the care we provide meets or exceeds all regulatory requirements and industry best practices.

Our Team

At the heart of Apopka Health and Rehabilitation Center is our dedicated team of professionals, including experienced nurses, certified therapists, and compassionate caregivers. Our interdisciplinary approach means that each resident benefits from a collaborative team working together to provide the best possible care.

Our leadership team and staff are not only experts in their fields but are also committed to making residents and families feel supported and cared for.

  • Licensed Nurses: Available 24/7 to provide skilled nursing care and oversee medical needs.
  • Therapists (Physical, Occupational, Speech): Specializing in rehabilitation and recovery to help residents regain independence and mobility.
  • Social Workers and Case Managers: Assisting families with care coordination, discharge planning, and support services.

Resident’s Choice Award

At Apopka Health and Rehabilitation Center, we believe in celebrating the caregivers who make a meaningful difference in the lives of our residents every day. Our Resident’s Choice Award is a monthly recognition, proudly selected by our residents themselves, honoring staff members who go above and beyond in delivering compassionate, quality care.

Each honoree represents the heart of our mission putting people first, fostering connection, and providing exceptional service with a smile.

A Community of Care

​We believe that residents thrive in a community where they can engage, connect, and participate in meaningful activities. Apopka Health and Rehabilitation Center provides a variety of social and wellness programs designed to enhance the daily lives of our residents, including:

  • Recreational Activities: Engaging events and activities to keep residents active and socially connected.
  • Spiritual and Emotional Support: Regular opportunities for spiritual services and emotional support through resident and family councils.
  • Family Involvement: We encourage families to be active participants in their loved one’s care journey, offering regular family meetings and open communication.
