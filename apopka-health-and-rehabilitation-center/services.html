SERVICES

  • Physical Therapy: Tailored plans to improve mobility, strength, balance, and coordination.
  • Occupational Therapy: Assistance with regaining independence in daily activities such as dressing, grooming, and eating.
  • Speech Therapy: Support for speech, swallowing, and cognitive challenges following injury or illness.
  • Outpatient Rehabilitation: Continued therapy services for those transitioning home but still needing therapy support.
  • Cardiac Care For patients recovering from a heart attack, surgery, or heart failure, our specialized cardiac rehabilitation program includes nutrition counseling, exercise planning, and careful monitoring of blood pressure and weight.
  • Respiratory Care Our team of specialists provides skilled respiratory care for residents with chronic and acute lung conditions, offering therapies designed to improve lung function and quality of life.
  • Wound Care Our wound care specialists offer comprehensive management for surgical wounds, bed sores, and other types of non-healing wounds. Our team focuses on individualized care plans for faster healing.
  • IV Therapy We offer skilled IV therapy services to manage your fluid balance and ensure drug effectiveness, providing end-to-end care for patients who require intravenous treatment as part of their recovery.
  • Bariatric Care For patients who have undergone bariatric surgery or need weight management care, we offer specialized support including nutritional counseling, customized exercise programs, and psychosocial care to aid in recovery.
  • Surgical Recovery Following surgery, patients benefit from professional, 24/7 post-surgical care that includes medication management, rehabilitation therapies, and daily assistance, all in a supportive, healing environment.

Long-Term Care

For individuals who can no longer perform daily activities independently, Apopka provides compassionate long-term care services in a safe and supportive environment. Our goal is to maximize the quality of life and ensure the well-being of every resident.

  • 24/7 skilled nursing care
  • Assistance with daily activities like dressing, bathing, and eating
  • Recreational activities and social programs
  • Personalized dining services
  • Regular health assessments and monitoring

Hospice and Palliative Care

For those facing a life-limiting illness or approaching end-of-life, Apopka provides comprehensive hospice and palliative care services. Our focus is on offering comfort, dignity, and respect during this critical time. We provide emotional, physical, and spiritual support for both residents and their families.

  • Pain and symptom management
  • 24/7 nursing support
  • Emotional and spiritual care
  • Family support and counseling
  • Coordination with hospice organizations

Amenities and Additional Services

At Apopka, we believe that high-quality care extends beyond medical services. Our range of amenities is designed to provide comfort, engagement, and well-being for every resident.

  • Private and semi-private suites
  • Personalized dining services
  • Transportation services for medical appointments
  • Daily recreational activities
  • Movie theatre
  • Dental, podiatry, optometry, and audiology services
  • Registered dietitian consultations
  • Respite care for short-term stays
