When to Visit the Dentist?

Many people only go to the dentist when something is wrong and that is truly a shame because they are missing out on so many preventive services that can save discomfort — and expense — down the road.

Regular dental visits are essential to ensure oral health problems, from tooth decay to oral cancer are detected and treated promptly. Some individuals may need to see the dentist more often than others to avoid situations like plaque buildup and gum disease, but everyone should go at least once per year.
