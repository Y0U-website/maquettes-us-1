Frequently Asked Questions

Does Creston Dental & Braces accept Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Medicaid at all 6 of our South Carolina locations, including SC Medicaid, Healthy Connections, Dentaquest SCHIP, and TRICARE.

Is dental care covered by Medicaid?

Yes, in most cases. Medicaid dental coverage typically includes exams, cleanings, X-rays, fillings, and emergency care, though exact coverage depends on your age and specific plan. Our team can confirm your coverage before treatment begins.

Does Medicaid cover pediatric dentistry?

Yes. Medicaid dental coverage typically includes routine pediatric dentistry services like exams, cleanings, X-rays, and fillings, and coverage for kids is often more comprehensive than for adults under most state Medicaid plans.

At what age can my child start using Medicaid dental benefits?

Most children can begin using Medicaid dental coverage as early as their first tooth, or by their first birthday, since early pediatric dental visits help catch issues before they become bigger problems.

Do I need a Medicaid pediatric dentist, or can any dentist see my child?

Medicaid does not require a specialized pediatric dentist for most routine care. General dentists who accept Medicaid, like our team, can typically see children for cleanings, exams, and preventive care.

Does Medicaid cover braces for kids?

Medicaid may cover orthodontic treatment for children when it’s medically necessary, such as for a severe bite issue, though coverage varies by plan. Our team can confirm what your child’s specific plan covers.

Can I use Medicaid for my child’s dental emergency?

Yes. Same-day and emergency appointments are available for children on Medicaid, just like any other patient.

Do I need to file my own Medicaid claims?

No. Once you provide your policy information, our billing specialists file your claims for you, so there’s nothing extra for you to manage.

What if my Medicaid coverage has changed or lapsed?

Contact your state Medicaid office to confirm your current coverage. If you’re between coverage, we also offer the Resolution Dental Discount Plan as an affordable option in the meantime.

Does Medicaid cover braces or oral surgery for adults?

Coverage for orthodontics and oral surgery depends on medical necessity and your specific plan. Our team can help confirm what’s covered before you begin treatment.

Getting Started with Medicaid at Creston Dental & Braces is Simple

Bring your Medicaid card and a photo ID to your first appointment, and we’ll verify your coverage and walk you through what’s included. From there, you receive care, and we handle the claims filing on the back end, so you never have to.
