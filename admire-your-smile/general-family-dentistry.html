The Three Types of Sleep Apnea

Your dentist will go over the three types of sleep apnea with you and let you know which one you have.

This is the most common type of sleep apnea. It occurs when your throat muscles become too relaxed while you’re sleeping. This causes your airway to collapse, which, in turn, interrupts your breathing patterns.

This is a less common form of sleep apnea. It’s caused when your brain doesn’t send the right signals to your breathing muscles. It’s not a breathing issue. It’s a neurological issue.

This is also called treatment-emergent central sleep apnea. It’s a combination of obstructive and central sleep apnea. It’s a rare condition. This form of sleep apnea usually occurs when you are undergoing treatment for obstructive sleep apnea and develop the symptoms of central sleep apnea during the treatment.

  • Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)
  • Central Sleep Apnea (CSA)
  • Complex Sleep Apnea
