Why Should I Get Dental Implants?

The Benefits Of Dental Implants

Natural Appearance and Feel Dental implants are designed to look and feel like your natural teeth. They are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth, ensuring a seamless blend with your smile. This natural appearance boosts your confidence and allows you to smile freely without feeling self-conscious.

Improved Oral Health Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants do not require the alteration of adjacent teeth. This means your natural teeth are left intact, promoting better long-term oral health. Additionally, implants help stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining the structural integrity of your mouth.

Enhanced Comfort and Convenience Say goodbye to the discomfort of removable dentures. Dental implants become a permanent part of your mouth, eliminating the need for messy adhesives and the inconvenience of removing them for cleaning. They provide the stability you need to speak and eat without fear of slipping or discomfort.

The Dental Implants Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Planning

Initial Consultation and Planning

Your journey to a restored smile begins with an initial consultation at Cornerstone Dental Lounge. During this visit, Dr. Jeremy Martens will conduct a thorough examination of your mouth, including X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess your jawbone&#x27;s condition and determine if you&#x27;re a suitable candidate for dental implants. He will discuss your treatment goals and create a personalized plan tailored to your needs.

Implant Placement

Implant Placement

Once you&#x27;re ready to proceed, the next step is the surgical placement of the implant. This is a minor procedure performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Dr. Martens will carefully insert the titanium post into your jawbone, where it will gradually fuse with the bone over the next few months. This process, known as osseointegration, is crucial for the implant&#x27;s long-term success.

Abutment and Crown Placement

Abutment and Crown Placement

After the implant has fully integrated with your jawbone, you&#x27;ll return to the office for the final steps. An abutment, a small connector piece, is attached to the implant, serving as the base for your new tooth. Finally, a custom-made crown is securely placed onto the abutment, completing your dental implant restoration. The result is a natural-looking and fully functional tooth that blends seamlessly with your existing smile.
