Delta Dental Insurance

We accept nearly all insurance plans at Apple Denture Center including Delta Dental. Recently, we were forced to make the difficult decision to no longer be a “Participating Provider” with Delta. What does this mean for those who have Delta Dental Insurance coverage?

My staff will be happy to provide accurate estimates for our fees and expected reimbursement you should receive from Delta based on your plans contract with Delta Dental. This can and should be done prior to your appointment. You can request this information by calling the office at (231) 773-8500

  • Apple Denture Center will no longer be listed on your companies’ list of dental providers, though we STILL accept your insurance.
  • The biggest change is the most notable and is unique to Delta and Blue Cross Blue Shield. It goes as follows:
  • Your dental benefit check will be sent directly to you, not to our office as before.
  • This necessitates that we collect the fee for services at the time they are performed. You will then be sent a check from Delta within a few weeks.
  • This is a dramatic change for those accustomed to having work performed and not being required to pay for it at that time.
  • Please note that most other insurance companies do not have that requirement.
