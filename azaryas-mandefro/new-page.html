INTRAVENOUS SEDATION Experience Comfortable Dentistry with IV Sedation

At our office, we proudly offer Intravenous (IV) Sedation , also known as Twilight Sedation — a unique service that sets us apart from many other dental practices. This specialized approach is part of what makes our care truly personalized and patient-centered.

IV Sedation is ideal for patients who feel anxious or uneasy about dental treatment. It allows you to remain deeply relaxed and calm throughout your procedure. Many patients describe the experience as peaceful and often have little to no memory of the treatment itself. It significantly reduces anxiety and makes longer or more complex visits much more manageable.

What makes our sedation option even more convenient is that Dr. Mandefro personally administers and monitors the IV sedation right in our office , eliminating the need for a hospital or outpatient facility — and avoiding the extra costs that typically come with it.

With IV sedation, you can feel confident knowing your comfort is our top priority. You’ll be in experienced hands, receiving gentle care in a calm, stress-free environment.

DENTURES How We Make Dentures at Our Office

Creating dentures is a custom process that starts with understanding your unique needs. During your initial visit, we take detailed impressions and measurements of your mouth to ensure a precise fit. From there, we work closely with a trusted dental lab to design your dentures based on these impressions, your bite, and your natural smile.

Throughout the process, we’ll schedule try-in appointments to make sure everything feels comfortable and looks natural before the final dentures are made. Once completed, we ensure the fit is secure and make any needed adjustments. Our goal is to help you feel confident and comfortable with a new smile that functions just the way it should.
