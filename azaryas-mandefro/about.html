0 Meet Dr. Mandefro Dr. Mandefro, our trusted dentist in Arnold, is known for delivering exceptional dental care with a compassionate touch. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine and holds a bachelor’s degree from the University of Missouri (Mizzou).

Dedicated to staying current with the latest advancements and techniques, Dr. Mandefro provides care that is not only prompt and effective but also safe and affordable—always tailored to help you achieve your dental goals.

He lives in Town and Country with his wife, Betty, and their four daughters. Dr. Mandefro is a proud member of both the American Dental Association and the Missouri Dental Association. He and his skilled dental team are passionate about creating a welcoming, comfortable environment where both new and returning patients feel right at home.
