Patient Info

Patients often tell us they’re amazed by how thorough this appointment is and how easy we make it to understand exactly what’s going on with their oral health. From the moment you walk in, we want you to feel like you’re in the right place.

  • X-rays of every tooth in your mouth
  • A three-dimensional X-ray of your entire jaw and teeth for the most complete view
  • A professional set of six photographs of your smile and teeth
  • A thorough review on our big screen, where you can see what Dr. Camenzuli sees – because when you can see the problem, the solution becomes much clearer

Dental Insurance

We believe your dental care should feel simple, not stressful.

If you have dental insurance through work, it can be a valuable way to save on the care you need. Our team is here to help make it easier. We’ll handle the paperwork and file claims on your behalf, so you don’t have to worry about the fine print.

What makes us different? Your care isn’t limited by a checklist. At Camenzuli Dental Excellence, we focus on what’s best for you, not what an insurance company says should come first. That means you get thoughtful, personalized care designed around your real needs.

Dental Financing

If paying for treatment all at once feels overwhelming, you’re not alone – and you don’t have to go it alone. That’s why we offer flexible financing options through Sunbit, Affirm, ProceedFinance and CareCredit, making it easier to move forward without the worry.

With smaller monthly payments, often at low or even zero interest, you can get the care you need now and pay over time in a way that works for your budget. Our team is here to walk you through the process, step by step, with answers and support along the way.

Membership Plan

Everyone should have access to dental care that feels both personal and affordable, even without insurance.

That’s why we offer a simple Membership Plan created just for patients like you. For one flat annual fee, you’ll receive the essential care your smile needs, plus discounts on many other treatments, including select restorative and cosmetic services.

There are no deductibles, claims or fine print. Just straightforward savings and support from a team that cares about your health and comfort.

Patient Forms

Your time and comfort matter, even before you sit in the chair.

To help us care for you safely and personally, we’ll need a bit of background from you, including your health history and insurance details. You’re welcome to arrive a few minutes early and fill out your forms here, or you can download them ahead of time and complete them at home.

Either way, we want your visit to feel smooth, easy and focused on you.

Easy, Secure Online Payments

With our online payment portal, you can take care of your balance anytime, from anywhere. It’s quick, secure, and made to fit your schedule. Interested in learning more? Give our office a call! After all, your care should feel comfortable from start to finish.
