Dr. Michael Maccaro

I’m Dr. Mike Maccaro, a pediatric dentist who joined Around the Mountain Pediatric Dentistry in 2023. I was born and raised in Ormond Beach, FL, where my father is a general dentist. I attended Florida State University and became a big Seminoles fan. I then went to the University of Florida for dental school and learned to tolerate the Gators.

I moved to Chinle, AZ, to work for the Indian Health Service and serve in public health. I met my wife Tierra in Chinle, and we have two children who keep us on our toes. I practiced on the Navajo reservation for almost 16 years while completing my pediatric dental residency at Texas A M College of Dentistry in Dallas, TX.

My family now lives in Flagstaff where I enjoy spending time with them, hiking, sports, and all things comedy. I am honored to be part of the Around the Mountain Pediatric Dental team and strive to provide excellent pediatric dental care while building relationships and trust with parents and families. I look forward to meeting you and your child!
