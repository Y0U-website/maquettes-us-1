Dr. Adam Holder

Adam grew up in Aitkin, MN and graduated from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2011. He was then commissioned as a Captain in the United States Air Force , where he completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency program in Washington, D.C. Dr. Holder completed his Air Force commitment by serving two years at Kirtland Air Force Base in Albuquerque, N.M. before returning home to Minnesota in 2014.

Dr. Holder is a member of the Minnesota Dental Association and the American Dental Association , a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry , and Master in the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology . He is the very proud founder of Carver Dental.
