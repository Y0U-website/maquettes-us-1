Is an Implant-Supported Bridge in Azusa Right for You?

You might be a candidate for a multiple tooth implant-supported bridge if you:

  • Have multiple adjacent missing teeth.
  • Possess adequate bone density for implant placement.
  • Maintain good overall oral health.
  • Desire a long-term, fixed tooth replacement solution.

Implant-Supported Bridge Dental Implants FAQs

If you have any questions about multiple tooth implant-supported bridges, we’ve got answers.

With proper care and regular dental maintenance, implant-supported bridges can last 15-25 years or even a lifetime. The titanium implants themselves are designed to be permanent, while the bridge portion may eventually need replacement due to normal wear. Your commitment to excellent oral hygiene and routine checkups with Dr. Altunis will significantly extend the lifespan of your restoration.

The cost typically ranges from $6,000 to $15,000 depending on the number of implants needed, materials used, and complexity of your case. While this represents a significant investment, implant-supported bridges often prove more cost-effective over time compared to repeatedly replacing traditional bridges or dentures. Dr. Altunis will provide a detailed treatment estimate during your consultation and discuss financing options to make your treatment affordable.

Implant-supported bridges have an excellent success rate of 95-98 % when performed by experienced practitioners, such as Dr. Altunis. Success depends on factors such as bone quality, overall health, oral hygiene, and following post-treatment care instructions. Most patients enjoy their implant-supported bridges for decades without complications.
