Direct the light exactly where you need it.

The dental professional’s vision is greatly improved when wearing a headlight; however, the light’s intensity and direction need constant adjustment. AirGuard™ integrated lighting eliminates time wasted removing gloves, making adjustments and re-gloving. Precision lighting allows the clinician to see hard-to-light areas such as maxillary posterior buccal surfaces.

Multicolored LED strips so you can see in any condition.

Many clinicians struggle with ineffective overhead lighting, often from low intensity or poor positioning. Integrated LED side strips on the AirGuard™ eliminate the need for overhead lighting and provide bright illumination through close placement to the working field.

Colors right where you need them.

Change the lighting color to warm white, cool white, amber or blue to coincide with your procedure. Avoid accidental exposure to the intense blue light of curing wands by simply switching to amber light. To support color matching for crowns, composite material and partial dentures, AirGuard™ provides two shades of white to fit your needs.
