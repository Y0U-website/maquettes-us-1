What Is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of teeth, gums, and overall smile aesthetics . While many cosmetic treatments also improve dental function, the primary goal is to enhance confidence and self-esteem through a more attractive smile.

At All Smiles Family Dentistry, we combine artistry, precision, and modern dental technology to offer cosmetic solutions tailored to your facial features, lifestyle, and dental goals.

Common cosmetic concerns we treat include:

  • Discolored or stained teeth
  • Chipped, cracked, or worn teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Uneven or misaligned teeth
  • Misshapen or disproportionate teeth

Frequently Asked Questions About Cosmetic Dentistry

Don’t Delete

Is cosmetic dentistry only about appearance?

While cosmetic dentistry focuses on aesthetics, many treatments also improve function, alignment, and oral health.

How long do cosmetic dentistry results last?

Longevity depends on the procedure and oral care habits. Veneers, bonding, and whitening can last several years with proper maintenance.

Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive. We use modern techniques to ensure patient comfort throughout treatment.

Can cosmetic dentistry fix crooked teeth without braces?

Yes, in many cases veneers or bonding can improve the appearance of mildly misaligned teeth without orthodontics.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost in Miami, FL?

Costs vary based on treatment type and complexity. We provide transparent pricing and personalized treatment plans during your consultation.
