The practice

Woodbine Family Dentistry provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages. Whether you are visiting for a routine cleaning, restorative treatment, cosmetic dentistry, or an unexpected dental concern, our goal is to make every appointment as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Led by Dr. Reid Hines, our team takes the time to listen, explain your options, and help you feel confident about your dental care. We are honored to care for families across Pace and the surrounding area with dependable service and a friendly hometown approach.

Dr. Reid Hines is proud to serve the Pace community through Woodbine Family Dentistry. A graduate of Milton High School, Dr. Hines continued his education at the University of Mississippi, where he earned his undergraduate degree in Biology before receiving his dental degree from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry.

Dr. Hines is committed to providing thoughtful, high-quality dental care in a comfortable environment. He enjoys helping patients understand their options, feel at ease during treatment, and maintain healthy smiles for years to come.

From preventive visits to smile restoration, Woodbine Family Dentistry offers a wide range of dental services to help you maintain a healthy, confident smile.

Routine exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canal therapy, periodontal care, and other essential dental services to support your long-term oral health.

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, bonding, crowns, bridges, and smile-enhancing treatment options designed to improve the appearance and confidence of your smile.

Dental pain, swelling, broken teeth, and urgent concerns should not wait. Our team is here to help you take the right next step when a dental emergency happens.
