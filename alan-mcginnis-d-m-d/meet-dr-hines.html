Meet Dr. Hines

Reid B. Hines, d.m.d.

Dr. Reid Hines is honored to serve the community he has always called home. A graduate of Milton High School, Dr. Hines continued his education at the University of Mississippi, where he earned his undergraduate degree in Biology and played football for Ole Miss.

He went on to receive his dental degree from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry in 1994. After dental school, Dr. Hines returned home to Pace to build a practice dedicated to serving the families, friends, and community he knows and loves.

Comfortable Care You Can Feel Good About

Dental care should feel personal, clear, and comfortable. Dr. Hines believes patients deserve to understand their options and feel confident about the care they receive.

At Woodbine Family Dentistry, our team focuses on creating a welcoming experience from the moment you walk through the door. We know that many patients feel nervous about dental treatment, so we work hard to make visits as calm, friendly, and stress-free as possible.

Our approach is simple. We listen first, explain clearly, and provide dental care with respect, honesty, and attention to detail.

Through Woodbine Family Dentistry, Dr. Hines provides comprehensive dental care with a hometown approach. His goal is to help patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident at every visit. Whether a patient is coming in for a routine cleaning, restorative treatment, cosmetic dentistry, or urgent dental care, Dr. Hines takes the time to listen, explain treatment options, and provide care tailored to each individual smile.

Dr. Hines lives in Pace with his wife, Alison, and their three children. Outside of the office, he enjoys sports, outdoor activities, local school involvement, and being active in the community. He is also involved in area dental study clubs and is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Hines continues to grow and enhance the practice with a strong interest in cosmetic dentistry, restorative care, and helping patients enjoy smiles that are healthy, functional, and confident.

Proudly Serving Pace Families

Woodbine Family Dentistry is proud to be part of the Pace community. Dr. Hines returned home to serve the people and families of this area, and that commitment continues to guide the practice today.

From children and teens to adults and seniors, our office welcomes patients of all ages. We are grateful for the trust local families place in us and work every day to provide the kind of care we would want for our own families.

General Dentistry

Routine dental exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canal therapy, periodontal care, and preventive treatment to help maintain strong oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, bonding, crowns, bridges, Snap-On Smiles, and other smile-enhancing options to help patients feel more confident in their appearance.
