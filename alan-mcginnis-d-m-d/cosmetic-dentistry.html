Cosmetic Dentistry in Pace, FL

Every smile is different, and your treatment plan should be too. During your visit, Dr. Hines will evaluate your oral health, listen to your goals, and discuss cosmetic options that may help you achieve a smile you feel good about.

  • Chipped, cracked, or worn teeth
  • Small gaps between teeth
  • Uneven or misshaped teeth
  • Old dental work that no longer looks natural
  • Missing teeth
  • A smile that feels dull, aged, or unbalanced
  • Confidence when smiling, speaking, or taking photos

Healthy First. Beautiful Next.

Cosmetic dentistry works best when it begins with a healthy foundation. Before recommending cosmetic treatment, our team will evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health.

Small dental problems can become larger concerns when left untreated. Hardened plaque and calculus can build up around the gumline, leading to gum irritation, gingivitis, periodontitis, tooth decay, and possible tooth loss over time.

That is why we focus on both appearance and health. Our goal is to help your smile look better, feel better, and function well for years to come.
