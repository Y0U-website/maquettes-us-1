Continuing Dental Education

Academy of General Dentistry

In July of 2022 Dr. Rick received his Fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry. Only 6 percent of general dentists in the United States and Canada have achieved this designation. An FAGD dentist has completed over 500 credit hours of high-quality approved continuing education and 350 of those 500 credits in live courses. Furthermore, they must pass an exam equal in difficulty to board certification exams.
