Dr. Lief Rasmussen , DMD

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Rasmussen spends his time looking for things he can restore, such as homes or classic cars. He is also a lifelong runner who competed in college and continues to pursue that passion by running marathons across the country, with a goal of completing one in each state—10 down and 40 to go. As the middle of seven children, he values connection and community, and that carries into the way he cares for his patients. In his free time, you can also find him water skiing, wakeboarding, mountain biking, or skiing out West.

Dr. Skylor Morton , DDS

Dr. Skylor Morton is thrilled to bring College Street Smiles to Dickson and the surrounding communities, offering comprehensive dental care with a deep focus on implant dentistry and full-mouth transformation.

Originally from New Mexico, Dr. Morton began his academic journey at Texas Tech University before earning early admission to Creighton University School of Dentistry. It was there that he discovered his passion for restoring smiles at every level of complexity — completing over 300 hours of continuing education in cosmetic and implant dentistry during his final year alone.

Dr. Morton has since built his practice around the cases others consider challenging. His clinical focus includes dental implants, advanced bone grafting, All-on-X full-arch restoration, porcelain veneers, and complete full-mouth rehabilitation. For patients who simply know their smile needs to be fixed — but aren&#x27;t sure where to start — Dr. Morton specializes in building a clear path forward, regardless of the difficulty or how many years a problem has gone unaddressed.

He is also deeply committed to giving back, having spent five weeks in the Dominican Republic providing free dental care to underserved communities.

Outside the office, Dr. Morton enjoys golfing, fishing, and spending time on the lake. His commitment to continuous learning ensures that every patient at College Street Smiles receives not just exceptional care, but exceptional results.
