The practice

At CARE Clinic, we believe in the power of compassionate healthcare. We envision a world where everyone can access the care they deserve, regardless of their circumstances. Every individual has the potential to define their future and positively impact the world, and we are here to support them on that journey.

CARE Clinic offers dental services to qualifying MN residents. We accept Medical Assistance and MinnesotaCare and offer services for a nominal fee to qualified uninsured patients. We also offer our mobile dental clinic for onsite dental services at schools, senior homes, churches, and other locations throughout both counties.

Dental Care READ MORE Medical Care CARE Clinic offers preventative, acute and chronic medical care. In addition to visits, we provide limited diagnostic services and medications to patients free of charge.

Mental Health CARE Clinic provides evaluations, individual, family, and couples counseling for adults and children. With the collaboration of Hispanic Outreach, we also offer counseling to Spanish-speaking households.

MNsure Navigator Program CARE Clinic is a certified MNSure Navigation Agency. We have trained navigators available to assist you in navigating the insurance marketplace via the MNsure website.
