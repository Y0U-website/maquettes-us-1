Dental Crowns & Bridges

  • Porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) : Combines the strength of metal with the appearance of porcelain
  • Zirconia : Extremely durable and resistant to chipping ideal for back teeth under heavy chewing pressure
  • Gold or metal alloys : Long-lasting and exceptionally strong, typically used on back molars

What to Expect During Treatment

The crown and bridge process typically requires two appointments. During the first visit, the affected tooth or teeth are prepared, impressions are taken, and a temporary restoration is placed. At the second appointment, your custom permanent crown or bridge is fitted, adjusted for comfort, and cemented into place.
