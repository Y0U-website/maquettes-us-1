What Is a Dental Filling?

A dental filling is a restorative material used to repair a tooth that has been damaged by decay. When a cavity forms, the decayed portion of the tooth is gently removed, and the resulting space is filled with a durable material to restore the tooth’s shape and function. Fillings prevent further decay from spreading and help protect the long-term integrity of the tooth making them one of the most important and frequently used treatments in dentistry.

Two Types of Fillings Amalgam and Composite

There are two main types of filling options: amalgam and composite. Each has its own set of advantages and disadvantages, and the right choice depends on the patient’s individual needs, preferences, and the location of the cavity.

Amalgam (Silver) Fillings : Amalgam has been used in dentistry for well over a century. It is highly durable, cost-effective, and performs well under the heavy chewing pressure of back teeth. Amalgam fillings are a reliable, long-lasting option for patients prioritising function and affordability.

Composite (Tooth-Coloured) Fillings : Composite resin is matched to the natural shade of your teeth, making it a discreet option that blends seamlessly with your smile. Composite bonds directly to the tooth structure, which can allow for a more conservative preparation. It is a popular choice for visible teeth and patients who prefer a more natural appearance.

At John Chung Family Dentistry, we believe in honesty and transparency. Our team will explain the pros and cons of each option clearly so you can make a confident, informed decision before any treatment begins.

The Filling Procedure What to Expect

The dental filling process is straightforward and typically completed in a single appointment. After numbing the area with a local anaesthetic, our dentist gently removes the decayed tissue and cleans the tooth thoroughly. The chosen filling material is then placed, shaped to match your natural bite, and finished for comfort. The entire process is quick and efficient, and most patients leave the appointment with little to no lingering discomfort.

The Benefits of Early Cavity Treatment

The most important thing to understand about cavities is that they don’t stop growing on their own. A small cavity treated today with a simple filling could become a much larger problem requiring a crown, root canal, or even an extraction if left untreated. Early treatment saves time, money, and discomfort in the long run. At John Chung Family Dentistry, we encourage all patients to attend regular checkups so that decay can be detected and treated in its earliest stages, keeping your smile healthy for life.

Caring for Your Filling Long-Term

With proper care, dental fillings can last many years. Maintaining good oral hygiene brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and attending routine checkups is the best way to protect both your filling and the surrounding tooth structure.
