What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the bone a process called "osseointegration" a custom-made crown is attached to the top to match the look and feel of your surrounding natural teeth. The result is a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer significant advantages over other tooth replacement options. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, which means they won’t slip, shift, or require removal for cleaning. Some key benefits include:

Natural appearance and feel : Implants are designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.

Bone preservation : Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically follows tooth loss.

Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Improved oral health : Adjacent teeth don’t need to be altered or used as anchors, preserving their natural structure.

Enhanced confidence : A complete, stable smile supports better speech, chewing, and self-esteem.

The Dental Implant Process

The implant process is completed over several appointments to ensure proper healing and optimal results. First, a thorough exam including x-rays is performed to evaluate bone density and overall oral health. If candidacy is confirmed, the implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. A healing period follows typically a few months during which the implant fuses with the bone. Once healed, an abutment and custom crown are placed, completing your new tooth.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants are adults in good general health with sufficient jawbone density to support the implant. Certain conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking, or active gum disease may affect candidacy. Patients who have experienced bone loss may require a bone graft prior to implant placement. During your consultation at John Chung Family Dentistry in Newport News, VA, we will review your full medical and dental history to determine the best treatment plan for your unique needs.

Long-Term Durability and Care

One of the most compelling reasons patients choose implants is their longevity. Unlike other restorations that may need to be replaced every 10 to 15 years, a well-placed and well-maintained implant can last decades often a lifetime. Caring for implants is straightforward: brush twice daily, floss regularly, and attend your routine checkups at our Newport News or Hayes office. With consistent care, your implant will continue to look and function beautifully for years to come.
