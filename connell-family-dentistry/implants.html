Implants in New Orleans, LA

  • Durability: Implants are designed to be permanent, so you won’t have to replace them every few years.
  • Oral Health: Dental implants don’t require adjusting other teeth. Since they are placed individually, it is also easier to brush and floss between them compared to other replacement teeth.

How Are Implants Inserted?

Implants are inserted during a surgical procedure, for which your mouth will be entirely numb. An incision is made in the gum where a replacement tooth is needed. Through the incision, the jaw bone is drilled to create room for the implant. Next, the small titanium screw is inserted, which extends into the jawbone. Then, the incision will be stitched. After the implant is inserted, you will have a few months to recover and allow the screw to bond with the jawbone. After this time, you will return to the office for crowns to be placed on the implant, resulting in a full, beautiful smile.
