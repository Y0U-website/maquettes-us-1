Signs It’s Time for Orthodontic Care

Wondering if your child needs orthodontic care? Here are some signs to look out for:

If you notice any of these signs, reach out to us for a comprehensive evaluation!

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Misaligned jaw
  • Frequent biting of the cheek or roof of the mouth
  • Teeth grinding or clenching
  • Speech difficulties
