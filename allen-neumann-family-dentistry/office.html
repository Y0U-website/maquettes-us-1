Jeffrey F. Allen, DDS

Dr. Allen grew up in eastern Iowa and graduated from Augustana College in Illinois with a degree in biology. He then earned his doctorate of dental science from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry in 1987.

Jeffrey F. Allen, DDS

Dr. Allen and his wife, Paula, moved to Des Moines, IA, where he worked in a group practice and she worked in management for the renowned department store, Von Maur. The 9 years they spent in Des Moines allowed Dr. Allen to gain valuable dental experience and establish good working relationships with several specialists.

Dr. and Mrs. Allen moved to Pella in 1996, where Dr. Allen opened his family dentistry office in a historic building downtown on the square. In 2003, the building was completely renovated and expanded to accommodate the growing practice. In addition to his private practice in Pella, Dr. Allen has served as an adjunct faculty member helping senior dental students in the Department of Family Dentistry at the University of Iowa.

The Allens participate in many community events and activities, and support the community, its schools, and local non-profit organizations. Their daughter, Katie, was involved in tennis and dance throughout her high school and college years.

Dr. Allen enjoys all aspects of dentistry, especially incorporating new technology and materials into his daily patient care. He feels fortunate to be in a profession in which he can help people enjoy a higher quality of life by providing exceptional dental care. He is proud to keep a warm and friendly atmosphere in his office so that his patients feel welcome and well-cared for.

In their free time, Dr. Allen and his wife enjoy exercising, hiking, playing tennis, biking, playing cards with friends, watching movies, and traveling.

Denise E. Neumann, DDS

Dr. Denise Neumann was born and raised in Iowa Falls, Iowa. As a young adult, a family friend and dentist gave her the opportunity to work in his dental office during high school. This experience introduced Dr. Neumann to her lifelong passion for dentistry.

Denise E. Neumann, DDS

Dr. Denise Neumann was born and raised in Iowa Falls, Iowa. As a young adult, a family friend and dentist gave her the opportunity to work in his dental office during high school. This experience introduced Dr. Neumann to her lifelong passion for dentistry. She attended Wartburg College, and graduated cum laude with a degree in biology in 1998. She then attended the University of Iowa College of Dentistry and earned a doctorate of dental science degree in 2002.

Dr. Neumann and her husband, David, moved to Oskaloosa, Iowa in 2002, following a career opportunity for her husband at Cargill. David also has a degree from Wartburg College in biology and chemistry. He works in management at the Cargill plant in Eddyville, Iowa.

Dr. Neumann has been an associate dentist since 2002 in Oskaloosa and Knoxville, Iowa. Those experiences allowed her to increase her dental expertise, and the time spent was invaluable for learning the methods of ensuring gentle patient care.

When not seeing patients, Dr. Neumann serves as a dental consultant for Principal Financial Group in Des Moines, Iowa, which allows her to help with another aspect of the dental profession.

The Neumann family moved to Pella in 2007. They have 2 children, Zack and Lizzie, and feel blessed to live in an area that has many wonderful people and outdoor activities to enjoy. Dr. Neumann and her husband enjoy supporting their children during their extracurricular activities. Together as a family they love spending time boating, vacationing, attending church functions, and cheering on the Iowa Hawkeyes.

Dr. Neumann is truly passionate about the dental profession and enjoys treating patients of all ages. Dr. Neumann continues to increase her knowledge by completing continuing education classes in both esthetic dentistry and Invisalign®.
