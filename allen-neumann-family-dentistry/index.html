The practice

Located in a turn-of-the-century building, the clinic was designed to embrace the character of Pella’s history and provide state -of-the-art comprehensive dental care. Patients, young and old, appreciate the details of this unique experience.

We offer highly personalized and empathetic care to patients of all ages and special concerns, such as heightened anxiety and dental fear. Enjoy relaxing in our modern, comfortable office, with amenities including pillows, blankets, and HD TV’s.

For optimal results, we perform comprehensive diagnosis and planning using advanced image technology. For example, digital xrays and intraoral cameras allow for more accurate and precise planning, all while optimizing your comfort throughout your visit

Dr. Allen grew up in eastern Iowa and graduated from Augustana College in Illinois with a degree in biology. He then earned his doctorate of dental science from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry in 1987.

Dr. Allen and his wife, Paula, moved to Des Moines, IA, where he worked in a group practice and she worked in management for the renowned department store, Von Maur. The 9 years they spent in Des Moines allowed Dr. Allen to gain valuable dental experience and establish good working relationships with several specialists.

Dr. and Mrs. Allen moved to Pella in 1996, where Dr. Allen opened his family dentistry office in a historic building downtown on the square. In 2003, the building was completely renovated and expanded to accommodate the growing practice.
