Gentle Care for Little Smiles

A child’s first dental experience sets the tone for future visits. That’s why we create a welcoming environment for children of all ages, from toddlers to teens. We make sure your child feels comfortable while learning good oral hygiene habits that will last a lifetime.

Sedation Dentistry

Does the thought of dental work make you feel anxious? We’re proud to be one of the only IV sedation dentists in the area. Sedation dentistry is a relaxing option to help you feel comfortable throughout your procedure.

Restorative Dentistry Solutions

We understand the importance of a healthy smile that functions well and lets you feel confident. We offer a variety of restorative dentistry options, including bridges, crowns, dentures, and bonding, to create a smile that’s beautiful and functional.

Same-Day Crowns

Say goodbye to multiple appointments and temporary crowns. We use digital scans and advanced technology to create natural-looking dental crowns in our office. Your crown will be made and precisely fitted in a single visit.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Want to show off your smile with confidence? Our cosmetic dentistry services can help. We can brighten and improve your smile if you have chipped teeth, discoloration, gaps, or unevenness. Cosmetic services we offer include:

Digital Scans We use the latest digital technology to provide you with the most comfortable and efficient care. In most cases, we can perform digital scans for crowns, dentures, and bite guards, eliminating the need for messy impressions.

  • Porcelain veneers to cover imperfections and create a flawlessly symmetrical look.

Amenities

We want you to feel comfortable when you’re here. We have blankets to keep you cozy, and we can play a movie for you or your child during your appointment.

Emergencies

Dental problems don’t take the weekend off. If you’re already a patient, you can text us if there’s a problem that can’t wait. We check messages and will determine if you need treatment right away. In some cases, we may recommend you go to the hospital.
