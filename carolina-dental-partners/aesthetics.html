Why Choose a Dentist for Aesthetics

Dentists have a unique advantage when it comes to facial aesthetics. During years of training, we become experts in the intricate anatomy of the face, mouth, and jaw. We understand the placement of facial muscles, nerves, and blood vessels, which translates to precise and safe injections.

Aesthetic Services We Offer

Botox and Xeomin

These neuromodulators are like tiny relaxers for your facial muscles. By temporarily reducing muscle contractions, neuromodulators smooth out the wrinkles and fine lines that can form around your eyes, brows, and forehead. Botox and Xeomin leave you with a refreshed and more youthful appearance that lets your natural beauty shine through.

Botox for TMJ and Migraines

Botox is a powerful tool that helps many people combat migraines. Botox targets the specific muscles that contribute to headaches and can reduce the frequency and intensity of migraines. We also use Botox to gently relax the muscles associated with TMJ (temporomandibular joint dysfunction), a condition that causes pain and discomfort in the jaw and surrounding muscles.

Juvederm Fillers

As we age, our faces lose volume, leading to wrinkles and a less defined appearance. Fillers restore that lost volume. Juvederm can give you plumper lips, smooth out smile lines, and enhance your cheekbones and overall facial structure.
