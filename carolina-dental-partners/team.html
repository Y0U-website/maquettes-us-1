Get to Know Your Dental Team

At Carolina Dental Partners, we believe healthy smiles start with a positive dental experience. That’s why we’re so focused on providing you with gentle, personalized care. We’re your neighbors in Kershaw and Hartsville, and we want to build a lasting relationship with you. Let us help you achieve your healthiest smile!

Dr. Sharla Price, DMD

A 2010 graduate of the Medical College of Georgia (MCG) School of Dentistry, Dr. Price went above and beyond with a general practice residency at MCG. This residency honed her general dentistry skills and equipped her to provide advanced services like treatment for medically complex patients and IV sedation for anxious patients.

Dr. Price’s expertise extends to oral surgery. She offers a comprehensive range of procedures, from wisdom teeth extractions to root canals, bone grafts, and dental implant placement. Patient safety and well-being are paramount to Dr. Price, and she stays at the forefront of general dentistry through regular continuing education. She is licensed for IV sedation to ensure your comfort during oral surgery procedures.

Outside the office, Dr. Price and her husband are active members of Calvary Baptist Church in Darlington. She enjoys spending time with family in her native Georgia, traveling, and getting lost in a good book.

Dr. Chase Ollis, DMD

Hailing from Hartsville, SC, Dr. Ollis is a Clemson University graduate with a passion for dentistry. After earning his biology degree, he pursued dental education at the University of South Carolina College of Dental Medicine. When he’s not creating smiles, you can find Dr. Ollis playing golf at Hartsville Country Club, fishing, exploring new places with his family, or simply enjoying the community that raised him.
