Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Barefield has two grown children and three grandchildren. In her spare time, she regularly gets involved with activities at the Concord Baptist Church and is a sustaining member of the Junior League of Dallas. She’s also passionate about volunteering as well when she isn’t busy at the dental office; this commitment includes being a provider at H.I.S Bridgebuilders Dental Clinic, Christina’s Smile, Give Kids a Smile, and Texas Mission of Mercy. When it comes to personal hobbies, she enjoys listening to jazz music, quilting, hiking, and traveling.
