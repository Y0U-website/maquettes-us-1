Get to Know Dr. Sharon Hill

Dr. Sharon Hill has three decades of experience in the world of dentistry, and she’s been loving every minute of it. Born and raised in Greensboro, North Carolina, she’s always wanted to create a positive impact in the lives of others. Her favorite thing about her job is getting to know and build relationships with her patients and their families! She loves educating people about their oral health and how to take care of their pearly whites, and she can’t wait to meet new patients looking for exceptional dental care.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

It was after college that Dr. Hill decided to pursue a career as a dentist, but her interest in dentistry peaked when she was in high school. She took a gap year between college and dental school so that she could decide between dentistry and medicine. After great deliberation, she aimed for dentistry, and she’s never looked back since!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Hill studied at the University of North Carolina for both her undergraduate degree and dental doctorate. Currently, she is in full-time academics training dentists to specialize in pediatric dentistry. She started her full-time academics three decades ago but later shifted to full-time private practice to have more family time while maintaining a part-time teaching position. After her youngest child graduated college, she sold her practice and went back to full-time academics. Her focus is teaching and providing dentistry in a hospital setting to patients with special health care needs.

Dr. Hill remains dedicated to seeking continuing education to expand and improve her skills and expertise. Additionally, she is a member of the Texas Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever she isn’t at the office, Dr. Hill enjoys being with her family and her beautiful son and daughter. Other than treating pearly whites and helping people, she also loves animals and has been a pet foster for a rescue organization for about two years. The group she works with saves animals with special medical needs whose treatment extends beyond the resources that local animal shelters can provide. She also enjoys being with her church family on Sundays and doing community outreach!
