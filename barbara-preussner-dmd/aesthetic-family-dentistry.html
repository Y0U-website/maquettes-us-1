Aesthetic & Restorative Solutions

Refresh and restore your smile and your health.

  • Dental Implants Whether you are missing one tooth or multiple teeth, dental implants with our team offer a lasting and natural looking tooth replacement solution.
  • Same Day Crowns Our in-office CEREC device combines CAD/CAM technology with a specialized digital camera, advanced software and a precision milling machine allowing our experienced team to quickly create a natural-looking ceramic crown in one visit.
  • Dentures Whether you need a partial set of dentures or a complete set, our team will assess your situation, make recommendations, answer all your questions, and deliver the option you choose.
  • Bonding Designed to match your natural tooth color, composite bonding beautifully repairs chipped or cracked teeth, reduces gaps or spaces between teeth, and hides discoloration or faded areas on the tooth’s surface.
  • Invisalign Clear Aligners Virtually invisible and removable, Invisalign’s clear aligners help patients achieve a beautifully straight smile without the challenge of traditional braces.
  • Crown & Bridges We custom design and place a crown to permanently cover a decayed, damaged, or cracked tooth. To replace missing teeth, we can also custom fit a dental bridge to fill a space that your tooth previously occupied.
  • Professional Teeth Whitening Professional whitening is one of the quickest solutions to refresh your smile. With professional-grade materials and years of expertise, clinical whitening treatment with our team is far more effective than over-the-counter options.
  • Porcelain Veneers Experience a stunning smile makeover in a short time with ultra-thin veneers designed to minimize any impact on your existing teeth. Bonded to the front of your teeth, these precision crafted teeth covers effectively treat discolored, worn, chipped, broken, misaligned teeth or teeth with gaps.
  • Dermal Fillers (Juvederm, Versa and RHA) For a non-surgical solution, we offer dermal filler options including Juvederm, Versa, and RHA to contour and shape your face for a younger-looking you. They are often used to plump thin lips, enhance shallow contours, smooth wrinkles and even diminish the appearance of scars.
  • Neuromodulators (Botox®, Xeomin, Daxiffy) Botox®, Xeomin, and Daxxify are used to smooth out wrinkles and effectively relieve TMJ symptoms and jaw tension to alleviate headaches, migraines and facial pain. Millions use Neuromodulators to restore a fresher, more youthful appearance.
