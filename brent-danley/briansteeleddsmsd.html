Brian Steele, DDS, MSD

Dr. Steele is a Texarkana native with deep knowledge of his community. He grew up in the dental environment watching his father work hard to help his patients every day and he knew he also wanted to serve and support his community in the same way.

After graduating from Pleasant Grove High School, Dr. Steele went on to attend Harding University, in Searcy Arkansas where he obtained his degree in dentistry at the University of Texas Dental Branch at Houston. He decided to stay at the University and complete his pediatric dental residency where he served as Chief Resident during his second year. Upon completing his residency, he returned home to open his own practice and follow in his father’s footsteps.

Dr. Steele is board certified in pediatric Dentistry and a diplomat of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He is also trained in sedation dentistry for children and delivers gentle and compassionate dental care with a hometown touch.
