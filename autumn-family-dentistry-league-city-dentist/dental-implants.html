The Procedure of Dental Implants

Step-by-Step Implant Placement in League City

Placing dental implants at Autumn Family Dentistry typically involves multiple visits over several months. The process can be broken down into three main steps:

  • During the main surgical visit, a small incision is made in the gums at the site of the missing tooth.
  • The implant screw is placed into the jawbone to act as a stable tooth root.
  • If there isn’t enough bone to support the implant, bone grafting may be performed.
  • After placement, it generally takes 3–6 months for the bone and surrounding tissues to heal and fully integrate with the implant.
  • During this period, a temporary crown or removable partial denture may be used to protect the surgical site and maintain appearance.
  • Once healing is complete, a custom tooth-shaped crown is attached to the implant.
  • The crown is designed to match your natural teeth in color and shape and functions just like a real tooth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
