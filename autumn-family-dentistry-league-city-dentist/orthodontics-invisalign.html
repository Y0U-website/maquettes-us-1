How Clear Aligner Treatment Works

Overview of the Process

Clear aligner treatment uses custom-made trays to gradually move your teeth into proper alignment. Each tray applies gentle, controlled pressure, and your progress is monitored by your dentist to ensure safe, predictable results. The process is convenient, discreet, and can even include optional whitening using the same trays.

Scan & Plan

We order a digital scan of your teeth and create a personalized treatment plan.

Custom Trays

Your trays gently move your teeth in small, precise increments. You’ll wear trays around the clock and progress to new trays about every 2 weeks until your teeth are gradually straightened to your satisfaction.

Monitoring

Track progress with our app and regular in-office check-ins.

Whitening

Use the same trays to safely whiten your teeth while aligning them, if desired.
