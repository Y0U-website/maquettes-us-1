Digital X-Rays

Our state-of-the-art digital X-ray technology provides clear, detailed images to help diagnose and treat dental concerns early. This technology is safe for children, with minimal radiation exposure, and allows us to monitor the development of teeth and jaw structures with precision.

Fluoride Treatments

Dental Sealants

Sealants are a protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of your child’s molars to prevent cavities. This simple, non-invasive procedure is an effective way to keep hard-to-reach areas free from decay.

Tooth Fillings

If cavities do occur, we offer gentle, minimally invasive tooth fillings to restore your child’s teeth. Our child-friendly techniques and materials ensure a natural look and comfortable experience, helping your child get back to their healthy smile quickly.

Soft Tissue Laser Procedures

We are proud to offer advanced soft tissue laser treatments using the Solea laser. This innovative technology is ideal for procedures like tongue and lip tie releases, providing:

  • Precision and minimal discomfort.
  • Faster healing times.
  • An anxiety-free experience, with little to no need for needles or drills.

Space Maintainers

If your child loses a baby tooth prematurely, a space maintainer can help preserve proper spacing for their permanent teeth. This preventive measure ensures that your child’s smile develops as it should, reducing the need for future orthodontic work.

Emergency Dental Care

Accidents happen, and we’re here to provide prompt, compassionate care for dental emergencies. Whether it’s a chipped tooth, severe pain, or another urgent issue, our team is ready to help your child feel better and get back to their day.

Education and Preventive Guidance

At Alpine Pediatric Dentistry, we believe that education is key to maintaining healthy smiles. We take the time to teach children about proper oral hygiene and empower parents with the tools and knowledge they need to support their child’s dental health at home.
