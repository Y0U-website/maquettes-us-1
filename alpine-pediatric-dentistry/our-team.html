Our Philosophy At Alpine Pediatric Dentistry, our team is more than just a group of dental professionals—we’re a family, committed to providing exceptional care for your child’s smile. Located in Lincolnton, NC, we specialize in creating positive dental experiences for children of all ages. Our compassionate team is dedicated to making every visit fun, comfortable, and stress-free, ensuring that kids leave our office with bright, healthy smiles and a love for dental care.

Led by Dr. Warren, a skilled and caring pediatric dentist, our team brings years of expertise and a shared passion for helping children achieve optimal oral health. From our front office staff who greet you with a smile, to our talented dental assistants and hygienists who ensure your child feels at ease, every member of our team is committed to creating a warm and welcoming environment.

Using state-of-the-art technology , including advanced laser procedures for soft tissue treatments like tongue and lip-ties, we provide the highest level of care in a kid-friendly setting. Whether it’s a routine dental check-up, preventative care, or specialized treatment, our team works together to support your child’s oral health journey every step of the way.
