Intraoral Scanner: Accurate Digital Impressions

Gone are the days of messy, uncomfortable dental impressions. With our advanced intraoral scanner, we can capture detailed, accurate digital impressions of your teeth and gums with ease.

  • Comfortable Process: The intraoral scanner uses a small, handheld device to capture precise 3D images of your mouth without the need for traditional impression materials.
  • Fast and Accurate: Digital impressions are not only more comfortable but also more accurate, reducing the need for retakes and ensuring a perfect fit for restorations like crowns, bridges, and Invisalign aligners.
  • Efficient Workflow: The digital impressions can be easily shared with dental labs, speeding up the process and allowing us to deliver your dental restorations more quickly.

Intraoral Camera: Enhanced Diagnosis and Patient Education

Our intraoral camera is a powerful tool that allows us to get an up-close look at your teeth and gums, helping us detect issues early and involve you in your treatment planning.

  • High-Resolution Imaging: The intraoral camera captures clear, high-resolution images of your mouth, highlighting areas of concern such as cavities, gum disease, or cracked teeth.
  • Patient Involvement: We believe that informed patients make better decisions about their oral health. With the intraoral camera, you can see exactly what we see, making it easier to understand your treatment options and why they’re necessary.
  • Early Detection: The detailed images provided by the intraoral camera enable us to spot potential problems before they become more serious, allowing for more conservative and effective treatments.
