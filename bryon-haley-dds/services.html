Dental Services

  • Preventive Care: Prevention is the foundation of our family dentistry services. We offer fluoride treatments, dental sealants, and regular check-ups to keep your teeth and gums healthy. Our goal is to detect and address any potential issues before they become more serious, saving you time and money in the long run.

Restorative Dentistry

When dental issues arise, our restorative dentistry services are here to repair and restore the function and appearance of your teeth. We use the latest techniques and materials to ensure that your restorations are durable, natural-looking, and comfortable.

  • Crowns & Bridges: If you have damaged or missing teeth, crowns and bridges are excellent options for restoring your smile. Crowns are custom-made caps that cover and protect weakened teeth, while bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent healthy teeth.
  • Fillings: Cavities are common, but they don’t have to compromise your smile. We use tooth-colored fillings to repair cavities and restore the strength of your teeth. These fillings blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring a discreet and effective restoration.
  • Scaling & Root Planing: For patients with gum disease, scaling and root planing are deep cleaning procedures that remove plaque and tartar from below the gum line. This treatment helps to prevent further progression of gum disease and promotes healing of the gums.
  • Root Canals: When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or damaged, a root canal can save the tooth and relieve pain. Our root canal treatments are performed with precision and care, ensuring your comfort throughout the procedure.
  • Tooth Extractions: Sometimes, a tooth extraction is necessary to preserve your overall oral health. Whether it’s due to severe decay, damage, or crowding, we perform extractions with gentle care, providing you with clear aftercare instructions to promote a smooth recovery.

Smile Makeovers

Your smile is one of your most important features, and we’re here to help you achieve the smile of your dreams. Our smile makeover services combine various cosmetic and restorative treatments to enhance your smile’s appearance and boost your confidence.

  • Veneers: Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth to correct imperfections such as discoloration, chips, or gaps. They provide a dramatic improvement in the appearance of your smile with minimal alteration to your natural teeth.
  • Teeth Whitening: Brighten your smile with our professional teeth whitening services. We offer both in-office and take-home whitening options that effectively remove stains and discoloration, giving you a radiant, white smile.
