The practice

Missing teeth? Dental implants offer a strong, long-lasting solution that looks and feels like your natural teeth. At 32 Village Dental in Elk Grove Village, we specialize in placing and restoring dental implants to help you regain your smile and confidence.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. After the implant fuses with the bone, a custom crown is attached, creating a tooth replacement that’s stable, natural, and built to last.

Most healthy adults who are missing one or more teeth are good candidates for implants. We’ll review your dental and medical history and may take 3D scans to ensure you have enough bone to support an implant. If not, bone grafting can often prepare your jaw.

When teeth are damaged, decayed, or replaced with implants, crowns are an excellent solution to restore strength, function, and appearance. At 32 Village Dental, we provide custom-made aesthetic and implant crowns that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Crowns are tooth-shaped caps placed over damaged or implant teeth to protect and restore their shape and size. They improve the tooth’s strength and give your smile a smooth, natural look.

If your gums bleed when you brush or feel sore and swollen, you might be dealing with the early stages of gum disease. At 32 Village Dental, we use scaling and root planing , also called deep cleaning, to remove buildup beneath the gum line and help your mouth heal naturally.

The process is done gently, often in stages, and we’ll make sure you’re comfortable the whole time.

Most patients notice a significant improvement in how their mouth feels just days after treatment. Your gums become less tender, and any bleeding usually stops. We’ll also talk to you about caring for your teeth at home to keep your gums healthy moving forward.
