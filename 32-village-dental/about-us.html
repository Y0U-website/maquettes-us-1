Outside the Office

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Philip enjoys spending time with his family, watching action movies, and continuing his dental education. He’s always learning and looking for ways to improve care for his patients.

Dr. Chichao Jin

Dr. Jin received his Bachelor’s degree from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. He then completed his Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the same institution.

Dr. Jin is committed to providing patient care with respect and warmth, He believes that honesty forms the foundation for treatment satisfaction. During treatments, Dr. Jin emphasizes the importance of understanding the “why” behind dental care rather than solely focusing on the “how.”

Outside of his professional practice, Dr. Jin enjoys building model kits and working on small-scale projects. During his undergraduate studies, he conducted research on corn and genetics – feel free to ask him about his research experiences and some funny pictures.
