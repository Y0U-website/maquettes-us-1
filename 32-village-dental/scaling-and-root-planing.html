Why It Matters

Gum disease is one of the most common dental problems, but it often goes unnoticed until it’s more serious. If left untreated, it can cause tooth loss and affect your overall health. Scaling and root planing help reverse early gum disease and prevent it from worsening.

  • Have bleeding or swollen gums
  • Notice bad breath that won’t go away
  • Feel your gums pulling away from your teeth
  • Haven’t had a dental cleaning in a long time

What Happens After Treatment?

Most patients notice a significant improvement in how their mouth feels just days after treatment. Your gums become less tender, and any bleeding usually stops. We’ll also talk to you about caring for your teeth at home to keep your gums healthy moving forward.

Related Services for a Healthy Smile

Pair deep cleaning with other preventive treatments like routine cleanings, fluoride treatments, and digital X-rays to stay ahead of oral health issues. Prevention is the best way to avoid costly dental work down the road.
