Who Is a Candidate?

Most healthy adults who are missing one or more teeth are good candidates for implants. We’ll review your dental and medical history and may take 3D scans to ensure you have enough bone to support an implant. If not, bone grafting can often prepare your jaw.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

  • Consultation & Planning We assess your oral health and create a personalized treatment plan
  • Implant Placement The implant post is placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia
  • Healing Period Over several months, the implant fuses with your bone (osseointegration)
  • Crown Placement A custom crown is attached, completing your new tooth
