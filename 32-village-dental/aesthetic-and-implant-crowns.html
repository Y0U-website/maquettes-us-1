Aesthetic and Implant Crowns

  • Implant Crowns Attached to dental implants, replacing missing teeth with a sturdy, lifelike restoration.

Benefits of Crowns

  • Protect weak or cracked teeth from further damage
  • Restore chewing ability and normal bite
  • Enhance your smile’s appearance with natural color and shape
  • Durable and long-lasting with proper care
  • Prevent sensitivity and discomfort

The Crown Procedure at 32 Village Dental

  • Examination & Preparation We examine your tooth and prepare it by removing decay or shaping it for the crown
  • Impressions Detailed molds or digital scans are taken for a perfect fit
  • Temporary Crown A temporary crown protects the tooth while your permanent crown is made
  • Permanent Crown Placement Once ready, your custom crown is securely bonded in place
  • Final Adjustments We ensure your bite and comfort are perfect before you leave

Why Trust 32 Village Dental for Your Crowns?

Dr. Philip uses advanced materials and techniques to create strong and natural crowns. Our focus on comfort and precision means your crown will feel like your own tooth and last for many years.
