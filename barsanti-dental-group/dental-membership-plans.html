Common Questions About the Care Club

Is this program the same as dental insurance?

No. The Care Club is an in-house membership program, not insurance. It is designed to provide preventive care and savings without third-party restrictions or approvals.

When do my benefits begin?

Your membership becomes active the same day you enroll. There are no waiting periods, allowing you to schedule care right away.

Why is there a lifetime activation fee?

The activation fee helps cover enrollment and administrative costs and includes your first month of membership, reducing upfront expenses.

Can I switch plans if my dental needs change?

Yes. If your oral health status changes, our team will help transition you into the membership tier that best supports your care.
