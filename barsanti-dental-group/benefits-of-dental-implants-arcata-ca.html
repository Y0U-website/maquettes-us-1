What are the dental implant options available?

  • Single-unit dental implants: The dentist pairs a dental crown with a titanium post to replace an individual missing tooth.
  • Implant-supported bridge: This option replaces a few missing teeth in a row. Unlike conventional bridges, we don’t need to shave the neighboring healthy teeth.
  • Implant-supported dentures: For anyone missing all or most teeth in one arch or both, you don’t need an implant for every tooth lost. The dentist uses as few as four implants to support a full mouth restoration. With this innovative option, you can eat and speak without worrying about embarrassing slippage.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

  • Stability: Imagine the freedom of eating or speaking without worrying about shifting or wobbly teeth! That’s the stability you enjoy with dental implants. They are anchored in the jawbone, which makes them secure like natural teeth.
  • Renewed oral function: Speak clearly and eat your favorite foods without holding back. Dental implants make everyday life simpler and more enjoyable.
  • Jawbone stimulation: When you lose teeth, the bone in that area starts to shrink. This creates an aged, sunken-in appearance. Dental implants prevent this by stimulating your jawbone, just like natural teeth.
  • Convenient maintenance: Keeping your implants in excellent shape doesn’t require any special care. Brush and floss them as you would with natural teeth.
  • Stand-alone restoration: To place dental bridges, the dentist has to grind down the adjacent teeth. Dental implants are anchored directly in the jawbone, providing stability without compromising the health of the nearby teeth.
  • Long-lasting fix: Dental implants can last several decades with good maintenance. Unlike dentures, they don’t require adjustments or replacements every few years. While they cost more upfront, the math works in your favor in the long run.
