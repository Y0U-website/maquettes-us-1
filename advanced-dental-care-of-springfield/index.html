If you experience a dental emergency (i.e., a lost tooth or damaged restoration), your first course of action should be to contact us. We prioritize same-day appointments to address your issue promptly.

However, if your dental emergency involves a life-threatening situation like impaired breathing or severe injury, it’s advised to seek immediate help at the nearest emergency room. While they may not have specialized dental equipment, they can stabilize your condition until you can receive the necessary care from us.
