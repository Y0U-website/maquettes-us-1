Full Mouth Rehab

At Center Street Dental, the full mouth rehab is an amazing procedure for those patients needing a full mouth rehabilitation of their entire mouth or just the upper or lower jaw. Generally, these patients have suffered from broken down, diseased, and painful teeth for years. This state-of-the-art procedure begins with a 3D Scan that provides information to the dentist and lab to design your new set of teeth.

At your next visit, the procedure will begin with removing any remaining teeth. The second step is the placement of 4, 5, or even 6 dental implants. These will be anchors for the denture that is secured into place by the dentist. By the end of the visit, you will have your new smile in place. Patients can have their lives transformed by a single procedure.

The Process Behind Your Implants

Getting implant-retained dentures involves a few steps, but they aren’t as scary as they seem.

Our dentist will assess your oral health and create a treatment plan that is customized to your needs. Once we figure out if implant-retained dentures are fitting for you, we will schedule a surgery date.

During the procedure, we will numb the area or provide you with a mild anesthetic so that you are comfortable. The Implants are then surgically placed into your jawbone.

The implants need time to fuse with your jawbone before proceeding to the next step, which is attaching small abutments to them.

Finally, your custom dentures are made to fit over the abutments.
