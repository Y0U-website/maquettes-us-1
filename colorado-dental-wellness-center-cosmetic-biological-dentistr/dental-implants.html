WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF DENTAL IMPLANTS?

There are a variety of tooth replacement options available to patients, such as bridges and dentures. However, dental implants offer unique benefits, such as:

  • Tooth implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth.
  • Unlike dentures, implants aren’t removable. They permanently restore your smile.
  • You can care for your implants just like natural teeth with good at-home care and regular checkups.
  • A full set of teeth completes your smile and improves your self-confidence.
  • Because they are made of biocompatible titanium, implants reverse the bone tissue loss that occurs after the loss of one or more teeth. This can help restore or maintain the natural look and shape of your face.
