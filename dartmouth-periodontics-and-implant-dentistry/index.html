The practice

Periodontal wellness starts with early detection and prevention. By monitoring your gums closely and treating inflammation before it progresses, we help protect the bone and tissues that support your teeth—supporting both a healthy smile and better overall health.

Tissue grafts and regenerative therapies are designed to rebuild and protect the gums and bone that support your teeth. Using advanced techniques and materials, we can cover exposed roots, strengthen weakened areas, and promote healing—helping you maintain comfort, stability, and a healthier smile over time.

LANAP LASER THERAPY DIGITAL RADIOGRAPHS & CBCT FOR DIAGNOSIS OPIOID-FREE PAIN MANAGEMENT OPTIONS LANAP laser therapy is a minimally invasive treatment that uses targeted laser energy to remove diseased tissue and bacteria while preserving healthy gum tissue. This advanced approach can reduce discomfort, speed healing, and help save teeth that might otherwise be lost to periodontal disease.

We use digital radiographs and CBCT imaging to create detailed 3D views of your teeth, bone, and gums. This advanced imaging helps us detect periodontal disease earlier, evaluate bone quality for dental implants, and plan treatment with a high level of precision, all while keeping radiation exposure lower than traditional X-rays.

We offer opioid-free pain management options, including Stellalife oral care products, to keep you comfortable before and after treatment. These plant-based rinses and gels are designed to reduce inflammation and soreness, support natural healing, and lower the need for narcotic medications, so you can recover with fewer side effects and a clearer mind.
