The practice

You can now restore your missing tooth permanently with a dental implant! If you are missing a whole set of your teeth, we have a permanent solution for that too! The dental procedure is implant-supported dentures. To know how these implants work, click the read more button.

Tracy Arch Dental, based out of Tracy in California, offers you the best of everything in dentistry. Our expert staff members are super friendly and warm with several years of years in dental care. We specialize in providing dental cleanings, restorations, replacement surgeries, gum disease treatment, and cosmetic enhancements.

Dr. Bipinjit Kaur focuses on offering you a personalized dental care while leveraging the latest in modern technology and innovative procedures that deliver a superior patient experience. Dr. Bipinjit Kaur along with her expert dental care team will work with you to prepare a personalized plan for your optimal dental care.

Our mission is to provide our patients with the highest quality dental care while building long-term and positive relationships.
