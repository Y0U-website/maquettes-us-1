Dental Services in Bryan, TX

Our Dental Services

From preventive care to restorative solutions, we’re dedicated to keeping your smile healthy.

Preventative Care

Our preventative services include regular exams, professional cleanings, and personalized hygiene guidance designed to catch issues early and keep your smile healthy. Using state-of-the-art diagnostic tools, our team helps you maintain optimal oral health and avoid the need for more extensive treatments later on.

New Patient Exams & Consultations

Our thorough new patient exams and consultations help lay the foundation for excellent oral health. At Arents Family Dentistry, we carefully review your dental history, perform comprehensive examinations, and discuss personalized preventive strategies to ensure you start your journey with confidence.

Dental Cleanings & Prophylaxis

Regular dental cleanings and prophylaxis are key to maintaining a healthy, sparkling smile. Our skilled hygienists utilize gentle, state-of-the-art techniques to remove plaque and tartar buildup, helping prevent cavities and gum disease while keeping your smile fresh in Bryan, TX.

Fluoride Treatments & Sealants

Boost your enamel’s strength with our fluoride treatments and sealants, designed to protect against decay. These preventive measures provide an extra layer of defense for both children and adults, reducing the risk of cavities and ensuring long-term oral health.

Restorative Treatments

When tooth decay, damage, or loss affects your smile, our restorative treatments—ranging from high-quality fillings to custom crowns and bridges—bring back functionality and natural appearance. We tailor our care plans to ensure both a comfortable fit and a beautiful result, restoring your confidence one tooth at a time.

Composite Fillings

When decay or damage impacts your teeth, our composite filling solutions restore both functionality and a natural appearance. Using tooth-colored materials, we carefully match and repair affected areas to bring back the strength and beauty of your smile.

Crowns & Bridges

Our custom crowns and bridges are expertly crafted to replace damaged or missing teeth, restoring your bite and enhancing aesthetics. By blending advanced technology with skilled artistry, we help rebuild a seamless, enduring smile that feels as natural as it looks.

Denture & Partial Denture Solutions

For patients needing tooth replacement, our denture and partial denture solutions provide comfort, improved function, and a renewed sense of confidence. We design these restorations with precision to ensure a secure, natural fit that supports everyday activities.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile with a variety of cosmetic treatments such as professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and comprehensive smile makeovers. Our approach balances art and technology to create a look that is both natural and radiant, ensuring you leave with improved aesthetics and self-assurance.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten and refresh your smile with our professional teeth whitening treatments. Designed to remove years of staining and discoloration, our safe, effective whitening services create a noticeably whiter smile and boost your overall confidence.

Porcelain Veneers

Enhance the appearance of your smile with custom porcelain veneers that correct imperfections such as chips, gaps, and discoloration. Each veneer is individually crafted to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth for a beautiful, lasting smile transformation.

Cosmetic Bonding & Contouring

Our cosmetic bonding and contouring services offer a minimally invasive solution to reshape minor imperfections and improve your smile’s overall harmony. By applying a tooth-colored resin, we can smooth, reshape, or fill areas for a more attractive and balanced look.

Smile Makeovers

For a comprehensive transformation, our smile makeovers integrate various cosmetic techniques to overhaul your smile’s appearance. We work closely with you to design a personalized treatment plan that improves aesthetics, boosts self-confidence, and highlights your natural beauty.

Endodontics

Our endodontic care focuses on preserving your natural teeth through precise root canal therapy and related treatments. By gently removing infected tissue and sealing canals, we effectively alleviate pain and prevent further damage while prioritizing your comfort throughout the process.

Root Canal Therapy

Our expert root canal therapy provides relief from painful infections while preserving your natural tooth. Using modern, minimally invasive techniques, we remove infected pulp, disinfect the canal system, and seal the tooth to restore its long-term function.

Endodontic Retreatment

When initial root canal treatment does not achieve the desired results, our endodontic retreatment services offer a second chance at saving your tooth. We reassess and carefully address persistent infections or complications to ensure renewed comfort and lasting dental health.

Periodontics

Dedicated to maintaining healthy gums, our periodontic services involve thorough evaluations, scaling and root planing, and advanced treatments to manage gum disease. Early intervention and ongoing care help protect your teeth and support your overall oral health, ensuring a strong foundation for your smile.

Periodontal Maintenance & Scaling

Regular periodontal maintenance and scaling are essential for keeping your gums healthy. Our preventive cleaning treatments remove plaque and calculus from below the gumline, reducing inflammation and preventing the progression of periodontal disease.

Gum Disease Treatment

We offer comprehensive gum disease treatments to manage and reverse periodontal conditions. With advanced diagnostic tools and tailored therapies, our approach targets the causes of gum disease, helping restore gum health and secure your smile’s foundation.

Laser Periodontal Therapy

Utilizing state-of-the-art laser technology, our laser periodontal therapy offers a precise and minimally invasive option to treat gum disease. This modern treatment reduces bleeding and discomfort while effectively removing diseased tissue and promoting faster healing.

Oral Surgery

We offer a range of oral surgical procedures—from simple extractions to more complex interventions—to address various dental challenges. With a focus on patient safety and comfort, our carefully planned surgical treatments help ensure smooth recovery and lasting results.

Tooth Extractions

Our gentle tooth extraction services ensure a smooth and comfortable removal of compromised or problematic teeth. With careful planning and modern techniques, we minimize discomfort and pave the way for restorative options that preserve your overall oral function.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

For patients experiencing pain or crowding, our wisdom teeth removal procedure offers safe and effective relief. We perform this common oral surgery with precision and care, reducing complications and ensuring a comfortable recovery for a healthier mouth.

Advanced Oral Surgery

Our advanced oral surgery services encompass complex extractions, bone grafting, and other surgical treatments tailored to your unique needs. With a commitment to safety and minimal discomfort, our experienced team ensures effective treatment and a smooth, speedy recovery.
