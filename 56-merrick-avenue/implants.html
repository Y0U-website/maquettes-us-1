Implants

Implant dentistry is the standard of care for missing teeth. At Polished Dental Care, we will help create a patient centered treatment plan that will address your aesthetic and functional needs. Dental implants are a great way to replace missing teeth. The actual dental implant can be thought of as a root replacement. The "fake" root is placed in the bone, which allows a future crown (tooth) to be attached to the root.
