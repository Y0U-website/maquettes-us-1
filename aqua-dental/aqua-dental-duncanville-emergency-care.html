  • Lost or broken dental restorations (fillings, crowns, etc.)
  • Abscessed teeth
  • Gum infections
  • Facial trauma

Emergency Dental Visit:

When you come in for an emergency dental visit, our team will first assess the severity of your condition and provide you with a treatment plan. We will then work quickly to alleviate your pain and address the underlying cause of your dental emergency. Our goal is to get you back to your normal routine as soon as possible.

Patient Comfort:

At Aqua Dental, patient comfort is our top priority, even in emergency situations. Our water-themed dental office provides a relaxing and calming atmosphere to help patients feel at ease. We also offer various amenities such as TV and music to help patients feel more comfortable during their visit.
