  • Scaling and cleaning. While brushing and flossing at home is a great help in limiting plaques and biofilms, only the professional tools and training of your dental team can remove tartar (calculus) and thoroughly clean your teeth. (Tartar is a hardened plaque that cannot be removed with home-based methods.) To remove tartar, your hygienist will use either a manual or ultrasonic scaler. They will also clean below the gumline using a root planer, a tool similar to a dental scaler, but is specialized for use in cleaning the area of the tooth below the gumline.
  • Periodontal charting. You have wondered what the hygienist is doing when they probe your gums and read out a series of numbers. This type of examination is known as periodontal charting. What the hygienist is looking for is the probing pocket depth (PPD) and bleeding on probing (BoP), which could indicate periodontal disease. They will also look for signs of pus production (suppuration), which could indicate a localized infection. The numbers the hygienist reads off indicate the level of inflammation; the higher the number, the more inflammation may be present.
  • X-ray examination. While visual examinations allow the dentist to diagnose issues on the surfaces of teeth, only through X-ray and CBCT scans can we detect issues below the gums, inside the tooth, and around the jawbone. These types of scans are critically important as most cavities occur between teeth and below the gums (these are also the areas that can be helped with regular flossing).
  • Polishing. Once your teeth have been examined and cleaned, the next step is to polish the tooth surfaces. While this process does help remove surface stains, which can help improve your smile’s appearance, the main purpose is to smooth the surface of the tooth so that it is easier to clean when brushing and flossing. The tool used for this procedure is known as a prophy cup. The polishing compound contains mildly abrasive compounds and fluoride, which work together to smooth and protect the tooth.

Before You Leave...

Before you leave, the dentist or hygienist will share recommendations for you to follow at home. They will also recommend a date to schedule your next checkup and cleaning.
