Meet The Team

Rocío

Rocío is a dental assistant with Bright Smile Family Dental. Over the past four years working in dentistry, she has developed a great passion for the field. Rocio says her favorite part of the job is delivering a great dental experience for patients.

Jacky

As the front desk coordinator, Jacky will likely be the first person to greet you as you walk through the doors of Bright Smile Family Dental. Jacky has worked in the dentistry field for three years and enjoys working with patients. Jacky’s daily goal is to ensure each patient arrives happy and leaves with a smile on their face.

Sami

Sami is our office manager at Bright Smile Family Dental. Sami actually started out as a dental assistant and has four years of experience in dentistry. She enjoys applying her skills and contributing to the advancements that are made each at our Blue Island practice. In addition to her hands-on experience, Sami also holds several certificates in dentistry.

Dr. Ronnie Ata

Dr. Ronnie Ata was born and raised in the Chicagoland area. In 2014, Dr. Ata graduated summa cum laude from Benedictine University. he would go on to earn his doctor of dental surgery (DDS) degree from Marquette University in 2018. He continuously strives to improve the quality of care the overall dental experience for patients. Dr. Ata is an active member of the Chicago Dental Society, American Dental Association, and Illinois State Dental Society. Along with the Bright Smile Family Dental team, Dr. Ata looks forward to meeting you and fulfilling all of your oral health needs.
