We are licensed and regulated by the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC). Students who graduate from Dallas Dental Assistant School receive their Dental Assistant Certificate of Completion. Additionally, students receive their BLS (CPR) certification in class through the American Heart Association.

After graduating from our program you will be sent a link to take state RDA license online, and once you are licensed you will be an officially Registered Dental Assistant.
