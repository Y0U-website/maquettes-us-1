We believe instilling confidence in young patients is key to ensuring long-term good oral health outcomes. So we go out of our way to partner with parents and caretakers on the common goal of building a child’s confidence through delivering a positive experience on each and every visit to our office.

This starts with making your child feel comfortable during their visit. So we pay attention to the mental and emotional needs of young patients alongside their dental needs. This is important to the success of the child’s first visit and every visit thereafter because positive healthcare experiences early on in a child’s life can help ensure better, long-term outcomes even outside of dentistry.

Les soins

We may recommend that rather than coming in every 6 months, your child would benefit from more frequent visits to reinforce their home care and newly acquired skills as well as enable us to focus on prevention of caries with you and your child.

Keep in mind that certain words like needle , pain, or extraction can trigger anxiety in children as well so try to avoid using words like these when referring to your dental visit.
