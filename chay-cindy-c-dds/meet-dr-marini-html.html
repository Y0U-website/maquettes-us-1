Meet Dr. Joseph Marini

Dr. Joseph Marini, D.M.D., comes highly recommended for his unique ability to simplify patient treatment experiences by delivering a wide variety of services all under one roof. From endodontics to both the surgical and restorative phases of dental implants, Dr. Marini has you covered.

Patient comfort is Dr. Marini’s motivating factor, which is why he is committed to utilizing the latest advancements in dental technology. He has extensive training in computer-guided dental implant placement and has developed techniques that ensure precision and efficiency. Dr Marini also utilizes therapeutic Botox injections to treat patients with TMJ eliminating the need for uncomfortable mouth guards.

Dr. Marini is known for his keen eye to detail. Patients find him easy to talk to, calming, and feel as though they are in good hands when in Dr. Marini’s dental chair. He assures that those who step into his practice are well-listened to and leave with a smile makeover that they can be proud of.

Dr. Marini received his Doctor of Medical Dentistry (D.M.D.) from the Fairleigh Dickinson College of Dental Medicine in 1987. He was inducted as an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry in 2002 and remains an active member.
