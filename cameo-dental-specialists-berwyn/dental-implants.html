Our 5-Step Dental Implant Process

At Cameo Dental Specialists, we ensure a seamless experience for our patients from start to finish. Our multi-step dental implant process is designed for maximum precision and patient comfort.

Our specialists begin with a comprehensive consultation, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess the health of your jawbone and determine the best treatment plan. During this visit, we’ll discuss your goals and provide a clear understanding of what to expect throughout the process.

In a minor surgical procedure, we place the titanium post securely into the jawbone. This procedure is performed with local anesthesia, though sedation options are available for those who prefer additional comfort.

The implant takes a few months to bond with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This step is essential for achieving a solid foundation for the new tooth.

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant post. The abutment is the base for your new tooth, connecting the post to the final crown.

The final step involves fitting a custom-made crown onto the abutment. This crown is crafted to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, providing a flawless finish.
