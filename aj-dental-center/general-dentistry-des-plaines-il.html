Building Healthy Habits at Home

Your Role in a Lifetime of Healthy Smiles

Great oral health is built both in-office and at home. That’s why we emphasize education and guidance to help you maintain excellent hygiene between visits.

Our team is always here to answer questions and support your daily routine.

  • Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss at least once per day
  • Drink water regularly, especially after meals
  • Eat a balanced diet, low in sugar and rich in calcium
  • Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol
  • Replace toothbrushes every 3 4 months
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth

Frequently Asked General Dentistry Questions in Des Plaines

  • Make Your Dental Visit Today! Most patients benefit from visits every six months. If you have gum disease or other oral health concerns, Dr. Jabbar may recommend more frequent appointments.
  • What’s Included in a Routine Dental Visit? A typical visit includes a professional cleaning, a thorough exam, X-rays (if necessary), and personalized oral hygiene advice.
  • Do You Treat Children at Your Dental Practice? Yes, Dr. Jabbar and our team provide dental care for children of all ages. Early visits help establish good oral hygiene habits, prevent dental issues, and ensure that your child’s teeth are developing properly.
  • What Insurance Plans Do You Accept? We accept a wide variety of dental insurance plans at Dr. Jabbar’s office. Please call our office to check if your specific plan is accepted, or visit our website for more details on accepted insurance providers.
  • What if I Don’t Have Dental Insurance? We offer flexible payment options and will work with you to find a solution that fits your budget. Call our office to learn more.
  • What Is General Dentistry? General dentistry involves the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of a wide range of dental conditions. It includes routine care such as checkups, cleanings, fillings, and dental exams. Dr. Jabbar and our team focus on maintaining overall oral health for patients of all ages.
  • Why Are Regular Dental Checkups Important? Routine dental checkups are essential for preventing oral health issues like cavities, gum disease, and tooth loss. By visiting Dr. Jabbar regularly, we can catch problems early, saving you time, money, and discomfort in the long run.
  • What Are Dental Sealants, and Do I Need Them? Dental sealants are thin coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth to prevent cavities. They are especially beneficial for children and teenagers who may have trouble brushing their molars thoroughly. Dr. Jabbar can determine if sealants are a good option for you or your child.
  • What Should I Do if I Have a Dental Emergency? If you’re experiencing a dental emergency such as a toothache, chipped tooth, or lost filling, contact Dr. Jabbar’s office immediately. We offer same-day emergency care to help alleviate pain and prevent further damage to your teeth.
  • What Are Dental Fillings? Dental fillings are used to repair cavities or other damage to a tooth. Dr. Jabbar offers tooth-colored fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring that your smile remains beautiful and functional.
  • What Is Gum Disease, and How Can It Be Prevented? Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, is an infection of the gums caused by plaque buildup. It can lead to tooth loss if untreated. Brushing and flossing daily, along with regular visits to Dr. Jabbar, are key to preventing gum disease.
  • What Should I Do to Keep My Teeth Healthy at Home? To maintain healthy teeth and gums, brush at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and avoid sugary snacks and drinks. Drinking plenty of water and eating a balanced diet also contributes to good oral health. Dr. Jabbar can offer personalized tips during your visits.
  • What Is Fluoride Treatment, and Do I Need It? Fluoride treatments help strengthen your teeth and prevent cavities. Dr. Jabbar often recommends fluoride for children, seniors, and adults at higher risk of tooth decay.
  • What Age Should My Child Start Seeing the Dentist? We recommend that children have their first dental visit by the age of one, or within six months of their first tooth appearing. Early visits with Dr. Jabbar help your child feel comfortable at the dentist and set a foundation for a lifetime of good oral health.
