Your First Visit

Your First Visit A Guide to Your First Visit How To Prepare Quality dental care requires a team effort. We understand your first visit to a new dentist can be stressful, but we want to provide you with a few suggestions to help you relax and to help us maximize our doctors’ time to address your dental health needs.

Schedule your first appointment on a date and time when you’ll be most relaxed. Complete your medical history and patient information forms prior to your appointment.

A Quick Run-Down of What to Expect 01. Timeframe Your first visit should take approximately 1.5 hours.

02. Arriving

Upon arrival, you will be greeted by our friendly front office staff. After we process your patient information, you’ll be escorted into one of our private treatment rooms.

03. Questions

We will listen to your dental health needs, concerns, and expectations. The dental examination will only begin after all your questions have been answered and you are completely comfortable with proceeding.

04. Imaging

We will take required x-rays. We offer digital x-rays with instantaneous results at 90 % less radiation than traditional radiography systems.

05. Cleaning

One of our talented hygienists will perform a comprehensive general assessment and cleaning of your teeth.

06. Exam

Your doctor will then provide a comprehensive dental examination. This involves screening for oral cancer, periodontal charting to assess the condition of your gums, and a tooth-by-tooth charting to identify and record the current condition of your teeth.

07. Coverage

We are currently preferred providers for most PPO insurances including Aetna, Delta Dental, Guardian, Humana, Metlife, and Assurant Dental Insurance. Our office will help you file any necessary paperwork and assist you in verifying your insurance benefits. Please call or email us if you are unsure about which coverage you have.

08. Payment

Whether copay or self-pay, we are equipped to process your payment quickly and securely. We currently accept American Express, Mastercard, Visa, Discover, cash, check, and care credit (please call our office for more information regarding the promotional financing options available using care credit).

09. Follow-up

We will then schedule your next appointment for preventive care or for a follow-up procedure with your dentist.

We look forward to meeting you

We want to thank you for giving us the opportunity to serve you and your dental needs. We promise to work hard to ensure your first visit is the prelude to a trusting and long relationship. During your first visit, our goal is to make you as comfortable as possible, to provide you with an honest assessment of your dental health, and to help you understand any treatment you require.
