Dr. Madison Butler

Dr. Madison Butler Dr. Madison F. Butler Dr. Madison F. Butler graduated from the University of Georgia with a Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Sciences and received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry in Birmingham, Alabama. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, the Hinman Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Butler values open communication and building strong relationships with her patients to ensure that her patients feel comfortable and confident in her care. Whether it’s a routine check-up, a restorative procedure, or cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Butler is committed to delivering exceptional dental experiences that prioritize the needs of her patients.

Dr. Butler and her husband grew up in Johns Creek, Georgia, but they now call the Canton area home. In her free time, Dr. Butler enjoys spending time with her husband and daughter, walks with her rescue dog, and visiting local coffee shops.
