Dr. Kristen McDonald

Dr. Kristen McDonald Dr. Kristen McDonald Dr. Kristen F. McDonald graduated from The Medical College of Georgia, School of Dentistry in May, 2007 and completed her undergraduate work at The University of Georgia in 2002. While in school, she had the opportunity to participate in internships providing dentistry to persons in Peru, Hawaii and in medically under-served counties throughout Georgia.

Dr. McDonald was born in Naples, Florida, but both she and her husband grew up in the Alpharetta area. She joined Cherokee Family Dental in 2015 and truly enjoys all aspects of dentistry, especially cosmetic. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, & The Hinman Dental Society. She is a Georgia Board of Dentistry approved dentist to administer injectables such as Botox and Dysport.

Dr McDonald is a busy mother of two sons and one daughter and when not in the office, she enjoys spending time with her family, as well as spending time on water, be it lake or ocean!
