Services We Offer

Emergency Dental Treatment

We can treat a variety of traumatic dental injuries, including teeth that have been chipped, moved or knocked out entirely. Please call our office for assistance.

Crowns

Crowns are a great option to repair and reinforce teeth that are decayed, broken, or weak, as well as teeth that have had a root canal completed.

Bridges

With a similar procedure to a crown, bridges restore the function and appearance of the mouth by replacing missing teeth.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers can enhance the esthetics of your smile by correcting cosmetic flaws and overall improving the appearance of the teeth.

Dentures

We offer both Partial and Full denture options, we use these removable appliance to replace missing teeth and restore the appearance and function of your smile.

Implant Restorations

An implant is great for replacing missing teeth that have been lost through extensive decay or trauma. Implants can feel like a natural tooth and function long term.

Professional Whitening Products

We offer professional grade at home whitening products that can help you achieve your perfect smile in a convenient, effective way, with minimal to no sensitivity!

Extractions

Teeth are commonly extracted in cases of severe damage or decay; this procedure provides patients with relief from pain, bacterial infections, and improves the overall health of the mouth. Extractions can range from simple to surgical.

Preventative Care

Prevention is the best way to maintain optimal oral health. Routine dental visits help our team to diagnose any issues that may arise. This way, we can treat potential issues in a timely manner that can save you more extensive and costly treatment down the road. Regular dental visits maintain overall well-being and confidence within you and your family’s smiles!

Comprehensive Oral Examination

Aspire Dental uses comprehensive exams to establish a relationship with our patients. During this exam we diagnose all complications of the mouth and create a personalized treatment plan to help you accomplish your smile goals.

Routine Cleanings

Annual care can range from 3 to 6 months. Prophylaxis, or cleanings, are crucial to maintaining the health of the tissue surrounding your teeth. Our expert hygienists remove any tartar, plaque, or build-up that can harm your gum tissue.

Radiographs

Also known as x-rays, are essential to the diagnosis of your teeth, structures surrounding your teeth, and jaw bones. Our office uses the latest digital radiation equipment and technology to minimize the amount of radiation for both you and our team.

Oral Cancer Screenings

A visual and physical evaluation of the tissues inside and outside of your mouth to look for any signs of cancerous or precancerous lesions.

Oral Hygiene Instructions

Completed during or after a cleaning, our expert hygienists will communicate with patients a personalized homecare routine to help optimize their oral health at home.

Therapies

At Aspire Health Dental, we provide a wide range of therapeutic services designed to address various dental needs and promote overall oral health. Our expert team utilizes the latest techniques and advanced technologies to deliver personalized care.

Periodontal (gum) Disease Therapy

This therapy may be indicated when a patient is diagnosed with periodontal disease. Other factors such as genetics and environment are considered when confirming diagnosis. Also referred to as a deep cleaning, periodontal disease therapy is necessary to remove years of build-up calculus, or tartar that inhibiting gum health. Once completed, routine maintenance may be necessary every three to four months to prevent disease from increasing.

TMJ/TMD Treatment

Pain or discomfort with the temporomandibular joint (jaw joint) can affect day-to-day function of the head, neck and mouth. Our office works side by side with Dr. Marvin Eberts, Chiropractor, and our physical therapists to treat any disorder that is present with laser therapy, counterstrain and chiropractic treatment.

If you have a life-threatening or severe injury, call 911 or go directly to the nearest hospital emergency room.
