Our Mission Statement

To fulfill this mission, we are committed to:

  • Listening to those we are privileged to serve.
  • Earn the trust and respect of patients, profession and community.
  • Exceed your expectations.
  • Ensure a creative, challenging and compassionate professional environment.
  • Strive for continuous improvement at all levels.

Hi! I’m Dr. Kyle Perkins!

Learn more about me!

I bring a unique combination of expertise and compassion to Aspire Dental. My extensive training in specialized procedures, including new smile makeovers, dental implants, wisdom teeth removal, grafting, and sedation dentistry, allows me to provide comprehensive care for patients with complex dental needs. Growing up on a ranch in Pagosa Springs, Colorado, I have developed the work ethic and dedication that define my approach to patient care today.

My path to dentistry began as a collegiate basketball player at the University of Colorado in Colorado Springs before transferring to the University of Utah in Salt Lake City. After earning my undergraduate degree in Emergency Medical Services Management, I continued at the University of Utah School of Dentistry, where I graduated with honors. My commitment to excellence led me to complete an intensive 18-month advanced training program focused on full mouth implant reconstructive surgery, prosthetics, and IV sedation.

Patients consistently provide praise for my gentle approach and ability to make even the most anxious patients feel comfortable. My expertise in sedation dentistry particularly benefits those who have avoided dental care due to fear or anxiety. Whether performing a complex All-on-4 procedure or a routine examination, I take time to listen to each patient’s concerns and develop personalized treatment plans. At Aspire Dental, I’ll combine state-of-the-art technology with compassionate care to create beautiful, healthy smiles for patients throughout North Dakota.

Hi! I’m Dr. Sammy Abdullah!

Learn more about me!

Dr. Sammy grew up in Grapevine, Texas, and completed his undergraduate studies at Southern Methodist University. Before entering the dental field, he owned and operated an automotive performance shop where he developed advanced mechanical and technical skills.

He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine. Since graduating, Dr. Sammy has been practicing in North Dakota and we are very fortunate to have him as part of our team.

Dr. Sammy has completed more than 800 hours of surgical continuing education and is an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. He is also actively pursuing board certification with the American Board of Oral Implantology, having successfully passed Part I of the written examination and preparing to complete Part II.

He is highly proficient in digital dentistry, comprehensive care, and dental prosthetics, allowing him to deliver advanced, predictable, and patient centered treatment outcomes.

Outside of the dental field, Dr. Sammy is an avid snowboarder, enjoys tinkering with engine components, and spends much of his free time hiking and exploring the outdoors.

Dr. Sammy is committed to providing high quality, compassionate care, and he brings unmatched dedication and surgical expertise to every patient he serves.

Hi! I’m Dr. Stephen Migliore!

Learn more about me!

Born and raised in the vibrant city of New Orleans, Dr. Migliore brings an unwavering passion for patient care to the world of dentistry. A proud graduate of Louisiana State University School of Dentistry, Dr. Migliore has dedicated his career to mastering the art and science of implant dentistry.

At Aspire Dental, Dr. Migliore blends clinical excellence with advanced technology, using tools like 3D printing and digital scanning to enhance precision, efficiency, and the overall patient experience. He is passionate about creating healthy, beautiful smiles that not only look great but function seamlessly—improving his patients’ quality of life one smile at a time. Above all, Dr. Migliore is committed to delivering the highest level of care with compassion and a patient-first mindset.

Outside the office, Dr. Migliore is an adventurer at heart. He enjoys traveling the world, snowboarding, hiking scenic trails, scuba diving, drumming, playing sports, and cheering on his favorite teams. His passion for learning extends beyond dentistry—he’s constantly exploring, growing, and bringing new energy into both his personal life and his dental practice.
