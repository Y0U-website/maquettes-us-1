Meet Our All Star Cast!

Kimberly Ardalan

Chief Financial Officer

Kimberly Ardalan was the office manager at Ardalan Pediatric Dentistry since before the office was even built. She was instrumental in overseeing the construction and design of the office prior to its doors ever opening. Kimberly attended both Florida Atlantic University and Barry University where she graduated at the top of her class and received her Bachelor’s Degree. Kimberly’s exceptional business background makes her an integral part of the team. Recently, Kimberly has taken on the role of Chief Financial Officer, as she oversees the growth of the practice.

After being born in Miami, Kimberly moved to Boca Raton, Florida at age 1. She spent most of her life in Boca Raton until moving to Port St. Lucie with her husband. In September 2008, Kimberly gave birth to her first child, Aidan Dean Ardalan. In 2010, she welcomed her second child, Layla Marguerite Ardalan, and followed that with the birth of Noelle Grace Ardalan in 2012. When she’s not taking care of the office, she enjoys working out, ice skating, rollerblading and spending time with her family.
