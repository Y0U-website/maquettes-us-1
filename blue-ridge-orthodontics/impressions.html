Impressions

Blue Ridge Orthodontics has developed Impressions, an at-home treatment based on the proven Invisalign® system for improving smiles.

Your Impressions Routine

You’ll wear each set of trays for 20 to 22 hours a day for about one week, removing the trays to eat, drink, brush and floss. You’ll change to the next trays in the series when the old trays feel loose and as if they are not moving your teeth anymore.

Everyone’s smile is different, but most people will complete their Impressions treatment within six to nine months.

Keeping That New Smile

You’ll also receive a set of retainers to help keep your smile healthy and beautiful after your treatment is complete. Upgrade to Vivera® retainers, made by Invisalign and mailed right to you, with four spare sets. You’ll wear these at night to keep your new smile just right.

You also have the option of choosing permanent retainers that are always there and aren’t visible, to help you maintain that perfect smile for a lifetime.

How Do I Get Started with Impressions?

To find out if Impressions is right for you, schedule a complimentary initial consultation today. We’ll perform an exam and scan your smile with our state-of-the-art Itero scanner. If Impressions is a good treatment option for you, your treatment plan will be designed by a top 1 % Invisalign provider – BRO! We’ll send you a complete set of Invisalign trays – made from Invisalign’s best material in six weeks. You’ll receive up to 25 aligners and have one refinement scan built in.

What if I Want More Check-in Appointments?

Of course, if you have an issue or need an exam as you manage your at-home treatment, we’ll be happy to schedule a special appointment for you for a simple consultative fee. You also have the option to upgrade to comprehensive Invisalign at any time during your treatment.

If you already know you want more appointments, but don’t want to pay the full price of Invisalign, keep reading to learn about Impressions+, the more advanced version of Impressions that includes 6 office visits.

Impressions+

The more advanced version of our at-home treatment.

Impressions+ is for the person that wants top-quality treatment but doesn’t want to pay full price for comprehensive treatment, or who doesn’t have the time to come to our office every 6-8 weeks. Impressions+ provides the perfect balance of appointments with your doctor, for 25 % less than comprehensive Invisalign .

Correct more smile issues, for less with Impressions+

More Appointments

Impressions+ includes fewer trips to the office than Invisalign, but more than Impressions. With the six appointments included, you can feel secure that your smile will be on track with the treatment plan, but not inconvenienced every 6-8 weeks to come to see us. Your trusted Blue Ridge Orthodontist will be there by your side to track your progress, tackle any potential issues, and answer whatever questions you may have along the way.

More Advanced Treatment

No longer only for patients with mild orthodontic issues, Impressions+ can treat up to severe spacing, crowding, over and underbite issues. How is this possible? We’ve added the option to add attachments to your teeth and the aligners. Giving our clear aligner trays more to hold on to, therefore, more of an ability to shift and move your teeth into the desired position. Before, this was only possible with full-comprehensive Invisalign.
