Smile Alignment with Care

Orthodontics is a dental specialty focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating irregularities in the alignment of teeth and jaws. Orthodontic treatment typically involves the use of braces, clear aligners (CAs), and other appliances to gradually move teeth into their proper positions, improving both dental function and appearance. This field not only enhances the aesthetics of a smile but also contributes to better oral health by addressing issues such as crowding, gaps, overbites, and underbites.

At 941 Dental Studio we work hard to create a balanced, functional bite and a harmonious facial profile. CAs allow us to position the teeth in a more favorable position before restorative procedures allowing us the ability to offer you a more conservative treatment option. As a result, we are able to achieve longevity, serviceability, and outcome for dental restoration.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners (CAs) are a modern orthodontic treatment option that uses a series of custom-made, transparent trays to gradually straighten teeth. These removable, nearly invisible aligners are worn over the teeth and are an alternative to traditional metal braces, offering a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a well-aligned smile.

CA trays are designed based on digital scans or impressions of your teeth, and they’re worn for most of the day, typically 20-22 hours, and are changed every 1-2 weeks as your teeth move. Benefits of CA include ease of oral hygiene, less dental emergencies, and not having to sacrifice the foods you love since they’re removable. Cons are you have to be complainant and wear the trays as much as possible for the best results.
