The practice

Our Sarasota dental office at 5643 Clark Road serves patients from across the south side of the city including Palmer Ranch, Gulf Gate Estates, Siesta Key, Osprey, Bee Ridge, and Nokomis. We are four minutes from I-75 with free on-site parking and flexible appointment times.

General dentistry at 941 Dental Studio includes comprehensive exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, oral cancer screenings, and tooth-colored fillings.

Our digital X-ray system produces images instantly and emits up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film, allowing Dr. Roque to detect cavities, hairline fractures, and early-stage bone loss that are invisible to the naked eye. Routine preventive visits every six months are the most cost-effective way to protect your oral health long-term.

Cosmetic dentistry at 941 Dental Studio includes professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and composite bonding, all customized to your facial structure, skin tone, and smile goals.

Dr. Roque takes a conservative approach to cosmetic work: the goal is always to enhance what is naturally yours, not to create a smile that looks artificial. Before any cosmetic procedure begins, you will see a full treatment plan with expected outcomes and costs. No surprises.

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants preserve jawbone density by stimulating the bone the way a natural tooth root does, preventing the facial structure changes that missing teeth cause over time.

For a knocked-out tooth, time is critical: reimplantation success rates decline significantly after 60 minutes, so call us before driving to the office.

Restorative dentistry at 941 Dental Studio includes dental crowns, bridges, full and partial dentures, and tooth-colored fillings designed to restore full chewing function and a natural appearance.
