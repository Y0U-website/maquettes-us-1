Why Are Some Dentists Available on the Weekends and Others Aren’t?

Dental offices are typically private practices not affiliated with an institution like a hospital. Because this is the case, they’re run like a business by the dentists that opened them. That means that the dentist can set the hours that the office is open, as they don’t have to adhere to the rules of an institution.

Another of the major reasons that dental offices have shorter hours is because of their patient load. Much of the time, there aren’t enough patients to justify the office being open for extended hours, particularly weekend hours. Additionally, staffing can be difficult for areas that are more rural. There is only so much availability in certain areas, and the needs of the patients don’t always justify keeping late or weekend hours, as much as dentists want to be available to their patients.

However, there are some dentists that do keep weekend hours. These dentists can be relied on in the event of an emergency. They’re available when you need them, particularly on the weekends when emergencies happen. This can make all the difference in maintaining a healthy smile or having your smile permanently damaged.
