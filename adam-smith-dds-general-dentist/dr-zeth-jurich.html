Dr. Zeth Jurich

in San Antonio, Helotes and Shavano Park, TX

Dr. Zeth Jurich is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care in a welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel comfortable and cared for. He believes that building lasting relationships with patients is just as important as delivering excellent clinical care.

Originally from Montana, Dr. Jurich earned his Bachelor’s degree from BYU Idaho before pursuing his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine Illinois. After completing dental school, he and his family relocated to Texas, where he is proud to serve the local community.

As a husband and father of seven children, Dr. Jurich understands the importance of family-centered care. He enjoys helping patients maintain healthy smiles throughout every stage of life and strives to make each dental visit a positive experience.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Jurich enjoys spending time with his wife and children, exploring the outdoors, and being actively involved in family life. He looks forward to welcoming you and your family and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile.
