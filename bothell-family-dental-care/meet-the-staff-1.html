Dr. Daniel Hwang

Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)

Dr. Hwang and his wife Ivana, a practicing veterinarian, moved back to the Pacific Northwest because he loves the state of Washington and wanted to be closer to family and friends. Dr. Hwang enjoys music and actively participates in a local community orchestra. He used to have other hobbies, but now he has kids.

Dr. Hwang has a strong commitment to his patients and his community. He often spends his days away from the clinic with the various community clinics of King County and Snohomish County, and is a annual volunteer for the Seattle/King County Clinic and other local Federally Qualified Health Centers. Dr. Hwang looks forward to meeting you and taking care of your dental needs!

Shauna

Front Office

Shauna enjoys spending time with her husband, Socrates, and two teen children. When she is not at the office she can be found crafting and spending time with her two dogs, Taylor and Loki. Shauna loves getting to know her patients and is excited to be part of the Bothell Family Dental Care team.

Ari

Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH)

She has been enjoying educating and encouraging patients through their dental journey ever since. Ari grew up in Iran, lived in Turkey for a few years prior to moving to the states and currently lives in Kirkland. She speaks Farsi, Turkish, and English. Ari enjoys the outdoors, art, traveling, and spending time with family and friends.
