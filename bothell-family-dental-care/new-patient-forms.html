Policies

Payment Our office accepts most insurance plans, and we will make every effort to help you determine the coverage available to you. A majority of insurance carriers will cover a certain percentage of treatment depending on the procedure. We are committed to assisting you in filing your claim and will handle insurance questions from our office on your behalf. If you have any questions regarding your insurance coverage, please contact us.

We also extend a 5 % discount to seniors and offer 3 months interest-free financing for qualifying patients.

Cancellation Policy Continuing care is an important part of your good health and we reserve your appointment time especially for you. If you are unable to make your scheduled appointment, we ask that you please provide us with 24-hours notice.

Insurance Providers

We are in-network with most major PPO/indemnity insurance plans. If you have questions about your coverage, call our office at (425) 486-4428 to have our insurance expert answer all your dental insurance related questions.
