Emergency dental services

Dental Emergencies

We understand that dental problems can happen at any time. They can range from an esthetically unpleasing chip to a life threatening emergency. A dental emergency is any condition that requires prompt attention from a dentist.

What are dental emergencies?

Toothache Lost fillings, crowns, or other restorations Fractured, cracked, or chipped teeth Swelling Jaw pain Loose or lost teeth Broken dentures Cuts, bumps, or bruises

What should I do for a toothache?

Call our office (425-486-4428) as soon as possible to schedule an appointment to evaluate and treat the cause of your dental pain. Do not attempt to chew on the side that hurts. Often times, same day treatment is available to get you out of pain and back to living your life.

If you experience any difficulty breathing or severe swelling around the lower jaw and neck, or if you are experiencing any other life threatening emergency, dial 911 or seek out the emergency room/urgent care immediately.
