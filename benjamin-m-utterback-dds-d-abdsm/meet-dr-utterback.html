Meet Dr. Utterback

Dr. Ben Utterback grew up in North Canton and graduated from Hoover High School in 1992. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Mount Union in 1996, followed by his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2000.

A dedicated and passionate practitioner, Dr. Utterback is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine (2019), achieving the highest recognition among dentists who treat sleep-disordered breathing.

He is an active member of the Stark County Dental Society, the Ohio Dental Association, the American Dental Association, and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. In 2025, he is proudly serving as vice president of the Stark County Dental Society and is set to become its president in 2027.

In early 2025, Dr. Utterback made a pivotal shift by selling his general dentistry practice to launch Dental Sleep Solutions of Northeast Ohio. This transition allows him to devote 100 % of his professional focus to dental sleep medicine. His new practice enables quicker consultations, timely treatment with oral appliance therapy, and close collaboration with medical providers to ensure comprehensive care for patients with sleep-related breathing disorders.

Outside the office, Dr. Ben enjoys playing golf, traveling, and tending to his koi pond. He lives in Canton with his wife, Torie, and their two daughters, Laurel and Addyson.
