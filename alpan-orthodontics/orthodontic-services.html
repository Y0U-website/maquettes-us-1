How much does orthodontic treatment cost in Los Angeles?

This is a question we hear quite often from the residents of Los Angeles county. Unfortunately, there’s no set price because various factors may affect the cost, such as the complexity of your case, the duration of the treatment, and the type of braces or appliances needed to straighten your teeth.

Am I too old to get braces?

Absolutely not! It’s never too late to achieve straight teeth and a beautiful smile. In fact, many adults in the Los Angeles area have often made excellent candidates for clear braces/ Invisalign.

Does orthodontic treatment cause pain?

Orthodontic treatment has advanced significantly over the years. While braces typically cause some tenderness and soreness for a few days, they are not inherently painful. Any discomfort can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relief. Modern braces are designed to be more comfortable, utilizing technology that minimizes discomfort. We use biocompatible braces and advanced techniques that apply gentle orthodontic force, along with high-quality materials, to help reduce both discomfort and treatment time.

What happens when my braces are finally removed?

Dr. Alpan will address any concerns, take photos/notes and x-rays if needed, and conduct a comprehensive examination. He’ll discuss your further treatment options, answer any questions regarding braces, and provide you with the information necessary to make the best decision for your care. Our team will ensure you feel comfortable and relaxed, so you can leave everything to us.
