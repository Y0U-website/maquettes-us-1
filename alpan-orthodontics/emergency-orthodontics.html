Handling Emergencies at Home Before Your Appointment

While professional care is essential, here are temporary steps you can take to minimize discomfort until you can visit Alpan Orthodontics:

These are temporary solutions meant to reduce discomfort, and you should contact us for a permanent resolution.

  • Broken Braces or Brackets: Fix the bracket in place using orthodontic wax if it is still attached to the wire.
  • Poking Wires: Use the eraser end of a pencil to gently reposition the wire or cover the tip with orthodontic wax.
  • General Discomfort: Over-the-counter pain medicine and warm saltwater rinses help alleviate soreness.
  • Detached Appliances: Keep removable parts, such as retainers or bands, in safe storage and bring them to your appointment.

Frequently Asked Questions About Emergency Orthodontics

What should I do if a wire is poking my cheek or gums?

You can use orthodontic wax, a pencil eraser, or tweezers to reposition or cover the wire. Be sure to call our office for a proper adjustment as soon as possible.

Is it an emergency if my braces detach?

Yes, a broken bracket or detached braces can delay your treatment and cause additional irritation. Contact us immediately to repair or replace the broken components.

Can I wait until my next appointment to address discomfort?

While mild discomfort after adjustments is normal, severe or persistent pain should not be ignored. Call us right away if your discomfort worsens or doesn’t subside.

What should I do if I swallow a piece of my braces?

Swallowing a small bracket or wire is rare but possible. Most cases are harmless, but contact us immediately for guidance and next steps.
