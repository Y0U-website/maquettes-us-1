Does Invisalign Hurt?

Some initial discomfort is expected when you first start wearing Invisalign aligners or after switching to a new set. This discomfort typically lasts only a few days or a week, indicating that the aligners effectively move your teeth. Over-the-counter pain relief can help ease this discomfort.
