Treatment Options for Adults

At Alpan Orthodontics, we offer a variety of options to meet the unique needs and preferences of adult patients. Our treatments are designed to fit your lifestyle while delivering exceptional results.

Invisalign for Adults

Transform your smile discreetly with Invisalign. These clear aligners are custom-designed to fit comfortably over your teeth, gradually shifting them into place. Invisalign is ideal for busy adults who want an effective and nearly invisible treatment option.

Braces for Adults

For those who prefer or require traditional orthodontic methods, we offer braces in a variety of styles, including clear, gold, and white ceramic braces, for a more subtle appearance. Our advanced technology ensures faster, more effective results with less discomfort during treatment.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are hidden behind your teeth, making them a perfect choice for adults looking for a completely discreet way to straighten their teeth.

Accelerated Orthodontics

With tools like OrthoPulse and PROPEL, we can speed up your treatment by up to 30 %, helping you achieve your desired results in less time without compromising on quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it too late to straighten my teeth as an adult?

Absolutely not! Orthodontic treatment is effective at any age. Many adults choose orthodontics to address both cosmetic and functional issues with their teeth.

How long does adult orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length varies based on your specific needs but typically ranges from 12 to 30 months. Accelerated options may reduce this time significantly.

Will orthodontic treatment fit into my busy lifestyle?

Yes! With options like Invisalign and flexible appointment scheduling, we ensure your treatment plan complements your lifestyle.

Are adult braces painful?

While there may be some initial discomfort as your teeth adjust, today’s advanced braces and aligner technologies minimize pain and maximize comfort.

How much does adult orthodontics cost?

The cost varies depending on the type of treatment and complexity of your case. During your consultation, we’ll provide a detailed cost breakdown and discuss flexible financing options to fit your budget.
