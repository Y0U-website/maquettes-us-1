How to Prepare for Your Child’s First Visit

Here are a few simple ways to ensure a smooth, stress-free appointment:

If your child feels nervous, don’t worry! Our team specializes in working with young children and those with dental anxiety , ensuring a and reassuring experience .

Frequently Asked Questions About the First Visit When Should My Child Have Their First Dental Visit? The AADP recommends children visit the dentist within 6 months of the eruption of their first tooth or no later than their first birthday.

  • Talk about the visit in a positive way let your child know that the dentist is a friendly helper who keeps their smile healthy.
  • Read a fun story about the dentist books and videos about first dental visits can help kids feel excited.
  • Schedule at the right time Choose a time when your child is well-rested and comfortable.
  • What If My Child Is Nervous? It’s completely normal for kids to feel a little unsure. Our friendly team will take things at their pace , using techniques and positive reinforcement to make them feel safe and comfortable.
