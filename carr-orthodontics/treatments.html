All Ages Phase One Teen Adult The American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends all children see an orthodontist by age 7. Many times, no treatment is recommended and your child will be placed on observation to monitor growth and development until treatment is recommended. However, there are some conditions that need to be treated early to prevent more complicated treatment in the future. Carr Orthodontics offers complimentary consults, so there’s no reason to wait!

The teenage years are a common time for orthodontic treatment because it’s a period of significant growth and change. Adolescence provides an ideal window of opportunity to address bite and alignment issues. Our teen orthodontic programs are thoughtfully designed to accommodate busy schedules while delivering effective results. We offer various treatment options, including traditional braces, clear braces, and clear aligners, to cater to each teen’s specific needs and preferences.

Orthodontic treatment is not limited to children and teenagers. Adults can also benefit from a beautiful, well-aligned smile. Whether you missed out on orthodontic care earlier in life or have experienced changes in dental alignment over time, it’s never too late to invest in your smile. We offer a range of options, including clear aligners and other innovative solutions, ensuring that you can achieve the smile you’ve always desired without compromising your daily routine.

Braces Traditional Metal Ceramic/Clear Traditional braces have long been used as a method for achieving a beautifully aligned smile. Comprised of high-quality stainless steel brackets and wires, they provide effective and efficient tooth movement. At Carr Orthodontics, we understand the significance of this classic approach, especially for cases requiring more extensive correction. We ensure that traditional braces are comfortable and produce remarkable results.

Ceramic braces offer an excellent solution for those seeking a more discreet orthodontic option. Made from translucent or tooth-colored materials, they blend seamlessly with your natural tooth color. At Carr Orthodontics, we understand the desire for subtlety, making ceramic braces an inconspicuous, yet highly effective choice.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a modern and discreet solution for achieving a beautifully aligned smile. These virtually invisible aligners gently and gradually shift your teeth into their desired position. Clear aligners offer the benefit of removing them when eating, brushing, and flossing, making oral hygiene and daily routines much easier. At Carr Orthodontics, our clear aligner systems are custom-made for your unique smile.

Retainers Retainers are a crucial part of the orthodontic journey, helping to maintain the beautiful smile you’ve worked hard to achieve. After your initial orthodontic treatment, we provide custom-fitted retainers to keep your teeth in their desired positions. We offer a range of retainer options, tailored to each patient’s specific needs. Our commitment is to ensure your smile remains healthy, functional, and radiant long after your treatment.

Our State-of-the-Art Technology 3D X-ray Digital Scanning 3D Printing We proudly utilize the i-CAT Cone Beam 3D Dental Imaging System, which uses cone beam computed tomography to create three-dimensional images of the head and neck. It is possible to capture 3D images of teeth, roots, TMJs, airways, and sinuses without distortion or magnification. The i-CAT 3D Digital Imaging device takes only 4.8 seconds to capture critical health information that will help Dr. Carr to accurately identify the best and least invasive treatment option.

Gone are the days of messy, uncomfortable impressions. With digital scanning, we create precise 3D images of your teeth, allowing for a more comfortable and accurate diagnosis. These digital models not only improve the precision of your treatment plan but also serve as a valuable visual aid, helping you better understand your orthodontic needs.

The revolutionary power of 3D printing has transformed orthodontic treatment, and we embrace this innovative technology at Carr Orthodontics. 3D printing allows us to create customized orthodontic devices with unparalleled precision and efficiency. Whether it’s crafting clear aligners, retainers, or other orthodontic appliances, 3D printing ensures a perfect fit and optimal comfort.
