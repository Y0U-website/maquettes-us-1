The practice

“I love exchanging stories and making connections with my patients.” — Dr. Julia Hill

At Breeze Dental, we are focused on providing a wide range of dental services for every type of dental need — from preventive to cosmetic dentistry. We want you to feel confident and comfortable in our care, so we will work with you to develop a treatment plan that fits your goals, needs, preferences, and budget.

Preventive Dentistry With routine cleanings, exams, and visits, you can supplement your overall dental health with preventive treatments to avoid serious issues such as decay, cavities, and gum disease. With the right preventive care, you can maintain a bright, healthy, and confident smile.

Crowns & Bridges Dental crowns and bridges are dental prosthetics used to restore your smile and its functionality, and repair a tooth or teeth after dental damage or decay. Crowns and bridges are durable and natural-looking solutions to missing or damaged teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry Whether you’re looking for a simple teeth whitening or a more custom and permanent treatment like veneers, we offer a variety of cosmetic dental treatments. Improve color, alignment, shape, and size so you can feel comfortable and confident with your smile.
