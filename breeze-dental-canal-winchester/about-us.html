We always like to remind our patients to Inhale, Exhale, and Smile!

Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays produce 80 % less radiation, making them safer and more accurate than traditional x-rays. Images can be viewed within minutes so Dr. Hill can assess your entire oral health and catch any lingering issues that are not visible to the naked eye.

Digital X-Rays

Intraoral Camera

Intraoral cameras are a great way for patients to get a look at the condition of their own teeth and mouth and discuss any issues or treatment plan with Dr. Hill. Unlike digital x-rays, intraoral cameras can show the exterior of the teeth rather than its interior.
