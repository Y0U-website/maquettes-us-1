Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are more than just a cosmetic fix; they&#x27;re a long-term investment in your oral health. Unlike traditional dentures, implants are anchored to your jawbone, offering stability and preventing bone loss. Plus, they look and feel just like your natural teeth!

How Does the Dental Implants Procedure Work?

The dental implants procedure at Advanced Dentistry is a testament to our commitment to excellence. Initially, a titanium post is inserted into the jawbone, which then fuses with the bone over a few months. After this integration, a custom-made crown is attached to the post, completing your new, natural-looking tooth.
