Orthodontics

Having a healthy and radiant smile is a vital aspect of one’s overall well-being.

A beautiful smile boosts your confidence and self-esteem and also enhances your oral hygiene. At Canandaigua Smiles, we provide the best possible orthodontic services to our clients. Our team of experienced and skilled orthodontists strives to offer our clients high-quality dental treatment options that are both effective and affordable.

Damon Braces System

If you’re looking for an effective way to correct the alignment of your teeth and jaws, the Damon Braces System would be an excellent option. The Damon Braces System utilizes self-ligating wires that apply gentle, continuous pressure to move your teeth into their correct position. This orthodontic treatment option requires fewer office visits, which means you can enjoy less hassle and more comfort with this method.

Invisalign/Clear Braces

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that uses clear aligners to straighten your teeth. These clear aligners are custom-made to fit your teeth and gradually shift them into the desired position. Invisalign is a discreet and comfortable option for both adults and teenagers who require minor to moderate teeth alignment.
