Do I Need an Appointment to Be Seen at Dakota Family Dentistry?

All patients are seen by appointment only. Should you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify the front desk as soon as possible. Failed appointments (less than 24-hour notification) result in a loss of time and inconvenience for others seeking timely dental care. The second failed appointment may result in a discontinuation of the patient/doctor relationship.

How Often Do I Need to Go to the Dentist?

You should see your dentist twice a year (every six months) for dental exams and cleanings. If you have a concern in between visits, you should make an appointment with us as soon as possible.

How Often Should I Change My Toothbrush?

Adults and children should change their toothbrush approximately every three months.

Why Do I Need to Visit the Dentist?

Visiting the dentist regularly will help keep your teeth and mouth healthy and help keep the rest of your body healthy. Dental care prevents tooth decay, gum disease, and keeps your smile looking bright!

How Can I Take Care of My Teeth Between Dental Checkups?

Taking care of your teeth in between routine dental checkups involves brushing your teeth at least twice a day and flossing regularly. You can also avoid tobacco and foods with a lot of sugar.

How Do You Guarantee Sterilization?

We use state-of-the-art-sterilization procedures. The latest barrier techniques and most current preventative concepts are always used for your protection.
