Dental Services

From preventive care to specialized treatments, our comprehensive services cater to all your child’s dental needs. Immerse yourself in a world of personalized oral health solutions designed for your child’s well-being.

Comprehensive Dental Care From routine exams to smile makeovers, our expert team provides comprehensive care for a happy and healthy smile.

X-rays and Diagnostics

Routine radiographs ensure a thorough evaluation by ruling out any pathology or cavities present. We provide precise diagnostics for a thorough understanding of your child’s oral health.

Professional Dental Cleanings

Our skilled hygienists use advanced techniques to remove plaque and tartar, leaving your teeth and gums feeling clean and refreshed.

Routine Dental Exams

Regular check-ups ensure the early detection of issues, promoting proactive care and maintaining optimal oral health.

Dental Sealants

This preventive application can stop cavities from developing in the deeper grooves of adult teeth. Dental sealants provide a protective coating on the chewing surfaces of teeth by acting as a barrier where food particles and bacteria traditionally accumulate. We provide expert dental sealant applications in a comfortable, one-visit appointment.

Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas, is a safe and effective option for children who may feel anxious or nervous during dental visits. Administered through a small mask placed over the nose, nitrous oxide induces feelings of relaxation and calmness, helping children feel more at ease throughout their dental procedures.

Composite Dental Fillings

Composite dental fillings are crafted to match the natural color of your child’s teeth, providing seamless repairs for cavities and tooth damage. Made of a durable resin material, composite fillings offer long-lasting protection for your child’s teeth while ensuring their safety and comfort.

Stainless Steel Crowns

Stainless steel crowns are an excellent choice for restoring severely decayed or damaged teeth, providing reliable support and strength to maintain your child’s oral health. When applied to a baby tooth, these crowns will not affect the developing adult tooth and should fall out as expected, safeguarding your child’s smile for years to come.

Root Canal Therapy

Primary teeth that have nerve involvement, whether due to cavities or trauma, can be treated successfully through the removal of affected nerve tissue. With a focus on pain management, we ensure that your child’s root canal therapy is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. Our dentists use advanced techniques to preserve the integrity of your child’s teeth.

Extractions

Our compassionate pediatric dental team provides gentle and supportive care during the extraction process, ensuring your child feels as comfortable and at ease as possible. Our skilled dentists guide you and your child through every step of the extraction procedure, making the experience as minimally invasive as possible, lessening the chance of discomfort and promoting faster recovery times.

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers can play a crucial role in ensuring proper alignment and development of your child’s teeth and jaw as they grow. By preserving the space left by lost baby teeth, space maintainers help prevent crowding and misalignment, reducing the need for orthodontic treatment in the future. Our office will fabricate and place personalized space maintainers to suit your child’s unique needs, ensuring optimal effectiveness and comfort.

Emergency Dental Exams

Our team understands that dental emergencies can happen at any time. That’s why we offer prompt and reliable emergency dental exams to address your child’s urgent needs. Our skilled professionals provide compassionate care and expert guidance during emergency dental exams, ensuring your child receives the attention and treatment that they deserve.
