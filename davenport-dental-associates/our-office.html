Insurance FAQs

What is a covered benefit?

A covered benefit is a dental treatment recommended by your dentist that is listed on your insurance carrier’s fee schedule and covered under the terms of your plan.

What is an optional treatment?

An optional treatment is one that is either not included on your fee schedule or exceeds the minimum required to restore a tooth to its original function.

What is the difference between indemnity, PPO, HMO, and discount insurance plans?

Indemnity (or traditional) insurance reimburses either the member or dentist based on the dentist’s Usual, Customary & Reasonable (UCR) fee. This type of plan allows you to visit any dental office without restrictions to a specific network or panel of providers.

PPO

PPO (Preferred Provider Organization) is a common dental insurance type. It offers a network of in-network dentists, with coverage typically at 50 % for major treatments (crowns, root canals), 80 % for basic care (fillings), and up to 100 % for preventive care (exams, cleanings). Annual maximums usually range from $1,000 to $2,000.
