Dr. Emily Bandeen

Using her expertise and modern technology to achieve your smile goals.

Dr. Emily Bandeen is from Chattanooga, Tennessee. She is a passionate dentist who only does orthodontics and loves providing custom treatment for simple and effective orthodontics.

Relocating to Battle Creek with her husband, Dr. Timothy Bandeen, she dedicated herself to community service at the Family Health Center before joining Bandeen Orthodontics. Dr. Emily’s motivation stems from her belief in the profound impact of orthodontics on people’s confidence and well-being.

Beyond her professional life, she finds joy in staying active through swimming, running, and competing in triathlons, while also cherishing time spent with her four children. Her dedication to dentistry extends beyond technical expertise, driven by a genuine desire to empower others through healthy and beautiful smiles.
