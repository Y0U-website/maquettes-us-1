Retainers & Mouthguards

Retainers and mouthguards are essential for protecting your smile. At Bandeen Orthodontics , retainers keep your teeth in place after braces, and wearing them as directed can help prevent shifting. We recommend full-time wear for several months, then nightly for life, as consistent use ensures a lasting, straight smile.

Mouthguards protect teeth during sports and should be clean and properly stored. Orthodontic-specific mouthguards allow your teeth to move with treatment, unlike standard mouthguards, which may restrict movement. We provide orthodontic mouthguards upon request and offer custom sports mouthguards after treatment.

Both retainers and mouthguards are key in maintaining a healthy, beautiful smile.

Sleep Apnea Give yourself and your loved ones a good night’s rest. Sleep apnea is more than just snoring—it’s a serious condition that disrupts sleep and impacts health. It occurs when your airway becomes blocked, causing pauses in breathing and lowering oxygen levels. Common signs include snoring and gasping during sleep.

At Bandeen Orthodontics and Center for Dental Sleep Medicine, we offer solutions. Orthodontic treatment can help by creating more space in your mouth to improve airflow. For those needing additional support, we also provide Oral Appliance Therapy, which uses a mouthpiece to keep your airway open.

During your consultation, we’ll discuss your options and work with your physician to ensure your sleep apnea is managed as part of a coordinated care team.
