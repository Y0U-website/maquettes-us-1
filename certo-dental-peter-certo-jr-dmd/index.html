The practice

"I went to Dr. Peter Certo for a dental procedure. As someone who hates pain, Dr. Certo was excellent at ensuring I felt no discomfort. I would highly recommend him for his services to my friends." Laurence B, Dental Patient

Dr. Certo grew up in Marlton, NJ. He is a graduate of Cherokee High School class of 1985. He attended AlbrightCollege in Reading, PA 1985-1988. Left Albright to attend Temple University School of Dentistry class of 1992. Came to Chichester Township in November of 1993 and worked with Dr. Chris Panarello until opening Certo Dental in the summer of 2003.

He now resides in Havertown with his wife Megan and their four children: Rebecca, Patrick, Peter and Kathryn.

Francine has been in dentistry since 2003 providing two roles: front desk receptionist and an X-ray certified chair side assistant. Francine came to Certo Dental in August 2006. She currently resides in Wilmington, DE with her husband Jason and their two German Sheppards.

Angela has been with Certo Dental since October 2011. She is a graduate of Sun Valley High School. She attended Moore College of Art for Life Drawing. She completed her hygiene studies at Delaware Tech and graduated in 2005. Her hobbies include Painting and Fresh Water Fishing with her Family. Angela has two children: Fiurio and Annabel.
