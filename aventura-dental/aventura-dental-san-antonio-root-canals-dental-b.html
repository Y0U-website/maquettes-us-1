Root Canals and Extractions Services at Aventura Dental

Root canals and extractions are dental procedures that are typically reserved for severe cases of decay, infection, or damage to a tooth. At Aventura Dental in San Antonio, TX, our team of experienced dentists and staff are equipped to perform these procedures with the utmost care and precision.

At Aventura Dental, we strive to provide our patients in San Antonio, TX with the highest quality dental care possible. Our team of dentists and staff are committed to making sure that each patient feels comfortable and cared for during every visit.

Aftercare

After a root canal or extraction, our team will provide you with detailed instructions on how to care for your mouth as it heals. This may include avoiding certain foods, taking pain medication as prescribed, and following a specific oral hygiene routine.
