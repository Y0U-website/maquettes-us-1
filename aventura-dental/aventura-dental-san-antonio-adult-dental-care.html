Adult Dental Care in Duncanville

Welcome to Aventura Dental, your friendly neighborhood dentist in San Antonio, TX! We are proud to offer comprehensive adult dental care in a superhero-themed office that is sure to delight patients of all ages.

At Aventura Dental, we believe that dental health is a crucial aspect of overall well-being. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with modern equipment and cutting-edge technology, allowing us to provide the highest level of care possible. From routine cleanings to complex restorative procedures, our team of experienced dental professionals is committed to helping you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile.

We provide thorough examinations of your teeth and gums to assess your overall dental health. Our dental professionals perform detailed cleanings to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains, promoting optimal oral hygiene.

We offer advanced X-ray and imaging technologies to capture detailed images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. These diagnostic tools aid in the early detection of dental issues and assist in creating personalized treatment plans.

Fillings, Crowns, and Other Restorative Procedures:

Our skilled dentists utilize various restorative techniques to address dental problems such as decayed, cracked, or broken teeth. We offer high-quality fillings, crowns, bridges, and other restorative procedures to restore the health, function, and aesthetics of your teeth.

Teeth Whitening and Other Cosmetic Treatments:

Enhancing the appearance of your smile is our priority. We provide professional teeth whitening services to brighten and rejuvenate your teeth. Additionally, our cosmetic dental treatments include veneers, which can correct imperfections, and Invisalign, a discreet orthodontic option for straightening your teeth.

Orthodontic Consultations and Referrals:

If you require orthodontic treatment, we offer comprehensive consultations to assess your specific needs. Our dental team can provide guidance and refer you to trusted orthodontic specialists who will design a personalized treatment plan to align your teeth properly.

We understand the importance of gum health and its impact on overall oral health. Our periodontal care services focus on preventing and treating gum disease, including professional cleanings, scaling, root planing, and gum disease management to maintain healthy gums and prevent tooth loss.

We are here for you in dental emergencies. Our experienced team provides prompt and compassionate care for issues such as severe toothaches, dental trauma, or sudden oral pain. We prioritize your comfort and aim to alleviate your discomfort as quickly as possible.

We understand that visiting the dentist can be a stressful experience for some adults, which is why we strive to create a welcoming, comfortable environment for all of our patients. Our lobby features a variety of amenities, including a full arcade, video game consoles, HD TVs, plush couches, and free Wi-Fi, to ensure that your visit is as enjoyable as possible.

Payment Options

At Aventura Dental, we are committed to making dental care accessible to all members of our community. We accept Medicaid, CHIP, and most PPO insurances, and offer affordable payment plans for patients without insurance.

Our friendly, professional staff is dedicated to providing the highest level of care possible, and we are always happy to answer any questions you may have about your dental health.
