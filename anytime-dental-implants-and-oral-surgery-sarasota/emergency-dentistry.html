What Counts as a Dental Emergency?

  • Severe toothache or dental pain
  • Broken, chipped, or cracked tooth
  • Knocked-out tooth
  • Lost or damaged crown, bridge, or veneer
  • Dental abscess or infection
  • Swelling in the jaw, gums, or face
  • Broken or lost dental implant restoration
  • Soft tissue injuries — cuts or lacerations
  • Lost filling or exposed tooth
  • First Aid Protocol

Will my insurance cover an emergency visit?

Many dental insurance plans cover emergency exams and some emergency treatments. We’ll help you understand your coverage when you call. Financing is also available if needed.

What if I need oral surgery for my emergency?

We have full oral surgery capabilities in-house — including extractions, bone grafting, and IV sedation. If your emergency requires surgery, we can handle it here without referring you elsewhere.

I have dental implants placed somewhere else — can you still help me?

Absolutely. We treat dental implant emergencies for patients whose implants were placed at other practices. Call us and we’ll evaluate your situation.
