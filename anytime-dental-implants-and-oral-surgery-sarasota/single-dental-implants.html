Is the procedure painful?

Implant placement is performed under local anesthesia — you feel nothing during the procedure. Most patients report only mild soreness for a few days afterward, similar to a tooth extraction. Sedation options are available for anxious patients.

How long do single dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more — often a lifetime. The crown on top may eventually need to be replaced, but the implant itself is designed to be permanent.
