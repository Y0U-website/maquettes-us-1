The practice

Ultradent’s Opalescence Boost and Phillips’ Zoom are effective professional tooth whitening procedures offered in our dental office that can significantly improve the appearance of your teeth in just a few short visits, delivering long-lasting and spectacular “pearly white” results.

Porcelain crowns and veneers are advanced cosmetic dental solutions that offer a natural-looking, durable, and long-lasting way to improve the appearance of damaged, discolored, or misaligned teeth, helping to achieve a brighter, healthier smile. *SAME DAY CROWNS AVAILABLE*

Maintaining optimal oral health and preventing dental problems are crucial, and teeth cleaning is an essential dental procedure that aids in achieving these goals by removing tartar from the teeth.

Effective anti-cavity care, which includes preventive measures such as dental sealants, fluoride treatments, and regular checkups, is crucial in maintaining optimal oral health and achieving long-lasting, beautiful smiles.

Going to your regular check ups will help to keep your gums and teeth healthy as well as detect any early problems such as gum disease, oral cancer and cavities. The best way to maintain good oral health is to visit your dentist on a regular basis.
