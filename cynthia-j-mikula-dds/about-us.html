Dr. Cynthia Mikula is a seasoned professional in the field of cosmetic dentistry, family dentistry and preventative dentistry.

She received her undergraduate degree from Wittenberg University and went on to attend Case Western Reserve University’s Dental School. She opened her first practice in Brunswick, Maine, but returned to Ohio a short time later to be closer to her family. She opened her second practice in Lorain but relocated it to the Bay Village location she occupies now which is closer to her hometown of Rocky River, where she spent most of her childhood growing up and currently resides.

Outside of enjoying the practice of dentistry, Dr. Mikula likes to study French, enjoys traveling (mostly to French-speaking countries), cooking, trying out new restaurants, exercising and gardening. She is also an avid supporter of the arts and visiting museums and dabbling in interior design.

As they say in France, “Mieux vaut prévenir que guérir,” which means: It is better to prevent than to heal. Bay Village is certainly blessed to have such an amazing dentist locally to help prevent dental problems and to be available to fix them if, and when, they arise.
