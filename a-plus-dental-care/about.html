Our Mission

At A Plus Dental Care, we believe that everyone deserves a healthy, beautiful smile. Our mission is to provide comprehensive dental services in a comfortable, welcoming environment where patients feel valued and cared for.

We combine state-of-the-art technology with time-tested techniques and a gentle touch to deliver exceptional results. Whether you need a routine cleaning, cosmetic enhancement, or complex restorative work, our experienced team is dedicated to exceeding your expectations.

Meet Our Doctors

Experienced professionals dedicated to your oral health

Dr. Hongmei Yang

Dr. Hongmei Yang received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University and has provided exceptional general and cosmetic dental care in Pennsylvania since 2006. She brings extensive expertise in orthodontics and implant dentistry, supported by advanced academic training and years of clinical experience. Dr. Yang is an active member of multiple professional dental organizations and is certified in Invisalign and implant treatment.

Dr. Chendi Jiang

Dr. Chendi Jiang is a compassionate dentist dedicated to providing gentle, high-quality care for patients of all ages. She graduated summa cum laude from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. Dr. Jiang is known for her thoughtful, patient-centered approach and commitment to making every visit comfortable and stress-free.
