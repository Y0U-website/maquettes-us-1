Dental Implants

Dental implants are essentially replacement teeth made from medical-grade titanium posts. The implant post interfaces with the bone of the jaw to hold a prosthesis such as a crown, bridge, or denture. Dr. Rizk provides both the surgical and restorative phases of implant treatment right here in our Allendale office — no need to be referred elsewhere.

  • Full surgical and restorative implant services in-office
  • Medical-grade titanium posts that fuse with your jawbone
  • Crowns, bridges, and implant-supported dentures
  • Candidates must have healthy gums and sufficient bone
  • Easy to care for — brush and floss like natural teeth

Porcelain Veneers

Dental veneers (sometimes called porcelain veneers or dental porcelain laminates) are wafer-thin, custom-made shells of tooth-colored materials designed to cover the front surface of teeth to improve your appearance. These shells are bonded to the front of the teeth, changing their color, shape, size, or length. Veneers are one of the most popular cosmetic treatments we offer at our Allendale office.

  • Ultra-thin, custom-made porcelain shells
  • Bonded to the front surface of teeth
  • Correct color, shape, size, or length
  • Natural-looking, stain-resistant results
  • Minimally invasive procedure

Dental Crowns

If teeth become weakened, cracked, or decayed, it can result in debilitating pain as the tooth becomes more fragile, and can also threaten surrounding teeth. A dental crown caps the entire visible portion of the tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance. Dr. Rizk uses tooth-colored crowns that blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

  • Restores cracked, decayed, or weakened teeth
  • Tooth-colored crowns for a natural appearance
  • Protects surrounding teeth from further damage
  • Used after root canals and large fillings
  • Durable, long-lasting results

Dentures

Dentures are natural-looking replacement teeth that are removable. There are two types of dentures: full and partial. Full dentures replace all teeth in the upper or lower jaw, while partial dentures fill in the gaps left by missing teeth. Dr. Rizk will work with you to find the best solution for your needs and lifestyle.

  • Custom-fitted for comfort and a natural look
  • Implant-supported dentures for added stability

  • Ongoing adjustments and maintenance
