Am I a Candidate?

Candidates for dental implants must have good general and oral health. Ideal candidates have:

  • Good general and oral health
  • Healthy gum tissue with no active periodontal disease
  • Sufficient bone density to anchor the implant post
  • Non-smoker or willing to quit (smoking affects healing)
  • Commitment to good oral hygiene and regular checkups

How It Works

Consultation & Exam

Dr. Rizk evaluates your oral health, takes X-rays, and determines if implants are right for you.

Implant Placement

A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia — most patients report minimal discomfort.

Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone, creating a strong, stable foundation.

Crown Placement

A custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing your new, natural-looking tooth.

Post-Implant Care

Dental implants are easy to care for. Brush and floss normally, just as you would with natural teeth. It is vital that you carefully follow Dr. Rizk&#x27;s post-operative instructions to ensure proper healing.

We strongly recommend regular checkups and professional cleanings so we can monitor the health of your implant and the surrounding tissue. With proper care, your implant can last a lifetime.
