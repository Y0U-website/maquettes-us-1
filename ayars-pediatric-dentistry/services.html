Cleaning and Prevention

Pediatric dentistry is founded on the principle of prevention. All patients of Dr. Todd J. Ayars, DDS PA are recommended biannual cleanings and examinations.

Crowns and Bridges

If your child has a damaged or decayed tooth, Dr. Todd J. Ayars, DDS PA can help you choose the best solution.

Extractions

We make every effort to preserve your child’s natural teeth. However, pediatric extractions are necessary when decay has made the tooth unsalvageable.

Fillings

Used to prevent the spread of tooth decay or correct any cosmetic damage, a filling is one of the ways we preserve your child’s health.

Conscious Sedation

As a pediatric dentist who’s fully accredited by the University of Maryland with biannual training certifications, Dr. Ayars is able to perform conscious sedation when needed.

Sealants

Given proper care and maintenance, dental sealants can protect damaged enamel and prevent cavities for up to a decade.
