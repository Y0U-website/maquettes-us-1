Health Protocols

PPE Installations

Through the implementation of glass barriers at check in and check out, we minimize potential spreading of the virus through face-to-face contact.

Masks

In compliance with state ordinances and to promote the well being of our patients and their families, masks must be worn at all times when inside the practice.

Disinfections

While each station has always been cleaned before and after each patient, we have introduced more powerful and more frequent anti-viral disinfectants.

Single Household Scheduling

To stop the spread of disease across families, we now only schedule patients within the same household together. This means that no two patients from different households will interact with each other.

Temperature Checks

We screen the temperature of every patient before their appointment begins in order to preserve the health of all of our patients.

Pre-Appointment Screening

Every patient and parent is asked to fill out an ADA screening form before their visit so any potential symptoms can be properly monitored.
