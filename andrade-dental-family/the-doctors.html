Our Doctors

Dr. Carly

Dr. Carly studied biology at New York University and dentistry at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. She is dedicated to our patient community, pursuing additional opportunities for education to provide exceptional care. She focuses on supporting overall health through dentistry, including an emphasis on nutrition and airway. She is grateful to work side by side with Dr. Andrade.

Dr. Carly has two children whom she adores. She loves to spend her free time with her family, prepare fresh meals at home, and enjoy family camping trips.

Dr. Andrade

Dr. Audrey Andrade was born and raised in Orange County. She studied psychobiology at UCLA and dentistry at USC. Dr. Andrade has been providing exceptional care to her patients at this same location for over 40 years and still enjoys coming to work each day. Her patients appreciate her caring, friendly personality and her conservative, gentle approach to dentistry.

Dr. Andrade has three wonderful grandchildren, and becoming a grandma has given her a new source of love and gratitude. Dr. Andrade enjoys playing pickleball, softball, and volleyball.
