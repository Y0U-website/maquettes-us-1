FINANCIAL OPTIONS

for Your Child’s Dental Care

Anna Kids Dentistry is committed to making quality pediatric dental care accessible and affordable for all families. We understand that financial considerations are an important aspect of your child’s dental journey, which is why we offer convenient financing solutions through CareCredit.

CareCredit is a healthcare credit card that allows you to spread out the cost of dental treatments over manageable monthly payments. It offers flexible payment plans with little to no interest, making it easier for you to fit your child’s dental care into your budget. With CareCredit, you can provide your child with the dental treatments they need without delay, ensuring their oral health is always a top priority.

To learn more about our financing options or to discuss your specific needs, please contact our friendly staff. We are here to answer any questions you may have and guide you through the process of applying for financing. Your child’s dental health is our priority, and we are committed to working with you to create a financial plan that suits your family’s needs.
