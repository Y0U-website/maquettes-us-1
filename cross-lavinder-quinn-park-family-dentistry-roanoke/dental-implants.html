Dental Implants

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a safe, durable replacement for adults who have had teeth decay or removed. By using the latest technology in the dental industry, your dental implants will function and appear just as your natural teeth would. Your dental implants may be paired with replacement crowns for full tooth replacement and may also be used to anchor dentures. Each patient’s situation is unique which is why we will recommend your teeth replacement plan based on your individual oral health needs.

Before our doctors go over procedures for dental implants, there are a few factors we consider first to see if you are eligible for dental implants:

Below is a list of some of the more common benefits of dental implants. For a more detailed list, talk to your doctor about dental implants and the benefits they can have for you and your smile.

  • Adequate bone support to anchor the implant
  • Healthy gum tissue
  • Sinus and nerve location
  • Overall health, including any chronic illnesses

A More Natural-looking Smile

Dental implants are crafted to closely resemble your natural teeth, restoring a more natural-looking smile.

Improved Oral Health

Since there is no need to alter surrounding teeth to anchor implants, more of your natural teeth are preserved intact, improving long-term oral health.

Improved Function

Dental implants function like your natural teeth, allowing you to eat without pain.

Protects Healthy Bone Structure

Empty spaces caused by tooth loss can lead to deterioration of the jawbone. Since dental implants function like tooth roots, they can prevent this type of deterioration, just as your natural teeth do.

A Long-term Solution

Dental implants, when cared for properly, can last a lifetime, making the investment now worth it for your future.

In some cases, we may recommend advanced treatment for your upper or lower jaw so you can have the bone support needed for implantation. As always, your treatment plan is created based on your specific needs for a healthier, happier smile.

Dental Implant FAQs

Your dental implants can be cared for just as you care for your natural teeth. Regular brushing and flossing are important to prevent the buildup of plaque as well as your regularly-scheduled dental cleanings and exams throughout the year.

With advanced digital planning and minimally invasive surgical techniques, many patients experience little and sometimes no post-operative discomfort. Our focus is on precise, gentle care that helps make your dental implant procedure as comfortable as possible.

With proper care, your implants can last the rest of your life, just like your natural teeth. Keeping them clean and cared for is key to stronger, healthier teeth and dental implants.
