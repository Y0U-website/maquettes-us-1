Dr. Cook

Dr. Loren Cook graduated Summa Cum Laude from Clemson University with a Bachelor of Science in Microbiology and a minor in Psychology prior to attending the Medical University of South Carolina, College of Dental Medicine and graduating in 2009. After graduation, Dr. Cook moved with her husband from South Carolina to Blacksburg, Virginia and began practicing dentistry. Dr. Cook is a member of the American Dental Association and the Virginia Dental Association. When she isn’t helping patients improve their smiles, she relaxes with her husband and three children.
