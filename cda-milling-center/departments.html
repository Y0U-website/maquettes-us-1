Model Room

Everything begins in the model room. Accurate impressions, both analog and digital, translate into accurate models and in turn crowns that seat with little to no adjustment. Our model room technicians have over 45 years of combined experience working with plaster, printed models, and articulation. Accurate weights and measures of stone and liquid are applied to every case resulting in a consistent product you can trust.

Waxing & Metal Finishing

Our team of waxers and metal finishers has been accurately creating copings, frameworks, and full contour units for case presentation and use for many years. Diagnostic wax-ups are a great tool to show your patients what their smiles could look like and our team creates amazing smiles with wax every day!
