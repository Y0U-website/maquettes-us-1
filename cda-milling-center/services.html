Products & Services

Custom Shades

Our lab has a beautifully designed shade-taking area where we employ the use of color-corrected lighting to attain accurate shades. We are an extension of your practice and treat your patients with care and professionalism. We connect with your patient—even providing a guided tour of our facility should they be interested in understanding how their unique restoration is made. Our talented porcelain technicians take a maximum of four shades per day by appointment only.

Material Options

We offer a large variety of materials—those most commonly used in our industry today. Emax and GC LiSi lithium disilicate offer translucency and aesthetics where strength and dark stumps aren’t a severe issue. 3M, Sagemax, and Jensen zirconia are used to create monolithic and hybrid layered zirconia restorations. Zirconia provides strength and aesthetics allowing us to hide dark stump shades and implant abutments. We create beautiful pfm’s and gold crowns as well. We will work with you to find the perfect material for your patient’s needs.

Case Consultation

Our technicians are available to consult with you to create a plan that results in the best outcomes for your patients. Our techs have worked through many different scenarios and through experience can often advise on the best materials and methods for case success. Reach out anytime to chat!

Support Staff

From phone calls, pick-ups and deliveries, to scheduling our workload, everything that goes on in the lab passes through our support staff’s capable hands. Our drivers, the face of our lab in your office, are courteous, professional, and set our service apart!
