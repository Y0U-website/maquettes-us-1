Become a Dental Assistant in 8 weeks

A focused, hands-on clinical training experience designed to prepare you for real practice from day one.

The practice

This course is designed to provide an introduction to the profession of dental assisting, covering topics including Ohio law and ethics, dental terminology, head and neck anatomy, infection control procedures, proper use and care of dental instruments and equipment, dental materials, dental specialties, and radiology.

Our facility is held in a fully functioning renowned dental office. Here our students will have exposure to an in house dental laboratory, 3D printing lab, as well as 11 operatories, and a whole lecture facility, where the biggest study club for dentists all over Columbus house speakers for continuing education.

It is our mission to teach and train students with the skills necessary to pursue a career in Dental Assisting. We provide ample lecture time as well as hands-on experience, supported by a strong curriculum. Our knowledgeable and professional staff will help inspire and educate our students.

Hands-On Clinical Training Train in a real dental office using modern equipment and real-world techniques.
