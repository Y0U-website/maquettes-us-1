Common Questions

Is cosmetic dentistry just about looks? Mostly, but not entirely. Closing a gap or straightening crowded teeth makes them easier to clean. Replacing chipped enamel protects what’s underneath. Cosmetic work often improves function and long-term oral health alongside appearance. They’re not always separate goals.

How much does teeth whitening cost? Cost depends on whether you do in-office whitening, take-home trays, or a combination. Take-home options are typically the most affordable and produce excellent results over a couple of weeks. We’ll go over your options and pricing during your consult so there are no surprises.

Are veneers permanent? Veneers are considered a permanent treatment because preparing the tooth involves removing a thin layer of enamel that doesn’t grow back. Veneers themselves last 10 to 15 years on average and can be replaced when they wear. We discuss this trade-off thoroughly before any veneer work so you can make an informed choice.

Will my insurance cover cosmetic work? Most dental insurance plans don’t cover purely cosmetic procedures. However, when a treatment serves both cosmetic and functional purposes (like a crown that also improves appearance), partial coverage is sometimes available. We’ll verify benefits and give you a clear estimate before treatment.

How do I know what treatment I need? Start with a consultation. Tell us what bothers you about your smile, and we’ll show you the options that fit. Sometimes the answer is simple: whitening or a single bonding repair. Sometimes it’s a coordinated plan. Either way, you’ll leave the consult with a clear understanding of your choices and what each one costs.
