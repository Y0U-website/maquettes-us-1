Meet the Doctor

Dr. Cameron R. Woodward

As a father of three young children, Dr. Woodward understands the importance of gentle, patient-centered dental care for the whole family. His calm and compassionate approach helps put even the most anxious patients at ease, making dental visits a comfortable experience for everyone.

Dr. Woodward grew up in Washington state and earned his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD) from Midwestern University in Arizona. His commitment to service led him to spend four years as a dentist in the United States Air Force, where he completed an advanced dental residency at Langley Air Force Base in Virginia and developed expertise in comprehensive dental care. He has also enjoyed serving in organizations such as St. Vincent de Paul, Give Kids a Smile, and Mission of Mercy.

Dedicated to providing the highest quality of care, Dr. Woodward has pursued additional training in Invisalign, dental implants, oral surgery, and pediatric dentistry. He has a special interest in helping patients find relief from orofacial and TMJ pain. As a fluent Spanish speaker, he welcomes the opportunity to serve our diverse community and ensure all patients can communicate comfortably about their dental care.

When not caring for patients, you’ll find Dr. Woodward making memories with his wife and children, whether they’re exploring local hiking trails or sharing their love of music. He enjoys staying active through biking and running, and often unwinds by playing piano and chess.

Dr. Woodward looks forward to welcoming you and your family to our practice, where creating healthy, beautiful smiles is our passion.
