Frequently Asked Questions About Opus Plasma

  • How much downtime does Opus Plasma require? Downtime typically ranges from 2 to 5 days, depending on treatment intensity. Most patients experience redness, mild swelling, and light flaking during the healing process.
  • Is Opus Plasma painful? A topical numbing cream is applied before treatment to ensure comfort. Patients typically describe the sensation as warmth with mild prickling.
  • How many Opus treatments will I need? Many patients see improvement after one session, but a series of 2 3 treatments may be recommended for optimal results, especially for acne scars or deeper wrinkles.
  • When will I see results? Initial improvements can be seen within a few weeks, with continued collagen stimulation and tightening developing over 4 8 weeks.
  • Is Opus Plasma safe for all skin types? Opus Plasma is safe for a wide range of skin types when properly evaluated. A consultation is required to determine candidacy.
  • What areas can be treated? Common treatment areas include the face, neck, chest, and areas with acne scars or stretch marks.
  • How does Opus compare to traditional laser resurfacing? Opus uses fractional radiofrequency plasma rather than relying solely on light energy. This allows for customizable depth and typically shorter downtime compared to fully ablative lasers.
