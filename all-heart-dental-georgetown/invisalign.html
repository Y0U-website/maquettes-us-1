The Invisalign Process

Step 1: Consultation

We’ll evaluate your smile, discuss your goals, and determine whether Invisalign is the best treatment option for your needs.

Step 2: Custom Treatment Plan

Using advanced digital technology, we’ll map out your smile transformation and create aligners customized specifically for you.

Step 3: Wear Your Aligners

You’ll wear your aligners for approximately 20 22 hours per day, removing them only for meals, brushing, and flossing.

Step 4: Enjoy Your New Smile

As you progress through your aligners, you’ll begin seeing noticeable improvements. Once treatment is complete, you’ll enjoy a straighter, healthier-looking smile with long-lasting results.
