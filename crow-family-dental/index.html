To keep your smile healthy, it is important to maintain a consistent at-home oral care routine and attend dental exams and cleanings twice a year. In some cases, we may recommend more frequent visits, especially if you have gum disease or are at risk of developing it. Our team will evaluate your oral health and suggest the appropriate number of preventive visits during your next appointment.
