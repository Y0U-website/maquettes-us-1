The questions we hear most.

  • How long does a session take? Essential is about 45 minutes. Signature is two visits, ~45 min each, spaced roughly two weeks apart. Diode laser is a single ~30-minute session performed with Sierra Tahoe Dental.
  • Which treatment should I pick? New clients typically start with Signature for the best value and longest-lasting result. Essential is for maintenance and sensitive teeth; diode laser is for stubborn staining or major events.
  • Will whitening damage my enamel? No. Our gel and LED protocols are dental-grade and enamel-safe. The gel lifts stain from within the enamel; it doesn’t strip it.
  • How soon before an event should I whiten? Ideally 1 2 weeks before. That lets any temporary sensitivity settle and gives your teeth time to fully rehydrate to their final shade.
