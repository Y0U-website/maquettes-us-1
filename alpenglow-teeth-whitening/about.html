About Alpenglow Teeth Whitening Boutique whitening, f inished with an Alpenglow.

Founded in 2022, Alpenglow began by offering mobile cosmetic teeth whitening and partnering with trusted salons, spas, and luxury resorts throughout the Reno Tahoe region before opening its boutique studio in 2025. Built by Chelsea, who brings over 15 years of experience working in the dental field, Alpenglow was created to deliver a more personalized, comfortable, and elevated whitening experience.

Chelsea is committed to staying at the forefront of the cosmetic teeth whitening industry, continually investing in advanced technology, proven techniques, and ongoing education to ensure every client receives the highest standard of care and exceptional results. Every treatment is thoughtfully tailored with comfort, confidence, and realistic expectations at the center of the experience.
