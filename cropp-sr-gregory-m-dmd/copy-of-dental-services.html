Gregory Cropp D.M.D

Dr. Cropp was born and raised in Erie, PA. He graduated from Cathedral Prep High School in 1989 and subsequently attended the University of Pittsburgh. He earned a B.S. in Combined Sciences and then attended the School of Dental Medicine where he graduated from in 1995. He then completed a General Practice Residency in Dentistry at St. Elizabeth’s Hospital in Youngstown, OH.

Dr. Cropp then returned to Erie and worked at two different private dental offices from July of 1996 until September of 1999. At that time he took a giant leap and opened his own dental office in the Yorktown Center off of West 12th Street. He practiced there for 10 years until he built his own building in the summer of 2009. This building is the current location of Lighthouse Dentistry. Dr. Cropp plans to one day retire from this office and never move locations again!
