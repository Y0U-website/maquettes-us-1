Dr. Cropp was born and raised in Erie, PA. He graduated from Cathedral Prep High School in 1989 and subsequently attended the University of Pittsburgh. He earned a B.S. in Combined Sciences and then attended the School of Dental Medicine where he graduated from in 1995. He then completed a General Practice Residency in Dentistry at St. Elizabeth’s Hospital in Youngstown, OH.
