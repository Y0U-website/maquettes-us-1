What Are All-on-4® Dental Implants?

Living with a full arch of missing or failing teeth affects more than appearance. Eating becomes a chore, speaking feels uncertain, and traditional Dentures can slip at the worst moments. All-on-4® Dental Implants solve this by anchoring a complete set of fixed teeth to just four strategically placed titanium posts in your jawbone. Two posts go in straight at the front, and two are angled toward the back to grip areas where bone is naturally thicker.

That angled placement is what sets the technique apart. Many patients who have been told they lack enough bone for conventional implants still qualify for All-on-4® Dental Implants without needing bone grafts first. Your dentist at Advanced Dental & Implant Center can often place all four posts and attach a temporary arch in a single visit, so you walk out the same day with a stable, functional smile.

Before Appointment

  • Arrange a ride home before your appointment, since sedation is typically part of the All-on-4® implant procedure.
  • Eat a soft, filling meal about two hours beforehand so you&#x27;re comfortable during what can be a longer surgical visit.
  • Bring a list of every medication you take, including blood thinners, since these can affect implant placement and healing.

After Appointment

  • Stick to cool liquids and blended foods for the first 48 hours, then transition to soft foods like mashed potatoes for two to three weeks.
  • Rinse gently with the prescribed antimicrobial mouthwash starting the day after surgery. Avoid swishing hard around the implant sites.
  • Skip brushing directly over the surgical areas for the first week. Use a soft-bristle brush on your other teeth and tongue only.

(719) 445-0490 Book Appointment How long do All-on-4® Dental Implants typically last? Most patients keep their All-on-4® Dental Implants for 20 years or longer. The titanium posts can last a lifetime with good oral hygiene, while the prosthetic arch may need replacement after 15 to 20 years due to normal wear.

  • Can I eat steak and crunchy foods with All-on-4® Dental Implants? Yes. Once fully healed, you can bite into apples, chew steak, and eat raw vegetables without worry. The four implants anchor the arch firmly to your jawbone, so it functions much closer to natural teeth than traditional Dentures.
  • Am I too old to get All-on-4® Dental Implants? Age alone is rarely a disqualifier. Patients in their 70s and 80s receive this treatment regularly. Your dentist evaluates overall health, bone density, and any medications you take to confirm you are a good candidate.
  • How often do I need checkups after getting All-on-4® Dental Implants? Plan on visits every six months for the first two years, then at least once a year after that. Your dentist checks the implants, cleans areas you cannot reach at home, and looks for any signs of wear on the prosthetic.
  • Do All-on-4® Dental Implants prevent jawbone loss? They do. The titanium posts stimulate your jawbone the way natural tooth roots do, which slows the bone shrinkage that typically follows Tooth Extractions. Most patients see stable bone levels for years after placement.
