What Are All-on-X Dental Implants?

Living with loose Dentures or a mouth full of failing teeth affects everything. Eating becomes stressful, speaking feels uncertain, and you start avoiding situations that used to be second nature. All-on-X Dental Implants solve this by anchoring a complete arch of fixed teeth onto just four to six implants placed at precise angles in your jawbone.

The "X" refers to the number of implants your dentist uses, which depends on your bone density and anatomy. Because the posts are angled to grip the thickest areas of bone, most patients qualify without needing a separate bone graft first. You walk in with teeth that shift and slip. You leave with a fixed set that stays put, bites hard, and feels like your own.

Adults who have lost most or all of their teeth on one arch, or who face Tooth Extractions for teeth too damaged to save, are often strong candidates. The same goes for people dealing with advanced Gum Disease that has weakened multiple teeth beyond repair.

You don’t need perfect bone density to qualify. Your dentist at Advanced Dental & Implant Center in Monument, CO can often place implants at strategic angles that work with the bone you already have. Younger adults and older patients alike respond well to this approach, and most people are surprised by how quickly they return to eating solid food after treatment.

Your dentist at Advanced Dental & Implant Center uses that scan to plot each implant angle and depth on screen before touching a drill. This digital surgical guide then transfers the plan to your mouth, so placement matches the model almost exactly.

The prosthetic arch itself has changed dramatically too. Earlier versions used bulky acrylic over a metal bar. Current arches use layered zirconia, a ceramic so dense it resists chipping better than natural enamel. The result is a thinner, lighter arch that looks realistic and holds up to years of daily use without the flex or staining older materials were known for.

On surgery day, the team places four to six titanium posts into your jawbone while you stay comfortable under sedation. A temporary set of teeth is attached that same visit, so you leave with a full smile. The whole process typically takes a few hours.

Expect some swelling and tenderness for the first week, manageable with over-the-counter pain relief and soft foods. Most patients feel noticeably better within five to seven days. Over the next several months, the posts fuse with your bone. Once healing is complete, your dentist fits the final permanent teeth.

Before Appointment

  • Arrange a ride home before your appointment, since All-on-X procedures involve sedation and you won&#x27;t be safe to drive.
  • Skip blood-thinning supplements like fish oil or vitamin E for at least a week prior, unless your doctor says otherwise.
  • Eat a soft, protein-rich meal two hours before arrival so you have energy for a longer procedure without a full stomach.

After Appointment

  • Stick to cool liquids and soft foods like smoothies, mashed potatoes, and broth for the first week while your implant sites heal.
  • Rinse gently with the prescribed antimicrobial mouthwash twice daily, but skip brushing directly over the surgical sites for 10 to 14 days.
  • Hold off on exercise, heavy lifting, and bending over for at least five days to keep swelling down around the implant posts.
