What Are Full-Mouth Dental Implants?

Living with multiple missing teeth or a failing set of teeth affects far more than appearance. Eating becomes a guessing game, certain words get harder to pronounce, and social situations start to feel uncomfortable. Full-mouth dental implants replace an entire arch of teeth, top or bottom, using a series of titanium posts set directly into the jawbone. Those posts act as artificial roots.

Over several months, the bone fuses around each post, creating a foundation that holds a fixed set of new teeth in place permanently. Unlike traditional Dentures that rest on the gums and can shift or slip, implant-supported teeth stay locked in. Your dentist at Advanced Dental & Implant Center can often combine Tooth Extractions and implant placement in the same visit, which cuts down on total healing time.

Before Appointment

  • Arrange for someone to drive you home, since full-mouth implant procedures typically involve sedation that makes driving unsafe.
  • Write down every medication, supplement, and blood thinner you take so the surgical team can review them before placing implants.
  • Eat a protein-rich meal two to three hours before your appointment, because you won&#x27;t be able to chew solid food for a while after surgery.

After Appointment

  • Stick to cool, soft foods like smoothies, mashed potatoes, and broth for the first week while your implant sites begin healing.
  • Rinse gently with prescribed antimicrobial mouthwash after meals instead of brushing near the surgical sites for the first 10 days.
  • Skip exercise, heavy lifting, and bending over for at least five days to reduce swelling and protect the healing implants.
