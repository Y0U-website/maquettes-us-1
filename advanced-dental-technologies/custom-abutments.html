Custom Abutments

​​Creating the perfect custom abutment requires skill, care and dedication to quality. ADT’s implant technicians understand the natural tooth root emergence profiles and can provide a highly accurate and esthetic abutment.

These quality products are available in zirconia or titanium. ADT’s site specific custom abutments can help you increase patient satisfaction in fewer visits, with less time and effort from you!

Each custom Abutment is ​designed with the result in mind

If the interface of your choice is not listed please call or contact us using the button below.

  • ADT’s dental technology implant staff completes all CAD design for each custom abutment to ensure proper form
  • ADT offers video review of all custom abutment designs prior to fabrication
  • ADT offers Abutment designs for platform switching, snug to soft tissue or contouring for ideal tooth form
