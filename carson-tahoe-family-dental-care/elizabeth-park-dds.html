Elizabeth Park DDS

Owner & DDS at Park Dental Management Company

About

Dr. Park has run her own practice since 2005. As a hands-on manager and respected dentist in the community, Dr. Park takes pride in providing the best care for her patients and cultivating a family-like environment in her community-based practice.

Over the years, she has also served as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. Her passion for teaching has made her an excellent role-model and teacher for students navigating their careers in dentistry.

In addition to holding several academic appointments and leaderships roles, Dr. Park has traveled extensively on mission trips, where she volunteered to provide dental services to underserved areas such as Mexico, Jamaica, and Bolivia. Dr. Park brings the highest quality of care and integrity to everything she does. She served as board member on the Board of Dental Examiners for the State of Nevada in 2019 and completed her 3 year term in 2021.
