WE ACCEPT YOUR INSURANCE

Dr. Elizabeth Parks DDS and staff always make you feel comfortable and at home with a relaxed bedside manner, light touch and known integrity.

The practice

At your first visit, you will meet our treatment coordinator and be given a tour of the office. After we take a few digital x-rays, one of our doctors will come in to complete the exam. All your initial questions and concerns will be answered, then we’ll review appointments and financial information.

We take most insurance including: Guardian, Diversified Dental Network, State of Nevada PPO, Humana, Operating Engineers, Delta Premier, AIG, Best Life, Hometown Health Plan, First Dental Health, Principal Financial Group

For new patients, please download the new patient forms . Or, please arrive 15 minutes early to fill it out in person.

Dr. Park has run her own practice since 2005. As a hands-on manager and respected dentist in the community, Dr. Park takes pride in providing the best care for her patients and cultivating a family-like environment in her community-based practice.

Over the years, she has also served as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. Her passion for teaching has made her an excellent role-model and teacher for students navigating their careers in dentistry.

In addition to holding several academic appointments and leaderships roles, Dr. Park has traveled extensively on mission trips, where she volunteered to provide dental services to underserved areas such as Mexico, Jamaica, and Bolivia.
