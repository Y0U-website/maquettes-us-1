Laser Frenectomy in Arkansas

Laser Frenectomy A Gentle, Precise In-Office Procedure

Laser frenectomy is the primary treatment we provide at Arkansas Tongue and Lip Tie Center a quick, minimally invasive procedure performed entirely in-office using advanced soft-tissue laser technology. Most procedures take only a few minutes and require no general anesthesia, making them safe and manageable even for newborns.

The laser targets only the restrictive tissue the frenum preserving all surrounding healthy structures. The entire visit, including evaluation and post-procedure feeding, typically lasts under an hour. Babies can often feed immediately after the procedure, which is one of the most reassuring aspects of this approach for nursing families.

The Advantages of Laser Over Traditional Techniques

Traditional frenectomy used scissors or scalpels this clinic has adopted soft-tissue laser technology because it delivers measurably better outcomes for infants and families.

Pinpoint Precision The laser removes only the restrictive frenum tissue with sub-millimeter accuracy, leaving surrounding healthy tissue completely intact.

Minimal Bleeding Laser energy cauterizes as it cuts, resulting in little to no bleeding during the procedure less stress for baby and parents alike.

Lower Infection Risk The laser’s heat sterilizes the treatment site in real time, significantly reducing the risk of post-procedure infection compared to blade techniques.

Faster Healing Laser wounds are cleaner and smaller, meaning tissue heals more quickly and post-procedure discomfort is typically brief and mild.

Before, During & After Your Procedure

Here’s exactly what to expect at every stage so your family arrives prepared and leaves confident.

Preparing for Your Visit

Feed your baby 2-2.5 hours before the appointment so they arrive a little hungry but not frantic they’ll feed better right after the procedure. Bring a comfort item (pacifier, swaddle, favorite toy for older children). If working with a lactation consultant, schedule a follow-up visit within 24 48 hours post-procedure. Complete any intake forms sent to you ahead of time to streamline check-in.

During the Procedure

A brief evaluation confirms the diagnosis and the precise area to be treated. A topical numbing agent is applied to the frenum area before the laser is used. The soft-tissue laser releases the restriction in seconds most releases take under 2 minutes to complete. Your baby is immediately returned to you for comfort nursing or bottle feeding.

Post-Procedure Care

Perform the prescribed wound-site stretches 4 6 times daily for 4 weeks to prevent reattachment. Expect some fussiness for 24 48 hours; infant acetaminophen can ease discomfort as directed. Follow up with your lactation consultant or feeding therapist promptly. Contact the clinic with any concerns our team is reachable for post-procedure support.

Our Care Continues After the Procedure

Our commitment to your family does not end when the laser is put down. Every family leaves with clear written aftercare instructions and direct access to our care team for any follow-up questions. Whether you have concerns about healing, need guidance on stretches, or want to check in about your baby’s feeding progress, we are here just a call or message away.

We Work With Your Whole Care Team

Tongue and lip tie affects feeding, speech, airway, and posture which is why a single-provider approach rarely achieves the best outcomes. The Arkansas Tongue and Lip Tie Center practices a collaborative model, partnering with a network of trusted specialists to support your child’s full recovery.

Lactation Consultants support optimal latch and feeding mechanics before and after the procedure. Speech-Language Pathologists address articulation and oral motor patterns in older children. Myofunctional Therapists retrain tongue posture and oral rest position to support long-term results. Chiropractors release associated tension patterns in the neck and jaw that can accompany tongue tie.

We will coordinate with your existing providers or help connect you with trusted partners in our network because your child’s outcome is our shared goal.

Common Questions About Laser Frenectomy

Here are honest answers to the questions families ask most before their procedure.

Is the procedure painful for my baby?

A topical numbing agent is applied before the laser is used, so most babies experience minimal discomfort during the procedure itself. Some fussiness in the 24 48 hours after is normal and manageable with infant acetaminophen as directed.

How long does the procedure take?

The laser release itself typically takes under 2 minutes. When you factor in evaluation, numbing, and post-procedure feeding, plan for a total appointment time of approximately 45 60 minutes.

When will we notice improvements?

Many families notice changes in latch and feeding comfort within the first few days. Full functional improvement especially in feeding, speech, or sleep often emerges over 2 6 weeks as the tissue heals and your child adapts. Consistent aftercare stretches and follow-up with your feeding team are key.

What if the tie reattaches?

Reattachment is uncommon when aftercare stretches are performed consistently. If you have concerns about healing or suspect reattachment, contact our clinic for further evaluation.
