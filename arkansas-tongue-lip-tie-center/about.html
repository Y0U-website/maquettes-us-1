About Arkansas Tongue and Lip Tie…

Compassionate Care from a Dedicated Specialist

Dr. Aaron K Forrester, D.D.S., is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist in Conway, AR. After graduating from the University of Central Arkansas in 2001 and the LSU School of Dentistry in 2009, he went on to complete his studies at the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio in 2014. Dr. Forrester’s first practice, Groovy Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, opened in August 2014.

He has completed five years as a general dentist and a two-year pediatric dental residency. His commitment to excellence means he stays up to date with skills and technology through continuing education classes and certifications. His top achievements include:

Diplomate, American Board of Pediatric Dentistry 2016

American Orthodontic Society Comprehensive Education 2010

Theta Chapter of Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society 2009

Baldridge Scholarship Recipient 2006 2008

C. Edmund Kells Dental Honor Society 2006 2009

LSU Operative Dentistry Award for Excellence 2009

When he’s not making groovy smiles, Dr. Forrester loves spending time with his wife, Sheila, and their children, Brady and Perry. In addition to family time, he enjoys working on his physical fitness, golfing, fishing, car detailing, and deer hunting.

Whole-Body, Collaborative Care for Every Family

We view tongue and lip tie treatment not as an isolated procedure, but as one important step in a broader, coordinated care plan. Our clinic partners with lactation consultants, speech-language pathologists, myofunctional therapists, and pediatric bodywork providers including chiropractors and craniosacral therapists to ensure every patient receives complete, whole-body support before and after their frenectomy.

Collaborative Care Network We work alongside your lactation consultant, speech therapist, and bodywork provider to ensure a complete, coordinated care plan.

Family-Centered Approach Every evaluation and treatment plan is built around the unique needs of your child and your family’s feeding and wellness goals.

Evidence-Based Treatment Our protocols follow the latest research in pediatric laser frenectomy, with thorough pre- and post-procedure support at every step.

A Calm, Family-Friendly Space in the Heart of Arkansas

Our clinic is a welcoming, purpose-built space designed to feel warm and calming for infants, children, and parents. Equipped with state-of-the-art CO2 laser technology, every detail of the environment has been considered to prioritize comfort and reduce anxiety for the whole family, from the moment you walk through the door to the moment you leave.

Conveniently located in Conway, Arkansas, our clinic offers ample parking, a stroller-friendly entrance, and easy access to major regional highways. Families travel from across Arkansas and neighboring states to receive the specialized tongue and lip tie care that is simply not widely available elsewhere in the region.
