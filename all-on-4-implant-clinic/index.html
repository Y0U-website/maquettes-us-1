The practice

Your smile is not one-size-fits-all, and your dental care should not be either. We offer a wide range of services under one roof, making it easier to get the treatment you need in a place you already trust.

Dental emergencies don&#x27;t follow a schedule, and neither do we. We offer same-day, Saturday, and late evening appointments after 5pm so you always know where to turn when something comes up.

Protecting your smile starts with consistent, thorough preventive care. Our team provides cleanings, digital X-rays, oral cancer screenings, night guards, and more to keep your teeth and gums healthy for the long term.

A beautiful result does not happen by accident. It comes from careful planning, honest communication, modern technology, and a team that refuses to settle for “good enough.” At Dental Arts San Diego, every detail is considered so your care feels as comfortable, clear, and precise as possible.

Advanced diagnostics and digital precision We use CBCT imaging, implant planning, nerve mapping, and digital TRIOS scanning to ensure accurate diagnoses and precise treatment outcomes. This technology means fewer surprises, better planning, and results you can count on.

Extensive dentistry under one roof From dental implants and full-arch restorations to root canals, full mouth reconstruction, wisdom teeth extractions, and cosmetic treatments, we handle complex, extensive dentistry in-house so you don&#x27;t have to travel to multiple providers.

A comfortable, judgment-free experience We know dental anxiety is real, and we&#x27;ve built our practice around making every visit as comfortable as possible. Pillows, blankets, headsets, warm towels, and a team that listens without judgment mean you can finally get the care you&#x27;ve been putting off.

Flexible access and financing options We accept most PPO and HMO insurance plans and offer financing through CareCredit, Cherry, and Sunbit, plus in-house payment plans. We also offer same-day, Saturday, and late evening appointments so care fits into your life, not the other way around.
