Comfort and technology, beautifully balanced

Dental Arts San Diego combines advanced diagnostic technology with genuine comfort amenities, so every visit feels as relaxed and precise as possible. From implant planning to root canal therapy, full mouth reconstruction, periodontal care, and cosmetic treatment, these tools allow us to approach your care with more confidence and fewer surprises.

CBCT imaging

Cone beam CT imaging gives Dr. Nawrocki a three-dimensional view of your teeth, jaw, nerves, and bone structure. This level of detail is especially important for implant planning, where precision directly impacts long-term success. It means fewer surprises and more confidence at every stage of treatment.

Implant planning and nerve mapping

Before any implant procedure, Dr. Nawrocki uses detailed imaging and nerve mapping to plan placement with accuracy. This careful approach supports safer outcomes and reduces recovery time, giving you a clearer picture of the process before it begins.

Digital TRIOS scanning

The TRIOS digital scanner replaces traditional impressions with a fast, comfortable intraoral scan. The result is a highly accurate digital model of your teeth that supports better-fitting restorations and a more streamlined treatment process from start to finish.

Digital X-rays

Digital X-rays provide clear, immediate images with significantly less radiation than traditional film. They allow the team to detect issues early, track changes over time, and share findings with you directly during your appointment.

Soft-tissue laser therapy

Soft-tissue laser therapy offers a precise, minimally invasive option for a range of gum and tissue procedures. Patients typically experience less discomfort and faster healing compared to traditional techniques, making it a welcome addition to the practice&#x27;s care toolkit.

Comfort amenities

Dental Arts San Diego is designed to feel like a place you actually want to be. Pillows, blankets, headsets, coffee, tea, and water are all available during your visit, and warm towels are offered after procedures. It is the kind of thoughtful, personal touch that patients consistently mention when they describe the practice to friends and family.

Rooted in our community

Dental Arts San Diego has been in the same El Cajon location for many years, serving patients from El Cajon, La Mesa, Mount Helix, Santee, Alpine, and surrounding communities. The building itself has a character patients often remember, with unique architecture and a history that makes the practice feel distinct from the moment you arrive.

As a privately owned practice, we are proud to be a long-standing part of this community. We welcome patients with a wide range of PPO and HMO insurance plans, offer flexible financing through Sunbit, CareCredit, and Cherry, and work with patients to find a path forward that supports their health and budget.

For us, the greatest compliment is simple: when patients trust us enough to refer their family and friends.
