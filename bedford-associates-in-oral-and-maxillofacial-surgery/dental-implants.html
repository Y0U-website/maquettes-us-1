The Implant Process: What to Expect

From your first consultation to your final restoration, our team prioritizes clear communication and your comfort. Our scheduling team works to accommodate your needs, and our friendly staff ensures you always know what to expect next. Here’s how the process works:

Initial Consultation & Planning

The process starts with a comprehensive evaluation, including advanced 3D imaging. Dr. Deljavan will assess your bone quality, discuss your goals, and create a customized treatment plan using our fully digital workflow.

Surgical Phase

During the surgical phase, the titanium implant is precisely placed into your jawbone using our digital workflow and 3D imaging guidance. Depending on your situation, you may receive a temporary tooth immediately, or we will allow 6 to 12 weeks for healing.

Final Restoration

Once osseointegration is complete and the implant has fully bonded with your bone, your restorative dentist will create and attach your custom restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime . The titanium implant itself is designed to be permanent, while the crown may need to be replaced after 10-15 years due to normal wear.

Is the procedure painful?

Can dental implants replace multiple teeth at once?

Yes. Dental implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even a full arch. For multiple missing teeth, we can place individual implants or use implant-supported bridges and dentures, which require fewer implants while still providing exceptional stability and function. During your consultation, Dr. Deljavan will recommend the most effective and efficient solution for your needs.
