Recovery and Follow-Up

Recovery is typically straightforward, with most patients returning to normal activities within a few days. For exposure procedures, your orthodontist will begin guiding the tooth into position during your regular orthodontic appointments. For extractions, we’ll see you for a follow-up visit to ensure proper healing.
