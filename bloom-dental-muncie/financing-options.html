Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay over time for my dental care?

Yes, you can now pay over time with Sunbit, which allows patients to split their bill into monthly payments up to 72 months with an approval rate of over 85 % of patients, even those with lower credit scores. It’s an exciting program that helps patients get the treatment they need for a healthy, confident smile. Learn more about how you can get your dental care today and pay over time.

Do you accept any discount dental plans?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts Essential Dental, a dental savings plan that provides immediate savings on dental care for you and your family. The plan has no waiting period and no annual maximums. Exams and x-rays are free. Individual, 2 individuals and family memberships are available starting at $99. For more information and to enroll, visit essentialdentalplan.com. Essential Dental is not insurance.

Will my insurance cover cosmetic dental care?

Insurance coverage and out-of-pocket expenses for cosmetic treatment vary. Our practice will work with your insurance provider to verify coverage and will provide you with different treatment options to achieve your cosmetic goals. We will review treatment options with you and help you find a payment option for your needs and your budget.

I’m worried about the cost of dental care I need. What are my options?

Does health insurance cover dental work?

Health insurance and dental insurance are two different types of coverage. Some procedures such as oral surgery may be covered under your health insurance. Call your office and speak with a Patient Care Representative to learn more.

Do I need dental insurance to get braces?

While many plans provide Orthodontic benefits, you do not need dental insurance. Braces are available to all patients regardless of dental insurance! We offer several payment options including No Money Down and monthly payment plans with autopay.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

Most dental insurance plans cover dental implants. Each plan is different so contact your dental insurance company for full details.

Does Great Lakes Family Dental accept Medicare?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts most dental insurance plans but does not currently accept Medicare.

Does Great Lakes Family Dental accept Medicaid?

Great Lakes Family Dental accepts most dental insurance plans but does not currently accept Medicaid.
