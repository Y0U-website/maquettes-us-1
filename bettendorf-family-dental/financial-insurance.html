Financial & Insurance

Your treatment plan will include a breakdown of all applicable fees, and we will inform you of all costs before treatment is administered. Full payment is required at the time of treatment. Our practice accepts Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover credit cards for your convenience. Third party financing is available through CareCredit.

If special arrangements are needed, please talk to our office manager prior to receiving service.

Our office is committed to helping you maximize your insurance benefits. We are a premier provider for Delta Dental. As a courtesy to our patients, we will bill insurance companies for services rendered. Because insurance policies vary, we can only estimate your coverage in good faith but cannot guarantee coverage due to the complexities of insurance contracts. Your estimated patient portion must be paid at the time of service.
