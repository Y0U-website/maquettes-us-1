Education and Pediatric Background

Children’s Hospital Colorado

Dr. Lara realized she could turn her high-school job of working for a pediatric dentist into a career of helping children get healthy smiles. She decided to complete her Pediatric Residency at the prestigious Children’s Hospital Colorado and obtained her doctorate at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Lara is proud to have worked in the Children’s Hospital system ! Her work with Children’s Hospital Colorado allowed her to treat children in a hospital setting as well as an office setting, so she learned how to make her patients feel comfortable in any environment.

Special Needs Pediatric Dentistry

Serving Special Needs Dental Cases

Dr. Lara is proud to serve patients, including special needs patients, at CPDH. She’s able to work on most special needs patients to make them comfortable and provide exceptional care.
