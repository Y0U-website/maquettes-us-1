  • Prevents Bone Deterioration – Without the structure of a tooth root, the jawbone lacks the stimulation and blood flow needed to thrive and will begin to deteriorate. Implants stimulate the jawbone to prevent this from occurring.
  • Versatility – From traditional dental implants to mini and full mouth dental implants, dental implants can be used for a single missing tooth or multiple missing teeth.
  • Prevent facial aging – Missing teeth can cause the cheeks to sink in, making your appearance look older. With implants in place, your facial structure is perfectly supported for a more youthful appearance.
