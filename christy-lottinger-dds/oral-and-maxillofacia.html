Emergency Dental Care

The UConn School of Dental Medicine offers Connecticut’s largest emergency dental service. Our mission is to provide people with the dental care they need when they need it the most. We offer emergency treatment of acute pain, bleeding, swelling, and trauma.

We Are the Center of Academic Excellence

We are not just a dental care provider. We are also dental care educators. We train the next generation of dentists and dental specialists with the latest knowledge and skills in dental medicine. We offer high-quality education and training to our students and residents, who work under the guidance and supervision of our faculty members. We are passionate about teaching and learning and are always ready to listen and answer your questions.

For current and prospective students, please visit the School of Dental Medicine’s website for more information.

Standards of Care

The UConn Health School of Dental Medicine’s Standards of Care define the pillars around which patient-centered care is delivered. The six standards are managed by the Quality Improvement Committee in conjunction with the Clinical Affairs Committee. These committees have oversight of policy change and implementation related to the quality of care delivery in our clinics.

  • Be provided in a safe manner.
  • Progress in a timely manner.
  • Be efficient .
  • Be effective and evidence-based, including relevant scientific evidence, patient needs and preferences, and clinician’s expertise.
  • Be delivered equitably and not vary in quality because of personal characteristics.
  • Be patient-centered and respectful of and responsive to individual patient preferences, needs, and values.
