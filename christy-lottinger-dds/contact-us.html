UConn Health General Inquiries

If you have a general inquiry about UConn Health, email contact@uchc.edu .

To protect your privacy, please do not include personal or confidential information in your message, such as date of birth, social security number, or health history.

UConn Health Webmaster

To report incorrect information or a bad link on a page of uconnhealth.org, please email web@uchc.edu .
