Meet The Doctor Crown 7 Dental

As pioneers of biomimetic dentistry in Butte, Montana, Dr. Cody Forkan and his team at Crown 7 Dentistry are committed to offering patients a conservative and effective alternative to traditional dental practices. Whether you’re seeking preventive care or treatment for existing dental issues, we invite you to schedule a consultation and discover how biomimetic dentistry can benefit you and your family.

Experience the difference at Crown 7 Dentistry, where preserving your natural smile is our priority.

  • Advanced Techniques: Our biomimetic bonding techniques are designed to: Thoroughly eliminate old bacteria and infection.
  • Create a durable seal against future infections.
  • Minimize sensitivity.
  • Restore the natural strength and functionality of the tooth.
