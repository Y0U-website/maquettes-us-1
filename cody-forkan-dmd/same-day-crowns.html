  • Digital Impressions : We use state-of-the-art digital technology to take accurate impressions of your teeth. This method is not only more comfortable than traditional putty impressions but also provides precise data for creating your custom crown.
  • Crown Design and Manufacturing : The digital impression is used to design your crown with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring it blends seamlessly with your natural teeth. Once the design is finalized, it is sent to our on-site milling machine. Depending on the material selected for your crown, the manufacturing time can vary between 20 to 40 minutes.
  • Placement : Once your crown is ready, we’ll securely fit and bond it to your tooth. Final adjustments are made to ensure the perfect fit and bite, completing the restoration of your tooth.

Crowns in One Day

The majority of our crowns are completed in just one day. This streamlined process is part of our commitment to efficient, patient-centered care. However, there are exceptions where a same-day crown may not be suitable. These exceptions typically involve cases with complex dental issues that require specialized materials or additional preparatory work. In such instances, we prioritize the quality and longevity of the outcome to ensure the best possible results for your dental health and satisfaction.

FAQ about Same Day Crowns

Q: How long does the entire process take?

A: The whole process, including digital impressions, design, milling, and placement, typically takes about two hours. This means you can walk out with a new crown in just one visit.

Q: Are Same Day Crowns as durable as traditional crowns?

A: Yes, Same Day Crowns are made from high-quality ceramic materials that are just as durable as traditional crowns. They are designed to last many years with proper care.

A: Absolutely. The ceramic material used in Same Day Crowns closely mimics the natural color and translucency of your teeth, ensuring a natural and aesthetically pleasing result.

A: The procedure for getting a Same Day Crown is generally no more uncomfortable than getting a traditional crown. We use local anesthesia to numb the area, ensuring you remain comfortable throughout the process.

Q: Can any tooth be restored with a Same Day Crown?

A: Most teeth that require a crown can be restored with a Same Day Crown. Dr. Forkan will evaluate your specific situation to determine if this is the best option for you.

A: Caring for a Same Day Crown is just like caring for your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups will help keep your crown and the surrounding teeth healthy.
