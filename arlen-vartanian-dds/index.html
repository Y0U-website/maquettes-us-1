The practice

We always invite new patients to become part of our dental family and look forward to seeing your smile.

We warmly welcome your entire family including kids. Helping little smiles stay healthy from an early age is important to our entire team. Many families we serve appreciate that we take care of everyone from toddlers to grandparents.

We believe in forging long-term relationships with the patients we serve. As we get to know you, we are better equipped to take care of your oral health. For over three decades, this dental practice has provided excellent dentistry with an emphasis on kind, compassionate care. Dr. Vartanian continues this tradition, and you will see many familiar faces from one visit to the next.

Unlike corporate dental offices, we can spend as much time necessary with our patients. This allows us to focus on education and helping our patients make the best choice for their goals and lifestyles. You only get one smile, and we want you to love yours​

We never want finances to keep you from having the smile you deserve, which is why we accept most PPO insurance plans and offer patient financing through CareCredit. Dr. Vartanian is happy to help you organize your treatment plan to phase it out over time. Our team will help you determine the best way to fit your dentistry into your family’s budget so you can have the dentistry you need most.

When it comes to cosmetic dentistry in our Alhambra dental office, you can count on natural-looking results that add confidence and improve your quality of life.

We also offer periodontal treatments and dental implants. We work closely with some of the most skilled specialists in the area to ensure the best outcomes possible.

If it is time for your next dental appointment, or if it has been a while since you’ve been to the dentist, contact us to arrange an appointment. We will never judge your oral health or make you feel guilty about your smile. We are here to help.
