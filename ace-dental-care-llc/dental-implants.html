Implant Options Available

Single Tooth Implant

Ideal for replacing one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. Natural appearance, independent replacement, and a long-term solution.

Implant Bridge

Used when multiple teeth are missing in the same area. Fewer implants needed while restoring chewing function and natural appearance.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Greater stability than traditional removable dentures. Improved retention, better chewing ability, increased comfort, and increased confidence.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

Full-arch solution using four strategically placed implants. Full smile restoration, reduced complexity, and improved chewing ability.

All-on-X Dental Implants

Designed for patients missing most or all teeth in an arch. Full-arch restoration, improved stability, and a long-term solution.
