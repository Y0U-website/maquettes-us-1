The practice

Periodontal Therapy - We offer services like scaling and root planing, which involves removing plaque build-up from below your gum line. As an integral part of your bone health, proper periodontal care helps to maintain healthy gums, prevent tooth loss and promotes overall oral health.

Restorative Dentistry - We offer a range of options to restore teeth that have been damaged by decay or injury. Our team will work with you to determine the best type of treatment-implants, dentures for your needs, ensuring a comfortable and long-lasting result.

Cosmetic Dentistry - If you are missing teeth or have other cosmetic concerns, we offer a range of options to restore your smile. From crowns, bridges to veneers, our team can help you achieve a beautiful, natural-looking smile. We also offer professional ZOOM whitening services to help you achieve a brighter, more youthful smile.

Oral Surgery - We understand that oral surgery can seem overwhelming. That s why we offer a range of oral surgery procedures, including extractions, bone grafts and implants, in a safe and comfortable environment. Our team is committed to providing high-quality care, so you can feel confident in your decision to undergo oral surgery with us.

Orthodontic Solutions - Clear aligners have transformed the way we treat conditions like crooked teeth, overbites, gaps, and other cosmetic issues. They are comfortable, easy to care for, and discreet. Our team will work with you to determine if you are a good candidate for clear aligners.

Pediatric Care - We offer pediatric dental care for infants through adolescence. Our focus on preventative, habitual dental care makes kids look forward to seeing the dentist, and taking care of their growing smiles.
