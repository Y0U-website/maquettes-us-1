Why Should I Get All-on-4 Implants?

Choosing All-on-4 implants can significantly enhance your quality of life. This treatment offers a durable and stable solution for those struggling with missing teeth, eliminating the discomfort and inconvenience of removable dentures. With All-on-4, you can enjoy the freedom of eating your favorite foods without worry and engage in social activities without the fear of your teeth slipping or falling out.

Not only that, but the All-on-4 implant system helps maintain your jawbone health, preventing the bone loss that often accompanies missing teeth. This helps preserve your facial structure and your overall oral health, too.

Did you know…

All-on-4 implants can be completed with a temporary set of teeth the same day as your surgery, transforming your smile instantly .

The Benefits Of All-on-4 Implants

Immediate Results With All-on-4, you can walk out with a brand-new smile after just one appointment. This immediate transformation means you don’t have to wait weeks or months to enjoy the benefits of your new teeth.

Cost-Effective Solution Compared to replacing each missing tooth with individual implants, All-on-4 is a more affordable option for replacing an entire row of upper or lower missing teeth. It uses fewer implants to support a full arch, reducing both the cost and time involved in the procedure.

Enhanced Comfort and Functionality All-on-4 implants are designed to feel like natural teeth, offering unparalleled comfort and functionality. You can chew, speak, and smile with ease, enjoying a lifestyle free from the limitations of traditional dentures.

The All-on-4 Implant Treatment Process

Consultation and Planning

Consultation and Planning

The journey to your new smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Culver Vista Dental. During this visit, Dr. Neda Shafai will assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if the All-on-4 implant is the right solution for you. Advanced imaging techniques will be used to plan the precise placement of your implants, ensuring optimal results tailored to your needs.

Implant Placement

Implant Placement

On the day of your procedure, you will receive local anesthesia to ensure your comfort throughout the process. Dr. Shafai will then strategically place four titanium implants into your jawbone. These implants serve as the foundation for your new teeth, and their strategic positioning maximizes stability and support. Once the implants are in place, a temporary set of teeth will be attached, allowing you to leave the office with a fully functional smile.

Final Restoration

Final Restoration

After a 3-6 month healing period, during which the implants integrate with your jawbone, you will return to Culver Vista Dental for the final restoration. Dr. Shafai will replace the temporary teeth with a custom-made, permanent set that perfectly fits your mouth. This final step ensures that your new smile not only looks amazing but also provides the comfort and functionality you desire.
