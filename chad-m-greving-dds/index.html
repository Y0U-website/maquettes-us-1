The practice

Our mission is to provide the highest quality dental care for all ages at an affordable price. Our office is dedicated to your entire family’s dental care, offering a warm and welcoming environment for children and adults alike.

By combining a calm atmosphere with modern technology, we have created a place where our patients in Elkhorn and the surrounding area receive the quality of dental care they need and deserve.

Drs. Chad Greving, Rebecca Greving, Julia Kennedy and Dr. Susan Eschendal and staff are dedicated to providing you with a pleasant visit and results that you’re proud to show off. Your comfort and confidence are just as important as your teeth.

Our experienced and friendly staff take great pride in keeping your smile beautiful. The moment you walk in the door, our team will make you feel comfortable, secure, and cared for. We are looking forward to working with you, your family, and friends.

We respect that each patient has different needs and desires when it comes to their dental treatment, and we promise to work with each patient individually to create a personalized treatment plan. We take pride in the wide array of dental services we provide.

If you have any questions regarding a certain service or procedure, or don’t see something listed that you are interested in, feel free to give us a call for more information. Our caring, friendly team of dental professionals is committed to helping you achieve a dazzling smile that you will be proud to show off.
