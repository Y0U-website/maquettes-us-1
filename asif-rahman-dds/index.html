The practice

Oral surgery is used to address a range of oral health issues. Through our procedures, we can help our patients improve their oral health. You are in safe hands with our experienced team.

We offer a range of sedation dentistry options, including nitrous oxide, IV sedation, and general anesthesia, to ensure your comfort throughout treatment.

The entire team at Metro Dentalcare Richfield Oral Surgery is committed to our patients. We perform every treatment with the utmost care and compassion, so each patient can achieve the best outcome for their smile and overall oral health.

Safe and gentle extractions of problematic teeth ensure patient comfort every step of the way.

We perform socket preservation to maintain the bone structure after tooth removal. This helps to prepare you for future dental work and preserve your oral health.

Our skilled professionals prepare your mouth for the placement of your dental prosthetics to promote the best possible fit and function.

A sinus lift is a treatment that helps increase the bone volume and density of your upper jaw so that it’s optimal for future implant placement. This treatment improves both functionality and aesthetics.

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. They restore your smile and provide unmatched stability, supporting your oral health in the years to come.
