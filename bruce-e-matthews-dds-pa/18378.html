What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for permanent tooth replacement.

Implants stand apart from other tooth-replacement options since they adhere to the jaw bone and stimulate bone growth. Dentures and bridges do not prevent bone recession or tooth shifting and may need to be replaced over time.

Dental implants are surgically installed into the area(s) where you are missing teeth, establishing a strong foundation for the placement of permanent, long-lasting prosthetic teeth. Implants are made from titanium and are securely anchored into your jawbone, providing permanent support to the implant restorations, or artificial teeth, that will fill the gap in your smile.

Implant restorations in Wilmington, DE, consist of either a crown or a bridge, depending on the number of missing teeth in the affected area. We provide custom-fitting restorations that fit safely and comfortably within your existing teeth and match the natural color of your smile.

Dental implants in Wilmington, DE look and function just like natural teeth, and no one will ever be able to tell the difference! Best of all, with proper care and maintenance, patients with dental implants can expect to enjoy them for many years. Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are a reliable permanent tooth-replacement option for many people who have missing teeth. The implant post naturally fuses to the jawbone, which makes it last for a lifetime.

However, the crown attached to the post can last anywhere between 10 to 15 years, or longer. The better the crown is cared for, the more likely it will last without premature wear or breakage.

Replacing a worn or damaged crown is essential for maintaining your oral health and the natural function of your teeth. Without a strong and stable crown, you can have issues chewing, biting, speaking, and socializing with ease.

Tips for Dental Implant Care

To maintain the function and strength of the dental implant and crown, we suggest that you:

  • Brush your teeth at least twice daily
  • Floss at least once daily
  • Get routine dental exams and cleanings twice each year
  • Avoid hard, crunchy, or chewy foods that could break the crown
