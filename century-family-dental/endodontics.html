Endodontic Procedures Offered at Century Family Dental

We provide advanced endodontic care using the latest techniques to ensure effective, comfortable treatment.

  • Root Canal Therapy: The most common procedure, root canal therapy removes infected or damaged pulp, cleans and disinfects the canals inside the tooth, then fills and seals them to protect from reinfection.
  • Endodontic Retreatment: If a previous root canal treatment fails or infection recurs, retreatment can save the tooth without extraction.
  • Apicoectomy (Root-End Surgery): In cases where infection persists after root canal therapy, a surgical procedure called an apicoectomy removes the infected tissue at the root tip.
  • Treatment of Traumatized Teeth: We treat teeth damaged due to injury or trauma, aiming to save and restore function whenever possible.

Benefits of Endodontic Treatment

  • Effective pain relief: Root canal therapy quickly alleviates severe tooth pain.
  • Natural tooth preservation: Avoid tooth extraction and maintain your natural bite.
  • High success rates: Modern endodontic procedures have success rates exceeding 90 %.
  • Improved oral health: Treat infections early to avoid complications like abscesses.
  • Cost-effective: Saving your natural tooth is often less expensive and more comfortable than extraction and replacement.

What to Expect During Endodontic Treatment

Most root canal treatments are completed in one or two visits with minimal discomfort.

  • Initial Consultation: Our team evaluates your tooth with X-rays and a comprehensive dental examination.
  • Clinical Care: The treatment area is numbed to ensure comfort.
  • Procedure: The infected pulp is removed, canals cleaned and shaped, then filled and sealed.
  • Restoration: After root canal therapy, your tooth will need a crown or filling to restore full function and protect it.
  • Follow-Up: Periodic check-ups ensure healing and long-term success.
