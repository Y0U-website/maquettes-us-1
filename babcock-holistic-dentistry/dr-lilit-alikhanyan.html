Dr. Lilit Alikhanyan

Holistic Dentist

Dr. Lilit graduated from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry in 2017. She began practicing in Colorado, and then in Texas before moving to Kansas City. Her interest in dentistry was ignited when, at the age of 16, she had the opportunity to observe the dentist working on her teeth through a mirror.

Originally from Armenia, Dr. Lilit moved to the United States with her mother when she was 14 years old, driven by the pursuit of the American Dream. She attended high school in Salida, Colorado, where she met and eventually married her future husband. Dr. Lilit attended the University of Colorado at Boulder, where she earned an undergraduate degree in Integrative Physiology.

Her husband, an engineer, secured a job in Kansas City, prompting their move to the area. Dr. Lilit embraces an active lifestyle, enjoying outdoor activities and exercise. Her affinity for wholesome, natural foods is reflected in her knack for creating incredible smoothies. She has a love for animals and has a Cat named Kimbo and a Husky named Brutus. She and her husband aspire to establish a foster farm one day, dedicated to caring for animals in need of special attention.

Dr. Lilit is committed to assisting you in attaining optimal health and well-being. Your journey towards superior oral health commences here, guided by the expertise and dedication of Dr. Lilit Alikhanyan.

Credentials: Member of IAOMT - SMART Certified AACA- American Academy of Clear Aligners AGD- Academy of General Dentists

Member of local study clubs: Invisalign, KC Women Dentists, Great Plains Study Club
