Lingual Braces

Hidden Braces for a Seamless Smile

Lingual braces are a discreet orthodontic option that lets you straighten your teeth without anyone noticing. Unlike traditional braces, lingual braces are placed on the back (lingual side) of your teeth, keeping them completely hidden from view.

Each set is custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth, offering a more comfortable and tailored fit. This behind-the-scenes solution is a favorite among actors, models, musicians, and athletes—anyone who wants to maintain a natural smile while improving it.

Lingual braces are ideal if you want effective orthodontic results without the visible brackets and wires. Whether you’re in the spotlight or simply want a more private path to straighter teeth, lingual braces offer the best of both worlds: powerful results and complete discretion.
