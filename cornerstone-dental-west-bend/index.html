We currently offer SureSmile clear aligners and will soon be able to offer traditional braces!

SureSmile clear aligners straighten teeth invisibly, without metal braces. They are removable so you can eat whatever you like. There are no wires to trap food or get in the way when you floss.

If one of our doctors believe you are a good candidate for SureSmile aligners, they will take photos and x-rays, then use a scanner or impressions to capture the shape of your teeth. You’ll wear a series of aligners customized to apply pressure to the teeth that need to move. SureSmile aligners are worn for at least 22 hours per day, progressing through each step until your treatment is done.
