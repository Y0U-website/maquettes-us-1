Our Doctors

Dr. Ellie Dowden

I graduated Summa Cum Laude from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2014. Since graduation, I have completed numerous hours of continuing education to advance my education and am pursuing my fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry. I am a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Wisconsin Dental Association (WDA), and Academy of General Dentistry.

What I really want you to know about me is that I love dentistry and I love people. I am passionate about achieving optimal oral and overall health and am committed to helping you do the same. I consider it a privilege and honor to work with people to help educate and motivate them to achieve their goals.

In my personal life, I have an amazing husband and three awesome kids. I love being a mom as well as reading and staying active by riding my Peloton, walking and doing yoga.

As you can probably tell from this bio, I love to chat. I promise to take the time to answer your questions and make you feel at ease. I enjoy being a part of the West Bend community and look forward to getting to know each and every one of you!

Dr. Alex Peroutky

I graduated cum laude from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2019. Since graduating, I have completed over 500 hours of continuing education in comprehensive dentistry, with a special emphasis on endodontics (a specialty in dentistry focused on dental pulp) and dental implants. I am a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Wisconsin Dental Association (WDA), and Academy of General Dentistry.

Many people are nervous about trips to the dentist, but I make it my goal to help educate my patients while caring for them. I understand that coming to the dental office can be a big deal for people. My team and I strive for providing the best comprehensive care tailored for your unique needs, all while making you feel as comfortable as possible.

In my personal life, I love living an active and healthy lifestyle. I enjoy hitting the gym, playing tennis, golf, and basketball. You’ll frequently find me cheering on Wisconsin sports, trying out new dinner recipes, or planning a new travel destination. When I need a break from my active lifestyle, there’s nothing quite as relaxing as reading a good book with my old dog Gilbert.

I’m thrilled to be a part of this community and vow to be attentive to all your questions and concerns. I look forward to taking the time in getting to know you and work towards achieving your goals in your oral health.
