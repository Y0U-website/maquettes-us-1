What Is a Deductible?

A deductible is the amount per year that you will have to pay out of pocket before your insurance covers the remaining costs for the work you have done. A deductible is something that has to be paid in addition to the amount that you pay per year for your insurance coverage. Sometimes, the deductible only applies to certain services. With most dental coverage, a deductible is only applied towards basic and major work, such as filling root canals or crowns, but not towards preventive work, such as x-rays or cleanings.

How Does it Work?

The In-Office Saving Plan works similarly to many dental plans on the market. You pay a monthly membership fee for benefit coverage on your dental expenses. This provides coverage that can be budgeted at an affordable monthly expense while receiving good benefits for your higher-cost dental services.
