How Does it Work?

The In-Office Saving Plan works similarly to many dental plans on the market. You pay a monthly membership fee for benefit coverage on your dental expenses. The Plan is designed to help cover preventive services such as cleanings and oral exams. This provides coverage that can be budgeted at an affordable monthly expense while receiving good benefits for your higher-cost dental services.

Who Administers the Plan?

Clinton Family Dental administers the In Office Savings Plan and works with you, the patient, directly to make all dental service decisions. There is no need to contact a third party for approvals. All questions regarding plan coverage, verifying benefits, changing coverage types, or any other questions should be directed to our team here at Clinton Family Dental.

How Do I Use a Specialist?

All dental services covered by the In Office Saving Plan are designed to be done at our office here at Clinton Family Dental. We may refer a patient to a specialist who performs other services that the patient may need that are not covered by the Plan. Clinton Family Dental has a complete listing of highly recommended specialists.
