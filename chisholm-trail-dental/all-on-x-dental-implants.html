What Are All-On-X Dental Implants?

A revolutionary approach to full-mouth tooth replacement.

All-On-X dental implants are a type of dental implant procedure where four or more titanium posts are strategically placed in the jaw to support a full arch of new teeth. This innovative method replaces missing teeth with a stable, natural-looking solution and is ideal for healthy adults who want a permanent solution to tooth loss.

Unlike traditional dentures, these custom dental implants are fixed in place, allowing you to enjoy life without worry.

FAQs about All-On-X Dental Implants

Your questions answered by our expert dental team.

  • Am I a good candidate for All-On-X dental implants? Most healthy adults with missing teeth and good oral hygiene are candidates. We’ll evaluate your needs during your consultation.
  • How long does the healing period take? Initial healing may take a few weeks, but the full healing process can last up to six months. You’ll receive temporary teeth during this time.
