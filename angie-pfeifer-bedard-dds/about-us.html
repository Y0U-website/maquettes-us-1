Many people feel uneasy about dental treatment. But if you haven’t been to the dentist for a while, you will be pleasantly surprised.

Comfortable Dentistry

Our gentle approach to care will make your visit a positive experience. Stereo headphones, nitrous-oxide sedation and other options help you feel as comfortable as possible.

Your Safety is Our Concern

Our commitment to your health involves more than quality dentistry. In light of today’s heightened awareness, we’ve established a sterilization program designed to ensure the safety of both patients and staff. You’ll probably notice our masks and gloves, but there’s more to it than that. We’re proud to insist on the highest safety protocols.
