Bleaching

In-Office Xtra Boost

Professional results—in about an hour. For a brighter, whiter smile without the wait, choose Opalescence Boost, an in-office, chemically activated whitening treatment that offers results in less than an hour. It’s ideal for people who want instant whitening gratification or those who have that special event coming up that requires a bright, white smile. At home whitening go trays will be included for at home use.

Custom Tray Whitening

Impressions of your teeth will be taken that will allow for custom trays to be made for your whitening procedure. Your custom trays should be worn once a day for 60 minutes for two weeks with Opalescence tooth whitening gel that contains PF (potassium nitrate and fluoride), which helps maintain the health of enamel throughout the whitening process.

Take Home Whitening

Want a quick and easy way to put some radiance back into your smile? Parkview Dental may have just the ticket! Our Opalescence Go take-home whitening kits give you the professional results you want in the comfort of your own home. A take-home whitening kit includes 10 preloaded trays with a 15 % bleaching solution. A bright, beautiful smile that can make you look years younger and give you a confidence boost each time you look in the mirror!

Straightening Teeth

Parkview Dental offers several short-term orthodontic options that will straighten your front teeth.

Six Month Smiles

This is a short-term adult orthodontic procedure that will straighten your front teeth in approximately six months. Everyone should smile with confidence. No more metal braces or wires, finally there is an affordable option that will give you that smiling confidence.

Clear Correct and MTM

These are clear aligner systems that allow straightening without the hassles of traditional braces. These virtually invisible aligners are easy to put in and take out while offering the ease of comfort. The MTM system is used for orthodontic retreat patient. Patients finally have nice, affordable orthodontic options to smile with confidence.
