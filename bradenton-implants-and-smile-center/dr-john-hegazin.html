About John Hegazin, DDS

Dr. Hegazin is recognized for his commitment to excellence in advanced and comprehensive dental care. From routine cosmetic and restorative dentistry to complex full-mouth rehabilitation and implant reconstruction, he is known for providing highly personalized treatment plans designed to restore not only oral health, but also confidence, comfort, and quality of life. His patient-centered philosophy, combined with extensive surgical experience and advanced clinical training, has earned him a reputation for delivering outstanding dental care with exceptional attention to detail.

"As a dentist, I am committed to remaining at the forefront of dental technology and advancements in the field. I place great importance on continuing education and regularly pursue advanced training to stay current with the latest innovations and techniques in dentistry. This ongoing dedication to professional growth allows me to provide the highest quality care and best possible outcomes for my patients."

Having practiced dentistry in Texas, California, and Florida, Dr. Hegazin has developed a distinguished clinical background treating a diverse range of patients and highly complex dental cases. He has completed more than 500 continuing education (CE) credits in implant dentistry and has successfully placed and restored over 5,000 dental implants throughout his career. His extensive advanced training includes digital dentistry, 3D printing and intraoral scanning, guided implant surgery, immediate loading protocols, full-mouth implant rehabilitation, and IV sedation.

He regularly participates in premier dental conferences, including the Florida National Dental Convention and the American Dental Association Annual Convention, ensuring his patients receive the most advanced, evidence-based care available today.

Q1. How often should I visit the dentist?

Most adults should visit the dentist every six months for a routine exam and professional cleaning.

However, some patients—especially those with gum disease, dental implants, or ongoing restorative treatments—may benefit from more frequent visits. At Bradenton Implants & Smile Center, we personalize your care schedule to protect long-term oral health and keep your smile healthy and confident.

Q3. Do you accept insurance and payment plans?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans and are happy to help you maximize your benefits. For uninsured patients, we offer flexible payment options to make high-quality dental care accessible and affordable. Our team will review all costs with you up front so there are no surprises.

Q4. How do I know if I need a filling, a crown, or an implant?

The best way to know is through a comprehensive dental exam and digital imaging.

A filling is usually recommended for small areas of decay. A crown is used when a tooth is more severely damaged or weakened but can still be preserved. A dental implant is advised when a tooth is missing or cannot be saved and a permanent replacement is needed.
