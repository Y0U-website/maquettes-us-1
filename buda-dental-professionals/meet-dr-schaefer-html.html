Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Schaefer grew up in Okemos, Michigan, but she considers Austin her hometown now. She lives in the area with her boyfriend and their two fur-babies, their Irish Wolfhound and poodle-mix, Marley, and their Maine Coon cat, Kygo. She enjoys going back to Michigan to take continuing education courses with her dad, who is a dentist and hosts various CE courses himself!

Whenever Dr. Schaefer isn’t at the office, she enjoys going golfing, hiking, wakesurfing on Lake Austin, and trying new restaurants in the area. She also goes to a lot of live music shows and concerts with her boyfriend, Chris. As an avid adventurer, Dr. Schaefer also enjoys traveling, SCUBA diving, working out, and competing in Hyrox. An interesting fact about her is that she was a collegiate athlete and was on the University of Michigan diving team!
