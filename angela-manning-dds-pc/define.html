Results

MORPHEUS8

Morpheus8 is a minimally invasive device for fractional coagulation of subcutaneous tissue. Morpheus8 provides an interchangeable portfolio of microneedling tips to deliver customizable fractional treatments.

DEFINE CHEEK AND DEFINE CHIN

Define Cheek and Define Chin headsets are hands-free facial technologies designed to address the lower face and neck. The patented technology harnesses the power of RF energy to treat facial tissue and improve its appearance.

FORMA

WHAT IS THE BENEFIT OF RADIOFREQUENCY ENERGY?

Radiofrequency energy heats the deep layers of the skin in a controlled way to revitalize collagen. It works to improve skin complexion and texture for a more radiant and youthful appearance.
