NOW ACCEPTING PATIENTS

The California Northstate University College of Dental Medicine Comprehensive Family Care Clinic at 2201 Broadway in Sacramento, CA [ Map ] is now accepting patients.

The practice

The Comprehensive Family Care Clinic provides dental care for children and adults. All care is provided by a dental student team under the direct supervision of faculty who are California-licensed dental professionals.

Note: When scheduling your first appointment at our clinic, please have your insurance information ready. This will help us streamline the check-in process and ensure you receive the benefits you’re entitled to during your visit.
