Dr. Samir Sulieman

Dr. Sulieman received a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennessee College of Health Science. He was born and raised in North Little Rock, Arkansas, and attended the University of Arkansas, where he received a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry. Dr. Sulieman currently resides in Little Rock with his wife, children, and dog.

Dr. Shirin Amirfaiz

Dr. Amirfaiz received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennessee College of Health Science in Memphis. She attended East Tennessee State University and received a Bachelor of Science in Biology. Dr. Amirfaiz is from east Tennessee and currently resides in Little Rock with her husband, children, and dog.
