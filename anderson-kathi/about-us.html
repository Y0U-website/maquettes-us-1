Dr. Bradley D. Lembke

Dr. Brad Lembke is a 1990 graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and has proudly served the Chanhassen community for over 29 years. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from St. Olaf College, where he also played four years of collegiate football.

Beyond his practice, Dr. Lembke is deeply connected to the Chanhassen community and values the relationships he’s built with his patients over the years. He believes in providing personalized care that helps each patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Outside the office, Dr. Lembke enjoys fitness, recreation, and spending time with his family. He is especially proud that both sons have chosen to follow in his footsteps Ryan, a 2024 graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, is currently practicing in Blaine, while Lucas is in his second year of the same dental school.
