About Us Arctic Dental

Dr. Jarod is a member in good standing with the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, Iowa Dental Association, and the Davenport District Dental Society.

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery, University of Iowa, 2013
  • Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry, University of Nevada, Las Vegas, School of Dental Medicine
  • Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

Community & Continuing Education

While in Las Vegas Dr. Jarod served as a Delegate to the Southern Nevada Dental Society in the Nevada Dental Association.

He was in the Las Vegas Community and has worked to bring Team Smile, a community outreach program, back to Las Vegas.

Dr. Jarod is an active member of the Muscatine Community. He regularly attends community events and has served a member of the board for the Musser Public Library.

Dr. Jarod also holds a position as an Adjunct Assisting Professor in Pediatric Dentistry and Preventive and Community Dentistry at The University of Iowa College of Dentistry and Dental Clinics.

He lectures throughout the country on pediatric dentistry, and has been published in numerous magazines.

Dr. Jarod has a daughter named Lydia, and a sons, William and Teddy. He enjoys spending time with his daughter and sons, cooking, grilling, and exploring the outdoors.
