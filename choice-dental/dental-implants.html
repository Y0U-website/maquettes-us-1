Answers to common questions about dental implant cost, All on 4, All on X, and financing at Choice Dental

What is the difference between All on 4 and All on X dental implants?

All on 4 replaces a full arch of teeth using four dental implants per arch, while All on X uses the number of implants your case actually needs, often four, six, or more, to support a fixed full arch of teeth. Both secure a complete set of natural looking teeth to your jaw for a stable, long lasting result. During your consultation in Marshall, Texas, we recommend the approach that best fits your bone structure and goals.

Can dental implants replace a single missing tooth?

Yes. A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting the healthy teeth beside it. It uses a titanium implant placed in the jaw, an abutment, and a custom crown that matches your natural teeth. This is a durable, natural looking way to fill a gap and protect your bite and surrounding teeth.

Do you offer implant supported dentures?

Yes. Implant supported dentures, sometimes called snap in dentures or overdentures, attach securely to dental implants instead of resting on your gums. They are far more stable than traditional dentures, so you can eat and speak with confidence and without slipping. This is a popular full arch option for patients in Marshall, Texas who want a secure, comfortable fit.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant fuses with your jawbone to form a stable foundation, and the crown or bridge on top can last many years before it may need routine maintenance. Caring for implants is much like caring for natural teeth, with daily brushing, flossing, and checkups.

Do you offer same day dental implants?

In many cases, yes. Our team can complete treatment efficiently, often with same day delivery so you leave with teeth in a day rather than waiting months. Whether same day placement is right for you depends on your oral health and treatment plan, which we review during your consultation at our Marshall, Texas dental office.
