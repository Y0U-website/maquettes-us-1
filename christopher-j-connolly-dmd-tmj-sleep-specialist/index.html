The practice

Dental implants are one of the most trusted and dependable tooth replacement options available today, as they are designed to replace teeth while still conserving the underlying jawbone integrity. Missing teeth can cause the jawbone beneath the teeth to deteriorate, resulting in face drooping and other maxillofacial issues. Dental implants help to arrest this process by keeping stimulation on the…

Dental implants are made of two parts: a titanium post and a crown that sits on top of the titanium implant. By actually fusing to the jawbone, these implants offer a very strong, permanent hold that helps to preserve facial structure as well as jawbone health.

If you are a good candidate for dental implants, your dentist at Connolly Family Dentistry looks forward to getting started right away.

Let’s start with the basics first. A dental implant is a replacement for a missing tooth’s root. It is a tiny post made out of titanium that is implanted directly beneath the gums, where it provides a sturdy base for the prosthetic tooth (or teeth) that is affixed on top. Dental implant procedures are completed in two phases: the initial implantation of each implant and their final restoration, or when the prosthetic tooth/teeth are affixed on top. At Connolly Family Dentistry, our team of doctors provides complete dental implant care from start to finish — so you will not have to worry about getting a referral to an outside office for the initial implantation…

It all starts with a consultation. During this initial appointment, our team will evaluate the health of your jawbone and determine whether or not you are a good candidate for this comprehensive replacement. You may need to have an initial bone grafting procedure performed first. This surgery adds volume to the weak or depleted jawbone to create a perfect environment for dental implants.

Once your jaw is ready, the procedure can begin. Your dentist uses x-rays and models of your surrounding teeth and jaw to precisely place each implant exactly where it needs to go. After implantation, recovery begins. It can take anywhere from four to eight months for the implants to fully heal together with the jawbone.
