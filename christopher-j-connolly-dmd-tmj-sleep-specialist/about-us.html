About Us Connolly Family Dentistry

At Connolly Family Dentistry, We Put Our Patients First!

Our dental practice has been located on Egg Harbor Road in Sewell, Washington Township, New Jersey, since 1979. Started by Dr. Chris Connolly, it has grown to include three full-time dentists and an ever-expanding auxiliary and clerical staff. Our office is dedicated to treating your entire family’s dental needs. We believe that a beautiful, healthy smile greatly improves your confidence and your overall wellbeing!

Prevention and Information

Our well-trained professional staff endeavors to treat each patient as an individual, not just a chart number. We provide comprehensive treatment plans and state-of-the-art restorative and cosmetic dental procedures. We make every effort to handle dental emergencies as quickly as possible. We also offer expanded office hours and Saturday appointments. We invite you to meet our doctors and our staff!

Professional Training

It is very important that our patients have the utmost confidence in our professional training and our dental expertise. Our doctors and our clinical staff participate in continuing education opportunities, so we can stay apprised of the most cutting-edge procedures, techniques, and equipment in the dental industry.

Safety

To protect the health of our patients and staff, we carefully adhere to the standards recommended by the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the American Dental Association (ADA) with regard to sterilization techniques and the control of infectious elements within the office.

Connolly Family Dentistry treats residents in Sewell, Mullica Hill, Washington Township and surrounding communities. Your family’s dental care is our priority, so please contact us now for your appointment!
