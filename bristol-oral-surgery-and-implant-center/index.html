Dental implants are biocompatible titanium screws that act as anchors in the jaw bones to allow a nearly natural replacement for a missing tooth or teeth.

Technological advances have allowed us to be able remove all of the teeth, place dental implants in the bone, and attached prefabricated custom designed teeth in one appointment.

Dental implants need to be placed completely in the jaw bone. If there is insufficient bone volume, the jaw can be augmented with bone grafting procedures.

Wisdom Teeth Most often, wisdom teeth or third molars don’t have sufficient space to fully erupt into the mouth. Sometimes they are blocked, or impacted from erupting. Removal when young and healthy is generally very safe and uncomplicated, whereas having them removed after disease arises, or older than 30 years of age it can be more difficult.
