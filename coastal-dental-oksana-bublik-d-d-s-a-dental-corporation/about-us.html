About Us

Dr. Oksana Bublik, D.D.S.

Dr. Oksana Bublik has been practicing as a general dentist in Orange County since 1991 and has been caring for patients at COASTAL DENTAL since 2020. With more than 35 years of experience and advanced training in both U.S. and European dental techniques, she provides thoughtful, personalized care for your family’s dental needs

COASTAL DENTAL is conveniently situated in the Capistrano Beach neighborhood of Dana Point, near San Clemente. Easily accessible from the I-5 via the Camino de Estrella exit, our dental office offers modern amenities, a private parking lot, and a welcoming atmosphere. Nearby landmarks include Sprouts Farmers Market, pediatrician Dr. Bob Sears, Kawamata Seafood, and Pines Park.
