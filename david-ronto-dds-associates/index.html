My name is David Ronto and I have had the privilege of providing dental care to southwest Michigan for the last 20+ years. I thought I would take this opportunity to tell you all a little about myself.

What brought me to Michigan originally, was college. I attended Andrews University and I really loved the area. I met my wife there and we always spoke of coming back to the area when I was done with graduate school. I attended Loma Linda University in Southern California where I received my Dental degree.

Those of you who know me understand the kind of person and dentist that I am. While I have not been able to make EVERY person happy, we have seen thousands of very pleased patients. So I am sorry to those who I could not help or have had misunderstandings with, and I am honored to take care of those who are with us. My one and only goal is to treat peoples dental problems honestly, kindly, and…

I perform most dentistry myself without referring out. While there are some procedures that I don’t do often enough to be proficient at which I will refer out, I hold my colleagues to the same standard I expect of myself. I believe that the specialists I work with are the best. However, I would guess that 95 % of all your dental needs will be met without the need for referral.

I truly believe that dentistry can be done with a minimal amount of discomfort and actually with a little bit of fun. Just ask our patients. They will tell you how much grief I get which seems to be a great source of entertainment for everyone. This is the reason that I don’t take myself too seriously, since truly the best medicine is often times laughter.

I understand that dentistry can be expensive and overwhelming. This is why I try to explain and plan as thoroughly as I can for every single person. Every person is different, and everyone’s situation and dental needs and desires are different.
