MEET OUR STAFF

Dr. Allaman completed his undergraduate studies at Mount Vernon Nazarene University in Ohio, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 2003. He then attended Ohio State University and also served in Gabon, Africa on a dental mission trip. He graduated with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2008. After completing a one-year residency at St. Elizabeth’s, a Level 1 Trauma Center in Youngstown, Ohio, Dr. Allaman worked in both private practice and public health in Ohio and North Carolina.

Dr. Allaman moved to the Martinsville area in 2012 and had the opportunity to work with mentor and friend, Dr. George Stermer, DDS. Dr. Allaman appreciates the educational and surgical aspects of dentistry, but says the best part of his job is interacting with patients and their families.

Dr. Allaman enjoys giving back locally to the Martinsville community and also hopes to complete another dental mission trip in the future. In his free time, Dr. Allaman enjoys reading, traveling, and relaxing with his wife, daughter, and two dogs.
