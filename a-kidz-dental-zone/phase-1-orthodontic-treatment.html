Phase 1 orthodontics, or interceptive orthodontics, provides early treatment for children ages 7 to 10. This first stage of treatment corrects teeth and jaw alignment, ensuring proper growth and development.

Investing in phase I orthodontic treatment can lead to a lifetime of benefits and a healthy, beautiful smile. Let us help your child achieve optimal oral health!

Early Detection and Prevention One of the main advantages of phase I orthodontic treatment is the early detection and prevention of potential problems. By starting treatment at a younger age, Dr. House can identify any underlying issues such as overcrowding, crossbites, or protruding front teeth. These issues may seem minor in childhood but can develop into more severe problems if left untreated. With phase I treatment, we can address these problems before they worsen and require more invasive procedures.

Promotes Proper Jaw Growth

The jaw is still developing during childhood and can easily be guided into a proper position with orthodontic appliances. Phase I treatment can help correct any misalignments or discrepancies in jaw size, ensuring proper development and reducing the risk of TMJ disorders and other oral health issues later on. By guiding the jaw into its ideal position at an early age, we can also create ample room for permanent teeth to grow properly, preventing overcrowding and potential tooth extractions.

Improves Oral Health Crooked or misaligned teeth can be challenging to clean, allowing plaque and bacteria to build up and cause dental problems such as cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. Phase I orthodontic treatment can help straighten teeth, making cleaning and maintaining good oral hygiene easier. This not only improves the smile’s appearance but also promotes better oral health.

Boosts Self-Esteem and Confidence

Children with misaligned teeth may feel self-conscious about their appearance, affecting their self-esteem and confidence. By addressing orthodontic issues early on, we can help children achieve a beautiful and healthy smile, boosting their self-confidence and self-esteem. This can have a positive impact on their overall well-being and social interactions.

Shorter Treatment Time

Another advantage of phase I orthodontic treatment is that it can reduce the time needed for future orthodontic treatment. By addressing any underlying issues before they worsen, the second phase of treatment (usually with braces) can focus solely on aligning teeth rather than correcting developmental problems. This results in shorter overall treatment time and may avoid the need for more complex procedures in the future.

Comfortable and Customizable Treatment

Every child’s orthodontic needs are unique, so we offer customized treatment plans tailored to each individual patient. Our phase I treatment involves comfortable and noninvasive appliances such as expanders or retainers, which can be adjusted to fit your child’s specific needs. We also use the latest technology and methods to ensure a smooth treatment experience for our young patients.
