GENERAL & COSMETIC DENTISTRY IN SCHUYLKILL COUNTY

For over 35 years, the Buehner family has been caring for the oral health of families in Minersville and throughout Pennsylvania.

The practice

After taking over the practice in 2015, Dr. Ryan Buehner has been honored to work at his father’s practice to provide the same quality of care patients have received since 1979. He has also worked to foster community growth, increase dental education and overall raise the level of dentisty in Schuykill County.

From the dentists at the practice to the support staff who ensure things run smoothly, everyone at Buehner Family Dental Care is truly dedicated to the health and care of each patient.
