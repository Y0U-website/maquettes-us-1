Dr. Manuel Vidrios, DDS Dr. Manuel Vidrios was raised in the Yakima Valley and completed his undergraduate degree in Microbiology at Washington State University. He earned his dental degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry, where he served as Vice President of the Hispanic Dental Association.

Growing up in White Swan, WA, Dr. Vidrios has always been dedicated to serving rural and underserved farming communities. He is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Washington State Dental Association (WSDA).
