About Us

Who We Are Cheyenne Orthodontics takes great pride in our friendly and well-trained team members! As a team, we are especially interested in making all of our patients feel right at home! We put your needs first to achieve efficient and comprehensive treatment in a supportive and nurturing environment. We promise to speak candidly with you about possible treatments as well as any risks and associated costs of treatment.

Our staff is uniquely trained and highly skilled. We strive to provide energetic and fun-loving service to each patient. Our staff consists of team members with specialized training and multiple certifications, and each one of our team members has years of experience in treating patients with the highest level of care. We strive to stay abreast of the very latest in technology and advances by regularly attending continuing education courses and seminars.

To experience the difference that only a well-trained, professional and courteous team can deliver, schedule your appointment with our office today!

Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Jason Bird, DDS

Dr. Bird received his undergraduate degrees in microbiology and German literature, as well as a minor in chemistry from Brigham Young University. He completed his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from Creighton University where he was class president during his sophomore, junior, and senior years. Upon graduation from Creighton, Dr. Bird moved to New York City for training in orthodontics from New York University. He is board certified by the American Board of Orthodontics.

Dr. Bird has been president of the Wyoming Association of Orthodontists and he served on the American Association Orthodontists National Council of Membership, Ethics and Judicial Concerns. He is currently a delegate to the Rocky Mountain Society of Orthodontists

Dr. Bird was raised in Oregon and moved to Casper, Wyoming before his senior year in high school. He is married to Susanna and is the proud father of six children, Abigail, Edward, Eliza, Willett, Wren and Phoebe.

Dr. Bird has trained for and competed in triathlons for over 25 years. In 2020, he decided to try the humbling sport of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. He has earned a blue belt and will continue to work towards a purple belt. He also coaches his son’s soccer team and when he finds time, he plays the piano.
