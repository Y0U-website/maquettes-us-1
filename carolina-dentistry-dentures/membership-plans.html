Membership Plans

Unlike a dental insurance plan, there is no third-party, no deductibles, copays or waiting periods. Enrolling in our In-House Membership Plan gives you the opportunity to obtain your dental treatment exclusively at our practice for reduced fees. It cannot be combined with dental insurance or other reduced fee dental plans.

Annual Enrollment Fee plus monthly charge for each enrolled member is refundable up to first 30 days of initial enrollment. If a refund is requested, the patient is responsible for all discounts applied while under plan.
