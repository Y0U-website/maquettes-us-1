Dental Implants

Whether you’re suffering from tooth loss, decay, gum disease, or an injury, dental implants are a way to regain your smile in a spa-like, judgment-free space.

Dental implants have revolutionized tooth replacement and are considered to be the best long-term tooth replacement option. In most cases, dental implants last 25+ years to life with proper maintenance. Dental implants provide tooth stability so you can chew, eat, and even speak easier and comfortably. They also provide a strong foundation for tooth replacement and help maintain bone to prevent the sunken-in appearance often associated with long-term tooth and bone loss.

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants are small anchors made of titanium that are placed into your jaw to replace your “tooth root”. Within a few months of placement, the implant anchors are strong enough to support the permanent attachment of your new replacement tooth.
