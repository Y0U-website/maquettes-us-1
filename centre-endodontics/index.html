The practice

After establishing a reputation across the Boston Metro Area for exceptional clinical quality and a conservative, tooth-preserving approach, he founded Centre Endodontics in 2022 to deliver specialty endodontic care at the highest level. His practice provides advanced root canal therapy, endodontic retreatment, apical microsurgery, and management of cracked teeth and traumatic dental injuries…

Dr. Roque is the Past President of the Massachusetts Association of Endodontists and serves as Vice President of the Harvard University Alumni Association (Graduate Schools). He has also held leadership roles with the Harvard Dental Alumni Association, the Metropolitan District Dental Society, and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Centre Endodontics has been named a Boston Magazine Best of Boston honoree for five consecutive years, from 2022 through 2026. He lives in the Boston area with his wife, Christine, and their three children.
