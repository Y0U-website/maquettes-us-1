Membership Plan

No Dental Insurance? No problem! We have a solution for you because everyone needs affordable yet high quality dental care.

With Four Lakes Dental Membership Plan , you will receive one complimentary cleaning and exam and will take advantage of up to 40 % courtesy discount on your treatments*. There is a one time fee associated with the plan that covers you preventive care based on the category you choose. There are no deductibles, no monthly payments, No maximum on how much you can save, and no missing tooth cause or waiting periods.
