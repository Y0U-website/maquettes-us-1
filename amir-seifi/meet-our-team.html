Meet Dr. Amir Seifi – A Passionate Expert in Dentistry

With over 20 years of experience , Dr. Seifi is a highly accomplished dentist dedicated to excellence in patient care. His extensive background spans private practice, research, teaching, and academia, ensuring that his patients receive top-tier, evidence-based dental treatment.

Dr. Seifi holds both a Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD) and a Doctorate (PhD) in Dentistry and Dental Sciences from the prestigious University of Washington in Seattle—home to one of the highest-ranking primary care programs in the nation and the world.

As a former Assistant Professor at Marquette University’s School of Dentistry—one of the most respected institutions in the region—Dr. Seifi played a vital role in educating the next generation of dental professionals. After his tenure in academia, he shifted his full focus to patient care, bringing his wealth of knowledge and expertise directly to his practice.

Beyond his clinical practice, Dr. Seifi has contributed significantly to the field of dentistry through multiple publications in prestigious peer-reviewed journals. He remains at the forefront of dental advancements by consistently pursuing continuing education (CE) courses and integrating the latest innovations into his practice. His expertise includes the most advanced digital dentistry technology, state-of-the-art cosmetic dentistry, and complex implant-based restorations.

Meet Dr. Wei Ting Ho – Dedicated to Excellence in Periodontics

With almost 20 years of experience, Dr. Wei Ting Ho is deeply passionate about his craft and dedicated to helping patients achieve optimal oral health. Specializing in periodontics, he focuses on preserving natural teeth, enhancing function, and improving aesthetics. Through advanced tissue regeneration techniques and dental implants, Dr. Ho helps patients rebuild their smiles with confidence.

Known for his gentle and friendly approach, Dr. Ho is highly regarded by his patients for his meticulous attention to detail and thorough treatment planning. He finds the most rewarding aspect of his profession to be the appreciation and gratitude of his patients, as well as the satisfaction of knowing he has made a meaningful impact on their oral health and well-being.

In addition to his expertise in periodontics, Dr. Ho is fluent in Mandarin, Chinese, and Taiwanese , allowing him to serve a diverse patient community.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ho enjoys an active lifestyle, including volleyball, running, and swimming . He is also an avid collector of Chinese antiques, often spending his free time antiquing in search of rare treasures. He currently resides in Lakeview, Chicago , and is the only member of his family living in the United States. Each year, he travels back to Taiwan to visit his mother and siblings.

Dr. Van Nguyen

With nearly a decade of experience in the dental field, including years as a dental assistant before becoming a dentist, Dr. Nguyen has developed a deep understanding of patient care and comfort.
