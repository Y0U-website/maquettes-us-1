Dental Implants

Dentist Grayslake, IL

Dental Implants can be an excellent option for replacing missing teeth. In past years, new types of implants have been introduced to dentistry.

In general, a dental implant consists of two main parts, an abutment and a crown section. An abutment is a titanium based section that is placed inside the alveolar bone. Titanium will integrate with the bone and can transfer the biting load to the bone similar to a root. When the abutment is placed and successfully integrated with the bone, a crown will be placed on top of the abutment.
