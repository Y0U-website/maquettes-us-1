Congenitally Missing Lateral Incisors Smile Transformation

This case was a beautiful example of combining cosmetic dentistry, restorative dentistry, and implant dentistry to create a natural, balanced smile.

The patient was born without the upper lateral incisors. Over time, the canine teeth erupted into the spaces where the lateral incisors normally belong, creating an uneven smile shape and tooth proportion. In addition, a retained primary canine was still present in the upper left canine position.

The goal was not simply to make the teeth “whiter,” but to redesign the smile in a way that looked natural, symmetrical, and appropriate for the patient’s facial features.

A customized treatment plan was completed using cosmetic veneers and crowns to reshape the teeth, improve proportions, and create the appearance of a complete, harmonious smile. The retained primary tooth was removed and replaced with a dental implant and a custom implant crown, allowing us to restore the missing canine position with a stable, long-term solution.

The final result is a brighter, more balanced smile with improved tooth shape, symmetry, and overall harmony — while still maintaining a natural appearance.

A Life-Changing Transformation with Implant Restoration

This patient came to us with severely compromised teeth, including multiple non-restorable teeth, significant misalignment, and extensive damage. Everyday activities like eating, speaking, and smiling had become a challenge, affecting both oral function and confidence.

To restore their smile, we developed a comprehensive treatment plan that included: Removing non-restorable teeth to eliminate infection and discomfort. Placing two dental implants for a strong and stable foundation. Designing and securing an implant-supported bridge to restore both function and aesthetics.

The final result is a complete transformation—providing a natural-looking, durable, and confident smile . With improved oral health and renewed self-assurance, this patient can now enjoy life without hesitation!
