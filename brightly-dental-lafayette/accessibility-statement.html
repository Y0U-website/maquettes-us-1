Accessibility Statement

Brightly Dental is improving website accessibility for patients, applicants, team members, and visitors using assistive technologies.

Our accessibility commitment

Brightly Dental wants patients, families, applicants, and team members to be able to use this website with a wide range of devices, assistive technologies, and browsing preferences.

The site uses semantic page structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, image alt text, and clear form and call-to-action labels.

Ongoing improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Brightly Dental continues reviewing pages for WCAG alignment, mobile usability, heading structure, color contrast, focus states, and screen-reader clarity.

Some scheduling, forms, maps, and embedded tools may be operated by third-party services. Brightly Dental can help route patients by phone or text if a third-party tool is difficult to use.

Need help using the site?

If you have trouble accessing information or completing a task, contact the Parker or Lafayette office by phone or text. The team can help with appointment requests, office information, patient questions, and next steps.

Contact the Parker West or Lafayette office by phone or text so the team can help with appointment requests, office information, or next steps.

Brightly can control its own website content most directly. Third-party booking, forms, maps, and embedded services may have separate accessibility behavior.
