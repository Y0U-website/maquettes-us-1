The practice

Each of our locations is configured with brand new, state-of-the-art technology. From cozy waiting rooms to the latest in digital x-ray imaging, our facilities ensure you receive top quality care that puts your comfort and needs first.

At Akridge Orthodontics, we’ve been providing families with extraordinary orthodontic care at an affordable price for over 40 years. While choosing to get braces can seem intimidating or like a major investment, they are truly transformative! Nothing can compare to the confidence you feel when the braces come off and you’re left with a beautiful, straight smile.

While most believe braces are solely for achieving a certain aesthetic, having straight teeth is also incredibly beneficial for improving your overall oral health. Braces can provide bite correction and make your teeth easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and other dental issues.

We honor all types of insurance and even take care of all insurance filing for you to make the entire process as smooth and accessible as possible. We’re proud to provide our community with life-changing orthodontic services that give you the results you’re looking for, without the high costs.
