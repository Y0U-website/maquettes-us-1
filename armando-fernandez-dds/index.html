The practice

Dr. Fernandez is a graduate of Columbia University School of Dental and Oral Surgery in New York City. At Columbia University, Dr. Fernandez achieved honors in Prosthetics (crowns and bridges), Periodontics (gum and the supporting bone of teeth) and Orthodontics (braces).

Dr. Fernandez continued his studies during his residency at Columbia Presbyterian Medical Center, where he gained valuable experience in Oral Surgery, care of medically compromised patients, advanced esthetic procedures and participated in the "pioneering" days of Dental Implants

Professional Licenses New York and New Jersey North East Regional Boards

Hospital Appointments Assistant Attending Dentist - Columbia Presbyterian Medical Center Attending Dentist - New York State Psychiatric Institute. Substitute Member, Pharmacy Committee, Presbyterian Hospital.

Co-Director, New York State Psychiatric Institute Dental Service.

Teaching/Research Appointments Instructor, Columbia University School of Dental & Oral Surgery Division of Maxillofacial Surgery

Society Memberships Academy of General Dentistry Academy of Osseous Integration
