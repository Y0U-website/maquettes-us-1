Dr. Davis and his team of dental professionals welcome you to their practice.

For over three decades, Dr. Davis has worked to create a world-class, high-tech dental office designed to meet the dental needs of his busy Morehead City patients. The result is a dental practice that stands above the rest. Dr. Davis has built his practice so that families can comfortably receive the care they need from experienced, compassionate dental professionals in a welcoming environment.

Dr. Brittany was born and raised in Beaufort, North Carolina, and has been part of our practice family since high school, when she began shadowing Dr. Davis and discovered her passion for dentistry. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Public Health from East Carolina University before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University School of Dentistry.

She is committed to providing compassionate, patient-focused care and strives to make every visit comfortable, personalized, and stress-free. She enjoys all aspects of general dentistry, including preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic treatments, crowns and bridges, fillings, and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through comprehensive dental care.

While attending dental school, she met her husband, Dr. James Goots, and together they made Morehead City their home to serve the community they love. They still frequent the country roads of West Virginia, where her husband is from, and they attend the Mountaineer football games at WVU. In April 2026, they welcomed their daughter, Gia.

Outside the office, she loves spending quality time with James and Gia, as well as with their friends and family. Whether they’re boating, enjoying the pool, soaking up the sunshine, or simply making memories together, family time is the highlight of her weekends. She also has a passion for traveling and adding stamps to her passport. Whether she is caring for patients or exploring a new corner…
