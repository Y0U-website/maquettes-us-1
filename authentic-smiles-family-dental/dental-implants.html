What to Expect: Dental Implant Process at Authentic Smiles

Step 1: Personalized Consultation

Dr. Omar will perform a thorough exam and cleaning to assess your bone structure and oral health. We’ll discuss your goals, medical history, and financial options.

Step 2: Implant Placement

We partner with trusted local specialists for implant surgery. Implants are gently placed into your jawbone where they integrate over a few months.

Step 3: Abutment & Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, we place a custom abutment and ceramic dental crown to restore the look and function of your natural tooth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime.
