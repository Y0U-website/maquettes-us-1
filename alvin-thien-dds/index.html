Columbia Basin Health Association

Columbia Basin Health Association (CBHA) provides healthcare services throughout Adams, Benton, and Franklin counties, as well as communities in Grant County.

The practice

CBHA clinics provide trusted, high-quality care for individuals and families across Central and Eastern Washington. Services vary by location and may include primary care, dental care, behavioral health, eye care, pharmacy services, and more.

Explore our clinic locations to find convenient, compassionate care close to home.

For after hours support please call your local clinic to reach an on-call provider.

For more information about the specific health services offered at your nearest Columbia Basin Health Association clinic, contact us today.

Our providers work closely together to take care of you as a whole to ensure that you receive the best possible care that will lead you to a healthier you.

Columbia Basin Health Association is a comprehensive outpatient healthcare provider. The communities we serve are remote, rural, and have not always had easy access to medical professionals. That is why Columbia Basin Health Association has made it our mission to make receiving quality health care easier for patients. We offer a broad range of services at all three CBHA clinics.

Call today for more information about the services available at your local Columbia Basin Health Association clinic. Our friendly, compassionate staff is ready to answer your questions.
