We Make Healthcare More Accessible

At Columbia Basin Health Association, we know not everyone feels they have access to affordable, high quality healthcare. We are here to help. We provide comprehensive, cost-effective health services to rural, underserved communities in Central Washington. Our Patient Benefits Department is available on-site at all three of our clinics to help patients get access to our healthcare services. We assist you with enrollment in state sponsored medical programs and advise on eligibility for cost-effective insurance programs.
