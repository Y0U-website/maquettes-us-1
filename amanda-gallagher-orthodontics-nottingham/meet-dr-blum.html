Meet Dr. Blum

Her professional affiliations include the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), American Dental Association (ADA), Maryland State Society of Orthodontists (MSSO), and Middle Atlantic Society of Orthodontists (MASO).

A proud Baltimore native, Dr. Blum is thrilled to be back home near her family, friends, and beloved sports teams: the Ravens and the Orioles. In her free time, Dr. Blum enjoys playing golf, taking pilates and barre classes, traveling, skiing, cooking, staying active outdoors, and spending quality time with family and friends.
