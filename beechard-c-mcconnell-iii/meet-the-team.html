Meet The Team

DR. BEECH

Dr. McConnell is committed to excellence in orthodontics and utilizes the most up-to-date technologies to treat children and adults. He is involved in many dental organizations and study clubs that ensure he is current with the best techniques available in orthodontics.

Dr. McConnell is an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Southern Association of Orthodontists, the South Carolina Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the South Carolina Dental Association.

Giving back to his community is extremely important to Dr. McConnell. He is a member of the Anderson Area Chamber of Commerce, served on the Board of the Anderson Area YMCA, and the Board of Visitors of Anderson University. In addition, he willingly donates to several local charitable organizations and law enforcement agencies. Dr. McConnell resides in Anderson and when not working, he enjoys pursuing his outdoor passions, traveling, and spending quality time with his family.
