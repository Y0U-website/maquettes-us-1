Patient Info

First Visit

We are always excited about meeting new patients during their first visit to our office. Your complimentary consultation will consist of a thorough examination and a discussion of potential treatment options. This important visit includes an in-depth review of digital photos /X-raws that wit rive us valuable insight into your orthodontic needs. Your appointment will last about an hour. We insist that our patients leave the office with a clear understanding of their specific needs, what the treatment will consist of, and how long it will take.

New Patient Consultation

During your first visit to our office, we will:

Perform an in-depth orthodontic evaluation, including digital X-rays, photographs of your teeth and face, and possible scan with iTero if interested in Invisalign®.

Please assist us by providing the following information at the time of your first visit:

  • Discuss whether orthodontic treatment is recommended by Dr. McConnell and, if so, determine the most appropriate time to begin treatment.
  • Create a customized orthodontic treatment plan that’s tailored to meet your specific needs and desires. Explain your orthodontic insurance benefits and design a payment plan that fits your budget.
  • We encourage you to ask questions as well Can orthodontics address my concerns?
  • Is treatment needed now or should treatment be delayed until appropriate growth, tooth eruption or other factors have occurred?
  • What treatment procedures will be used to correct the problem?
  • Do any permanent teeth need to be removed?
  • How long will treatment take?
  • How much will it cost?
  • What are my payment options?
  • Any panoramic X-ray taken within the past six months.
  • If you have orthodontic insurance, bring your insurance card. By providing this information at the first visit, we will be able to give you an estimate of your costs.

What Happens Next? (Appointments)

  • Once your diagnostic records and treatment plan are complete, we will schedule your first appointment to put your braces on or deliver your first set of Invisalign® aligners. Your first appointment generally lasts one hour and is one of our longer appointment times.
  • After you begin treatment, your orthodontic appointments will typically be scheduled every 6 to 10 weeks, and they generally last 20 minutes. Our team makes it a priority to see every patient on time, and we will work with you to make sure your appointments are as convenient as possible.

Financial and Insurance

If orthodontic treatment is recommended by Dr. McConnell, our treatment coordinator will discuss the cost of your treatment and each of our affordable payment options with you, so you are able to make the best choice that fits your budget. Our office is committed to helping you maximize your insurance benefits. Because insurance policies vary, we can only estimate your coverage in good faith but cannot guarantee coverage due to the complexities of insurance contracts. If you have any questions, our courteous team is always available to answer them.

For your convenience, we are proud to offer the following:

  • Payment via cash, check, automatic payments, online payments, VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and Care Credit
  • Discounts for payments made in full at the beginning of treatment, for families with multiple members in orthodontic treatment, no insurance, AnMed employees, Military, First Responder, Professional discount for employees of other dental offices
  • Interest-free payment plans
  • If you have orthodontic insurance benefits, or a health savings or flexible spending account, we will help you determine the amount of coverage you have available. Once you begin treatment, we will file your insurance claims for you.
