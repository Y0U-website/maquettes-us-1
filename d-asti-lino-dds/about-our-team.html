About Our Team

Diane

Office Coordinator

Diane has been working in the dental field for over 27 years, mainly in a corporate environment. She was eager for a change and wanted to be part of a more family oriented dental office. She joined Dr. D’Asti’s practice in 2015 and has greatly enjoyed building a personal rapport with the patients. Her devotion to her family, two children, and two grandchildren occupies most of her free time as well as year round trips to the shore.

Stephanie

Hygienist

Linda

Hygienist

Linda graduated from Camden County College in 2010 with an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene. With her extensive experience in treating patients from pediatrics to geriatrics, Linda became an asset to the office when she joined Dr. D’Asti’s practice in 2016. In maintaining continuing education study, she takes great pride in educating her patients so they may reach their optimum oral health. Her biggest passion is spending time with family and friends, and being outdoors with her two boxers, Bella and Bane.

Sarah

Hygienist

Dr. D’Asti is ecstatic to welcome Sarah as the newest member to his team. She is from Shanghai, China and moved here almost twenty years ago when her husband pursued a postdoctoral degree in stem cell research at the University of Pennsylvania. Sarah has a degree in marketing and in 2019 obtained an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene from Camden County College. She and her husband have two musically talented children. Her daughter plays piano, violin, and flute. Her son plays piano and saxophone. Sarah herself plays piano and is an avid music and art enthusiast.

Nancy

Assistant

Nancy has been with the practice since 2004. Despite her 54 years of dental assisting, she is fully committed and devoted to her work as she has always been. She enjoys indoor and outdoor gardening, is an avid reader, and the proud owner of an innumerable collection of books. Nancy’s biggest passion is her love for animals and her three cats.
