Advanced Tech, Patient-Focused Amenities

At our office, we’ve made investments in the latest orthodontic technology to simplify your experience. And we don’t forget the little things, either! From free drinks while you wait for your visit to a sweet treat when you depart, we make your exams, checkups, and adjustments as comfortable as possible.

Intraoral Scanner

Our intraoral scanner allows us to develop a 3D image of your teeth, gums, and mouth. It also eliminates the need for goopy, putty-based dental impressions, which many patients find to be uncomfortable.

3D Printer

We have a SprintRay 3D Printer, which is one of the most advanced dental 3D printers on the market. This allows us to build our own clear aligners in-house, as well as many other orthodontic appliances. This simplifies treatment, speeds up the process, and keeps your orthodontic care affordable.

Fresh-Baked Cookies

Our patients rave about our cookies, which are freshly baked every single day. We’ll always leave you with something sweet when you’re done with your consultation or routine checkup!

Coffee & Water Station

Our coffee station has everything you need to make your morning joe the way you like it. We also offer complimentary bottled water to all of our patients. It’s important to stay hydrated!

iPad Games

If your kiddo needs a distraction, our iPad station is loaded up with all of the games they need to stay occupied. Sit back, relax, and wait for their appointment while they stay busy.

Tactile Wall

We also offer a lighted pegboard wall that’s great for kids who don’t need any more screen time. Kids of all ages can enjoy making different patterns and expressing their creativity in our play area.
