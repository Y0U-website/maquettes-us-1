Our enhanced screening and safety protocols include temperature and questionnaire screening, medical grade HEPA air purification, acrylic barriers, N-95 masks, safety goggles, face shields and very strict sterilization allows our dental office to be very safe.

We value your safety especially during this time of the COVID-19 pandemic. We have invested in an air purification system to prevent the spread of viruses in our dental office. This technology clears out air particles and pollutants that might bring disease-causing viruses and germs. At Artistry Dental you can trust that you breathe in clean and safe air during your appointment.
