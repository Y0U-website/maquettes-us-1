Types of Braces for Everyone

Which types of braces are best for you?

Metal / Traditional Braces

When you hear the word, "braces," metal braces are usually what come to mind! They are made from metal and wire, and utilize rubber bands to move teeth. They are a less expensive option than some other treatments, plus you can express yourself with colored bands!

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces are a more aesthetic option to metal braces. Ceramic braces are made just like traditional braces, except the brackets. The brackets are the same color as your teeth, so they blend in and are less noticeable! Tooth-colored wires are also available.

Hidden / Lingual Braces

Planning a wedding or upcoming graduation? Are you seeking employment and need to look your best? You can still look amazing and straighten your teeth at the same time - the secret is hidden braces. Hidden braces, or lingual braces, are secured behind your teeth - so no one will ever know!

Clear Braces / Aligners

Ever wonder what "invisible braces" means? Invisible braces are a series of clear aligners that replace the need for metal wires, brackets, and rubber bands. Aligners are customized for your smile. Every few weeks, you’ll get a new set of aligners that will shift your teeth into place.

Faster Treatment Options

In addition to multiple types of braces, we also offer faster treatment options. If you’d like to speed up the length of time your treatment takes, ask us how! Most orthodontic treatments can be accelerated with an easy-to-use hand-held device called AcceleDent® Aura.

Ready for Braces?

Getting braces is a great way to enhance your smile and look your best! However, it is a commitment, and requires care on your part. Do you have questions about getting braces?
