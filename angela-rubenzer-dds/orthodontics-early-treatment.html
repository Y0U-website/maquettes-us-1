Early Orthodontic Treatment Growth and Development

Timing is everything, even when it comes to your child’s orthodontic treatment. Some orthodontic problems may be easier to correct if they are found and treated early. Early treatment, also called interceptive treatment, means treatment that is performed while some baby teeth are still present.

Orthodontic problems don’t improve as children age, they simply become harder to treat. It’s easier to treat many orthodontic problems during adolescence because the body is still growing rapidly at this time.

Orthodontics is in the scope of pediatric dentistry because it includes growth and development of the dental arches. An early growth and development evaluation may provide your child with a more positive experience should they need early orthodontic treatment from our doctors.
