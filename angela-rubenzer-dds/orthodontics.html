Meet Our Orthodontic Providers

At Dental Kidds, we understand how important it is for children to feel comfortable and confident when they visit the dentist. That’s why we strive to create a positive experience at every appointment. In addition to pediatric dental care, we now offer orthodontic services, making it easier to monitor your child’s smile and provide early treatment when needed—all in one familiar, friendly office.

When it comes to orthodontics, timing matters. Some orthodontic concerns are easier to correct when they are identified early. This type of care, known as interceptive orthodontic treatment , is provided while some baby teeth are still present. Early evaluation allows us to guide jaw growth, create space for permanent teeth, and address developing bite issues before they become more complex.

Orthodontic problems do not improve on their own as children grow. In many cases, they become more difficult to treat over time. Evaluating your child’s growth and dental development at the right age helps us determine whether treatment should begin now or if it’s best to simply monitor their progress until the time is right. An early orthodontic evaluation allows our doctors to identify potential concerns, recommend the most appropriate treatment plan, and help your child enjoy a healthy, confident smile for years to come.
