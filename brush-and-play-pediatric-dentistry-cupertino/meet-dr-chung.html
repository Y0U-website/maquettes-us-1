Meet Dr. Esther Chung Brush and Play

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Chung enjoys figure skating, having competed in regional leagues in South Korea, and now skates recreationally as an adult. She is also a talented pianist and loves exploring new coffee shops and restaurants with her husband. Having grown up in Australia, Singapore, Korea and China, she developed an appreciation for diverse cultures and languages. Fluent in English, Korean, and Mandarin Chinese , Dr. Chung easily connects with families from various backgrounds and is proud to call the Bay Area her home
