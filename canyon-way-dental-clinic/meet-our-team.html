Canyon Way Dental

Over the past decade, Dr Brett Hulet and his wife Jennifer have called the Oregon Coast their home. Prior to arriving here, he served in three different branches of the military, attended dental school at the University of Michigan and practiced in Texas and Arizona. He greatly enjoys serving as a mentor for both dental implants and CAD/CAM. Brett and Jennifer have four children and little free time. Sporting events and practices usually dominate their schedule. When Dr. Hulet is not coaching or attending games he can usually be found at the gym, on the water, or the golf course.

Jen has been a dental hygienist for over 20 years. She is married with four sons. Jen enjoys spending time in the outdoors; camping, hiking, paddle boarding, walking her chocolate lab pup on the beach, and watching her son’s sporting events.

Stevie has been a dental hygienist since 2011. She loves spending time with her daughter, family and friends. Hobbies include gardening, camping, wakesurfing and snowboarding.

Chelsea moved to Oregon in the spring of 2015 from Colorado. She is married to her high school sweetheart and has three amazing children. In her spare time, she enjoys walking on the beach, camping, and spending time with her family.

Nicole is bilingual and loves to help people. She finds it very rewarding when issues get resolved, and patients leave happy. In her free time, Nicole can be found with her boyfriend and dog Vinnie.
