Dr. Sam is a Michigan native, born and raised in Rochester Hills. He graduated from the University of Detroit Jesuit High School, where the school’s values of service, compassion, and leadership shaped much of who he is today.

Before pursuing dentistry, Dr. Sam served as a Detroit firefighter while taking science classes at Wayne State University. It was during this time—working, studying, and serving his community—that he discovered his true calling and joined the University of Michigan School of Dentistry.

While working at a Detroit community health clinic, he noticed how quickly he connected with kids and how much he loved teaching them about their health. That spark grew into a passion for pediatric dentistry.
