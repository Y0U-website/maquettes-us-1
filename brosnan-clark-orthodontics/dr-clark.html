LeeAnn Clark, DDS

During my orthodontic residency, I was trained with state of the art technology and the most current treatment techniques. In our practice, we uphold the same philosophy by staying current in the most up-to-date advancements. I regularly attend continuing education seminars and participate in study clubs to stay current in the latest techniques. I love collaborating with other dental specialists in creating the ideal treatment plan for our mutual patients.

I take great pride in offering my patients top notch treatment to help them achieve the smile they have always wanted.

I grew up in South Carolina and moved to New York City for dental school. My husband Ken and I live in Rutherford, and we love the feeling of community that abounds. We enjoy taking long walks with our goldendoodle Louis all around town. I spend my free time in creative endeavors such as singing. painting. or arranging flowers from my garden. I love exploring New York City, seeing Broadway shows and visiting museums.
