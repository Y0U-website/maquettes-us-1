  • Your identification card
  • Your insurance card
  • List of medications
  • Your completed patient information form
  • You will need to bring payment for any co-pays or balances owed

YOUR FIRST VISIT IS ALL ABOUT YOU: YOUR COMFORT, YOUR HAPPINESS, AND YOUR HEALTH

During your first visit, we will review your dental and medical history forms, and take X-rays of your teeth and mouth. A hygienist will gather information and will likely clean your teeth. One of our dentists will meet with you to discuss your X-rays, help you create your individual treatment plan, and answer any questions you may have.
