Emergency Services

During office hours, all clinics accept emergency patients on a walk-in-and-wait basis. Please contact the location nearest you to determine if the clinic is still scheduling appointments for emergency patients for that day.

Please note: Emergency visits are meant to examine a specific area of concern or pain and to determine necessary treatment. While we try to treat patients on the same day as their emergency exam, same-day treatment is not guaranteed and is subject to schedule availability.

We cannot guarantee same-day treatment for emergencies.

For patients of record with non-life-threatening weekend emergencies, please call (952) 955-8994

For life-threatening emergencies, please call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.
