Do You Need a Root Canal?

Signs that you may need a root canal typically include persistent tooth pain, especially when chewing or applying pressure to the affected tooth. Sensitivity to hot or cold that lingers even after the stimulus is removed can also be a red flag. Additionally, swelling or tenderness in the gums near the painful tooth, or the presence of a pimple-like bump on the gum, may indicate an infection or abscess. In some cases, the tooth may darken in color, signaling damage to the pulp inside.

Understanding these signs early can prevent more serious dental problems. At Abide Family Dental Eastern Shore, we encourage patients to monitor changes in their oral health and contact us if they notice any concerning symptoms.
