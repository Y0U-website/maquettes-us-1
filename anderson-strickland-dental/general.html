Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can be daunting and painful, requiring prompt treatment to alleviate discomfort and preserve tooth health. Fractures may occur from trauma, grinding, or biting hard objects, while fillings and crowns can become damaged or dislodged. Severe pain warrants an immediate dentist appointment, as it typically worsens without care and can impact overall health. Choose our compassionate team as your Denham Springs emergency dentist.

Reasons to seek emergency care include:

  • Severe toothache or dental pain
  • Broken or fractured teeth
  • Knocked-out (avulsed) teeth
  • Lost or damaged fillings or crowns
  • Gum bleeding or swelling
  • Severe mouth or jaw pain
  • Jaw dislocation or injury
  • Broken orthodontic appliances (braces, wires)
  • Loose teeth due to injury or periodontal disease
  • Post-operative complications from dental surgery

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Sleep apnea is a serious sleep disorder characterized by repeated pauses in breathing during sleep, leading to disrupted sleep patterns and inadequate oxygen supply to the body. These interruptions can occur multiple times per hour, causing individuals to partially awaken, resulting in loud snoring, choking, or gasping sensations.

Read More About Sleep Apnea:

There are two primary types: obstructive sleep apnea, where blocked airways often due to relaxed throat muscles or excess body weight prevent airflow, and central sleep apnea, which arises from a lack of signals from the brain to the muscles that control breathing.

If left untreated, sleep apnea can lead to severe health issues such as cardiovascular problems, high blood pressure, and even an increased risk of auto accidents due to daytime drowsiness. Diagnosis typically involves sleep studies, and treatment options range from lifestyle changes and CPAP machines to dental devices and surgery, depending on the severity of the condition.

Common signs of sleep apnea include:

  • Loud snoring
  • Frequent awakenings during the night
  • Gasping or choking sensations while sleeping
  • Morning headaches
  • Excessive daytime sleepiness
  • Insomnia
  • & More

How Can A Dentist Treat Sleep Apnea?

We recommend that patients undergo a panoramic x-ray every 3 to 5 years during routine cleanings. This allows our dentists to assess your airway. If it’s narrow, one of our hygienists or doctors may inquire about your sleep habits, snoring, and nighttime awakenings. A digital scan may be used to create a customized night guard that advances the jaw, opening your airway. This impression is sent to the lab to create the guard, which has adjustable bands for optimal fit and comfort.
