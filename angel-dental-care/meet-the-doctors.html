Meet The Doctors

Our Angel Dental team of Dentists are deeply committed to providing you and your family with quality, comprehensive dental care. Our experienced dentists work with patients of all ages, from all over the Cleveland area and work hard to ensure that every patient receives compassionate care and expert services while they are in our dental chairs.

DR. FRANK OMERZA

Dr. Omerza’s treatment philosophy centers on proper timing and aggressive treatment which ensures that you or your child is treated with the highest quality results in the fewest number of appointments. Most patients can complete active treatment in 18 months or less, while many simple malocclusions can be treated in 12 months or less.

DR. WALID THAHET

Dr. Thabet attended Jordan University of Science and Technology, where he received his bachelor’s degree in Dental Surgery. After making the decision to move to the United States, Dr. Thabet went on to complete a Preceptorship of Prosthodontics and Implantology at the University of Texas Health & Science Center of San Antonio, which led him to become the Chief Resident at the General Practice Residency Program at MetroHealth Hospital.

Dr. Thabet is quite the family man as well. He loves to play soccer and watch movies with his friends and family. When he needs his alone time, Dr. Thabet enjoys getting lost in a book or playing music.

DR. HANI ASHQAR

Dr. Ashqar is an empathetic dentist that provides his patients with the highest level of oral care and comfort. During his free time, he enjoys spending time with his family, playing soccer, and reading.

DR. Natasha Katsman Sheridan

Dr. Natasha Katsman Sheridan has been a practicing dentist since 2013, when she graduated from the Ohio State College of Dentistry. She is an active member or the ADA, ODA (elected to both the council on membership and the sub council on new dentists), and GCDS (elected to the House of Delegates and helps plan continuing education events), along with Alpha Omega Dental Fraternity. She is currently working towards a fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Katsman is an empathetic dentist and has a passion for treating patients with kindness and compassion. She has volunteered for the last several years with MedWorks, providing free dental care in Cleveland. She looks forward to treating you and your family at Angel Dental Care!

DR. HADDADEEN

Dr. Haddadeen serves as the full-time dentist at Angel Dental Care’s Akron location, where he provides patients with superior customer care by being gentle and caring. Dr. Haddadeen, like most dentists, has dedicated his life and career to becoming the best possible dentist he can be.

After graduating from the university of Jordan in 2002. Dr. Haddadeen began on his journey to the United States. When coming to the United States, he completed the general practice Residency programs at St. Mary’s Hospital and Metro Health. Dr. Haddadeen has always contributed as a role model for his colleagues and peers and proved it by earning a Peace Medal form the United Nations for Peace Keeping in Sierra Leone.

During his personal time, Dr. Haddadeen enjoys spending time with his family, reading and playing and watching soccer. We’re so lucky to have him on our team.
