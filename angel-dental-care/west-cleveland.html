ABOUT WEST CLEVELAND

Our West Cleveland office is located in the middle of the Variety Village Historic District on 117th street between Lorain and Governor Avenues. We’re just a block away from Holyland Imports and the Arab American Community Center. We’re surrounded by a diverse assortment of restaurants and bakery choices.

Less than a block away, you’ll find the Variety Theater which has been a part of the landscape since 1927 and is the recent recipient of a planned $15 million restoration.

If you prefer the great outdoors, a short drive away is the Cleveland Metro Zoo, where there are incredible animal exhibits along with the opportunity for overnight ’safari’ campouts at the African Safari Exhibit.

INSURANCE AND PAYMENT OPTIONS

At all of our offices, you’ll find doctors who care about their patients and the community. We strive for excellence. In addition to all the traditional dental services available such as cleanings, root canal treatments, and dental crowns, we also offer pediatric dentistry and cosmetic dental services.

Our location at 117th Street also offers specialized orthodontic services, under the care of Dr. Frank Omerza, where you can get either traditional braces or Invisalign. Dr. Omerza’s primary area of study was early craniofacial development. He put that training to use when he served in the U.S. Navy as the Orthodontic Director at the U.S. Naval Hospital in Naples, Italy and as the staff orthodontist for the department of oral and maxillofacial surgery at the National Naval Medical Center in Bethesda, Maryland.
