The practice

At Chillicothe Family Dental, we believe dentistry is about more than just smiles it’s about relationships.

From your child’s first check-up to advanced treatment for complex dental needs, our team is here to provide personalized care at every stage of life.

Dr. Kellie Schwalbach and our dedicated doctors are proud to serve the Chillicothe community with trusted, modern dentistry in a welcoming environment.

Care and Commitment Learn more From the front office to the exam room, our practice is made up of professionals who take your oral health very seriously. Using state-of-the art equipment designed with safety and efficiency in mind, we are able to offer top-notch care.

Dental Care Made Easy We know that your time is valuable to you, and that’s why we offer extended office hours on select days, dental appointment reminders, and a quick turnaround time on calls and appointment requests. We are happy to answer any questions or concerns in the office, over the phone or via email.

Honesty and Affordability We know that the cost of care can be confusing. In addition to offering a number of in-office payment options to fit your budget, we also give you up-front cost information on your treatment ahead of time.

Led by Dr. Kellie Schwalbach, our growing team of doctors and staff is committed to making every visit a positive experience.
