IV Moderate Sedation Dentistry in Chillicothe, Ohio

IV moderate sedation (intravenous sedation) provides a deeper level of relaxation and is the most advanced sedation service available at Chillicothe Family Dental.

IV sedation is especially helpful for patients who need:
