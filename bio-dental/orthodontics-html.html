Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners utilize custom-fitted, plastic aligners that are BPA-free and FDA-approved to gently and strategically shift your teeth into their ideal positions. This versatile treatment can address bite misalignment, crowding, and gaps between teeth in a convenient way that doesn’t require you to completely alter your daily life. Since the trays are removable, you’ll be able to eat all of the same foods you normally do and maintain good oral hygiene over the course of the process.
