Brian S. Myers DDS

General Dentist

Dr. Brian Myers earned his bachelor’s degree from Michigan State University in 2002 and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in 2006. Since 2009, he has practiced dentistry in Grand Blanc.

Dr. Myers believes excellent dentistry begins with listening to his patients, understanding their goals, and providing thoughtful care in a comfortable environment. He has a strong interest in modern dental technology and incorporates digital imaging, 3D scanning, and other advanced techniques to help make treatment more efficient, predictable, and comfortable.

His interest in dental technology began long before dental school while working alongside his father, Dr. Terry Myers, a pioneer in dental laser technology and founder of the Institute for Advanced Dental Technologies. That early experience helped shape Dr. Myers’ continued commitment to combining technology with personalized, patient-centered care.

When he’s not practicing dentistry, Dr. Myers enjoys spending time with his wife and children and being involved in their activities.
