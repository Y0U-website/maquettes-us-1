What Dental Implants Do For You

  • Natural Appearance: Dental implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth, providing a seamless and aesthetically pleasing solution.

Preparing for Dental Implant Surgery

Preparing for dental implant surgery involves a thorough evaluation, which may include a comprehensive dental exam, a review of your medical history, and a treatment plan. You will also discuss anesthesia options and post-surgery care instructions with your dental care team.

What to Expect During the Procedure

The dental implant procedure involves several stages, including damaged tooth removal (if needed), jawbone preparation (if needed), implant placement, bone healing, abutment placement, and finally, the placement of the artificial tooth. Implant placement itself generally takes less than 10 minutes, and each restoration session typically takes less than an hour. The entire process can take several months, as it involves time for healing and new bone growth.
