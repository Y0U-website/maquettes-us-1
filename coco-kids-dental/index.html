The practice

Every child deserves exceptional dental care that makes them feel safe, comfortable, and even excited about their dental visits. At COCO Kids Dental, we specialize in creating positive experiences that build confidence and trust from the very first appointment.

Our experienced team understands that young patients have unique needs, which is why we’ve designed our entire practice around providing gentle, age-appropriate care using advanced technology and kid-friendly techniques. We’re here to support their journey to optimal oral health with compassion, expertise, and a touch of fun.

At COCO Kids Dental, we’re more than just a dental office. —We’re a fun, safe place where kids feel at home and parents feel at ease. Here’s why families trust us with their children’s smiles:

Every visit feels like an adventure. Our office features colorful murals, playful decor, and engaging toys designed to help children feel relaxed and happy. Our goal is to make dental appointments feel like a trip to a playground, so kids leave with smiles and look forward to coming back.

We understand that families are busy. That’s why we offer same-day appointments and treatments whenever possible, making it easier to fit high-quality dental care into your schedule without added stress.

We focus on building trust and lasting relationships. Our team cares for every child as if they were their own, creating a warm and welcoming environment for both kids and parents.

From natural-looking, tooth-colored fillings to thorough preventive care, we support growing smiles every step of the way. Dr. Sun’s expertise in cosmetic techniques allows us to deliver natural, beautiful results for kids that boost their confidence.

With Dr. Sun’s gentle and child-friendly approach, even nervous patients feel calm and confident. Our advanced anesthesia technique and compassionate care help children build positive associations with dental visits.
