Preparing for Your Child’s First Visit

To make sure everything goes smoothly, here are a few simple tips to prepare for your child’s first dental visit:

  • Keep it Positive: Talk about the visit in a fun, positive way so your child feels excited. Let them know that they’ll get to sit in the special dental chair and be a “superstar” during the exam.
  • Bring Your Insurance Info: Bring your insurance information or any other necessary documents.
  • Ask Questions: If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask our doctor and staff during the visit. We’re here to help!
