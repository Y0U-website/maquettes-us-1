The Full-Arch Implant Process

Previous Previous Next Next Personalized Consultation 1 Your journey begins with a visit to our office, where our doctors will take 3D CBCT scans, digital photos, and intraoral scans to evaluate your bone levels and gum tissue. These tools help us create a smile-driven plan tailored to your anatomy, lifestyle, and goals. We&#x27;ll review your treatment options and go over pre-op instructions, medications, and financial details so you feel fully prepared and confident moving forward.

Implant Surgery & Temporary Smile

On surgery day, we take meticulous care to remove any remaining teeth, place 4–6 dental implants per arch (based on your unique needs), and add bone grafting if needed. After placing your implants, we’ll capture a final set of digital scans to design your temporary smile. You’ll head home that evening to rest while we custom-fabricate your interim fixed prosthesis in-house. It will be ready and delivered the very next day.

Snap-In Overdenture Option : Implants are placed and left beneath the gums to heal for 3–6 months. You’ll wear a traditional removable denture during this time.

Fixed Full-Arch Option : You’ll receive your temporary, fixed smile the day after surgery so you never have to go without teeth.

Healing & Follow-Ups

As your implants integrate with your jawbone over 3–6 months, we’ll see you for a few check-ins to monitor your healing and ensure everything is on track. You’ll continue wearing your temporary prosthesis throughout this process.

Final Smile Reveal

Once healing is complete, we’ll take new scans, refine your design, and craft your final smile—a stunning, permanent restoration made from high-quality zirconia for unmatched durability and aesthetics. Whether you chose a snap-in or fixed option, your final prosthesis will be built for strength, comfort, and confidence.

The benefits of single-tooth implants

Long-lasting results

Dental implants will last for the rest of your life with proper care. This means full-arch implants last a lot longer than traditional dentures.

Improved stability

With full-arch implants, your teeth will be anchored to your jaw bone. This improves their overall stability, and ensures that you have less movement when you eat or speak.

Natural look & feel

Full-arch implants use tooth-like materials to create a more natural-looking smile, and since your implants are attached to your jaw, things like chewing and biting feel a lot more natural compared to traditional dentures.

Protects your oral health

After tooth loss, your jawbone naturally begins to shrink because it’s no longer stimulated by the pressure of biting and chewing. This bone loss can happen even if you wear traditional dentures. Full-arch dental implants, however, work differently. Because the implants act like tooth roots, they stimulate your jawbone every time you eat—helping to preserve bone density, maintain facial structure, and prevent that sunken-in look that often comes with dentures. By preventing bone atrophy, full-arch implants help you maintain a healthier, more youthful appearance.

What’s the difference between zirconia and 3D-printed bridges?

We offer two full-arch options: a 3D-printed resin bridge and a premium zirconia bridge. Our 3D-printed option is ideal for patients looking for a durable, cost-conscious solution with annual refreshes. Zirconia, on the other hand, is our most long-lasting and natural-looking option—resistant to chips, stains, and odor, and crafted to last for decades.

Why choose implants over traditional dentures?

While dentures may seem more affordable upfront, they often lead to long-term challenges like slipping, discomfort, and bone loss. Our implant solutions are more stable, support your jaw health, and restore over 98 % of natural bite function—making them a better investment in the long run. We also offer transparent, all-inclusive pricing to help you plan with confidence.
