Can a zirconia bridge really last a lifetime?

Yes, when properly cared for, zirconia bridges can last for decades. Their strength and resistance to wear make them one of the most durable options available for full-arch tooth replacement.

Is a zirconia bridge right for me if I’ve had dentures in the past?

Absolutely. Many of our patients come to us frustrated with loose or uncomfortable dentures. A zirconia fixed bridge offers a stable, permanent solution that looks beautiful and restores full function. No slipping, no adhesives, no compromises.
