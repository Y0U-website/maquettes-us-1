Healing and Follow-Up Care

Healing time varies depending on the type of treatment you choose:

Snap-In Denture Solutions: Your implants will be placed and left to heal beneath the gums for 3–6 months. We&#x27;ll provide you with a comfortable traditional, removable denture during this time, so you’re never without teeth. Once healing is complete, we’ll take new scans and create a custom overdenture that securely snaps into place.

Fixed Full-Arch Solutions: You’ll go home to rest the evening of surgery, and return the next day for your 3D-printed interim smile the very next day. After 3–6 months of healing, we’ll take new scans and refine the design to craft your final, permanent bridge.

We want to make the process easy for you so we&#x27;re always guided by your comfort, timeline, and long-term success. If you have any questions or concerns, we&#x27;re just a phone call away.

What Services Are Included in Smile Makeovers?

Single-Tooth Implants

This implant option is often used to treat patients missing a small number of teeth. Single-tooth implants consist of three parts: the implant that acts as an artificial tooth root, the connector piece called the abutment, and the crown that acts as an artificial tooth.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Implant-supported bridges are dental prosthetics anchored securely to dental implants surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as sturdy foundations. They offer a natural-looking and durable solution to replace multiple missing teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics to your smile.

Full-Arch Implants

Full-arch implants use a series of 4-6 implants that are placed in the upper, lower, or both arches of your teeth. These implants are used to support a removable implant-supported denture or a non-removable fixed bridge.

Fixed Full Arch

Fixed Full Arch Implants offer a permanent solution for patients missing a full set of teeth by anchoring a custom-designed prosthetic bridge onto dental implants, restoring both function and aesthetics in a durable, natural-looking manner. This innovative dental procedure allows patients to regain confidence in their smile and enjoy improved oral health and chewing ability.

Implant Snap in Dentures

Implant snap-in dentures are a type of denture that securely attaches to 2-4 dental implants for lower arch and 4 for your upper arch. This provides greater stability and comfort compared to traditional removable dentures. They allow for improved chewing ability and speech, offering a more natural and confident smile.
