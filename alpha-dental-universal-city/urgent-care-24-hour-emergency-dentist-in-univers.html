What Should You Do for Knocked-Out or Chipped Teeth ?

If a tooth is knocked out or chipped, acting fast improves outcomes. Follow these steps:

Following these steps can improve the chance of saving the tooth and restoring function.

  • For knocked-out teeth : Handle gently: Hold the tooth by the crown (the chewing surface), not the root. Rinse carefully: Rinse with water to remove dirt — don’t scrub or dry the tooth. Try to reinsert: If you can, place the tooth back in the socket. If not possible, keep it moist in milk or saline. Seek immediate care: Contact Alpha Dental or go to an emergency dentist right away.
  • For chipped teeth : Rinse your mouth: Clean the area gently with warm water. Use a cold compress: Apply to the outside of your cheek to reduce swelling and ease pain. See a dentist: Schedule an appointment to evaluate the damage and discuss fixes like bonding or a crown.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I experience a dental emergency outside of office hours?

If a dental emergency happens after hours, try to contact a 24-hour emergency dentist like Alpha Dental via online booking or the emergency contact provided. If you can’t reach a dentist, manage symptoms by applying a cold compress for swelling, taking over-the-counter pain relievers as directed, and avoiding anything that increases pain until you can get professional care.

Are there specific signs that indicate I need to seek emergency dental care ?

Yes. Seek emergency care for severe or worsening tooth pain, noticeable swelling of the face or gums, bleeding that won’t stop after 15 minutes of pressure, a knocked-out tooth , or signs of an abscess (severe localized pain, swelling, or fever). These symptoms warrant prompt attention to avoid complications.

How can I prepare for my emergency dental visit?

Bring any relevant medical history, a list of current medications, and note your symptoms and pain levels. Arriving a few minutes early helps with any paperwork. If you’re anxious, bring a trusted friend or family member for support.

What types of sedation options are available for anxious patients during emergency procedures?

Alpha Dental offers options to help you feel comfortable, including nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for mild relaxation, oral sedatives for deeper calming, and IV sedation for patients with significant anxiety or for more complex procedures. Discuss your concerns with the dentist to pick the right approach.

How can I manage pain after an emergency dental procedure?

Follow your dentist’s instructions closely. Over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen or acetaminophen can help, and a cold compress reduces swelling. Avoid hard, crunchy, or very hot foods for a few days and maintain gentle oral hygiene. If pain increases or lasts longer than expected, contact your dentist.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency while traveling?

If you have a dental emergency away from home, seek care from a nearby dentist or an emergency room. Many offices will accept walk-ins for urgent cases. Check your insurance for out-of-network coverage and, until you can be seen, manage pain with over-the-counter meds and protective measures like a cold compress.
