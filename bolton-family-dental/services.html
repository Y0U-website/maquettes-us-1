What We Offer

COSMETIC | RESTORATIVE | PEDIATRIC | GENERAL

Bolton Family Dental offers a wide range of general and cosmetic dental procedures. With more than forty years combined experience in private practice, Dr. Marian and Dr. Bryant are dedicated to providing you and your family with the highest quality in personalized dental care.

Aesthetic and Cosmetic

Whitening

Enjoy professional, safe tooth whitening right at home! We begin by taking a full mouth scan in our office, and then your fully personalized whitening kit is fabricated for you to pick up at your convenience.

Veneers

Cosmetic veneers improve the appearance of teeth by covering the visible front surface of the teeth with a custom-made overlay. This durable option requires minimal tooth reduction in comparison to crowns.

Bonding

With cosmetic bonding , a tooth-colored material is used to replace missing dentin and enamel. It can be shade matched to your teeth and used to fix chipped, discolored, misshapen teeth and in many other applications.

Restorative

Fillings

Fillings repair and restore the surface of a tooth that has been damaged by decay, fracture, or wear. In our office, fillings can either be done with a white tooth-colored material or with silver amalgam as requested.

Crowns

When a tooth cannot be sufficiently repaired with a filling, dental crowns (also known as caps) can be used to protect and restore the decayed or fractured tooth while maintaining a healthy, beautiful smile.

Bridges

If you are missing one or more teeth, a dental bridge is a great way to restore the look and function of your teeth. Dental bridges include a prosthetic tooth that is typically secured by crowns on either side.

Implant Crowns

Dentures

When teeth have been lost to injury, gum disease, or tooth decay, full dentures are a removable option that.can restore your smile, improving your health and appearance while making eating and speaking easier.

Partials

When you’re missing one or more teeth, the other teeth can shift, affecting your bite and leading to increased wear and decay. Partial dentures are a removable solution to restore the appearance and function of your teeth.

General and Pediatric

Pediatric Care

Bolton Family Dental offers caring, friendly dental care for the whole family, especially our pediatric patients! Children should have their first dental visit between the ages of 1 and 3 or earlier if the parents have concerns.

Sonicare

In our opinion, Philips Sonicare is the best electric toothbrush on the market for maintaining tooth and gum health. For your convenience, we sell their top-of-the-line toothbrushes and replacement heads right in our office.

Bite Guards

TMJ can cause discomfort and headaches. Bruxism (grinding and clenching of the teeth) causes premature wear and breakage. Occlusal guards are appliances worn at night that are designed to relieve the effects of TMJ.

Imaging

Comprehensive radiographic exams are necessary to ensure continued oral health. Today’s digital radiographs allow us to quickly and efficiently process x-rays while significantly reducing radiation exposure to the patient.

Impressions

Digital impressions are a game changer in dentistry. The messy, unpleasant impressions of yesterday have been replaced by the intraoral scanner, a device that offers greater comfort, precision, and accuracy than ever before.

Cleanings

Regular hygiene visits are the cornerstone of preventative dentistry. Our top notch hygienists perform comprehensive oral exams with thorough teeth cleaning, periodontal charting, and radiographs as needed.
