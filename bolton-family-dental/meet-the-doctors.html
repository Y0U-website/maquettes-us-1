Meet the Doctors

With decades of experience in the field, including over 10 years of teaching at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, Doctors Marian and Bryant stay at the forefront of dental technology, allowing them to offer the very latest in general, aesthetic, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry.

Joanne Marian, DMD

General Dentist, Co-Owner of Bolton Family Dental

Dr. Joanne Marian did her undergraduate work at the University of Florida. She then received her doctorate (DMD) from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine where she also was an associate clinical instructor for 10 years.

Dr. Marian’s focus is on comprehensive oral health care. She is passionate about the cosmetic and restorative aspects of dentistry and stays current with the latest technological advances. Dr. Marian takes time to listen to your concerns, ensures that she addresses them, and makes sure that you fully understand your treatment options. Her goal is to provide the best care for you and your family.

Dr. Marian is a member of the American Dental Society , Massachusetts Dental Society , Wachusett District Dental Society , the New England Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (NEACD), the International Team for Implantology (ITI), and the national and local Spear Study Clubs .

Dr. Marian and her husband have two grown children. In her spare time, she enjoys playing with her dogs, watching all Boston sports teams, reading and doing yoga.

Melinda Bryant, DMD

General Dentist, Co-Owner of Bolton Family Dental

Dr. Bryant attended the University of Connecticut for her undergraduate studies where she received her degrees in Physiology and Neurobiology/Molecular Cell Biology. She earned her doctorate from Temple’s Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia where she graduated summa cum laude.

During her time in dental school, Dr.Bryant participated in dental mission trips and was an active member of the public health club which provides community services promoting oral health care. Dr. Bryant is also passionate about the aesthetic and cosmetic aspects of dentistry and has always paid close attention to detail, using the latest technology and techniques in her practice. She is committed to providing relationship-based, comprehensive care for all of her patients.

Dr. Bryant is a member of the American Dental Society , the Massachusetts Dental Society , and the New England Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry .

She is a native of Hopkinton, Massachusetts and enjoys spending time on the lake with her husband, family, friends, and dog.
