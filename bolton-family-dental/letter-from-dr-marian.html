A Letter from Dr. Marian

To my most valued patients,

It is with bittersweet emotions that I write to inform you that I have decided to retire from the practice of dentistry at the end of April 2027. It has been my honor to have been given your trust, and the opportunity to take care of your oral health for all these years. I have dedicated a large part of my life to dentistry, but it is time for me to focus on other life opportunities.

It has been my pleasure to work alongside Dr. Bryant for the last 10 years. She is a very gifted and talented dentist. Her intellect and dedication to the field of dentistry is extraordinary. She shares the same morals and values as me. I can confidently say she will put the same level of love and integrity into taking care of you that I have.

I will continue to see patients through the spring and am thrilled to be a part of the Bolton Family Dental team for the duration.

Dentistry is a special profession, and I am thankful that I made the decision so many years ago to make it my career. I want to humbly thank you all for making this part of my life such a blessing. I will miss taking care of you and your family. It has been a privilege.
