Braces

Braces is another popular option for straightening your teeth. The advantages include a beautiful smile, improved chewing, and improved speaking. Metal wires and brackets attach to your teeth and gradually bring your teeth into alignment.

There are a variety of options involving braces, and your dentist can help you decide what is best for you. You will be given clear and specific instructions for receiving the maximum benefits braces has to offer.
