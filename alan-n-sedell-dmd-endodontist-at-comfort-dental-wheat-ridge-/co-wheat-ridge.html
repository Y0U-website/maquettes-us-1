Services Offered at Comfort Dental – Wheat Ridge

  • General Dentistry: We provide full-scope general dentistry, including professional cleanings, exams, and preventive care to maintain healthy teeth and gums. Our focus is on early detection, patient education, and long-term oral health. We treat patients of all ages with personalized care and attention.
  • Implants : Dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring your smile. They function like natural teeth and help preserve bone structure and facial support. Our team uses advanced techniques to ensure precise placement and excellent long-term results.
  • Mini-Implants: Mini-implants are a minimally invasive option for stabilizing dentures or replacing smaller teeth. They often require less healing time and can be an affordable alternative to traditional implants. We’ll evaluate your needs and determine if mini-implants are the right fit for your situation.
  • Wisdom Tooth Extraction/Oral Surgery : When wisdom teeth become impacted or painful, extraction may be necessary to protect your oral health. We offer gentle, in-office oral surgery procedures with a focus on patient comfort. Our dentists explain every step so you feel informed and confident about your treatment.
  • Zirconium Crowns: Strong, metal-free, and highly aesthetic, zirconium crowns are ideal for restoring damaged or weakened teeth. These crowns blend beautifully with your natural enamel and are built to last. We customize each crown for a precise fit and optimal appearance.
  • Fillings : We provide both tooth-colored composite fillings and traditional silver fillings to repair cavities and restore strength to damaged teeth. Our team helps you choose the right material based on your preferences and needs. Each filling is placed with precision to ensure comfort and longevity.
  • Clear Aligners : Clear aligners offer a subtle and convenient way to straighten your teeth without metal brackets or wires. These removable trays are custom-designed to move your teeth gradually and comfortably. They’re a great option for many teens and adults looking to improve their smile with minimal disruption to daily life.
  • Bleaching: Our professional whitening treatments can dramatically brighten your smile in just one visit. We use safe, effective bleaching solutions to lift deep stains caused by food, drinks, or aging. It’s a quick way to enhance your appearance and boost your confidence.
