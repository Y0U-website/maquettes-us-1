The Gold Plan at Comfort Dental

Invest in Your Smile Today

Making dental care affordable and accessible is at the heart of what we do at Comfort Dental. Our Gold Plan is designed to help you get the quality dental services you need without the burden of high upfront costs or complicated insurance requirements. Whether you’re looking for routine preventive care , orthodontic treatment , or restorative dentistry, the Gold Plan offers a straightforward payment solution that works for your budget.

With low monthly payments, transparent pricing, and the flexibility to choose from a wide range of services, the Gold Plan removes the financial barriers that often prevent people from visiting the dentist regularly. We believe everyone deserves a healthy smile, and our Gold Plan makes that goal achievable for more families across our communities.

An Overview of Our Gold Plan

Exact benefits vary by Comfort Dental location and may not apply to services provided by a dental specialist (such as a periodontist, endodontist, orthodontist, or oral surgeon), nor to dental hygiene products such as chlorhexidine and toothbrushes. Remember that this is not an insurance plan. This is an annual contract with auto-renewal, and you can opt out at any time through the Member Portal. If you do so within the first 30 days without using any of the benefits, you will get a full refund (specific terms vary by state).

To get started, simply visit the Gold Plan site , choose your local Comfort Dental office, choose the type of membership you’re interested in, and start saving and smiling! With none of the waiting periods of an insurance plan, you can start at any time.

Getting the Most Out of Your Dentist with the Gold Plan

Regular visits to the dentist are one of the smartest investments you can make in your health, and the Gold Plan makes it easier than ever to stay on top of your dental care. When you enroll in the Gold Plan, you gain access to preventive services like cleanings, exams, and X-rays at predictable monthly rates. This means you can schedule your appointments with confidence, knowing that your costs are manageable and there are no surprise bills waiting for you.

Preventive care is the foundation of good oral health, and having a payment plan that supports regular dentist visits helps you catch potential problems early, before they become painful or expensive. The Gold Plan empowers you to take control of your dental health by making it financially feasible to see your dentist twice a year, just as recommended.

With the Gold Plan, you’re not just visiting the dentist for emergencies or when something hurts. Instead, you’re building a relationship with a trusted dentist who knows your history, understands your goals, and helps you maintain optimal oral health over the long term. That consistent care leads to better outcomes, fewer complications, and a healthier, more confident smile.

Your Affordable Dentist Solution Awaits

At Comfort Dental, we work hard to help you achieve your smile goals without financial stress. The Gold Plan is designed with transparency, affordability, and flexibility in mind, making it easier for patients to receive the dental care they deserve. Whether you’re interested in maintaining your oral health with routine visits to the dentist or more extensive treatment like braces or restorative dentistry, the Gold Plan offers a solution that fits your life and your budget.

what makes these plans unique?

All of the Comfort Dental Gold Plans offer the access of group dental care, plus the individualized attention of private care. The plans provide up to two simple teeth cleanings, local anesthetic, examinations, x-rays, and other preventive dentistry at no additional charge.* Braces and other services are also provided at reduced fees.

Our Gold Plan options are discounted programs that are not health insurance policies and do not make payments directly to dental service providers. Members are obligated to pay for all dental services, but may receive discounts on dental services from participating providers. The discount range will vary depending on provider type and dental services received.
