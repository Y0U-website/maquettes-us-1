Services

  • Exams With the help of his team, Dr. Adair will examine your teeth, review x-rays and conduct an oral cancer screening to determine the current standing of your oral health. This examination will help determine what measures need to be taken to keep your oral hygiene at its best.
  • Sealants Dental sealants are a thin, protective coating that shield the chewing surfaces of your back teeth from harmful, cavity-causing bacteria. If you are more prone to sensitivity or cavities, getting sealants can be a useful preventative measure.
  • Flouride Fluoride treatments strengthen tooth enamel, making them less susceptible to bacteria and cavities. They can also slow and may even reverse the development of decays and cavities by harming the bacteria which causes the cavities. Fluoride varnish can protect teeth for several months, and it works best if reapplied every three to six months.

General Dentistry

No matter your dental needs, Dr. Adair and his team can help. Our experienced and attentive staff will ensure you leave our office happy. We provide dental care for dentures, root canals, extractions, wisdom tooth removal, bridges and more.

  • Implant Restoration A dental implant is a small post that replaces a missing tooth’s root. As the only permanent tooth replacement solution, dental implants will instantly improve your quality of life and oral health. Dr. Adair is highly experienced in implant restoration. Using state-of-the-art technology and skilled precision, he offers the best implant treatment and long-term successful outcomes.
  • Root Canals A tooth that has significant decay or fractures can cause significant pain or discomfort and a root canal may be needed. A root canal is a procedure that repairs or saves damaged or infected teeth instead of removing them. During a root canal, inflamed or infected pulp is removed and cleaned. The root canal is then filled and sealed. After the procedure, the dentist places a crown or other restoration on the tooth.
  • Extractions An extraction is performed to remove a tooth due to disease, crowding, or damage. When extractions are required, the area around the tooth will be numbed and Dr. Adair will remove the tooth.
  • Fillings A dental filling is an artificial substance that fills cavities in teeth. Dr. Adair uses fillings to treat small holes, gaps, or damaged teeth to improve the health and comfort of your mouth.
  • Wisdom Teeth Wisdom teeth can cause crowding with your other teeth, which can lead to discomfort and crooked teeth. At Adair Family Dental we can remove wisdom teeth and will make sure you are comfortable throughout the procedure.
  • Gum Treatment The gums, ligaments, and bone which surround the teeth form the foundation for your teeth. All structures are also referred to as the periodontium. When the periodontium is not healthy, it jeopardizes the teeth just as a bad foundation would threaten the stability of a house. With proper gum treatments, however, it may be possible to return gum tissue to a healthy state. The treatment usually involves a deep cleaning or root planning, done under a local anesthetic, along with local antibiotic agents.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Let us help you create a smile that you are proud of through our cosmetic dentistry services. Whether you want to whiten up your smile, fix and protect damaged teeth, or straighten things up with aligners, we’ve got what you need.

  • Veneers Dental veneers are custom-made, thin coverings that fit over the front surfaces of teeth. They can help improve the appearance of teeth by concealing cracks, chips, and stains.
  • Bonding Similar to veneers, dental bonding is a cosmetic treatment that can improve your smile. Both are made to blent in with the color of the surrounding teeth, however, bonding uses a composite resin material rather than the ceramic substance used for veneers. Bonding is a faster, most affordable method, but may lack the durability of veneers.
  • Teeth Whitening Whitening is a popular procedure to make teeth whiter and brighter, giving them a more attractive appearance. Tooth discoloration happens to everyone. Over time, these stains can get darker, which may hinder a confident smile. Our office offers two methods of whitening: In-Office and At-Home. Ask our team for more details.
  • Aligners Clear aligners straighten teeth with clear, custom, removable dental devices which move the teeth a little bit at a time. Clear aligners are great alternative to traditional braces.
  • Bridges & Crowns Crowns and Bridges are used to restore and enhance teeth that are damaged, or to take the place of missing teeth. A crown, also referred to as a cap, is used to entirely cover a damaged tooth. A crown not only strengthens a tooth, but it can dramatically improve a tooth’s appearance, shape, and alignment.
