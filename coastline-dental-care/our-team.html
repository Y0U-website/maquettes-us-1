Patients from across the South Shore

Most of our patients live in Oceanside, but plenty drive in from the surrounding towns. We regularly see families from Rockville Centre, East Rockaway, Baldwin, Lynbrook and Island Park, all of which are a short drive from the office.

People usually find us while looking for a dentist who handles most of their care in one place instead of sending them out to specialists. If you are new to the area or looking to switch, we make it easy to transfer your records and get the whole family scheduled together.
