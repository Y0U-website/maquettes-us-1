Meet the Doctors

Dr. Mike is a native of the Memphis area. He is married to his wonderful wife, Jammie. They have 3 grown kids and one grandson.

Dr. Mike attended the University of Memphis before pursuing his dental degree at the University of TN in Memphis. After graduating in 2002 with an award for Excellence in Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Mike taught undergraduate pediatric dentistry at UT before enrolling in the pediatric dentistry residency program. He completed his residency in 2005 and began working in private practice. In 2009, with the help of his supportive family, Dr. Mike moved his practice to Marion and opened what would become Children&#x27;s Dental Group.

Dr. Mike is board certified with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and has held staff appointments at both LeBonheur Children&#x27;s Hospital and Crittenden Regional Hospital. He returned to part-time teaching at UT as an Assistant Professor in the Graduate Pediatric Dentistry department where he worked for several years.

Away from the office, Dr. Mike enjoys relaxing at home with Jammie and their 3 fur babies.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Todd Smith is a board-certified pediatric dentist with more than ten years of experience in both teaching and practicing the pediatric dentistry. He is a proud graduate of the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff, where he received his Bachelor of Science in biology. Following college, he was accepted into the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry and completed his training in 2007. During his matriculation through dental school, Dr. Smith received numerous honors for both academic and clinical accomplishments.

After graduation, he became a full-time faculty member at the college of dentistry because of his passion for teaching students. His passion for children led Dr. Smith back to school to complete a residency in pediatric dentistry in 2010. He is also a fellow of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and currently serves as both a writer and oral clinical examiner for the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

On a personal note, Dr. Smith is an active outdoorsman who loves to ride bikes and to fish. He stays very active in his community and his church. He volunteers for numerous health fairs and mission trips and heads up the pediatric dental section of Mid-South Mission of Mercy on a yearly basis.
