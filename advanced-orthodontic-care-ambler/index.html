The practice

Improving your smile is one of the best investments you can make in your life. Here at Azizi Orthodontics, we provide you with more than just a beautiful smile we create a healthy one as well.

During your orthodontic consultation, Dr. Benjamin Azizi will review your aesthetic and functional needs, and develop a plan uniquely suited to meet those needs, whether with clear or metal braces , Invisalign clear aligners, or accelerated orthodontic treatment. At the end of your time with us, you’ll leave with ideal bite function and a smile you love to share.

Azizi Orthodontics has two convenient office locations in Ambler and Glenside , PA. We serve patients of all ages in Spring House, Blue Bell, Jenkintown, Horsham, and all surrounding communities. To learn more about orthodontics and schedule an appointment, just give us a call. We look forward to welcoming you to our practice, and starting you on the path to a beautiful and confident smile!
