We care for more than just teeth.

To put it simply, you’re the reason why we come to work every day. We’re not just here for the dental wins (although a cavity-free mouth is always great). We love getting to know our patients, supporting them and helping them celebrate the big moments in life.

We believe your trust should be earned, not bought. We’re on a mission to do exactly that. In collaboration with you, our team and our community, we aim to create healthy smiles and positive futures for all.

Our values live beyond a place on our wall.

We’re a great dental practice to work for, too.

Here at Contemporary Family Dentistry, a "day at the office" is anything but boring. We share a mutual love and respect for our patients, as well as each other.
