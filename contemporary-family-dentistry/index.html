The practice

We follow the highest standard of care, use state-of-the-art technology and take care of not just your oral health – but your overall health. More importantly, we take the time to get to know you and ask the right questions. We treat you like family so you can relax, sit back in the chair and, dare we say it – even have some fun!

Oral care isn’t just about your mouth (although we take your mouth very seriously). It’s about relationships and mutual respect. From floss bosses to sweet tooths, every mouth is welcome here. We believe in combining our knowledge and experience with our patients to work together towards a common goal – a confident smile.

The same goes for emergencies! Every second counts – and can feel like a lifetime when you’re in pain. We’re on a mission to put ‘care’ back into healthcare. Our team will try to see you the same day or the next.
