Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures might be right for you if:

Even if you are not a candidate for implant dentures right away, you may become one after necessary preliminary treatment. Most patients who are interested in this procedure can qualify for it.

  • You have suffered extensive tooth loss.
  • Your jawbone is healthy enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease.
  • You are in fair overall health.
  • You have good habits that will support your implants’ long-term success.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Some of the most remarkable benefits of implant dentures include:

  • You can regain a strong bite force that can handle virtually any food. Plus, implant dentures do not slip and slide around the mouth.
  • We carefully design implant dentures so they look as natural as possible.
  • Easy maintenance. In some ways, caring for implant dentures is just like caring for natural teeth.
  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants support the jawbone and prevent the deterioration that normally occurs after tooth loss.
  • Dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime, while the dentures on top of them can also last for years.

Implant Denture FAQs

Are implant dentures removable?

There are two different types of implant dentures: removable and fixed. Many patients prefer fixed because they allow them to forget they have dentures and feel like they did before tooth loss. These dentures don’t require any special care besides excellent oral hygiene.

In some cases, patients may need or prefer removable implant dentures in Brick Township. These are equipped with clips or clasps to help take them out of the mouth for cleaning.

How do I care for my implant dentures?

Unlike traditional dentures, caring for your implant dentures in Brick Township is very easy. To maintain them, be sure to brush your dentures and your gums twice a day, as well as around the dental implant abutments. Also, be sure to keep up with your regular preventive exam and cleaning appointments at Nu Dental Brick.

How many teeth can I replace with implant dentures?

Your implant dentures in Brick Township can replace an entire arch of teeth! Choosing an implant denture is a safe, natural looking way to affordably restore your smile without having to deal with the frustration of traditional dentures.

How long do implant dentures last?

Although your implant dentures in Brick Township probably won’t last forever, the denture itself can last for up to 10 years without needing to be repaired or replaced, and the dental implants they connect to could potentially last the rest of your life!

To ensure that your implant dentures last as long as possible, be careful when eating hard or tough foods like nuts, seeds, and popcorn, never use them to bite fingernails or open packaging, and be sure to keep them clean with regular oral hygiene practices and visits to Nu Dental Brick for your preventive exams and cleanings.
