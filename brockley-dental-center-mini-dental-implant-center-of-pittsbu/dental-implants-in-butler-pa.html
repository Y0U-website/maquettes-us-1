Dental Implants

There are several different types of dental implants, including mini dental implants and standard implants.

At Brockley Dental Center, we provide mini implants to replace one or more missing teeth or to add stability to dentures. These smaller screws have several advantages over the standard, more invasive procedure.

  • Broken teeth
  • Loose dentures
  • Tooth loss due to periodontal disease or tooth decay
  • Aesthetic concerns

Mini Dental Implants vs. Standard Dental Implants

Mini implants set themselves apart from standard dental implants in a number of ways.

Smaller Dental Implants Size

The most important way that mini dental implants differ from standard dental implants is in their size of their titanium screws. Mini implants are significantly smaller in diameter than their standard predecessors. While the traditional screws average about 5 millimeters wide, the mini versions are less than 3 millimeters in diameter.

Less Invasive Procedure

The procedure itself is another way in which mini implants differ from standard implants. Mini implants are essentially a simplified version of the traditional implant process.

They require a one-piece fixture instead of the multipiece unit that standard prosthetics use. This means that one post will be attached to the jawbone to support the artificial tooth. For this reason, the mini implant placement procedure is often much faster than the procedure for traditional implants.

Lower Cost

The cost of restorative and cosmetic procedures like implants can be unmanageable for many patients. Since mini implants are less invasive and involve fewer pieces of hardware, they are often around half the cost of traditional implants. This is a great benefit for patients, as it’s common to be concerned about the expense of getting new teeth placed.

Shorter Recovery

Both types of implants involve a recovery period. However, recovering from mini implants is simpler and less extensive than with the standard version. With their smaller size and minimal hardware, mini implants have less of an impact on the gumline and jawbone. This means you can get back to your normal activities much sooner—with a new, healthy smile!
