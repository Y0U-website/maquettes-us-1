Disease or Decay

One major factor in tooth loss is periodontal disease. Untreated gum infections damage the tissue that holds the teeth in place, eventually leading to tooth loss. Tooth decay also destroys teeth over time. When a tooth is fully decayed, or in the advanced stages of decay, the only treatment is extraction.

After these diseased or decaying teeth are removed, many patients wish to replace them with implants to maintain a full smile. Mini dental implants are a great option when it comes to quickly replacing a tooth after extraction.

What Is It Like to Receive Mini Dental Implants?

Brockley Dental goes above and beyond to make the dental implant process as simple as possible. Most mini dental implant procedures are completed in one day, but some may require two separate visits. This is a significant difference from other dental restoration procedures that can require several visits to multiple doctors.

During your mini dental implant procedure, you will receive the level of sedation necessary to keep you comfortable. For some patients, this is nitrous oxide (laughing gas). For others, conscious oral sedation, where you take a pill that relaxes you to sleep, is the best option. For more complex treatments, we may collaborate with an anesthesiologist to put you fully under anesthesia.

Once you are sedated, any necessary extractions will be performed using a minimally invasive technique. Your mini implants will then be placed one at a time.

With a single-piece screw, each implant will be attached to the jawbone to later integrate. When all of your implants have been placed, you will be transferred to our aftercare area until you are ready to go home.
