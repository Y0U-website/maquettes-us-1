Dental Fillings

Composite Fillings

A composite dental filling is a tooth-colored filling used to restore decayed teeth. Composite filling is also used to make cosmetic improvements of your smile by changing the color or reshaping disfigured teeth. They can be used on front or back teeth and preserve natural look of your teeth.

Sedative Fillings

Sedative dental filling is a temporary filling used to repair a decayed, cracked, or chipped teeth. Temporary filling is intended to relieve pain and is used to give the patient time to decide what can be done with the tooth. When teeth have extensive decay and are approaching the pulp (nerve), we may choose to place a sedative filling to soothe the pulp, then arrive at the proper treatment for the problem. Making the best decision while not in discomfort.

Replace failing Amalgam Fillings
