William Boyczuk, D.D.S., M.D.

Dr. William Boyczuk attended St. John Fischer College from 1991 to 1994, majoring in biology. He then attended the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, where he obtained his degree of Doctor of Dental Surgery in 1998. Dr. Boyczuk completed a six year Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency in 2004. During this training, he also received his M.D. degree from MCP Hahnmann University (now Drexel) in 2001.

Dr. Boyczuk is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He is also a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the NYS Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the WNY Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, the American Medical Association, the Medical Society of the State of New York, American Dental Association, NYS Dental Association, Erie County Dental Society, the University of Buffalo Dental Alumni Association, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon.

Dr. Boyczuk is an active Clinical Assistant Professor at the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is on staff at Erie County Medical Center, Mercy Hospital, and Bertrand Chaffee Hospital.

Dr. Boyczuk is married with three children and resides in Orchard Park.

Margarita Varer, DDS, MD

Dr. Margarita Varer is a board certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon, and a native New Yorker. She has been enjoying private practice surgery since 2020.

Dr. Varer graduated from the State University of New York at Binghamton with her Bachelor of Science degree in cell & molecular biology. She then went on to study at the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated Magna Cum Laude with a minor in oral & maxillofacial surgery. Along with high honors Dr. Varer received the American Academy of Oral & Maxillofacial Pathology Award and the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology Horace Wells Award for exceptional interest in anesthesia and pain control.

Dr. Varer then went on to complete her 6-year post-doctoral training in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery at the prestigious University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she also obtained her medical degree.

As a Diplomate with the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons she practices to the highest standard in caring for all patients. She is licensed to practice dentistry and perform deep sedation/general anesthesia for adults and children alike.

She maintains certification in General anesthesia, Basic Life Support, Advanced Cardiac Life Support, and Pediatric Advanced Life Support.

When not in the office treating patients Dr. Varer can be found enjoying the outdoors, hiking, gardening, or simply enjoying the company of her husband and son.

Justin Au
