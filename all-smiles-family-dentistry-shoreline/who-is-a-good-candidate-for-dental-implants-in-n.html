Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants in North Seattle?

As you get older, you’ll start to regret not taking care of your teeth well. They may start to become loose, causing them to fall out. Even if you did take care of your teeth, you can still end up losing your teeth and needing fake teeth, such as dental i mplants in North Seattle .

There are different types of fake teeth, with dental implants becoming one of the most popular. They look like natural teeth, and they’re even designed to function like the real deal. This can be great for long-term confidence, but not everyone is a candidate for these types of false teeth.

You Need Healthy Gums and Good Oral Hygiene

Your dentist will take a look at the health of your mouth. Implants in North Seattle are placed directly into the jawbone, which means the gums need to be healthy. You need to have a good oral hygiene routine, and you’ll need to make sure your gums are free from any sort of disease or infection.

If you get implants with gum disease, you can end up weakening the whole structure. The mouth may not even heal properly, and this leads to future health issues and the need to have the implants removed.

Remember that the implants are placed directly into the jawbone, which means you need to have enough bone there to do this. The bone needs to be an anchor for your implants in North Seattle , and if it’s weakened or if there’s been loss or deterioration, the dental surgeon may not be able to place the structures to hold the implants in place.

However, talk to your dentist about your options. A bone graft could be an option to help, but this is an added cost that you’ll need to consider.

There is a lot of focus on your health and the way you look after your body. Because placing the implants in North Seattle is surgical, there is a healing process, and if you have poor health overall, your body may not be able to heal from this. This can include having certain disabilities or chronic illnesses. Even those with uncontrolled diabetes can end up being recommended to look at something else.

Likewise, your lifestyle will play a part. For example, smokers will find that their healing time is slowed and there’s a restriction of blood, and this leads to failure of the implants fixing to your jawbone properly. If you don’t clean your teeth properly now, your dentist may be worried that you’ll continue not doing so, which leads to a higher risk of gum disease, even with the implants.

The aim is for success. Your dentist will want to make sure you get something that helps you gain the confidence you want but also fits into your lifestyle and your preferences. Your health and age will also be taken into consideration, and it’s something that you need to be serious about. If you really want to get dental implants in North Seattle , talk about the steps that you’ll need to do afterward and start working on them now to prove that you are serious.
