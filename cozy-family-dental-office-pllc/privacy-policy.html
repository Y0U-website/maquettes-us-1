2. 🏥 How We Use Your Information

We may use your health and personal information for the following purposes:

We only collect and use the minimum necessary information to fulfill these purposes.

3. 🔄 Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose your PHI in the following situations:

We do not sell or share your PHI for marketing purposes without your explicit authorization.

  • To You or Your Authorized Representative
  • For Treatment (e.g., referrals to specialists or labs)
  • For Payment (e.g., submitting claims to insurance providers)
  • For Healthcare Operations (e.g., administrative tasks, auditing)
  • When Required by Law (e.g., public health reporting, court orders)

4. 🔐 Your Rights Under HIPAA

As a patient, you have the following rights regarding your PHI:

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us in writing at the address below.

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of your health records.
  • Right to Amend: You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You may ask for a list of disclosures of your PHI.
  • Right to Request Restrictions: You may ask us not to use or share certain information.
  • Right to Confidential Communications: You can request contact through specific methods or locations.
  • Right to File a Complaint: You may file a complaint if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

5. 🔒 Safeguarding Your Information

We implement physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your PHI from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. This includes:

  • Secure storage of physical records
  • HIPAA-compliant software for digital records
  • Staff training on privacy and data protection

6. 🧾 Website & Online Communications

If you submit personal or medical information through our website (e.g., appointment requests or contact forms), it will be treated with the same confidentiality as in-office data. However, please avoid sending sensitive health information via unencrypted email.

Our website may use cookies or analytics tools to improve user experience, but these do not collect or store PHI.
