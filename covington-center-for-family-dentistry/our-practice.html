About Covington Family Dentistry: Our Practice

At Covington Center for Family Dentistry, we provide compassionate care that goes above and beyond to make our patients feel at ease. Specializing in family dentistry, our dental practice conveniently provides care to patients of varying ages.

Our dental practice is home to a highly-trained team of dental professionals and staff who create a worry-free experience for you and your family. State-of-the-art technology, a sterile environment and comfortable amenities, make our family dentistry practice stand out from the rest. With HD TV’s placed above the dental chairs, patients are welcome to enjoy a movie or their favorite show while receiving treatment. Hot towels, blankets and pillows, a beverage station, and access to WiFi to improve your time with us.

A Covington Dental Practice with Many Services

Advanced technologies allow our family dentistry practice to offer a variety of dental services in-office, eliminating the need to travel to numerous specialists for dental care. Whether you need a routine cleaning or an in-depth procedure, these services are made affordable through a variety of payment options. We accept all major PPO plans.

With a focus on not only adult oral care, but also children’s dentistry, Covington Center for Family Dentistry offers convenient and affordable care for the whole family. For more information on the comprehensive services offered by our Covington, GA dental practice, call us at (678) 306-6000 or schedule an appointment with us online.

We look forward to getting to know you and your family!
