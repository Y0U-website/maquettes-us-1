About

Evidence-Based Dentistry, Gentle Care, and Clear Explanations

We believe excellent dental care starts with listening and is guided by evidence-based decision-making. At Cosmo Dental, treatment recommendations are grounded in current scientific research, clinical experience, and established best practices.

Both doctors are active members of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Massachusetts Dental Society (MDS), reflecting their commitment to ethical standards, continuing education, and high-quality patient care.

We take time to understand each patient’s concerns, explain treatment options clearly, and move at a pace that feels comfortable. Patients are never rushed and never pressured into decisions.

The Principles That Guide Our Practice

We prioritize comfort, patience, and a calm environment to reduce anxiety at every visit.

We provide clear, straightforward explanations and recommend care based on need, not pressure.

Our dentists bring extensive training, ongoing education, and real-world experience to deliver consistent, high-quality care.

We offer same-day and emergency care when possible and accept MassHealth and most major insurance plans.

Flexible Payment Options to Fit Your Budget

Cosmo Dental accepts all major dental insurance plans, including MassHealth. We also offer payment plans and financing options to help make dental care accessible and affordable. Our knowledgeable team is happy to review your benefits, explain costs upfront, and answer any billing questions so there are no surprises before treatment begins. We welcome new patients and accept walk-ins for dental emergencies whenever possible.

Modern Communication Designed Around You

Cosmo Dental uses the Weave communication system to make your experience simple and convenient. With Weave, patients can receive calls, text messages, appointment reminders, and secure links directly on their phones. New patient forms and paperwork can be completed digitally in advance, saving time during your visit. This allows you to check in smoothly and focus on your care when you arrive.
