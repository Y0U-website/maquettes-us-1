Restorative Dentistry

Restore Strength and Function with Natural-Looking Care

Restorative dentistry repairs teeth affected by decay, damage, or wear. Whether you need a filling or a crown, we use tooth-colored materials designed to blend naturally with your smile. Our approach focuses on durability, comfort, and preserving natural tooth structure.

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Crowns
  • Core build-ups

Endodontics (Root Canals)

Relieve Tooth Pain and Save Your Natural Tooth

Root canal treatment relieves infection and pain while preserving the natural tooth. Modern techniques make treatment far more comfortable than most patients expect. Same-day care is available for urgent toothaches when possible.

  • Root canal treatment
  • Evaluation of dental infections
  • Emergency pain management

Oral Surgery

Safe, Gentle Tooth Extractions When Necessary

Extractions are performed only when necessary and with a focus on comfort and safety. We explain all options clearly and provide careful aftercare. Emergency extractions are available when needed.

  • Simple extractions
  • Surgical extractions
  • Emergency extractions

Dental Implants

Replace Missing Teeth with Natural-Looking Dental Implants

Dental implants provide a long-term solution for missing teeth. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Bone grafting options are available when needed.

  • Dental implants
  • Implant-supported crowns
  • Bone grafting

Prosthetics (Dentures)

Restore Your Smile and Confidence with Custom Dentures

Dentures replace multiple missing teeth or full arches. We offer both partial and complete dentures designed for comfort, function, and natural appearance.

  • Complete dentures
  • Partial dentures
  • Denture adjustments

Cosmetic & Clear Aligner Orthodontics

Enhance Your Smile with Confidence and Discreet Alignment

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your smile while maintaining oral health. We also offer clear aligner orthodontic treatment for patients looking to straighten their teeth discreetly and comfortably. Clear aligners, such as Invisalign®, are custom-designed to gradually move teeth into proper alignment without traditional metal braces.

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Clear aligners (Invisalign® and similar systems)

Emergency & Same-Day Care

We treat dental emergencies including severe pain, broken teeth, lost fillings, and swelling. Walk-ins are welcome and same-day care is available whenever possible.
