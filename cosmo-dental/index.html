The practice

Built on the trusted care of Cordial Dental in Worcester, we offer comprehensive dental services with same-day appointments and emergency walk-ins available whenever possible.

Cosmo Dental is led by Dr. Sansriti Narain, DDS, owner of the trusted Cordial Dental practice in Worcester. With over a decade of experience in family and pediatric dentistry, she is known for her calm leadership, gentle care, and patient-first approach. She now brings the same standard of care to Cosmo Dental in Hudson, serving families throughout the MetroWest area.

Dr. Narain is joined by Dr. Sandeep Singh, DDS, whose experience in restorative and endodontic care allows our team to provide comprehensive dental services in a comfortable, unhurried environment focused on clarity and trust.

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services, from routine preventive care to restorative and emergency treatments. Our experience and modern technology allow us to complete most procedures under one roof, making care convenient and efficient for our patients.

Exams, cleanings, and digital X-rays to maintain long-term oral health.

Root canal treatment to relieve pain and save natural teeth.

Partial and complete dentures to restore function and confidence.

If you’re experiencing tooth pain, swelling, or a dental emergency, Cosmo Dental offers same-day and urgent appointments whenever available. We focus on relieving discomfort quickly and explaining next steps clearly.
