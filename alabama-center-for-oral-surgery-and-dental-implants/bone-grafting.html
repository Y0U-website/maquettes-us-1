Bone Grafting

What is Socket Preservation Bone Grafting?

Socket preservation bone grafting is a procedure that helps to maintain the bone structure after a tooth is extracted. When a tooth is removed, the bone around the socket (the hole where the tooth was) can start to shrink or deteriorate. Socket preservation prevents this bone loss, making future dental procedures like implants more successful.

Benefits of Socket Preservation include:

Prevents Bone Loss

Maintains Jaw Structure

Prepares for Future Implants

Preserved bone provides a strong foundation for a future dental implant, reducing the need for additional bone grafting later. Dental implants can typically be placed 3-4 months after socket preservation.

Improves Healing

Socket preservation can promote faster and more predictable healing after tooth extraction.

Key Purposes of Bone Grafting are:

• Preserve bone for future dental implant placement. • Maintain the shape and size of the jawbone for better aesthetics and function. • Prevent bone loss that could lead to changes in facial appearance.
