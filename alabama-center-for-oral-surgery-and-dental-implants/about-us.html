About Us

Bryan Kendricks,

DMD, MD

Dr. Bryan Kendricks holds an undergraduate degree from Florida State University and a master’s degree from Mississippi College. He earned both his dental and medical degrees from the University of Louisville, where he also completed a one-year general surgery internship and an oral maxillofacial surgery residency.

Dr. Kendricks is a member of AAOMS (American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery) and the ADA (American Dental Association).

Board Certified

Board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS). This certification requires a rigorous peer evaluation process and demonstration of the requisite training, knowledge, and experience in the field.

What Sets Us Apart?

Our aim is to help each one of our patients live healthier lives by providing exceptional oral surgery in a caring, nurturing environment. Our team is committed to your comfort and health.

The staff at The Alabama Center for Oral Surgery is always ready and willing to help make your time with us exceptional. We do this by ensuring every aspect of patient care is carefully designed and that patients are involved in the decision-making process.
