What is Full Arch Reconstruction with Implants?

Full arch reconstruction involves placing several dental implants in the jawbone to support a full set of prosthetic teeth, either on the upper or lower arch (or both). This method offers a more permanent and stable solution compared to traditional dentures.

Benefits of Full Arch Reconstruction

Permanent Solution

Natural Functionality

Improved Aesthetics

Bone Preservation

Enhanced Comfort

Key Features of Full Arch Reconstruction

• Uses 4 to 8 implants per arch (commonly 4, known as the All-on-4 technique) • A fixed bridge or denture is attached to the implants, giving a natural look and function • The implants help maintain bone structure and prevent bone loss

Full Arch Reconstruction Procedure

1. Consultation and Planning

Dr. Kendricks will take a thorough medical history, X-rays, and possibly a CT scan to assess bone structure and plan implant placement.

2. Pre-surgery Preparations

If necessary, bone grafting or sinus lift procedures may be performed prior to implant placement to ensure sufficient bone.

3. Implant Placement

4. Temporary Teeth

5. Abutment and Permanent Prosthesis

6. Immediate Load Implants ("Teeth in a Day")

In some cases, it’s possible to place a temporary set of teeth immediately after surgery.

This approach, known as Teeth in a Day, can provide immediate aesthetics and functionality, though the final prosthesis will still be fitted after healing.
