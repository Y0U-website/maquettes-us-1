Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health are candidates. We&#x27;ll evaluate your bone density and oral health during the consultation.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10-15 years.

Is the procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report less discomfort than expected and manage well with over-the-counter pain medication.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Many insurance plans cover a portion of implant treatment. Our team will help you understand your coverage and financing options.

What is the success rate?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % when placed by an experienced implantologist like Dr. Amores.

What is Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root, topped with a custom ceramic crown that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth for a permanent tooth replacement solution.

Dr. Dennis Amores is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), bringing decades of specialized expertise to every implant procedure. Whether you need a single tooth replaced or a full-arch restoration, our team delivers results that look and feel natural.
