Education and Training

Dr. Chang’s work exemplifies the advanced education and training he has received. A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Chang completed his postdoctoral specialty in prosthodontics at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where he also earned a Master of Medical Science degree. This program provided him with extensive training in both clinical specialty practice and research methodologies.

Additionally, Dr. Chang completed a surgical fellowship in implant surgery at the University of Geneva, further expanding his expertise. His comprehensive education and dual skills in both restorative and surgical treatments make the Dental Restorative Group a top choice for patients seeking durable, comfortable, and aesthetically pleasing dental outcomes, all in one location.

Personal

In honor of his late daughter Dana, a talented young violinist, Dr. John Chang founded the Dana Chang Foundation. The foundation is dedicated to celebrating Dana’s legacy by supporting rising young musicians and raising awareness for young patients with congenital heart conditions, like Dana. Learn more at www.danachangfoundation.org

A devoted husband and father, Dr. Chang is proud that his eldest daughter, Dayoon, is following in his footsteps by studying at Penn Dental School.
