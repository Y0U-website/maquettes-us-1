Ethan Ake, D.D.S.

B.A. Chemistry, Hendrix College D.D.S. University of Tennessee Health Science Center

Originally from Conway, Arkansas, Dr. Ake is a graduate of Hendrix College and attended dental school at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center.

Dr. Ake met his wife, Ashley, in middle school and they have been together ever since. When he’s not at work, he enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, Andrew and Aubrey, being outdoors in the Natural State, hunting, playing pickle ball or disc golf, and woodworking. Anytime Green Bay is playing, you can always find Dr. Ake cheering on his team!

Dr. Ake is a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and the Arkansas State Dental Association. He volunteers for several outreach programs, treating communities that often can’t afford routine dental care. He volunteers annually at the Arkansas Mission of Mercy and monthly at a local church, where he treats patients experiencing dental emergencies.

Dr. Ake has practiced in Stuttgart and Conway and is proud to open his own practice, Ake Family Dentistry, on the strong foundation Dr. Weber has built. He is committed to providing knowledgeable, considerate dental care in a relaxing environment and is honored to serve the families of Little Rock.
