One of the most important aspects of your overall oral health is keeping your teeth and gums clean and strong. To help in this effort, we provide dental cleanings and exams for your entire family. The goal of dental cleanings and exams is to provide preventive measures to stop tooth decay before it emerges while also beginning any necessary treatment planning.

When decay occurs to your tooth enamel, our dentists will likely use a dental filling to repair the damage. Dental fillings are dental restorations made from a variety of materials; they are directly applied to the surface of your tooth to fill in the empty spaces left once decay is removed.

If you are embarrassed to smile because of multiple dental flaws, or if you are simply looking for a more permanent restoration, Dr. Boyd may suggest porcelain veneers. Veneers are wafer-thin shells that are bonded to the front of your teeth to perfect the shape, color, size, and alignment of a damaged or decayed tooth. Les soins

If you are missing one or more teeth, Dr. Boyd may recommend you consider dental bridges. A dental bridge is a permanent dental restoration ideal for replacing a single tooth or multiple teeth that are missing in a row.
