How Invisalign Works

Invisalign uses clear, plastic trays to apply gentle pressure to your bite, gradually shifting teeth into alignment over the course of 12 to 18 months. In addition to being virtually undetectable, the aligners relieve some of the discomfort associated with traditional braces as teeth are not made to pull against the surrounding teeth to shift.

For the treatment to work effectively, you’ll have to wear the Invisalign aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day, or as otherwise directed by Dr. Alkhoury. However, the trays are removable, meaning you can take them out for convenient eating and cleaning.

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Did you know that until the 1970s, all orthodontic treatment was considered cosmetic? Bite correction was considered a vanity and a luxury available to only the wealthiest members of society. Today, it’s clear that more patients want to leave overcrowding, overbites, and other common orthodontic problems in the past. Fortunately, Invisalign can correct mild to moderate cases, making the path to a straighter smile considerably more discreet.

Crowded Teeth

When there’s not enough space in your mouth for all of your teeth, your smile can appear overcrowded. This problem can also cause discomfort and increase your risk of common oral health problems, like tooth decay. The good news is that Invisalign is often an option for patients with mild to moderate cases. So, if you want to straighten your teeth without the metal, schedule a consultation with us so we can determine if you’re a good candidate.

Gaps Between Teeth

Noticeable gaps between your teeth can be considered a cosmetic concern. However, they can also increase your risk of harmful bacteria build-up and throw off your bite alignment. That’s why we recommend scheduling a consultation at our office sooner rather than later! Once we have assessed the severity of your misalignment and the health of your smile, we can determine if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign.

Overbite

While Invisalign is mostly known for being able to fix crooked and gapped teeth, it can also fix several common bite problems, including overbites. So, if you are struggling with chronic jaw pain or low self-esteem because your upper teeth jut out beyond your lower ones considerably, then Invisalign could very well alleviate your discomfort and restore your confidence.

Underbite

If your lower teeth sit in front of your upper ones when you bite down, then there’s a chance that you have an underbite. Unfortunately, even relatively mild cases of this bite issue can lead to chronic jaw pain, low self-esteem, and trouble enunciating clearly. That’s why we recommend scheduling an appointment with our team at Advanced Dental Center! The sooner you do, the sooner we can determine if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign.

Crossbite

If only a few of your upper teeth sit behind your lower ones, then you likely have a crossbite. This bite problem, like the others on this list, can result in chronic jaw pain and premature wear on your teeth. If you think you may be struggling with this orthodontic problem, you’ll be happy to hear that traditional braces usually aren’t the only option. Invisalign clear aligners can be used in tandem with other orthodontic accessories, like rubber bands, to correct crossbites as well.

Open Bite

When you bite down, your upper and lower teeth should meet together evenly. If they don’t because there is a space between them, then you might have an open bite. Oftentimes, Invisalign is still an option, and, if that’s the case for you, then your orthodontic treatment won’t involve any metal at all.

Living with Invisalign Aligners

Prior to committing to Invisalign, you don’t just want to know the price, the benefits, and how much time you need to invest in the teeth-straightening process; you want to know what your day-to-day life will look like with braces. So, we’ve dedicated this next section to exactly that! Below, you will find helpful information regarding cleaning your trays, what to do if you accidentally lose one, and more.

Wearing Your Trays

There aren’t too many rules that come with Invisalign, but there are a few important ones, including that you must wear your trays for a minimum of 20 hours a day. This is crucial because, if you don’t wear them consistently, you won’t stay on-track with your treatment timeline.

One of the easiest ways to meet this goal day after day is by only taking your aligners off when it’s absolutely necessary and if you can sticking to a routine that works for you as often as possible.

Cleaning Your Aligners

In order to keep your aligners clean and clear, you need to take good care of them. That starts with using a soft-bristled toothbrush and clear, mild dishwashing liquid to clean them after each meal and extends to rinsing them with clean, cool water each time you take them off. Outside of that, just take good care of your smile and soak them occasionally in the cleaning crystals or a clear aligner solution!

Eating & Drinking

Good news: you don’t need to completely alter your diet with Invisalign. In fact, since the aligners are removable, you can continue to eat all of your favorites throughout the teeth-straightening process. Just do your best to fill your plate with foods that benefit your oral health instead of harming it. After all, even a small cavity can bring your orthodontic treatment to a temporary stop.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

Although losing or damaging a tray can lead to considerable treatment plan delays, do your best not to panic. Instead, call us. Once we know a little more about what happened and have a chance to see where you are in the teeth-straightening process, we can determine what to do next whether it’s moving onto the next set in the series a little early or wearing your previous one while we have a replacement made.

Routine Check-Ins

The progress visits usually happen every 6-8 weeks, and each and every one is important. Why? Because these visits allow us to continuously monitor the movement of your teeth. If something is wrong, we can quickly intervene with the necessary care. If everything looks good, we can provide you with your next set of aligners, schedule your next visit, and send you on your way!

Benefits of Invisalign

Oftentimes, patients are initially interested in Invisalign because it uses clear, discreet aligners instead of noticeable, metal brackets. The reality is that this is just one of the many reasons this orthodontic treatment is so popular. Here are a few noteworthy benefits to consider if you’re currently deciding which one is best for you.

The Average Treatment Timeline Is 12-18 Months

While traditional braces usually take 2-3 years, Invisalign delivers results in an average of just 12-18 months. Not only that, but the aligners are clear and removable. So, you’ll have an unobstructed view of your teeth as they begin to move into their properly aligned positions!

There Aren’t Monthly Adjustment Appointments

To ensure gentle, continuous pressure is placed on the teeth, patients with traditional braces need to attend monthly adjustment appointments. Although important, they can be inconvenient and time consuming. Fortunately, Invisalign is a bit different: the progress visits are every 6-8 weeks instead!

Keeping Your Smile Healthy Is Easy

Typically, patients with traditional braces need to buy special oral hygiene products, like interproximal toothbrushes, to properly clean around the brackets and wires. As you can imagine, this can be quite time-consuming. The good news is that Invisalign aligners are removable, so you don’t need to make any changes to your dental care regimen (as long as you’re brushing and flossing consistently, of course).

There Aren’t Any Dietary Restrictions

Another considerable perk: you don’t need to abide by a long list of dietary restrictions with Invisalign. Instead, simply do your best to eat healthy, well-balanced meals so your teeth and gums stay healthy throughout the teeth-straightening process. If you have attachments, then exercise caution with crunchy foods, like hard pretzels, as well.

The Aligners Are Basically Invisible

Of course, one of the biggest perks of Invisalign is that the aligners are basically invisible. As a result, your braces won’t be the first thing others notice when you smile. In fact, even the people you see daily, like your family and coworkers, may have a hard time telling when you’re wearing your trays!

There Aren’t Any Metal Components

Not only are metal-free braces more aesthetically pleasing, but they also are considerably more comfortable. So, if you haven’t committed to Invisalign yet because you’re worried that straightening your teeth will hurt, don’t worry you won’t have to deal with mouth sores or soft tissue irritation. Plus, taking OTC pain medication as directed, eating soft foods, and sipping on cool water will help alleviate any soreness or pressure that does surface from the movement of your teeth.

Factors that Affect the Cost of Invisalign

In broad terms, the cost of your Invisalign treatment is going to depend on the severity of your misalignment. More specifically, it will fluctuate based on factors such as:

  • How many trays you’ll need. Most patients treat both their upper and lower arches of teeth, but if you only need to correct one arch, your treatment could be less expensive.
  • Whether you’ll need extra accessories . Some patients have more complex orthodontic issues that require more tray customization. Elastics and other attachments would be considered an extra accessory.
  • The length of your treatment. Shorter treatment times generally mean fewer trays and fewer costs so be sure to wear your Invisalign as instructed!

Invisalign VS Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

If you’ve been searching the web to find the most cost-effective orthodontic treatment for your smile, you’ve likely seen some mail-in or DIY options. These treatments are usually cheaper at least upfront. However, since a qualified professional is not overseeing the orthodontic process, many consumers and DIYers end up paying more money to correct a botched treatment. As the saying goes, you get what you pay for !

Invisalign FAQs

Invisalign in Parma Heights is a discreet, convenient alternative to brackets and wires. Despite the advantages, it’s normal to have a few concerns before you’re ready to agree to the treatment. Don’t worry, we will explain everything during your consultation to calm your nerves. In the meantime, here are the answers to the most frequent questions we are asked about Invisalign.

What does Invisalign look like?

Invisalign is completely metal-free. Instead, a series of clear aligners are designed using an impression of your mouth. While you can see the aligners when they are in your hand, they are difficult to detect when worn over your teeth. With the right maintenance and care, your aligners will stay transparent to enjoy a nearly invisible treatment.

How long does Invisalign take?

No two mouths are identical, so there isn’t any way to determine how long you’ll need to wear aligners without first creating your treatment plan. On average, patients are finished with their series in about 12 to 18 months, however, the duration of your treatment depends on various factors, like the complexity of your case.

After examining your mouth and mapping the movements of your teeth, your cosmetic dentist in Parma Heights will explain the length of your journey. You can ensure there aren’t any delays by wearing your aligners for at least 22 hours daily and switching to the next one in the series on time. Your dentist will monitor your treatment progression to achieve a straight, healthy smile quickly.

Does Invisalign hurt?

As with any orthodontic treatment, you can expect mild discomfort when getting your new aligners. They may feel tight, but that’s a sign that they are doing their job. After a few days, your discomfort will improve; however, it can return briefly when switching to a new aligner, which will happen less often the further into your treatment. Here are some tips for handling the discomfort:

Your aligners will be smooth and sleek for a comfortable fit. In no time at all, you may forget you’re even wearing them!

  • You can manage any tenderness using an OTC pain reliever.
  • Applying a cold compress to the outside of your mouth can temporarily numb the area. Your dentist will recommend softer foods for a few days until any discomfort subsides.
  • You won’t have to worry about the aligners irritating your gums because they are designed to fit your teeth like a glove.
  • You also won’t have to deal with brackets or wires poking the inside of your mouth.

Is Invisalign cheaper than braces?

Believe it or not, both treatments are about the same because various factors affect the cost, like the number of arches being treated and the severity of your alignment issues. Not to mention, many dental insurance plans offer coverage for orthodontics, including clear aligners. After meeting your deductible, your benefits may pay a set portion of the cost to lower your out-of-pocket expenses. Our office offers multiple financial solutions for any remaining balance, like third-party financing. We will help you invest in the straight smile you deserve while staying within your budget.
