Allen Skibba, DDS

GENERAL DENTISTRY

As a member of the Navy’s Dental Corps, Dr. Skibba worked with a group of dentists and specialists to provide comprehensive care. It was this group dynamic that led him to DHA, where he finds a collaborative environment in DHA’s group practice as the best approach for providing excellent care to all patients.

Dr. Skibba and his wife have four children, including twins. They stay active traveling from Arizona to Boston to Minnesota as all their children live and work out of state. They enjoy family vacations, attending professional sporting events and participating in activities like tennis, golf, hiking, and pickle ball. Dr. Skibba also enjoys running and biking.
