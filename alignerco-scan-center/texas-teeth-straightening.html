What ALIGNERCO Clear Aligners Can Help Correct

Crowding

When there isn’t enough space, teeth overlap or twist out of place.

Overbite

Upper front teeth sit too far over the lower teeth when you bite down.

Underbite

Lower teeth sit in front of upper teeth, which can impact bite balance and appearance.

Crossbite

Some upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth instead of outside, often on one side.

Open bite

Spacing / Gaps

When teeth sit too far apart, creating visible gaps that can affect smile balance

Deep bite

A deeper-than-normal overbite where upper teeth cover too much of the lower teeth

Midline shift

The center of your top and bottom teeth doesn’t line up as it should

Edge-to-edge

When the top and bottom front teeth meet directly, instead of overlapping.
