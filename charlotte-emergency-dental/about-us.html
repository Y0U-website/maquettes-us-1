Emergency FAQ

Do I have to have an emergency to visit?

Not at all. Charlotte Emergency Dental offers comprehensive dental care for both urgent concerns and everyday needs, including implants, dentures, crowns, and fillings.

How can I pay for my treatment?

We have a variety of payment plans available to help make your treatment as affordable as possible.

Do you perform oral surgery?

No. We do not have oral surgeons on staff, however, our general dentists will extract most teeth based on diagnosis. All of our dentists are licensed in the state of North Carolina. Can I have a tooth extracted on my first consultation? At Charlotte Emergency Dental, it is our mission to provide same day treatment whenever possible. There are certain situations in which delayed treatment or referral may be necessary.

Do you accept insurance?

While we are not in-network with dental insurance, our team provides all the required documentation to help patients maximize their insurance reimbursement.
