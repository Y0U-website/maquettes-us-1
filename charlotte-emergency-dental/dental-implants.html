Understanding Dental Implants

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone that serves as the foundation for a replacement tooth. Once it bonds with the bone, a custom crown is attached to restore function and aesthetics.

• Bone loss • Shifting of surrounding teeth • Difficulty chewing • Changes in facial structure • Reliance on removable dentures

For many patients, an implant is the most durable and comfortable replacement option available.

Who Is a Good Candidate

Most healthy adults with sufficient bone structure can receive implants. You may be a good candidate if you experience:

• A missing tooth • A tooth that cannot be saved • Loose or uncomfortable dentures • Difficulty chewing • Changes in bite or speech after tooth loss

If bone loss has already occurred, additional procedures such as grafting may be recommended to support a stable implant.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

We believe that clear expectations lead to better outcomes and a calmer experience. Here is what your implant journey typically includes.

Step 1. Consultation and Imaging

We evaluate your jawbone, gum health, bite, and overall readiness for implants using detailed imaging.

Step 2. Implant Placement

A titanium post is placed into the jawbone in a safe, controlled procedure. Your comfort is carefully managed throughout the appointment.

Step 3. Healing and Integration

The implant fuses with the bone. This creates a secure foundation for the final restoration.

Step 4. Custom Crown Attachment

A natural looking crown is crafted to match your smile and is attached once healing is complete.

Step 5. Long Term Maintenance

Regular checkups help ensure the implant remains strong and healthy for many years.
