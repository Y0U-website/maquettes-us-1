🔗 What Are Dental Bridges?

A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth by “bridging” the gap. It consists of one or more artificial teeth (pontics) supported by crowns on neighboring teeth or dental implants.

Benefits of Dental Bridges:

  • Restore chewing and speaking ability
  • Enhance the appearance of your smile
  • Prevent adjacent teeth from shifting
  • Support facial structure and jaw health

🛠️ The Crown & Bridge Process

Consultation & Evaluation

Dr. Xia will perform a detailed exam using digital imaging and 3D scanning to assess your oral health and determine the ideal treatment for your unique needs.

Tooth Preparation & Impressions

We carefully shape the affected tooth or teeth and take digital impressions to ensure your crown or bridge fits with precision. You’ll wear a temporary restoration while your custom crown or bridge is being crafted by our lab.

Final Placement

Once your permanent restoration is ready, we’ll place it using strong dental cement or secure it over an implant. Dr. Xia will make precise adjustments to ensure the perfect fit, bite, and comfort.

Aftercare & Support

We’ll provide you with detailed care instructions and schedule follow-up visits to monitor the health and integrity of your crown or bridge.
