FAQs About Dental Implants

Our dentists in Hamilton, MT answer the important questions about implant dentistry.

  • Are dental implants expensive? Dental implants do have a higher cost compared to other solutions. However, since dental implants are such a long-lasting solution, in terms of long-range costs and function, they are a great cost-effective choice.
  • Am I a good candidate for dental implants? Almost anyone who has missing teeth is a candidate for dental implants. However, our dentists in Hamilton, MT Learn more about our two dentists , will need to properly examine you first to assess if dental implants are the right treatment for you and your needs.
  • How long do implant last? Dental implants are a permanent solution to replace your missing teeth. Plus, unlike natural teeth, dental implants are not vulnerable to decay. Nonetheless, a good oral hygiene routine, regular check-ups, and professional cleanings are important for long-lasting implants.
  • Do dental implants hurt? We work hard to make your implant surgeries as pain-free as possible. We provide sedation therapy, so you can enjoy your treatment while you are in a state of relaxed consciousness. After dental implant surgery, your discomfort will be minimal. Any pain can be cared for with over-the-counter medication.
  • Can I remove my dental implants on a daily basis? No. Dental implants are fixed into your jawbone and mimic your natural tooth roots. This allows your new restoration to enjoy a more stable foundation.
