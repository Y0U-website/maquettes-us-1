Dr Simmons is passionate about dentistry, values his patients and finds satisfaction helping improve one smile at at a time. His main focus is to provide compassionate care to improve day to day lives while enjoying changing how people perceive dentistry. All while enjoying watching his team excel in their life endeavors.

Dr Simmons has provided nearly 20 years of service at the Trapper Creek Job Corps This link opens a new tab to the Trapper Creek Job Corps website restoring simple to very complex cases of rampant caries and many cases of methamphetamine abuse and neglect (meth mouth).

Outside the office Dr Simmons enjoys time on his farm, white water rafting, kayaking, snow skiing, water skiing, scuba diving, flying, motorcycling, 4-wheeling, spelunking, climbing and hiking and spent many years in triathlons and obstacle course racing. But most of all he enjoys doing everything with his 3 amazing sons whom he loves passionately. And considers fatherhood his greatest accomplishment.
