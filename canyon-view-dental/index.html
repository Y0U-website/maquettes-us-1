Are you missing a tooth due to an accident, decay, or premature tooth loss?

Losing a tooth can make it difficult for you to speak clearly, chew your food properly, or smile with confidence. As your Hamilton restorative dentists Learn more about our dentists , we have the best solution for you: Dental implants.

Dental implants are a permanent and long-lasting solution made to look and feel just like your natural teeth.

Made of small titanium posts, they are directly placed into the jawbone where a tooth or teeth are missing. Your bone will then bond with the titanium, creating a strong foundation for your artificial tooth restoration.
