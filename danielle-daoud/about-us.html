Who We Are

Lakewood Smiles has been a part of our great community for over 30 years. A Message from Our Dentists

Dr. Danielle M. Daoud

A Midwesterner born and raised, she completed her Doctorate degree in Dental Medicine at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, OH. Upon graduation, she decided to continue her education and completed a residency at the Montefiore Medical Center in New York.

Dr. Daoud enjoys all aspects of general dentistry, and believes there is always something new to learn in the field. She is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Greater Cleveland Dental Society . She also attends continuing education courses annually to stay at the forefront of the profession. She values treating each patient with care and compassion while creating only the most comfortable environment for her patients.

When Dr. Daoud isn’t in the office, she enjoys being in the company of her friends and family, traveling with her husband, and being outdoors.

Dr. Oliver Sun

He moved to the United States to attend dental school in Cleveland, OH. He graduated 2nd in the class from the Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine in 2015. It was in dental school that he met his wife, a dentist and a native of Westlake, OH.

After practicing general dentistry for three years on the East Coast, and completing a six month mini-residency for esthetics and implant dentistry, Dr. Sun and Dr. Daoud moved back home, closer to friends and family in Ohio.

Dr. Sun is proficient in several areas of dentistry, such as cosmetic restorations, fillings, veneers, crowns, and bridges. Dr. Sun’s extensive training allows him to provide more complex procedures to our patients, such as root canals, oral surgery procedures, bone augmentation, gum surgery, Invisalign, as well as the surgical placement of implants and implant restorations.

Dina is an adjunct faculty instructor at the dental hygiene program at LCCC. Outside the office, Dina enjoys spending time with her little family and working at her little wood-shop. She loves to travel, explore different places, and try new restaurants.
