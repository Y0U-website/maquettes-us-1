Dental Financing FAQs

Do I need good credit to qualify?

Not always. Some options, like Cherry, offer approvals without a hard credit check. CareCredit may require a stronger credit profile. Our team can help you choose the best option based on your situation.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Cherry uses a soft credit check, which does not affect your score. CareCredit may use a hard credit inquiry, which can have a small, temporary impact.

Can I pay off early?

Yes. Both Cherry and CareCredit allow early payoff. There are no penalties for paying off your balance ahead of schedule.

Which options is better for me?

It depends on your needs. Cherry is great for fast approval and smaller monthly plans. CareCredit works well for larger treatments and longer-term financing. If you’re unsure, our team can guide you.

Can I use financing for any treament?

In most cases, yes. Financing can be used for preventive, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. This includes cleanings, fillings, crowns, Invisalign, and more.

How long does approval take?

Approval is usually fast. Many patients receive a decision within minutes after applying online.

What payment options are available?

You can choose from a range of monthly payment plans depending on the provider and your approval terms. This allows you to fit treatment into your budget.

Do I have to apply online?

Yes, applications are completed online through secure portals for both Cherry and CareCredit. If you need help, our team can walk you through it.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Both Cherry and CareCredit use secure, encrypted systems to protect your personal and financial information.

What if I have questions before applying?

You can call our office anytime. We’ll explain your options and help you feel confident before moving forward.

Not Sure Which Option Is Right?

Our team can help you choose the best financing option based on your treatment and budget.
