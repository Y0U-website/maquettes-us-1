Frequently Asked Questions About Our Dental Services

What Are Dental Crowns and When Do You Need One?

A dental crown is a custom-fitted cap placed over a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, strength, and function. Carrier Dentistry in Grand Prairie recommends crowns for teeth with large cavities, cracks, or after root canal therapy . Crowns are made from porcelain, ceramic, or metal alloy and typically require two visits to complete. Call 972-642-2423 to schedule a crown consultation.

Does Carrier Dentistry Offer Dentures?

Carrier Dentistry provides both full and partial dentures for patients missing multiple teeth. Traditional dentures require several weeks of healing and custom adjustments for the best fit. For patients considering a permanent solution, dental implants can serve as anchors for implant-supported dentures. Call 972-642-2423 to discuss your options.

What Are Dental Sealants and Who Needs Them?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth (molars) to prevent cavities. At Carrier Dentistry in Grand Prairie, TX, sealants are recommended for children once their permanent molars come in, usually between ages 6 and 12. Adults with cavity-prone molars also benefit from sealants. The procedure takes minutes, requires no drilling, and provides years of protection against decay. Sealants are part of our pediatric dentistry and preventive care programs.

How Often Should You Get a Professional Dental Cleaning?

The American Dental Association recommends a dental exam and professional teeth cleaning every six months. During a cleaning at Carrier Dentistry, our hygienists remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing miss, particularly along the gumline and between teeth. Patients with gum disease may need cleanings every three to four months. Regular exams and cleanings lower your risk of cavities, gum disease, and tooth loss. Deep cleanings target bacteria below the gumline and differ from the routine cleanings most patients receive every six months .

Are Fluoride Treatments Necessary for Adults?

Fluoride treatments are beneficial for both children and adults. Professional fluoride applied at Carrier Dentistry strengthens enamel and makes teeth more resistant to acid attacks from bacteria. Adults with dry mouth, a history of cavities, or gum recession are especially good candidates. The treatment takes just a few minutes during your regular dental visit and provides lasting protection against decay.

What Is a Dental Bridge and How Does It Replace Missing Teeth?

A dental bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth. It consists of an artificial tooth (pontic) held in place by crowns attached to the teeth on either side of the gap. Bridges restore your ability to chew and speak normally and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting out of position. Carrier Dentistry in Grand Prairie offers both traditional and implant-supported bridges. For patients missing several teeth, dental implants or dentures may be a better fit.

What Is Dental Bonding and How Long Does It Last?

Dental bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair chips, cracks, gaps, or discoloration in a single visit. At Carrier Dentistry, bonding typically lasts 5 to 10 years with proper care and is one of the most affordable cosmetic dental treatments. For more extensive changes in tooth shape or color, dental veneers provide a longer-lasting alternative.

Does Carrier Dentistry Use Tooth-Colored Fillings?

Yes. Carrier Dentistry in Grand Prairie uses tooth-colored composite fillings that repair cavities while matching the natural appearance of your teeth. Composite fillings bond directly to the tooth, preserving more natural structure than traditional metal fillings. Most dental insurance plans cover fillings as a basic restorative procedure.
