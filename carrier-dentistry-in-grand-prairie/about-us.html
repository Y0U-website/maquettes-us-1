ABOUT CARRIER DENTISTRY

At Carrier Dentistry , we built our practice around one belief: dentistry should feel clear, calm, and honest. Serving Grand Prairie families for years, our sister-and-brother dentist team brings 40 years of combined experience and a conservative philosophy that protects your long-term oral health, not just today’s symptoms.

Dr. Hien To

Dr. Hien To is a trusted dentist at Carrier Dentistry in Grand Prairie, bringing more than 20 years of experience to the families she serves. A 2001 graduate of the LSU School of Dentistry, she now helps lead three North Texas practices, combining technical skill with a calm, patient-first approach.

Dr. To has cared for the Grand Prairie community since 2005 and is known for her precise work, gentle manner, and strong focus on long-term oral health. Her partnership with her brother, Dr. Kenneth To, also extends her care to the Duncanville area. She provides treatment in implants, emergency dentistry, and dental splints, and continues advanced training in implant dentistry, Botox, and modern restorative techniques. She is also an active member of the American Dental Association.

Outside of dentistry, she volunteers with ADX’s dance studio and the Chey-Anne drill team at South Grand Prairie High School. She also enjoys dabbling in craft projects in her free time.

At Carrier Dentistry, Dr. To is dedicated to giving patients a comfortable, personalized experience from the moment they walk in. She looks forward to welcoming new patients and helping the Grand Prairie community maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Kenneth To

Dr. Kenneth To has been caring for Grand Prairie families for over a decade as co-owner of Carrier Dentistry. He earned his DDS from LSU School of Dentistry in 2008 and has always believed dental care should feel simple and honest.

He focuses on prevention and early treatment. If something is small, he handles it before it becomes big. He takes time to explain what he sees using digital X-rays and 3D imaging, so you understand your options and never feel rushed.

Dr. To has experience in dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, and emergency care. Whether you need to fix a problem or improve your smile, his goal is care that feels comfortable and lasts.

Outside the office, he stays involved in the Grand Prairie community through youth programs, church, and family life. At Carrier Dentistry, he treats patients the way he would want his own family treated.

Dr. Kolli

Dr. Kolli is a traveling general dentist dedicated to providing exceptional surgical dental care while helping patients and their families feel comfortable, informed, and confident every step of the way. She partners with dental practices across the Dallas–Fort Worth area to offer specialized surgical services, including wisdom tooth extractions and other oral surgical procedures, allowing patients to receive expert care in the familiar environment of their trusted dental office.

Understanding that dental surgery can often feel overwhelming, Dr. Kolli is known for her gentle approach, compassionate nature, and calming chairside manner. She believes that every patient deserves to feel heard, respected, and fully informed before any treatment begins. By taking the time to answer questions, address concerns, and build lasting trust, she creates an experience that is as reassuring as it is clinically excellent.

Whether performing a routine extraction or managing a more complex surgical case, Dr. Kolli is committed to delivering safe, precise, and personalized care tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

A graduate of the New York University College of Dentistry, Dr. Kolli earned her dental degree in 2010 and has been serving the Dallas–Fort Worth community ever since. With a primary focus on surgical dentistry, she has built a reputation for clinical excellence, compassionate patient care, and a commitment to making oral surgery a positive and comfortable experience for patients of all ages.

For Dr. Kolli, every procedure is more than just treatment—it’s an opportunity to provide comfort, restore confidence, and help patients achieve better oral health with care they can trust.
