The Process: Your Journey to a New Smile

Getting an implant supported bridge is a process that occurs over several months, allowing for proper healing. Here’s what you can expect when you choose Sunflower Dental Spa:

  • Comprehensive Consultation: Your journey begins with a detailed examination. We use advanced 3D imaging to assess your jawbone density and precisely plan the implant placement. This is when we’ll determine if you are a good candidate and create your personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: During a comfortable procedure, the small, titanium implant posts are surgically placed into your jawbone. We ensure you are completely numb and comfortable throughout.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: This is the most crucial phase. Over the next 3-6 months, the implants will fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a rock-solid foundation for your new teeth.
  • Abutment Placement: Once healed, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to each implant. This is what will ultimately hold your bridge securely.
  • Your Custom Bridge : We take precise impressions of your mouth to craft your final bridge. We pay meticulous attention to the shade, shape, and size of the teeth to ensure your new smile is both functional and beautifully natural.
  • Bridge Attachment: Finally, your custom-crafted implant supported bridge is securely attached to the abutments. We make any minor adjustments needed to ensure a perfect, comfortable fit.

Implant Supported Bridge Aftercare and Maintenance

Your new implant supported bridge is designed to last for decades. Proper care is essential for its longevity. Here’s how to keep your smile healthy and functional:

By following these simple steps, you can protect your investment and enjoy your beautiful, functional smile for years to come.

  • Brush Twice Daily: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush to gently clean around the bridge and remaining natural teeth.
  • Floss Under the Bridge: Use specialized tools like floss threaders, superfloss, or a water flosser to clean meticulously under the prosthetic teeth every day.
  • Focus on the Gum Line: Pay special attention to the area where the bridge meets your gums to prevent plaque buildup.
  • Attend Regular Check-ups: Visit Sunflower Dental Spa every six months for professional cleanings and examinations.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: Refrain from chewing on extremely hard items like ice, hard candy, or pens to prevent damage.
  • Consider an Antibacterial Mouthwash: Use an alcohol-free, antibacterial mouthwash to reduce bacteria.
