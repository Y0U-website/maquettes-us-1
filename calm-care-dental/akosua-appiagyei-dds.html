Meet Dr. Akosua Appiagyei

Trusted Family Dentist in Monroe, MI

Dr. Appiagyei grew up in Accra, Ghana (West Africa) and moved to Baltimore, Maryland to pursue further education and to fulfill her dream of becoming a dentist. She then moved to Michigan with her family and is continuously looking for recommendations on ways to explore and enjoy the true meaning of Pure Michigan. She’s still adjusting to the snow!

Dr. Appiagyei enjoys learning about new cultures and loves to travel to experience new places and their food. Share your favorite restaurant or recipe with her and she’ll try it if she can! She also enjoys learning new languages and loves to get to know her patients. Otherwise, you may find her catching up on her favorite T.V shows or listening to a podcast ask her about Survivor or Chopped on the food network. Dr. Appiagyei and her team are excited and looking forward to meeting you and sharing a calming dental experience with you.
