About Us

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality dentistry for infants, children and adolescents in a child-friendly, safe, state-of-the-art environment. We focus on caring for each child like they are our own, implementing prevention and education for parents and children. Here at Bright Starr Pediatric Dentistry, we strive for all visits to be a healthy and nurturing experience in a welcoming atmosphere.

Our Staff

Our friendly staff and fun and age-appropriate toys, educational books about teeth and kid friendly tv shows/movies in our waiting area and treatment rooms allow children of any age to feel comfortable and safe. If You have any questions or concerns our staff will always make sure to answer those.

Our Location

Located at Vista Gardens Marketplace, our pediatric dental office is convenient for all families living in many communities. Dr. Thrower and her staff are dedicated to making your child’s dental visit as enjoyable as possible and we look forward to helping your child have a bright and healthy smile!
