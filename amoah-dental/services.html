Restorative dentistry

Preventative dentistry

Prevent problems before they start with routine visits and proactive guidance.

  • Preventive cleanings : Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar to keep gums healthy and smiles fresh.
  • Comprehensive exams : Thorough exams and x-rays help detect concerns early and guide proactive care.
  • Custom mouthguards : Custom 3D-printed mouthguards are created from digital scans for a precise fit.
  • Oral cancer screenings : Quick, routine screenings add peace of mind and support early detection.
