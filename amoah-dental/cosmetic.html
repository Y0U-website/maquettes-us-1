Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile with Confidence

Your smile is one of your most powerful features—and if you’re not completely happy with it, cosmetic dentistry can help. At Amoah Dental, we offer a full range of cosmetic treatments to improve the appearance of your teeth and help you smile with confidence.

Personalized treatments for a natural-looking smile.

Explore treatments designed to enhance the color, shape, and balance of your smile.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile safely and effectively with professional whitening options. Choose in-office treatment for instant results or a take-home kit customized for your smile.

Invisalign® & SureSmile® Clear Aligners

Straighten teeth without metal braces. We offer two discreet and comfortable systems to suit your needs and budget. Both options use clear trays to gradually align your smile, with minimal disruption to your daily routine.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells custom-made to improve the color, shape, and symmetry of your teeth. An excellent option for chipped, discolored, or slightly misaligned teeth.

Gum Contouring

Using advanced laser technology, we can gently reshape your gumline for better balance and aesthetics—no scalpels or stitches required.

Smile Design

Looking for a complete smile makeover? We’ll create a customized treatment plan tailored to your goals, facial structure, and bite, combining artistry with precision for transformative results.

Comfortable, precise cosmetic care.

What sets our cosmetic dentistry apart is our use of modern tools that improve comfort, efficiency, and accuracy. Our 3D scanners enable us to design clear aligners and veneers with incredible precision—no messy impressions needed. With in-house technology like the Solea laser, many soft-tissue adjustments can be made painlessly and without downtime.
