Meet Dr. Alex L. Givens, DDS

Dr. Alex Givens completed his Bachelor of Science in 2013 and Doctor of Dental Surgery Degrees from the University of Detroit-Mercy in 2015 as a member of the 7 year BS/DDS program. Since graduating, Dr. Alex has attended over 850 hours of continued education focusing on communication, complex treatment planning, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, traditional and aligner orthodontics, and implementing technology in dentistry .

When Dr. Alex is not doing dentistry, he usually is giving back to his professional and local communities.

Dr. Alex served as a Board Member of the Michigan Dental Association Foundation where he revamped their Scholarship Program to improve the impact it had to help young fellow dental professionals.

He has been actively involved in the Frankenmuth Junior Chamber, where he was a key member to raise $1 Million and built the Frankenmuth Jaycee Spray Park. He is also involved with their World Expo of Beer project that is one of the largest beer festivals in Michigan. All proceeds from this event are donated to local charities and organizations .

Dr. Alex enjoys spending the rest of his time with his wife, Tianna, and their three children. He is an avid outdoorsman, car enthusiast, and foodie.

Core Values

Fenton Smiles was built on eight core values, and we are committed to both our patients and the community. These core values are what we promise our patients, and what we practice each day.

Personal Accountability: Fenton Smiles takes ownership and accountability with everything we do, and we value our work. This increases confidence, and helps our team to provide exceptional care.

Customer Commitment: Our commitment is to make each patient’s experience personal, and tailored to their individual needs. Our practice is always implementing new techniques to improve the patient experience, and build upon what we already have in place.

Cutting Edge Technology: Fenton Smiles provides patients with state of the art technology to create customized treatment plans and exceptional results. We are always expanding on our current technology an introducing new tools and techniques to our practice.

Gratitude: We are thankful for our patients, and always treat our patients with kindness and respect.

Pursue Growth and Learning: Our practice works to consistently educate our doctor and staff, and grow with continued education. This allows our practice to keep up with the latest technology available in the dental industry, and gives us the ability to always provide patients with exceptional dental care.

Be Resourceful and Help Each Other: Our team works together to support one another, and we treat our patients with the utmost respect. Our staff works above and beyond to give patients the very best experience.

Funergetic: Our team spreads both happiness and love to everyone who visits our office. Supporting one another and our patients to give them superior care is what we work to do each day.

Intentional: We provide the best care for our patients and with purpose. Our goal is to give patients a quality experience at Fenton Smiles, and we want our patients to feel comfortable and welcome every time they visit our office.
