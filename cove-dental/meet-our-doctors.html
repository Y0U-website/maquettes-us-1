Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Dhaval Thakkar

Dr. Thakkar’s journey in dentistry began with a solid foundation in Computer Science, earning his bachelor’s degree in 2005 from Chicago, Illinois. After moving to Boston, he attended Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he further honed his passion for healthcare. In his senior year, Dr. Thakkar had the privilege of serving veterans at the VA Hospital in Togus, Maine—a rewarding experience that deepened his commitment to patient care.

Upon graduating from dental school in 2009, Dr. Thakkar dedicated two years to providing dental care to undeserved communities in South Chicago. In 2011, he made the move to Texas, captivated by the warm weather and welcoming spirit of the people here, where he has continued his practice ever since.

With over fifteen years of experience, Dr. Thakkar is deeply committed to delivering the highest standard of care.
