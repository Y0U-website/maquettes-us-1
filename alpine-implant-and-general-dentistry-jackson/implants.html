Who is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with good oral health and sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. If you have missing teeth, ill-fitting dentures, or a failing bridge, an implant dentist near you can assess your condition and determine the best treatment plan.

Dental Implants vs. Dental Bridges – Which is Right for You?

Both dental implants and dental bridges can restore missing teeth, but they serve different purposes.

Dental Implants

Surgically placed in the jawbone, offering a permanent solution with no impact on adjacent teeth.

Dental Bridges

Supported by surrounding teeth, which may require filing down healthy enamel.

Implants are often the preferred choice due to their longevity and natural feel, but a consultation with a dental implants dentist will help determine the best option for you.

Dental Implants & Dentures – A Perfect Combination

If you’re missing most or all of your teeth, dental implants and dentures can be combined for a more secure fit. Implant-supported dentures snap onto strategically placed implants, eliminating the need for adhesives and reducing discomfort. This option provides greater stability than traditional dentures.
