Meet Dr. Foleck Dr. Adam S. Foleck, DMD Dr. Adam Foleck , a graduate of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and Temple University School of Dentistry (1997), has extensive post-graduate training in dental implants and full mouth rehabilitation. Dr. Foleck is an ABOI Diplomate in North America. That means he’s passed the most rigorous board certification process in implant dentistry.…

Since 2007, he’s lectured internationally on dental implants, grafting, and CAD/CAM surgical integration. In 2023, he authored a chapter on ‘Implants in the Esthetic Zone’ in the top-selling textbook, Modern Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Foleck combines the latest dental technology with his expertise to provide exceptional care, specializing in esthetic restorations and mentoring other dentists in implant dentistry. When you’re trusting someone with a procedure that affects your health and confidence for decades, credentials matter.
