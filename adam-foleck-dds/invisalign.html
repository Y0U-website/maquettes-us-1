Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on your specific case, but most patients see results in 6–18 months. Consistently wearing your aligners for 20–22 hours a day helps keep your treatment on track.

How frequently will I get a new aligner?

You’ll typically switch to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks, depending on your treatment plan. Each new set brings you one step closer to your perfect smile.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign ® ?

You’ll remove your aligners when eating or drinking anything other than water. This means you can enjoy all your favorite foods without the restrictions that come with traditional braces.

Will I need a retainer after Invisalign?

Yes. Wearing a retainer after Invisalign ® is essential to keep your teeth in their new position. Your dentist will create a customized retainer and guide you on how often to wear it to protect your results.

How much does Invisalign ® cost?

The cost depends on the complexity of your case and treatment length. During your consultation, we’ll provide a personalized estimate and review payment plan options to make treatment affordable.

Explore our financing options and important information about insurance.

Is Invisalign ® covered by insurance?

Many dental insurance plans offer coverage for Invisalign ® just like they would for traditional braces. Our team can review your plan and help maximize any benefits to make treatment more affordable.

FASTBRACES ® look like traditional braces but work much faster, with some cases seeing results in as little as 60-90 days.
