Preventing Bone Loss

Types of Dental Implants

Endosteal Dental Implants

Endosteal Dental Implants in Hampton Roads – What You Need to Know

When it comes to permanent tooth replacement options, endosteal dental implants are the most commonly recommended solution. These implants involve a titanium post surgically placed directly into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root to support a crown, bridge, or denture. This type of implant is ideal for patients with adequate bone density and healthy gums.

Endosteal implants (also called in-bone implants) are a trusted, long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. Because they are embedded into the bone, they offer superior stability, improved bite function, and a natural appearance—making them a preferred choice at many advanced implant dentistry centers in Virginia.

Key benefits of endosteal implants include:

  • Strong integration with the jawbone (osseointegration)
  • Long-lasting durability and stability
  • Compatibility with single tooth crowns or full-arch restorations
  • Preservation of bone structure and facial shape

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Single-Tooth Dental Implants – A Permanent Tooth Replacement Solution

A single-tooth dental implant is a highly effective, long-lasting solution for replacing a missing or severely damaged tooth. This advanced procedure involves surgically placing a titanium dental implant post into the jawbone, which functions as an artificial root. Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone through a process called osseointegration, it becomes the stable foundation for a custom dental crown that mimics the look, feel, and function of a natural tooth.

A single-tooth implant replaces both the root and the crown of a missing tooth, offering a permanent and aesthetic restoration. It prevents surrounding teeth from shifting, helps maintain jawbone density, and supports proper bite alignment.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Implant-Supported Bridges in Hampton Roads, VA – Replace Multiple Missing Teeth with Stability and Strength

Implant-supported bridges are a modern, highly effective solution for replacing multiple missing teeth in a row. Unlike traditional bridges that rely on natural teeth for support, implant-supported bridges are anchored directly to the jawbone using dental implants, creating a more secure, durable, and long-lasting restoration.

An implant-supported bridge consists of two or more artificial teeth (pontics) connected together and supported by dental implant posts rather than adjacent natural teeth. The number of implants required will vary depending on the size and location of the bridge, but typically two implants can support a bridge of three or more teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-Supported Dentures in Hampton Roads, VA – A Secure, Modern Alternative to Traditional Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer a secure, stable solution for patients who are missing most or all of their teeth. Unlike conventional dentures that rely on suction or adhesives, these advanced dentures are anchored directly to the jaw using dental implants, providing a snug fit, improved function, and long-term oral health benefits.

Implant-supported dentures are removable or fixed prosthetic appliances that attach to two or more dental implants placed in the jawbone. These implants act as artificial tooth roots, giving the denture superior retention, stability, and comfort compared to traditional full or partial dentures.
