In-House Dental Savings Program

Our in-house dental savings program is designed to provide affordability and greater access to quality dental care. We want your family to have access to quality dental care and want to make your experience with us as pleasant as possible. Offering coverage options for one person or the entire family at two different levels, allows flexibility for your and your family.

The Brookhaven Dental Center’s In-House Dental Savings Program Covers:

  • Comprehensive exams for new patients
  • 2 routine examinations per year
  • 1 set of Bitewing x-rays per year
  • Complete series or Panorex (every 3 years)
  • Periapical x-rays
  • 2 preventive dental cleanings per year
  • 2 fluoride treatments per year
  • 2 oral cancer screenings per year
