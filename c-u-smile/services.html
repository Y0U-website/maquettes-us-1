0 Our Services With a variety of treatments to choose from, we are sure you will be happy working with us. Cosmetic Dentistry Veneers Veneers are thin coverings placed over the front of the tooth. They are used to cover discolored teeth and close gaps between the teeth.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings are fillings that blend with the natural color of their teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a simple process which lightens the color of your teeth and makes them brighter.

Dentistry for Children

Fillings and Sealants

Dental sealants are placed on the grooves of your child’s back teeth to protect them, keep them healthy and free from pit and fissure decay for as long as possible.

Extractions and Space maintainers

When a tooth is removed prematurely a space maintainer is needed to keep the primary tooth in position until the permanent tooth erupts.

Pulpotomies and Crowns

The removal of the coronal portion of the pulp for treating primary teeth with carious pulp exposures. The stainless steel crown is an extremely durable restoration after a pulpotomy.

Dentures

Complete Dentures, Relines and Repairs

We offer Complete dentures including immediate dentures the same day that your teeth are extracted.

Fixed Bridges Another permanent solution to replace teeth is fixed bridges.

Removable Partial Dentures

We offer several types of partial dentures to replace some missing teeth such as, cast partials, flexible partials and acrylic partials.

Root Canal Treatments and Crowns

The inflamed or infected pulp is removed from the inside of the root canal, then filled and sealed. A crown is usually placed on the tooth to protect and restore it to full function.

Maintenance and Deep Cleanings

Deep cleaning or periodontal scaling and root planning, focuses on the outer surface of the roots and below your gum line. This is needed when tartar buildup has caused infection of the gums.

Extractions and Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution to replace missing teeth, they are inserted into the jaw bone, to mimic your natural teeth* and then we restore them with crowns.

Other Services

Short Term Orthodontics

This is for adults with crooked, spaced, or misaligned teeth. We use discreet clear braces and tooth-colored wires. Barely noticeable. The average time is only six months!

Nitrous Oxide Analgesia

Nitrous oxide and oxygen, or laughing gas, is a safe and effective means of managing pain and anxiety in dentistry.*

Mouth Guards

Most cases of Bruxism and Clenching can easily be treated by wearing a night guard while you sleep.
