The practice

As a dentist, I’m committed to providing high-quality, patient-focused care to individuals and families in Chatham and the surrounding communities. I take pride in helping patients achieve healthier, more confident smiles through comprehensive treatment that combines precision, advanced techniques, and a personalized approach.

My clinical focus includes cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and full-arch rehabilitation. I’m passionate about creating natural, lasting results—whether I’m restoring function, improving aesthetics, or transforming a patient’s entire smile. No two cases are the same, and I enjoy the challenge of customizing each treatment plan to deliver the best possible outcome.

What I enjoy most about dentistry is the balance of science, skill, and connection. I strive to make every visit comfortable and collaborative, and I’m dedicated to earning the trust of my patients through transparency, respect, and consistent results.

I stay current with the latest advancements in materials, digital workflows, and surgical techniques so I can offer the most modern and effective care available. Being part of the Chatham community is something I truly value, and I’m proud to serve the people who live and work here.
