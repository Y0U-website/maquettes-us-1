Dentistry Made Simple

Are you ready to schedule an appointment with Dr. Schrimper, Dr. Belton, or Dr. Perry? Before you do, we invite you to take some time to review the information on this page. Our goal is to make your first visit as smooth-running and hassle-free as possible. If you have any questions or need help accessing and filling out important documents, don’t hesitate to get ahold of our friendly team by calling our dental office.

Your First Visit

If you’re visiting us for a traditional checkup and cleaning, our team will input your information into our system and guide you back to one of our treatment rooms to take X-rays, conduct a visual examination of your teeth and gums, and do a professional cleaning. If you’re visiting us for a smile restoration or sleep apnea therapy consultation, your appointment will be a bit different. If you’d like to know what to expect, be sure to ask our friendly team member on the phone when you’re scheduling your visit.

Dental Insurance

Our team is happy to accept PPO dental insurance plans and file claims on our patient’s behalf to help maximize their benefits. Although we aren’t in-network with any specific insurance plans, we’re sure to guide our patients through the financial process of paying for treatments and breaking down each financial option available to them. If you need help navigating your dental insurance plan, click on the link below or reach out to our team.

Dental Financing

We’re happy to offer additional financing outside of dental insurance to help make our patients’ treatments more affordable and accessible. At your consultation, we’ll be more than happy to break down the cost of your procedure and what financing is available to you. If you have any concerns or questions, our team is more than happy to address them to make sure that you receive the dental care you need.
