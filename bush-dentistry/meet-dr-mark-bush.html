Beyond the Practice

I grew up in Kearney and Kim and I have lived here since 1991. We’ve raised our children and now our grandchildren in the community as well. We feel fortunate to have many members of our extended family living in the area; we love being with them.

I enjoy spending my free time in the outdoors. I also like to travel, work out, and hunt and fish.

Kim and I feel strongly about mission work and undertake missions as often as possible. We’ve traveled the globe to offer dental care and share the love of Jesus Christ in parts of Africa, Romania, Brazil, and Guatemala.

Additionally, I’ve served on the Kearney YMCA board for more than 18 years. I’ve also lead Bible studies, headed up Operation Candy Buy Back for our overseas troops for many years, and coached many youth sports.
