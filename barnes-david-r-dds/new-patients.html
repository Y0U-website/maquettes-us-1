  • Brush teeth twice a day This lowers the amount of plaque bacteria that can cause decay and gum disease.
  • Floss regularly Floss or choose another inter-dental cleaner to use between teeth daily. Also, get in the habit of using an antimicrobial mouth rinse.
  • Avoid smoking and chewing tobacco Not only do these stain teeth and cause bad breath, these habits can lead to pre-mature tooth loss. Nothing is more unattractive than spaces between teeth. Talk to Dr. Barnes about alternatives and options to quit tobacco use.
  • Protect your teeth Many teens, as well as adults, play sports, but many do not think to use a mouth guard until it is too late. Many chipped teeth, root canals, and extractions could have been prevented if a mouth guard had been used. Also, some guards can be used at night to prevent wear on teeth.
  • Avoid oral piercings and oral mouth jewelry Although some think this trend looks cool, it can have many negative oral side effects. Cracked teeth, gum recession, choking, and oral infections are just a few problems often seen with oral piercings.
