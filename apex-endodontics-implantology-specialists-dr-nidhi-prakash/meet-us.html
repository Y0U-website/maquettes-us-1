Dr. Prakash is a dedicated and passionate endodontist with 25 years of experience, committed to providing the highest quality of care in a comfortable and friendly environment. Her expertise and compassionate approach have made her a trusted name in endodontics throughout the Bay Area.

A proud graduate of Boston University, Dr. Prakash earned both her dental degree and postgraduate speciality degree in endodontics from this esteemed institution. She also has extensive clinical experience in implant dentistry. Over the past 15 years, she has been serving the Bay Area community, combining advanced clinical skills with a gentle touch to deliver exceptional endodontic and implant care.

Dr. Prakash takes the time to listen to each patient’s concerns, thoroughly explains treatment options, and is deeply committed to helping her patients alleviate their dental pain. Her practice is equipped with state-of-the-art technology, ensuring that every procedure, from root canals to endodontic microsurgery to placing dental implants, is as efficient, effective, and comfortable as possible.

Dr. Prakash is particularly passionate about patient education. She believes that informed patients can make the best decisions about their oral health. Whether it’s a complex endodontic procedure or follow-up care, Dr. Prakash and her friendly team are dedicated to providing comprehensive information and compassionate guidance every step of the way.

Outside the office, Dr. Prakash enjoys a vibrant personal life in Saratoga, where she resides with her husband and children. In her spare time, she loves travelling with her family, playing badminton, listening to music, and dancing. These hobbies reflect her belief in maintaining a healthy work-life balance and staying engaged with the community she serves.

Dr. Prakash’s blend of expertise, empathy, and personalized care makes her a standout endodontist.
