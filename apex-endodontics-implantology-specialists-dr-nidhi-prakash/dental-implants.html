Comprehensive Evaluation

A thorough assessment of oral health, jawbone density, and gums is conducted using advanced 3D imaging and CBCT scans to ensure candidacy and plan implant placement. If necessary, preparatory procedures such as bone grafting may be recommended.

Patient Consultation

During the consultation, treatment options, benefits, timeline, costs, and aftercare are discussed to create a personalized plan tailored to your goals and medical history.

Surgical Procedure

Implant placement is done under local anesthesia using precise surgical techniques and guided technology to ensure safety, comfort, and quick healing.

Implant Placement & Osseointegration

A titanium implant is securely placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the surrounding bone over several months (osseointegration), creating a strong foundation for the restoration. Good bone quality and patient care impact success.

Post-operative Care & Monitoring

Patients receive detailed care instructions and are monitored regularly to support healing, manage discomfort, and optimize implant integration.

Completion of Restoration

After healing, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, restoring natural appearance, function, and confidence.

Bone Grafting for Implants

Besides providing structural support, bone grafting helps preserve facial contours and prevents the sunken look often caused by tooth loss, improving the overall aesthetic result. Healing time is typically required after grafting before implants can be placed. Our team carefully assesses each case to create a personalized timeline that ensures the best outcome with minimal downtime.

After Implant Placement: Maintenance and Care

Once your dental implants are in place and your new smile is complete, proper maintenance and care are essential to ensure their longevity and success.

Good Oral Hygiene

Brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and using antimicrobial mouthwash help prevent plaque buildup and maintain healthy gums around the implants. Implant-friendly floss and interdental brushes are recommended for optimal cleaning of implants.

Regular Dental Checkups

Avoiding Hard Foods

While dental implants are durable, excessive force from chewing on hard foods or objects can damage them. Stick to a balanced diet and avoid biting down on anything too hard, such as ice, hard candy, or pen caps.

Quit Smoking

Smoking can compromise the healing process and increase the risk of implant failure. If you smoke, consider quitting to improve the long-term success of your dental implants. Smoking impairs blood flow and interferes with the body’s natural healing mechanisms, particularly during the process of osseointegration.

Protective Mouthguards

If you participate in contact sports or grind your teeth at night, wearing a mouthguard can help protect your implants from damage. Custom night or sports guards can safeguard your investment and prevent undue pressure on implant restorations.
