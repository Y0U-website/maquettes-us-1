Patient Info

Craig Dental Center is equipped with state of the art technology and equipment!

We are taking sanitation and safety measures to give you the best dental experience in the Waukee area.

We are proud to use digital x-ray imaging in our office. Digital x-ray units lessen radiation exposure while enhancing quality. A sensor is used to capture the image, which is downloaded into our computer system in seconds. This creates a much cleaner, sharper image and reduces the time spent waiting for the image to “develop.”

Each patient dental chair has its very own television monitor. While receiving treatment you are in control of the remote control to select your favorite program. This is especially helpful with young children… Our dental operatories are equipped with the newest in electric hand pieces instead of traditional dental “drills.” This results in less noise and higher efficiency, resulting in an overall more enjoyable experience.

Financial options for your best possible care

We do our best to enable you to get the dental treatment that you need! We accept cash, personal checks, and most major credit cards. We also participate with multiple insurance plans. Though dental insurance is a private contract between you and your insurance company, we are happy to file your claim and help you receive the maximum in benefits.
