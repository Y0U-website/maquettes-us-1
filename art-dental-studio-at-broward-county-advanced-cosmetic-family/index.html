Les soins

Routine 6-month cleanings, daily brushing and flossing. With proper care your implant typically lasts 25+ years.

For most patients with healthy adjacent teeth, dental implants are the better long-term option. Implants last longer (25+ years vs 8-15 for bridges), preserve jawbone (bridges allow continued bone loss), and don’t require modifying healthy neighboring teeth…

Recovery from single implant placement is easier than most patients expect. Mild to moderate discomfort for 3-7 days, manageable with prescribed pain medication. Most patients return to work and normal activities within 1-2 days.

Removable, nearly invisible clear aligners custom-made to gradually shift teeth without fixed brackets or wires. Art Dental Studio is an Invisalign Preferred Provider.

Many orthodontic cases at Art Dental are part of a larger smile transformation. Having Invisalign and veneers in the same practice means seamless planning and faster results.
