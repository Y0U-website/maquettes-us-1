What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is the dental specialty focused on diagnosing, preventing and correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. Misalignment — whether crowding, gaps, crossbite, overbite or underbite — affects how you chew, speak and maintain oral hygiene. Over time, untreated alignment issues contribute to TMJ pain, accelerated wear, gum disease and even sleep apnea.

Modern orthodontics has expanded far beyond traditional metal braces. Clear aligners like Invisalign use a series of removable, transparent trays to gradually move teeth into position without the appearance or food restrictions of braces. Clear sapphire braces offer a discreet alternative for patients who need fixed appliances. Treatment plans now use 3D scanning, digital simulation and predictive modeling — so you can see your final result before you start.

Adult orthodontics is one of the fastest-growing areas of dentistry. The majority of our orthodontic patients at Art Dental Studio are adults who are addressing alignment as part of a broader smile transformation or after years of postponing it. There is no upper age limit for orthodontic treatment — what matters is the health of your teeth and gums, not your age.

How often do I need to wear my Invisalign aligners?

Invisalign aligners should be worn 20-22 hours per day for treatment to progress on schedule. You remove them only for eating, drinking anything other than water, and brushing/flossing. Patients who don’t commit to the wear time often see treatment extend significantly — wear discipline is the biggest factor in success.

Am I too old for orthodontics?

No. There is no upper age limit for orthodontic treatment — what matters is the health of your teeth and supporting bone, not your age. The majority of Art Dental Studio’s orthodontic patients are adults. Many patients in their 40s, 50s and 60s successfully complete Invisalign treatment.

Do I need retainers after Invisalign or braces?

Yes — lifetime retention is required after any orthodontic treatment. Teeth naturally try to shift back to their original positions, and without retainers most patients see significant relapse within 1-2 years. We provide your first set of retainers as part of your treatment.

Can I get Invisalign while pregnant?

Yes — Invisalign treatment is considered safe during pregnancy. No anesthesia, no X-rays specifically required for routine progress visits. Notify Dr. Yilian if you become pregnant during treatment so we can adjust the visit schedule and skip non-essential X-rays.
