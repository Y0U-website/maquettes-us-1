New Patients

New patients are always welcome at Alliance Endodontics. During your first visit, we will evaluate your dental concerns, review your medical and dental history, and determine the best treatment plan for your needs. Our goal is to provide expert care in a comfortable environment while ensuring you understand every step of your treatment. We look forward to helping you achieve optimal oral health!

What To Expect At Your First Visit

Welcome & Check-In

Upon arrival, our friendly team will greet you and assist with any necessary paperwork. We’ll also review your medical and dental history to ensure we have a complete understanding of your oral health before treatment.

Comprehensive Exam & X-Rays

Dr. Barton will carefully examine your tooth, using specialized diagnostic techniques to assess the health of the pulp and surrounding structures. This evaluation helps identify the source of pain or infection and determine the most effective treatment to save your natural tooth.

Precise Diagnosis & Treatment Recommendations

Accurate diagnosis is a key part of our approach. After evaluating your case, we’ll explain our findings and discuss the best treatment options to relieve pain and preserve your natural tooth. We’ll also review the procedure, answer any questions, and ensure you feel informed and comfortable moving forward.

Next Steps & Scheduling

If treatment is needed, we’ll schedule your next appointment at a time that works for you. Our team will provide all the details you need to prepare for your procedure and ensure a smooth experience.

Dental Insurance

We are a fee-for-service office and are not in network with any dental insurance providers. Payment for treatment is due at the time of your appointment.

As a courtesy, we will electronically file your dental insurance claim for you. Any out-of-network benefits that your plan allows will be sent directly to you by your insurance provider. If you have questions about your specific coverage or benefits, we recommend contacting your insurance company or plan administrator.

Our priority is to provide you with the highest quality care and to make your experience as smooth as possible. Please let us know if you have any questions about our insurance process. Payment Plans We accept Care Credit, a convenient monthly payment program with a variety of no interest and low interest plans. Applying for Care Credit is quick and easy and a decision can be received immediately. Ask our office staff for assistance or visit www.carecredit.com .
