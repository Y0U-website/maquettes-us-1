Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy is a specialized procedure designed to save a tooth that has become infected or damaged deep inside. When the pulp—the soft tissue within the tooth—becomes inflamed due to decay, trauma, or cracks, it can cause pain and lead to serious infection. During the procedure, the infected pulp is removed, the inside of the tooth is carefully cleaned and sealed, and a protective restoration is placed to restore function. Root canal therapy helps relieve pain, prevent further infection, and preserve your natural tooth for years to come.

Root Canal Retreatment

Sometimes, a tooth that has undergone root canal therapy doesn’t heal as expected or develops new issues over time. Root canal retreatment is a procedure that removes the previous filling, thoroughly cleans the root canals, and reseals the tooth to eliminate infection and restore its health. This treatment helps save the tooth and prevent the need for extraction, offering a second chance for long-term function and comfort.

Endodontic Microsurgery

Endodontic microsurgery is a specialized procedure used when a standard root canal treatment isn’t enough to save a tooth. The most common type, an apicoectomy, involves removing infection or inflammation at the tip of the tooth’s root to promote healing. Using advanced technology and precision techniques, endodontic microsurgery can help preserve natural teeth and prevent the need for extraction.

GentleWave® Procedure is an alternative to traditional root canal surgery which allows the doctor to reach and clean microscopic spaces of the root canal system while preserving the natural tooth structure. GentleWave® procedure promotes faster healing time and longer-lasting results while preserving the structure of the tooth. GentleWave®’s minimally-invasive procedure takes place in a single visit in most patient cases and includes little to no post-op discomfort.
