Dental Hygiene

If you notice any of these signs, we urge you to visit our dental hygienist in Bloomfield Hills, MI, for early intervention.

  • Toothache
  • Redness or swelling of gums
  • Gum bleeding
  • Stains on the tooth surfaces
  • Yellowish discoloration
  • Hardened plaque deposits
  • Bad breath
  • Foul taste
  • Oral ulcers
  • Gum recession
