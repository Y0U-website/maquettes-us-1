Dental Implants in Bloomfield…

  • The treatment can be customized to restore any number of missing teeth, even a full arch.
  • They allow you to eat more comfortably and enjoy your favorite foods.
  • These replacement teeth do not cause speech disturbance, unlike full dentures.
  • Implants are biocompatible and merge well with the surrounding natural tissues.
  • They prevent the risk of future bone loss and also promote healthy bone formation.
  • They blend look and feel natural, allowing you to smile more confidently.

Our Dental Implant Process in Bloomfield Hills, MI

After your initial consultation and oral exam, our Bloomfield Hills, MI, will create a precise treatment plan for dental implants. The number of implants you may need depends on the number of missing teeth, as well as your jawbone thickness. During the process of implant surgery, we place an incision on your gum line to expose the bone. Next, we will surgically insert the implant posts in the jawbone and secure their position.
