FAQs About Invisalign

Invisalign is a clear aligner system used to straighten teeth without traditional metal braces.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of the case, but many patients complete treatment within 12 to 18 months.

Invisalign aligners are made from clear plastic and are designed to be nearly invisible when worn.

How many hours per day should I wear Invisalign?

Most patients are advised to wear their aligners for about 20–22 hours per day for best results.
