FAQs About Perio Protect

Perio Protect is a gum disease treatment that uses custom trays to deliver medication below the gumline where harmful bacteria live.

Perio Trays ® are custom-fitted dental trays designed to hold medication and gently deliver it deep under the gums.

Most patients wear their trays for a few minutes each day as directed by their dentist.

Does Perio Protect replace dental cleanings?

No. Perio Protect therapy works alongside professional dental cleanings to help manage gum disease more effectively.
