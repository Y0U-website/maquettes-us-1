Appointments

We know you have many choices when choosing a Dentist in Williamsburg, VA so we have made requesting an appointment a simple process via our website. If for any reason you cannot keep a scheduled appointment, or will be delayed, please call us as soon as possible.

Insurance and Billing

We accept most traditional insurance plans, contact our office to verify acceptance of your plan. Colonial Family Dentistry does not participate in Health Management Organizations or Medicaid/Medicare; however, we will be happy to file any other insurance claims for you.

Payment Options

We accept checks, cash or credit cards. We also offer a flexible payment plan. Please see our Financial Coordinator for details. We are happy to file insurance for your reimbursement as long as you are free to choose your own dentist.

Financing Options

We offer financing through Sunbit. See our Payment Options page for more information.
