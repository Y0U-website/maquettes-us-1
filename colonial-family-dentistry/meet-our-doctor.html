Meet our doctor

Dr. Danielle DiGuardia is a native of Woodbridge, Virginia and currently resides in Richmond, Virginia. Dr. DiGuardia earned a B.S. in Microbiology from George Mason University and then went on to attend Virginia Commonwealth University to receive her Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS.) She has served in several Mission of Mercy projects in Virginia as well as volunteering in various community clinics.

Dr. DiGuardia is dedicated to providing comprehensive and proactive dental care while helping maintain overall health and wellness for her patients. She focuses on personalized dental treatment to help boost confidence, health, and quality of life while maintaining excellent care. Dr. DiGuardia is committed to continuing education and stays current with the latest technology and techniques.

Dr. DiGuardia enjoys interacting with patients of all ages and backgrounds while prioritizing that patients feel informed and comfortable throughout all phases of care. She believes in a mutual commitment with each patient to create plans that help achieve beautiful, comfortable, and functional long term results.

Outside of the office, Danielle enjoys traveling, hiking, and eating at her favorite local restaurants with family and friends.

Dr. Harris Salom

Originally from southwest Virginia, Dr. Salom brings a friendly, down to earth approach to dentistry that puts patients at ease. He completed his

undergraduate studies from Washington and Lee University. While there, he played DIII collegiate soccer and received his B.S. in Biology. He then went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Virginia Commonwealth University. Dr. Salom believes the best care starts with listening. His goal is to build real relationships with patients, treating each person like family and making sure they feel comfortable, informed, and in control of their oral health. His care is grounded in trusted values, evidence-based treatment, and a genuine desire to help people feel confident in their smiles.

Outside the office, he enjoys staying active with soccer, golf, pickleball, hiking, and snowboarding. As he settles into Williamsburg, he’s looking forward to becoming part of a practice that shares his commitment to quality care and community.
