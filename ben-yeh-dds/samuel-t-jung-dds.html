Samuel T. Jung, DDS

New York University College of Dentistry

Dr. Samuel T. Jung received his dental degree from National Yang Ming in Taiwan. He proceeded to complete and earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from NYU College of Dentistry, where he stayed and became an assistant clinical professor for nearly 30 years. Dr. Jung loves to teach and has been awarded one of the favorite faculty members by dental students.

On his time off, you can find Dr. Jung spoiling his adorable daughters and enjoying family time. Dr Jung also loves to travel and loves to explore new restaurants all over NYC with his wife.
