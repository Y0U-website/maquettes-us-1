Approach to care

The goal at every visit is the same: you should leave knowing exactly what was done, what comes next, and why. No pressure, no surprises on the bill, no judgment about how long it’s been since your last cleaning.

Languages & accessibility

Culpeper is home to a growing Spanish-speaking community, and Dr. Sanie’s practice is built to welcome everyone in it. Our team provides Spanish-speaking accommodations for appointments, treatment consultations, and financial conversations, ¿habla español? Estamos aquí para ayudarle. If translation support would make your visit easier, just let us know when you call.

The office is wheelchair accessible, parking is steps from the door, and the team is happy to accommodate sensory or mobility needs. If there’s anything that would make your appointment more comfortable, please tell us ahead of time so we can plan for it.
