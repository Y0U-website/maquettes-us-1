Why you’re here matters

You probably landed on this page for one of a few reasons. Maybe you’re new to Culpeper and looking for a dental home. Maybe you’ve had a bad experience somewhere else and you’re ready for something gentler. Maybe it’s been a while since your last visit and you’re hoping to find someone who won’t make you feel bad about that.

Whichever it is, you’re welcome here. This page exists to give you a feel for the practice before you ever step in the door.

What you can expect from your visit

Your time will not be rushed. You’ll get a careful exam, an honest conversation about what you need, and a written plan you can take home and think about. Nothing happens until you say so. Dr. Sanie does most procedures himself, in this office, including things many practices refer out, like root canals and implants, so you keep the same dentist from start to finish.

You won’t be handed off to a different provider every visit. You won’t be double-booked. You won’t feel like a number. That’s by design.

What we believe, and how that shapes your visit

You’re welcome here, whoever you are

Culpeper is one of the most diverse small towns in Virginia. That’s a strength, and your dental home should reflect it. ¿Habla español? Estamos aquí para ayudarle. No insurance? Ask about the Virginia Dental Club. Anxious about dental visits? Tell us, we’ll adjust the entire visit around it. There aren’t hoops to jump through to be seen here.
