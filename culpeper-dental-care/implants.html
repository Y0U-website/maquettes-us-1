The CBCT difference

Modern implant placement starts with a 3D scan. Our CBCT (cone-beam computed tomography) imaging gives Dr. Sanie a precise view of your jawbone, sinuses, and nerves before he makes any decisions. The scan takes seconds and dramatically improves outcomes, we know exactly where the implant should go and what to avoid.

Frequently asked questions

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Local anesthetic numbs the area completely during placement, and we take extra time and walk you through each step if you’re anxious. Mild soreness for a day or two after is typical.

Am I a candidate?

Most healthy adults are. Heavy smokers, patients with uncontrolled diabetes, or significant jawbone loss may need additional planning (a bone graft, for example) or alternative treatment. The CBCT scan and consultation give you a clear answer.

How long until I have a finished tooth?

End-to-end usually takes 4 to 6 months for a single implant: placement, 3 to 6 months for the bone to integrate, then crown placement. We use a temporary tooth in the gap during healing for visible front teeth.
