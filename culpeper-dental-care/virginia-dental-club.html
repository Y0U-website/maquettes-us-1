How it compares to traditional insurance

Most dental insurance plans cap annual benefits around $1,500-$2,000, have waiting periods of 6 to 12 months for major work, require claim forms, and impose out-of-network denials. The Virginia Dental Club has none of that:

  • No annual maximum. Insurance caps your benefits in dollars; we don’t. Use your discount on every visit.
  • No waiting period. Need a crown the week you join? You get the member rate.
  • No claim forms. Your discount is applied at checkout. No mail, no phone calls to a benefits administrator.
  • No network denials. Because Culpeper Dental Care is the network. You pay the membership rate, that’s the only network you need.

Stacking with CareCredit and Sunbit financing

For larger work like implants, full-mouth restoration, or orthodontics, your member discount stacks with our CareCredit and Sunbit financing options. That means you get the procedure at the member rate and split the cost into 0 % interest monthly payments, making major treatment workable without dipping into savings.

How is this different from dental insurance?

There are no claim forms, no waiting periods, no deductibles, no annual maximums, and no out-of-network denials. You pay a flat annual fee and your preventive care is covered. Most insurance caps benefits near $1,500 to $2,000 a year; the Virginia Dental Club has no cap.

When do benefits start?

Immediately. There&#x27;s no waiting period. The day you join is the day you can use your membership, including your first cleaning and exam.

Can I use it for my whole family?

Yes. Family pricing is available, ask us when you call. Each family member gets the full preventive package plus member discounts on all other treatments.

What if I need a major procedure like an implant or root canal?

Your member discount (typically 20 to 30 %) applies to the procedure. Combined with our in-house CareCredit and Sunbit 0 % financing, most patients find they can plan major treatment without dipping into savings. We provide a written estimate before any procedure begins.

Can I cancel?

Yes, membership is annual, not a long-term contract. You can choose not to renew at any time. There&#x27;s no cancellation fee.
