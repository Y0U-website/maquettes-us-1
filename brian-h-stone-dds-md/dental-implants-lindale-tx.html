2. How long does the dental implant procedure take?

The duration of the implant procedure varies depending on factors such as the number of implants being placed and the complexity of your case. On average, a single implant placement takes about an hour. Your oral surgeon will provide a more precise estimate after evaluating your individual needs.

3. Who is not a candidate for dental implants?

While dental implants are a suitable option for many people, certain conditions may affect candidacy. Patients with severe bone loss, untreated gum disease, or specific medical conditions that impact bone healing may require additional considerations. An oral surgeon can assess your situation and determine if dental implants are the right choice for you.

4. How can I ensure the longevity of my dental implants?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Maintaining excellent oral hygiene through regular brushing and flossing is essential. Regular dental checkups are also crucial to monitor your oral health and the condition of your implants. Following your oral surgeon s post-operative instructions will promote a smooth recovery and contribute to the long-term success of your implants.
