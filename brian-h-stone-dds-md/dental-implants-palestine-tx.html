Dental Implants in Palestine, TX

Dental implants provide multiple benefits as a tooth replacement option.

Improved Functionality:

Dental implants are a reliable and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike removable denture options, implants are securely placed in the jawbone, allowing patients to eat and speak normally without worrying about slipping or discomfort.

Enhanced Aesthetics:

Dental implants are made to resemble natural teeth. These artificial teeth are individually created to match the shape, size, and color of your current teeth, resulting in a seamless and appealing smile.

Preserved Oral Health:

Dental implant surgery is a tooth replacement option that does not involve altering or removing healthy teeth. This preservation of natural teeth promotes oral health and prevents jaw bone loss.

Long-Lasting Solution:

Dental implants are a durable tooth replacement option that can last a lifetime with proper care. Unlike other alternatives, dental implants provide a long-lasting and reliable solution for missing teeth, without the need for periodic replacements or repairs.

Dental Implant Surgery

The first step of any treatment is a consultation with one of our experienced oral surgeons. During this appointment, we will evaluate your oral health and determine if you are a suitable candidate for dental implants. We will take 3D scans and discuss your treatment options, including the number of implants needed and any additional procedures, such as extraction or bone grafting, that may be necessary.

Once you have decided to proceed with dental implant surgery, we will schedule your procedure. On the day of the surgery, you will be given anesthesia to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. The oral surgeon will then carefully place the titanium implant posts into your jawbone, which will act as replacement roots for the artificial teeth .

After the implant placement, there will be a healing period during which the implants fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This typically takes several weeks, during which you may be provided with temporary teeth to wear.

Once the healing period is complete, you will return to our office. During this appointment, your surgeon will attach abutment pieces to the implants, which will connect to the natural-looking crown. These custom-made restorations will be designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, providing you with a seamless and natural-looking smile.

During the healing period and recovery, it is important to follow the aftercare instructions provided by our team. It is also important to maintain good oral hygiene practices and avoid hard or sticky foods that could potentially disrupt the healing process.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, allowing you to eat any foods you desire and enjoy a confident smile. Our compassionate and comprehensive care, along with the expertise of our oral surgeons and staff, ensures that you will receive the highest quality treatment and personalized attention throughout your dental implant journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the recovery like for dental implant procedures?

During the recovery period after dental implant procedures, it is normal to experience some soreness and discomfort. However, your oral surgeon will provide you with aftercare instructions and medications to manage any pain. It usually takes several weeks for the implants to fully secure in the bone beneath the gums. In the meantime, it is important to follow a soft food diet and avoid putting excessive pressure on the implant area. Most patients are able to return to their regular routines within a few days.

2. Is the dental implant procedure painful?

The dental implant surgery is not painful. Your oral surgeon will administer the appropriate anesthesia to ensure your comfort during the procedure. While you may experience some soreness and discomfort during the recovery period, any pain can be managed through prescribed medications and following the aftercare instructions provided by your oral surgeon.

3. What if there is not enough bone left for a secure implant placement?

If there is not enough bone left in your jaw for a secure implant placement, your oral surgeon may recommend a bone grafting procedure. This involves transplanting bone from another part of your body or using synthetic bone material to build up the jawbone. Bone grafting helps to create a solid foundation for the dental implant and ensures its long-term stability. Your oral surgeon will assess your specific situation and determine the best course of action to ensure the success of your dental implant procedure.
