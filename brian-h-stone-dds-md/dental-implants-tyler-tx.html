Dental Implants in Tyler, TX

There are numerous benefits to choosing dental implants as a tooth replacement option.

Improved Functionality:

Dental implants provide a stable and durable tooth replacement option. Unlike removable dentures, implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, allowing for normal eating and speaking without the fear of slippage or discomfort.

Enhanced Aesthetics:

Dental implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth. They are custom-made to match the shape, size, and color of your existing teeth, providing a seamless and attractive smile. This natural appearance can boost confidence and self-esteem.

Preserved Oral Health:

Dental implant surgery does not require the alteration or removal of healthy teeth, which is often necessary with other tooth replacement options. By preserving your natural teeth, dental implants help maintain proper oral health and prevent bone loss in the jaw.

Long-Lasting Solution:

Dental implants are a permanent tooth replacement option that can last a lifetime with proper care. Unlike other alternatives that may need to be replaced or repaired periodically, dental implants offer a durable and reliable solution for missing teeth.

Dental Implant Surgery

The first step of any oral surgery at our practice is a consultation with one of our oral surgeons. During this appointment, they will evaluate your oral health and determine if you are a suitable candidate for the surgical procedure. They will discuss your options and answer any questions or concerns you may have.

Dental implant surgery involves placing tiny titanium posts, which act as artificial tooth roots, into your jawbone. These posts will serve as a secure foundation for the replacement teeth. The surgical procedure itself is performed under anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Our team at Southern Surgical Arts offers different anesthesia options, including local anesthesia and full IV sedation, to meet your needs.

After the dental implant surgery, a healing period is necessary for the implants to fully integrate with the bone. During this time, temporary teeth may be provided to maintain functionality and aesthetics. Once the healing period is complete, the second phase of the procedure begins, which involves attaching permanent crowns or a bridge to the implants. These replacement teeth are custom-made to match the appearance of your natural teeth, giving you a beautiful and seamless smile.

The entire dental implant process can vary in length depending on the number of implants required, but generally, it takes 30-60 minutes for a single implant and 1-3 hours for multiple implants . Our skilled surgeons and staff will ensure that you feel comfortable throughout the procedure and will provide detailed aftercare instructions to promote a smooth recovery.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy individuals who are able to undergo dental procedures like extractions are suitable candidates for dental implants. However, certain factors such as bone loss around the jaw may affect the placement of implants. In some cases, bone grafting procedures can be performed to restore bone density, making implant placement possible.

To determine if dental implants are right for you, it is best to schedule a consultation with an experienced oral surgeon. The oral surgeons at Southern Surgical Arts in Tyler, Texas, can evaluate your oral health and discuss the best treatment options for your specific needs.

Cost of Dental Implants

The cost of dental implants can vary depending on several factors, including the number of teeth being replaced, the need for additional procedures such as bone grafting or sinus lifts, the type of anesthesia used during the procedure, insurance coverage, and more. When you come to Southern Surgical Arts, we can provide an estimated cost of treatment during your consultation.

While the initial cost of dental implants may be higher compared to other tooth replacement options, they offer long-term value and benefits. Dental implants provide a potential lifelong solution, preventing bone loss and dental shifting, and allowing for comfortable eating, speaking, and smiling.

What is recovery like for dental implant procedures?

Recovery from dental implant procedures varies for each individual. After implant placement, it takes several weeks for the implants to fully secure in the bone. During this time, a soft food diet may be necessary, and aftercare instructions will be provided to ensure proper healing.

What if there is not enough bone left for a secure dental implant placement?

If there is not enough bone left for a secure implant placement, bone grafting procedures can be performed to restore bone density. This allows for safe and successful implant placement.
