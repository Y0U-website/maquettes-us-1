The practice

Dental implants are a long-term tooth replacement solution that restores oral health and function with natural-looking results.

Wisdom teeth, also known as third molars, can be comfortably and safely removed by our experienced oral surgeons.

Jaw surgery can improve a patient’s ability to eat, speak, and breathe comfortably, and bring balance to the facial structure.

Southern Surgical Arts - Dental Implant Experts delivers comprehensive and compassionate care to patients of all ages. Drs. Charles Stone, Jr, Brian Stone, and Wade Barker are experienced oral and maxillofacial surgeons who aim to provide an exceptional patient experience for every patient.

Our offices in Tyler, TX, Palestine, TX, and Lindale, TX, are equipped with the latest technology to ensure we deliver safe and predictable results. Oral surgery services include dental implants , wisdom teeth removal , full-arch restoration , corrective jaw surgery , tooth extractions , oral pathology and biopsy , and more.
