During the childhood years, orthodontists can identify bite problems and take advantage of kids growth to create space for eruption of the larger adult teeth while correcting jaw discrepancies and bad habits.

Clear aligners are a very esthetic treatment option for patients that do not want to use braces but still correct their bite and improve their smile.

Temporomandibular disorders include pain and dysfunction in the jaw joint. Orthodontists can help by correcting bite problems to ensure proper jaw function, as well as utilizing new technological advances, such as shockwave therapy, to improve symptoms and stimulate tissue regeneration.
