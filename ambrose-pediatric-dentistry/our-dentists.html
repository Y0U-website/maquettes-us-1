Dr. James Hall

Dr. James Hall lived as a native of Macon, Georgia with his wife Beverly from 1973 to 2019. Dr. Hall leaves behind two adult daughters. Dr. Hall was involved in his church and community for many years.

Education & Experience

Dr. Hall was a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. He was also a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, Central District Dental Society, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and American Society of Dentistry for Children. He was past president of the Georgia Dental Association and served dentistry on many boards and committees.

Dr. Hall served on the Board of the Macon Volunteer Clinic and as a Public Policy Advocate for the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. He published numerous articles in both scientific and law journals. In 2004, Dr. Hall received the Georgia Dental Association’s highest honor, the Award of Merit, in recognition of his contributions and service to the profession.

Dr. Hall dedicated his adult life to Dentistry and will be missed. 1941-2019.

  • Mercer University, Bachelor of Science in Chemistry and Mathematics, 1963
  • Northwestern University Dental School, Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, 1967
  • Northwestern University Dental School, Pediatric Dentistry Master of Science degree, 1969
  • Two-year residency in Pediatric Dentistry, Children’s Memorial Hospital, 1967-1969
  • Served two years in the United States Army, Chief of Pediatric Dentistry, 1969-1971
