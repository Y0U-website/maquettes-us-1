Dental Implants

Dental implants are a very reliable source for effective tooth replacement. Whether you have lost one tooth or many, implants can provide a permanent solution that looks and functions normally.

Transform Your Smile with Dental Implants

Dental implants are a cutting-edge solution for replacing lost teeth, providing stability and aesthetics. At Collins Dental, we specialize in delivering tailored implant procedures to meet your unique needs. Our dental implant dentist ensures a seamless process from consultation to placement.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically inserted into the jawbone, serving as artificial roots for replacement teeth. They provide a permanent foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Titanium is biocompatible, meaning the material is nontoxic and doesn’t harm living tissue. This allows the implants to fuse with the bone, creating a stable and durable base that mimics natural tooth roots. This integration helps preserve bone structure and prevents the deterioration often associated with tooth loss.

Dental implants are a preferred choice for tooth replacement because of their longevity, ability to maintain bone levels, comfort, and natural feel and appearance.

Dental Implant Process at Collins Dental

1. Consultation & Exam: Comprehensive assessment, including 3D scans.

The first step in getting dental implants is a consultation and exam. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health to see if you’re a good candidate. This assessment includes 3D scans to understand your jawbone structure, gum health, and overall dental condition. This info helps create a treatment plan tailored to your needs, ensuring the implants are placed correctly for long-term success. Discussing your medical history and any risk factors helps to ensure the procedure is safer and more effective.

2. Tooth Extraction & Bone Grafting: Necessary for optimal implant placement.

When a tooth is lost or extracted, the surrounding bone can deteriorate, leaving insufficient support for an implant. Bone grafting involves adding bone material to the jaw to rebuild and strengthen the area, ensuring there is enough bone density to hold the implant securely. This procedure is crucial for providing a stable base that promotes proper integration of the implant with the jawbone, ultimately leading to long-lasting and successful results. Bone grafting will take a few months to heal before your implants can proceed.

3. Implant Placement: Secure insertion of titanium posts.

With the site ready, your dentist attaches the titanium implant to your jawbone. The gum is then secured over the implant with stitches, allowing the area to heal and the implant to fuse with the jawbone. This process is known as osseointegration, and it typically takes a few months. Follow-up appointments will ensure the implant is healing properly.

4. Healing: Osseointegration period for strong jawbone support.

When a titanium implant is placed in the jawbone, the bone has the remarkable ability to fuse with the titanium. This process is called osseointegration and takes about 3 months. Over time, the bone cells grow around and attach to the implant, essentially making it a part of the jawbone. This fusion provides a stable and strong foundation for attaching your dental implant.

5. Abutment & Crown Placement: Final steps for a natural look.

After the gum has healed and the implant has fused with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, the next step is to place the abutment. The abutment is a connector that extends from the implant to the surface of the gum. It helps support the final artificial tooth or dental prosthesis.

Once the abutment is in place, a custom-made artificial tooth, also known as a crown, is attached to it. The crown is designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, providing a durable and natural-looking replacement for the missing tooth.

This completes the process of getting a dental implant, and you’ll have a fully restored and functional tooth that looks and feels natural.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are dental implants considered a long-term solution for missing teeth?

Dental implants are designed to stay in place for years because they fuse directly with your jawbone. This strong connection not only supports your new tooth but also helps maintain your bone structure, which is something other tooth replacement options can’t always do.

What happens during the first step of the dental implant process?

The process begins with a detailed consultation where our team evaluates your oral health and takes 3D scans. This helps us understand your bone structure and create a treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Why might bone grafting be needed before an implant?

If your jawbone isn’t strong enough to support an implant, a bone graft may be recommended. This procedure helps rebuild the area so the implant has a stable foundation, improving the chances of long-term success.

What is osseointegration in dental implants?

Osseointegration is the healing phase where your jawbone naturally fuses with the titanium implant. This process creates a stable base, helping the implant feel secure and function much like a natural tooth.

What completes the dental implant process?

Once healing is complete, a connector called an abutment is placed, followed by a custom-made crown. This final step restores the appearance and function of your tooth, giving you a result that blends naturally with your smile.
