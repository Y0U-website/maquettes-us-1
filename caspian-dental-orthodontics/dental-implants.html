Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

The dental implant procedure typically involves placing a titanium post into the jawbone under local anesthesia. The post then integrates with the bone over several months before a crown is attached. The procedure is generally well-tolerated, and any discomfort is managed with pain relief options.

Recovery time can vary, but most patients experience a healing period of 3 to 6 months for osseointegration to occur. Post-surgery, you might experience minor swelling and discomfort, which usually subsides within a few days.

Most people are candidates for dental implants, but certain factors like insufficient jawbone density or severe gum disease might affect eligibility. A thorough evaluation by our dentists will determine if implants are the right option for you.

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups are essential. Maintaining good oral hygiene will help ensure the longevity of your implants.
