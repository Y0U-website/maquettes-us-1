About Dr. Shephard Dr. Andrew…

Today, my wife and I have three children and are very excited to see them grow up and experience many of the same activities that we enjoyed as kids in Evansville. In our rare downtime from work and the daily grind, we enjoy short camping trips, riding bikes, and having a good time with family and friends. My staff and I all share the belief that our patients should be treated in the same manner that we would like to be treated. If this philosophy resonates with your own values, we would love to have you as a part of our dental family!

I was born and raised on the north side of Evansville to a hard-working, middle-class family. My foundational values were shaped by parents that demonstrated and demanded a life led according to the “golden rule”. While simple in instruction, it is the basis for every decision that I make in my personal and professional life.

Existing patients must call the office to schedule all appointments.

We are in-network providers for select insurance companies (Delta Dental Premier, Paramount), accept most out-of-network insurance plans, and will strive to maximize your benefits. We currently do NOT accept state-funded dental plans (Medicaid, Healthy Indiana Plan). Please do not schedule a New Patient Appointment if one of these is your insurance provider.

Please note that you will be asked to indicate whether your visit is for an emergency or a comprehensive exam after initial information is entered. Additionally, your first visit with us will NOT include a teeth cleaning. Cleanings can be scheduled as a separate appointment following the completion of a comprehensive exam.
