The practice

Our mission is to develop lifelong relationships with our patients based upon trust, compassion, integrity, and “The Golden Rule.” Our family-oriented establishment stands by our reputation as a conservative and comfort-focused practice, dedicated to clinical excellence in regard to the oral and overall health of our patients.

Our practice was started in 1975 and moved to its current location in 1988. Dr. Shephard began with the practice in 2010 and in 2012 formally became Andrew D. Shephard, DDS LLC. The office has been updated with digital x-rays, digital photography, and one-appointment crown technology. We are in-network providers for select insurance companies (Delta Dental Premier, Paramount), accept most out-of-network insurance plans, and will strive to maximize your benefits. We currently do NOT accept state-funded dental plans (Medicaid, Healthy Indiana Plan). Please do not schedule a New Patient Appointment if one of these is your insurance provider.

Please note that you will be asked to indicate whether your visit is for an emergency or a comprehensive exam after initial information is entered. Additionally, your first visit with us will NOT include a teeth cleaning. Cleanings can be scheduled as a separate appointment following the completion of a comprehensive exam.
