0 Our Team Meet our doctors Dr. Matar, DDS Dr. Matar has more than 17 years of experience in general dentistry, He is a skilled and compassionate dentist and connects well with his patients.

He completed his residency in Advanced general dentistry from. New York University Langone Dental Medicine. After his residency program, he practiced dentistry at Harvard street health center in Boston, where he treated complex cases and supervised dental students from Harvard university.

Dr. Matar also holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery Degree from Alexandria University in Egypt. He and his wife Omnia have Two kids Ryan 6 years and Jana 5 y old and his wife is a doctor of pharmacy.

In his free time he enjoys spending it with his kids.

Dr.Williamson DDS

Dr. Williamson (“Matt”) has over 40 years of dental experience. He attended the University of Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) – and graduated with a Registered Pharmacy (R.P h.) Degree.

He practiced pharmacy for seven years after that.

In 1978, Dr. Williamson Graduated from VCU as a Doctor of Dental Surgery. Shortly after, he opened his dental practice as Dr. M M Williamson DDS at the Spotsylvania Courthouse. It is now known as Brilliance Dental.

In his free time, Dr. Williamson enjoys gardening and raising animals on his small farm.

Over the years, Dr. Williamson has had the pleasure of participating in a number of mission trips to Brazil, Romania, and Jamaica. He has been married to Betty Williamson since 1993, and together they have two sons, Seth and Luke Williamson, daughter-in-law Shannon, and two grandchildren.

Dr.Toor DDS

Born and raised in Fairfax, VA, Dr. Toor attended George Mason University, earning his bachelor’s degree in economics with a focus on pre-dental studies. Dr. Toor earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS) from the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry in Nebraska. After training, Dr. Toor found his way back to his roots in Northern Virginia, where he enjoys spending quality time with family and friends. Dr. Toor is a charismatic provider determined to deliver the highest quality of care that tailors the specific needs of all patients in the DMV area.

Dr. Toor offers many dental procedures emphasizing restorative and esthetic dentistry. He enjoys taking a pragmatic and practical approach to your oral health care needs and is passionate about educating his patients on obtaining optimal oral health. Dr. Toor is committed to staying on top of the latest developments and technological advances in dentistry through continuing education.

In his free time, Dr. Toor can be found traveling, trying new restaurants, playing basketball, or spending time with family and friends. He is quite a sports enthusiast and is an avid DC sports fan.

Meet our entire team

For more than 41 years, our dental practice team have been providing dental care to the Spotsylvania community. We are so proud to be part of the community.

Our office philosophy incorporates a team approach to best serve our patients. Our general dentists and dental hygienist work together to help the patient achieve their goals. We strive to deliver the highest standard of care and to treat each patient as if they were a member of our family.

We’re not just another dentist in Spotsylvania, we’re a team of dental professionals in Spotsylvania serving our community.

At Brilliance Dental, we are fully equipped to help you with all of your dental needs!
