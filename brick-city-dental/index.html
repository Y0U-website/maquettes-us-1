I grew up on a small beef cattle farm in Wisconsin. There I learned that I loved working with my hands and fixing things. In school I excelled in my science classes. And from my earliest days I’ve been driven to care for people. To me, dentistry is an incredible combination of those three cores of my personality. It’s a career that I love and am excited for every day.

While in Wisconsin I attended Marquette University and earned a Bachelors degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology. At Creighton University in Omaha Nebraska I earned my Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from one of the best clinical dentistry educations in the nation.

My wife and I love living in Mexico. We have enjoyed making it our home and contributing to the wonderful community here. Recently, we had a daughter and we are excited for her to grow up in this wonderful community.
