Dr. David Kang graduated from some of the most prestigious universities around the US and world! Dr. Kang grew up in Charlotte, North Carolina and earned his bachelor’s degree in Computer engineering from Georgia Tech.

Dr. Kang worked as a computer engineer at IBM before he joined the Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore, Maryland for his master’s degree in Biotechnology.

He worked with Samsung Biologics briefly before he joined the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.

Upon the completion of his dental degree, Dr. Kang joined Endodontics residency at Harvard University in Boston, Massachusetts. Dr. Kang earned his second master’s degree in Oral Biology Medical Science and a certificate in Endodontics from the Harvard School of Dental Medicine in Boston.

Dr. David Kang believes patient education is a critical component in successful treatments. Dr. Kang always spends time with his patients going over details of diagnosis and prognosis based on clinical evaluations and research studies recommending evidence-based treatments. All of his patients and referring doctors complement and appreciate him for his great chair-side manner!
