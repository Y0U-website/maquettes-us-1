Which Orthodontic Treatment Is Right for Your Smile?

Would you like to straighten your smile, but aren’t sure which treatment is right for you? Before you come in for a consultation with Dr. Buchwald in our Frisco or Prosper orthodontic office, you can take our fast and easy quiz below to see the treatment that could be the perfect fit for your smile. As a specialist in orthodontics, Dr. Buchwald offers many options for children, teens, and adults. Contact us to schedule your consultation at one of our two locations and get the customized care you deserve.

Question 1: Are you 18 or older?

Question 2: Do you still have baby teeth?

Question 3: Are you missing any teeth?

Question 4: Why are you interested in orthodontics?

Question 5: Do you have a misaligned bite (e.g., crossbite, overbite, underbite)?

Question 6: Do you experience headaches or jaw pain more than once a week?

Question 7: Do you have a particular treatment deadline you want to meet?

Question 8: What are you most concerned about with orthodontic treatment?

Question 9: Have you experienced orthodontic treatment before?

If you answered A five or more times . . .

The treatment that will most likely be the best option for you is braces. Traditional braces aren’t what they used to be. The materials are smaller and more streamlined than ever, and they help especially in complex cases for both children and adults. If you want a guaranteed great-looking smile, braces are a safe, reliable bet.

If you answered B five or more times . . .

You may be a great candidate for Invisalign clear aligners. This orthodontic treatment is designed for teens and adults who want to correct mild to moderate misalignment in their teeth without metal brackets hindering the appearance of their smile. Plus, the clear aligners are removeable, so you can eat whatever you’d like and brush your teeth as usual.
