The Benefits of Choosing an In-Network Orthodontist

We are in-network with Cigna. This means that your orthodontist has a contract with the insurance company to establish a fair and average fee for covered orthodontic services that they agree to charge. Cigna then covers a percentage of that cost. Alternatively, out-of-network orthodontists can charge higher fees for their services, but you will need to pay the difference in price in addition to your out-of-pocket percentage of the insurance company’s pricing. By visiting an in-network orthodontist, you know that you will be responsible for the lowest possible cost.
