How Do All-On-4 Implants Work?

For All-On-4 to work, our team will need to embed four dental implants into your jawbone so that they can directly support a full denture to your facial structure. Due to the positioning and angle of each metal post, this treatment can be viable for people who don’t have much jawbone density. Once the titanium posts are placed, you can expect to get a temporary denture while we fabricate your permanent one. Once it’s completed, you’ll return to have your provisional one replaced.

Am I a Candidate for All-On-4 Implants?

The only way to know for sure if you’re eligible for All-On-4 implants is by undergoing a consultation with our team, that way we can evaluate your oral health and jawbone. If you require preliminary services, such as gum disease treatment or tooth extractions, we can help prepare your mouth for the procedure. In many cases, patients can qualify for All-On-4 if they:

  • Are missing most or all of their teeth on an entire arch
  • Have sufficient jawbone volume (without needing bone grafting)
  • Remain committed to maintaining good oral hygiene habits every day
  • Do not have periodontal disease or other oral health complications

Benefits of All-On-4 Implants

With All-On-4 implants, you can typically expect to enjoy a wide range of advantages for you and your oral health, including:

  • Enhanced biting power so you can enjoy nearly all of your favorite foods
  • Simplified maintenance due to the dentures being permanently fitted to your mouth
  • Jawbone preservation due to the dental implants stimulating the bone tissue
  • Natural-looking and stable results that won’t move out of place during meals or conversations

All-On-4 vs. Traditional Implants

Since they often won’t require you to undergo bone grafting, even if you don’t have adequate jawbone density, All-On-4 can provide a quicker and more cost-effective method for replacing missing teeth compared to traditional dental implants. Not only is the procedure less invasive, but it also often involves one surgery instead of two or more. That said, if you want optimal strength and stability, then traditional dental implants are usually preferred. Our team will be sure to discuss the details of both treatments so that we can develop the best solution for rebuilding your smile.
