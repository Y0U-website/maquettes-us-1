How Dental Implant Salvage Works

Dental implant failure and salvage treatment is like a rescue mission for troubled dental implants. If you suspect any problems, don’t wait! The sooner we catch issues, the easier and cheaper they are to fix.

In this procedure, we’ll carefully check the implant’s condition and address any worries right away. This might include a deep clean, removing any infected parts, or even replacing the implant. Every case is unique, so the treatment method varies. Rest assured you’ll get a plan that fits you perfectly. Acting early not only boosts the chances of success, but also keeps discomfort and costs down.
