Meet Dr. Jenny Park

I’ve always been drawn to the intricate complexity of orthodontic treatment planning and the unique challenges each case presents. What inspires me most is the meaningful impact orthodontic care can have on a patient’s confidence, health, and overall quality of life. I truly enjoy creating personalized treatment plans that help each patient achieve a smile they feel proud of.

One of the most rewarding parts of being an orthodontist is the opportunity to build lasting relationships with my patients and their families. I love getting to interact with them every day and being part of their journey from start to finish. It’s an incredible privilege to see a patient smile confidently at the end of treatment and know I played a role in that transformation.

Dr. Jenny Park, DMD, MS

When not in the office I love spending time outdoors and being active. I enjoy cooking and baking at home most recently mastering sourdough bread, canelé, and cherry chocolate chip cookie recipes and spending hours at the beach with a good book.

Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists

The Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists is a regional professional organization for orthodontists and one of the eight constituent organizations of the American Association of Orthodontists. Founded in 1926, it represents more than 1,300 orthodontists across Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, western Pennsylvania, and the Canadian province of Ontario

The Michigan Association of Orthodontists (MAO) is the statewide professional organization representing orthodontic specialists throughout Michigan. It serves as the voice of the orthodontic profession at the state level, advocating for both orthodontists and their patients on issues related to healthcare policy, licensing, regulation, and patient care.
