After Before What’s the difference between a dentist and an orthodontist? An orthodontist is a dental specialist who has completed additional years of training, specifically in orthodontics and the safe and healthy movement of teeth and jaws. This formalized education beyond general dental school makes doctors specialists on how to move your teeth and jaws safely, into a beautiful and healthy smile you’ll love.

At Central Lakes Orthodontics, our goal is to exceed your expectations by delivering beautiful smiles and exceptional patient service. Our doctors’ specialized education, skills, and expertise make them highly qualified partners for properly diagnosing orthodontic concerns, recommending the most effective treatment and timing, and guiding an individualized and pleasant treatment journey and smile transformation.
