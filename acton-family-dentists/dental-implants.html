Are Dental Implants Painful?

The only painful part of the dental implant process is extracting any teeth that the implant will replace. However, during the extraction step, we will use anesthesia to make sure you are comfortable at all times. You are also able to take over-the-counter pain medications such as ibuprofen to help manage the pain.

Caring For Your Dental Implants

Some discomfort is normal after your dental implants have been placed. You will likely experience minor bleeding and minor pain near the implant site. Our office recommends that patients take Motrin for 24-48 hours after their procedure. If the bleeding persists for multiple days, it is best to contact our office. Swelling is also normal after treatment, and placing an ice pack on your cheek for 10 minutes will help. It is important to care for your dental implants so they can last as long as possible. Remember to treat them like normal teeth brush and floss them twice a day!
