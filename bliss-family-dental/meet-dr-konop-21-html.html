Meet Dr. Konop

My philosophy is to provide quality dental care in a peaceful, comfortable environment. I feel it is important to cultivate relationships with my patients in order to develop a customized plan to achieve and maintain their oral health. I want my patients to feel confident with their smiles.

Since 1988, I have called Minnesota my home. I attended the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry where I earned my DDS degree. After I graduated I practiced with a large group practice prior to Bliss Family Dental. This experience offered me the opportunity to work with and learn from many talented dentists. I also enjoy taking classes in continuing dental education. This allows me to stay on top of the newest techniques and technologies.
