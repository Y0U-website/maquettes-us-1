Dana first joined All About Smiles back in 2006 with Dr. Oliver. She started out as a dental assistant, but in 2013 she decided to go back to school to become a Dental Hygienist. She graduated as a Restorative Hygienist and takes her restorative work very seriously. Dana is always eager to learn new techniques and tips to keep improving her skills. She genuinely loves what she does, and it really shows — her restorations are consistently excellent.
