At Amaus Dental Services, we welcome patients from all backgrounds, nationalities, and religious affiliations. Because we are all volunteers, we are able to provide professional dental service free of charge. Patients travel to Amaus from all over Central New York and beyond. Our operatories are equipped with state-of-the-art dental technology. We require only that patients be uninsured and over…

Amaus Dental Services opened in 2014. We are a ministry of the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception. Our founders, Msgr. Neal Quartier; Dr. Lynn-Beth Satterly, MD; and Dr. David Dasher, DDS, recognized that many in the neighborhood could not afford to see a dentist.
