Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at (315) 802-6741 if you cannot find an answer to your question.

Amaus Dental Services provides free dental care for adults without dental insurance. Our Dentists, Dental Assistants, Hygienists, and Office workers are all volunteers. We are a ministry of Syracuse’s Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception.

Exams, x-rays, fillings, cleanings, routine extractions, and some other procedures recommended by the dentist. At your first appointment, the dentist will take x-rays, provide a thorough examination, and draft a Treatment Plan. The office volunteers will, then, schedule you accordingly. Amaus Dental Services does not offer dentures, implants, orthodontia, and other complex, costly services. Because we see patients by appointment only, we are not, unfortunately, able to provide rapid care necessary to treat emergencies

Any patient eighteen or older, without any form of dental insurance, including Medicaid. To locate a Medicaid Dental provider, please visit the NYS Department of Health Website https://www.health.ny.gov/diseases/aids/general/resources/dental_resource_directory/medicaid_medicare.htm Patients not fluent in English are welcome, but must bring their own translator.

Telephone: 315-802-6741. To speak with an office volunteer, call Tuesday afternoon (1:00-5:00 p.m.) or leave a voicemail and we will call you back.

In downtown Syracuse at 259 East Onondaga St. or 421 South Warren St. For security concerns, the door is kept locked. When you arrive for your appointment, call the number on the door, and the office volunteer will let you in. Because we do not have a parking lot, you must park in the metered parking on the street or in a parking lot or garage close by.
