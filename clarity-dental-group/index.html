With proper care, dental implants are designed to last 15 years or more, and many patients enjoy results that last decades. Longevity depends on factors such as oral hygiene, overall health, and routine dental visits. At Clarity Dental Group, we provide clear guidance on how to care for your implants to help ensure long-term success.

The implant dentistry process involves several steps. Patients begin with a consultation and personalized treatment plan. Implant placement is followed by a healing period, known as osseointegration, where the post bonds to the bone. After healing, the final implant restoration is attached. Caring for permanent dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brushing twice daily, flossing, and maintaining regular dental exams are essential. During professional cleanings, non-metal instruments should be used to protect the surface of your restoration. For patients who grind their teeth, a custom night guard may be recommended to prevent damage.
