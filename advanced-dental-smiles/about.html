About

After earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Rutgers University’s School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Bakun completed her Residency at Lincoln Medical Center – Weill Cornell Medical College.

Bringing a gentle, patient-first approach to every visit, she’s passionate about helping individuals and families achieve strong, healthy smiles through prevention, education, and personalized care. She’s also committed to supporting your long-term oral health and offers a wide range of general, cosmetic, and restorative services to help you love your smile.

When she isn’t caring for smiles, Dr. Bakun stays active with hiking, running, and snowboarding.

Dr. Ahmed Mostafa

Credentials

Originally from Egypt, Dr. Mostafa brings over 15 years of dental experience across four countries and is a graduate of Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry. A valued part of our team since 2024, he’s known for his calm demeanor, empathetic approach, and ability to put patients at ease.

He enjoys helping patients understand their oral health condition and feel empowered about their treatment options, and from fillings and crowns to smile makeovers and implant services, he’s committed to delivering high quality care that’s always focused on your comfort and experience.

In his free time, Dr. Mostafa loves being with his wife and three children, and spending time at the gym.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine, Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry

Dr. Dilip Dudhat

Credentials

Dr. Dudhat earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry and has decades of experience in caring diligently for smiles. Providing full-scope dental services, including preventive, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, he’s passionate about continuing education and is a proud member of the American Dental Association, the Pennsylvania Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the International College of Dentists, and more.

From routine care to complex needs, Dr. Dudhat is committed to helping you and your loved ones achieve optimal oral health and smile aesthetics through minimally invasive, comprehensive, and progressive dentistry. Along with the support of his caring team, he will work closely with you to fulfill your unique vision for your smile and guide you to the right solutions for long-term success and satisfaction.

Away from dentistry, Dr. Dudhat is a devoted family man who loves spending time with his wife and children. He’s a die-hard sports fan who enjoys cheering on the Phillies, Flyers, and the Eagles, and since 2021, he’s proudly served as the Official Dentist of the Philadelphia Eagles !

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine, Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry
