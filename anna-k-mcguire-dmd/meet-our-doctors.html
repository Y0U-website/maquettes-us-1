Dr. Anna McGuire

Dr. Anna McGuire is a 1988 Centerville High School graduate. She then attended Rider University in Lawrenceville, NJ graduating in 1993. She graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts in 1997 and then completed her General Practice Residency in 1998 at Miami Valley Hospital.

Dr. McGuire is certified in Bioclear. The Bioclear method involves wrapping a tooth with a heated composite resin. Specially shaped matrices hold the composite in place and ensure that the treatment produces a perfectly shaped tooth. It usually takes the place of a regular composite filling, filling in gaps in the teeth, and helps avoid root canals and it takes just one appointment to complete!

She lives in Washington Township with her three children. Dr. McGuire is on staff at Kettering Hospital and Miami Valley Hospital.

Dr. Shikha Sohal received her BDS degree from India and her dental degree (DMD) from Boston University. She loves all aspects of dentistry, especially restorative, endodontics, oral surgery and removable/fixed prosthodontics.
