About CLOUD FAMILY DENTAL

At our dental office we offer a complete range of dental services so patients can stay with the care they know and trust for a long time. From when you walk in the door until you leave, you feel like you are receiving the best care. It’s a perfect blend of old school and new school. We will even call and check on our patients after procedures, as well as having the Dr’s personal cell phone number for any issues that may arise after hours.

We have a beautiful office which all have a great view to see while being treated. We have bird feeders to attract the beautiful birds that come in the fall and winter seasons. We want it to feel like you’re home at our office.

Modern tech is important to the practice: Right now we have the CEREC, digital radiography, Cone Beam Scanner, Diagnodent cavity detection, Operating Microscope, employees always wear magnification, intra-oral cameras, and TV’s in all operating rooms so we can show pictures and videos to our patients.

If you have any questions or want to make an appointment, you can call our office today. We would love to answer any questions or concerns that you might have while seeking a new dentist for all your dental needs.
