He enjoys spending time with his family - hiking, running and playing basketball.

Fun fact: His wife is also a practicing General Dentist. Her name is Dr. Blakely Cingolani.

He has three children Ethan, Abbie, and Asher who are his pride and joy! Dr. Kinard is certified in the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.
