Frequently Asked Questions

Dentist Foxboro

How often should I visit the dentist?

Dr. Pushee recommends a visit every six months for a routine checkup and cleaning. However, if you have specific concerns or conditions, more frequent visits may be necessary.

What should I do in case of a dental emergency?

Contact Charm Dental immediately. We offer emergency appointments and will do our best to see you as soon as possible.

Are dental X-rays safe?

Yes, dental X-rays are a safe and vital tool that helps us diagnose underlying issues that can’t be seen during a regular dental exam.

How can I improve the whiteness of my teeth?

We offer various treatments to improve the brightness of your smile, including professional teeth whitening and advice on avoiding stain-causing foods and drinks.

Can I get dental care for my whole family at Charm Dental?

Absolutely! We provide dental care for patients of all ages, from children to the elderly.
