How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental visits are crucial to ensure the health and longevity of your implants.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants are a safe and well-established treatment. They are the closest you can get to healthy, natural teeth and are typically made from biocompatible materials like titanium.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most people find dental implant surgery very manageable. Local anesthesia is used during the procedure, and most patients report that implants involve less pain than a tooth extraction.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth, including brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants can vary based on several factors, including the number of teeth being replaced and the need for any additional procedures. Dr. Pushee can provide you with a detailed estimate during your consultation at Charm Dental in Foxboro.
