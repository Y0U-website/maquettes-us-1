Dentist David Huang

Dr. David Huang

Dr. David Huang was raised in Cleveland, OH, and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine. He is a member of the American Dental Association and the Ohio Dental Association.

On his days off, Dr. Huang enjoys attending seminars, study clubs, and conferences to keep up to date with the latest changes and techniques in dentistry.

When Dr. Huang is not practicing dentistry, he likes to explore new recipes at home and travel.

“I am blessed to work in such a wonderful community as Centerville. There is nothing more rewarding than sharing smiles with my patients, and I look forward to working with you.”
