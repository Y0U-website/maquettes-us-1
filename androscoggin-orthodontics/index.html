The practice

Comprehensive care tailored to your smile, your lifestyle, and your goals.

Braces remain one of the most reliable and effective ways to achieve a straight, healthy smile. At Androscoggin Orthodontics, we offer both traditional metal braces and ceramic braces, giving patients flexible options that deliver consistent, confident results.

Our clear aligner program brings something most orthodontic offices can’t offer: in-house fabrication powered by 3D scanning and printing technology. Rather than relying on traditional impressions, we use precise digital scans to map your teeth and manufacture your custom aligners right in our own lab. (No more goopy molds!) That means a faster, more comfortable process, fewer outside delays, and…

Clear aligners are a great fit for patients who want a discreet, removable option for straightening their smile without interrupting their daily life

The earlier we identify potential orthodontic issues, the easier they are to address. Our early treatment options are designed specifically for younger patients, allowing Dr. Bauer and our team to guide proper dental development and prevent more complex problems down the road. Investing in your child’s smile today can mean simpler, shorter treatment later.

For patients whose needs go beyond traditional orthodontic treatment, our team brings together orthodontic expertise and surgical proficiency to restore both function and aesthetics. We work closely with patients to plan and execute treatment that achieves optimal results, no matter the complexity.

Androscoggin Orthodontics is a warm, welcoming practice rooted in the belief that excellent orthodontic care should feel personal. Every day, Dr. Bauer and our team work hard to exceed expectations through kindness, respect, and genuine clinical skill. We want our patients to look forward to coming in, feel at ease during treatment, and leave with outcomes they love.
