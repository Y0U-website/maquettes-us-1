Our Process

Our Process 1 Call Us to Schedule Your Consultation Your journey begins with a phone call. Our friendly team will help schedule a consultation to meet with the doctor.

Having your dental insurance card handy will allow us to look-up your dental coverage before you arrive.

Text Message Appointment Confirmation

You should receive a text message confirming your appointment time. Save this number as our text messaging system also allows you to send messages at anytime securely to our team.

If you did not have your insurance card with you when you scheduled your appointment, you may text us a picture of the front and back of the card to this number and a copy will automatically be saved to your chart.

Visit Our Office & Meet the Team

When you arrive, you’ll be welcomed into a relaxed, family-friendly environment. You’ll meet our team, tour the office, and get comfortable before we begin.

We believe orthodontic care should feel personal—not rushed or intimidating.

Digital X-Rays & Facial Imaging

We use advanced digital imaging to take x-rays and photographs of your teeth and face. These tools allow us to see the full picture and plan your treatment with precision and accuracy.

Everything is done efficiently and safely—with your comfort in mind.

Interactive Smile Evaluation

After Dr. Beaudoin examines your teeth and dental images, you’ll sit down to discuss your goals and design a treatment plan tailored specifically to you.

Common questions like differences in treatment outcomes with braces or Invisalign, estimated treatment time, financial options and insurance will be discussed. No pressure, no guesswork. Just honest guidance.

Starting Treatment

Once your plan is finalized, we’ll schedule your treatment start. Whether you’re getting braces or Invisalign, our team ensures everything is placed comfortably and correctly.

We’ll also guide you through care instructions so you feel confident from day one.

Progress Visits & Your Final Smile

You’ll visit us periodically for adjustments and progress checks. These appointments keep treatment on track and allow us to make fine-tuned improvements.

And then—the best part. A confident, healthy, beautiful smile that lasts a lifetime.
