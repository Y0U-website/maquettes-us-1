What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium screws that are inserted into the jaw and topped with a natural-looking prosthetic crown. Dental implants are able to bond with the jawbone, providing the most stable replacement method for missing teeth and one that can perform just as well!

Dental implants allow patients to eat and chew any foods without restriction and, because they are fixed, they won’t slip out when you are talking, laughing and smiling.
