Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You could be a suitable candidate for implant dentures if:

Even if you are unsure whether you qualify for implant dentures, we still encourage you to book a consultation. Most people can undergo this treatment, either immediately or after some preparatory care.

  • You are missing all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches.
  • Your jawbone is strong enough to support dental implants.
  • You are in fair oral and overall health.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Some of the most remarkable benefits of implant dentures include:

  • Strength and stability. Implant dentures do not slip and slide around the mouth, and they are strong enough to stand up to virtually any food, including tough and chewy items.
  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants stimulate the surrounding bone tissue, helping to keep it healthy and strong.
  • Beauty and confidence. We carefully design implant dentures with the goal of making them look as natural as possible.
  • With proper care, dental implants may last a lifetime. The dentures on top of them may also endure for many years.

Implant Denture FAQs

What are implant dentures?

Implant dentures in Rochester are dental prosthetics that combine the convenience of dentures with the durability of dental implants . They are ideal for anyone who wants to replace an entire arch of teeth, but don’t want to deal with the hassle of removable dentures.

How many teeth can I replace with implant dentures?

Implant dentures in Rochester can replace an entire arch of teeth in just one prosthesis and are ideal for anyone who wants to restore multiple teeth in a row.

How long does the entire implant dentures process take?

The entire process for getting implant dentures in Rochester will vary depending on the patient, but typically takes a few months, including several appointments with your dentist. At your first appointment, you’ll receive a full evaluation, and then if you require no preliminary treatment, you’ll return for your dental implant placement procedure. Next, you’ll return to have your denture fitted, and then once it has been customized for you at the dental lab, you’ll visit our office one last time to have it attached to your dental implants!

How do I care for my implant dentures?

One of the best things about implant dentures in Rochester is how easy it is to care for them! Unlike traditional dentures, implant dentures can remain in your mouth 24/7, so you don’t need to remove them for cleaning or soaking. You can clean them by brushing them twice a day and flossing daily, just as you would with your real teeth.

Furthermore, even if you have no remaining natural teeth, be sure to visit us every six months for your regular preventive oral health checkups and cleanings. These are our opportunities to verify that your dental implants and dentures are in good condition, as well as to check the health of your jawbones, gums, and any remaining teeth.
