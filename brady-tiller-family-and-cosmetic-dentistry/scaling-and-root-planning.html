Who is more prone to get periodontal issues?

Even though anyone can have periodontal issues but the following factors are primarily responsible for this problem :

  • Poor maintenance of oral hygiene.
  • Poor nutritional conditions.
  • Aging.
  • Changes in hormones.
  • Consumption of tobacco.
  • Family history of periodontal problems.
  • Any other medical conditions.
