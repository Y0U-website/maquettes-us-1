About us.

At Braswell Family Dentistry we tailor our dental services to your individual needs. No two patients are the same, so neither is their treatment. From preventive care to implant surgery to full mouth rehabilitation, we deliver skilled and comprehensive dental care for you and your family through efficient visits and a relaxing environment.

And if you or a loved one are in dental pain, same-day emergency visits are available to quickly resolve the problem. Additionally, we respect and understand that many patients experience dental anxiety, which is why we also offer nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation services to alleviate your concerns during procedures. For these reasons, we know that you have come to the right place for your dental care.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Temiko Braswell

Dr. Braswell is a phi beta kappa, magnum cum laude graduate of Howard University with a major in microbiology and minor in chemistry. She received her doctor of dental surgery degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (GO TARHEELS!!!). She proudly served as a dentist with the United States Air Force.

This opportunity introduced her to the Warner Robins area. After serving four years with the Air Force, the chance to serve our community in the private sector presented itself. This opportunity has been a blessing and honor. One of her deepest desires is to positively influence those whom she contacts. God has given her an amazing opportunity to serve his people and she sincerely honors His blessing. Her blessings include two gorgeous girls and a loving, supportive husband.
