About Cape Fear Orthodontics

Welcome to Cape Fear Orthodontics, your premier destination for exceptional orthodontic care in Leland. With a commitment to excellence and a passion for transforming smiles, our experienced team of orthodontists is dedicated to providing personalized and top-quality treatments to patients of all ages.

At Cape Fear Orthodontics, we are dedicated to providing you with the highest level of care. We offer fully customized brackets and treatment plans tailored to your unique needs. Our commitment to excellence includes exploring the use of advanced technologies like LightForce brackets (pending), ensuring precise and personalized orthodontic solutions.

Your satisfaction is our top priority. We go above and beyond to deliver exceptional customer service throughout your orthodontic journey. Our friendly and knowledgeable team is here to answer your questions, address your concerns, and provide a comfortable and welcoming environment for you and your family.

Say goodbye to messy and uncomfortable goop impressions. We embrace digital technology to capture highly accurate images of your teeth, eliminating the need for traditional impressions. This streamlined process ensures a more comfortable and efficient experience during your orthodontic treatment.

We believe in staying at the forefront of technology to enhance your orthodontic experience. Our practice is fully digital, from treatment planning to progress monitoring. This digital approach allows for precise treatment planning, seamless communication, and efficient workflows, ensuring optimal results and convenience for our patients.

Even after your orthodontic treatment is complete, we continue to prioritize your comfort and convenience. With our intraoral scanner, we can take detailed digital impressions of your teeth to create custom retainers. Say goodbye to uncomfortable traditional impressions and enjoy a more comfortable and accurate fitting process for your retainers.

We understand that your time is valuable. That’s why we strive to make your orthodontic experience as convenient as possible. We offer flexible scheduling options, efficient treatment plans, and a range of orthodontic solutions tailored to meet your lifestyle needs. Our goal is to provide you with the highest level of convenience while achieving your dream smile.
