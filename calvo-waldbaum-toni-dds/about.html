Toni Calvo Waldbaum, DDS

Dr. Toni Waldbaum’s passion for dentistry started as a young girl watching her father, Sidney, start the family practice in Burien. With her mother as the office manager, Toni witnessed the importance of family working together to support and care for each other.

After graduating from the University of Washington Dental School, Toni joined the family practice in 1988. She and her mother, Barb, continue the family business in the tradition of compassionate, gentle dentistry and her practice thrives from her ability to ease patients’ anxiety.

Patients travel from all over the city, including out of state, to continue their care with Dr. Waldbaum. She is deeply connected to the community—growing up in Burien from elementary through Highline High School—and considers her patients an extension of her family. Toni met her husband, Ron, while attending the University of Washington, where her sons Solomon, Gabriel and daughter, Shayna, also studied.

Dr. Waldbaum is committed to continually learning new skills and ways to provide excellent oral health to her patients in the most delicate style possible. Her practice is built upon offering the best care with the latest materials, instruments, and techniques, combined with knowledge gained from years of experience. Dr. Toni Waldbaum is a member of the ADA (American Dental Association) and the Seattle-King County Dental Society.

Our team

Our outstanding staff is committed to providing the best care possible to our patients. Whether working with us for over 34 years or new to the office, our team members feel like they’ve always been part of the family.

They all work seamlessly together to provide the best care possible and keep each other informed of the latest trends to improve our knowledge in the field collectively.

We have a lot of fun and try to pass on our enthusiasm for good oral health to our patients.
