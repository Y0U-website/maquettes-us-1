The practice

We are proud to offer high-quality dental care to our new and legacy patients to achieve the brightest, whitest smile. We provide a full range of services, from preventative care and restorative services that maintain healthy teeth and gums, to cosmetic services that can repair damaged teeth, improve your natural bite, and boost confidence.

We rely on state-of-the-art technology to help monitor your dental health and use digital x-rays , which decreases exposure to radiation by 70 % .

When you visit our clinic, you are part of our family. Bringing gentle family dentistry to the Burien and Normandy Park community since 1965, the Calvo family has a proud legacy of individualizing care for our patients and partnering in your health.

Dr. Toni Calvo Waldbaum joined her father’s practice in 1988 and continues to use the latest technology and kindest approach to enhance your smile.

We specialize in gentle family care for all ages , offering personalized pediatric and geriatric care in addition to general dentistry. Our patient staff is trained to work with dental anxiety and can make your visit an enjoyable experience.
