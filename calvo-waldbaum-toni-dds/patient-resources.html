Our cancellation policy

We understand that your time is valuable and unforeseen circumstances may arise, where you are no longer able to make it to your appointment with our office. If you need to cancel your appointment, please call our office directly with at least 48 hours notice or more so that we may make accommodations that best suit you as well as our practice and staff.
