All-On

The All-on-4 method of permanent attachment will restore up to 90 % of the natural chewing function. Traditional, removable dentures will restore only up t0 20 % of this function.

The dentures anchored to the implants will not slip or move within the mouth. Because the All-on-4 is a permanent attachment, there will be less food restrictions.

There will be savings in both money and time. The All-on-4 procedure eliminates the need for multiple appointments for adjustments and re-fittings. The full denture set can be placed in one appointment.

Unlike removable dental prosthetics, implants actually strengthen the jawbone and reduce the potential for bone loss or osteoporosis.

A natural smile will be restored sooner because of the shortened healing time and the one-appointment placement of the denture set.

The natural smile regained by the All-on-4 implant procedure will improve both lifestyle and self-image. One cannot experience a better lifestyle without an improved self-image.
