Implant Surgery

At Atlantic Periodontics & Implants, our specialists focus exclusively on the placement of dental implants. The implant surgery process involves making a small incision in the gum tissue, preparing the bone, and inserting the implant. Once the implant is securely placed, the gum tissue is sutured, and the healing process begins.

After the implant has integrated with the bone, patients will visit another office for an abutment and custom-made dental prosthetic to be attached in order to complete the restoration.

Preliminary Treatment

A CBCT or 3D X-ray will be taken in our office to measure your bone height and width for suitability for an implant before anything is ever done. We will digitally plan and place your implant before treating you to insure accuracy.

Bone Grafting

In some cases, patients may require a bone graft before receiving a dental implant. Bone grafting is a procedure that helps to increase the volume of the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for the implant. Our team of specialists will assess your individual needs and determine if bone grafting is necessary to ensure a successful implant procedure.

Sinus Lifts

A sinus lift, also known as sinus augmentation, is a procedure that may be required for patients with insufficient bone in the upper jaw. During a sinus lift, the sinus membrane is carefully elevated, and bone graft material is placed beneath it to create more bone volume. This procedure helps to prepare the area for dental implant placement and ensure a successful outcome.
