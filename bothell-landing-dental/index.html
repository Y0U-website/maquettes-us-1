The practice

No matter how long it’s been since your last visit to the dentist, there’s never any judgment at Bothell Landing Dental.

Our goal is always to provide exceptional patient care in a comfortable environment. We will listen with compassion so that you are heard and understood. This is part of what makes us different, part of what drives us, and all of what you deserve. We are here for you, to get to know you, to take care of you, and help with the best in dental treatment together.

We also use the most proven dental technology and stay on top of the evolution in dentistry in a responsible and safe way. Extensive training and education allows us to provide the best that dental care has to offer.

We are fortunate to provide care in an area with family as a focus and we extend that to our practice. We see many generations of the same families and take pride in getting to know you and your loved ones. We value these relationships and take an interest in you and your family, and not just as patients. We love taking the best care of you while learning more about you.
