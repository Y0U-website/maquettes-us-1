Meet Us

Dr. Casey Carmody, DDS

While growing up in Yakima, WA, I was infused with a sense of community. Say hello to the person walking by you on the street, help your neighbors, and be there for your friends and family unconditionally. You get to know people on a personal level with sincere interest. I take these values and carry them into my practice of dentistry. It is my goal that each patient feels genuinely cared for, looked after, and valued. People come in with different issues, personalities, anxieties, and needs. However, human compassion comes in only one way…the right way.

I am an avid sports fan, following all Seattle sports teams and especially the Washington Huskies. I also enjoy playing basketball (rarely stepping inside the 3-point line), cooking, grilling, and spending time with family and friends. I have an amazing ability for useless sports information and knowing college mascots. Maybe it will help with my goal of someday being on Jeopardy.

My wife, Ara, is also a dentist and we cherish our time with our three young children.

I am very lucky to love what I do, and my absolute favorite part is building long-term relationships in our practice while having the privilege to care for our patients and the positive impact we can make on your oral health.
