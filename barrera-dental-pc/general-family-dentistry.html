Minimally invasive dental care is starting to grow in popularity across the USA. If you have an aversion to drills, this will appeal to you! The goal is to treat bacterial infections before there is a need to make any repairs. Simply put we will remove the bacteria before the cavities ever occur.

Regular dental exams are essential. We will look for any signs of damage or disease during a dental exam. If specific dental issues are caught early, we can minimize their impact. We will also recommend improvements you can make to your dental hygiene.

As a dental provider, we believe you deserve safe treatment whenever you visit our clinic. That’s why we offer mercury-free fillings for our patients needing an effective and safe solution for tooth decay. This innovative dental alternative is less invasive, easier to repair, and safer for your body and the environment. Our mercury-free fillings are even comparable or can last longer than amalgam fillings. Your oral health deserves the best, and that’s Barrera Dental PC.

Sometimes, periodontal disease can damage the bone and tissue surrounding your teeth. Here at Barrera Dental PC, we can perform a procedure to help regenerate this lost tissue and slow the spread of the damage. We do this by removing the harmful bacteria which is causing the damage. There are a variety of different options when it comes to periodontal treatment. We will work with you to find a solution to your dental issues that works for you and your insurance policy.

As advocates for oral health, our team is dedicated to providing services that focus on today’s smile and your smile years down the road. By taking preventative measures like stain removal, fluoride applications, adolescent sealings, and denture adjustments, you can keep your smile healthy and avoid future dental problems that can be painful and costly to repair.
