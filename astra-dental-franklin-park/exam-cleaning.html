Regular Cleaning Deep Cleaning Gum Disease Treatment

We offer the full spectrum of preventive and periodontal care under one roof in Franklin Park.

Regular Dental Cleaning Removes plaque and tartar buildup that brushing can’t reach. Recommended every 6 months to prevent cavities and gum disease. Quick, comfortable, and essential for long-term oral health.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing) Recommended when gum disease is present. Removes bacteria and tartar from below the gumline and smooths tooth roots to help gums heal and reattach. Stops gum disease progression.

Gum Disease Treatment Periodontal (gum) disease can lead to tooth loss if left untreated. We provide targeted treatments including deep cleaning, antibiotic therapy, and maintenance cleanings to restore gum health.
