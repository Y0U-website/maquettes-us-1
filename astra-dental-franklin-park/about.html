About Astra Dental

Advanced Family Dentistry in Franklin Park, IL

Astra Dental opened in 2025, bringing together two experienced doctors with over 10 years each of dental expertise. We deliver state-of-the-art, compassionate care to the diverse Franklin Park community.

Expert Care, Personal Touch

Dr. Rushi Sanghani, DDS University of Southern California Dean’s List 2012 & 2013 Dr. Sanghani is a dedicated general dentist who blends advanced training with a calm, conversational approach to care. A proud Trojan, he earned his DDS from USC in 2013. With 12+ years of experience, Dr. Sanghani has completed advanced programs in implant dentistry, orthodontics, and cosmetic procedures bringing cutting-edge techniques to every patient visit.

Dr. Darshil Ranpariya, DMD Boston University School of Dental Medicine, 2012 Dr. R (as patients love to call him) is passionate about dentistry and constantly keeps himself updated with advances in the field to deliver the best, most creative solutions. Member of the ADA, Illinois State Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, and Dentalxp. In his spare time he enjoys reading, sports, running, and family.
