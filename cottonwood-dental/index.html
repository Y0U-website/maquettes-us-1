Our Services Cottonwood Dental embraces a holistic approach to dental care, focusing on biological and functional dentistry. This approach recognizes the interconnected relationship between mouth and body.

Improve breastfeeding challenges and speech issues using laser technology for quick and effective results.

Our minimally invasive approach uses laser dentistry for frenectomies, gum reshaping, cavity treatment, and more to enhance your comfort and recovery time.

We harness ozone’s natural properties to promote healing, reduce bacteria, and enhance overall dental health.

We assess your oral health to detect sensitivity to dental materials to ensure treatments align with whole-body health.

Metal-free, biocompatible tooth replacements offer durability and a natural appearance while promoting holistic wellness.

What Is a Biological Dentist? Dr. Brown takes a functional and biological approach to dentistry. What does this mean? What happens in your mouth can affect your whole body, and what’s happening in your body can affect your mouth.
