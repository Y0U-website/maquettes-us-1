Meet the Team at Cottonwood Dental

Dr. Rachel Brown-McDonald, DDS

Dr. Brown-McDonald’s journey into dentistry began during her high school and college years when she worked as a dental assistant. This early exposure allowed her to appreciate the detailed, individualized care dentistry offers, sparking a deep interest in the field.

Focus on Functional and Biological Dental Care

With a passion for dentistry rooted in her desire to help patients achieve their health goals, Dr. Brown-McDonald focuses on enhancing oral health by balancing the oral microbiome. She has a particular affinity for helping mothers and babies overcome nursing challenges and assisting those with speech, feeding, and sleep issues, as well as helping children and adults achieve better quality sleep and reduced muscle tension. Her work often involves collaboration with other functional health providers to offer comprehensive care.

Dr. Brown-McDonald is a respected member of The Breathe Institute (TBI) and serves as a Breathe Ambassador. She is also an accredited member of the International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology (IAOMT), trained in the Safe Mercury Amalgam Removal Technique (SMART) , and a fellow of the American Academy for Oral Systemic Health (AAOSH).

Personal Life and Interests

Outside her professional life, Dr. Brown-McDonald cherishes time with her husband, Austin, and their two children, Mara and Aiden. She loves exploring the world with her family, whether traveling, enjoying new foods, or simply engaging in outdoor activities. These days, she finds joy in discovering ways to reduce toxic exposures at work and home, aligning with her commitment to a healthier lifestyle.

Dr. Brown-McDonald continues to be inspired by dentistry s unique opportunities to meaningfully impact her patients lives and strives to offer tailored and compassionate care at Cottonwood Dental.
