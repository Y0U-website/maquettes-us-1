The People Who Shape the Experience

A team united by precision, professionalism, and a shared commitment to exceptional patient care.

Dr. Isela Cantuarias, DDS

Cosmetic & General Dentist

Originally from Lima, Peru, Dr. Isela Cantuarias earned her dental degree before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at New York University and further training at Hackensack University Medical Center. Her background combines advanced clinical education with a strong commitment to personalized patient care.

With a passion for cosmetic dentistry and Digital Smile Design , Dr. Cantuarias focuses on creating natural, refined smiles tailored to each patient’s features and goals. She is known for combining aesthetic precision with a warm, supportive approach that helps patients feel comfortable and confident throughout their experience.

Dr. Charles Ro, DMD

Dentist & Implant Specialist

Dr. Charles Ro specializes in dental implants and advanced restorative treatments, combining years of clinical experience with advanced implant training from New York University. He completed his residency at Hackensack University Medical Center, where he also mentored future dentists.

Using technologies such as 3D imaging and digital treatment planning , Dr. Ro focuses on delivering precise, long-lasting implant solutions tailored to each patient’s needs. His approach prioritizes function, comfort, and results designed to support long-term oral health and confidence.

Education & Certifications

Qualifications That Support a Higher Standard of Care

Continuous education and advanced certifications allow us to stay at the forefront of dentistry, ensuring every treatment is guided by proven techniques, modern technology, and a commitment to long-term patient health.

Dr. Isela Cantuarias’

Professional Certifications

Training at NYU Rosenthal provided a strong foundation in aesthetic dentistry, advanced techniques, and evidence-based care, supporting treatment decisions that are precise, thoughtful, and focused on long-term health.

The Kois Center is recognized worldwide for its expertise in smile design, function, facial harmony, and long-term treatment planning. This education helps ensure every smile looks natural, feels comfortable, and is designed to last.

Membership in the AACD reflects a commitment to cosmetic excellence, continuing education, and high standards in smile design, helping ensure care that is thoughtful, natural-looking, and centered on long-term results.

Dr. Charles Ro’s

Professional Certifications

Advanced training through the Resnik Implant Institute supports a deeper approach to implant diagnosis, bone evaluation, surgical planning, and restoration. This helps create implant treatment that is carefully planned for stability, comfort, function, and a natural-looking result that supports long-term health.

The Misch Fellowship reflects advanced education in implant dentistry, bone biology, bite function, and surgical-restorative planning. Patients benefit from care that considers the full picture: how the implant heals, functions, supports the bite, and contributes to a confident, lasting smile.

The MaxiCourse Implant Program provides comprehensive implant training from diagnosis and treatment planning to surgery and final restoration. This allows each implant case to be approached with more precision, predictability, and attention to comfort, natural appearance, and long-term success.

Beyond Dentistry

Care That Extends Into the Community

At Complete Dental Works , we believe meaningful care goes beyond the walls of our practice. Through ongoing partnerships, charitable initiatives, and community support programs, we remain committed to making a positive impact where it matters most.

St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital

We proudly support St. Jude through monthly contributions that help advance care and research for children facing serious illnesses. Their mission reflects our shared values of compassion, hope, and long-term impact.

Champion of Literacy Award

Complete Dental Works was honored with the Champion of Literacy Award by Galaxy Press for supporting initiatives that encourage reading, education, and youth development through the Boys & Girls Clubs.

St. Peter’s Haven Clifton Food Pantry

Our Complete Dental Works family is proud to volunteer at St. Peter’s Haven, a place dedicated to serving our hungry and homeless neighbors. Showing up for our community is a humbling and heartwarming experience. Because caring for people goes beyond the dental chair.

Heroes for Children & NJ Children’s Alliance

Through fundraising initiatives and community events, we support organizations dedicated to helping children and families affected by abuse by providing advocacy, healing resources, and protection programs across New Jersey.

Agustinos Foundation & Food Assistance Programs

Through partnerships with organizations in Peru and local food support initiatives, we help provide meals and resources to underserved communities with limited access to basic necessities.
