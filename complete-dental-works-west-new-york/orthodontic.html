Know Your Options

Not Every Orthodontic Solution Is the Same

The best orthodontic treatment isn’t the same for everyone. That’s why we don’t start with a product, we start with you. By understanding your smile , bite, goals, and daily life, we can guide you toward the option that delivers the most comfortable, effective, and personalized result possible.

Clear Aligners

Virtually invisible trays designed to gradually move your teeth into position without brackets or wires.

  • Want a straighter smile without visible braces.
  • Prefer a removable option that fits their lifestyle.
  • Feel self-conscious about smiling in photos, social events, or professional settings.
  • Have mild to moderate crowding, spacing, or bite concerns.
  • Nearly invisible during treatment
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing
  • Comfortable with no brackets or wires
  • Easy to maintain oral hygiene throughout treatment

Traditional Metal Braces

A time-tested solution that offers maximum control for correcting a wide range of orthodontic and bite concerns. Best for patients who:

  • Need more comprehensive tooth or bite correction.
  • Have crowded, rotated, or difficult-to-move teeth.
  • Want a treatment that works continuously without needing to be removed.
  • Prefer a proven and reliable orthodontic option.
  • Effective for simple and complex cases
  • Constant correction without relying on patient compliance
  • Strong, durable, and highly predictable
  • More comfortable and streamlined than traditional braces of the past

Ceramic Braces

A more discreet alternative to metal braces that blends better with your natural smile while providing similar corrective power. Best for patients who:

  • Want the effectiveness of braces with a less noticeable appearance.
  • Feel uncomfortable with visible metal brackets.
  • Need more correction than clear aligners may provide.
  • Want a balance between aesthetics and performance.
  • Less noticeable than traditional metal braces
  • Effective for many alignment and bite concerns
  • Fixed in place for continuous treatment progress
  • Combines cosmetic appeal with reliable results
