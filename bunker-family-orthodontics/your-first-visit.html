Treatment Options

Bunker Family Orthodontics has the specialized knowledge to consider all possibilities based on variables like your age, possible jaw imbalances, differences in the size of your teeth, and more. We will work with you so you can make the best decision – for your best smile.

Metal braces

Metal braces are the most common, and the Damon Metal Self-Ligating Braces we use are more comfortable than regular braces and have smoother tooth movement, potentially reducing discomfort and appointment durations.

Clear braces

For a more discreet braces option, consider our Damon Clear Self-Ligating Braces . Clear braces are made of transparent materials and are much less visible on your teeth than metal braces.

Clear aligners

Clear aligners are removable, almost invisible, comfortable trays that straighten your teeth like braces. Because you can take them off temporarily, you can eat and drink what you want while in treatment, and brushing/flossing is less of a hassle.
