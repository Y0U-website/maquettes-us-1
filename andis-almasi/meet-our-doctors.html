About Dr. Andis Almasi Teddy Dental

Dr. Almasi is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist. She specializes in behavior management and anxiety relief for children. She and her staff enjoy working with patients with special healthcare needs.

Her educational background, including a Master’s degree in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology and two years of post-doctoral Pediatric Dental training at New York University, enables her to apply new research to ensure her patients receive the most advanced dental care. She has received extensive training to perform lip and tongue tie releases utilizing the most advanced laser technology .

She is very passionate about working with underserved communities and providing dental care for children and oral health education for families.

She is committed to several humanitarian organizations locally and across the globe. She has provided oral healthcare to children in Mexico, Panama, Nicaragua, Belize, Thailand, the Philippines, Cambodia, and Jamaica.

Dr. Almasi Mayes and her husband, Chad Mayes, live in Rancho Mirage with their daughter Mila.

Dr. Astrid Noguera

Dr. Astrid Noguera, a Los Angeles native with a heart as sunny as her hometown. This dedicated General Dentist brings smiles to the Coachella Valley, combining her love for community and dentistry. Her academic journey began at California State University, Northridge, where she dove into the world of biology, emerging with a Bachelor’s degree. Her passion for dentistry led her to the prestigious University of Southern California, where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery, proudly cheering, "Go Trojans!" along the way.

Dr. Astrid thrives cherishes working with people from all walks of life. One of her most rewarding experiences was a mission trip with AYUDA International in Rosarito, where she extended her care to underserved communities, a testament to her compassionate heart. Her commitment to making a difference continued with her involvement with USC’s Dental Humanitarian Outreach Program (DHOP) in Eleuthera, Bahamas, further showcasing her dedication to global dental health.

More than just a skilled dentist, Dr. Astrid values the deep connections she’s forged with patients and colleagues, a reflection of her warm and genuine approach to her profession. When she’s not in the dental office, you can find Dr. Astrid reveling in the joy of life—whether it’s running in the great outdoors, embarking on travel adventures, or cherishing moments with family and friends. Dr. Astrid Noguera is not just a dentist; she’s a beacon of care and community, lighting up the Coachella Valley one smile at a time

Dr. Nineli Zadourian

Meet Dr. Nineli Zadourian, affectionately known as Dr. Nini, Teddy Dental’s beacon of positivity and dental brilliance. Her journey into dentistry began at UC Irvine, leading her to the Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry at USC, where she earned her degree in 2016. Dr. Nini stands out in her field by transforming dental visits into enjoyable experiences for people of all ages.

Dr. Nini’s expertise goes beyond routine dental care; she’s passionate about creating fun and adventurous dental visits for both kids and adults. Her life outside the clinic is as colorful as her personality, filled with activities that range from exploring new places, hiking, cooking, DJing, to dancing. Above all, she values the time spent with her grandmother, embodying a spirit of joy in every aspect of her life.

Her commitment to making a difference shines through her volunteer work with USC’s Dental Humanitarian Outreach Program, where she helps bring smiles to underserved communities worldwide. Known as the coolest auntie to her friends’ children, Dr. Nini has a special place in her heart for kids.

Joining Teddy Dental in January 2023, Dr. Nini has continued to spread happiness and provide exceptional dental care. With fluency in English, Armenian, Farsi, and a bit of Spanish, she is a powerhouse of positivity, dedicated to making dental care a positive experience for everyone. Experience the enjoyable side of dentistry with Dr. Nini at Teddy Dental!
