Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which cosmetic treatment is right for me?

The best option depends on your goals, current dental condition, and long-term considerations. A consultation allows us to walk through each option clearly and recommend what fits best.

Will my results look natural?

Yes. Every treatment is planned with attention to proportion, color, and facial harmony so your smile looks like a refined version of itself.

Is cosmetic dentistry only about appearance?

While aesthetics are the focus, many treatments also improve alignment, function, and overall oral health.

How long do cosmetic results last?

Longevity depends on the treatment and your habits, but we prioritize materials and techniques designed for durability.

Can I combine multiple treatments?

Yes. Many patients benefit from a phased or combined approach, especially with Smile Makeovers.
