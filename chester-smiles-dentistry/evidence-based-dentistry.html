Evidence based dentistry is the integration of three key elements: The best available scientific research
  • Clinical expertise and experience
  • Your individual goals, preferences, and health history

How This Approach Impacts Your Care

Using an evidence-based framework allows for more precise decision-making and helps avoid unnecessary or overly aggressive treatment.

It also helps create consistency—so you can feel confident that your care is based on sound principles, not guesswork.

  • More accurate diagnoses based on measurable data
  • Treatment planning that prioritizes long-term success
  • Conservative care when appropriate, comprehensive care when necessary
  • Clear explanations so you understand the reasoning behind recommendations

Evidence based dentistry is not about doing more—it is about doing what is necessary, at the right time, for the right reasons. By focusing on long-term results, treatment becomes more intentional and less reactive.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does evidence based dentistry mean for me as a patient?

It means your care is based on proven research, clinical expertise, and your individual needs—not trends or assumptions.

Does this approach limit treatment options?

No. It helps refine options so you receive recommendations that are appropriate, effective, and necessary.

Will I still have choices in my treatment?

Yes. We provide guidance and explain options clearly so you can make decisions that align with your goals.

Is evidence based dentistry more conservative?

Often, yes. It prioritizes preserving natural structure whenever possible while still addressing issues effectively.

How does this improve long-term results?

By using proven methods and thoughtful planning, treatment is more predictable and designed to last.
