How Invisalign Works

Who Invisalign Can Help

The good news is that Invisalign can help image-conscious teens and adults with everything from overcrowding to open bites. To find out if you’re a candidate for clear aligners, it’s best to come in for a consultation with Dr. Ulloa so he can learn more about your dental history and smile goals. Until then, however, you can read on to learn more on the topic!

Crowded Teeth

Ideally, your teeth would sit in their perfectly aligned positions. If there simply isn’t enough room in your mouth, however, then your teeth will crowd together instead, overlapping in the process. Unfortunately, this can negatively impact the look, health, and function of your smile, so we strongly recommend coming to our office to find out if you’re a candidate for Invisalign sooner rather than later.

Gaps Between Teeth

If your teeth are spaced out whether there is a prominent gap between your two front teeth or several smaller gaps throughout your smile don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with us. If we determine that you’re a candidate for Invisalign, then we can gradually bring your teeth into alignment with a series of clear, comfortable, and custom-made aligners.

Bite Alignment

Do your teeth come together evenly when you bite down? If the answer is no, then it’s time to explore your orthodontic treatment options. After all, overbites, crossbites, and other common bite problems can take a serious toll on your self-esteem as well as your chewing and speaking patterns. We want to intervene with the necessary care before that happens.

The Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign offers countless benefits that make it a top choice among patients of all ages. Some of the advantages you’ll be able to enjoy include:

  • Clear orthodontic aligners that are very discreet.
  • No dietary restrictions (simply take your trays out before eating!).
  • Easier to maintain good dental health throughout treatment since trays are removable.
  • Shorter check-in visits with your dentist in Durham.
  • Can address most orthodontic concerns.

Invisalign FAQs

Can Invisalign Fix Overbites?

Actually, yes! So, if your upper teeth protrude beyond your lower ones considerably, making it difficult for you to smile confidently, enunciate clearly, and chew properly, then you shouldn’t hesitate to schedule an appointment with our talented cosmetic dentist in Durham, Dr. Ernesto Ulloa. That way, he can assess the severity of your case and determine if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign. If the answer is yes, then he will answer any questions you have before solidifying the details of your treatment plan.

How Should I Clean My Invisalign Aligners?

Fortunately, keeping your aligners clean and clear doesn’t require a lot of time or money. Just rinse them with clean, cool water each time you take them off. Then, use mild dishwashing liquid and a damp, soft-bristle toothbrush to gently remove plaque, food particles, and saliva from the surface. When you’re done, rinse your aligners again before putting them back on.

Tip: If your aligners need a deeper clean, you can use the cleaning crystals that came in your welcome kit, too!

What Should I Do If I Lose One of My Aligners?

The best thing to do is to retrace your steps, starting from the last place you know you had your aligners. It’s important to be very thorough in the process since they are clear, so they can easily blend in with their surroundings. It’s also a good idea to check in the not-so-obvious places, like the waste bin in your kitchen.

If you can’t find your aligners, then call our team at Crafting Smiles. That way, we can determine if it’s safe for you to progress to the next set in the series a bit early. If that’s not an option, then we will likely ask you to wear the previous pair until a replacement arrives.

Can I Drink Coffee with Invisalign?

Good news: you don’t have to give up coffee if you choose to straighten your teeth with Invisalign in Durham. You do, however, need to remove your aligners beforehand. Not only will this prevent the plastic from warping or staining, but it will also reduce your risk of developing tooth decay.

Tip: Drink your morning cup of coffee with your breakfast instead of slowly sipping on it throughout the morning. This small adjustment to your routine will make meeting the daily wear time significantly easier.

Does Invisalign Hurt?

Since your teeth are being moved into entirely new positions, it is normal to experience some soreness and pressure from time to time. The good news is that there aren’t any metal components with Invisalign. Not only that, but there aren’t monthly adjustment appointments. As a result, the teeth-straightening process is considerably more comfortable and convenient!

Important reminder: If you do experience some discomfort from the movement of your teeth, don’t remove your aligners! Instead, sip on cool water, stick to soft foods, and, if needed, take OTC pain medication as directed. You can also switch to each new set in the series right before bed so you sleep through the period of time when your teeth are moving the most.
