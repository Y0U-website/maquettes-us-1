When You Should See a Pediatric Dentist

Believe it or not you can begin regular oral care for your child routine right as the first tooth appears. As the kids get older and more teeth come in excess food can get caught anywhere a tooth is present. Once teeth start showing up in children, it’s highly recommended to clean them with a small and soft-bristled brush dampened with lukewarm water. Children’s dentists recommend that kids need to practice the art of flossing and brushing their teeth. In many cases this is an exercise children love to do themselves.

Your Child’s First Dental Appointment

It’s considered that the best time to schedule an appointment with a dentist for your child is around six months of age when the child’s first tooth appears. And at All Family Dental Care we will make it a fun experience for your child. It important to develop a good relationship with a Dentist early on so kids are not apprehensive about oral care later in life. But of course as your child grows older, regular check-up are important. That way a Pediatric Dentist can diagnose and anticipate future oral disorders.

Kids Dental Care Tips

• Use of fluoride toothpastes as they help strengthen teeth against tooth decay. • Hold the brush at 45 degree angle; brush in small and gentle strokes. • Parents assist children in brushing to assure a thorough job. • Change your child’s toothbrush regularly. • Parents promote healthy eating habits; limiting the consumption of sodas and sweet snacks. • Do not put a baby to bed with a bottle as milk can harm teeth if left exposed for long periods. • Immediately consult with a Pediatric Dentist in case of any sort of tooth pain.

Truly, it’s the responsibility of the parents to make sure that their children’s teeth are strong and healthy. With regular examinations, cleaning, and proper habits; your children can easily maintain healthy and strong teeth.

What is Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric Dentistry that provides preventive oral care for children all the way from infants, to small children, to adolescents. Pediatric Dentists are specially trained to meet the oral health care needs of children and to monitor their development. Providing consultation and therapy early in life can help your kid develop a winning smile.

The major goal of pediatric dentists is to prevent cavities and diagnose any disorders early on. They even demonstrate proper flossing and brushing techniques to children and parents. Also a Pediatric Dentist can give you advice on what foods to eat and which ones to stay away from.

It’s highly beneficial to visit us at All Family Dental Care early on. The stages when your kid is getting their teeth is the best time to see us. We can help ensure good dental hygiene in your children.
