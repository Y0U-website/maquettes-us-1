Academic Focus & Specializations

As a clinician-educator, Dr. McElveen specializes in the surgical management of patients with moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea, as well as complex head and neck trauma, pathology, and reconstruction.

His teaching roles at Johns Hopkins and Howard University reflect his commitment to elevating surgical standards and inspiring the next generation of oral and maxillofacial surgeons.

Personal Mission

Dr. McElveen is driven by a personal mission to provide exceptional surgical care and improve the lives of his patients. He takes the time to thoroughly explain all treatment options, ensuring every patient feels informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.

Outside the operating room, he enjoys riding bikes and playing video games with his children a reminder of what truly matters.
