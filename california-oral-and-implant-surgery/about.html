Welcome to California Oral and Implant Surgery

California Oral and Implant Surgery is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgery practice located at 22926 Three Notch Road in California, MD. Led by Dr. John McElveen — who holds triple doctorates in dental surgery (DDS), medicine (MD), and pharmacy (PharmD) — our practice provides expert dental implants , wisdom tooth removal , bone grafting , corrective jaw surgery , TMJ treatment , sleep apnea surgery , and IV sedation in a comfortable, patient-focused environment.

We proudly serve patients throughout St. Mary’s County — including Lexington Park , Leonardtown , Great Mills , Mechanicsville , Hollywood , and Piney Point — as well as neighboring communities in Calvert County , Charles County , and Prince George’s County . Whether you’ve been referred by your general dentist or are exploring treatment options on your own, our team is here to provide compassionate, expert care from your first consultation through full recovery.

Call us at (301) 685-5688 or request a consultation online to get started.
