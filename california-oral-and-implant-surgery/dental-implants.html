The Implant Procedure, Simplified

A clear, patient-friendly overview of the process—from planning to final restoration.

1. Consultation & Planning

Your journey begins with a one-on-one consultation at our California, MD office to discuss your goals, followed by an examination and a CT scan to create your personalized treatment plan.

2. Site Preparation

If needed, we may perform preliminary work like a bone graft to ensure your jaw provides a strong and healthy foundation for the implant.

3. Implant Placement

On surgery day, our surgeons uses advanced, computer-guided technology to precisely and gently place the implant into your jawbone.

4. Healing & Aftercare

We provide complimentary PRP treatment for sedated patients to speed up healing and guide you through post-operative care for a smooth recovery.

5. Final Restoration

Once you are healed, we take digital impressions of your mouth for your general dentist to create the final, beautiful crown, saving you time and extra visits.

Dental Implant FAQs

Common questions about dental implants answered by our board-certified oral surgeon.

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once healed, they support a custom crown, bridge, or denture that looks and functions like natural teeth. They are the most durable, long-lasting tooth replacement option available.

Most adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. If bone loss has occurred, bone grafting can often restore the foundation needed for implant placement. Dr. McElveen will evaluate your individual situation during a consultation.

With proper care and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant post itself is designed to fuse permanently with your jawbone. The crown or restoration on top may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear.

Most patients report that the procedure is far more comfortable than they expected. We use local anesthesia and offer IV sedation for maximum comfort. Many patients take only over-the-counter pain medication during recovery and return to normal activities within a day or two.

All-on-4 is a full-arch restoration technique that uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth. Unlike individual implants (one per missing tooth), All-on-4 replaces a full set of upper or lower teeth in a single procedure — often with temporary teeth placed the same day.

Many dental insurance plans now cover a portion of dental implant treatment. Coverage varies by plan. Our team will verify your benefits and explain your out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins. We also offer flexible financing options to make implants affordable.

The full process typically takes 3–6 months, depending on healing time and whether bone grafting is needed. The surgical placement visit itself usually takes about an hour per implant. After a healing period for the implant to fuse with bone, your general dentist places the final crown.
