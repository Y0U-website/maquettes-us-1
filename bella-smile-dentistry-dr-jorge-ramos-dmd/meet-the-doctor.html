Meet the Doctor

Thirty Years of Trusted Care

Dr. Jorge Ramos Mendoza, DMD, leads Bella Smile Dentistry with a simple philosophy: listen first, explain clearly, and recommend only what genuinely serves each patient.

That approach has shaped a boutique, one-doctor practice where patients feel known, not processed.

Jorge Ramos Mendoza, DMD

I’ve been practicing dentistry for 30 years, and one of the things I value most is taking the time to really get to know my patients. I believe everyone has different needs, goals, and priorities, so I make it a point to listen and provide treatment options that fit each patient’s lifestyle and preferences.

For me, dentistry is not just about treating teeth. It’s about building relationships, earning trust, and helping my patients feel confident in the decisions they make about their oral health.

When I’m not in the office, I enjoy spending time with my grandchildren, playing dominoes, and going fishing.
