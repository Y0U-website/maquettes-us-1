Accessibility Statement

Welcoming Every Visitor

Bella Smile Dentistry - Dr. Jorge Ramos, DMD strives to ensure that our services and website are accessible to people of all abilities.

We believe every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence.

General

Bella Smile Dentistry - Dr. Jorge Ramos, DMD has invested significant resources to help ensure that this website is easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities.

Accessibility

We make available a built-in accessibility widget you can open anytime from the icon on the page. With it, you can adjust text size (normal, large, or extra-large), turn on a high-contrast display mode, highlight links, reduce motion and animation, switch to a more readable font, and increase letter and line spacing. Your choices are saved in your browser so they carry over on your next visit.

Enabling the Accessibility Menu

The accessibility menu can be enabled by clicking the accessibility icon that appears at the corner of the page. After triggering the menu, please wait a moment for it to load in its entirety.

Disclaimer

We continue our efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of our site and services. Despite our efforts to make all pages and content fully accessible, some content may not yet be fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.

Here for You

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on our website or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us during normal business hours and we will be happy to help.
