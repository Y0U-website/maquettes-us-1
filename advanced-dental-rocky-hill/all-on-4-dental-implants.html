What Are All-On-4 Implants?

The All-On-4 technique uses four titanium implants anchored into the jawbone to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, which rest on the gums, All-On-4 implants are fixed in place, offering superior stability, function, and aesthetics.

This modern solution is ideal for patients who:

  • Are missing most or all teeth in one arch
  • Have failing teeth due to decay or gum disease
  • Struggle with ill-fitting dentures
  • Want a permanent alternative to removable prosthetics

Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

Patients choose All-On-4 implants because they deliver fast, full-arch restoration with fewer implants and less downtime.

This procedure is often referred to as “Teeth in a Day” or “Same Day Full Mouth Implants” , and it’s changing lives across Connecticut.

  • Same-day smile restoration in most cases
  • Only four implants needed per arch
  • No bone grafting required for many patients
  • Natural-looking aesthetics and improved speech
  • Eat, smile, and live confidently again
  • Fixed, non-removable teeth that feel like your own

FAQs About All-On-4 Implants

  • Are All-On-4 dental implants permanent?: Yes. With proper care, All-On-4 implants can last decades. The prosthetic teeth may need occasional maintenance or replacement.
  • How long does the All-On-4 procedure take?: Implant placement and temporary teeth are often completed in one day. Full healing and final restoration may take several months.
  • Is bone grafting needed before All-On-4?: Often, no. The angled implant technique helps avoid areas with low bone density, reducing the need for grafts.
  • Are All-On-4 implants better than dentures?: All-On-4 implants provide a stable, fixed alternative to removable dentures—offering better chewing function, appearance, and comfort.
  • How much do All-On-4 dental implants cost in Connecticut?: Costs vary by case complexity and materials used. During your consultation, we’ll provide a personalized estimate and discuss financing options.
