What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that serves as an artificial tooth root. Once placed into the jawbone, it forms a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

  • A single missing tooth
  • Multiple teeth with a fixed bridge
  • An entire upper or lower arch with implant-supported dentures or All-On-4 implants

FAQs About Dental Implants

  • Are dental implants safe and long-lasting?: Yes, dental implants are one of the safest, most predictable restorative treatments available—often lasting 20 years or more.
  • Will the implant procedure hurt?: It’s a surgical procedure, but most patients experience only mild discomfort. We offer IV sedation for a stress-free visit.
  • What’s the cost of dental implants in Connecticut?: Cost varies based on how many teeth are being replaced and what type of restoration is used. We’ll provide transparent pricing at your consultation.
  • Can implants replace dentures?: Absolutely. We offer full arch options like All-On-4 for a fixed, secure alternative to removable dentures.
  • Do I need a referral from another dentist?: No referral is needed. Simply contact us to schedule your personalized consultation.
