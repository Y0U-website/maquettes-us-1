Dr. Meagan Scarborough

At 12 South Dental Studio, Dr. Scarborough combines her unique background in psychology with her dental expertise to provide holistic, patient-centered care. Her approach seamlessly blends restorative dentistry, cosmetic procedures, and sleep medicine with a gentle, empathetic touch. Dr. Allyson Fesmire Abrams is a compassionate dentist with deep roots in our community. Born and raised right here, she returned home after graduating from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, eager to serve the area she loves. Dr. Fesmire Abrams believes in building strong relationships with her patients, focusing on comprehensive care that treats you as a whole person, not just a set of teeth.

Outside the office, you might spot Dr. Fesmire serving hot dogs or paddle boarding in Westhaven. Dr. Fesmire looks forward to caring for you soon!
