A Dental Practice for Everyone in the Family

At the Center for Family and Implant Dentistry, we take pride in being a dental practice that caters to everyone in your family. From toddlers to grandparents, our team, led by Dr. MacConnell, ensures that patients of all ages receive exceptional care for their general dentistry needs and dental implants.

Located conveniently in Bluff City, scheduling appointments for every member of your family has never been easier. Our dedicated dentist and his proficient staff offer a wide range of vital dental services, including periodontics, cosmetic dentistry, routine exams and cleanings, preventative dental services, restorative dentistry, and general dental services.

What sets us apart is our ability to provide comprehensive care under one roof. With an array of services available at our office, you can have all your various dental needs met without the hassle of multiple referrals to specialists. We understand the importance of continuity in care and strive to offer convenience without compromising on quality. We stay abreast with the latest technical innovations in dentistry to ensure that you benefit from cutting-edge technology during your visits.

Today’s dentistry aims to provide ease and comfort for patients like you; hence, it remains our constant endeavor to embrace these advancements while striving toward excellence. In choosing the Center for Family and Implant Dentistry as your trusted oral healthcare provider, you can expect nothing less than personalized attention coupled with modern techniques aimed at enhancing both your oral health and smile.

Our Wide Range of Dental Services

Our team at the Center for Family and Implant Dentistry understands the importance of comprehensive oral care, which is why we offer a wide range of services to cater to our patients’ needs. Dr. MacConnell specializes in periodontal services, addressing gum disease directly so that patients do not have to seek external referrals. With restorative dentistry options like dental implants and crowns, we strive to restore the functionality and aesthetics of your teeth.

Our commitment to preventative dentistry ensures that we provide preventive treatments and thorough check-ups, helping you maintain optimal oral health. Additionally, our cosmetic dentistry procedures are designed to enhance your smile’s appearance, allowing you to feel more confident and satisfied with your overall look.

Special Services

Dr. MacConnell’s expertise in oral implantology is truly exceptional. With his additional training and certification by the American Board of Oral Implantology, he stands out as a true professional in this specialized field. Patients can rely on him for comprehensive implant procedures, knowing that they are receiving the highest level of care from a trusted dentist. Patients who have been seeing him for routine dental check-ups or treatments can also benefit from his advanced skills when it comes to implants, ensuring continuity and convenience in their dental care journey.

Patient Experience

At our dental practice, our patients and their comfort are always our priority. With a well-established reputation for making our patients happy with their treatment, we go above and beyond to ensure they feel comfortable during every visit.

Our commitment to excellence drives us to provide exceptional care and make each encounter a great one. Offering an array of services, we pride ourselves on delivering oral health care precisely when you need it most.
