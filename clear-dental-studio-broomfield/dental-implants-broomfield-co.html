What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are tooth-replacement solutions designed to replace missing teeth and restore oral function. An implant uses a small post placed in the jawbone to provide support for a replacement tooth or dental restoration.

Unlike removable options, dental implants are designed to provide a stable foundation for your restoration. Your treatment plan will depend on factors such as the number of teeth being replaced, your oral health, gum condition, and available bone.
