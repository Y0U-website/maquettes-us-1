Discover the Best Tooth Replacement Option

Patients with missing teeth and who want Anoka dental implant have to undergo rigorous screening primary examination. This is done to ensure that the patient is a suitable candidate for an implant. Certain patients are exempted from getting a dental implant –

  • Patients with poor oral hygiene
  • Patients with debilitating medical conditions like diabetes, cardiac diseases, osteoporosis, etc.
  • Patients either on or who might have to start radiation therapy
  • Patients who smoke
  • Patients without healthy relationships and ample moral support

First Step

Once the patient is given the go-ahead to proceed with the implant procedure, their oral cavity is examined. The dentist at Anoka Dental will advise a series of digital images to be taken to assess bone health. Additionally, any teeth showing signs of decay and damage are taken care of before the implant placement.

Second Step

With the help of local anesthesia or a sedative, the patient is prepped for implant surgery. The gum is cut at the site of placement and the bone is exposed. Using a surgical drill, space is made in the jaw bone and the implant post is placed. The gum is stitched back into place and the patient is sent home.

The dentist at Anoka Dental will prescribe painkillers to help with post-operative soreness. Ice packs are recommended to help with the swelling.

Third Step

This is the waiting period for the implant to fuse with the jaw bone. This process of fusion is called osseointegration. This is what gives an implant its long shelf-life and brag-worthy success rate. The dentist at Anoka Dental will ask the patient to come in regularly to ensure proper wound healing. Moreover, the fusion is also assessed.

This process can take anywhere from 6 to 12 weeks.

Fourth Step

This is the penultimate stop of the implant process. Once the wound has healed and the implant is securely anchored within the jaw, the abutment is placed.

The dentist at Anoka Dental will then proceed to take impressions of your upper and lower jaw. These are sent to the dental lab to fabricate the dental crown/bridge/denture. The patient can choose if they want the prosthesis to be fixed or removable.

Fifth Step

This is the last step in the Anoka dental implant process. Once the tooth replacement comes back from the dental lab, the dentist does a final check to see if it fits. The crown/bridge/denture is then attached to the implant.

The patient is advised to follow proper oral hygiene steps to take care of not just the implant, but also the mouth. Regular follow-ups with the dentist are also recommended to assess the implant’s progress.

Advantages Of Dental Implants

There are numerous advantages of dental implants. Some of them are as follows –

  • Because of the fusion with the jaw bone, implants not only look but also feel like your natural teeth. The osseointegration also makes them a permanent restorative option.
  • With older replacement options like bridges, the healthy teeth had to be cut down to make space. But implants don’t require interference with the remaining natural teeth. Hence, not only are they the most conservative option, but they also improve long-term dental health.
  • Flossing is also easier between teeth and individual implants, contributing to a healthier mouth.
  • Dental implants are pretty durable and can last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance.
  • Ill-fitting dentures can be embarrassing because they tend to slip within the mouth. This causes difficulty in eating and slurred speech. With implants, a person can carry out all the basic functions and more without any worries. Implants also allow you to eat a better range of foods, which is iffy with dentures.
  • Owing to bone fusion, dental implants eradicate the discomfort that comes with wearing dentures.
  • Dental implants improve your self-esteem by giving you a smile makeover.
