Is there any scope for complications in Dental Implants?

You must visit your dentist for the examination of your permanent implants if you feel something is wrong.

The scope for dental implant complication is the least and can be resolved easily. The dental implant complications may include-

The best way to avoid various implant complications is to do proper care at home and visit your dentist frequently.

During the dental implant procedure, the dentist examines the surrounding hard and soft tissues to avoid any complication.

  • The infection surrounding the dental implant area.
  • Nerve or tissue damage leading to numbness or pain in the gums, lips or teeth.
  • Excessive pressure on the implant area can cause the dental implant to fall out.
  • Sinus problems if the implant touches the sinus cavities. It only happens when the implants are placed in the upper jaw.
