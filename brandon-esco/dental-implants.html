Dental implants also strengthen the quality of your jawbone and maintain the alignment of neighboring teeth. Missing teeth, if not replaced quickly, are no longer stimulating the jawbone during chewing and function, causing the bone levels to shrink, making replacement options more difficult. Neighboring teeth will also begin to shift, tilting into the space, compromising their integrity. It is important to replace missing teeth to avoid future tooth loss.

At Brookwood Dentistry, we use 3D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to map out both dental arches in order to guide our implant placement to areas of greatest bone volume, while also emerging from the gum tissue at natural angles to increase the longevity, functionality, and beauty of the final tooth in your smile. Our 3D printed surgical guides ensure precision, predictable outcomes, and improve the healing process.

YOU SHOULD NEVER GET AN IMPLANT PLACED WITHOUT 3D X-RAYS!

If you wear complete or partial dentures, dental implants can also be used as anchoring attachments to increase both retention and comfort.
