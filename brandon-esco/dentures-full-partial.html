Full Dentures

Full dentures are designed to replace all of the upper or lower teeth. Porcelain or acrylic replacement teeth built into a base give the appearance of natural teeth emerging from the gums. We help our patients to select teeth that appear most natural and harmonious with their smile and personality. Modern implant technology offers more options to enhance the fit and retention of dentures to your jaw through stabilizing connectors.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are designed to fill the gaps from missing teeth in your smile. They utilize your natural teeth to provide a reliable, secure appliance that is easy to use. Where teeth are missing, natural-looking replacement teeth are fitted into a gum-colored acrylic base. In addition to filling in gaps and improving chewing ability, partial dentures will also keep your natural teeth from tilting and shifting, reducing the risk of losing those teeth in the future.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are dentures that are attached securely to dental implants for added comfort and stability. We offer both removable implant-supported dentures that you can easily take out for cleaning, and fixed implant-supported dentures that are even more stable.

Denture Stabilization

We recommend using dental implants to stabilize your dentures. The more implants used, the greater the stability. With several implants per arch we can even do away with the upper palate material that covers the roof of the mouth improving the ability to taste food, reduce gag reflex and, for some, improves the ability to speak or sing.

Relines and Repairs

We do relines and most repairs right in our office. For more complicated repairs we may send out to a local dental lab to get the quickest and most efficient service.

We offer a variety of options for replacing teeth in our dental office, including dentures, partial dentures and implants. All of these solutions can dramatically improve your quality of life, restoring proper function in chewing, speaking and esthetics. We look forward to discussing your options to give you back a confident and glowing smile.
