Implants Patient Education

  • Prevents bone loss
  • Easy to clean (brushing/flossing)

Bridge

  • Fixed in place
  • Adjacent teeth must be shaped
  • Bone loss may still occur
  • Special flossing required
  • 10–15 year lifespan

Denture

  • Removable
  • May slip or shift
  • Bone loss continues
  • Requires daily removal/cleaning
  • Less natural feel

The Implant Process

Implant treatment is completed in stages over several months. First, the implant post is placed in the jawbone. After 3–6 months of healing and bone fusion, an abutment and custom crown are attached. The final result is a beautiful, permanent tooth replacement.

Not everyone is an immediate candidate for implants — adequate bone density and healthy gums are important. Dr. Virk will evaluate your situation and discuss the best approach during a consultation.
