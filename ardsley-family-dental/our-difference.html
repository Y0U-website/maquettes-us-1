easy scheduling

Fitting in dental appointments around work, school, and activity schedules can be difficult, but that doesn&#x27;t mean you should let your oral health slide. We aim to make visits to our dental office as easy and stress-free as possible by providing convenient scheduling options. We are open from 9:30am to 6pm Monday through Thursday, and we are open till 4:00pm from Friday through Sunday. That means you can fit in your exam, cleaning, or other treatment before or after your work or school day. Whatever works best for you!
