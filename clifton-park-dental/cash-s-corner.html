CASH’S CORNER

A VERY SPECIAL PART OF THE CLIFTON PARK DENTAL FAMILY.

For many years, Cash was the heart of our office. From his puppy days to his final visits, he spent nearly every day at Clifton Park Dental spreading happiness to everyone he met. Patients and staff alike looked forward to seeing his wagging tail and friendly personality. Whether he was greeting visitors at the door, relaxing near the front desk, or making his way through the office to say hello, Cash had a way of making everyone smile.

Cash crossed the rainbow bridge in November 2023, but his spirit is still very much a part of our office. He reminded us daily of the joy that simple kindness and companionship can bring, and he will always hold a special place in our hearts.
