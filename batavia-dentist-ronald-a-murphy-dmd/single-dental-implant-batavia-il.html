Health Benefits of Dental Implants in Batavia IL

A dental implant is a metal post that an oral surgeon will place in your gums. This post takes over the job your tooth’s root had, which is to stimulate your jawbone and keep it healthy and strong. It also provides a solid base to which your new tooth can be attached.

Dr. Murphy recommends implants to his patients with missing adult teeth because they offer several health benefits:

  • Helps maintain a healthy jaw and gums
  • Restores the appearance of your smile
  • Prevents your other teeth from shifting
  • Allows you to chew and speak easily
  • Prevents bone loss in your jaw

What Dr. Murphy Can Do for You

For patients missing one adult tooth a single dental implant is the best way to replace your tooth and keep your jawbone healthy. Dr. Murphy can attach a crown to your dental implant to restore your smile. He will match the crown’s shape and color to the rest of your teeth; so it looks natural.
