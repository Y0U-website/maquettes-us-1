Integrity, Service, and Excellence

Serving in the United States Air Force, Dr. Corbin lived by the organization’s three Core Values: Integrity, Service, and Excellence . Those values now serve as the backbone of the daily operations at Corbin Dental. Our goal is to craft a dental health treatment plan customized for each patient and ensure all your dental care needs are met. We want to be the difference in your smile.

Integrity: We do the right thing, every time. Every patient will be treated with their best interest in mind. When you visit Corbin Dental, we understand that your care is our top priority. By choosing us, we know you are entrusting your dental future to us and we take that responsibility seriously.

Service: We serve you with compassion and care. The team at Corbin Dental will provide a premier patient experience from the moment you schedule your appointment until your treatment plan is complete. Our entire team is dedicated to providing sincere care to make your dental visit as comfortable as possible.

Excellence: We provide premier dental treatment right here at home. You want to know that your care is the best attainable and we take pride in providing that. Corbin Dental aims high to provide extraordinary treatment backed by Dr. Corbin’s advanced residency training, the latest equipment, top-tier materials, and thorough training of our entire team.
