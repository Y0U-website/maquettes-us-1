Meet the Team

Dr. Matt Corbin

Dr. Matt Corbin is an Air Force veteran with premier dental training, local roots, and a deep passion to serve his community. Born and raised in Franklin, he draws on a nearly 200-year heritage in Macon County. His passion for the local community and desire to serve others is what drove Dr. Corbin to the field of dentistry.

Dr. Corbin is so honored to be back and serve Macon County, North Carolina, this great place we call home.

Dr. Ashley Collins

Dr. Collins knew she wanted to be a dentist since the 5th grade. As a young child, she received dental treatment that instantly relieved pain and initiated a curiosity and passion for the art and science of dentistry. She realized the immediate impact she can make for patients, and she began the road towards achieving that goal.

Dr. Collins is a North Carolina native, hailing from Southern Pines, NC. She received her Bachelor of Science in Human Biology from North Carolina State University. During her freshman year, she met her husband, Hobe. Not long after meeting, Hobe brought Ashley to his hometown of Franklin, where she fell in love with the beauty of this quaint, mountain town.

Dr. Collins loves dentistry because she loves getting to meet and form relationships with patients of all ages. She has a passion for learning and loves the dynamic qualities the field of dentistry provides. Dr. Collins is a foodie and loves to cook and try new restaurants. She lives in Franklin with her husband and fur baby, Banks. Dr. Collins feels so fortunate to be a part of this wonderful community and the Corbin Dental family!
