Teeth Whitening

The term “teeth whitening” covers a wide range of procedures and products from toothpastes, gels, and in-office bleaching treatments. Whitening toothpastes can lighten your teeth, but they can abrade the outer surface of the tooth in the process.

A much better option is take-home whitening gel that penetrates the outer layer of the tooth and whitens the tooth from the inside, giving your smile a natural whiter glow! The custom-made trays and quality of whitening gel is what separates our bleaching process from over the counter options.
