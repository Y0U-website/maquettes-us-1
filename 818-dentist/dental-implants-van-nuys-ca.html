The Amazing Benefits of Dental Implants

At our Van Nuys, CA office, we not only provide expert dental implant surgical care, but aesthetically-driven restorative care and comprehensive follow-up appointments as well to ensure your implants are healing correctly and functioning properly. Approaching implant care with their long-term success as our primary focus, we provide treatment that not only meets patient expectations, but oftentimes exceeds them. The benefits of dental implants include:

Health

Dental implants act like natural teeth, from the root to the crown. They are made of highly durable and strong materials that can resist the force of eating tough, hard, or chewy foods like meat, nuts, and raw fruits and vegetables.

Function

Dental implants integrate with the jaw bone, providing the same level of stimulation as that of natural tooth roots. Whenever you bite or chew, implants take on this pressure and help to keep the jaw bone healthy.

Aesthetics

Dental implant crowns are created to look and feel just like natural teeth. Color, translucency, size, shape, and positioning are all considered when restoring dental implants . These artificial teeth are indistinguishable from natural teeth!
