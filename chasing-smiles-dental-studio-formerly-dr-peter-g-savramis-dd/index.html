The practice

Meet your dentist Dr. Fatima Maarouf Dr. Fatima Maarouf brings 15 years of experience to a style of dentistry shaped by precision, artistry, and real relationships. Raised in Lebanon and inspired by her own childhood experiences as a dental patient, she understands how much trust it takes to sit in the chair and how meaningful it can be when a dentist truly listens.

After studying biology at the American University of Beirut, Dr. Maarouf earned her dental degree from Virginia Commonwealth University and completed a general practice residency at Tufts University in Boston. She also earned her FAGD in 2024, reflecting a continued commitment to advanced education and thoughtful, high-quality care.

What matters most to Dr. Maarouf is the trust that builds over time — seeing patients year after year, understanding their goals more deeply, and helping them feel more confident in their smile with every visit.

Healthy smiles are built over time. With routine cleanings, periodontal care, oral cancer screenings, and clear guidance at every visit, we help you stay ahead of small concerns before they become more complex. It is everyday care with long-term health in mind.

Cosmetic dentistry Cosmetic dentistry is not about creating a smile that looks like everyone else’s. It is about helping your smile feel more natural, balanced, and true to you. From whitening and bonding to veneers, Dr. Maarouf approaches cosmetic care with an artist’s eye and a dentist’s attention to function.

Why patients choose us An elevated dental experience Every patient brings a different story, a different concern, and a different idea of what a healthy smile means to them. We take those details seriously. Before recommending treatment, Dr. Maarouf and the team take time to understand what matters to you, explain what we see, and outline clear next steps so you never feel rushed or left guessing.

Care that starts with understanding We ask questions before we make recommendations. What is bothering you? What are you hoping to change? What has made dental care difficult in the past? Those answers help us create a plan that fits your health, comfort, timeline, and goals.

Gentle, detail-focused treatment From routine visits to restorative and cosmetic care, our approach is calm, careful, and thorough. We pay attention to the small details that affect how your treatment feels, how your smile functions, and how confident you feel afterward.
