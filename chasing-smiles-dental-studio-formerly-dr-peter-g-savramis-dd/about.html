Meet the dentist behind the practice

Get to know Dr. Fatima Maarouf

Originally from Lebanon, Dr. Maarouf grew up in a family that placed a strong emphasis on education and service. Several of her siblings pursued careers in medicine, and her own experiences as a dental patient, including braces, extractions, root canals, and crowns, inspired her interest in the profession. After shadowing the dentist who had cared for her for years, she discovered a passion for dentistry&#x27;s unique blend of science, artistry, and problem-solving, along with the opportunity to make a meaningful difference in people&#x27;s lives.

Dr. Maarouf earned her bachelor&#x27;s degree in Biology with High Distinction from the American University of Beirut before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with Honors from Virginia Commonwealth University. She then completed a General Practice Residency at Tufts University, where she expanded her training in comprehensive dental care and advanced clinical procedures.

Following her residency, Dr. Maarouf owned and operated a private dental practice in Winthrop, Massachusetts, just outside of Boston, for seven years. There, she built lasting relationships with patients and families while earning a reputation for gentle, personalized care and clinical excellence. A few years ago, she and her family relocated to the Washington, D.C., metropolitan area to be closer to family and establish long-term roots. Today, she is proud to call Maryland home and is excited to serve the community where she and her family are building their future.

Outside the office, Dr. Maarouf enjoys spending time with her husband and two young children. She loves baking, crafting, hiking, weightlifting, and fitting in a Peloton ride whenever she can. She believes the same patience, kindness, and dedication that guide her family life are reflected in the way she cares for her patients every day.

The people you’ll see at every visit

A team that takes the time to know you

A good dental experience is shaped by more than treatment alone. It is shaped by how you are greeted, how your questions are answered, and whether you feel like the people around you are paying attention.

Our team is here to make each visit feel calm, organized, and clear. From your first phone call to the moment you leave, you can expect friendly communication, helpful guidance, and a pace that gives you room to ask questions.

Here’s what your first visit looks like

starting with clarity

Your first visit is a chance for us to get to know you, not rush you through a checklist. Dr. Maarouf will take time to talk through your dental history, current concerns, past experiences, and what you would like your smile to feel like long term.

From there, we will evaluate your oral health, explain what we see in plain language, and walk you through your options. You will leave with a clearer sense of where things stand and what comes next, without pressure to commit to treatment before you are ready.

Patient Experience Expect a calm, conversational visit. We explain what we are seeing, answer questions as we go, and help you understand the “why” behind any recommendations. If a treatment needs to be referred to a trusted specialist, such as an impacted wisdom tooth, we will help coordinate that step and stay involved in your care afterward.

What to Bring To make your first visit smooth, please bring:

Your dental insurance card, if applicable

Any recent dental records or X-rays from a previous provider

Questions or concerns you would like to talk through

Modern tools making easy

Technology should make dentistry feel more precise, more comfortable, and more transparent. We use modern tools to improve the way we diagnose, plan, and explain your care, so you can feel more confident in every decision.

TRIOS intraoral scanner

Our TRIOS scanner captures detailed 3D images of your teeth without traditional goopy impressions. It is fast, comfortable, and precise, supporting restorative work, crowns, aligners, and other treatments that benefit from a highly accurate digital model.

CBCT imaging coming soon

We are adding cone beam computed tomography, or CBCT, to the practice. This advanced imaging technology creates detailed 3D views of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, helping us plan with greater accuracy in more complex cases.
