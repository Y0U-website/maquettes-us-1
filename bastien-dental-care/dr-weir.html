Dr. Lauren Wier

Dr. Lauren Wier Gentle, Advanced Dentistry with a Focus on Patient Comfort Dr. Lauren Weir is committed to delivering high-quality dental care in a calm, supportive environment. With advanced training and a compassionate approach, she is especially known for helping patients feel relaxed and confident, no matter their level of dental anxiety.

Dr. Weir earned her Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Sciences from the University of Florida, and continued on to become a “double Gator,” graduating from the University of Florida College of Dentistry. During her dental education, she was selected for membership in the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society, recognizing her academic achievement and clinical excellence.

She further refined her skills through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at UT Health San Antonio, where she received advanced training in comprehensive and complex dental care.

Dr. Weir is also board-certified in IV sedation, allowing her to safely and effectively care for patients who experience dental anxiety or require more involved procedures.

Get to know Dr. Wier

Dr. Weir has a special interest in treating patients who feel nervous or apprehensive about dental care. She understands that a positive experience can make all the difference, and she is dedicated to making every visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Her goal is simple: to help every patient feel at ease while receiving exceptional care.

Raised in a military family, Dr. Weir has lived in eight different states as well as Germany. This unique background has given her a strong ability to connect with people from all walks of life and provide care that is both understanding and personalized.

Outside of the office, Dr. Weir enjoys spending time with her husband, Dr. Miller, and their two sons, Tripp and Henry. She loves visiting St. George Island, enjoying the outdoors, and making lasting memories with family and friends.

Patients value Dr. Weir’s calming presence, attention to detail, and dedication to their comfort. Her ability to ease anxiety while delivering excellent results makes her a trusted provider for individuals and families alike.
