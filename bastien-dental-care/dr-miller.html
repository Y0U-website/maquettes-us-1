Dr. Harrison Miller

Dr. Harrison Miller Hometown Care. Modern Dentistry. A Commitment to Excellence. Dr. W. Harrison Miller is proud to serve the Tallahassee community he has called home his entire life. Combining strong clinical training with a patient-first approach, he is dedicated to providing high-quality, comfortable dental care built on trust and long-term relationships.

Dr. Miller was born and raised in Tallahassee and attended Maclay School from Pre-K through to high school. He went on to earn his Bachelor of Science in Biology, graduating magna cum laude from the University of Georgia.

He returned to Florida to pursue his dental degree at the University of Florida College of Dentistry, where he was inducted into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society, a distinction recognizing excellence in academics and clinical performance.

Returning to practice in his hometown is especially meaningful to Dr. Miller. He takes pride in caring for individuals and families in the same community where he grew up.

Get to know Dr. Miller

Dr. Miller believes that great dentistry begins with listening. He takes time to understand each patient’s concerns and goals, providing thoughtful recommendations tailored to their needs.

Patients appreciate his calm demeanor, attention to detail, and commitment to making every visit a positive experience.

Outside the office, Dr. Miller enjoys reading, hunting, and spending time with his wife and their sons. His strong family values and deep community roots influence the care and dedication he brings to his patients each day.

Dr. Miller is honored to provide care in his hometown and is committed to delivering dentistry that is both modern and compassionate.
