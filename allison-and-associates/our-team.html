Michael Almony, DDS

Dr. Michael Almony was born in Germany and grew up in a proud military family. His family eventually relocated to beautiful North Carolina where Michael attended Campbell University, earning a Bachelor of Science Degree. His desire to serve others led him to pursue a career in dentistry and attend the nation’s first dental school, the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Michael graduated in 2017 with a DDS degree and joined the Army where he was able to provide oral health care to other soldiers.

While in the Army, Michael served his country while deployed to Kuwait and Iraq in support of both Operation Spartan Shield and Operation Inherent Resolve. Dr. Almony continued to enhance his dental education by completing courses in endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, restorative dentistry, as well as training in digital dentistry. After completing his army service, Dr. Almony decided to make the Sandhills area his permanent home.

During his free time, he enjoys spending time visiting local attractions with his wife Katie, his son Cooper and his two wonderful huskies. He loves calling North Carolina home.

Dr. Kirsten Grismer, DDS

Dr. Kirsten Grismer brings warmth, precision, and a deep commitment to personalized care to every patient she treats. A graduate of Baylor University and the University of Texas Health Science Center School of Dentistry in San Antonio, Dr. Grismer combines a rigorous clinical foundation with the kind of genuine human connection that makes patients feel truly at ease.

Her path to dentistry has been shaped by a life well-lived. Growing up in a military family, Dr. Grismer moved 14 times before putting down roots — an experience that gave her an exceptional ability to meet people where they are, adapt quickly, and build trust with patients from every background and walk of life. It was at Baylor University that she met her husband Eric, and together they chose to make Moore County home in 2024. She has embraced this community wholeheartedly and is committed to being a meaningful part of it for years to come.

Clinically, Dr. Grismer has a special passion for restorative dentistry . She believes that a healthy, fully functioning smile is about far more than aesthetics — it is about confidence, comfort, and quality of life. Whether restoring a single tooth or rebuilding a smile comprehensively, she approaches every case with artistry, clinical rigor, and a sincere investment in each patient’s long-term oral health.

As a new mother to a baby boy, Dr. Grismer brings an especially personal understanding of why preventive and restorative care matters. She knows firsthand what it means to protect the health of someone you love — and that same devotion extends to every patient in her chair. She is passionate about helping families establish lifelong healthy habits, understanding that the care we invest in today shapes the health of generations to come.

Outside the office, Dr. Grismer recharges by running, reading, and working on embroidery projects. She treasures time with her husband Eric and their baby boy, and the family is proud to be planting roots right here in the Pinehurst area.
