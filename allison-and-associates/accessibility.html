Accessibility

Our Commitment Allison and Associates is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities. We work continuously to improve the digital experience for everyone and to maintain alignment with recognized accessibility standards, including the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time checklist. We regularly review our website and make improvements as technology and standards evolve.

Screen Reader Compatibility Our website is built to support screen reader software, allowing content to be interpreted and read aloud for users with visual impairments.

Keyboard Navigation Core website functions, including navigation menus, contact forms, and page content, are accessible using only a keyboard. Users can navigate using Tab, Enter, and arrow keys without requiring a mouse.

Text Size Adjustment Visitors can increase or decrease text size using their browser’s built-in zoom and text settings without loss of content or functionality.

Color Contrast Our website uses color combinations designed to maintain sufficient contrast for users with color vision differences. Users may also apply their own browser or operating system contrast settings.

Descriptive Link Text and Image Alternatives We use descriptive link text and provide alternative text for meaningful images to support users who rely on assistive technologies.

Assistive Technology For additional support, we recommend the following commonly available tools:

Screen readers such as JAWS, NVDA, or VoiceOver (built into Apple devices) Speech recognition software such as Dragon NaturallySpeaking Browser zoom and magnification tools available in all major browsers Operating system accessibility settings on Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android

Media and Multimedia Where video or audio content is available on this site, we make reasonable efforts to provide accessible alternatives such as captions or transcripts. If you encounter media content that is not accessible to you, please contact us and we will do our best to assist.

Known Limitations While we strive for broad accessibility, some third-party content, embedded tools, or legacy materials on this site may not fully meet accessibility standards. We are actively working to address these gaps and welcome your feedback to help us prioritize improvements.

Feedback and Support If you encounter any barriers on this website or have difficulty accessing any content, please reach out. Your feedback helps us improve.
