Dental Implants

If you are missing teeth or having to deal with uncomfortable dentures, you may want to consider dental implants. They are the second best thing to your natural teeth and will leave you smiling in no time. We, at Creve Coeur Advanced Dentistry, use cutting-edge technology to deliver the best teeth implants available!

Dental implants are restorations designed to replace missing teeth, with incredibly natural looking and feeling results. They are considered restorative dentistry procedures because they restore the full function of your teeth, and also fall into the category of cosmetic dentistry because they deliver beautiful, aesthetic results.

Implants involve surgically placing a titanium post in your jawbone to replace your tooth’s natural root. They provide a stable foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth, dentures, or bridges, including all-on-four implants, which utilize four dental implants to support a full arch of teeth. Ceramic crowns are the preferred restorative material for implants because they feel, look, and function just like your natural teeth.

The best candidates for implants are those who have healthy gums, sufficient bone mass to allow the implant to anchor in the jaw, are non-smokers, and have a commitment to meticulous oral hygiene practices. If needed, a bone graft will rebuild the bone, allowing you to enjoy the benefits of dental implants.

What Are the Advantages of Dental Implants?

Dental implants have been shown to be one of the safest procedures in dentistry. They are the best alternatives to healthy, natural teeth, providing stability, and restoring the way they look, feel, and function. You will be able to eat all the foods you love and will chew with ease. You will be able to speak clearly and confidently. Dental implants are durable, with the potential of lasting a lifetime.

Dental implants are the only restorations that work to preserve healthy bone mass, and will also prevent teeth from shifting into the gap left by missing teeth, preventing a host of problems. With our aesthetic dentistry solution, you’ll restore your natural facial structure and your smile.

What Is the Procedure for Getting Implants?

After a complete radiological and oral exam, a titanium implant will be surgically inserted which will fuse to your jawbone over some time in a process called osseointegration. Next, we attach a connector called a healing abutment, and wait for the soft tissue to heal. We will make impressions of your mouth which we will use to construct your prosthetic tooth. If restoring multiple teeth, we will fit you with custom-made bridges or dentures.

The process will typically take several months to complete, with most of the time involving waiting for healing. While waiting, we will outfit you with temporary restorations, so you don’t have to go without teeth a single day.

You will need to commit to meticulous oral hygiene for the long-term success of your restorations. Brush and floss regularly, and make sure to keep up with your dental checkups and cleanings. With proper care, your implants can last a lifetime!
