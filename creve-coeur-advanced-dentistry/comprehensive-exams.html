Comprehensive Exams

Have you been keeping up with your dental exams? Many people do not realize how essential these routine checkups are for maintaining their dental health and preventing any oral problems from progressing. We, at Creve Coeur Advanced Dentistry, conduct thorough examinations aimed at ensuring long-term oral health. We strive to help you and your family members enjoy healthy smiles for years to come!

We recommend that you schedule your dental exams and cleanings every six months. Most dental insurances cover 100 percent of the cost of your routine preventive and diagnostic care, including checkups and cleanings. We find it very helpful if you would help our staff obtain your prior dental x-rays and any outstanding treatment plans you may have.

During your visit, we will examine every aspect of your oral health. We will use dental x-rays to identify and diagnose any potential problems and will conduct a periodontal exam to check on the health of your gums. We will perform a tooth by tooth examination to check for chips or cracks, along with signs of tooth decay or cavities.

We will also analyze your bite and conduct an oral cancer screening. We will identify any condition that needs treatment and may recommend cosmetic dentistry procedures such as teeth whitening to keep your smile white and bright.

During your appointment, we will also perform a professional dental cleaning to prevent tooth decay and gum disease from developing. Our dental hygienist will remove any plaque and tartar, leaving your mouth minty clean and bright!

We, at Creve Coeur Advanced Dentistry, believe that our comprehensive dental exams offer numerous oral health care benefits. Your checkup is an essential component of preventive dentistry which is the practice of preventing dental disease and keeping your mouth healthy. You will need less involved procedures down the road such as root canal treatments, and you may potentially avoid tooth loss resulting from advanced gum disease.

Based on our exam, we may recommend ways to enhance your oral hygiene practices and will develop a comprehensive treatment plan offering the best solutions for any existing or potential problems. Your individualized plan will address immediate needs, preventive measures, and any cosmetic procedures you may want to consider. You will have the opportunity to make informed decisions about your dental care.

Maintaining proper oral hygiene practices at home plays a significant role in promoting healthy teeth. We recommend brushing at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, flossing daily, and rinsing using an antibacterial mouthwash.

Make sure to avoid sugary foods and beverages, carbonated drinks, and tobacco products to lower your risk of developing cavities or gum disease. Try to consume a healthy, balanced diet to ensure your teeth are getting the nutrients needed to stay healthy.
