The practice

Her dedicated team has been serving patients in the Tallahassee area since 1991. She’s earned the trust of her patients by providing reliable care in a compassionate manner.

Our natural-looking dental implants are surgically attached so they won’t come out or move around. They provide a firm hold, allowing you to eat your favorite foods in comfort. Dental implants look and feel fantastic while protecting your smile.

Regular check-ups are essential for maintaining a healthy and attractive smile. We provide professional cleaning and a complete dental exam to protect your smile from cavities and gum disease. We’ll keep your smile looking good and feeling fresh.

If you’re not happy with your smile, we can help. Teeth that are stained or damaged can be repaired with cosmetic dentistry so they look like new. We can also enhance the size and shape of your teeth. We’ll give you a bright, beautiful smile you’ll be happy to share.

Dr. Leadbeater uses the latest dental technology. Our imaging equipment and other diagnostic tools make your care faster, safer, and more affordable. It also speeds up your recovery time.

The KaVo Cone Beam CT scanner creates a low-radiation, 3D scan of the teeth and surrounding tissues. It’s vital for the correct placement of implants, locating extra canals in the teeth for root canal treatment, and finding the positions of larger nerves in the jaw.

Our office is always dedicated to the safety of our patients. During these trying times, we know it’s especially vital to be open about our commitment to infection control.

We now provide Medify ® Medical Grade Filtration H13 True HEPA Air Purifiers in the waiting room and each treatment room to catch any harmful particles. Additionally, every surface in our office waiting room and treatment rooms is disinfected hourly to ensure your safety.
