The consultation is designed to answer your questions regarding treatment and give you a chance to meet the Orthodontic Team at Anderson Orthodontics.

There will be dedicated time with Dr. Anderson

He will perform an orthodontic examination and discuss treatment options. If needed, orthodontic records are taken (X-Rays, Photos, and Models) at this time. The consultation and records are at no charge and questions are always welcome.
