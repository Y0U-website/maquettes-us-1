Pocasset Family Dental provides a full line of dental services for patients of all ages. Our team of dentists is trained in the latest dentistry techniques, and we offer sedation dentistry.

At our practice, we welcome patients of all backgrounds and needs. While our facility is handicap accessible, we’re happy to accommodate and assist however we can.

Our comprehensive services include Family Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, Teeth Whitening, Orthodontics, Dental Implants, Root Canal Therapy, Periodontal Treatments, Oral Surgery, Sedation Dentistry (Nitrous for adults), and Full Mouth Rehabilitation.

Dr. Jawad Shakarchi is certified and licensed to provide Botox.

For more precise and personalized care, we use advanced tools like 3D Imagery, a powerful diagnostic tool that provides detailed images of your teeth and jaw.

At Pocasset Family Dental , our experienced team is committed to providing personalized, high-quality dental care for patients of all ages. We proudly offer services in English, Arabic, and Spanish , ensuring clear communication and a comfortable experience for our diverse community.

Whether you’re visiting for routine preventive care or a complete smile transformation, our team is dedicated to providing compassionate care, advanced technology, and exceptional results.
