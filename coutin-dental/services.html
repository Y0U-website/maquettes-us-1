COMPREHENSIVE DENTAL SERVICES

Fillings

Fillings are used to treat small holes in the surface of your teeth such as cavities. We use mercury-free tooth-colored material to replace decayed tooth structures.

Extractions

We are able to remove teeth or tooth fragments in their entirety

Dentures

We provide dentures, which are removable prostheses that replace missing teeth.

Invisible Appliance Teeth Straightening

We offer the Essix System for straightening mild to moderate crowding of teeth. Treatment consists of a series of clear plastic retainers that are worn in sequence until the teeth are in place.

Implants

Implants include a titanium post, which is surgically placed in the jaw bone. This post then supports an artificial tooth or a cap. Implants replace extracted teeth.

TMJ

Jaw Joint Disorder often causes difficulty opening one’s mouth or chewing. Symptoms can be headaches, popping or clicking of the jaw joints, or grinding teeth. We help manage these symptoms with treatment plans.

Cleanings

Cleanings are essential to remove deposits and debris from the teeth, which promote the proliferation of harmful bacteria, toxins, and irritants.

Crown/Cap

We can place a dental cap, a prosthesis otherwise known as a crown, that fits or covers the entire top of the tooth to protect and fortify a weak tooth.

Bridges

We offer dental bridging, a fixed prosthesis bonded to existing teeth adjacent to a toothless area. The surrounding teeth support the false teeth that replace missing or extracted teeth.

Zoom! Whitening

Zoom! It is a tooth-whitening system that whitens teeth to at least their original, pristine appearance. The system we commonly use is Phillips Zoom Whitening.

Soft Tissue/Periodontal Laser Surgery

We are equipped and certified with a dental laser that can be used for various soft-tissue gum modifications, removal of aphthous ulcers, or removal of oral fibromas. We use the Sapphire Diode Laser.

Root Canals

To save an abscessed/infected tooth from extraction, the nerve is removed, and the inside chamber and canals of the tooth’s roots are cleansed, reshaped, and filled.
