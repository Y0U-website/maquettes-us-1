The practice

We are a full service family, general and cosmetic dental office. Take a look at the list below of some of the services we provide.

We look to prevent oral problems through regular dental visits, oral health education, and proper oral hygiene. This means you will be able to maintain optimum oral health and avoiding costly oral health issues.

Drs. Arthur & Nicholson are experienced cosmetic dentists who understand how to customize each procedure exactly to fit your needs.

When cavities, lost or loose teeth, and tooth pain disrupt your daily schedule, our doctors provide advanced restorative dentistry

We use state-of-the-art technologies in all of our procedures in order to provide our patients the best care possible.
