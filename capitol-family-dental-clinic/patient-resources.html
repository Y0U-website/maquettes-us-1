Patient Resources

Our goal is to ensure you leave feeling confident, cared for, and excited about taking the next step in your dental health journey.

  • Paperwork & Insurance Review If you haven’t filled out your forms in advance, we’ll provide them when you arrive. We’ll also review your insurance and explain your coverage so you know exactly what to expect.
  • Comprehensive Exam You’ll meet with Dr. Sidhu for a full dental exam. This includes a review of your oral health history, a gentle evaluation of your teeth and gums, and digital X-rays if needed.
  • Professional Cleaning Our hygienist will provide a thorough cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, leaving your teeth feeling polished and refreshed.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan Dr. Sidhu will walk you through your results and answer all of your questions. If any treatment is recommended, we’ll create a plan tailored to your needs, timeline, and budget.
  • Next Steps Before you leave, our team will help you schedule future visits and discuss payment or financing options if needed.

Coverage That Works for You

We accept a wide variety of dental insurance plans to help make your care more affordable and accessible. If you have dental coverage, chances are we work with it. Our team will gladly verify your benefits and help you get the most out of your plan.

Get the Care You Need Without the Stress

Flexible Financing Options

At Capitol Family Dental, we believe everyone deserves access to exceptional dental care without the financial stress. That’s why we offer flexible financing options through trusted providers like CareCredit and Cherry. Whether you’re planning a cosmetic procedure or managing an unexpected dental emergency, our financing plans allow you to pay over time with low or 0 % APR options available.

Cherry Financing

We also offer Cherry , a modern financing solution that lets you split your treatment cost into easy monthly payments. With a fast application process and no hidden fees, Cherry makes it simple to say yes to the dental care you need.

Cherry makes it easier to start treatment right away, so you don’t have to delay important dental care due to cost.

  • Apply in seconds with no hard credit check
  • Approval based on more than just credit score
  • Multiple payment plan options to choose from
  • Transparent terms with no hidden costs or surprises

CareCredit Dental Financing

At Capitol Family Dental, we proudly accept CareCredit , a trusted healthcare credit card that helps patients manage the cost of treatment over time. With CareCredit, you can get the care you need today and pay for it in convenient monthly installments.

Whether you’re coming in for a routine cleaning, a crown, or cosmetic care, CareCredit makes treatment more accessible and stress-free.

  • Quick and simple application process apply online or in our office
  • Flexible payment plans that fit your budget
  • Special financing options available (subject to credit approval)
  • Use it for preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental services
