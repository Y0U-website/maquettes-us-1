Finding dental implant financial assistance and grants can be challenging to both find and get. With some ingenuity and hard work, it is possible to find financial support for the procedure. We recommend that you reach out to a tax advisor to ensure you’re proceeding in a legal and correct manner.

Private Financial Assistance for Dental Implants Private financial assistance for dental implants is limited. Generally, missing teeth is not a condition that is life-threatening. As such we may recommend dentures as a lower cost alternative. That said, private options do exist that may make a complete smile more affordable.

Financing and Loans

Personal loans and CareCredit can be used to finance dental implants. These are the most likely avenue of financial help. Complete the online application form. If approved, the lender may approve in as little as 24 hours. You will need to repay the lender with interest, so plan accordingly. Most insurance plans contain long waiting periods before coverage before dental implants begins. By borrowing money instead, your periodontist can start your procedure immediately.
