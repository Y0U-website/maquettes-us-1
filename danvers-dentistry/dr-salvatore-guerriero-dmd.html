Dr. Salvatore Guerriero DMD General dentist

Dr. Salvatore Guerriero, DMD is an experienced general dentist dedicated to providing high-quality, comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages at Danvers Dentistry. With a strong focus on preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Guerriero is known for delivering personalized treatment plans that prioritize each patient’s comfort, long-term oral health, and individual goals. His patient-centered approach emphasizes education, trust, and clinical excellence.

Dr. Guerriero earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and completed his residency at Berkshire Medical Center. With decades of clinical experience, he remains committed to advancing his skills through continuing education and staying current with the latest dental techniques. His broad expertise allows him to effectively diagnose and treat a wide range of dental conditions while maintaining the highest standards of care.

Throughout his career, Dr. Guerriero has built a reputation for compassionate care and professional integrity. He takes pride in creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable and confident in their treatment. Dedicated to lifelong learning and excellence in dentistry, Dr. Guerriero continues to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through modern, evidence-based care.
