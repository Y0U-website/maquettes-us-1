Family Dentist in Winder, GA | Dr. John Baxter

Outside the office, Dr. John is all about family and exploring the world around him. He loves spending time with his wife Emily, a school counselor at Winder-Barrow High School, and their young son, Duard. Whether they’re taking weekend adventures, enjoying time outdoors, or just being together at home, family is at the heart of everything he does.

He’s also an avid outdoorsman who enjoys kayaking, hiking, camping, fishing, hunting, and bird and wildlife watching. If it involves fresh air and a bit of discovery, he’s in. His interests run deep—especially in military, World War II, and naval history, as well as biology, herpetology, ichthyology, ornithology, ecology, and wildlife science.

When he’s ready to relax, you’ll often find him cheering on his favorite teams: the Atlanta Braves, Atlanta Falcons, Atlanta United, and the Tennessee Volunteers—Go Big Orange! (a fun twist that started during his residency). But he still cheers for the Georgia Bulldogs, though! And if he’s unwinding with a movie, chances are it’s something from Star Wars, his lifelong favorite.
