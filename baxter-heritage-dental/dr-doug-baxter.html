Family Dentist in Winder, GA | Dr. Doug Baxter

Outside the office, Dr. Doug’s world revolves around family. He and his wife, Shannon, have been married for more than 30 years and love any chance to spend time together. Their three children—John (married to Emily), Libby (married to Nick), and Mary (married to Avery)—continue to grow the Baxter crew through marriage, new babies, and plenty of beloved grand-pets. Their first grandchild, little Duard, has brought a whole new kind of joy to the family, and another grandbaby is on the way in early 2026.

Whether they’re gathered at home, taking a trip to the grocery store, or enjoying a meal together, he believes anything is fun when the family is gathered. Dr. Doug also feels grateful to spend time with his mother, who still plays an important role in their close-knit family. His father, Dr. D.O. Baxter—his lifelong hero—has instilled in him the values of positivity, hard work, and caring for others with a full heart.

And if you ever need a restaurant recommendation, he’s your guy. Dr. Doug is a proud lifelong foodie who loves exploring new recipes at home and hunting down the best local spots when traveling. For him, good food, good company, and a good laugh are the perfect way to end any day.
