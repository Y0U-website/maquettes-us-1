0 Pediatric Dentistry with Heart and Expertise At Cottonwood Kids Dental, we believe every child deserves a dental experience that’s positive, empowering, and even fun. Located in the heart of Cottonwood, AZ, our practice is built on compassion, transparency, and a whole lot of smiles. We’ve created a warm, welcoming space where kids feel safe and supported—and where parents feel confident knowing their child’s care is in expert hands.

Dr. Jordan Janis brings over 8 years of experience in preventive and comprehensive children’s dental care. After serving families in Phoenix and Flagstaff, he opened Cottonwood Kids Dental with a mission: to create positive dental experiences through compassionate care, trusted information, and a playful, supportive space that helps every child smile with confidence.

Originally from Florida, Dr. Janis earned his Bachelor of Science in Nutrition and Master’s in Public Health from the University of Florida before completing his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health (ASDOH). He is certified in conscious sedation and is an active member of the American Dental Association, Arizona Dental Association, and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

As a father of two, Dr. Janis brings a deeply personal understanding to every patient interaction. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his wife and daughters, golf, and exploring the natural beauty of Northern Arizona.

Our Values Transparency. Confidence. Kindness. Family. We empower families with clear, honest communication. From treatment options to long-term care, we guide every decision with integrity—always prioritizing what’s best for your child’s health.

We’re experts in pediatric care—and we love what we do. Our confidence comes from experience, skill, and a deep belief in making dental visits something kids can actually enjoy.

Kindness is at the heart of everything we do. We lead with empathy, patience, and compassion—because a gentle approach makes all the difference for little smiles.

We care for every child like our own and value every parent as a partner. Our practice is built on trust, connection, and creating a space where families feel genuinely supported
