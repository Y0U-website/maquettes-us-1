Dr. Jordan Janis brings over 8 years of experience in preventive and comprehensive children’s dental care. After serving families in Phoenix and Flagstaff, he opened Cottonwood Kids Dental with a mission: to create positive dental experiences through compassionate care, trusted information, and a playful, supportive space that helps every child smile with confidence.

Originally from Florida, Dr. Janis earned his Bachelor of Science in Nutrition and Master’s in Public Health from the University of Florida before completing his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health (ASDOH).
