The practice

Albany Endodontics is conveniently located near I-87 and Stuyvesant Plaza, offering abundant parking for our valued patients. Additionally, our office features an ADA accessible ramp at the back entrance of the office, ensuring ease of access for all individuals.

0 Welcome to Albany Endodontics As Endodontists, we specialize in root canal treatment and have a passion for saving teeth. Our priority is to ensure the utmost comfort for all our patients throughout their treatment journey.

We aim to provide personalized care, utilizing the latest techniques and technology to deliver the best possible outcomes. Your oral health and comfort are our top priorities.

With a focus on patient-centered care, we are dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, functional smile. Your satisfaction and well-being are at the core of our practice.

At Albany Endodontics, we understand that visiting the dentist can be a source of anxiety for many individuals. That’s why we are dedicated to creating a warm, welcoming environment where your comfort is our top priority. Our expert endodontists and caring team are committed to providing gentle, personalized care to alleviate any concerns you may have.

0 Our Doctors Dr. Tamanna Gandhi, DMD Dr. Gandhi grew up in Southern New Hampshire and received her Bachelors of Science degree in Biology from Adelphi University on Long Island, New York. She received her DMD degree from Boston University School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated summa cum laude and was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honors Society.

After graduating from dental school, she completed a one year General Practice Residency at the Veterans Affairs Boston Healthcare System with the Harvard School of Dental Medicine. She then returned to Boston University School of Dental Medicine for another two years of specialty training in Endodontics.
