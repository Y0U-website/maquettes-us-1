The practice

We are proud to offer a wide range of dental services across our six convenient locations throughout New York. Since our founding in 2006 with a single office in West Harlem, we have expanded to six locations and now have a team of 22 dental specialists.

Whether you’re dealing with a dental emergency or need a routine check-up, we offer same-day appointments to ensure you receive timely, expert care. Our services are not only convenient but also affordable. We work with most insurance plans to make high-quality dental care accessible for you and your family.

Explore our extensive range of dental services tailored to meet your needs. From preventative care to specialized treatments, we offer comprehensive solutions to ensure your smile stays healthy and radiant.

Restorative dentistry focused on replacing missing teeth with prosthetics like dentures and dental implants.

Transform your smile with aesthetic dental procedures that enhance the appearance of your teeth.

Specialized treatment focused on saving infected or damaged teeth through root canal therapy.

Professional teeth whitening treatments designed to remove stains and brighten your smile.

Prevent oral diseases and maintain dental health with regular checkups and cleanings.
