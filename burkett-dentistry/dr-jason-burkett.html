I make it a priority to establish an open, cooperative relationship with our patients. The goal is to partner with you to achieve optimum health for you and your child…

Dr. Burkett’s Dental Journey

Dr. Burkett finished his undergraduate studies at the Southwest Texas State University of Texas in San Marcos. He acquired his doctor of dental surgery at Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, TX. He has always envisioned fostering a cooperative relationship with the patients to provide them with a healthy environment with sessions tailored to every individual’s needs.

Dr. Burkett completed his mini-residency in Pediatric and Special Needs Dentistry from the University of Florida. He has a license to operate in the State of Texas by the Western Regional Examination Board. Being a respectable member of the Dental community and with affiliation with the American Dental Association, the State of Texas Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry, Dr. Burkett has always prioritized the health of his patients.
