The practice

At Cadent, we are passionate about transforming smiles through advanced orthodontic care and a full range of cosmetic and general dental services.

Whether you re looking to straighten your teeth, enhance your smile with veneers, or improve your oral health, our team is here to provide high-quality, personalized care every step of the way.

We offer multiple orthodontic treatment options including four types of braces designed to fit your needs, lifestyle, and budget.

Metal braces remain one of the most effective and widely used orthodontic systems worldwide. Known for their strength and precision, they utilize high-quality stainless steel brackets and wires to gradually align teeth into their optimal functional and aesthetic position.

Function: Correct tooth alignment and bite issues for improved function, health, and appearance.

Sapphire braces offer a highly aesthetic alternative to traditional metal brackets. Made from monocrystalline sapphire, these brackets are completely transparent, blending seamlessly with the natural color of your teeth making them an ideal choice for patients seeking a more discreet orthodontic solution without sacrificing effectiveness.

Especially in patients seeking aesthetics and discretion, they are the most viable option as they are not stained with the patient s habits.

Self-ligating braces use a specialized bracket system that does not require elastic or metal ties to hold the archwire in place. Instead, they feature a built-in sliding mechanism that reduces friction and allows for more natural tooth movement.
