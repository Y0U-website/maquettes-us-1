As a patient of Dr. Jamie Shepler, you’ll know you are in good hands. He has deep appreciation for everyone who enters the practice. Seeing his patients every day is the fulfillment of his life’s dream. His consistent confidence and focused personality allow people to feel at ease, while his passion for dentistry, eye for detail, and commitment to a higher standard are notable.

Dr. Shepler enjoys taking care of his patients’ general and cosmetic dental needs, as well as providing them with tooth-colored fillings, crowns and bridges, dentures and partials, and teeth whitening. Dr. Shepler is our resident expert at tooth extractions and root canals, as he takes pride in his ability to help alleviate patient’s pain.

Born and raised in Dayton, Dr. Shepler graduated from Northmont High School in 1986, then attended The Ohio State University on a track scholarship. He graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 1993.

Because he believes in being knowledgeable about the newest advances in dentistry, he keeps himself abreast by enrolling in continuing education classes. He enjoys treating his patients as people, wanting to help them achieve and maintain the healthiest smile possible.

When he isn’t busy in the office, Dr. Shepler, his wife and family love to spend time boating, water skiing, rock climbing, snow skiing, go-kart racing, and playing airsoft.
