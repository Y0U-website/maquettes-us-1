Orthodontic Services | Rialto, CA

If you notice any of these issues in yourself or your child, an orthodontic evaluation is a smart first step.

  • Widely spaced teeth that leave unwanted gaps in your smile
  • Overbite where the upper teeth extend too far over the lower teeth
  • Underbite where the lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth
  • Crossbite where upper and lower teeth do not meet properly
  • Open bite where the front teeth do not make contact when biting

What to Expect During Your Orthodontic Consultation

Your first visit starts with a thorough exam. Dr. Alvarez will review your teeth, your bite, and your jaw alignment. He may take digital X-rays to get a complete picture of your dental structure. From there, he will explain your options, walk you through the treatment timeline, and answer every question you have. There is no pressure and no rush. Our goal is to make sure you feel fully informed before any treatment begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • At What Age Should a Child Have Their First Orthodontic Evaluation? Most orthodontists recommend an evaluation by age seven. Early assessment allows Dr. Alvarez to spot developing issues and plan treatment at the right time.
  • How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Usually Take? Treatment length varies depending on the complexity of the case. Some patients complete treatment in as little as 12 months. Others may need up to two to three years. Dr. Alvarez will give you a realistic timeline during your consultation.
  • Is Orthodontic Treatment Painful? Some soreness after adjustments is normal and typically fades within a few days. Overall, most patients adjust quickly and find treatment very manageable.
  • Can Adults Get Orthodontic Treatment at Michael Alvarez, DDS? Yes. We treat patients of all ages. It is never too late to improve your bite and straighten your smile.
