WELCOME TO Compassion Endodontics Trusted Endodontist in Reading, PA

At Compassion Endodontics , we use modern technology to provide high quality, evidence-based endodontic specialty care while treating patients with the utmost compassion .

The practice

Compassion Endodontics is a state-of-the-art dental specialty practice which provides a full range of endodontic services, including but not limited to: Cone Beam Computed Technology (“3D Imaging”), minimally invasive root canal therapy, root canal retreatment, dental trauma, dental infections, microsurgical endodontics (apicoectomies).

Dr. Thomas P. Graham, D.M.D. is an American Board Certified Endodontist. After relocating to Pennsylvania from Alaska in 2003 to pursue his dentistry career, Dr. Graham completed his undergraduate degree at Lock Haven University, earning his Bachelors of Science in Biology and Chemistry. Therefore, to show compassion to the community, we are partnering with Hope Rescue Mission.

Hope Rescue Mission has been in Reading for over 125 years as a refuge for men struggling with addiction, homelessness, and unemployment. Their goal is to shine a light into the lives of those experiencing dark times with the utmost respect and compassion.
