About Us

At Compassion Endodontics , we use modern technology to provide high quality, evidence-based endodontic specialty care while treating patients with the utmost compassion .

Compassion Endodontics is a state-of-the-art dental specialty practice which provides a full range of endodontic services, including but not limited to: Cone Beam Computed Technology (“3D Imaging”), minimally invasive root canal therapy, root canal retreatment, dental trauma, dental infections, microsurgical endodontics (apicoectomies).

The Doctors

Thomas Graham, DMD

Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics

Dr. Thomas P. Graham, D.M.D. is an American Board Certified Endodontist. After relocating to Pennsylvania from Alaska in 2003 to pursue his dentistry career, Dr. Graham completed his undergraduate degree at Lock Haven University, earning his Bachelors of Science in Biology and Chemistry.

Tom was commissioned in the United States Army in 2006 as a Second Lieutenant while attending The University of Pittsburgh Dental School. Upon graduation in 2010, he was promoted to the rank of Captain and began his active duty service.

Since 2017, Dr. Graham has addressed the endodontic needs of patients throughout Berks, Lehigh Valley and Lancaster counties.

Dr. Graham is a member of the following organizations: American Association of Endodontics, Pennsylvania Dental Association, American Dental Association, Berks County Dental Society and College of Diplomates of the American Board of Endodontics.

Tom and Betsy are active in their church and various organizations supporting adoption. Their 4 children are adopted from Seoul, South Korea.

COMPASSION IN THE COMMUNITY

“We must learn to regard people less in light of what they do or omit to do and more in the light of what they suffer.” – Dietrich Bonhoeffer

The mission of Compassion Endodontics is to compassionately care for those who are suffering with dental disease and pain. We believe everyone is worthy of compassion. Not only do we want to care for the people that come into our office, but also the community of Reading at large. Therefore, to show compassion to the community, we are partnering with Hope Rescue Mission.

Hope Rescue Mission has been in Reading for over 125 year as a refuge for men struggling with addiction, homelessness and unemployment. Their goal is to shine a light into the lives of those experiencing dark times. They do this by not only providing for physical needs, but for caring for the whole person- spirit, soul and body. They do this with the utmost respect and compassion.

Because Compassion Endodontics and Hope Rescue Mission share the same values, Compassion Endodontics has pledged that for every root canal performed, a man experiencing homelessness is sheltered overnight and fed three meals in an Emergency Shelter Program.
