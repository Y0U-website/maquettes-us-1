Patient Compliments and Complaints

It is the goal of Rochester Regional Health to provide high-quality care with compassion. We appreciate any comments, feedback, or suggestions you may have for us regarding your care and the service provided. You may send patient compliments or complaints securely using our online patient compliments & complaints form to our Patient Relations Coordinator.

If you have a complaint, regardless of whether you have first used the internal hospital complaint process or not, you may submit it directly to the New York State Department of Health by calling their complaint hotline at 1-800-804-5447 (NYS Department of Health, Centralized Hospital Intake Program, 433 River Street, 6th floor, Troy, New York 12180).

Compliance Hotline

Administered through an independent company, the Rochester Regional Health Compliance Hotline is a toll-free, 24-hour, 7-day-a-week telephone and web-based resource that allows staff and patients to report concerns, including suspected illegal or unethical behavior; non-compliance with laws, regulations and policies; safety violations; criminal offenses; or other concerns. Calls and reports submitted may be made confidentially and completely anonymous if preferred. When remaining anonymous, one must provide enough information to facilitate a thorough investigation.
