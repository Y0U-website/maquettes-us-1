Children’s Dental Services specializes in children’s oral health care, treating children from infancy to age 26, pregnant women, and family members of all ages, regardless of family income. CDS does not discriminate based on race, color, sex, national origin, disability, religion, sexual orientation, or gender. CDS accepts multiple forms of private insurance and Medical Assistance.

No one will be denied access to services due to inability to pay. CDS offers a discounted/sliding fee schedule based on family size and income. If you are interested in applying for sliding scale services CDS employs a team of multilingual insurance and discounted care navigators to assist you.

We have staff members available to speak with you in over 20 different languages.

Children’s Dental Services provides a full range of dental services to children from birth until age twenty-six, and to pregnant women and people of all ages.
