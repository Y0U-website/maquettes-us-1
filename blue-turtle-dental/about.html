What To Expect During Your First Visit

Our goal is to make your first visit as easy and as comfortable as possible. You can be rest assured all of your appointments are free of anxiety and uncertainty.

Request Appointment

Choose from our 4 Blue Turtle Dental locations when submitting your appointment request.

We Will Text Or Call You

Within 3-5 business days, our team will reach out to you via text or call and direct you to fill out a new patient form. Be sure to have this completed before your first visit.

Your First Visit

Your first visit will include a comprehensive exam, x-rays, and a professional cleaning if appropriate. Here we will also review your health history, discuss your oral health needs and goals, as well as create a personalized treatment plan for you.
