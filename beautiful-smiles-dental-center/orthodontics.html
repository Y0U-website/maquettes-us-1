The Health Benefits of an Aligned Smile

Orthodontic treatment is commonly undertaken for it’s benefits to cosmetic problems, such as in the case of teeth that are crowded. The cosmetic improvement can help with confidence and anxiety, improving your overall mental well-being. If you feel self-conscious about your smile, then right orthodontic treatment can be incredibly freeing, leaving you completely free to enjoy the look of your smile without worry.

As well as the cosmetic benefits, there are also physical benefits to orthodontic treatment that can help to make it easier to maintain your teeth and keep them clean. Two of the benefits that orthodontic treatment in Chicago can provide, are healthier gums and a balanced bite.

A prominent dental issue in in the USA is gum disease, which affects a large proportion of the population. Many of the symptoms of gum disease can appear as a result of teeth that are crooked or crowded two issues that expert orthodontic treatment can help to reduce or eliminate completely.

Having straight teeth leads to easier cleaning of your teeth and gum line, reducing the risk of serious problems occurring, like swollen gums and rotten teeth.

Orthodontics for a Balanced Bite

Aligned teeth can improve the balance of your bite, which in the event of serious imbalance, can lead to feeling pain when eating, speaking, or moving your jaw. A balanced bite will make chewing easier, and relieve pressure on your gums, teeth, and muscles. When your bite is balanced, cleaning your teeth and maintaining good oral health is much easier.

When Can You Have Orthodontic Treatment?

In most cases, both children and adults are suitable candidates for orthodontic treatment, at the discretion of the dentist. A key requirement of all orthodontic treatment is good oral hygiene, which is essential before orthodontic treatment is undertaken.

Generally, orthodontic treatment for children is not suggested until such a time where the majority of the baby teeth have been replaced by adult teeth. In certain situations, the options available for orthodontic treatment may be limited. This can depend on the age of the patient, with older patients often having less options.
