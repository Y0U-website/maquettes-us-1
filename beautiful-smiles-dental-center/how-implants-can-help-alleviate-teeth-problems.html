How Implants Can Help Alleviate…

It’s worth noting that before getting a dental implant, you should have proper oral health and enough bone density in the jaw to support the implant. A thorough examination and x-ray by a dentist would be required to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants.

  • Loose or ill-fitting dentures: Implants can be used to secure dentures, making them more stable and comfortable to wear.
  • Bridges: Implants can be used to support a bridge, which can be used to replace one or more missing teeth.
  • Bite and jaw problems: Dental implants can help restore proper bite and jaw function, alleviating pain and discomfort caused by missing or damaged teeth.
  • Preservation of Jawbone : When a tooth is missing, the jawbone in the area begins to deteriorate. Dental implants act as replacement tooth roots, which stimulate the jawbone and help to preserve its integrity. This can also help to maintain the natural shape of your face.
