Invisalign Works in a Simple, yet Effective Way

When you choose Invisalign, your dentist at Beautiful Smiles Dental in Gurnee will provide a series of aligners to be worn for about two weeks each. Each separate aligner is designed to shape your teeth slightly more towards perfection.

The aligners are a clear mold of your teeth , to be slid over your teeth and worn all day and night. They will gently move your teeth closer to perfection over a couple of weeks. You will change aligners every two weeks and each set of aligners in the series will be closer and closer to your end result.

The aligners should be worn for the full day during the treatment, though they should be removed to eat, drink, and carry out the usual oral hygiene routines like brushing and flossing. The best results will be achieved if they are in use for all of the time other than during these activities.

We know that you will want to be wearing braces for the shortest time possible, and Invisalign works its magic quickly and efficiently, leaving you with beautifully straight teeth.

Good Oral Heath The Importance of Teeth Alignment

Straightening your teeth is incredibly important for your overall oral health. Problems like open bite and deep bite can cause much bigger issues to occur, above and beyond the appearance of teeth that are not properly aligned.

Gapped teeth can very quickly lead to ongoing problems with your gums. Overly crowded teeth can result in crooked teeth and the accumulation of plaque. Many of the problems, such as underbite and open bite, can also lead to abnormal pain when eating, or just going about your daily routine. Leaving these problems untreated can cause you to suffer undue pain, and potentially make the appearance and health of your teeth take a turn for the worst.

When properly fitted by the Beautiful Smiles Dental Center expert team, Invisalign braces will work quickly to start transforming your smile. They are molded to your gum line and fit incredibly snugly in the mouth, so none of your day-to-day activities are hindered.

With Invisalign, the whole system is subtle and understated, making it feel and look as natural as possible often, you’ll hardly notice that you’re wearing braces at all!

Invisalign in Gurnee IL The Process

At Beautiful Smiles Dental Center we want to make sure you get the very best dental solutions so before going ahead with Invisalign we’ll make sure that it’s going to give you the results you want.

Once you decide to go ahead, a mold will be taken of your teeth. Using advanced 3D technology a series of molds are created that are made to slot snugly over your teeth. The number of aligners in the series will depend roughly on how different your end result is going to be from the beginning there could be as many as 48 sets of aligners in the series, or as few as 12. The process in the lab is overseen by professional orthodontists who will adjust the aligners as they see fit.

At your next visit to Beautiful Smiles Dental Center, you’ll receive your first set of braces. You should try to wear these for most of the day, 22-23 hours a day for the best results. You can (and should) remove them to eat, drink, and brush and floss your teeth. Your aligners will be worn for two weeks, at which time you’ll return to your dentist to receive your second set, and so on. Your teeth are gradually repositioned over time.

When you finish the two weeks of your final set of aligners, your treatment is finished. Your teeth will look exactly the way you want them to, and it’s time to show them off as much as possible!

Best of all, you will have gone through the entire procedure without metal wires or bands irritating your mouth and drawing attention. The invisible design of Invisalign is barely noticeable, and won’t disrupt your lifestyle in any way.
