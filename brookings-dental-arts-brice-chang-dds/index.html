Replacing a tooth with an implant is a process that takes several steps.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that serves as the replacement for the root of your missing tooth. Dr. Brice Chang places the implant into your jaw, where it will naturally fuse with your bone to become a solid anchor. After the implant surgery, patients must wait several months to let their gums heal.

The next step is to place a metal cylinder, called an abutment, into the implant. The final step is securing the fake tooth – called a restoration – onto the abutment.

Once the dental implant process is complete, you can chew, brush and floss as normal. And you can smile with confidence because your implant is designed to match the rest of your teeth.

If you are considering a dental implant, it’s crucial to work with a highly trained dentist who has extensive experience. Tooth loss and other dental issues are complex. You need a dentist with the experience to diagnose and explain the best options for your unique situation. There’s no one-size-fits-all when it comes to dental implants.
