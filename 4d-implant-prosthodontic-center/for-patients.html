For Patients

At 4D Implant Prosthodontic Center, we are dedicated to crafting beautiful smiles and restoring your confidence. With years of advanced training and expertise, our team possesses a deep understanding of both the functional and aesthetic aspects of your smile.

Here you will find information on what to expect during your first appointment along with a link to our new patient form.

What to expect. Whether you are seeking to correct a single tooth imperfection or undergo a complete smile makeover, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

To prepare for your visit, be ready to discuss your medical history and share any past x-rays or other useful information. Please bring your new patient form linked above to expedite check-in.

Dr. Mourad will review any previous medical records and will meet with you one-on-one to talk through concerns and goals and create a treatment plan tailored specifically to your needs.

Please arrive approximately 15 minutes prior to your consultation so that we can verify any information. A parent or guardian must accompany patients under the age of 18.

We accept most insurances. If you have questions about insurance coverage, or would like to talk through treatment plan options, someone on the 4D team will be able to assist you before or after your visit.

A new you starts here.
