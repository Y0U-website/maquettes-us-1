Dr. Steven DeLisle, DDS, is a highly experienced children’s dentist and special needs dentist serving families in the Missoula area, Lolo, Frenchtown, and across Western Montana. A graduate of the prestigious University of Washington School of Dentistry, Dr. DeLisle went on to complete a specialized residency in Dental Anesthesiology at Loma Linda University.

During his residency, he successfully treated thousands of children and patients with special needs under general anesthesia and sedation dentistry , gaining extensive expertise in caring for anxious, medically complex, or vulnerable individuals.

Today, Dr. DeLisle is a dedicated advocate for special needs dentistry in Montana , ensuring that children and adults who face barriers to care receive gentle, high-quality dental treatment. As a Medicaid provider , he makes essential dental services accessible to more families, including many children who might otherwise go without care.
