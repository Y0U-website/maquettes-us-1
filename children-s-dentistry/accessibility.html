Accessibility Children’s Dentistry in Montana

We recognize accessibility is an ongoing effort and continuously work to improve the usability of our Website for all visitors.

  • Accessibility Children’s Dentistry in Montana Skip to content Accessibility Lolo Children’s Dentist is committed to making our website and services accessible to all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and to ensure that every parent, guardian, and visitor can easily use our website and access the information they need. Our Commitment We aim to provide an accessible digital experience for all users. Our ongoing efforts include: Designing the website with accessibility principles in mind
  • Using readable fonts, clear structure, and simple navigation
  • Ensuring images include alternative text when appropriate
  • Maintaining proper color contrast for readability
  • Making the site usable with assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnifiers, and keyboard navigation
  • Regularly reviewing and updating website features to improve accessibility
