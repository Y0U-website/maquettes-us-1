About Us

Our Mission

Our goal at Children’s Dentistry is to provide a fun dental experience for your children in a safe place. Our highly trained team of dentists, assistants, and front desk personnel work together to provide the highest-quality care for your family.

Your child is not just a file number at Children’s Dentistry of Lolo . They are a cherished part of our family, and we care for them like our own. We proudly serve all of Western Montana, including Missoula, Lolo, Florence, Victor, Stevensville, Corvallis, Hamilton, and Darby. Plus, we accept Montana Medicaid and most private dental insurance plans.

We are well-equipped to provide premium dentistry to all children, including those with special needs . You can trust that your child is in caring and compassionate hands with us.

Our Services

We pride ourselves on providing the highest-quality dental services for children in the Missoula area.

Our preventative dental services protect your children’s teeth and prevent any problems from occurring. We take x-rays of their mouths to detect the slightest complications. Then we use procedures like fluoride treatments, dental cleanings, and dental sealants to give them stronger teeth.

Our restorative dental care allows us to treat dental caries of any depth in children using white composite fillings , dental crowns , root canals, extractions, space maintainers, and deep cleanings.

About Dr. Delisle

Dr. Steven DeLisle, DDS, is a highly experienced children’s dentist and special needs dentist serving families in the Missoula area, Lolo, Frenchtown, and across Western Montana. A graduate of the prestigious University of Washington School of Dentistry, Dr. DeLisle went on to complete a specialized residency in Dental Anesthesiology at Loma Linda University.

During his residency, he successfully treated thousands of children and patients with special needs under general anesthesia and sedation dentistry , gaining extensive expertise in caring for anxious, medically complex, or vulnerable individuals.

Today, Dr. DeLisle is a dedicated advocate for special needs dentistry in Montana , ensuring that children and adults who face barriers to care receive gentle, high-quality dental treatment. As a Medicaid provider , he makes essential dental services accessible to more families, including many children who might otherwise go without care.

Conveniently, Dr. DeLisle offers Saturday appointments at his practice—ideal for parents and patients who can’t miss work or school during the week.

Outside the office, Dr. DeLisle enjoys managing his guest ranch in Montana, where he raises Highland cattle and bison.

If you’re searching for a children’s dentist near Missoula , a special needs dentist , or a sedation dentist accepting Medicaid in Montana , contact Lolo Children’s Dentistry today to schedule an appointment with Dr. DeLisle.
