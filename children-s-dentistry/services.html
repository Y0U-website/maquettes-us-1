Preventative Dental Care

Taking a preventative approach to your children’s dental health is the way to go. Dental caries can start forming as soon as the baby teeth appear. Early check-ups and preventative measures are necessary to ensure your children do not develop cavities. Preventive dental care can also give your children a healthier smile and better general health while helping them avoid later restorative work.

  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Dental Cleanings
  • X-rays
  • Dental Sealants

Restorative Dental Care

Even if you teach your children the best oral health practices, they may still develop cavities. Dental restorations can treat these cavities to maintain the tooth’s structure and prevent further dental problems. Restorative dentistry can also prevent damage to other teeth, restore the functionality and aesthetics of the tooth, and promote good oral health.

  • Composite Fillings (White)
  • Dental Crowns
  • Root Canals
  • Extractions
  • space Maintainers
  • Deep Cleanings
