Meet Anis Tebyanian DMD, MD

More About Anis Tebyanian DMD, MD

Diplomate of American Board Of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Dr. Tebyanian is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon whose education and training reflect a lifelong commitment to excellence. After earning his dental degree from the University of Connecticut, he went on to complete his medical degree at Stony Brook School of Medicine. He then completed rigorous residency training in oral and maxillofacial surgery, gaining expertise in complex surgical procedures that span both dental and medical disciplines.

His advanced background allows him to provide a wide range of treatments, including dental implant placement, full mouth reconstruction, corrective jaw surgery, TMJ disorder treatment, facial cosmetic procedures, and care for facial trauma. Patients appreciate not only his surgical skill but also the way he listens, explains options clearly, and helps them feel at ease.

For Dr. Tebyanian, patient relationships are just as important as clinical results. He takes pride in forging long-term connections and helping people improve their health, function, and confidence. Living in Rockville with his wife and daughter, he understands the needs of the community and is dedicated to serving families across Maryland with honesty and compassion.
