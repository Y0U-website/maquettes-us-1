Get to Know Your Endodontist

Meet Dr. Eshwar Arasu

Dr. Eshwar Arasu is a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics and a trusted specialist in root canal therapy and surgical endodontics. His career began with a degree in Biomedical Engineering from the University of Michigan, where his senior project—a root canal filling device for veterinarians—sparked his interest in dentistry. That passion led him to the Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where he earned his DMD, followed by a residency and Master’s in Dentistry at Virginia Commonwealth University.

Throughout his training and career, Dr. Arasu has been drawn to the meticulous nature of endodontic care . His clinical research has focused on 3D imaging in endodontic surgery, and his work reflects a strong commitment to staying at the forefront of modern dental technology. Whether he’s using advanced diagnostic tools or performing complex procedures under a microscope, he approaches every case with technical precision and a steady hand.

Before opening Arbor Endodontics, Dr. Arasu spent eight years practicing in Nashville, TN, where he became a partner in a group endodontic practice and developed a strong foundation in patient care and collaborative dentistry. He also presented numerous continuing education courses to general dentists, deepening his expertise in both the clinical and educational aspects of the field.

Today, Dr. Arasu is excited to bring his skills back to the Midwest. He and his wife, a Grand Rapids native, are proud to call West Michigan home as they raise their two daughters. Whether you’ve been referred for treatment or are experiencing an unexpected dental emergency, Dr. Arasu and the Arbor team are here to guide you with empathy, clarity, and care you can count on.

Your Visit, Simplified

What to Expect at Your First Appointment

Your first visit typically begins with a consultation, where we’ll gather digital imaging, assess your tooth, and explain your diagnosis and treatment options in detail. If treatment is recommended and you&#x27;re ready, we may be able to begin that same day. Our team is here to answer questions and help you feel confident every step of the way.

A Calming Experience We know that endodontic care can feel overwhelming—but it doesn’t have to be. Our office is designed to be peaceful and private, and our team is trained to offer kind, judgment-free support. We’ll explain everything clearly, give you options, and help make your visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

Referral information from your general dentist

Any questions or concerns—we’re here to help!

Advanced Tools and Thoughtful Touches for Better Care

Our technology allows us to treat your tooth with accuracy, efficiency, and less discomfort. At the same time, our amenities are designed to make your visit feel calm and supportive—so you can relax and focus on healing.

GentleWave®

This advanced irrigation system cleans complex root canal anatomy more thoroughly than traditional methods, offering better results and faster healing with less post-op sensitivity.

CBCT Imaging

Our 3D cone beam imaging gives us an ultra-detailed view of your tooth structure, helping us pinpoint the cause of your discomfort and plan more precise, effective treatment.

Zeiss Operating Microscopes

We use surgical-grade microscopes for all treatments, ensuring enhanced visibility and accuracy—especially in complex or previously treated cases.

Blankets & Noise-canceling Headphones

We offer blankets and noise-canceling headphones so you can tune out during your procedure and feel at ease throughout your visit.

Digital Patient Forms

All of our intake forms are available online and integrated with our secure patient portal, so you can complete everything from home—no paperwork required at check-in.

Emergency Availability

For patients of record, we offer after-hours support for urgent dental pain. Simply leave a voicemail and it will forward to our emergency line.

Giving Back, Growing Roots

Conservation-Minded Dentistry

At Arbor Endodontics, we believe in doing our part to support the natural beauty of our home. That’s why we plan to participate in local conservation efforts and use Arbor Day as an opportunity to promote environmental awareness. From sourcing office materials locally to future partnerships with local nonprofits, we’re building a practice that honors the community we serve.

We know that when a dental office gives back, everyone benefits. Our outreach programs will focus on preserving both smiles and nature—and by choosing Arbor Endodontics, you’re helping us grow those efforts from the ground up.
