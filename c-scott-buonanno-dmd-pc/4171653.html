Our Staff

Dr. C. Scott Buonanno

Dr. Scott Buonanno graduated from Fairfield University in 1987 with a B.S. in Biology, and in 1991, graduated from the University Of Connecticut School Of Dental Medicine and received his DMD degree. Five years later, he joined the practice of Dr. Derrol Rudnick, and shortly afterwards, became the sole provider at the practice.

Dr. Buonanno is always seeking additional training and education to provide his patients with cutting-edge, complete dental care. He is certified in surgical placement and restoration of implants and was

one of the first clinicians in Connecticut to implement the

practice philosophy: It has always bothered me that people find taking care of their teeth to be so intimidating. With a little TLC, some humor, and some really great technology, we can make it easier for our patients to have the smiles they have always wanted.
