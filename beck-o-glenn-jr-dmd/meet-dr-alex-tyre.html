Dr. Tyre was born and raised in Jacksonville, Florida. He lived in Utah for 4 years, where he loved to ski, hike, and explore the beautiful National Parks. He is happy to be back home in Florida and close to family. Outside of work, he spends his free time on the farm with his wife, Ally, their son, Will, and their dogs and horses.
