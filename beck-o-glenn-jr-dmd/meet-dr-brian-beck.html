Personal Life

Born and raised in Tallahassee, Dr. Beck left to pursue his education and then returned in 2013 to begin practicing at Tallahassee Dental Associates. Dr. Beck loves Tallahassee and its citizens. Outside of the office, you will find Dr. Beck spending time with his lovely wife and their Yellow Labrador Retrievers, Buster and Chester. He is an avid sports fan and especially enjoys playing tennis and golf.

Giving back to the community is one of Dr. Beck’s passions. He’s actively involved with the We Care Dental Network, where he is able to deliver state-of-the-art dental treatment to the underserved in our community at no cost to them. Since so many of these patients seek treatment for severe dental pain, Dr. Beck is able to quickly treat them and relieve them of their pain, thereby enhancing their quality of life.
