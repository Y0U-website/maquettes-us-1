Crowns

  • Capturing a Model of the Tooth: An impression or digital scan of the prepared tooth is taken to create a precise model for the crown design. This model serves as the blueprint for your custom crown.
  • Designing the Crown: Utilizing the model, the dental technician or a computer-aided design (CAD) system crafts the design of the crown, ensuring it matches the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth.
  • Fabricating the Crown: The crown is then fabricated using the chosen material, which ranges from metals to porcelain, depending on the specific requirements and aesthetic considerations.
  • Cementing the Crown: Once fabricated, the crown is carefully fitted and adjusted as necessary before being permanently cemented to the prepared tooth, fully restoring its function and appearance.

Revolutionizing Restoration: Same-Day Crowns (CEREC)

Same-day crowns, also known as CEREC (Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics), represent a significant advancement in dental technology. Through CAD/CAM (computer-aided design/computer-aided manufacturing) technology, this innovative process allows for the design, creation, and

installation of a custom-fitted crown in a single dental visit. Here’s how it revolutionizes the traditional dental crown process:

This innovative technology not only streamlines the process but also enhances patient comfort and satisfaction by delivering high-quality restorations with impressive speed and precision. Whether you’re dealing with a broken tooth or severe decay, same-day crowns offer a convenient and effective solution without compromising quality.

  • Efficiency: CEREC eliminates the need for multiple appointments, making it a time-saver for patients and dentists alike.
  • Precision: Digital impressions are more accurate, reducing the risk of errors and ensuring a better fit.
  • Convenience: With no need for a temporary crown, patients experience less discomfort and inconvenience.
  • Material Quality: CEREC crowns are made from high-quality ceramic, offering durability and a natural appearance.
