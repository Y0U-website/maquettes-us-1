Les soins

At Bedford Dental Care we believe in bringing out your best smiles and keeping you healthy. Our focus on preventive dentistry helps you avoid cavities, gum disease, enamel wear, and more.

Dental implants are able to give you a superior level of support and stability when used with dentures. Implant-supported dentures use a few implants to keep your denture in place. A bar is placed on top of the implants to help attach a denture to keep it in…

They don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support-you do not need to damage the neighboring teeth to make a bridge to replace a missing tooth.
