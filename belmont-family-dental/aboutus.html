About Us

History

Dr. Peter Juliano opened the practice doors in 1960. From then on, our practice has been serving the Brockton and South Shore community. In 1976, Dr. Richard Celli joined the practice. Dr. Alison Hubbard joined the practice in 2016. Our practice has enjoyed treating patients and delivering quality dental care for 50 years.

Our Mission is to deliver individually tailored programs of high quality, complete dental care in a caring and comfortable environment with an emphasis on our patient’s dental health education.

Dentists

owner

Dr. Alison Hubbard, DMD

Originally from Buffalo, NY, Dr. Hubbard moved to Massachusetts for college, fell in love with the state and has never left. After meeting her husband, a Brockton native, in dental school, she decided to make Brockton her home as well.

She enjoys skiing, traveling and working out. She loves playing with her two dogs, Boomer and Cruiser. She also enjoys cheering on the Buffalo Bills.
