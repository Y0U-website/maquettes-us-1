Appointments Dental Implants Perhaps the greatest advance in the field of dentistry over the past generation has been the advent of the osteointegrated dental implant. Today’s dental implant can provide a patient with the ability to replace partial and full dentures and to restore missing teeth without the necessity of using other teeth as anchors. Fixed bridgework can replace removable partial dentures and implants also allow for the stabilization of previously loose ill-fitting dentures.

In essence, the implant will replace roots of teeth and will allow the dentist to fabricate restorations over these artificial roots. Dental implants are designed to provide a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel and function like natural teeth. The person who has lost teeth regains the ability to eat virtually anything and can smile with confidence, knowing that their teeth will appear natural.

In order to determine whether an area where a tooth or teeth are missing would be a good site for a dental implant, it may be necessary to do a full radiographic study of the area including a CBCT 3-dimensional scan. Once a determination that the site is satisfactory to accept an implant is confirmed, a complete treatment plan must be determined by your periodontist and the general dentist or prosthodontist who will be restoring the teeth.

There are many different ways a dental implant and the replacement tooth may be place. In certain cases, it is possible that a replacement tooth can be place over an implant in one visit. More frequently, however, placing of a dental implant and restoring a tooth is generally done in several stage.

There are some implant systems (one-stage) that do not require this second step. These systems use an implant which already has the extension piece attached. We will advise you on which system is best for you.

Return to Our Services Frequently Asked Questions What are dental implants and how do they replace missing teeth? + Dental implants are biocompatible titanium posts placed into the jawbone that act as artificial tooth roots and support lifelike replacement teeth that look and function like natural teeth.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Good candidates are adults with missing teeth, adequate jawbone density, and healthy gums. A CBCT 3D scan and consultation will determine if implants are right for you.

What is the dental implant procedure and how long does it take?

The implant process includes surgical placement of the implant into the jawbone, a period of osseointegration, and attachment of a crown or restoration. The total timeline varies by case.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and routine dental checkups, dental implants can last many years and often a lifetime.

What are the benefits of dental implants compared to bridges or dentures?

Implants preserve bone, look natural, function like real teeth, and do not require filing down adjacent teeth like traditional bridges do.

Are dental implants painful or require sedation?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during implant placement, and sedation options are available to ensure comfort throughout the procedure.

Do dental implants require special maintenance?

No, implants are cared for with regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings, similar to natural teeth.

What types of dental implant treatments do you offer?

We provide solutions including single tooth implants, multiple teeth implants, and implant‑supported dentures to restore function and aesthetics.

Will my dental insurance cover the cost of dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan. Many PPO dental insurance plans cover portions of implant treatment, including stages like implant placement and crown attachment.

Can dental implants be placed immediately after a tooth extraction?

In some cases, immediate implant placement is possible and may reduce the number of surgical visits, but this depends on your individual bone and gum health.
