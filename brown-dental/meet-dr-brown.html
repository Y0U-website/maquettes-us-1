Meet Dr. Brown

At Brown Dental, we’re proud to offer comprehensive pediatric dental services tailored to your child’s needs — from preventive care and restorative treatments to early orthodontic intervention.

Dr. Brown founded Brown Dental with a clear vision: to create a pediatric dental practice where children feel safe, comfortable, and excited about taking care of their teeth. His approach combines clinical excellence with genuine compassion, ensuring that every young patient receives personalized care that addresses both their dental health and emotional well-being.

Dr. Brown is committed to serving families throughout Stockbridge and Henry County, accepting Medicaid and most major insurance plans to ensure that quality pediatric dental care is accessible to all children in the community. Whether performing routine checkups, treating cavities, or providing early orthodontic intervention with Invisalign and phase one treatments, Dr. Brown approaches every case with the same dedication to excellence and patient-centered care.

Philosophy of Care

Dr. Brown’s approach to pediatric dentistry is built on trust, patience, and creating positive experiences that shape how children view dental care for the rest of their lives. He takes the time to build genuine connections with young patients and their families, using a calm demeanor and gentle techniques to help even the most anxious children feel comfortable. Whether it’s a routine cleaning or a more complex procedure, every visit at Brown Dental is designed to be educational, stress-free, and encouraging for both children and parents.
