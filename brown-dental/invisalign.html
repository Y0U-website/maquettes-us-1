Is Invisalign Right for Your Child?

Invisalign can effectively treat many common orthodontic issues in children and teens, including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. However, it is not the right fit for every case. Very complex bite problems, severe tooth rotations, or cases requiring permanent tooth extractions may achieve better results with traditional braces. Some children may also benefit from phase one orthodontic treatment first to address jaw growth or severe crowding before proceeding with Invisalign during the teen years.

Compliance is another important factor. Aligners must be worn 20 to 22 hours per day to be effective, and teens who are not yet ready to commit to that consistency are unlikely to see good results. During your child’s consultation, Dr. Brown evaluates the complexity of their case and their maturity level to determine whether Invisalign is the right choice, and he provides honest recommendations based on what will achieve the best outcome for your child’s smile.

Benefits of Choosing Invisalign

Invisalign offers advantages that appeal to both parents and teens. The nearly invisible appearance means your teen can smile confidently in photos and social situations without feeling self-conscious, and because the aligners are removable, brushing and flossing remain simple and effective. This reduces the risk of cavities and gum problems that can develop when food gets trapped around traditional brackets and wires.

Beyond appearance, the lifestyle benefits are meaningful. Athletes can continue playing sports without worry, musicians can play instruments without interference, and there are no dietary restrictions to navigate throughout treatment. The aligners are made from smooth plastic with no sharp edges, so there is no irritation to cheeks or gums and no emergency visits for broken brackets. Treatment time is often comparable to traditional braces, with many cases completing in 12 to 18 months depending on the complexity of the case.

Supporting Your Child’s Treatment Success

Dr. Brown schedules progress appointments every 6 to 8 weeks to ensure teeth are moving as planned, check compliance, and provide the next sets of aligners. These visits are typically quick and fit easily into busy family schedules. Throughout treatment, we celebrate milestones along the way to keep young patients motivated and engaged as they watch their smiles transform.
