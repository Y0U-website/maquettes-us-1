Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Beverly Gonzales

Dr. Beverly Gonzales graduated in 1999 from UCLA School of Dentistry after earning her BS in Biological Sciences from UC Irvine. She is a member of the ADA (American Dental Association), CDA (California Dental Association) and OCDS (Orange County Dental Society). She continues to improve her clinical skills by taking continuing education courses on all aspects of dentistry, including patient management.

Outside of the dental office, her charities include Operation Gratitude, A3M Hope, Heart for Home, American Heart Association and local organizations such as John F. Kennedy High School & Los Coyotes Elementary School. During her free time, she likes to run, cook, read, do yoga & Zumba. Recently she explored creative writing but will definitely keep her day job as she is a much better dentist than writer. Most of all, she loves to travel and make memories with family and friends.

Patty Corrales

Patty is our Office Manager. She has been in the dental field and in our office for over 8 years! She started as a dental assistant and worked her way up to management. Her goal is to make sure your dental visit is as comfortable as possible. From insurance information to treatment procedures, she makes sure that she addresses all of your questions and concerns. She deeply cares about the health and well being of all our patients! On her free time she enjoys spending time with her family, exploring a good place to eat and traveling.

Karen

Mayra

I’m Mayra, a dental assistant currently based in La Palma. With 3 years of experience in dentistry. I’m passionate about doing meaningful work. My favorite part of working in dentistry is helping patients feel confident in their smiles. Seeing someone leave happier and healthier than when they came in is incredibly rewarding.

I love building trust with patients, especially those with dental anxiety and showing them that compassionate care can change their entire experience. I’m known for my great attitude, honesty, kindness and respectfulness. I enjoy spending time with family and friends in my free time and I’m always open for new adventures and laughter. I’m excited to connect with Beverly Dentistry’s patients and share my expertise.

Mishell

With over 8 years of experience in the dental field, Mishell brings not only technical expertise but also a deep passion for patient care. A graduate of dental school in Boston, Massachusetts, she has built her career on the foundation of delivering high-quality service while creating meaningful connections with each person who walks through the door.

Now based in California, Mishell is excited to be part of a new dental office where she looks forward to building long-term relationships with both the team and the patients. Her approach goes beyond clinical care she genuinely enjoys getting to know her patients and ensuring their experiences are positive, personal, and even enjoyable.

Outside the office, Mishell leads an active lifestyle. She loves spending her free time at the gym, going on hikes, and embracing new challenges that push her physically and mentally. She believes that the same dedication she brings to her personal goals carries over into her professional life, where she strives to grow continuously and make a lasting impact.
