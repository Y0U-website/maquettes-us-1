Financial Information

As a fee for service dental practice, payment is due at the time of service unless prior arrangements have been made with our office.

Cash, Personal Checks, Money Orders, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, CareCredit, and Cherry.

We are happy to work with third party financing companies to help make treatment more affordable. However, please note that we do not offer in house financing or payment plans.

For established patients undergoing larger procedures, additional payment options may be available through our financing partners.

If you have any questions about payment or financing through CareCredit or Cherry, our team is happy to review your options with you before treatment begins.
