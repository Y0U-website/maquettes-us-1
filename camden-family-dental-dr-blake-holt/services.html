Our Dental Services

Preventative/Diagnostic

Preventative dentistry is a broad term describing various dental procedures that treat tooth decay and other forms of early dental disease. We keep your teeth, gums, and mouth as vibrant and healthy while actively working to avoid tooth loss.

Crowns

Dental crowns are specialty created coverings placed over your existing tooth when it is decayed, damaged, or simply discolored. Crowns protect damaged and weak teeth, reinforcing them and working to stop additional breakage or decay.

Dental Restorations

Restorative dentistry is the management of oral disease in order to keep and maintain a healthy and functional mouth. This includes dental implants, fillings, crowns, bridges and dentures.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are the virtually invisible orthodontic treatment that make misaligned and crooked teeth more straight. Clear aligner therapy is a great option over traditional brackets and wires as they are removable and much more convenient.
