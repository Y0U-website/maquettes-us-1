Dental Care for a Healthy Smile in Davison, Michigan

Maintaining your oral health is crucial to detecting and addressing dental issues promptly, from tooth decay to oral cancer. At Dr. Aimee Vakula-Rollins, we prioritize your well-being above all else. Our goal is to ensure your teeth remain healthy, fully functional, and aesthetically pleasing. From routine cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments, all your essential dental needs are met right here.

Explore Our Range of Dental Solutions

Explore the diverse array of dental services we provide:

General Dentistry: We perform regular checkups and teeth cleanings to keep your smile in optimal health.

Family Dentistry: Our practice offers dental care for individuals of all ages, ensuring a healthy smile for your whole family.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Improving the appearance of teeth, gums, and smile through procedures like whitening, veneers, bonding and reshaping.

Professional Teeth Cleanings: Maintain excellent oral health with our regular cleaning services.

Periodontal (Gum) Disease Therapy: Preserve your teeth and gum health, preventing tooth loss.

Sealants: Protect your children’s teeth from decay with our preventive sealant treatment.

Oral Cancer Screenings: Detect diseases at a curable stage with our thorough screenings.

TMJ/TMD Treatment: Find relief from chronic jaw pain and discomfort with our specialized treatment.

Fillings: Strengthen your teeth and restore their health.

Bonding: Repair small chips or cracks, restoring your teeth’s strength and appearance.

Veneers: Enhance your smile and conceal teeth deformities with this cosmetic procedure.

Porcelain Veneers: Repair larger chips and cracks while reshaping your teeth for a stunning smile.

Teeth Whitening: Brighten your faded or discolored smile, restoring its radiance.

Root Canal Treatment: Save an infected tooth, preserving your natural smile.

Crowns & Bridgework: Replace substantial tooth loss with durable solutions tailored to your needs.

Dental Implants: Benefit from the longest-lasting restorative treatment, providing permanent tooth replacements.

Removable Dentures and Partials: Regain your smile’s brilliance and function with our expertly crafted dentures and partials.

Orthodontic Treatment: Achieve the perfect alignment of your teeth for a confident, healthy smile.

Emergency Dental Care

In the event of a dental emergency, your safety is our utmost priority. If you experience a life-threatening or severe injury, please call 911 or head directly to the nearest hospital emergency room for immediate assistance. We are here to address a variety of traumatic dental injuries, including chipped, dislodged, or completely knocked-out teeth. Do not hesitate to contact our office for urgent dental care.
