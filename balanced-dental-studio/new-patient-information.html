Frequently Asked Questions

What is general dentistry? General dentistry is the evaluation, diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of diseases and conditions of the body’s oral and maxillofacial region (ADA, American Dental Association). Rather than specializing in one area, general dentists offer restorative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, dental exams and teeth cleanings, cavity fillings, pediatric dentistry, and more.

What do I need to know about general dentistry services? General dentistry services are available for individuals from age 4 to adults. Whether preventative, restorative, or emergency dental, we provide care for all types of dental issues.

What is the cost of general dentistry services offered? The cost of general dentistry services greatly depends on the complexity of the dental issue, oral health history, and more. For an accurate quote, schedule an appointment and we’ll provide a cost for your unique situation.

What are the benefits of getting general dentistry services? You can provide great care for your teeth through regular preventative check ups and cleaning, and by addressing common dental issues. By giving care to your teeth now, you’ll ensure your teeth’s longevity and better overall health for years to come.
