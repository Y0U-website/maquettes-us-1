What to expect at your first appointment intros & office tours At your Free First Exam our New Patient Concierge will give you a tour of the office, introduce you to Dr. G, answer any questions you may have, and make sure you feel comfortable and excited about getting started. You will spend the majority of your appointment in one-on-one consultation in our private Treatment Coordination office.

Digital records & impressions

We’re a digitized office! This means no more sticky impressions or paper records. We use an iTero Element® 3D scanner to digitally scan your teeth and get a series of interactive, high-resolution, 3D images. This provides us with more accuracy and less mess. During this time you will also take a few simple xrays and photos to add to your digital new patient chart.

Meet Dr. G

We proudly set ourselves apart by making Dr. Gochnour available to you at every single appointment. At your first appoint, when your initial work up is complete, Dr. G will go over your customized treatment plan with you and help manage your expectations for starting treatment. He will also be able to answer any questions you may have regarding orthodontics, Invisalign® aligners or traditional braces .

Cost & Financing

We know cost can be a concern. Our Treatment Coordinator will provide you with a quick, straightforward financial discussion. We offer a variety of affordable financing options and insurance plans, so you can stress less about money and focus more on transforming your smile.

Schedule next appointment

We hope that by the end of your new patient exam you are as excited as we are to start your treatment! we’ll help you schedule your next appointment before you go, and you can expect an appointment reminder before your next visit, which could be in as little as four weeks!

We now offer Virtual Appointment Options for new Invisalign patients! Ask our Treatment Coordinator if this option is right for you!
