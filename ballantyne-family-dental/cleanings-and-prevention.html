How Often Should You Get a Dental Cleaning?

Most patients should schedule a cleaning every six months. This twice-a-year rhythm allows us to catch small issues, like early decay or mild gum inflammation, before they turn into something that requires more involved treatment. Patients with a history of gum disease or a higher risk of cavities may benefit from more frequent visits.

Signs You May Need More Than a Routine Cleaning

While regular cleanings prevent most issues, contact us if you notice:

These can be early signs of periodontal disease , which may require periodontal treatment beyond a standard cleaning. If a cavity is found during your visit, we can typically address it with a simple composite filling .

  • Gums that bleed easily when brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Gums that appear red, swollen, or are pulling away from your teeth
  • Sensitivity or visible dark spots on a tooth
  • Loose or shifting teeth

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most patients should visit every six months for a cleaning and checkup. Your dentist may recommend a different schedule based on your individual oral health needs.

Do dental cleanings hurt?

Routine cleanings are generally comfortable for most patients. If you have sensitive teeth or gums, let your hygienist know so they can adjust their approach to keep you comfortable.

What&#x27;s the difference between a regular cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A regular cleaning focuses on the visible surfaces of your teeth and just below the gumline. A deep cleaning, or scaling and root planing, treats more advanced buildup below the gums and is typically recommended if signs of gum disease are present.

Can preventive care really help me avoid cavities and gum disease?

Yes. Removing plaque and tartar before it causes damage, combined with fluoride and sealants when appropriate, significantly reduces your risk of developing cavities and gum disease.

Will my insurance cover a cleaning?

Most dental insurance plans cover routine cleanings, often at little to no cost to you. Our team can help verify your benefits, and you can learn more on our financing and insurance page .
