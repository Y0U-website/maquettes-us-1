How long do results last?

With proper aftercare, results can last several months. Regular touch-ups and good oral hygiene will help keep your smile bright.

Can I eat or drink after my appointment?

For the first 24-48 hours , avoid foods and drinks that could stain a white shirt, including:

  • Coffee
  • Red wine
  • Soda
  • Dark berries
  • Tomato sauce
  • Soy sauce
  • Tobacco products

Who is a good candidate for teeth whitening?

Most healthy adults and teens with permanent teeth are good candidates. Whitening works best on natural teeth and will not whiten crowns, veneers, fillings, or bonding.

Can I whiten if I have crowns or veneers?

Yes, but whitening only affects natural teeth. Existing dental work will remain the same color.

How often should I whiten?

Most clients maintain their smile every 3–6 months, depending on their lifestyle and habits.

Is there anything I should do before my appointment?

We recommend brushing your teeth beforehand and arriving with clean teeth. Please try to brush no more than 4 hours prior to your appointment. For best results and to eliminate sensitivity, come well hydrated. Please let us know at time of booking if you have naturally sensitive teeth. This allows us to reserve one of out specialized sensitivity-friendly whitening lamps for your session.

Do I need a dental exam first?

If you have untreated cavities, gum disease, severe tooth pain, or other dental concerns, we recommend seeing your dentist before whitening.

Hydrate Before Your Appointment!

Hydrate, hydrate and hydrate some more. We can’t stress this enough! The only sensitivity you should experience from our products would be due to dehydrated nerves. It is important that you drink at least a gallon of water a full 24 hours before the appointment. Don’t wait the day of your appointment to start hydrating. Start 24 hours prior! The better hydrated you are, the better results you’ll have.

Eat Before Your Appointment

You will not be able to eat for 2 hours after your appointment. Eat before your scheduled time.

After Care Instructions

Avoid eating and drinking for the next 2 hours, with the exception of water . Drinking a lot of water (room temperature) for the next 2 hours will help to rehydrate your teeth and avoid sensitivity. We do have take home desensitizing treatments available for purchase. If sensitivity presists, Sensodyne toothpaste can be used, but should go away within 24-48 hours.

For the next 24-48 hours after treatment:

Avoid smoking, chewing tobacco, as well as vapes and hooka products.

Avoid eating and drinking anything that is too hot or too cold.

Avoid colored dental products; such as mouthwash or colored toothpaste.

Use a mouthwash or toothpaste designed for sensitivity, such as Sensodyne

A pain reliever can be used if you feel any discomfort.

We have take home desensitizing treatments available for purchase. (Recommended for people who experience sensitivity on a regular basis)

Eat and drink clear foods and beverages. Avoid any foods with color that can restain your teeth.
