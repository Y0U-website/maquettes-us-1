How Do Dental Veneers in Yucaipa, CA Work?

Dental veneers are one of the most effective cosmetic dental solutions available today, and at Calimesa Dental, we tailor the process entirely to you. These thin, custom-made shells are designed to cover the front surfaces of your teeth, instantly improving appearance and masking imperfections.

Once your plan is in place, the next step is preparing your teeth for veneer placement. This involves carefully removing a very small amount of enamel from the front surface—just enough to ensure a perfect fit and a natural-looking result. Although minimal, this preparation is essential for creating a smooth bonding surface. After preparation, new impressions or digital scans are taken to ensure your veneers are crafted with absolute precision.

Your custom veneers are then fabricated by skilled technicians at a trusted dental laboratory. Using advanced technology, they craft thin, durable shells in the exact shape, size, and color needed to achieve your desired smile. This process typically takes a couple of weeks. During this time, we often place temporary veneers, allowing you to protect your teeth and preview your upcoming smile.

Once your E-max veneers are ready, you’ll return to our office for the exciting final step. Dr. You will check the fit, shape, and shade, making any small adjustments necessary for perfection. The veneers are then bonded to your teeth using a strong dental adhesive. A special curing light hardens the adhesive, locking your veneers into place for a secure, long-lasting fit.

After placement, we’ll provide clear instructions for caring for your veneers, from brushing and flossing to dietary choices. With the right care, your veneers will give you a radiant, confident smile for many years.

Caring for Your Dental Veneers in Yucaipa, CA

To ensure your veneers last as long as possible, we recommend following these care guidelines:

With these simple steps, your veneers will stay beautiful and strong for many years.

  • Brush your teeth twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to remove plaque and food particles from between your teeth and veneers.
  • Schedule professional cleanings and dental checkups every six months.
  • Visit us regularly for dental examinations to check the condition of your veneers.
  • Limit staining foods and beverages such as coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco.
  • Rinse your mouth with water or brush soon after consuming staining substances.
  • Avoid biting into very hard foods like nuts, popcorn kernels, or sticky candies.
  • Wear a night guard if you grind or clench your teeth to prevent damage.
  • Use a mouthguard when playing contact sports to protect your veneers and natural teeth.
