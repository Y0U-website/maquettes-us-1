Dr. David You, DDS

Principal Dentist at Calimesa Dental

Dr. David You is a dedicated and compassionate dentist with extensive training and experience in providing comprehensive dental care. He lived much of his younger years in Canada, then came to his dream school, Loma Linda University , and graduated with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. You is committed to staying at the forefront of dental technology and practices through continuous education and advanced training.

At Calimesa Dental, Dr. You specializes in a wide range of services including cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, surgical procedures, and advanced restorative treatments including biomimetic dentistry. Dr. You is an active member of several prestigious dental associations, such as the American Dental Association (ADA) , the California Dental Association (CDA) , the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) , and the Tri-County Dental Society (TCDS) , reflecting his commitment to professional excellence.

Dr. You believes in a patient-centered approach, ensuring that each patient feels comfortable and informed throughout their dental journey. By combining state-of-the-art technology with a gentle and friendly manner, Dr. You and his team aim to make every visit a positive experience.
