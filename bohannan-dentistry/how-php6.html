Implants

If you have a missing tooth and are looking for options, we suggest asking us about dental implants. In many cases of missing teeth the best treatment option is a dental implant.

  • Preserve bone in the jaws that sustains youthful and healthy facial features
  • Completely replaces a missing tooth or several teeth
  • Preserve healthy teeth
  • Allow you to eat what you want
  • Improved quality of life
  • An alternative to dentures
  • Provide better retention for existing dentures

Crowns & Bridges

When teeth are fractured, severely discolored, or missing, the best option may be a crown or bridge. Crowns are used to restore individual teeth, and bridges are used to replace missing teeth if implants are not an option. We can also use crowns and bridges to straighten the appearance of teeth if orthodontic care is not chosen. Many times we will use crowns as the best cosmetic option for a patient, and the results are beautiful!

Root Canal Therapy

If you require root canal treatment, we will be able to meet your needs. Our office offers the latest diagnostic and treatment techniques when performing endodontics. Root canal therapy is often required for teeth with damaged nerves. Deep cavities, broken teeth, and large fillings are some of the common factors that lead to injured nerves. We understand the apprehension that some patients may have, so it is our goal to relax you as much as possible with our anesthesia techniques that may include nitrous or "laughing gas".

Partials & Dentures

When partials and dentures are required to replace teeth, we utilize the most realistic restorations available. It is our desire that you have great fitting restorations and are able to eat the foods you choose. Our office has decades of experience fabricating natural and good fitting dentures and partials.

TMJ

Many people suffer from joint dysfunction in their jaw that can often be treated with intervention therapy. Common symptoms include popping and clicking sounds on mouth opening as well as frequent jaw pain. Many people often experience headaches and muscle tension in their head and neck area that may be connected to joint problems. We will perform a complete head and neck evaluation and clinical exam so that we can offer you the best treatment.

Fresh Breath Treatment

If you often struggle with bad breath, we would like to help you solve this problem. Chronic halitosis is a serious strain on your social life and self esteem. We were one of the first offices in the Dallas Fort Worth community to establish an organized program and therapy specifically diagnosing and treating breath malodor. Bohannan Dentistry utilizes the latest technology that allows us to monitor the intensity of bad breath and guides us to develop a treatment plan to eliminate the cause.
