ABOUT THE AFGHAN DENTAL RELIEF PROJECT

The Afghan Dental Relief Project is a 501c3 non-profit NGO founded in 2003 by Dr. James Rolfe, a practicing dentist in Santa Barbara, California. It was established to provide, primarily free, dental services and all supplies that are needed to accomplish these efforts.

Funding is primarily through donations along with a portion of Dr. Rolfe s dental practice salary. The clinic is staffed by volunteers from all over the globe and within Afghanistan. Practitioners trained by ADRP also volunteer lots of their time.

THE ADRP BOARD OF DIRECTORS

The Afghanistan Dental Relief Project has a dedicated, all-volunteer board of directors from across the globe who donate their time and expertise in serving the project and help advance the dental infrastructure in Afghanistan. All our board members travel to Afghanistan regularly to help train, oversee, and to advance the mission of our project.

DR. MARTIN KIM

PERIODONTIST, BOARD MEMBER

Dr. Kim is a periodontist from Vancouver, BC, Canada. He’s an active member of the dental community and an expert in his field. He has also been our main negotiator with the Afghan government. Aside from his own private practice he is also an instructor in the field of periodontology. He has been a part of the Afghanistan Dental Relief Project since 2012.

Dr. Kim volunteers his time and travels yearly to the clinic in Kabul and works closely with the clinic staff to help advance phases of the project. DR. ARYA YOUSUFI

ADRP BOARD MEMBER

Dr. Arya Yousufi is an Afghan general dentist from Denmark. With experience treating both children and adults, she believes that prevention, education, and compassionate care are the foundation of lifelong oral health

Following her volunteer mission, Dr. Arya joined the Board of the Afghanistan Dental Relief Project and continues to support the organization through voluntary work. She has also organized an oral health prevention campaign, raising funds to provide toothbrushes and toothpaste for children and adults in Afghanistan, helping to promote better oral hygiene and prevent dental disease.
