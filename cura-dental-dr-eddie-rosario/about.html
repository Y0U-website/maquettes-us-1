0 Meet Dr. Eddie Rosario Dr. Rosario completed his undergraduate degree at Marist University in 2017 and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from New York University (NYU) College of Dentistry in Manhattan in 2021. After dental school, he completed a rigorous residency program at NewYork-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital, where he gained advanced clinical training in emergency dental care, root canal therapy, oral surgery, and orthodontics.

Dr. Rosario is a strong believer in lifelong learning and consistently pursues continuing education to stay current with the latest advancements in dentistry and patient care.

He grew up in Manhattan and has lived and worked in Northern New Jersey since completing his residency. He now lives in Jersey City, where he’s proud to serve the diverse communities of the New Jersey metropolitan area.

When he’s not at the office, Dr. Rosario enjoys hiking, exercising, reading, and spending time with his dog, a dachshund named Oscar.
