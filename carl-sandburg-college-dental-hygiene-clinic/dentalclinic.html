Dental hygiene clinic

Low-cost dental hygiene services available to the community

The dental hygiene clinic at Sandburg is located at 2400 Tom L. Wilson Blvd. in the Science & Technology Center. Follow the dental hygiene patient signs to park in either parking lot A or C , and enter through the Science & Technology Center by the roundabout.

The clinic offers low-cost dental hygiene services to our community during the spring semester, with no insurance necessary for patients.

Get your teeth cleaned for as little as $25, and take advantages of services including full-mouth series X-rays and panoramic X-rays for patients of all ages.

Services are provided by second-year students in Sandburg’s dental hygiene program who are supervised by experienced, licensed faculty members and dentists. Appointments last about 3.5 hours each, and it typically takes at least two appointments to complete a cleaning.

  • Mon. through Fri. 8-11:30 a.m. / 1-4:30 p.m.

Prices for the 2025-26 academic year

These are flat fees due at the time of appointment. Appointments last 3.5 hours each; multiple appointments may be necessary.

  • Adult cleaning: $25
  • Sandburg student with a current student ID: $15
  • Children’s cleanings (17 & under): $15
  • Seniors (60+): $10
  • Full-mouth series X-rays: $15
  • Panoramic X-ray: $15

Schedule an appointment today.

For more information or to schedule an appointment, call the clinic at 309.344.2595 / fax 309.324.7185 . For general inquiries, email dhg@sandburg.edu .

Please complete the medical history form , list your primary doctor under Primary Physician; if you don’t have one, list a local hospital near you. Don’t sign and date the form until the date of your appointment.
