How do I log in?

Your initial password is the last six digits of your Social Security number.

Students

Your username is your first initial, last name and last four digits of your student ID. For example, let’s say your name is James Doe, and your student ID is #7654321; your username would be: jdoe4321@student.sandburg.edu .

Employees

Your username is the first letter of your first name and your full last name. For example, if you’re James Doe, your username would be jdoe@sandburg.edu .
