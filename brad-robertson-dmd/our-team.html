0 Our Team Dr. Brad Robertson, DMD Dr. Robertson is a 2001 graduate of University of Louisville School of Dentistry and a 1997 graduate of Murray State University. He has practiced in Murray for the extent of his career. Dr. Brad and his wife, Catie, enjoy frequent travel and live music among several other hobbies. In his free time, Dr. Robertson enjoys golfing, boating, and is a co-host for the soul music show The Jive House on WKMS. He is also a long-standing board member for the Murray Art Guild.
