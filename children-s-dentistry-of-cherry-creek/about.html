Our Mission

Our goal at Children’s Dentistry of Cherry Creek is to be there for you and your child through every stage of their dental journey. We believe that both education and prevention are key to paving the way for a lifetime of great oral health. Helping your child keep their healthy smile for life is what we strive for. We aim to positively impact the lives of the children we treat, both immediately and in the long term, through promoting healthy habits.

Meet Our Pediatric Dentists

Dr. Lisa Carlson-Marks and Dr. Lacey Bowen Goergen both share an admirable passion for pediatric dentistry. Both doctors have years of additional and specialized training beyond dental school dedicated to pediatric care. You can trust in our doctors to care for your child’s dental needs through every stage of their young life. They are experts in healthy and happy teeth!

Lisa Carlson-Marks, DDS

Dr. Lisa was born and raised in Reno, Nevada. She earned her Bachelor of Science in Molecular and Cellular Biology from the University of Arizona. She completed her dental education at the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine in 1995, followed by a two-year general practice residency program at University Hospital. With over two decades of experience in pediatric dentistry starting with her residency at Children’s Hospital of Colorado, we are proud to have Dr. Lisa helping our families smile!

Lacey Bowen Goergen, DDS

Dr. Lacey, a Wisconsin native, earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2015. She is a passionate pediatric dentist who earned her Pediatric Dentistry Certificate from Children’s Hospital of Colorado, where she still maintains privileges. Her kindness and ingenuity were recognized in 2021 when she was featured in Incisal Edge Magazine’s 40 Under 40 list.
