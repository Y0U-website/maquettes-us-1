Orthodontics focuses on correcting misaligned teeth, bite issues, and spacing concerns to improve both oral function and smile appearance. At Alani Dental, we offer modern orthodontic solutions using Invisalign clear aligners to help patients achieve straighter smiles comfortably and discreetly.

Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, support better oral health, and can improve chewing, speech, and overall confidence. Invisalign treatment uses custom clear aligners designed to gradually move teeth into their ideal position without the appearance or discomfort commonly associated with traditional braces.
