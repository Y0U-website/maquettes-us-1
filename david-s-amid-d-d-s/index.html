WHAT SHOULD I EXPECT IF MY DENTIST AND I DECIDE DENTAL IMPLANTS ARE RIGHT FOR ME?

It is normal to experience some discomfort for a day or two following a dental implant procedure. Many people only need to take an over the counter pain medication such as Advil or Alieve. However if more inflammation and soreness occur it can be managed with a prescription of Vicodin, hydrocodone or codeine. You may be asked to eat only soft foods for a short time until the surgical site heals.
