Dr. Krupa Patel

Dr. Krupa Patel, DDS – Advanced Cosmetic & Implant Dentist

Dr. Krupa Patel brings nearly a decade of dental experience to Alpine Creek Dental. She is committed to creating a comfortable, welcoming environment where patients feel heard, informed, and confident in their treatment decisions. By taking the time to understand each person’s needs and goals, Dr. Patel delivers personalized care rooted in trust, clear communication, and compassion.

Educational & Professional Background

A Chicagoland native, Dr. Patel grew up in Joliet, Illinois, and attended Joliet Catholic Academy before pursuing her passion for dentistry. She completed her undergraduate studies in Biomedical Sciences and earned her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from Marquette University. Since graduating in 2017, Dr. Patel has provided comprehensive dental care to patients throughout the Chicagoland area.

She is excited to bring her experience and commitment to patient-focused care to the Elmhurst community as part of the Alpine Creek Dental team.

Continuing Education

Dr. Patel is deeply committed to lifelong learning and continuously expanding her clinical skill set to provide the highest standard of care. She regularly pursues continuing education opportunities to stay current with advancements in dentistry, with a particular interest in Invisalign® , restorative dental implants, and cosmetic dentistry .

Through ongoing coursework and her membership with Spear Education, Dr. Patel remains dedicated to refining her techniques and offering thoughtful, modern treatment options. She is certified in implants, crowns, bridges, Invisalign®, and more, and is an active member of both the Illinois State Dental Society and the Chicago Dental Society.

Treatment Philosophy

Dr. Patel’s treatment philosophy centers on personalized, ethical, and comprehensive care. She knows that every patient deserves individualized attention and clear communication, which is why she takes the time to listen, educate, and collaborate on treatment decisions. By combining clinical precision with a compassionate approach, Dr. Patel strives to create positive dental experiences that support long-term oral health and patient confidence.

Dentistry is more than a profession for Dr. Patel—it is a true passion. She empowers her patients through education, attention to detail, and meaningful, long-term relationships built on trust. The opportunity to support patients in their dental health and overall wellness journeys, combined with the ever-evolving nature of dentistry, is what inspires her most each day.

Community & Personal Background

Outside of the office, Dr. Patel enjoys spending quality time with her husband and their mini goldendoodle. Together, they call downtown Chicago home and love making the most of city life. Dr. Patel has a passion for traveling and exploring new cultures, whether that means discovering new destinations, trying local cuisine, or learning from different traditions. She also enjoys cooking and hosting time with family and friends.
