FAQs

How Often Should I Brush and Floss?

You should brush your teeth at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and floss once a day to remove plaque and food particles from between your teeth. Good daily habits, combined with regular dental visits, are essential for maintaining your mouth’s health and preventing cavities and gum disease.

What Can Be Prevented With General Dentistry?

General dentistry helps prevent many common dental issues, including tooth decay, gum disease, bad breath, and enamel wear. We also screen for more serious issues, such as oral cancer, which have better outcomes when caught early.

What if I Haven’t Seen a Dentist in a Long Time?

That’s okay! If you haven’t seen the dentist in a long time, the best time to get back on track is now. Our team will start with a gentle, comprehensive exam and discuss any concerns you may have. At our practice, you can always expect to receive expert dental care in a judgment-free environment.
