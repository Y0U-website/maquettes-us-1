Maintaining a healthy smile starts with proactive, preventive care. From routine cleanings and exams to cavity fillings and gum disease treatments , we help you avoid future issues before they start. Our goal is to keep your smile healthy and bright, year after year.

For those dealing with missing teeth, our dental implant services in Downtown Pittsburgh provide a permanent and natural-looking solution, including advanced full-arch restoration options such as All-on-4 dental implants. Restorative Dentistry

When dental issues arise, our restorative treatments provide effective, long-lasting solutions. We offer everything from dental crowns , bridges , inlays and onlays and implants to full-mouth reconstructions . FAQs About Atlas Dental Specialists In Downtown Pitt

Does your Downtown Pittsburgh office accept new patients?

Yes, we are always happy to welcome new patients to our Downtown Pittsburgh office. Switching to our practice is simple — just contact us and we will assist you in transferring your records and scheduling your first appointment. No referral is needed to see one of our prosthodontists.

What services are available at the Downtown Pittsburgh location?

Our Downtown Pittsburgh office provides a full range of dental and prosthodontic services, including dental implants, All-on-4 full-arch restoration, dentures, implant-supported dentures, porcelain veneers, CEREC same-day crowns, Invisalign, teeth whitening, dental bonding, smile makeovers, full-mouth reconstruction, gum disease treatment, tooth extractions, and routine cleanings and exams.

Do you offer same-day dental procedures at the Downtown location?

Yes. We use CEREC technology and in-office milling to complete crowns, veneers, and many other restorations the same day. You can come in with a damaged or missing tooth and leave with a finished, custom restoration in a single visit, without the need for a temporary crown or a return appointment.

Can you treat complex dental cases?

Absolutely. As prosthodontists, Drs. Nicholas and Mara Mangini have three additional years of specialty training beyond dental school focused specifically on restoring and replacing teeth. We handle complex cases including full-mouth reconstruction, All-on-4 implants, and multi-procedure smile makeovers entirely in-house without outside referrals.

What makes Atlas Dental Specialists different from other Downtown Pittsburgh dentists?

Atlas Dental Specialists is led by prosthodontists, a dental specialty that requires three additional years of advanced residency training beyond dental school. We handle virtually every procedure in-house, including implant placement, crown fabrication, and prosthetic design, using advanced technology like 3D imaging and CEREC CAD/CAM milling. Our family-owned practice has served Downtown Pittsburgh for over 35 years.
