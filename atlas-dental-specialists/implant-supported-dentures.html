What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures, also called overdentures, are removable prosthetic devices that snap onto dental implants rather than simply resting on your gums. This distinction matters far more than it might seem. Where traditional dentures rely on gum contact and adhesives for retention, implant-supported dentures attach to titanium posts that fuse with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation that won’t shift when you talk, laugh, or eat.

Typically, two to four implants per arch provide enough support, though the exact number depends on your bone structure and treatment goals. The implants integrate with the surrounding bone over time, providing stimulation that prevents the bone loss often seen in long-term conventional denture wearers. According to research published in the Journal of Clinical Medicine , implant overdentures have demonstrated a 96.6 % implant survival rate in patients with complex medical histories, showing how reliable this treatment is across a wide range of candidates.

The Implant-Supported Denture Process

Every treatment at Atlas Dental Specialists begins with a thorough evaluation, including 3D imaging to assess your bone structure and map out optimal implant placement. We take the time to understand your goals before developing a plan.

Implant Placement and Healing

During the surgical phase, we place two to four titanium implants per arch in strategic positions based on your jaw anatomy and prosthetic design. Many patients receive temporary dentures on the same day as surgery, so there’s no need to go without teeth during the healing period. Over the next three to six months, the implants undergo osseointegration, a process in which the surrounding bone fuses to the implant surface, creating a stable, lasting anchor.

Final Denture Fabrication

Once your implants have fully integrated, we take precise impressions to fabricate your custom overdenture. The denture is carefully designed to connect securely to the implants and fit comfortably in your mouth. When the final denture is complete, we attach it, verify fit and function, and walk you through how to remove, clean, and reattach it properly at home.

Caring for Your Implant-Supported Denture

Maintaining your overdenture is straightforward and helps keep the implants and denture in place longer. Remove the denture each night and clean it with a soft brush and denture cleanser. Avoid regular toothpaste, which can be too abrasive. Clean the attachment sites and surrounding gum tissue with a soft toothbrush, and always rinse the denture before reinserting.

The attachment components inside the denture gradually wear and typically need replacement every 12 to 18 months to maintain good retention. Your denture may need relining every three to five years as natural gum changes occur. With consistent care and regular checkups, the implants themselves can last for decades.

Are You a Candidate for Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures may be a strong fit if you currently wear traditional dentures and struggle with stability, are missing all teeth in one or both arches, or want improved function without committing to a fully fixed restoration. Patients who have had implant-supported dentures in the lower arch often return to add them to the upper arch as well after experiencing the stability they provide.

Good candidates have adequate bone density or are willing to undergo bone grafting for implants if needed. Reasonable overall health and a commitment to proper cleaning and maintenance are also important factors. During your consultation, we assess your bone structure and oral health thoroughly to determine whether implant-supported dentures are the right choice or whether another approach through our prosthodontic solutions would serve you better.
