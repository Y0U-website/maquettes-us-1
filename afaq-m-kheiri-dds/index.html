The practice

About Our Fishers Dentist Although our Fishers dental office has been around for over 30 years, Dr. Kheiri purchased it in 2017 when our previous doctor retired. Since then, he has continued our mission for quality dental care.

Most people know Dr. Kheiri for his soft-spoken, kind nature. He truly takes his time to listen to each patient and help them achieve their oral health goals. In addition to being a family dentist, Dr. Kheiri also has extensive training in areas of cosmetic dentistry, allowing us to provide comprehensive implant, aligner, and smile makeover treatments under one roof.

Our Fishers practice is located in an old farmhouse (many people refer to it as the “dental home”) but boasts some of the most cutting-edge dental technology available. For example, we’ve invested in state-of-the-art CAD/CAM equipment like CEREC to reduce treatment times and provide services like same-day dental crowns.

Some of Our Services CEREC One Day Crowns Why wait weeks for a crown when you can walk out with one the same day? Using our advanced CEREC technology, Dr. Kheiri designs, mills, and places your permanent crown in a single appointment — no temporaries, no second visits, no hassle. Just a beautifully restored tooth before you even leave our office.
