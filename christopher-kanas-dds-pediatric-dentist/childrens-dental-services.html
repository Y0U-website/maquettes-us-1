Composite Fillings

There may be a time when your child has a cavity. Sometimes tooth decay becomes severe enough that a filling is required to stop the problem from worsening.

For hundreds of years, fillings have always been the go-to for treating cavities. We’re able to use metal-free fillings, which preserve your child’s tooth structure and blend seamlessly with his or her smile.

  • Advantages Since our fillings are tooth colored, they will closely match with your child’s tooth color. Also, it requires less removal of natural tooth structure, saving the integrity of the tooth.
  • Disadvantages Metal-free fillings still require some removal of the natural tooth to stop the cavity from growing. Don’t worry! We’ll do everything in our power to ensure your child is comfortable throughout the entire treatment.

Sedation Dentistry

It doesn’t hurt our feelings – some people are fearful of the dentist. As a result, some children – and even adults – avoid getting the dental care they need unless it’s an absolute emergency. In turn, this can cause devastating effects on your child’s oral and overall health.

Sedation dentistry can ease dental anxiety by helping your child feel relaxed and taking his or her mind off the tasks at hand.

  • Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) at both iGrin locations
  • Disadvantages Nitrous oxide may not be suitable for children who have extreme dental fear. In this case, oral sedation or general anesthesia may be more effective. The effects of oral sedation are not immediate, unlike nitrous oxide and general anesthesia. Additionally, sedation dentistry is not a substitute for local anesthesia. Rest assured, we want your child to have the perfect kiddo experience. We will work with you to find a sedation method or treatment plan that works best for your child.

Space Maintainers

Life is unpredictable. There may be a case where a child loses a baby tooth early or extracted because of dental decay. It’s important to leave that gap open so the permanent tooth can erupt and come into place.

A space maintainer is an appliance that can help those permanent teeth come in properly. It can either be removable or cemented into the child’s mouth. We’ll gladly review with you and your child how to clean it properly to ensure optimal oral health.

  • Advantages Space maintainers allow permanent teeth to come into place. Without it, the child’s teeth can shift into the open space, disrupting the permanent tooth’s placement. This could lead to future orthodontic treatment.
  • Disadvantages The child may feel discomfort when the space maintainer is placed. It takes a few days for the child to get accustomed to wearing the appliance.

Baby Root Canal (Pulpotomy)

When a cavity is deep enough to infect the tooth nerve, a baby root canal (also known as a pulpotomy) is needed to save the tooth. Without this treatment, an extraction may be required.

A baby root canal maintains the integrity and health of a baby tooth, gum tissue, and bone. This process is necessary to sustain the health and development of adult teeth.

  • Advantages This treatment is the last resort to saving a baby tooth to ensure the proper growth of permanent teeth.
  • Disadvantages The child may have some soreness and discomfort after the treatment. However, it will subside and can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

Dental Crowns

If not caught early, a cavity could be devastating to a child’s tooth structure, causing severe decay. As a result, a filling won’t be enough to save the tooth.

Our dental crowns are capable of stopping tooth decay and restoring your child’s tooth back to full function and strength. We work closely with our dental lab to ensure the crown is a precise fit and blends with your child’s smile.

  • Advantages Crowns are an excellent solution to save a severely decayed tooth. It will restore the tooth back to its full function and strength. Plus, we can blend the crown with the smile, so hardly anyone will know you received treatment.
  • Disadvantages While crowns are strong and stable, it is still crucial to maintain excellent dental care. They will not protect your child’s smile from gum disease.

Extractions

There may be many reasons a child will need a tooth extraction. Some possibilities could include: the tooth is beyond damaged and decayed, the need to remove wisdom teeth, or need to make room for orthodontic treatment.

  • Advantages While pulling a tooth out may seem scary, it will benefit your child in the long run. We even have solutions you can pursue after the extraction – ask us the next time you visit us!
  • Disadvantages An extraction will remove a tooth from the mouth, leaving a gap. There are solutions we can pursue to help your child receive the best smile he or she will be proud of.
