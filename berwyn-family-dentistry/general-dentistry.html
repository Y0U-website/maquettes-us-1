Digital X-rays

We utilize digital X-rays to further evaluate your smile and make certain that you receive the appropriate dental care you need. Dental X-rays are used to diagnose diseases affecting the teeth and the bones since the inside of these structures is not seen when dentists look in your mouth. They provide important information to help plan the appropriate dental treatment. They may be used to identify: Number, size, and position of the teeth. The more precise the diagnostics, the better the treatment outcomes.

Emergency Check-Up

Accidents and emergencies happen and we are here to help you through them. If an emergency arises, please call our 24-hour emergency line for immediate assistance.

What qualifies as an emergency?

If your tooth is accidentally cracked, broken or knocked out, or if you experience sudden pain, swelling or bleeding. You shouldn’t wait to treat an emergency dental situation, as they can turn serious quickly.

Periodontal Disease Therapy

Gum disease, also called periodontal disease, is a degenerative condition caused by bacteria found in plaque. The gums become infected and inflamed and if left untreated can cause them to pull away from teeth and result in bone loss and tooth loss, as well as health conditions such as heart disease and strokes. Signs of gum disease are swelling, redness, tenderness or if the gums bleed easily. Other symptoms are loose teeth, painful chewing, persistent bad breath, and receding gums that make your teeth appear longer than normal.

Dr. B. will look for gum disease at every check-up. Depending on its severity, she may recommend scaling and root planing (deep cleaning) to address it. In more severe cases or for localized therapy, she may also recommend placing an antibiotic called Arestin into the infected pockets of the gums to heal infection.
