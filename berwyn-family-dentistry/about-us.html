Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where are you located? Berwyn Family Dentistry is located on the second floor, suite 201, within a freestanding building located at 1301 Lancaster Avenue in Berwyn, Pennsylvania. Just a few steps from the Daylesford Train station on the Paoli-Thorndale SEPTA Regional Rail line.
  • Do you provide 24/7 emergency care? Yes, we do provide emergency services for true dental emergencies. Patients may call our emergency line to have their emergency assessed. Emergency Line: (484) 302-8494
  • Do you improve smile through porcelain veneers? Yes, these custom-created very thin porcelain shells perfect the look of teeth by closing gaps, whitening, and improving irregularly shaped or misaligned teeth.
  • Do you provide digital smile makeovers? Yes, we can take images of your current smile and show you the possibilities for perfecting it.
  • Do you treat Children? Yes, we are a family practice. We treat children as young age 2.
  • Are there financing options? Yes, we offer Care Credit with interest-free financing, to make paying for your dental care convenient and affordable.
  • Is there an in-house payment plan? Yes, through the Berwyn Savings Plan, members who do not have dental insurance can pay a membership fee and access care and discounts on treatment provided.
  • Do you accept medicare or medicaid? No, not at this time.
  • How often should I get my teeth cleaned? We recommend cleanings every six months to best prevent oral health issues and save you money in the long run.
  • Do you offer pain-free dentistry? Dr. B. goes the extra mile in thoroughly numbing areas of the mouth she’s working on and providing nitrous oxide when necessary. She won’t begin work until she’s sure you’re comfortable. Patient anxiety is lessened in the calm and tranquil environment.
