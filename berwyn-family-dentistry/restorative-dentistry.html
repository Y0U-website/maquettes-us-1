Cavities & Fillings

At our practice we take the time to work with parents and children to make sure they are using the best brushing and flossing techniques. We recommend fluoride treatments as a preventative measure to help avoid cavities and will restore the teeth with fillings if decay is detected. This common procedure involves removing any damaged areas of your tooth and replacing them with a composite resin. At Berwyn Family Dentistry, we use advanced technology and high-quality materials to ensure that your fillings are sturdy and properly restore your tooth’s health and structure.

For those who are cavity-prone, Dr. B. strongly advises sealants and preventive resin restoration, where applying a thin layer of resin to the deep grooves on teeth to protect them from decay.

Crowns

To restore your teeth to their optimal functioning, a dental crown can replace a tooth that’s cracked, damaged, or decayed to the point that a filling will not provide a successful restoration. The crown is custom made to fit over the entire tooth, beginning at the gum line, in order to restore the tooth to its original shape and size. Other purposes for crowns include to anchor a dental bridge, protect a tooth following root canal treatment, to complete a dental implant, enhance the smile, improve a misshapen tooth or strengthen a tooth that’s fractured or weakened.
