Dr. Levitt has been following the progress of the Corona virus pandemic very closely and has been observing all the recommendations and closures being suggested or mandated.

Despite our best efforts to continue full operations, at this point in time, for the safety of our patients and staff it is mandated by the American Dental Association that dental offices cease providing non urgent services through May 15, 2020. (However, this may be subject to change, given the continuously evolving situation.)

We will be available for urgent treatment only, and we will postpone all routine treatment, including hygiene appointments for now. Our team will be working remotely to check phone messages and emails. If needed the doctor will be available for consultation via telephone, email and video.

We apologize for the inconvenience and look forward to seeing you all soon.
