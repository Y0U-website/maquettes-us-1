Brodie

A true Colorado native, Brodie grew up in Highlands Ranch and has always been drawn to the connection between science, wellness, and helping others. She knew early on that she wanted a career in healthcare, and dental hygiene became the perfect fit — combining her passion for patient care with her natural attention to detail. A top graduate of Concorde Career College, Brodie developed a strong appreciation for the relationship between oral health and overall systemic health, an approach that continues to shape the way she cares for patients today.

At Aurum, Brodie is passionate about creating a personalized, comfortable experience where patients feel genuinely cared for and confident in their smiles. Her warm personality, calming presence, and educational approach help patients feel at ease while empowering them to take an active role in their health. Outside the office, Brodie enjoys spending time outdoors in every Colorado season, walking her two Yorkies, and being with family and friends.
