What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants consist of artificial tooth roots, usually made from titanium, that integrate seamlessly with your jawbone. This integration forms a strong and lasting anchor that can support replacement teeth. Ranging from single implants to complete sets of permanent dentures, dental implants can stabilize various types of implant-supported restorations, ensuring both functionality and aesthetic appeal.

Our Implant-Supported Restorations

We currently offer single dental implants as well as implant dentures at Augusta Family Dental. Single dental implants offer numerous advantages depending on your specific circumstances. Not only can they avoid damaging surrounding teeth by only replacing the damaged tooth, but they can also be more visually appealing than a full bridge. Single dental implants also integrate well into the jawbone and can be easier to maintain.

Am I an Appropriate Candidate for Dental Implants?

Elements like healthy gums, overall health, and sufficient jawbone density are vital in evaluating your eligibility for dental implants. We thoroughly examine these elements to enhance the success of your implant treatment. For individuals with inadequate jawbone density, bone grafts or sinus lifts may be suggested to prepare for implant placement.

Extractions to Protect Your Oral Health

While it is essential to maintain your natural teeth, if extraction becomes necessary due to damage or decay, our experienced team provides extractions with your oral health as a priority. We will discuss replacement alternatives, frequently recommending dental implants for their capacity to mimic your natural tooth structure flawlessly.
