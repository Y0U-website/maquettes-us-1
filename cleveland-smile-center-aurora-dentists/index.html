Dr. Sam Glick founded Cleveland Smile Center in 2018. He comes from a long lineage of dentists, including his father, uncle, and grandfather. As CEO of Cleveland Smile Center, he manages four private practices around the suburbs of Cleveland, Ohio.

Dr. Glick has achieved advanced education in oral surgery, including dental implantology and total mouth reconstruction with Full Mouth All-On-4 Dental Implants . He also has a special interest in providing orthodontic treatment for both children and adults, helping them benefit from beautiful, straight smiles with both clear aligners and traditional straight wire orthodontics.
