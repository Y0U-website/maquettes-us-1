Meet Dr. Dominic Italia and the Broomfield Premier Dentistry Team

Your Dental Health, Personalized and Gentle

We believe that exceptional dental care begins with listening, understanding, and tailoring every visit to your individual needs. Whether you’re here for a routine exam, a cosmetic enhancement, or a complex restorative procedure, you’ll receive thoughtful care designed around you — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Dr. Dominic Italia, DDS Gentle Care You Can Trust

Dr. Italia is dedicated to delivering dental care that feels different — from his gentle technique during cleanings to the way he explains your treatment options clearly and thoroughly. His focus on understanding each patient helps even anxious adults and first-time visitors feel comfortable and informed throughout their visit.

Unlike many dental practices where dental hygienists or assistants perform most cleanings, Dr. Italia personally performs the majority of cleanings himself. This means you receive consistent, dentist-led care at every visit — a level of attention that builds trust and improves long-term outcomes.

Our Team Friendly, Skilled, and Ready to Help

Our team is committed to providing high-quality dental care with compassion and patience. We take the time to learn about your concerns, answer your questions, and make sure you feel confident about your treatment plan. Many of our patients appreciate the personalized communication and calm atmosphere we bring to every appointment.

We serve patients of all ages — from grandparents to little ones — and we speak both English and Spanish to better support our diverse community.

Patient-First Philosophy

Honesty, transparency, and respect guide everything we do. We provide only the treatments you truly need, explained in a way that makes sense so you can make informed decisions about your care.

Gentle Techniques + Personal Attention

We believe dental visits should feel calm, considerate, and respectful of your comfort. Many patients tell us they choose our practice because of Dr. Italia’s gentle hands and thorough approach.

Commitment to Continuous Improvement

Our team stays current with the latest techniques and dental technologies to enhance patient comfort and improve outcomes. Continuous education and modern equipment help us deliver safe, effective, and efficient care.

Our Community and Values

We are proud to serve families throughout Broomfield, Superior, Lafayette, Westminster, Arvada, and surrounding communities.

We strive to be more than a dental office — we want to be a trusted partner in your oral health journey. Whether it’s a child’s first visit, ongoing preventive care, or restorative treatment, our focus is on building long-lasting relationships and healthier smiles for life.
