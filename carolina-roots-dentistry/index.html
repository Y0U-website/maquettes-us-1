The practice

At Carolina Roots Dentistry, our top priority is helping you love your smile. Our team’s experience and gentle approach ensure your gums and teeth stay healthy.

Let’s face it—visiting the dentist isn’t everyone’s favorite thing. Dental anxiety is common, but we go out of our way to make your visit as pleasant and easy as possible.

Our mission is to provide excellent, evidence-based dental care while guiding you in making informed decisions about your oral health. We believe in serving others and enriching the lives of our patients.

We deeply value the trust our patients place in us, and we see it as an honor to care for our community. This responsibility is something we take seriously.

We work with many insurance providers to make your dental care affordable. If you’re unsure whether we participate with your insurance, give us a call! No insurance? Ask about our office membership savings plan.
