Dental Services

Our Services

Your smile is personal and one of the first things many people notice. A beautiful smile effortlessly expresses confidence, joy, and excitement. However, for many, the embarrassment of crowded, worn, chipped, or broken teeth keeps them from having the confidence to express themselves. Discover how Carolina Roots Dentistry can transform your smile with our comprehensive dental services. Our skilled team is dedicated to providing the smile you’ve always dreamed of.

Cosmetic Dental Services

Invisalign Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners address crowding and spacing issues by gently shifting teeth into their appropriate positions. Your treatment begins with a consultation and records appointment to gather information about your specific case. We then tailor a plan to move your teeth into their ideal aesthetic position. Clear trays are fabricated for daily wear until treatment completion, typically within 6 to 12 months.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening services remove stains accumulated over time from food and drinks like coffee and red wine. A single hour-and-a-half treatment lifts years of staining, resulting in a brighter smile. Our experienced dentists perform whitening procedures safely, ensuring maximum results without damage to surrounding soft tissue and minimizing sensitivity.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are thin layers of aesthetic porcelain bonded over existing teeth to improve their appearance. They are ideal for treating chipped, discolored, or misshapen teeth. Each veneer is custom-tailored to match the shape and color of your teeth. Veneers can enhance one tooth or transform your entire smile with minimal to no adjustment to your natural teeth.

Preventive Dental Services

Dental Cleanings

Routine cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, preventing cavities and gum disease. Our hygienists use advanced tools to thoroughly and gently clean your teeth, ensuring a healthier smile. Regular cleanings are essential for maintaining overall oral health and catching potential issues early, making this process comfortable and efficient.

Dental Exams

Comprehensive dental exams include a thorough inspection of your teeth, gums, and mouth. Our dentists check for signs of decay, gum disease, and other oral health issues. Regular exams help detect problems early, allowing for prompt and effective treatment to maintain your oral health.

X-Rays

Dental X-rays are crucial for diagnosing hidden issues like cavities, bone loss, and impacted teeth. Our state-of-the-art digital X-ray technology provides clear images with minimal radiation exposure. These images help us create precise, conservative, treatment plans tailored to your needs, ensuring the best possible care.

Restorative Dental Services

Fillings

Fillings repair cavities and restore the function and integrity of damaged teeth. Our dentists use high-quality materials that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. This procedure prevents further decay and preserves as much of the natural tooth structure as possible.

Crowns

Crowns cover and protect damaged or decayed teeth, restoring their shape, size, and function. Made from durable materials, crowns provide long-lasting results and blend with your natural teeth. This restorative option strengthens teeth and enhances their appearance.

Implants

Dental implants replace missing teeth with a durable and natural-looking solution that mimic your original tooth structure. The implant serves as a sturdy foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. This procedure restores your smile’s function and aesthetics while preventing bone loss.

Bridges

Bridges fill the gap created by one or more missing teeth. Anchored by crowns on adjacent teeth, bridges restore your smile’s function and appearance. This solution helps maintain facial structure and prevents remaining teeth from shifting out of position.

Dentures and Partials

Dentures and partials replace multiple missing teeth, improving your ability to eat and speak. Custom-fitted for comfort and natural appearance, these appliances enhance your smile and overall oral health. They provide a removable option for tooth replacement that suits your lifestyle.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy effectively treats infected or damaged tooth pulp, relieving pain and preventing further complications. This procedure helps remove the infected tissue, clean the root canals, and fill them to restore the tooth’s function. This treatment saves your natural tooth and eliminates the need for extraction.
