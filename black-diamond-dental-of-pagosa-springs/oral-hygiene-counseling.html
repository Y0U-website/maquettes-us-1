Oral Hygiene Counseling

What you do at home between dental visits has a major impact on the long-term health of your teeth and gums. At Black Diamond Dental, oral hygiene counseling is a natural part of every appointment. Dr. Justin Nylund and our hygiene team take the time to give each patient personalized, practical guidance based on their specific oral health needs.

Whether you are struggling with gum sensitivity, unsure about the right products to use, or want to help your children develop good habits early, we are here to help. Our goal is to give you the knowledge and tools to maintain a healthy smile every day not just on the days you visit us.

Personalized Guidance Includes

Brushing Technique Proper timing, pressure, and motion to clean effectively without damaging enamel or gum tissue.

Flossing and Interdental Cleaning How to floss correctly and the best alternatives for patients who find traditional flossing difficult.

Product Recommendations Guidance on toothbrushes, toothpaste, mouthwash, and other tools best suited to your specific needs.

Diet and Its Impact on Teeth How the foods and beverages you consume affect your enamel, gum health, and cavity risk.

Children’s Oral Health Age-appropriate guidance to help parents establish healthy habits from the very beginning.
