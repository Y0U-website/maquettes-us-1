Mobile Health Unit (MHU)

Henry J. Austin Health Center (HJAHC) brings healthcare directly to the community through our Mobile Health Unit (MHU). The MHU provides essential services such as primary care, behavioral health, dental screenings, COVID-19 vaccinations, preventive screenings, and health insurance enrollment support.

In addition to regularly scheduled community stops, the MHU also participates in school-based outreach and local events throughout the Trenton area, furthering our mission to deliver equitable, accessible care.

Please complete the Event Request Form below and a member of our team will follow up. While we do our best to accommodate all requests, participation is based on scheduling availability and alignment with our mission.

Please submit requests at least 30 days in advance to allow enough time for planning and coordination.

All MHU event requests require at least 45 days’ notice.
