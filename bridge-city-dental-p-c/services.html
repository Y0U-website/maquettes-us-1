Root Canals

When a tooth becomes severely decayed or the pulp inside becomes infected, a root canal can often save it from extraction. During this procedure, we carefully remove the damaged or infected pulp, clean and reshape the inner canal, and then fill and seal it with a durable material to restore strength and function. This treatment allows you to keep your natural tooth while eliminating pain and preventing further damage—helping you maintain a healthy, confident smile.

Dental Implants

If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants offer a strong, natural-looking, and long-lasting solution. At Bridge City Dental, we use implants to restore both the appearance and function of your smile.

A dental implant consists of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a sturdy foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture. This treatment not only replaces missing teeth but also helps preserve bone structure and maintain facial symmetry.

Our team will work closely with you to create a personalized treatment plan, ensuring a comfortable experience and results that look and feel just like your natural teeth.

Clear Aligners

Straighten your smile discreetly and comfortably with clear aligners from Bridge City Dental. Designed as a modern alternative to traditional braces, clear aligners use a series of custom-made, removable trays to gradually move your teeth into proper alignment.

Our aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to maintain your natural appearance while improving your smile. Because they’re removable, you can continue to eat your favorite foods and brush and floss with ease.

Whether you’re looking to correct minor crowding or achieve a more balanced smile, our team will develop a personalized aligner plan to help you reach your goals—comfortably, confidently, and conveniently.
