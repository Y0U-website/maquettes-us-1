The Importance of General Dentistry

General dentistry is important for maintaining a healthy, natural smile. Several studies suggest that oral health is directly related to overall systemic health as well. Infections in your mouth, including cavities and decay, have been linked to medical issues such as heart disease, diabetes, and systemic infections. Making sure you visit our office twice a year for examinations allows us to detect these issues before they become a larger problem. Furthermore, only a professional cleaning from our general dentists can ensure your teeth are as healthy, white, and beautiful as possible.
