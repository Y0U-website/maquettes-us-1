Benefits to the Patient

Complimentary initial consultation and panoramic x-ray.

Quoted fees are inclusive of entire treatment including retainers. There are no hidden costs.

No additional fees are charged if treatment takes longer than expected. Cases are finished as efficiently as possible but quality will never be sacrificed.

Retainers will be replaced at no charge for normal wear and tear.

In House lab promotes better fitting appliances which usually results in less emergencies and more comfort.

Younger patients may be monitored for extended periods until we reach the Best Time to treat. There are never any charges until orthodontics begins.

Many general dentists have family members treated at our office which shows great confidence in our care.

Doctor and staff pride themselves on scheduling properly and staying on time. (minimal wait time)

No additional charge for ceramic (clear) braces.

We accept most insurances and will accept assignment (we will fill out forms and do the work for you).

Multiple payment options creating great flexibility to fit the budget of almost any family.

Dr.Arnold and staff are always available. Emergencies are handled in a timely manner.

Very experienced and well trained staff. Most staff members have been with the practice for over 10 years.

We always stay abreast of the latest and greatest in the orthodontic world through continuing education.
