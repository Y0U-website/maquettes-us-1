The practice

Dr. Charmaine Barizo is originally from Southern California, growing up in the suburbs of Los Angeles. She graduated from the University of California, Los Angeles with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at Loma Linda University. She and her husband, Brandon, moved to Seattle in 2001 and keep busy with their children, Layni and Logan.

Dr. Barizo continues to advance in her training by attending Continuing Education courses and participating in both the Seattle Study Club and the Jet City Study Club. She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Washington State Dental Association, and the Snohomish County Dental Society.
