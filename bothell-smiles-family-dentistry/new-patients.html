What to Expect

For all our new patients, we start with a comprehensive exam with x-rays to determine your dental needs. We will work with you to develop a treatment plan that is just right for you. We aim to provide comprehensive treatment to help you obtain oral health and achieve the smile you’ve always dreamed of.

To help make your appointment flow more smoothly, we ask that you fill out the New Patient Forms prior to your appointment, or please arrive about 15 minutes before your appointment to allow for time to fill out the paperwork in-office. You will receive appointment reminders leading up to your appointment, starting with 1 week email reminder and subsequent text message reminders.

Insurance Plans Accepted (PPO or Preferred Provider Organization)

We accept most PPO dental plans. Please contact us to make sure we are in-network with you prior to your appointment.
