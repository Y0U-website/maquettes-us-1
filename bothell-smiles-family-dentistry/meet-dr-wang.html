Christine Huang, DMD, Family Dentist

ABOUT DR. HUANG

Dr. Christine Huang is dedicated to providing compassionate, comprehensive dental care with a gentle touch. She believes in empowering patients by helping them understand their oral health and make confident, informed decisions about their treatment.

Dr. Huang earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Pittsburgh and completed a General Practice Residency at Allegheny General Hospital in Pittsburgh, PA. Originally from Taipei, Taiwan, she is fluent in Mandarin and values connecting with patients from all walks of life.

Her passion for dentistry comes from its unique blend of science and human connection. The most rewarding parts of the profession include restoring smiles, relieving pain, and building trust with her patients. To stay at the forefront of care, she continually advances her skills through continuing education and dental conferences.

Now proud to call the Pacific Northwest home, Dr. Huang enjoys the region’s endless outdoor adventures, diverse cuisines, and vibrant seasons. Outside the office, you’ll often find her hiking, skiing, or relaxing with a matcha latte or bubble tea. She also loves spending time with her fiancé, family, and friends.
