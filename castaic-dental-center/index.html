Dentistry is my passion! Since my teenage years, I’ve been drawn to the idea of becoming a dentist , inspired by my family’s medical background. I attended Carmel Covenant School in Chandigarh, India, from elementary to high school, receiving a solid education.

After graduating, I pursued my dental studies at Himachal Dental College in India, where I gained valuable knowledge and skills. Following that, I worked as a general dentist in the private sector for a year, dedicating my spare time to volunteering in rural areas across India.

In 2001, I married my husband, Amit, and moved to the US. Thereafter, I furthered my dental education at UCLA, passing the California Dental Board Exam in 2004 to become a licensed dentist in the US. I now regularly return to India, offering dental care to underserved schools and villages.

Happily married to Amit, who works in finance, I’m also a proud mother of beautiful twin daughters, Mahek and Mannat. Outside of dentistry , I cherish family time, enjoying hobbies like cooking, traveling, listening to podcasts, and simply being together.
