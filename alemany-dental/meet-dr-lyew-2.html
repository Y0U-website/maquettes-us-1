DR. CHAD LYEW’S STORY

Dr. Lyew, was born in San Jose, California. He is a graduate of the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry. Dr. Lyew, has advanced training in Invisalign (Level 2 Certification), Zoom! whitening, cosmetic dentistry, and implant dentistry. In addition to practicing dentistry, Dr. Lyew is a member of the California Dental Association, American Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, San Francisco Dental Society, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and Merced Extension Triangle Neighborhood Association.

In addition to providing excellent cosmetic dentistry at Alemany Dental, Dr. Lyew volunteers his time to give back to the community. Such as offering free dental exams at San Francisco elementary schools to help parents become more aware of their children’s dental needs. Dr. Lyew, also makes presentations on oral health and dental education, to groups like the retirement homes of San Francisco. Furthermore, Dr. Lyew, is an active member of Dental Lifeline Network: Donated Dental Services, providing services to the elderly, disabled and those in need of dental care.
