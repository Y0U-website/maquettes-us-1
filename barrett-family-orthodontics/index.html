The practice

We can create a beautiful smile; but orthodontics is about way more than just straight teeth. An aligned smile can help restore your bite for optimal health and function. When you choose orthodontic treatment, you’re investing in a lifetime of benefits.

A board certified orthodontist, Jeremy Barrett specializes in treatment for both children and adults. After graduating with distinction from the University of Nevada, Barrett then obtained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at UCLA. Upon completion of this program, he was accepted to the UCLA orthodontics residency, where he received three years of specialized training in the field of orthodontics.

Barrett understands that each patient is unique, and tailors every treatment plan according to the needs of the individual. Taking health, function, and aesthetics into consideration, his comprehensive approach helps patients achieve and maintain beautiful, healthy smiles for years to come.
