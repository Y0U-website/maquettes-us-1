Dr. Tsai is also a certified dental technician (CDT) in ceramics and implants in the US, and a member of the American College of Prosthodontists and Academy of Osseointegration.

A prosthodontist is a dentist who has completed dental school plus three additional years of advanced training and education in an ADA-accredited prosthodontic graduate program. They specialize in treating and handling dental and facial problems that restore missing teeth and jaw structures.

A prosthodontist is highly trained in cosmetics, dental implants , crowns, bridges, dentures , temporomandibular disorders (TMJ/TMD), and more.
