Full-Service Oral Surgery Provider Serving Patients Across Williamsville, Buffalo, East Amherst, NY & Beyond

Amherst Oral Surgery is a full-service oral surgery provider specializing in tooth extractions in Buffalo , Tonawanda , Amherst , Lancaster , Orchard Park , Clarence , Cheektowaga , Niagara Falls , East Amherst & Williamsville , NY. We are proud to offer the highest quality oral surgery procedures for our patients, and we work hard to make sure your visit will be as brief as possible.

Our patients are treated on an outpatient basis and are able to recover from even the strongest anesthetics rapidly so they can go home quickly after surgery. We treat impacted teeth, gum grafts, infections, and more. For additional information on our outpatient oral surgery services, please contact our office today to schedule an appointment!
