Impacted Teeth Removal for Patients in Buffalo, Williamsville, Cheektowaga, Tonawanda, NY & Beyond

Impacted teeth are often characterized as teeth that do not break through the gum. In most cases, impacted teeth need to be removed sooner rather than later to avoid unpleasant symptoms that could come about. The cause of impacted teeth is typically a lack of space or the tooth being at the incorrect angle to push through the gum line.

Symptoms of Impacted Teeth

Some symptoms of impacted teeth include red or swollen gums, bleeding gums, bad breath, jaw pain, and swelling around the jaw. These symptoms could be a result of your impacted tooth trying to come in and instead hitting other teeth or they could be a sign of infection. Either way, your impacted teeth or tooth would need to be removed to stop the symptoms.
