Services

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dental services aim to protect and maintain oral health by preventing dental problems before they arise.

These services include regular cleanings, exams, fluoride treatments, sealants, and patient education on proper oral hygiene practices.

Preventative care helps reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues, supporting a healthy, long-lasting smile.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry not only repairs teeth but also helps restore confidence by bringing back a natural, functional smile. By addressing issues like decay, cracks, or tooth loss, restorative treatments play a key role in maintaining overall oral health and preventing further complications.

Treatments include fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants, which help restore chewing ability, which will prevent further dental issues.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry enhances the appearance of a person’s smile, focusing on improving the color, shape, alignment, and overall aesthetics of teeth.

Common procedures include teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and reshaping. These treatments boost confidence by creating a more polished, attractive smile while sometimes providing additional functional benefits.

Periodontal Dentistry

Periodontal dentistry focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and other conditions affecting the supporting structures of the teeth, such as the gums, bone, and connective tissues.

It aims to maintain healthy gums, prevent tooth loss, and improve overall oral health through procedures like deep cleanings, scaling, root planing, and, in advanced cases, surgery.
