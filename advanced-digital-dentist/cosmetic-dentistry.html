Introduction to Cosmetic Dentistry

Confidence with your smile can boost your self-esteem. Often this means considering dental care options to maintain a healthy mouth and some that are dedicated to improving the appearance of your teeth.

Options to consider include teeth whitening, dental crowns, veneers, implants or a complete smile makeover. We are glad to have this discussion with you and explore options. Treatment may include a simple procedure completed in one visit or complex procedures completed over multiple visits where we work closely with dental specialists.

The goal of cosmetic dentistry is to provide a smile that you have always wanted! Changes may include the color, position, shape, size, and alignment of your teeth. Replacing missing teeth can improve your smile and jaw stability. Your smile can become brighter, healthier and benefit from our choice of today’s cosmetically pleasing dental materials.

Popular Cosmetic Treatments

Teeth whitening is a popular cosmetic dentistry procedure that can remove pigments trapped within the enamel of your teeth, not just the surface stain. This procedure may significantly brighten your smile, but results may vary for individuals.

Dental veneers are thin coatings of bonding or porcelain placed over the front of your teeth to improve dental conditions such as chipped teeth, discolored teeth or minor tooth misalignment.

Our in-office CAD-CAM technology allows scanning of your tooth instead of a messy impression and no need for a temporary crown or return visit. The crown is designed and completed right here in one visit. Our patients are pleased and have benefited from this technology for over 20 years!

Missing teeth can be evaluated for an implant restoration. This requires multiple visits. The implant replaces the missing root and is restored with precision fit components resulting in a natural looking implant abutment and crown restoration.

Broken teeth that have damaged nerves or infections can be evaluated for root canal therapy. This allows the tooth root to be retained after the infection is treated and canal system is sealed. Once the root canal is completed, the next step is to restore the tooth. Placement of a post and crown restoration can restore the tooth to a natural appearance.

Orthodontics to Straighten Teeth - Braces/Aligners

The benefits of straight, well aligned teeth improve your smile, benefit your jaw relationship and improve your ability to maintain your oral health. Braces and aligners are used to change the position and relationship of teeth. Hardly visible clear aligners make cleaning your teeth easy while in treatment. This is a popular adult treatment choice. Traditional braces may be required for more complex tooth movement treatment. Orthodontic specialists can evaluate and propose a treatment plan that will work best for your smile.

Factors to consider when selecting treatment choices:

Complexity: Complex treatment choices will require more time, commitment and expense. Dental specialists may be recommended for evaluation or to complete treatment steps.

Materials: Material used in the procedures impact treatment time, longevity and cost. Alternate treatment options, costs and benefits will be discussed before deciding on a treatment plan.

Your dental health: Your overall dental health and medical history should be considered when making treatment choices. Treatment of urgent needs and an overall evaluation to address underlying dental issues are necessary steps in achieving a healthy, lifetime smile!
