We will listen to your needs and will educate you on today’s dental health care to design treatment options that fit your lifestyle. We believe in the need to develop a partnership to achieve your healthy smile. Your patient experience will benefit from our 30+ years of genuine care and concern. Our goal is to ensure every appointment is comfortable, relieves any discomfort and gives you the smile you’ve always wanted!

Lizabeth Sloane, D.M.D. Dr. Sloane is a graduate of The Henry M. Goldman Boston University School of Graduate Dentistry. Prior, she completed her Bachelor of Science degree at Boston College in Mathematics and Biology. She is a member of the American Dental Association and New Jersey Dental Association. She is a past president of her local component, The Central Dental Society. She works with her patients to make a plan for a healthy mouth with a focus on prevention and comfort while achieving a lifetime of smiles.
