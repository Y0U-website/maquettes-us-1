Preventative Care at Advanced Digital Dentist

Introduction to Preventative Dental Services

Preventative dental services focus on maintaining oral health. Preventative care starts with a schedule of regular dental recall visits. Getting to know you and following changes in your oral health is a great diagnostic tool. Detecting dental issues early usually involves less treatment with less time and expense. Optimizing your oral health is our mission. This is why regular dental checkups are a central part of preventative dentistry.

Preventative dentistry is crucial for all ages. Establishing good oral hygiene habits and a consistent recall schedule from a young age can benefit your lifetime oral health and well-being. Advances in technology offer increased comfort and efficiency for your dental care in the office as well as with products for home use. Guidance in your dental preventative care journey starts with an appointment with Dr. Sloane.

Most dental insurance plans offer coverage for preventative dental services with minimal to zero out of pocket costs. Now is the time to keep your mouth healthy. Use your benefits for early detection to reduce dental costs and spend less time in the dental chair.

Diagnostic Services

Diagnostic dental services accompany initial and recall preventative visits. Digital x-rays are taken in the lowest possible setting to evaluate hard tooth structure and bone health. These areas are not visible during a visual exam. Hidden areas of decay between teeth, infections, impacted teeth, and changes or loss of bone can be detected with use of diagnostic equipment. Our digital x-ray machines are registered and evaluated by physicists as required by the state of NJ.

Dental Prophylaxis Services

Professionally cleaning your teeth or providing dental prophylaxis, removes buildup on your teeth. Recommendations concerning the effectiveness of your daily care routine and the health of your teeth and oral tissue are being clinically evaluated with this preventative service. We need to learn about your overall health and medications.

Preventative Services for a Healthy Mouth

Other preventative services can address your specific area of concern. A tooth with weak enamel may benefit from fluoride treatment or dental sealants with the goal to prevent decay. Excessive tooth wear may indicate grinding of your teeth. Further evaluation and a night guard might need to be considered to prevent additional excess wear to your teeth. Maintaining the health of your gum and bone is the foundation for keeping your teeth. Determining if you need additional periodontal care or screenings with a specialist are part of planning your best preventative dental program.

At Advanced Digital Dentist Dental, we believe that patient education is an important part of maintaining good oral health. We are in partnership with you. We provide our patients with the information they need to make informed decisions about their dental care.
