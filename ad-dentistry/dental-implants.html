Implant Dentistry

Once the implant post is placed, a healing period is necessary. During this time, the jawbone naturally fuses with the biocompatible titanium post in a process known as osseointegration. This integration is crucial as it ensures the stability and durability of the implant.

After the healing phase, Dr. Donald and Dr. Rattner will attach the dental restoration to the implant post. The type of restoration can vary based on your specific needs and the number of teeth being replaced. It might be a single dental crown for one missing tooth, a dental bridge for several missing teeth or a denture for multiple missing teeth. Each restoration is custom crafted to match the appearance and function of your natural teeth, ensuring a comfortable and aesthetically pleasing result.

Replacing missing teeth with dental implants offers numerous benefits. Not only do they restore the appearance of your smile, but they also improve oral health and functionality. Dental implants help maintain the structure of your jawbone, preventing bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth. Additionally, they do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, preserving the health of your remaining natural teeth.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective solution in the long run. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are essential to maintain the health and longevity of your implants.

FAQ

What is a dental implant?

+ A dental implant is a type of prosthetic tooth. It consists of two parts: a post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone to act as a replacement for the root of a missing tooth, and a restoration (such as a crown, bridge, or denture) which caps the post to restore the visible portion of your tooth and give you back your smile. Dental implants create a stable and natural-looking replacement that mimics the function and appearance of your natural teeth.

What are the benefits of implant dentistry?

+ Implant dentistry offers numerous benefits, including improved oral health, enhanced appearance, and restored functionality. Dental implants help maintain jawbone structure, preventing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth. They provide a permanent and stable solution that looks and feels like natural teeth. Additionally, implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, preserving the health of your remaining teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

+ With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Good oral hygiene practices, such as regular brushing and flossing combined with routine dental checkups, are essential for ensuring the longevity of your implants. While implants last a lifetime, the restoration atop the implant post may occasionally need to be replaced due to wear over time.

Who is a candidate for implant dentistry?

+ Most individuals who are in good overall health and have sufficient jawbone density can be candidates for dental implants. It is important to have healthy gums and be free of conditions that could impair healing. During a consultation, our dentists will evaluate your specific circumstances, including your medical history and oral health, to determine if dental implants are the right choice for you.
