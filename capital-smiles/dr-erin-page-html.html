How Do I Get Emergency Dental Care?

If you ever find yourself in urgent need of dental care, call our practice right away. We are often able to welcome emergency patients on a same-day basis, and we can usually provide practical guidance over the phone to help you manage the situation in the meantime.

If your emergency is after-hours, you might be tempted to visit the local ER. However, with only a handful of exceptions, hospital ER personnel do not have the necessary equipment or training to handle dental emergencies.

How Do I Find the Best Place to Get Dental Work Done?

The “best” place for dental work depends on your personal preferences and needs, so it is important to think about what you want in a dental practice. For example, are you interested in a specific treatment? Are you looking for someone who will help you maximize your dental insurance? Do you have some dental anxiety and want to be sedated during procedures? All such factors — and more — can play a role in whether a particular practice is right for you.

Often, a practice’s website or their online reviews can help you get a fair idea of whether you want to schedule an appointment with them.

How Much Does a Dental Checkup & Cleaning Cost in Schenectady?

The average cost of a dental checkup and cleaning throughout the U.S. is $200 - $350. Of course, that number can vary based on several factors, such as the cost of living in a particular area, the experience and reputation of a dental practice, and more.

If you have insurance, your plan is likely to cover 100 % of the cost of preventive care, so you may end up paying nothing out of pocket.

How Many Dentists Are in Schenectady?

Estimates vary as to how many dentists are in the Schenectady area. One source estimates there are more than 300, while another says there are around 100. As a general rule of thumb across the United States, there is around 1 dentist for every 2,000 people.
