Dentures and partials, often referred to as removables, are prosthetic devices used to replace missing teeth. A full denture replaces all the teeth in either the upper or lower arch or jaw, while a partial denture replaces only the teeth that are missing and attaches to the remaining natural teeth.

For patients needing immediate results, temporary dentures can be made to ensure you don’t go without teeth during the healing process. Additionally, dentures and partials can be supported and better secured with implants and attachments for improved comfort and function.
