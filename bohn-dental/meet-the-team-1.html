Meet the Doctors

Keegan Bohn DDS

Moving back to Grand Rapids allowed Dr. Keegan to spend more time with his family and explore the Great Lakes. In his spare time, he enjoys golfing, going to the beach and spending time with his wife Abby, son Cal, daughter Penelope, and dog MJ. Dr. Keegan looks forward to meeting you.

Alexus Little DDS Dr. Little is from Cadillac, Michigan and received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2025. She served as philanthropy chair of her dental fraternal organization, Delta Sigma Delta’s Alpha Chapter, and was a member of the Victors for Veterans project, where she provided free dental treatment for uninsured veterans. Dr. Little is currently a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and Delta Sigma Delta’s Detroit Chapter.

In her free time, Dr. Little loves staying active—whether that’s strength training, walking, or playing soccer, golf, and pickle ball. She enjoys spending quality time with friends and family, watching sports, listening to music, and staying passionate about health and wellness. Dr. Little is thrilled to meet you!
