Dr. Tranchau Hoang, better known as Dr. Tran, is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist with a rich background and a passion for her field. Moving to the United States from Vietnam at seventeen, Dr. Tran’s journey is one of remarkable determination and success, illustrating her unwavering commitment to her goals.

A Summa Cum Laude graduate from Rutgers University, Dr. Tran earned her Bachelor of Science in Genetics, showcasing her academic prowess early on. Her journey continued at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, where she was awarded a prestigious Dean Scholarship, reflecting her leadership, academic achievements, and dedication to community service.
