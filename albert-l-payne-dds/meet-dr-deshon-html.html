Meet Dr. Nicole M. DeShon

Nicole M. DeShon, DMD

Dr. Nicole DeShon grew up in Gastonia, and attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, where she completed a Bachelor of Science in Biology in 2007. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Pittsburgh’s School of Dental Medicine in 2011 . In dental school, she was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the prestigious national dental honor society. She earned her Master of Science in Orthodontics and a Certificate in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics from West Virginia University School of Dentistry in Morgantown in 2014.
