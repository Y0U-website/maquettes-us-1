About Our Pediatric Dentist in…

We built Building Smiles to be the kind of place parents feel relieved to bring their kids — and kids feel genuinely excited to visit. From the waiting room to the treatment chair, every element of the experience was chosen to reduce anxiety, increase comfort, and deliver the kind of care that only comes from combining the right technology with the right team.

Kids&#x27; play area

The waiting room at Building Smiles isn&#x27;t just a row of chairs — it&#x27;s a space designed to get kids excited before they even reach the chair. Our dedicated play area helps little ones burn off nerves and arrive at their appointment feeling at ease, which makes everything that comes next go a whole lot smoother.

Overhead TVs and in-room entertainment

Every treatment room is equipped with a TV overhead so patients have something to focus on other than what&#x27;s happening in their mouth. We also offer headphones and in-room gaming options — because sometimes the best way to get through a dental appointment is to be genuinely distracted from it.

Pain-free anesthesia with the SOAN device

Intraoral scanner

Gone are the days of messy impression trays. We use a digital intraoral scanner to capture precise images of your child&#x27;s teeth and mouth — making it faster, more comfortable, and more accurate to create dental appliances like space maintainers, crowns, and mouthguards. Kids almost always think it&#x27;s pretty cool.

Sedation dentistry

For children who need a little extra support — whether due to dental anxiety, special needs, or the nature of the treatment — we offer the full spectrum of sedation in-office: laughing gas (nitrous oxide), oral conscious sedation, and general anesthesia. Having all three options available means no child has to go without the care they need simply because a single approach doesn&#x27;t work for them.

Digital, paperless intake

New patient forms are sent digitally before your appointment so you can complete everything from home, on your schedule — no clipboards, no rushing through paperwork at the front desk. It&#x27;s one less thing to stress about on the day of the visit.
