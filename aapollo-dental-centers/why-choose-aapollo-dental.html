About Aapollo Dental

Why You Should Choose Aapollo Dental

At Aapollo Dental, we believe in providing exceptional dental care with respect for your teeth, time, comfort, and budget. Learn what makes us the trusted choice for emergency and family dentistry in Atlanta.

Respect For Your Teeth

We believe that you should keep your own healthy teeth for your entire lifetime. Prevention is always more comfortable and affordable than treatment. That’s why we offer a comprehensive program of preventive dental care tailored to you, using the most advanced and long-lasting materials when treatment is necessary.

Respect For Your Time

We value your time and always listen to your concerns. During your initial free visit, we take time to learn your needs and preferences. We also make every effort to see you on time, because nobody likes waiting at the dentist.

Respect For Your Comfort

Dental anxiety is real, and your comfort is our top priority. We provide a relaxing environment with options such as personal headphones and DVD players in treatment rooms, so you can feel at ease throughout your visit.

Respect For Your Budget

We believe cost should never be a barrier to dental health. We offer free consultations and examinations, provide upfront pricing, and help you explore flexible payment options. Affordable emergency dentistry is always available at Aapollo.

Additional Benefits of Choosing Aapollo Dental

Weekend Availability

We understand that dental emergencies don’t wait for weekdays. That’s why we’re open on Saturdays, Sundays, and weekends to provide care when you need it most.

Walk-in Appointments

No appointment necessary for urgent dental care. We accommodate walk-ins daily to ensure you get the care you need when you need it.

Free Consultations

We offer free initial consultations so you can make informed decisions about your dental care without any financial pressure.

Emergency Services

When dental emergencies strike, we’re here to help. Our team is trained to handle urgent dental situations with compassion and expertise.
