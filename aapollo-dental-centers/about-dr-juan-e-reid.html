About Dr. Juan E. Reid

Dr. Juan E. Reid, DDS

Dr. Reid graduated from Creighton University Dental School in Omaha, Nebraska, and his multicultural life experiences—from flying as a commercial pilot during dental school to studying with Australian Aborigines—enrich his patient care philosophy. At Aapollo Dental, Dr. Reid leads a dedicated team focused on delivering exceptional dentistry you’ll want to share with others. Here, there is ’Always An Open Door’.
