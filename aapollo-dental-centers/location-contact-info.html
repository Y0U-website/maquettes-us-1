How to Get Here

From Downtown Atlanta

Take I-85 South to Cleveland Avenue exit. Turn right on Cleveland Avenue, then left on Metropolitan Parkway. We’re on the right, beside Kroger.

From Hartsfield-Jackson Airport

Take I-85 North to Cleveland Avenue exit. Turn left on Cleveland Avenue, then left on Metropolitan Parkway. We’re on the right, beside Kroger.
