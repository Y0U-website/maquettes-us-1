How Our Insurance Process Works

1. Verification

We verify your insurance coverage before your appointment to ensure you understand your benefits and any out-of-pocket costs.

2. Pre-Authorization

For major procedures, we handle all pre-authorization paperwork with your insurance company to avoid surprises.

3. Direct Billing

We bill your insurance company directly, so you only pay your portion at the time of service.

4. Flexible Options

If you don’t have insurance or need help with costs, we offer flexible payment plans and discount options.
