Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child see a dentist for the first time?

It’s recommended that children have their first dental visit by age 1 or when their first tooth erupts. Early visits are essential for monitoring dental development and establishing a dental home.

How often should my child visit the dentist?

Children should visit the dentist every six months for regular check-ups and cleanings to maintain healthy teeth and gums.

Do you offer sedation for anxious children?

Yes, we offer sedation options such as laughing gas (nitrous oxide) and oral sedation to help calm anxious children during treatments.

What are dental sealants, and why are they important?

Dental sealants are thin coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars to prevent cavities by protecting teeth from decay-causing bacteria and food particles.

Do you accept Medi-Cal for children’s dental care?

Yes, we accept Medi-Cal and most major insurance plans. Our team will help verify your coverage and provide payment options to ensure your child receives the care they need.
