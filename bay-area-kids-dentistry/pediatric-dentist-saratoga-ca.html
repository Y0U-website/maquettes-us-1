Frequently Asked Questions

When should I schedule my child’s first dental visit?

It’s recommended that your child visit the dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth erupts. Early visits help identify potential issues early and establish a positive dental routine.

How often should my child visit the dentist?

Children should visit the dentist every six months for regular check-ups and cleanings. This helps keep their teeth healthy and prevents major issues in the future.

What is the best way to prevent cavities in children?

The best way to prevent cavities is by maintaining a good oral hygiene routine that includes brushing twice a day, flossing, and visiting the dentist regularly for exams and cleanings.

Does Bay Area Kids Dentistry offer orthodontic care?

Yes, we offer early orthodontic evaluations and treatments such as braces and clear aligners to help with bite issues and alignment concerns.

Can I use Medi-Cal for my child’s dental visits?

Yes, we accept Medi-Cal and most major insurance plans. Our team can help verify your insurance coverage and provide options to make your child’s care affordable.
