Frequently Asked Questions

When should I take my child to the dentist for the first time?

It’s recommended that children visit the dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth erupts. Early visits help establish a dental home and allow for early detection of potential issues.

How often should my child see the dentist?

Children should see a pediatric dentist every six months for regular check-ups and cleanings. These visits help prevent dental problems and ensure optimal oral health.

Does Bay Area Kids Dentistry offer orthodontic services?

Yes, we offer early orthodontic evaluations to assess your child’s growth and development. We provide treatments like braces and clear aligners for teens to ensure a healthy bite and smile.

What should I do if my child has a dental emergency?

If your child experiences a dental emergency, contact our office immediately. We offer emergency dental care for situations like knocked-out teeth, broken teeth, or severe toothaches. Our team will provide prompt care to alleviate discomfort and prevent further damage.

Does Bay Area Kids Dentistry accept Medi-Cal?

Yes, we proudly accept Medi-Cal and most major insurance plans. Our team is happy to assist with verifying coverage and explaining available payment options.
