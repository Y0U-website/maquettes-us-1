Surgical Exposure and Orthodontic Guidance

Our impacted canine treatment in Pinole includes a variety of specialized procedures designed to safely expose and guide unerupted canines into position.

Orthodontic Traction and Alignment

Once the impacted tooth is exposed, orthodontic anchorage is used to apply light, continuous force, pulling the canine into the correct path of eruption. The process may take several months, but it ensures optimal alignment and functional integration of the tooth. This guided eruption prevents potential impacted canine complications and promotes lifelong oral health.

Closed Exposure Technique

This approach involves lifting the gum tissue to access the unerupted canine, attaching an orthodontic bracket with a small chain, and then repositioning the gum. The orthodontist later applies gentle traction to help guide the tooth into place naturally. Closed exposure is ideal for palatal impacted canines that require controlled eruption.

Open Exposure Technique

In open exposure, the gum covering the impacted tooth is partially removed, leaving it visible to naturally erupt over time. This approach is typically recommended for canines closer to the gum surface. The technique promotes gradual eruption and minimal discomfort, allowing orthodontic adjustments to proceed smoothly.
