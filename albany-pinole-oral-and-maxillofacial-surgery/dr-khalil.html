Dr. Adel S. Khalil

Dr. Khalil is a member of numerous local and national organizations and he is an attending staff at Alta Bates Hospital in Berkeley. Dr. Khalil has previously served as President of The Berkeley Dental Society.

Dr. Khalil is an anesthesia examiner for the Dental Board of California, helping the state and our community stay safe.
