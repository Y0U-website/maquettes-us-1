0 DR. JENNY SHULSKY Dr. Jenny, a dedicated and passionate orthodontist, grew up in the vibrant city of Baltimore. Her journey in the field of dentistry began at the prestigious Lake Erie School of Dental Medicine, where she honed her skills and developed a deep love for patient care. Following her graduation, Dr. Jenny pursued an orthodontic residency at the Georgia School of Orthodontics, further refining her expertise in creating beautiful, healthy smiles.

After completing her residency, Dr. Jenny returned to the DMV area, driven by a determination to join a practice known for its excellent office structure and commitment to patient-centric care. Her favorite aspect of orthodontics is the opportunity to build lasting relationships with her patients and guide them through their transformative journey to a smile they truly love. Dr. Jenny is grateful to have found a team that shares her values and fosters a positive, supportive environment where every staff member is dedicated to providing an exceptional patient experience.

Dr. Jenny is excited to meet each new patient and be a small, positive part of their smile story. Her dedication to her craft and her patients is evident in every interaction, ensuring that everyone who walks through her doors feels valued and cared for.

Beyond her professional life, Dr. Jenny is a loving wife and mother. She and her husband are the proud parents of a five-year-old daughter and a toddler. In her free time, Dr. Jenny enjoys being outdoors, often found chasing after her energetic three-year-old and their playful golden retriever puppy, Clarence.

Dr. Jenny’s blend of professional excellence and personal warmth makes her a cherished member of the orthodontic community. She looks forward to welcoming you to her practice and helping you achieve the smile of your dreams.
