0 Dr. Matt Beecroft View fullsize Helping others build confidence through a great smile has been a dream of mine since my own father decided to get braces at the age of 59. It was amazing to watch his transformation as he went from a man who never smiled to a man who wouldn’t stop smiling.

I hope to give that same excitement and joy to each patient I meet by providing excellent care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. I appreciate every patient who gives me the opportunity to provide this service for them and I hope to honor that trust by making their experience with us one that positively affects their life.

“I appreciate every patient who gives me the opportunity to help them realize their best smile.”

My orthodontic residency was completed at Vanderbilt University where I served as chief resident. I was invited and was proud to join the adjunct faculty at Vanderbilt where I enjoy giving back by sharing my knowledge and experience with current residents.

I recognize that continuing education is extremely important in an ever-changing field. I participate in various seminars and courses each year as well as local and national organizations. These experiences help me stay at the front of the orthodontic field.
