Meet Dr. Sherri Wilkerson-Cox…

0 DR. Sherri Wilkerson-Cox Dr. Cox is a distinguished orthodontist with a robust educational background and a passion for helping patients achieve their best smiles. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. Following this, she completed her orthodontic residency at the prestigious Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry, where she honed her skills and deepened her expertise in orthodontics.

Dr. Cox is a proud member of the American Dental Association, reflecting her commitment to staying updated with the latest advancements in dental and orthodontic care. Her professional journey is marked by a dedication to excellence and a genuine concern for her patients’ well-being.

"My goal is to work with every patient to help them achieve their best possible smile."

In her personal statement, Dr. Cox expresses her philosophy of care. This statement encapsulates her patient-centered approach, emphasizing collaboration and individualized care in her practice. Dr. Cox is not only a skilled orthodontist but also a compassionate caregiver dedicated to making a positive impact on the lives of her patients.

Beyond her professional life, Dr. Cox values her family above all else, considering them her primary source of joy and inspiration. She also demonstrates her commitment to community service by volunteering at the Moss Dental Clinic alongside Dr. Gregory, providing much-needed dental care to underserved populations.
