Scaling and Root Planing

Gum health is a cornerstone of oral hygiene, yet conditions like periodontal disease can compromise it if left untreated. Scaling and root planing is an effective treatment for individuals experiencing gum disease that targets the issue’s root causes. This non-surgical procedure, performed by our experienced periodontist in Atlanta, GA , helps restore gum health and lays the foundation for long-term oral wellness.

What Is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing is often referred to as a deep cleaning procedure and is designed to treat gum disease. Unlike routine dental cleanings, which focus on the visible surfaces of teeth, this specialized treatment addresses bacteria and tartar buildup below the gumline, where periodontal disease thrives.

The procedure consists of two main steps:

Together, these steps halt the progression of gum disease and pave the way for healthier gums.

  • Scaling: This process removes plaque and tartar (hardened) from the tooth surfaces, especially beneath the gumline. Bacteria accumulate in these areas, leading to gum inflammation and pockets that separate the gum tissue from the teeth. Scaling eliminates these harmful deposits.
  • Root Planing: After scaling, the root surfaces of the teeth are smoothed to prevent future bacterial buildup. Root planing helps gums reattach to the teeth by reducing rough spots, promoting healing and reducing pocket depth.

When Is Scaling and Root Planing Necessary?

Scaling and root planing is typically recommended for individuals diagnosed with periodontal disease, especially in its early to moderate stages. Signs that you may need this treatment include:

Our experienced periodontist will evaluate your gum health through a comprehensive exam, which may include measuring pocket depths and assessing the extent of tartar buildup . Contact us for the best scaling and root planing treatment to get healthy gums and improved oral health.

  • Persistent gum inflammation or bleeding
  • Deep pockets between the teeth and gums
  • Bad breath that doesn’t improve with brushing or flossing
  • Gum recession, where the gums pull away from the teeth

The Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing

Undergoing scaling and root planing in Atlanta, GA, offers numerous benefits for patients struggling with gum disease , including :

  • Prevention of Tooth Loss: By eliminating bacteria and restoring gum attachment, the treatment helps preserve the teeth and their supporting structures.
  • Improved Oral Health: Scaling and root planing reduce s inflammation, allowing the gums to heal and preventing further damage.
  • Fresher Breath: Removing bacterial deposits below the gumline eliminates one of the primary causes of persistent bad breath.
  • Foundation for Maintenance Therapy: The procedure often serves as the first step in periodontal maintenance therapy, ensuring long-term management of gum health.

What to Expect During the Procedure

Scaling and root planing is performed under local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The procedure is often divided into quadrants, with one or two quadrants treated per session, depending on the severity of the condition.

After the treatment, it’s normal to experience some sensitivity, swelling, or mild discomfort for a few days. Our periodontist may recommend over-the-counter pain relievers, warm saltwater rinses, or special toothpaste for sensitivity to alleviate these symptoms.

Supporting Gum Health After Scaling and Root Planing

To maintain the procedure’s benefits and prevent future issues, adopting a proactive approach to oral hygiene is essential. This includes:

  • Brushing Twice Daily: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste to clean your teeth thoroughly.
  • Flossing Daily: Flossing removes food particles and bacteria from between the teeth and along the gumline.
  • Using Antimicrobial Mouthwash: A mouthwash recommended by your dental professional can help reduce bacteria and inflammation.
  • Attending Regular Dental Check-Ups: Routine visits allow our dentist to monitor your gum health and address any concerns promptly.
