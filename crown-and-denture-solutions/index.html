The practice

Implants anchor your tooth (or dentures) right into the jaw, restoring bite strength and stability. A lifelong upgrade for your smile.

These dentures lock into implants so they stay put, but come out when you need. More secure than traditional, more freedom in your day.

A crown (or cap ) covers your natural tooth to restore strength, protect against future damage, and avoid more serious work later.

We create and place your custom dentures right after extractions using our in-house lab. No long waits. No awkward gaps.

When implants aren’t a fit, traditional dentures offer a confident, comfortable smile, often the same day. We’ll guide you to what works best for you.

Get a full diagnostic exam and X-rays at no cost, so you can make decisions with clarity, not pressure.

From extractions to same-day dentures, everything happens right here in our Bossier City clinic. No waiting. No runaround.

We believe in fair, transparent pricing. If you bring us a lower quote, we’ll match it, and often do better.

Les soins

A dental bridge replaces missing teeth using your natural ones for support. Ideal if you’re not ready for implants.

If a tooth needs to go, we handle it gently, often the same day as your denture or implant. No referrals. No delays.
