Dental Care Insurance We work with insurance every day and are happy to help you make the most of your benefits. While our recommendations are always based on what is best for your health, we will also help you understand your coverage and estimated out-of-pocket costs.

We accept all employer-provided insurance plans and submit claims on your behalf. If your benefits include dental care, your out-of-pocket costs may be reduced.

Please note, state-funded Medicaid and Medicare plans are not accepted.
