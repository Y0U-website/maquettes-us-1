Restorative dentistry focuses on restoring oral health, function, and appearance, and relieving pain and/or infection. schedule an appointment services tooth-colored fillings Cavities are common in children, but they’re easily treatable. We use tooth-colored fillings to repair cavities, ensuring the restoration blends seamlessly with your child’s natural teeth. Our fillings are durable and long-lasting, providing both function and aesthetics.
  • crowns For more extensive decay or damage, a crown might be necessary to protect and restore the tooth. We offer stainless steel crowns, which help preserve the tooth structure and maintain proper chewing function.
  • extractions Sometimes, a tooth may be too damaged to save and needs removed. We perform extractions gently and carefully, ensuring your child’s comfort throughout the procedure.
  • nerve treatment For more severe cases affecting the tooth’s nerve, nerve treatment may be needed. This treatment involves removing the infected tooth or damaged nerve tissue, and restoring the tooth with a crown.
  • general anesthesia We provide comprehensive dental care in office and in a hospital setting under general anesthesia. *In-office general anesthesia is available for healthy patients who meet certain criteria. Our team will help determine the safest and most appropriate setting for your child’s care.
