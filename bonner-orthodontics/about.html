Our Team

Kerry

Kerry loves to hear your story! Her own story includes adventures living in Southeast Asia and also knowing NWA when pastures outnumbered highways. Home is where the heart is, and we are so lucky to have Kerry call Bonner Orthodontics home! She is here to make your journey to a new smile smooth and seamless.

Jacky

Jacky visited Dr. Bonners office as a student observer. After finishing her dental assisting program in 2020, she knew she didn’t want to work anywhere else and has been a part of the team ever since. Her favorite part of working in orthodontics is getting to build a relationship with each patient she sees. In her free time, she loves to spend time outdoors in our beautiful, natural state, where she was born and raised.

Elizabeth

Elizabeth, a northwest Arkansas local, has been a part of the dental world since finishing her assisting program in 2019. She spent a year in general dentistry before making the switch to orthodontics. She enjoys helping each of our patients along their unique smile journey, especially the patients that start with us as youngsters. Outside of work, Elizabeth likes going on picnics and walks with her family and their Jack Russell named Palomita, which translates to Popcorn

Hunter

Hunter wanted to work in orthodontics as a child and started that journey after completing her assisting program in 2013. She finds joy in seeing a completed treatment plan for a patient who started with a very difficult occlusion turn into a very comfortable and functional bite. Outside of the clinic she enjoys spending time with her family, baking, and being a home maker.

Megan

Megan, a lifeline of Bonner Orthodontics, was originally Dr. Bonners babysitter when the practice first opened. After moving into a laboratory technician role in 2021 she decided to pursue her license as a dental assistant and started as a clinician in 2024. Her favorite thing about working at Bonner Orthodontics is seeing the patient’s excitement as they progress through their treatment. In her spare time, she enjoys working at her family’s green house in downtown Bentonville and going to country concerts with her little sister.

Makayla

Makayla, an Oklahoma dweller, jumped right into general dentistry after completing her assisting program in 2021 where she stayed until 2023 when she shifted her focus to orthodontics and joined the Bonner crew. She takes delight in seeing our patients develop confident smiles after completing their treatment. In her time away from the office, she loves painting and teaching children’s ballet classes at a studio in her hometown.

Abby

Growing up in Northwest Arkansas, Abby manages a part-time role making everything run smoothly behind the scenes. A jack of all trades, she runs our 3D print lab, fabricates aligners, and communicates with our referring dental offices. Her favorite part of working at Bonner Orthodontics is learning the mechanics behind making our in-house aligners and going to lunch at Chick-Fil-A with her coworkers. With a wide array of interests, some of her past times include ceramics and pottery classes.
