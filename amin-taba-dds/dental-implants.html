Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer numerous advantages over traditional tooth replacement options like dentures and bridges. Some key benefits include:

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants are designed to last for decades with proper care. The titanium post integrates with the jawbone, providing a stable and long-lasting solution.

Dental implants closely resemble natural teeth in both appearance and function. They are custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size of surrounding teeth, ensuring a seamless look.

Because dental implants are anchored into the jawbone, they provide superior chewing strength compared to removable dentures. Patients can enjoy their favorite foods without restrictions.

When a tooth is lost, the underlying jawbone can deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Dental implants help maintain bone density by mimicking the function of natural tooth roots, preventing bone loss over time.

Unlike ill-fitting dentures that may slip and cause speech difficulties, dental implants remain securely in place, allowing for clearer speech and improved confidence.

Unlike traditional bridges that require grinding down neighboring teeth for support, dental implants do not compromise the integrity of adjacent natural teeth.

Dental implants eliminate the discomfort and inconvenience associated with removable dentures, such as slipping, adhesive use, and nighttime removal.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Initial Consultation

The first step in getting dental implants is a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Amin Tabatabaian. During this visit, we evaluate the patient’s oral health, take digital X-rays or 3D scans, and discuss treatment goals to determine candidacy for implants.

Treatment Planning

If dental implants are deemed suitable, a personalized treatment plan is created. This includes determining the number of implants needed and addressing any necessary preparatory procedures, such as bone grafting.

Bone Grafting (If Necessary)

If a patient has insufficient bone density, a bone graft may be performed to strengthen the jawbone before implant placement. Healing time varies but can take several months.

Implant Placement Surgery

During this procedure, the titanium implant post is surgically inserted into the jawbone. The area is allowed to heal over several months as osseointegration occurs, fusing the implant with the bone.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant has integrated with the bone, an abutment is placed on top of the implant post. The abutment serves as a connection point for the final prosthetic tooth.

Custom Crown, Bridge, or Denture Placement

After the gums heal around the abutment, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing the restoration. The new tooth is designed to blend naturally with surrounding teeth. Contact us to learn more.

Types of Dental Implants

Single-Tooth Implants

Used to replace a single missing tooth, a single-tooth implant consists of one implant post, an abutment, and a custom crown.

Implant-Supported Bridges

For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge provides a secure alternative to traditional bridges without the need for support from adjacent teeth.

All-on-4 and All-on-6 Implants

These full-arch restoration solutions involve securing an entire row of teeth using four or six strategically placed implants. They provide a stable and permanent alternative to traditional dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures provide greater stability than traditional dentures, preventing slippage and improving comfort.

Caring for Dental Implants

Proper care and maintenance ensure the longevity of dental implants. Here are some essential care tips:

  • Practice Good Oral Hygiene: Brush at least twice a day and floss daily to keep the implant area clean.
  • Use a Soft-Bristled Toothbrush: This helps protect both the implant and surrounding gum tissue.
  • Avoid Hard or Sticky Foods: Excessive force on the implant can cause damage.
  • Regular Dental Check-Ups: Routine visits to Dr. Tabatabaian ensure that the implant remains in optimal condition.
  • Quit Smoking: Smoking can increase the risk of implant failure by interfering with healing and osseointegration.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants should:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Possess sufficient jawbone density (or be willing to undergo bone grafting)
  • Maintain good oral health and hygiene
  • Not have uncontrolled conditions like diabetes that may impair healing
  • Be non-smokers or willing to quit
