Preventative Dentistry

Protect your smile with Preventive Dentistry. Prevention is key to keeping oral health in check. It’s never too early to start thinking about preventing future dental problems. Aby Dental doesn’t just focus on the symptom, we look at the whole picture and provide you with essential preventive dental services.

We should all floss and brush well. But we don’t — and plaque can build up and turn to tartar over time. When this happens, you need a Deep Cleaning. The procedure removes plaque and tartar on the surface of the enamel up under the gums. A Deep Cleaning leaves your smile brighter, healthier, and improves your overall oral health. Abys Dental specializes in deep cleanings. Schedule an appointment today.

Preventative Care

In addition to restorative dentistry and oral surgery, Abys Dental also provides crucial preventative care. It is important that you visit us at least once every six months for your comprehensive examination and thorough cleaning. These cleanings can prevent tooth decay, periodontal disease and even more serious problems as well as give Dr. T. the opportunity to check for nascent issues.
