Our modern facility is equipped with advanced technology from digital x-rays and CBCT 3-D scanning to digital impressions and 3-D printing. These tools allow us to diagnose more accurately, treat more efficiently, and give you a clearer view of your oral health.

We’re proud to serve patients from Kannapolis, Concord, China Grove, Lexington, and beyond. You’ll find us right next to the Social Security office in Salisbury, NC.

At Brown & Fortner Dental Group, we’ve been creating confident, healthy smiles in Salisbury since 1962. What began as a small practice with Dr. Lomax has grown into a modern dental clinic known for its state-of-the-art care, warm atmosphere, and a team that treats patients like family.

Today, Dr. Tyler Brown and Dr. Don Fortner are proud to carry on the tradition of excellence while embracing the latest technology and techniques to make your visits more comfortable and your results more precise.
