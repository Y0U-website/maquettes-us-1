Implants

Benefits Replace a missing tooth without drilling on a neighboring tooth. Maintain the same chewing force as a natural tooth (a denture has 1/3 to 1/4 the force as a natural tooth) Stabilize a denture – multiple implants hook on the inside of a denture to secure it during use Implants come in different sizes for different types of bone. Dr. La Monica primarily uses the implant that will be best fit for your bone type and situation.

How do I get an implant? Call or click contact us to schedule you complimentary implant consultation.

What can I expect during my consultation? Dr. La Monica will evaluate your dental needs and see if you are a good candidate for dental implants. He will evaluate bone thickness and gum health. He will personalize your treatment plan to fit your needs and desires.

  • Implants Dental implants are one of many options to help you achieve your ideal smile. What is a dental implant? A dental implant is an artificial root placed in the jaw bone to support either a crown, bridge, or denture. The implant is titanium and permits your body to fuse (osteointegrate) with the implant
