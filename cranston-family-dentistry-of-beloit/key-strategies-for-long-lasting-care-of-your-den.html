  • Visit Your Dentist Regularly: Routine check-ups are essential for monitoring the condition of dental implants. Dr. Timothy Cain encourages patients to visit Cranston Family Dentistry at least twice a year or as recommended. During these appointments, any potential issues can be identified early, and professional cleanings can be performed to keep your implants in optimal condition.
  • Use Antimicrobial Mouthwash: Incorporating an antimicrobial or antiseptic mouthwash into your oral care routine can help reduce bacteria in your mouth, decreasing the risk of infection around the implant sites.
  • Be Mindful of Your Diet: A balanced diet plays a vital role in keeping dental implants healthy. Avoid hard foods that might exert excessive pressure on the implants, as this can lead to damage. Focus on nutrient-rich foods that promote oral health, such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and nuts. Staying hydrated is also essential, as it helps maintain oral moisture and aids in overall health.
  • Avoid Tobacco Products: Smoking or using tobacco products can significantly impact healing and increase the risk of implant failure. If you are a smoker, consider seeking support to quit before undergoing dental implant surgery and while caring for your implants.
