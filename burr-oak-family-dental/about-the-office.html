Meet Dr. Mark Monheiser

Dr. Mark has been caring for patients from Blue Springs since 1983. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University of Missouri - Kansas City School of Dentistry and takes many continuing education courses each year so he can continue to provide his patients with the best care dentistry has to offer. He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Dental Implant Association. Besides dentistry, he enjoys spending time with his family, especially his grandchildren, playing golf, running, and coaching high school football.

Meet Dr. Cole Prstojevich

Dr. Cole completed his undergraduate at the University of Missouri-Columbia before going on to graduate from University of Missouri-Kansas City. He completed his Residency at University Health-Lakewood. His favorite part of dentistry is being able to make a positive impact on the lives of others. He married his high school sweetheart, Shelby and they have a beautiful baby girl, Rowen. They have a lab named Bailey and a cat named Walter. He enjoys spending time with family and friends at the lake, barbecuing, golfing, and traveling.

Giving Back to Our Community

At Burr Oak Family Dental, we love being a part of the Blue Springs community.
