Meet Dr. James M. Wilson

Dr. Wilson is an East Bay native. He ventured to the Central Valley for college at California State University Fresno. He came back to attend dental school and graduated form the University of California at the San Francisco school of dentistry in 1993. He has completed hundreds of hours of advanced education in various aspects of dentistry.

Constant progress dependable excellence. Nevertheless Dr. Wilson believes there is always room to improve, so he constantly refines his abilities to bring you the best care possible.

He is a very busy family man with 3 sons. Ben, Tommy and Matt, and let’s not forget his wonderful and supportive wife Eileen. Together they enjoy participating in all their son’s soccer, baseball and basketball activities. Dr. Wilson even takes time out to coach and umpire.
