Meet Dr. Mark Nakhla

Dr. Mark Nakhla brings a wealth of knowledge and a caring touch to every patient interaction. A Bay Area native, he always felt a calling to follow his family’s path into healthcare. His academic achievements include earning his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the prestigious University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco. Before that, he obtained a Bachelor of Science in Neurobiology, Physiology, and Behavior from the University of California an educational foundation that shapes his holistic approach to patient care.

Beyond his professional pursuits, Dr. Mark expresses his passion for community and family. When he’s not in the office, you can find him enjoying time with his loved ones, mentoring youth in his church’s ministry, or shooting hoops with friends. A proud supporter of Bay Area sports teams especially his cherished Golden State Warriors Dr. Mark’s warmth and relatability extend well beyond the dental chair.

Committed to providing exceptional care, Dr. Mark takes the time to understand your needs, address your concerns, and explain treatment options tailored to your health goals. With the aid of advanced dental technologies, he ensures every visit is a blend of comfort and innovation. To Dr. Mark, building lifelong relationships with patients and sharing a few smiles along the way make his work truly meaningful.

Schedule your next appointment today and experience the compassionate, expert care that Dr. Mark and his team at Castro Valley Family Dentistry are proud to provide.
