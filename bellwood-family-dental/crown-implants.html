What to Expect When Receiving a Professional Crown Implant

The first step in the process is our consultation, which involves a discussion between our dentist and the patient about treatment goals. We then will proceed with dental X-rays and an oral examination! Our dentist will also answer questions the patient has and help them make an informed decision about treatment.

Each patient may require preparations such as bone grafting and then the placement procedures are performed afterward. It takes several weeks, if not longer for their mouth to heal properly following implant placement in a manner that is both aesthetic and functional so they can eat or speak clearly again!
