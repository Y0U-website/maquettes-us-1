OFFICE POLICY

The endodontists at the Southern California Endodontic Group diagnose and provide treatment based on his or her professional judgment and NOT on the cost. Endodontic treatment will be based on the extent of treatment.

Because of the unique nature of our retreatment practice and the specialized kinds of cases we treat, as well as, our contracts with insurance companies, it is not possible for us to accept insurance in lieu of payment. As a courtesy, we will submit the initial claim and the necessary paperwork for that claim to your insurance company. However, it is our policy that your care if paid for at the time of treatment.

During your visit the endodontist will discuss your treatment and the administrative staff will discuss the fees involved. Our endodontists prefer to concentrate on you and your treatment and leave the fees and insurance to our administrative staff.

For those patients with dental insurance:

Our administrative staff, will be happy to assist you with any questions or concerns regarding our office policies or procedures your account or your insurance.

We are proud to be a multilingual office. Languages spoken in our office are: English, Spanish, Farsi, Korean and Russian.

JUST FOR YOU.....

Our patients are important to us, so in an effort to be as accommodating as possible and to make life a little easier, we have selected certain insurance companies to participate with. This is hugely based on requests from our patients. We accept most indemnity and select PPO plans. We do not accept HMO plans. If you have questions or concerns, please call our office. We will be more than happy to assist you.

We are proud to be a multilingual office. Languages spoken in our office are: English, Spanish, Farsi, Korean, Russian, Greek and Vietnamese.

DENTAL INSURANCE:

We accept most traditional insurance. However, should you have questions or concerns about your insurance plan or benefits, we recommend that you contact your insurance company prior to your visit.

CREDIT CARDS WE ACCEPT:

FINANCING AVAILABLE:

http://www.carecredit.com/apply/index.html

CareCredit is a flexible patient payment program, specifically designed for healthcare expenses. CareCredit makes it easier for you to get the treatment you want or need. CareCredit is ideal for deposits, deductibles, treatment fees, and procedures not covered by your insurance. We offer the 6-month, interest-free plan, with no interest if the balance is paid within the 6 months. You can apply online, using your computer, tablet or smartphone. Just bring the account information with you to your visit and we can easily process the payment for you.
