David M. Pokras, D.D.S.

Dr. Pokras has practiced Endodontics since 1996. He was trained in micro-surgical endodontics at the U.C.L.A. School of Dentistry, where he graduated with highest honors. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from U.C.L.A., and then completed his formal dental studies at the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco, where he graduated at the top of his class also with highest honors. Currently he is an Associate Professor at the U.C.L.A. School of Dentistry when he is not practicing in our offices.
