Catherine, RDH, BS

Born and raised in New Orleans, Catherine graduated from the LSUHSC School of Dentistry’s Program in Dental Hygiene in 1999 receiving two distinctive awards. After graduation, she continued her work with the local, state, and national dental hygiene associations and went on to join the Board of Trustees for the American Dental Hygienists’ Association.

Throughout her work at ADHA, Catherine worked on numerous committees, published articles in national publications, gave presentations at meetings around the country and received several more honors and recognitions. Several years after her graduation, she accepted a Faculty position at LSU School of Dentistry coordinating a public health program throughout Louisiana.

She continues to work in private practice with the same passion and enthusiasm she had when she started her career — grateful to serve in the same office for over 26 years, seeing patients spanning three and four generations. Catherine and her husband, Andrew, have three children and can be spotted having fun in and around New Orleans.

Amanda, RDH, BS

Originally from Baton Rouge, Amanda moved to New Orleans to pursue her dental hygiene education at LSU School of Dentistry. She graduated with her bachelor’s degree in 2010 and is a member of the New Orleans Dental Hygiene Association.

With over 15 years of experience, Amanda is dedicated to providing quality dental care with attention to detail while creating a comfortable and positive experience for every patient. She enjoys building lasting relationships with her patients and helping them maintain optimal oral and overall health through gentle, personalized care and patient education.

Outside of the office, Amanda enjoys spending quality time with her husband and two daughters. As a family, they love taking vacations where they can hike, explore, and stay active together. She also enjoys dining out in New Orleans, attending music festivals, exercising, and gardening.

Candice, Office Manager

Candice, born and raised in New Orleans, has great love for the city and its unique culture. Candice handles the day-to-day operations of our practice to keep everything running smoothly and efficiently for our patients. With over 39 years of dental field experience — including a strong foundation as a former Dental Assistant — she possesses a comprehensive understanding of every moving part in clinical care.

Candice is deeply committed to making each patient’s visit comfortable, welcoming, and stress-free. She takes great pride in guiding patients through their insurance options, helping to make quality dental care affordable and accessible, while ensuring our office stays up to date with modern dental technology.

Outside the practice, Candice loves exploring the vibrant New Orleans culinary scene, traveling to new destinations, and cherishing every moment spent with her grandchildren, Emmaline and Briggs.

Alyleen, EDDA

Alyleen, a proud New Orleans native and bilingual Spanish-speaking EDDA, has been part of the Campo Dentistry team for the past three years. She earned her certification through the LSU School of Dentistry and has spent nearly 10 years building her experience in the dental field.

Known for her versatility and welcoming personality, Alyleen can be found helping patients both in the front office and chairside in the back. As the office Insurance Coordinator, she enjoys helping patients better understand their benefits and making the dental process as stress-free as possible.

Alyleen also has specialized pediatric training and experience assisting with hospital general anesthesia cases, allowing her to confidently care for patients of all ages and needs. Her passion for dentistry comes from creating comfortable experiences, building patient trust, and helping every smile leave brighter than when it arrived.

Sophia, Front Desk Coordinator

Sophia is the warm and welcoming face behind the front office at Campo Dentistry. As Front Desk Coordinator, Sophia helps keep the office running smoothly by handling insurance verifications, confirming appointments, coordinating schedules, and managing all of the important behind-the-scenes details that help each patient experience feel seamless and stress-free. Known for her outgoing personality and quick sense of humor, she loves chatting with patients in the lobby and making everyone feel comfortable the moment they walk through the door.

Born and raised with deep New Orleans roots, Sophia grew up in the heart of the French Quarter and comes from a family woven into the city’s rich history and culture. She is the granddaughter of legendary rock and roll pioneer Cosimo Matassa, whose influence helped shape the sound of New Orleans music and earned him a place in the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.

Outside of the office, Sophia enjoys spending time with family, discovering great food around the city, and surrounding herself with animals — especially horses, which have always held a special place in her heart. Her vibrant personality, love for people, and strong New Orleans spirit make her an unforgettable part of the Campo Dentistry team.
