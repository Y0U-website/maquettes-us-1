What are Ceramic Dental Implants?

Ceramic dental implants are similar to those made of titanium, only they contain no metal. However, the base still serves as an artificial tooth root, allowing your new teeth to remain firmly in place for decades.

By using these dental implants, ceramic offers optimal strength and heat resistance. You do not have to worry about an allergic reaction, and it produces beautiful aesthetics that blend in seamlessly with the rest of your smile.

Titanium Dental Implants vs. Ceramic Dental Implants

While the two types of dental implants deliver the same lasting results, there are major differences between them, such as:

  • Ceramic is considered biocompatible, while titanium often consists of a titanium alloy. This can cause some patients to experience an allergic reaction, resulting in inflammation, pain, and irritation.
  • Titanium may release some small amounts of metal into the body over time, while ceramic is safe and non-toxic.
  • Ceramic dental implants offer greater aesthetics because no titanium can potentially show.
  • Titanium implants can cause greater sensitivities because they are more thermally conductive.

Am I a Candidate for Ceramic Dental Implants?

To determine if you are a qualified candidate for ceramic dental implants, it’s important that you first schedule a consultation with Dr. Holinbeck. This will allow her to examine your oral and overall health as well as the density of your jawbone.

Ceramic dental implants can be an ideal option if you are sensitive or allergic to metals like titanium, you want something that is more aesthetically pleasing, and you want a solution that is mindful of your whole-body health. If you have healthy gums and sufficient bone density, there is a good chance you’ll be recommended for this type of treatment.

Benefits of Ceramic Dental Implants

While many dental offices offer only titanium dental implants and swear by their longevity, compatibility, and function, you will find that by choosing ceramic dental implants, you can enjoy:

  • A more confident form of tooth replacement that removes any metal yet delivers the same great results.
  • A biocompatible, hypoallergenic option for tooth replacement.
  • Prosthetics that do not corrode over time.
  • Improved aesthetics because there will be no grayish metal attempting to peek through over time.
  • Reduced plaque and tartar build-up because of the smooth finish that ceramic offers.
  • Stable, strong dental implants that fuse well with bone tissues.
