Dr. Holinbeck Provides Comprehensive Therapies

Dr. Holinbeck received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Southern California in Los Angeles. Though she is no longer in school, she believes strongly in the importance of continuing education and has dedicated hundreds of hours of additional learning to the following passions:

  • Ozone/oxygen therapies
  • Zirconia (metal-free) Bioceramic implant placement and restoration
  • Biomimetic dentistry
  • Cosmetic smile enhancements
  • Full mouth rehabilitations
  • IAOMT SMART certification for safe amalgam removal
  • Advanced CEREC certification
  • Certified Invisalign provider
  • Active in the prestigious Spear Study Club
