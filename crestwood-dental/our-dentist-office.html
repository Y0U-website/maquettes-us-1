About The Tuckahoe, NY Dental…

We don’t believe in rushing you through your care. Instead, we take the time necessary to get to know you and understand exactly what you want and need. With a supportive environment, our patients are more likely to continue their oral health care . It can lower dental anxiety and help patients stick to their treatment plans.

We also work with you in a collaborative way to help you understand your oral health. We also review your options so that you are prepared for your treatment. When you have an active role in your oral health, it can help you stay motivated and set the pace for your treatment. We work with you to get your smile repaired and revitalized for a lifetime.

If this is the dental care you have been looking for, give us a call! We look forward to getting to know you. Serving Tuckahoe, Yonkers, Eastchester, New Rochelle, and the surrounding communities of Westchester County, Crestwood Dental is your local choice.
