The practice

You really do deserve to be treated like the unique and special individual you are – especially when it comes to something as important as your smile. That’s why we listen carefully to your needs, get to know you on an individual basis and go out of our way to educate you thoroughly on everything you need to feel and look your healthy best.

We don’t believe in rushing you through your care. Instead, we take the time necessary to get to know you and understand exactly what you want and need.

We also work with you in a collaborative way to help you understand your oral health, review your options and set the pace for your treatment. We work with you to get your smile repaired and revitalized for a lifetime.

We build personal relationships with every patient and that starts with taking some time to get to know each other better.

Using the latest in dental technology in our Tuckahoe dentist office provides our patients with many treatment advantages.

Completing our patient forms can make your visit more efficient. Please take a moment before your visit to download and complete our forms.

Our office will work with you to file your dental insurance claim and maximize your benefits. We also offer financing for your treatment plan.
