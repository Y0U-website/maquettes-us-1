Meet Our Dentist in Tuckahoe, NY: Dr. Brad Gorsky

Upon graduating from Muhlenberg College, Dr. Brad Gorsky completed his dental training at Temple University’s School of Dentistry, receiving his doctoral degree in 1999. After practicing as a general dentist for multiple years in the greater Philadelphia area, he relocated to New York City in 2003 and proceeded to open his own midtown Manhattan practice in 2008. Dr. Gorsky joined Crestwood Dental in 2023.

Dr. Gorsky is a firm believer in continuing education so that he can deliver the best possible care to his patients. He spends much of his free time studying innovative new techniques to help patients improve their smiles, through the use of cosmetic procedures such as porcelain veneers, clear aligner therapy, and teeth whitening.

Dr. Gorsky completed the core curriculum at the prestigious Dawson Academy. He focused on comprehensive treatments including smile design for the worn dentition and TMJ analysis. He can help diagnose proper joint and muscle function so that patients can keep their teeth healthy and working properly for a lifetime.

To stay true to his ongoing commitment to continuing education, Dr. Gorsky recently underwent advanced training in Airway Focused Dentistry. Airway Focused Dentistry is rooted in a comprehensive multidisciplinary approach to the oral-systemic connection between your mouth and body, and how it relates to sleep-disordered breathing, sleep apnea and airway deficiency. Dr. Gorsky is also currently attending the Pankey Institute curriculum to complement his Dawson Academy training and continue to expand his knowledge and skill.

Conveniently located in Tuckahoe, New York, Dr. Gorsky commits to providing his patients with personalized care, efficient service and state-of-the-art dentistry. He is a gentle and understanding professional who will listen to your questions and concerns. He will work with you on a comprehensive treatment plan to uncover your best smile.
