We are a comprehensive dental solution for your child. From infancy through adolescence, All Star Pediatric Dentistry can guide them on their journey to a healthy, beautiful smile…

The practice

Located along 355/Hungerford Drive across from Rockville Town Square, we are proud to serve patients from Rockville, Derwood, Bethesda, Potomac, Olney, Gaithersburg, Germantown, Silver Spring, and all of Montgomery County. We make every effort to accommodate for your child’s individualized dental needs and family’s busy schedule.

Our brand-new facility offers the latest in dental technology such as digital x-rays, intra-oral photography, soft tissue laser, and sedation dentistry to help your child have a comfortable, positive dental experience. Our mission is to grow happy and healthy smiles for kids of all ages. We are committed to delivering quality dental care and are dedicated to treating all children as our own.

We are a trusted dental home for kids, conveniently located in your neighborhood and cater to your child’s schedule including early morning, after school and weekend openings. Should your child have a dental emergency, we provide access to a dental provider 24 hours a day.

Because your child’s comfort is important to us, we offer many of the modern amenities you’ve come to expect in a dental office including TV’s, tablets, video games and prize rewards. We believe that whatever your child’s dental needs may be, your child will be treated with the highest quality dentistry available and with the most compassionate, respectful care you deserve.

Our office staff is fluent in Korean, Vietnamese, Spanish, and Chinese. We value diversity and welcome those from all different social and ethnic backgrounds.
