COVID-19 Update

With all the necessary changes we would have a copay of $10 covering all additional cost which will be incurring during your future visit.

We look forward to seeing you again and are happy to answer any questions you may have about the steps we take to keep you, and every patient, safe in our practice. To make an appointment, please call our office at office number or visit our website at web address.

Thank you for being our patient. We value your trust and loyalty and look forward to welcoming back our patients, neighbors and friends.

  • We have hand sanitizer that we will ask you to use when you enter the office. You will also find in other places in the office for you to use as needed.
  • Use of non-touch thermometer
  • Specialized suction systems proven to control 90 % of aerosols created by dentistry
  • You may see that our waiting room will no longer offer magazines, children’s toys and so forth, since those items are difficult to clean and disinfect.
  • Appointments will be managed to allow for social distancing between patients. That might mean that you’re offered fewer options for scheduling your appointment.
  • We will do our best to allow greater time between patients to reduce waiting times for you, as well as to reduce the number of patients in the reception area at any one time.
  • New HEPA Filtration system throughout the building to remove particles
  • Use of the highest level of sterilization- we sterilize and wipe all surfaces including countertops, chairs, stools, doorknobs and surgical equipment
  • Our Doctors and team members will be wearing all the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) including surgical gowns, face shields, head covering and N95 masks

Sincerely,

Advanced Dentistry of Harleysville
