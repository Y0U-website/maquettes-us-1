Straighten Teeth Discreetly with Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a wonderful way to straighten your teeth comfortably and confidently. These clear, custom-made trays fit right into your daily life you can remove them to enjoy your favorite meals and keep your teeth clean with ease. They gently guide your smile into place without the look or feel of metal braces, so you can feel good about your appearance every step of the way. Whether you have mild crowding or bigger spacing issues, Clear aligners offers a smooth, predictable path to a healthier, more beautiful smile, and we’re here to support you throughout your journey.

Why Clear Aligners Outperform Traditional Braces

Clear Aligners are made from medical-grade plastics and are virtually transparent-letting you straighten your teeth without announcing it to the world. Unlike metal braces that dominate your smile, these custom trays blend seamlessly. They are perfect for professionals, teens, and anyone who prefers to straighten their smile discreetly.

The smooth BPA-free plastic won’t irritate your cheeks like metal brackets. Remove them for important meetings, date nights, or enjoying Michigan’s famous caramel corn no dietary restrictions or emergency wire repairs needed.

While treatment times vary, most Clear Aligners cases at our Brighton office show noticeable improvement in as little as 3 months, with full results typically achieved 30 % faster than traditional methods. Fewer appointments mean less time away from work or school.

Our in-house 3D scanner eliminates messy impressions we’ll design your entire treatment plan during your first visit. 2 weeks after we take your initial scan we will have your trays ready to begin treatment.
