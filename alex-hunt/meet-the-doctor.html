Meet The Doctor

Dr. Alexander J. Hunt D.D.S.

Your Partner in Lifelong Dental Health

Alexander J. Hunt, D.D.S., knew from an early age that he would make dentistry his profession. A proud graduate of the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, Dr. Hunt practices dentistry at Distinctive Dental and proudly serves the Brighton, Howell, and surrounding areas. With the help of his experienced dental team, Dr. Hunt delivers high-quality, comprehensive dental care to his patients.​

I feel blessed to serve such wonderful communities and to treat patients of all ages. Whether it be a child’s first dental appointment and trip to our legendary’ toy chest, or full-mouth reconstruction on an adult patient, I’m committed to helping my patients achieve dental excellence, he says.

​As a general dentist, Dr. Hunt enjoys all aspects of dentistry including crowns, bridges, and dental implants. He believes in the importance of a beautiful smile, and offers a complete range of cosmetic procedures including teeth-whitening, bonding, and porcelain veneers.​

Taking time to understand my patients’ specific needs is important to me. I enjoy getting to know my patients and I always allow time to discuss everything from treatment options to how their day is going, he says.

​Dr. Hunt offers both evening and weekend hours in order to accommodate his patients’ busy schedules. Outside of the dental office, Dr. Hunt enjoys spending time with his family and friends. He has travelled on several dental mission trips to help underserved people around the world.

Outside of the office Dr. Hunt enjoys spending time with his wife and two daughters. He is looking forward to caring for you and your family for many years to come.
