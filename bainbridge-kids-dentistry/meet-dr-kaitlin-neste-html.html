Meet Dr. Kaitlin Neste

Dr. Kaitlin Neste

Hi, I’m Dr. Kaitlin Neste. I am a Kitsap County native and am so grateful to be serving the community that I grew up in .

I graduated from Central Kitsap High School and obtained my undergrad degree in biochemistry from the University of Washington. I liked being a Husky so much that I stuck around for dental school at the UW too. I graduated with my D.D.S., finishing in the top 5 of my class, and I was also inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society.

In my spare time I love staying active. I met my husband, Jeff, while at the UW, and together we love cheering on the Huskies. We love the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest, and you can find us most weekends out running, hiking, hanging out at the beach, trying out new restaurants, and spending time with friends and family. We have one daughter, two sons, and one fur baby. Just ask me and I’ll show you lots of pictures of them!
