Dr. Lisa Do

Dr. Lisa Do graduated from Midwestern University Arizona. She is a friendly and compassionate dentist who truly enjoys getting to know her patients. She loves to educate patients chairside, creating an inviting and comfortable experience for all. Dr. Do regularly attends continued education courses to stay up to date with the advancing field of dentistry.

When she is not practicing dentistry, she enjoys baking, cooking, photography, and spending time with her family.
