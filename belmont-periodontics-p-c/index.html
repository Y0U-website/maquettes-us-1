Dental implants are successfully used to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or a full arch of missing teeth.

Dental implants are an ideal option for people in good general oral health who have lost a tooth or teeth due to periodontal disease, an injury, or some other reasons. Patients should have healthy gums and adequate bone to hold the implant and be committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental exams.
