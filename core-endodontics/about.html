About us

Mark Germack DDS

Endodontist

I was born and raised in Spokane, Washington. Like any young kid I had dreams of being a point guard for the Sonics. At the age of six I figured out that wasn’t going to happen and I set my sights on becoming a dentist.

I made it to Western Washington University and was committed to becoming a dentist. I graduated with a degree in Cellular and Molecular Biology and was then accepted to the dental school at Marquette University. After dental school, I returned to Washington where I owned a successful general practice in Issaquah for 10 years.

Having experienced some episodes of extreme dental pain myself, I could always relate to patients who were in distress. Even though I had a great practice I felt compelled to practice endodontics and surgery. I decided to apply and was accepted into the residency program in Endodontology at the prestigious Oregon Health and Science University.

During that time my research focused on antibiotics and the prescribing habits of dental practitioners. Our research was published in the Journal of Endodontics and was used to help develop the most recent presiding guidelines for dental infections.

We are very excited to be a part of the dental community in downtown Seattle and are sincerely here to be a resource for our patients and referring providers.

Traditional root canal therapy can be stressful and overwhelming. I want to change that.

Dr. Anusha Kalaichelvan, DDS (Dr. Kalai)

Endodontist

About seven years ago, I found myself needing a root canal. I had just started dental school at New York University when I began experiencing severe tooth pain. That pain eventually led me to see an endodontist. At the time, I wasn’t sure what to expect and, like many people, was afraid it was going to be a painful experience. However, the care I received was so positive and reassuring that it became the inspiration behind my own path into endodontics—and the kind of care I strive to provide every patient I see.

My goal is simple: to provide expert care and ensure you feel comfortable and at ease during every step of your visit.

I was born and raised in Ottawa, the capital of Canada. Growing up, I was interested in living in a fast-paced city, which led me to New York City, where I earned my DDS from NYU. During my time there, I also completed a certificate in public health and graduated with honors in endodontics. Afterward, I completed a general practice residency at The Mount Sinai Hospital before returning to NYU for advanced specialty training in endodontics. My residency provided me with invaluable experience in all aspects of endodontic care.

I am currently an active member of the American Association of Endodontists and was honored to be recognized with a Top 10 Research Presentation Award in 2025.

Now, I’m thrilled to be joining the Seattle-area dental community and look forward to making your visit as smooth and stress-free as possible. If you have any restaurant, hiking, or other PNW recommendations—I’m all ears!

Dr. Vicky Nguyen

Endodontist

Vicky Nguyen, DDS, MSD (or “Dr. V” as she is known in our office) was born and raised here in San Jose, CA. She attended Notre Dame High School and Santa Clara University and naturally roots on the 49ers and Warriors. Dr. Vicky specializes in conventional root canal therapy, cases of traumatic injury, and surgical endodontics.

She values educating patients about their oral conditions, prides herself on her interpersonal/communication skills, and is well known in the office for her calming demeanor.

Dr. V is thrilled to be practicing back in the Seattle area and strives to provide the highest quality of dental care possible to her patients. She regularly attends continuing education courses to stay up to date with continuously evolving technologies and values minimally invasive techniques to best conserve a patient’s existing dentition. She has volunteered to provide dental services to underserved populations both locally and overseas and intends to continue to give back to her communities.

When not in the office, Dr. Vicky can often be found cruising around town with her golden retriever Murphy, golfing at one of the many beautiful local courses, snowboarding, or enjoying some of the fine cuisine that the Seattle area has to offer.
