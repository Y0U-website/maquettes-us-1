The practice

Dr. Lisa Babb and Dr. Giana Lupinetti know that getting braces, Invisalign, or other corrective treatments can be stressful for you and your loved ones. It’s a big change! That’s why we will listen carefully to your concerns and evaluate every patient’s teeth, mouth, and face as entirely unique.

We strive to get to know each of our patients personally, so that children, teens and adults will all feel comfortable in her care. Dr. Babb and Dr. Lupinetti want orthodontic treatment to be a fun experience for everyone. And believe it or not, it really can be!
