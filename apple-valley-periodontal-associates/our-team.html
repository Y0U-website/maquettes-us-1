We’re proud to be the source for excellent quality periodontal treatments and dental implants in Apple Valley, CA. Learn more about our team of experts below!

Caitlyn K. Affrunti, D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Affrunti’s philosophy is to treat and serve her patients with compassion and care and set the standard of excellence in the field of dentistry. She is well-trained in all aspects of periodontics and implantology.

Growing up in a dental family, he knew at an early age that dentistry would be his calling. His earliest experiences in dentistry started with dental assisting at his father’s practice during his high school years. He enjoyed working with his father and saw how he was able to help his patients with their oral health and overall well-being.

His experiences as an assistant further reinforced his desire to become a dentist. He was accepted to dental school in the summer of 2000. Dr. Mark graduated with a D.D.S. degree from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in 2004. After practicing for a year as a general dentist in Lake Arrowhead, he continued his studies in dentistry and completed his residency in Advanced Periodontics at Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in June of 2008.

He joined his father’s practices in Upland and San Bernardino, CA during the summer of 2008 and is now working in several locations in southern California. Dr. Mark is trained in all aspects of periodontal therapy including, LANAP laser surgery, bone regeneration, implants, and periodontal plastic surgery.

When he is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Mark enjoys spending time with his wife, Stephanie, and their four kids, Katelin, Trinity, Joshua, and Leah. Their activities together center around the outdoors and being in nature. They enjoy fishing, snowboarding, skiing, snowshoeing, camping, and hiking.

Thaer Alqadoumi, D.D.S., M.S.

Dr. Thaer Alqadoumi, DDS, MS, earned his first Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Jordan in 2012 and later completed a second DDS at Loma Linda University in 2016. He pursued advanced training in Periodontics and Implant Surgery at Loma Linda University, where he also obtained a Master of Science degree.

Outside of his professional commitments, Dr. Alqadoumi enjoys spending time with his family, playing soccer, and traveling.
