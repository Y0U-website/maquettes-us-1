Dr. Isaac Chinitz

Dr. Isaac Chinitz is a board certified Orthodontist practicing in Westchester, New York. After both dental school and orthodontics residency at Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine on Long Island, Dr. Chinitz wanted to bring his expertise to his hometown. Dr. Chinitz grew up in Wykagyl and attended New Rochelle High School. He is so excited to bring smiles back to his friends, family, and community!

Dr. Chinitz is committed to patient happiness and comfort, while always striving for the absolute best result possible. Every patient gets Dr. Chinitz’s personalized attention and expertise, and sometimes that classic dentist humor as well. He can’t help it!

Dr. Chinitz is also dedicated to a lifetime of learning and keeping up with all of the modern techniques and technological advances. Not all orthodontists are Board Certified, and it is important to Dr. Chinitz that he maintains the highest level of care associated with being Board Certified.

Additionally, Dr. Chinitz is a Clinical Assistant Professor at the Touro College of Dental Medicine, training dental students in basic orthodontic concepts, and is currently building the orthodontics residency program set to start in 2023. He is excited to teach advanced orthodontics and spread his knowledge to the next generation of orthodontists.

Dr. Chinitz is a husband and father of two very young and very cute children. He is obsessed with the Yankees, and enjoys skiing, ultimate frisbee, tennis, and anything else you can do outdoors. He especially enjoys making homemade pizza in the wood fired pizza oven he built by hand in his backyard. It’s really cool. Come by and ask him about it!
