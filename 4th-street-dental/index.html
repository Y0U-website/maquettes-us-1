A healthy smile starts with prevention. Our preventive care program is a team effort between you, your dentist, and our dental staff to protect your natural teeth and support structures—helping to prevent the onset, progression, and recurrence of dental issues.

At home, prevention begins with daily brushing, flossing, and a balanced diet. In our office, it continues with professional cleanings, dental exams, and x-rays to monitor and maintain your oral health.

We also offer preventive treatments like sealants and fluoride to give your teeth extra protection.

By prioritizing prevention, you can avoid serious and costly dental problems—and enjoy a confident, healthy smile for life.
