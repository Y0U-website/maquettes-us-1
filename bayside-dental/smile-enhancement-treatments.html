Maintaining Your New Smile

After completing your cosmetic treatment, proper care is essential to preserve your beautiful results and oral health:

Our team provides personalized care instructions and support to help your smile last for years.

  • Brush teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to keep gums and teeth healthy.
  • Schedule regular dental cleanings and checkups.
  • Avoid hard foods or habits that may damage restorations.
  • Use protective mouthguards if you grind your teeth or play sports.
