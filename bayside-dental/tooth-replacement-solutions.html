Tooth Replacements

These benefits make dental implants the preferred choice for patients seeking a lasting tooth replacement.

  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and facial sagging.
  • Durability: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Improved Comfort: Unlike removable dentures, implants do not slip or cause discomfort.
  • Better Oral Health: Implants do not require altering adjacent teeth as dental bridges do.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with sufficient jawbone density are eligible for dental implants. During your consultation, we evaluate your medical and dental history to identify any factors that might affect healing, such as smoking, diabetes, or certain medications.

Sometimes, additional procedures like bone grafting or sinus lifts may be recommended to strengthen the jawbone and prepare it for implants. Our team will explain all options thoroughly to ensure your treatment plan fits your health needs.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Maintaining dental implants is easier than many expect. Employ these habits to keep your new teeth healthy:

Following these guidelines helps protect your investment and preserves your smile.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth and implants.
  • Use antimicrobial mouthwash if recommended.
  • Attend regular dental checkups for professional cleaning and monitoring.
  • Avoid hard foods and habits that can damage implants, such as chewing ice.
