The practice

Deep Cleaning for a Fresh Start Deep cleaning is an essential service for patients with gum disease or concerning levels of plaque buildup. This thorough cleaning removes tartar and bacteria that regular brushing can miss. By getting deep cleaning promptly, you protect your gums and improve your overall health.

This service can prevent gum recession, bad breath, and tooth loss. Our hygienists use advanced techniques combined with gentle care to ensure your comfort during every visit.

General Dentistry Services for Lifelong Care Preventive dentistry keeps problems small and manageable. Our general dentistry services include regular exams, cleanings, sealants, and X-rays. We also provide restorative treatments such as fillings and crowns to repair damaged teeth and restore function.

Regular dental visits detect early signs of cavities, oral cancer, and other conditions before they become serious. Bayside Dental Care encourages scheduling twice-yearly exams to maintain optimal oral health.

Invisalign is comfortable, discreet, and suitable for a range of dental corrections. We work with you to develop a treatment plan and monitor progress to ensure the best results.

Porcelain Veneers for a Radiant Smile Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth that correct discoloration, chips, and misalignment. These custom veneers provide a natural look and durable finish that can last for years with proper care.

Our cosmetic dentistry experts design veneers to enhance your unique smile and complement your facial features.

At Bayside Dental Care, you are in expert hands with our dedicated dental professionals who are committed to quality care and your comfort.
