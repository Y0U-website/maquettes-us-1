Meet the Team

A team that knows your name.

From your first call through each visit, the Churchville Dental Care team brings skill, steadiness, and a genuine welcome to every generation of your family.

A personal approach to every visit.

Dr. Price is a general dentist who loves helping patients of all ages feel at ease and leave with a smile they are proud of. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry and completed an advanced Clinical Fellowship in Oral Medicine and Oral Pathology through the University of Maryland and Perry Point VA Medical Center.

For Dr. Price, dentistry begins with people. He takes time to get to know each patient, listen to their concerns, and build a care plan that fits their life and goals. When he is not in the office, he enjoys spending time outdoors with his wife and young son.

Familiar faces, thoughtful care.

The people who welcome you in are every bit as important as the care you receive. Meet the team members who help Churchville Dental Care feel like a comfortable place to come back to.

Whether you&#x27;re in for a routine six-month visit or you&#x27;ve been away from the dentist for a while and feel a little uneasy about it, Carlie will meet you where you are.
