A word from Dr. Price

Dr. Price is a general dentist who genuinely loves what he does — helping patients of all ages feel at ease and leave with a smile they&#x27;re proud of. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry and went on to complete an advanced Clinical Fellowship in Oral Medicine and Oral Pathology through the University of Maryland and Perry Point VA Medical Center.

For Dr. Price, dentistry has always been about people first. He takes the time to get to know each patient, listens to their concerns, and works with them to build a care plan that fits their life and goals — not just their teeth. His aim is simple: make every visit feel less like an appointment and more like a conversation with someone who&#x27;s genuinely in your corner.

When he&#x27;s not in the office, you&#x27;ll find him outdoors with his wife and young son, chasing adventures and making the kind of memories that matter most. He can&#x27;t wait to be part of your family&#x27;s dental journey for years to come.
