Here for your family. Here for the long run.

For more than 20 years, Churchville Dental Care has cared for local families from the same address at 2832 Churchville Rd. Dr. Naftali Price DDS leads a familiar office where questions are welcome and your dental history has a place to live.

A local practice with real continuity

The same doors at 2832 Churchville Rd have welcomed Churchville families for more than two decades. Patients come from Churchville, Bel Air, Aberdeen, Abingdon, Forest Hill, Havre de Grace, and throughout Harford County because they value a dental office that feels familiar over time.

Dr. Price leads one established office with a familiar team, not a rotating roster of providers. Dentistry has always been about people first for him, so we listen closely, explain what we see in plain language, and give you time to decide what feels right.

If you use dental insurance, our team can help you understand the benefits available for your visit and make the most of them. Call (410) 838-1133 with your plan information, and we will help you prepare before you arrive.
