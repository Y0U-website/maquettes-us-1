Meet Dr. Jason Bourne

Dr. Bourne is constantly involved with continuing education, attending study clubs, seminars, lectures and conferences to remain on the cutting edge of new advances in research, technology, and techniques.

He is also involved in organized dentistry, having served as the head lecturer of the Northwest Chapter of the Seattle Invisalign Study Club, and on the Executive Committee of the Snohomish County Dental Society. Dr. Bourne also frequently lectures to dentists, hygienists and assistants in dental offices around Snohomish County.

Dr. Bourne Loves The Community

Community involvement is a priority for Dr. Bourne. He is a large supporter and donor to area school districts, many local sports teams, and various civic organizations, including the Boys and Girls Clubs, and the YMCA. To acknowledge his outstanding community support, Dr. Bourne was awarded the Lake Stevens Chamber of Commerce Business of the Year.

His Favorite Ways To Stay Active

In his free time, Dr. Bourne enjoys staying active by running and participating in multi-sport races, playing soccer, and spending time outdoors by hiking and fishing during the summer months, and snowboarding during the winter. Most of all, Dr. Bourne loves spending time with his family, which includes his wife and three children.
