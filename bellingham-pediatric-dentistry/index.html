The practice

Bellingham Pediatric Dentistry is pleased to be home to K.Sawyer Negro, DDS, MSD, a pediatric dental specialist. He has worked at Bellingham Pediatric Dentistry since 2016. He is excited to continue its development into a community leader in children’s oral health.

His passion in the office focuses on early dental visits (often starting at birth, if complications of tongue/lip ties occur) which lead to much lower rates of cavities for the community. Sawyer sees all of his children in the practice as his own, and vows to have them grow up with healthy smiles and low dental fear or anxiety.
