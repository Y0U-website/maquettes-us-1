It all started when…

Clinton Family Dentistry was founded on May 1, 2005 by Dr. Daniel VanBuskirk. Dr. VanBuskirk practiced in Clinton for a decade before moving to North Dakota.

Dr. Robert Knapp was practicing in southeast Missouri and fell in love with being able to provide individualized care to his patients, something that can only come with practicing in a smaller community. In 2015, the opportunity arose for him to move closer to home and continue that personable care that he had become accustomed to by serving the Clinton community at Clinton Family Dentistry.

Dr. Knapp met his wife, Jessica, in 2020 and they welcomed their daughter, Harper, in 2022. When you come in for your appointment, you may even meet Harper or one of Dr. Knapp’s French Bulldogs, Sophia and Wednesday.

We would love for you to join our family at Clinton Family Dentistry!
