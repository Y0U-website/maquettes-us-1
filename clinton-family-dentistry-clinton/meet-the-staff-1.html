Meet the Staff Clinton Family Dentistry

Dr. Knapp graduated from the University of Central Missouri in 1998 and then joined the United States Navy. Returning to Missouri after his time in the service, he attended the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry and received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2013. In addition to dentistry, he also enjoys mountain biking the many trails in central Missouri, skiing, camping, and anything outdoors.

Miranda is a Registered Dental Hygienist who has been apart of the Clinton Family Dentistry team since September 2021. She has been in the dental field for seven years and is very passionate about her job. Miranda loves empowering her patients by means of education and “cleaning those pearly whites!” She has lived in the Clinton area for four years now and enjoys hiking and spending time with her son when she’s not in the office.

Joey has been the Office Manager for Clinton Family Dentistry since March 2025. Joey enjoys showing horses, going on cruises, taking in live music, and spending time outdoors.
