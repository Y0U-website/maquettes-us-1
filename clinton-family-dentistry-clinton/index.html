The practice

We want to help you have the smile that you’ve always wanted. Whether it’s been awhile since your last exam, you just moved to the area and want to establish care or are simply looking for a few fresh faces, we have appointments available for you.

From baby’s first dental checkup to removing and restoring missing teeth with dentures, Clinton Family Dentistry would love to be top choice for all your dental needs.

Our office uses the most up-to-date technology to offer superior care to our patients in a comfortable, relaxing environment. Our services range from dental cleanings to implants, endodontic treatments, and even clear braces.

We provide dental care for the entire family. We strive to provide the highest quality care in a fun, relaxing environment, and we are always accepting new patients.

We provide services varying from routine exams and cleanings to restorative procedures, such as fillings, bleaching, clear braces, porcelain or metal crowns, root canal treatments, extractions, dentures, and more.

We are dedicated to making your dental care affordable. Aside from self-pay options, we accept most major dental insurance carriers and are in network with several, however, we do not accept Medicare, AARP Medicare Advantage Plans, or Medicaid/MO Healthnet at this time.
