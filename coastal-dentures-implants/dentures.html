Full Dentures

Sometimes dental conditions, such as tooth decay or gum disease, can become unmanageable, leading to the decision to remove your remaining teeth on one or both jaws. In such cases, full dentures are an efficient solution to restore your function and smile.

A complete denture consists of a thin acrylic base that rests on the gum tissue and matches the color of your gums. Porcelain or acrylic replacement teeth built into the base mimic the appearance of natural teeth emerging from the gums. Recognizing that your smile is unique, our experienced team helps you select teeth that look most natural and harmonious with your smile. This customization allows you to choose whether you want a “new” smile or simply want to restore your original one.

While dentures have a long history of successful use, modern technology offers improved options to enhance their fit and appearance. In many cases, dentures can be more securely attached to your jaw using dental implants, which are placed in the jaw and connected under the base of the denture. We can discuss these options with you and help you understand how we can improve your experience with dentures.

Digital Dentures

The Digital Denture is the newest and most advanced process on the market. We use milling technology that results in a light weight yet durable denture with a highly accurate fit. Digitally enhanced design and monolithic manufacturing results in no shrinkage and high resistance to breakage or tooth pop offs. This denture is adapted to the patient’s mouth so you will experience a more comfortable fit, with fewer adjustments.

Cast Metal Partial

Cast Metal partial dentures contain a thin metal framework that is designed to rest close to your gums and allow for a smooth, non-bulky feel. The framework is supported by your natural teeth to provide a reliable, secure appliance that is easy to use. Where teeth are missing, natural-looking replacement teeth are attached to the frame, and gum-colored acrylic is used to make the appliance blend with your mouth.

Again, your mouth and your needs are unique, and our team works with skilled technicians to create a solution that is comfortable and esthetic.

Acrylic Resin Partial

Acrylic partial dentures are removable dental prosthetics that replace one or more missing teeth. They are made of an acrylic resin base with replacement teeth attached, and the base is colored to match the gums. Acrylic partial dentures are a popular choice because they are affordable, durable, and can look natural.

They can also be used as a temporary tooth replacement while a patient heals from an extraction or before dental implants are placed.

Flexible Partial

Flexible partials are designed to replace missing teeth and restore the appearance and functionality of natural teeth. Flexible partial dentures are softer and thinner than metal partials, and they don’t have metal parts, which can make them more comfortable and aesthetically pleasing.

They can also be less likely to cause irritation and can bend and fit into your mouth better. They are designed to be comfortable and functional, and should not restrict your ability to eat or speak.

Whole-mouth and snap-in dentures typically take more time in order to ensure they are made to the correct specification. Rest assured that our team will work diligently to ensure your dentures arrive as quickly as possible, so that you can start enjoying your new smile.
