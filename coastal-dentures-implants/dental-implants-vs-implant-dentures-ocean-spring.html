What Are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures combine the stability of dental implants with the affordability and convenience of removable dentures. Instead of relying solely on suction or adhesives, the denture attaches securely to implants placed in the jaw.

Patients comparing implant-supported dentures often appreciate the improved comfort, reduced slipping, and greater confidence while eating and speaking.

Patients looking for dental implants near me are often seeking a permanent solution that closely resembles natural teeth. Others may discover that implant-supported dentures provide the stability they want at a lower cost than a fixed full-arch restoration.
