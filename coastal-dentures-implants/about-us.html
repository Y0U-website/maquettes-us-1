Jeffrey Allen, DMD

Dentist at Coastal Dentures and Implants

He is deeply committed to serving his community, volunteering with several organizations, including the Rush to Brush Pediatric Dental Clinic and Mission of Mercy. He is an active member of the South Mississippi Dental Society, the Mississippi Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

When he’s not helping patients achieve healthier smiles, Dr. Allen enjoys volunteering with his church’s “Auto Ministry,” offering free car repairs to those in need, and advocating for oral health through state and local initiatives.

Dr. Allen is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care tailored to the needs of each patient. He looks forward to continuing to serve the community with the same dedication and expertise here at Coastal Dentures and Implants!

Bart Doherty

Operations Manager

Bart has spent his career in the dental laboratory business covering a multitude of disciplines with significant achievements in his field. Having served many practices specializing in Dentures and Implants throughout MS and AR, he is an expert in denture services with over 20 years’ experience fabricating high quality dentures for his dentists, and over 10 years as a certified technician.

Bart is a service specialist, and a perfectionist in product as well as practice and lab workflow. He takes particular pride in the successful operation of the practice, and ultimately each patient’s complete satisfaction. Our practice is proud to have Bart as an integral part of our practice, and we’re confident that our patients will feel the same through Barts support and dedication.

Bart lives in Gulfport with his wife and 2 children. They enjoy travel, quality time with family and community, and the unique experiences that life in coastal MS brings.
