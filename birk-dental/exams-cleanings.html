Why Routine Dental Checkups Matter

Even if you brush and floss every day, it’s still important to visit your dentist at least twice a year. Here’s why:

1. Prevent Cavities & Gum Disease

Plaque buildup can harden into tartar, which can’t be removed with brushing alone. Left untreated, it can lead to cavities and gum inflammation. Our professional cleanings remove this buildup and help keep your gums healthy.

2. Catch Problems Early

During your exam, Dr. Birk carefully checks for early signs of tooth decay, gum disease, infection, cracked fillings, and more. Early detection means simpler and less costly treatment.

3. Protect Your Overall Health

Oral health is closely linked to overall wellness. Gum disease has been associated with heart disease, diabetes, and other systemic conditions. Routine dental visits support both your oral and total health.

4. Monitor Dental Work & Smile Changes

If you’ve had fillings, crowns, implants, or other dental work in the past, we’ll make sure everything is still functioning properly. We also keep an eye out for signs of grinding, enamel erosion, or shifting teeth.

How Often Should You Schedule a Cleaning?

Most patients benefit from cleanings every six months. However, some people especially those with a history of gum disease or high cavity risk may need more frequent visits. Dr. Birk will recommend a schedule that fits your specific needs.

Exams & Cleanings – FAQ

1. How often should I get a dental exam and cleaning? Most people should visit the dentist every six months for a professional cleaning and checkup. These routine visits allow Dr. Birk and our team to catch small issues early, remove plaque buildup, and keep your smile healthy and bright between appointments.

2. What happens during a dental exam at Birk Dental? We perform a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, and mouth. This includes checking for cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer. Digital X-rays help us see below the surface, while your hygienist gently removes plaque and tartar for a fresh, polished finish.

3. Are dental cleanings painful? Cleanings shouldn’t be painful, though you may feel slight sensitivity if your gums are tender or inflamed. Our hygienists use gentle techniques and can apply a numbing gel or take breaks as needed to ensure you’re comfortable throughout your visit.

4. Why are professional cleanings important if I brush and floss daily? Even with excellent home care, plaque and tartar can accumulate in hard-to-reach areas. Professional cleanings remove these deposits before they cause decay or gum disease and help your dentist monitor your overall oral health over time.

5. Does insurance cover dental exams and cleanings? Yes, most dental insurance plans fully cover two exams and cleanings per year.
