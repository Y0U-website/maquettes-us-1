Our In-Office Implant Process

At Birk Dental, your entire dental implant journey takes place under one roof. This allows for better communication, fewer delays, and a more seamless experience. Here’s what you can expect:

We’ll begin with a comprehensive exam and digital 3D imaging to assess your jawbone and determine the best placement strategy. You’ll receive a personalized treatment plan and have all your questions answered before moving forward.

Dr. Birk will surgically place the dental implant into the jawbone using precise, guided techniques to ensure optimal positioning and healing. The area is allowed to heal for several months while the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration.

Once the implant has successfully integrated, we place an abutment (connector post) and design your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Your final restoration will look natural and feel completely secure.

Dental Implants – FAQs

1. What are dental implants? Dental implants are permanent replacements for missing teeth. Each implant consists of a titanium post placed in the jawbone, a connecting abutment, and a natural-looking crown. Together, they restore full function and appearance just like a real tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

2. Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most healthy adults with sufficient bone structure qualify for implants. During your consultation, Dr. Birk will review your X-rays, medical history, and goals. Even if you’ve been missing teeth for years, bone grafting or other treatments can often prepare your mouth for implants.

3. How long does the dental implant process take? The full process typically spans several months, allowing time for the implant to fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration. Once healed, your final crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached for a stable, long-lasting result that looks and feels completely natural.

4. Are dental implants painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after placement. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and mild soreness afterward can be managed with over-the-counter pain relief. Patients are often surprised by how comfortable and straightforward the process feels.

5. How long do dental implants last? With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last decades often a lifetime. They’re designed to be a permanent solution that supports your jawbone, prevents shifting, and provides a confident, natural-looking smile for years to come.
