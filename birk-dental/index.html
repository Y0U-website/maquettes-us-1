Whether you’ve lost a tooth due to decay, injury, or gum disease, dental implants can give you back the function and confidence of a complete smile.

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they act as artificial tooth roots. After the implant integrates with the bone, a crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to complete your restoration.

Implants are the gold standard in tooth replacement. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care.
