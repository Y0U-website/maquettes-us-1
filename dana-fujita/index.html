A permanent tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions like the original, without affecting neighboring teeth.

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone where a tooth is missing. Once it integrates with the bone, we attach a custom-made crown on top. The result is a stand-alone replacement tooth, no drilling on the teeth beside it, no removable plate to take in and out at night.

The implant itself is placed by an oral surgeon we know and trust, typically Dr. Gregory Pyle at Alpine Oral & Facial Surgery. Once the implant has healed, we take over: making the abutment, designing the crown, and seating the final restoration in our office.
