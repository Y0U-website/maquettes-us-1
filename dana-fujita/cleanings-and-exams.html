What a visit looks like

New to Sunshine? Your first visit usually runs 60–75 minutes so we can build a complete baseline. Bring a list of medications and, if available, recent X-rays from your previous dentist.

  • Medical & dental history review : quick check-in on anything new since your last visit.
  • Digital X-rays as needed : low-dose imaging to spot decay between teeth and below the gumline.
  • Professional cleaning : careful removal of plaque and tartar, followed by polishing.
  • Gum health check : periodontal measurements to catch gum disease early.
  • Comprehensive exam : tooth-by-tooth review by your dentist.
  • Oral cancer screening : visual and tactile exam of head, neck, and oral tissues.
  • Plain-language plan : what we saw, what’s optional, what (if anything) needs attention.

Why six months?

For most adults, twice-yearly hygiene visits catch decay and gum issues early, when treatment is simpler, cheaper, and far less invasive. Some patients, especially those with a history of periodontal disease, benefit from a three- or four-month interval. We’ll recommend the cadence that actually fits your mouth, not a one-size-fits-all schedule.

What we look for during the exam

  • Cavities and early decay (using both visual exam and X-rays)
  • Wear from grinding or clenching
  • Cracks, chips, and failing fillings
  • Gum recession and bone loss
  • Bite alignment and TMJ discomfort
  • Signs of oral cancer in the lips, tongue, throat, and soft tissues

Kids welcome
