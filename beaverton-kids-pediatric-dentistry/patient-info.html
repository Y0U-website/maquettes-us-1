Patient Information

Patient Information Fun First Visits! At Beaverton Kids, we make your child’s first appointment welcoming, fun and easy. Our office is designed just for kids, with a cheerful atmosphere, friendly staff and prizes to celebrate a great visit. From the moment your family walks in, we take time to help little ones feel safe, relaxed and excited to be here.

During the visit, children can explore the space, meet our team and ease into care at their own pace. Parents are always welcome to stay with their child and ask questions. Whether it is your child’s very first appointment or a move to a new office, we make every visit a positive start to lifelong dental health.

Insurance & Financing

We are committed to making dental care accessible for every family. Our office welcomes commercial insurance and OHP, and our team can help you understand and use your benefits. If you are unsure about coverage, we are happy to walk you through the details and answer questions.

We also offer flexible payment plan options to fit different budgets. Whether you are using insurance or paying out of pocket, we will work with you to make quality care simple and comfortable for your family.
