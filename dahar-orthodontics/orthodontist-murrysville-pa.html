Retainers and Long-Term Support

After treatment, retainers help hold your new smile in place. We provide several types of retainers based on your needs and comfort level. Our Vivera® retainers from Invisalign are a popular choice for strength and durability.

Wearing your retainer regularly is just as important as the treatment itself. We guide you on how to clean, store, and maintain them properly.

TMJ and Sleep Solutions

Orthodontics is not just about straight teeth. Jaw pain, headaches, or trouble sleeping may point to deeper alignment issues. Our team can design custom solutions that relieve pressure on your joints and improve function.

Custom nightguards or bite appliances can reduce:

  • Clicking or popping in the jaw
  • Facial soreness or headaches
  • Tension from teeth grinding
  • Sleep interruptions caused by poor airway alignment
