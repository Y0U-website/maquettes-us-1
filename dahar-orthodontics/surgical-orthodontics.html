What Is Surgical Orthodontics ?

Surgical orthodontics, also known as orthognathic surgery, is a specialized treatment used to correct severe jaw misalignment, improper bites, and facial asymmetry. Unlike traditional orthodontic care, which focuses on repositioning teeth, surgical orthodontic treatment addresses structural issues within the jaw that cannot be resolved with braces alone.

At Dahar Orthodontics, we take a collaborative approach, working closely with experienced oral and maxillofacial surgeons to ensure the best possible results. This combination of orthodontic expertise and surgical precision allows us to correct complex bite issues, improve facial balance, and enhance overall function.

Because jaw growth plays a crucial role in treatment success, surgical orthodontics is only recommended for adults whose jaws have fully developed—typically 16 years or older for females and 18+ for males. Before surgery, patients undergo orthodontic preparation with braces, followed by a carefully planned surgical procedure to reposition the jaw. Afterward, orthodontic treatment continues to fine-tune the bite for optimal results.

What to Expect from Orthognathic Surgery

Undergoing orthognathic surgery is a major step toward correcting jaw misalignment, improving function, and enhancing facial symmetry. At Dahar Orthodontics, we ensure patients are fully informed and supported throughout the entire process.

Pre-Surgical Preparation

Before surgery, patients wear braces to align the teeth in preparation for jaw repositioning. Regular check-ups at Dahar Orthodontics help track progress and ensure readiness for surgery.

The Surgery

Performed in a hospital setting by a maxillofacial surgeon, the procedure typically takes several hours, depending on the complexity. The surgeon carefully repositions the upper jaw, lower jaw, or both to achieve proper alignment.

Recovery & Post-Surgical Care

Patients usually require two weeks of recovery before resuming normal activities. Initial swelling and discomfort are managed with medication and a soft-food diet.

Post-Surgery Orthodontic Phase

Once the jaw heals, braces remain in place for 6-12 months to fine-tune the bite. After braces are removed, a retainer is worn to maintain results.

At Dahar Orthodontics, we provide comprehensive care every step of the way—ensuring a smooth recovery and long-term success in your surgical orthodontic treatment.
