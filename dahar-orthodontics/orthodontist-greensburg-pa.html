Comprehensive Orthodontic Services ?

As a trusted orthodontist in Greensburg, PA, we proudly offer a wide range of services to meet your needs:

  • Orthodontic Treatment – Comprehensive orthodontic care to align teeth, correct bites, and improve overall oral health.
  • Braces – Traditional metal braces remain a reliable and effective choice for patients of all ages.
  • Invisalign in Greensburg, PA – Clear aligners offer a nearly invisible option to straighten teeth, ideal for teens and adults looking for a discreet solution.
  • Self-Ligating Braces – Braces that use a special clip instead of elastic ties reduce friction and often shorten treatment time.
  • Dentofacial Orthopedics – Treatment focused on guiding facial growth and jaw development, especially useful for younger patients.
  • Surgical Orthodontics – For severe cases involving jaw misalignment, we work closely with oral surgeons to achieve optimal results..
  • TMD / TMJ Treatment – We diagnose and treat temporomandibular joint disorders to relieve jaw pain and improve function.
  • Sleep Apnea Treatment – Orthodontic solutions to help manage sleep apnea and improve breathing during sleep.
