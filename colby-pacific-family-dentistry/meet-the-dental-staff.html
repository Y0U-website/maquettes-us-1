Get to Know Your Dentists

Dr. Heather Eggenberger grew up in Marysville, WA and attended the University of Washington for her college education. She received her bachelor of science degree in Neurobiology and chose to follow in her father’s footsteps by becoming a dentist. Dr. Eggenberger was pleased to accept the offer to attend UW School of Dentistry after comparing it to many other programs. She received her doctorate in 2008, graduating at the top of her class, and received numerous awards by the faculty upon graduation.

To gain additional training beyond dental school, Dr. Eggenberger completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency with the Veterans Affairs in San Antonio, Texas. This highly respected program is ranked in the top tier of such programs and has an emphasis in implant dentistry and complex reconstruction. In addition, she was mentored by specialists in all fields of dentistry.

Dr. Eggenberger has a strong belief that learning is a life-long commitment and she remains active in continuing education and dental study clubs. She especially focuses on remaining up-to-date with the latest product recommendations, materials, and techniques in dentistry. It is important to Dr. Eggenberger that she is providing the best care possible to her patients and that they have a comfortable experience.

In her spare time, Dr. Eggenberger loves spending time with her two children who keep her busy! And together, they love to be outside camping, skiing, seeing new places or spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Apple Xiaorui Zhu is originally from China and was raised as the only child in the family. Prior to moving to the U.S., Dr. Zhu earned a bachelor’s in dentistry from Sun Yat-sen University in China, where she practiced as a dentist for a year. She then moved to the U.S. with her husband and attended Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dentistry, earning her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2021. Dr. Zhu graduated with high honors and moved to Seattle with her family upon graduation.

After settling down in the greater Seattle area, Dr. Zhu practiced in Shoreline for three years before joining our practice. Dr. Zhu is experienced in restorative dentistry, oral surgery, emergency dentistry, root canals, pediatric dentistry, Invisalign, etc.

Away from dentistry, Dr. Zhu enjoys spending time with her husband Yao, her son Terry, and their lovely dog (a Miniature Schnauzer) Yazoo as well as traveling, hiking, and cooking.
