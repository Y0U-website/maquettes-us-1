Orthodontic & Invisalign Dental…

Misaligned teeth and bite issues do more than affect your smile’s aesthetics they can contribute to uneven wear, increase the risk of tooth decay and gum disease, hinder proper chewing, and even cause jaw discomfort. If you experience any of these concerns, an orthodontic evaluation can help determine the best course of treatment.

Enhanced Appearance & Confidence: A straight, aligned smile boosts self-esteem and can positively impact social and professional interactions.

Improved Oral Hygiene: Straight teeth are easier to clean, which reduces plaque buildup and lowers the risk of cavities and gum disease.

Better Bite Function: Correcting bite issues improves chewing efficiency and helps prevent abnormal tooth wear, jaw pain, and headaches.

Speech Improvement: Properly aligned teeth and jaws can aid clearer speech and reduce issues caused by misalignment.

Long-Term Oral Health: Well-aligned teeth support healthy gums and jawbone, helping maintain your smile for life.

Invisalign: A Modern Approach to Orthodontics

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic option that uses clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten your teeth without the look or discomfort of traditional metal braces. At Colby Pacific Family Dentistry, we proudly offer Invisalign as an effective and discreet alternative for patients seeking orthodontic treatment.

Benefits of Invisalign include the following:

Nearly Invisible: Clear aligners are virtually undetectable, making Invisalign popular among adults and teens who want to keep their treatment subtle.

Comfortable & Convenient: The smooth plastic aligners cause less irritation than metal brackets and wires and can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing.

Customized Treatment: Invisalign aligners are tailored precisely to your teeth using advanced 3D imaging technology for optimal results.

Predictable Results: The treatment plan is mapped out digitally, allowing you to see your projected smile transformation before you even begin.
