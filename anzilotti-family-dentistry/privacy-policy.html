Anzilotti Family Dentistry Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how Anzilotti Family Dentistry collects, uses, and shares your personal information in connection with non-consumer (A2P) messages. We are committed to protecting your privacy and will only collect and use your information in ways that are consistent with this policy and the CTIA best practices. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to ensure your personal information is handled appropriately. Our website address is https://afdentalstl.com.

Collection of Personal Information

At Anzilotti Family Dentistry, we collect information to provide better services to all our visitors and clients. We collect information in the following ways:

Information you give us. For example, our services may require you to sign up for an account, and when you do, we’ll ask for personal information, like your name, email address, telephone number, or credit card to store with your account. We may also collect information through contact forms you submit on our website, including queries, feedback, or other messages you choose to send us.

Information we get from your use of our services. We may collect information about the services that you use and how you use them. This information could include device-specific information (such as your hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information including phone number), log information, location information, and local storage.

We collect personal information about you when you:

• Provide your phone number to us • Opt in to receive A2P messages from us

The personal information we collect may include your name, phone number, and other information that is relevant to the purpose of the A2P message.

Use of Personal Information

We use the information we collect from all our services to provide, maintain, protect, and improve them, to develop new ones, and to protect Anzilotti Family Dentistry and our users.

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

• To send you A2P messages • To measure the effectiveness of our A2P messages • To improve our A2P messages • To prevent fraud and abuse

We may use the name you provide for your Anzilotti Family Dentistry Profile across all of the services we offer that require an Anzilotti Family Dentistry account. In addition, we may replace past names associated with your Anzilotti Family Dentistry account so that you are represented consistently across all our services.

When you contact Anzilotti Family Dentistry, we keep a record of your communication to help solve any issues you might be facing. We may use your email address to inform you about our services, such as letting you know about upcoming changes or improvements.

Sharing of Personal Information

We may share your personal information with third-parties in the following circumstances:

• With service providers who help us send A2P messages • With analytics providers who help us measure the effectiveness of our A2P messages • In response to a legal request, such as a subpoena or court order • To protect our rights or property

With your consent. We will share personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Anzilotti Family Dentistry when we have your consent to do so.

For external processing. We provide personal information to our affiliates or other trusted businesses or persons to process it for us, based on our instructions and in compliance with our Privacy Policy and any other appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

For legal reasons. We will share personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Anzilotti Family Dentistry if we have a good-faith belief that access, use, preservation, or disclosure of the information is reasonably necessary to meet any applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

Opting Out of A2P Messages

You can opt out of receiving A2P messages from us at any time by replying STOP to any A2P message we send you.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You also have the right to object to the processing of your personal information and to have your personal information transferred to another organization. To exercise these rights, please contact us at afdentalstl@gmail.com

Security

We work hard to protect Anzilotti Family Dentistry and our users from unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction of information we hold. In particular:

We encrypt many of our services using SSL. We review our information collection, storage, and processing practices, including physical security measures, to guard against unauthorized access to systems.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The most current version will always be posted on our website

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at afdentalstl@gmail.com .

Thank you for choosing Anzilotti Family Dentistry. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring your experience with us is as safe and enjoyable as possible.
