Dr. Jaebeom Lee

During his academic period, he was on the dean’s list all four years and received a scholarship. Also, he was inducted in OKU (Omicron Kappa Upsilon), the highest national dental honor society.

Dr. Lee is very comprehensive with dental treatment planning and always tries to give the highest quality of dental service to patients. He also believes that committing himself to quality continuing education courses is crucial, which will add value to his work and improve his career.

He is a member of several important dental associations including the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, the Los Angeles Dental Association, and the Contemporary Dental Institute.

Aside from dentistry, Dr. Lee enjoys working out playing golf when he can. His biggest passion is cooking, for which he has become quite famous amongst family and friends recently. Dr. Lee also enjoys traveling abroad, discovering rural cities and visiting new places.
