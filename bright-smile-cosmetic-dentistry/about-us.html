About San Marino Dentistry

Upholding a Tradition of Excellence For over 40 years, San Marino Dentistry has dedicated itself to caring for the community.

Dr. Jonathan Song is honored to continue the legacy of providing ethical and compassionate dental care.

We strive for excellence in customer service, keep ourselves updated with the latest techniques for improved treatments, and routinely upgrade the level of our technology.

We wouldn’t want anything less for ourselves, or our loved ones and we know you feel the same.

Visit us today, and experience the San Marino Dentistry difference.
