Dr. Keion Yoo

After graduating from Harvest Christian Academy, Dr. Yoo earned a Bachelor of Arts in Religion with a minor in Biology from Pacific Union College. Prior to dental school, he spent four months in Mongolia serving as a missionary, where he taught English and supported local outreach programs. He later earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from Loma Linda University, during which he continued his passion for service by participating in mission work in Ensenada, Mexico, providing dental care as a student volunteer.

Dr. Yoo is deeply committed to helping people and continually advancing his clinical skills. He is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), San Gabriel Valley Dental Society (SGVDS), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the Korean American Dental Association (KADA). He regularly attends continuing education courses and participates in local study clubs to stay current with the latest advancements in dental care.

Outside of his professional life, Dr. Yoo enjoys spending time with his wife, Julie, and their dog, Chorong. Together, they love taking daily walks and enjoying the simple moments of life.
