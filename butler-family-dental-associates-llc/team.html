Dr. Steven Wong

Dr. Steven Wong graduated Magnum Cum Laude and is a James B. Angell Scholar from the University of Michigan. He received his dental degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, where he was awarded the Barnett Scholar in recognition of his scholarly accomplishments and leadership. He then studied Endodontics at the University of Pennsylvania, where he was a researcher in the Department of Endodontics and recipient of the Endodontics teaching award.

Dr. Joanne Hwang

Joanne Hwang completed her undergraduate studies at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where she earned a B.S. in Biology. She then went on to graduate from the University of Maryland, Baltimore College of Dental Surgery, where she earned both a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (D.D.S) as well as an advanced Education in General Dentistry residency certificate. Skilled in implant restoration, laser dentistry, crowns and bridges, and esthetic procedures, she is dedicated to providing patients with the highest level of dental care in a warm environment.

Dr. James Fang

Dr. Fang was born in Taiwan, immigrated to the US at age 14 and was raised in Queens, New York. He graduated from New York University and received a Bachelor of Arts in Biochemistry. He then attended Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and Mailman School of Public Health where he received his DDS and MPH degrees. He then began an internship in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at University of Medicine & Dentistry of New Jersey, Newark, NJ. and completed a residency in oral and maxillofacial surgery at the Brooklyn Hospital Center in Brooklyn New York.

Dr. Brandon Blades

Dr. Blades completed his undergraduate studies at Boston College where he earned a Bachelor of Science. He then attended UMDNJ graduate school in Biomedical Sciences and received a Master of Science in Microbiology. Dr. Blades completed his dental degree at Fairleigh Dickinson School of Dentistry in 1979. He was elected to the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society and the American Academy of Oral Medicine. After 40 years of solo practice in Teaneck, Dr. Blades has joined our practice to continue providing quality care to our patients.

Dr. Natalie Yam

Dr. Natalie Yam, DDS, is not just your ordinary periodontist; she’s a passionate advocate for vibrant smiles and lasting oral health. Graduating from NYU Dental with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, Dr. Yam’s journey in dentistry began at an early age, fueling her passion through shadowing experiences and exposure to the field even before high school. Following her dental education, she pursued a residency in Periodontics at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where she trained with and learned from renowned experts in the field.
