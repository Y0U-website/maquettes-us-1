The practice

We give you the ability to practice on real patients not just models and other students. In addition, we allow you to complete 32 hours of externship in one of our local dental offices. (optional)

Our class sizes are capped at 10 students. A class of fewer than 20 students often results in more individual attention, increased participation, and better communication between the instructor and students.

Our classes are virtual Monday through Friday (including a virtual review held each week on Friday). On Saturdays we offer hands-on learning to receive all of the quality dental education and training you need in a shorter amount of time.

Our application process is easily completed online with a required minimum deposit. Enroll today !

Our hybrid format allows students to absorb information more effectively than traditional weekend-only programs. This flexible structure supports deeper learning and retention

Unlike many other schools where instructors often have only dental backgrounds, our curriculum will be delivered by seasoned educators with dental experience. This combination ensures that our students not only receive accurate clinical information but are taught in a way that promotes long-term understanding and success

Access to multiple practice sites will create a rich internship environment, offering students a well-rounded clinical experience.

Prioritizing the quality of our students rather than just enrollment numbers will help build a strong reputation and foster organic growth through word-of-mouth referrals.
