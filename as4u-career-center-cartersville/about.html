About Our Dental Assistant Program

Dental Assistant Academy

AS4U Career Center was established in 2014 as a training academy for dental assistant students. The need for good quality training in a working clinic environment was what led to this launch, with other schools in Alpharetta not hitting the mark in this area. By offering a more accessible schedule, we allow our students the ability to maintain a regular job while going to school to prepare for their new career. By showing them the inner workings of a multi-specialty practice, we allow our students to see and plan for the areas that most interest them.

AS4U Career Center, LLC., is authorized under the nonpublic postsecondary educational institutions act of 1990. AS4U Career Center adheres to affirmative action policies designed to promote diversity and equal opportunity for all faculty and students.
