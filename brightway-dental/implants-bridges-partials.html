What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are durable, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth. They consist of a titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root, topped with a custom crown that mimics the look and function of a natural tooth. Implants are securely anchored in the jawbone for maximum stability and strength.

What are Dental Bridges?

Dental bridges are a reliable solution for replacing one or more missing teeth. This fixed restoration works by bridging the gap left behind, using the surrounding teeth as support. A dental bridge is made up of one or more artificial teeth that are securely anchored by crowns placed on adjacent natural teeth or dental implants. Bridges not only restore the appearance of your smile, but they also help improve chewing function, support facial structure, and prevent nearby teeth from shifting out of place.

What are Dental Partials?

Partial dentures, or partials, are a removable option for patients who are missing multiple teeth but still have some healthy natural teeth remaining. A partial consists of replacement teeth attached to a durable, gum-colored base that fits comfortably around your existing teeth. Partials help restore your ability to eat and speak with confidence while supporting your facial structure and preventing other teeth from shifting. We provide custom partial dentures tailored for a secure, natural-looking fit.
