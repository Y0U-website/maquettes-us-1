About Brightway Dental

Brightway Dental exists to collaboratively deliver compassionate, high-quality dental care that fosters a sense of family within our practice and the broader community. We strive to provide a positive and caring experience that transcends conventional dentistry to nurture long-term relationships with our patients and their families.

Advanced Technology

Experience the convenience of CEREC same-day crowns, where precise digital impressions and an in-house milling unit allow for a crown in just about 2 hours. Our digital radiographs, using the low-dose NOMAD handheld X-ray unit, reduce radiation by 80 % compared to traditional film X-rays. Our cone beam computed tomography (3D scan) provides detailed 3D images to evaluate teeth, nerve paths, and jaw bone, allowing for precise implant placement. Our intraoral cameras give us an accurate diagnosis and allow you to be educated about your oral health conditions.

Our Values

We strive to make every interaction seamless and positive, from appointment to follow-up. We create a welcoming, friendly, and professional atmosphere, making every appointment as comfortable as possible for the entire family.

We continually demonstrate our commitment to delivering quality dental care that goes beyond the appointment, choosing to remain focused on the long-term benefits and education for our patients.

We are partners in creating a health-conscious community focused on proactive dental care. We educate and empower families to make informed decisions about their dental health while building lasting, trusted relationships.

We deliver clear and concise information to ensure patients fully understand our recommendations. We provide a personalized dental plan and patient-centered experience.

Our team maintains a positive attitude, prioritizing solutions rather than emphasizing challenges. We work together to cultivate a supportive internal culture that extends to continually improving our patient experience.
