An Overview of Orthodontic Treatment

Straightening teeth is more than just making your smile look good. It also helps the top and bottom teeth fit together the right way when you chew or talk. Crowded or crooked teeth can trap food and germs in small spaces, which may lead to cavities. By gently moving teeth into better positions, a dentist in Hudson, NY , helps keep your mouth clean, healthy, and protected from decay.
