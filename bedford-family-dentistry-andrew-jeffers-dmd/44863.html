Meet Dr. Andrew Jeffers

Dr. Andrew Jeffers

Dentist

Dr. Andrew Jeffers has been caring for Bedford families since 2016, when he joined the practice as an associate. In 2017, he purchased Bedford Family Dentistry from Dr. James Seitz, continuing the practice s legacy of excellent care while bringing his own vision and energy to it.

Dr. Jeffers earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Saint Vincent College in Latrobe, PA, in 2006, followed by his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University School of Dentistry in Philadelphia in 2010. He is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry, and has served as President of the Central PA Dental Society since 2019.

Dr. Jeffers is deeply committed to lifelong learning and staying at the forefront of modern dental care. In June 2026, he was awarded the prestigious Fellowship designation from the Academy of General Dentistry at its convocation ceremony in Las Vegas. The AGD Fellowship is one of the most respected honors in the dental profession and is achieved by only about 6 % of general dentists nationwide. To earn this distinction, Dr. Jeffers completed over 500 hours of continuing education and passed a rigorous examination covering all aspects of general dentistry.

What drew Dr. Jeffers to dentistry was a deep desire to give back and make a positive impact on people s lives. "Helping others in a medical field felt right for me," he shares. Today, what he values most about his work is the trust and respect he shares with his team. "The level of trust and respect my team and I have for each other only strengthens our practice," he says.

Outside the office, Dr. Jeffers prioritizes staying active and believes physical activity is vital for a healthy body and mind. You ll often find him hiking, biking, or walking through his neighborhood. He also loves traveling to new places with his wife, Krissy, and exploring everything the world has to offer.

A passionate enthusiast of American muscle cars, Dr. Jeffers has a special love for all things Mustang from owning and racing them to researching their history and mechanics. And while he may come across as serious and reserved at first, those who know him quickly discover his keen sense of humor.
