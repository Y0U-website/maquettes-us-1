The Process of Getting Dental Implants

Start to Finish Convenience

Our team has taken countless hours of training specifically dedicated to placing dental implants. Our team has spent years learning how to provide custom crowns, bridges, and dentures. Initial Dental Implant Consultation

During your consultation, your dentist will thoroughly evaluate your oral health via a visual examination and a CBCT scan. They will let you know whether you are currently a candidate for dental implants or if you must undergo some preparatory treatment first. This appointment is also your opportunity to ask any questions you may have about dental implants. Our team will provide you with a cost estimate for your treatment plan and help you explore payment options.

Dental Implant Surgery

During your surgery, our in-house specialist will insert your dental implants into your jawbone at precise, pre-planned locations. We make every effort to ensure our patients are comfortable during this minimally invasive procedure. Your appointment may last a few hours. Afterward, some swelling and soreness are normal, but we will give you detailed instructions to help make your recovery as easy as possible. Most patients are able to go back to work within a day or two of the surgery.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Once your implants are in place, a remarkable process known as osseointegration will begin. The implants will actually fuse with the surrounding tissue, enabling them to act as a sturdy base of support for the prosthetic teeth that you will eventually receive. Following osseointegration, which usually takes a few months, small connector pieces, called abutments, will be attached to the tops of your implants during a second minor surgery. The abutments will help to secure your new teeth onto your implants.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Your dentist will carefully design your implant-supported crown, bridge, or denture with the goal of providing you with optimum function and aesthetics. The size, shape, and color of your new teeth will be customized for your unique mouth. After we attach your restoration to your implants, you will be able to go off and begin to enjoy the amazing benefits of your implant-supported smile!

5 Reasons Not to Worry About the Process of Getting Dental Implants

  • High Success Rate : Dental implants have a very high success rate at between 95 %- 97 %, which is higher than many other procedures.
  • The Implant Procedure is Precise and Planned: Dental Implant surgery is carefully planned ahead of time with state-of-the-art technology. We use tools like digital X-rays and CT imaging to plan the best location for each implant to maximize its chances of long-term success.
  • Biocompatible Materials : Dental implants are titanium posts that are planted in the bone of the jaw. Because titanium naturally works in harmony with bone, this allows new cells to grow and adhere to the surface of the dental implant. This unique ability allows the bone and implant to fuse together, creating a strong, durable connection.
  • There Is Very Little Discomfort Following the Procedure - With precise planning and advanced techniques, there is next to no discomfort during or after dental implant surgery. Implants are placed in healthy bone, and inflammation is rarely a problem. It should be easy to manage any soreness after the surgery with the pain medication we prescribe or over-the-counter pain relievers such as Ibuprofen or Tylenol.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

Types of Dental Implants

Two of the most prominent factors that determine how much you will spend on your dental implant treatment is the number of teeth you are missing and the type of restoration you will need. Dental implants are extremely versatile and can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire arch.

  • Single Tooth: When you’re only missing a single tooth, you will receive an implant crown that will be custom-made to blend in with the rest of your smile.
  • Multiple Teeth : If you are missing two or more consecutive teeth, our team can provide what is known as an implant-retained bridge that is secured to the top of your implants and restores your smile in its entirety.
  • All Teeth : When missing all your teeth from the top or bottom arch, the costliest solution is an implant-retained denture. When considering the number of adjustments and replacements you’ll need with regular dentures, an implant-retained denture will prove much more cost-effective in the long run.

The Stages of Dental Implant Treatment

Dental implant placement does not occur in a single appointment. There are multiple stages you must go through to receive your new smile. First, you meet with one of our expert dental professionals for an initial consultation. During this visit, they will examine your smile and offer a personalized treatment plan based on your needs. Impressions of your smile are taken and sent to a dental lab where technicians will begin to craft your restoration.

The following appointment will include the surgical placement of your dental implants. Depending on the number of missing teeth that need to be replaced, the procedure can take some time to complete. Once the implants are in place, you will spend the next 3-6 months healing at home and allowing osseointegration to take place.

When the implants are fully fused with the bone and surrounding tissue, you will return to have your metal abutment(s) put into place as well as your custom restoration, giving you the new and improved smile you desire.

What to Do Directly Following Implant Surgery

With good post-operative care and making sure you get plenty of rest, your recovery should be quick and complication-free. Some important tips that we recommend to help keep your blood clot intact include:

  • Avoid spitting
  • Don’t drink through a straw
  • Stop using tobacco products leading up to and after your surgery
  • Avoid touching the surgical site with your tongue and fingers
  • Place a cold compress on your mouth to help with any pain
  • Take any prescriptions as advised by your dentist

Common Side-Effects You Can Expect During Recovery

Knowing what signs to look out for that could signify a problem is developing is very important. Our team wants you to feel prepared and confident when you go home to heal, but we also want you to have realistic expectations as you recover. While you’ll likely feel some discomfort as well as experience bleeding and swelling, these should get better as time passes. You can help minimize these by taking prescribed medications, sleeping with your head slightly elevated, and maintaining a soft diet. If at any point you have questions, please give our team a call.

Post-Operative Health & Oral Hygiene Tips

Following your surgery, it’s crucial to keep your mouth clean and prevent the accumulation of plaque and tartar. What Can & Can’t I Eat With Dental Implants?

When opting for dental implants, you will be pleased to learn that there are very few food restrictions. One of the great advantages of implants is that they offer greater functionality when chewing and eating. However, a good general rule of thumb is to cut up harder, larger pieces of food, especially those that could cause damage to a tooth. While you may enjoy a nice, juicy apple, try cutting it up into more manageable pieces instead of biting into it. This is a good practice whether you have dental implants or not.

Will I Need a Bone Graft or Sinus Lift Before Getting Dental Implants?

Patients who do not have enough bone density can expect to undergo a bone graft or sinus lift before receiving dental implants. This decision will be made during your initial consultation. It is at this time that we will examine your oral cavity and facial structure to determine how strong your jawbone is and whether it can hold up your implants. If not, it will be necessary to add bone minerals to these weaker areas and allow it to grow before we can begin the process of implant placement.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implant

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Even though your dental implant cannot suffer from tooth decay, it’s essential that you continue to practice optimal oral hygiene habits. This will keep your gums and the other connective tissues that help hold your dental implant in place strong and healthy. Your daily dental care routine should include two minutes of brushing your teeth twice a day, daily flossing, and regular rinsing with mouthwash.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Other tooth replacement options only restore 30 % or so of your original biting force. However, dental implants can bring back 80 % or more of your chewing power! Put this to good use by enjoying a varied diet full of healthy and nutritious foods. Make sure you are getting plenty of vitamin C and calcium, as these play a key role in keeping your dental implant (and the rest of your smile) in good shape.

Break Bad Habits

Dental implants are durable and highly successful, but they are not indestructible. If you want to make the most of your dental implants, you should strongly consider breaking these bad habits:

  • Smoking or chewing tobacco, which increases your risk of dental implant failure
  • Chewing on hard, non-food objects like fingernails, ice, and pen caps
  • Using your teeth as tools to open bags, rip off price tags, or to help with other tasks
