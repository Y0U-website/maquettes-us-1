Schedule Appointment Common Orthodontic Issues We Treat Misaligned teeth and bite problems can lead to more than just cosmetic concerns. Orthodontic treatment helps improve function, comfort, and long-term dental health. At Auld Family Dentistry, we treat a variety of orthodontic issues, including:

Teeth don’t have enough space to align properly, causing crowding, overlapping, or shifting.

The upper teeth extend too far over the lower teeth, which can lead to excessive wear or jaw discomfort.

The lower teeth extend beyond the upper teeth, affecting chewing and speaking.

The upper and lower teeth don’t align correctly, leading to uneven pressure on teeth and potential enamel damage.

A noticeable gap remains when the jaws are closed, which can interfere with chewing and speech.

Gaps between teeth can create alignment problems and impact overall dental function.

Treatment Options at Auld Family Dentistry

A well-aligned smile supports better oral health, improves function, and enhances confidence. At Auld Family Dentistry, we offer a range of orthodontic treatments to fit different needs and lifestyles. TRADITIONAL METAL BRACES

Metal braces remain one of the most effective solutions for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues. Today’s metal braces are more comfortable and efficient than ever, using smaller brackets and advanced materials to move teeth gradually into the right position.

Benefits

  • Highly effective for even the most complex alignment issues
  • Durable and designed to withstand daily wear
  • Often more affordable compared to other orthodontic treatments
  • Customizable with different band colors for a personalized look

CLEAR CERAMIC BRACES

Benefits:

  • Less visible than metal braces, making them ideal for teens and adults
  • Strong and effective for treating moderate to severe misalignment
  • Provides predictable and steady movement of teeth
  • Offers a cosmetic advantage without compromising results

INVISALIGN® & CLEAR ALIGNERS

Benefits:

  • Nearly invisible, making them ideal for those wanting a discreet option
  • Removable, allowing for easier eating, brushing, and flossing
  • No brackets or wires, reducing the risk of irritation or discomfort
  • Requires fewer office visits for adjustments compared to traditional braces

Benefits:

  • Hidden behind the teeth, making them unnoticeable when speaking or smiling
  • Provides the same level of effectiveness as traditional braces
  • Custom-fit for each patient to ensure comfort and precision
  • A great choice for professionals, performers, and those who want a completely invisible orthodontic solution

RETAINERS & POST-TREATMENT CARE

Benefits:

  • Keeps teeth in their corrected position long-term
  • Helps avoid the need for additional orthodontic treatment later
  • Available in different styles, including removable and fixed retainers
  • Custom-fit for comfort and effectiveness
