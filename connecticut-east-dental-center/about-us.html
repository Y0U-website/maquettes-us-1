About Us

Our Journey to Dental Excellence

At Connecticut East Dental Center, our experienced dentists are dedicated to providing top-quality family dental care. Since 2007, we have been committed to ensuring the dental health and satisfaction of our patients in Vernon and Willimantic, CT. Our family-owned practice is known for its friendly, laid-back atmosphere, and we offer services in both English and Spanish. Whether you need cleanings, exams, x-rays, fillings, or more advanced procedures like root canals and crowns, we are here to make your visit enjoyable and comfortable.

Meet Our Skilled Team

At Connecticut East Dental Center, we pride ourselves on having a team of highly skilled and compassionate professionals. Our team consists of experienced pediatric dental care professionals who are committed to staying current with the latest advancements in dental care. In January 2025, Dr. Lwanga joined our Willimantic team, bringing expertise and dedication to patient care.
