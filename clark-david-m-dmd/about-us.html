MEET THE DOCTORS

BOARD CERTIFIED ORAL SURGEON

Dr. David M. Clark, DMD

Dr. David M. Clark received his Bachelor, Biology and Neuroscience Degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine and received specialty training in oral, facial and implant surgery during his residency at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center. In addition, he has had extensive training in the administration of general anesthesia and intravenous sedation. For almost twenty years, Dr. Clark has provided his patients with the most modern advancements in wisdom teeth removal, dental implants, TMJ surgery, oral pathology, facial trauma and orthognathic surgery.

Dr. Clark is an active member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, Pennsylvania Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and Pennsylvania Dental Association.

​ As a result of this extensive training, Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons are well-prepared to identify, diagnose and assess the source of pain and anxiety within the scope of their discipline, and to appropriately administer local anesthesia, all forms of sedation and general anesthesia. Further, they are experienced in airway management, endotracheal intubation, establishing and maintaining intravenous lines, and managing complications and emergencies that may arise during the administration of anesthesia.

Board Certified Oral Surgeon

Dr. William Wilkie, DMD

Dr. Wilkie earned his D.M.D. degree from Temple University in 1985. Following that, he served in the Navy Dental Corps from 1985 to 1989 and completed a one-year general practice residency at Bethesda Naval Hospital. He finished his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency at the University of Louisville School of Medicine and Dentistry in 1993. Dr. Wilkie is board certified by both the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and the National Dental Board of Anesthesia.

Dr. Donald Santry, DMD

Dr. Santry attended and received his BS Biology and DMD from the University of Pittsburgh, School of Dental Medicine. He did his General Anesthesia and Oral Surgery residency at the West Virginia University. Wheeling Division. Dr. Santry is an active member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, American Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons, Harrisburg Area Dental Society, Hershey Area Study Club and the Lebanon Area Dental Society.

Dr. Santry received his certification from the American Board of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons in 1979.
