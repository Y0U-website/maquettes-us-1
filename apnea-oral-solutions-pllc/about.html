Education and Early Training

Dr. Trey Kalbaugh is a dedicated and forward-thinking dental professional, originally from Southwest Missouri. Dr. Kalbaugh earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry. Following graduation, he completed a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency through New York University’s satellite program in Springfield, Missouri. This additional training provided him with advanced clinical skills and a strong foundation in comprehensive patient care.

Professional Growth and Leadership

After his residency, Dr. Kalbaugh worked as an associate dentist for 3 years. He took the next step in his professional journey by acquiring a dental practice in Nevada, Missouri, which he rebranded as Advanced Dentistry of Nevada, MO. Under his leadership, the practice transformed from a small rural office into a $1.5 million, state-of-the-art facility over the course of just four years. This remarkable growth was driven by Dr. Kalbaugh’s commitment to high-quality clinical care, adoption of cutting-edge dental technologies, and effective team leadership.

Commitment to Patient-Centered Care

Throughout his career, Dr. Kalbaugh has remained committed to ongoing learning and is especially passionate about providing comfortable, patient-centered care. In May 2025, he sold his successful practice to pursue a new entrepreneurial venture in Sleep Apnea Appliance Therapy in Austin, Texas—combining his clinical expertise with a desire to make a broader impact on health and wellness. Let’s welcome Dr. Kalbaugh to the Austin Community!
