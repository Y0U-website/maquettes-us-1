How to Recognize Sleep Apnea

Many people with sleep apnea are unaware they have it. Recognizing the signs and symptoms is the first step to getting help. Common symptoms include:

If you or a loved one in Austin experience these symptoms, it’s important to seek evaluation and care.

  • Loud, Chronic Snoring: Especially if it’s punctuated by pauses, gasping, or choking sounds.
  • Excessive Daytime Sleepiness: Feeling tired or drowsy during the day, even after a full night’s sleep.
  • Morning Headaches
  • Difficulty Concentrating or Memory Problems
  • Waking Up with a Dry Mouth or Sore Throat
  • Irritability or Mood Changes
  • Witnessed Breathing Pauses: A partner or family member may notice you stop breathing during sleep.
