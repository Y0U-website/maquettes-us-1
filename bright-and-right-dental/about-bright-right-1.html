Meet the Team

At Bright and Right Dental, we truly care about our patients. Our professionalism, dedication, and compassionate approach help patients achieve healthy smiles and greater confidence.

With over 30 years in dentistry and a degree from Parkland College, Susan loves helping patients feel comfortable while keeping their smiles healthy. She specializes in gentle, thorough cleanings, preventive care, and digital X-rays.

Outside the clinic, Susan enjoys family time with her husband, three kids, two daughters-in-law, grandson, and her dog, Bader. You’ll often find her cheering on Illini football and basketball, the St. Louis Cardinals, and the Chicago Bears!

With over 25 years of experience as a Certified Dental Assistant, Mary’s top priority is making sure every patient feels completely comfortable and at ease in the dental chair. She truly loves working alongside the doctor to help craft beautiful, confident smiles!

Outside the clinic, Mary enjoys spending quality time with her family and taking her Golden Retriever on long walks. In her free time, you’ll often find her baking, working on a good puzzle, cheering on her favorite football team, or traveling back home to Michigan for a visit.

With over 30 years in the dental field including more than two decades as a dental assistant I bring a wealth of hands-on clinical knowledge to my role in the front office. My passion is helping patients navigate the complexities of dental insurance so they can fully understand and maximize their benefits with confidence.

Outside of the office, I love spending quality time with my husband, friends, family, and my cat, Gus. I’m an avid sports fan, which means you’ll find me cheering on the Bears and Illini every fall, and rooting for the Cubs come springtime! I also look forward to attending my weekly Bible study.
