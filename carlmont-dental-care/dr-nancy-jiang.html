Frequently asked — Cosmetic & General Dentistry

What types of cosmetic dentistry do you offer?

Invisalign, porcelain veneers, professional whitening, cosmetic bonding, and crown-based smile rebuilding. Treatment selection is based on bite, enamel quality, and long-term durability goals — not aesthetics alone.

How do I know if I am a candidate for Invisalign or veneers?

A comprehensive exam with photos and digital scans is the most accurate way to evaluate candidacy. We provide a written plan with options, sequencing, and realistic expectations before any treatment begins.

How long do veneers last?

Well-planned porcelain veneers can last many years with proper maintenance. Longevity depends on bite forces, hygiene, parafunction (grinding), and follow-up care quality — not just the material itself.

Do you offer cosmetic consultations in multiple languages?

Yes. We provide cosmetic and general dentistry consultations in English and Mandarin, which helps many patients discuss treatment details more comfortably.
