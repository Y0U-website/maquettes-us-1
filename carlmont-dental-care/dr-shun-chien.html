Frequently asked — Oral Implantology

How do I know if I need a bone graft before an implant?

3D imaging (CBCT) shows actual bone volume and quality at the implant site. If anatomy is insufficient, grafting improves the biological environment for predictable long-term implant success. We explain findings clearly before any surgical decision.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

Both are fixed full-arch implant concepts. The implant count is chosen based on anatomy, bone availability, bite forces, and prosthetic design goals — not simply &#x27;more is better.&#x27; Case selection drives success.

How long does the implant process take?

Timelines vary by graft requirements, site biology, and prosthetic complexity. Some cases allow simultaneous placement; others stage healing. We provide a written timeline before treatment starts.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report less discomfort than expected. Local anesthesia and modern surgical protocols make the procedure routine. Post-op management is well-planned for comfort and predictable healing.
