Augustine Borgmeyer, DDS Dr. Augustine Borgmeyer, a true local gem, hails from South St. Louis, Missouri, where he grew up and discovered his passion for dentistry. His educational journey took him through Rockhurst University, where he graduated with honors in 2003, and the University of Missouri, Kansas City, where he earned his DDS in 2007.

As a devoted member of the dental community, Dr. Gus is actively involved in organizations such as the American Dental Association, Missouri Dental Association, and Greater Saint Louis Dental Society, alongside various study groups and clubs.

After completing dental school, he dedicated himself to providing essential dental care to underserved populations in the St. Louis area at Grace Hill Family Dentistry. In 2009, Dr. Gus continued his family’s legacy by joining his father at Borgmeyer Dental.

At home, Dr. Augustine Borgmeyer shares his life with his children, Carmen and Auggie, in the charming Compton Heights neighborhood. Dr. Gus balances his time between Borgmeyer Dental and the operation of STL Urgent Dental, and we cherish every moment we get to spend with him around the office. Join us in celebrating his unwavering commitment to our community’s dental well-being.

Kevin Schmidt, DDS Dr. Kevin Schmidt, a familiar face in Webster Groves, Missouri, began his journey toward dental excellence right in our community.

He graduated from DeSmet Jesuit High School in 2009 and continued his education at Marquette University, where he laid the groundwork for his dental career from 2009 to 2013. This led him to Marquette Dental School, where he honed his skills and deepened his commitment to patient care.

Since 2016, Kevin has been delivering exceptional dental care at Compass Health in Wentzville. In January 2020, he became an essential part of the Borgmeyer Dental team, frequently lending his expertise on Fridays.

Dr. Kevin Schmidt’s strong ties to our community and dedication to precision align perfectly with our values. As a cousin, nephew, godfather, and esteemed member of our dental family, he embodies the warmth and expertise that define our practice. Join us in celebrating his commitment to your dental well-being.
