Invisalign are the virtually invisible and hygienic alternative to braces that lets you keep smiling during your treatment. Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear, removable aligners that gradually move teeth toward the smile you’ve always wanted. Invisalign is so discreet; most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them.

Clear / Ceramic Braces

Now you have no excuse to shy away from orthodontic treatment! Clear braces are the perfect alternative to traditional metal braces. These ceramic braces are just as effective, while being much less noticeable.

Traditional Braces

Traditional metal braces are the simplest kind of braces, most commonly worn by teens. These braces have a low profile, making them more comfortable and less irritating. When using metal braces, you have the option of getting different colored elastics to suit your unique style!

iTero® Element™ Intraoral Scanner

With this scanner, we can digitally capture a detailed 3D model of your teeth and gums. You can breathe and swallow as you normally would. You can even pause during the process if you need to sneeze or want to ask a question. We want to make a good impression!
