Meet Dr. Tony

Tony W. Liu, DDS

Dr. Tony Liu was born and raised in Taiwan and has lived all around the world. He attended Northwestern University prior to completing dental school at the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry. He founded Acacia Family Dental 35 years ago and since then has provided quality care and a dental home for generations of patients.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Liu enjoys spending time with his wife and two daughters, especially traveling with them. He also enjoys playing tennis, riding his bike, and staying active.
