What is Myofunctional Therapy?

Myofunctional Therapy is designed to restore the muscles of the neck and face to create proper alignment and resting relationship of the tongue, lips, and teeth. Oral Myofunctional Therapy (OMT) combines gentle manipulation, with simple exercises that are clinically proven to have drastic results in healing all of the above listed symptoms, and more!

WHY OMT?

So many benefits...

NON-INVASIVE

Myofunctional Therapy is a low risk, conservative approach to a number of conditions that would otherwise require expensive treatments and/or medication, bulky appliances, or uncomfortable, long term treatment. With all the benefits and none of the risk of other more invasive treatment options, OMT is safe for the the whole family!

QUICK AND EASY

Instead of a long treatment plan that can take a number of months, or even years, with Myofunctional Therapy most patients see results in as few as 12 sessions. Not only do you get results quickly, but you can maintain those results easily with simple exercises you can do at home.

COST EFFECTIVE

Most treatment options are expensive and some are even denied coverage through your insurance carrier as a cosmetic or unnecessary procedure. With Myofunctional Therapy the cost can be as little as $200 a session and reimbursable through most major insurance carriers!
