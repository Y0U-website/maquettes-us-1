Our Mission

To provide high quality personalized oral health care in an environment that is comfortable and professional in a relaxing atmosphere in an effort to improve the overall health of all patients.

Hi there! I’m Kristin, the new owner and independent dental hygienist at Compassionate Dental Wellness. I’m so happy to welcome you to our practice! My passion is helping people feel comfortable and confident about their smiles while making dental visits as relaxed and positive as possible.

At Compassionate Dental, we believe in friendly, honest care and taking the time to get to know each of our patients. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning or to get back on track with your oral health, we’re here to help you every step of the way.

I can’t wait to meet you and be part of your journey to a healthier, happier smile!

Kristin is an Expanded Practice Dental Hygienist with a special permit that allows her to practice outside of the traditional dental office without the supervision of a dentist. As an Expanded Practice Dental Hygienist, she is excited about being able to provide care to those in need.
