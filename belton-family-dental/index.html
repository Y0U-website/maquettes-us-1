The practice

Dr. Littlefield is a Central Texas native. He was born in Temple and grew up in Gatesville, TX on a farm and ranch learning the value of hard work. Afterwards he attended Texas A&M University in College Station and graduated Summa Cum Laude from Texas A&M with a degree in Biomedical science.

He then graduated first in his class from the University of Texas School of Dentistry in Houston, earning multiple departmental awards for his achievements while in dental school. Dr. Littlefield practiced in private practice for 4 years before starting Belton Family Dental in 2017.

Dr. Littlefield lives in Belton, TX and enjoys the local community, participating in many activities in the area. He enjoys playing soccer and disc golf on the weekends in the local leagues and spending time with his wife Megan, daughters Ava and Clara and his dog Ellie.
