3-D Dental Imaging

3-D dental imaging is an exciting dental technology that allows your dentist to get a full view inside your mouth, allowing for better diagnosis and treatment of dental issues. With 3-D imaging, your dentist creates a 3-D rendering of the inside of your mouth and facial structures, including jaw joints and bone. This is superior to traditional 2-D x-rays because it offers your dentist a look at your entire dental structure in 3-D, providing more vivid detail and a variety of perspectives.

Your dentist will be able to zoom in on areas, rotate the view, and enjoy a variety of angles, which makes for a much more in-depth imaging experience. Creating the 3-D image is simple and easy, taking only a few minutes. 3-D imaging is beneficial when planning dental surgeries, extractions, orthodontic evaluations, or jaw treatments.
