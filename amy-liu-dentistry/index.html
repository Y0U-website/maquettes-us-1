The practice

When you visit us for your appointment, you can expect a warm welcome from our dental staff, who will be eager to help you in any way they can.

Our spacious waiting room is designed for your comfort. It is clean, cheerful, and roomy enough to accommodate your entire family. To keep your little ones entertained, we provide an additional play area for children equipped with toys and a television.

During your appointment, Dr. Onley will listen to all of your concerns and give you a thorough dental exam. Afterward, we will give you our honest opinion on your dental needs. We never recommend unnecessary dental procedures, and we always work with you to make sure you are comfortable with any potential treatments.

At Olive Dental, we want you to be comfortable with your child’s dental visits. Our dentist will be happy to listen to any concerns you have about your child’s dental care, and we will provide customized care for your child based on his or her needs.

We believe everyone deserves quality dental care, which is why we provide a variety of payment options for your dental services. We accept most dental insurance plans in addition to payment plan options, like CareCredit and Sunbit. We also offer an in-house membership program.

If you have any questions or concerns about paying for your dental treatment, please reach out to our office for assistance.

We want you to enjoy a happy, healthy smile! Reach out to Olive Dental today to schedule dental appointments for you and your family members.
