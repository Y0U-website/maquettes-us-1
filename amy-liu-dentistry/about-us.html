Meet the Team at Olive Dental Family Dentistry

At Olive Dental Family Dentistry, we strive to make your family feel well-cared for and comfortable. When you visit our Warr Acres dental office, our friendly, dedicated dental team will be eager to welcome you and get to know you.

Our dentists, Dr. Onley uses his experience and skill in dentistry to ensure that your smile stays healthy and beautiful while offering you personalized care in an understanding, family-friendly environment. We encourage you to ask our dental team questions about your dental care and treatment during your appointments. Our team members are dedicated to your care, and we are happy to discuss procedures with you.

We love caring for people of all ages, and we especially enjoy providing dental care to children. Our dental team is made up of honest, empathetic individuals who will provide the top-notch dental care your family needs to achieve excellent oral health.

We are committed to serving our community to the best of our abilities. To do so, we offer varied appointment times so that you can enjoy a convenient dental experience. We also have a Chinese-speaking dentist and a Vietnamese-speaking staff member in our office to better serve you.

Dr. Brandon Onley

Dr. Brandon Onley knew that he wanted to help people keep their smiles healthy and beautiful from a young age. His interest in dentistry began in eighth grade when his family dentist fitted him with braces, and he knew then that he would always be passionate about dentistry. Dr. Onley graduated from Oklahoma State University with a degree in Chemistry before pursuing dentistry at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where he graduated with distinction.

He lives with his wife, Caitlin, and his three children, Ellie, Graham and Hudson, in Okarche, and he is dedicated to serving the Oklahoma City area community.

Dr. Onley enjoys building relationships with patients, truly getting to know each and every individual who walks through the door. He is dedicated to providing excellent, personalized care to his patients, and he tries to make dental appointments as convenient and easy as he can. He knows going to the dentist isn t always fun, but he goes out of his way to make every visit to Olive Dental Family Dentistry as pleasant as possible.

Dr. Onley enjoys treating patients of all ages and backgrounds, and he is very good with children. He is an open-minded, friendly dentist who will work with parents to make sure their children get the right treatment for their situation. He wants to see every patient leave with a beautiful, happy smile on his or her face. He looks forward to getting to know you and your dental needs!
