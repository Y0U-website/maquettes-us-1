Braces vs. Invisalign: Which Is Better?

Neither is universally better. At Bailey-Welling both are priced the same, so the decision is purely clinical.

Braces May Be the Better Fit When…

  • The case is more complex or requires precise control
  • You’re treating a younger child where consistent wear may be a concern
  • Maximum bite correction is the priority
  • You want a treatment schedule that doesn’t depend on you

Invisalign May Be the Better Fit When…

  • You want something discreet at work, in photos, or on Zoom
  • You’re an adult or a responsible, motivated teen
  • You need to remove aligners for sports or a musical instrument
  • Flexible, removable treatment fits your lifestyle better

Common Orthodontic Problems We Fix in Farmington

If you recognize your problem below, you’re in the right place. We treat all of these at our Farmington office.

Crowded Teeth

Not enough room in the jaw for teeth to sit properly.

Overbite / Deep Bite

Upper front teeth overlap the lower teeth too much.

Underbite

Lower teeth sit in front of the upper teeth. Best addressed early.

Crossbite

Upper and lower teeth don’t align side to side.

Open Bite

Front teeth don’t meet when the jaw is closed.

Gap Teeth / Spacing

Spaces between teeth, from a single gap to general spacing.

Crooked Teeth

Teeth that are rotated, tilted, or out of position.

Overjet / Protruding Teeth

Upper front teeth that stick out horizontally. Very common, very treatable.

Airway & Breathing Issues

Jaw structure that affects breathing, sleep, or development.
