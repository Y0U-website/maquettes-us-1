Dr. Aranmolate

Dr. Aranmolate is also known as Dr. "A". She believes her gift is improving children’s lives through dentistry. Her philosophy is “every child deserves a good dentist.” She treats every child like a star. Her goal is to provide superb dental care while making your child happy. Coming to visit her will be an enjoyable and relaxing experience for your child.

Dr. Aranmolate is a board certified pediatric dentist. She completed her pediatric dental training at Rainbow Babies and Children’s Hospital/Case Western Reserve University in 2005, her dental education at The Ohio State University in 2003, and her undergraduate education from West Virginia State University in 1999 summa cum laude. Dr. Aranmolate was born and raised in Spartanburg, SC.

Dr. Aranmolate serves in United States Army Reserves. She has been in the military since 1999. Serving her country through dentistry is one of her biggest honors. She has completed a tour of duty in Iraq and several humanitarian missions.

Dr. Aranmolate and her husband, Lateef, have a daughter Gabrielle who they enjoy spending time with. Her hobbies are running, swimming, biking, reading and traveling.

Treating Children in Canton, Ohio and the surrounding areas since 2006.
