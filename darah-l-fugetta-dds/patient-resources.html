Frequently asked questions

How can I tell if I have gingivitis or periodontitis (gum disease)?

While gingivitis – gum inflammation – can be common for many, it’s important to understand the distinction between this condition and the more severe gum disease diagnosis. Inflamed, bleeding gums are common in both cases when you brush. If you have periodontitis, you’ll find that the gum pulls away from the teeth, leading to your teeth becoming loose. Whether you just have sore gums or you have loose teeth, we’d recommend booking an appointment with our team as soon as possible.

Why is it important to use dental floss?

While your toothbrush effectively removes much of the plaque from your teeth, between your teeth is a far harder-to-reach area. Floss helps you finish the job and ensures every part of your teeth has plaque removed effectively.

How can cosmetic dentistry help improve the appearance of my smile?

If you struggle with confidence due to stained teeth, missing teeth, or even a crooked smile, cosmetic dentistry can help. We offer a range of advanced dental care treatments designed to enhance your smile, from teeth whitening to crowns and bridges.

What should I do if I have bad breath?

If you have bad breath, this can signify something wrong with your mouth or teeth. If you feel you are brushing your teeth, tongue, and gums effectively, making an appointment with our team to find the problem is an excellent place to start.

How often should I brush and floss?

The American Dental Associated recommends that you should brush your teeth twice per day, morning and night, with suitable toothpaste. You should floss at least once per day in addition to brushing.

How often should I have a dental exam and cleaning?

We recommend an exam and cleaning twice a year with our highly trained hygienists and Dr. Dunaway.

What are porcelain veneers, and how can they improve my smile?

Porcelain veneers are a form of porcelain restoration that covers the front portion of your tooth. Veneers are an alternative full crown coverage, providing color and shape change.

What can I do about stained or discolored teeth?

For some, stained and discolored teeth may be down to diet and what you drink. For others, darker tooth coloration is natural. You could consider veneers or tooth whitening if you’d like to restore your teeth to a whiter, natural shade.
