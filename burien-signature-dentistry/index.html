The practice

We believe the best dental treatment is the one you never need. Our preventative approach focuses on keeping your natural teeth healthy and strong through regular care, early detection, and patient education.

Every patient who walks through our doors gets the same thing: an honest conversation about what is actually going on with their teeth. Dr. Andy Kim built this practice around a simple idea. Protect what you have, treat only what you need, and explain every step before any work begins. That means we start with a thorough exam and digital imaging to understand the full picture of your oral health…

This conservative, preventative approach is not just a philosophy on a wall. It shapes every treatment plan we create. If a tooth can be saved with a filling, we are not going to recommend a crown. If your gums are healthy, we are not going to push deep cleaning you do not need. We believe informed patients make better decisions, so we take the time to walk you through your options, your costs…

When treatment is necessary, we always recommend the most conservative solution that preserves your natural tooth structure while achieving lasting results.
