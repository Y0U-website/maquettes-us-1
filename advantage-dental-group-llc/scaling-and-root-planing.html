Scaling and root planing

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. If you are experiencing signs of gingivitis or periodontitis, Scaling and Root Planing (a professional deep cleaning) can remove plaque and tartar from below the gum line. By smoothing the roots of your teeth, we make it harder for bacteria to adhere, allowing your gums to heal and reattach. This foundational treatment is essential for preventing tooth loss and protecting your systemic health.
