A Legacy of Excellence in Comprehensive Dentistry

Since 1993, Dr. Sajjad Khan has been a cornerstone of the Connecticut dental community. A clinician who balances a "master’s touch" with a passion for modern technology, Dr. Khan’s career is defined by a lifelong pursuit of excellence. After ranking in the top ten of his graduating class at the University of Dhaka, he earned his DDS from the State University of New York at Buffalo, followed by a prestigious appointment as Chief Resident at Bronx-Lebanon Hospital.

His clinical skill was so well-regarded that he was invited to stay as an Attending Physician , mentoring the next generation of dentists a love for teaching that he still carries into his practice today.

As the founder of Advantage Dental Group and a former Dental Director for the Community Health Center of New London, Dr. Khan has spent decades shaping the local dental landscape. He is a Master of the International College of Oral Implantologists , having trained under world-renowned experts like Dr. Carl Misch. Today, he integrates this mastery with advanced LASER and CAD-CAM dentistry, offering a holistic approach that treats the patient, not just the tooth.

His Philosophy: To provide "best-in-class" dentistry that marries 30 years of experience with the most gentle, modern technology available.
