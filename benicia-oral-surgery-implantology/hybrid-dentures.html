How are hybrid dentures different from traditional dentures?

Traditional dentures are removable and usually stick to your gums using an adhesive. This can cause problems when eating and drinking your favorite foods and beverages, and while speaking. The adhesives often allow your dentures to slip around your mouth, making it hard to chew and you might notice yourself slurring your words.

Hybrid dentures are a sturdier option because they attach securely to dental implants. Since the implants act as artificial tooth roots, your hybrid dentures won’t move around in your mouth. With hybrid dentures, you can enjoy your favorite foods and don’t have to worry about them falling out. You don’t have to take them out at night, and since your implants stimulate your jawbone, you won’t experience bone loss like traditional denture wearers.

How can i take care of my Hybrid Dentures?

One of the best parts about opting for hybrid dentures is that you care for them just like you would your natural teeth. Brush twice a day, but just make sure you are using a non-abrasive toothpaste. Make sure you are brushing the acrylic gum portion of your hybrid dentures, too.

Compared to traditional dentures, hybrid dentures are much easier to care for. With traditional dentures, they need to be taken out at night and soaked in special solutions. Hybrid dentures stay permanently in your mouth.

Talk to your oral surgery provider about the best strategies for caring for your hybrid dentures.

How long do hybrid dentures last?

Unlike traditional dentures, with proper care and regular visits to your surgeon, your hybrid dentures can last indefinitely. Traditional dentures need to be replaced every five to eight years.

What is non-abrasive toothpaste?

Toothpaste has various abrasive elements inside which aid the removal of plaque. Toothpaste varies in the amount of these abrasive elements that they contain. You can know the abrasion level of a toothpaste by looking for the RDA value. Low abrasion values are between 0 and 70. The highest recommended safe limit of a toothpaste is 250. A toothpaste at this level would be considered as extremely abrasive.
