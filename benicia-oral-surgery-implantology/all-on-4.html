What is the All-on-4 dental implant procedure?

There are only three steps involved in getting All-on-4 dental implants.

STEP 1

During your first appointment, your Bay Area OSM provider will extract any remaining teeth that may get in the way of your implants.

A CBCT scan is used so the surgeon can be able to see if you have any abnormalities that could impact the dental implant procedure.

STEP 2

After waiting a few weeks to allow for healing, your surgeon will implant small titanium screws into your jawbone that will act as artificial tooth roots.

For a full set of hybrid dentures, usually, four dental implants are needed. Each case is different, and your Bay Area OSM provider will provide you with details for your personal case.

Once implants are installed, you will need to wait three to six months while they fuse with your jawbone to provide the sturdy foundation for your hybrid dentures.

STEP 3

After the implants have finished fusing, your surgeon will then attach your new hybrid dentures, which have been created and modified to perfectly fit your mouth.

What is recovery time for All-on-4 dental implants

Recovery time will vary from person to person. Typically, you can expect to fully recover between 3 to 4 weeks . However, most patients are able to return to work after a couple days. This is much quicker than traditional dental implants because the titanium screw forms an immediate bond with the jawbone. This means you don’t have to wait for the dental implant to fuse with the jaw bone.

After the surgery, you will have gauze packs secured over the surgery site and you will be required to gently bite down on them. After 30 minutes, the bleeding should stop. If it doesn’t, then you will need to continue to replace them every 30 minutes at home until the bleeding stops. At home, you will need to avoid irritating the tender areas inside your mouth. You may brush your teeth gently with a soft toothbrush. However, avoid vigorous rinsing. If you wish to rinse your mouth, you can do so with salt water.

Mix a teaspoon of salt with an 8oz glass of warm water. Don’t spit out the solution, instead, let it flow out of the mouth. You should also stop smoking, as smoking will extend the healing process.

You may return to work after two days if you work in an office or have any other type of similar work that doesn’t require physical exertion. Be careful not to lift any heavy objects, avoid bending over and standing up too quickly.

You should also get plenty of rest and eat soft foods for 3-4 days.

HOW DO I CLEAN ALL-ON-4 DENTAL IMPLANTS?

The best thing about choosing this type of dental implant is the ease of care. To clean your All-on-4 dental implants , you only need to brush your teeth twice a day, just like you do with natural teeth.

It’s recommended that you use non-abrasive toothpaste to ensure the dentures look the best and last the longest. Be sure to brush the acrylic gum part of the dentures as well.

With proper care and regular surgeon visits, All-on-4 dental implants can last indefinitely.
