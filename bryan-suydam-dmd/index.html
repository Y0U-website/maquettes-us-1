The practice

The office of Dr. Bryan Suydam is here to help you obtain the quality dental care that you need. Please contact us at 412-741-1234. Our address is 1099 Ohio River Boulevard, Sewickley, PA.

Dr. Bryan Suydam is a general dentist devoted to restoring and maintaining the oral health of his patients. He utilizes modern practices and conservative techniques that result in beautiful, long lasting smiles!

Dr. Suydam received his undergraduate degree from Temple University in 2004. He continued on to earn his dental degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine in 2008. Upon graduation, he was accepted to a general practice residency program with the University of Vermont and Fletcher Allen Health Care. There he received more advanced surgical and clinical training.
