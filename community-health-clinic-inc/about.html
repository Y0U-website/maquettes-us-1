Our Values

Teamwork: We foster an atmosphere of camaraderie and cooperation.

Quality: We strive to exceed the expectations of all our clinic users by committing to quality and value in every aspect of our work. We will invigorate our quality care with all our patients by educating them about their health and empowering them to be partners in their care.

Our Mission

The mission of the CHC is to provide quality, affordable comprehensive primary healthcare services, across the lifecycles and regardless of the ability to pay, to all residents of the Allegheny, Armstrong, Butler, and Westmoreland Counties.

A particular emphasis is placed on the low-income, underinsured, and uninsured populations.

We believe that together we are improving quality of life by providing excellent quality and affordable comprehensive primary healthcare.
