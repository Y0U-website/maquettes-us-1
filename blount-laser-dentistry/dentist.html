We Treat Our Dental Patients Like One of Our Own Family

Blount Laser Dentistry has been a family owned and operated dental practice since 2005. We are located in Maryville, TN and provide our services to the residents of Maryville and Blount County. We are committed to providing our patients with the highest quality dental care in the same warm, caring, and professional manner we would extend to members of our own family. We are an "old fashioned" family style practice, complete with family values.

Meet Our Friendly and Professional Staff

Robert V. Ingle Jr., D.D.S.

Dr. Ingle comes from a long line of dental professionals extending back several generations. He is a lifelong Maryville resident, having graduated from Maryville High School in 1997 and Maryville College in 2001. After completing dental school at The University of Tennessee, Memphis, in 2005, Dr. Ingle returned to Maryville to enter private dental practice. He enjoys spending time with his wife, daughter, son, and is an avid outdoorsman, musician, and football fan.

Julie Ingle, Registered Dental Hygienist/Office Manager

After completing her undergraduate studies at The University of Tennessee Knoxville, Julie graduated from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center with a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene in 2003. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with family camping, traveling, and cheering on the VOLS!

Michelle Henderson, Registered Dental Assistant

Michelle has been privileged to be a Dental Assistant for over 35 years. She loves to greet each patient with a smile and brighten everyone’s day. She is great at making our patients feel comfortable and each experience one of a kind! God has blessed her with a loving husband and two amazing young men and most recently a daughter-in-law. When she is not in the office, you can find her working on crafts or taking a nap!

Maddy Baldwin, Registered Dental Hygienist

With her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene, Maddy has been a registered dental hygienist since 2018. Maddy is passionate in her care for others and is always excited to meet new members of her dental family. At home, she spends her time with her loving husband and son, as well as her dog and her cows.
