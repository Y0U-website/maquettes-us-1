The practice

Our mission is to cultivate highly skilled, compassionate dental professionals who are equipped to make a meaningful impact in the field of oral health. We focus on excellence in education, innovation in research, and commitment to patient care.

Columbia Dental School offers a unique learning environment where students gain hands-on experience under the guidance of expert faculty. Our state-of-the-art facilities, comprehensive curriculum, and personalized mentorship ensure every student reaches their full potential.

We provide a variety of programs including Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), Advanced Dental Education, and continuing education courses. Our curriculum is designed to balance theoretical knowledge with practical clinical skills, preparing students for success in all areas of dentistry.

Our campus features modern dental clinics, simulation labs, and research centers equipped with the latest technology. Students have access to advanced tools and resources, enabling them to learn and practice with precision and confidence.

At Columbia Dental School, we believe that student life is an essential part of the learning experience. We offer numerous opportunities for professional growth, extracurricular activities, and community engagement, helping students build strong connections both inside and outside the classroom.

Be part of a vibrant and supportive community at Columbia Dental School. Whether you are a prospective student, current student, or dental professional seeking continuing education, we invite you to explore all that our school has to offer.
