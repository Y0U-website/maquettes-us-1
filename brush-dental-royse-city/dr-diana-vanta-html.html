Get to Know Dr. Diana Vanta

Dr. Vanta has been a practicing dentist for over a full decade now, and she never gets tired of being able to educate patients about the lifelong importance of oral health. After studying at the University of Arizona in Tucson, she completed her dental doctorate at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health.

Dr. Vanta’s family left their country during the Vietnam War and settled in Peoria, AZ, where all of them (excluding her) still live today. She has two older brothers. When she isn’t busy at the dental office, her favorite activities include doing pretty much anything outdoors with Gracie (her rescue Australian Shepard mix) and traveling. In fact, she’s hiked Machu Pichu in Peru before!
