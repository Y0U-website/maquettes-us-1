Get to Know Dr. Jessica Chen

As one of our general dentists, Dr. Chen goes above and beyond to support our patients’ oral health and help them smile brighter. With over a full decade of experience, she has plenty of clinical talents to share, and she never gets tired of meeting new faces in the treatment chair. If you’d like to learn more about her, just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Ever since she can remember, Dr. Chen has wanted to work in the healthcare field and help others improve their wellbeing. She shadowed professionals at several different types of dental offices to see what the day-to-day work was like, and in the end, dentistry won out as the one that interested her most and all these years later, she’s never regretted her decision!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Chen is originally from San Jose, CA and attended high school there. She then pursued her undergraduate degree at the University of the Pacific and decided to remain at the school for the completion of her dental doctorate.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Chen’s parents currently live in Taiwan, and she also has a sister in Las Vegas who currently works as a pediatric dentist. Here’s an interesting fact about her: she first came to America when she was only four and was able to choose her own name! Here in the DFW area, she lives with her husband and cat. During her spare time away from the dental office, she enjoys practicing yoga, trying new fitness activities, traveling on international trips with the whole family, reading science fiction books, and watching really good (and really bad) movies/TV shows, often on Netflix.
