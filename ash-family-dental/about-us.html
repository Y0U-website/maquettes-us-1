Patient-Focused Family Dentist in Canton, OH

Our experienced team is dedicated to providing comprehensive and gentle general , cosmetic , pediatric , and orthodontic dental care in a comfortable, family-friendly environment. We also aim to educate our patients on all of their available treatment and financial options.

When you step foot in our office, you can be sure we will treat you like family!

Since 1991, Ash Family Dental has served patients of Canton, OH, and beyond with a variety of affordable dental services. Our practice began in Belden Village and Canal Fulton but consolidated in 1994. Due to staff and patient growth, we moved to our current location in 2001. In 2018, we combined practices with and are happy to serve residents across Stark County from our convenient office location !

Our Staff

Dr. Jon Ash, DDS

Jon graduated from Ohio State University Dental School in 1991 and has been practicing in the Belden Village area ever since. He is currently on staff at Mercy Medical Center and works as a clinical instructor and mentor with Mercy’s General Practice Residency Program. Jon serves as a participating provider for the Bethlehem Project and Dental Options and is also the team dentist for the University of Akron.

A past-president and current board member of the Stark County Dental Society, Jon is also a CEREC In-Office Instructor. He is actively involved in a number of community activities and groups, including the Canton South Rotary.

Jon met his wife, Dorene, while attending dental school. She completed the periodontal program at the University of Kentucky in 1994 and her practice is just up the street from Ash Family Dental. The couple have two children, Maggie and Sam. In his spare time, Jon enjoys playing basketball and tennis and golfing with Dorene. He and his family attend The Chapel in Green. Jon is an avid sports fan for all three Cleveland franchises, OSU, and the University of Akron.

Dr. Brian Ash, DDS

Brian received his Bachelor’s Degree in Mechanical Engineering from Ohio State University. Following graduation, he went on to the University’s dental school where he completed rotations in oral surgery, pediatrics, geriatrics and orthodontics.

Brian is a member of the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association and the Stark County Dental Society. He is Invisalign certified and CEREC trained. Born and raised in Perrysburg, OH, Brian is the son of Dr. Jon’s oldest brother, Chuck Ash.

Brian graduated from Perrysburg High School in 2001 where he was captain of the football and track teams and also played basketball. He is married to his highschool sweetheart, Katie, and they have three children together, Mason, Brooklynn and Landon. In his free time, he enjoys golfing and playing basketball. He is also an avid Ohio State and Cleveland Cavaliers fan.

Giving Back

At Ash Family Dental, our commitment to care doesn’t end at the office doors. Every June, Dr. Jon Ash travels with his wife, Dr. Dorene Ash, and their son Sam to Ichmul, Mexico, for a week-long mission trip. There, they provide dental care to underserved communities, bringing relief, education, and compassion to those in need. These trips are a reflection of the values that guide our practice: kindness, service, and community — both locally and globally.
