About Us

At Cody Family & Cosmetic Dentistry, our dedicated team is committed to providing exceptional dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment. With a passion for helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles, our skilled professionals use the latest techniques and technology to deliver personalized treatments tailored to your needs.

KEEP SMILING, BECAUSE LIFE IS A BEAUTIFUL THING AND THERE’S SO MUCH TO SMILE ABOUT

Dr. Cameron Critchfield, DDS

Dr. Critchfield is a native of McKinney, TX, and received a Bachelor’s degree from Brigham Young University. He graduated with honors in research from the University of Texas Health Science Center and completed a General Practice Residency through the University of Utah, where he received extensive training in implant placement and restoration, extractions, root canal therapy, and hospital dentistry. When not in the office, Dr. Critchfield loves spending time with his wife and kids, woodworking, running, playing soccer, and exploring beautiful Wyoming.

Lauren Williams

Lauren graduated from Sheridan College in 2007 and practiced dental hygiene in Colorado for 10 years before returning to her hometown of Cody, Wyoming. She has worked with Dr. Critchfield since 2020. Lauren loves patient interaction and believes that proper dental hygiene can be taught at any age, knowing the value of keeping teeth to live a healthier and happier life. Outside the office, you’ll find her on the golf course with her husband and two wild boys!
