How Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Work?

Am I a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Most people who have lost all of their teeth in one or both arches are good candidates for All-on-4. If you have experienced bone loss related to your tooth loss, you might still be eligible because of the strategic angle placement used for the procedure. A patient who would normally need bone grafting for implant dentures might not require it to get All-on-4. Just remember that with any kind of tooth replacement, maintaining an excellent dental hygiene routine is necessary.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants offer many benefits. Here are some of the most prominent ones.

  • Fixed: All-on-4 is fixed to the mouth, so your replacement teeth can be brushed like natural teeth. You don’t need to remove your dentures to clean your smile.
  • Comfortable: All-on-4 feels more like natural teeth than traditional dentures. After the adjustment period, they shouldn’t feel too much different.
  • Bite Force: All-on-4 dental implants restore more chewing power than traditional dentures do. This allows patients to enjoy a full, nutritious diet without restriction.
  • Healthy Jawbone: Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 prevents jawbone loss.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Implants

All-on-4 has a lot in common with the implant dentures. Here are a few things that make them unique from each other.

  • Number of Implants: Implant dentures require up to eight dental implants for a stable smile, making them more expensive.
  • Bone Grafting: To get implant dentures, patients are more likely to need bone grafting.
  • Quicker: It takes many months to get implant dentures. All-on-4 sometimes allows patients to show off their smile on the same day.
