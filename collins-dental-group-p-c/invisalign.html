FAQs About Invisalign & Clear Aligners

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment length depends on the complexity of your case. Many patients complete Invisalign treatment in 12 to 18 months, while minor alignment issues may take less time.

Is Invisalign painful?

Most patients experience mild pressure when switching to a new set of aligners. This sensation is temporary and generally less uncomfortable than traditional braces.

How often do aligners need to be worn?

Aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours per day and removed only for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing.

Can Invisalign correct bite problems?

Invisalign can treat many bite concerns, including overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites. A comprehensive orthodontic evaluation determines the most appropriate approach.

Is Invisalign only for adults?

No. Invisalign can be suitable for children, teens, and adults depending on dental development and treatment needs.
