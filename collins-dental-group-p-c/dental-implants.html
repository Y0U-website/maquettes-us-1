Our Dental Implant Process

The dental implant procedure typically follows these steps:

If you are considering alternatives to implants, such as full or partial dentures , our team can review all your options to find the best fit for your smile and lifestyle.

  • Initial Consultation : Our dentists evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine your candidacy for implants.
  • Treatment Planning : Digital imaging and scans are used to develop a precise surgical plan. If necessary, bone grafting may be recommended to strengthen the implant site.
  • Implant Placement : A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a strong foundation for the new tooth.
  • Healing and Osseointegration : Over a few months, the implant fuses with the bone to provide lasting stability.
  • Restoration Placement : Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached. For those needing updates to existing implants, we also offer dental implant restoration .
