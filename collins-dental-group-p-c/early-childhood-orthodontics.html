Early orthodontic treatment options

Treatment recommendations depend on a child’s age, development, and specific orthodontic needs.

Growth-guidance and monitoring

Some children benefit from careful monitoring rather than immediate treatment. Regular evaluations allow orthodontic issues to be addressed at the most effective time.

Palatal expanders and interceptive care

When jaw width or bite alignment is a concern, early orthodontic treatment may include palatal expanders to guide proper jaw development and create space for erupting teeth.

Transition to braces or aligners

Early treatment does not always eliminate the need for braces or aligners later, but it can simplify future care. As children grow, orthodontic treatment may transition to braces or Invisalign and clear aligners to finalize alignment.

FAQs About Early Childhood Orthodontics

At what age should my child see an orthodontist?

Children should receive an orthodontic evaluation by age seven. Early evaluation allows orthodontic issues to be identified and treated at the most effective time.

Does early orthodontic treatment mean my child won’t need braces later?

Not always. Early treatment often reduces the complexity or length of future orthodontic care, but some children will still need braces or aligners as they grow.

Is early orthodontic treatment painful?

Early orthodontic appliances are designed to be gentle. Children may experience mild pressure as the jaw or teeth adjust, but discomfort is typically minimal and temporary.

How long does early orthodontic treatment last?

Treatment length varies depending on the child’s needs. Some treatments last several months, followed by a monitoring period as the child grows.

Is early orthodontic treatment covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Our team can review your benefits and help you understand coverage and payment options during your consultation.
