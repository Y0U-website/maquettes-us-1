FAQs About Braces

How long does braces treatment take?

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of alignment and bite issues. Many braces cases are completed in 18 to 24 months, though some may take more or less time.

Are braces painful?

Patients may experience mild soreness after braces are placed or adjusted. This discomfort is temporary and usually resolves within a few days.

Do adults benefit from braces?

Yes. Braces are effective for adults and can correct bite issues, crowding, and alignment concerns at any age.

How often are orthodontic visits needed?

Most patients with braces are seen every four to eight weeks to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments.

Are braces better than Invisalign?

Neither option is universally better. Braces may be recommended for more complex tooth movements, while Invisalign can be ideal for others. Your orthodontic consultation will determine the best option.
