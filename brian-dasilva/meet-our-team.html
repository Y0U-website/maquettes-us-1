Dental Hygienists

Our clinical staff are all highly qualified dental hygienists who are passionate about the oral health of their patients. They have specialized knowledge in preventative dental care, expertly clean teeth, examine patients for signs of oral diseases, and educate patients on proper oral hygiene. Our hygienists can’t wait to meet you!

Carol

She has enjoyed her time working for the practice for the last 40 years and she’s been a dedicated member of the team. Her favorite part of her job is building relationships with all of the wonderful patients who have come to her for cleanings over the years.
