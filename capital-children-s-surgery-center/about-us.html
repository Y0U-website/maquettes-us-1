We are thrilled to announce Dr. Heng-Ying Iwin Chu, a highly accomplished oral and maxillofacial surgeon, to our team at Capital Children’s Surgery Center. Having graduated with a Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, Dr. Chu excelled academically, ranking 7th in his class and earning a spot on the Dean’s list. He also holds a Bachelor of Arts in Biology with a minor in Applied Chemistry from New Jersey Institute of Technology, graduating Magna Cum Laude.

Dr. Chu is licensed to practice in New Jersey, Maryland, Virginia, and the District of Columbia, ensuring comprehensive coverage for our patients. In his previous role at Integrative Oral & Facial Surgery, Dr. Chu demonstrated exceptional skill and dedication to patient care.

His leadership experience, including serving as Administrative Chief Resident and Vice President of the Asian American Dental Club, showcases his ability to effectively collaborate with colleagues and contribute to a positive team environment. We are confident that Dr. Chu’s expertise and commitment will inspire trust in our referring providers and their patients, as we continue to deliver exceptional oral surgery care at our surgery center for patients on D.C. Medicaid.
