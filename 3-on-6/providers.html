Find a 3 on 6 Provider Near You!

All 3 on 6™ providers are fully trained and licensed. Tiers reflect their experience in providing 3 on 6 treatment. Preferred (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze) indicates the most cases completed, followed by Licensed Providers. Smile Systems also provides continuing education for all providers every year to keep them up to date on the latest protocols and technology, and the preferred providers have received that education.

New 3 on 6 Providers

New Providers listed here have completed the training but haven’t completed the required number of cases to be listed on the map as an independent licensed provider. These providers are authorized to perform 3 on 6 surgeries and will receive direct support from Smile Systems until they are certified to perform 3 on 6 treatment without assistance.
