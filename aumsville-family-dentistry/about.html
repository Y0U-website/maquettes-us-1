About Us

Dr. Anderson was born in Utah and has lived in many states, including Utah, Arizona, Idaho, California, Hawaii, and graduated dental school at Creighton University in Omaha Nebraska, home of the Blue Jays. He is a firm believer in the importance of educating patients on the reasons for the work we do. He is skilled in oral surgery, restorative dentistry, and endodontics. He is also licensed in minimal sedation which allows him to offer an anxiety-free dental visit.

On the weekends, Dr. Anderson enjoys spending time in the outdoors with his wife and two beautiful children. He likes working with his hands and has won several awards for his paintings. He also enjoys sculpting, metal casting, and woodworking.

While achieving his Bachelors Degree in Physics from Idaho State University, Dr. Anderson participated in their Science Outreach program where he put on Physics demonstrations for elementary-middle school children and hosted career fairs for high school students. Dr. Anderson still enjoys sharing physics demos with his own children and his nieces and nephews and loves inspiring kids to seek science-based careers.

Dr. Melanie Rawlings was born in Austin, Texas & the novel pleasure of grey, rainy days has never worn off. For her undergraduate studies, she attended the University of WI – Madison, receiving her Bachelor of Science in biochemistry with a double major in Spanish. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of MN in Minneapolis, moved out to the Pacific Northwest, met an Oregonian and decided to stay.
