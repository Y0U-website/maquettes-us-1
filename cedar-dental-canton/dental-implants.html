What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is not just a false tooth. Specifically, it is a titanium root replacement. It is placed directly into the jawbone. Therefore, it provides a strong, permanent foundation for various restorations.

Once the implant heals, we attach the visible part of the tooth. This could be a crown, bridge, or denture. As a result, the replacement looks and feels natural.

Versatile Options for Every Need

Dental implants are incredibly versatile. At Cedar Dental, we design them to restore both function and aesthetics. We offer several types of implant restorations:

  • Single-Tooth Replacement: Perfect for replacing one missing tooth without affecting neighbors.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: These provide far more stability than traditional dentures.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Ideal for filling larger gaps securely.

Are You a Candidate for Implants?

Implants are a highly effective solution. However, not everyone is an immediate candidate. According to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) , certain criteria must be met.

  • Healthy Gums: You must have generally healthy gums and teeth.
  • Bone Density: You need sufficient jawbone density to support the implant. However, if you lack bone, a grafting procedure might help.
  • Oral Hygiene: You must maintain good hygiene. This includes brushing, flossing, and visiting the dentist twice a year.
  • Smoking Habits: Ideally, you do not smoke. If you do, you must be willing to quit to ensure the implant heals properly.
