Giving Your Child a Positive Start

Your child’s smile deserves the best possible start. However, many parents feel anxious about that first dental trip. Therefore, you might be looking for a pediatric dentist in North Canton who understands these concerns.

At Cedar Dental, we prioritize comfort and compassion. In fact, we know that early experiences shape a child’s view of oral health. Consequently, our goal is to make every visit positive. Ultimately, we want to be the North Canton pediatric dentist your child actually looks forward to seeing.

Your Child’s First Visit

First, when should you schedule that initial appointment? Surprisingly, the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP) recommends a visit by age one. This might seem early. Nevertheless, it effectively establishes a dental home for your little one.

As a result, we keep this initial visit low-stress. For example, we perform a gentle exam to check oral development. Additionally, we discuss proper brushing techniques. Generally, we begin routine cleanings around age three. However, we may start sooner if you have specific concerns.

Preventing Common Dental Issues

Unfortunately, tooth decay is the most common chronic disease in children. Fortunately, it is largely preventable. Thus, we emphasize education and preventative care.

Fighting Tooth Decay

First of all, good habits start at home. Therefore, we teach both parents and children about proper hygiene. Furthermore, we discuss diet choices that affect oral health. In addition, regular checkups allow us to catch potential cavities early.

Sealants and Fluoride Treatments

Moreover, we offer extra protection for young teeth. Specifically, dental sealants are a thin coating painted on the chewing surfaces of back teeth. They act as a shield against germs and food. Similarly, we provide fluoride treatments. These strengthen enamel and help resist decay. For more details, you can read about the benefits of sealants from the American Dental Association (ADA) .

Caring for Older Children and Teens

Eventually, as your child grows, their dental needs change. Consequently, we are here to support them through every stage of development.

Pediatric Orthodontics

For instance, misaligned teeth or jaws can cause long-term issues. In fact, they can increase the risk of decay and affect speech. Therefore, we offer modern orthodontic options. This includes treatments like Invisalign. As a result, your child can maintain a healthy, confident smile during their crucial teen years.

Custom Sports Mouthguards

Finally, is your child involved in local sports? Then, protecting their teeth is vital. Unlike store-bought versions, we provide custom-fitted mouthguards. These offer far superior protection. Additionally, we can personalize them with your child’s favorite colors or team logo. This keeps safety fun and stylish.
