Meet Our Expert Dental Team in Closter Birch Dental Studio

Trained in Paris at the École Supérieure des Arts et Techniques de la Mode, Erbert Chong holds a Bachelor’s in Accessory Design and a Master’s in Communication and Marketing. His early career at LVMH Paris immersed him in the rigor and discipline of the luxury industry, where he worked in brand strategy and talent development before launching his own label in Hong Kong.

At Birch Dental Studio, he extends this discipline of savoir-faire into wellness through spatial composition, material dialogue, and atmospheric restraint. His work reconciles function and emotion, forming environments that move with balance, proportion, and quiet conviction, spaces where precision becomes human and the tactile turns contemplative.
