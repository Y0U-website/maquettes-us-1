I grew up in West Haven, Utah. My dad was a mechanic and school teacher and my mom was an xray tech. I was taught to work hard, do a good job, and be kind to others. I had wonderful neighbors who lived and reinforced these values. I am grateful to be back among those who have so greatly influenced my life.

I enjoy serving people and continually learning. I base my practice of dentistry on 3 values: integrity, quality, and service. It is important to me that you have a good experience, feel cared about, get the best care I can provide you, and that you feel like you are getting good value for your money.

My goal as your dentist is to provide you with quality dentistry based on current research and techniques that will improve your oral and overall health. I work hard to stay up to date with current advancements in dentistry both for my own fun and enjoyment and for the benefit of my patients. I strive to do my best for you and I appreciate the trust you place in me.

Outside of dentistry I enjoy spending quality time with my amazing wife and family. I am the proud, overprotective dad of 3 beautiful daughters, an awesome son, and a crazy fur baby (golden retriever). My personal interests include skiing, fishing, camping, hunting, gardening, and sports, especially football and baseball.
