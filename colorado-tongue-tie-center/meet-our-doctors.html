Our Mission

at Colorado Tongue Tie

We feel honored to offer the highest quality of care for patients with tethered oral tissues. We pride ourselves on our compassionate approach to tongue and lip tie release procedures, and we are dedicated to providing this life-changing treatment to patients of all ages. To ensure that we deliver the best care possible, we use state-of-the-art laser technology that reduces both treatment time and pain. Call us today to schedule an appointment!

Our Comfortable Office

Quality Care in a Warm Environment

At Colorado Tongue Tie, your comfort is one of our top priorities. We want you and your family to feel welcome and at ease in our office, so we offer amenities to ensure that your visit with us is a positive experience. Our refreshment center includes a coffee bar and bottled water to keep you hydrated in the waiting room. We also have tranquil breastfeeding rooms for mothers, so you can try nursing immediately after your baby’s treatment. This keeps you and your little one comfortable, and allows our team to immediately measure the effectiveness of treatment.
