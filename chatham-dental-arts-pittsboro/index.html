When patients with a missing tooth ask for the best and most realistic looking replacement option, Dr. Karen Tiwana usually recommends a dental implant. With this light surgical procedure, she can permanently attach a new (fake) tooth directly to the jawbone so that it functions like a normal tooth. If you have a missing tooth (or teeth) and are in good health overall, you are likely a strong candidate for dental implants. Since implants must be attached to a sturdy jawbone, candidates must either have strong bone or be willing to undergo an additional surgery that bolsters the jawbone.
