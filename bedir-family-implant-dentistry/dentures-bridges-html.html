DENTURES (COMPLETE DENTURES, PARTIALS AND IMPLANT)

Partial Dentures If you have some teeth remaining, partial dentures may be a good option. Although partial dentures are attached to your natural teeth with nylon or metal clasps, they are also fully removable. Full Dentures Full dentures are complete sets of artificial teeth. They’re fully removable and are primarily held into place by suction. Implant Dentures Also known as overdentures, implant dentures are full dentures that are set into place by dental implants (small screws attached to your jawbone). Implant dentures remain stable in your mouth while also being fully removable.
