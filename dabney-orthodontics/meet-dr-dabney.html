Meet Dr. Dabney

William Dabney, DDS

Meet Dr. C. William Dabney, your skilled orthodontic specialist in Midlothian, Virginia. With over three decades of experience in private practice and a passion for utilizing the latest techniques, he is committed to providing top-notch orthodontic care for his patients.

At his practice, you’ll find a state-of-the-art technology like Invisalign, 3D Predict, and Dental Monitoring, which helps ensure that you receive the most effective treatment possible.

Dr. Dabney has an impressive background, holding a Bachelor of Science from Hampden-Sydney College (Magna cum Laude), a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the Medical College of Virginia, and completing his orthodontic residency at Fairleigh Dickinson University. He is a proud member of several dental societies, including the American Association of Orthodontists, Virginia Association of Orthodontists (where he served as former president), American Dental Association, and the Virginia Dental Association. He has even been honored as a Fellow in The World Federation of Orthodontists.

In addition to his private practice, Dr. Dabney is a sought-after speaker and educator, sharing his knowledge and expertise as a guest speaker at national and international events.

When he’s not helping patients achieve their perfect smile, Dr. Dabney is giving back to his community through his involvement with Smiles Change Lives, an orthodontic nonprofit organization that provides braces to kids from low-income families. He proudly serves as the Virginia director for this amazing program.
