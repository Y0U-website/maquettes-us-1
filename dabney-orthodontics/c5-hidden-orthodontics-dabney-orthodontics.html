HOW DOES C5 WORK?

  • Ultra-Thin Wires: About the width of a hair, customized to your anatomy.
  • Strategic Placement: Bonded discreetly behind your teeth to guide movement.
  • Adjustment Visits: Return every 7–8 weeks for repositioning as your smile progresses.
  • Remote Monitoring: Use AI-powered technology for progress tracking between visits — fewer office trips, more convenience.
