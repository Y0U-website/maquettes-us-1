Dr. Roger S. Karp, DDS, MSD

Over 20 years implant experience | thousands of implants placed successfully

Dr. Roger S. Karp, DDS, MSD is a periodontist specializing in treating gum disease, performing cosmetic procedures and placing dental implants. Dr. Karp is an expert in the relationship between oral health and overall health. The Cleveland Dental Implant Center is located in Parma, Ohio and serves patients in greater metropolitan Cleveland, Ohio.

Dr. Karp is a Case Western Reserve School of Dental Medicine Alumnus

Dr. Karp received his undergraduate, dental and advanced periodontal specialty training from the Case School of Dental Medicine . Dr. Karp has a faculty appointment from the dental school, is on the staff of MetroHealth Medical Center , and is a fellow of the prestigious Midwest Implant Institute . Dr. Karp is active in several dental societies, including the International College of Dentists and is past president of the Ohio Academy of Periodontists .

Dr. Richard J. Bobulsky, DDS, MSD

Specializes in the treatment of periodontal disease, periodontal plastic surgery, regenerative therapy for both hard and soft tissues, and the placement of dental implants.

Dr. Bobulsky is a lifelong resident of Cleveland and has dedicated his career to advancing dental care in the region.

Dr. Bobulsky earned his undergraduate degree from Baldwin Wallace University before continuing his education at Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine . There, he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree , followed by a Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD) and a specialty certificate in Periodontics .

Dr. Bobulsky is a fellow of the International College of Dentists and is a past president of the Cleveland Society of Periodontists.

Dr. Bobulsky is also an active member of the American Academy of Periodontology , the American Dental Association and other professional dental organizations.

His expertise in periodontics is complemented by his commitment to ongoing professional development and his deep involvement in local dental communities.

Dr. Gazab Bhandal BDS, MSD

Dr. Bhandal is a periodontist specializing in prevention and treatment of periodontal disease, hard and soft tissue regeneration and placement of dental implants. After finishing her dental school in India, she served as a research associate at CWRU dental school with a focus on periodontal disease in post-menopausal women. After practicing in Columbus, she brings to the practice over a decade of experience delivering quality periodontal care.

Dr. Bhandal joined the reputable periodontal program at Case western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine in 2013 and received Masters of Science in dentistry and periodontics certificate in 2016.

Dr. Bhandal strongly believes in prevention of periodontal disease and focuses her effort to save the natural dentition, whenever possible. She is committed to safety and high standards of care in every procedure she offers and will take time to explain each procedure in detail.

When not working, she enjoys outdoors with her husband and daughter. She is also fond of cooking, traveling and gardening In her spare time.
