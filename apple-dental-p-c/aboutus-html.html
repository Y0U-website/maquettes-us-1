Apple Dental P.C.: General and Cosmetic Dentistry Practice

Apple Dental P.C. operates at times that are very convenient to your work and life schedules. We welcome kids. We offer late evening and weekend appointments. We accept most major dental insurance plans and can process check or credit card payments.

Apple Dental P.C. is the result of a vision to create a dental practice which will make your visit memorable and comfortable. You will see that vision being realized every time you step into our warm and inviting office environment. From the smiling front desk personnel who will address you with a smile and get you started, to the relaxed ambiance in our waiting area, we want to take out the anxiety associated with your dental visits.

We have incorporated LCD TVs in our waiting areas for your entertainment. Our operatories are bright, warm, spacious, and equipped with state-of-the-art dental equipment and technology. We have incorporated computers, digital X-Rays, and intra-oral camera technologies with our in-operatory LCD displays for patient education and to involve patients in their treatment. We are also a kid-friendly office and encourage you to ensure that your children get their dental exams and cleanings done on a routine basis.

Dr. Madhuri Punaty D.M.D. is the driving force behind Apple Dental P.C. Her vision of a better and different kind of dental practice shapes every aspect of Apple Dental P.C. Dr. Punaty is a graduate of Boston University’s Goldman School of Dental Medicine. She practiced dentistry in Massachusetts and Illinois. Her experience in private practices and public health environments has been influential in how Apple Dental P.C. operates.

Dr. Punaty believes in learning as a lifelong endeavor. She keeps current with new developments in dentistry by reading, attending educational seminars, and training with experts. She is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), Illinois State Dental Society (ISDS), and Chicago Dental Society (CDS).
