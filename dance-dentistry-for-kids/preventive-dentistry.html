This step to guiding the healthy mouth journey is typically recommended every 6-12 months. Why is this so critical? Because cavities can progress swiftly in baby teeth, and it allows us to monitor the ongoing growth and development of children effectively.

Dental Sealants

Sealants are a very thin, white filling material painted in the deep pits and grooves of teeth. Like caulking wood cracks or grouting between tile pieces, this makes teeth more smooth and easy to clean. Sealant blocks food and bacteria from marinating in deep crevices to inhibit the cavity process. Since we are following children during their transition from baby teeth to permanent teeth, we help fortify the first line of defense in new molar grooves by placing sealants as soon as they erupt into the mouth.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings. This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps.
