Dr. Andy grew up in Southern New Jersey and graduated from St. Augustine Prep before heading to upstate New York to attend Hamilton College, earning his degree in 2001.

He returned to the Philadelphia area for dental school at the University of Pennsylvania, then completed his specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at the Children’s Hospital of Pittsburgh, where he discovered his passion for making dental visits fun, comfortable, and stress-free for kids of all ages.

Dr. Danielle grew up in Marlton, New Jersey, and earned her undergraduate degree from George Washington University before returning home to attend Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry.

She went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Newark Beth Israel Hospital, where she also met her husband, Jeff, and then earned her pediatric specialty certificate at Temple University’s Episcopal Hospital.
