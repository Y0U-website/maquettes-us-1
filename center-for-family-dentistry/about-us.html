Center for Family Dentistry

Center for Family Dentistry, LLC has served Irvington Town, NJ since 1997. Our practice is dedicated to providing patients with the highest standard of dental care, tailored specifically to each patient’s needs. The staff at Center for Family Dentistry, LLC is committed to providing excellent, comprehensive dental care, including Cosmetic Crowns and Veneers, Implant and Preventive Dentistry. We are happy to hear from you, answer your questions, and schedule an appointment. Call us at (973) 375-0400.

Our doctors will first listen to your needs and will try to come up with best treatment plans that are suitable for your situation.

Our office provides various dental procedures, including dental implants, teeth whitening, esthetic veneers, crowns and bridges, root canal treatments.
