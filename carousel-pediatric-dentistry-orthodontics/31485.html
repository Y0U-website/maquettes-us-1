How Space Maintainers in Apple Valley Work

Space maintainers are small custom devices that fill empty sockets when a baby tooth is lost early. These devices are not uncomfortable for your child.

Besides space maintainers, we offer palatal expanders when the jaw is too small or narrow to accommodate adult teeth without overlapping. Your dentist will also evaluate your child for early or traditional orthodontic treatment, such as braces.

The goal of interceptive orthodontics is to limit the time your child spends in braces later or to eliminate the need for them.

3 Types of Interceptive Orthodontics

Interceptive orthodontics is an early intervention approach designed to guide proper dental and jaw development in children, usually before all their permanent teeth have erupted. The goal of interceptive orthodontics is to detect and address problems early and minimize or even eliminate the need for more extensive orthodontic treatment down the road.
