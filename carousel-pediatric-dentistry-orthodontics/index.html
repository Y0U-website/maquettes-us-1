Welcome to Carousel Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics! Drs. Katie and Kyle Nordeen are pleased to offer comprehensive pediatric dentistry and orthodontic care for children of all ages, from infants to teenagers, at their warm and welcoming Apple Valley dental office.

Dr. Katie has completed residency training in orthodontics as well as pediatric dentistry, making her one of only a few dual-trained pediatric dentists/orthodontists in Minnesota.

At Carousel Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics, we offer all the foundational care children need , including preventive care, restorative treatment such as fillings and crowns, and comprehensive orthodontics. From an exam and cleaning to braces, and everything in between, your child can get the care they need at our Apple Valley dental office.
