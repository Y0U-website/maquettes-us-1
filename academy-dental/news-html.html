Academy Dental is excited to announce its investment in a state-of-the-art sterilization center. Improving on efficiency, efficacy, and safety, AD’s new Instrument Management System (IMS) implements a steel cassette system and semi-automated workflow to provide a more safe and sterile healthcare environment. Under the experienced direction of Stacey Smith, our sterilization process offers new levels of safety and infection control for our staff and our patients.
