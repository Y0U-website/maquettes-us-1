Robert S. Clark , D.M.D.

Dr. Clark earned his Bachelor of Science degree in chemistry with honors from the University of Kentucky in 1983. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dentistry degree, with high distinction, also from UK in 1987. Dr. Clark completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at the University of Texas Southwestern/Parkland Memorial Hospital in 1991 and then practiced dentistry in the greater Atlanta, Georgia, area until 1993 when he returned to Kentucky to join the KCOMS practice.

Dr. Clark and his wife, Dr. Evelyn Clark, reside in Lexington, along with their three children. Dr. Clark enjoys playing tennis, basketball, and reading, as well as painting.
