Orthodontics

For many people, the goal of a beautiful, healthy smile is the main reason for seeking orthodontic care. If you re embarrassed or feel self-conscious about misaligned teeth, braces will significantly improve the way you feel about your appearance.

As an adult, there are a number of reasons why you may want to consider orthodontic treatment. Maybe you’ve never had braces, or you did, but neglected to wear your retainer, which allowed your teeth to change position. Or, we may have identified a bite with your problem that needs to be corrected with a combination of orthodontics and orthognathic surgery.

Children s Orthodontics

For kids, there’s an optimal window of time to begin orthodontic treatment. It’s always preferable to address certain dental problems sooner rather than later. Early treatment ensures that we can achieve the best possible results with the least amount of time and expense.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that the first evaluation occur around age seven or as soon as we detect any sign of orthodontic problems. By this age, your child’s first permanent molars and incisors should have emerged, allowing us to evaluate crowding, crossbites, and other alignment issues.

Orthodontic treatment may not be necessary at this stage, but it’s critical to monitor your child’s dental development so we can anticipate the need for braces. Dr. Whang will evaluate your child’s situation carefully and make appropriate recommendations. Invisalign for Adults and Teens

Older teens and adults will appreciate the benefits of Invisalign to address alignment problems. Invisalign is a progressive system of clear plastic aligners that gradually shift teeth into their ideal positions. The aligners are smooth and comfortable, virtually invisible when worn, and completely removable for eating, brushing and flossing.

As with any orthodontic treatment, we must evaluate your alignment issues before we can decide if Invisalign is a good choice for you. Each case is unique, but treatment for adults can usually be completed in about a year. Treatment times for teens are usually the same as with traditional orthodontics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it important to see an orthodontist for braces?

Orthodontics is a special area of dentistry that concentrates on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial abnormalities. As an orthodontist, Dr. Julie Whang has specialized training beyond dental school, including three years of additional post-graduate studies. Orthodontists don’t just straighten teeth; their training enables them to correct misaligned jaw structure and improve the overall function of your smile.

At Arcadia Dentaldontics, we understand the benefits of enjoying a perfectly aligned smile. Many people choose to have orthodontic treatment because they want to improve their appearance. But crooked teeth can lead to problems that go beyond esthetics.

If your teeth are not properly aligned, you re at risk for a variety of oral health problems. Crooked and crowded teeth make proper oral hygiene difficult, which can result in periodontal disease. Misaligned teeth also lead to problems with your dental bite, which in turn can lead to teeth grinding, excessive wearing of teeth, and even TMJ.

Dentistry has many areas of specialty, and choosing an orthodontist for your braces makes sense. Advanced skill and training allows Dr. Whang to create a plan for treatment that takes your overall oral health into consideration.
