Frequently Asked Questions

  • When should my child see an orthodontist? Around age 7 for an initial evaluation, even if no issues are obvious.
  • Do adults need orthodontic treatment too? Yes many adults choose braces or Invisalign to improve alignment and oral health.
  • What’s the difference between braces and Invisalign? Braces are fixed and highly effective for complex cases; Invisalign uses clear aligners and is more discreet.
  • How long does orthodontic treatment take? Typically 12 24 months, depending on the case.
  • Does insurance cover orthodontic treatment? Some plans partially cover treatment, especially for children. Call us and we can review your options!
  • Do braces hurt? There may be mild discomfort after adjustments, but it usually subsides within a few days.
  • What foods should be avoided with braces? Sticky, hard, or chewy foods like caramel, popcorn, and hard candy..
  • How do I brush and floss with braces? Using special tools like floss threaders and interdental brushes helps keep teeth clean.
