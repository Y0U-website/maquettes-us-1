Our Story

Hello! We are Dr. Mehdi Hasan and Dr. Nilofer Badar .

Cloverly Family Dental has been around since 2006, started by the hard work of Dr. Nilofer Badar. We have been treating patients as our family since then. We now have joined in order to expand our services and to provide you, our patients, the best possible care. We are committed to our clinic and the patients we value deeply.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Mehdi Hasan, DDS

Mehdi enjoys adventures outside, exploring, and traveling. He loves to be with his family and spends a lot of his time with his nieces and nephews. He has a passion to serve the community and he continues to work at a community clinic, and also serves as a board member at the local mosque.

Dr. Nilofer Badar, DMD

Dr. Badar is a down-to-earth, friendly, respectful, and trustworthy dentist who values patient care and patient connections in her work. Dr. Badar finished her dental school in India and then completed 2 years of rotations in the U.S. from Tufts University in Boston. She then worked as an associate general dentist at multiple locations in Rockville, MD. In 2006 she opened Clovely Family Dental as her own practice and has been serving the community since then.
