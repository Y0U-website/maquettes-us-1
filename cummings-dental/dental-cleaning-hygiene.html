What to Expect From Dental Cleaning & Hygiene

A Warm Welcome and Comprehensive Exam

From the moment you walk into our historic Hays Market location, you’ll be greeted with kindness and care. Your visit begins with a review of your dental and health history, followed by a gentle yet thorough exam. Dr. Cummings and your hygienist will assess the condition of your teeth and gums, using tools like digital X-rays or advanced scanning technology if necessary, all designed to ensure your experience is efficient, comfortable, and precise.

Thorough Yet Gentle Cleaning

Our hygienists are experienced in delivering cleanings that are both effective and comfortable. Using specialized instruments, they’ll carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup without causing discomfort. Polishing follows to eliminate surface stains, and a fluoride treatment may be applied for added protection.

Personalized Prevention Plan

After your cleaning, Dr. Cummings will sit down with you to review findings and make personalized recommendations. This might include brushing or flossing tips, information on diet, or suggestions for follow-up care if needed. Our team is passionate about education and prevention, we want you to understand your oral health and feel supported long after you leave our office.
