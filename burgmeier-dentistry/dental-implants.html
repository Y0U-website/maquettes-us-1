Burgmeier Dentistry in Mason City, IA offers dental implants, which are one of the best ways to make your smile whole again. Unlike dentures, dental implants are fixed and won’t move, fall out, or affect speech and chewing.

  • Step 1 During the consultation, we’ll review your teeth, gums, X-ray, and medical history to ensure you’re a candidate for dental implants. Patients must have enough jawbone and must also have healthy gums to proceed with the dental implant procedure.
  • Step 2 The procedure includes placement of the implant, a small titanium post, into the missing tooth’s bone socket. The jawbone fuses with the post so that it’s fixed. Next, an abutment, which is a small connector, is attached to the post where the new tooth (AKA the crown) goes.
  • Step 3 Impressions are taken of your teeth so that a tooth can be created to match your existing smile. The new tooth is custom-made to look and feel like your own natural tooth and is known as the crown. The crown is attached to the abutment.
