The Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign is nearly invisible when worn, and does not affect speech. This makes it a top choice for adults who wish to avoid wearing highly visible traditional braces. Invisalign is also removable, so you can eat what you want throughout treatment, and clean your teeth with ease. Because Invisalign moves your teeth more gently than braces, it causes less discomfort. Plus, most Invisalign treatments only take about a year!

Caring for aligners

Typically, you’ll need to wear your aligners at least 22 hours per day. They should only be removed to eat, drink beverages (except water), and clean your teeth. Wearing your retainers for less than the recommended time will prolong your treatment and delay your results. It is important to follow all instructions given by Dr. Ganatra to keep your treatment on track and to enjoy your new smile as soon as possible.

You can gently brush the aligners after use, or use an ultrasonic bath to clean the aligners when needed.
