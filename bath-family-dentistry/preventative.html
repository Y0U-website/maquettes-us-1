Preventative Care

Comprehensive exams provide a better understanding and complete picture of your oral health and hygiene. We use state-of-the-art digital technology that reduces radiation and allows us to see any problems that need addressed.

Routine cleanings for all ages through our family dentistry services will remove tartar and plaque to keep your teeth and gums clean and prevent problems. We also show you proper dental hygiene techniques so you can keep up with the maintenance of your oral health at home.

Fluoride is a mineral that is naturally found in water and many foods. While you receive a certain amount of fluoride from your diet and from using toothpaste with fluoride, we may recommend fluoride treatment to further strengthen your teeth and prevent cavities.

Preventive Restoration Resin (dental sealants) is a highly effective way of protecting the biting surface of the teeth from decay. We paint a tooth-colored acrylic onto the tooth surface to seal the deepest grooves where cavities often start.

We recommend brushing and flossing daily, along with regularly scheduled dental appointments to keep your teeth healthy and prevent cavities. Minimize the intake of sugar and starchy foods to reduce harmful bacteria growth in your mouth as well.
