Meet your Dentists & Specialists at Lane & Associates

Born in Detroit in 1953, Dr. Lane found his way to Fuquay-Varina in high school, where Southern charm and hospitality quickly made it home and where he would later put down roots…

The practice

He earned a B.S. in Chemistry from UNC-Chapel Hill in 1976, then continued to the UNC School of Dentistry, earning his DDS in 1980.

Two years later, in 1982, he opened the very first Lane & Associates practice in Fuquay-Varina a single chair built on the belief that patients always come first.

From that first office, Lane & Associates has grown to more than 50 practices across North Carolina. Dr. Lane still leads as Chairman of the Board and personally mentors the team of over 100 doctors.

In 2021, that reputation for excellence made Lane & Associates the official team dentist of the NHL’s Carolina Hurricanes.

Married to his wife Phyllis for more than 45 years, Dr. Lane is a devoted father of two daughters and grandfather of four. When he isn’t making people smile, you’ll usually find him doting on animals with a Diet Rite in hand.

As much as I would like to consider myself a Southerner, technically I am a Yankee. I was born in Detroit, Michigan in 1953 and lived with my father and grandmother for a few years before being whisked off by my mother to Englewood, California. It was here where my mother met my step-father, a North Carolina native, hence how I eventually found myself in lovely Fuquay-Varina.

My childhood had its ups and downs. We moved around a lot which led me to attend over 10 different schools. I do feel like these experiences molded me into the person I am today, being able to adjust and adapt quickly.

By my Junior year in high school, we had settled in Fuquay-Varina, North Carolina. The place I will always call home.

Les soins

Providing the best dental and orthodontic care to families and individuals in Raleigh and across all of North Carolina for more than 45 years.
