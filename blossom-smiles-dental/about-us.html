About Us

Committed to the Dental Excellence We are a team of 3 professional dentists with extensive experience with the latest trends and technologies in the dental industry.

Our Work Is Heavily Shaped by Our Core Values

Compassion

We prioritize understanding and addressing the needs of our patients and colleagues by actively listening and empathizing with their perspectives.

Respect

Respect everyone we meet and treat them like we want to be treated. Whether it is a colleague or a patient, understanding and respect matter.

Progression

We want to keep adapting to different situations, with a desire to learn and striving to be better. Learning new trends and technologies in the industry.

Courage

We are the experts in our field. Therefore, it is important to have the courage to do what is right knowing that it is for the better good of the person ahead.

Open Early, Late, and Saturday We understand that your schedule can be hectic, so we offer flexible hours to accommodate your busy life. Our office is open early in the morning, late in the evening, and even on Saturdays, ensuring that you can find a convenient time for your dental care. Whether you need a routine check-up before work, a treatment after school, or a Saturday appointment to avoid taking time off, we’re here to make dental visits easy and accessible for you. Your oral health is important, and we’re committed to providing the care you need, when you need it.

Dental Insurance Maximized

If you have dental insurance, then we are the practice for you! We’re in-network providers for most major PPO insurance plans, and we’ll even handle the entire claims process on your behalf to make sure you’re getting the most out of your benefits. Don’t worry about figuring out what is and isn’t covered…we’ll do all the hard work for you! With us, saving on your care will be the easiest part of your appointments.

In-House Discount Plan At Blossom Smiles Dental, our In-House Discount Plan offers an affordable solution for patients without dental insurance. This plan provides significant savings on a wide range of dental services, including routine checkups, cleanings, and advanced treatments. With our In-House Discount Plan, you can maintain optimal oral health without the worry of high costs. Join today and enjoy quality dental care at a fraction of the price!

No Referrals and Running Around

Finding the right dental practice for your family is hard enough, and nothing is more frustrating than that office sending you to another office because they can’t provide everything you need. That’s why we’re happy to offer such a vast array of services right here in one convenient location. Time for your checkup and cleaning? We’ve got you covered. Need a few teeth repaired? We have options. Interested in getting dental implants? We can handle everything from start to finish. With us, no matter what your smile needs, you’ll always be able to work with the same doctor and team that you trust.
