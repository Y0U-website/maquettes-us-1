The practice

Dr. Charles White has been practicing family dentistry for more than 20 years.

Our experienced staff can care for individual patients or entire families

Serving Acadiana since 2005, Dr. White provides a lifetime of beautiful healthy smiles.

Dr. White was born and raised in Lafayette. He is a graduate of Millsaps College and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery from LSU School of Dentistry in 1996. After practicing in New Orleans for nine years, he returned to the hometown he loves.

Dr. White takes pride in his practice philosophy. He schedules his day to minimize wait times and makes sure to spend adequate time with each patient. His patients appreciate that he treats them like family. If a dental issue arises that needs to be addressed, he provides a relaxed, zero pressure environment to discuss all options available.

He and his wife, Jane, love spending time with their two sons, Charlie and Lewis. In his free time, Dr. White loves to spend time outdoors, especially during hunting season. He enjoys listening to his extensive music collection and appreciates the festivals and culture of his South Louisiana home.

Jennifer graduated from hygiene school in 2005 and has been working with Dr. White ever since. Her phenomenal skills and upbeat personality are just some of the qualities that ensure patients feel at ease while they are here getting their teeth cleaned. She loves working with patients of all ages and it shows each and every day

Renee’ Young joins us full time as a hygienist having practiced since 2008. It is obvious that Renee’ loves her chosen profession. She is a people person and she loves to educate patients on what their dental needs are. Renee’ is always eager to answer any questions patients may have and loves getting to know her patients. She loves to travel and spend time with her family and friends.
