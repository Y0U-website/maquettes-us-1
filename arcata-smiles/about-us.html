Meet Dr. Choi

Dr. Choi was born in 1969. He grew up in Claremont California. He graduated from Claremont High School in 1987. He attended UCLA in the summer of 1987. He received his bachelor of science degree in Neurobiology in 1991. He then attended UOP dental school of San Franciso that summer and received his DDS degree in 1994.

In 2004 I met my wife Tenille, and in 2007 we moved to humbolt county and married each other in October 2007. A year later our son Remington was born. We plan to stay here and raise our Remy in this beautiful natural redwood community.
