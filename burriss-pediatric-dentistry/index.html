The practice

Dr. William B. Burriss is a certified pediatric dentist in Collierville, Tennessee, specializing in dental treatment for children and teens.

He also provides occlusal treatment (braces) for his patients.

Dr. Bill attended The Ohio State University for his undergraduate education. He obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in 1986, and received his masters from UTHSC in 1988. He opened his private practice in Collierville, Tennessee in 1988.

Dr. Bill is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Orthodontic Society and Gnathos , an orthodontic education organization.

He is an active member of the Collierville United Methodist Church where he teaches 6th grade confirmation, he loves traveling and playing golf when he can and enjoys spending time with his granddaughter Mclaine born in early 2018.
