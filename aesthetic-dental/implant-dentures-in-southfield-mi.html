In some cases, food can get trapped underneath your snap-on dentures. While implants help prevent this from happening, it’s always important to brush your dentures and rinse them off after eating.

Food left under the prosthetic can trap bacteria, which may ultimately lead to decay or gum disease . Maintaining a consistent oral hygiene routine is still essential even if you’ve lost all your teeth, since gum health is the foundation for secure implants.

Remember to brush, floss, and attend regular dental checkups to keep your dentures in good condition and your gums healthy.

The cost of implant dentures is influenced by various factors that collectively determine the overall expense. These key factors include the number of implants required for your specific case and the type of denture you choose.

Generally, the more implants needed, the higher the cost. However, mini implants are significantly more affordable than traditional implants, often costing half as much.

The type of dentures you choose also affects the price of your implant dentures. Each type has a unique price point, reflecting varying levels of convenience and stability. Additional procedures and preparatory treatments, such as extractions , can also affect the overall cost.
