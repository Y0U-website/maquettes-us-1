  • Minimally invasive procedure: Placing smaller implants isn’t just faster—it’s also less invasive. Rather than requiring incisions and stitches, we use more gentle techniques, prioritizing patient comfort throughout the process.
  • Faster results: Because mini implants cause less tissue disruption during placement, they stabilize in the bone more quickly. This shortens your overall treatment time, allowing us to restore your tooth in just a few weeks, rather than several months.

The Mini Implant Process

The process of getting mini implants is simple and streamlined from start to finish.

Free Consultation

Your treatment journey begins with a free, no-obligation consultation.

During this visit, we’ll discuss your dental concerns and review your medical history. We’ll also perform a dental exam and take advanced CBCT X-rays. These precise 3D images allow us to evaluate the condition of your teeth, gums, and jawbone.

The information we gather from this visit helps us assess your eligibility for mini dental implants and determine the best solution for your smile. After discussing your preferences and budget, we will then tailor a personalized treatment plan to address your needs.

Implant Placement

To place your mini dental implants, we will first administer a local anesthetic. This numbs the treatment area, making you feel more comfortable throughout your procedure. At our office, we prioritize patient comfort, so you can rest assured that we’ll make the implant process as stress-free as possible. If you need help relaxing, we also offer oral conscious sedation or nitrous oxide (laughing gas).

Once the area is fully numb, we carefully create a small guide hole through the gum and into the jawbone. Using this as a path, we gently place the mini implant into the bone. The mini implant process is incredibly simple and efficient, allowing us to place your implants in just one appointment.

After securing the mini implants in your jaw, we will mount the temporary or final restoration, giving you a brand-new smile! If you’re wearing a temporary, we’ll replace it with your fully functional, final restoration in just a few weeks.

Financing Options for Mini Implants

While mini dental implants are more affordable than traditional implants, we recognize that costs remain a concern for many patients. At Aesthetic Dental, we’re committed to making quality oral care accessible to everyone .

Frequently Asked Questions

Tooth replacement with mini dental implants will restore your bite force, but it’s important to watch what you eat in the first few days following your procedure.

As your bone heals around the implants, it’s best to avoid hard foods or anything with small seeds. At Aesthetic Dental, we provide implant patients with a detailed aftercare and recipe guide filled with helpful tips and safe meal ideas. Following these steps will support your healing and help you get back to enjoying all your favorite foods sooner!

Even after your implants have fully healed, proper care ensures they last as long as you need them to. While the implants themselves won’t decay, good oral hygiene keeps your gums, jawbone, and surrounding teeth healthy—an important part of long-term implant success.

Daily brushing, flossing, rinsing, and regular dental checkups at Aesthetic Dental will help keep your smile strong and healthy.

In many cases, mini dental implants let you skip the bone graft altogether! Their slim design allows them to stay secure even in areas with minimal bone. This means fewer procedures, lower costs, and a faster path to restoring your smile.

At Aesthetic Dental, we use a local anesthetic to keep you comfortable throughout the procedure. And while we place mini implants using a minimally invasive technique, we understand that some patients need extra help relaxing for their treatment. That’s why we also offer nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation. These options help ease anxiety and create a calm, stress-free experience.

Because mini implant placement is much gentler than conventional implant placement, you can expect a faster and more comfortable healing experience, too.

However, if you want to heal like your younger self, we offer L-PRF to accelerate recovery. By using the natural healing properties in your own blood, we create a membrane that supports healing at the treatment site. After implant placement, L-PRF treatment can help you recover more quickly and experience less discomfort.

If you feel a little sore after your mini implant procedure, don’t worry. Most patients find that over-the-counter pain relief works just fine.
