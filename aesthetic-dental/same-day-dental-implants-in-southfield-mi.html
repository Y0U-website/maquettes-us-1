Same-Day Dental Implants

  • Convenience: Same-day dental implants are known for their convenience since they require only one appointment for placement. It’s an efficient solution that eliminates the need for extended waiting periods and complex procedures.
  • Quick healing: The minimally invasive nature of the same-day implant procedure leads to quicker healing. Our gentle technique minimizes post-operative discomfort and enables a faster return to regular activities.
  • Long-term jaw health: When a tooth is lost, the jawbone loses the vital stimulation once provided by the tooth’s root. This stimulation is responsible for maintaining the health of your jawbone. Without it, the bone deteriorates, losing mass and density. However, mini dental implants replace your missing tooth roots, preventing bone loss. The implant’s integration with the bone is crucial for the long-term stability of your dental restoration and overall oral health.
  • Effectiveness: Mini implants demonstrate high success rates in supporting dental prosthetics. Their stability and durability ensure a reliable solution for tooth replacement, with a long track record of effectiveness.
  • Affordability: Mini dental implants are a cost-effective alternative to traditional implants. Their reduced size and simpler placement procedure lower overall treatment costs, making them an accessible choice for patients seeking reliable tooth replacement without breaking the bank.

The Same-Day Procedure

At the start of your appointment, we will administer a local anesthetic to numb the areas around your gums and jaw. If a tooth cannot be saved, we will first extract the damaged tooth.

To place your mini implant, we create a small pilot hole in your gums and carefully position the metal post in your jaw. Thanks to the straightforward design of the implant and the efficiency of the minimally invasive placement technique, the procedure typically takes less than an hour to complete.

Once the implant is secure, we’ll attach a temporary or final restoration on top to restore your smile!

How Long Do Same-Day Implants Last?

While the mini implant placement itself takes only a short time, your implants will become a permanent fixture in your mouth. With proper care, same-day mini dental implants can last a lifetime.

In the initial days after your procedure, you should steer clear of sticky or hard foods, as they may compromise the implant’s stability. At Aesthetic Dental, we simplify the healing process by providing you with an aftercare recipe book, full of alternative meals to enjoy during your recovery. Once your mouth has healed, you can enjoy your favorite meals again.

For the long-term maintenance of your mini dental implants, developing an excellent oral hygiene routine is crucial. While the single-piece design of a mini implant reduces the risk of bacterial buildup between implant parts, you still need to care for your gums and any remaining teeth. Good oral hygiene is the key to keeping your implants healthy and protecting the overall health of your mouth.

Your routine should include brushing your teeth twice a day with a soft-bristle toothbrush, flossing daily, and rinsing with an antibacterial mouthwash.

Additionally, if you tend to grind your teeth at night, we recommend using a nightguard ! This protective measure helps prevent damage to your implants and ensures their continued durability and function.
