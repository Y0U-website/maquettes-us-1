Having the proper bone quality and dimensions is critical. Think of it this way: placing an implant in your bone is a little like hanging a picture on a wall. While you can hang a frame without finding a stud, it will eventually fall without a solid anchor. The same applies to implants: you need a strong, healthy jawbone to support the implant and help ensure successful tooth replacement.

Unfortunately, many patients lack the jawbone structure that conventional implants require. However, we can often use additional procedures, such as bone grafts, to rebuild the bone and help patients restore their smiles.

Bone Graft

If you’ve lost bone due to missing teeth, bone grafting surgery can help restore it. We use your own bone, donor tissue, or a safe synthetic material. We place the graft material onto or into the area needing repair. The graft gradually fuses with your jaw and acts as a scaffold for new bone growth.

Because this is an oral surgery , your body will need time to recover before implant placement. To speed up healing, we may recommend L-PRF therapy. This treatment uses a small sample of your blood to create a special healing membrane that helps reduce post-operative discomfort and support bone growth.

After a healing period of a few months, we can proceed with your implants.

Implant Placement

To place dental implants, we start with a local anesthetic to keep you comfortable. Then, we create an incision in your gums to reach the jaw underneath. Using a series of drills, we carefully make a hole in your bone, large enough to fit the implant body. Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implant screw will integrate with your jawbone, forming a strong connection.

Once the implant body is stable, we’ll reopen your gums to place the abutment. This connector piece attaches a replacement tooth to the implant body. We may place a temporary restoration to maintain the aesthetics of your smile as your mouth heals.

While you’re healing, you’ll need to follow some special guidelines. At Aesthetic Dental, we take the guesswork out of the healing process, providing you with a patient care and recipe book.

Then, once your final custom restoration is ready and your mouth has fully healed, we’ll complete your tooth replacement journey.

Explore the Mini Dental Implant Difference

While conventional dental implants are a reliable solution, they may not be the best option for every patient.

If you’re looking to skip the long healing periods and additional procedures, you may be a good candidate for mini dental implants !

These titanium posts are half the size of a standard implant, measuring less than 3 mm wide. Because of their size, more people are eligible for implant solutions than ever before.

These narrow implants are ideal for individuals with bone loss who may not have qualified for traditional options or were told they needed a bone graft. With mini implants, bone grafting is typically unnecessary.

These implants also improve the design of the traditional model. Mini implants are a single unit featuring an integrated connector. The connector end, which looks like a small ball, attaches to artificial teeth using a rubber O-ring.

While mini implants reduce the size and simplify the design of a standard implant, these aspects don’t diminish their effectiveness. Studies indicate high survival rates equivalent to those of standard implants!
