Dr. Dan is currently the president of the New York State Ninth District Dental Society, which serves Putnam, Westchester, Dutchess, Rockland and Ulster counties. He has also served on their Board of Governors. Dr. Dan previously served as the president of the Putnam County Dental Society. He has completed over 1500 hours of continuing education course work in dentistry.

Dr. Dan has been a member of the Putnam County Board of Health for over thirty years, serving as the president since 2002. Dr. Dan provides dental services thru his volunteer work with Donated Dental Services.
