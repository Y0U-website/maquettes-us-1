Experience Comfortable, Modern Care

When you come to Cedar Smiles Dentistry for the first time, you’ll be met by our friendly, warm and welcoming staff. Check-in is conveniently streamlined through our office iPad, and you can relax with a refreshment from our beverage station before your appointment. We’ve outfitted our office with televisions and streaming services, and offer headphones, so while you’re in the chair, you can tune in as much or little as you’d like. After a thorough exam and cleaning, we will work together to develop a treatment plan that fits your needs, lifestyle, and goals.

Modern Technology For Quality Treatments

As part of our commitment to holistic, modern dentistry, we use cutting-edge tools like digital x-rays and iTero intraoral scanners to get an in-depth, accurate look at your smile, helping us treat and diagnose with the utmost precision. Dr. Girgis and our team practice holistic dentistry, so we’re proud to use safe mercury amalgam removal techniques for fillings , Piezo tools for effective cleanings, and Ozone therapy to eliminate any harmful bacteria in your mouth. We have everything a modern dental office needs to keep our patients safe and comfortable.
