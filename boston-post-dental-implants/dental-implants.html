Why Patients Choose Dental Implants

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants replace the entire tooth structure , including the root. This means they work just like your natural teeth. A small titanium post is placed into your jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over time. Once healed, a custom crown is attached to complete your new tooth.

The result? A permanent solution that doesn’t slip, doesn’t require messy adhesives, and doesn’t damage neighboring teeth. Implants also help preserve your jawbone, which naturally deteriorates when teeth are missing. This keeps your face looking fuller and your smile looking youthful.

What to Expect During Your Implant Journey

Initial Consultation and Planning Your first visit includes a full exam, medical review, digital X-rays, and advanced imaging to plan precise implant placement. If bone grafting is needed, we’ll explain your options and review costs, insurance, and financing so you know what to expect before treatment begins.

The Placement Procedure On procedure day, local anesthesia keeps you comfortable, with sedation available if needed. The titanium implant is placed through a small gum incision, usually taking one to two hours per implant, and most patients return to normal activities within a day or two.

Healing and Final Restoration Healing takes three to six months as the implant fuses with the jawbone. Once complete, we attach a connector and your custom crown, ensuring a natural look, comfortable bite, and confident smile before you leave our office.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance for dental implants?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. While dental implants are often considered a cosmetic procedure, many insurance companies provide partial coverage, especially when tooth loss resulted from injury or disease. Our team will verify your benefits and provide a clear cost breakdown before treatment begins.

How long does the entire implant process take?

From consultation to final crown placement, the process typically takes four to eight months. This includes time for healing and osseointegration. Some patients qualify for same-day implants in specific cases, which we can discuss during your consultation.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Local anesthesia keeps you comfortable during placement, and over-the-counter pain medication usually manages any soreness afterward. Any discomfort typically resolves within a few days as healing begins.
