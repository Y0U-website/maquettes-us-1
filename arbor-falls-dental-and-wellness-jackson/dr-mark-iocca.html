Meet the Dentist

Dr. Mark Iocca

Dr. Mark E. Iocca was born in Springfield, IL, on November 4, 1949. His family moved to Jackson, MI, in 1951. A product of Jackson’s parochial school system, he graduated from St. John’s High School in 1967. Focused on pursuing a career in dentistry, he graduated from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 1974.

Dr. Iocca believes strongly in giving back to the community that has been so supportive of him. He is active in his support of Special Olympics; Disability Connection, and he has been a long-time member of the JCC.

He and his wife Kathy have six children, fifteen grandchildren, and several great-grandchildren! They are members of St. Mary’s Catholic Church and currently resides in Spring Arbor Township.
