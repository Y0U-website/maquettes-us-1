Meet the Dentist

Dr. Elizabeth Garcia

Elizabeth earned her D.D.S. at the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry and completed her general practice residency at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry . She also holds a master’s and bachelor’s degree from Central Michigan University .

Elizabeth and her husband Gustavo currently reside in Jackson with their three children, Marco, Mateo and Monica. When she’s not spending time with patients, Elizabeth loves reading (Harry Potter for life!), traveling, golfing with her family, and continuing her dental education.
