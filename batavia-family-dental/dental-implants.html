Full Arch Implant-Supported Denture Replacement

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, an implant-supported denture offers a stable, secure alternative to traditional removable dentures. Instead of resting on the gums, this type of denture attaches to several strategically placed implants. Patients often describe the difference as noticeably more comfortable and secure, especially during meals and conversations.

At-Home Care For Dental Implants

Caring for your dental implants is simple and helps ensure long-term success. Follow these steps for best results:

With consistent care, your implants can last for many years and help maintain your smile’s strength and beauty.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled brush.
  • Floss or use an interdental cleaner or water flosser daily.
  • Avoid chewing hard objects like ice or hard candy.
  • Schedule professional cleanings and exams twice a year.
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth. Ask our friendly team about our customized nightguards!
