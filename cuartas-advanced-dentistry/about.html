About Us

Dr. Sandra P. Cuartas

DDS. Prosthodontist

Introducing the driving force behind our practice, Dr. Sandra Cuartas. Her dental journey began at the distinguished Universidad del Valle in Cali, Colombia, where she earned her dental degree in 1992. Driven by a genuine passion for her craft and a continuous pursuit of knowledge, she pursued a two-year specialization in Oral Rehabilitation at the Universidad Autonoma de Manizales School of Dentistry.

Following two years in a successful private practice, Dr. Cuartas returned to academia. After moving to South Florida with her family, she completed the competitive Prosthodontics residency program at Nova Southeastern University. During her time at Nova, Dr. Cuartas conducted research on various prosthodontic topics, with a focus on implant-supported over-dentures. She also gained valuable experience through a two-month rotation at the West Palm Beach VA Medical Center, emphasizing removable prosthodontics.

Dedicated to staying abreast of industry developments, Dr. Cuartas actively engages in dental continuing education to ensure she remains well-informed about evolving trends and treatments. Her comprehensive skill set spans traditional dental procedures like crowns, bridges, and dentures to the intricacies of whitening, veneers, and state-of-the-art dental implants.

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Cuartas finds joy in traveling during her free time and values quality moments with her family. At our practice, her commitment to providing quality care with humility and expertise ensures that every visit is met with trust and a genuine sense of well-being.

Get to Know the Friendly Faces Behind Your Care

Dr. Maria Isabel Guevara

A native of Colombia, Dr. Guevara brings years of specialized experience in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, including training from the University of Illinois in Chicago. She offers a range of orthodontic care for patients of all ages—braces, clear aligners, and early treatments for children—all aimed at creating healthier, more confident smiles at Cuartas Advanced Dentistry.

Dr. Susana Wilhelm

Dr. Wilhelm is a highly experienced dentist with over 30 years in the field. She earned her dental degree from Universidad Central de Venezuela in 1992, followed by a postgraduate degree in Endodontics from Venezuela Red Cross Hospital in 2003. After running her private practice in Caracas, she continued her education in the U.S., completing a General Dental Practice program at the University of Miami and Jackson Memorial Hospital in 2014. Now a licensed Florida dentist, Dr. Wilhelm combines her extensive expertise and passion for Endodontics to provide top-quality care to our patients.

Dr. Steven H. Berkowitz

Dr. Berkowitz has been a dedicated dentist for 17 years, committed to excellent patient care. After earning his degree from Towson University, he completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery and a Master of Science in Periodontics at the University of Maryland. A member of esteemed organizations like the ADA and the Academy of Osseointegration, he has received awards from the Northeast Society of Periodontics and the International College of Dentists. Outside the office, Dr. Berkowitz enjoys tennis, playing guitar, and spending time with his wife and four children.
