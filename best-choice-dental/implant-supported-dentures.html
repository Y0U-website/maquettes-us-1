How Does the Process Work?

Dr. Choi will evaluate your oral health and discuss your goals to determine if implant-supported dentures are the best option for you.

Implant Placement

Titanium implants are surgically placed into your jawbone. These implants act as anchors for your dentures. Depending on your unique case, four to six implants may be required per arch.

Healing Period

Over the next several months, the jawbone bonds with the implants through a process called osseointegration , ensuring a stable foundation.

Denture Attachment

Once the implants are fully integrated, your custom denture is attached to the implants, restoring your smile’s function and appearance.
