The Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation

Dr. Choi will evaluate your oral health, take detailed images, and discuss your goals to create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. This serves as the foundation for your new tooth or teeth.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone, ensuring stability and durability.

Restoration Placement

Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant, completing your smile.

Dental Implants FAQ

Implants fuse with the jawbone, providing unmatched stability and a natural feel compared to other options.

The procedure involves placing a titanium post in the jawbone, allowing it to heal and integrate before attaching a custom restoration.

Missing teeth can lead to jawbone loss, shifting teeth, and difficulties with chewing or speaking, which implants help prevent.

While smoking can impact healing, many smokers can still receive implants. Dr. Choi will provide personalized advice based on your oral health.

Implants are cared for just like natural teeth—maintain good oral hygiene and visit your dentist regularly.
