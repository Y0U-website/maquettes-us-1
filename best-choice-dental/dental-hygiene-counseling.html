Dental Cleanings and Exams FAQ

During your visit, Dr. Choi and our team will:

Our goal is to empower you with the knowledge and skills needed to maintain excellent oral health for years to come.

  • Brush after every meal or at least twice a day using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Spend at least two minutes brushing, covering all tooth surfaces, including the back teeth and along the gumline.
  • Floss at least once a day, preferably before bedtime, to remove plaque and debris from between your teeth and under the gumline.
  • Limit sugary and acidic foods that can erode enamel and contribute to decay.
  • Include plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and dairy to support strong teeth and gums.
  • Schedule checkups and cleanings at least every six months to catch potential issues early and maintain a clean, healthy smile.
  • Review Your Routine : Discuss your current brushing and flossing habits and identify areas for improvement.

  • Answer Questions : Address any concerns you have about your oral health or home care practices.
