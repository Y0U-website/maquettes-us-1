5. SMS Messaging & Mobile Data Privacy

If you provide your mobile phone number and consent to receive SMS or MMS messages from Chemung Family Dental , we use your information solely to deliver the messaging services you have opted into.

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. We may share your Personal Data, including your SMS opt-in or consent status, with third parties that help us provide our messaging services, including but not limited to platform providers, phone companies, and any other vendors who assist us in the delivery of text messages.

You may opt out of receiving SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message or by contacting us at info@chemungfamilydental.com or (607) 734-2045 . Upon receipt of your opt-out request, we will promptly stop sending SMS messages to your number.

Consent categories

  • Transactional messages - appointment reminders, appointment confirmations, and account or billing updates - are sent only to patients who have provided their mobile number and opted in.
  • Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Message frequency may vary.
  • Reply HELP at any time for assistance, or contact us at (607) 734-2045.

Written opt-in form

A downloadable Patient Communication Opt-In Form is available for in-office use: Download the Patient Communication Opt-In Form (PDF) . Your consent to receive SMS or email communications is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time.
