The Al Dente Dental Spa of Roslyn Advantage

Advanced Technology. Long-Lasting Smiles.

Equipped with cutting-edge technology, Al Dente Dental Spa of Roslyn is dedicated to giving patients unparalleled precision in crafting their smiles. We prioritize getting it right the first time, utilizing advanced diagnostic tools to identify and address the root cause of dental concerns. This commitment ensures that our treatments are effective and tailored to deliver lasting results, promoting optimal oral health.

1 Cone Beam Computed Tomography CBCT scanners revolutionize diagnostic imaging in dentistry, providing detailed 3D images of the oral and maxillofacial structures. These scanners offer a comprehensive view, allowing our team to visualize intricate details of teeth, bones, nerves, and soft tissues with remarkable precision.

With the ability to capture detailed cross-sectional images, CBCT scanners enhance treatment planning accuracy, guiding dentists in providing personalized and effective patient care.

2 Digital X-Rays Digital X-rays offer a technological leap, providing immediate, high-resolution images with significantly lower radiation exposure than traditional film X-rays. The efficiency of digital X-rays allows for swift diagnosis, aiding our team in promptly detecting issues such as cavities, infections, or bone abnormalities. The digital format facilitates easy patient record storage, retrieval, and sharing, contributing to streamlined and eco-friendly dental practices.

3 3D Printers 3D printers have transformed dentistry, creating precise and customized dental appliances such as crowns, bridges, and even intricate prosthetics. These printers utilize digital models to layer materials and construct three-dimensional dental structures with exceptional accuracy, optimizing the fit and functionality of the final product. Integrating 3D printing technology enhances efficiency in dental laboratories, allowing for rapid prototyping and the production of patient-specific solutions, ultimately elevating the quality of dental care.

4 Intraoral Scanners Intraoral scanners have revolutionized dental impressions, replacing traditional molds with a digital, non-invasive method. These compact devices capture detailed 3D images of the patient’s teeth and oral structures, providing a more comfortable and efficient experience.

Intraoral scanners enhance the accuracy of impressions for restorations like crowns and bridges and contribute to streamlined workflows in dental practices, ultimately improving the overall patient experience.

5 Smile Design Smile design is a comprehensive approach in dentistry that focuses on creating aesthetically pleasing and balanced smiles tailored to each individual’s unique features. We use advanced techniques like digital imaging and mock-ups to analyze facial proportions, tooth shape, and color, allowing for a personalized and precise smile transformation. Through smile design, patients can view their new smile based on their customized treatment plan before any treatment begins.
