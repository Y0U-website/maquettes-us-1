General & Cosmetic Dental Services

Painless and Affordable Dental Services

Dental Exams and Cleanings

Ensure excellent oral health with this twice-a-year treatment that prevents periodontis (gum disease). Did you know research suggests chronic gum disease is associated with an increased risk of developing heart disease? Keep more complicated dental procedures and other health issues at bay with this preventative measure.

Oral Cancer Screening

This short visual and physical precautionary exam can reassure patients there are no apparent problems. The initial examination of the oral cavity and connected tissues is often the trigger for further diagnosis and treatment. A dentist may suggest a screening in response to learning a patient smokes, uses tobacco products, or consumes alcohol.

Dental Sealants

A protective barrier between tooth enamel and plaque and acids, dental sealants help prevent decay in the impressions and grooves of the premolars and molars. Children and teenagers are great candidates for them and adults can benefit from them, too.

Dental Crowns and Ceramic Restorations

A tooth-shaped cap cemented onto a damaged or broken tooth, dental crowns, which come in a variety of types including ceramic, porcelain, resin and metal, look, function and feel like a regular tooth. With the latest CEREC® technology, patients can receive an all ceramic crown, onlay or inlay in a single visit.

Full and Partial Dentures and Bridges

We help you choose the best solution for extensively damaged or decayed teeth. When you have a missing tooth or a number of missing teeth, there are several replacement options to consider that will alleviate problems. A dental bridge or dentures can restore the ability to chew food properly, maintain the shape of your face, and restore speech and pronunciation.

Non-Surgical Peridontal Therapy

Known as scaling and root planing, these basic treatments allow the gums and surrounding bone to heal properly.

Occlusal Splints and Mouth Guards

Custom-made to prevent sore jaws and damaged teeth, mouth guards are made from a thermoplastic and are made based on a detailed mould taken of the patient’s mouth and teeth. Thickness is determined based on level of grinding and clenching. They can also be made for the protection of the teeth during sports.
