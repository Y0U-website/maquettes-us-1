Patient Information

You’re In Great Hands!

Your comfort and care are our top priority.

During your visit, Dr. Jones will review your dental and medical history. He will examine your teeth and take X-rays of your mouth. He will explain any issues and guide you through the best options for your treatment. We typically proceed with cleaning at this time or at a time that is most convenient for you.

For a shorter time in the waiting room, please click to print out our new patient forms below and take 5 minutes to fill them out in advance of your visit. We look forward to seeing you and providing a pleasant dental experience.

Payment Options
