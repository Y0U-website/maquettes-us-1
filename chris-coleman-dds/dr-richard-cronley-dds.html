General Dentistry

Prevention and treatment

The best way to avoid major dental work is to see your dentist on a regular basis. Periodic cleanings will help us determine if there are any issues that need to be corrected before they require costly dental procedures to correct.

cosmetic dentistry

bright smiles, Straight Teeth

White straight teeth is the hallmark of a great smile. You may not have been born with either but we can help you get a brighter smile and great looking teeth - getting you the confident smile you ve always wanted.

Tongue and Lip Tie correction

Ankyloglossia

Tongue and lip ties can affect your baby s oral development, including how effectively your child nurses, eats, speaks and swallows. A complete oral evaluation is necessary to determine if your child needs treatment.

Invisalign Straightening

alternative to braces

Invisalign is a virtually invisible option to braces. Many patients find the process more comfortable as well. Invisalign aligners are custom made for every patient and the use over time will straighten and shift teeth into their proper position.

Dental Implants

Natural looking replacements

Permanent replacement tooth roots that look and feel like your natural teeth, and provide a foundation under a crown, bridge or denture.

surgical Procedures

With Sedation Options

Surgical and non-surgical extractions, wisdom teeth extractions and dental implant surgery. IV sedation is an option for patients who might be a little nervous about the procedure and want to sleep through the procedure.
