The best way to avoid major dental work is to see your dentist on a regular basis. Periodic cleanings will help us determine if there are any issues that need to be corrected before they require costly dental procedures to correct.

Surgical and non-surgical extractions, wisdom teeth extractions and dental implant surgery. IV sedation is an option for patients who might be a little nervous about the procedure and want to sleep through the procedure.

Invisalign is a virtually invisible option to braces. Many patients find the process more comfortable as well. Invisalign aligners are custom made for every patient and the use over time will straighten and shift teeth into their proper position.
