After surgery, the body sends white blood cells to the surgical area to begin the healing process.

This healing process takes several days, and also brings pain, swelling, and inflammation.

We can now collect these healing cells ahead of time from your own blood.

Your blood is drawn into a vial and used to create Platelet Rich Fibrin.

The healing cells are now present immediately, giving you a three-day head start on the healing process.
