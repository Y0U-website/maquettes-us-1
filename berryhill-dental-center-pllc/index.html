The practice

We understand that every patient’s needs and goals are unique, and we take the time to listen and understand your individual concerns. We work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that addresses your specific needs and helps you achieve the smile of your dreams.

We are conveniently located in the heart of Cordova neighborhoods, making it easy for you to access the care you need. Our state-of-the-art facility is designed to be comfortable and welcoming, and our friendly staff is dedicated to making your visit as pleasant as possible.

Dr. Eunjung Edwards is the owner of Berryhill Dental Center. She attended the University of Memphis and is a graduate of the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry (Class of 2009). She has been a Cordova resident for 12 years and has devoted most of her career to serving the communities surrounding the University of Memphis, where she built a reputation for attentive, compassionate care.

We provide a wide range of dental services, including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry and more.

Dr. Edwards is an experienced dentists dedicated to helping you achieve a healthy and beautiful smile.
