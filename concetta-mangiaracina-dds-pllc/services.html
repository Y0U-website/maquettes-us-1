Services

We proudly offer a full-range of dental services for the entire family. Whether you need a routine cleaning and check-up, or a more advanced dental procedure, our practice can assist you in your dental needs.

There are several different types of restorations that can be done to restore missing tooth structure lost from a cavity, broken or from a defect in enamel. Composite "white filling", amalgam "silver filling", and porcelain inlays and onlays which are fabricated in a lab. Fillings are done to restore the function, integrity and morphology of teeth.

A dental crown is a restorative procedure done to repair and strengthen damaged teeth. Crowns can improve the appearance of teeth including color, shape and alignment. There are a variety of materials used to make crowns, which are fabricated in a dental laboratory.

Porcelain veneers are done to cosmetically enhance your smile. They are a thin shell of porcelain fabricated in a dental lab, that is bonded to the prepared tooth. They are designed to cover the front surface of teeth to improve the appearance of color, shape, size or length. Porcelain veneers do not stain over time and have light reflecting properties of natural teeth. Veneers are routinely done to fix discolored teeth from tetracycline stain, excessive fluoride, enamel dysplasia, chipped or broken teeth, and misaligned teeth.

Root canal treatment is necessary when the nerve tissue inside a tooth is dying or dead. Nerves can become affected from a deep cavity in a tooth, trauma, breaking a tooth can cause nerve exposure, or excessive grinding. Nerves inside teeth sense hot and cold. When the nerve is removed from a tooth, it can still function normally. If you’re experiencing hot or cold lingering pain, pressure when biting, discoloration, swelling or no sensation at all you may require a root canal.

Dentures are fabricated in a lab to replace missing teeth. There are two types of dentures: complete dentures replace all missing teeth and partial dentures are made when missing some teeth. With advances in materials, dentures and partials can be made esthetically to appear like natural teeth. Tooth loss solutions however do not only consist of remove-able prosthesis appliances. You can call for an appointment to discuss these options.

An alternative form of orthodontic teeth movement without having metal brackets and wires on your teeth. A series of clear aligners are fabricated to aid in the movement and correct the position of your teeth. There is little to no discomfort, and some treatments can be done in less than 6 months. If you are unhappy with your smile, whether due to spaces or overlap from crowding ,come for a consultation and see if you’re a candidate for invisalign or clear correct.

Professional teeth whitening can lighten the color of teeth that are naturally discolored or stained by food, tobacco use, and age. We offer our patients both in-office and at-home bleaching trays to help improve the color and overall brightness of teeth.

The Zoom chair-side whitening system is a safe, effective and fast way to brighten your teeth in one office visit.
