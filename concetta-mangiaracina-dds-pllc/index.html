The practice

Our warm and welcoming staff will ensure your next dental visit is a personalized and comfortable experience. We specialize in a variety of dental procedures, from routine cleanings to more advanced procedures, and are equipped to service adults, children, and those with special needs, in our relaxed and inviting atmosphere.

Whether you need a routine exam, cleaning and x-rays, or more specialized care such as fillings, crowns, implants, dentures, veneers or teeth whitening services, Dr. Mangiaracina is a dedicated and caring professional that will make your next visit a pleasant one.

To request an appointment, please enter your name and phone number and our office will contact you within 24 hours to schedule your appointment.

Interested in our services? Want to schedule an appointment? Send us a message and our office will respond within 24 hours.

Dr. Concetta Mangiaracina was born and raised in Nassau County, Long Island, and is one of six siblings.

She completed her undergraduate studies at the State University of New York (SUNY) at Stony Brook and received a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) degree in Biology.

Dr. Mangiaracina continued her education at New York University (NYU) College of Dentistry, focusing on clinical research, and graduating with a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree (D.D.S) in 2009.

Dr. Mangiaracina then furthered her dental studies, completing a one-year General Dentistry Residency program at Nassau University Medical Center in East Meadow, NY.
