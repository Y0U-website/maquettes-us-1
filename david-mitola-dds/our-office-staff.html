Our Dental Team

DAVID MITOLA, DDS

DAVID LIVES IN CLIFTON PARK WITH HIS WIFE ANDREA, DAUGHTER ABBY AND SON LUCAS.

He graduated from the University of Maryland College Park in 1999 and earned his D.D.S. from the University of Maryland Dental School in 2005. Dr. Mitola completed a post-graduate fellowship in geriatric dentistry at the University of Maryland in 2006.

Dr. Mitola has treated patients in his private practice in Cohoes since 2009. He practiced in Maryland from 2005-2009. Professionally, he is a member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, Past-President of the New York State Third District Dental Society, Past-President of the Troy Dental Study Club and a Fellow of the International College of Dentists.

Community positions included, Past President of the Cohoes Rotary Club and Past President of the Cohoes Community Center. Dr. Mitola also coaches youth baseball, softball and wrestling.

RORY OGDEN, DDS

Dr. Rory Ogden joined Mitola Family Dentistry in 2025, bringing a wealth of experience and a strong commitment to community health. She graduated from Holy Cross in 2008 and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Creighton University in 2013. Following dental school, she completed her Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) Residency at Lutheran Medical Center in 2014.

Dr. Ogden served as the Chief Dental Officer at Hometown Health starting in 2018, where she played a key role in developing and opening a brand-new 30-operatory clinic dedicated to serving underserved populations. Her dedication to expanding access to quality dental care has been a driving force throughout her career.

Since joining our team, Dr. Ogden has helped Mitola Family Dentistry grow and enhance the level of care we provide to our community.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, and the National Network for Oral Health Access. Dr. Ogden currently serves as a board member of the New York 4th District Dental Society.

Outside the office, Dr. Ogden has been coaching youth softball since 2014, combining her love for the sport with a passion for mentorship. She is also a proud aunt to six nieces and nephews and enjoys spending quality time with her family.

Our Dental Hygienists

Kristy

Kristy has been a valued member of our dental team for an incredible 18 years. Her dedication, professionalism, and warm personality make her a favorite among patients and colleagues alike. Kristy takes pride in providing excellent care and building lasting relationships with those she treats.

She earned her Dental Hygiene degree from Hudson Valley Community College and also holds a degree in Liberal Arts. Her commitment to continued learning and patient-centered care has made her an essential part of our practice’s success.

Outside the office, Kristy enjoys camping, hiking, and spending time with her family. As a busy mom, she proudly takes on the role of mom bus, keeping up with her children’s active sports schedules and family adventures.

Alison

Alison has been a part of the Mitola Family Dentistry team since 2019, the same year she graduated from Hudson Valley Community College with her degree in Dental Hygiene. Since then, she has become a trusted and familiar face to many of our patients.

In her daily role, Alison performs thorough dental cleanings, educates patients on proper oral hygiene techniques, and conducts screenings for oral diseases such as gum disease and oral cancer. She takes time to assess each patient’s individual needs, providing personalized care plans and recommendations to maintain and improve their oral health. Alison also assists in taking dental x-rays and collaborates closely with the dental team to ensure comprehensive care. Her gentle touch and calm demeanor help create a welcoming, stress-free environment for patients of all ages.

Alison is known for her genuine care and dedication to patient comfort, always striving to make each visit as pleasant as possible.

Outside of the office, Alison enjoys staying active through yoga and hiking, and she loves spending time outdoors whenever she can.

Julia

Julia is a dedicated dental hygienist who brings enthusiasm, compassion, and a commitment to patient care to every appointment. She earned her Dental Hygiene degree from Hudson Valley Community College (HVCC) and holds a Bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Women and Gender Studies.

With one year of clinical experience, Julia has developed a gentle yet thorough approach to dental care, ensuring each patient feels comfortable, informed, and confident about their oral health.

Outside the office, Julia enjoys spending time outdoors and exploring new and exciting places around the Capital Region. Whether hiking local trails or discovering hidden gems in nearby towns, she loves staying active and connecting with her community.

Our Dental Assistants

Lyndsey

Lyndsey joined our practice in 2020 and brings over 16 years of hands-on experience in the dental field. She attended the HVCC Dental Assistant program and later shared her expertise as a teacher at the Adirondack Dental Assisting School, helping train the next generation of dental professionals.

In her daily role, Lyndsey assists dentists during procedures, ensuring all instruments and materials are prepared and sterilized for optimal patient care. She provides patients with clear explanations and compassionate support throughout their visits, helping to ease any anxieties. Lyndsey is also responsible for taking dental x-rays, maintaining patient records, and coordinating with other team members to ensure smooth and efficient clinical operations. Her attention to detail and teamwork contribute significantly to the high standard of care our patients receive.

Lyndsey is known for her compassionate approach and ability to put patients at ease. Her dedication and experience make her a valued member of our clinical team.

Outside of work, Lyndsey loves soaking up the sunshine and staying active. She enjoys watching her daughters play soccer year-round, as well as spending time outdoors fishing, kayaking, and boating.

Theresa

Theresa has been a dedicated dental assistant since 2011 and became part of our team in 2022. With over 11 years of hands-on experience in general dentistry, she brings a wealth of knowledge, skill, and compassion to every patient interaction. Theresa’s passion for dentistry and patient care, along with her friendly and approachable nature, make her an invaluable part of our team.

Our Patient Care Coordinators

Dawn

Dawn has been a dedicated member of Mitola Family Dentistry since 1993, beginning her journey when the practice was under Dr. Frazier. With over three decades of experience, she brings a wealth of knowledge, compassion, and reliability to both her clinical and patient-facing roles.

As a Dental Assistant and Patient Coordinator, Dawn wears many hats. She assists chairside during procedures, helps manage patient flow, and supports the team in ensuring each visit runs smoothly. Her attention to detail, calming presence, and extensive familiarity with our patients’ needs make her an invaluable part of our team.

Dawn is known not only for her skill and professionalism but also for the meaningful relationships she’s built with generations of patients and staff. Her deep-rooted commitment to the practice shines through in everything she does.

Outside the office, Dawn cherishes time with her family and five amazing grandkids, often spending weekends surrounded by laughter, love, and plenty of activity.

Kelly

From managing appointments to coordinating specialist referrals, Kelly helps guide patients through their care with compassion and efficiency. Her friendly demeanor, strong communication skills, and natural ability to make patients feel at ease make her a valued and trusted member of our team.

Outside of work, Kelly enjoys spending time with her husband and their four wonderful grandkids, often finding joy in family gatherings and creating special memories together.
