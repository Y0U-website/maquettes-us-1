Different By Choice

Dr. Louis Giuliani first opened Excellence by Choice Dentistry over 50 years ago in Morgantown, WV and today, Dr. Chris. J. Giuliani upholds the family tradition with over 2 decades of experience. At our conveniently located dental office in Morgantown , we are devoted to restoring and enhancing the natural beauty of your smile using conservative, state-of-the-art procedures that will result in beautiful, long lasting smiles!! Our excellence in restorative and cosmetic dentistry is what keeps us in business, but our family-like atmosphere is what keeps our patients coming back.

We have built an exceptional reputation in the communities of Morgantown, Westover, Fairmont, Clarksburg, Cheat Lake, and neighboring areas (and among professional colleagues) for excellence in restorative and cosmetic dentistry, and we treat our patients like respected members of our family. Patients also choose us because we provide comprehensive family dentistry care for all their needs from one convenient location, and because we go out of our way to make our patients happy.
