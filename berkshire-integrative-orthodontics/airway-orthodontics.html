Treatment Solutions

We are proud to offer the Myobrace® system as part of our integrative approach to orthodontics. This alternative orthodontic treatment utilizes myofunctional techniques to address poor muscular habits that can be the underlying causes of crooked teeth and applies light intermittent force to align the teeth. The Myobrace® appliance can even be used in concert with traditional orthodontics to assist with the correction of myofunctional issues of the tongue, lips or cheeks. Studies have shown that Myobrace® can speed up orthodontic treatment and support the creation of a balanced bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is airway-focused orthodontics?

Airway orthodontics is a more holistic approach to orthodontic treatment. Traditionally, orthodontics has been focused on the appearance of the teeth and jaw function; the mouth is essentially treated as if it is isolated from the rest of the body. Airway orthodontics acknowledges the connection between jaw development, teeth alignment, facial structures, and breathing. With proper treatment, impaired breathing can be improved by optimizing the airway. This improvement in breathing can have a significant impact on overall quality of life and well-being.

Can braces improve breathing?

Yes, braces can improve breathing, but not all orthodontists are trained in airway orthodontics. If you wish to improve your (or your child’s) breathing, it’s important to seek out an orthodontist who specializes in airway orthodontics, like Dr. O’Donnell and the team at Berkshire Integrative Orthodontics.

What is myofunctional orthodontics?

Myofunctional orthodontics is one of the tools we use in airway orthodontics to address oral habits that can cause crowding and jaw development issues which, in turn, affect breathing and speech.

Does Myobrace really work?

Yes, Myobrace is an effective treatment option for retraining improper breathing, swallowing patterns, and other habits. Once these are corrected, your child’s jaw and facial development will be able to reach its full potential.

How long should you wear Myobrace?

Dr. O’Donnell will let you know how long you (or your child) will need to wear your Myobrace appliance each day. For most patients, appliances are worn for 1 to 2 hours during the day, and then overnight.
