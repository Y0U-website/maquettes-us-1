Integrative Orthodontics – Integrative orthodontics is a multidisciplinary approach that combines conventional braces with alternative therapies in order to address the underlying causes of malocclusion, rather than simply focusing on the symptoms. The ultimate goal of an orthodontist who practices integrative orthodontics is to improve overall wellness and health, not simply correct misaligned…

Airway Orthodontics – As part of our holistic approach to orthodontic treatment, we provide care that optimizes breathing through improving airway mechanics, muscle function, tongue posture, and arch development. Our state-of-the-art CBCT digital scanning technology allows us to diagnose airway disorders, which can then be treated with an appropriate orthodontic therapy.

MyoBrace – MyoBrace is an intraoral appliance system that corrects poor oral habits, improves jaw alignment, and optimizes facial development, which leads to better health. The appliance retrains your child to breathe through their nose and and corrects the tongue position, helping them keep their lips together and swallow correctly.
