Doctors and Team

Dentist Milwaukie, OR

Our team of highly-skilled doctors are dedicated to providing you with excellent, personalized dental care . We are active educators and leaders in the dental community, working with organizations such as the Oregon Health Sciences University (OHSU) , Oregon Dental Association (ODA) , and the American Dental Association (ADA) to advocate for ethical values and high-quality care in dentistry.

The utmost goal of our team is to promote long-term healthy outcomes for you and your family.

Kenneth L. Chung, DDS, MPH

Dr. Kenneth Chung has practiced clinical dentistry since 1986, and founded ComfortCare Dental in 1995. In his 30 years of clinical dental practice, he has devoted himself to caring for the long-term health of thousands of patients of all ages. He is known for his warm and compassionate manner and his meticulous attention to detail, which comes through in the quality of his work.

Dr. Chung was born and raised in San Francisco, California, and graduated from the UCLA School of Dentistry in 1975. He received a Masters degree in Public Health (MPH) from The University of Michigan in 1977, where he remained as a member of the faculty until moving to Portland in 1980. He was a Research Assistant Professor of Biostatistics, an Associate Professor of Public Health Dentistry and Practice Planning at OHSU before starting in private practice in SE Portland. In 1990, he moved his clinic to Milwaukie, where he has remained since.

Dr. Chung has taught and mentored at the OHSU and Clackamas Community College . He has served as a member of the Board of Directors of the North Clackamas Chamber of Commerce, and as Chairman from 2002–2003. He currently sits on the Board of Trustees of the ODA.

Dr. Chung speaks fluent Cantonese and Mandarin. He is happily married with two grown children, two grandchildren, and an adopted cat.

Travis M. Cochell, DMD

With more than 20 years of experience, Dr. Cochell has worked in both public health and private practice, completed evidence-based research with the University of Washington and OHSU, and volunteered for ten consecutive years providing dental care in remote areas of Mexico. Now serving patients in his hometown at Comfort Care Dental in Oregon City, he combines his technical expertise with genuine compassion and a dedication to long-term oral health.

Dr. Cochell is currently a member of good standing with the American Dental Association & the Oregon Dental Association. Dr. Cochell enjoys many other activities & hobbies outside the dental field including leather working, woodworking, traveling, fishing, camping, hiking and volunteering for the church he attends.

Dr. Bermen Wong, DDS

After growing up in Eugene, Oregon, Dr. Bermen Wong left Oregon briefly to receive his bachelor’s in Biology from University of the Pacific in Stockton, California and then his DDS from the Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco. Since graduating in 2016, he’s made his home in the Portland area and has since adopted a cat named Al. He seeks to serve the community as he would his own family; with compassion, as comfortably as possible, and by just doing what is right for everyone he sees.
