Our Practice

Milwaukie, OR

At ComfortCare Dental we have been serving families in the Milwaukie , Oregon City , and Clackamas communities for over 30 years. We care deeply about the well-being of our patients across all generations, having seen many of them from childhood through adulthood. We are dedicated to helping you maintain your oral health for a lifetime.

Whether you have a dental emergency or need routine dental care , or even a Back-to-School check-up, we’ve got you covered. Our doctors and skilled dental team members will help you design an individual treatment plan most appropriate for your needs and provide you with personalized, gentle care.

High Standards in Dentistry

A standard of excellence in personalized dental care enables us to provide the quality dental services our patients deserve. We provide comprehensive treatment planning and use restorative and cosmetic dentistry to achieve your optimal dental health. Should a dental emergency occur, we make every effort to see and care for you as soon as possible.

Community-focused

We are actively involved in our local community through our work with the North Clackamas County Chamber of Commerce and sponsorship of local school programs and athletic teams. ComfortCare Dental doctors and staff volunteer their expertise with local and global organizations providing dental care to under-served populations. Additionally we mentor and provide externship opportunities to future generations of doctors and dental assistants from OHSU , Clackamas Community College , and Concorde Career College . ComfortCare Dental is an authorized provider of vaccinations in Oregon.

Education & Prevention

Uncompromising Safety

Infection control in our office is also very important to us. To protect our patients and ourselves, we strictly maintain sterilization and cross contamination processes using standards recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) , the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) , and the Center for Disease Control (CDC) .

Training & Expertise

As your dental health professionals, we want you to be confident knowing that we are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians. To give you the best possible service and results, we are committed to continual education and learning. We attend dental lectures, meetings, and conferences to stay informed of new techniques, the latest products, and state-of-the-art equipment that a modern dental office can utilize to provide the best care. Our participation in various professional dental associations helps us to stay abreast of the changes and recommendations for our profession.

A Positive Experience

Building a foundation of trust by treating our patients as special individuals is vital to our success. We understand how uneasy some patients may feel about their dental visits, and how we can make a difference in providing a relaxing and positive experience. Our entire team is dedicated to providing you with excellent, personalized care and service to make your visits as comfortable and pleasant as possible.

We thank you for allowing us to take care of your dental needs and look forward to serving you.
