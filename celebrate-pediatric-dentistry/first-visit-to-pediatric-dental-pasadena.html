Meet Cely!

We know that for some kids, not knowing what to expect is the hardest part. So we created Cely’s First Dentist Visit , a printable social story featuring Cely — our bear mascot — who walks through every single step of a first visit, from walking in the door to picking a prize on the way out.

We recommend printing it out and reading it together a day or two before your appointment. Kids who know what’s coming tend to feel calmer, more in control, and way more ready to show off their brave faces.
