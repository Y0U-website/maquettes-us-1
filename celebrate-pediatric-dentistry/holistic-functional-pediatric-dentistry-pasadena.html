Airway Dentistry & Myofunctional Therapy

  • Overview We do not just focus on teeth here at Celerbrate! Your breathing and facial development play a role in the big picture of your health. Signs like grinding, snoring, mouth-breathing are “normal,” but not helpful. They can affect your child now and into young adulthood. (ie headaches, focus, daytime fatigue, behavioral problems and even cavities!) Myofunctional therapy is accessible and easy; Dr Aquino is your certified “coach” who can evaluate and guide you to clearer breathing and get your child back to normal function and great health.

Special Needs

We welcome special needs patients through age 18 and help with transition to adult providers when the time comes. Contact us to discuss how we can best support your child’s dental health journey.

  • Overview We proudly provide specialized dental care for children with all abilities. Our team has advanced training to create comfortable, positive experiences for children with special healthcare needs. Our Approach to Special Needs Care: Individualized Treatment tailored to each child’s unique needs and comfort level
  • Extra Time & Patience allowing each visit to proceed at your child’s pace
  • Sensory Accommodations to create a calming, supportive environment
  • Clear Communication using techniques that work best for your child
  • Family Collaboration because your input is essential to successful care

Services by age

  • Infant 0 - 1 year Your baby’s first dental visit is a milestone worth celebrating! We focus on establishing a strong foundation for lifelong oral health through gentle examinations, first tooth celebrations, and parent education. Prevention is key! Our team provides essential guidance on teething, thumb-sucking, breathing/airway concerns, cleaning techniques, and nutrition to ensure your baby’s smile starts strong.
  • Toddler 1 - 3 years During these foundational years, we transform dental visits into playful adventures that build positive associations with dental care. Through interactive experiences and gentle techniques, we guide your toddler in developing healthy oral habits while providing expert care for their growing faces and smiles. We work closely with parents on practical strategies for your families needs and home care success.
  • Junior 4 - 10 years As children develop more independence, we embrace their natural curiosity about dental health. Our approach combines engaging education with comprehensive care, including cleanings, cavity prevention, airway function and early orthodontic screening. We celebrate their growing responsibility while monitoring their developing smile and providing guidance for long-term dental success.
  • Teen 11 year + We understand that teenagers have unique dental needs and preferences. Our teen-focused care combines clinical expertise with respect for their growing independence. From health- focused coaching to aesthetic concerns, we provide personalized care that helps teens feel confident about their smile while developing lasting healthy habits they’ll carry into adulthood.
