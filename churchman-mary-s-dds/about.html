Our Dentists

Dr. Mary Seale Churchman, DDS Dr. Mary Seale Churchman received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans. Dr. Churchman is very passionate about dentistry and continues to love and respect her chosen field.

Everyday, Dr. Churchman gets the opportunity to meet great people, their kids and families, and work together with an exceptional staff. Dr. Churchman continues to take advanced dental training in various aspects of dentistry.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association, Southwest District Dental Society and the Better Business Bureau of SWLA.

Dr. Katie Courville Roberts, DDS Dr. Katie Courville Roberts is a Lake Charles native and graduate from Barbe High School. She received her bachelor’s degree in biological sciences from LSU and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from LSU School of Dentistry New Orleans.

Dr. Roberts shares Dr. Churchman’s practice philosophy of providing comprehensive dentistry through working with each patient to meet the individual’s distinctive oral health goals. She brings enthusiasm, poise, and a kind gentle nature with her to our practice.

Dr. Roberts is a member of the American Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association, Southwest District Dental Society and the Better Business Bureau of SWLA.
