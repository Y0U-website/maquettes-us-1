Pediatric Services

Kids aren’t just little adults: Their bodies are constantly growing and changing, and they have very special oral health needs from age one through adolescence. At All-Star Family Dentistry, we strive to give kids the top-notch care they deserve. Whether it’s a dental sealant or fluoride treatment to help keep cavities from forming, or a custom-made mouthguard for a young athlete, we can provide just what your children need. Kids (and parents) find coming to our friendly and professional office a stress-free experience.

Emergency Services

Sometimes, a toothache starts in the middle of the night and gets even worse in the morning; other times, a sports injury or a blow to the mouth results in sudden, sharp pain even breakage or loss of a tooth. No matter what the cause, dental emergencies need prompt and appropriate treatment. If you experience a dental emergency, call All-Star Family Dentistry. We’ll effectively relieve your pain and treat your injury, whether it’s a chipped, broken or knocked-out tooth all the way from wound care to a natural-looking restoration.
