State-of-the-Art Dental Office

When designing her new office in Hopewell Junction, Dr. Kristen included only the most modern digital technology. Digital x-rays expose patients to significantly less radiation than conventional dental x-rays and are far easier to process, view and share. And with her intra-oral camera, Dr. Kristen can show you pictures taken inside of your own mouth that will help you understand your dental condition and the treatments she is recommending.

Treating Children & Adults

Flexible Scheduling

It’s always best to take care of minor dental problems before they become giant and expensive headaches. But we know it’s not always to easy to leave work or take the kids out of school to get needed care. That’s why we offer evening and weekend hours to fit the busiest schedules. Let us know how we can help you keep to a regular schedule of preventive visits, or take care of any dental issues that are bothering you right now. We’ll do our best to accommodate you.
