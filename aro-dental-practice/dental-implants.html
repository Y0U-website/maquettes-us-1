Comprehensive Tooth Replacement Options

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Replacing a single missing tooth without altering or crowning adjacent healthy natural teeth. The titanium post acts as a stable root, topped with a custom-milled porcelain crown.

Multi-Tooth Implant Bridges

Designed to replace multiple consecutive missing teeth with a fixed bridge anchored by two or more strategically placed implants, eliminating the need for partial dentures.

Full-Arch Implant-Supported Restorations

Complete full-jaw solutions using implants to securely anchor removable or fixed full-arch bridges (such as implant-retained custom Geneva 2000™ prosthetics), offering total stability without slippage.

Implant Site Preparation & Bone Augmentation

Advanced site prep including bone grafting and sinus lift procedures to rebuild deficient jawbone density and ensure a rock-solid foundation for future implants.
