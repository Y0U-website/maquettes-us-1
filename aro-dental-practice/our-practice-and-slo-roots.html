What is the background and community history of Aro Dental Practice in San Luis Obispo?

Aro Dental Practice, located at 1100 Grove St, San Luis Obispo, CA 93401, has been an established dental care provider on the Central Coast since 2003. Founded and led by Dr. Ghassan Aro, DDS, the practice is deeply integrated into the San Luis Obispo medical district near French Hospital Medical Center, providing general, restorative, cosmetic, and specialty denture care for over 20 years.
