What is Orthodontics?

A Specialized Branch of Dentistry Focused on Aligning Teeth and Jaws Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry that specializes in diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. The purpose of orthodontic treatment is to correct the alignment of the teeth and jaws to improve oral health and function.
