Directions from Bridgewater

If driving from Bridgewater, proceed to RT18 either from RT104 past Bridgewater Pediatrics or past Bridgewater State University, or from RT28 past Bridgewater Auto Group. Follow RT18 through the heart of Bridgewater. You will pass the Bridgewater Busy Bee on your left and then East Bridgewater Middle/High School on your right until you reach the intersection of RT18 and RT123 in Abington. Turn left on to RT123 and follow until you reach 360 Brockton Ave, a green medical building on your left. Enter through the main doors and use either the elevator or the stairs to the second floor.
