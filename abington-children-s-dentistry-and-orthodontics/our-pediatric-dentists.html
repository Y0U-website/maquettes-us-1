Meet Our Doctors!

Proudly serving families of the greater Abington area, including Whitman, Hanson, Halifax, the Bridgewaters, Rockland, Hanover, and Pembroke.

Meet the doctors who make Abington Children’s Dentistry & Orthodontics so special. Whether it’s them or any of our clinical team, all of our professionals pursue continued education to maintain the highest level of accreditation and expertise. And all know what it means to chase after nimble toddlers, clean up spilled milk every morning, and repeatedly answer the infamous question, “why?”.

Anne R. Kuczma, DMD

Patients with special needs hold a near and dear place in Dr. K’s heart. In 2009, she completed her residency in pediatric dentistry at Children’s Hospital Boston where she conducted research on the oral health of children with cerebral palsy. She currently maintains an affiliate staff position at Boston Children’s Hospital. Dr. Kuczma is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

THE FUN STUFF Dr. K is mom to three young kids. When she’s not in scrubs, you can find her at her children’s sporting events or volunteering at various community programs.

THE FINE DETAILS Dr. K lives in Norwell with her husband, Damian, and children Callan, Andrew, and Bridget. In her free time, she loves being active with running and swimming, and most enjoys spending time with her family.

Kristine A. Grazioso, DMD

THE NERDY STUFF Dr. Grazioso (a.k.a. Dr. G) is a 1988 graduate of Boston College, where she received her B.S. in Biology, and a 1993 graduate of The University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine where she was awarded her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. Dr. Grazioso received her Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry from St. Christopher’s Hospital for Children in Philadelphia, PA, in 1996. She served as Chief Dental Resident and received numerous awards for excellence in Pediatric Dentistry, Orthodontics, and Oral Surgery.

Dr. G founded South Shore Children’s Dentistry (ABCDO’s sister practice) in 1999 – and all these years later, she still enjoys every part of being a pediatric dentist and running a small independent business. She has mentored many young men and women on their journeys to careers in healthcare. She is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. An activist within her profession, she is involved in grassroots efforts on the state and national level to promote issues important to dentistry and to the patients she serves.

THE FUN STUFF In addition to believing that it takes a village in the local community, Dr. G also likes to participate in global outreach. She has been fortunate enough to travel to Ethiopia as the Dental Director on a medical service trip; to Mexico with the nonprofit Amigos de Los Ninos as a dentist caring for children with no other access to dental care; and Haiti with her husband and sons as part of a medical team providing much needed healthcare to underserved communities. She hopes to continue to spread light and love at home and around the globe for many years to come.

THE FINE DETAILS Dr. G resides in Marshfield with her husband, Henry. They can not believe they are parents to young adult sons – time surely has flown by! In Dr. G’s spare time she loves to travel with family and friends, while making new friends wherever she may be!

Paul Fitzgerald, DMD

Dr. Paul Fitzgerald established Hingham & Duxbury Orthodontics in 2000. This practice quickly expanded over the last 15 years and has become the premiere orthodontic services provider in the communities of Hingham, Duxbury, Kingston, Scituate, Cohasset, and Norwell. After collaborating with Dr. K and Dr. G for years, the three dentists partnered to open ABCDO. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the American Association of Orthodontists, and the Mayflower Study Club.

Dr. Fitzgerald is very active on the board of the Tufts Association of Orthodontists. Dr. Fitzgerald graduated from Boston College in 1992. He completed his DMD from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 1996 and continued there to pursue his orthodontic certificate which he received in 1998.

Dr. Fitzgerald has participated in numerous volunteer missions to provide dental care to impoverished regions in Nicaragua, Venezuela, Dominican Republic, and the Brazilian Amazon. For many years, he volunteered at “Bridge Over Troubled Waters” in Boston providing treatment for at-risk youth. Presently, Dr. Fitzgerald donates his time to Smiles Changes Lives to provide orthodontic treatment for children from low-income families.

Hannah Cooperman, DMD

THE NERDY STUFF Dr. Hannah Cooperman is originally from Westchester, NY. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Rochester before heading to Boston University, where she completed both a Master’s in Oral Health Sciences and her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD). She continued at BU to complete her Certificate of Advanced Graduate Study (CAGS) in Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Cooperman knew early on that dentistry was the path for her—after a visit to the National Museum of Dentistry at age 15, she was hooked. An internship at her own pediatric dental office sealed the deal, where she saw first-hand the impact that great care can have on a child’s life.

She is especially passionate about patient and family education and creating a welcoming, positive dental experience for every child. She’s also an active advocate for improving access to dental care, particularly for children with special healthcare needs.

THE FUN STUFF Dr. Cooperman loves exploring the outdoors and checking out new hiking trails around Boston with her fiancé Charlie. She’s also a big fan of dinner parties—hosting friends for a good meal is one of her favorite ways to unwind.

THE FINE DETAILS Soon after joining ABCD&O, Dr. Cooperman and her fiancé Charlie will be tying the knot!
