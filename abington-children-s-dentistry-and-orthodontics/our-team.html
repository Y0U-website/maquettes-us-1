Our Assisting Team

Paige

We originally met Paige through an externship program and after meeting her, we just knew she would be a great fit for the office! After graduating from Whitman – Hanson High School, Paige earned her CDA certificate from South Eastern Technical Institute. In her free time, you can find Paige shopping or skiing. What is Paige’s favorite part about working at ABCD&O? “Meeting new patients and helping them have a fun and memorable experience at the dentist! In her free time, you can find Paige shopping, skiing, or playing with her dog Ash.

Emily

Emily found her way into dentistry early in life because her mom is a dental hygienist at our sister practice, South Shore Children’s Dentistry! She earned her dental assisting and radiology license through Boston University and is now working her way towards becoming a dental hygienist. Born and raised in Braintree, you can find Emily walking her dogs or trying new workout classes and restaurants in her free time. What is Emily’s favorite part about working at ABCD&O? “All the girls here are so fun to work with. They keep me laughing and smiling all day long!”

Sarah

We were lucky to have Sarah, an Abington native, as an extern during her time at Southeastern Technical Institute. She loved the office so much; she decided to stay at ABCD&O as a Certified Dental Assistant! Sarah has also earned a Bachelor’s Degree from Bridgewater State University and is currently pursuing a degree in dental hygiene. In her free time, you can find Sarah at the gym, running, hanging out with friends, or watching movies with her cats, Larry & Louie. What is Sarah’s favorite part about working at ABCD&O? “The positive environment and the best team and patients ever!”

Jill
