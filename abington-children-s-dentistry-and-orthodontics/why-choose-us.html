THE REASON PARENTS HAVE TRUSTED US FOR YEARS

Why bring your child to Abington Children’s Dentistry and Orthodontics?

Proudly serving families of the greater Abington area, including Whitman, Hanson, Halifax, the Bridgewaters, Rockland, Hanover, and Pembroke.

Our primary focus is to spread light and love to our patients, their families, the community, and the environment. Kind, compassionate, and highly advanced pediatric dentistry is simply how we do it! Our practice’s treatment philosophy differs from others in that we believe in prevention and education for your child because their total wellbeing should be built on behaviors, not procedures. The lessons they’ll learn here will last them a lifetime.

Individualized attention for you and your child are a top priority. We have remained committed to being an independent practice, not influenced by the financial goals of a larger corporation. This means our family-owned small business will treat you like family, not a number.

The full individual is what we care about most, not just a mouthful of teeth. Your child’s beautiful smile is just a gateway to their soul so we aim to take good care of more than their pearly whites.

Why bring your child to Abington Children’s Dentistry and Orthodontics?

Our team is committed to helping raise a generation of children that love going to the dentist rather than fearing it, and that guiding principle shows up in everything we do. We love having fun and laughing with your children while they’re in our chairs just as much as we love educating and helping them maintain the best possible level of oral health. When you and your children walk through our doors, we hope you feel as warm as a summer day at the beach!

Step into our office.

We like to think of our office as the perfect combination of state-of-the-art equipment and small comforts that make going to the dentist enjoyable. Our treatment areas are decorated with fun themes and we give prizes to all our Smile Stars so that a trip to the dentist really does feel exciting.

We embrace digital x-rays and use pediatric-sized x-ray sensors along with up-to-date technology that gives us the ability to operate in a chartless environment. This allows us quick and easy access to your child’s important information all while giving them the most optimal care tailored to their age and size – not to mention helps us save a ton of trees.

While we’re all for the best possible early dental care, we also believe in an “Image Gently” approach. Though x-rays can be a necessary component of preventative health, we do believe there is a balance. We only take x-rays when we feel they are absolutely necessary and do not believe in routinely taking them.

Our values

The reason we do the work we do is that we love children and serving our community. It’s only natural that this extends into various outreach initiatives that directly benefit the kids and families on the South Shore of Massachusetts and beyond. Whether it’s a school educational visit or a mission trip to Mexico, we love leaving a positive mark on the world.

Our sister practice

South Shore Children’s Dentistry , is a partner in spreading light and love throughout the community! Drs. Grazioso, Kuczma, Choi, and Fraone work hard to bring children and families in the South Shore area the best, personalized care and attention available.
