What is a dental implant crown?

A dental implant crown, as described by our team, is a custom-made replacement tooth designed to look, function, and feel just like your natural teeth. It is connected to a dental implant, which is a small titanium post that functions as a replacement root for a tooth. Together, the implant and crown provide a secure, durable, and natural-looking solution for replacing a missing tooth.

How is a dental crown attached to a dental implant?

The attachment process involves several steps. First, the dental implant is surgically placed into your jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over a period of months. Once the implant is securely integrated, Dr. Sneha Naik adds an abutment, which is a small connector placed on top of the implant. The dental crown is carefully secured onto the abutment, providing a strong, permanent restoration.

Are dental implant crowns different from traditional crowns?

Yes! Dental implant crowns are different from crowns placed on natural teeth. Traditional crowns are placed on top of existing teeth that may have been altered or shaped to ensure a proper fit. Alternatively, a dental implant crown is anchored to an implant, making it an ideal option for replacing a completely missing tooth. Both options are custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural existing teeth.

Can dental implant crowns look like natural teeth?

Absolutely! One of the biggest benefits of dental implant crowns is their ability to mimic the appearance of natural teeth. Using advanced technology and techniques, Dr. Sneha Naik and her team ensure that each crown is customized to match the color, size, and shape of your existing teeth. The result is a seamless smile that you can confidently show off!

What is the lifespan of dental implant crowns?

With proper care, our high-quality restorations, including our dental implant crowns, are extremely durable and often last many years. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups with Dr. Sneha Naik effectively extend the life of your crown and maintain the health of the surrounding gum tissue. Many of our patients here at City Family Dental enjoy the benefits of their dental implant crowns for 10 to 15 years or longer.
