How We Treat Dental Emergencies

Treating your dental emergency starts with a phone call to our office. Once you inform a member of our team of your situation, we will not only schedule a time for you to come in but also provide first-aid tips to help you manage your pain. Upon your arrival, you will be escorted to a treatment room, where we will take digital scans of your smile, and Dr. Ma will perform a thorough examination of your affected tooth or damaged area.

After determining the severity of the problem, he will create a treatment plan and discuss the details with you. Same-day treatment is often available; however, if more extensive care is required, you may need to undergo a more complex procedure at a later date.
