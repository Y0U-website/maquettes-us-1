We are the only medical health provider in De Leon providing access to a wide array of primary health care services including medical and behavioral health care for the entire family.

Cross Timbers Health Clinics, now AccelHealth, began providing services to the community in 1967 and was formally chartered in 1986.

AccelHealth has a very rich history in De Leon, Texas and surrounding communities.

Medical services include: Primary Healthcare, Women’s Health, Family Planning, Immunization, Chronic Care, Laboratory Services and Hepatitis C Screening and Treatment. Along with Family Dental Care, AccelHealth offers Behavioral Health services including treatment for Addictions and Substance Use Disorders.

Our Medical and Behavioral Health clinic locations include De Leon, Brownwood and Stephenville. Dental services are provided at our Stephenville clinic.

During the past 54 years, AccelHealth has made significant progress in expanding access to care in Central Texas, particularly for those most in need.

We are an FTCA deemed Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC).

AccelHealth provides affordable health care for patients of all income levels through a sliding fee scale based on income as compared to Federal poverty guidelines.
