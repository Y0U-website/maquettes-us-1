Advantages of All-On-4 Implants

The benefits of All-On-4 implants include:

  • They’re cost-effective. Instead of paying for a single implant for each lost tooth, the patient replaces an entire dental arch at a single, pocket-friendly price.
  • All-On-4 implants treatment eliminates the need for multiple surgeries and visits.
  • All-On-4 implants keep the jawbone stimulated, preventing bone loss.
  • Implant-supported dentures are easier to care for as they don’t require removal during cleaning.
  • Most patients don’t require bone grafting to receive All-On-4 implants.
