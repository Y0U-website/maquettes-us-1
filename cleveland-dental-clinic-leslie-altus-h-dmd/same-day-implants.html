Same Day Implants Same day dental implants are the most convenient way to get a perfect smile. Now, you can get it all done in just one trip instead of spending weeks or months waiting for new teeth.

Traditional dental implants are generally the process with complicated procedures that obviously takes more time. The extracted tooth needs 3-4 months healing time and then finally the implant placement procedure completes in the duration of 5-10 months.

How do same day implants work?

On the same day, dental implants still need time for the gums and jaw to heal. This means that the crown cannot be transplanted until the mouth is completely healed.

You will receive a temporary tooth on the day of surgery. When the mouth is completely healed, permanent teeth will be sent to you to replace the temporary teeth. In other words, a bright smile on the first day and a long-lasting smile a few months later.

Why same day implants?

  • You get back that confidence of your glorious smile on that particular day.
  • The implants are easily fused to the bone.
  • The gum healing process is easier.
  • These implants help you get away with the bacteria.

Different types of same day dental implants

  • Single-tooth dental implant
  • Full-arch dental implant
  • Implant-supported dentures
