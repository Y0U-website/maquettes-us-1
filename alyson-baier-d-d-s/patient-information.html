Patient Safety

Our instrument sterilization equipment is maintained on a daily basis to Center for Disease Control standards.

Our sterilization follows all universal precautions in compliance with the OSHA guidelines and standards.

Our equipment is professionally tested and monitored.

Air filtration systems prevent contamination.

Patient Comfort

Our office has been designed and decorated specifically so that it doesn’t resemble a typical dental office.

Our surroundings will help minimize dental anxieties.

Relaxing music is throughout the office, with stereo headphones in all treatment rooms for additional relaxation.

We also have freshly laundered blankets in all patient rooms.
