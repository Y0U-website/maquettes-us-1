About Dr. Dague

Dr. Dague loves caring for patients of all ages across every area of dentistry, and she especially enjoys performing root canals and extractions. She stays current with the latest advances in dentistry and takes pride in providing gentle care in a comfortable environment — helping put even the most nervous patients at ease.

“There is nothing quite like the feeling of helping someone attain a healthy smile or have more confidence in their smile. It’s the best part of my job.”

Dr. Dague acquired Mike McCool’s practice when he retired in November 2018 and Dr. Jim Gimbel’s practice in December 2021. The practice is now conveniently located just off 53rd Street at 5335 Eastern Avenue in Davenport.

Outside the office, Dr. Dague and her husband, Mathias, live in Maquoketa, Iowa, near the Maquoketa Caves, where they were both born and raised. They have two young children, Evelyn and Elliott, a Labrador Retriever, and four cats. The family are avid outdoor enthusiasts who love to hike, canoe, camp, fish, and ski.

Comprehensive Dental Care from Dr. Jolene Dague in Davenport, IA

As a trusted family dentist in Davenport, IA , Dr. Jolene Dague offers a full range of care under one roof. From routine cleanings and exams through family dentistry to cosmetic dentistry that brightens and reshapes your smile, Dr. Dague tailors every treatment plan to your goals, comfort, and budget. Patients across Davenport and the greater Quad Cities appreciate her calm, unhurried approach and clear explanations at every step of the visit.

When dental problems arise, Dr. Jolene Dague delivers gentle, effective treatment — including root canals to relieve pain and save natural teeth, tooth extractions when a tooth cannot be saved, and emergency dental care for unexpected injuries and severe toothaches. For patients who want a straighter smile without traditional braces, she is a certified Invisalign provider offering clear, removable aligners.

Dr. Dague earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry and has cared for Davenport families since 2012. A member of the American Dental Association and the Iowa Dental Association, she invests in continuing education so her patients benefit from the latest techniques and technology. Whether you are bringing your child in for a first visit or restoring your own smile, you will find a welcoming, judgment-free environment at Dague Dental Solutions.
