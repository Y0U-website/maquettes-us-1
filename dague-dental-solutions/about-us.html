About Dague Dental Solutions

Welcome to Dague Dental Solutions , where your smile and overall health are our top priorities. Led by Dr. Jolene Dague in Davenport, IA, we’re committed to providing exceptional dental care to every patient in a warm, friendly environment. We believe prevention is the cornerstone of dental care, recognizing that a healthy mouth is essential to your overall well-being. Our state-of-the-art equipment, including infrared cavity-detecting cameras and digital x-rays, allows us to diagnose issues early and educate patients thoroughly on their oral health.

Our skilled team offers comprehensive services from routine check-ups to advanced procedures, combining cutting-edge technology with a gentle, personalized approach. As your neighbors and fellow community members, we’re invested in the health of Davenport and surrounding areas. Dr. Dague and our entire team look forward to building long-lasting relationships with our patients, guiding you towards a lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles through high-quality treatment and preventive care.

Dr. Jolene Dague, DDS

Dr. Jolene Dague opened her practice in Davenport in 2012. Over the years it’s grown to include two established local practices — Mike McCool’s, which she took over in 2018, and Dr. Jim Gimbel’s, in 2021. That means some of the families walking through our door have been coming to this address for decades, long before her name was on the sign.

She earned her bachelor’s degree from Luther College in 2004 and her dental degree from the University of Iowa in 2008, and belongs to both the American Dental Association and the Iowa Dental Association. She’s also Invisalign certified, and the practice uses digital X-rays, intra-oral cameras, and infrared cavity detection to catch problems early and show patients exactly what’s going on.

Dr. Dague treats patients of every age and handles the full range of care herself, from routine cleanings to root canals and extractions — procedures she particularly enjoys. Her focus stays on whoever’s in the chair: making sure even a nervous first-timer leaves more at ease than when they walked in.

Taylor, Dental Hygienist

Taylor’s favorite part of being a dental hygienist? The moment a patient walks out with a smile they feel confident about. For her, great dental care isn’t just about clean teeth — it’s about making sure every patient feels truly heard and well cared for from the moment they sit down.

At Dague Dental Solutions, Taylor brings a compassionate, determined approach to every appointment. She wants patients to know their needs come first, and that they’re in a place where the whole team genuinely has their back. That’s something she sees every day in Dr. Dague — a doctor who puts her heart and soul into her work and makes sure every single person who walks through the door gets the care they need.

Outside the office, Taylor is a full-time sports mom running a wonderfully busy schedule. Her kids keep her on the go with motocross, basketball, and wrestling — and she wouldn’t have it any other way. When things slow down, you’ll find her reading, enjoying live music, or planning her next beach trip (preferably somewhere in the Caribbean). At home, she’s kept company by Gemma, a gentle giant Rottweiler, and Sadie, an energy-filled Yellow Lab — and, of course, a good cup of coffee.
