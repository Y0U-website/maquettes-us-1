Dental Insurance & Financing

Flexible Dental Insurance and Financing Options for Your Care

Insurance and Billing

We accept most traditional insurance plans; contact our office to verify your plan’s acceptance. We don’t participate in Health Management Organizations (HMOs); however, we are happy to file insurance for your reimbursement as long as you are free to choose your dentist.

Payment Options

We accept checks, cash, or credit cards and offer a flexible payment plan through Cherry , our financial partner . Please see our Financial Coordinator for details.

We Accept Medicare

At Dague Dental Solutions in Davenport, IA, we proudly accept Medicare to ensure our senior patients receive the dental care they need. Our experienced team is dedicated to providing comprehensive, personalized care while helping you navigate your Medicare benefits for a stress-free experience. Let us help you maintain a healthy smile with the quality and affordability you deserve.
