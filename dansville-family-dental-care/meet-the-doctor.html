Meet Dr. Teachout

Welcome to my Dansville Family Dental Care practice!

For me, the comfort and satisfaction of my patients is foremost. I thrive to deliver superior service to every patient that steps into my clinic. Treating every patient with individual attention and extending suitable treatments which meet your dental concerns and expectations is my priority.

Professional and skilled services are custom made to meet your needs.

Dr. Rosemeire Santos-Teachout DDS, MS is the Dansville Family Dental Care Dentist who makes every smile unique. Her impeccable background, training, and reputation reflect her dedication to dental excellence.

Dr. Teachout was born in Brazil and received her Bachelor of Dental Surgery degree in 1992 from Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais, Brazil which is one of the most reputable as well known for their clinical and research Dental program in the State of Minas Gerais. After receiving her bachelor’s, she worked on her own private practice and community dental clinic. She also conducted Research on Antibody response to gum disease pathogens in individuals with Down syndrome.

Dr. Teachout has dedicated herself to provide superior and quality dental services to the families for 25 years.

Dr. Teachout is recognized and has been invited around the world including The World Health Organization to lecture about the Oral Care for Individuals with Developmentally Disabilities through the University of Rochester. She is also known for her Publications in textbooks, articles and various presentations. In addition to her position as a full-time General Dentist here at Dansville Family Dental Care.

Dr. Teachout strives to provide the highest standard of care and quality of work to her patient’s by using the latest techniques, technology and materials in dentistry today.

Recently, Dr. Teachout received Associate and a Bachelor of Ministry Degrees from Nation to Nation Christian University, sponsored by her home church Celebrate Family Church , Leicester, NY under the leadership of Pastor Eric Scott. ( See her teachings in her Youtube channel-Link in on top right of page)

Her kind and gentle way of delivering dentistry has won her a loyal patient following. Her faith and mission to console her patient’s soul as she cares for their oral health, makes her a unique dentist. She is a dentist who has your best interests at heart and backs it up with a distinguished record of experience.
