In the French language, le mieux means the best. Our goal is to help each of our guests achieve and maintain their best, healthiest smile. Our caring staff is committed to providing highest quality services, with genuine care for you and your comfort. Our team is dedicated to staying at the leading edge of our profession, while striving to understand the individual needs and desires of each patient.

A native of the Kansas City area, Dr. Lemieux was captivated by the charm of the Brookside neighborhood while interning in this very office as a dental student. After graduating from the University of Kansas with his undergraduate degree in biology, Dr. Lemieux attended the UMKC School of Dentistry. As a board-certified dentist, he joined the practice that operated here and went on to purchase it.

Dr. Katelyn Suter has been coming to our office almost her whole life, first as a pediatric patient here. Along the way, she became fascinated with teeth and pursued dentistry as her career.
