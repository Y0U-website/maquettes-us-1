DR. CUMMINGS

Dr. Cummings graduated from the Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2016. She came back to Alaska and worked in public health after becoming a dentist, giving back to the communities she grew up in.

In 2022 she was inducted as a Fellow into the International College of Dentists. Then in 2024 she earned her Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry. Joining our team in 2024 Dr. Cummings is excited to provide treatment for our patients sharing the same compassion in care as Dr.Earnest. In her free time Dr. Cummings enjoys the remote Alaskan wilderness with her husband and children.
