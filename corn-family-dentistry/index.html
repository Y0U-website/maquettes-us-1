The practice

Dr. Corn takes great pride in offering the most up-to-date dental techniques in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere. We know that every great smile – your smile – is the sign that we’ve done our job to provide the highest level of care in the industry.

Welcome to Corn Family Dentistry! Dr. Nathan A. Corn and his dedicated team strive to provide every patient with a personalized experience, and our hope is to exceed your expectations about dentistry every time you visit one of our locations either in Franklin or Edinburgh, IN.

You’ll notice that when you visit, Dr. Corn spends his time with you throughout your appointment. He’s not rushing off to tend to other patients because we believe that every person deserves our undivided attention while they are here.

Besides amenities to make your visit more pleasant such as massage chairs, blankets, neck pillows, and beverages, you’ll also find that both of our dental offices are equipped with state-of-the art technology designed to make your treatment procedures more comfortable. At our practice, dental care is truly tailored and personalized for each patient.
