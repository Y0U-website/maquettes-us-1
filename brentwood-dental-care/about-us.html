About us

No matter your dental needs, our team combines skill, precision, and artistry to deliver results you can trust.

  • Preventive Dentistry – Fluoride treatments, sealants, and personalized hygiene plans to prevent decay and disease.
  • Restorative Dentistry – Fillings, crowns, bridges, and dental implants to restore strength and function.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry – Teeth whitening, veneers, and smile design for a confident, beautiful appearance.
  • Emergency Dental Care – Same-day appointments for urgent issues such as pain, infection, or broken teeth.

Your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities.

We understand that visiting the dentist can make some patients nervous — that’s why we’ve designed a relaxing, welcoming environment from the moment you walk in.

We take the time to listen to your concerns, explain every treatment option, and tailor your dental plan to meet your specific needs and goals. Our gentle approach and use of modern technology ensure every visit is stress-free and effective.

At Brentwood Dental Care, we’re not just creating beautiful smiles — we’re building lasting relationships with our patients and our Bay Shore community.
