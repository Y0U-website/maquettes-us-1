What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the modern standard for replacing teeth lost to injury, decay, or disease. Functioning as an artificial tooth root, the implant is typically a small post made from biocompatible titanium that is surgically positioned into the jawbone where it fuses naturally over time (osseointegration).

This creates an incredibly stable and durable foundation for attaching a custom-made replacement tooth (dental crown) or other prosthesis. Unlike removable dentures or traditional bridges that rest on the gums or adjacent teeth, dental implants become a permanent part of your mouth, offering unmatched stability and a remarkably natural feel.

Benefits

Preserve Facial Structure

Dental implants safeguard against facial sagging by maintaining jawbone stimulation naturally. Replacing the entire tooth prevents bone deterioration, helping to preserve youthful facial features.

Achieve Enduring Results

With appropriate oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last for decades, or even a lifetime. Their integration with the jawbone offers a stable, durable foundation unmatched by alternatives that require periodic replacement.

Protect Overall Oral Health

Implants support neighboring natural teeth by preventing them from shifting into empty spaces. The implant post functions like a natural root, helping maintain proper alignment and reducing future dental risks throughout your mouth.

Experience Comfort & Stability

Designed to mimic natural teeth, implants deliver exceptional comfort and unwavering stability during daily life. Unlike removable dentures prone to slipping, implants are firmly anchored, enabling completely normal chewing and speaking.

Types of Dental Implants

  • Single Implants : Ideal for replacing one missing tooth without altering adjacent healthy teeth. A single titanium post supports a custom abutment and natural-looking dental crown perfectly matched to your smile.
  • Multiple Implant s: Used to replace several missing teeth, often supporting a fixed dental bridge or a partial denture securely. This preserves nearby natural teeth that would otherwise require preparation for traditional bridgework.
  • Full-Arch Restoration : Replaces an entire upper or lower arch of teeth using just a few strategically positioned implants (often four or more) to anchor a complete, fixed prosthesis.
  • Zygomatic & Pterygoid Implants : These advanced options provide solutions for patients lacking sufficient bone in the upper jaw for traditional implants. Implants anchor securely into the cheekbone (zygoma) or pterygoid plate instead to avoid extensive bone grafting procedures.
  • Remote Anchorage Implants : For specific complex cases with limited bone availability, specialized implant types and placement strategies utilize alternative bony sites for secure, long-term anchorage, expanding possibilities for fixed restorations.
  • Same-Day Custom Provisional Crown : Leveraging our unique in-office Exocad design and 3D printing workflow, we can often place an implant and attach a precisely fitted, aesthetically pleasing temporary crown during the very same surgical appointment for immediate results.
