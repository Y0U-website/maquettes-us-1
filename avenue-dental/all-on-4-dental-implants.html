All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 Dental Implants in Saint John

All-on-4 dental implants are permanently secured prostheses that resemble dentures. However, unlike dentures that can be removed, All-on-4 implants are secured to the jaw bone using permanently fitted dental implants.

What Are the Benefits of All-on-4 Implants?

Most patients prefer the increased stability provided by All-on-4 implants compared to dentures. Because they are fitted permanently in the mouth, All-on-4 implants are more comfortable to wear and allow the wearer to eat and speak more confidently. In addition, they don’t cause a gag reflex, and you won’t have to worry about clicking sounds that are common when you wear removable dentures.

Caring for Your All-on-4 Dental Implants

Take care to gently brush all the surfaces of your prosthetic arch, using a soft bristle brush so you don’t damage it. Floss daily in the areas around the implant and the gums to remove any food particles that may have accumulated.
