The practice

We deliver the highest quality general and restorative dental care in an atmosphere of warmth, kindness, and respect. Our caring staff welcomes children, adults, and seniors and guarantees the comfort of every member of your family. Our goal is not just to correct any dental problems but to help you prevent dental problems in the future.

If you are looking for a highly trained and experienced dentist in the Southern Tier, you have come to the right place. Dr. Gretta Tomb, a Corning native, is a national award-winning dentist for excellence in her field. She calls on over 40 years of professional experience to ensure that your dental needs are met in a comfortable and patient-focused environment.

By Word Of Mouth Dentistry uses the latest state-of-the-art equipment and cutting-edge technology and upholds the strictest sterilization techniques for our patients. We know that some patients may feel anxious about their visit to the dentist; that is why our goal is to make your visit with us as pain and anxiety-free as possible.

Our people set us apart. We’re passionate, dedicated, and friendly. Oh yeah, and we’re great at what we do!

For Dr. Tomb, dentistry is not just drilling and filling, but the perfect blend of art and science. Although she loves many facets of dentistry, Gretta most enjoys getting to know her patients. To her, the heart of patient care is taking a personalized approach because each patient and their smile is special.

Erica is a highly trained certified dental assistant who stays current with continuing dental education classes-a commitment she takes seriously to help patients maintain their dental health. Our patients appreciate her gentle and compassionate chairside manner.

Emilee is our Registered Dental Hygienist. She is patient-focused and driven to provide a personable experience along with the highest quality of care possible. Emilee enjoys building relationships with her patients to create a comfortable experience and educating them on the importance of their overall oral health.

Gretchen is a dedicated and skilled dental hygienist with 27 years of experience in providing comprehensive dental care and hygiene services. Gretchen is passionate about educating patients on the importance of oral health and implementing preventive dental care practices.
