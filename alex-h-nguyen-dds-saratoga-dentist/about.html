About Saratoga Dentist

Your Dentist

In 2003, I relocated the practice to the beautiful Saratoga, California. The office was built with a design to help make patients feel at ease when going to the dentist. Modeled after the ambience of a spa, it is often described by our patients as the best dental experience that they have ever had.

In the past 32 years my practice has evolved into a comprehensive treatment practice with emphasis on Preventive Care, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Implant Dentistry. Our patients span generations of families, professionals, as well as individuals from all walks of life. I believe that as a health care provider, my mission is to educate my patients on how to best take care of their dentition. When treatments are needed, we work together to achieve the best possible outcome that fits each individual situation.

As a life long learner I travel the world to study from the best educators in the field so that I can continue to bring the latest technology and treatment methods to my patients. Dentistry is evolving quickly with the incorporation of digital technology which helps make dental treatments more comfortable for the patients and helps achieve predictable results at the same time. I invite you to experience a personalized level of care with my team, and please send me a note in the “contact” page if you have any questions.

Our Office

Nestled in the foothill at Saratoga Village , our office is a tranquil space of eclectic design elements combined with high tech equipments of a modern dental office. From the moment you enter the door to the moment you leave, you will feel at ease and completely cared for.

Our treatment rooms are private and personalized for your comfort and safety. All treatments are done with the latest proven techniques using the finest materials and equipments including Digital Xrays, 3D Digital impression, Lasers, Computerized 3D Imaging Technology, Bite Analysis Devices, etc...
