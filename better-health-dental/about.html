About Dr. Sharifa Walker

The dentist who changed how you feel about dentistry.

Dr. Sharifa Walker brings over 20 years of expertise, a warm and personal approach, and an unwavering belief that every patient deserves to feel genuinely cared for — not just treated.

The most important part of dentistry? The relationship.

At Better Health Dental, we believe that when you trust your dentist, you take better care of your teeth. Dr. Walker has built her entire practice around earning and keeping that trust — with patience, transparency, and a genuine warmth that patients notice from the very first visit.

Gone are the days of cold, transactional dental chains where you never see the same face twice. Here, you are known. Here, you are seen. Here, you are heard. Here, you are valued.

More Than 20 Years. One Unwavering Purpose.

Originally from Huntsville, Alabama, Dr. Sharifa Walker graduated from Meharry Medical College in 2002 — one of the nation’s most storied historically Black medical institutions — and went on to complete her general practice residency at Bronx Lebanon Center in 2003.

Over the next two decades, dentistry took her down many rewarding paths — each one deepening her expertise, expanding her perspective, and affirming the single truth that has always guided her: the ability to change someone’s life through their smile is the greatest privilege in this profession.

After 20 years, Dr. Walker returned to private practice with a clear vision: to serve the beautiful city of Decatur, Georgia with the kind of personalized, expert dental care that large corporate chains simply cannot replicate. Better Health Dental is that vision made real.

She has called Georgia home for over 15 years, and she plans to be here — serving this community — for decades to come.

Meharry Medical College D.D.S. graduate, 2002. One of the nation’s premier historically Black medical colleges, Meharry instilled in Dr. Walker the philosophy of Service to Mankind that guides her practice today.

General Practice Residency Bronx Lebanon Center, 2003. Post-doctoral residency training in complex general dentistry, emergency care, and patient-centered treatment across diverse urban populations.

The values behind every single visit.

At Better Health Dental, how we care for you is just as important as what we do for you. These are the principles we build every appointment around — not policies, but genuine commitments.

Respect

We listen before we act. Your past experiences, your fears, your questions — all of it matters and shapes how we care for you. You will never feel rushed or dismissed in our office.

Understanding

Dental anxiety is real. We have heard "I hate the dentist" more times than we can count — and we have never taken it personally. We take the time to understand where you’re coming from, and we meet you there.

Care

From the moment you walk in until the moment you leave, your comfort and wellbeing are our highest priority. Dental wellness is whole-person wellness — and we treat it that way, every single day.

A dentist. A mother. A member of this community.

When Dr. Walker isn’t in the office, she’s spending time with her beloved daughter, traveling, entertaining, and enjoying life fully with the people she loves most.

She is not just your dentist — she is your neighbor. She cares deeply about Decatur and about building a practice that reflects and serves this community for generations.

She chose this city deliberately, and she is here to stay.
