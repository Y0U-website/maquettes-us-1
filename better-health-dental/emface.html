Two technologies. One extraordinary result.

EMFACE combines synchronized Radiofrequency (RF) energy and HIFES (High-Intensity Facial Electromagnetic Stimulation) delivered simultaneously through hands-free applicators placed on targeted facial zones. Your skin and muscles are treated at the same time, in the same session, with no preparation and no recovery.

Radiofrequency (RF)

Synchronized RF energy elevates tissue temperature to stimulate collagen and elastin production improving skin texture, firmness, and visibly reducing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles. For TMJ patients, RF also relieves symptoms associated with muscle spasms and improves local circulation around the jaw.

HIFES Stimulation

High-Intensity Facial Electromagnetic Stimulation induces involuntary muscle contractions activating and strengthening the facial muscles that lift and define your natural contours. For TMJ patients, HIFES directly targets the jaw muscles to treat dysfunction, ease associated pain, and support or increase mandibular range of motion.

Synchronized Results

Because RF and HIFES work simultaneously, each technology amplifies the other. You receive a treatment that addresses volume, lift, texture, tone, and when indicated functional jaw health. All in a single comfortable 30 35 minute appointment, with a brief break between applicator positions.
