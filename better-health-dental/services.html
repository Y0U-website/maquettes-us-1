Root Canal Therapy

We know those two words can sound alarming. They shouldn’t. Root canal therapy is a routine procedure that relieves pain — it doesn’t cause it. When a cavity advances deep into the tissue of a tooth, this treatment removes the source of infection, preserves your natural tooth, and sets you up for a comfortable recovery.

Performed with local anesthetic — you will be comfortable

Most procedures are completed in one or two appointments

A crown is typically placed after to protect the treated tooth

Far less uncomfortable than an untreated infection
