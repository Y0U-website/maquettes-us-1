Preventive Dentistry

Preventive Dentistry | Rolling Meadows

Preventive dentistry focuses on preventing oral health problems before they arise. It involves a combination of regular dental check-ups, professional dental cleanings at least two times per year, good dental hygiene practices at home, and education on proper oral health care.

Dr Sunkara, Rolling Meadows dentist at 32 Dental 4U knows that there is a strong link between dental health and overall physical health. With a simple daily routine, Dr Sunkara and her team will help you maintain your dental health.

What is a dental check-up?

One of the key components of preventive dentistry is regular dental check-ups. These appointments typically involve a comprehensive examination of the teeth, gums, and oral tissues and the tongue. During the dental exam, the dentist will look for signs of health and areas of tooth decay, gum disease, bone loss, oral cancer, and other oral health issues. Early detection of oral changes can help prevent more issues.

Dr Sunkara, your Rolling Meadows dentist will use dental instruments, low dose dental x-rays and intra-oral camera to perform her examination so she can give you a comprehensive diagnosis of your dental health.

Why do I need to get my teeth cleaned?

Good oral hygiene practices are also essential for preventive dentistry. Brushing the teeth twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, flossing daily, and using mouthwash can help remove plaque and bacteria from the mouth. Plaque is a sticky film that forms on the teeth and can lead to tooth decay and gum disease if not removed. Regularly removing plaque through proper oral hygiene practices can help prevent these issues from developing.

In addition to regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene practices, education on proper oral health care is also an important aspect of preventive dentistry. Patients can learn about the importance of a healthy diet, the risks associated with tobacco use, and how to properly care for their teeth and gums. This education can help patients make informed decisions about their oral health and prevent oral health issues from developing.

Preventive dentistry is important for people of all ages, but it is especially important for children. Children who develop good oral health habits at a young age are more likely to continue those habits into adulthood. Regular dental check-ups, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants can help prevent tooth decay in children and ensure that they have a healthy smile as they grow.
