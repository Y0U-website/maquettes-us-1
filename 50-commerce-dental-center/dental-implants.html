Dental Implants in Newark, NJ

A Long-Term Solution for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are titanium posts that act as artificial roots for missing teeth. They are placed in the jawbone and topped with crowns, bridges, or dentures—providing unmatched stability, function, and esthetics. They help preserve bone and protect surrounding teeth.

Whether replacing one tooth or many, implants are a reliable, long-lasting alternative to removable dentures or bridges—and they look and feel just like natural teeth.

Get your smile back with the confidence and comfort of dental implants.

What to Expect During Dental Implant Treatment

Evaluation & Planning

We assess your bone health, take digital scans, and review your goals. Using 3D planning tools, we design a treatment plan tailored to your anatomy and determine if bone grafting or additional prep is needed for long-term implant stability.

Implant Placement

The implant post is surgically inserted into the jawbone with the aid of digital guides for accuracy. Over the next few months, the bone integrates with the implant through a natural process called osseointegration, creating a strong foundation.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach your final crown, bridge, or denture. The result is a fully functional and esthetic tooth replacement that looks and feels just like the real thing—restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Precision-Guided Care From Start to Finish

We use CBCT 3D imaging, surgical guides, and digital planning software to ensure every implant is placed with precision. This reduces risk, improves healing, and results in a restoration that functions comfortably and blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Every case is custom-planned—from bone assessment to restoration type—so you can expect efficient healing and long-term success, whether you’re replacing one tooth or an entire arch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with healthy gums and sufficient bone can qualify. We’ll evaluate your health and jaw structure during your consultation.

Is the implant procedure painful?

No. It’s done under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort—similar to a tooth extraction.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants can last 20+ years. They’re one of the most durable and reliable options in dentistry.

What if I need a bone graft?

If your jaw lacks bone support, we can perform a graft before implant placement to ensure stability and success.

Can implants replace multiple teeth?

Yes. Implants can support a single crown, a bridge, or even a full arch denture depending on your needs.
