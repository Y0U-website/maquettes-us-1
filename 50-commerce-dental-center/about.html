Our Story

Dr. Saul Weiner founded 50 Commerce Dental Center to bring full-scope, high-quality dental care to both residents and professionals in the heart of Newark. With more than 40 years of experience as a prosthodontist and dental educator at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Weiner envisioned a practice where advanced treatment, patient comfort, and education all come together.

Today, we’re proud to offer everything from routine cleanings to complex implant restorations under one roof — with a team that puts your well-being first.
