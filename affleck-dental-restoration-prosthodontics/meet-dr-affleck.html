Meet Doctor Affleck

Dr. Michael S. Affleck, is a general family dentist and prosthodontist , a specialist that places perfect fitting dental prostheses. He practiced in Ogden, UT from 2001 to 2016, and has been practicing in Clearfield, UT since 2016

Dr. Affleck completed dental school at the University of Iowa, then completed three years of additional training as a prosthodontist — a specialist who fixes severely damaged teeth and replaces missing teeth. He spends roughly half of his time doing general dentistry. The other half of his time is dedicated to his specialty of Dental implants, dentures, veneers, crowns, and bridges.

Dental Veneer Specialist

Dr. Affleck received additional specialty training on the prepping and placement of veneers. He has successfully completed more than one hundred dental veneer jobs that include composite, zirconia, porcelain and Lumineer veneers. This is a growing area of specialty and emphasis at Affleck Dental.
