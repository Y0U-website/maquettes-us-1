Welcome New Patients!

Welcome to Your New Dental Home in Cornelius, OR

Choosing a new dentist is an important decision, and we’re excited to welcome you to Cornelius Dental Clinic. Our team is committed to making your first visit comfortable, informative, and stress-free. Whether you’re new to the area or simply looking for a dentist you can trust, we’re here to support your long-term oral health. We proudly serve individuals and families throughout Cornelius, OR, and surrounding communities including Tualatin, Forest Grove, Hillsboro, and Washington County.

What to Expect on Your First Visit

Warm Welcome & Office Tour

Our friendly team will greet you, help you get settled, and review your health history. We’re happy to answer any questions you may have about your visit, insurance, or treatment options.

Comprehensive Dental Exam

Dr. Emerson Rowley will perform a gentle, thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health to identify any current concerns or areas to monitor.

Digital X-Rays (If Needed)

We use low-radiation digital imaging to capture clear, detailed views of your teeth and jaw, allowing for accurate diagnosis and early detection of dental issues.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Dr. Rowley will review any findings, explain your options clearly, and create a personalized treatment plan designed to support your long-term oral health and goals.

With comprehensive services under one roof, a family-friendly environment, and a team that truly cares, it’s easy to see why families throughout Cornelius, OR trust us with their smiles.
