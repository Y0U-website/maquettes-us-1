Dr. Benjamin Halpern - Periodontist

Dr. Benjamin Halpern is a Periodontist specializing in the treatment of periodontal disease and dental implants.

Dr. Halpern grew up in Martinez CA. He attended University of California San Diego where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry and Cell Biology. He was involved in many extracurricular activities and volunteered in the pre dental society serving as a dental assistant in community clinics. He was also a member of the rowing team as well as captain of the water ski team.

Dr. Halpern then attended the University of California San Francisco where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. He continued his education at UCSF and completed his residency in Periodontics as well as his Masters of Science in Oral & Craniofacial Sciences. He is also Board Certified in Periodontology and Dental Implant Surgery.

Dr. Halpern is currently an active member of the American Academy of Periodontology as well as the International Team for Implantology.

In addition to dentistry, he enjoys reading, hiking, skiing and is always ready to play a board or card game

Dr. Kathrina Sarmiento

Dr. Kathrina Sarmiento comes from a Navy family. As a child of a serviceman, she had the unique privilege of growing up in multiple countries. She received her bachelor’s degree in Public Health from the University of Southern California, and continued at USC for her dental training, graduating from The Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry.

Dr. Sarmiento earned her dental degree through the Navy Health Professions Scholarship Program and joined the Navy following graduation. She served as a dental officer and provided care to Marines and Sailors in Okinawa, Japan and San Diego, CA. After completing her service with the military, she moved to the Bay Area to be closer to family.

Dr. Sarmiento believes in the importance of a patient’s dental experience, and has committed herself to provide high quality care in a conservative, educative, and comfortable manner. During her free time, she enjoys restaurants, concerts, binge-worthy TV shows, travel, and her puppy, Cruz.

Dr. Eilkay Samadi

I moved to America when I was 15 and only had one goal in mind: I wanted to become a Dentist. I always loved dentistry because it is a combination of care, passion, science, and art. I truly enjoy helping people and I love working with my hands. With the many obstacles I had in front of me, I remained focused and promised to make my 15 year-old self reach her dream.

After graduating with Magna Cum Laude in Bachelor’s Degree from California State University, San Marcos, I attended University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry where I earned my Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree. I then matched into an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency offered by NYU Langone Health, where I did a one year residency doing more complex and advanced procedures.

My passion is driven by building relationships with my patients and I take pride in educating them. I am here to help improve your oral health and maintain it. I listen to your needs and will come up with a treatment plan that benefits you the most.

My favorite activity is hiking. I love being active while enjoying the nature. I also like traveling to new places and becoming exposed to different cultures. Working out is a great way for me to destress from daily challenges.
