About Us

Drs. Kristian (K.C.) and Kaitlin Dietz met at the University of Richmond in Virginia where they completed their undergraduate studies. They decided to make dental school a family affair and got married while pursuing their dental degrees at Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, also in Richmond, VA.

The Dietz’s spent the next five years in Joplin, MO practicing together at a federally qualified health center caring for the under-served. They moved to Bentonville in 2014 after opening Bright Ridge Dental, with a passion for Bentonville family dentistry! Brecken, 11, Zachary, 9, and Emma, 4, keep them busy outside of the office!

In late 2018, Dr. Amy Bell joined the practice. She is an experienced dentist and NWA-native who shares our conservative treatment philosophy, gentle touch, and commitment to quality dentistry!

Dr. Deborah King, DDS

Dr. Deborah King is an Arkansas native. She graduated in 2004 from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, TN. She then moved to Atlanta, GA where she practiced for 14 years. In 2018, she and her family moved back to Arkansas. With a strong focus on patient education, Dr. King believes that understanding your oral health and its impact on overall health is the foundation of long-term wellness.

In addition to comprehensive general dentistry, Dr. King has advanced training in sleep-related dental care for all ages, adult oral conscious sedation, Invisalign and therapeutic Botox®. These services allow her to help patients manage dental anxiety, improve comfort, and address concerns such as jaw tension and sleep-related issues in a calm, supportive environment.

Dr. King and her husband have 3 children. She enjoys cooking, hot yoga and watching her kids play soccer and basketball.
