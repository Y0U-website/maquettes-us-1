Full Mouth Dental Implants &…

  • Am I too old for full mouth dental implants? Age alone is rarely a determining factor for implant candidacy. We ve successfully placed implants in patients well into their 80s and 90s. The key factors are overall health, bone density, and healing capacity. During your consultation, we ll evaluate your individual situation and discuss whether implants are right for you, regardless of your age.
  • Can I get implants if I have gum disease or bone loss? Gum disease and bone loss don t automatically disqualify you from implant treatment, but they do require careful evaluation and possibly additional procedures. We offer bone grafting and socket preservation services to rebuild lost bone structure when necessary. Our advanced techniques, including All-on-4 protocols, can often work around areas of bone loss to achieve successful results.
  • How painful is the full mouth implant procedure? Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is. We offer comprehensive sedation dentistry options to ensure your comfort during surgery, and our precise digital planning minimizes surgical trauma. Post-operative discomfort is typically manageable with prescribed medications and usually subsides within a few days to a week.
  • Will I have teeth during the healing process? In most cases, yes. We can often provide immediate temporary teeth on the same day as your implant surgery, so you won t leave our office without a smile. These temporary restorations allow you to maintain your appearance and basic function while your implants heal. Your final, permanent restoration will be placed once healing is complete.
  • How do I care for my full mouth implants? Caring for full mouth implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings are essential. We ll provide you with specific instructions and may recommend special cleaning tools. Regular checkups at our office allow us to monitor your implants and provide professional maintenance to ensure their longevity.
