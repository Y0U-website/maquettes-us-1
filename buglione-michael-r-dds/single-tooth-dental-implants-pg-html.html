Single Tooth Dental Implants

  • Is the implant procedure painful? Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the implant procedure is. We use local anesthesia to ensure you feel no pain during surgery, and many patients report that the discomfort afterward is less than they experienced with the original tooth extraction. We also offer sedation options for anxious patients to ensure maximum comfort throughout the procedure.
  • Can anyone get a dental implant? Most people with good general health and adequate bone support are candidates for dental implants. Age is rarely a limiting factor we ve successfully placed implants in patients from teenagers to those in their 90s. During your consultation, we ll evaluate your bone density, overall health, and specific circumstances to determine if implants are right for you. If bone grafting is needed, we offer advanced bone grafting services to create ideal conditions for implant success.
  • How do I care for my dental implant? Caring for a dental implant is very similar to caring for a natural tooth. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings are essential for long-term success. We ll provide you with specific instructions and may recommend special cleaning tools. Regular checkups at our office allow us to monitor your implant and provide professional maintenance to ensure its longevity.
  • Will my dental implant look natural? Yes, when properly planned and executed, dental implants are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. Our custom restorations are designed to match your existing teeth in color, shape, and size. We use high-quality materials and precise digital technology to ensure your implant crown blends seamlessly with your natural smile. Many patients report that even they forget which tooth is the implant.
  • What happens if I don t replace my missing tooth? Leaving a space where a tooth once was can lead to several problems over time. Adjacent teeth may shift into the empty space, affecting your bite and potentially requiring orthodontic treatment. The underlying bone will gradually deteriorate without the stimulation provided by a tooth root, which can affect your facial structure and make future tooth replacement more complex. Additionally, the opposing tooth may over-erupt, creating bite problems and increasing the risk of damage.
