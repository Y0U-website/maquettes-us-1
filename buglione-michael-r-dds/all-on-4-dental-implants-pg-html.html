The All-on-4 Dental Implant Process

At Vestal Dental Associates, we ve refined our All-on-4 process to ensure maximum comfort, precision, and satisfaction:

1. Comprehensive Consultation

Your journey begins with a thorough examination using our advanced imaging technology . We ll evaluate your oral health, bone structure, and discuss your goals to determine if All-on-4 is right for you.

2. Personalized Treatment Planning

Using our Digital Dental Implant Treatment Planning system, our dentists conduct a virtual surgery before ever touching your mouth. This allows for precise implant placement and predictable results.

3. Implant Surgery

On your procedure day, we ll ensure your comfort with sedation dentistry options. The four implants are strategically placed typically two straight implants in the front and two angled implants in the back of the jaw to maximize stability while avoiding sensitive anatomical structures.

4. Same-Day Teeth Placement

Using our CEREC One-Visit Dentistry system, we ll attach a temporary yet fully functional prosthesis to your implants that same day, allowing you to leave with a complete set of teeth.

5. Healing and Final Restoration

After a healing period of 3-6 months, your final custom restoration will be placed, meticulously designed to ensure perfect fit, function, and esthetics for long-lasting results.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Am I a candidate for All-on-4 dental implants? Most adults with multiple missing teeth or failing dentition can qualify for All-on-4 implants. The technique was specifically designed to work with minimal bone volume, making it suitable for patients who may not be candidates for traditional implants. During your consultation at Vestal Dental Associates, we ll perform a comprehensive evaluation to determine if All-on-4 is right for you or if another dental implant solution would be more appropriate.
  • How long does the All-on-4 procedure take? The actual surgical procedure typically takes 2-3 hours per arch. Because of our CEREC technology , we can often place a temporary full set of teeth the same day as your implant surgery. The entire process, from initial consultation to receiving your final, permanent restoration, usually takes 4-6 months, as we need to allow time for proper healing and bone integration.
  • Are All-on-4 dental implants painful? We prioritize your comfort throughout the All-on-4 process. The procedure is performed under appropriate anesthesia, and we offer sedation options to ensure you feel little to no discomfort. Many patients report that the procedure was much more comfortable than they anticipated. After surgery, some mild discomfort is normal and can be managed with prescribed pain medication. Most patients return to work and normal activities within a few days.
  • How do I care for All-on-4 dental implants? One of the benefits of All-on-4 implants is their relatively simple maintenance. You ll brush twice daily and floss regularly using tools specifically designed for implant-supported prosthetics. We ll provide detailed care instructions and recommend specific products for cleaning around your implants. Regular professional cleanings and check-ups at Vestal Dental Associates are essential to maintain your implants health and longevity. With proper care, your All-on-4 implants can last a lifetime.
  • What s the difference between All-on-4 and traditional dental implants? Traditional full-arch restoration might require 6-8 implants per arch and often necessitates bone grafting for patients with bone loss. All-on-4 uses just four strategically placed implants, with the back implants angled to maximize contact with available bone. This innovative approach typically eliminates the need for bone grafting and allows for immediate placement of teeth. You can learn more about different implant types in our blog post about dental implant procedures .
