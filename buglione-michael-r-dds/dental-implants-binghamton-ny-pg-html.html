  • Do Dental Implants Feel Like My Real Teeth? Absolutely! Your new dental implant will look, feel, and chew just like your normal teeth. This will allow you to enjoy all the foods you once did and give you a new sense of confidence every time you smile.
  • Will I Miss Any Work When Getting Dental Implants? Typically, patients will take anywhere from a few hours to a few days off from work for the healing process. Your procedure can be scheduled before a weekend to give you a few extra days of rest.
  • How do I Care for My Dental Implants? You will care for your dental implants just as you would your normal teeth. This includes brushing, flossing, and routine cleanings by your dentist.
  • What if I am Missing a Lot of Teeth? If you have many teeth missing in your mouth, our doctors may suggest an immediate full-arch replacement solution. This is a great solution for those who need the quantity of teeth normally provided by a denture, but don’t wish to deal with the inconveniences that come along with dentures.
