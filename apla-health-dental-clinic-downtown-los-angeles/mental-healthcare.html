Mental Healthcare

Mental Healthcare Mental health plays an important role in your health overall.

At APLA Health, we provide a comprehensive range of mental and behavioral health services fully integrated within our Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) model of care.

Our multidisciplinary team includes licensed clinical psychologists, licensed clinical social workers, and licensed marriage and family therapists, as well as Associate Clinical Social Workers (ACSWs) and Associate Marriage and Family Therapists (AMFTs) who deliver care under the supervision of licensed professionals in accordance with state and federal regulations.
