Board of Directors

A unique aspect of a Federally Qualified Health Center is that it must be governed by a community board composed of a majority (51 % or more) of health center patients who represent the population served. The Catherine’s governing Board of Directors define and preserve the mission of the organization, while serving as the voice of the community.
