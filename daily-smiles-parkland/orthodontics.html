Orthodontic Treatment Options

At Daily Smiles in Parkland and Pembroke Pines, we offer various orthodontic treatment options to cater to your specific needs. Our experienced orthodontist will conduct a thorough examination to determine the best approach for your unique situation. Typical orthodontic treatments include:

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just about appearance; it’s about function and health. When your teeth and jaws align correctly, they work harmoniously, making eating and speaking more comfortable and efficient. The advantages of orthodontic treatment extend beyond aesthetics, ultimately contributing to a healthier and more confident you.

  • Braces : Traditional metal braces have evolved and are more comfortable and efficient. They are an effective solution for addressing a wide range of orthodontic issues.
  • Invisalign : Clear aligners offer a discreet and comfortable way to straighten teeth without needing metal brackets and wires. They are nearly invisible and can be removed for eating and oral hygiene.
  • Lingual Braces : These braces are attached to the back of your teeth, making them virtually invisible to others.
  • Orthodontic Appliances : In some cases, specialized orthodontic appliances may correct specific issues, such as expanders or retainers
