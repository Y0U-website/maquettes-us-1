Award Winning Service

Access Dental Care has been providing award-winning service and care for twenty-five years. Some of our latest awards include receiving:

2023 American Dental Association’s Humanitarian of the Year Award

The ADA Humanitarian Award is a recognition open to member dentists who have distinguished themselves by giving a minimum of 10 years to improving the oral health of underserved populations in the United States. Bill was selected from over 15,000 practicing dentists.

2023 Helen C. “Holly” Riddle Distinguished Service Award, from the NC Council on Developmental Disabilities

The Helen C. "Holly" Riddle Distinguished Service Award recognizes professionals who have made lasting contributions towards improving opportunities, breaking down barriers and promoting increased quality of life for people with Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities in North Carolina.

Access Dental Delivers

Over the past many years, as Access Dental Care has provided high-quality dental care throughout the state of North Carolina, we’re constantly asked by organizations to help them establish their own mobile dentistry practice.

While we were happy to assist in any way we could, it became obvious that the need and interest extended well-beyond North Carolina and was growing with each passing year. In order to address this need and interest, in early 2023, Access Dental Care created a separate division within our organization, Access Dental Delivers.

Access Dental Delivers is managed within the Access Dental Care organization, and has two major initiatives:

  • To provide consulting services to those individuals and/or organizations interested in establishing their own mobile dentistry.
  • To provide a turnkey mobile dentistry delivery system consisting of a fully-upfitted new vehicle (box truck) along with all the operatories of dental equipment mobilized for easy transport.
