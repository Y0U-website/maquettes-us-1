Exclusive Mobile Delivery

Access Dental Delivers has developed and mastered a unique mobile delivery system that can help you overcome the barriers to delivering oral healthcare inside schools, health facilities and other locations.

Our custom designed trucks help you transport all your operatory equipment to any location. All the dental equipment is designed to be easily rolled into an alternate location, so that you can treat patients where they are.
