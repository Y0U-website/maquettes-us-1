Meet Our Doctor

Tracy Chan, DDS

Welcome! Dr. Tracy Chan has been serving the Bay Area to provide the most comfortable and personalized dental care for over two decades. She graduated from the prestigious UCSF School of Dentistry and has worked in private practice ever since performing a wide scope of dental procedures. Now, she is excited to invite you into her new practice-Chestnut Dental Care.

Dr. Chan provides patients with the highest level of care. Her clinical expertise in dentistry is coupled with her genuine concern for the unique needs of each patient. She embraces the opportunity to know her patients and offer them the means to a healthy smile through both education and care.

In the field of cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Chan is known as an artist with a keen eye for detail. She is highly trained in the most advanced cosmetic dental treatments. She invites you into her practice to join a long-standing tradition of clinical excellence.
