Vaccination Efforts

  • Reduce transportation and access challenges to vaccination services.
  • Foster community connections to promote health equity and preventive care.

San Ysidro Health offers COVID-19 and flu vaccines for everyone, regardless of insurance status.

Learn More About COVID-19 Resources Here Check out our calendar for upcoming public community vaccination events near you. In-Home Vaccination Program Need vaccines but can’t leave your home? We bring the protection straight to you, at no cost and no insurance required. Our Homebound Vaccination Program provides essential vaccines for those with health concerns, mobility limitations, or transportation barriers. *Availability may vary. Age and eligibility requirements apply.

  • Service available in select Central, South, and East San Diego ZIP codes.
  • Minors must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian ‍
  • COVID-19
  • Flu (during flu season)
  • Tdap (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis)
  • Hepatitis A
  • Pneumococcal
  • Shingles
  • Monkeypox
