Pay My Bill

As a San Ysidro Health patient, you can pay your bill online. For patients with a MySanYsidroHealth patient portal account, click the Log In button below. By accessing MySanYsidroHealth, you can also view your account balance, set up automatic payments, and get an estimate for your care. If you have not yet signed up for a MySanYsidroHealth account, we encourage you to register today in 5 minutes by clicking here . You also have an option to pay as a guest by clicking the second button below. Want to make a payment another way? Scroll down for more information on paying by phone or via mail.

Want to get a payment estimate?

Patients without a MySanYsidroHealth account can get an estimate for how much their care will cost. Click here to access this information. Please note that estimates are only available for certain services like annual wellness exams, physicals, and well child visits.

Need help?

Sometimes the payment process can be confusing.

For help, including checking your account balance, setting up a payment plan, and general financial assistance, call our patient financial specialists at (619) 205-6327. Staff are available to support your needs Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. PT.
