Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures might be a good option for you if:

If you are not a candidate for dental implants right away, you may become one after some preliminary procedures.

  • You are missing all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches OR your remaining natural teeth are in poor condition.
  • You are healthy enough to undergo minor surgery.
  • You are free of active gum disease and other conditions that might adversely affect your implants.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

With over half a million dental implants placed each year, these life-changing prosthetics are quickly becoming one of the most popular ways to replace missing teeth. When combined with the convenience of dentures and thanks to their unique placement inside the jawbone, this modern tooth loss solution provides an amazing array of benefits that you can’t get with dentures alone! Implant dentures have the ability to dramatically improve your confidence, health, and your quality of life in one amazing treatment!

Day-to-Day Benefits

You won’t have to wait long to appreciate all the wonderful perks of implant dentures near Abingdon. These dynamic prosthetics boast a variety of daily benefits you won’t find with traditional dentures, including:

  • The restored ability to chew all your favorite foods.
  • Easier to brush and floss than removable dentures.
  • The ability to smile with confidence, knowing your teeth look realistic and will never accidentally slide out of place.

Health Benefits

In addition to the wonderful daily benefits of your new implant dentures, there are many benefits to your oral and overall, health, too. These include:

  • Keeping a strong, healthy jawbone and avoiding deterioration and bone loss.
  • Supporting any remaining surrounding teeth by protecting the jawbone and preventing drift.
  • Allowing you to once again eat a nutritious diet and thereby reducing your risk of malnutrition.
  • Not causing tooth sensitivity or gum sores like traditional dentures do.
  • Easier cleaning means you’ll have better oral health which leads to better overall health.

Long-Term Benefits

Finally, temporary solutions like traditional dentures and dental bridges can’t come close to the long-term benefits of implant dentures, such as:

  • An impressive 95 % success rate, even 10 years after placement.
  • Your implant dentures won’t need to be replaced every five to ten years like traditional dentures and dental bridges.
  • Implant dentures could potentially save you a lot of time and money when compared to other options.
