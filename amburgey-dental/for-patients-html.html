Getting Started with Our Welcoming Dental Office

Dental appointments shouldn’t be complicated. At Amburgey Dental, we want to take the stress out of that whole process. On this page, you’ll find information that you should know before visiting us for the first time. Please take a moment to go over this information so that you can be better prepared to have an efficient, positive dental experience with us.

Your First Dental Visit

Your first appointment will start with a warm greeting from our front desk staff. They’ll make sure you’re comfortable during the very short time you’ll be waiting. You’ll soon meet with Dr. Amburgey and one of our hygienists, who will get to know you and your smile goals before examining your mouth, discussing their findings with you, and, if need be, developing a treatment plan. We’ll provide educational materials to help you fully understand any treatment we recommend.

New Patient Forms

In order for our team to begin getting to know you, we must collect some information from you first, such as your medical and dental history and your insurance information. We will text the new patient forms to you in advance of your appointment or you can complete these forms in our dental office.

Dental Insurance Welcome

If you have dental insurance, rest assured that you’ll be able to use it to save here at Amburgey Dental. We can help you take full advantage of your benefits and ensure that the care your smile needs is affordable. We’ll even file claims on your behalf! If at any point you need help understanding your coverage, just ask one of our expert team members.

We proudly accept Delta Dental.

Ask our friendly team if we take your plan.

CareCredit Financing Available

At Amburgey Dental, we realize that paying for the entire cost of treatment all at once may not be feasible for some patients. That’s why we have partnered with CareCredit, which lets you break up your payment into smaller monthly installments that are much easier on your wallet.
