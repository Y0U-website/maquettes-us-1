The practice

Located at 54 Colchester Ave we provide premium dental services such as Wax-ups, Dentures, Frameworks, Relines, flexible Partials, Flippers and expert quality repairs for them. In addition we offer local pick-up for cases. We have delivered quality dental services for nearly 60 years and we strive to ensure that your Dentures, Wax-ups, Relines, Partials, or Flippers feel like a natural extension…

We are a proud associate of the Donated Dental Services which provides free donated Dentures and Partials to those less fortunate.

Vermont’s oldest Removable Dental Laboratory. Founded by Joseph Wark [CDT] and his wife Nyla Wark. They started the process of ensuring all Vermonters had a beautiful smile regardless of what life threw at them. We pride ourselves in having provided so many with the ability to smile without worry or shame. Since it’s founding in 1967 the Wark family have always provided exceptional quality…

Brian Wark Owner and Lead Technician of Burlington Dental Laboratory has been a fixture of dental expertise for decades. In addition he has been doing removable dental prosthetics for over 40 years. He is a proud graduate of the Connecticut School of Dental Technology.

Tammy Wark Dental Technician, and wife to Brian Wark. She has been doing removable dental prosthetics for over 30 years. Working closely with Brian to deliver premium dental prosthetics to those that need them.
