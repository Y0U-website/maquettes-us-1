Healthy smiles start with routine care

Great dental care feels better when it’s truly built around you! Our team focuses on prevention, early detection, and long-term oral health. We offer general dentistry that adapts to your goals, your schedule, and your comfort level. At Buffalo Family Dentistry, it starts with a genuine conversation and ends with an educated plan customized for you!

Preventative Cleanings and Exams

At Buffalo Family Dentistry, our experienced and compassionate hygienists and doctors provide thorough, gentle routine exams and cleanings in a comfortable, welcoming environment. Regular preventive dental visits are important because they help detect problems early, prevent cavities and gum disease, and keep your smile healthy for a lifetime.

Pediatric, family friendly dentistry

We love helping children discover that going to the dentist can be a fun, positive experience. By creating a welcoming, compassionate environment and establishing a dental home at an early age, we build the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Deep Cleanings and Periodontal Treatment

Healthy gums are essential to your overall health. If signs of gum disease are present, we provide gentle, effective deep cleanings and personalized periodontal treatment to stop the progression of disease, restore gum health, and help you keep your natural teeth for years to come.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can happen when you least expect them, and we’re here when you need us most. Whether you’re experiencing a toothache, broken tooth, swelling, or dental trauma, our team will provide prompt, compassionate care to relieve your pain and restore your smile.

Treatments

Most common- At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we offer comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages using the latest technology and a personalized approach. Explore some of our most common treatments below, and let our experienced team help you achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Straighten your smile with Invisalign clear aligners the comfortable, nearly invisible alternative to metal braces. Using a series of custom-made aligners, we’ll gently and predictably move your teeth into alignment while fitting seamlessly into your everyday life. At Buffalo Family Dentistry, your treatment is carefully planned and monitored every step of the way, helping you achieve a healthier, more confident smile with comfort and confidence.

Retainers

Retainers are the key to protecting your new smile and preventing teeth from shifting over time. With our digital scanner, we can create new retainers without messy impressions and conveniently ship them directly to your home making replacement easy, comfortable, and hassle-free.

Crowns/Bridges

Crowns and bridges help restore and protect damaged or missing teeth, bringing back strength, function, and a natural-looking smile. Custom-designed to fit your bite and blend beautifully with your existing teeth, they provide a durable solution for teeth that need extra support.

Veneers

Veneers are a minimally invasive way to transform your smile by improving the appearance of teeth that are discolored, misshapen, worn, or chipped. Custom-designed to look natural and beautiful, veneers can help you achieve the confident smile you’ve always wanted with a carefully planned streamlined process.

Teeth whitening

Achieve a brighter, more confident smile with professional-grade whitening tailored to your needs. We offer Pola in-office whitening for fast, effective results with reduced sensitivity in less than an hour, along with Opalescence professional take-home options for convenient whitening from the comfort of your home.

Therapeutic & Cosmetic Tox

At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we offer Xeomin and Dysport treatments to help relax overactive jaw muscles, relieve TMJ-related tension, and enhance facial aesthetics. Each treatment is customized to your goals, whether you’re seeking comfort from jaw discomfort or a refreshed, natural-looking appearance.

Composite tooth colored fillings

Composite fillings are a durable, natural-looking solution to repair cavities, chips, and minor tooth damage while preserving the beauty of your smile. These white, tooth-colored restorations blend seamlessly with your natural teeth for a comfortable and confident result.

Sealants

Dental sealants are a simple, preventive treatment that helps protect the grooves and hard-to-reach areas of back teeth from cavities while making the tooth surface easier to clean. By creating a protective barrier over the chewing surfaces, sealants help reduce the risk of decay and keep smiles healthier for years to come.

Drill Free Dentistry

At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we offer innovative drill-free dentistry options to help treat early cavities while preserving healthy tooth structure whenever possible. Treatments such as Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF), ICON resin infiltration, and Curodont can help stop or reverse early cavity progression and provide comfortable, minimally invasive alternatives to traditional treatment. Your provider will evaluate your unique needs and discuss whether one of these options is right for you.

Night Guards and Mouth Guards

At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we provide custom oral appliances designed to protect your smile and improve your comfort, including night guards for clenching and grinding, sleep apnea appliances, and athletic mouthguards. Using our digital scanner, we create a precise, comfortable fit without the need for messy traditional impressions.

Root canals

At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we provide comfortable, precise root canal therapy using advanced 3-D CBCT-guided technology to enhance diagnosis, treatment planning, and accuracy. This technology allows us to carefully treat infected teeth while preserving your natural smile and helping relieve dental pain.

Dental Implant Restorations

Dental implant restorations are a durable, natural-looking solution to replace missing teeth and restore your smile’s function, comfort, and confidence. At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we custom-design implant crowns, bridges, and dentures to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth for a beautiful, long-lasting result.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer superior stability, comfort, and function by securely attaching to dental implants. Our doctors will evaluate your smile, bone health, and goals to determine whether a hybrid fixed denture, locator-retained overdenture, or another implant-supported restoration is the best solution for your unique needs.

Extractions using CBCT 3D surgical planning

When a tooth cannot be saved, we provide gentle extractions using advanced 3D CBCT imaging for precise surgical planning. This technology allows us to better evaluate your anatomy, improve safety, and create a more comfortable treatment experience.

Bone Grafting/ Socket Preservation

After an extraction, bone grafting and socket preservation help maintain the natural shape and strength of your jawbone, reducing bone loss and preparing the area for a future dental implant if desired. Our goal is to promote optimal healing and preserve your smile for years to come.

Dentures

Custom dentures are a comfortable and affordable solution for replacing all of your missing teeth, restoring your smile, speech, and ability to eat with confidence. At Buffalo Family Dentistry, we create natural-looking dentures that are customized to fit your unique needs and lifestyle.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are designed to replace one or more missing teeth while preserving your remaining healthy teeth. Each partial is custom-made to provide a secure fit, improve chewing, and restore the appearance of your smile.
