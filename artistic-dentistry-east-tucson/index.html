Originally from Lima, Peru, Dr. Keys is a highly trained prosthodontist. Known for her detail-oriented approach, compassionate care, and commitment to excellence, she partners closely with patients to develop customized treatment plans that restore both function and confidence. Whether you are seeking aesthetic improvements or full-mouth rehabilitation, you can trust that you are in expert hands.

Dr. Keys received her dental degree from the Metropolitan Autonomous University in Mexico City and completed her prosthodontic residency at the University of Connecticut Health Center . She went on to complete a fellowship in implant dentistry at the same institution. With more than 20 years of experience, she has developed advanced expertise in aesthetic, reconstructive, and implant dentistry.

Her focus includes complex oral rehabilitation, including crowns, bridges, dentures, and full-mouth reconstructions. She is passionate about restoring smiles and improving the quality of life through high-level dental care. Dr. Keys is also a proud member of the American Dental Association and the American College of Prosthodontics .

Outside the clinic, Dr. Keys enjoys traveling, hiking, and spending quality time with her family and friends.
