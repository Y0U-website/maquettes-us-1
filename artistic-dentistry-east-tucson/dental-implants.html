Dental Implants in Tucson and Chandler, AZ

Dental implants are a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for missing teeth. They consist of a small titanium post that’s gently placed into the jawbone, where it acts like a sturdy tooth root. Over time, the implant bonds with the bone, creating a strong foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Implants help restore your smile’s appearance, improve chewing and speaking, and prevent bone loss that can occur after tooth loss. The result? A confident, comfortable smile that looks and feels just like your own teeth.

Here at Artistic Dentistry, we offer a variety of implant options catered to ideal results, including single implants, partial-mouth implants, full-mouth implants, All-on-4 implants, and snap-in dentures.

The Healing and Aftercare Process

Proper aftercare helps ensure the best implant results. After your procedure, it’s normal to experience mild swelling or tenderness, which can be managed with recommended medications and cold compresses. Stick to soft foods for a few days and avoid chewing near the implant site. Gentle brushing and flossing are important, but take care around the area as it heals. Keep all follow-up appointments, so we can monitor your progress.

FAQ

  • What types of dental implants are available? We provide single implants, implant-supported bridges, implant-supported dentures, partial mouth, and All-on-4 solutions based on your restoration needs.

  • When should I get a dental implant? If you’re missing a tooth or facing an extraction, a dental implant may be the perfect long-term solution. If you want a durable, low-maintenance option that supports jaw health, it’s a great time to explore dental implants.
