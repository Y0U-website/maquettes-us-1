All-on-4 Dental Implants

For patients an entire arch, most or all of their teeth, All-on-4 dental implants offer a transformative solution.

This advanced technique uses just four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, giving you a fixed, natural-looking smile that restores both function and confidence.

Why choose All-on-4?

  • Replaces an entire upper or lower arch of teeth with fewer implants
  • Provides a secure, non-removable alternative to traditional dentures
  • Restores the ability to chew, speak, and smile naturally
  • Helps prevent further bone loss and maintains facial shape
  • Often allows for faster treatment and recovery compared to multiple single implants

All-on-4 may be ideal if you:

During your consultation, Dr. Hazzouri will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and goals to determine if All-on-4 is the best treatment option for you.

  • Are missing most or all of your teeth
  • Struggle with loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Have sufficient jawbone to support the implants (or are a candidate for grafting)
  • Want a long-term solution that looks and feels like natural teeth

Signs you may need a tooth extracted

Don’t ignore ongoing tooth pain or signs of infection. You may need a tooth extraction if you’re experiencing:

Removing the problem tooth can often be the first step toward restoring your overall oral health and smile—and may also prepare the site for a future dental implant.

  • Sharp or throbbing pain in your gums or jaw
  • Persistent toothaches
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Severe sensitivity or discoloration
