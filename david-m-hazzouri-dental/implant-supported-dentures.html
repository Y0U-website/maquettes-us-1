Implant-supported dentures are a type of full or partial denture that is stabilized by dental implants. Instead of relying solely on suction or adhesives, the denture snaps onto a series of implants surgically placed in your jawbone. Benefits include: A more natural look and feel
  • Improved speech and chewing ability
  • Enhanced stability—no more slipping or clicking
  • Prevention of bone loss and facial sagging
  • Greater comfort compared to conventional dentures

How long do implant dentures last? With proper care and regular dental checkups, implant-supported dentures can last many years. The implants themselves often last a lifetime.

What to expect Initial Consultation Dr. David Hazzouri will examine your mouth, gums, and bone structure using X-rays or scans. He’ll review your goals and recommend the best treatment plan.

Implant Placement Small titanium posts are surgically placed into your jawbone. These serve as anchors for your new dentures. Healing time may vary depending on your individual needs.

Healing and Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implants fuse with your bone, a process called osseointegration. Temporary dentures may be worn during this time.

Custom Denture Design Once healed, impressions are taken to create your custom-fitted denture. You’ll have the opportunity to choose the shape, size, and shade of your new teeth.

Attachment of Denture The final denture is securely attached to the implants. You’ll leave the office with a stable, natural-looking smile that functions like real teeth.
