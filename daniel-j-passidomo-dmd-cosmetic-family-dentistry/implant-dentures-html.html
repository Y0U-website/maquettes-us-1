What Are Implant Dentures?

Dental implants are small posts, usually made of titanium, that act as prosthetic tooth roots. They get surgically placed into the jawbone, where they form a bond with the surrounding tissue. Then, they are able to provide a strong base of support for various types of restorations, including crowns, bridges, and even full dentures.

There are two main categories of implant dentures, and the one that is best for you will depend on your unique situation.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

As their name implies, fixed implant dentures are permanently attached to their support posts; they should only be removed by a dental professional. Many patients are attracted to this option because they want their smile to be complete 24/7. However, these prosthetics typically require more implant posts than removable options, which makes the process slightly more invasive.

Removable Implant Dentures

If you have lost a bit of bone density or volume in your jaw, you might be a candidate for removable implant dentures. These may require as few as two implants per arch. The implants have small attachments on them that allow the dentures to easily snap into and out of the mouth. This option combines the convenience of traditional prosthetics with the strength of dental implants.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You might be a good candidate for implant dentures if:

If you are not sure if you qualify for implant dentures, attend a consultation with our team. Most patients can get this form of tooth replacement, either right away or after preliminary care.

  • You are missing all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches.
  • Your jawbone and other oral tissues are healthy enough to support dental implants.
  • Your overall health is good enough for you to undergo minor surgery.
  • You have good habits that will support your implants’ long-term success.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures offer some outstanding benefits:

  • Stability and strength: Depending on the number of implants used, you may regain most of your original bite force. Plus, your prosthetic teeth should never slip out of place.
  • Beauty: Dr Passidomo carefully designs implant dentures so they look and feel very natural.
  • Longevity: Dental implants can last for several decades. The restorations on top of them also have a long life expectancy.
  • Jawbone preservation: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and help to keep it strong and whole.
