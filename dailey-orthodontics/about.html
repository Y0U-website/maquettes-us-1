About Our Practice

About Dailey Orthodontics

The team at Dailey Orthodontics strives to provide friendly, caring service backed by our unmatched experience and knowledge in the field of orthodontics. Founded by Dr. Curtis Dailey’s father in 1971, Dailey Orthodontics has provided exceptional orthodontic care to more than 10,000 patients in Hampton, Poquoson, and throughout the Virginia Peninsula.

Orthodontist Dr. Curtis Dailey

A practicing orthodontist since 2002, Dr. Dailey has specialty training as a pediatric dentist as well. This gives him a wide range of experience providing innovative orthodontic care to children, teens, and adults. He and our talented technicians and staff are committed to providing patients of all ages the best orthodontic experience in a welcoming, comfortable environment.

Innovative Orthodontic Care You Can Trust

At Dailey Orthodontics, we utilize the latest technology and techniques to give you the most advanced orthodontic care available today. We offer traditional braces , clear braces, Invisalign , Invisalign Teen, BOOST clear aligners , and more. Regardless of the dental issues you may be facing, we work with you to find orthodontic solutions that meet your specific needs.

Interceptive orthodontic treatment for children ages seven and older helps correct teeth and bite-related issues while the jaw is still growing. This proactive approach can help prevent many common dental problems in the future.

Modern, high-tech orthodontic tools like AcceleDent, Propel, and state-of-the-art 3D printers and scanners can help you achieve a straight, beautiful smile in less time than other types of traditional treatment. We want you to feel confident in our ability to give you and your family healthy, happy smiles for life.

Advanced Training In Orthodontics

Dr. Dailey and our orthodontic technicians have extensive training and eagerly continue to learn and grow. Hands-on training sessions, webinars, conferences, and other continuing education opportunities enable us to provide the top-notch service and innovative orthodontic care our patients have come to expect.

Dr. Dailey and his team continually strive to improve their expertise through multiple professional memberships, including the prestigious Schulman Study Group. The Schulman Study Group is an invitation-only, nationally recognized association of orthodontists that seeks to promote exceptional orthodontic care, medical and technical innovation, and continuing education for its members.
