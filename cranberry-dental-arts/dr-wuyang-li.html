Meet Dr. Wuyang Li

Get to know your Cranberry Township dentist

Dr. Wuyang Li leads Cranberry Dental Arts with a focus on thorough, comfortable care for patients of all ages in Cranberry Township and the surrounding communities.

Dr. Li began his journey in dentistry in China, where he completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery. He continued his studies in the United States, earning a Master of Science in Oral Biology and a Doctor of Philosophy in Oral & Craniofacial Sciences at the University of Pittsburgh. His research centered on the temporomandibular joint (TMJ) and temporomandibular joint disorders (TMD).

Following his doctoral work, Dr. Li completed additional clinical training in the advanced standing program at the University of Pittsburgh and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD). He offers a wide range of services, including exams and cleanings, fillings, crowns, bridges, root canal treatment, extractions, bone grafting, dental implants, pediatric dentistry, and care for dental emergencies. He is also certified to provide treatment with nitrous oxide sedation.

A welcoming, patient-first approach

Dr. Li takes the time to listen, explain options clearly, and help each patient feel at ease. Fluent in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese, he is glad to serve a diverse community and to make care approachable for every family.

Outside the office, Dr. Li enjoys time with his wife and son, along with sports such as badminton, table tennis, skiing, and tennis.
