Treatment Options Using Dental Implants

Dental implants can support several types of restorations depending on the extent of tooth loss.

Single-Tooth Implants

A single implant can replace one missing tooth without affecting adjacent healthy teeth. A custom crown is attached to the implant, restoring both appearance and function.

Implant Dentures

Patients who wear dentures may benefit from implant-retained or implant-supported dentures. These options provide greater stability than conventional dentures, helping reduce movement while eating and speaking.

The Dental Implant Process

Treatment typically involves the following steps:

Consultation and Examination

Our team evaluates your oral health, discusses your goals, and determines whether dental implants are right for you.

Implant Placement

The implant posts are surgically placed within the jawbone to serve as replacement tooth roots.

Healing and Integration

Over the following months, the implants fuse with the surrounding bone to create a stable foundation.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implants to restore your smile.

The overall treatment timeline varies depending on individual needs and treatment complexity.

Determining Candidacy for Dental Implants

Many individuals with missing teeth may be candidates for dental implants. Healthy gums, sufficient bone support, and good oral hygiene habits can contribute to successful treatment outcomes.

Even if bone loss has occurred, additional procedures may help prepare the area for implant placement. A professional evaluation is the best way to determine the most appropriate treatment approach.
