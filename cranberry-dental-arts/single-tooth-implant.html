Single-Tooth Dental Implant

Because dental implants integrate with the jawbone, they provide a stable foundation for the replacement tooth while supporting long-term oral health. Patients missing multiple teeth may also benefit from implant dentures , which provide a secure and comfortable tooth replacement option.

  • clearer speech
  • jawbone preservation
  • reduced tooth shifting
  • better bite support
  • natural appearance
  • long-lasting results

Who May Be a Candidate for Treatment?

Many adults with a missing tooth may qualify for dental implant treatment, particularly those with healthy gums and adequate jawbone support. A comprehensive evaluation helps determine whether a dental implant is the right option for your needs. Based on your oral health and treatment goals, our team will create a personalized treatment plan.

The Single Tooth Implant Process

Comprehensive Evaluation and Treatment Planning

Treatment begins with a thorough examination and consultation. Advanced imaging helps assess your oral health and determine the ideal placement for the implant.

Implant Placement

The implant post is placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. As the area heals, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone to create a stable foundation.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom dental crown is attached to the implant. The restoration is designed to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth while restoring function and enhancing the appearance of your smile, similar to the goals of cosmetic dentistry .
