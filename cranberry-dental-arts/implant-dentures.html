Implant-Supported Dentures

This treatment offers improved stability and function while creating a smile that looks and feels more natural. Patients interested in learning more about implant dentures can explore how this solution restores both comfort and confidence.

  • The implants gradually fuse with the surrounding bone, creating a stable foundation.
  • A custom denture is then attached to the implants and may be removable or fixed.
  • The result is a secure, natural-looking restoration that improves comfort and function.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Enhanced Stability

Implant dentures are securely supported by dental implants, helping to reduce unwanted movement while speaking, eating, or smiling.

Improved Chewing Function

Because implant-supported dentures provide a stable foundation, patients are often able to enjoy a wider variety of foods with greater comfort.

Natural-Looking Results

Each restoration is custom-designed to complement your smile and facial features, creating an appearance that looks both natural and attractive.

Support for Jawbone Health

Following tooth loss, the jawbone can gradually lose density over time. Dental implants help stimulate the bone and support the structures that contribute to long-term oral health.

Long-Term Reliability

With proper care and routine dental visits, implant-supported dentures can provide a durable and dependable solution for tooth replacement. Patients missing only one tooth may benefit from a single tooth implant as an alternative restorative option.

Are Implant Dentures Right for You?

Implant dentures may be an ideal option for patients who are missing multiple teeth, struggle with traditional dentures, or want a more secure tooth replacement solution. Candidates should have sufficient bone support for dental implants and be committed to maintaining good oral health.

Patients comparing treatment options may also find it helpful to learn more about traditional dentures and how they differ from implant-supported restorations.
