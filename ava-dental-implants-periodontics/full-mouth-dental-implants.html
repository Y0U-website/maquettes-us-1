Long-Term Solutions for Total Tooth Loss

Modern dental implant technology offers several life-changing options to replace all of your teeth giving you a stable, natural-looking smile that feels and functions like your own. Here’s how each solution works.

All-on-4 / All-on-6 Implants

This advanced technique uses a minimum of four (or six) strategically placed dental implants to support a full, fixed bridge. The result is a permanent set of teeth that never moves or comes out.

Implant-Supported Overdentures

This solution uses 2–4 dental implants to securely anchor a removable denture in place. It snaps in and out easily for cleaning but stays stable and firm when chewing, talking, and smiling.

Teeth-in-a-Day Protocol

For qualified patients, this streamlined approach allows extractions, implant placement, and a temporary fixed bridge, your brand-new smile, all completed in a single appointment.
