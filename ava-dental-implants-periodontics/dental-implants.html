Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults missing one or more teeth are good candidates for dental implants, but the best way to know for certain is through a personalized evaluation. During your consultation at AVA Dental in Davenport, Dr. Dib will assess your oral health, bone density, and overall medical history using advanced 3D CBCT imaging. You may be a good candidate if you have:

Sufficient jawbone density (or are open to bone grafting)

Good overall health and a commitment to oral hygiene

A desire for a permanent, non-removable solution

Bone loss in the jaw (treatable with bone grafting or sinus lifts)

Smoked previously (though smoking can affect healing)

Been told by another dentist you weren&#x27;t a candidate

Dr. Dib specializes in complex cases that other dentists turn away. As a board-certified periodontist with advanced training in bone regeneration, he can often make implants possible when others say no.
