Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You might be a good candidate for implant dentures if:

If you are not sure whether implant dentures are suitable for you, visit us for a consultation. Most people with severe tooth loss can qualify for this procedure!

  • You have lost all or most of your teeth.
  • Your jawbone is strong enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease and other health conditions that might compromise your implants’ success.
  • You have good habits that will support your implants’ health.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Some of the most noteworthy benefits of implant dentures include:

  • A strong and stable bite. Unlike traditional dentures, implant dentures do not slip and slide around the mouth. They can stand up to virtually any food.
  • A long-lasting fit. Dental implants prevent the jawbone from deteriorating and changing shape, so your dentures can keep their fit for a long time.
  • Dental implants tend to last for decades.
  • Easy maintenance. In some ways, caring for implant dentures is just like taking care of natural teeth.
  • We design implant dentures with the goal of making them look and feel as natural as possible.
