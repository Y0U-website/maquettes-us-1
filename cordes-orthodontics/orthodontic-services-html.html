Traditional Braces

Traditional braces, just like the ones you saw growing up, use a system of brackets and wires to correct misalignments in the teeth and jaws. They are still one of the most effective treatments available when it comes to correcting simple and complex problems alike, and thanks to their durability and affordability, they are ideally suited for younger patients. Thankfully, braces have gotten a cosmetic makeover as time has gone by, giving them a more subtle appearance that makes it easier for a patient to smile during treatment.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces work the same trusted way as traditional metal ones, but they have a distinct advantage. The brackets are made from a clear ceramic material designed to blend with your teeth, and tooth-colored wires keep the whole appliance nearly invisible. For patients who want effective orthodontic correction without a mouth full of metal, 3M Clarity Clear Ceramic Brackets offer a subtle, social life-friendly path to a straighter smile.

Invisalign® & 3M™ Clarity™ Clear Aligners

Invisalign and 3M Clarity Clear Aligners enable patients to get the orthodontic care they need without altering their daily appearance in the process. The aligners are made of thin, see-through plastic and practically disappear when placed on the teeth. Overtime, they place the right amount of pressure to slowly shift the teeth into their ideal positions, and they are easily removable for unhindered eating and brushing as well. With them, only you and Dr. Cordes will know you’re undergoing treatment.

Phase I Pediatric Orthodontics

Phase I Pediatric Orthodontics is typically prescribed for very young patients to help them correct ongoing orthodontic issues, prevent potential ones, and eliminate habits that might lead to future problems (such as thumb sucking or tongue thrusting). The main goal is to give a child’s adult teeth the best possible chance of coming in straight, reducing or eliminating the need for full braces later on.

Orthodontic Appliances

In addition to braces and clear aligners, Dr. Cordes is also able to use a multitude of other orthodontic appliances to enhance our patients’ smiles and oral health. Retainers make sure newly shifted teeth stay in place after the braces come off, an RPE can expand a patient’s hard palate to alleviate crowding, and a thumb crib can help a child quickly break this bad habit.

Dentofacial Orthopedics

While traditional orthodontics is focused on correcting the position of the teeth, dentofacial orthopedics is designed to do the same for the bones of the face and jaw that support the teeth. Using this approach, Dr. Cordes can fix and even prevent bite alignment problems, and it can also help with aesthetics by giving a person achieve a more symmetrical and attractive facial appearance. While it is most effective in younger patients, it can also be very effective with adults as well.

Orthodontic Emergencies

Most of our patients are able to go through their treatment without any bumps along the way, but as we all know, life just happens sometimes. Should you or your child experience a broken bracket, persistent pain, or any other kind of problem with your orthodontic appliance, don’t sit and suffer – go ahead and give our orthodontic office a call! We don’t use an answering service after hours. Your emergency call will be taken by Dr. Cordes or one of our team members. That way we can take care of the problem and keep your treatment on track.

Teen Orthodontics

We welcome teenage patients to come to us for care. We offer a range of orthodontic treatments that are suitable for adolescents, including traditional metal braces, clear braces, and two different types of clear aligners. Addressing your teen’s dental alignment issues now can spare them from future oral health problems and give them a big confidence boost. Plus, we offer appointment slots before and after school so your teen will not need to worry about missing out on their education.

Adult Orthodontic Treatment

Despite orthodontics typically being associated with children and adolescence, 1 in 5 orthodontic patients in the US is actually an adult. Thanks to the latest advancements, older patients are now able to straighten their teeth as well as benefit from treatments that are designed to be aesthetically pleasing. If you never had braces as a child or you did and your teeth have shifted over the years, know that it’s never too late to get the smile you’ve always wanted with Dr. Cordes.

Braces with Missing Teeth

Just because you’re missing teeth doesn’t mean that you can’t undergo orthodontic treatment. During your consultation with us, we will discuss your smile goals and examine the current state of your teeth. Then, we will put together a custom treatment plan to meet your needs. Everybody’s teeth are unique, and tooth loss is very common, so we’re happy to help you get closer to your dream smile!

Spark Clear Aligners

Spark Clear Aligners are a new form of orthodontic treatment that works similarly to other aligner systems. Consisting of clear trays that offer a more subtle approach to orthodontics, these aligners shift teeth in the right direction over several months to generate a straighter, healthier smile. With Spark, patients can enjoy improved comfort while avoiding the bulkiness of metal or lackluster clarity of other similar solutions.
