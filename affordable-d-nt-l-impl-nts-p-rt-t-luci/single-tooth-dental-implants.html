Why Choose a Single Tooth Implant Over a Bridge?

When you lose a tooth, your jawbone begins deteriorating without root stimulation. Adjacent teeth drift into the empty space, creating bite problems and increasing the risk of additional tooth loss.

A single tooth implant solves these problems.

Single Tooth Implant Procedure in Boynton Beach, FL

Understanding each phase helps you plan accordingly. Most patients complete their single tooth replacement in four to six months.

Initial Consultation and Treatment Planning: Your first visit at our Boynton Beach office includes a thorough examination and 3D CT imaging to evaluate jawbone density. We’ll discuss your timeline, answer questions, and provide exact costs upfront. If you still have the damaged tooth, we can often extract it and place the implant during the same appointment.

Implant Placement Surgery: The surgical procedure takes 30 to 60 minutes under local anesthesia. Most patients report feeling pressure but no pain. We can provide sedation options if needed.

Healing and Osseointegration: Over the next three to six months, your jawbone fuses with the titanium post. You can return to normal activities within a day or two. We may place a temporary tooth during healing, especially if the missing tooth is visible in your smile.

Crown Placement: Once healing is complete, we attach the abutment and take impressions for your custom crown. The final appointment involves permanently attaching the crown and walking out with your complete smile.

Am I a Good Candidate for a Single Tooth Implant?

Most adults with one missing tooth qualify for dental implant treatment. Ideal candidates have adequate bone density, healthy gums free of active disease, and good overall health.

Health Considerations: Your general health matters because healing depends on your body’s ability to grow new bone around the titanium post. Uncontrolled diabetes, recent radiation therapy, or certain medications may affect healing. We’ll discuss any concerns during your consultation.

Bone Loss Concerns: If a tooth has been missing for an extended period, the surrounding bone often deteriorates. Our zygomatic implant options and bone grafting procedures make implants possible for many patients who initially lack adequate bone volume.

Single Tooth Implant Recovery Timeline

First 48 Hours: Expect minor swelling and tenderness around the implant site. Ice packs and over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen typically provide adequate comfort. Stick to soft foods and avoid the surgical site when chewing. Most patients return to work the next day.

First Week: Swelling peaks around day three, then gradually subsides. Discomfort diminishes significantly after the first three days. You can incorporate more foods as comfort allows.

Long-Term Care: Care for your implant like your natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss daily, and visit for routine cleanings every six months. The crown may need replacement after 10 to 15 years, but the implant post typically lasts a lifetime.
