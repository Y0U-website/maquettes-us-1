Why Choose Zygomatic Implants Over Bone Grafting?

This procedure offers a direct route to long-term, full-arch stability without the delays or uncertainty of bone rebuilding procedures.

  • No Bone Grafting Required: Using the cheekbone for implant anchorage eliminates the need for bone grafts or sinus augmentation. This reduces complication risks and shortens the overall timeline.
  • Faster Treatment Time: By skipping bone grafting and healing stages, patients receive their prosthetics sooner. Many patients receive temporary teeth the same day as their surgery.
  • Stronger Foundation: The zygomatic bone is naturally dense and reliable. That makes it ideal for supporting full-mouth restorations, especially when combined with conventional implants in a hybrid protocol like quad zygomatic implants.
  • Immediate Function: In most cases, you can have temporary teeth attached right after surgery. You’ll leave with a functional smile that lets you eat and speak normally.

What to Expect from Your Zygomatic Procedure

We handle everything in our office, from planning to surgery to your final teeth. This keeps things simple and delivers consistent, high-quality results.

  • Personal Consultation 3D Imaging: During your visit, we use an on-site 3D CT scanner to evaluate bone structure and map out a customized surgical approach.
  • Digital Surgical Planning: Your procedure is virtually planned ahead of time, allowing for precision-guided implant placement. This leads to shorter chair time and highly predictable outcomes.
  • Implant Placement Surgery: Using a combination of local anesthesia and optional IV sedation, your implant surgeon places the zygomatic (and additional) implants during a single visit. Temporary teeth are often placed the same day.
  • Final Restoration: When healing is complete, permanent full-arch bridge restoration is secured to the implants, providing lasting comfort, function, and aesthetics.
