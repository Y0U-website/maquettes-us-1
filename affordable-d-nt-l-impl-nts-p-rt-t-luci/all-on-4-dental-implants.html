What Are All-on-4® Implants?

All-on-4 implants work differently than traditional dentures. Instead of relying on adhesives or sitting on your gums, four titanium implants are placed in your jawbone to support an entire arch of fixed replacement teeth. Two implants go in the front of your mouth, and two are angled toward the back for maximum stability.

The result? Teeth that stay put when you eat, speak, or smile. No more worrying about dentures slipping at the wrong moment or avoiding certain foods.

This treatment has helped millions of people worldwide because it’s efficient, more affordable than placing individual implants for every tooth, and gives you long-term stability you can count on.

Step by Step All-on-4® Process

Our streamlined process means fewer appointments and less time in the dental chair, while our advanced imaging helps place everything exactly where it should be.

  • Complimentary Consultation & 3D Scan: Your first visit begins with a free consultation. Using our on-site 3D CT scanner, our specialists create a precise image of your jawbone and design a custom treatment plan tailored to your needs.
  • Personalized Smile Design: Once we know exactly what we’re working with, we’ll show you where the four implants will be placed and give you a preview of what your new smile will look like. You’ll see the results before we begin the process.
  • Same-Day Surgery: On the day of your procedure, four implants are surgically placed into areas of strong bone. Two are placed vertically at the front, while two are angled toward the back for maximum stability. A temporary bridge is attached immediately, so you leave our office with a new set of functional teeth the very same day.
  • Healing and Final Restoration: Over the next few months, your implants will naturally bond with your jawbone through osseointegration. Once everything has healed properly (usually 3–4 months), we’ll attach your final bridge made from strong, natural-looking materials.
