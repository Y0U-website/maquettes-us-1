What Are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures (also called implant-retained dentures or snap-in dentures) are removable teeth that attach securely to two to four dental implants placed in your jaw. These implants provide a strong, stable foundation that lets you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Unlike traditional dentures that rest on your gums and may slip or require adhesives, implant dentures lock onto titanium posts placed in your jawbone. This added stability helps prevent slipping and gives you a more natural feel.

How Do Snap-In Dentures Work?

This simple design means your dentures won’t shift when you bite into food or laugh, yet you can still remove them easily for thorough cleaning and maintenance, giving you control over your oral care routine.

  • We place 2–4 dental implants in your jawbone during a minor surgical procedure.
  • Implants are fitted with connectors called locators or attachments.
  • Your custom denture is made to snap securely onto these connectors.
  • When you’re ready to clean them, just unsnap and rinse as needed.

The Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Upgrading from traditional dentures to removable implant-supported dentures comes with a wide range of advantages:

  • Better eating experience: Enjoy apples, steaks, corn on the cob, and other foods you may have avoided.
  • No more adhesives: Stop dealing with creams, powders, and the mess they create.
  • Clearer speech: Talk confidently without worrying about slipping or clicking sounds.
  • Healthier jawbone: Implants help maintain bone structure and facial support.
  • Long-term stability: Fewer adjustments needed compared to traditional dentures.
  • Natural look: Custom-designed to complement your facial features.
  • Daily comfort: Secure fit without pressure points or movement.
