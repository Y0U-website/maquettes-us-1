About Us

What Sets Our Fenton Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Birch Tree Dental is a general dental practice located in Fenton, Michigan. Our dental office specializes in comprehensive preventative dentistry, restorative dentistry, surgical procedures, and cosmetic dentistry. We know you have a choice in your dental care provider, and we are honored you chose us! Here, patients are family. We’re focused on you and your oral health, and we think you’ll find a friend in your dental professional during your visit. Contact us today to get started.

Meet Your Dentists

Skilled and Compassionate Professionals Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Breanna Whittemore

Background

Dr. Breanna Whittemore is originally from Arlington, Ohio and is a proud graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. She first began her career in dentistry working as a dental hygienist in Lima, Ohio and eventually moving to Columbus. She is a member of Ohio Dental Association and American Dental Association.

Fun Facts

In her free time, Dr. Whittemore loves to cook, travel, and spend quality time with her husband and little boy, Harvey. She is also a huge Elvis Presley fan!

Dr. Rachel McGue

Background

Dr. Rachel attended the University of Detroit Mercy for 3 years and then went on to receive her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Michigan. She is a member of the Michigan Dental Association and Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Rachel is committed to lifelong learning and strives to create a comfortable and positive experience for her patients!

Fun Facts
