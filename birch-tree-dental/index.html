The practice

Dr. Breanna Whittemore, Dr. Rachel McGue, and our experienced dental team are committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, stress-free, and friendly atmosphere. As skilled dental professionals, with a personal commitment to our community, our team works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the dental care they deserve.

We go above and beyond standards for cleanliness and safety, as recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA.

Our family dentistry prides itself on offering our patients an exceptional environment where they can feel comfortable knowing their oral health is in good hands. We develop an individualized plan for each patient. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, we are committed to you. Contact us to get started.

Visiting your dentist at least twice a year is critical to your dental and overall health. Good dental hygiene is incredibly important in preventing conditions such as tooth decay, gum disease, and other problems. We offer regular and periodontal cleanings, imaging, exams, and oral cancer screenings for both adults and children. Other preventative care services include athletic mouthguards…

Cosmetic dentistry can enhance your smile and improve function. If you are interested in our office helping transform your smile, feel free to ask us for more information regarding veneers, dental bonding, whitening services, clear aligners, and more.

Dr. Breanna Whittemore is originally from Arlington, Ohio and is a proud graduate of The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. She first began her career in dentistry working as a dental hygienist in Lima, Ohio and eventually moving to Columbus. She is a member of Ohio Dental Association and American Dental Association.

Dr. Rachel attended the University of Detroit Mercy for 3 years and then went on to receive her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Michigan. She is a member of the Michigan Dental Association and Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Rachel is committed to lifelong learning and strives to create a comfortable and positive experience for her patients!
