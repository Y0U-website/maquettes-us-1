Meet Dr. Strassberg

Meet The Doctor

Michael Strassberg D.D.S. – Periodontist

Our periodontist Dr. Michael Strassberg graduated from Boston University with a BA in Art History and received his dental education at Tufts School Of Dental Medicine in Boston, where he earned his DMD degree. He was commissioned as a dental officer in the United States Army Dental Corps, where he served in Germany where he was awarded the US Army Air Defense Artillery Certificate Of Achievement for exceptionally meritorious service.

After his honorable discharge he returned to Tufts Dental School where he completed his residency in periodontics in 1987, after which he returned to his home state of New Jersey. He has been practicing periodontics in New Jersey since 1987. Dr Strassberg takes many continuing education courses to remain current in his specialty. He is skilled in all aspects of periodontics and enjoys developing good relationships with his patients and staff. Dr Strassberg enjoys spending time with his family and dog Casper, as well as exercising, reading, travel and keeping up with friends.
