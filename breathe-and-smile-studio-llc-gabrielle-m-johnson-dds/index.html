The practice

We create positive dental experiences for children and families through early evaluations, behavior-guided pediatric dentistry, and compassionate care that builds trust and confidence.

We look beyond teeth to evaluate how breathing, sleeping, swallowing, and oral function impact whole-body health. Services include sleep apnea treatment, myofunctional therapy referrals, TMJ evaluations, frenectomy treatment, and early childhood airway assessments.

Feel confident and comfortable with treatments that support both function and appearance, including teeth whitening, clear aligners, Botox, lip filler, and dental laser therapy

Les soins

Healthy teeth and gums support a healthy body. Through comprehensive oral health evaluations, hygiene care, oral cancer screenings, and customized prevention plans, we help patients protect their long-term health.

When damage or tooth loss occurs, we restore health, comfort, and function with tooth-colored fillings, crowns, implant restorations, dentures, and partials using high-quality materials designed to look and feel natural.
