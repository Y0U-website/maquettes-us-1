Affordable Care. Flexible Financing

Schedule an Appointment A healthy, confident smile should never be out of reach. High-quality dental care is an investment in your overall well-being, which is why the office offers flexible payment solutions designed to fit any budget.

Whether you are planning a transformative cosmetic procedure or staying on top of essential restorative work, these third-party financing options allow you to receive treatment now and pay over time.

Sunbit: Smile Now, pay over time

Sunbit is designed for everyday needs, offering a 90 % approval rate and a lightning-fast application process. It allows you to split your dental costs into manageable monthly payments without the stress of traditional credit checks.

Alphaeon Credit: YOUR BEST YOU IS CLOSER THAN YOU THINK Alphaeon Credit provides a wide array of monthly payment plans for dental procedures that aren t typically covered by insurance. With higher credit limits and exclusive financing options, it is an ideal choice for comprehensive restorative or cosmetic dentistry.
