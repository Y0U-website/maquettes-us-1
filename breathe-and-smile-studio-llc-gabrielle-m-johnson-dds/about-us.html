About Us

Putting Your Smile and Your Health First

Dr. Gabrielle Johnson DDS I created Breathe and Smile Studio with simple belief: oral health is deeply connected to overall health, and every patient deserves to feel heard, cared for, and confident in their treatment.

As a dentist, my passion extends beyond fixing teeth- I love helping people improve their quality of life

through personalized, thoughtful care that supports long-term wellness. Whether we are restoring a smile, helping someone sleep better, relieving discomfort, or guiding a family through preventive care, my goal is always to provide solutions that support the whole person.

At Breathe & Smile Studio, we believe exceptional care happens through teamwork, kindness, and lifelong learning. My team and I are committed to creating a warm, caring environment where patients feel comfortable asking questions, understood in their concerns, and empowered to make informed decisions about their health.

I value continuing education and staying current with advances in dentistry so we can bring modern techniques, technology, and evidence-based care to every experience.

It is a privilege to care for our community, and I look forward to helping you breathe better, smile brighter, and live healthier.

Our Team At Breathe and Smile Studio, our team is dedicated to providing exceptional care in a warm, welcoming environment where patients feel like family. We believe that great dentistry starts with genuine relationships, compassionate service, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

From the moment you walk through our doors, you ll be greeted by a team that values kindness, clear communication, and personalized care. We work together to ensure every visit is comfortable, educational, and tailored to your unique oral health goals. Whether you re here for preventive care, restorative treatment, or advanced holistic dental services, our goal is to help you achieve a healthy smile that supports your overall well-being.

We look forward to getting to know you and being your trusted partner in lifelong oral health.
