Dentistry made more achievable for complex needs.

At Aashvi Smiles Dental Group, we provide dental care for patients with special health care needs, medical complexities, dental anxiety, and age-related conditions. We care for children and adults with developmental disabilities, seniors with mobility or cognitive challenges, and patients who may find traditional dental visits difficult.

We work closely with families, caregivers, and physicians to make dental care comfortable and achievable for each patient. When needed, we also offer home-based dental services for patients who are unable to travel to the office due to medical or mobility limitations.
