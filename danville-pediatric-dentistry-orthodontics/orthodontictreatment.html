We truly love caring for generations of smiles.

COMPLIMENTARY CONSULTATION

Every smile is uniquely yours—and your care should be too. That’s why we take the time to sit with each new patient, thoughtfully reviewing dental history, concerns, and goals for a healthy, confident smile. From there, we design a personalized treatment plan that is both efficient and effective, creating harmony between your smile and your natural features

EARLY INTERCEPTIVE TREATMENT

While baby teeth are still present, we take a comprehensive, proactive approach to evaluating your child’s dental growth, jaw development, and airway health. This allows us to identify early signs of crowding, bite concerns, or developmental challenges before they become more complex.

Interceptive treatment focuses on gently guiding proper growth—often reducing the need for more extensive or costly procedures later on. By addressing concerns early, we can support healthier function, improved breathing, and optimal facial development using minimally invasive, child-friendly techniques.

We collaborate closely with our dedicated pediatric team to take a whole-child approach. By working together, we develop a personalized plan that meets your child’s unique needs, laying the groundwork for a lifetime of healthy, confident smiles.

ADULT TREATMENT

Our most asked question? “Do you see adults?” The answer is yes! We care for the orthodontic health of your entire family—from you to your kids.

Adult treatment can address a variety of needs, from shifting teeth to comprehensive care that helps prevent or manage dental conditions like gum recession. We offer clear aligner options, including Angel Aligners and Invisalign .

And what’s even better—getting treatment alongside your child sets a positive example while helping you achieve your own healthy, confident smile!
