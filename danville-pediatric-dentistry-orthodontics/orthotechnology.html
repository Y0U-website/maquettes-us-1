Orthodontic Technology

Our top priority is always our patients. Every tool, technique, and innovation we bring into our office is thoughtfully chosen to support your experience and help you thrive every step of the way.

ITERO DIGITAL IMPRESSIONS

If you had braces as a kid, you might remember the messy goop used to make molds. We don’t do that anymore!

With the iTero digital scanner, we use a “magic” wand to create a seamless, non-invasive 3D digital model of your teeth. This is used for braces, clear aligners, or custom mouthguards—and even better, updates for retainers are quick and easy, all with a simple wave of the wand.

DIGITAL X-RAYS

X-rays are one of the procedures we’re asked about most—and we get it, they can sound intimidating!

With our ultra low-radiation digital X-ray system, getting images of your teeth and jaws is safer than many everyday activities—for example, it exposes you to far less radiation than a short flight. Plus, the process is quick, comfortable, and takes just a few seconds, giving us the detailed information we need to plan the best care for your smile.

ZOOM WHITENING

Whether you’re looking to refresh your smile for a special occasion or simply want to boost your confidence, Zoom Whitening delivers noticeable results in a short amount of time. It’s a simple, convenient way to achieve a brighter, healthier-looking smile that complements your orthodontic care.
