How to Know If You Need a Dental Orthodontic Retainer​

Dental orthodontic retainers are essential for keeping teeth in their correct positions after braces or aligner treatment. They prevent shifting and protect the results you’ve worked hard to achieve.

Custom retainers for teeth may also be recommended for patients with minor alignment issues to avoid further movement. Wearing your retainer as directed ensures your smile stays straight, healthy, and beautiful for years to come.

  • Maintains teeth alignment after orthodontic treatment.
  • Prevents unwanted shifting and relapse.
  • May be used for minor alignment corrections.
  • Supports long-term oral health and aesthetics.
  • Essential for protecting your orthodontic investment.

Types of Custom Dental Retainers We Offer

Retention is a vital part of any orthodontic treatment, ensuring your teeth remain in their new, correct positions. Once your braces or clear aligner treatment is complete, we provide customized retainers tailored to your needs.

Our options include traditional wired Hawley retainers, clear plastic ESSIX® retainers, and bonded lingual retainers that stay in place. Each type is designed for comfort, durability, and effectiveness, helping you maintain your beautiful smile for years to come.

FAQs About Orthodontic Retainers in San Marcos

  • Why are orthodontic retainers necessary after braces? Retainers help stabilize teeth after treatment and prevent shifting caused by natural forces like chewing and jaw movement.
  • How long should you wear retainers after orthodontic treatment? Most patients wear them full-time initially, then nightly long-term to maintain alignment.
  • What happens if you don’t wear retainers regularly? Teeth may shift, causing spacing or crowding that could require additional treatment.
  • Can I get a retainer without braces? Yes, depending on your dental history and alignment needs.
  • How much does a replacement retainer cost? Costs vary by type, but your orthodontic provider will give an estimate upfront.
  • Where can I get a custom retainer near me? Ault & Ault Orthodontics provides custom options in San Marcos.
  • How often should retainers be replaced? Most retainers last several years with proper care.
  • What should I do if I lost my retainer? Contact your orthodontist immediately to prevent shifting.
  • Are retainers for teens and adults different? Designs vary based on lifestyle, growth, and alignment needs.
