About the practice

Who we are, what we do

Da Vinci Dental is your local dentist . Located in the Sentry Office Plaza on Haddon Avenue in the Westmont section of Haddon Township. We’re across the street from the recently constructed Haddon Towne Center, and our Haddon Ave neighbors include Treno, Westmont Hardware, and Planet Fitness at the Westmont Theatre. The Westmont PATCO station is practically in our backyard. We’ve been here since 2006.

We do general dentistry . What’s that mean? It means we do the everyday dentistry that patients need: check-ups, cleanings, fillings, crowns, and extractions. We also do more complex procedures like root canals, bridges, dentures, and implants. We can and do treat patients of every age, children through senior citizens. If you need to see a specialist, we’ll let you know, and we’ll coordinate the work to be done. Otherwise, as a general dentist, we perform all of the procedures that you and/or your family members are likely to need. Consider us your family dentist .

We also do cosmetic dentistry . Cosmetic dentistry is not a specialty the way orthodontics, endodontics, or oral surgery are, but rather the ability of the general dentist to perform certain procedures on patients that enhance the esthetics of their teeth and smile. Procedures like teeth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers are among the ways that smiles are improved. We love doing cosmetic dentistry and take great pride in our ability to help patients achieve the smile of their dreams.

We understand that going to the dentist is not something most people look forward to., but we can truly say that we’ve helped many patients overcome their fears or reluctance of going to the dentist by simply being courteous, listening carefully, explaining what we’re doing, and never, ever being mean, grouchy, or insensitive.

We’re really friendly , and we just want to help you with your dental needs. We hope you let us serve you!
