The practice

Clocktower Dental is a modern Franklin Square-based dentistry practice located in the heart of the Town of Hempstead. We’ve been proudly serving our community for over 25 years, including patients from neighboring areas such as Garden City, West Hempstead, and even as far as Islip.

Today, the dental practice is a state-of-the-art facility that features many advanced technologies, from dental lasers to low-radiation 3D scanning to 3D printing and intra-oral scanning. That means an approach that focuses on comprehensive treatment, thorough planning, and dental treatment’s highest precision and accuracy.

The philosophy at Clocktower Dental is always patient-centered. When you walk through our doors, our goal is to be warm and soothing and to deliver the utmost quality and professional service. That’s reflected in the attention to detail from our doctors and a commitment to your health day in and day out.

Our doctors focus on all areas of dentistry, from pediatric and restorative dentistry to oral surgery, endodontics, and cosmetic dentistry. Our goal is not just to fix dental problems but to identify the root causes. We seek out risk factors and help establish and recommend sound routines and products that are evidence-based in helping our patients maximize their dental and overall health.

We look forward to meeting you and letting you experience Clocktower Dental and all we have to offer. Come visit our practice and meet our wonderful and dedicated team of dentists, hygienists, and dental assistants. We hope you will also become a part of our family.

We offer the latest in cosmetic dentistry and functional replacement for missing teeth: dental implants. Implants require dental surgery, but they become embedded teeth replacements — unlike dentures and dental bridges you don’t have to remove them to clean them. You treat them just like your natural teeth.
