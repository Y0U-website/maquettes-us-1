Dr. Lloyd Turner

Dentist

I began my dental practice in Glendale over 35 years ago, taking over from a highly respected local dentist. Over the years, I have gained extensive experience in all phases of general dentistry for both children and adults. My services include fillings, crowns, bridges, partials, dentures, periodontal treatments, routine root canals, and cosmetic procedures such as teeth whitening and veneers.

Additionally, I place and restore dental implants and have successfully completed selected orthodontic cases using the Clear Correct method of adult aligner therapy.

Practicing dentistry continues to bring me joy. I am inspired and energized by the opportunity to help patients achieve their best oral health, functionality, and appearance. Advances in science, materials, and technology have made it possible to deliver efficient, comfortable care with lasting results.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to the field of dentistry in various capacities, both as a clinical practitioner and in administrative roles. The personal and professional rewards I’ve gained from helping patients and collaborating with colleagues remain deeply satisfying.
