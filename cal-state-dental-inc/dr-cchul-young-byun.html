Dr. Cchul Young Byun

Dentist

With decades of extensive experience, I am dedicated to providing the highest level of dental care in a gentle, compassionate manner. In 1984, I earned my Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from UNIMEP Dental School in Brazil, laying the foundation for my lifelong commitment to excellence in dentistry.

As a multicultural professional fluent in four languages Korean, Portuguese, Spanish, and English I am uniquely positioned to connect with patients from diverse backgrounds. My general practice specializes in Restorative and Cosmetic Dentistry, where I take pride in helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.

For over 23 years, I successfully cared for patients at my private practice in Santa Ana, CA, located in Orange County. Now, I am proud to bring that same level of care and expertise to Artesia, CA, as part of the dental team. My goal has always been to ensure every patient feels comfortable and confident in their dental care journey.

We are the local dentist near you!
