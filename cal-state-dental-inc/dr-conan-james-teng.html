Dr. Conan James Teng

Dentist

Dr. Teng is a highly trained oral and maxillofacial surgeon with a distinguished academic and professional background. He earned his D.D.S. from the Ostrow School of Dentistry at the University of Southern California in 2013, following a Ph.D. in Silicon-Polymer Chemistry and a B.S. in Chemistry from the same institution.

Dr. Teng’s extensive training includes an OMS residency at the University of Mississippi Medical Center in Jackson, MS (2018 2019), and OMS internships at Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, TX (2013 2014) and UT Health Science Center in San Antonio, TX (2014 2015). Additionally, he furthered his medical education at Texas Tech University School of Medicine in Lubbock, TX (2015 2016).

Passionate about serving his community and underserved populations worldwide, Dr. Teng has traveled extensively across the country and abroad to provide volunteer dental care to those in need. He specializes in all aspects of oral surgery, with a particular focus on full-mouth reconstruction, helping patients regain both functionality and confidence. Dr. Teng’s dedication to excellence and compassion makes him a trusted and respected figure in the field of oral surgery.
