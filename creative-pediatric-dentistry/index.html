Creating healthy smiles has always been my passion

With over a decade of advanced education, I hold both Master’s and Doctorate degrees in Oral Health.

The practice

Though I originally hailed from Prattville, Alabama, my heart found its home in West Virginia when I met my husband, Dr. Matt O’Dell (O’Dell Orthodontics). He swept me off my feet and into a life full of “Wild and Wonderful” things. Since 2013, I’ve been honored to serve the children of West Virginia with compassion, creativity, and clinical excellence.

At Creative Pediatric Dentistry, we believe that every child deserves exceptional care–delievered with compassion, creativity, and purpose . It’s our honor to serve this community because our children–and our families–deserve nothing but the best.

Dr. Lizzie is especially passionate about advocating for children with special needs. One way she does this is through a partnership with Autism CARES , a program dedicated to supporting children on the autism spectrum.

To better serve these amazing kids, Dr. Lizzie created a signature 6-step behavior therapy program designed to help them feel safe, supported, and successful during their dental visits.

Dr. Lizzie believes that education is empowerment . She stays on the cutting edge of pediatric dentistry so that she can provide the best possible care–and then shares what she’s learned with others. Whether it’s speaking at local dental meetings or mentoring colleagues, she is proud to be an educator and advocate for new behavior management therapies and evidence-based treatment modalities.

Every child is a dreamer, a doer, and a light in the world. At Creative Pediatric Dentistry, it’s our privilege to help protect their smile, their confidence, and their future.

To say that Dr. Lizzie is “dedicated” to her patients is truly an understatement. Her mission is to provide not only the highest quality dental treatment but also the most positive and personalized experience for every child who visits our office.

While she’s honored to be recognized nationally and proud to be a member of several respected professional organizations, her greatest reward is the trust you place in her to care for your child. You are a valued part of the Creative Pediatric Dentistry family–and together, we can build a healthy, confident smile .
