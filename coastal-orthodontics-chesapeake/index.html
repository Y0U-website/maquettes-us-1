The practice

We make orthodontics a smooth transition for teens. From traditional braces to nearly invisible clear aligners, we provide treatment options that give teenagers effective results while accommodating their busy school and social lives. Our fun, supportive team helps teens feel at ease every step of the way. If you need teen orthodontic care, get in touch with us today.

Early intervention can pave the way for a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our early orthodontic treatments are designed for children showing signs of potential dental issues, helping guide jaw development and create space for emerging teeth. By addressing problems early, we can minimize the need for extensive treatment later. Give your child the best start to a strong, healthy smile!

Dr. Pardeep Kaur combines world-class training with a down-to-earth, compassionate approach to orthodontics. As the heart of Coastal Orthodontics, she is dedicated to providing families in Virginia Beach and Chesapeake with beautiful, confident smiles that last a lifetime.

Dr. Kaur’s philosophy is simple: treat every patient with the same care and attention she would want for her own children. She takes the time to listen to your goals, answer your questions, and create a personalized treatment plan that fits your unique needs.

Dr. Kaur’s passion for orthodontics has driven her to pursue the highest levels of education and training. Her exceptional qualifications allow her to handle even the most complex cases with skill and confidence.

Dr. Kaur’s dedication to innovation extends beyond her clinical practice. She has contributed to groundbreaking research on ultra-low-dose CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) technology, which has revolutionized orthodontic diagnostics.
