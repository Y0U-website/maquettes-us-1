Our Treatments

Coastal Orthodontics is proud to provide teeth whitening, braces, Invisalign, and orthodontic treatment in Chesapeake. Below are some of the services we offer:

Adult Orthodontics

It’s never too late to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. At Coastal Orthodontics, we offer advanced adult orthodontic treatment options like clear aligners and custom braces designed to fit your lifestyle. Whether you’re correcting long-standing dental issues or enhancing your smile, our personalized care ensures discreet, efficient, and comfortable solutions for adults of all ages. Start your journey to a confident, bold smile today.

Teen Orthodontics

We make orthodontics a smooth transition for teens. From traditional braces to nearly invisible clear aligners, we provide treatment options that give teenagers effective results while accommodating their busy school and social lives. Our fun, supportive team helps teens feel at ease every step of the way. If you need teen orthodontic care, get in touch with us today.

Early Orthodontics

Early intervention can pave the way for a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our early orthodontic treatments are designed for children showing signs of potential dental issues, helping guide jaw development and create space for emerging teeth. By addressing problems early, we can minimize the need for extensive treatment later. Give your child the best start to a strong, healthy smile!
