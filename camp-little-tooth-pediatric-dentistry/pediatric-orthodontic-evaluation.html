Pediatric Orthodontic Evaluation

The American Association of Orthodontists has officially recommended that children should see the orthodontist or at least have an initial orthodontic evaluation between the ages of seven and nine years old. Even if your child doesn’t have all of his or her permanent teeth, the growth pattern of the teeth can still be predicted quite accurately.
