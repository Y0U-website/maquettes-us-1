Orthodontic Treatments in…

All Ages

The American Dental Association recommends that children see an orthodontist by age 7, as this is an ideal time to evaluate growth and identify potential concerns early on. At Bloom Orthodontics, Dr. Gerkowicz takes a gentle approach to monitoring developing smiles and recommending treatment only when the timing is right. Early visits allow us to guide healthy jaw and tooth development while creating a positive, comfortable experience that helps kids feel confident about their smile journey from the very beginning.

The teen years are often the ideal time for orthodontic treatment, as most permanent teeth are in place while the smile is still developing. At Bloom Orthodontics, Dr. Gerkowicz creates personalized treatment plans designed to fit busy schedules, active lifestyles, and growing confidence. Whether using braces or clear aligners, our goal is to make treatment comfortable, efficient, and supportive so teens can feel great about their smile throughout the process.

It’s never too late to achieve a healthy, confident smile. Dr. Gerkowicz loves treating adult patients and understands the unique goals and lifestyle considerations that come with orthodontic care later in life. Whether you are looking to improve function, enhance your smile, or revisit treatment you never had before, we offer discreet, personalized options designed to fit comfortably into your daily routine while delivering lasting results.

Braces

Traditional braces are a reliable and highly effective way to correct a wide range of orthodontic concerns, from crowding and spacing to bite alignment issues. Today’s braces are more comfortable and streamlined than ever, making treatment easier and more efficient for patients of all ages. At Bloom Orthodontics, Dr. Gerkowicz customizes every braces treatment plan to ensure healthy function, beautiful results, and a positive experience from start to finish.

Ceramic braces work just like traditional braces but use clear or tooth-colored brackets for a more subtle appearance. They are a great option for teens and adults who want effective orthodontic treatment with a less noticeable look. At Bloom Orthodontics, Dr. Gerkowicz carefully designs each treatment plan to combine comfort, aesthetics, and reliable results, helping you feel confident throughout your smile journey.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a comfortable, virtually invisible orthodontic option that gently moves teeth into alignment using a series of custom-made, removable trays. Because they can be taken out, you can continue enjoying your favorite foods and maintain your normal brushing and flossing routine. They are a popular choice for teens and adults who want a discreet and flexible treatment experience. At Bloom Orthodontics, Dr. Gerkowicz designs clear aligner treatment plans to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile that fits seamlessly into your everyday life.

Appliances

Orthodontic appliances play an important role in guiding proper growth and development, especially for younger patients. At Bloom Orthodontics, we use a variety of custom appliances to support healthy jaw alignment, create space for developing teeth, and correct bite concerns early on. Each appliance is carefully selected based on your child’s unique needs and growth stage to ensure the most effective and comfortable treatment experience.

We offer expanders to gently widen the upper jaw, space maintainers to preserve room for permanent teeth, functional appliances to help guide jaw development and improve bite alignment, and habit appliances designed to discourage thumb sucking or tongue thrusting. These tools allow us to address concerns at the right time. They can be used on their own or in combination with braces or aligners.

Retainers

Retainers are the final stage of orthodontic treatment, also known as the retention phase. After your teeth have been moved into their ideal positions, retainers help keep your smile stable and prevent shifting over time. Wearing your retainer as recommended by Dr. Gerkowicz is an important part of protecting your investment and maintaining your beautiful, healthy results for years to come.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is a simple and effective way to enhance your smile and boost your confidence. At Bloom Orthodontics, we offer whitening options designed to safely brighten your teeth and complement your orthodontic results. Whether you are finishing treatment or simply looking to refresh your smile, professional whitening can help you achieve a noticeably brighter appearance.

Technology

Our advanced 3D intraoral scanner is a modern alternative to traditional dental impressions, allowing us to create highly accurate digital models of your smile without messy molds. As your smile is scanned, you can watch the process happen in real time on the computer screen and see a detailed digital version of your teeth being created instantly. This technology also allows you to preview how your smile may look after treatment, helping you better understand your options and feel confident about your orthodontic journey.

Digital X-rays are an important tool that allow us to capture clear, detailed images of your teeth, roots, and jaw structure quickly and accurately. Because they use low levels of radiation compared to traditional X-rays, they are a safe and efficient way to evaluate your teeth and monitor treatment progress. Digital imaging also helps Dr. Gerkowicz create a treatment plan that’s right for you.

Digital photography allows us to capture detailed images of your smile, teeth, and facial profile to help plan and monitor your orthodontic treatment. These photos help Dr. Gerkowicz evaluate changes over time, track your progress, and ensure your treatment is tailored to both function and aesthetics. Digital photography also gives you the opportunity to see your smile transformation from start to finish, making your orthodontic journey more visual, informative, and exciting.
