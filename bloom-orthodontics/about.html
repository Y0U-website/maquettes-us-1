Meet Dr. Lauren Gerkowicz

Dr. Lauren Gerkowicz believes orthodontic care should be personalized, comfortable, and empowering for every patient. She takes the time to understand each person’s goals and lifestyle, creating customized, evidenced-based treatment plans that support healthy function and confident smiles. Her warm, relationship-focused approach helps patients of all ages feel informed, supported, and excited throughout their smile journey. Dr. Gerkowicz has extensive training in using clear aligners to correct orthodontic issues, in addition to traditional braces.
