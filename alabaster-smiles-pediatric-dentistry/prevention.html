Prevention

By working closely with your child’s dentist, you can help ensure a lifetime of healthy smiles.

  • Clean teeth: Professional cleanings can help remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing may miss.
  • Apply fluoride treatments: Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent cavities.
  • Provide oral hygiene instruction: Your dentist can teach you and your child proper brushing and flossing techniques.
  • Identify and address oral habits: They can help correct harmful habits like thumb-sucking or teeth grinding.
