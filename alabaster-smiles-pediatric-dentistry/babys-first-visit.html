Your Baby’s First Visit

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children visit a dentist to receive an initial dental exam within six months of when their first tooth erupts. These first dental visits are designed to establish a dental home and help your child begin developing positive associations with visiting the dentist.

Dental Care Starts When Teeth Appear

Your baby’s first dental visit is a big milestone! It’s a great opportunity to establish a positive relationship with a pediatric dentist and set the stage for a lifetime of healthy smiles. The dentist will gently examine your baby’s mouth, checking for any signs of potential problems and offering guidance on oral hygiene.

Remember, the goal of this initial visit is to introduce your baby to the dental environment in a fun and stress-free way. The dentist will likely focus on teaching you how to properly clean your baby’s teeth and gums, and may even apply a protective fluoride coating to their teeth. By starting early, you’re taking an important step in ensuring your child’s oral health for years to come.
