About Us

Convenient locations in Dearborn & Brownstown, MI.

Our Doctors have both completed General Practice residencies. During their residencies they treated some of the worst mouths imaginable. They have taken many post graduate courses to stay on the cutting edge of what is available to treat your mouth. Our commitment to life long learning ensures you the highest level of care both today and tomorrow.
