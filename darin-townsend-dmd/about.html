Our Philosophy

Townsend Family Dental Center’s dental philosophies are established on personal integrity and science. Dr. Townsend informs his patients of the best treatment needed and goes above and beyond the standard of care to the best of his ability.

This alone can make a dentist successful, but in his care a patient will receive more than that. Dentistry is a very personal service that requires the establishment of a rapport and trust. Dr. Townsend provides family dentistry with a personal touch and makes a point to be an excellent listener to his patients’ needs.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to present necessary treatment plans and provide competent, quality dentistry that exceeds patients’ expectations in a warm, loving atmosphere.

We will serve and support Liberty County and surrounding areas as a center for dental care and as an active supporter of the medical community. We will contribute to the dental care of the poor and the underserved here in our community.

We will always expand and improve our services by being progressive, technologically current, and receptive to the wants and needs of our patients and our community.

Meet Dr. Gregory Ganzkow

Dr. Greg Ganzkow is a highly experienced and compassionate dentist with nearly four decades of clinical excellence in comprehensive and airway-centered dentistry. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry from Gonzaga University in 1982, followed by his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 1986. Dr. Ganzkow completed a General Practice Residency in Dentistry at the University of Mississippi Medical Center in 1987, where he developed a strong foundation in full-scope patient care.

Dr. Ganzkow began his dental career serving patients with limited access to care through a full-service mobile dental practice in the Seattle and Pacific Northwest region. In 1991, he established his private practice in Shoreline, Washington. His practice then evolved into a health-, body-, and airway-focused practice with a strong emphasis on gnathologic restorative dentistry, orthodontics, and early intervention.

What Dr. Ganzkow values most about dentistry is the ability to improve overall health by addressing airway, occlusion, and function while restoring confident, healthy smiles. He takes pride in offering a wide range of services—including dental sleep medicine, orthodontics, implant dentistry, periodontal therapy, full-mouth restoration, and advanced therapies for TMJ and head and neck pain—while working alongside a highly trained and collaborative team.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ganzkow enjoys traveling with his wife, Karen, staying active through biking and skiing, spending time with their dogs, and volunteering with local community organizations.
