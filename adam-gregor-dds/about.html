Dental Expertise Rooted in Care and Community

Meet Dr. Adam Gregor

Dr. Gregor combines impressive dental credentials with a deep commitment to personal patient care. He and his wife, Mari-Jo, have strong connections to Western New York, enriching our practice’s community-oriented ethos. A proud alumni of the University at Buffalo, Dr. Gregor obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, graduating Magna Cum Laude.

Throughout his educational journey, Dr. Gregor’s exceptional skills and caring nature earned him multiple accolades. These included the Class of 1952 Scholarship, the Dental Alumni Association Award, and the Dr. Joseph Maffei Memorial Scholarship. These recognitions attest to his ethical and caring approach to patients, leadership, community service, and a sense of humor that puts patients at ease.

Determined to remain at the forefront of the dental field, Dr. Gregor continues his education, aiming to join the prestigious ranks of the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD) Fellows.
