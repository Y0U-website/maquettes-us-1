What Is a Dental Implant, And How Does It Work?

Dental implants explained by Dr. Madhavan, your Sammamish specialist.

A dental implant is an artificial tooth root anchored into the jaw to replace a missing tooth or support crowns, bridges, or dentures. Implants act like natural teeth roots, stimulating bone growth, maintaining jaw structure, and preserving your smile’s natural appearance.

How do we place a dental implant?

The entire implant procedure, from diagnostics to final placement of the crown or denture, is conveniently done right in our office. So there’s no need to travel to another office for any part of the procedure!

Dental implant treatment has long-lasting results that will keep you smiling confidently for years. Our dentist ensures precise implant placement using advanced technology, including 3D CT scans and guided surgery software. Dr. Asha Madhavan’s expertise and genuine care create a comfortable treatment experience for you, with thorough explanations and continuous check-ins. Our team provides post-procedure follow-up to ensure your well-being.

Here is the process so you know what to expect:

  • High-Tech Diagnostics And Meticulous Planning Before your procedure, the dentist will meet with you for a comprehensive consultation. Using X-rays and intraoral photos, they will meticulously plan your procedure. Where necessary, the dentist will use CT scans or special guided implant surgery software to precisely plan the placing of the implant.
  • Extractions Dr. Asha Madhavan will perform gentle extractions if necessary as part of your dental implant procedure.
  • Bone Grafting And Sinus Augmentation The dentist will perform bone grafting in case there’s insufficient bone to successfully support dental implants. They will perform a sinus lift for patients whose sinuses have extended down into the part of the upper jaw where the implant will be placed.
  • Maintaining Your Comfort Our team will make sure you are comfortable and relaxed during your treatment. We use the latest equipment and techniques to make sure your treatment is minimally invasive, efficient, and nontraumatic. Dr. Asha Madhavan makes every effort to explain everything so that you are clear about the procedure. For long procedures or anxious patients, we offer multiple sedation options. We provide blankets and pillows, televisions, and sound-canceling headphones for added comfort.
  • High-Quality, Durable New Teeth After your implant placement, you will receive a temporary restoration. Once the implant site has fully healed, you will receive your durable, beautiful final restoration. Your final crowns, bridges, or full-arch fixed teeth will be made out of top-quality materials. The dentist will place your final restorations and make any needed adjustments, so you are delighted with the way they look, feel, and fit. Your new teeth will have a natural appearance and will function very much like real teeth.
