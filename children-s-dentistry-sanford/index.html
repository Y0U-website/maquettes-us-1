The practice

At Montshire, we’re teaming up with patients and their parents to prevent tooth decay and other dental issues through early detection of disease, education, and a focus on cutting edge preventative care. When treatment is necessary we are dedicated to achieving more with less: less pain, less cost, less stress, and less interference with natural teeth.

Our minimally invasive approach focuses on preventative and restorative treatments to tackle tooth decay, the most common dental issue. These treatments are inexpensive, safe, and comfortable, and best of all they significantly reduce the need for "drill and fill" procedures, creating a more positive dental experience for the whole family.
