Glass Ionomer:

We use glass ionomer, a versatile white material, for various treatments, including sealants, fillings, and crown placements. This material stands out because it releases minerals that rebuild and strengthen natural tooth structures. Its antimicrobial properties help combat decay, and its biocompatibility ensures it works harmoniously with your child’s teeth. Glass ionomer’s thermal properties match those of natural teeth, reducing the chance of leakage. Its straightforward application process requires less isolation, making treatments quicker and more comfortable.

Minimally Invasive Treatment for MIH:

Molar Incisor Hypomineralization (MIH) is a common condition affecting both baby and adult teeth. It presents two main clinical challenges: weakened tooth structure, which is more susceptible to breakdown, and hypersensitivity due to an increased concentration of nerve endings. To address these issues, we use Silver Diamine Fluoride and Glass Ionomer, which have proven highly effective. By following the technique outlined below, we can enhance your child’s long-term dental health while minimizing the need for multiple uncomfortable visits.

  • Minimally Invasive Treatment for Molar Incisor Hypomineralization Published November 2018

Fluoride Safety:

Fluoride helps to remineralize teeth, kill harmful bacteria, and prevent future tooth breakdown. While the benefits of fluoride for oral health are undeniable, it is also true that fluoride ingested in large concentrations can be harmful to human health. At Montshire, we take a careful and informed approach to fluoride use to maximize its benefits while ensuring safety for all our patients.

Fluoride Safety

  • 1. Controlled and Targeted Application: Our use of fluoride is highly targeted and localized. Fluoride varnish and silver diamine fluoride (SDF) are applied directly to the teeth by trained professionals in minimal amounts, ensuring maximum benefit without significant systemic exposure.
  • 2. Minimized Systemic Exposure: Unlike fluoride in drinking water, which is ingested, fluoride treatments in our office act directly on the tooth surface. For toothpaste, we recommend small, age-appropriate amounts to minimize swallowing while ensuring effective cavity prevention.
  • 3. Extensive Research and Endorsements: Fluoride has been studied extensively and endorsed by major health organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), as a safe and effective way to prevent cavities when used appropriately.

Fluoride in Toothpaste

Fluoride toothpaste remains one of the simplest and most effective ways to maintain healthy teeth at home. By brushing twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, you strengthen enamel, prevent cavities, and lay the foundation for lifelong oral health. To ensure safe use, monitor children’s brushing, use only the recommended amount based on age, and encourage spitting out toothpaste rather than rinsing.
