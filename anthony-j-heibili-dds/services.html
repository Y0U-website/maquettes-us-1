We all realize that having a healthy, bright, beautiful smile enhances our appearance and allows us to smile with confidence.

General dentistry includes an assortment of services and procedures, helping patients preserve their teeth as long as possible.

Caring for your teeth and keeping them as healthy as possible helps prevent cavities, gum disease, enamel wear, and more.

We meet with you 1-on-1 to investigate and assess your overall dental health. This evaluation will consist of a view of your oral structures and medical history.

After your initial evaluation, impressions may be taken and your dentist will create a treatment plan to address any areas of concern.

Here, restorations may be placed, adjustments are made to prescribed appliances, and your doctor will go over any questions or concerns you may have.
