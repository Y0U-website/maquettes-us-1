Take a look at the incredible before and after transformations of our patients’ smiles.

View Smile Transformations From misaligned teeth to beautifully aligned and confident smiles, our orthodontic treatments have changed lives!

BEFORE AFTER Sutter arrived with excessive overlapping of his front teeth, also called a deep bite. Orthodontic treatment corrected Sutter’s deep bite to help prevent future wear of his teeth and keep his new smile handsome for years to come.

BEFORE AFTER Jordan presented to our office with fairly severe crowding. Crowded teeth can be more difficult to clean, which can lead to future decay. Improving the alignment of his teeth made them easier to clean and maintain.

BEFORE AFTER Sydney’s lower jaw was back relative to her upper jaw. Using the Herbst Appliance, we were able to improve her facial balance as well as the function of her bite by taking advantage of her remaining jaw growth.

BEFORE AFTER Juan initially presented with an anterior open bite, meaning his front teeth do not touch, making it hard to chew his food. He started with Invisalign treatment to improve alignment then moved into braces for a short period to bring all of his teeth together.

BEFORE AFTER Carolyn arrived with her back teeth hitting heavily and her front teeth in cross-bite. New advances in Invisalign treatment helped to correct her cross-bite and gave her an even bite from front to back.

BEFORE AFTER Steven came to our office unhappy with the alignment of his teeth. A digitally customized treatment plan included the removal of four permanent teeth to improve his alignment and allow him to show off his amazing new smile.
