Dental Veneer Cementation

With no prep veneers, Dr. Kumar will not need to remove any enamel from the front surface of your teeth. This minimally invasive procedure enhances your smile without altering your natural teeth, ensuring maximum comfort without the need for anesthesia.

With no prep veneers, Dr. Kumar will not need to place temporary veneers since the natural teeth remain unaltered during the procedure. This eliminates the need for a protective phase and reduces the risk of gum inflammation. Your no prep veneers are custom-fabricated in a top dental lab, ensuring a seamless and efficient process without the need for temporary solutions. If you prefer to try the final result, a mock-up can still be temporarily cemented.

Advantages of Porcelain Veneers

Enhanced Aesthetics

  • Dental veneers offer a significant improvement in the appearance of your smile by covering up imperfections such as discoloration, stains, chips, cracks, and gaps between teeth.
  • In Avon and Farmington Valley, Dr. Kumar and his team specializing in dental veneers can create custom-designed veneers that match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, resulting in a beautiful and natural-looking smile.
  • With the expertise of skilled cosmetic dentists in the area, patients can achieve a smile transformation that boosts their confidence and self-esteem.

Minimally Invasive Procedure

  • The process of getting dental veneers is minimally invasive compared to other cosmetic dental procedures.
  • Skilled team of Dr. Kumar (cosmetic dentist) in Avon and Farmington Valley takes great care in preparing the teeth for veneers, ensuring that only a minimal amount of enamel is removed to accommodate the veneers.
  • This conservative approach preserves the natural structure of the teeth while still achieving stunning aesthetic results, making dental veneers a popular choice among patients seeking cosmetic enhancements.

Durable and Long-lasting

  • Dental veneers are made from durable materials such as porcelain or composite resin, which are resistant to stains and discoloration.
  • Dr. Kumar in Avon and Farmington Valley uses high-quality materials and advanced techniques to fabricate and bond veneers, ensuring their longevity and durability.
  • With proper care and maintenance, dental veneers can last for many years, providing patients with a lasting improvement in their smile’s appearance.

Customized Treatment

  • Dental veneers not only enhance the appearance of the smile but also offer functional benefits by strengthening and protecting the teeth
  • Veneers provide an additional layer of protection against enamel erosion, reducing the risk of tooth decay and sensitivity.
  • Dr. Kumar in Avon and Farmington Valley ensures that the veneers are bonded securely to the teeth, promoting long-term oral health and stability.

Quick and Convenient

  • The process of getting dental veneers is relatively quick and convenient, requiring only a few visits to the cosmetic dentist’s office.
  • Dr. Kumar uses advanced technology and techniques to streamline the veneer placement process, minimizing discomfort and downtime for patients.
  • Patients can enjoy a dramatic improvement in their smile’s appearance in a short amount of time, making dental veneers an ideal choice for busy individuals seeking cosmetic enhancements.
