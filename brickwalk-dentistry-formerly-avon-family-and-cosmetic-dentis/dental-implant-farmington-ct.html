Bridge vs. Dental Implant: Which is the Right Choice for You?

When replacing missing teeth, both bridges and dental implants offer effective solutions, but they differ significantly in terms of functionality, longevity, and impact on your oral health. At Brickwalk Dentistry, Dr. Kumar provides personalized care to help you choose the option that best suits your needs.

Dental Implants are a modern, long-term solution for tooth replacement. An implant involves placing a titanium post into the jawbone, which acts as a natural tooth root and supports a custom crown. Implants offer unmatched durability, preserve jawbone health by stimulating bone growth, and require no alteration to surrounding teeth. They are ideal for those seeking a permanent and independent solution.

Dental Bridges , on the other hand, involve creating a prosthetic tooth anchored by crowns on the adjacent natural teeth. While bridges can be completed more quickly and don’t require surgery, they do necessitate reshaping healthy teeth to support the structure. Bridges don’t stimulate the jawbone, which can lead to bone loss over time.

The Single Unit Implant Process at Brickwalk Dentistry

Dr. Kumar begins with a thorough examination and consultation to evaluate your oral health and determine if you’re a candidate for dental implants. He may take digital X-rays or 3D scans to assess your bone structure and plan the ideal placement for the implant.

A customized treatment plan is created based on your unique needs. Dr. Kumar will discuss the procedure, timelines, and expected outcomes, ensuring you feel informed and confident every step of the way.

The dental implant is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation, depending on your comfort level. Over the next few months, the implant will integrate with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration, forming a secure foundation.

Once the implant has fully integrated, Dr. Kumar will attach an abutment, a small connector that holds the custom crown. The crown is crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth for a seamless appearance.

After your implant is complete, Dr. Kumar schedules follow-up visits to ensure everything is healing properly. With regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene, your implant will provide lasting results.
