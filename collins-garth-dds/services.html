Services

We are committed to providing high-quality general and family dental care in order to improve the health, function, and appearance of your smile. We are pleased to offer a wide range of services in order to meet your family’s dental needs. Our caring and friendly dental team will make sure that you are as comfortable as possible when you come in for any of our many dental treatments. The services Garth Collins D.D.S. provides include:

Preventive Services

Keep your smile healthy and prevent diseases with our preventative services. Our preventive services include:

-Dental Cleanings and Exams

-Tooth Extractions

-Oral Hygiene

-Periodontal Care

-Emergency Care

-Composite (White) Fillings

Cosmetic Services

Improve your sparkling smile and overall health with our cosmetic services. Our preventive services include:

-Opalescence Teeth Whitening

-Partial &Full Dentures

-Veneers

Restorative Services

Restore your smile and feel more confident with our restorative services. Our preventive services include:

-Dental Bridges

-Dental Crowns

-Dental Implant Restoration
