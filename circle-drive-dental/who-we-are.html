A graduate of University of Illinois at Chicago: College of Dentistry, Dr. Beatty is also a member of the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, and various local study clubs. Dr. Beatty served as past president for Zumbro Valley Dental Society; member for Good Samaritan Dental Clinic advisory board; and supervising dentist at Rochester Community and Technical College: Dental Hygiene Program.

Dr. Beatty is a Rochester, MN, native, and graduated from Lourdes High School. He later obtained majors in Biology and Art at Luther College in Decorah, IA. Outside of the office, Dr. Beatty enjoys spending time with his wife, Megan, and his three children, Ryan, Hannah, and Clara. They stay active in their church community, and, when their busy schedule allows, they love going to the lake!

Community

November 2021: We collected Halloween candy as part of Operation Gratitude’s Halloween Candy Giveback Program. We hand delivered bags of candy to local first responders to show our gratitude for the many ways they serve our community.

August 2021: We shopped for and donated back to school clothes for the children at Jeremiah Program in Rochester. We were honored to partner with such a wonderful organization. Their mission is to help end poverty, two generations at a time.

May 2021: We held a raffle and donated all of the proceeds to MN Adult and Teen Challenge. A $1,000 check was hand delivered to the director of MNTC on behalf of CDD because of the generosity of people who bought raffle tickets.

December 2020: We collected toys for Toys for Tots. Thank you to all who donated toys!
