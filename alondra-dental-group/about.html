Alondra Dental Care Office in Norwalk: Why Choose Us?

Our mission is to create beautiful smiles and comfortable patient experiences.

Our Dental Office

With seasoned doctors and state-of-the-art equipment , we offer top-notch care to our patients. You can expect first-rate treatment and a completely sanitized, cozy, and clean office each time you sit in one of our chairs.

Alondra Dental Care offers extended office hours on select days and sends convenient appointment reminders. We also offer a quick turnaround time on calls and appointment requests. We’re happy to answer any questions you have via email, over the phone, or in our Norwalk dental office.

We are qualified to handle all aspects of patient care in our Norwalk dental office and, when needed, only refer to colleagues with similar professional principles.

We currently accept any PPO (not HMO or Medi-Cal). We’ll be glad to go over your insurance coverage with you when you book an appointment.

At Alondra Dental Care, we make your patient experience as enjoyable as possible by creating a treatment plan tailored to your individual needs, clearly communicating the next steps and what to expect, offering dental sedation, and encouraging you to bring music to listen to with headphones.

Meet Our Team

Dr. Maria Victoria Abad

Dr. Maria Victoria Abad is the founder of Alondra Dental Care. Her vision and dedication to personalized dental treatment have created comfortable patient experiences over the past 22 years. Along with her passion for beautiful and healthy smiles, she brings the latest technology to her patients.

Dr. Abad utilizes the newest teeth cleaning equipment, including a CBCT (3D x-ray) , an intraoral scanner , and intraoral cameras. Combined with a treatment-based approach, advanced technology personalizes the patients’ experience for optimal results. She appreciates furthering her research and is a member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Harbor Dental Society, and American Association of Clear Aligners.

Aside from being a dentist, Dr. Abad is a doting mother of four who enjoys reading, gardening, and traveling.
