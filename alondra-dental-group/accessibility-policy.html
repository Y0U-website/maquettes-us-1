Accessibility Policy | Quality Care

Alondra Dental Group is committed to ensuring accessibility of its website to people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone, and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

We are working to ensure new and updated web content produced by our company will meet W3C WAI WCAG 2.1 Level AA, by December 2022.

Existing web content produced by our organization will meet that standard by December 2022.

Content provided for our site by third-party developers will meet W3C WAI WCAG 2.1 Level A, by December 2022. This does not include user-generated content.

We will review this policy on or before December 2022. This policy was last reviewed on October 2021.

For questions regarding our efforts to maintain accessibility, please contact info@alondradental.com .
