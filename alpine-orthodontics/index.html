The practice

For over 30 years, our orthodontic practice in the heart of Alpine, CA, has been dedicated to transforming smiles and changing lives. We proudly serve children, teens, and adults with the highest quality, state-of-the-art orthodontic care—right here in our local community.

Beyond straightening teeth, we are committed to inspiring confidence, fostering lasting relationships, and enriching the lives of the families we are honored to meet. Our mission is to make advanced, high-tech orthodontics accessible and affordable while creating a warm, welcoming environment where every patient feels valued and supported.

At our practice, we don’t just create beautiful smiles—we help build brighter futures, one patient at a time.

From braces to clear aligners, help your teen achieve their dream smile!

Our office believes knowing your treatment options and understanding is the first key to a great experience. We make sure you know your diagnosis, treatment choices, insurance and financial options. We work hard to accommodate your family with the highest standard of care, all right here in Alpine. Outstanding customer service and patient care are our highest priorities.
