The practice

Cracked, chipped, or missing teeth? No worries, we ve got your back! Whether it s a minor fix or a major overhaul, our team will make sure your smile is healthy and gets the attention we all know you deserve.

Whether it s gums or roots, we re not squeamish about diving deep. Tooth aches and gum problems stink, but they are our speciality. Our menu of deep cleanings, scaling and root planing or root canals will make sure we get you back to good. And let s be honest, food is WAY too good to not be able to chew it. Let s fix that.
