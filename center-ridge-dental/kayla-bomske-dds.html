Meet Dr. Bomske

Dr. Bomske Provides Compassionate Care You Can Trust

As a first-generation dentist, Dr. Bomske knows how important quality dental care is and strives to make every visit a positive experience. She creates a reassuring atmosphere during every appointment, helping patients feel at ease while prioritizing their experience in the chair. She wants you to leave happy and healthy and won’t settle for less.

A Life Beyond Dentistry

Dr. Bomske grew up on Cincinnati’s East Side and earned her Bachelor of Science from Xavier University. She completed her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at The Ohio State University, where she met Dr. Harris (GO BUCKS!). She and Dr. Harris have been married for two years and recently welcomed their first child, Ruby. Outside the office, Dr. Bomske enjoys running, skiing, playing volleyball, traveling, and cheering on the Browns.
