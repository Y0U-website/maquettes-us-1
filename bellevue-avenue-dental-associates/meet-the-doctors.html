MEET THE DOCTORS

Dr. Leitman

Dr. Daniel Leitman is a distinguished graduate of Fairleigh Dickinson University, having obtained a Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology, Summa Cum Laude, class of 1995. He later pursued his dental education at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in Newark, graduating in 1998. Dr. Leitman further advanced his expertise by completing his General Practice Residency, with an emphasis on Implant and Cosmetic Dentistry, at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, New York, after which he served as Chief Resident for an additional year.

Dr. Leitman is a member of several distinguished dental organizations, including the American Dental Association, the New Jersey Dental Association, and The Academy of General Dentistry. His impressive qualifications and commitment to staying at the forefront of the latest in dental advancements inspire great confidence in his patients, ensuring that they receive the best possible care.

Dr. Delzotto

Dr. Anthony Delzotto attended Boston College, where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He then went on to rank amongst the top of his class at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Delzotto’s dedication to his profession led him to complete a rigorous General Practice Residency at Hackensack Meridian-Jersey Shore University Medical Center in Neptune, where he gained extensive clinical experience in all areas of general dentistry including emergency dentistry, oral surgery and cosmetics. In addition, Dr. Delzotto has experience with some of the latest dental technologies such as digital scanning, laser dentistry and 3-D imaging.

Dr. Delzotto is a member of several professional dental organizations, including the New Jersey Dental Association, the American Dental Association and The Academy of General Dentistry, and is dedicated to staying current with the latest advancements in the field of dentistry, providing his patients with exceptional care.
