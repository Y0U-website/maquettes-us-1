Fillings

We use the best available technology and techniques to ensure a long lasting, comfortable, and esthetic result.

Laser Therapy

We provide diode laser therapy for a variety of uses including treating herpetic lesions (cold sores), apthous ulcers (canker sores), and more involved treatments such as gingivectomies or minor gum surgeries.

SEDATION DENTISTRY »

If anxiety has been keeping you from making an appointment, we encourage you to consider our sedation options. Our comfortable and stress-free services can help you take good care of your teeth. Your comfort is our priority, and so is your safety.

Sleep Apnea

We use the Seattle Protocol to assist in managing sleep apnea. We use at home sleep studies to determine what treatment will best serve you and can fabricate various mandibular advancement appliances to help with symptoms.

Fluoride & Minimally-invasive Treatments

Preventative treatments such as Minimal Intervention (MI) Paste and Silver Diamine Fluoride.

TMJ Treatments

We provide and are trained in microneedling, Xeomin/Botox, occlusal equilibration and more traditional orthotic treatment for TMJ disorder.

Night Guards & Sports Guards

Prevention is the key to protecting your teeth. We fabricate custom night guards to protect your dentition.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Crowns

We will recommend a crown if your tooth is significantly broken or has too much decay for a traditional composite filling.

Veneers

We may use dental veneers, thin coverings bonded to the front of teeth, to improve the appearance and continuity of your smile.

Orthodontics

We are trained in invisalign and clear aligner therapy to treat dental crowding and esthetic needs.

Botlulinum Toxin or Botox

We are trained in injectables for both facial/dental esthetics and TMJ symptom relief. We use Xeomin.

Restorative Dentistry

Dental Implants »

Implant treatment consists of placing artificial roots within the jawbone.

Traditional Bridges

For decades, bridges have been an effective way to restore missing teeth.

Partial & Complete Dentures

If you are missing some or all of your teeth, one of the simplest solutions is to have teeth that are removable.

Extractions and Bone Grafting

For those who are in need we can provide both surgical and nonsurgical tooth extractions. We also are able to provide bone grafting to support you for future implant therapy.

Endodontics

We provide root canal therapy or endodontics using rotary endodontics.
