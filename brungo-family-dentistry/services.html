Services

Please contact our office to discuss these or any other dental services.

Cosmetic Dentistry Performed to look as natural as possible, cosmetic procedures can include veneers, porcelain inlays, onlays, crowns, and tooth-colored fillings.

Crowns and Bridges Watch Video A crown is a cap that’s placed over a weak or damaged tooth. A bridge is a series of crowns fused together to replace one or more missing teeth.

Dental Bonding We bond a tooth-colored resin to the tooth with a special curing light to restore or improve a patient’s smile.

Dentures and Partials Watch Video A denture is a removable tooth replacement used when all the teeth are missing. (See also Fixed Implant Hybrid Dentures .) We use partial dentures when some natural teeth remain.

Digital Radiography Our digital radiography system reduces radiation exposure, improves x-ray diagnostics, and helps save the environment.

Extractions A dental extraction (exodontia) is the removal of a tooth. Extractions are performed for a wide variety of reasons, including decay that has destroyed enough tooth structure to prevent restoration, or a fracture that is beyond repair.

Fixed Implant Hybrid Dentures Watch Video We can fabricate dentures that are screwed in to implants. This hybrid denture combines the advantages of fixed and removable dentures, allowing function as close to natural teeth as possible .

Gum Treatment We treat active periodontal disease of the gums and bone to slow or stop disease progression.

Invisible Braces Watch Video Invisible braces use a series of thin, removable, clear plastic mouthpieces to gradually move teeth into alignment, usually without the use of metal braces.

Implant Retained Dentures We can have an oral surgeon place implants that will help hold your dentures stable while they are in your mouth.

Orthodontics Braces apply steady pressure over time to correct crooked or misaligned teeth and improve the appearance of your smile. We believe preventive orthodontics at an early age can decrease treatment time and create a more stable result.

Pediatric Dentistry We see children of all ages for preventive oral health, treatment of dental diseases, and the prevention and treatment of misaligned teeth.

Porcelain Veneers Watch Video Porcelain or resin veneers are very thin shells that cover the front surface of teeth to change their shade, shape, or length.

Preventive Dentistry Prevention is the key to a long-lasting, healthy smile. We recommended exams and cleanings at least twice a year to keep your teeth in good shape and prevent small problems from becoming bigger.

Root Canal Treatment This treatment repairs a badly decayed or abscessed tooth by removing the nerve and cleaning and sealing the inside of the tooth. We refer most root canals to our local endodontists.

Sealants Watch Video We can apply thin plastic coatings to grooves on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth to protect against decay by keeping germs and food particles out of these grooves.

Teeth Whitening Teeth can become stained from coffee, tea, soda, smoking, and aging. Our take-home whitening system safely whitens your teeth in 2 to 3 weeks, with results lasting 3 to 5 years.

TMJ/TMD Therapy When the harmony among your teeth, jaw muscles, and temporomandibular joints is disturbed, Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMD or TMJ) results in pain and discomfort. We can perform a comprehensive exam and develop a personalized treatment plan to alleviate symptoms.

We now have a 3D scanner the replaces the need to take physical impressions of your teeth. We are able to use these scan to make crowns and bridges on both teeth and implants, nightguards, orthodontic aligners and retainers. No more impression trays full of putty!
