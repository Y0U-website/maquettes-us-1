All-on-6 is a full-arch approach in which six dental implants may support a fixed set of replacement teeth. The added implant positions can provide another way to distribute support when anatomy and the restorative design allow.

More implants are not automatically better for every person. A personalized clinical evaluation is required to determine the safest and most appropriate configuration.

How All-on-6 Is Planned

The dental team uses an examination and 3D CBCT imaging to evaluate bone volume, anatomy, oral health, and restorative space. Implant positions are selected in coordination with the planned full-arch restoration.

All-on-6 Compared With Other Options

All-on-6 is one of several full-arch possibilities. All-on-4, All-on-5, implant-supported dentures, and traditional dentures may also be considered depending on oral health, lifestyle, goals, and budget.

What to Expect

The treatment plan may include preparation, implant placement, healing, and delivery of a final restoration. Timing varies. The team will explain temporary-tooth options and recovery instructions that apply to your case.

Protecting Your Investment

Daily cleaning, professional maintenance, and periodic evaluation are important for implant and restoration health. Individual instructions will be provided as part of ongoing care.
