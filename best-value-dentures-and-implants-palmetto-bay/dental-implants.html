What Are Dental Implants?

Replace one missing tooth with a titanium implant and custom crown that looks, feels, and functions like your natural tooth.

Replace multiple adjacent missing teeth with implants supporting a dental bridge, avoiding the need to modify healthy neighboring teeth.

Replace an entire arch of missing teeth with our All-on-4 or All-on-6 implant solutions for a permanent, non-removable smile.

Secure your dentures with strategically placed implants, eliminating slipping and improving comfort and chewing ability.

In select cases, receive your implant and temporary restoration in a single visit, minimizing treatment time.
