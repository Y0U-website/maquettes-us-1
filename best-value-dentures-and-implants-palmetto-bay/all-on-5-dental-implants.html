A thoughtful path from questions to confident next steps.

All-on-5 is one possible configuration for supporting a fixed full-arch restoration. A fifth implant may be considered when the treating dentist determines that the anatomy and restorative plan could benefit from an additional support point.

The right implant count cannot be selected from a website alone. 3D imaging, an oral examination, medical history, bite forces, and the planned restoration all inform the recommendation.

Why Implant Count Varies

Full-arch treatment is personalized. Bone quality and volume, the shape of the arch, opposing teeth, and the intended prosthesis can influence whether four, five, six, or another number of implants is appropriate.

Comparing Full-Arch Options

During a consultation, the team can explain how All-on-5 compares with All-on-4, All-on-6, removable implant-supported dentures, and conventional dentures. Each option has different maintenance, cost, and clinical considerations.

A Digitally Planned Process

CBCT imaging and digital planning help the team evaluate available bone and plan implant positions. Treatment steps and timing are reviewed before care begins and may include surgical and restorative phases.

Maintenance Matters

Fixed full-arch restorations require consistent home care and professional maintenance. Follow-up visits allow the team to monitor the implants, tissues, bite, and restoration.
