Our Professional & Friendly Dentists

Dr. A Farid Bolouri DMD PC

Dr. Bolouri attended Oregon State University and received his Doctorate at Oregon Health Sciences University School of Dentistry in 1992. Certified conscious sedation, completed training in restoring and placing dental implants. Received certification for Invisalign and is a member of the international congress of oral implantologists.

He resides in the city of Portland with his wife and two sons who enjoy travel, soccer, golf and family time together.

Dr. Sandra Katanic, DDS

Dr. Katanic obtained her bachelor’s degree from UC Berkley and graduated from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. Dr. Katanic is Serbian and is very active in the community. She moved to Portland from southern California with her husband and beautiful baby boy. She is a tremendous asset to our clinic and is loved by our staff. Since joining our team, Dr. Katanic gave birth to twin boys bringing her total to 3 sons, what a beautiful family!

Dr. Wen Li, DMD

Dr. Li joined our team in 2023, he graduated from OHSU with his D.M.D. Dr. Li is Chinese and speaks Chinese, Mandarin, Cantonese and Taishanese. His passion is advanced dentistry and helping people in need. Dr. Li loves indulging in tasteful culinary foods. He enjoys hiking and traveling, his goal is to travel the world with his beautiful wife. Since joining our team, Dr. Li’s wife gave birth to a beautiful baby boy...CONGRATS!
