Complete Dentistry Care

Services at Our Clinic

General Dentistry

Comprehensive care for the whole family. We evaluate, diagnose and treat with complete exams, x-rays, fillings, and dental cleaning.

Dental Implants

Pediatric Dentistry

Mindful, caring dentistry for children up to young adults. Don’t let little problems turn into lifelong concerns.

Cosmetic Dentistry

From whitening and shaping to closing spaces and replacing, we offer a wide range of approaches to improving the look of your smile.

Dentures

We offer a number of removeable dentures such as cast metal, acrylic partials, and flexible partial dentures. Fixed bridges are available as well.

Emergency Dentistry

We treat dental trauma due to accidents, dental pain as a result of tooth decay, damage to dentures, crowns, bridges.

Invisalign

Straighten teeth more predictably and comfortably. Our clear aligners can correct complex shifts faster than braces and at a lower cost.

Periodontal Services

Treatment of the structures that support your teeth and in placing dental implants.

Root Canal Treatment

Teeth Extraction

Wisdom teeth removal and extraction of excessively damaged teeth as a last resort.

Bone Grafting

Reconstruction of deteriorating or weakened jawbone by transplanting different types of bone.

Teeth Whitening

Professional diagnosis of the cause of discoloration and safe restoration to natural, healthy color.
