New Patient Process

We do our best to make it easy to work with us and strive to be the provider of choice for all your dental healthcare needs

Step 1

Contact Us

Call us during business hours to discuss your condition. We’d be glad to answer your questions and schedule an in person visit. You can alternatively email us your questions. Walk-ins are welcome. Please be sure to respect and abide by all COVID-19 precaution measures. We can not admit patients with COVID-19 symptoms. You can learn more about COVID-19 Symptoms and precaution measures here at the CDC website.

Step 2

Registration Forms, Insurance & Financing

After making your appointment, please print and complete below forms as best you can. We need this information to register you in our patient management system. It will be a good idea to email them to drbolouridmd@bolouridentalgroup.com for processing prior to arrival.

We accept the following insurance plans: Aetna , Ameritas , BlueCross BlueShield BCBS , Delta Dental , Guardian , GEHA , Humana , Metlife , MODA , Principal Financial, Regence , State Insurance Oregon Health Plan ODS/OHP , Family Dental Care , and MBA. We also accept members of the Teamsters Union. Please call us to verify if we are registered with your plan.

Financing services are available if you’d like to take advantage of deferred monthly payments. We work with CareCredit for dental financing. For the most part, you should be able to finance most preventive dentistry, restorative procedures and treatments.

Step 3

First Visit

Please plan to arrive 15 minutes earlier. Do not forget your registration forms if you haven’t emailed them. Please be sure to ware a facemask and take all necessary COVID-19 related precautions. Please contact us and avoid visiting the clinic if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms . We’ll gladly reschedule your appointment.

Step 4

Regular Checkups

It is necessary to monitor your dental health on semi annual basis to verify that all is well. Our goal is to identify and treat issues early before severe damage to your teeth or gums.

Step 5

Emergencies

For dental related emergencies, call us during business hours to schedule immediate care. You will receive recorded instructions on what to do when the clinic is closed.
