What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a perm anent restorative option f or replacing a missing tooth. The implant is a metal post, similar to a screw, that is placed into the gums of the missing tooth, to replace the root. After the post is inserted, during the course of a few months, it will fuse into your jawbone, providing security and stability. At this time, we are able to restore the tooth. We connect an abutment to the metal post, and then we will top it with a custom-created crown. The crown can be placed in just one visit using CEREC technology!

An implant is less likely to damage the jaw bone than bridge or dentures. Because implants involve one or multiple surgeries, we require patients to receive a comprehensive dental exam, usually including dental x-rays, as well as an individualized treatment plan tailored to your unique needs.

Who Can Receive a Dental Implant?

Dental implants may be an option for you if:

You have one or more missing teeth. Your jawbone is fully grown and developed. Your jaw contains a sufficient amount of bone to hold an implant in place. You have healthy oral tissues. You don’t have health conditions that could affect the healing of your jaw bone. You don’t want to or cannot wear dentures. You want to improve your speaking and chewing functions. You can commit several months to the completion of the process.
