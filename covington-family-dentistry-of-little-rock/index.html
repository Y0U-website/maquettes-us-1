The practice

Welcome to Covington Family Dentistry, a place where you walk in as patients, but leave as family. Our desire is to build lasting relationships by taking the time to listen and get to know your individual goals and needs. Our team cares for patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors. We look forward to partnering with you to achieve a healthy and radiant smile.

“I believe every patient deserves our very best. Whether you come in for a simple checkup or extensive treatment, our team is committed to providing an exceptional experience with an excellent result.” -Dr. Jonathan Covington

Dr. Jonathan Covington is a native of Cabot, Arkansas. He attended the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. He acquired Platt Family Dentistry from Dr. George Platt in 2022 and is excited to continue serving and connecting with people in Central Arkansas.
