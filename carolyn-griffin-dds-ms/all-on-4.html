Good CandidateS For All-On-4 Dental Implant Restorations

If you are missing most or all of your teeth, or you have serious oral health issues like severe tooth decay or advanced gum disease, All-On-4 may be right for you. Good candidates for All-On-4 will:

  • Have good overall oral health with a strong jaw bone
  • Be healthy enough for surgery and anesthesia
  • Not be taking any medications that can impair the healing process
  • Be a nonsmoker. Smoking triples the failure rate of dental implants, so you must either stop smoking, or at the very least cease smoking for the duration of the procedure and healing process.
