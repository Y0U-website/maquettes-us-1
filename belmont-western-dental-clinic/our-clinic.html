Our Mission

Our trusted family dental offices are committed to providing quality dental care at competitive prices. Whether seeking preventive care, cosmetic treatments, or emergency services, we offer many options for patients of all ages. With a focus on patient-centered care and the latest technology, we strive to give you the best dental experience possible.

At EK Clinics, we prioritize your oral health and overall well-being. Our experienced team of dental professionals is dedicated to delivering personalized care and treatment plans tailored to your unique needs. Whether you require a routine cleaning or a complex procedure, we are here to help you achieve optimal oral health and a confident smile.

Meet Our Dentists Click on a photo to learn more about our experienced dental professionals

Dr. Ali Ektera graduated from UIC and founded EK Clinics in 1995. For over 30 years he has been dedicated to providing exceptional patient care to families all across Chicago. He is an active member of the Chicago Dental Society, Illinois Dental Society, and the American Dental Association. Certified in Surgical & Advanced Restorative Aspects of Branemans System Implants.

Dr. Navid Kia joined the practice in 1996 as a graduate of UIC. Dr. Navid Kia takes the time to understand each patient&#x27;s unique cosmetic or orthodontic needs and designs customized treatment plans to achieve the best results. His interest is orthodontics (Braces), bonding, Porcelain veneers, laminates, and crowns to create a perfect smile.

Dr. Jeel Daftary is a general dentist and graduate of University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered dental care for patients of all ages. She combines modern, evidence-based dentistry with a gentle, personalized approach. Dr. Daftary is committed to helping every patient feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their smile. She proudly serves patients in English, Hindi, Urdu, and Gujarati.

Dr. Minh Luong earned her DDS from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) and a PhD in Restorative Dentistry. Inspired by her father’s dental practice, she developed a passion for dentistry at an early age. She has extensive experience in dentoalveolar surgery through hospital-based practice at UIC and has served as an Assistant Project Scientist in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at UCLA. Dr. Luong is happy to join Belmont & Western Dental Clinic and serve the Chicagoland community.
