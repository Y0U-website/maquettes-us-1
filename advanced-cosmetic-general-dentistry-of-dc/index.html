We want everyone who visits dentistrydc.com to feel welcome and find the experience rewarding by being able to access our content, and learn more about products and services.

To help us make dentistrydc.com a positive place for everyone, we’ve been using the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C)’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 . These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities, and user friendly for everyone.

The guidelines have three levels of accessibility (A, AA and AAA) and we have chosen Level AA as the standard for all our content and services offered through dentistrydc.com. For validating the accessibility of our website, we rely on automated tools such as axe Devtool to catch up to 30 % of accessibility issues.
