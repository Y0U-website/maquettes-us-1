Dental Implants

Zygomatic Implants Teeth in a Day Zygomatic Implants are an alternative technique for grafting procedures such as sinus lifting and block grafting. The procedure involves anchoring implants in the check bone, giving a very high success rate and high implant stability. A fixed set of teeth can be secured to these implants. Zygomatic Implants are the most advanced and innovative solution ever developed for edentulous patients who are not candidates for bone grafting.

A sinus lift surgery adds bone to the upper jaw in the area of the molars and pre-molars. Unfortunately, sometimes there is not enough bone present to place implants in this area. The bone graft material is precisely placed between the upper jaw bone and the maxillary sinus. To make room for the bone graft the sinus membrane is gently lifted up .

Ridge augmentation is frequently completed at the time of tooth extraction or when teeth are already missing. The procedure is sometimes necessary to prepare the jaw bone for future implant placement. The alveolar ridge is the bone that surrounds the roots of natural teeth. When the tooth is lost, the alveolar ridge has a tendency to resorb with a decrease in its height and width. A ridge augmentation procedure places graft material in the area of the defect to create the necessary foundation for placement of dental implants.
