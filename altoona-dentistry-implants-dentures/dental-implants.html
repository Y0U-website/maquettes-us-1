Implants for Permanent Tooth Replacement

Restore Your Smile with Dental Implants

At Altoona Dentistry Implants Dentures , we offer a full range of implant solutions designed to fit your needs, lifestyle, and budget. Whether you are missing one tooth or an entire arch, dental implants are the most advanced and reliable way to bring back your smile. They don’t just replace teeth — they restore confidence, function, and long-term health.

Single-Tooth Implants are the perfect solution when you’ve lost just one tooth. Unlike a dental bridge, an implant stands on its own and doesn’t rely on surrounding teeth for support. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone to act as a natural root, and a custom crown is attached for a seamless, natural look. The result? A replacement that looks, feels, and functions exactly like your original tooth — strong, stable, and designed to last a lifetime.

No matter which option you choose, dental implants give you back your smile — with the comfort, strength, and natural look you deserve.

Dental Implants

A dental implant is a small titanium rod that is surgically placed in the jaw and acts as the root for a replacement tooth. Over time, the implant will fuse to the bone, and we will attach a crown (artificial tooth).

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Full mouth dental implants, or all-on-4 dental implants, offer a full-arch smile restoration, delivering lasting stability, natural appearance, and renewed confidence in one streamlined procedure.

Single Tooth Dental Implants

A single implant replaces a missing tooth with a strong, natural-looking solution that blends seamlessly and preserves surrounding teeth and bone health.

SnapFIT Dentures

SnapFIT Dentures securely “snap” onto dental implants, offering improved comfort, stability, and bite strength without messy adhesives or slipping.

Why Should I Choose an Implant?

  • ^ Implants function, look, and feel like your natural teeth
  • ^ Implants are permanent solutions, and unlike removable dentures or bridges, they do not need to be remade as they wear.
  • ^ You’ll feel confident in social situations because you don’t have to worry about a removable appliance slipping or falling out.
