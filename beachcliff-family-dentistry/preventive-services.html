FAQs About Preventive Dentistry

  • How often should I schedule a dental exam? Most patients should visit the dentist every six months for exams and cleanings. Some may require more frequent visits depending on their oral health.
  • Are dental cleanings necessary if I brush daily? Yes, professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that cannot be eliminated through brushing alone.
  • What happens during a dental exam? A dental exam includes a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, and overall oral health, along with any necessary X-rays.
  • Can preventive care stop gum disease? Yes, regular cleanings and early intervention can prevent or manage gum disease effectively.
  • Do you accept insurance for preventive services? Yes, we accept multiple dental insurance plans. Contact our office for details about your coverage.
