What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the bone, a custom crown is placed on top to complete the restoration.

Each implant is custom-designed to match your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless and comfortable fit.

  • Replacing a single missing tooth
  • Supporting a dental bridge
  • Securing dentures for improved stability

FAQs About Preventive Dentistry

  • Are dental implants permanent? Dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution. With proper care, they can last many years or even a lifetime.
  • Is the implant procedure painful? The procedure is typically well-tolerated, and we take steps to ensure your comfort throughout the process.
  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most patients with good oral and overall health are candidates. A consultation is needed to determine eligibility.
  • How do I care for dental implants? Care for implants just like natural teeth brush, floss, and maintain regular dental visits.
  • Does insurance cover dental implants? Some insurance plans may offer partial coverage. Contact our office to learn more about your options.
