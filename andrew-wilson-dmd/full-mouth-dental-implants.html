Our Comprehensive Implant Process

A clear and predictable path toward a restored smile.

1. Advanced Diagnostic Evaluation

Your treatment begins with high-resolution 3D imaging. This allows us to analyze your bone anatomy, identify areas of ideal support, and design a strategic plan for implant placement. We also use digital X-rays and intraoral cameras to evaluate soft tissues and remaining structures with precision.

2. Personalized Treatment Planning

During your consultation, we review your clinical findings and outline a treatment plan built specifically for your needs. This includes implant placement sequence, restoration design, sedation options, healing timelines, and expected outcomes. Transparency is a core part of our process; we want you to understand every step before treatment begins.

3. Comfortable, Precise Implant Placement

4. Temporary Restorations for Function and Aesthetics

Most patients receive temporary restorations while the implants integrate with the bone. This allows you to maintain your ability to smile, speak, and eat softer foods throughout healing without feeling self-conscious.

5. Final Customized Restorations

Once healing is complete, your final prosthetic teeth are fabricated to achieve natural aesthetics, a balanced bite, and long-term durability . Each restoration is designed to complement your facial features, support healthy chewing patterns, and provide lasting comfort.

Proper care is essential for maximizing the longevity of your implants. We provide personalized maintenance guidance based on your oral health and restoration type. Most patients can return to normal daily activities quickly, with full healing typically completed within three to six months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do full mouth dental implants last? With proper care, implants can last several decades or longer.

Can I eat normally with full mouth implants? Yes. Once fully healed, implants allow you to enjoy a wide range of foods with improved bite strength.

What if I’ve worn dentures for years? Many long-term denture wearers are still candidates. 3D imaging helps us determine whether bone grafting or other preparatory steps are needed.

How much do full mouth implants cost? Pricing varies based on the number of implants, restoration type, and clinical needs. You will receive a complete breakdown before treatment begins.
