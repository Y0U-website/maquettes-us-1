Dental Implant Options at Cornerstone Dentistry

Every patient’s needs are different, so we offer several implant solutions for different levels of tooth loss.

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

A single implant replaces one missing tooth without relying on neighboring teeth for support. This is often a great option when one tooth has been lost due to decay, injury, infection, or extraction.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you are missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can replace them using fewer implant posts. This provides strong support without needing a removable partial denture.

Same-Day Dental Implants

For qualifying patients, same-day dental implants may allow an implant and temporary restoration to be placed during the same treatment process. This can help reduce time spent with a visible gap.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants are designed to replace a full arch of missing or failing teeth using four strategically placed implants. This option is ideal for patients who want a fixed alternative to dentures.

Full-Mouth Dental Implants

For patients missing most or all teeth, full-mouth implant treatment can restore the entire smile with strong, natural-looking restorations.

Dental Implants vs. Dentures and Bridges

Dental implants are not the only tooth replacement option, but they offer unique long-term benefits.

Dental Implants vs. Dentures

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and are removable. While they can restore appearance, some patients struggle with slipping, sore spots, reduced chewing strength, or adhesives.

Dental implants are anchored in the jawbone, offering better stability and helping preserve bone structure. For many patients, implants feel more secure and natural than removable dentures.

Dental Implants vs. Bridges

A dental bridge can replace a missing tooth by using neighboring teeth for support. While bridges can be effective, they often require reshaping healthy teeth.

An implant replaces the missing tooth independently, which helps preserve nearby natural teeth.

Dental Implant FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are small posts placed in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They support crowns, bridges, dentures, or full-mouth restorations.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades. Many patients enjoy long-term success with regular cleanings and good home care.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during placement because the area is fully numbed. Mild soreness after treatment is common and usually manageable.

Am I too old for dental implants?

Age alone does not prevent implant treatment. Many patients in their 60s, 70s, and 80s are successful candidates if their overall health and bone support are appropriate.
