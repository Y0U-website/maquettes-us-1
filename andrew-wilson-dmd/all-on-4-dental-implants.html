All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dentures

Many patients considering dentures eventually ask whether implants may offer a more stable long-term solution.

Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures rest directly on the gums and are removable. While they can restore appearance, some patients experience:

  • Slipping or shifting
  • Difficulty chewing certain foods
  • Sore spots or irritation
  • Adhesive dependence
  • Bone loss over time

All-on-4 Implant Dentures

All-on-4 provides a more stable solution because the teeth are attached directly to implants placed in the jawbone.

Benefits compared to removable dentures may include:

  • Greater stability
  • Stronger chewing ability
  • More natural feel
  • Reduced movement
  • Improved confidence
  • Better long-term jawbone support
