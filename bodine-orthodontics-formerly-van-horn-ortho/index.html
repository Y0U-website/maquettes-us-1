The practice

Whether you’re a kid getting your first set of braces or an adult exploring clear aligners, we’re with you every step of the way — from consultation to your final confident smile.

Tried, tested, and trusted — braces remain one of the most effective ways to achieve a beautifully aligned smile at any age.

Braces have been straightening smiles for decades, and today’s options are more comfortable, efficient, and even more discreet than ever before. At Bodine Orthodontics, we offer traditional metal braces as well as ceramic (tooth-colored) braces so you can choose the look that fits your lifestyle.

Braces work by applying consistent, gentle pressure to your teeth over time, gradually guiding them into their ideal positions. They’re suitable for patients of all ages and can address a wide range of orthodontic issues — from simple crowding to complex bite problems.

The most common and cost-effective option. Today’s metal braces are smaller and more comfortable than earlier designs. Metal brackets and wires work together to move teeth efficiently, and you can even add colored bands to personalize your look.

Ceramic braces use tooth-colored or clear brackets that blend with your natural enamel, making them far less noticeable than metal. They work the same way as traditional braces but offer a more aesthetic option for patients who want a subtler appearance.

Your first visit includes a thorough exam, X-rays, and photos so Dr. Bodine can build a complete picture of your teeth and jaw. We’ll walk you through your treatment options and timeline, and answer every question you have — no pressure, no rush.

Placing your braces typically takes about an hour to an hour and a half. Brackets are bonded to your teeth and connected with an archwire. You may feel some soreness for the first few days as your teeth begin to respond — this is completely normal and usually resolves quickly.
