Our Smile Restoration Process

Using the CBCT scan and digital impressions, we’ll have custom-fabricated surgical guides made that will assist us during your surgery. The surgical guides may be fabricated by a dental laboratory or we may utilize the proven precision of our X-Guide® from X-Nav Technologies to design a dynamic surgical guide. Using the surgical guides, your dental implants are placed in the pre-determined locations in your bone to maximize their primary stability and long-term function. We’ll provide custom sedation or anesthesia for this procedure, so you can remain comfortable during your surgery.

Digital planning of your new smile to fit both your aesthetic and functional goals is completed early in the treatment process. In doing this, your restorative dentist can design a temporary removable denture or we can have your temporary replacement teeth prepared and ready to be attached to your implants the day of your surgery.
