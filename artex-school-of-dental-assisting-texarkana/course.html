The dental assisting school is broken down into 10 weeks. Each week is scheduled to learn a new basic skill. These include but are not limited to:

Tray Set Ups Basic Dental Hygiene Composite and Amalgam Fillings X-Rays Impressions and Trimming Coronal Polishing How to Fabricate Temporary Crowns

Students will also certify in BLS CPR and Nitrous Oxide Administration.

Students will be responsible for shadowing a minimum of 20 clinical hours at any dental office of their choice. This will be done on their own outside of class before week 10.

Students will be responsible for one set of black scrubs as well.

Each class is broken down into book work and hands on lab work that we will perform on each other in class.

We will cover 4 chapters per week in the book and we will test each week over those 4 chapters.

State Board will be administered on week 10 of the class.

This includes coursework, training materials, the nitrous oxide and CPR certifications, as well as cost of state testing and licensing.

We strive to make it as affordable as we can and offer several payment plans to make it even easier to get started in your new career!
