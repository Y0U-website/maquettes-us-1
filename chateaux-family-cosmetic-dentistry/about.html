Get to Know the Heart Behind Chateaux

Dr. Brandi Bottoms, DDS A Broomfield Native with a Passion for Transformative Care

Dr. Brandi Bottoms has been part of the Broomfield community her entire life—and for over 20 years, she’s been transforming smiles and lives across the Denver metro area. Her journey in dentistry began as a dental assistant, where she quickly fell in love with the hands-on nature of the profession and the opportunity to make a real difference in people’s lives. That experience sparked her dream to one day lead her own dental practice, one rooted in compassion, artistry, and clinical excellence.

She earned her DDS from the University of Colorado School of Dentistry and holds active memberships in the American Dental Association, Colorado Dental Association, Metro Denver Dental Society, and Delta Sigma Dental Fraternity. Over the years, Dr. Bottoms has become known not only for her advanced training and gentle approach, but for her unwavering dedication to making each patient feel seen, heard, and cared for.

At Chateaux, Dr. Bottoms focuses on the integration of whole-mouth health with facial aesthetics. She is particularly passionate about Botox and Juvederm as part of both cosmetic enhancement and therapeutic care for TMJ, offering treatments that restore balance, comfort, and confidence. Her philosophy is simple: every patient deserves dental care that feels empowering, never overwhelming.

When she isn&#x27;t working, Dr. Bottoms delights in being with her family and ten grandchildren. To make her patients feel more at ease, she combines her sharp wit with the companionship of her of her tiny pups, named Hope and Cooper. Through her calming influence, Dr. Bottoms strives to provide the most comforting dental experience possible.

Dr. Michelle Raleigh-Gutierrez

Dr. Michelle Raleigh-Gutierrez is a compassionate dentist who takes pride in helping patients achieve confident, healthy smiles. She earned both her Bachelor’s and Doctor of Dental Surgery degrees from Creighton University, where she built a strong foundation in comprehensive oral health care. Dr. Raleigh-Gutierrez continues to expand her knowledge through continuing education, with recent focus on Invisalign and clear aligner therapy.

Dr. Raleigh-Gutierrez practices general dentistry with an emphasis on cosmetic and restorative treatments. She provides high-quality care in areas such as crowns and bridges, veneers, bonding, implant restoration, and dentures, ensuring that each patient’s needs are met with precision and attention to detail.

Outside of the office, Dr. Raleigh-Gutierrez enjoys spending time with her husband and two children, cheering for Nebraska football and the Detroit Lions, and relaxing with a good book.
