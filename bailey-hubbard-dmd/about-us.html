About Grace Health

Our purpose is to heal people and we strive to do that with excellence. Grace Health is built on the foundation of Jesus Christ and our Four Pillars to Success: Access, People, Quality and Community. We focus on excelling, serving, reaching, and healing as our core values at Grace Health. We are a building a healthcare team focused on you.

Every patient, Every Encounter, Every Time

Our Mission

To show the love and share the truth of Jesus Christ to southeastern Kentucky , through access to compassionate, high- quality primary health care for the whole person.

Our Vision

To have healthy people and healthy communities.

Our Goal

To expand and enhance clinical programs and services for our communities.
