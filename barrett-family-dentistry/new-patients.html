Insurance and Payment Info.

We are happy to file ALL dental insurance on your behalf and are a Preferred Provider for Delta Dental. Please remember that we do our best to find out what type of coverage your Dental Insurance plan has; however, insurance plans vary widely and your plan is an agreement between YOU and the INSURANCE COMPANY. You are responsible to know your plan and its limitations as best you can. Care Credit payment plans are also available to those who qualify.

We accept cash, check, and all major Credit Cards as payment.

What To Expect At Your First Visit

As a new member of our dental family, you can expect to be greeted personally by Dr. Barrett and her friendly staff.

In order to get an accurate understanding of your mouth and future treatment planning, we ask that you stay for a set of comprehensive intra-oral exams. Typically, this also includes a full mouth series of digital radiographs, oral cancer screening, and thorough cleaning. Dr. Barrett will listen to your concerns and develop treatment options that best fit your budget and your long term oral health goals.
