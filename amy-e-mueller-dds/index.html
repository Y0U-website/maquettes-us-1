The practice

"Amy is a fantastic dentist who is able to explain things very clearly. She has done a lot of work on my mouth and each experience with her is great. The support staff is excellent as well."

Dr. Amy Mueller grew up in her dad’s dental practice, so when she was in high school, it just made sense for her to start learning the family business. She loved working with her hands and had already formed personal relationships with many of her dad’s patients and staff. So, it was the perfect fit. She instantly fell in love with dentistry.

In fact, the assistant who first trained Dr. Amy in 2002 still works by her side! Lasting, long-term relationships are just one of the things that makes Mueller and Associates so special.

With Dr. Armand Di Benedetto and her father, Dr. Jay Mueller, Dr. Amy would like to welcome you to Mueller and Associates.
