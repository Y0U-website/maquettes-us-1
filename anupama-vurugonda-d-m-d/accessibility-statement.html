Current Accessibility Features

We have implemented the following accessibility features on our website:

  • A Skip to Content link at the top of every page to allow keyboard users to bypass navigation
  • Descriptive alt text on images throughout the site
  • Logical heading structure (H1, H2, H3) for screen reader navigation
  • Sufficient colour contrast ratios meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards
  • Responsive design that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • HTTPS secure connection across all pages

Known Limitations

While we strive to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards across our entire website, we are aware of the following areas currently under remediation:

  • Online appointment form: Our appointment booking form relies on JavaScript. We are working to ensure it is fully accessible to keyboard-only users and screen readers. In the meantime, please use the phone numbers below to book an appointment.
  • Embedded Google Maps: Interactive map embeds on our Locations pages may have limited accessibility for keyboard and screen reader users. Addresses and phone numbers are provided as text alternatives on all location pages.
  • Third-party content: Some content provided by third-party services (such as financing providers) may not fully meet our accessibility standards. We are working with these vendors to improve accessibility.

Details on ADA Title III Compliance

As a healthcare provider, Midwest Family Dental Care recognizes the importance of complying with ADA Title III. This section of the Americans with Disabilities Act mandates that public accommodations must be accessible to individuals with disabilities. For our online presence, this translates to ensuring our website does not present barriers that prevent patients from accessing information or services.

Compliance with ADA Title III means we are committed to providing equal access to our digital services. This includes ensuring that our website can be navigated and understood by individuals with various disabilities, mirroring the physical accessibility we strive for in our dental offices. We continuously review and update our site to meet these requirements.

Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act (ACA)

Midwest Family Dental Care is committed to upholding Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act (ACA). This crucial legislation prohibits discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability in health programs and activities that receive federal financial assistance. Our website accessibility efforts are an integral part of this commitment.

By ensuring our digital platforms are accessible, we are actively working to prevent discrimination and promote inclusivity in healthcare. This means providing equitable access to health information and services for all individuals, regardless of their background or any disabilities they may have. Our accessibility statement and ongoing efforts directly support the principles outlined in Section 1557.

Section 508 Accessibility Standards

We are also dedicated to meeting the accessibility standards set forth by Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act. This federal law requires that all electronic and information technology developed, procured, maintained, or used by federal agencies be accessible to people with disabilities. While we are not a federal agency, we adopt these rigorous standards to ensure our website is as accessible as possible.

Section 508 standards cover a broad range of accessibility requirements for information technology. By incorporating these principles into our website development and maintenance, Midwest Family Dental Care aims to provide a robust and inclusive online experience. This includes considerations for users who rely on assistive technologies like screen readers and keyboard navigation.
