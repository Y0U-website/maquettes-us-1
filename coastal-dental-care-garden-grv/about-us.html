OUR PROCESS

Committed to Modern Dental Excellence and Personalized Patient Care

Coastal Dental Care Garden Grove and Omega Family Dental provides a comprehensive range of general, cosmetic, restorative, and emergency dental services to families in Garden Grove and the neighboring Orange County communities. Our clinic is a reputable location for multigenerational dental care because we welcome patients of all ages, from young children to seniors. Our team prioritizes comfort, accuracy, and prevention while using cutting-edge dental technology and tried-and-true methods to deliver effective, compassionate care.

We’re open Monday through Friday, with extended hours available by request. Our team includes experienced dentists, hygienists, and support staff who genuinely care about your oral health.
