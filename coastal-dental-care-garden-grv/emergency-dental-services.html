EMERGENCY DENTAL SERVICES

If you experience any of these symptoms, it’s critical to seek care right away. Delaying treatment can result in further damage or permanent tooth loss.

  • Chipped, broken, or knocked-out teeth
  • Swollen gums, cheeks, or jaw
  • Loose crowns, fillings, or bridges
  • Abscesses or visible infection
  • Bleeding that doesn’t stop
  • Broken orthodontic appliances are causing pain
