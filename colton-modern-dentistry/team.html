Our Dentists

Dr. Chuck S. Kon

Dr. Kon is a graduate of the Loma Linda University School of Dentistry and holds fellowships with the Academy of General Dentistry and the Academy of Dentistry International. Dr. Kon has practiced here in Colton for over 45 years and will be able to help you in all aspects of dentistry, including your general and cosmetic needs. He focuses on getting to know you and always does his best to address all of your concerns regarding treatments and procedures.

Dr. Kon has also worked as the supervising dentist at United Education Institute. He is very involved in the community and routinely mentors high school and college students regarding their career paths and interests in the dental field. At home, he enjoys maintaining an active lifestyle and spending time with his family.

Dental Associations: American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Academy of Dentistry International, National Association of Seventh-day Adventist Dentists

Dr. Christina Sang

Dr. Christina Sang attended the University of Southern California for both her undergraduate studies and for dental school, graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Health Promotion and Disease Prevention and a Doctor of Dental Surgery. During school, she went on mission trips to provide dental care to the underserved children and families in El Salvador and Guatemala. Her experiences showed her the importance of oral health awareness and patient education.

Dr. Christina enjoys working alongside her father and has been at the practice for 10 years. She is a fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and finds performing restorative and cosmetic dental work very rewarding. She enjoys working in a family practice that makes patient comfort a priority and loves that many of her younger patients have parents who have been seeing her father since they were children.

While not practicing dentistry, she enjoys being outdoors and spending time with her two sons.

Dental Associations: American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Tri-County Dental Society
