The All-on-X Dental Implant Process

Comprehensive Consultation

Your journey begins with a complimentary consultation at our Mays Landing or Somers Point dental office. Dr. Nosti evaluates your oral health, discusses your goals, and uses CBCT imaging to assess bone structure. We’ll answer all your questions and create a customized treatment plan.

Treatment Planning

Using advanced software, our dentist will map out the precise placement of your implants. This ensures optimal stability and aesthetics. We’ll discuss sedation options to keep you comfortable during the procedure.

Dental Implant Procedure

On the day of your procedure, Dr. Nosti places four to six implants in your jawbone using minimally invasive techniques. Temporary prosthetics are often attached the same day, allowing you to leave with a functional smile.

Healing and Final Prosthetics

Over the next 3 to 6 months, the dental implants fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. Once healed, we replace the temporary prosthetics with a custom-crafted, permanent arch designed for durability and beauty.

Follow-Up Appointments with Our Dentist Near You

We provide detailed aftercare instructions and schedule follow-ups to ensure your implants remain in top condition. Regular teeth cleanings and exams at our dental office near you will keep your smile healthy for years to come.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-X Dental Implants?

Dr. Nosti carefully assesses each patient to ensure the best outcomes. The key factors that make someone an ideal candidate for All-on-X implants include:

  • Patients with Multiple Missing Teeth : If you’ve lost most or all teeth in one or both arches, All-on-X dental implants can restore your smile efficiently.
  • Denture Wearers Seeking Stability : Those frustrated with loose, uncomfortable dentures often benefit from the fixed, secure nature of All-on-X implants.
  • Individuals with Bone Loss : All-on-X’s angled implant placement often eliminates the need for bone grafting, making it suitable for patients with reduced bone volume.
  • Good Overall Health : Candidates should be free of uncontrolled medical conditions (like diabetes) that could affect healing. Smokers may need to quit temporarily to ensure success.
  • Patients Seeking Long-Term Solutions : If you want a durable, low-maintenance alternative to dentures, All-on-X implants are ideal.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does the All-on-X procedure take? Placing dental implants typically takes 2 to 4 hours per arch, with temporary prosthetics often placed the same day. The full process, including healing, takes 3 to 6 months.
  • Are All-on-X implants covered by insurance? Some plans cover a portion of the dental implant cost, especially if medically necessary. Our team will verify your benefits and maximize coverage.
