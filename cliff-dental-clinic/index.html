We are excited to welcome you to our Graham, TX dental practice! At Cliff Dental Clinic, Dr. Caddell and our team know that excellent dental care isn’t just about the treatments…

The practice

You can be assured that every time you visit us you will be welcomed warmly by our friendly, caring team. We are all dedicated professionals who want to help you achieve optimal oral health in a comfortable and stress-free setting.

Our professional team has many years of experience in providing outstanding patient care, and we all go out of our way to get to know you and treat you like an important member of our family.

We value the trust that you place in us to handle your oral health care. Learning about your goals, lifestyle, and expectations helps us to customize your care to fit your unique needs.

It is essential for you to be informed and comfortable with every treatment. We will take all the time needed to answer your questions and make sure you understand why a certain procedure is necessary and how it will benefit you.

We know that dental emergencies can be a frustrating and stressful experience. At our Graham dental office, we understand how crucial it is for you to get the care you need when you need it most. If you or your child have a dental emergency, we will be happy to make arrangements to see you the same day.

The cost of treatments and dental insurance coverage are important topics for everyone these days. We believe that you should never have to compromise your good oral health due to financial considerations.

We accept most major dental insurance plans, and a knowledgeable member of our team will be happy to guide you through the often complicated world of co-pays, minimums, exclusions, and out-of-pocket expenses. If you don’t have insurance, we will help you apply for CareCredit, a convenient financing option.

Our team is committed to providing superior dental care by building trusting relationships that last a lifetime. We believe that you’ll notice the difference that personalized care makes the first time you visit our office.
