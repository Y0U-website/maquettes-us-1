The link between implants and overall oral health

Replacing missing teeth with implants helps preserve jawbone density, which prevents bone loss over time. When implants integrate with your bone, they help maintain facial structure and oral function. Compared to removable dentures, implants also reduce the risk of gum irritation and improve chewing efficiency.

Caring for your implants

  • Brush twice daily and floss around implant crowns
  • Attend regular dental cleanings and checkups
  • Maintain a balanced diet
  • Avoid smoking and manage conditions like diabetes
  • Follow any specific post surgical care instructions

FAQs about All-on-4 implants and dental implants in Philadelphia, PA

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once healed, they support crowns, bridges, or dentures that look and function like natural teeth.

All‑on‑4 is a full‑arch tooth replacement method using four implants to support a fixed prosthesis. It provides a stable alternative to removable dentures and often allows patients to receive temporary fixed teeth on the same day.

With good oral hygiene and regular check‑ups, dental implants can last many years and often a lifetime. Proper care is essential for long‑term success.

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia and sedation, if needed, help ensure your comfort during implant placement. After surgery, prescribed or over‑the‑counter pain relief typically manages any soreness.
