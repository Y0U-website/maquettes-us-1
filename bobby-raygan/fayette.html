Welcome to Fayette, Dr. Raygan!

Dr. Raygan is thrilled to announce his association with Dr. Stewart Griswold in the lovely city of Fayette, Alabama!

Dr. Griswold has served the Fayette community for many years and announced his retirement in 2020. Dr. Raygan has acquired the practice and is excited to be serving the community!

Dr. Raygan has been practicing dentistry in Hueytown for over 36 years, where he has lived for his whole life. He is happily married to Traci, and together they raised their children, Lindsey and Carson who now have gotten married and blessed us with our wonderful grandchildren. In his spare time he enjoys travelling with his family, fly fishing, and worshiping at his church.

In this season of life, Dr. Raygan is humbled and honored to serve patients in Hueytown and Fayette, alongside a team of terrific associate Dentist, all of whom he considers to be family.
