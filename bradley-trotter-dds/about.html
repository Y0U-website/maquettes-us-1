Our Surgeons — Military-Trained, Board-Certified Oral Specialists

Dr. Kyle Lee, DDS — Board-Certified OMS

Dr. Kyle Lee is a board-certified diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our practice, surgeons, and care.

Are your surgeons board certified?

Yes. Dr. Kyle Lee and Dr. Koby Ferguson are board-certified diplomates of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Fellows of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. Dr. Brad Trotter is board certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and has completed an accredited OMS residency. All three practice full-scope oral and maxillofacial surgery.

Do you see military patients?

Absolutely. We are a military-friendly practice that welcomes active-duty members, veterans, and their families. We will help you understand your coverage and file your out-of-network benefits. Our surgeons’ own military experience means they are especially attuned to the needs of service members.

How do I know which surgeon will perform my procedure?

Your referring dentist or our team will match you with the surgeon best suited to your case. All three surgeons are highly trained and capable of performing the full range of OMS procedures. If you have a preference, just let us know and we will schedule you with the surgeon you choose.

What is the difference between an oral surgeon and a general dentist?

An oral surgeon is a dental specialist who completes four to six years of additional surgical training after dental school. General dentists handle routine cleanings, fillings, and basic extractions. Oral surgeons are trained to perform complex surgical procedures like wisdom teeth removal, dental implants, corrective jaw surgery, and reconstructive surgery. For any surgical need, an oral surgeon provides the highest level of expertise.
