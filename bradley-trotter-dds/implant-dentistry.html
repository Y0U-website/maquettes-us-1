Dental Implants

Standard dental implants replace one or more missing teeth with titanium posts that fuse to your jawbone. A custom crown is then attached for a natural appearance and lasting function.

02 Immediate Molar Implants In select cases, a molar implant can be placed on the same day the tooth is extracted. This reduces overall treatment time while preserving bone and gum tissue.

03 Bone Grafting for Implants If your jawbone lacks enough volume or density to support implants, bone grafting rebuilds the foundation. This procedure is often performed before or during implant placement to ensure long-term stability.
