Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. These posts provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Dental implants function and feel like natural teeth, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile confidently.

Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, dental implants are integrated into your jawbone, preventing bone loss caused by missing teeth. They are durable, reliable, and can last a lifetime with proper care.
