Dr. Lennie D. Wong, DMD, FAGD

Lennie D. Wong, DMD, FAGD

“As much as I enjoy hearing people tell me I am an excellent dentist, I feel more pleased when they tell me they have never felt so cared for in a dental office.” Dr. Lennie Wong is a native Oregonian, having lived most of his life in the Portland area. He has lived and practiced in the Beaverton area for the past 27 years, and he is thrilled to be a part of this community.

Having been a partner and owner of Beaverton TenderCare since 1998, he is always seeking ways to accomplish their mission statement, which is, “We strive for the Best patient experience.” From the moment you walk in the door, you will see how Dr Wong and his team want you to feel comfortable in their “green” office.

His dental office was recognized as the first and only dental practice in Oregon to be awarded the highest level of certification previously recognized by the ECO Dentistry Association. This Gold Level certification made Beaverton TenderCare arguably the greenest dental office in the state.

Dr. Wong is constantly enrolled in post-doctoral education in all aspects of dentistry, including cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and orthodontics through Invisalign. In 2007, he distinguished himself amongst his peers by being awarded the highly regarded Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry in a special ceremony in San Diego, California.

In 2019, Dr. Wong became a member of the International Academy of Facial Aesthetics. His training has made It possible for him to perform cosmetic and therapeutic Botox and dermal fillers. He has had remarkable results in relieving patients suffering from migraines, headaches, TMJ, and jaw pain. Dr. Wong loves to spend time with his family as well as traveling, cooking, “Traegering” his specialties, and DIY projects.

Associations:

– American Dental Association – Academy of General Dentistry – International Academy of Facial Aesthetics – American Academy of Clear Aligners.
