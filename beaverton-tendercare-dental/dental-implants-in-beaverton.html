The Dental Implant Process

Getting dental implants involves several key steps to ensure a successful outcome. Here’s what you can expect when you choose our dental implants in Beaverton:

During your first visit, we will assess your oral health, review your medical history, and discuss your goals. Advanced technology, such as digital X-rays and 3D imaging, allows us to create a detailed treatment plan tailored to your needs.

A small titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone. Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your bone in osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation.

Once the implant has integrated with your jawbone, we attach a small connector piece called an abutment, which holds your replacement tooth securely in place.

Finally, we customize and attach a crown, bridge, or denture to the abutment, restoring your smile’s appearance and function.
