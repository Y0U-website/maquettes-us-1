667-401-3232 image Contact Us What Are 3D Dental X-Rays? A 3D dental X-ray, also known as a Cone Beam CT (CBCT) scan , is an advanced imaging technique that provides a full 360-degree view of your oral structures. Unlike traditional X-rays that only capture flat, two-dimensional images, CBCT technology creates a 3D model of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding tissues , allowing for more precise diagnoses and treatment planning.

We’ll have you stand or sit comfortably in the imaging machine while the scanner moves around your head.

In just a few seconds, the scanner captures a full 3D view of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures.

Your dentist will review the 3D images immediately, ensuring a faster, more precise diagnosis and treatment plan.
