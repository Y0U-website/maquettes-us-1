What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a permanent replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth. It consists of:

Titanium Post – Surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a root for the new tooth.

Abutment – Connects the post to the dental crown.

Custom Dental Crown – A lifelike, durable replacement tooth designed to match your smile.

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth , providing better stability than dentures or bridges.

The Dental Implant Procedure

We assess your oral health, take X-rays, and create a personalized implant plan.

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone, serving as the root for the new tooth.

Over a few months, the implant fuses with the jawbone, ensuring stability.

A natural-looking dental crown is attached, restoring your smile permanently.
