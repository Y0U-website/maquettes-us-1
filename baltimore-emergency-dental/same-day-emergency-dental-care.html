What Is Considered a Dental Emergency?

If you’re experiencing any of the following symptoms, you may need immediate dental care:

Severe or persistent tooth pain that won’t go away

Swollen gums, face, or jaw (could be a sign of an infection)

A loose or lost filling, crown, or dental appliance

Numbness in your mouth or jaw (possible nerve damage)
