When Do You Need a Tooth Extraction?

Tooth extractions are necessary when a tooth is too damaged to be saved or is causing serious issues. You may need an extraction if you have:

A badly decayed or infected tooth (abscess)

A broken or fractured tooth that can’t be repaired

Impacted wisdom teeth causing pain or crowding

Swelling in the gums or jaw from an infection

If you’re unsure whether you need an extraction, our team will assess your situation and provide the best treatment option for your dental health .
