The practice

Welcome To Tenafly Orthodontic Associates At Tenafly Orthodontics, we’re proud to be a leading orthodontic practice serving Tenafly, Cresskill, Englewood, Closter, and the greater Bergen County area . Known for our compassionate, patient-focused approach, we create beautiful, healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

Dr. Chris Kojanis and Dr. Renée Kojanis-Cuomo provide expert orthodontic care for children, teens, and adults , offering a full range of treatments including clear aligners, traditional braces, and early interceptive orthodontics.

Our practice is built on personalized attention and teamwork . Both doctors collaborate closely to design customized treatment plans that fit each patient’s needs and lifestyle. With the latest orthodontic technology and a caring touch, we make your smile transformation comfortable, efficient, and rewarding.

From your first consultation to your final appointment, you’ll enjoy a clean, welcoming, and friendly environment where your comfort and satisfaction come first. At Tenafly Orthodontics, we’re committed to helping our community smile with confidence — one patient at a time.
