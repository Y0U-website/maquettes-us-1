What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants have become the leading method for replacing missing teeth thanks to their strength, reliability, and lifelike appearance. Unlike traditional bridges or dentures that rest on the gumline, implants work from the foundation up. They include two key parts. A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone, where it acts as a new tooth root. This post fuses naturally with the bone over time, creating a secure and stable base. Attached to this post is a custom made crown , bridge, or denture that blends smoothly with your surrounding teeth.

Because implants mimic natural tooth structure, patients enjoy a replacement that looks and feels realistic. The biting power, chewing function, and comfort provided by implants are unmatched. With a success rate that consistently ranges between 95 and 98 percent, implants remain one of the most dependable choices in modern dentistry. With proper care, they can last for many decades and often for life.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer a wide range of benefits that go beyond filling a gap in your smile. They support your oral health, your confidence, and the long term function of your mouth.

A Stable, Permanent Solution

Implants are fixed into the jawbone, giving them unmatched stability. Unlike dentures that can slip or move during meals or conversations, implants stay firmly in place. This confidence allows you to enjoy foods like apples, corn, nuts, and steak without hesitation. There is no need for adhesives or special cleaning routines. Implants become part of your smile permanently.

Long Term Durability

Titanium is extremely strong and biocompatible, which means your body accepts it without irritation. Once fused with the jawbone, implants can withstand everyday chewing forces with ease. Patients who maintain consistent dental checkups and good oral hygiene often enjoy their implants for a lifetime.

A Natural Appearance

Every implant restoration is custom crafted to blend with the shape, color, and size of your natural teeth. Since the titanium portion remains below the gumline, only the crown or prosthetic is visible. This ensures a seamless appearance that makes it nearly impossible for anyone to detect that you have a replacement tooth.

Preservation of Jawbone Health

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone begins to shrink due to lack of stimulation. Implants prevent this bone loss by providing the same pressure a natural tooth root would. This helps maintain facial structure, prevents premature aging, and protects your oral health.

Support for Surrounding Teeth

Gaps can cause nearby teeth to shift out of place, leading to misalignment and bite problems. Implants fill the space securely, keeping your teeth in their correct positions.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Most healthy adults qualify for dental implants. Ideal candidates should have good oral health, healthy gums, and sufficient jawbone density to support the implant posts. Even if bone loss has already occurred, bone grafting may be an option that allows implants to succeed. During your consultation at our Broomall office, Dr. White or Dr. Al-Azzawi will perform a thorough exam, take digital images, and evaluate your overall health history. This helps determine whether implants will meet your needs and what type of restoration will provide the best results.

What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process is completed in stages, each designed to ensure precision, safety, and long term success. Once Dr. White or Dr. Al-Azzawi determines that you are a good candidate, a trusted specialist will surgically place the titanium posts into the jawbone. This part of the procedure is performed with advanced technology that improves accuracy and patient comfort.

After placement, the healing phase begins. During this period, the jawbone gradually fuses with the implant in a process known as osseointegration. This typically takes several months and creates a secure foundation similar to a natural tooth root. Temporary restorations may be provided to keep your smile functional while healing takes place.

Once the implants have fully integrated, you will return for the final restoration phase. Dr. White or Dr. Al-Azzawi will attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture to the implant posts. These restorations are carefully designed to complement the rest of your smile, both in appearance and function. The final result is a stable, comfortable, and beautiful tooth replacement that allows you to smile, speak, and eat with confidence.
