What Are Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are thin protective coatings placed on the chewing surfaces of back teeth. At Angleton Family Dental in Angleton, TX, sealants are offered as part of preventive dental care to help give busy little smiles, growing grins, and hard-working family teeth an added layer of protection.

Once placed, the sealant creates a barrier over the tooth surface where food and bacteria can more easily settle. It is a small preventive step that can help back teeth keep up with school lunches, snack breaks, family dinners, and all the everyday chewing that happens between dental visits.

Dental sealants help protect back teeth by giving their chewing surfaces extra coverage in the spots that can be harder to keep clean with brushing alone.

FAQs About Dental Sealants

What are dental sealants?

Dental sealants are thin protective coatings placed on the chewing surfaces of back teeth. They help cover small grooves where food and bacteria can settle.

Are dental sealants only for children?

No, dental sealants are often recommended for children and teens, but some adults may also benefit if they have unsealed back teeth. The dentist can check whether sealants make sense based on tooth shape, cavity risk, and dental history.

How long do dental sealants last?

Dental sealants can last for several years, but they should be checked during routine dental visits. If a sealant becomes worn or chipped, the dentist can decide whether it needs to be repaired or replaced.

Are dental sealants safe?

Yes, dental sealants are widely used as a preventive dental service for children, teens, and adults. At Angleton Family Dental, the dentist can explain whether sealants are a good fit for each patient’s smile.

Do dental sealants replace brushing and flossing?

No, dental sealants do not replace brushing, flossing, or regular dental visits. They add protection to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, but daily home care is still important.
