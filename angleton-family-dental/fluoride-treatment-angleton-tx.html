FAQs About Fluoride Treatment

What is fluoride treatment?

Fluoride treatment is a preventive dental service that places fluoride directly on the teeth to help support stronger enamel. At Angleton Family Dental, it may be recommended as part of routine care for patients who need added cavity protection.

Is fluoride treatment safe for children?

Yes, professional fluoride treatment is commonly used for children when recommended by a dentist. The amount, timing, and type of fluoride are selected based on the child’s needs, age, and cavity risk.

How often should patients get fluoride treatment?

Some patients may receive fluoride treatment at routine dental visits, while others may only need it when their dentist recommends extra support. Angleton Family Dental can decide the right timing based on enamel health, cavity history, and daily habits.

Can adults benefit from fluoride treatment?

Yes, adults can benefit from fluoride treatment, especially if they have sensitivity, dry mouth, enamel wear, gum recession, or a history of cavities. Fluoride is not only for children because adult teeth can still need extra protection.

How long after fluoride treatment can patients eat or drink?

After fluoride treatment, patients may be asked to wait before eating or drinking so the fluoride can stay on the teeth longer. The team at Angleton Family Dental will explain any aftercare instructions before the visit ends.
