About Us

About Us Our Mission At Ames Orthodontics, our mission is to deliver personalized, detail-oriented orthodontic care within a warm, caring environment where every patient and family member feels valued and informed.

We provide braces, Invisalign ® , early treatment, and retainers—customized to each individual’s needs—so every smile can shine with confidence.

With advanced technology, gentle techniques, and flexible in-office, interest-free financing, we make treatment accessible and comfortable for patients of all ages.

Serving Ames and Nevada, IA, we strive to build lasting relationships, empower healthy habits, and foster smiles that inspire confidence for a lifetime.

Meet Dr. JJ Grabouski

Dr. JJ Grabouski, D.D.S., M.S. is the owner and lead orthodontist at Ames Orthodontics and DSM Orthodontics, with over 10 years of clinical experience crafting healthy, beautiful smiles. He worked in an orthodontic partnership with his dad, Dr. Tim Kuntz, Dr. Rick Wagner, and Dr. Tim Kuntz for 9 years in Sioux City, IA.

Dr. Grabouski earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Iowa in 2013. He completed specialized orthodontic training and earned his Master of Science in Orthodontics in 2016 at the University of North Carolina in Chapel Hill, NC.

Outside the office, Dr. Grabouski is a father to his 4 wonderful kiddos (James 8, Henry 7, Jack 4, and Mila 2). He enjoys golfing, anything on the water, and staying current with advancements in orthodontic technology to bring the best care to his patients.
