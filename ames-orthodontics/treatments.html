Treatments

Treatments Care for All Ages At Ames Orthodontics in Ames, Iowa, we provide personalized orthodontic care for patients of every age.

We begin evaluations as early as age 7 to guide jaw development and prevent more complex issues.

Our teen-focused treatments, including ceramic braces and clear aligners, help adolescents achieve confident smiles.

Whether it’s Invisalign ® or discreet clear aligners, we offer flexible options tailored for busy adult lifestyles.

Sometimes the best orthodontic treatment is to wait! Observation checks are always free and can provide parents peace of mind so nothing gets missed.

Braces

Traditional and ceramic braces remain the most effective method for correcting complex orthodontic issues.

Our modern bracket systems provide comfortable, predictable results for patients of all ages.

Durable and cost-effective, metal braces use brackets and wires to gently shift teeth into proper alignment.

Ceramic braces blend with your tooth color for a less noticeable option without sacrificing effectiveness.

Clear Aligners

Clear, removable aligners use precise 3D scanning to shift teeth subtly and effectively.

Enjoy eating, brushing, and flossing without brackets while achieving your best smile.

As the clear alternative to braces, Invisalign aligners offer custom-fit trays for comfortable, nearly invisible treatment.

Clarity aligners combine strength, transparency, and exceptional fit for precise tooth movement and a discreet orthodontic solution.

Retainers

Maintain your new smile with custom retainers designed for comfort and long-term stability. Impressions are a thing of the past. When your orthodontic treatment is coming to a conclusion, the brackets are digitally removed from a scan so retainers are ready immediately when the orthodontic appliances are removed.

Palatal Expanders

We offer palatal expanders to widen the upper jaw, creating space and improving bite alignment. This gentle appliance can help guide proper growth and make future orthodontic treatment easier.

Advanced Technology

We leverage cutting-edge digital tools to enhance precision, comfort, and treatment predictability.

Fast, radiation-free scans capture precise 3D images for accurate treatment planning and aligner design.

Cone beam CT delivers detailed 3D X-ray images for precise diagnosis with minimal radiation exposure.

High-resolution intraoral and facial imaging ensures precise records and treatment visualization.

Surgical Orthodontics

For patients requiring orthognathic surgery in conjunction with orthodontic treatment, we provide comprehensive surgical orthodontic care.

Our collaborative approach with oral and maxillofacial surgeons ensures coordinated treatment planning for optimal functional and aesthetic results.

We align your teeth in preparation for surgery, creating the ideal foundation for surgical correction of jaw discrepancies.

After surgical healing, we complete the final orthodontic movements to achieve your perfect bite and smile aesthetics.
