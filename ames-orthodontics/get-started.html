Get Started

Get Started First Visit Every smile at Ames Orthodontics begins with your new patient exam. You’ll meet our caring team, receive a complete oral evaluation with digital X-rays, and discuss which treatment options—braces or Invisalign ® —best suit you.

We’ll review your health history, craft a personalized treatment plan, and go over all financial information, insurance benefits, and in-office financing options to make your care stress-free.

Financing & Insurance

Ames Orthodontics accepts most major insurance plans including Hawk-i insurance and will file your claims electronically for quick, convenient coverage. We are not currently accepting Medicaid.

We offer in-house 0 % interest financing as long as payments are kept up with. Those who pay in-full will receive a 3 % discount with no additional fees for credit card payments. In-office, interest-free financing and flexible payment plans are available to fit your budget—our treatment coordinator will work with you to find the best option.
