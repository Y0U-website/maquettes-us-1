What to Expect at Our Basalt Dental Office

The Basalt Dentistry experience.

We understand choosing a new dentist is an important decision.

From your first phone call to your first appointment, our goal is to make the process simple, comfortable, and welcoming.

Your first visit includes a comprehensive evaluation, digital imaging as needed, a discussion of your goals and concerns, and personalized recommendations for achieving long-term oral health.

We look forward to getting to know you and helping you feel confident about your smile.

Our 10-Point Exam Medical History Review We will check your history to identify recurring problems, any potentially connected issues, and potential obstacles to your health.

Finance Basalt Dentistry is a fee-for-service dental practice.

This means our recommendations are based on what is best for your health, not what an insurance company chooses to cover.

While we are not contracted with insurance companies, we are happy to submit claims on your behalf and help you maximize any available out-of-network benefits.

By remaining independent, we are able to spend more time with our patients, utilize the highest-quality materials and laboratories, and provide treatment based on excellence rather than insurance limitations.

Our commitment is simple: exceptional care, personalized attention, and treatment recommendations made solely with your best interests in mind.

  • Dental History Review We will pay attention to your current and past dental issues to understand your oral health and needs.
  • Oral Cancer Screening A simple, efficient assessment to spot oral cancer in its tracks.
  • Periodontal Screening We will assess your gum health, as it can have serious effects on your overall health and your teeth.
  • 3D X-Rays This allows us to accurately diagnose any issues and develop the best treatment plans.
  • Intraoral Photos High-definition images allow you to see what we see, so you understand what needs to be done.
  • TMJ and Airway Analysis We can identify issues related to jaw joints and sleep apnea by looking at these areas.
  • Bite Analysis Examining your bite allows us to assess whether you need assistance in alignment.
  • Personalized Plan We will develop a tailored treatment plan, explaining every step to you and answering any questions.
  • Gentle Cleaning Our expert hygienists will then comfortably clean your teeth, giving you fresher breath and starting your wellness journey.

Insurance

Dental insurance can be a helpful benefit, but it is important to understand that most plans were designed to assist with the cost of care, not pay for all treatment.

As a courtesy, we will file claims electronically and help you understand your benefits whenever possible.

Because every plan is different, estimated benefits are never a guarantee of payment. Any portion not covered by insurance remains the responsibility of the patient.

Our team is always happy to answer questions and help you make informed financial decisions regarding your care.
