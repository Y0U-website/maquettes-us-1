The practice

Dr. Jonathan Haerter has been serving patients in the Roaring Fork Valley for more than two decades.

Known for his commitment to excellence, advanced training, and attention to detail, Dr. Haerter provides comprehensive care ranging from preventive dentistry to complex cosmetic and restorative treatment.

His passion is helping patients achieve healthy, functional, and beautiful smiles through personalized treatment and lasting relationships.

Dr. Maggie Arceneaux provides a personalized, detail-driven approach to cosmetic and restorative dentistry. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the Louisiana State University School of Dentistry in 2022, before moving to the Roaring Fork Valley, where she quickly found her place at Basalt Dentistry and has been a valued part of the team ever since.

She is passionate about enhancing each patient’s natural aesthetics through thoughtful, comprehensive care. From crowns and clear aligners to facial aesthetic treatments like Botox and dermal fillers, her work is designed to support both your oral health and overall well-being.

Care with Dr. Maggie is both comfortable and precise. With a focus on digital dentistry and a collaborative approach, she delivers efficient, modern treatment while ensuring every patient feels genuinely heard, supported, and at ease.

Many people carry stress and disappointment from rushed past dental visits. At Basalt Dentistry, we do dentistry differently. We listen to your concerns carefully, explain the problem clearly, and care for you gently and expertly.

Rebuild your smile’s function and beauty with our advanced care. From simple fillings to full-mouth rehabilitation, our cutting-edge techniques help you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
