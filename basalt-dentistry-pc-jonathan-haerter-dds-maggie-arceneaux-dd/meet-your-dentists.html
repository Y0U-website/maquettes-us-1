Meet Maggie Arceneaux , DDS

Thoughtful cosmetic and restorative Basalt dentist, with a focus on natural, lasting results.

Dr. Maggie Arceneaux takes a personalized approach to care, helping patients feel confident in both their smile and overall appearance. She combines cosmetic and restorative dentistry with facial aesthetic treatments to create results that look natural, feel balanced, and fit each person’s goals.

By understanding what matters most to each patient, she creates treatment plans that feel thoughtful, comfortable, and tailored to their lifestyle—whether that means enhancing a smile or subtly refining facial aesthetics.

Originally from Lafayette, Louisiana, Dr. Maggie has long had a connection to the Roaring Fork Valley, having grown up skiing in Aspen and even participating as a 1998 Powder Panda alumna. Returning to the area felt like coming home. Outside of the office, she enjoys skiing, fly fishing, traveling, and cooking, and has a passion for exploring new restaurants and cuisines—a love she proudly credits to her South Louisiana roots.

  • Undergraduate studies in Neuroscience and Psychology at Tulane University
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery from Louisiana State University School of Dentistry (2022)
  • Emphasis on personalized cosmetic and restorative treatments
  • Offers facial aesthetic treatments, including Botox and dermal fillers
  • Committed to natural-looking results designed for long-term health and longevity
  • Comprehensive approach to dentistry, supported by advanced digital dentistry
  • Caring for patients at Basalt Dentistry since 2022

Meet Bethany Haerter , RDH

Office Manager and Hygienist at Basalt Dentistry.

Bethany Haerter brings experience and a passion for patient care to Basalt Dentistry. She met her future husband, Dr. Jonathan Haerter, during dental hygiene school, and together they pursued their dream of living in the mountains, beginning their dental careers in Colorado in 2000. Since then, Bethany has excelled as both the office manager and a dental hygienist, first at Vail Dentistry and now at Basalt Dentistry since 2019.

Outside the office, Bethany is an avid outdoor enthusiast. She enjoys snow skiing, water skiing, hiking, mountain biking, stand-up paddleboarding, golfing, and spending quality time with her two ski-racing daughters. Her commitment to an active lifestyle and her community shines through in everything she does, making her a cherished member of both the dental practice and the local area.

  • Bachelor of Science Degree from the University of Minnesota, 1999
  • University of Minnesota Dental Hygiene Program, recipient of the Procter and Gamble CREST Oral Hygiene Excellence Award and the University of Minnesota Alumni Association Outstanding Student Award
  • Hundreds of hours in both dental practice management and clinical hygiene practice
