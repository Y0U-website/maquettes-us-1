The practice

We accept referrals from general dentists looking for specialized care for their patients.

Expert care so you can enjoy healthy gums, prevent tooth loss, and improve your oral health.

A comprehensive range of surgical treatments to address complex dental issues.

Sedation options that make even the most complex treatments comfortable.

Gentle and effective techniques, like scaling and root planing, remove bacteria to restore optimal gum health.

Pulp therapy is a form of specialized treatment that helps prevent damaged baby teeth from needing to be extracted.

Using the latest techniques, our team performs soft tissue grafting to help improve your gum health.

Socket preservation is essential to maintain bone health and provides a solid foundation for future treatment, such as implants.
