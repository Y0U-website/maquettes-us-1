Why a Pediatric Dentist and Why Choose Darien Pediatric Dentistry?

Pediatric dentists complete two to three years of specialized training beyond dental school, focused entirely on the dental care of infants, children, teenagers, and patients with special needs. That additional training is why families choose a pediatric dentist for their children.

At Darien Pediatric Dentistry, board-certified pediatric dentists Dr. Kate Kiguradze and Dr. Sara Golkari have cared for Darien families since 2010, combining clinical expertise with a gentle, child-centered approach in a calm, welcoming office.
