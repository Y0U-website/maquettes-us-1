Why Branton Family Dentistry?

Individualized Dental Care

Branton Family Dentistry is committed to providing each patient with an exceptional level of care and attention. At Branton Family Dentistry, we are proud to provide dental needs for the entire family. Our team works together to help everyone feel at ease in a relaxing "feels like home" environment.

Branton Family Dentistry is conveniently located in the northern Bossier City area. We offer extended hours on Thursdays and accept same-day appointments (based on availability).

Branton Family Dentistry accepts most major insurance plans and offers Care Credit payment plans at no interest to you.
