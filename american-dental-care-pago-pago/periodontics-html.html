What is periodontics?

Periodontics is the dental specialty focusing exclusively in the diagnosis, prevention and treatment of the inflammatory disease that destroys the gums and other supporting structures around the teeth, called periodontal disease.

There are two types of periodontal disease: Gingivitis and periodontitis.

Benefits of periodontics

  • Betters gums and teeth aesthetic
  • Betters the functionality of gums and teeth
  • Reduces the risk of dental loss
  • Reduces teeth movement and shifts
  • Reduces dental bleeding
  • Helps control halitosis or bad breath

Why should I go to a periodontist?

  • A high percentage of patients have some sort of periodontal disease without knowing it.
  • Because infection is progressive, periodontal diseases can cause gum retraction and bone loss. This can cause teeth loss if not treated in time.
