Academic Excellence & Advanced Training

Dr. Gong earned her BS degree in dental hygiene at UNC-Chapel Hill, then returned to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the same institution. During dental school she was one of only eight students selected for advanced implant courses — and even had the opportunity to place implants as a student.

After graduation, she completed a six-month mini implant residency, and has since pursued extensive training in IV sedation, TMD/occlusion, and periodontology.
