Why Patients Choose the Accent Care Plan

As an independent, family-owned practice in Angier, NC, we created the Accent Care Plan because we believe every patient deserves access to quality dental care — regardless of whether they have insurance. Unlike corporate dental chains that prioritize volume and profit, we prioritize relationships and long-term oral health.

The Accent Care Plan eliminates the frustration of insurance — no waiting periods, no annual maximums, no claim forms, and no surprises. You pay one simple annual fee and receive comprehensive preventive care plus meaningful discounts on everything else.
