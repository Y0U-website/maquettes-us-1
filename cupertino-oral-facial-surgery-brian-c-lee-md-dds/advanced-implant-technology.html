Advanced Implant Technology –––

TRIOS 6 Intraoral Scanner: No More Goopy Impressions

Traditional impression trays are uncomfortable and prone to distortion. The TRIOS 6 digital scanner captures your teeth and gums in minutes with a small handheld wand, producing a digital model more accurate than conventional impressions. Scans transfer instantly to your restorative dentist and the dental lab, which means faster turnaround and restorations that fit right the first time.

iCam4D Photogrammetry: Full Arch Precision in Minutes

For All-on-X and full arch cases , iCam4D photogrammetry is the technology that sets our practice apart. After your implants are placed, iCam4D photographically records their exact three-dimensional positions with microscopic accuracy, a level of precision conventional impression techniques cannot achieve across a full arch. The result: your final prosthesis fits precisely the first time, with fewer appointments, fewer adjustments, and a faster path to your permanent teeth.

Digital Planning and Computer-Guided Surgery

Every implant case is planned on the computer before it happens. Dr. Lee merges your CBCT scan and digital impressions into a single 3D plan, determines the ideal implant position, and fabricates a surgical guide that translates that plan to your surgery with precision. Guided surgery means smaller incisions, shorter procedures, less swelling, and predictable results, whether you are replacing a single tooth or a full arch.

Straumann Implant Systems

Dr. Lee is a Straumann Roxolid provider, reflecting significant experience with one of the most researched implant systems in the world. Straumann implants are backed by decades of clinical data on longevity and success rates, so the foundation of your new smile is as proven as the planning behind it.
