Our Office Has Gone Green

It is everyone’s job to take care of the planet and each other. At Avila Dental, those are two jobs we take very seriously and strive to excel in.

Our digital imaging technology means our patients are exposed to 90 % less radiation, and we eliminate the need for toxic x-ray development chemicals.

Steam-based, surgical-grade instrument sterilization contains no harmful chemicals and reduces water use. We do not use toxic cold-sterile solutions.

We recycle as much as we can in our office, reuse packing material, and avoid purchasing plastic bottles or paper cups.

We use eco-friendly products for general housekeeping and biodegradable sterilization to keep our examination areas clean.

Our special water filtration system prevents pollution of local waterways with the environmentally sound disposal of old mercury fillings.

We use cloth sterilization pouches instead of disposable ones, which are safe and very effective. Additionally, we use cloth bibs instead of plastic-lined bibs.

Our operatory lights use DentalEZ, which is the first in the U.S. to design lights with 100 % true direct LED technology.
