Financial Policy

We file all dental insurance!

We will do our best to give you an estimate of your investment into your dental health for each upcoming visit, based on your individualized treatment plan. You will be given our best estimate of your next visit’s total bill. With a proper diagnosis and treatment plan, most estimates we provide are very close to your projected portion. Our team is highly trained in dental insurance and is happy to help you understand your benefits and assist with claim submissions.

We ask that outstanding balances are handled within 30 days of treatment. Delinquent balances over 90 days may be subject to our Collection Agency of choice.

Your dental appointments are scheduled carefully. Time, trained personnel and dental equipment are reserved for each of your procedures. We request 24 hours notice for rescheduling.

Office Policy For Patients With Dental Insurance

Please bring your insurance card and photo ID to your first visit.

Our team is trained to assist you with questions about how your claim was filed or to help provide any additional information your insurance carrier may need. Please don’t hesitate to ask if you have any questions.

  • We will always do our best to maximize your benefits.
  • We will file claims for you as a courtesy. Your dental insurance policy is a contract between you, your employer, and your insurance company.
  • Your treatment plan is personalized based on your individual needs and is not influenced by your dental insurance coverage or lack of benefits
  • Not all services are covered by every insurance plan, and some carriers may choose not to cover certain procedures. It is your responsibility to understand your policy’s coverage and limitations. We are unable to mediate insurance decisions. If a service is deemed non-covered, disallowed, or cosmetic, charges may not be submitted to insurance and will be the responsibility of the patient or account guarantor.
