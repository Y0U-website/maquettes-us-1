Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates typically:

Even if you’ve been told you don’t qualify in the past, advances in bone grafting and implant technology may still make you a candidate. Dr. Cox will evaluate your oral health and discuss all available options during your consultation.

  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone to support an implant
  • Are non-smokers or willing to quit
  • Are free of uncontrolled medical conditions (like diabetes or gum disease)
  • Are committed to good oral hygiene and regular dental visits

Types of Dental Implant Restorations

We provide implant solutions to meet a variety of needs, including:

No matter how many teeth you’re missing, we’ll create a plan to help you restore your smile with lasting results.

  • Single-tooth implants – Replace one missing tooth with an implant and custom crown
  • Implant-supported bridges – Replace multiple missing teeth without relying on adjacent natural teeth
  • Implant-supported dentures – Stabilize a full or partial denture with two or more implants for a secure fit

The Dental Implant Process: What to Expect

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process, typically completed over several months:

Step 1: Consultation & Planning

Dr. Cox will assess your teeth, jawbone, and overall health using digital imaging. We’ll develop a custom treatment plan that fits your goals and budget.

Step 2: Implant Placement

The implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Healing typically takes a few months while the implant fuses with the bone a process called osseointegration.

Step 3: Abutment & Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment (connector) and place your final crown, bridge, or denture. The result is a natural-looking, fully functional replacement tooth.

Throughout the process, we’ll guide you every step of the way to ensure a comfortable and successful outcome.

Caring for Dental Implants

Dental implants are low-maintenance but still require good hygiene to stay healthy:

With proper care, dental implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime.

  • Brush and floss daily
  • Visit us regularly for cleanings and checkup
  • Avoid tobacco and hard objects that could damage the restoration
