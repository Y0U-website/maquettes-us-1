Dental Implants A Permanent Solution for Missing Teeth Missing teeth can affect more than just your smile they can impact how you eat, speak, and feel about yourself. At Cox Family Dentistry, we offer dental implants in Sikeston, MO as a long-lasting, natural-looking solution to restore the health, function, and confidence of your smile.

Whether you’re missing a single tooth or need to replace multiple teeth, Dr. Sam Cox will walk you through your options and provide expert care every step of the way.

A dental implant is a small, titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. It is surgically placed into your jawbone to support a replacement tooth, such as a crown, bridge, or denture. Because implants fuse with the jawbone over time, they provide unmatched stability and help prevent bone loss.

Dental implants are considered the gold standard in modern tooth replacement for their durability, natural appearance, and ability to function just like real teeth.

At Cox Family Dentistry, we focus on providing personalized, high-quality implant solutions tailored to your needs.

Even if you’ve been told you don’t qualify in the past, advances in bone grafting and implant technology may still make you a candidate. Dr. Cox will evaluate your oral health and discuss all available options during your consultation.
