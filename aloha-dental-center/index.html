0 Hi, we are Aloha Dental Center. Welcome to Aloha Dental Center, where we are proud to have served the Aiea community for over 15 years with compassionate, high-quality dental care. Since our founding in 2010, our practice has been dedicated to helping patients achieve lasting oral health and confident smiles in a welcoming, respectful environment.

Aloha Dental Center was founded by the late Dr. Benjamin Rodriguez, a dentist known not only for his clinical skill but for his deep empathy and genuine care for his patients. His commitment to excellence, integrity, and patient-centered care continues to guide everything we do.

Following Dr. Rodriguez’s passing, his family has continued his legacy with the same values and dedication. Today, the practice remains focused on providing thoughtful, personalized dental care rooted in trust, consistency, and long-standing relationships within the Aiea community.

At Aloha Dental Center, we believe dentistry is about more than teeth — it’s about people. Whether you are visiting us for a routine exam or more advanced treatment, our team is committed to making your experience comfortable, transparent, and tailored to your individual needs.

Thank you for trusting Aloha Dental Center with your dental care. We look forward to continuing to serve Aiea and our surrounding communities for many years to come.
