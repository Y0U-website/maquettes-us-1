Dental Implants

For many patients, dental implants are the best tooth replacement option. You can restore these titanium tooth roots with permanent dentures, dental crowns, or tooth bridges.

Patients find that it is easy to adjust to life with dental implants. They feel confident in their bright and beautiful smiles. They also love that dental implants preserve the jawbone so that they can avoid facial collapse.
