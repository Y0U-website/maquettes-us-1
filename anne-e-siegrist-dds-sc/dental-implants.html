Signs You Might Need Dental Implants

Dental implants can be a life-changing solution for many patients. At Anne E. Siegrist, DDS, SC, we’ve helped countless individuals regain their smile and confidence. Here are some signs you might benefit from dental implants:

If you’re experiencing any of these issues, don’t hesitate to contact our Appleton office for a consultation.

  • Missing one or more teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Sunken facial appearance
  • Jawbone deterioration
  • Self-consciousness about your smile
