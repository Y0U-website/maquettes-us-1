Dr. Ricardo F. Jara, DDS, FICOI

Dr. Ricardo F. Jara served in the United States Navy as a Hospital Corpsman for six years. He obtained his bachelor of science degree from University of California, Riverside. He furthered his education by obtaining his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University of California, San Francisco in 2017. In 2019 he became a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI).

Dr. Ricardo Jara served in the United States Navy as a Hospital Corpsman for six years. During his military service, he safely provided efficient and effective medical and dental healthcare for five-hundred sailors and marines attached to Navy Hospital Camp Pendleton. He later obtained his bachelor of science degree from University California, Riverside. He furthered his education by obtaining his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry in 2017.

In 2019, he became a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI). He is an active member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Jara has extensive experience in clinical, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, including hundreds of hours in continuum courses studies in all phases of implant dentistry.
