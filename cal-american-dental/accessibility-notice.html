Accessibility Notice

Cal American Dental is committed to ensuring that this website is accessible to all visitors, including individuals with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities. It is our intention that all persons be able to access information regarding our services and practice, and to initiate contact with our office, without encountering unnecessary barriers.

Cal American Dental has adopted the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), as the standard by which this website’s accessibility is measured. This standard is widely recognized by courts and regulatory authorities as the applicable benchmark for accessible website design. This website has not been formally tested or audited against WCAG 2.1 Level AA at this time; the foregoing standard is stated as an adopted goal rather than a completed audit.

Accessibility is maintained as an ongoing obligation rather than a one-time undertaking. This website is reviewed periodically, and accessibility considerations are incorporated whenever new content, features, or functionality are added.

No individual is required to use this website to access the services of Cal American Dental. Any person who experiences difficulty using any portion of this website may contact our office directly by telephone, and our staff will provide the requested information or assistance, including scheduling of appointments, by an alternative method.

Certain content on this website may be provided or hosted by third parties, including, without limitation, embedded map services and any third-party scheduling or preview tools linked from this site. Cal American Dental does not control the accessibility of such third-party content and cannot guarantee that it conforms to the standard set forth in Section 2. Where such content is inaccessible to a visitor, Cal American Dental will provide the same information by an alternative means upon request.

Any person who encounters an accessibility barrier on this website, or who requires information in an alternative format, is encouraged to contact our office using the information below.

To assist us in responding effectively, please identify the specific page or feature involved and describe the difficulty encountered. Cal American Dental will make reasonable efforts to respond to such requests within five business days and to remediate confirmed barriers within a reasonable period thereafter.

A copy of this Accessibility Notice is available at our office and on our website.
