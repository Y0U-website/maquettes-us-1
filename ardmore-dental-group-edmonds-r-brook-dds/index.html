The practice

We are a locally owned dental practice caring for patients of all ages from Ardmore, OK, and the surrounding areas of Ringling, Wilson, Madill, Marietta, Elmore City, and Long Grove.

We offer a full range of dental services under one roof. From routine hygiene cleanings and professional dental exams, restorative services, pediatric treatments, and dental implants to complete smile makeovers, we can help you maintain a beautiful smile for life at Ardmore Dental Group.

Ardmore Dental Group has been providing exceptional dental care to the Ardmore community since 1986. Our mission is to be a relationship-focused practice that provides exceptional patient care.

Whether you are overdue for a dental exam or are just looking for a new dental home for your family, you have come to the right! Ardmore Dental Group can take care of all your dental needs. We see patients of all dental backgrounds and never judge or lecture patients. We focus on finding individualized solutions to help each patient achieve and maintain optimal oral health.
