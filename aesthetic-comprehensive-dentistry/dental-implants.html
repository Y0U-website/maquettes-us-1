Dental Implants Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Longevity: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime—unlike bridges or dentures that typically need replacement every 5–10 years
  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate jawbone growth, preventing the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth
  • Natural Function: Eat, speak, and smile with confidence, without the slipping or discomfort associated with dentures
  • Improved Oral Health: No need to alter adjacent teeth as with traditional bridges
  • Aesthetics: Implants look and feel just like natural teeth

Cons

  • Initial Investment: Implants have a higher upfront cost than other options, though their longevity often makes them more affordable over time
  • Treatment Time: The process typically takes 3–6 months to complete as the implant integrates with your jawbone
  • Surgical Procedure: Implant placement requires minor surgery, though most patients report minimal discomfort

Are Implants Worth It?

  • Lifetime Durability: Many patients never need to replace their implants, while dentures and bridges require periodic replacement
  • Preserved Facial Structure: Implants prevent the sunken appearance that develops when the jawbone deteriorates after tooth loss
  • Superior Comfort: No adhesives, no movement, no sore spots—just teeth that feel completely natural
  • Confidence: Eat, laugh, and smile without worrying about your teeth shifting or slipping
  • Overall Health Benefits: Better nutrition through improved chewing ability and reduced risk of gum disease

Dental Implant Process

  • Comprehensive Evaluation: Dr. Sheikh assesses your oral health, bone density, and medical history to confirm you’re a good candidate for implants.
  • Personalized Treatment Planning: Using 3D imaging, we create a precise surgical guide for optimal implant placement.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed in your jawbone. Many patients are surprised by how comfortable this procedure is.
  • Healing Period: Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration.
  • Abutment Placement: Once healing is complete, we attach the connector that will hold your new tooth.
  • Final Restoration: Your custom-crafted crown is attached, completing your smile with a tooth that looks and functions naturally.
