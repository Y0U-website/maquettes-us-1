The practice

Our team cherishes every opportunity to care for your child, treating them with the warmth and kindness they deserve. We consider it a privilege to be trusted with your child’s smile and are dedicated to making each visit a comforting, efficient, and joyful experience!

At Children’s Dentistry For Alabama, Dr. James Osborn, Dr. Rayne Osborn, Dr. Natalie Carruth, Dr. David Ashley, and Dr. Elon Zora treat each child as if they were their own. Our team is committed to providing high-quality, gentle care that starts each child on a lifelong dental health journey and enthusiasm for oral care.

Our family-oriented approach combines compassion and personalized attention with encouragement and education. We aim to make dental care a positive part of children’s lives, helping them develop healthy, confident smiles.

Located in central and southern Alabama, we serve communities from Selma to Tuscaloosa to Montgomery. Contact us to learn more or to schedule your child’s next visit. We welcome new patients and accept Alabama Medicaid and all major insurance plans.
