When You Arrive

Our waiting area is designed to feel comfortable and unhurried. If you have any questions before you&#x27;re called back, our team is happy to help.

Sedation Options

For patients who experience dental anxiety, we offer several options to make your visit as comfortable as possible:

We discuss your options before every appointment so you walk in feeling confident and prepared, never caught off guard.

  • Relaxation options are available to help ease anxiety during your appointment so you can feel calm and comfortable from start to finish
  • Oral sedation is available for patients who need a deeper level of comfort during more involved procedures

In the Chair

Once you&#x27;re with your provider, we&#x27;ll take a full set of digital X-rays and conduct a comprehensive exam. This includes:

This is also your time to talk. If you have concerns, areas of discomfort, or treatments you&#x27;ve been curious about, bring them up. We&#x27;re listening.

  • A full oral health evaluation
  • Digital X-rays to assess what&#x27;s happening below the surface
  • An oral cancer screening
  • A periodontal assessment to check the health of your gums
  • A professional cleaning to remove buildup and leave your teeth polished

Treatment Planning

Depending on what your exam shows, your treatment plan may include:

  • Restorative care, such as fillings, crowns, or root canals to address decay or damage
  • Periodontal treatment if your gum assessment shows signs of gum disease
  • Orthodontic options if alignment is a concern, from Invisalign to traditional braces
  • Implant solutions for any missing teeth, with complimentary consultations available
  • Cosmetic treatment such as veneers, whitening, or a full smile makeover, also with a complimentary consultation

A few things to keep in mind:

  • Nothing moves forward without your full understanding and approval
  • We prioritize urgent needs first and help you plan additional treatment at a pace that works for you
  • If cost or timing is a concern, we&#x27;ll work with you on a sequenced plan and discuss financing options through CareCredit
  • Our goal is to give you a clear, honest picture of your oral health so you can make informed decisions with confidence

Checkout and Follow-Up

At checkout, our team will review your next steps, help you schedule any follow-up appointments, and answer questions about your treatment plan or insurance coverage. If you&#x27;d like to explore financing through CareCredit, we can walk you through that before you leave.

We&#x27;ll send you a reminder before your next visit so nothing falls through the cracks. And if anything comes up between appointments, we&#x27;re always just a phone call away.
