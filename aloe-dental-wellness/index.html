The practice

After graduating as a dentist in 2010, she worked in private practice in Medellin. During this time, she also completed a research project with Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, Texas, where she met her now husband Dr. John Feusier, an orthodontist in Santa Barbara.

After deciding to move to America, she was accepted into the international program at the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, one of the top programs in the world, where she then completed another two years of post-graduate training.
