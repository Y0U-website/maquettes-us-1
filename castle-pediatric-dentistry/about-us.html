Our Dental Goals

Meet Dr. Keren Castellucci Keren Castellucci is the owner and founder of Castle Pediatric Dentistry . Opening the practice in 2022, she was thrilled to bring her years of experience to Winter Springs and the surrounding communities. As she shares, “It has been a lifelong dream of mine to be a Pediatric Dentist in the community where I grew up and where I am raising my family.”

Being raised locally, Dr. Castellucci was proud to attend the University of Central Florida, earning a Bachelor of Science in Molecular and Microbiology. Go, Knights! She completed her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Nova Southeastern University in Ft. Lauderdale, FL. Dr. Castellucci’s passion for pediatric dentistry radiated in her commitment to education and giving back to the community throughout dental school. Upon graduation, she received the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry Certificate of Merit Award.

After completing dental school, Dr. Castellucci attended a two-year pediatric dentistry residency at Nova Southeastern University and Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital. She served there as Chief Resident during her second year. During her residency, she focused her training on providing comprehensive care for pediatric patients, including those with special health care needs. Immediately after completing residency, Dr. Castellucci returned to her hometown to provide pediatric dental care to the children in Central Florida.

Dr. Castellucci is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She has been an advocate for the profession throughout her career and served as the 2019-2020 President of the Florida Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

“Working with children reminds me of how magical the world can be and encourages me to ignite my imagination and find creative ways to relate to children of all ages.”

Dr. Castellucci has an enthusiastic attitude towards providing dental education and care to her patients and their families. She prides herself in making each visit a comfortable experience, creating a relationship where her patients enjoy going to the dentist. Dr. Castellucci feels truly blessed to have the opportunity to make a difference in the lives of children daily.

Dr. Castellucci married her high school sweetheart Jonathan. They have two beautiful daughters and a standard poodle. You might even see Winnie-the-Poodle at the office working alongside Dr. Castellucci and the Castle Pediatric Dentistry Team!

Meet Dr. Mehreen Kassoo

Mehreen Kassoo is a fun-loving and compassionate board-certified pediatric dentist who joined Castle Pediatric Dentistry in 2026. Born and raised in Central Florida, she earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Central Florida before pursuing her dental education at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. She then completed her pediatric dental residency at New York University, where she focused on providing care for infants, children, and teens, and served as Chief Resident in her second year.

Dr. Kassoo has a true passion for working with children and helping little smiles grow big and strong. She believes that visiting the dentist should be a positive—and even fun—experience, and she creates a welcoming environment where children feel safe, comfortable, and excited to learn about oral health. Known for her gentle touch and cheerful personality, she strives to turn every dental visit into a happy, stress-free adventure while teaching children healthy habits that support lifelong smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Kassoo enjoys spending time with her family, going to the beach, traveling, and exploring local parks with her husband and two children. She looks forward to meeting families and helping every child shine with a bright, healthy smile.
