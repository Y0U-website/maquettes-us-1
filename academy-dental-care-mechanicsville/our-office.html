Meet our clinical team

Jennifer

Jennifer is one of our dental hygienist. She was born and raised in Southern Maryland. After attending the college of southern Maryland for her associates, she received her Bachelors Degree of Science in Dental Hygiene from Old Dominion University in 2011. She is very passionate about her job! Jennifer and her husband, Mark, have two children, Noelle and Junuh. She enjoys trying new recipes and spending time outside as well as reading and learning new things.

Emily

Emily has been in Southern Maryland since 1994, when her family retired from the military. She got into the dental field by first filing paper charts while in high school at Academy Dental Care. She obtained her training at the College of Southern Maryland in 2010, and has since been working in the field. Emily’s favorite part about working at Academy Dental Care is the atmosphere with her team, and everyone coming together to give patient’s the best care possible. When Emily is not working she enjoys spending time with her husband and two sons.

Kayla

Born and raised in St Mary’s County, Kayla joined our team in 2022. Kayla obtained her training at the College of Southern Maryland and has been working in the dental field ever since. Kayla’s favorite part about working at Academy Dental Care is making patients feel good about themselves and working with such a great team. She feels like going to work every day is like “getting to hang out with good friends while building relationships with patients”. Outside of work Kayla loves spending time with her family and being a mom to her perfect baby girl!

Giving Back

Our community is the backbone of Academy Dental’s care. So we’re honored to uplift our local communities by championing hope and creating accessible opportunities for our families and neighbors. Whether it’s food drives with Southern Maryland Food Bank, clothing drives for our local churches, or donating to our nearby Veterans hospital, we’re thankful to give back to the communities we love and call home. Let’s do some good together.
