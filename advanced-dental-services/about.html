Our Approach to Care

We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful for some patients, which is why we go out of our way to create a relaxing and positive environment. At Advanced Dental Services, we believe in:

Patient Education

We empower our patients with the knowledge they need to make informed decisions about their oral health. Dr. Kleven and our team take the time to explain treatment options and answer any questions you may have.

Comprehensive Care

We offer a full range of dental services, from preventive care and routine check-ups to advanced restorative and cosmetic treatments, ensuring that all your dental needs are met in one convenient location.

Ethical Practice

We prioritize doing what’s right for our patients, always recommending treatments that are in your best interest. Dr. Kleven’s ethical approach to dentistry is reflected in the trust and loyalty of our long-term patients.

Comfort and Convenience

Our office is designed with your comfort in mind, and we offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your busy life. We also provide a range of amenities to make your visit as pleasant as possible.
