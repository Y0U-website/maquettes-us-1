Comprehensive Dental Implant Restoration

In addition to placing implants, we also specialize in dental implant restoration. This service is ideal for patients who already have implants but need new crowns, bridges, or dentures to restore their functionality and appearance.

What Implant Restoration Involves:

  • Custom-Made Crowns: We design crowns that match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth for a seamless look.
  • Bridges and Dentures: If you’re missing multiple teeth, we offer implant-supported bridges and dentures to restore your smile fully.
  • Maintenance and Adjustments: We provide ongoing care and adjustments to ensure your implant restorations remain secure and comfortable.

Our Dental Implant Process:

Explore our other restorative services, including dental crowns and bridges and full mouth reconstruction , to learn more about how we can help restore your smile.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation: We begin with a thorough examination, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess your oral health and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan: Based on your needs and goals, we develop a customized treatment plan that outlines every step of the process.
  • Surgical Placement: After your dental implant has been surgically placed by a trusted oral surgeon and fully healed, Dr. Kleven carefully restores the implant with a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture—ensuring a natural look, proper function, and long-term comfort.
  • Restoration: After the implant integrates with your jawbone, we attach the custom-made restoration, completing your new smile.
