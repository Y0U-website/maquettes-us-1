How long do implant-based restorations last?

With proper care and regular dental check-ups, implant restorations can last 15–25 years or longer. The implant post can last a lifetime, while the crown or bridge may need periodic replacement.

How do I maintain my implant restorations?

Caring for implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Here are the simple steps to maintain your dental implants.

  • Brush twice daily and floss regularly
  • Be consistent with routine dental cleanings and check-ups
  • Eat a healthy, dental-friendly diet
  • Don’t use your teeth to bite or chew on non-food items
  • Wear a bespoke mouthguard if you grind your teeth or play contact sports
