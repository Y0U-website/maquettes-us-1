What To Expect At Your First Visit

Your first visit is all about you. We take the time to listen to your concerns, understand your goals, and get a complete picture of your oral health. Together, we will review our findings, answer your questions and discuss treatment options so you can feel informed and confident about your care. Our goal is to provide a through evaluation and establish a foundation for your long-term oral health.

Calming Comforts & Amenities

You deserve to enjoy going to your West Hartford dentist. That’s why we’ve included soothing comforts and exclusive amenities for a delightful dental experience from start to finish.

Refreshment Bar

You won’t be waiting long, but feel free to enjoy sparkling water or coffee before your visit at our refreshment bar.

TVs With Netflix

Sit back, relax, and stream. Each operatory has ceiling-mounted TVs so you can watch your favorite Netflix show as Dr. Kaur polishes your smile.

Noise-Canceling Headphones

Looking to tune out during your treatment? We’ve got noise-canceling Bose headphones to help you get in the zone.

Warm Towels

We want to make you feel truly pampered and cared for during your stay. Reach for a warm towel after your visit to destress and unwind.

Cutting-Edge Tools & Tech

Click on boxes to learn more

Cerec

Struggling with a cracked or damaged tooth? Our same-day CEREC crowns let you get your smile back in just a single hour-long visit.

Laser Therapy

Say goodbye to noisy, invasive drills. Laser therapy allows us to perform a wide range of procedures, from simple cavity prepping to more complex surgeries. Thanks to lasers, you can experience a comfortable, quick, and pleasant treatment.

Nitrous Oxide

To keep care stress-free and comfortable, we offer nitrous oxide with any treatment. Relax during your procedure then resume your daily activities with a smile.

How We Give Back

Committed To Our Community

Dr. Rabia Kaur and the team at Arch Dental are committed to enhancing the lives of those within our practice and beyond our doors. That’s why we are proud to partner with The Village , a non-profit organization that provides a full range of behavioral health, youth development, and support services for homeless families in West Hartford. Arch Dental will donate an oral hygiene kit to a family in need at The Village for every new patient that schedules an appointment.
