TRADITIONAL BRACES

Metal braces are the most widely used type of braces. They are very strong and can withstand most types of treatment. Unlike the metal braces of the past, today’s metal braces are small, more comfortable and more attractive.

INVISALIGN

Our Orlando Invisalign treatment combines breakthrough multiple dimension technology with the science of Orthodontics. Invisalign aligners are designed to move your teeth in small steps to the desired positions prescribed by your orthodontist.

CERAMIC BRACES

Ceramic braces or clear braces are made of composite material. These brackets create a system with the highest transparency available for outstanding aesthetics and are virtually invisible against any tooth shade and stay crystal clear without staining.

CLEAR CORRECT

ClearCorrect is the clear and simple alternative to braces. It’s a series of clear, custom-made, removable aligners that gradually straighten your teeth as you wear them. Ask your doctor for more information on how ClearCorrect can help you get the smile you’ve always wanted.
