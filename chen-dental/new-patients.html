What to expect at your first visit...

At Chen Dental , we strive to make your first visit as welcoming and comfortable as possible. From the moment you arrive, you’ll be greeted by a friendly, smiling face, ready to assist you. Our office features a cozy living room equipped with a massage chair, a kitchenette offering coffee, drinks, and snacks, and a relaxing space designed to make you feel at ease.

We’ll start your visit with a tour of our office to familiarize you with our state-of-the-art amenities. Our Comfort Menu includes options such as headphones, a warm blanket, and a neck pillow to ensure your utmost relaxation during your appointment.

To begin your dental care, we use digital radiographs for accurate and efficient imaging. We’ll also take the time to get to know your personal smile goals and discuss your dental aspirations. This allows us to create a personalized, step-by-step treatment plan tailored to your needs, which will be reviewed and examined by your doctor.

We look forward to providing you with an exceptional experience that sets the standard for dental care.
