Braces

Braces remain one of the most effective and reliable ways to achieve a straight, healthy smile. Whether correcting bite issues or aligning teeth, they provide precise, long-lasting results for patients of all ages.

Beyond aesthetics, braces give orthodontists full control over tooth movement, ensuring highly accurate and predictable results. Their effectiveness in treating even the most complex cases makes them a trusted choice for achieving a well-aligned, functional smile.

For kids and teens, braces can be a fun way to express personality by adding colorful elastics to match their style, school colors, or even the seasons.

Invisalign ®

Looking for a discreet way to straighten your teeth? Invisalign offers a nearly invisible solution with clear, removable aligners that fit seamlessly into your lifestyle. Unlike traditional braces, you can eat, brush, and floss without restrictions while your teeth gradually shift into place.

Each aligner is custom-made using advanced digital technology for precise, predictable results. Whether you’re a teen or an adult, Invisalign provides a comfortable and flexible treatment option that lets you achieve a confident smile without interruption.

Retainers

Your orthodontic journey doesn’t end when braces come off or aligners are finished—retainers are essential to keeping your smile in place. After treatment, teeth naturally tend to shift, and retainers help maintain your hard-earned results for the long term.

We offer both removable and fixed retainers, customized to fit your needs and lifestyle. Wearing your retainer as directed ensures your smile stays straight, healthy, and exactly where it should be for years to come.

Palatal Expanders

Palatal expanders are used to create more space in a child’s upper jaw by gradually widening the palate. This treatment is often recommended in early orthodontics to help correct bite issues, improve airflow, and prevent crowding as permanent teeth come in. By guiding proper jaw development, expanders can reduce the need for more invasive treatments later on.

Custom-made for each child, expanders guide jaw growth to allow teeth to fit properly. They play a key role in preventing more complex orthodontic issues later, making future treatment easier and more effective.

Functional Appliances

Functional appliances help correct jaw alignment and bite issues by guiding jaw growth and improving overall function.

Forsus appliance, a discreet alternative to headgear, is used to correct overbites by gradually repositioning the lower jaw. It works alongside braces to enhance bite alignment, making treatment more efficient and reducing the need for extractions or surgery.

The Herbst appliance is used to correct overbites by encouraging forward growth of the lower jaw. Commonly recommended for younger patients, it helps improve jaw balance, facial harmony, and long-term bite stability while reducing the need for future corrections.

Carriere appliance is designed to correct bite issues early, often shortening overall treatment time. By aligning the bite before braces or aligners, it makes the next phase of orthodontic treatment more efficient and effective.

Technology

We use the latest advancements in orthodontic technology to ensure precise, efficient, and comfortable treatment.

Say goodbye to messy goops and impressions with iTero! This advanced 3D scanner captures highly detailed digital images of your teeth in minutes, ensuring a more comfortable and accurate experience. It enhances treatment precision, creating perfectly fitted aligners, braces, and retainers while giving you a clear preview of your future smile.

Our low-radiation digital X-rays provide clear, high-resolution images for a safer and more detailed look at your teeth and jaw. This advanced technology enhances diagnosis, allowing for more precise treatment planning with minimal exposure.

High-quality digital photos help track your orthodontic progress and provide a detailed visual of your treatment plan. These images give us a comprehensive view of your smile, ensuring accuracy from start to finish.
