What are All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 implants are a type of dental implant used to support a full arch of teeth. Unlike a traditional or single implant, which requires a separate implant for each tooth, All-on-4 implants use just four artificial tooth roots to support an entire upper or lower denture for your teeth.

This makes the surgery less invasive and helps to speed up the healing process when replacing multiple missing teeth. First, dental implants are placed in the jawbone, and over time, the bone grows around the implants to secure them in place. Once the dental implant has healed, artificial teeth are then attached to the implants.

All-on-4 dental implants provide a more secure and stable fit than traditional dentures, and they can help to improve chewing function and speech if you are missing teeth. Dental implants are also more comfortable than traditional dentures and can help restore a natural teeth appearance.

Benefits of Dental Implants

There are many benefits to choosing All-on-4 Implants over conventional implants, including:

  • All-on-4 Implants require less extensive treatment than traditional implants.
  • They are more comfortable than traditional dentures.
  • They secure dentures, so you won’t have to worry about them being loose.
  • They can help to restore your facial structure and give you a healthy smile.
  • They can help stop further bone loss in the jawbone.
