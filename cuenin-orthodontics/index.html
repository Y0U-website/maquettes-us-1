The practice

For patients requiring braces, Cuenin Orthodontics offers personalized treatment plans tailored to each individual’s needs. Whether correcting a bite issue, closing gaps, or aligning teeth with Invisalign or traditional braces , the practice is equipped with the latest advancements in orthodontics. They also help patients maintain their successful smile outcomes with retainers .

Cuenin Orthodontics also places importance on the protection of smiles by providing custom mouthguard s to safeguard teeth during sports or recreational activities.

One of the team’s primary focus areas is children’s orthodontics . They understand the unique needs of young patients and ensure a positive experience for children undergoing treatment. Utilizing state-of-the-art techniques and technology, they correct misalignments and guide proper dental development.

Cuenin Orthodontics also offers orthodontic consultations that allow patients to discuss their concerns and explore treatment options, in addition to providing advanced surgical orthodontics.

With a commitment to delivering exceptional orthodontic care, Cuenin Orthodontics is one of the most trusted practices for comprehensive orthodontic treatment for residents of Danville, California. Patients can expect personalized attention and the latest innovative techniques to help them achieve beautiful, healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

To book an appointment, call today or request an appointment online for a follow-up call from the friendly staff.

An orthodontic consultation is an initial appointment with your provider at Cuenin Orthodontics to assess the alignment and overall condition of your teeth and jaw. The meeting is free and lasts about 20 minutes. They help determine if you need orthodontic treatment and what treatment options suit your needs.

Before your orthodontic consultation, your provider performs a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, which involves a thorough examination of your teeth, jaws, and facial structures. They may also take X-rays, photographs, and 3D scans to gather additional diagnostic information.
