Orthodontic Consultation Q & A

What is an orthodontic consultation?

An orthodontic consultation is an initial appointment with your provider at Cuenin Orthodontics to assess the alignment and overall condition of your teeth and jaw. The meeting is free and lasts about 20 minutes. They help determine if you need orthodontic treatment and what treatment options suit your needs.

Does an orthodontic consultation involve an evaluation?

Before your orthodontic consultation, your provider performs a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, which involves a thorough examination of your teeth, jaws, and facial structures. They may also take X-rays, photographs, and 3D scans to gather additional diagnostic information.

After they gain a complete overview of your oral health, they offer a free orthodontic consultation that consists of sharing their findings with you and also discussing your concerns and goals.

What are the benefits of an orthodontic consultation?

One of the main benefits of an orthodontic consultation is having the opportunity to discuss your concerns and goals with Dr. Robert Cuenin or Dr. Kyle Cuenin. They listen to your needs and develop a deep understanding of your unique situation. This open dialogue allows you to ask questions, express any oral health issues you might be worried about, and gain a clear understanding of the recommended treatment options.

Your orthodontist also provides a comprehensive assessment of your teeth and jaw alignment. They identify any orthodontic issues or underlying problems that might need intervention. With this information, they can explain your potential treatment options and recommend the best course of action.

Once you decide on the right treatment for you, your orthodontist creates a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.
