Full Mouth Dental Implant Timeline

Digital Records

Implant Placement

With your customized implant placement plan, we precisely place implants to support a fixed set of temporary teeth. Many patients can be put under sedation for this appointment. Teeth removal is also completed during this step. The procedure can take anywhere between 4-6 hours.

Post-op appointment

Several follow ups are completed to ensure that proper healing is happening. Sutures are removed and instructions are given to begin using a Waterpik.

Approximately 4-6 months after the implant procedure, new records are taken, a new mock up is designed and approved by you. We use your feedback on shade, shape, bite, and esthetics to ensure you are happy. We obtain your approval to process the final teeth at this step. Changes cannot be made after this step.

  • Will I be able to eat right away? Yes, you will be able to eat from the first day. A soft food diet with nothing harder than scrambled eggs or fish is encouraged. Eating hard or chewy foods, like steak or nuts, too early can damage your implants
  • Are full mouth dental implants painful? During the first 48-72 hours, soreness and swelling are normal. There could be bruising that occurs that takes 1-2 weeks to resolve. Having said that, most patient can manage their pain by following our protocols and many are feeling better after the first few days.
  • Why should I get my implants at Ascend Dental? We have the benefit of being a private practice dental office. This allows us to treat patients on an individualized basis and allows us to create a customized treatment plan for them. We pride ourselves in caring for all patients long term. You have a dental home here at Ascend Dental.
  • Can dental implants fail? Like normal teeth, dental implants can fail if not properly maintained. Smoking and diabetes are large contributing factors to dental implant failures. We will work with you a create a customized plan to minimize the chances for implant failure.
  • What are my sedation options? We offer IV sedation which allows for moderate level of sedation. Most patients do not remember the procedure when under these medications. Being under moderate sedation allows you to maintain your airway without the use of a tube.
  • What are my eating restrictions? For a short time after implant placement, you should eat a soft food diet consisting of nothing harder than scrambled eggs and fish. You should refrain from eating anything hard or chewy like steak and nuts initially.
  • How should I care for my new implant teeth? We recommend using a Waterpik, beginning at 2 weeks after placement. It is important to not use the water flosser prior to give the gums time to heal and close properly.
  • What will my implant teeth be made out of? The implants are made from medical grade titanium, a similar material used in joint replacements. The temporary teeth are made from acrylic. The final teeth are made from beautiful zirconia, a tough and esthetic material.
