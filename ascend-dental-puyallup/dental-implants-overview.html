Which Dental Implant Option Is Right for You?

| Full Mouth Dental Implants - “Teeth In A Day”

Replace failing teeth or loose dentures with a beautiful set of fixed teeth designed to help you eat, smile and speak confidently again.

Full mouth dental implants, also called “All-on-4” or “Teeth in a day” utilize four or more dental implants per arch to provide permanent fixed teeth that allow you to function, smile, and speak with confidence. These teeth are non-removable and many times you can get a fixed set of teeth the same day as surgery!

| Snap-In Implant Dentures Snap-in dentures, also called overdentures, use two dental implants in the lower arch to improve retention. They are a cost effective alternative to traditional dentures, which have minimal retention and rely on denture adhesive “glue”.

These snap-in dentures are removable to allow for easy cleaning and give you stability in comparison to traditional dentures.

Snap-In dentures increase confidence, function, and quality of life!

$12,000

We generally recommend snap-in implant dentures for the lower arch. In our experience, upper snap-in dentures often do not provide enough additional benefit to justify the investment, so we typically recommend either a well-designed traditional upper denture or fixed implant-supported teeth instead.
