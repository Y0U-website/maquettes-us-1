The practice

Located in Gulf Breeze, less than 15 minutes from East Hill and only 20 minutes from Navarre, our conveniently located office offers seamless access for all your restorative and aesthetic dental needs. Visit our award-winning dentist, Dr. Nicole Barrineau and our skilled dental hygienists to have an exceptional dental care team on your side.

Our office in Gulf Breeze, Florida, can treat your entire family, offering an extensive scope of services to foster preventive care and dental health for all ages. We specialize in comprehensive dentistry, meaning you can come to us for cosmetic, restorative, and oral health treatments.

Let us help you achieve the smile you have always wanted. Cosmetic dentistry is different from general dentistry; it is both an art and a science. By providing cosmetic dental care, Dr. Barrineau is able to offer smile enhancement, restoration, and maintenance treatments for optimal aesthetics and dental health.

We respect both your time and the lasting beauty of your smile. At Barrineau DDS, PA, we offer CEREC same-day crowns, allowing you to restore the function and beauty of your teeth in just one visit. Using state-of-the-art digital imaging and milling technology, Dr. Barrineau crafts custom ceramic crowns that fit seamlessly with your natural teeth.
