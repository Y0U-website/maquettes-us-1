About Us

Trusted Family Dentist in Redwood City, CA

At Bay Area Dental Office, we provide comprehensive dental care services for patients of all ages in Redwood City, California. Our team focuses on preventive dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, restorative dentistry, and family dentistry in a welcoming and comfortable environment. Whether you need a routine checkup, teeth whitening, dental implants, or orthodontic care, we take the time to explain your options and help you choose the treatment that best supports your long term oral health.

Meet The Dentists

Dr. William Meza was born in Peru, where he began his dental career. He later advanced his education by attending the University of the Pacific in San Francisco, California, where he earned his dental license. Since then, he has practiced dentistry for several years and has had the opportunity to open his own practice in Redwood City, California, where he is grateful to work alongside a dedicated team and valued colleagues.

Dr. Meza is passionate about dentistry and committed to providing excellence in preventive, clinical, and cosmetic dental care. His experience includes treatments such as implants, fillings, crowns, bridges, veneers, teeth whitening, orthodontics, and dentures. Through his work, he has helped improve oral health and brought confident smiles to thousands of patients.

He has also completed many continuing education courses that have allowed him to learn and implement digital techniques that enhance patient care. Dr. Meza is a member of several professional dental organizations, including the CDA, ADA, and SMCDS. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his children and family, exploring new places around the Bay Area, and relaxing at home.

Dr. Hernandez, DDS

Dr. Hernandez Morales is enthusiastic and passionate about providing cosmetic, family, and general dentistry services to patients at Infinity Dental Group, located in San Jose, California. Dr. Hernández-Morales was born in Michoacán, México. He is a Bilingual Licensed General Dentist in Mexico and a Doctor of Dental Surgery in California. He was introduced to the dental field when he was very young thanks to his father, an excellent human being and dental professional.

He graduated from Universidad de la Salle Bajío, in León, Guanajuato-México with an outstanding academic achievement recognized from The Center of National Evaluation of Higher Education. Pursuing his goal, he graduated in 2018 with his doctorate from the International Dental Program at De la Salle University in México, allowing him to receive his dental license as a Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Nevada Las Vegas, in Las Vegas.

His greatest satisfaction has always been helping others. Dentistry has given him the opportunity to get closer to people and improve their quality of life as well as their dental health. Dr. Hernández-Morales is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and the Santa Clara County Dental Society.
