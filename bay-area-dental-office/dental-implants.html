Why Choose Dental Implants

Implants are widely considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They preserve the jawbone, do not require alteration of adjacent teeth, and with proper care can last a lifetime. Patients who replace missing teeth promptly also avoid the bone loss and shifting of surrounding teeth that can occur over time.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with good general health and adequate jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. Patients with significant bone loss or certain medical conditions may require additional evaluation before treatment. Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a crown is attached on top to create a restoration that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement after 10 to 15 years depending on wear.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so most patients experience little to no discomfort during placement. Some soreness and swelling in the days following surgery is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How do I care for dental implants?

Implants are cared for just like natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss regularly, and keep up with routine dental visits. Avoiding tobacco and hard foods also helps extend the life of your implant.

How much do dental implants cost in Redwood City?

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan. Some dental insurance plans cover a portion of implant-related costs. Our team can help you understand your benefits and discuss available financing options.
