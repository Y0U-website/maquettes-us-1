Accessibility

Our commitment and approach to maintaining an accessible website.

We believe in providing an inclusive online environment where everyone can access our content, products, and/or services regardless of their abilities. Our commitment to accessibility is not just about compliance but about striving to provide an excellent user experience for all.

We are continually working to maintain our website compatibility with most browsers and devices.

Our website is designed to be navigable by keyboard.

We strive to maintain a consistent layout throughout the website.

We review color contrast ratios on an ongoing basis.

We endeavor to adhere to all other AA accessibility standards. We are continually working to maintain our website compatibility with most browsers and devices.

We endeavor to adhere to all other AA accessibility standards.

This site uses cascading style sheets (CSS) to control all aspects of layout and presentation.

Most fonts on this site are sized using relative font sizes and will adjust automatically to your browser’s default text size.

Content contained within this site will degrade to a usable text-only design when CSS is not available.

Javascript is employed to enhance usability and aesthetic functionality.

Javascript used on this site is implemented in a non-invasive manner and is not required to use the site or access content within.

We are committed to providing captioning on media.

We hope you have a great experience using our website and we value your feedback on how we may improve our accessibility features. If you encounter accessibility barriers or have suggestions on how we may improve, please contact us.
