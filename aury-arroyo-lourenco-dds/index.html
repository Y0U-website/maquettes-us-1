The practice

Dr. Arroyo combines personalized care with advanced dental technology, including a Sirona cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) intraoral scanner with panoramic imaging, digital X-rays, a NOMAD handheld intraoral X-ray, and a VELscope oral cancer screening device. These technologies help patients benefit from precise diagnostics and efficient treatment planning.

My Tooth Place offers a wide range of services to meet both routine and complex dental needs. Patients can visit for dental exams and cleanings , composite resin fillings , crowns , bridges, veneers , Zoom! teeth whitening , and smile makeovers .

More advanced care includes dental implants , bone grafts , crown lengthening , root canals , extractions , wisdom teeth treatment, dentures , frenectomy, and full mouth reconstruction .

My Tooth Place also provides treatment for gum disease, including scaling and root planing, along with preventive services such as sealants, oral hygiene guidance, night guards , and sports mouth guards.

With a focus on high-quality care, modern techniques, and natural-looking results, we aim to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles they can feel good about.
