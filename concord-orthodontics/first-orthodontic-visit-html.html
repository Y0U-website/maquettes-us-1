First Orthodontic Visit | Concord NH Orthodontics | Dr. Sheila Othman

During your first visit, we will get to know you, your unique smile and discuss ways we can improve it. Allow time for xrays, a doctor’s exam and a review of your medical history. Dr. Othman will review everything together with your goals and compose treatment options for you to make a decision on what works best for you, which will be presented on your next visit.

You will be introduced to our front desk team, treatment coordinators, orthodontic assistants and of course, Dr. Othman.

Please save time by having our patient forms completed prior to coming in.

We are so happy you found us. We specialize in complete orthodontic care for children, teens and adults. Please call the office at 603-333-2538 with any questions or to schedule a complimentary orthodontic appo intment for you or your child. We are available Tuesday - Friday from 8:00 to 4:30. Our office observes major holidays. We are always on call for emergencies.

When you visit our orthodontic office, you or your child’s smile is our top priority. Our specialist is Orthodontist, Dr. Sheila Othman. Our team of orthodontic assistants will make the transition to a new beautiful smile easy and fun.
