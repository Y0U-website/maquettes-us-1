Dr. Sheila Othman, Orthodontist

Dr. Sheila Othman is a board-certified orthodontist whose journey has taken her across both the East and West Coasts. Born and raised in a small town in southern West Virginia, she began her academic path at Wake Forest University in North Carolina. She then traveled west to attend dental school at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health before returning to the East Coast to complete her orthodontic specialty training at Einstein Medical Center in Philadelphia.

Dr. Othman loves being part of her patients’ smile transformations and the journey from start to finish. An East Coast girl at heart, Dr. Othman is thrilled to be back closer to home. Outside of the office, she enjoys traveling, spending time outdoors and in nature, and making memories with family and friends. She truly believes that sharing your smile with the world is a precious gift, and it is her personal mission to help make every smile as beautiful and confident as possible.
