Belcher Family Dentistry is conveniently located on the Southwest corner of 180th & Q Streets (18021 R Plaza, Suite 1).

Dr. Belcher graduated from Millard North High School in 1997. He did his undergraduate work at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and then went to the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry in Lincoln for his doctorate of dental surgery degree, graduating in 2004.

After working around the Omaha area for a couple of years, Dr. Belcher opened up his own practice in 2007. Dr. Belcher and his professional staff take a lot of pride in caring for their patients and look forward to meeting new patients. Dr. Belcher is a member of the American Dental Association, Nebraska Dental Association, and the Omaha District Dental Society.
