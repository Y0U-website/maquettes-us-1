No Insurance? We’re Here to Help!

Don’t let the lack of dental insurance stop you from accessing quality dental care. Aaron Family Dentistry has created an exclusive membership plan at a reduced fee to cater to individuals and families without dental insurance. With this annual membership, you can receive exams, X-rays, cleanings, and more at an affordable cost. Even if you experience a dental emergency and need unplanned treatment, you can still sign up for this plan.

What sets this membership apart is that there are no deductibles, maximums, waiting periods, or limitations on procedures. You can even sign up for this plan in case of unexpected dental emergencies requiring immediate treatment. Once you pay the annual fee in full, your membership will be active for 12 consecutive months, and your entire family, including unmarried children under the age of 26, can benefit from it.

The membership fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, and it will renew automatically yearly. If you want to learn more, don’t hesitate to ask us for additional information.
