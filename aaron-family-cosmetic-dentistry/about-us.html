Dr. Nathalie Anton

Dr. Nathalie Anton grew up in Lebanon then moved to Houston, where she attended Memorial High School. She then pursued her undergrad at University of Houston and graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Biology. She received her graduate training at The University of Texas School of Dentistry at Houston with a Degree in Dental Surgery. During her training, she was the chair of the Wellness committee.

Dr. Anton’s focus is providing comprehensive, patient-centered oral health care. She strives to create a stable dental home for patients to feel comfortable and excited about improving their healthy smile. Dr. Anton continues to grow in her career through continuing education and by staying involved in organized dentistry. She enjoys giving back through mission work where she provides dental care to underserved communities.

In her spare time, Dr. Anton loves cooking, painting, and spending time with her husband and two children.
