Implant Restorations Clemmons NC

Once everything falls in place, no one can tell your implant is a tooth replacement. It functions and looks just like the rest of your teeth.

  • Healing: Healing takes about 3-6 months, during which the jawbone grows around the implant post through osseointegration. Once fusion occurs, your implant post becomes just as stable as your natural tooth root.
  • Abutment and dental restoration impression: After healing, we take an impression of the gum area around the implant to allow for fabrication of the abutment and restoration. An abutment connects the implant to the crown or any other tooth restoration.
  • Dental restoration: The final procedure is restoring your tooth with a natural-looking denture, crown, or bridge.
