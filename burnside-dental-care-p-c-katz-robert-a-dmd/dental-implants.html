What Does The Restorative Treatment Entail?

Are you thinking about dental implants? This procedure may be a dependable way to replace lost teeth and return your smile to its natural beauty and performance.

Don’t feel like you’re stuck with an incomplete smile when the right dental treatment can offer you a uniform set of teeth you can love. Give Katz Family Dental Care, Burnside Dental Care, or Farmington Avenue Dental Care a call to book your implant consultation!

  • Middle-Aged Adults: May help restore oral health and confidence to improve overall quality of life.
  • Seniors: Offers durability and stability to promote lifelong independence and good oral health.
  • Placement: First, a small titanium post, known as the implant, is placed into your jawbone. It is designed to serve as a stable base for your brand-new restoration.
  • Osseointegration : Over the duration of several months, osseointegration takes place, which involves the bone around the implant healing and growing to hold itself in place.
  • Abutment : Once the implant is able to successfully integrate with your jawbone, an abutment is fixed on top of the implant, which is linked to your prosthetic tooth or crown.
  • Customized Restoration: In conclusion, dental implants can securely fasten a customized crown or bridge onto the abutment so that it resembles and functions like natural teeth.
