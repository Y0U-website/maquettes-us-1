Orthodontics

About Orthodontics

At Artisan Smiles, our orthodontic specialists address your individual needs to provide you with the beautiful, confident smile you have always wanted. We provide the highest quality of orthodontic treatments and services, and we offer a variety of treatment options such as metal braces, ceramic braces, Invisalign, mouth guards, and retainers.

Orthodontic Treatment

The Artisan Smiles orthodontics team will begin your treatment with an initial consultation to gather the basic information needed to create an individualized treatment plan tailored to your goals and needs. Throughout your orthodontic experience, we will ensure you feel comfortable and confident. When your treatment is complete, you will have a smile that exceeds your expectations!

An Orthodontist can help to straighten crooked teeth, fix overbites, underbites, crossbites, close spaces between teeth, and alleviate overcrowding of teeth. Additional complexities and problems with the jaw can also be treated by an orthodontist.

Orthodontic appliances are made up of metal, ceramic, or plastic. They can be removable or rely on brackets bonded to the teeth. By adding a constant, gentle force in a controlled direction, orthodontic treatments slowly move teeth to the proper position.

Orthodontics treatments can vary in length from ten months to three years, depending on the patient’s age, treatment method, and the severity of the case. Additionally, the more involved and committed a patient is, the more efficient their treatment course is. Your doctor will discuss your options with you at your initial consultation.
