Why Choose Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry isn’t just about aesthetics. It helps patients improve their smiles, boost confidence, and enhance self-esteem, while also making daily life (like chewing or cleaning your teeth) easier.

At Winwood Dental, we provide comprehensive care and, in addition to cosmetic services, offer a range of other dental solutions. Teeth Whitening for a Brilliant Smile

Wave goodbye to coffee, wine, or age-related stains. Enjoy the convenience of professional whitening options, including ZOOM in-office treatments and take-home kits that you can use comfortably at home. A single whitening treatment can take years off your grin.

To maintain your whitening results, it’s important to follow daily care routines and meet the requirements for ongoing smile maintenance. Our practice provides professional-grade whitening products to help you achieve and keep your brightest smile.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are thin shells that transform cracked, chipped, or discolored teeth into something worthy of a close-up. Small imperfections can often be corrected with tooth-colored fillings before veneers are applied.

It is important to resolve any underlying dental issues, such as cavities or decay, before proceeding with veneers to ensure the best results. To apply veneers, your dentist will prepare the tooth surface, take impressions, and bond the custom-made veneer to your tooth.

How much does cosmetic dentistry cost?

It depends — whitening can last a year or more, while veneers and crowns can last 10-20 years with good care.
