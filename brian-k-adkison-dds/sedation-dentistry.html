Overcome Anxiety & Fear With Our Gentle Care

We provide dental sedation to help treatment patients feel calm and relaxed during any dental procedure while protecting your oral health. Our sedation dentistry options range from mild sedation to moderate sedation and deep sedation, depending on your needs.

Nitrous Oxide (Inhalation Sedation)

Also known as nitrous oxide inhalation, this form of anxiolysis sedation offers fast-acting, mild sedation. Patients typically recover quickly and can resume normal activities.

Oral Sedation / Oral Conscious Sedation

A convenient option for patients who prefer deeper relaxation. You’ll receive conscious sedation that allows you to stay responsive but deeply at ease throughout your procedure.

General Anesthesia & Anesthesia Services

For more complex cases or procedures like dental implants , an oral surgeon may recommend deep sedation or general anesthesia to ensure complete comfort.

Our goal is to deliver effective, comfortable care so you can receive dental treatments safely—without fear or stress, even during urgent visits like emergency dentistry .

Is Sedation Dentistry Safe?

Yes—sedation dentistry is a safe and extremely common technique used to make dental care more comfortable when performed by trained professionals. Whether you need a routine cleaning, root canal, or another dental procedure, we tailor sedation levels to your needs—from mild sedation for mild anxiety to moderate sedation or deep sedation for more complex care.

Sedatives administered may include nitrous oxide, the most frequently used method for mild sedation, or medication administered orally so you can remain awake and relaxed. For patients who receive deep sedation, we provide continuous monitoring to ensure safety throughout treatment.

Your safety comes first, with personalized care every step of the way.

FAQs

Can I eat before my sedation dentistry appointment?

It depends on the sedation type. For deeper sedation, you may need to avoid food for several hours. We’ll provide clear instructions beforehand.

Will sedation dentistry affect my current medications?

Some medications may interact with sedatives. We’ll review your medical history to ensure everything is safe.

How will I feel the next day after sedation?

Most patients feel normal by the next day, though mild drowsiness may last a few hours.

Is sedation dentistry suitable for children or older adults?

Yes, sedation can be safely adjusted based on age, health, and individual needs.

What if I’ve had a bad dental experience in the past?

Sedation dentistry helps create a calm, comfortable experience so you can feel more at ease moving forward.
