What is General Dentistry?

Maintaining a beautiful smile and optimal oral health is the primary goal of general dentistry. General dentists are responsible for managing routine checkups, examinations, and cleanings to ensure ongoing oral health.

We offer several services to achieve this, including dental cleanings and exams, gum disease treatment, preventive treatments like fluoride application and dental sealants, and custom-made mouthguards for athletes or teeth grinders. General dentists also develop customized dental hygiene plans for each patient.

Regular dental care is crucial in preserving the health and beauty of your smile. In addition, general dentists provide oral health education and personalized advice on hygiene techniques and dietary choices.

Enjoy a Brighter Smile With General Dentistry Treatments

It just makes good sense to take care of your smile. A healthy, beautiful grin can take you far in life. In fact, a recent survey found that 45 percent of people feel that a smile is a person’s most attractive feature, no matter their age.

That’s why we offer several types of general dentistry:

Dental Cleanings and Exams

Routine teeth cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup to help prevent tooth decay and gum disease. These visits also include a comprehensive evaluation to detect early signs of issues and support long-term oral health.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are used to restore damaged or weakened teeth by covering and protecting them after decay or injury. They improve both strength and appearance, often following treatments like root canals or large fillings.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are custom restorations designed to replace missing teeth by anchoring to surrounding teeth. They help restore your bite, maintain facial structure, and prevent remaining teeth from shifting out of place.

Dentures

Dentures provide a reliable solution for replacing multiple or all missing teeth, improving both function and aesthetics. They allow patients to eat, speak, and smile with confidence while supporting overall oral health.

Root Canals

Root canals treat infection deep inside the tooth, removing damaged tissue and sealing the area to prevent further problems. This procedure helps save your natural tooth and avoid the need for extraction while relieving pain and restoring function.

With a strong foundation in dental medicine from an accredited dental school, general dentists provide a wide range of dental procedures and collaborate with dental specialists and oral surgeons when advanced care such as tooth extraction is needed. FAQs

How often should I replace my toothbrush?

Replace your toothbrush every three to four months or sooner if the bristles are worn. This helps ensure effective cleaning.

Can diet affect my oral health?

Yes, sugary and acidic foods can lead to cavities, while a balanced diet supports strong teeth and gums.

What should I do if I have sensitive teeth?

Use toothpaste for sensitivity and avoid extreme temperatures. If it continues, visit your dentist.

Is it safe to use over-the-counter teeth whitening products?

They are generally safe when used properly, but professional treatments are more effective and customized.

What are the signs that I might be grinding my teeth?

Symptoms include jaw pain, headaches, and worn teeth. Your dentist can recommend treatment if needed.
