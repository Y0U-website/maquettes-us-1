How Do Dental Implants Work?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that bond with your jawbone — they’re like new tooth roots! Dental implants mimic the function and appearance of a natural tooth, providing a restoration that looks and feels natural. The placement of the implant is a precise procedure to ensure stability and support for the restoration.

Once healed, they hold your crown, bridge, or denture in place so it feels just like your natural teeth. The replacement teeth are securely attached to the implant, restoring full biting, chewing ability, and speaking confidence.

Dental implants are a long-term solution, typically lasting a lifetime, and can replace lost or missing teeth for comprehensive tooth replacement and improved aesthetics.
