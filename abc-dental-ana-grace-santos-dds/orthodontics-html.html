Orthodontics

Many people value the beauty of straight teeth. But did you know that straight teeth are also healthier? Overlapped, crooked, or rotated teeth create challenges for cleaning hard-to-reach areas. Guiding teeth into the proper alignment builds beautiful, healthy smiles that are easier to maintain.

Many adults have learned that straight teeth aren’t just for kids! Adults often value the confidence that comes with a warm smile, and they make dedicated orthodontic patients. Thanks to today’s technologies, it’s never too late to discuss the options for a bright, straight smile. If you’re not sure traditional braces are for you, don’t worry. Invisible methods can work extremely well in many cases, and Dr. Santos can help you explore the possibilities.

Orthodontics combines teeth straightening with proper jaw alignment and considers beauty and function. Identifying problems during a child’s growth years may lead to easier treatment, but it’s never too late to discuss ways to enhance any smile!

Invisalign®

A straight smile has never been easier to achieve. Clear plastic trays, known as aligners, can work the magic of braces without the metal look and feel of traditional braces. Combined with sophisticated 3D software, this technology brings a new smile within reach for most adults and mature teens.
