Why Two Offices?

Simply put - it was time for the orthodontics industry to move adults out of a pediatric setting.

The office needs of an adult are much different than that of a family with a child. So after years of planning, we opened our new office - which is essentially two offices in one location. One separate side for adults ; one separate side for children and teens.

Dr. M has designed our orthodontic offices to deliver maximum hospitality - hospitality that continues throughout the course of treatment, which allows both children and adults to feel comfortable in their environment.
