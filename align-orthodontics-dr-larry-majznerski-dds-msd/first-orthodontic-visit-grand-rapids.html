What To Expect: Your First Visit To Align Orthodontics

All About Your First Orthodontic Visit

Choosing the right orthodontic office can be a difficult decision, so we appreciate it when patients make Align Orthodontics their top choice. From the moment you enter, you’ll find a friendly, energized staff with plenty of smiles to go around.

Our office is unique in that it is divided into two separate practices, one for adults only and one for children and teenagers. Whether you’re here for your own treatment or for your child’s, you can expect quality service and details on what to expect throughout your orthodontic treatment.

An Office Optimized For You

We have separate practices for adults only and for children and teenagers, all under one roof!

Professional & Highly Educated Orthodontist

Dr. Majznerski is a graduate of the Mayo Clinic’s School of Medicine with more than 30 years of experience.

Thorough Communication

You’ll get a detailed treatment plan, whether it’s for orthodontics, sleep apnea and snoring issues or rebuilding damaged teeth.

Best Overall Smile Evaluation

You may have several options for orthodontic treatment. We’ll help you examine them all and determine what’s best for you.
