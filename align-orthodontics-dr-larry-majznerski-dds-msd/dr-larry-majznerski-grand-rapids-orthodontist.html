Dr. Majznerski

Providing exceptional orthodontic care for over 30 years.

Dr. Larry Majznerski has been providing West Michigan including Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Grandville, Jenison, Byron Center, and Hudsonville with unparalleled excellence in orthodontics for over 30 years.

Dr. Majznerski earned his undergraduate and doctoral degrees from the University of Michigan before completing a post-doctoral residency at the Sinai Hospital of Detroit and a post-doctoral master’s degree at the Mayo Clinic’s School of Medicine. Dr. Majznerski wants ALL patients to have an appropriate, comfortable environment to receive orthodontic care. Most orthodontic offices are designed for children and teens, Dr. Majznerski recognized it was time for the profession to shift the focus and consider comfort for all ages.
