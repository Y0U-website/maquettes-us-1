At Align Orthodontics, we have just as many adult patients as we do teens and children.

We recognize that adults generally want to be treated in a more private, comfortable, and refined environment. We appreciate that Dr. Majznerksi saw the need for two distinct treatment areas - one for adults, and one for kids and teens - becoming one of the first orthodontist offices in the nation to do so.

At Align Orthodontics, we believe that kids deserve respect. That’s why Dr. M and our exceptional orthodontics team treat our younger patients with kindness and consideration - making sure they feel heard and cared for.

We’re also passionate about parental involvement in your child’s orthodontic journey. Because of that, all parents are welcome in the treatment area - which helps keep kids comfortable and allows parents to observe and ask questions.
