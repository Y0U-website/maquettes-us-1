What Are Dental Implants?

The next best thing to natural teeth.

Dental implants are the gold standard in modern dentistry for tooth replacement. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants replace your entire tooth—from the root to the crown—restoring full function and a natural look.

When you replace one or more teeth with implants, you protect your jawbone from deterioration, support your gums, and achieve long-term oral health. Patients often find that dental implants are more comfortable and reliable than dentures, with fewer complications.

With implants, you’ll no longer worry about slipping dentures or avoiding certain foods. You’ll be able to laugh, eat, and smile with confidence again.

Our dental implant procedure in Durham, NC, involves two main steps:

  • Post placement —Drs. Turner, Butler, and Cole carefully place a titanium post into your jawbone. Over three to six months, the post fuses with the bone, providing ongoing stimulation like a natural tooth root.
  • Abutment and crown —Once healing is complete, we secure an abutment and attach a custom crown. Each crown is designed to match your surrounding teeth for a seamless, natural smile.
