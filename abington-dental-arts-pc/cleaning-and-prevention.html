Take the Fight to Dental Health Issues With Oral Screenings!

Abington Dental Arts will also help you take preventative measures for your dental health. We not only check your teeth, but we also scrutinize your gums and the underlying bone structures, and with each visit, we take one set of dental X-rays.

We also perform oral cancer screenings, identifying issues long before they would become apparent to you.
