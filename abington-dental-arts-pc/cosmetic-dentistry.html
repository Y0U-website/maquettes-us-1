What Exactly is Cosmetic Dentistry?

A great smile can give your self-confidence a huge boost. To the contrary, you can feel very self-conscious if your teeth are discolored, stained, chipped or broken, or misshapen.

The definition of cosmetic is “treatment to improve or restore a person’s appearance.” Cosmetic dentistry is simply restoring and improving the appearance of your teeth. It can provide you a positive change to your life with a brand-new smile, whether it is dental implants, porcelain veneers, or teeth whitening!

If you are ready for a change with cosmetic dentistry, our dentists at Abington Dental Arts can develop a treatment plan with you! There are many cosmetic dentistry avenues we can pursue with you to help you look and feel better:

Teeth Whitening: This procedure brightens and whitens your teeth from discoloration and staining.

Teeth Shaping: Our dentists can reshape a tooth by removing some of the enamel, usually without pain and with quick results.

Cosmetic Bonding: We will bond tooth-colored materials to your teeth to improve their appearance.
