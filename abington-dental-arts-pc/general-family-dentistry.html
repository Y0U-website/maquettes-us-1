We Apply Dental Sealants and Bonding

Our friendly, professional dentists at Abington Dental Arts can expertly handle your dental sealing and bonding needs.

Dental sealant is a thin coating that is applied to the chewing surfaces of your teeth to prevent decay. This sealant will fill the tooth’s grooves, forming a protective barrier over the enamel, sealing it from food and plaque.

As well, dental bonding happens when a tooth-colored resin is applied and hardened.

This bonds the resin to the tooth, which can restore or improve your smile. It will repair your decayed, chipped or cracked teeth; improve the appearance of your discolored teeth; close gaps between your teeth; change the shape of your teeth; and protect your tooth’s root if a receding gum problem has exposed it.

Trust Abington Dental Arts for all of your sealant and bonding needs.

Our Dental Staff Also Treats TMD

Temporomandibular disorder, or TMD, is a painful circumstance. Let Abington Dental Arts care for you with our TMD treatments.

The temporomandibular joint, or TMJ, acts like a hinge, connecting your skull and your jaw. TMD will cause you pain and discomfort in that joint and the surrounding muscles. Other symptoms include an aching pain in or around your ear, difficulty chewing, facial pain, and difficulty opening your mouth, due to the joint locking up. With these symptoms, there could also be a clicking or grating sound emanating from your jaw.

While the exact cause of your TMD may be difficult to diagnose, we can help you! Our treatments will give you some of the relief for which you are searching.
