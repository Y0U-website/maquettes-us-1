Chris Hudson, DDS

Dr. Chris Hudson has been working in the dental field for over two decades. Originally from Southwestern Louisiana, his family moved to the peninsula when he was a child and he grew up in the Soldotna area, graduating from Soldotna High School in 1991. He and his wife Kari, also from the Soldotna area, knew they wanted to return to the Kenai Peninsula after completing their education to start a dental practice and raise their family.

After receiving his Bachelor of Science degree in Chemical Engineering from the University of Southwestern Louisiana, he decided that he wanted a career in a more stable field where he wouldn’t be required to move every few years and pursued a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University. Upon graduation, he returned home to Alaska and in the fall of 2000 took over an existing dental practice, which has continued to grow and thrive.

Dr. Hudson is dedicated to providing the highest quality of conservative dental care to his patients and their families. He keeps his education current with continuing education opportunities in a variety of dental disciplines, is a member of the American Dental Association, and the Alaska Dental Society.

Dr. Hudson’s hobbies outside of work include participating in shooting sports, fishing, camping, and spending time with his family and friends.
