Meet Dr. Tara After more than 16 years of practicing dentistry, Dr. Tara is excited to bring her passion for caring for patients and their smiles to the new Century Farms development in Antioch, TN. Dr. Tara values integrity and respect and believes this is the key to building lasting relationships with patients and the community.

Dr. Tara received her B.S. in Biology from Clark Atlanta University and went on to complete her Doctor of Dental Surgery at Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry. Dr. Tara then went on to complete a General Practice Residency at the University of Michigan Hospital located in Ann Arbor, MI. She has also acquired membership in various dental associations and societies.

Dr. Tara has vast experience working in public health dentistry as well as private practice and her goal is to create a dental experience that makes every patient feel comfortable.

When Dr. Tara is not busy staying up-to-date on the most current practices in the field of dentistry, she enjoys spending quality time with her husband, two children and her Frenchie--Bella Rose.
