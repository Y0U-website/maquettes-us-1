Dental Implants

Dental implants are long-lasting and cost-effective.

Century Farms Dental follows a comprehensive, personalized approach to guarantee the success of dental implant treatments. This is a brief overview of the procedure:

Initial Consultation: During your first appointment, Dr. Tara will conduct a thorough exam, including digital radiographs and 3D images, to determine your oral health. She will also assess whether you’re a good candidate for dental implant surgery. We can create a customized treatment plan to meet your specific needs and goals during this consultation.

Implant placement: This step involves surgically implanting the dental implants into the jawbone. The procedure is done under local anesthesia for your comfort. Dr. Tara carefully positions the implant to get the best possible results.

Healing Period: The implant must integrate with the jawbone for at least several months after being placed. During this period, you will be fitted with temporary restorations to maintain your functionality and appearance.

Placement of the Abutment: After the implant has fused successfully with the bone, an abutment will be attached. The abutment acts as a connector between the implant and the final restoration.

Final Restoration : This is the final step, which involves placing the custom-made bridge, crown, or denture on the abutment. Dr. Tara ensures that your restoration is fitted perfectly and matches your natural color and shape, giving you a beautiful smile.

Century Farms Dental offers a wide range of dental implant options to suit your needs.

Single Tooth Implants are ideal for replacing one missing tooth. They consist of a single implant and a crown.

Multiple Tooth Implants : If a patient is missing multiple teeth, several implants can be used as a support for a partial denture or bridge.

Full arch implants: If you have lost all your teeth on one or both arches of the mouth, full arch implants are a great alternative to dentures. They provide stability and security.

Dental implants are suitable for most adults with good dental and general health. However, certain factors can affect your eligibility, including:

Bone Density: The implant needs sufficient bone density. In cases of severe bone loss, bone grafting is sometimes required to rebuild the bone structure.

Gum Health is essential to the success of dental implant placement. Any periodontal disease must be treated before implant placement.

Overall Health: Chronic diseases like diabetes and heart disease can impact healing and implant success. Dr. Tara reviews your medical history to determine whether you are a good candidate.

Dental implants are only as good as their care and maintenance. Here are some helpful tips for keeping your dental implants in great condition.

Good Oral Hygiene - Brush your teeth twice daily and floss every day to prevent plaque and gum disease. Protect your implants and the surrounding tissues with a non-abrasive toothbrush and toothpaste. Visit Century Farms Dental regularly for professional cleanings and check-ups. Dr. Tara monitors the health of your implant and will address any issues immediately.

Healthy Lifestyle- Limit your intake of acidic and sugary foods and beverages and avoid smoking. Healthy eating and a healthy lifestyle are important for the success of dental implants.
