Why Adult Orthodontics?

Orthodontics isn’t just for kids—teeth can be realigned at any age! At Clawson Orthodontics, we offer options tailored to adult needs, including discreet solutions like Spark clear aligners and LightForce braces, along with traditional metal braces for more comprehensive cases.

Benefits of Adult Orthodontics

At Clawson Orthodontics, we understand that adult patients have unique orthodontic needs. Here’s how orthodontic treatment can make a difference in your life:

01. Improved Oral Health:

Aligning crooked or misaligned teeth can enhance oral health by making it easier to clean your teeth effectively, reducing the risk of gum disease and tooth decay. Correcting bite issues can also alleviate jaw pain, headaches, and wear on teeth.

02. Enhanced Aesthetics and Confidence:

Whether you’re dealing with crowding, spacing, or bite issues, adult orthodontics can help you achieve a beautifully aligned smile. This can boost self-confidence and improve comfort in social and professional interactions.

03. Long-Term Dental Stability:

Orthodontic treatment aligns your teeth and bite, ensuring biting forces are distributed evenly. This reduces the likelihood of excessive wear, fractures, or other long-term dental issues.

04. More Discreet Treatment Options:

Many adults prefer subtle orthodontic solutions. At Clawson Orthodontics, we offer discreet options like Spark clear aligners and 3D-printed LightForce brackets for a seamless experience.

05. Increased Comfort and Convenience:

Modern orthodontic advancements prioritize patient comfort. From smooth, efficient brackets to digitally designed aligners, today’s orthodontic treatments fit easily into busy adult lifestyles.

06. A Lifetime Investment:

Adult orthodontics is an investment in your health and well-being. A straight, functional smile can provide lifelong benefits, improving your quality of life and confidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is orthodontic treatment effective for adults whose jaws have stopped growing?

Absolutely. While healthy bone structure is required, teeth can be safely aligned at any age. We routinely help adults get healthy, aligned smiles using highly aesthetic options like clear aligners or translucent LightForce custom braces .

How will orthodontic treatment impact my professional or social life?

Modern options are designed to be incredibly low-profile. Our Spark Aligners are virtually invisible in meetings and social settings, and they can be removed entirely for brief periods or special events.

Can adults get braces if they have missing teeth, crowns, or implants?

Yes. Teeth with crowns or fillings can be safely moved. While dental implants cannot move once placed, we can design your orthodontic blueprint to move the surrounding teeth around the implant perfectly.

I had braces as a teen but my teeth shifted. What are my options?

This is known as orthodontic relapse, and it is very common. For minor shifts, you may be an excellent candidate for our quick and affordable in-office custom aligners rather than undergoing full comprehensive treatment again.

Is adult orthodontic treatment more painful than treatment for kids?

No, the biological process of moving teeth is identical. While you will feel some mild tightness or pressure for a couple of days following adjustments, choosing advanced, customized systems like LightForce minimizes overall discomfort due to precision engineering.
