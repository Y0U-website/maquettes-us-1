Our Orthodontic Options in Clawson MI

We provide a variety of treatment options tailored for patients of all ages. Your orthodontic care will be customized to meet your specific needs and preferences.

Metal Braces

At Clawson Orthodontics, we use Pitts 21 brackets, which are self-ligating. These innovative brackets have built-in doors that securely hold the wires in place, ensuring effective and comfortable treatment.

LightForce Braces

LightForce braces are the first fully customized 3D-printed brackets. This technology enables us to design your treatment plan with your ideal smile as the focus from the very beginning.

Spark Clear Aligners

Our preferred clear aligner system is Spark. These aligners allow us to accomplish the majority of orthodontic treatments with a discreet and effective solution.

Custom Aligners

We also create custom aligners in-office, providing a cost-effective option for minor adjustments without requiring full comprehensive treatment.

Early Orthodontics

Early orthodontic care focuses on jaw development rather than the teeth. In most cases, we aim to avoid using braces during this phase unless absolutely necessary.

Adult Orthodontics

For adult patients, we offer a range of esthetic options, including Spark clear aligners and LightForce braces, alongside traditional metal braces to suit your lifestyle.

Orthodontic Emergencies

While we strive to make your orthodontic experience smooth, occasional mishaps may occur. Rest assured, we’re here to guide you through any unexpected challenges.
