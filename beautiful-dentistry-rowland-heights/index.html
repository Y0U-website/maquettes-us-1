Intra-oral scanners are devices for capturing impressions of the patient’s mouth. Digital optical impressions produce better accuracy and reduce patient discomfort by eliminating plastic molds. Our intra-oral scanner accurately captures impressions for crowns, dentures and implants for guided surgery as well as orthodontics for fabricating aligners and custom-made devices.
