Emergencies

Whether an accident happens during our normal business hours or not, know that you can call us and have your child treated promptly.

Call our office. If it is after hours or on the weekend, a staff member will get your message and promptly respond. We always have a doctor on call for emergencies whether or not Dr. Lambert is in town.

We schedule our days to allow for unexpected visits.

We also have a list of frequently asked questions that are helpful.
