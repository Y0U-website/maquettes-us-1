Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Orthodontic treatment with braces typically takes 12 to 24 months for teens, depending on the complexity of alignment and bite issues.

Are braces uncomfortable?

During your braces treatment, you may experience mild soreness or pressure after placement or adjustments, but this usually fades within a few days as teeth adjust.

How often do I need to visit the orthodontist?

You’ll need to visit our orthodontists every 5 to 8 weeks for adjustments and progress checks to keep treatment on track.

Can I still play sports with braces?

Yes. You can continue playing sports with braces. However, we recommend wearing a protective mouthguard to prevent injury.

What foods should I avoid with braces?

Ensure you avoid hard, sticky, or chewy foods, such as popcorn, gum, hard candy, and ice, as they can damage your braces and delay treatment.

Is it better to get braces from a dentist or an orthodontist?

Yes, it’s generally better to get braces from an orthodontist. Orthodontists receive specialized training focused specifically on tooth movement, bite correction, and braces treatment, allowing them to create more precise, effective, and long-lasting results.
