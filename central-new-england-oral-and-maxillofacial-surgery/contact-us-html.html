About Our Practice

Dr. Kashmanian is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon with more than two decades of experience. He is a highly regarded industry professional and a member of several professional dental organizations. He splits his time between his two private practices in Pomfret, CT, and Southbridge, MA.

Dr. Kashmanian specializes in performing maxillofacial surgery, oral surgery, TMJ surgery, wisdom tooth extraction, and facial trauma surgery. He also specializes in inserting dental implants, performing bone graft procedures, removing impacted canines, and other procedures . We perform anesthesiology services in-office to make our patients feel more comfortable during their procedures.

Our staff is fast and efficient, providing careful, personalized care to every patient. Our prices are competitive, and we accept many types of insurance. Prior to your appointment, please fill out the new patient paperwork and bring it with you to your appointment to expedite the registration process.
