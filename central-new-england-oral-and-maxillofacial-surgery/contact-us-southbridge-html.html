Families in Southbridge, MA in need of an oral surgeon have turned to Central New England Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery for years. We look forward to having you as a patient!

About Us

Our practice is led by Dr. John R. Kashmanian , D.M.D. , who splits his time between our Southbridge, MA and Pomfret, CT offices. The doctor, a graduate of Tufts Dental School and the Tufts and Boston University combined oral and maxillofacial surgery program, has more than two decades of experience in the industry.

In addition to oral and maxillofacial surgery , Dr. Kashmanian is an expert at wisdom tooth extraction, TMJ surgery, and facial trauma surgery. He also regularly performs other procedures , including dental implant insertion, impacted canine removal, and bone grafts. We perform anesthesiology services in-house to accommodate patients with dental anxiety.

Our friendly staff members provide each patient with excellent customer service and personalized care. We accept many forms of dental insurance and our procedures are designed to be competitively priced. Please fill out the new patient paperwork prior to your appointment to speed up the registration process.
