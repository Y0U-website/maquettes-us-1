About us

At Advanced Dental Arts, we understand the power of a beautiful smile. That’s why we’re committed to providing high-quality dental care in a warm, welcoming environment. From routine checkups to advanced treatments, we offer a full range of dental services to meet all your family’s needs under one roof.

Our mission is to ensure every visit leaves you with a positive and memorable experience. Our compassionate team goes above and beyond to make you feel comfortable and at home from the moment you walk through our doors. Ready to brighten and enhance your smile? Contact us today at (816) 452-1888 to schedule your appointment!

Geoffrey Riley, DDS As a member of the American Dental Association , Missouri Dental Association , and Greater Kansas City Dental Society , Dr. Riley is committed to staying current in the field. Dr. Riley is dedicated to providing high-quality, comprehensive dental care in a comfortable setting for all patients.

Dr. Riley graduated in 2007 from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry at Lincoln. He also completed one year of advanced training in a general dentistry residency at the University of Missouri in Kansas City. He has been a part of the Kansas City community as a dentist since then and has owned his practice since 2011.

Dr. Riley is a member of the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Society and the Greater Kansas City Dental Society. He is also a faculty club member of Spear Education. He frequently travels to Phoenix during the year for continuing education and is also active in the local Spear Study Club.

He strives to provide comprehensive high-quality dentistry in a comfortable environment.

Dr. Riley recently married his wife Sara, who is a counselor who works with children on the autism spectrum. In his spare time, Dr. Riley enjoys attending sporting events, traveling and catching up with friends.
