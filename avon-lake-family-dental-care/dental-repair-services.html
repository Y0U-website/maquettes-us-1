Dental bonding is a versatile solution for repairing minor chips, cracks, or discoloration. This procedure involves applying a tooth-colored resin that is carefully shaped and hardened to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. Bonding is quick, minimally invasive, and preserves the majority of your tooth structure, making it an excellent option for enhancing both aesthetics and function.

Crowns and Bridgework for Extensive Repair

Black tooth icon on a white background. Black tooth icon on white background. Black tooth on a white background. Black tooth illustration. When a tooth has suffered significant damage or when one or more teeth are missing, crowns and bridgework offer a reliable solution. Crowns encase and protect damaged teeth, while bridges fill the gaps created by missing teeth. Both options restore chewing function, improve aesthetics, and maintain the alignment of surrounding teeth, ensuring your smile stays balanced and functional.

Black tooth silhouette. For patients missing one or more teeth, dental implants offer the most durable and natural-looking solution. Implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they act as a sturdy foundation for crowns or bridges. This approach not only restores your smile but also preserves jawbone integrity and prevents the shifting of surrounding teeth, providing both aesthetic and functional benefits.
