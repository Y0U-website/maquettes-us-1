What Can be Done About a Gummy Smile?

For many of our patients, an injection of Botox can address a gummy smile and make it far less noticeable. It works because Botox temporarily freezes the muscles that control your upper lip, reducing how much of it is exposed when you smile. This simple and quick procedure can be done in just a few minutes, and the results typically last several months.

In more severe cases of a gummy smile, Dr. Larsen may recommend crown lengthening or gum contouring to reshape your gums and make them less prominent.
