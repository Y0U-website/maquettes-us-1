Dental Implants

The most permanent, natural-looking solution for missing teeth — built to last a lifetime.

Replace Your Tooth. Keep Your Life.

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants integrate directly with your jawbone — preserving bone, protecting neighboring teeth, and giving you a restoration that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.

Dr. Vasu uses 3D cone beam CT imaging for precise, guided implant placement — reducing risk and improving outcomes significantly compared to conventional techniques.

What to Expect

3D Consultation

We take a full 3D CBCT scan to evaluate your bone density and map the exact placement for optimal results.

Implant Placement

A small titanium post is surgically placed in the jawbone under local anesthetic. Most patients report minimal discomfort.

Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your bone — creating an incredibly strong, natural foundation.

Crown Placement

A custom-crafted crown is attached — indistinguishable from your natural teeth in color, shape, and function.

Why Choose Implants?

Bridges and dentures are effective options, but implants are the only restoration that replaces the tooth root — preventing bone loss and preserving your facial structure over time.

Unlike bridges or dentures, implants stimulate bone growth, preventing the facial shrinkage that occurs with tooth loss.

Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy teeth on either side. Implants stand completely on their own.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime — making them the most cost-effective long-term solution.

Implants don’t slip or shift. Enjoy any food you love without restriction or embarrassment.
