The Clear Path to a Straighter Smile

As a certified Invisalign provider, Dr. Vasu uses the latest digital planning technology to design a clear aligner system custom-built for your teeth. Each set of aligners gradually and gently shifts your teeth toward their ideal position — with no metal, no irritation, and no interruptions to your lifestyle.

Whether you have crowding, spacing, or a bite issue, Invisalign can often achieve results comparable to traditional braces — in about the same time or less.

Simple. Comfortable. Effective.

3D Digital Scan

We take a precise digital impression of your teeth — no messy molds. You see your projected smile outcome on-screen before committing to treatment.

Custom Aligners

Your series of custom aligners arrives within a few weeks. Wear each set for 1–2 weeks, removing them only to eat and clean your teeth.

Progress Check-ins

We monitor your progress at regular appointments, making adjustments as needed. Most treatments are complete in 12–18 months.
