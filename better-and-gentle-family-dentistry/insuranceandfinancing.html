0 Insurance and Financing We accept most major insurances. We strive to make quality dental care accessible and affordable for our patients. That’s why we accept many major insurance plans and will assist you in maximizing your benefits. Our knowledgeable team is happy to verify your coverage, file claims on your behalf, and answer any questions about your plan. If you have any concerns about insurance or payment options, we’re here to help make your visit stress-free!

No insurance? Don’t worry. We offer an affordable in-house membership plan to help you get the quality dental care you need at a predictable cost. Our plan includes routine cleanings, exams, and discounts on treatment—all for a low annual fee, with no deductibles, waiting periods, or hidden costs. It’s a great alternative to traditional insurance, providing simple, stress-free savings for you and your family.

Dental Financing We understand that dental care is an important investment in your health, which is why we offer flexible financing options through CareCredit and Cherry. These payment plans allow you to get the treatment you need now and pay over time with low monthly payments and little to no interest options. Whether it’s a routine visit or a major procedure, CareCredit and Cherry make it easier to fit dental care into your budget.
