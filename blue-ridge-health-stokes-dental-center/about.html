How it Started

Blue Ridge Health is not just a health center. It’s a community health center with roots in compassionate care for ALL of our region’s most vulnerable populations.

1963

Blue Ridge Health was founded by Claire Hayden Burson, a public health nurse with a vision of eliminating health disparities for the migrant farmworkers of Western North Carolina . Blue Ridge Health is among the first of the migrant health centers opened in the US during this time.

1987

Did you know that Blue Ridge Health was originally named The Migrant Health Center? Over time, we expanded to respond to the needs of a more diverse group of patients and rebranded ourselves as Blue Ridge Community Health Services, a year-round health center with an aim to serve the entire community.

1992

We opened our first pediatric practice. Today, we operate Rainbow Pediatrics in Hendersonville, and are the home site of Dr. Lisa Abraham, our region’s only pediatric neurologist. We also offer pediatrics as part of our comprehensive services in clinics throughout the western part of the state.

1993

Blue Ridge Health opened our first school-based health center in Henderson County. School-based health centers offer comprehensive healthcare under one roof, including medical, behavioral health, nutritional services, and more. Today, we operate school-based health locations all across Western North Carolina, helping to break down barriers to equitable child healthcare, including the need for parents to take time off of work to attend appointments and the need for families to find transportation to and from clinics.

2013

We partnered with MAHEC to become a Teaching Health Center. Learn more about our Residency Program and the ways our residents serve the people of Western North Carolina.

2021

Meridian Behavioral Health Services was integrated into Blue Ridge Health, allowing us to reach more patients than ever before with judgment-free support for issues relating to mental illness, substance use, and more. Our belief that recovery is possible for EVERYONE helps us break past the fear and shame that keep people from seeking help.

2023 Beyond

With a vision to the future, our leadership team is always striving to respond to what is happening in our region as it happens. That’s the benefit of a community health center. With boots on the ground, living right where we serve, we can easily pivot to meet the ever-changing needs of our patients. In 2023, we will have served our patients for 60 years, and we look forward to continuing to improve the health of our region for many, many more.

FQHC Status Information

Blue Ridge Health is honored to serve as your Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC).

This means that we are a non-profit, community-based health organization. FQHCs like Blue Ridge Health provide comprehensive primary care, as well as dental and mental health services to patients right in the community.

Our board members are also our patients.

FQHC guidelines require over half of Blue Ridge Health’s Board of Directors to be actual Blue Ridge Health patients. This means that our organization and the care it provides to WNC is directly impacted by real patients and members of our communities.

We support high-quality healthcare services to all.

As an FQHC, Blue Ridge Health offers the same high-quality healthcare services to the uninsured and underinsured populations of Western North Carolinas it offers to those with insurance. Blue Ridge Health prides itself in providing accessible, affordable healthcare for all.

How does it work?

FQHCs are not free clinics, but they do provide services to all people regardless of their ability to pay. Sliding-fee scales are available for all service types, including medical, dental, and more. Patients will be qualified for discount services based on family size and documented income.
