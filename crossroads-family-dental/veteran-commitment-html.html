Veteran Commitment | Crossroads Family Dental, Schererville IN

We are proud to provide care for those who served our country and defended our freedom.

Each year around Veterans Day, our office teams up with other local dental professionals to provide free dental care to U.S. Veterans.

Annual Free Dental Care Day for U.S. Veterans

As a veteran-owned dental office, we are proud to support our community and our country. We offer same-day crowns created in-office and partner with trusted local U.S. labs whenever possible.

We are proud to support our service men and women during our annual Free Dental Care Day for U.S. Veterans. Each year around Veterans Day, our office teams up with other local dental professionals to give back to those who gave so much.

Veteran-owned. Community-rooted. Honored to serve those who served.
