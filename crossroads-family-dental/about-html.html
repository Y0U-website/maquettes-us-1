  • Continuing education since 2010 Spear, CDOCS, Academy of General Dentistry. Hundreds of hours. Not for the resume for the best in dentistry when your teeth are the ones needing treatment.

Patient-led, in three parts.

First, we take a photographic tour of your mouth. You see what the diagnosis is based on, on your own teeth, before we describe what’s going on.

Then we present tiered options. There isn’t one recommendation you have to accept or refuse. There are paths and even the most conservative one moves your oral health forward.

Then it’s your turn. If you decline a treatment, we’ll explain the risks honestly and we’ll respect the decision. The decision belongs to you.
