The practice

At Cranston Orthodontics in Cranston, RI, our mission is to provide our patients with the highest quality of treatment in the most warm, caring, and fun environment possible.

Dr. Cosmo Haralambidis and our talented, experienced staff strive to provide state-of-the-art orthodontic service in a comfortable and family-friendly environment. We are dedicated to rewarding each of our patients with the lifelong gift of a beautiful and healthy smile, which leads to improved self-esteem and makes a positive difference in their lives.

Using precision electronic nerve stimulation, the device intercepts pain signals before they’re perceived, eliminating discomfort throughout your orthodontic treatment with no injections, no numbing agents, and no recovery time. Whether you’re anxious about dental visits or simply want the most comfortable experience possible, this changes everything.

We monitor jaw and facial growth to guide treatment at the optimal time for lasting results.

Healthy breathing is essential. We assess airway function to support better sleep and overall wellness.

We evaluate tongue posture, swallowing patterns, and speech to address functional concerns that impact orthodontic outcomes

When needed, we work alongside sleep physicians and ENT specialists to ensure a fully integrated, multidisciplinary approach—especially for growing children.

Dr. Cosmo is a recognized leader who actively pursues the latest advancements in the field. Our patients benefit from techniques and technologies that most practices have yet to adopt — giving you a genuine edge in care.
