Our Services

CLEANING AND PREVENTION

Most patients are recommended biannual cleanings and examinations. Some patients who suffer from periodontal disease may need more frequent maintenance to prevent bone loss and inflammation in the gums. Radiographs are recommended once a year to help examine between the teeth and under the gums to detect the start of problems.

Crowns, Bridges, and Veneers

If you have a damaged or decayed tooth, we can help you choose the best solution. Crowns help to protect weakened teeth by covering the tooth in question. Bridges help to replace missing teeth and veneers are used to address cosmetic concerns.

dentures

Missing teeth can make your cheeks look hollow and your mouth look sunken. More importantly, your diet can suffer and you might be reluctant to talk or smile with those around you. Those missing multiple teeth in a row may want to consider dentures for not just cosmetic purposes but also overall wellness. Depending on the amount of teeth missing, we may suggest partial or full dentures.

EXTRACTIONS

Our dentists make every effort to preserve your natural teeth. However, extractions are necessary when decay has made the tooth unsalvageable or when you have an advanced periodontal disease. When a tooth is malformed, damaged, impacted or ingrown, different procedures are used, but all extractions are considered surgery. Depending on which tooth is removed, we can offer you a replacement in the form of a dental implant or oral prosthetic.

fillings

Used to prevent the spread of tooth decay or correct certain cosmetic damage, a filling is one of the ways our dentists preserve your health.

NIGHTGUARDS

Excessive jaw clenching and bruxism can accelerate tooth and jaw deterioration; it can impact the quality of your own sleep and that of those around you.

Whitening

Our in-office whitening service offers a fast, safe, and effective way to brighten your smile. Using professional-grade whitening agents and advanced techniques, we can lighten your teeth in just one visit. Whether you’re preparing for a special occasion or simply want to enhance your smile, our in-office whitening delivers immediate results giving you the confidence to smile brightly.
