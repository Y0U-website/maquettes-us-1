Dr. Steven Chavez

Meet Your Manhattan Family Dentist, Steven Chavez

Welcome to Blue Line Dental, where Dr. Steven Chavez and his team are dedicated to providing conscientious and exceptional care to patients of all ages in the Midtown East neighborhood of New York City. With his warm and approachable nature, Dr. Chavez is committed to exceeding patient expectations, ensuring that every visit leaves you and your family smiling.

A Lifetime of Commitment to Oral Health

Born and raised in Miami, Florida, Dr. Steven Chavez has always been passionate about oral health. His journey began in high school when his father encouraged him to apply for a job at a local orthodontist’s office. Throughout his undergraduate years at Florida International University, Dr. Chavez continued to grow his commitment and education in oral health by working part-time at the orthodontist’s office and becoming certified as an orthodontic assistant.

During this time, Dr. Chavez realized dentistry was his true calling. He pursued his dream by attending the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. With a thirst for knowledge, Dr. Chavez remains enthusiastic about continuing education and keeping up with the latest trends in the field to provide his patients with the most comprehensive care possible.

A Global Impact and Local Dedication

Dr. Chavez’s passion for making a positive impact extends beyond his dental practice. He has participated in various volunteer opportunities overseas, providing vital oral health support to underserved refugees and community members in Kathmandu, Nepal, and Managua, Nicaragua. Closer to home, Dr. Chavez has lent his expertise to Survivors of Torture, a nonprofit group that offers comprehensive dental treatment to international torture survivors. He has also contributed to the Odyssey House Community Outreach Program, providing dental care for community members dealing with substance abuse.

Bringing Family Back to NYC Family Dentistry

Dr. Chavez not only runs his dental practice in Midtown East, but he also lives in the neighborhood. As a proud resident, Dr. Chavez and his wife are raising their family here, embodying the idea of a lifetime family dentist for community members.

In a time when families may find themselves bouncing between different dental practices, Dr. Chavez aims to revive the concept of having one trusted dentist for the whole family. He understands the importance of building lasting relationships and providing continuity of care.

Comprehensive Care for Every Age Group

At Blue Line Dental, Dr. Chavez offers a wide range of treatments to address the unique needs of both adults and children. From veneers, dental crowns, and bridges to root canals and fillings, our office can give you the care you need, no matter the age or stage of your dental health journey.

Beyond Dentistry

When Dr. Chavez is not busy caring for his patients, you can find him pursuing his passions outside of the dental office. He enjoys mixed martial arts, rock climbing, paddleboarding, and, most importantly, spending quality time with his wife and two children.
