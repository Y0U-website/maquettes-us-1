Implant-Supported Bridges Replace Multiple Teeth

Missing two or three adjacent teeth? An implant-supported bridge uses two implants as anchors to support a multi-tooth restoration without relying on natural teeth for support. It’s more stable than a traditional bridge and preserves the bone beneath the missing teeth.

This is a popular option for patients from Waynesboro and Staunton who’ve lost several teeth in one area. The bridge is custom-fabricated to match your existing teeth in color, shape, and size so the result looks completely natural.

  • Replaces 2-4 consecutive missing teeth
  • Anchored by implants no natural teeth ground down
  • Preserves bone beneath all missing teeth
  • More stable and comfortable than traditional bridges
  • Custom-matched to your natural tooth appearance

The Implant Process Step by Step

We know the process can sound intimidating. Here’s exactly what to expect, from your first call to your final crown.

Consultation & Planning

We review your dental history, take 3D scans, and create a personalized treatment plan. We’ll walk you through every option and answer every question.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is.

Osseointegration

Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone a process called osseointegration. This creates the permanent anchor for your crown.

Abutment & Crown

Once integrated, we attach an abutment and place your custom-made crown matched precisely to your natural teeth in color, shape, and size.

Ongoing Care

Regular checkups and good oral hygiene are all it takes to keep your implant healthy for decades. We’ll guide you through the maintenance routine.
