The practice

Ready for a transformation? Call or text us today to schedule a complimentary new patient exam and take the first step towards the smile you’ve always dreamed of.

A confident smile is more than straight teeth. At Aspen Orthodontics, board-certified orthodontists plan treatment around how your smile looks, how it functions, and how it fits your face—for kids, teens, and adults.

We evaluate jaw relationships and facial proportions so your result supports a natural profile—not just a row of straightened teeth.

Crowding, spacing, and rotations are corrected so teeth meet cleanly, look even, and are easier to keep healthy.

Where appropriate, we develop a fuller smile corridor so teeth fill the smile gracefully from corner to corner—without an aged or hollow look.

Upper tooth edges are planned to follow the lower lip curve for a lively, personalized smile line—not a flat, one-size finish.

We aim for a natural amount of tooth and gum showing when you smile—confident and authentic, without a “gummy” or under-displayed look when we can help.

Upper and lower midlines are guided toward facial center so your smile feels balanced with your features.
