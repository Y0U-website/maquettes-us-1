Ensuring the Health and Safety of Our Patients and Staff

To prioritize the well-being of everyone in our office, we have implemented these protocols. These measures are designed to minimize risk and provide a safe environment for both patients and staff. We kindly ask for your cooperation in following these guidelines to help maintain a safe and comfortable experience for all.

Before Your Appointment Arrive 15 minutes early for check-in.

Complete and submit your online forms 24 hours before your appointment.

Please confirm your appointment by text or call at least 24 hours prior to avoid the possibility of cancellation.

Upon Arrival Please have your current photo ID, insurance card and the referral letter ready if you are referred to our office.

Only the patient should come to the appointment; if necessary, one legal guardian and/or translator may accompany them.

During Your Appointment Staff will wear additional PPE.

HEPA filters have been added to improve air circulation.

During treatment, we use the ADS Extraoral Dental Suction System to reduce aerosol exposure.

The office is equipped with an OSOPURE air disinfecting purifier for continuous contaminant removal.

Creating a Calm and Focused Treatment Environment

To provide the safest and most focused care possible, we kindly ask that only the patient enters the treatment room during procedures. Our team is trained to help your child feel comfortable and supported at every step. For parents who wish to stay close by, you are welcome to relax in our reception area—we will keep you informed and involved as needed. Thank you for understanding and helping us create a calm and effective treatment environment for all our patients.
