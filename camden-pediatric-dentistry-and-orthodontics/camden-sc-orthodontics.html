Orthodontics FAQ

The average treatment time is approximately 18 to 20 months, but it can range from 6 to 30 months depending on individual needs

Common options include traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners (such as Invisalign).

Phase I (interceptive treatment) occurs early, while baby teeth are still present. Phase II is the full, comprehensive treatment that follows later.

Often, no referral is needed; you can schedule a consultation directly. However, some insurance plans might require one.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial evaluation by age 7. At this age, most children have a mix of baby and adult teeth, allowing us to spot potential issues early while the jaw is still growing.

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of the case, but most children wear braces or aligners for 18 to 24 months. We will provide a more specific estimate during your initial consultation after reviewing your child’s records.

Your child may feel some minor soreness for a few days after their braces are first placed or adjusted, but this is temporary and manageable. Modern wires and brackets are designed to be much more comfortable and lower-profile than they were in the past.

The cost depends on the length and type of treatment required, and we offer flexible payment plans to fit your family’s budget. Many dental insurance plans include orthodontic coverage, and our team will help you maximize your benefits.
