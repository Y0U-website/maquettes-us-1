About the Practice

At Camden Pediatric Dentistry and Orthodontics, our board-certified pediatric dentists are committed to providing exceptional, personalized care tailored to your child’s unique needs as they grow.

We offer a comprehensive range of services, including pediatric dentistry and orthodontics, along with gentle, child-friendly sedation options to help ensure your little one feels relaxed and secure in the dental chair. You can trust us to value your time and give our full attention to your child, creating a positive and comfortable experience during every visit.

Don’t wait call us today to schedule an appointment and invest in your child’s dental health!

Dr. Varsha Kapoor, DMD

Dr. Varsha Kapoor believes every child deserves to feel safe, comfortable, and maybe even a little proud when they leave the dental chair. Her approach is simple: treat every patient the way she would want her own children treated, with patience, genuine care, and plenty of high-fives.

Dr. Kapoor has a natural gift for connecting with kids, especially those who come in nervous. With specialized training in behavior management and pediatric dentistry, she’s skilled at turning anxious first visits into moments of courage and laughter. She takes particular interest in children with complex behavioral needs, and parents consistently note how quickly she puts even the most hesitant kids at ease.

She earned her Master’s degree in Biomedical Science from the University of South Carolina School of Medicine, her DMD from MUSC, and completed her pediatric residency at East Carolina University. Her clinical focus centers on preventive care and minimally invasive treatment tailored to each child’s needs.

Outside the office, Dr. Kapoor spends her time with her husband and two kids, Kendall and Carter, who she says are her best reminder of why this work matters. She enjoys traveling, experimenting in the kitchen, and making the most of family time.

She looks forward to meeting your family and making every visit something your child actually wants to come back for.

Dr. Kent T. Washington

Dr. Kent T. Washington has lived in Columbia since 1993. He is a graduate of Spring Valley High School and earned his undergraduate degree at The Citadel, The Military College of South Carolina, in 2005.

Following graduation, he moved to Washington, D.C. to attend Howard University College of Dentistry and finished in 2010. After graduating from dental school with honors — Omicron Kappa Upsilon — he pursued his orthodontic residency program, also at Howard. During residency training, he conducted extensive research work in the Department of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, some of which served as the focus of his thesis.
