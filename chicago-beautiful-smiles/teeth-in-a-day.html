The Teeth in a Day Process

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation and digital planning appointment. Using 3D imaging and precise measurements, we design your implant placement and new smile before treatment day.

On the day of your procedure, any remaining failing teeth are gently removed, implants are placed, and a custom temporary bridge is secured—all in one visit. You’ll leave our office with a full smile that looks natural and feels secure.

After a healing period, your final custom restoration is placed. This permanent set of teeth is designed for durability, comfort, and aesthetics, completing your transformation.

Caring for Your Teeth in a Day Implants

Proper care is essential to ensure your implants last for decades. The good news is that caring for Teeth in a Day implants is similar to caring for natural teeth.

Daily brushing and flossing help keep your gums healthy and protect the implant foundation. Regular dental visits allow our team to monitor healing and ensure your bite remains balanced.

We also recommend avoiding very hard or sticky foods during the initial healing phase and wearing a nightguard if you grind your teeth. With good care and routine maintenance, Teeth in a Day implants can last many years—often a lifetime.
