The practice

Every case is hand-designed and custom-fitted for your patient by Peter. He will make sure you get the service you need from full-mouth restorations to a single crown, all made with quality, care and attention to detail. All work is guaranteed for 3 years.

With over 40 years of experience in all phases of dental ceramics from IPS e .max® and zirconia all porcelain ceramics, porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns, implants and veneers, Peter of Colonial Crown Dental Lab provides the expertise and skill necessary to excel in today’s dental ceramics industry.

Colonial Crown Dental wants to ensure that every case you send to our team is streamlined and stress-free. Our online case submission tool makes sending both traditional and digital impressions easy. Sending your case to our lab takes just a couple of minutes because everything you need is compiled in one convenient location.
