Our Story

For years, this practice has served as a cornerstone of the Auburn community. Known as C-K Family Dental, it was built on the kind-hearted care and dedication of Dr. Theresa Casper-Klock . As she prepares for her next chapter, we are honored to carry that torch forward as Auburn Modern Dentistry , preserving the warm, "down-to-earth" environment that generations of families have come to trust.

This new chapter is led by Dr. Krishna , who is deeply honored to work alongside the same "rockstar" team of hygienists, assistants, and office staff you have known for years. By combining the legendary compassion of the past with the advanced tools of modern dentistry, we are committed to providing thorough, high-end care in a place that still feels like home. We aren t just your dental office; we are your neighbors, and Dr. Krishna looks forward to sharing a smile with you soon.

Devoted to Your Comfort

Our skilled team combines years of local experience with a modern commitment to your well-being and peace of mind.

Swati Krishna Kulkarni, DMD

Dr. Swati Krishna Kulkarni affectionately known as Dr. Krishna brings over eight years of clinical expertise to the Auburn community. She holds degrees from Pennsylvania State University, Rutgers University, and the University of New England, complemented by a year of advanced hospital training at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital. Honored to carry forward the legacy of compassionate care established by Dr. Casper-Klock, Dr. Krishna remains deeply dedicated to a thorough, gentle approach for every patient she serves.

Your Comfort is Our Standard

At Auburn Modern Dentistry, we believe high-end care should be a seamless experience. From our streamlined digital workflows to our attentive, gentle team, every detail of our practice is curated to ensure you feel respected and at ease from the moment you arrive.

The Modern Advantage

Auburn Modern Dentistry integrates the latest clinical advancements to provide precise, efficient, and comfortable care. By investing in high-end technology such as digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and same-day restorations Dr. Krishna ensures more accurate diagnoses and significantly reduced treatment times. We are committed to remaining at the forefront of dental innovation to provide our Auburn community with the highest standard of modern care.
