The Full Arch Implant Process

A full arch implant replaces your complete arch of missing teeth with a dental bridge supported by your implants.

PHASE 1: Implant Placement

  • Strategic Design: 3D imaging and virtual surgical planning enable dental implants to be strategically placed in the jaw.
  • Reduced Surgery: The unique tilting of the implants maximizes the use of available bone, ensuring greater stability and reducing the need for bone grafting.

PHASE 2: IMMEDIATE FUNCTION

  • Same-Day Teeth: Typically, a temporary set of fixed teeth can be placed on the same day of surgery. This allows you to get back to everyday life right away.
  • Healing: After a short healing period, your restorative dentist will place the final, non-removable bridge.

BENEFITS OF FINAL RESTORATION

The final bridge is a secure, fixed solution that:

  • Restores your ability to chew and enjoy a wide variety of foods.
  • Is significantly more stable and comfortable than unsecured dentures.
  • Is supported by a scientifically proven method.
