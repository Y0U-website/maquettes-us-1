Matthew Nondorf, DDS, DHSc

Dr. Nondorf believes in lifelong learning. He attends multiple continuing education events and is a member of the Excellence in Orthodontics Study Club and president of the Dental Excellence in Northwest Indiana Study Club.

These activities enable Dr. Nondorf to stay current on the latest techniques in orthodontics, including the use of Invisalign and laser dentistry. By remaining current on developments in the field, he ensures his patients receive the best possible care.

  • Doctorate of Dental Surgery Indiana University School of Dentistry
  • Awarded the Maynard K. Hine award, given to the dental student who displays the highest level of professionalism as voted by peers
  • Doctorate of Health Sciences Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health
  • Orthodontics certificate, chief resident Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health

Personal Life

Dr. Nondorf was raised in Valpariso and attended Valparaiso High School where he met his wife, Jamie. They live in Valparaiso with their six children: Ella, Anna, William, Jack, Ben, and Emmy. When not practicing orthodontics, Dr. Nondorf enjoys exercising, reading, helping coach at his kids events, and being roadie to his family rock band.
