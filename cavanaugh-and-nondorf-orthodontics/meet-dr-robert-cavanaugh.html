Robert Cavanaugh, DDS

Dr. Cavanaugh is committed to providing the highest level of care to each of his patients. As part of that commitment, he completes many hours of continuing orthodontic education each year. By attending conferences, seminars, and lectures, he remains current on new technologies and treatments. Dr. Cavanaugh is also an active member of the Dental Excellence in Northwest Indiana Study Club.

Personal Life

Dr. Cavanaugh and his wife Heather live in Valparaiso with their five children: Samantha, Matthew, Tommy, Rebecca, and Brady. He is active in his community as a member of the Valparaiso Rotary Club and the Chamber of Commerce and enjoys coaching his kids’ youth sports teams, playing basketball, and flying.
