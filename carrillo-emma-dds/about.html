McColl Dental Center

McColl Dental Center is an Edinburg based family dental practice that treats patients of all ages. We are committed to delivering the highest quality dental care and do so using advanced dental equipment. We are a general dental practice that offers services like checkups, teeth cleaning, crowns, veneers and more.
