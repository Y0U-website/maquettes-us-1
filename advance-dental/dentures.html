What Are Dentures?

Dentures are artificial teeth attached to an acrylic base that’s colored to look like your natural gums. They’re removable, which allows for easy cleaning and maintenance. When fitted properly, dentures allow you to speak and eat just like you would with your natural teeth.

There are two types of dentures: partial and full. Partial dentures are used when you still have some existing permanent teeth and would like to replace a section of missing teeth. Full dentures are used when you are missing all of your teeth on the upper or lower jaw.

Our dentures are custom-made to fit your mouth, and after a brief adjustment period they’ll feel just like your natural smile. In addition to dentures , we offer dental implants and bridges as tooth replacement options. Our dentists can help you determine the best solution for your missing teeth.

Caring for Your Dentures

Dentures must be brushed just like your natural teeth in order to avoid bacteria and food buildup, which causes odor and staining. You’ll also need to brush your gums and tongue once your dentures are removed in order to maintain your oral hygiene.

At night, your dentures must be soaked in water or a cleansing solution in order to prevent cracking. Be sure the water isn’t hot, though, as that can cause them to warp!
