Porcelain Veneers

If you’re self-conscious about your teeth, porcelain veneers might be right for you. Veneers are an affordable, effective cosmetic dentistry option for those looking to improve their smile. Whether you have a discolored tooth that resists whitening or a tooth that’s been chipped, porcelain veneers can make your teeth look just like new.

What Are Veneers?

A veneer is a thin porcelain shell that is bonded to the front of a damaged tooth to conceal imperfections and improve appearance. Veneers are custom-made to fit your mouth, which means they’re color-matched to your surrounding teeth and blend in perfectly with your existing smile.

Porcelain veneers are stain-resistant and durable, which means they can last a long time. However, you should avoid putting too much stress on the affected tooth, as just like a normal tooth, veneers can crack under the right circumstances.

What Can Veneers Do?

While veneers may not be right for everyone, they’re a very versatile solution that can tackle a variety of dental issues.

Here are just a few things that porcelain veneers can correct:

With proper care, your porcelain veneers can last for over a decade without needing to be repaired or replaced.

  • Chipped or cracked teeth
  • Uneven teeth
  • Short teeth
  • Slight gaps between teeth
  • Stained or discolored teeth
