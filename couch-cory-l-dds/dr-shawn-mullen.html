Dr. Shawn Mullen

0 Shawn Mullen, DMD Dr. Shawn Mullen, is a proud Florida native hailing from the charming town of Land O’ Lakes. Dr. Mullen’s passion for science and medicine led him on an academic journey, culminating in a Biomedical Science degree from the University of South Florida. Eager to delve deeper into the field, he pursued further education, obtaining a Masters of Medical Science and a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the esteemed Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine (LECOM).

Settling in Tallahassee with his beloved wife, Cami, Dr. Mullen finds joy in the vibrant community and abundant outdoor opportunities it offers. When he’s not crafting beautiful smiles, you can find Dr. Mullen casting his line into Florida’s picturesque waters, pursuing game amidst the state’s lush landscapes, or simply relishing nature’s wonders with his cherished family and friends.
