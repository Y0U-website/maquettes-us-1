The practice

Testerman Dental is proud to be your local, family-friendly dental office. We care for families from Lebanon, OH, and the surrounding communities of Mason, Maineville, Waynesville, Middletown, Morrow, and all of Warren County.

Our skilled, compassionate team manages everything from regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to surgical implant placement, TMJ dysfunction, pediatric dentistry, Invisalign, root canals, fillings, and cosmetic dentistry.

Testerman Dental combines state-of-the-art technology and unparalleled customer service to create a truly unique patient experience. As a family-friendly and family-owned dental practice, we take pride in offering services designed to suit an assortment of needs for patients of every age.

Rest assured that regardless of your dental needs, we will treat you with the most compassionate, respectful care available.
