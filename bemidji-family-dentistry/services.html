CEREC Scanning and Milling AKA “Same Day Crowns”

CEREC is an acronym for Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics, or CEramic REConstruction. Simply put, CEREC is an advanced technology that allows us to design and craft porcelain crowns and bridges right in our office. The process usually goes like this:

-We prepare your tooth to receive the crown. We may need to remove a bit of enamel so it will fit well under your new restoration.

-We use the CEREC scanning technology to take a digital impression of your tooth and the surrounding area.

-Once the design is complete, our in-house milling unit creates the new restoration out of a single block of a high-quality dental material of the choosing.

-Your dentist verifies that the crown is shaped ideally for your teeth, then cements it permanently in place IN JUST ONE VISIT!

Only a handful of dental practices around the country use CEREC technology; most stick with traditional restorative procedures. Dental Cone Beam Tomography [Dental CBCT] is a specialized type of x-ray that provides more information than conventional dental or facial x-rays. This computerized scan uses advanced technology to generate three-dimensional[3-D] images.

The benefits of a Dental CBCT scan are that it provides 3-D images of dental structures, soft tissues, nerve paths and bone which are considerably more detailed than conventional two-dimensional dental x-rays. It alllows for more precise diagnosis and treatment planning. It can also simply and comfortably be used to take image of both bone and soft tissue simultaneously.

A dental CBCT scan is commonly used to: evaluate the position of teeth, bone structure, jaw joints and the airway. It aids in accurate placement of implants, surgical planning for the removal of impacted wisdom teeth, 3-D orthodontic evaluation, and complex root canal diagnosis and treatment.

- Veneers are made out of a porcelain material, they are custom made by a dental laboratory technician, and are bonded onto the fronts of anterior teeth. They are utilized for a variety of situations: discoloration, crooked teeth, cosmetically for the "movie star" smile, closing spaces, creating new shapes for misshaped teeth, or chipped/worn teeth. They allow for a beautiful long lasting smile.

- Crowns "Caps" are made out of a variety of material: gold, porcelain, or metal. It encases the whole crown of the tooth. It is custom made by a dental laboratory technician to fit your exact needs for shape, size, and color. It is needed for a variety of reasons: broken tooth, cracked tooth, large decay, large old fillings, dental anomalies, prior root canal completion, ect. Crowns allow you to add function, strength, and beauty back to your teeth.

Composite "White fillings" are created by using a composite resin material, which is a mix of plastics and glass, that is bonded to the tooth to restore areas of teeth that are lost to decay. Composite fillings are tooth colored, require less tooth removal, and bond to enamel. This bond helps strengthen the tooth, as well as prevents it from breaking down further. Composite, because it requires less removal of tooth structure, allows the tooth stucutre to stay stronger longer.

Root canals, also known as endodontics, is required for a tooth with an infection or for a tooth that is severely broken down and there is decay or bacterial within the pulp of the tooth. During the procedure, the pulp is removed from the roots, the bacterial, debris, and inflammation within the canals are cleaned out, and canals are sealed by being filled back in with a material called gutta putcha. It is usually advised that after a root canal is preformed, a crown is placed onto of the tooth’s crown for added support and strength.

Sleep Apnea and TMD (night guard) Appliances

Sleep Apnea and TMD Appliances come in a variety of designs, which are custom made by a dental laboratory technician. They are worn during sleep and fit ideally to the patient’s teeth. They fit like a sports mouth guard or an orthodontic retainer.

The sleep apnea appliance supports the lower jaw in a forward position, similar to the idea of jaw thrust with CPR, it works by opening the air way while you sleep. Research shows that oral appliance therapy is an effective treatment option for patients with a mild form of sleep apnea.

The TMD/Nightguard appliances aid in patients who suffer from clenching and grinding at night. The goal of a custom guard is the prop the patients jaws open to the patient’s specific centric relation to help ease stress on their jaw joint, help decrease stress in their facial muscles, and to relieve the stress on their teeth. Clenching and grinding causes adverse effects on the teeth, such as cracks and fractures, facial pain, and daily chronic migraines.
