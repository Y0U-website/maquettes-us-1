Dr. KATHRYN TUCKER, DDS

Athens

Dr. Tucker&#x27;s path to dentistry is remarkable. Before dentistry, she worked at a pediatric oncology lab at UT Southwestern and as a primate zookeeper at the Dallas Zoo. She actually witnessed her first dental procedure at the zoo… a root canal on a tiger. She went back to school, earned her DDS from Texas A&M College of Dentistry, and brought her scientific curiosity and genuine love for people straight into her practice.

"Our smiles are as unique as we are, and our dental care should be the same. No sin­­gle treatment will fit each person&#x27;s needs and goals, and I am committed to working with my patients to help them achieve their healthiest, most beautiful smiles."
