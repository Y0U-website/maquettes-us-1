In-Office Teeth Whitening Treatment

Whitening toothpastes and kits from the drugstore can help keep your teeth white, but a dentist will be able to make the biggest and fastest change to your smile. We can make your teeth several shades whiter in about an hour because we are allowed to use a stronger form of peroxide. Our in-office whitening is a great choice if you have an important event coming up and don’t have time to wait for a whiter smile.
