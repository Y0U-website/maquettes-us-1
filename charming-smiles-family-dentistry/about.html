Dr. Natalie Kelley

Dr. Natalie Kelley was born in Baltimore, MD but was raised in Houston, TX. Dr. Natalie moved back to the Washington, DC area in 2002, to attend dental school at Howard University and has been practicing in the Maryland area for 10 years.

Dr. Kelley has experience treating pediatric as well as adult patients and believes that dental health starts with family motivation and education. It has been her desire since graduating from dental school to establish a welcoming practice that will meet each person’s unique needs.

Outside the office, Dr. Natalie attends various continuing education courses, seminars and conventions to stay up to date with the latest professional requirements. She also enjoys spending time with her family, which includes keeping up with the growing demands of her 3-year-old son.
