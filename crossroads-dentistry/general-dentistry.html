Periodontal Disease

Gum (or periodontal) disease is a serious health condition that typically starts with

gum inflammation and can eventually lead to lost teeth or bacterial infections.

Typically, symptoms show up early in the form of red or swollen gums (gingivitis). Later stages of periodontal disease can result in serious health complications such as coronary artery disease or respiratory problems.

Fortunately, there are many methods to treat gum disease that can involve cleaning or medication. If you suspect that you may have gum disease, it is important to contact a specialist immediately.

Dental Exams

Dental exams are important to ensure that your teeth and gums stay healthy. While regular brushing and cleaning are vital to your overall well being, they alone are not a comprehensive solution for all aspects of your dental health.

By scheduling routine examinations with your dentist, you are allowing him to get an overall picture of your current state and spot any potential causes for disease. It is recommended to schedule an exam with your dentist at least every six months.

Dentures Dentures serve as a replacement for missing teeth and come in two classes: full dentures and partial dentures. Just as their names suggest, full dentures are a solution that replaces all teeth in your mouth while partial dentures only add individual teeth to replace missing ones. While dentures are not your real teeth, it is important to care for them as if they are.

Therefore, you should maintain standard hygienic practices (i.e. brushing regularly). Additionally, we may provide you with a cleaning solution to soak your dentures overnight and kill any lingering bacteria. It is important to use cool or warm water when cleaning your dentures as hot water will cause them to warp. For details, please call us today.
