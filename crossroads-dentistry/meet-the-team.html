Meet Our Team

Dr. McGrath

I am dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered dental care in a welcoming, family environment. I focus on preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry to help every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile. My office uses a conservative approach to diagnosing to eliminate unnecessary treatment and cost. Most of my patients have been with me from the beginning, and I enjoy welcoming new patients in hopes of making new, lifelong relationships.
