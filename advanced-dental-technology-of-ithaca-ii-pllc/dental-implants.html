Dental Implants

At Advanced Dental Technology of Ithaca II PLLC in Ithaca, NY, we understand that a missing tooth or teeth can not only affect your smile, but it can impact how you eat and speak. Dental implants are often the best solution for replacing missing teeth, and since they are artificial, they can’t decay. That said, however, oral hygiene is still crucial to ensuring that the gums and bone support stay healthy.

This multi-step procedure starts with a consultation with the oral surgeon, who determines if you are a good candidate for this type of restoration. The implant patient then returns to our office for preliminary impressions, which will assist the surgeon in planning the placement of the implant.

Benefits of Dental Implants

There are many benefits to dental implants, some tangible like easier eating, and others are intangible, like improved self-esteem. Whatever the reason for getting dental implants, it’s easy to see how they are an overall improvement in your quality of life.

Dental implants look and feel like your own teeth, which gives you that boost of confidence you deserve. If you been hiding your smile because of missing or broken teeth, you owe it to yourself to explore all the benefits that dental implants can provide.
