Meet Our Doctors

MEET DR. LEAH ROMAY

Growing up in rural Pennsylvania on her parents’ farm, Dr. Romay was raised with a love for the country, gardening, old houses, and antiques. Her parent’s farmhouse was a constant source of projects and she learned how to refinish furniture, use power tools, frame walls, design spaces, and paint (she even painted the walls of Baltimore Dental Co. by hand - no painter’s tape for the corners required!). This is why she loves the historic building that houses Baltimore Dental Co. and is always looking for a good antique.

Dr. Romay is self-proclaimed as crunchy and you can often hear her advising patients to shop at local farmers markets and how to make healthier choices. Health and prevention are the key tenants to Dr. Romay’s dental practice and she practices what she preaches in her personal life as well. When she’s not helping patients, she is a mother to her son, Grant, and her two stepchildren Alexey and Alyona. She also has two cats, Stella and Finn. You can typically find her in her vegetable garden or the kitchen cooking everything from scratch to nourish her family.

MEET DR. CHELSEA CROSS

Dr. Chelsea Cross grew up in Catonsville, MD. After 4 inspiring years in South Carolina studying biology and psychology at Clemson University (Go Tigers!), she returned home to Maryland for dental school and to be closer to her family. She graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2018 and then completed a one-year residency in general dentistry at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York, NY. Her residency experience taught her invaluable skills in treatment planning and patient communication and her time in New York made her quite the pizza snob!

Dr. Cross began working in private practice to make dentistry more accessible and comfortable for her patients. During the first 5 years of her career, she noticed the need for dentists who treat the entire person rather than just addressing specific dental needs. This patient-centered approach to dentistry led her to her new home at Baltimore Dental Co.!

Dr. Cross is passionate about the relationship between oral health and overall well-being, and she enjoys sharing that enthusiasm with her patients. She treats each of her patients as if they were part of her family, taking the quality time to listen to their needs and goals so that she can best serve them. Forming lasting relationships with her patients is her favorite part of being a dentist.

Dr. Cross lives in Hampden with her boyfriend, Greg, and they have a blast walking around Wyman Park and eating at their favorite neighborhood restaurants. She is dedicated to living a healthy lifestyle focused on exercise and trying new recipes! In her downtime, you can find her reading thriller novels, vintage shopping, watching The Office for the fifth time, or listening to her favorite records.

Heather’s journey to dental hygiene was anything but conventional. She earned a BA in Sociology from St. Mary’s College of Maryland before spending a decade as a veterinary assistant. In 2004, she transitioned to human health and discovered her true passion helping patients achieve their best oral health in a comfortable and supportive environment.

Rachel is passionate about holistic health and nutrition, with a deep interest in the body’s ability to heal through lifestyle changes. As a Certified Natural Health Professional (CNHP) and Certified Nutritional Consultant (CNC), she brings a unique perspective to dental care by blending clinical hygiene with nutritional counseling.

Rachel also coordinates Baltimore Dental Co.s oral microbiome testing, Oligoscan, and Personalized Wellness Review programs. As a Supplement Specialist, she enjoys helping patients understand how their oral health and potential mineral deficiencies can impact their overall well-being. Committed to ongoing growth in the natural health field, Rachel continually brings the latest knowledge and resources to our patients.

As a native of beautiful western Maryland, Rachel enjoys country living. She is happy to call Westminster home, where she lives with her husband and son. When she’s not caring for patients, Rachel enjoys practicing yoga, spending quality time with family and friends, and staying active in her church community.

Elizabeth witnessed the life-changing impact of dental care as an expanded-function assistant and has been dedicated to the profession ever since. Elizabeth leverages her unique skills from dental assisting to seamlessly integrate clinical plans with financial aspects, helping patients feel empowered and excited about their dental care.

Elizabeth and her husband, Ryan, reside in Carroll County with their three wild and wonderful children. Elizabeth is often biking the NCR trail, perfecting her homemade kombucha, or planting flowers in her garden. In peaceful moments she can be found reading a good book, painting, or writing. When seeking a reprieve from the mayhem of parenting, Elizabeth and Ryan enjoy attending concerts and visiting their favorite restaurants.
