CLEAR ALIGNER THERAPY STEPS IN BALTIMORE COUNTY, MD: SURESMILE

SureSmile is a modern orthodontic treatment using clear, removable aligners to straighten teeth without traditional braces. These aligners are custom-made for each patient, gradually shifting their teeth into the desired position over time. With its discreet appearance and comfortable design, Baltimore Dental Co. offers a convenient alternative for achieving a straighter smile. The steps to your clear aligner therapy treatment include:

Each patient will undergo an evaluation to determine if clear aligner therapy is the right treatment for them. We will assess the bite, discuss goals, and discuss pricing options.

If you qualify for invisible aligners, Baltimore Dental Co. will take 2D and 3D photographic images of your teeth, mouth, and jaw to create a precise model of your teeth.

From this digital model of your teeth, our clear aligner laboratory will fabricate sets of aligners that will create your new smile. The treatment is designed to shift your teeth into proper alignment with gentle, consistent movement.

Your dentist will make any adjustments or modifications at the aligner delivery appointment. The team at Baltimore Dental Co. will educate you on the proper removal, wear, and care of your new aligners!

You will wear the aligners for 1-2 weeks depending on your unique orthodontic movement. It is imperative to wear your aligners for 22 hours each day. During the 2 hours they are not in, you will be able to eat comfortably and clean your teeth.

We will check your progress - usually after every 5th aligner. This is to ensure your teeth are shifting correctly. We will also provide you with your next few sets of aligners.

At your final aligner checkup, we will confirm that you are pleased with the results and that your teeth have ideal positioning for proper function. Any attachments that may have been made will be removed and we will send data to the clear aligner laboratory to create retainers.

For the first 2 months after the conclusion of movement, you will continue to wear the retainers for a minimum of 22 hours a day. After the initial 2 months, you will transition to wearing the retainers at night. It is important to be consistent with the nighttime retainer wear to preserve the tooth alignment.
