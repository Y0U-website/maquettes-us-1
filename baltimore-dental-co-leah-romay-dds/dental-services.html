FUNCTIONAL DENTISTRY

Baltimore Dental Co. practices functional dentistry, a comprehensive approach that moves beyond standard dental care by seeking the underlying causes of oral health issues, not just treating symptoms. Instead of generic advice, we take into account a patient’s medical history, lifestyle, diet, sleep, breathing, and oral microbiome to develop a personalized plan focused on addressing root causes and preventing future problems through health-centered lifestyle changes, recognizing that oral health is interconnected with overall well-being. Our Functional Dentistry services include:

  • Comprehensive Examinations
  • Oral Microbiome Testing
  • Nutrition Guidance
  • Oral Cancer Screenings & Diagnostic Imaging
