About Us

Clayton Kids Dentistry was established January 1st, 2019 under the ownership of Dr. Shraddha Patel Kolappa. Dr. Patel took over the practice previously known as Clayton Pediatric Dental Center.

We’re not just a dental office; we’re your child’s dental home. At Clayton Kids Dentistry, we believe that a lifetime of healthy smiles starts with feeling safe, comfortable, and even a little excited about going to the dentist. That’s why everything about our practice, from our friendly team to our fun atmosphere, is built around one thing: your child.

Our team is dedicated to the dental and emotional needs of infants, children, and adolescents. Our goal is to set our patients on the right path toward obtaining healthy and beautiful smiles for a lifetime.
