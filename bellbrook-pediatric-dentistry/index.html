The practice

Our team is passionate about providing the highest-quality care and creating a dental home for your children.

We believe kids deserve to have a great time at the dentist! We provide a fun environment where children of all ages have a positive experience visiting our office.

Healthy dental habits can affect your child’s overall health. Dr. Carpentino is specially trained to treat children while helping them feel comfortable.

We are happy to answer all of your questions and concerns. Please contact the office to talk with one of our helpful team members or to schedule an appointment.

Your child’s smile is our priority! We provide quality dental care that meets the unique needs of children. Parents are welcome to join children in exam rooms.
