The practice

We are accepting new patients. Our office participates with all CSEA dental insurance plans and we also work with most dental insurance, submitting claims for our patients.

Our practice is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and our team is dedicated to staying updated with the latest advancements in dental care. We offer a wide range of services, from routine exams and dental hygiene to emergency dental care and cosmetic dentistry. Our goal is to make every visit to our office a pleasant experience for you and your loved ones.

Dr. de Ubl grew up in New York City and attended Georgetown University in Washington, D.C. for his undergraduate and dental degrees. He is a veteran, having served in the naval reserve while working as a commissioned officer dentist in the U.S. Public Health Service in Gallup, New Mexico.
