Our Technology

At Beaudoin Dental, we invest in advanced dental technology that enhances your experience from start to finish—making your visits more comfortable, more efficient, and more precise. Here’s how our tools help us deliver the highest level of care to every patient, every time.

CBCT/Panorex 3D Imaging

Our cone beam tomography system captures a full 360° image of your head, jaw, teeth, and sinuses—all in a single scan. This comprehensive view allows us to detect structural issues, infections, bone abnormalities, and other conditions that may not appear on traditional x-rays. It’s a powerful diagnostic tool that helps us provide thorough, informed care.

Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays provide crisp, high-resolution images instantly, helping us diagnose cavities, bone loss, and other concerns with pinpoint accuracy. Even better—they emit up to 80 % less radiation than traditional x-rays, making them a safer choice for you and your family.

Oral Cancer Screenings

We perform regular oral cancer screenings using specialized light technology that reveals tissue changes not visible to the naked eye. Early detection is key to successful treatment, and we’re committed to identifying potential issues before they become serious. These screenings are especially important for patients who smoke or drink regularly.

Digital Impressions

If you’ve ever had a dental impression, you know it can be uncomfortable. Thankfully, our intra-oral scanner offers a faster, more comfortable alternative. This digital technology captures highly accurate 3D models of your teeth without the need for messy impression material. It also allows for instant, secure data transfer and integrates seamlessly with modern dental labs—ensuring quicker, more precise results for treatments like crowns, inlays, and more.

Rotary Endodontics (Root Canal Therapy)

Root canals don’t have to be stressful. We use electric rotary tools for greater accuracy and speed, helping us gently and thoroughly clean infected tooth roots with less discomfort. This technology also shortens treatment time and improves outcomes.

Electric Handpieces

Unlike traditional air-driven drills, our electric handpieces offer smoother, quieter, and more controlled performance. This means we can remove decay and complete restorations more quickly and comfortably—getting you back to your day sooner.
