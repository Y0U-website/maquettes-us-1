The practice

Boulevard Dental PLLC is a privately owned dental practice serving Fairview and the surrounding Williamson County community. We offer comprehensive general and preventive dentistry in a comfortable, unhurried environment — because we believe the best care starts with actually listening.

You’ll see some familiar faces when you walk through our doors, along with some great new additions to our team. We’re all here for one reason — to take good care of you.

Dr. Hayman graduated from Indiana University with a B.S. in Biology and minors in Chemistry and Psychology. She earned her dental degree from Midwestern University in Chicago. She and her husband moved to Tennessee in 2015 and instantly fell in love with their community. She had a dream of opening a private practice to serve patients in Williamson County — a vision she’s proud to continue at…

She is known for her warm, fun-loving personality and approach to dentistry. Dr. Hayman has a passion for learning, constantly furthering her education to provide the most up-to-date technology and procedures for her patients.

Outside of dentistry, she stays busy raising three kids with her husband, Jared. She has a zest for good food, wine, traveling, and reading.

Dr. Weiss graduated from Louisiana State University, where she received a Bachelor of Arts in History with Upper Division Honors. Upon graduation, she was accepted to the University of Edinburgh, Scotland, where she received a Master of Science with distinction. She then returned to LSU to earn her dental degree.

She is thrilled to return home to Williamson County to join Dr. Hayman and the team at Boulevard Dental. She is passionate about creating a welcoming environment for her patients and wants every patient to feel confident about their smile. She is excited to build long-lasting relationships with her patients and community.

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys travel, cooking, hiking, pickleball, golf, and exploring Nashville with her friends.
