About Blakeney Pediatric Dentistry Discover the Skilled Team at Blakeney Pediatric Dentistry At Blakeney Pediatric Dentistry, we recognize that parents always strive to provide the best for their children, especially when it comes to selecting healthcare providers. We are honored to be an integral part of each family’s healthcare journey.

Our dedicated pediatric dental team is committed to delivering top-notch dental care, helping children and teenagers achieve beautiful, confident smiles that can last a lifetime. We take a professional and personalized approach, creating tailored treatment plans and oral hygiene routines that cater specifically to your child’s needs.

Our mission is to ensure that children have a positive dental experience in a warm, friendly, and enjoyable atmosphere, making them excited about their dental visits!

When should my child have their first dental appointment?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) suggests that your child should see a dentist for the first time within six months after their first tooth appears or by their first birthday. Usually, the first front tooth emerges around six months of age. This is an ideal time to talk about preventive care to keep your child’s teeth healthy and to identify any potential issues early.

At Blakeney Pediatric Dentistry , our pediatric dentists are well-equipped to care for children as young as one year old, ensuring they have a fun and positive dental experience.

We are dedicated to prioritizing your child’s oral health by focusing on education, prevention, encouraging positivity , and providing high-quality treatment .
