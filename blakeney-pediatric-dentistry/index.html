The practice

Ceramic crowns are strong and long-lasting, so they can protect your child’s smile while looking natural.

Let’s beat dental anxiety together! With dental sedation, your child can be relaxed and fearless at the dentist’s office.

Our team’s gentle approach and compassionate service enable us to provide the care that every child with special needs deserves.

If your child’s smile has been damaged by decay or injury, our ceramic crowns can protect the tooth and blend in naturally.

Sealants help to protect the smile from decay by creating a barrier between the enamel and cavity-causing bacteria.

Our preventative exams are bi-annual appointments performed to ensure your child’s smile remains in healthy condition.
