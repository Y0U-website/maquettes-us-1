The story behind the chair.

Dr. Sang Kim, DMD leads the team at Bridgewater Dental Center with a simple philosophy: every patient brings a story worth honoring. Whether you&#x27;re a Bridgewater College student in for your first cleaning in years, a growing family looking for a dental home, or a longtime Valley resident ready to rebuild your smile — Dr. Kim treats you like a neighbor first and a patient second.

A graduate of Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, Dr. Kim blends modern techniques — Invisalign, sedation dentistry, digital imaging, and same-day restorations — with the kind of unhurried, human conversation that has quietly disappeared from a lot of modern dental offices. No lectures, no shaming, no jargon.

  • You&#x27;re heard first. Every visit starts with a real conversation — what brought you in, what you&#x27;re worried about, what you want your smile to feel like.
  • You get clear answers. X-rays, diagnoses, options, costs — all in plain language, before any decisions get made.
  • You leave with choices, not pressure. Information and options — not a same-day hard sell.
  • Your anxiety is met with compassion. Sedation options, extra time, and a pace that feels right to you.

Let&#x27;s write the next chapter of your smile&#x27;s story.
