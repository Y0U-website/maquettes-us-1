Meet Our Team

Katie Hogan, Office Manger

Katie has been in the pediatric dental field for 20 years. She began volunteering at a pediatric dental office at the age of 16, and started her career as a dental assistant eventually moving to the front office. Her dual experience in the clinical aspects field as a dental assistant and the administrative front office skills make her great asset to our practice. She has been Dr. Louca’s office manager since 2013. Katie graduated from the University of North Texas with a degree in social work, and she is certified in CPR, HIPAA, and OSHA.

Katie resides in Frisco with her husband and daughter.

Melanie Sowards, Insurance Coordinator/Patient Coordinator

Melanie has worked in the dental field since 2000. She joined our practice in 2012, and has embraced the transition to pediatric dentistry. She enjoys serving our patients and helping to coordinate their dental needs. Melanie is certified in CPR, HIPAA, and OSHA.

Melanie resides in The Colony with her husband Kevin and son Kyle. She enjoys spending time with her family along with her two boxers. She spends free time exercising, boating and attending StonebriarCommunity Church.

Sallye Bales, Receptionist

Sallye has been the receptionist for Coppell Professional Plaza since September of 2000. She just celebrated her 15th year anniversary! Sallye has enjoyed greeting our patients as they walk through the door and watching them grow up year after year. She is a graduate of Texas Christian University with a degree in Education.

Sallye lives in Coppell and is a pillar in the community. She has three married sons and eleven grandchildren who all happen to be Dr. Louca’s patients! She enjoys Bible study, needle point, and gardening.

Shana Harding, Registered Dental Hygienist

Shana received her Dental Hygiene degree in 1991 from Amarillo College graduating with honors. She joined the practice in 1999 and has found complete joy and fulfillment specializing in the field of pediatric dentistry. She is especially passionate about working with special needs children.

Shana stays active in community volunteer work through her church and PTA. She also served at the Nelson-TebedoCommunity Dental Clinic in Dallas, and most recently with the Serving Him Ministries Community Dental Outreach Program.

Emma Perez, Registered Dental Assistant

Emma is the newest member of our team. She joined Dr. Louca’spractice in January of 2015. She has been a clinical assistant since 2009 and brings knowledge, fresh ideas, and experience from working in a high volume dental practice. Emma is certified in CPR, Coronal Polishing, Nitrous Oxide Monitoring, Radiation and Sealant Application. She also has current certificates in HIPAA and OSHA training.

Emma grew up in Lewisville and resides there with her family. She enjoys spending time with her kids and taking family vacations.

Sandra Lucio, Registered Dental Assistant

Sandy was Dr. Louca’s full-time clinical assistant from 2001 to 2012. She currently works part-time as needed while working full time for her husband’s company. Sandy has over 30 years of dental assisting experience. She is certified in CPR, Coronal Polishing, Nitrous Oxide Monitoring, Radiation, and Sealant Application.

Sandy resides in Little Elm with her husband and has two grown boys. Sandy loves going to the movies and is famously known for her love of cats.
