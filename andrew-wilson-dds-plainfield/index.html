Dr. Andrew Wilson One of the most rewarding aspects of my job is when we’re able to coach a nervous child through their treatment and change the way they view dental visits.

The practice

Dr. Andrew Wilson was born in Evansville, IN and knew he wanted to be a dentist as early as second grade.

Growing up, he had an incredible dentist who had great relationships with his patients, thus inspiring him to do the same.

He’s a kid-at-heart and strives to make every child’s dental experience fun!
