Origin Beyond+ Multi

16 Classic Shades + 0M2, BL1, BL2, BL3, BL4

Beyond+ Multi-Y layered discs have more intense chroma in the cervical and decreasing chroma towards the incisal area, offering the most efficient production speed combined with the natural look found in the shade tab. Each disc will give you the exact shade after simply milling and sintering. In addition the incisal blue gray effect has been incorporated on the top 1/3 area of the disc to reduce the white L* value and present the natural tooth blueish grayish translucency. (Patented Technology)
