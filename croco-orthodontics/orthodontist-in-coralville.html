Clear Aligners in Coralville, Iowa

Discover the convenience of clear aligners in Coralville, Iowa. Clear aligners offer an obscure and convenient alternative to traditional braces. They consist of a set of custom-made, removable trays that gently and gradually move teeth into their desired positions. Our treatment process starts by conducting a thorough assessment and taking digital scans. This enables us to create a customized plan that is perfectly suited to your individual requirements.

The benefits of clear aligners are numerous: they are virtually invisible , removable for easier eating and cleaning, and comfortable to wear, making them a popular choice among patients seeking subtle orthodontic treatment . At Croco Orthodontics, we guide you through every step of your aligner journey, ensuring a seamless transition to a beautifully aligned smile.

Braces in Coralville, Iowa

As specialists in braces treatment , Croco Orthodontics offers both traditional braces and advanced LightForce brackets.

Traditional braces are known for their durability and effectiveness, making them a trusted choice for many individuals seeking orthodontic treatment. They work by applying steady pressure to move teeth into their correct positions over time, effectively correcting misalignment and bite issues.
