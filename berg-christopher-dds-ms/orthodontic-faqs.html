Orthodontic FAQs

What Is the Definition of Orthodontics ?

Orthodontics refers to a form of dentistry that concentrates on diagnosing, treating, and preventing malpositioned teeth and jaws.

What Does an Orthodontist Do ?

An orthodontist is a dental professional who has completed an additional two or more years of training. Orthodontists specialize in straightening teeth and fixing misaligned jaws.

What Are Braces?

Braces are one of the most common types of orthodontic treatments and can align and straighten teeth. Braces are available in many different types, including metal, invisible, clear, ceramic, lingual and self-ligating.

When Should I Visit an Orthodontist ?

While orthodontists treat many children and teenagers, you can see an orthodontist at any age. Whether it’s crooked teeth or an overbite, feeling unhappy about the way your smile looks is a good enough reason to see an orthodontist. Orthodontists can treat patients of all ages.

Are Braces Painful ?

A common concern people have about wearing braces is the pain level. The truth is that you may feel some discomfort for the first few days as your mouth gets adjusted to your braces. If this bothers you, consider taking an over-the-counter pain reliever.

Do I Need to Make Changes to My Oral Hygiene Routine ?

When you have braces, your oral hygiene routine will be a little more involved. For starters, you will have to brush your teeth after every meal to remove plaque and food particles from your braces. You must also floss thoroughly every day.

There are certain foods you should avoid, including hard candy, popcorn, caramel, nuts and corn on the cob, as they can get stuck in your braces.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take ?

How long you have to wear braces will depend on the severity of the problem. Generally, treatment can take anywhere from six to 30 months.

Should I Still Schedule Dental Checkups ?

Yes, it’s crucial for you to still see your dentist every six months for a checkup. When you have braces, food can get stuck between your teeth easier, increasing plaque formation. Your dentist will check on the state of your teeth and gums and recommend treatments if necessary.

Will Braces Prevent Me from Participating in School Activities ?

While there may be an adjustment period, braces shouldn’t interfere with school activities, like sports or playing an instrument. If you play a contact sport, remember to wear a mouthguard to protect your braces from damage.
