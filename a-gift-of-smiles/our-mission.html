Making Quality Dental Care Accessible for Every Smile

Everyone deserves the opportunity to be healthy — and good dental health is an essential part of overall wellness. At A Gift of Smiles, our mission is to remove barriers and expand access to dental healthcare for individuals in the central Pennsylvania community who have intellectual and developmental disabilities.

This is the problem we are here to solve.

Across the United States, more than 12.5 million children and adults live with special healthcare needs, and the greatest unmet need they face is access to dental care.

For many families, finding affordable, compassionate dental treatment is a constant challenge — and for some, it feels impossible. Dental health issues often go untreated, with serious consequences for overall health and quality of life.

Has a Gift of Smiles Made an Impact in Your Life?

Have you or a loved one been a recipient of our services? We’d love to hear from you, whether it’s a small anecdote or something that touched you deeply. By telling us your story, we’ll be able to share the impact A Gift Of Smiles has on the community and inspire others. We would love to hear from you!

A Gift Of Smiles foundation has provided dental treatment to over 500 patients since we treated our first patient in 2015. With the financial support of businesses and other individual donors in our local community, we have raised over $700,000.

Our foundation exists solely due to the generous donations from those who support our mission. We simply could not do what we do for our patients and their families without each and every one of your giving and caring hearts. Our future can remain bright with continued funding assistance from our local businesses, colleagues, and others like you.

Thank you to everyone who joins us in helping to deepen the impact in our local area. Together, we can improve the health of disabled people living in our community and build better lives for everyone, one smile at a time.

— Dr. Dwight Ashby, Founder of A Gift Of Smiles Foundation
