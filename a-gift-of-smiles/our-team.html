Dwight Jennings Ashby, DDS

Dentist

Dwight is a board-certified pediatric dentist with more than 40 years of experience caring for children and individuals with special healthcare needs throughout the Harrisburg metropolitan area.

Born in Anderson, Indiana and raised in northwest Indiana, Dr. Ashby earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Purdue University before completing dental school at Indiana University. He then completed specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at Children’s Hospital of Cincinnati, where he developed a lifelong passion for treating patients with complex medical and developmental needs.

Dr. Ashby practiced for decades at Kearns & Ashby Pediatric Dentistry, becoming known for his passion, patience, and empathy in caring for children and patients with intellectual and developmental disabilities. He also served as an adjunct faculty member in the pediatric dental residency program at Geisinger Medical Center in Danville, PA.

He currently holds hospital privileges at UPMC Harrisburg Hospital, allowing him to provide comprehensive dental treatment ( including care under general anesthesia) for patients who cannot be safely treated in a traditional dental office setting.

Dr. Ashby is an active member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Pennsylvania Association of Pediatric Dentists, Harrisburg Area Dental Society, and the Pennsylvania Dental Association Leadership Academy (2025–2026). He also volunteers at Hope Within Ministries dental clinic in Elizabethtown.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ashby enjoys his faith, cycling of all kinds, skiing, woodworking, cheering for his beloved Chicago Blackhawks and spending time with his wife, their four grown sons, and two grandsons.

Stephanie

Dental Hygienist

Stephanie brings years of experience providing dental hygiene care for patients with physical, developmental, and cognitive disabilities. She is deeply committed to delivering compassionate, patient-centered care and helping create a comfortable, supportive environment for every individual she serves.

Stephanie’s dedication aligns closely with our mission to ensure that all patients receive dental care with dignity, respect, and understanding. She is passionate about removing barriers to care and making positive experiences possible for individuals who often face challenges accessing dental services.

We are grateful to have Stephanie as part of our team and for the care and heart she brings to our patients and families each day.

Shanna

Office Administrator

Shanna G. serves as the Administrative Assistant for A Gift of Smiles, where she manages patient appointments and paperwork while keeping the office organized and running smoothly. She brings 40 years of experience in the nonprofit sector and a deep passion for service and advocacy, particularly for individuals with disabilities.

Shanna is dedicated to helping ensure every patient receives compassionate, dignified access to care in a supportive environment. She lives in Camp Hill with her husband, Aaron, and their two children — Elia, a pre-professional dancer, and Caleb, who enjoys playing basketball and baseball.

Outside of work, Shanna loves running as a form of relaxation and meditation and hopes to run another half marathon in the fall. She also serves as Vice President of the PTO at her son’s elementary school, is a proud dog mom to two golden retrievers named Rosie and Winston, and says her favorite color is pink.

She has a passion for working in pediatrics and with patients who have special health care needs. Sharing Our Mission

A Gift of Smiles’ board of directors consists of local leaders with a wide range of experience who share our mission. They are comprised of individuals from the dental, medical, legal, financial, legislative and advocacy sectors of our community. Many of our board members have experienced firsthand both the challenges and the triumphs associated with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Their personal stake in A Gift Of Smiles mission provides our volunteers and staff with wisdom, inspiration, and guidance.

Janet Ashby, MSN, CRNP

Board Member

Janet’s commitment to special needs children and their families can be traced back to her early career as a NICU nurse in her native Indiana and continues today as a nurse practitioner for The Women’s Healthcare Group, York, PA. She obtained her BS in Nursing from Purdue University and her MSN, CRNP from the University of Maryland.

Janet has been chairman of the annual A Gift Of Smiles fundraising gala since the first gala in 2015. With her dedication to the foundation and fund raising skills, Janet and her committee have raised over $495,000, providing funding for the dental treatment of our patients. She and Dwight share their love of travel, biking, skiing and being the parents of four sons and two grandsons.
