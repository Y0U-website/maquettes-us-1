Our Doctors

Dr. Bodnar and family are enthusiastic supporters of the arts and life of Cleveland. From the Rock Hall to the Cleveland Museum of Art, farmers markets to the West Side Market, an Indians game or a party in the park the Bodnar family is more than just a part of this city. Like each of us, they are the core of this city. He believes in what Cleveland and more specifically what the near west side stands for. Simply put, a city and beliefs well founded in God, family, and trusted friends.

Dr. Tom is an adventurous traveler, domestic and abroad, and spends his leisure time in an array of diverse activities. He cooks for friends and family, with an emphasis on Hungarian, Italian and Thai foods, and is an avid hiker, climber and skier. Tom is also a poet, writer and advocate for a more peaceful green planet.
