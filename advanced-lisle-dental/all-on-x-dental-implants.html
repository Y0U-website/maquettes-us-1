Discover Who Can Benefit From This Restoration

All-on-X implants are designed to offer more flexibility and customization, leading to better long-term outcomes for some patients. They are tailored to your bone density, bite formation, and are even ideal for patients with severe bone loss or irregular jaw anatomy.

Patients Seeking A Long-Term Investment: You can have a permanent bridge supported by implants in one day and replace your dentures with this implant solution. All-on-X implants are a wise investment in your oral health because they are crafted to be long-lasting with the right maintenance.

Individuals With A Healthy Jawbone: In addition to being in good general health and free of oral infections, patients are typically expected to have enough healthy jawbone to support the implants.

Patients Wanting A Confidence Boost: You can feel more at ease in social and professional situations when you have a complete and healthy set of teeth to show off. For patients who prioritize and prefer customization and a more durable restoration, All-on-X implants are ideal!
