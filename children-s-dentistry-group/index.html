When it comes to caring for your child’s smile, your family deserves a pediatric dental team that understands the unique needs of young patients. At Children’s Dentistry Group we see children as young as six months old, kids with special needs and older teens.

Each of our dentists and clinical staff has extra training and experience assisting children (and parents) who feel nervous about the dental experience. Our Tell-Show-Do approach to the appointment makes visits less intimidating. Nitrous oxide ( laughing gas ) also helps!
