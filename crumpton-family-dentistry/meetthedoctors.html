Meet the Doctors

We strive to maintain the highest level of expertise by continuing education about new techniques, sciences and technologies. Get to know a little bit about the doctors here.

Dr. Brianna Crumpton, DDS

Dr. Brianna Crumpton, DDS Dr. Brianna Crumpton is excited to continue the tradition of compassionate and comfortable dentistry in East Tennessee.

She received her B.S. at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, in Biochemistry and Cellular and Molecular Biology and then continued on to the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, to attain her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2015. She is an active member of the ADA, TDA, PFA, ICD, SEAP, board member of ETADPA.

Dr. Reid Crumpton, DDS Dr. Reid Crumpton, DDS Dr. Reid Crumpton graduated from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Dentistry in Memphis, TN, in 1982.

After nearly 40 years of practicing compassionate dentistry, he retired from regular dental practice in 2021. For those who know Dr. Reid, it won’t surprise you to hear he keeps busy volunteering with Trinity Dental Clinic, renovating his 1928 house, and stopping by the office to fix things (as well as accompanying Dr. Brianna to dental conferences to keep his continuing education up to date he never stops learning!).
