Meet you

Cascade Family Dental is a family dental office, conveniently located south of Hwy 26 (Powell Blvd) off of Highland Dr. in Gresham, OR. Like all good Gresham dental offices, we have an excellent dentist, Dr. Grace Park. She is proficient in the practice of general dental procedures and dental hygiene techniques.

However, unlike other Gresham dentist offices, we are able to serve patients of all ages with an even broader variety of dental procedures, all in our state-of-the-art office. Some of the traditional dental services we provide are: fillings, dental crowns, root canals, bridges, surgical tooth extractions, and dental implants.

We also strive to stay abreast of modern technological advances and training in the dental industry. We are committed to bringing these services to you in beautiful Gresham, OR.

Trusted hands, kind hearts
