General Dentistry

At Centerline Dentistry, we are committed to preventative care. If you have found yourself

waiting to go to the dentist until there is an issue with your oral health, you are not alone, but we hope to change your perspective by educating you on what proper dental hygiene entails. Regular dental appointments are imperative to recognizing tooth decay or even cancer. We treat these issues as soon as possible to save you time, money, pain and discomfort.

So how often should you go to the dentist? Though some patients suffer from issues like

abnormal plaque accumulation that can lead to more serious problems such as gum disease and require more frequent visits, the majority of our patients only require a yearly dental check up.

We offer a full range of restorative dentistry services that will prevent cavities, gum disease, root canal infections, and missing teeth. These issues compromise your oral health, overall vitality, and confidence. If your smile isn’t the brightest it can be, we’ll use our products, techniques, expertise, and technology to enhance your oral hygiene. Our restorative dentistry services include composite fillings, periodontal therapy, temporomandibular joint therapy, and root canal

Our cutting edge technology allows us to diagnose and treat diseased or damaged teeth and gums, ensuring the restoration of your overall health and appearance. At Centerline Dentistry, we can promise the highest level of dental care. If you are experiencing dental trauma, a toothache, oral pain or an increase of sensitivity to cold liquids or biting pressure, please contact us as soon as possible.
