Doctor

Dr. Lana El-shaar

Dr. El-shaar was born and raised in Akron, Ohio. She went to Walsh Jesuit High School and later graduated from the University of Akron with a Bachelor’s degree in Biology. She then continued her education at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry where she earned her doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Dr. El-shaar is looking forward to building life-long relationships with her patients and their families. She enjoys treating patients of all ages and is committed to providing compassionate and comprehensive dental care. When she isn’t working, Dr. El-shaar loves spending time with her husband and two boys . In her free time, she likes playing tennis, running, and traveling.

Doctor

Dr. Nour Hejazin

Dr. Hejazin is proud to be a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, a distinction earned by only a small percentage of general dentists nationwide. This achievement reflects her strong commitment to lifelong learning, clinical excellence, and high-quality patient care. She is also a member of the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland. Known for her gentle approach, attention to detail, and commitment to patient comfort, Dr. Hejazin strives to create a positive dental experience while delivering modern, high-quality care tailored to each patient’s needs.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Hejazin enjoys traveling and exploring new places and cultures. She also has a creative side and enjoys reading, drawing, and practicing calligraphy — hobbies that reflect the precision and artistry she brings to her dental work.
