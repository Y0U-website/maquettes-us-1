Meet Dr. Cawley Dr. Cawley graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry. He obtained his specialty certificate in Endodontics from UNMC College of Dentistry and is board certified with the American Board of Endodontics. He has lived in the Omaha metro area since 2014.

I am from a suburb of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Although I did not have any dentists in my family, I began to contemplate dentistry as a career in high school. This interest brought me to Marquette University in Milwaukee, Wisconsin for my undergraduate degree and dental school.

After dental school, I practiced general dentistry in Omaha for several years. It was during this time I developed an enthusiasm for endodontic procedures, leading me to pursue endodontic specialty training at the UNMC College of Dentistry.

In addition to my specialty certificate, I have also earned board certification with the American Board of Endodontics. While not required to practice as an endodontic specialist, this distinction is considered the highest level of achievement in endodontics. Only about one quarter of endodontists have accomplished board certification. In order to provide my patients with the best care possible…

Outside of dentistry, my time is spent with my wife and our three children. My other hobbies include barbecuing, bowling, and watching hockey.
