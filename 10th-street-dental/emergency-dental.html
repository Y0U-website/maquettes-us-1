Emergency Dental Services

Our Sioux Falls emergency dentists offer tooth repair, sedation dentistry, and relief for mouth pain resulting from broken or cracked teeth, canker sores, abscesses of the gum, general toothaches or otherwise injured teeth or gums. If something is causing you pain in your mouth, don’t wait, contact us for immediate relief.

If your tooth has been completely knocked out, step 1 is to call us immediately to make an emergency appointment. Do your best to keep the tooth moist until we can evaluate it for reimplantation. When possible, keep the tooth stored in cold milk, and when handling be sure to handle by the white part, avoiding any contact with the root itself to prevent further damage. If this method is not possible, try to reinsert the tooth temporarily into place. The amount of time your tooth is out of its socket plays a big part in the success of saving the tooth, so act fast!
