Understanding the Importance of Bone Health for Dental Implants

Bone health is crucial for the success of dental implants, as a strong jawbone provides the necessary support for the titanium post. If there is insufficient bone density, the implant may not integrate properly, leading to complications. Therefore, assessing bone health is a vital part of the initial consultation at Newsham Dental.

In cases of bone loss, procedures such as bone grafting can be performed to enhance the jawbone’s structure, making it suitable for implant placement. This proactive approach ensures that patients can still achieve successful outcomes with their dental implants, even if they initially face challenges related to bone density.
