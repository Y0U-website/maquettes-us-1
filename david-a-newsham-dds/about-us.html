What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants Compared to Other Tooth Replacement Options?

  • Jawbone Preservation : Implants prevent bone resorption through osseointegration, maintaining facial structure and preventing a sunken appearance.
  • Full Chewing Power : They restore a high percentage of natural bite force, allowing for a wider variety of dietary choices.
  • Natural Aesthetics : Custom-designed crowns blend seamlessly with existing teeth , enhancing appearance.
  • Long-Term Durability : Implants have a success rate exceeding 95 % and can last many years with proper care, unlike dentures or bridges that may require replacement.
  • Improved Oral Health : They protect adjacent teeth and support healthy gum tissue, unlike bridges that require reshaping neighboring teeth .

Which Types of Dental Implants and Procedures Are Available at Newsham Dental?

  • Traditional Dental Implants : Suitable for those with sufficient jawbone, involving a titanium post placed into the jawbone.
  • Implant Crowns : Custom-designed crowns attached to a single dental implant , matching the appearance of natural teeth .
  • Implant-Supported Dentures : Dentures anchored to multiple implants for improved stability and function.
  • All-on-4 Implant Dentures : A technique that supports a full arch of teeth with just four implants, allowing for immediate or early loading in many cases.

When Is Bone Grafting Recommended and How Does It Support Oral Health?

Bone grafting is recommended when a patient’s jawbone lacks sufficient density or volume to support a dental implant , often due to long-term tooth loss or advanced periodontal disease . It supports oral health by rebuilding deficient areas of the jawbone, creating a robust foundation for the implant, which is essential for long-term stability and success. This process helps prevent further bone loss and maintains the structure of the jaw, contributing to overall oral health .
