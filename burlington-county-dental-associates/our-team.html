Meet the Team

Jay Aphale, DDS

Dr. Jay Aphale is a general dentist with over 10 years of experience. He graduated from NYU College of Dentistry in 2015 and has been practicing in South Jersey ever since. He is experienced in all areas of general dentistry including dental fillings, crowns, bridges, root canals, dentures, and extractions. He believes in an evidence-based approach to dental care while also prioritzing patient comfort.
