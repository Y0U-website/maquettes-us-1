We stand behind our work. Follow-up visits for adjustments will never be charged.

Dr. Huwyler received his undergraduate degree from Rutgers and his D.M.D. degree from UMDNJ. He taught at UMDNJ as a Clinical Assistant Professor of Prostodontics and Dental Materials.

Dr. Huwyler was born in Switzerland and is a graduate of the Montclair public school system. His interest in dentistry began as a technician in his father’s Montclair dental laboratory, Swiss Art Precision Labs. There he developed his expertise in the crafting of crowns, bridges, dentures and appliances.

He is proud to participate in the Donated Dental Services program.

The Carriage House is a Montclair Landmark. In 1983 the building was moved forward on rollers to its current location and fully renovated.
