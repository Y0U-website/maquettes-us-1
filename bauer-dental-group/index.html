The practice

Dr. Theodore (“Ted”) Bauer grew up in Ottawa, Ohio. He graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he was involved in numerous student organizations and student government associations at varying levels, including President of the Inter-Professional Council. Dr. Bauer is a recipient of the Ohio State College of Dentistry Alumni Leadership Award and was recommended to this…

Dr. Bauer and his two sons Will and Nolan live in Upper Arlington where they are gradually renovating and restoring their historic home. When he isn’t taking care of the kids or working on the house, he likes to spend his free time supporting local businesses, causes, and artists.

Dr. Bauer assumed ownership interest in the practice from Dr. David Rummel in 2013.

Dr. Bauer is currently a: Member of the American Dental Association Member of the Ohio Dental Association (ODA) Member of the Columbus Dental Society Lifetime Member of the OSU Dental Alumni Society Sustaining Member of the OSU Alumni Association

Dr. Matt Paulson was born and raised in Dayton, Ohio. He attended Wittenberg University where he earned a B.A. degree in Biology before attending the Case Western Reserve College of Dental Medicine. Dr. Paulson enlisted in the army National Guard as an undergraduate and proudly served well into his time as a dental student, eventually graduating with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Case…

Dr. Paulson and his wife moved to Upper Arlington to pursue his first career opportunity in dentistry. He joined a private practice on the southside of Columbus where he was soon offered partnership and eventually became sole owner. After 18 years of ownership, Dr. Paulson sold his practice, and after working for the new owners for an agreed upon amount of time, he joined Bauer Dental Group in…

In his time outside of the office, Dr. Paulson likes to get outdoors, particularly enjoying golf, hiking, and snow skiing. He likes to spend his time inside homebrewing, playing chess, and woodworking. Dr. Paulson is a lifelong Buckeye football fan and enjoys relaxing with family and friends when he’s not pursuing one of his many interests outside of work.

"By the time I reached high school, I knew I wanted to be a dentist. The profession provides a great platform for someone who likes to tinker with his hands, has an engineer mind, and wants to help people."
