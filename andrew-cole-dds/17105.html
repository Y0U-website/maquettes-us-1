Dental Implants

Losing a tooth due to decay or injury has a dramatic impact on your lifestyle and self-confidence. If you have missing teeth, it is crucial to replace them; simple actions like speaking and eating can be a challenge, and a missing tooth can even change your facial contours.

Is a Dental Implant Right for You?

If you’re concerned about your appearance and frustrated by the inconvenience of living with missing teeth, dental implants may be the answer you’re looking for.

Chehalis dentist, Dr. Andrew Cole, offers several tooth replacement options, and the type that is most popular with our patients are stable, durable dental implants.

Dental implants are a whole tooth replacement option that mimics natural tooth structure. An implant improves your appearance, and since it replaces the tooth root, it is also a healthy, long-term solution to tooth loss.

What Is a Dental Implant?

An implant has two main parts. Dr. Cole places a small titanium post in your jaw that’s left to heal and integrates with bone and tissue to become a permanent part of your dental anatomy. The functional, visible part of the implant is a restoration like a crown, bridge, or denture that completes your smile.

When we finish the implant process, you’ll be able to smile with confidence because no one will ever suspect you have a replacement tooth!

The Healthy Solution for Missing Teeth

Your teeth are designed to work together efficiently. When you lose a tooth, stress is placed on your remaining teeth when you eat and chew. This excess force over time can lead to chipped, cracked or broken teeth.

Missing teeth also cause alignment problems. With nothing to hold them in place, teeth next to the space will gradually move out of place, shifting their alignment. Tooth misalignment can lead to decay and gum disease because it’s harder to keep teeth clean when they are not aligned properly.
