Cindy Feagler, OMT, RDH

Cindy Feagler is an Orofacial Myofunctional Therapist who helps patients with tongue thrusting habits, snoring, poor tongue posture, mouth breathing, and other myofunctional dysfunction every day.

She has a passion to provide quality care, education, and therapy to all of her patients. She wants people to understand the detrimental effects of myofunctional impairments and the incredible benefits of the therapy she provides. She is especially interested in promoting full cranial facial development in children and helping with snoring and sleep disordered breathing.
