Dental Hygiene Opelika, AL Dentist Professional dental cleanings are important for maintaining great oral health. However, it is vital that you continue good dental hygiene at home as well. What you can do at home: Dr. Abigail Northcutt Adams recommends that you brush your teeth at least twice per day and floss daily. This helps prevent tooth decay, periodontal disease, and other oral health issues.
  • There are additional ways to help keep teeth strong and healthy. Toothpastes and mouth rinses with fluoride are available over-the-counter. Eating a balanced diet and limiting sugary snacks can also contribute to good oral health.
  • When you can, drink a glass of water after meals. This can help wash away sugars from foods and drinks.
  • If something you eat or drink contains acids, such as sodas or candies, wait 30 minutes before brushing. This will allow your saliva time to neutralize the acids and protect your teeth.
