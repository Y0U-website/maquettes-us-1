About CoreCare

Specialist care, delivered with warmth .

CoreCare Periodontics and Dental Implants is a specialty practice in Bedford, NH, caring for patients across New Hampshire, Northern Massachusetts, Maine, and Vermont.

Periodontics and dental implants — exclusively.

A modern home for periodontal specialty care.

CoreCare was built around a simple idea: specialist periodontal and implant care should feel calm, clear, and personal. Our team brings together advanced clinical training and a quiet, modern environment so patients can focus on what matters — their long-term oral health.

We work closely with referring dentists across the region to coordinate care that fits each patient&#x27;s individual needs.

Thoughtful, evidence-based specialty care.

To provide patients and referring partners with clear guidance, modern periodontal treatment, and implant solutions that support healthy smiles for a lifetime.

Specialists and care team.

A close-knit team of specialists, hygienists, assistants, and coordinators — each committed to a calm, personal, and clinically excellent experience.

Dr. Irina Adler, DDS, MS

Dr. Irina Adler completed her undergraduate studies at Pace University in New York, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biology with a Minor in Mathematics. She went on to graduate from the New York University College of Dentistry in 2003.

She completed a combined Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and General Practice Residency at New York Presbyterian Hospital of Cornell and Columbia Universities, together with Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center. In 2007, she completed her residency and specialty certification in Periodontology at Rutgers University.

Dr. Adler brings deep experience in periodontal and reconstructive surgery, sinus grafting, dental implants, oral pathology, and non-surgical periodontal therapy. She has practiced in private specialty settings across New Hampshire and Massachusetts since 2007 and serves as a board member of the Seattle Study Club.

Her clinical philosophy centers on personalized medicine and whole-person health — treating each patient as an individual, not a case. She lives in Southern New Hampshire with her husband and three children, and enjoys skiing, hiking, and cooking.

Dr. Ning Yu, DDS, PhD

Dr. Ning Yu grew up in Handan, China, and earned her dental degree from China Medical University in Shenyang before pursuing advanced training in the United States. She completed her periodontal specialty training at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor.

During her residency, she received the Orban Award from the American Academy of Periodontology in recognition of excellence in periodontal research. She currently holds a part-time teaching appointment at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where she continues to contribute to the education of the next generation of specialists.

Dr. Yu is known for her calm, thorough approach and her focus on long-term relationships with patients and referring doctors. Clear communication, compassionate expert care, and close collaboration guide everything she does clinically.

Outside of the practice, she enjoys reading non-fiction, running 5Ks, and relaxing by the pool with a good book. She lives with her husband Mao and their daughter Emma.
