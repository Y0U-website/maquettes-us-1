Welcome to A Family Dentist! For over 20 years, our dental office has been serving patients in Sun City Center, Bradenton, Apollo Beach, and surrounding areas with high-quality care.

Dr. Chi T. Nguyen and Dr. Cong "Anthony" Nguyen know that there is no such thing as one-size-fits-all dentistry. Every one of our valued patients is unique, and we provide comprehensive care and personalized treatment plans that we tailor to your specific needs.

But we don’t just tell you what we think you need; we use technology like digital x-rays and intraoral photos to show you exactly what the problem is and why you need a particular procedure. In dentistry, there is often more than one solution to a problem, and we will explore all your options and help you make comfortable, informed decisions about your treatment.
