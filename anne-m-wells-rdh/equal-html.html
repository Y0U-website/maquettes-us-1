About US

It is the policy of Sea Mar Community Health Centers not to discriminate against, or harass, any person employed or seeking employment or patient care with Sea Mar Community Health Centers on the basis of race, color, ethnicity, national origin, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, religion, familial status, physical, mental or other disability, war veteran status or age.

Sea Mar Community Health Centers abides by the Americans with Disabilities Act, and will provide reasonable accommodations to ensure access. Meetings and the provision of services will be provided in venues which are accessible. Individuals are encouraged to request that any barriers to access be addressed, including the use of auxiliary aids. Sea Mar uses the Washington Relay TTY (text to telephone) service, which may be accessed by dialing 7-1-1 to connect to a relay operator.
