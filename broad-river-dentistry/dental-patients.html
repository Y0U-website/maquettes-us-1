New Patient Appointment

At Broad River Dentistry of Irmo we prefer your first appointment be with the Doctor. New patient appointments are scheduled for 90 minutes to allow ample time for a complete evaluation and review of the treatment plan with the patient. The new patient appointment includes a comprehensive exam of hard and soft tissues, appropriate radiographs, evaluation of the gum and bone health, and intraoral photos. The recommended type of hygiene appointment will also be determined during this appointment.

Insurance

We want to make your dental insurance claim worry free! Be aware that insurance benefits don’t always include every part of your dental treatment. Please be sure to review your insurance benefits before your treatment to be aware of any out-of-pocket expense you may incur.

It is important to understand the value of your dental care and to not neglect your treatment due to your insurance limitations. We offer CreditCare financing to help you manage any unexpected expenses you may incur that are not covered by your insurance. We are happy to talk to you about your financial payment options so you don’t have to neglect the dental care you need based on your financial situation.
