Dental Vision/Mission Statement

Dr. Orman

Dr. Patrick Orman was born and raised in Charlotte, NC. He has ties to the area as both his parents are from Columbia and are USC Alumni. He attended the University of South Carolina earning his BS in Biology. He continued his education at MUSC in Charleston earning his DMD.

Dr. Patrick Orman focuses on patient centered dentistry where the patient is treated like his own family. He believes in using the current technology and techniques to deliver high quality dentistry in an honest, gentle and caring environment. Outside of dentistry, his passions include spending time with family, golfing, and sports. He is proud to be a girl dad to his 2 daughters, Emersyn and Sophie.

Kyndall, Dental Assistant

Originally from Blythewood, Kyndall has always loved going to the dentist as a child. She is fairly new to dentistry but has known that this was her passion since she was a little girl! She has been with our team since 2022. Kyndall is a cat mom to her fur baby “Mila”. Angie, Dental Assistant

Originally from Charlotte, NC, Angie attended Western Carolina University to study Speech Pathology, then later transitioned into dentistry in 1999. With her years of experience, Angie’s goal is to ensure patients feel comfortable and fully informed throughout their visit.

In her free time, Angie loves spending time with her husband, Kevin, her two children, Savanna and Carson, and 4 fur babies, just hanging out as a family. She also enjoys traveling and going to concerts.

Karen, Treatment Coordinator

With over 20 years experience, Karen brings compassion, clarity and efficiency to every patient interaction. She serves as a vital bridge between clinical care and patient understanding, helping them navigate their dental treatment options with confidence and ease. Her goal is to ensure that every treatment plan aligns with our commitment to providing excellent, personalized dental care.

Outside the office, Karen enjoys reading, spending time with her husband, Matt, and her 2 dogs, Winston and Nashville.
