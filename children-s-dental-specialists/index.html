The practice

Dr. Ifechide Nwabueze (Dr. Ife) graduated from the University of Benin, Nigeria, with a degree in dental surgery in 2003. Dr. Ife worked for two years as a general dentist in an Army hospital in Nigeria, where she specialized in pediatrics, orthodontics, restoration, oral surgery, and periodontics. It was during this time that she discovered her passion for working with children.

Dr. Ife earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, in 2010. She went on to complete her Pediatric Dentistry training at the Children’s Hospital of Michigan in Detroit. During her time there, she focused on pediatric clinical and hospital dentistry, including conscious sedation, dental surgery, and emergency dental services. Also, she worked with…

Upon successful completion of her pediatric residency training, Dr. Ife worked as an associate in Dr. Amy Parker’s pediatric dental practice and also in Children’s Dental Care, all in Michigan.

Dr. Ife is committed to caring for children’s teeth in a clean, comfortable, and welcoming environment. She has a true passion for caring and treating children, and she strives to make each child’s visit to the dentist a fun and pleasant one.

In addition to teaching undergraduates at the University Of Detroit Mercy Dental School, Dr. Ife has published numerous articles relating to pediatric dentistry and pre-veneered stainless steel crowns on primary molars. Dr. Mary Hannawa Calek (Dr. Mary) is a Michigan native. She completed her dental school training at the University of Detroit Mercy. She worked for a couple of years as a general dentist before she went back for pediatric dentistry training at the Children’s Hospital of Michigan and became a pediatric dentist.

Dr. Mary’s goal is to make sure each child has a positive experience at the dental office. She is an active member of the American Association of Pediatric Dentistry, The Michigan Dental Association, and The American Dental Association.

In her free time, she and her husband enjoy spending time with their family and friends. Dr. Mary also likes reading, traveling, and taking her dog Oreo on long walks.
