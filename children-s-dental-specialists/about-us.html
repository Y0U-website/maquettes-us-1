Meet Dr. Mary

Dr. Mary Hannawa Calek (Dr. Mary) is a Michigan native. She completed her dental school training at the University of Detroit Mercy. She worked for a couple of years as a general dentist before she went back for pediatric dentistry training at the Children’s Hospital of Michigan and became a pediatric dentist.

Dr. Mary’s goal is to make sure each child has a positive experience at the dental office. She is an active member of the American Association of Pediatric Dentistry, The Michigan Dental Association, and The American Dental Association.

In her free time, she and her husband enjoy spending time with their family and friends. Dr. Mary also likes reading, traveling, and taking her dog Oreo on long walks.

Meet Dr. Hiba

Dr. Hiba AlSafi is a pediatric dentist. She completed her Bachelor of Science degree at the University of Florida. Later, moving to Ann Arbor, she completed her DDS degree at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She practiced general dentistry for two years. Then, she decided to follow her passion and continue her education in the Pediatric Dentistry program at Children’s Hospital of Michigan, where she became a pediatric dentist.

Dr. Hiba is married and has two young children. She enjoys traveling with family, and she loves to cook and try out different recipes.

Meet Dr. Jordan

Dr. Jordan Duarte grew up in Michigan and completed her undergraduate degree at the University of Detroit Mercy. She then moved to Boston to complete her graduate degree in dentistry (DMD) at Boston University.

Once back in Michigan, she completed her pediatric dental residency at Children’s Hospital of Michigan.

Dr. Jordan is married to her husband, Michael. They have a dog named Mila, who is the cutest little havashu, and they love her very much. She loves to read books, bake, and craft when she can.

Meet Dr. Kim

Dr. Kim is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. Prior to pursuing a career in dentistry, she was a consultant working in Washington, DC. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kim enjoys cooking, traveling, and spending time with her family.
