Early Treatment

The right treatment at the right time can make all the difference.

Why Consider Early Treatment?

We evaluate how your child’s teeth and jaws are developing to determine if early treatment may be beneficial.

Early treatment can help guide jaw growth and create room for permanent teeth when appropriate.

Addressing certain issues early may reduce the need for more complicated treatment later.

Sometimes the best treatment is simply monitoring growth until the timing is right.

Early Treatment Before & After

Early Treatment FAQs

Why should my child see an orthodontist by age 7? +

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends every child have an orthodontic evaluation by age 7. At this age, we can identify developing problems and determine whether treatment is needed now or simply monitor growth.

Does every child need early treatment? +

No. Many children do not need treatment right away. During your child’s evaluation, Dr. Cureton will determine the best time to begin treatment based on their individual needs.

Is the consultation free? +

Yes! We offer complimentary orthodontic evaluations to determine whether your child is ready for treatment or should simply be monitored as they grow.

What problems can early treatment help with? +

Early treatment may be recommended for issues such as severe crowding, crossbites, underbites, certain bite problems, and jaw growth concerns.

Will early treatment prevent braces later? +

Sometimes. In many cases, early treatment makes future treatment simpler and more predictable, but every child is different. We’ll discuss what to expect during your consultation.

Not sure if your child is ready?

The best way to know is with a complimentary orthodontic evaluation. We’ll answer your questions, evaluate your child’s smile, and recommend the right treatment at the right time—without any pressure.
