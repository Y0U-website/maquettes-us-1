Dental Implants Q&A

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium devices that look similar to screws. They go into your jawbone to provide a strong foundation for dental restorations.

Thanks to your jawbone’s natural ability to grow around your titanium implants in a process called osseointegration, dental implants are a sturdy solution to missing teeth.

They allow you to eat and speak as you did before losing your teeth while also preventing bone loss, tooth migration, and other complications of missing teeth.

Dr. Cannon reviews the images to find out if you need a bone graft before your procedure to plan your implant surgery.

What kinds of restorations can dental implants secure?

Dental implants can secure multiple types of custom dental restorations in place, depending on the number of teeth you’re missing. Using dental implants for support, Dr. Cannon can secure:

Dental crowns

If you’re only missing one tooth, a single dental implant supports a custom crown designed to look just like the original that your general dentist will complete.

Bridges

Implant-supported bridges are similar to traditional bridges, but they don’t rely on healthy teeth for support. Instead, they include two crowns to latch onto implants and one or more pontics (false teeth) in-between.

Dentures

Several implants, usually four, can support a full row of dentures for your upper or lower jaw.
