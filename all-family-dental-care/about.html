Restorative & Family Dentistry

Dr. Witte’s restorative experience includes crowns, bridges, veneers, fillings, implant restorations, dentures, overdentures, and simple extractions. She has experience designing CEREC crowns and working with digital scanning systems including Primescan and Omniscan.

She also provides routine continuing-care and new-patient examinations, with treatment planning focused on patient education and prevention.

Communication First

High-quality dental care depends on communication. Dr. Witte values collaboration with the full dental team so patients have a smooth and supportive experience throughout their visit.

Whether a patient is visiting for preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic questions, or a dental emergency, her focus is the same: listen first, explain clearly, and provide care with skill and compassion.
