You stay with your child for every visit, every step. No closed doors, no separation anxiety.

The practice

We narrate everything in kid-words, go slow, and let your child set the pace. No restraint, no scare tactics.

If your child isn’t ready, we wait. We never upsell, never push. You leave knowing exactly what’s needed.

Gentle Cleanings A spa visit for tiny teeth. Soft tools, fluoride, fun flavors. Most kids leave smiling.

Fixing Cavities Without the Stress Tooth-colored fillings done in fewer, longer visits. Sedation available. We explain everything before we start.

Sealants & Fluoride The treatments that prevent most cavities before they start. Often covered fully by insurance.

Sedation Dentistry for Kids For anxious children or longer procedures. Always optional, always your decision.

Special Needs Dentistry Extended appointments. Quiet rooms. Experience with autism, ADHD, and sensory processing differences.

Full breakdown of pediatric dental care available at our North Port office.

Les soins

First-Visit Checkups A no-pressure introduction. We count teeth, take photos, give a tour, and answer every question no procedures unless you’re ready.

Every service is delivered with parents in the room, age-appropriate language, and the option to slow down or stop at any moment.
