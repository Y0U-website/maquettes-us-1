The practice

Ensuring your questions about the safety and efficacy of fillings and the materials used is at the forefront of our care: we make sure you know exactly what the plan is, and how we will help protect your healthiest smile.

A dental crown or “cap” is a covering that fits over a damaged, decayed or unattractive tooth. It can even replace a tooth entirely as part of dental bridgework, and can help you build an even better smile.

Dentistry encompasses an array of services and procedures with a common goal: to help you to preserve your natural teeth, ensure your oral health, and keep you looking and feeling great.

An important part of your smile is making sure it looks fresh and clean. Whitening procedures have effectively restored the site of people with stained, dull, or discolored teeth.

Before the development of dental implants, dentures were the only alternatives to replacing a missing tooth or teeth. Now with implants, you can have longer-lasting results that also help preserve tooth-supporting bone.

Dental care means not only caring for the teeth themselves, but also ensuring the health of the structures that surround them: the gums and tooth-supporting bone to prevent gum disease.

Properly aligned teeth help you to bite, chew and even speak more effectively. They are also easier to clean, which helps keep your mouth free of tooth decay and gum disease.
