Aesthetic Services

0 Aesthetic Services The goal of medical beauty is to achieve a balance between health and beauty rather than aim for unachievable results that may not enhance a patient’s natural appearance, confidence, and comfort in their own skin. For some, aesthetic services provide a more natural approach to anti-aging treatments than invasive cosmetic surgery.

Neuromodulators

Neuromodulators are wrinkle-relaxing injections of botulinum toxin – commercially known as Botox®, Xeomin® or Jeuveau® – that are used to treat wrinkles, frown lines and crow’s feet. These are quick and easy ways to “refresh” the face for a more relaxed appearance.

Injectables

As we age, the “padding” in the face starts to break down and lose its fullness, leading to the appearance of wrinkles or deep lines. Fillers add volume back to the face with a naturally occurring substance in the body called hyaluronic acid. These treatments can be applied to various parts of the face to lend a more youthful appearance.

Veneers

A veneer is a wafer-thin layer of super-strong porcelain that convincingly substitutes for natural tooth enamel. When bonded to your teeth, veneers can create a natural-looking, beautiful new surface.

Invisalign ®

Invisalign ® clear aligners work the same way traditional braces do: by applying carefully controlled forces to teeth to move them into a better position. They are made of clear, flexible plastic for a more comfortable experience and natural appearance.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening done in a professional dental setting is a safe, effective way to brighten your smile. It’s also perhaps the most economical cosmetic dental procedure. Depending on the whitening method you choose, results can be dramatic: in-office whitening, for example, can lighten teeth three to eight shades in a single hour.
