Frequently Asked Questions

Request Appointment At what age should my child have an orthodontic evaluation? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7. This allows us to identify potential problems early—even if treatment doesn’t begin right away.

Are orthodontic treatments only for teens?

Not at all. Adults can benefit greatly from orthodontic care, especially with options like clear aligners that offer discretion and flexibility. We regularly help adult patients correct bite issues, spacing, or relapse from past treatments.

How do I know if I need braces or clear aligners?

During your consultation, we’ll assess your teeth, bite, jaw function, and lifestyle needs. Some cases respond better to braces, while others may be ideal for aligners. In certain cases, a hybrid approach is recommended.

How long will orthodontic treatment take?

Most treatments range from 12 to 30 months depending on complexity. Complex cases may require additional time, but we’ll give you a detailed plan and estimated timeline up front.

Will insurance cover orthodontic care?

Many dental insurance plans offer partial coverage for orthodontic treatment, especially for children. We’ll help verify your benefits, explain costs, and offer flexible payment plans if needed.
