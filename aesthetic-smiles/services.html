Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

Dental implants are ideal for many adults with missing teeth, though candidacy depends on bone health, medical history, and personal goals. We assess this during your initial consultation.

How long do cosmetic procedures like veneers or whitening last?

Veneers can last 10–15 years or more with proper care, while professional whitening may last several months to a few years depending on lifestyle habits.

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes. All forms of sedation we offer are administered following rigorous safety standards and tailored to your individual medical profile and procedure type.

Do you accept insurance or offer financing?

We accept most dental insurance plans and provide flexible payment solutions through CareCredit and in-house financing to make care more affordable.
