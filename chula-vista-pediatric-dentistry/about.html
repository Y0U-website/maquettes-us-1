Dr. Patricio Andres

Pediatric Dentist

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Pat believes he can make a difference in this world, one child at a time, one tooth at a time. He realized his interest in dentistry while attending San Diego State University.

After graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Biology, Dr. Pat left his native Southern California to attend dental school at New York University and graduated in May of 2000. It was during his Advanced Education in General Dentistry at Lutheran Medical Center in Brooklyn, NY that his interest in caring for children grew.

He went on to further his education and ability to care for the dental needs of children by attending the Pediatric Dentistry residency program at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, NY. Upon graduating in 2007, Dr. Pat couldn&#x27;t wait to return to his home in San Diego, CA to care for and serve the dental needs of the children of San Diego. He also serves the dental needs of children in Henderson, NV.

Dr. Pat is very passionate about caring for the children that are entrusted to him, in both private practice and the various volunteer missions that he has taken part in.

In his time away from the dental office, Dr. Pat enjoys having fun and laughing with family and friends, cooking, watching his favorite sports teams compete, watching TV and movies, snowboarding in the winter, playing golf year-round in Southern California, and traveling.

Dr. Marco Savittieri

Board-Certified Dentist Anesthesiologist

Dr. Marco Savittieri

Board-Certified Dentist Anesthesiologist

Dr. Marco Savittieri is a board-certified dentist anesthesiologist specialist providing general anesthesia, intravenous sedation and pain management services for patients of all ages requiring restorative dentistry or maxillofacial surgery in a private office setting. His goal is to provide safe, compassionate, hospital-quality care, tailored to the patient’s specific needs and medical history, while fostering a peaceful and calm environment.

Upon completion of his residency, Dr. Savittieri practiced as a mobile anesthesia provider for multiple enterprises – Comfort Dental Anesthesia, The Los Angeles Center of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, and Desert State Dental Anesthesia. Most of his practice is dedicated to pediatric and special needs patients. He is also devoted to servicing dental-phobic adult and geriatric patients, with complex medical conditions, requiring extensive dental rehabilitation.

Dr. Savittieri currently lives and practices in San Diego. He is a diplomate for the American Dental Board of Anesthesiology and the American Society of Dentist Anesthesiologists. He holds current Advanced Cardiac Life Support and Pediatric Advanced Life Support certification. Dr. Savittieri loves his job, which perfectly combines his passion for science and enthusiasm for quality patient care. Dr. Savittieri is a dedicated family man. In his free time, Dr. Savittieri enjoys all that sunny California has to offer surfing, hiking, and sandy beach days with his wife and kids.

Dr. Regina Dowdy

Board-Certified Dentist Anesthesiologist

Dr. Regina Dowdy

Board-Certified Dentist Anesthesiologist

A San Diego native, Dr. Gina Dowdy is happy to be back in hometown providing anesthesia services in her community. During her time at USC she developed a passion to care for pediatric and special needs populations, which was further promoted during her residency.

Dr. Gina attended The Ohio State University for her dental anesthesiology residency. Here, she was able to serve patients of all ages and health complexities. Her favorite rotations were spent working at Nationwide Children’s Hospital and Ohio State Wexner Medical Center’s medical and surgical intensive care units. During her residency she was fortunate to speak at anesthesia conferences, participate in research, and publish articles.

Humanitarian work is a large part of Dr. Gina’s training. She has travelled globally, volunteering either as a general dentist or a dental anesthesiologist. She has served in Ghana, Mexico, Peru, Fiji, and the Dominican Republic. The Dominican Republic was an especially rewarding experience as she was able to utilize her anesthesia training and adapt it to the equipment and supplies that were available, differing from her traditional hospital operating rooms.

In her free time, you may catch Dr. Gina down in Ocean Beach playing softball or with her dogs at dog beach soaking in the San Diego sun.

Our Technology

Using the latest in dental technology helps us make sure your child is having the best dental experience possible. Digital X-rays require up to 90 % less radiation than traditional films and give us a clearer image to make sure your child’s bones and teeth are developing properly. Sedation dentistry helps us work quickly while your child is relaxed. This makes for a faster appointment and a positive dental memory for your child.
