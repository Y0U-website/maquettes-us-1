Hygienists, dental assistants, and front-office staff — the people who make Balsam Dental work day to day. All of them bring years of experience. All have been chosen with care.

Tammy

Tammy has been a dental hygienist since 2004 and previously worked as an outstanding dental assistant. With over 36 years in dentistry, she truly loves what she does, caring for patients who often become like extended family over time. Known for her gentle approach and attention to detail, Tammy takes pride in helping patients feel comfortable, heard, and confident in their oral health. She especially enjoys taking the time to educate patients in a way that feels easy to understand and never rushed.

Originally from Maine, Tammy relocated to southwest Florida as a young adult, where she and her husband of 25 years raised their family. These days, she enjoys mountain life and all the beauty it has to offer. When she’s not in the office, you’ll likely find her spending time with family, enjoying the outdoors, or simply appreciating a quiet moment in nature.

Tammy is known for her calm, compassionate presence and her ability to make patients feel truly cared for, comfortable, and at ease throughout every visit.
