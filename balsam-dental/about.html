About

A small practice with deep roots.

Family- and veteran-owned. Two doctors, one team, both in dentistry since 2012 — now practicing together in downtown Waynesville.

Two Army dentists, one community.

Welcome to Balsam Dental, a veteran and family-owned dental practice serving Waynesville and the surrounding Western North Carolina community.

We believe dental care should feel personal. It should fit into busy lives, feel comfortable for patients of all ages, and be built on relationships that last.

Welcome to the family

Dr. Serene Nagia and Dr. Andrew Sharman first met while serving on active duty in the U.S. Army. After graduating from dental school in 2012 and spending years caring for patients across the East Coast, they discovered a shared vision for what a dental practice could be.

Not just a place to receive treatment, but a place where people feel known.

That vision led them to Waynesville and eventually to Balsam Dental.

Named after the balsam trees that surround these mountains, our practice was built on values that have guided us throughout our careers: integrity, consistency, compassion, and service.

Today, we continue to serve Haywood County and Western North Carolina with the same philosophy we started with:

Treat people well. Listen first. Provide excellent care. Build trust over time.

Whether you’re bringing in your child for a first visit, keeping up with routine care, or looking for a long-term dental home, we’re glad you’re here.

Three things we believe.

Unhurried appointments.

Visits are scheduled with enough time to explain what’s happening and answer questions. No rush.

Clear treatment plans.

Every exam ends with a written treatment plan: priorities, timing, and estimated costs in plain English.

Modern equipment.

Digital imaging, Primescan impressions, CEREC same-day crowns, and cone-beam 3D scanning, used routinely.

Dr. Serene Nagia

DDS · University of Detroit Mercy, 2012 · U.S. Army Veteran

Dr. Serene Nagia was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan, and earned her dental degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in 2012. Following graduation, she served as an active-duty dentist in the U.S. Army, where she met her husband, Dr. Andrew Sharman. During her military service, she had the privilege of caring not only for service members, but also for military working dogs, providing essential treatment such as extractions and root canals for active-duty canine patients.

While Dr. Serene excels in all areas of general dentistry, she has a particular passion for replacing missing teeth with dental implants and helping patients restore both function and confidence through improved esthetics. She is committed to creating a positive, comfortable dental experience and takes time to educate patients and clearly explain each step of care.

Having lived and practiced in several locations along the East Coast, she feels most at home in the mountains of Western North Carolina and is proud to serve the community she now calls home.

Outside the office, she continues her passion for animal care by volunteering with rescue organizations and providing treatment for exotic and wild animals, including lions, tigers, bears, and more. She shares her home with her dog, Lucy, and her cat, Lemon.

Dr. Andrew Sharman

DDS · University of Buffalo, 2012 · U.S. Army Veteran

Dr. Andrew Sharman was born and raised in Buffalo, New York, where he spent 26 years bravely surviving the winter weather — and developing a strong respect for snow shovels. He earned his dental degree from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 2012 and went on to complete a residency with the U.S. Army, where he served as an active-duty dentist. During his military service, he earned his Airborne "Jump Wings," embraced the discipline of military life, and met his wife, Dr. Serene Nagia.

Following his service, Dr. Sharman practiced at the Asheville VA Hospital, providing comprehensive dental care to veterans. He has a strong passion for all aspects of general dentistry, with a particular focus on full-mouth rehabilitation cases that restore both function and long-term confidence.

Outside the office, he can usually be found fly fishing, exploring Western North Carolina’s mountains, or being outvoted at home by his dog Lucy and cat Lemon. A devoted Buffalo Bills fan, he brings humor, humility, and dedication to both his patients and his community.
