Orthodontics for Teens in Lafayette

Confidence That Grows With Them

The teen years are an ideal time for orthodontic treatment. Growth is still active, habits are developing, and confidence is taking shape.

Whether your teen is starting treatment for the first time or entering Phase II treatment after a previous Phase I treatment, Dr. Donald Balhoff makes the process smooth, efficient, and supportive.

We understand that teens balance school, activities, and social commitments. That’s why we offer treatment options that fit their pace and help them feel comfortable at every stage of their smile transformation.

Treatment Options for Teens

Every teen’s smile is unique. Dr. Balhoff provides a full range of treatments designed to match comfort, lifestyle, and orthodontic goals, including:

Whichever option your teen chooses, we create a personalized plan focused on great results, day-to-day comfort, and long-term confidence.

  • Modern metal braces: a durable, effective option trusted for reliable results
  • Clear braces: tooth-colored ceramic braces for a more discreet appearance
  • Clear aligners: removable trays ideal for active teens who want flexibility

Benefits of Teen Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment during the teen years sets the stage for lifelong oral health. With Dr. Balhoff’s guidance, treatment during the teen years is started at the optimal time based on the patient’s growth stage and eruption of permanent teeth. By starting the proper treatment at the proper time, more complicated treatments, sometimes involving jaw surgery, can be minimized or avoided in the future. Our patients’ care is structured to fit naturally into busy schedules while helping teens achieve a strong, stable bite and a well-aligned smile.

Confidence That Lasts

A straight, healthy smile gives teens meaningful confidence, whether they’re in class, on the field, or taking photos with friends.

Healthier Teeth & Gums

Aligned teeth are easier to brush and floss, helping reduce the risk of cavities, gum inflammation, and enamel wear. Good alignment supports long-term oral health and function.

Flexible Options

From removable clear aligners to low-profile clear or metal braces, we offer solutions that fit your teen’s lifestyle and comfort preferences, keeping them motivated, engaged, and excited about their smile.

Orthodontics for Adults in Lafayette

It’s Never Too Late for a Smile You Love

More adults than ever are choosing orthodontic treatment, and with today’s discreet, efficient options, it’s easier to fit into a busy lifestyle. Clear aligners, subtle clear braces, and modern digital planning make it possible to improve your smile comfortably and confidently at any age.

At Balhoff Orthodontics, adults can address shifting teeth, improve bite function, and boost overall oral health. Whether this is your first time seeking treatment or you’re returning after years, our approach is streamlined, flexible, and tailored to your goals and schedule.

Why Adults Choose Orthodontics

Adult orthodontics is about more than aesthetics. Straightening your teeth supports long-term oral health and can help prevent future complications. Treatment can:

  • Make brushing and flossing more effective
  • Reduce enamel wear and jaw strain
  • Help prevent gum disease and bone loss
  • Boost confidence at work, at home, and everywhere in between

Treatment Options for Adults

We offer solutions designed with comfort, discretion, and efficiency in mind:

Whichever option you choose, Dr. Donald Balhoff provides care that’s digitally planned, comfortable, and built for long-term stability.

  • Clear aligners : nearly invisible and removable for meals and cleaning
  • Clear braces : smooth ceramic brackets that blend naturally with your teeth
  • Metal braces : modern, low-profile brackets that deliver reliable, precise movement

Better Health, Better Function
