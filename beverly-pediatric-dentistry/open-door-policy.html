Reducing anxiety early helps prevent dental avoidance later in life and builds lifelong healthy habits.

Early dental visits shape how children feel about dentistry for years to come. Children who feel emotionally supported during early visits are more likely to return willingly and maintain regular preventive care.

For many children, especially toddlers and younger patients, separation can increase fear, but a parent’s calm presence can make a significant difference in how they perceive their dental experience.
