Who We Are

As the leading boutique pediatric dental practice, with two offices conveniently located in McLean, VA , and Dupont Circle, Washington D.C. , we are dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for children of all ages, from toddlers to teenagers, ensuring every child is supported with care and attention.

Our team of skilled pediatric dentists and dental professionals is committed to creating a welcoming and comfortable environment where young patients can feel safe and at ease.

We offer comprehensive dental services, including routine check-ups, cleanings, fillings, extractions, and orthodontic evaluations. We aim to help children develop healthy oral habits that will last a lifetime.

Book an appointment for you kids now! Our two clinics are convenient for families live in Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia, or towns in Maryland that surround Washington D.C.

Vision To be the premier pediatric dental practice, renowned for providing compassionate, innovative, and family-centered care that exceeds the expectations of our patients.

Mission We are committed to fostering a welcoming and supportive environment where children can receive the highest quality dental care in a comfortable, nurturing setting.

By emphasizing prevention, conservative approaches, and involving parents in the treatment process, we strive to create positive dental experiences that build lifelong oral health and confidence.

Our Community Involvement Over the years, we proudly sponsored many local sports teams, including McLean Little League, McLean Youth Soccer, the Capitol Hill Little League, and Northwest Washington Little League.

We hold an Annual Charity Golf Tournament to raise awareness for childhood cancer, and neuroblastoma. Every year, we sponsored the Oral Cancer Foundation 5K Walk/Run for Awareness in memory of Peter McGee Hoffman.

We participated in Virginia’s Missions of Mercy Program, which provides free dental care to underserved populations in our community.
