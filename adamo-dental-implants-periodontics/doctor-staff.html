Doctor Staff

We have an energetic group of people who are ready & able to serve you!

Dr. Charles Adamo

Dr. Charles Adamo grew up in San Antonio Texas. He graduated from the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio, Dental School in 1989 and immediately joined the Air Force. After serving seven years as a general dentist, he was granted an honorable discharge so that he could pursue specialty training at the Medical College of Georgia. This intense three-year training program gave him expertise in periodontics, dental implants, and intravenous sedation. Upon graduation, he moved to Central Texas with his family to open his Periodontics and Dental Implant practice.

He has been married to his wife, Wendy, for over 30 years. They have twin sons and a daughter.

Fun Fact about Dr. A: His patients appreciate the excellent care he provides while putting them at ease with his endless supply of really bad jokes .
