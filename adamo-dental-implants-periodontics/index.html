Les soins

Dr. Adamo is proud to offer multiple levels of oral surgery and care services including Dental Implants, Cosmetic Surgery, and Periodontal Disease prevention and treatment.

Dental implants are tiny titanium posts placed into the jawbone where teeth are missing. Dental implants are placed in your jaw to hold a bridge or tooth in place. The great thing about dental implants is that they do not rely on neighboring teeth to stay in…

When teeth are missing, the bone that supported those teeth deteriorates. With dental implant surgery, the bone can be preserved by replacing tooth roots with dental implants. The person who has lost teeth regains the ability to smile with confidence.
