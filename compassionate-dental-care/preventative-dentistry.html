Frequently Asked Questions

How often should i really visit the dentist?

Learn More What happens during a dental cleaning? Learn More How do i prevent cavities and gum disease? Learn More Q How often should I visit the dentist for preventative care?

Most patients benefit from visiting the dentist every six months for professional cleanings and exams. However, some patients with specific oral health concerns may require more frequent visits based on their individual needs.

Why are professional dental cleanings important?

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that cannot always be removed with brushing and flossing alone. Regular cleanings help reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease, and bad breath while supporting overall oral health.

What happens during an oral cancer screening?

During an oral cancer screening, we examine the mouth, gums, tongue, and surrounding tissues for unusual changes or abnormalities. The screening is quick, comfortable, and performed as part of your routine preventative visit.

Dental sealants are commonly recommended for children, but some adults may also benefit from sealants depending on their oral health needs and cavity risk.

A custom night guard helps protect teeth from grinding and clenching during sleep. Night guards can reduce tooth wear, jaw discomfort, and damage caused by bruxism.

What are the best ways to prevent cavities at home?

Brushing twice daily, flossing every day, reducing sugary foods and drinks, and maintaining regular dental visits are some of the best ways to help prevent cavities and maintain healthy teeth.
