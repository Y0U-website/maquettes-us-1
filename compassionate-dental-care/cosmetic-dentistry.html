Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of teeth, gums, and overall smile aesthetics while also supporting dental health and function.

What treatments are included in cosmetic dentistry?

Common treatments include teeth whitening, veneers, dental bonding, tooth-colored fillings, and gum reshaping procedures.

How long do cosmetic dental results last?

The longevity depends on the treatment. Teeth whitening may last months to years, while veneers and bonding can last many years with proper care.

Is cosmetic dentistry only for appearance?

No, many cosmetic treatments also improve dental function, protect teeth, and support long-term oral health.

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive and involve little to no discomfort. Your dentist will ensure you are comfortable throughout treatment.

How do I know which cosmetic treatment is right for me?

A cosmetic consultation allows your dentist to evaluate your smile and recommend the most suitable treatment options based on your goals.
