Dr. Aakriti Chaudhry

Dr. Aakriti Chaudhry always knew she wanted to pursue a career in healthcare, and dentistry became her passion as she discovered the impact a confident and healthy smile can have on a person’s well-being. She earned both her Bachelor of Science and Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degrees from The Ohio State University and is committed to making every dental visit a comfortable and positive experience.

Originally from Columbus, Dr. Chaudhry is excited to bring her expertise to Cincinnati, where she provides comprehensive care for patients of all ages. With a strong background in pediatric and family dentistry, she is dedicated to helping patients build lifelong healthy habits. She stays up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry and is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and local and national study clubs.

Outside the office, Dr. Chaudhry is a huge sports fan—a proud supporter of the Ohio State Buckeyes and the Cleveland Cavaliers. Now that she’s in Cincinnati, she can’t wait to cheer on the Bengals! She also enjoys baking, exploring new restaurants, practicing yoga, and spending time with her family. Dr. Chaudhry looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients and becoming part of the Cincinnati community.
