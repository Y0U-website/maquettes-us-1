Frequently Asked Questions

A single tooth implant replaces just the missing tooth without affecting any neighboring teeth. A bridge requires grinding down the two teeth on either side of the gap to serve as anchors permanently altering healthy tooth structure. An implant also preserves the jawbone beneath the gap, which a bridge cannot do. For most patients with a single missing tooth and adequate bone density, an implant is the superior long-term solution.

The complete process typically takes 3 to 5 months. After the titanium post is placed, your jawbone needs 2 to 4 months to fuse with it. Once that is complete, we place the abutment and take impressions for your custom crown. The crown is usually ready within 2 to 3 weeks. Some patients qualify for same-day implants if bone density is sufficient, which we assess at your consultation.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and sedation is available for anxious patients. Most patients report feeling pressure but no pain during the placement. Afterward, mild soreness for 1 to 2 days is typical and is usually managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients return to normal activities the next day.

Yes. The crown is custom-fabricated from high-quality porcelain and matched precisely to the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. We take detailed impressions and shade measurements to ensure the crown is indistinguishable from your natural teeth. Most patients tell us they genuinely cannot identify which tooth is the implant after a few weeks.

Insufficient bone density is one of the most common concerns patients have, but it is often solvable. Bone grafting can rebuild the jawbone in the area of the missing tooth, creating a stable foundation for the implant. We perform bone grafting at Anderson Smile Center and will assess whether it is needed at your consultation.

Caring for a single tooth implant is straightforward you brush and floss it exactly like a natural tooth. There are no special adhesives, no removal, and no soaking required. We recommend using a soft-bristled toothbrush and flossing daily around the implant. Regular dental check-ups every six months allow us to monitor the implant and ensure everything remains healthy.
