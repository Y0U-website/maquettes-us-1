After dental school, Dr. Corbin completed her residency at Texas A&M College of Dentistry (formerly Baylor) and trained at both the Children’s Medical Center of Dallas and Texas Scottish Rite Hospital, gaining specialized pediatric experience.

Outside of her practice, Dr. Corbin enjoys spending time with her husband Zach, their two children Collins and Keller, and their beloved cat Burreaux. Whether she’s cheering on the LSU Tigers or traveling with her family, she cherishes life’s joyful moments.

Dr. Corbin is a proud member of the ADA, LDA, AAPD, LAPD, and is a Diplomate of the ABPD, demonstrating her commitment to the highest standards in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Kleefisch continued her education with a residency in pediatric dentistry at Virginia Commonwealth University, training at the Children’s Hospital of Richmond. During this time, she also earned a Master of Science in Dentistry and was named chief resident, receiving the prestigious Alexander Fellowship for research.

Beyond her professional accolades, Dr. Kleefisch is deeply committed to family. She is married to Ray, a dentist in the U.S. Navy, and together they have two daughters, Eliza Jane and Margaret Katherine, and a golden retriever named Goose.
