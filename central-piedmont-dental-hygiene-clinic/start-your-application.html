New Students Who Want a Degree, Diploma, or Certificate

Step 1: Check or establish your state residency and apply to Central Piedmont .

Step 2: Set up your Central Piedmont account and email. Sign up for multifactor authentication with Microsoft Authenticator.

Step 4: Submit your transcripts and meet with your advisor.

Learn more about the application steps for new students .

Returning Students

Step 1: Complete this Returning Student form if you previously submitted a college credit application and have not attended Central Piedmont within the past 12 months.

Step 2: Once you receive an email with your account information, activate your account .

Step 3: Log into MyConnect . Schedule an appointment with your academic advisor to discuss your academic plan and register for classes.

Step 4: Search and apply for more than $4 million available in Central Piedmont scholarships, apply for financial aid, and enroll in the tuition payment plan to spread payments out over time.

Earn College Credit While in High School

Step 1: Apply to Central Piedmont . Complete the Career and College Promise application.

Step 2: Visit the dual enrollment page to learn more about the enrollment process.

Veterans and Military Families

Continuing Education

Step 1: Explore our continuing education course offerings .

Step 2: Register and pay online, via phone, or in person.

Step 3: Establish your student login if your course is online.

Step 4: Sign up for multifactor authentication.

Continuing Education students do not need to complete the Central Piedmont application.

Find continuing education classes and learn how to register .

Healthcare Students

Step 1: Explore our healthcare programs .

Step 2: Complete this form and review the Health Careers Orientation and Advising video.

Step 3: Check or establish your state residency and apply to Central Piedmont .

Step 4: Set up your Central Piedmont account and email.

Step 6: Submit your transcripts and meet with your advisor.

Step 5: Submit your healthcare program application.

Learn more about applying for a healthcare program .

International Students with an F-1 Visa

Step 1: Submit the international student application and the application fee.

Step 2: Provide evidence of financial ability and signed guarantee of financial support.

Step 3: Submit official copies of your diploma and transcripts.

Step 4: Submit your immigration documents.

Step 5: Provide evidence of English competency.

Step 6: Set up your Central Piedmont account and email.

Step 8: Meet with your advisor and register for classes.

Learn more about applying as an international student .

Finish High School, Get Career Training, Learn English

1. Submit an online application . Depending on your chosen program , you may be asked to submit supplemental materials.

2. Attend orientation and, depending on your program, complete a placement test.
