Request Accommodations for a Student Meeting or Event

If you need an accommodation(s) in order to participate in a student-related meeting or event, email Disability and Access Services at least 48 hours prior to the event to discuss how we can best accommodate you.

Request Academic Accommodations

  • Apply to Central Piedmont Apply to Central Piedmont and obtain a student ID number.
  • Complete the Student Intake Form Complete the Disability and Access Services Student Intake Form in Central Piedmont Accommodate. Documentation of your disability is required in order to complete the intake form . Refer to the Guidelines for Health Documentation (PDF) to learn more about submitting health documentation.
  • Check Your Student Email A Disability and Access Services counselor will contact you to schedule an appointment about accommodation requests. The process of being approved for accommodations may take up to 10 business days. Request accommodations as soon as possible for timely review of requests and arrangement of approved accommodations.

Student Information Release Authorization (FERPA)

You have rights as a student of higher education under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). You are entitled to decide who is given information about your education. If you would like for your parent, guardian, or other entity to have access to your educational information from Disability and Access Services, contact Disability and Access Services for assistance with completing the form (refer to the ’Contact Us’ section on this page phone number and email information.)
