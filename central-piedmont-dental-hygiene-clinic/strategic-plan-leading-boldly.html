Strategic Plan: Leading Boldly

  • Opportunity - We believe talent is everywhere. We open doors, elevate capability and expand access so every individual can learn, prosper and contribute to a thriving region.
  • Partnership – We collaborate boldly—with colleagues, employers and community partners—to align programs, accelerate workforce opportunity and extend the college’s impact.
  • Impact – We act with purpose, agility and accountability—driving meaningful outcomes that transform lives, strengthen the workforce and enrich the communities we serve.

Strategic Priorities

Our focus is clear and defined. We will advance four strategic goals that best reflect student, community and workforce needs across Mecklenburg County and the surrounding region. Each strategic goal has specific corresponding initiatives that will guide the college’s work over the next three years.

  • EMPOWER students to be workforce leaders How we will EMPOWER: Ensure more students persist in earning workforce credentials.
  • Enhance transfer pathways to better facilitate the transition to four-year institutions.
  • Increase access by streamlining processes to remove student financial barriers.
  • Expand access to transformative student services and experiences.
  • ACTIVATE partnerships to deliver exceptional programs and services How we will ACTIVATE : Strengthen partnerships with local employers to expand and scale academic and training programs and corresponding curriculum.
  • Launch relevant credentials and training that are responsive to local and regional workforce needs.
  • Maximize outreach to high-growth community segments, increasing awareness and engagement.
  • Propel student awareness and participation in on-the-job training experiences.
  • CULTIVATE a culture where people excel and engage in meaningful work How we will CULTIVATE: Position the college to compete for and retain high-quality talent.
  • Strengthen leadership and management capability.
  • Deepen employee engagement, trust and retention.
  • Expand growth and career development pathways.
  • Enhance workplace systems, practices and environments to support well‑being, high performance and organizational agility.
  • EXEMPLIFY future-focused, mission-anchored stewardship How we will EXEMPLIFY: Strengthen a culture of continuous improvement and data‑informed decision‑making.
  • Align enterprise-wide resources to drive organizational excellence and operational effectiveness.
  • Expand institutional agility to remain responsive to changing and emerging needs.
  • Advance long‑term financial strength through diversified revenue and transparent stewardship.
  • Leverage space strategically to improve student experience, enhance operations and support the institution’s long‑term vision.
