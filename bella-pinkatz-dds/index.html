The practice

Preventive care is the foundation of excellent dental health and a place where the Kew Gardens Dental P.C. team stands out. Comprehensive dental exams, digital imaging, and professional teeth cleanings keep smiles healthy and happy.

When issues arise, restorative dentistry options include fillings, crowns , bridges, dental implants , and root canal treatments. A conservative approach strives to preserve as much healthy tooth tissue as possible.

Cosmetic dentistry is also an area of focus at Kew Gardens Dental P.C. From teeth whitening and tooth-colored fillings to dental veneers and more, patients have access to the very latest in cosmetic dental treatments.

The best way to explore all that Kew Gardens Dental P.C. offers is to book a time to come in and meet the team. Online scheduling offers a convenient way to connect with the practice, and visits can also be set up by phone during normal business hours.

Bella Pinkatz, DDS, is a board-certified dentist with over 20 years of experience. She founded Kew Gardens Dental P.C. in 2008 and has served the Kew Gardens, New York community ever since.

Dr. Pinkatz earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the New York University College of Dentistry in New York City. After graduating, she entered private practice and discovered her passion for making dental care accessible and enjoyable for all.

At Kew Gardens Dental P.C., Dr. Pinkatz and her team strive to create a comfortable, relaxing environment. She takes the time to understand each patient’s unique needs, crafting personalized treatment plans and working to establish life-long relationships.

Dr. Pinkatz sees both adults and children so the whole family can enjoy convenient, high-quality dental care in one place. From dental restoration and teeth cleaning to cosmetic treatments and whitening, patients of all ages can be confident in her capable hands.
