Meet your Orthodontist

Dr. Michael Chiang

Dr. Michael Chiang practiced general dentistry prior to discovering his true passion was for orthodontics. He went on to complete his orthodontic training at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). Dr. Chiang is a board-certified orthodontist and certified Invisalign provider. He&#x27;s dedicated to providing excellent orthodontic care and beautiful straight smiles to you and your family.

Dr. Chiang&#x27;s Treatment Philosophy

Dr. Chiang strives to provide the best orthodontic experience for his patients. He attends international meetings regularly to stay on top of the latest orthodontic technique and technology. He is a certified Invisalign provider and has extensive experience working with bone-anchored mini-implants. He is experienced in seeing patients from all age groups and recommends everybody to have their first orthodontic exam as early as seven years old (also recommended by the American Association of Orthodontists).

Outside the office

Dr. Chiang grew up in various parts of the world, including the US, Canada, Taiwan, and China. He moved to Houston in the summer of 2015 and has since fallen in love with the city. He is determined to become a part of the community and help create better smiles for the people here. In his leisure time, he loves traveling, eating, cooking, and playing basketball.
