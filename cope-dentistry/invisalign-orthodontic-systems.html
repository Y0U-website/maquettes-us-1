Invisalign® Orthodontic Systems

Invisalign is the clear alternative to metal braces that offers advantages over traditional metal wire and band braces. Dr. Lindy Cope has completed extensive Invisalign training and is an approved provider of this revolutionary teeth straightening system.

To learn if you are a candidate for Invisalign, contact our office to schedule a consultation. During this visit, you will learn your estimated treatment length and you will be able to see a computerized simulation showing how your teeth will move over time.

Of course, if you prefer traditional braces, our dentists are happy to refer you to an orthodontist in Prairie Village or the Kansas City area.
