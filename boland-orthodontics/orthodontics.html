  • Will wearing braces or clear aligners cause significant pain? Mild soreness or discomfort is normal when beginning orthodontic care or receiving a new aligner set. This pressure indicates that your teeth are moving into proper position. Most patients describe the feeling as temporary tenderness rather than severe pain. Dedicated orthodontic specialists in Elmhurst, IL offer practical management tips, including applying dental wax to smooth brackets and using over the counter pain relievers when necessary. Rinsing with warm salt water can also soothe irritated oral tissues.
  • Why are retainers necessary after completing orthodontic treatment? Retainers are crucial because teeth naturally tend to shift back toward their original positions after active appliances are removed. This post treatment phase stabilizes surrounding bone and gum tissue while they adapt to the updated alignment. Patients usually wear retainers full time initially before transitioning to night wear. Wearing your custom retainer as instructed protects your structural investment and preserves your new smile for a lifetime.
  • What dietary changes should patients make while wearing metal braces? When wearing traditional metal braces, avoiding hard, sticky, or chewy foods is vital to protect wires and brackets. Foods like popcorn, whole apples, hard candies, nuts, and chewing gum can bend wires or dislodge brackets, causing unnecessary delays in treatment progress. Instead, choose soft options like pasta, yogurt, soups, cooked vegetables, and scrambled eggs. Cutting foods into small, bite sized pieces helps prevent damage when eating firmer items.
  • How can patients pay for comprehensive orthodontic treatment options? Affordable orthodontic care should be accessible to every family seeking a healthy, beautiful smile. Many dental insurance policies offer partial coverage for orthodontic care, significantly reducing out of pocket expenses. Top orthodontic specialists in Elmhurst, IL offer flexible payment plans, interest free monthly financing, and discounts for paying in full upfront. Healthcare flexible spending accounts or health savings accounts can also be utilized to pay for treatment with pre tax dollars.
  • How do I maintain good oral hygiene while wearing braces? Maintaining pristine oral hygiene with braces requires extra attention because food particles easily hide around brackets and archwires. Brushing after every meal using a soft bristled toothbrush removes trapped debris and prevents plaque buildup. Flossing daily with specialized threaders or water flossers cleans thoroughly between teeth where normal brushes cannot reach. Knowledgeable orthodontic specialists in Elmhurst, IL recommend fluoride mouthwash to strengthen tooth enamel and prevent white spot lesions during treatment.
