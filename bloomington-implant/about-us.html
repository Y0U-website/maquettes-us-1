Steven E. Lynn, DDS: Quality Care You Can Trust 🦷

He is the only dentist in the area certified in both the surgical and restorative phases of dental implants. Whether you need a single tooth, multiple teeth, or denture implants, Dr. Lynn guarantees your implants for life. Our friendly and dedicated staff members are x-ray certified and use state-of-the-art equipment to ensure optimal results for every treatment and visit. Contact us today to schedule an appointment or a free implant consultation. At Steven E. Lynn DDS, we treat people, not just teeth.

Dr. Lynn’s multifaceted career and hobbies reflect his dedication to excellence in both his professional and personal life.

Steven E. Lynn, DDS Steven E. Lynn, DDS is originally from Southern Indiana where he attended Indiana State University as a Pre-Dental major before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Indiana University School of Dentistry. He furthered his education at the Midwest Implant Institute, earning Distinguished Fellow status, and holds Prosthetic and Surgical Fellowships with the International Organization of Implant Dentists and the American Academy of Implant Dentists. Additionally, Dr. Lynn trained with the Doctor’s Organization for Conscious Sedation.

A pioneer in dental implant technology, Dr. Lynn was the first U.S. dentist to provide a custom-made titanium framework attached to dental implants. He collaborates with notable companies like Keystone Dental, Imtec, and Dentatus AB/USA to integrate advanced technologies into his practice.

Dr. Lynn is married and proud of his two children: Dr. Bryce Lynn, a Board Certified Interventional Cardiologist, and Brittany, a Registered Nurse.

Dr. Lynn was involved in professional motorsports for over 15 years. He held an international FIA-B racing license, as well as those in SCCA, Grand-Am, ALMS, and NASA. He drove and tested LeMans Prototypes for a French-owned team, as well as Porsche GT3 and Mustangs for Kenny Brown Racing. He has raced in such prestigious races as the 12 Hours of Sebring, Timex 25 Hours, and the Rolex 24 Hours of Daytona

The Doctor and his wife also own two Giant Schnauzers, Jake and Secret. Jake was the youngest Giant Schnauzer in the country to qualify for the prestigious Westminster Dog Show in 2015 and ended the year being ranked #5 in the breed.

The following year Dr. and Mrs. Lynn were the proud owners of Skansen’s Music Man, aka Maestro, who finished the year as the #2 male Giant Schnauzer in the Country. Maestro is no longer being shown but is breeding as Skansen Kennels in California.

Dr. Lynn keeps busy these days with his two talented Reined Quarter Horses, Doc and Mrs. Jones (aka MJ). Doc has achieved significant milestones in the past, including finishing 3rd overall in the 2019 ARHA World Championship Show in America’s Top Ranch Hand. Although Doc is no longer being shown, his legacy remains a highlight of Dr. Lynn’s accomplishments.

Dr. Lynn’s current show horse, Mrs. Jones, continues to excel in competition. In 2022, MJ finished 4th in the World NRCHA Celebration of Champions out of 141 entries. She was also crowned the South Eastern United States Hackamore Champion in 2022 and has earned numerous versatility ranch horse awards. Dr. Lynn’s passion for equestrian events, including working cattle, roping, and reining, remains a defining part of his life and achievements.
