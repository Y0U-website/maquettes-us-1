Your First Appointment

All of us here at Bighorn Pediatric Dentistry treat you just as special as we would our own family and love to see both the parent and the child leave with a smile on their face and excitement for their next trip here.

Sedation

For some children who have a lot of dental anxiety, we offer some different options for their treatment.

First we have nitrous oxide which may be used for kids who are just a bit nervous or uneasy. This helps to calm their nerves and make them more comfortable during their appointment. It will leave their system before they walk out of our office.

Our second option is for younger kids who may not tolerate treatment well. Oral Conscious Sedation as well as IV Sedation are available for children with extensive treatment needs and would not be able to cooperate fully. We have great success with these forms of sedation and we are certified by the State of Wyoming to use these methods in our office. The doctors will discuss with you their recommendations and come up with a plan of action that you understand and are comfortable with.

State of the Art Technology

Our office is equipped with digital x-rays, which makes diagnosing your child’s dental needs easy and as accurate as possible. We have new state of the art equipment which helps us provide quality care to your family in a timely manner. Also, we have TV’s above each dental chair so your child can enjoy their favorite show while they keep their smile looking as healthy as possible. We have wi-fi available for those parents that need to check their email or take care of other things while visiting our offices.

Surgery Center

For some children, dental rehabilitation at the Sheridan Surgical Center is the best option. We use this for children who may be medically compromised, or they are unable to tolerate treatment in the dental office setting. Our doctors can discuss these options with you and our staff is great to answer any questions you have about getting treatment completed for your child.

Financing Options

We work with most insurance companies and are contracted with Delta Dental of Wyoming, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Wyoming, as well as Wyoming and Montana Medicaid. For larger out of pocket portions, we are able to provide monthly payments through our partnership with Cherry and Care Credit. We also have the capability to send electronic statements and you can pay your bill using our convenient online system.
