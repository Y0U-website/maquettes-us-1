Our Motto

Colby Dental is a family-oriented practice with a passion for serving others. We believe in over-delivering on service and making outstanding oral care attainable so that you can enjoy a long and healthy life. The comfort and wellbeing of our patients is our top priority and we always strive to provide the highest quality care to the community of Northwest Indiana.

We value kindness and the power of human connection. When you walk into our Highland, IN, office, you’ll be greeted with a smile and treated like a friend.

That’s just the way we do things around here.

Meet Dr. Oleg Simkovic

Dr. Oleg Simkovic grew up in La Grange, Illinois, a southwest suburb of Chicago. After graduating high school, he enlisted in the United States Marine Corps, where he began his college education while serving his country. He later earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Loyola University Chicago.
