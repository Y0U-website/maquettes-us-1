Implant Dentistry options

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Replace a missing tooth with a strong, natural-looking implant that integrates with your jawbone. Atwood Dental provides permanent solutions that restore function, preserve bone structure, and protect surrounding teeth.

Full-Arch Dental Implants (All-on-x)

Restore a full arch of missing teeth with strategically placed implants that support a secure, non-removable smile. This advanced solution delivers improved stability, immediate function, and renewed confidence.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Upgrade traditional dentures with implant support for a more secure, comfortable fit. Enjoy improved chewing ability and freedom from slipping or messy adhesives.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Replace multiple missing teeth without compromising healthy adjacent teeth. Implant-supported bridges provide long-lasting strength, stability, and preservation of facial structure.
