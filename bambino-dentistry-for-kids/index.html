Located in the heart of Newark’s Ironbound neighborhood, our experienced team is proud to be the trusted choice for families seeking compassionate care for both kids and adults.

Dr. Ryan brings his passion for the performing arts and education into his role as a pediatric dentist. Dr. Ryan is dedicated to helping each child build a strong foundation for lifelong healthy and confident smiles.
