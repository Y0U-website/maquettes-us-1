Meet the Team Bay Area Family Dentistry

0 Our Dedicated Team Christi, Business Coordinator Christi was born and raised in this area and finds great joy in being a part of this community.

With 30 plus years’ experience in the dental office Christi understands the power of a healthy smile and how it can positively impact lives. Christi strives to make every patient feel valued and informed. Christi’s goal is to see you smile; by having you feel welcome from the moment you walk in the door.

Christi and her husband have 6 children and 12 grandchildren. When she is not in the office, she enjoys family time, glamping, boating, hunting, and traveling.

Katelyn, Registered Dental Assistant

Katelyn, from LaPorte, grew up fishing and enjoying all the bay area has to offer. She was active in Girl Scouts and FFA and loves kids and animals.

She enjoys helping & serving the community, while trying to brighten people’s days. Katelyn thrives at being adaptable, solving issues with new solutions, with a brighter outlook. She is a great listener, and passionate about mental health and strives to make everyone feel accepted and appreciated.

Katelyn wants to help people not be afraid, nervous, or embarrassed at the dentist by making them to feel seen and not judged. She wants all of her patients to have a confident and healthy smile! She tries to live by the thought that you choose how to react/feel and being happy is way more fun.

Katelyn and her husband are proud parents of a tuxedo cat named Oreo. They enjoy traveling, especially on cruises.
