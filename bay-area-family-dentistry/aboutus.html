  • Digital Radiographs (X-rays) Digital x-ray sensors have drastically improved the quality of radiograhic images, allowing the precision and clarity of the images to be much more accurate and easier to read. They are uploaded immediately to the practice management software which streamlines the process saving time compared to darkroom processing. Additionally, any radiation that patients are exposed to throughout the process is dramatically reduced by up to 80 %.
  • Cone Beam Computed Tomography Sirona’s premier CBCT (3D panoramic imaging machine) provides major advances in visualizing details, as well as treatment planning for our patients. Dr. Williams finds that the image quality is an unmatched tool in diagnosing possible dental diseases that may not be as apparent on 2D films. It allows incredible improvement in the navigation of more complex procedures, such as root canals, extractions and implants, as well as potential airway issues associated with sleep disordered breathing (aka Sleep Apnea).
  • Cerec PrimeScan Gone may be the days of uncomfortable, distasteful dental impressions. Digital dental scanning has replaced the need for many, if not most situations that would traditionally require one of those impressions. Scanning allows for a much faster, more comfortable, and most importantly, more accurate and reliable result. The high quality of imaging results in better fitting restorations. Dr. Williams and her team strive to provide the highest quality treatment in the most comfortable settings, so obtaining a digital scanner was a no-brainer!
