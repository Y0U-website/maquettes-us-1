Supplemental Ancillary Procedures ( Necessity of these procedures depend on each individual case )

The fee per implant placement could be different also by the complexities of the procedures. Range for the fee is due to situations where we might charge more or less for a particular patient treatment due to the difficulties or complexities of the treatment situation. For Example: If Implant being placed at a remote area with difficult to reach area, poor bone quality, Bad Angle for restoration, esthetic Zone and etc will be usually charged more. Implant being placed is at easy to reach area with good quality of bone and esthetic is not a concern will be charged less.
