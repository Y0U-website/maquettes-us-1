Dr. Cristina B. Georgescu

“Dr. Cristina” as she is known by her patients and their parents is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist.

She was born in Romania and spent a year in Austria before calling Fresh Meadows, Queens her permanent home. She received her B.S. in Biology from St. John’s University in 2002 and attended Columbia University School of Dental Medicine to fulfill her dream of becoming a dentist. After receiving her D.D.S. from Columbia University, she completed a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center.

As a young child, Dr. Cristina did not have access to proper dental care which resulted in numerous procedures and visits to the dentist. Experiencing first-hand the stress and anxiety of these visits but having compassionate and knowledgeable dental providers, Dr. Cristina decided to pursue her dream and become a pediatric dentist. She attended Stony Brook University for her Pediatric Dental residency and completed her program in 2015 where she served as Chief Resident.

Dr. Cristina has enjoyed doing outreach missions with a Cleft, Lip and Palate team to help underserved children restore their confidence and smiles. She has also enjoyed speaking and educating children and parents about the importance of good oral health at local schools, library events and fairs in and around Westchester.

Dr. Cristina feels truly blessed for her tiny miracle who decided to arrive into this world 10 weeks earlier in July 2020. She loves taking him to the park and watching him develop and grow into a fun-loving little man!

Dr. Julianna Bair

Julianna Bair, DMD, is a dual board certified pediatric dentist and endodontist, dedicated to providing individualized and quality care to patients in New York City.

Dr. Bair grew up in Livingston, New Jersey, and attended Rutgers University in New Brunswick, New Jersey, where she earned her Bachelor of Science in molecular biology/biochemistry and Spanish literature. She received her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston. She then went on to complete a general practice residency at New York-Presbyterian Hospital, affiliated with Columbia University, and a pediatric dental residency program at Interfaith Medical Center in Brooklyn.

As a pediatric specialist, Dr. Bair saw a need for herself to learn more about endodontics. This passion led her to complete a residency program in endodontics at Einstein Medical Center Philadelphia in Pennsylvania. As a dual specialist at an endodontic practice, Dr. Bair delivers the highest quality care with that extra gentle touch of a pediatric dentist.

In her free time, she enjoys jogging, skiing, traveling the world, and spending time with family and friends. She is also fluent in Mandarin.

Dr. Ashleigh Alex

Dr. Ashleigh Alex is a 3rd generation dentist originally from Long Island. Prior to dental school, Dr. Alex attended College of the Holy Cross and graduated with a degree in Biology. After college, she worked at Massachusetts General Hospital as a Clinical Research Coordinator in Gastrointestinal Oncology.

She obtained her DDS from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. Dr. Alex has a passion for research and while at Columbia won several awards including the prestigious Dr. Milton Schlesinger Award for Excellence in Research. After dental school, Dr. Alex decided to further her expertise in the craniofacial and dental fields and pursued a specialty in Orthodontics. She completed a three-year residency in Orthodontics and also received a Master of Science degree from Columbia University.

Dr. Alex is proficient in multiple treatment modalities and is committed to delivering high-quality individualized orthodontic care to her patients. She is an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists and American Dental Association. In her free time, Dr. Alex enjoys traveling with her husband Tyler and taking yoga classes.

Dr. Erica Fish

Dr Erica received a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry from St. Bonaventure University. She was awarded a Doctorate of Dental Medicine and continued on to receive a Certification of Graduate Studies in Pediatric Dentistry from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Having both a great fear of dentists herself as a child and also a gift for building relationships, she committed to a lifetime of making positive experiences at the dentist.

Dr Erica has been a resident of Westchester County for many years and is a Mom in the local community. She is proud to have treated the kids in our community for decades and is looking forward to continuing with her new family at Smile Starters.

Extremely grateful for the many blessings in her life, Dr Erica is dedicated to community service and has pledged to give back to the world. She is trained as a suicide hotline counselor for the Trevor Project and proudly supports the LGBTQ community, guiding dental offices to become more LGBTQ friendly. With a love for helping others to do good, she sits on a community service committee at a local synagogue. With her family, she is actively involved with organizations to tackle food and housing insecurity, animal welfare and rehabilitation, environmental causes, and many more.

Dr Erica is a proud parent to her two delicious teenage kiddos. In their time off, their family can be found somewhere on a wild outdoor adventure; favorites recently have been hiking the Appalachian Trail and bike packing across NY State. Dr Erica loves digging in her garden, walking her dog, doing yoga, and cheering on the Buffalo Bills.
