Early Orthodontic Treatment for Children

Early orthodontic treatment, also known as Phase One, is recommended around age seven. Smile Starters Pediatric Dentistry emphasizes the importance of early evaluation to correct jaw growth and bite problems, making room for permanent teeth and reducing the likelihood of extractions.

Signs that your child may need early orthodontic treatment include issues like early or late loss of baby teeth, difficulty chewing, mouth breathing, thumb-sucking, or crowded front teeth. Early intervention not only addresses these concerns but also sets the stage for a healthy, beautiful smile as your child grows.

Surgical Orthodontics

In cases of severe orthodontic concerns, surgical orthodontics, or orthognathic surgery, may be necessary. Smile Starters Pediatric Dentistry collaborates with oral and maxillofacial surgeons to correct bad bites, jaw abnormalities, and malocclusion. Surgical orthodontics is typically recommended for adults when jaw growth is complete, and orthodontic treatment alone cannot achieve proper alignment.

Before considering orthognathic surgery, your orthodontist will assess your case’s severity and alignment, ensuring that surgery is the appropriate course of action. Risks associated with orthognathic surgery are minimal, and the rewards include a beautiful, healthy smile that lasts a lifetime.

Invisalign

At Smile Starters Pediatric Dentistry, we offer Invisalign treatment, a revolutionary alternative to traditional braces. Invisalign uses a series of virtually invisible, removable aligners to straighten teeth comfortably and discreetly. Our orthodontic expertise, combined with Invisalign’s advanced technology, allows us to treat a wide range of cases, from simple to complex, for patients of all ages.

Invisalign Teen

For our teen patients, Invisalign Teen provides a unique teeth-straightening solution that is both precise and customized. Align Technology’s constant advancements, such as the SmartTrack® material, contribute to faster smile transformations. Invisalign Teen allows teens to enjoy the activities they love while undergoing treatment, and the removable aligners make brushing and flossing easy.

The Invisalign Teen Guarantee offers the flexibility to switch to wires and brackets within the first six months if desired. In comparison to traditional braces, Invisalign treatment corrects both simple and complex cases, is covered by orthodontic insurance, and provides up to 50 % shorter treatment times. With no pain from rubbing brackets or broken wires, teens can continue enjoying their favorite foods and activities.

Smile Starters Pediatric Dentistry is committed to delivering a superior teeth-straightening experience, providing removable trays for easy oral hygiene and increasing the likelihood of a boost in self-esteem for our teen patients.
