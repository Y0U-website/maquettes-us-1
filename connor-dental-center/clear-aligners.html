Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are an effective solution for straightening teeth, often providing results comparable to traditional braces in many cases.

However, one disadvantage is that they can be easily misplaced, as they need to be removed regularly for eating and brushing teeth.

Clear aligners are most effective when the patient is diligent and wears them consistently for 20-22 hours a day.
