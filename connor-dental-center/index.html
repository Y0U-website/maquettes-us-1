Les soins

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure used to treat gum disease, addressing tartar buildup and bone loss around the teeth.

Special tools are used to clean the roots beneath the gums, removing hard-to-reach tartar to restore gum health.

Dental implants are the most advanced and reliable solution for replacing missing teeth.

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating misaligned teeth and jaws. It involves the use of braces, clear aligners, and other devices to correct teeth and jaw alignment, improving both function and appearance.

Covers routine checkups, preventive care, cosmetic treatments, and restorative procedures like fillings, crowns and implants.
