Wylie Bell, DDS | Dentist

Dr. Wylie Bell has had the privilege of serving the southeast Kansas community at Countryside Dental + Wellness since 2003. Born in Emporia, Kansas, he was raised in Tonganoxie and graduated from Tonganoxie High School. He went on to earn his Bachelor of Science degree from Pittsburg State University in 1997 and later graduated from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry in 2002.

Dr. Bell is married to Lisa Bell, and together they have four wonderful children: Jessica, Aaron, Sean, and Elayna. In his free time, he enjoys working out, running, and playing catch with his kids. A dedicated sports fan, he closely follows Pitt State football and KU basketball.

Dr. Bell enjoys many facets of dentistry, from cosmetic procedures to oral surgery—and treats patients of all ages. His commitment to continuing education, combined with state‑of‑the‑art technology, allows him to provide high‑quality, modern dental care.
