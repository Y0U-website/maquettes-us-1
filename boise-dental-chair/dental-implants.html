Let’s face it. When you lose a tooth due to injury, dental decay or gum disease, it can be devastating. It’s important to replace a lost tooth to avoid problems for adjacent teeth and your overall dental health.

Boise Dental Chair offers dental implants that are the single best choice to replace lost teeth. With our well-designed implant services, your implant will mimic the look, feel and function of your natural tooth. Implants are the only method of tooth replacement that doesn’t require the preparation or involvement of your adjacent teeth.

If you’re wondering what an implant is, we can help! An implant is a small surgical fixture made of biocompatible metal or ceramic materials. It is placed into the jawbone and functions in the same manner as the root of your tooth.

Once the implant fully integrates with the surrounding bone, it provides a stable and durable foundation for a replacement tooth. Implants can support the crown for an individual tooth. They can also be used as abutment teeth for a dental bridge, or strategically placed to help stabilize dentures.

Let us help to determine if you are a dental implant candidate. We’ll look for proper bone density and ensure you have a strong immune system. Dental implants require strict oral hygiene. To see if you’re a good candidate for a dental implant, call our office at (208) 912-0555 to schedule an appointment.
