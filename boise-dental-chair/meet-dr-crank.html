Meet Dr. Crank

Dr. Joshua Crank, DMD

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Crank loves to spend time with family and friends. You will meet his wife, Kelly, as she is the technician of their In-House Lab at the Boise Dental Chair. They have two kids, Emmett and Adalane that keep them on their toes. They also have a dog, Faye, and three cats, so there is never a dull moment in the household! Dr. Crank enjoys watching the Boise State football, fishing for sturgeons, and golfing when weather permits.

Dr. Crank is passionate about making his patients feel comfortable while they are in his care and finds great joy in serving the people of Boise and the surrounding communities with quality family-oriented dental care.
