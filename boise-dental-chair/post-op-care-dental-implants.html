Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

Immediately After Surgery:

• Apply ice packs to the surgical area for 20 minutes at a time, several times a day, to reduce any swelling. • Avoid strenuous activity and heavy lifting. • Take prescribed antibiotics and/or pain medication as directed. • Do not smoke or use tobacco products.

First Few Days:

• Eat soft, bland foods that require minimal chewing. • Avoid hot drinks and foods. • Use a straw when drinking to avoid suction on the surgical site. • Rinse your mouth gently with warm salt water several times a day. • Brush your teeth gently, avoiding the surgical area. • Continue to take antibiotics as prescribed.

Week 1:

• Start gradually reintroducing harder foods to your diet. • Continue rinsing with salt water and brushing gently. • Attend any follow-up appointments and continue on prescribed antibiotics. • Use an oral irrigator if recommended by your dentist.

Weeks 2-6:

• Continue to brush and floss regularly, being careful around the implant site. • Use an interdental brush or dental floss to clean between the implants. • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption. • Attend follow-up appointments as scheduled.

Long-Term Care:

• Brush and floss your teeth twice a day, including the implant area. • Visit your dentist for regular checkups and cleanings. • Consider using an electric toothbrush or water flosser for deeper cleaning. • Protect your implants from damage by avoiding hard or sticky foods, such as nuts, candy, and ice. • If you experience any pain, swelling, or discharge, contact your dentist immediately.

Additional Tips:

• Get plenty of rest to promote healing. • Elevate your head while sleeping to reduce swelling. • Avoid using a toothbrush on the surgical site for the first few days. • Do not chew on the implant site until it is fully healed. • Follow your dentist’s instructions carefully for optimal recovery.
