About Our Practice

At Comstock Family Dentistry, our goal is to provide our patients with the best care possible .

While we believe in treating each patient with the same degree of compassion and empathy, we also recognize that dentistry is not a “one size fits all” profession. Every client is unique, especially when it comes to their oral health. This is why our staff works closely with each patient to develop an individualized treatment plan that is best suited for their own specific needs.

Comstock Family Dentistry has been providing high-quality, comprehensive dental care to people of all ages since Dr. Frank Arcangeli started the practice in 1983. In 2019, he sold the practice to Dr. Evan Kowalski, who strives to continue Dr. Arcangeli’s tradition of excellence. Here at Comstock Family Dentistry, we treat each of our patients like family. We utilize the latest treatment and diagnostic modalities in a relaxing and professional environment. We can’t wait to meet you!
