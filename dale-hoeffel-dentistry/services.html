0 our primary services Cleanings and Exams Arguably the most important piece of the puzzle! Our team is here to make sure that you are able to keep your smile healthy and beautiful for years to come and prevention is the most important part, we’re excited to help your entire family today and into the future!

composite bonding and fillings Composites have come a long way, we can do minimally invasive procedure to change the shape and size of your front teeth. We also use composites to restore teeth that have been damaged by bacteria or trauma.

teeth whitening We utilize KOR whitening to help our patients achieve amazing results with limited sensitivity. It’s an amazing way to boost confidence and keep your character. We offer various levels of treatment depending on your age and brightness.

Crowns and veneers We can determine what is the best fit for each tooth and each person. Some teeth that have more dental history may be ready for crowns to be protected. If you’re looking to change the shape and size of your teeth with minimal drilling veneers are an amazing option!

Clear Aligner THerapy We utilize clear trays to gently push teeth where we want them to be. Our patients can go through an initial consultation to see if they are a good fit for the system! We utilize Orthobrain aligners which means we coordinate your care with an orthodontist the entire time.

implant restorations Implants have totally changed the dental game. We now have a fixed solution for missing teeth that nearly replicates the real deal. Most patients are good candidates for dental implants to replace missing teeth but in the scenario you’re not we can always discuss dental bridges and removable partial dentures.

Full mouth reconstruction Each person has a different set of factors that impact your smile. and if you’ve incurred a lot of damage or are in a lot of pain it may be time to consider a full mouth reconstruction. We will thoroughly plan our your smile so you have the confidence to move forward with restoring each tooth that needs it.
