Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants Near Everett, WA

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last many years, often decades. Regular checkups and good oral hygiene help maximize their lifespan.

What is the dental implant timeline?

The process typically spans several months from initial evaluation through placement, healing, and final restoration. Exact timing depends on your individual bone health and treatment needs.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone density are good candidates. Factors like gum health and certain medical conditions are evaluated during a consultation. Dr. Izoh Ashour, our practice’s periodontist and implantologist, is involved in evaluating candidacy and placing implants for patients in the Everett area.

Does dental implant surgery hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so discomfort during placement is minimal. Some soreness afterward is normal and typically manageable with over-the-counter medication.
