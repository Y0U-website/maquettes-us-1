How Dental Implants Replace More Than Just a Tooth

Unlike traditional bridges, implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support, and they actively stimulate the jawbone in the same way that natural roots do. That stimulation is what helps preserve bone density over time and prevents the structural changes that can develop when a missing tooth goes unaddressed for years. For patients who have put off replacing a tooth, implants may also help reduce the ongoing bone loss that silently progresses in the absence of a root.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants Near Snohomish, WA

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last many years, often decades. Regular checkups and good daily hygiene help maximize how long your implant lasts and keep the surrounding gum and bone healthy.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone density are good candidates for dental implants. A thorough evaluation using 3D imaging, along with visiting Board Certified Periodontist Dr. Izoh Ashour, DDS, MS, helps confirm the best approach for your specific situation.

What is the dental implant timeline?

Most implant treatments span several months from initial consultation through placement, healing, and final restoration. Your exact timeline depends on factors like bone health and whether any preparatory steps, such as bone grafting, are needed.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn’t feel pain during placement. Some soreness afterward is normal and is typically manageable with over-the-counter medication.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan and provider. Our office can help review your benefits before treatment begins so you have a clear picture of your costs and options.
