Dr. Chad Vanourny is passionate about providing his patients with gentle, effective dental care that helps them develop great oral health and wonderful smiles. He is equally passionate about the relationships he develops with each patient, becoming friends with them over the course of treatment, sharing stories and laughter.

I love my career, meeting and working with so many new and unique people. As a dentist, I am able to enjoy one-on-one face time with my patients, and really get to know them and their families. I strive to make a positive impact in my community, by providing exceptional oral health services in a relaxed and professional setting. Dr. Chad Vanourny

He is adept at working with patients who have dental-related phobias and anxieties, providing reassurance and encouragement to help them work through their concerns and learn to relax and be comfortable while in his care. Dr. Vanourny does not judge his patients, but rather listens to them and assists them, allowing them to set the pace of their dental care. His approach brings peace of mind to…

Born and raised in Iowa, Dr. Vanourny now calls Lake Wylie, SC his home. He and his wife, who is a dermatologist, have three children: twin sons and a daughter. Their family also includes a dog, Gracie. While his family is his first priority, and he spends much of his free time playing with his children, Dr. Vanourny also enjoys running, biking, golfing, and cheering on the Hawkeyes.

Believing in giving back to the community, Dr. Vanourny volunteers with Mission of Mercy and Give Kids a Smile, programs which provide dental care to the underserved and patients in need.
