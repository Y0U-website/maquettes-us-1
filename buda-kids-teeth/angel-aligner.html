Signs Your Child Needs It

If your child has crowded teeth, spacing issues, or bite concerns like overbite or underbite, Angel Aligner™ may be a great solution. Many parents also choose aligners for kids and teens who want a more discreet option than braces or who prefer flexibility in their daily routine. We evaluate your child’s smile and growth to determine if this treatment fits their needs.

What to Expect

We begin with a consultation and digital scan to map out your child’s treatment—no messy impressions required. Our orthodontist, Dr. Claire Foreman , reviews the scan and creates a detailed plan that shows how each aligner will move the teeth step by step. Your child will wear each set of aligners for about 1–2 weeks before moving to the next. For best results, they should wear aligners 20–22 hours per day. We schedule periodic check-ins to track progress and keep everything moving smoothly. Most kids adjust quickly and enjoy the flexibility aligners provide.

Aftercare

After treatment, your child will wear a retainer to keep their new smile in place. We’ll guide you on how often to wear it and how to care for it. Consistent brushing, flossing, and aligner cleaning during treatment help maintain the best results and keep your child’s smile healthy.
