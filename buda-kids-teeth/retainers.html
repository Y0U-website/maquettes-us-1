Signs Your Child Needs It

Your child needs a retainer after completing orthodontic treatment like braces , Invisalign® , or Angel Aligner™ . If you notice teeth starting to shift or spacing returning, it often means your child hasn’t worn their retainer consistently. Retainers play a key role in maintaining results, especially during the first several months after treatment.

What to Expect

We take digital scans of your child’s teeth to create a custom retainer that fits comfortably and securely. Dr. Claire Foreman designs the retainer to match your child’s smile and ensure proper retention. Your child will wear the retainer as instructed—often full-time at first, then transitioning to nighttime wear. We’ll explain exactly how to use and care for it so your child feels confident and successful from day one.

Aftercare

Retainer care plays a big role in keeping your child’s smile healthy. Encourage your child to clean their retainer daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or retainer cleaner. Always store it in its case when not in use to prevent loss or damage. Avoid exposing the retainer to heat, which can warp the material. Regular checkups allow us to monitor fit and make adjustments if needed.
