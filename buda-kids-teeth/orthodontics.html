Our Orthodontic Treatment Options

We offer several modern orthodontic solutions for kids, teens, and adults, with options designed to fit different needs, lifestyles, and treatment goals.

Invisalign®

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gently straighten teeth over time. They’re comfortable, nearly invisible, and easy to take out for meals, brushing, and flossing. Invisalign can be a great option for teens and adults who want flexibility and discretion.

Angel Aligner™

Angel Aligner clear, removable trays are designed with digital planning for precise tooth movement. They offer a comfortable fit and discreet look, with the freedom to remove them for eating and cleaning. Angel Aligner may be an option for qualifying teens and adults.

Braces

Traditional braces use brackets and wires to correct tooth alignment and bite concerns reliably. They’re effective for simple to complex cases and work continuously without daily compliance. Braces are often ideal for younger patients or anyone who prefers a fixed option.

Retainers

Retainers help keep teeth in position after orthodontic treatment. Wearing a retainer as directed prevents shifting and protects your results. We provide custom retainers and guidance to make maintaining a straight smile simple for kids, teens, and adults.

Adult Orthodontics

It’s never too late to improve your smile. Adult orthodontics offers modern solutions to straighten teeth, improve bite alignment, and support long-term oral health at any age. With options like clear aligners and braces, treatment can fit your lifestyle while enhancing both appearance and function.

Airway Orthodontics

Airway orthodontics (also known as airway dentistry) uses palatal expansion to guide upper-jaw development, create room for growing teeth, and improve certain types of bite alignment and breathing concerns. We offer custom metal and Invisalign® Palatal Expanders personalized to your child’s needs.
