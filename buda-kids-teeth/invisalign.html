Signs Your Child Needs It

If your child has crowded teeth, gaps, an overbite, an underbite, or shifting teeth, Invisalign® may be a great option. Many parents also choose Invisalign® for teens who feel self-conscious about traditional braces or who want a more flexible orthodontic solution. During your visit, we evaluate your child’s smile and let you know if Invisalign® fits their needs.

What to Expect

We start with a consultation where Dr. Claire Foreman , evaluates your child’s teeth and creates a personalized treatment plan. We take digital scans—no messy impressions—and map out each stage of tooth movement. Your child receives a series of custom aligners, each worn for about 1–2 weeks before switching to the next set. The aligners gently shift teeth over time. Your child should wear them 20–22 hours per day for best results, removing them only for eating, brushing, and flossing. We schedule periodic check-ins to monitor progress and keep everything on track.

Aftercare

Once treatment ends, your child will wear a retainer to keep their new smile in place. We provide clear instructions on when and how to wear it. Encourage consistent brushing, flossing, and aligner care throughout treatment to maintain oral health and achieve the best results.
