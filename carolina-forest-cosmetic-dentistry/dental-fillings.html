The Filling Procedure

We usually use local anesthesia to make your filling procedure comfortable. We gently remove the decayed or damaged area of your tooth and replace it with filling material. Dr. Holladay takes care to remove as little tooth structure as possible while providing a good bond for the filling material. He then polishes the tooth and checks for proper occlusion (fit between the upper and lower teeth). This is a quick easy procedure that can make a huge difference in your smile. Not only that, but it can also help protect you from TMJ problems or a bite that feels off.

In some cases where the decay is too great, we may recommend other solutions. One option may be to use veneers which are thin pieces of porcelain that are bonded on to the tooth. They give the appearance of a very natural looking smile and can correct other cosmetic problems.

The other option is a porcelain crown. Crowns generally fit over the whole tooth after what is left of the existing tooth is prepped to receive the dental crown. These are also very strong and natural looking and can last for many years to come.

What To Do After Getting A Dental Filling

Because there are so many new advancements in dentistry and the materials that can fix teeth, you might want to slack on oral hygiene. However, when we get our teeth fixed, we need to take pride in the restored health and appearance of our smile. Brushing and flossing regularly can help ensure your smile stays healthy. Too often people who get their teeth fixed are back in the office later because they didn’t take care of them after treatment. Prevention of cavities will keep you out of the dental office and your hard earned money in your pocket.
