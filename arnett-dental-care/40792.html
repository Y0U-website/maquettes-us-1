Frequently Asked Questions

How should I care for my dental implant?

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care, and maintaining them is surprisingly straightforward. At Arnett Dental Care, Dr. Arnett and Dr. Pruitt recommend treating your implants just like your natural teeth to ensure their longevity.

Daily Care Routine

Brush your implants at least twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste. Floss carefully around each implant once per day, or use interdental brushes specifically designed for implants. Water flossers can be particularly effective at cleaning hard-to-reach areas around the implant crown and abutment.

Regular Professional Maintenance

Schedule regular dental checkups and professional teeth cleanings every six months. During these visits, our dentists will examine your implants for any signs of complications and remove plaque buildup that can lead to peri-implantitis, an infection that threatens implant stability.

Lifestyle Considerations

Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice, pens, or hard candy, which can damage the implant crown. If you grind your teeth at night, ask about a custom nightguard to protect your investment. Smoking significantly increases implant failure risk, so quitting is highly recommended.

Watch for Warning Signs

Contact our Murray, KY office immediately if you notice bleeding, swelling, looseness, or discomfort around your implant. Early intervention can prevent serious complications.

With consistent home care and regular professional monitoring, dental implants in Murray can provide decades of reliable function and a beautiful smile.

Are dental implants covered by dental or medical insurance?

In most cases, insurance does not cover dental implants. However, they may cover extractions or crown placement (the tooth that tops your dental implant post).

Our team can evaluate your dental or medical insurance plan to determine whether it covers aspects of your dental implant placement.

Dental Implant Options

  • Single Dental Implants: If you have a single missing tooth, a single implant is the solution.
  • Implant-Supported Prosthetics: If you have full or partial tooth loss, talk to the dentist about implant-retained dentures and bridges. We use four to six dental implants to secure an arch of upper or lower teeth to the jaw.

Dental Implants Technology

At Arnett Dental Care, Dr. Arnett and Dr. Pruitt use advanced CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) 3-D imaging technology to provide superior diagnostic capabilities for our Murray, KY, patients. This state-of-the-art system captures detailed three-dimensional images of your teeth, jaw, sinuses, and surrounding bone structure in a single scan.

CBCT imaging enables precise treatment planning for dental implants, root canals, extractions, and orthodontics while using significantly less radiation than traditional CT scans. With more precise visualization and more accurate measurements, we can deliver safer, more predictable outcomes for your dental care.
