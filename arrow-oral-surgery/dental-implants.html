DENTAL IMPLANTS

  • Multiple Tooth Implants: Replaces several missing teeth using implant-supported bridges or partial dentures for added stability.
  • Full-Arch Restoration (All-on-X): Restores an entire upper or lower arch with a fixed bridge supported by a few strategically placed implants.
  • Zygomatic Implants: Ideal for patients with significant bone loss in the upper jaw—these implants anchor into the cheekbone for reliable support.
  • Zirconia Implants: A metal-free, ceramic option for patients who prefer a white, biocompatible alternative to titanium.

What to Expect During Dental Implant Surgery

Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. We’ll review your oral health, take 3D scans, and discuss the best treatment plan for you.

During the procedure, your oral surgeon places the implant post into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation for maximum comfort. After placement, the implant will heal and integrate with your bone over several months, creating a strong base for your new restoration.

Once healed, your dentist will attach a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis, completing your smile. Our team will guide you through recovery and long-term care to ensure the best outcome.
