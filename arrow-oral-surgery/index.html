The practice

Removal of a tooth that is damaged, infected, or causing crowding to protect your oral health.

Dental Implants A long-lasting replacement for missing teeth using a titanium post placed in the jawbone to support a restoration.

Full Arch Restoration A treatment that replaces all teeth in the upper or lower jaw with a fixed set of implant-supported teeth.

Oral Pathology The diagnosis and treatment of unusual lumps, sores, or growths in the mouth to rule out or manage disease.

IV Sedation A safe and effective way to relax during your procedure using medication delivered through a small IV in your arm.

Missing or failing teeth can affect how you look, eat, and feel. Dental implants are a long-lasting, natural-looking way to replace missing teeth and restore your confidence. Implants help preserve your facial structure, prevent bone loss, and make it easy to enjoy the foods you love again.

At Arrow Oral Surgery, we specialize in personalized dental implant solutions. Our team designs each treatment plan to fit your unique needs, using proven techniques and modern technology to deliver strong, healthy, and beautiful results. From your first consultation through your final restoration, you’ll receive expert care every step of the way.

Because they are permanently anchored, implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth—without slipping or adhesives. With proper care, they can last for decades or even a lifetime.

Les soins

Dental implants are small titanium or ceramic posts that act as artificial tooth roots. Once placed in the jawbone, they naturally bond with your bone to create a stable foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration.

Teens and young adults are ideal candidates for removal, as roots and bone are still developing, which allows for faster recovery. However, adults of any age can safely undergo wisdom tooth extraction when needed.

Bone Grafting A procedure to rebuild bone in the jaw where it has been lost, often in preparation for a dental implant.
