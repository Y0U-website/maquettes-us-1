WISDOM TEETH

Teens and young adults are ideal candidates for removal, as roots and bone are still developing, which allows for faster recovery. However, adults of any age can safely undergo wisdom tooth extraction when needed.

  • Gum infection around partially erupted teeth
  • Damage to adjacent teeth or roots
  • Jaw stiffness or limited mouth opening
  • Sinus discomfort related to upper wisdom teeth
  • Shifting or crowding of nearby teeth after orthodontic treatment

Common Signs You May Need Wisdom Teeth Removed

If you notice any of these symptoms, schedule an evaluation at Arrow Oral Surgery in Mequon to determine whether extraction is recommended.

  • Pain or Pressure: Persistent aching or throbbing in the back of the mouth or jaw.
  • Swollen Gums: Redness or tenderness near the erupting teeth.
  • Difficulty Opening Your Mouth: Overcrowding or impaction can limit movement.
  • Sinus Pressure: Upper molars pressing on sinus cavities can cause congestion or headaches.
  • Bad Breath or Unpleasant Taste: Bacteria trapped around partially erupted teeth can lead to infection.

What to Expect During Wisdom Teeth Removal

Your visit begins with a detailed consultation, including a review of your medical history and x-rays to assess tooth position and bone structure. Together, you and your surgeon will decide on the best anesthesia option for a comfortable experience: local anesthesia, nitrous oxide, or IV sedation.

During surgery, the oral surgeon carefully removes the wisdom teeth.

After the procedure, you’ll receive step-by-step post-operative care instructions, including how to manage swelling, control discomfort, and prevent complications such as dry socket. Most patients return to normal activity within a few days, using rest, ice packs, and prescribed medication as needed for comfort.

  • For erupted teeth: The procedure is quick and minimally invasive using gentle extraction techniques.
  • For impacted teeth: A small incision in the gum provides access for safe removal, followed by sutures to promote healing.

Recovery and Aftercare

Mild swelling or soreness is common and typically improves within a few days. To ensure optimal healing:

Our team remains available throughout your recovery to answer questions and provide guidance as you heal.

  • Keep gauze in place as instructed to control bleeding.
  • Apply ice packs during the first 24 hours to reduce swelling.
  • Follow a soft-food diet and stay hydrated.
  • Avoid smoking, straws, or vigorous rinsing to protect the blood clot.
