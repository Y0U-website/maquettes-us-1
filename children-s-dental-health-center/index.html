Welcoming Dr. Claudia Amezquita-Lee

Dr. Claudia Amezquita-Lee is a caring and dedicated board certified pediatric dentist who brings a unique blend of global experience and public health expertise to her practice.…

The practice

Before becoming a dentist, Dr. Claudia worked as a dental hygienist and served children in public health clinics in Texas. She is also a National Health Service Corps scholar, committed to ensuring every child receives quality dental care, especially in underserved communities.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Claudia loves traveling both in the U.S. and internationally, trying new cuisines, and spending quiet time at home with her family and their dog, Lulu. She is fluent in English and Spanish and is passionate about helping families feel safe, supported, and empowered in their dental health journey.
