Meet Our Dental Care Team

Dr. Aaron

Hello, my name is Dr. Aaron. Coming from a long line of dentists, I knew I had always wanted to be a dentist. I was born and raised in Las Vegas, NV, and grew up helping around my father’s dental practice. In my earlier years of life, I worked in a local dental lab fabricating dental crowns. I also assisted an orthodontist in college. I attended Southern Utah University to study my undergraduate coursework before returning home to attend the School of Dental Medicine at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, where I obtained my Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.

I plan to continue Antigo’s Complete Dental Center’s traditional approach to treatment. We will continue to listen to you to ensure that all your dental concerns are being addressed and encourage honest, open communication regarding your oral health care and education. Our calm and patient personality is especially effective for helping you feel comfortable even during those slightly apprehensive moments when seeing a dentist.

My wife Elizabeth enjoys being involved in playing the cello for various central Wisconsin music performances.

In my free time, I enjoy an active lifestyle with my family: wakeboarding, snowboarding, skiing, backpacking, camping, and working out. We love exploring the Northwoods with our three beautiful daughters: Chloe, Sage, and Lucy!
