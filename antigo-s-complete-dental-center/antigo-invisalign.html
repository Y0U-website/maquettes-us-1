Invisalign In Antigo, WI

Antigo’s Complete Dental in Antigo, WI, can help you with custom Invisalign trays. The visual impact of the metal on one’s smile is one of the significant concerns people frequently have about wearing dental braces. The idea of having to put on unsightly metal braces for an extended period can be incredibly discouraging, especially for adults. The practically invisible Invisalign method straightens teeth quickly and doesn’t need metal.

A series of personalized alignment trays make up the Invisalign treatment. The dentist adjusts the trays every few weeks to accommodate the new tooth arrangement. In addition to having a less noticeable effect, Invisalign aligning trays can be temporarily taken out for special events, allowing the patient to decide how long their treatment will last. Many clients express satisfaction with both the Invisalign procedure and the fantastic outcomes.

Commonly corrected with Invisalign :

Overcrowding occurs when there is too little space for the teeth to align normally in the mouth. Overcrowding can cause tooth decay and increase the likelihood of gum disease.

Significant gaps between teeth can sometimes occur because teeth are missing or the jaw continues to grow abnormally.

Crossbite This common dental problem occurs when one or multiple upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth. Consequently, uneven wear can lead to bone erosion and gum disease.

Overbite This problem occurs when the upper teeth project further than, or completely cover, the lower teeth. Eventually, jaw pain and TMJ may occur.

Underbite is the inverse of overbite; the lower teeth project further than, or completely cover, the upper teeth. Eventually, jaw pain and TMJ can occur.
