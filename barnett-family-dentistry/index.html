Les soins

We recommend dental cleanings are performed every 6 months. During a routine cleaning, a dentist and/or hygienist will clean and evaluate your teeth.

Crowns are designed to cover a damaged tooth. A crown strengthens the tooth, as well as improves the appearance, shape, and alignment of the damaged tooth.

Each Veneer is custom made to fit your mouth and bonded to your teeth with your natural enamel. This bonding process is done throughout multiple visits to our office.
