It’s the back-to-school edition of your #CCDCFact! Dental pain is a leading cause of missed school. We encourage you to schedule a back-to-school dental visit. They matter!

July’s #CCDCFact is heating up! Hot summer temperatures can do more than make you thirsty — they can affect your oral health, too! When you’re dehydrated, your mouth produces less saliva, reducing your body’s natural defense against tooth decay

June’s #CCDCFact gives us the perfect opportunity to celebrate National Oral Health Month! A healthy smile is about more than just your teeth. It’s an important part of your overall health. This month is the perfect time to refresh your oral hygiene routine and encourage your family to do the same!
