Your Clear Aligner Treatment at Club K Holistic Dental

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation using our 3D imaging technology . Dr. Knapton examines your teeth, jaw alignment, airway, and overall oral health to determine whether SureSmile clear aligners will achieve your desired results while supporting your whole-body health.

If SureSmile fits your needs, we create digital impressions of your teeth. These scans generate a virtual treatment plan showing exactly how your teeth will move from their current position to their final alignment. You see a preview of your expected outcome before starting treatment, helping you make a fully informed decision about proceeding.

Once your custom aligners arrive, we provide detailed instructions on wearing and caring for them. Proper maintenance keeps your aligners clear throughout treatment. Regular check-ins every six to eight weeks allow us to monitor your progress and provide new aligner sets as you advance through your treatment plan while ensuring your overall oral health remains excellent.

Benefits of Clear Aligner Treatment in Springfield

The removable design of clear aligners allows you to maintain better oral hygiene compared to fixed braces, reducing your risk of tooth decay and gum inflammation during treatment. Research from the National Institutes of Health confirms that patients using clear aligners typically have better gingival health during treatment compared to those with fixed appliances, largely due to the ability to maintain normal brushing and flossing.

You can continue eating all your favorite foods without restrictions by simply removing your aligners before meals. The smooth plastic material causes less irritation to your cheeks and gums than metal brackets and wires, and the nearly invisible appearance allows you to smile confidently in social and professional settings throughout treatment.

Maintaining Your Results After Treatment

After completing your SureSmile treatment, retainers help maintain your newly aligned smile. Your teeth naturally want to shift back toward their original positions, so wearing retainers as directed prevents this movement and protects your investment. Most patients wear retainers full-time initially, then transition to nighttime wear. Dr. Knapton provides specific retention guidance based on your unique case. Continuing excellent dental hygiene and attending regular checkups supports both your orthodontic results and your overall oral health long-term.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Clear aligner treatment typically takes 6 to 18 months depending on the complexity of your orthodontic needs. Mild cases may be completed in as little as 6 months, while more complex alignment issues may require up to 18 months. Dr. Knapton provides a specific timeline during your consultation based on your individual treatment plan and goals.

Do I have to wear clear aligners all the time?

For optimal results, you should wear clear aligners 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink anything besides water, brush, and floss. The more consistently you wear your aligners, the faster and more predictable your results will be. Not wearing aligners for the recommended time can extend treatment duration.

Are clear aligners painful to wear?

Clear aligners typically cause less discomfort than traditional braces. You may experience mild pressure or soreness for the first few days when starting treatment or switching to a new set of aligners, but this usually subsides quickly. The smooth plastic material does not irritate your cheeks and gums the way metal brackets can.

Can I eat and drink with clear aligners in?

You should remove clear aligners when eating or drinking anything besides water to prevent staining and damage. The removable nature allows you to enjoy all your favorite foods without restrictions, unlike traditional braces. Always brush your teeth before reinserting aligners to maintain good oral hygiene and prevent cavities.

How do I clean my clear aligners?

Clean your clear aligners daily by gently brushing them with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or a specialized aligner cleaner. Avoid using toothpaste, which can be abrasive and cause cloudiness. Rinse thoroughly with cool water and avoid hot water, which can warp the plastic material. Store aligners in their case when not wearing them.

Will clear aligners affect my speech?

Most people adjust to speaking with clear aligners within a few days. You may initially notice a slight lisp or different feeling when talking, but this typically resolves quickly as your tongue adapts to the aligners. The thin, smooth design of clear aligners causes minimal speech interference compared to traditional braces.

Do clear aligners work as well as traditional braces?

Clear aligners are highly effective for treating mild to moderate orthodontic issues, with success rates comparable to traditional braces for appropriate cases. However, complex orthodontic problems may still require traditional braces or other treatments. Dr. Knapton evaluates your specific needs to determine the most effective treatment approach for your situation.

Can I switch from braces to clear aligners mid-treatment?

In some cases, patients can transition from traditional braces to clear aligners to complete their treatment, though this depends on your specific orthodontic needs and treatment progress. Dr. Knapton evaluates your current treatment status and determines whether switching to clear aligners would be beneficial for completing your smile transformation.

What is SureSmile?

SureSmile is a clear aligner system that uses advanced 3D treatment planning software to calculate the precise forces needed to move each tooth into its ideal position. Dr. Knapton chose SureSmile specifically for its precision technology, BPA-free biocompatible materials, and alignment with our biological dentistry philosophy. SureSmile delivers more efficient tooth movement and often shorter treatment times compared to generic aligner brands.
