The practice

We offer medical, dental, and behavioral health services for patients of all ages and in multiple languages, we have many bilingual Spanish speaking staff. We also have a social worker(s) who assists patients in finding community support services and a financial advocate who helps with eligibility and enrollment assistance as well as the sliding fee scale.

24-hour on-call coverage is available, and we serve several counties surrounding Fort Dodge as well as Mason City, Dayton, Eagle Grove and Clarion. Our caring staff is committed to your well-being and getting you back to feeling like yourself again. If you would like to schedule an appointment with us, be sure to contact us today.
