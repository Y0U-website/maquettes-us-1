Expertise Built on Experience and a Drive to Improve

That commitment shows up in the numbers. While roughly 26 % of orthodontic patients nationally require extractions, less than 1 % of Dr. Jesse’s patients do — the result of leaning on expansion, distalization, and temporary anchorage devices (TADs) instead of taking the more common path. It’s also why Dr. Jesse no longer takes on surgical cases: between appliance therapy and expansion (including for adult patients well past the age most orthodontists consider expansion possible), he and his team consistently find non-surgical paths for cases that would typically be referred out.

Since completing his residency and joining the practice in 2012, Dr. Jesse has built on the foundation his father established when he opened Carmen Orthodontics in 1978. What began as a single office has grown to four locations across Central Ohio, and Dr. Jesse continues to bring the same hands-on, evaluate-and-improve mindset to every case. For him, expertise isn’t something to coast on; it’s something to keep working at, approaching every case with a deeply personal sense of responsibility to provide care worthy of the Carmen name.

Technology and Orthodontics: How Dr. Jesse Is Advancing Modern Care

One of the biggest changes to his practice over the years has been the increased use of Dental Monitoring, a remote system that lets Dr. Jesse and his team regularly evaluate a patient’s progress without requiring an office visit every few weeks. For many patients, that means going seven to eight months between in-person appointments while staying connected to the Carmen Orthodontics team the entire time.

That efficiency shows up directly in treatment outcomes. Dr. Jesse’s Invisalign cases average about 16 months and roughly 8 total appointments (consult, start, and debond included) compared to a national average closer to 19 visits. His braces cases average a similar 16 months and about 11 total visits. The result is an approach to orthodontics designed around the patient rather than the appointment calendar — excellent results in the shortest practical timeframe, giving patients and families more of their time back.
