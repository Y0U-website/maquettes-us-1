Meet Our Doctor

John A. Clements, DMD

General Dentist

Dr. John, originally from Hamilton, Ohio, is a graduate of Stephen T. Badin H.S., Earlham College, and the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. He completed a general practice residency program at the UK-VA Medical Center in Lexington, Kentucky.

Dr. John attends over 20 hours of continuing education each year, and stays abreast of the latest technology and techniques with membership in The American Dental Association, The Ohio Dental Association and The Keely Dental Society.

Dr. John has served on the boards of the American Red Cross, American Cancer Society, YMCA, Hamilton Rotary Club and Colonial Senior Services Board of Trustees. He and his family are members of Queen of Peace Church in Hamilton.
