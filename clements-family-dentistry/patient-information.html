Financial Policy

We accept the following forms of payment: Cash, Check, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and CareCredit (described below).

Payment for services is due at the time services are rendered unless prior arrangements have been made with the doctor and the billing receptionist.

The parent that accompanies the minor child/children to the appointment is responsible for any payment due. For unaccompanied minors, non-emergency treatment will be denied unless charges have been pre-authorized before the appointment date or previous arrangements have been made with the doctor and billing receptionist.

Checks that are returned to our office from your financial institution are subject to a $20.00 returned check fee. This fee covers the processing fees that are charged to our office.

Scheduling & Office Hours

Clements Family Dental is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday from 8am-5pm. We will attempt to schedule your appointment at your earliest convenience. If you are currently experiencing pain or an immediate emergency situation, we will make every attempt to see you that day.

To make an appointment, or to discuss any questions or concerns you have, please call as at (513) 887 - 7027.

We have reserved dedicated time in our office for your dental appointment. If you find it necessary to cancel, please contact us at least 24 hours prior to your appointment time. Without proper notice, you may be charged a $25 fee which is subject to change without notice.
