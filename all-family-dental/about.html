A Part of Our Community

As dental care providers, we serve a critical role in our community, and we take pride in that responsibility. We’re committed to continually building lasting relationships with our patients by giving back to our Oakdale community. In addition to providing quality dental care to our patients, we volunteer with the Chamber of Commerce in both Oakdale and Woodbury to help build up other practices and businesses that make our community unique and keep our economy thriving.

Always Growing

Our experienced dentists use their skilled care and advanced technology to offer quality orthodontic , cosmetic and restorative dentistry for your family. To provide you with comprehensive dentistry, we offer everything from root canals to dental implants and exams. We are always improving our services and are pleased to use high-tech equipment like CEREC, digital and panoramic X-rays, DIAGNOdent laser cavity detection, dental lasers, a cone beam CT scanner, and more.

A Warm Welcome

We will always treat you and each other- the way we would like to be treated. Our staff will greet you warmly, act professionally, and otherwise provide you with the level of service we would want for ourselves and our families. You can feel confident we have your best interests at heart.
