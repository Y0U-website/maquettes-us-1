Dental Implants

  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most adults in good oral and general health are candidates. We assess bone density with X-rays and discuss options if bone grafting is needed.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime. Good oral hygiene and regular checkups are essential for long-term success.
  • Does getting a dental implant hurt? The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is generally well tolerated. Most patients report minimal discomfort and a fast recovery after placement.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Brush and floss as you would natural teeth and attend regular dental visits. We provide detailed instructions and ongoing support for implant care.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Coverage varies by plan. We’re insurance-friendly, offer transparent pricing, and have a Dental Savers Plan for uninsured or retired patients.
  • Can I get implants if I already have dentures? Yes! Implants can anchor new or existing dentures for improved fit and function. We’ll review your specific needs during your consultation.
