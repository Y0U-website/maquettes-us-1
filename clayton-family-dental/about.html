General Dentist & Owner

Dr. Jayesh Patel, DMD, MsC

Meet Dr. Jayesh Patel, the owner and lead dentist at Clayton Family Dental. Originally from South Africa, Dr. Patel moved to the United States at the age of nine years old. He spent part of his childhood right here in the Triangle before his family relocated to Massachusetts. Dr. Patel’s passion for dentistry is personal; he credits a childhood accident with guiding him toward the field and giving him a unique empathy for his patients.

Dr. Patel is an active faculty member at Boston University Dental and CDOCs Go, where he shares his passion for educating both patients and other dentists. His comprehensive care philosophy means patients receive personalized treatments designed not just for their oral health but their overall well-being.

Dr. Patel is thrilled to return to the Triangle to serve the Clayton and Johnston County communities with honest, compassionate care. When he’s not practicing dentistry, Dr. Patel enjoys hiking, exploring national parks, tinkering with cars, and savoring local ice cream shops. Deeply connected to his family and community, he aims to bring a personal touch to Clayton Family Dental, ensuring every patient feels cared for and valued.

First Visit

What to expect when you trust us with your smile

From the moment you step into Clayton Family Dental, you’ll feel right at home. Your first visit begins with a warm welcome, followed by a personalized consultation to understand your needs. With state-of-the-art tools and a focus on thorough care, we’ll provide a detailed assessment and create a tailored plan designed to help you achieve and maintain your healthiest smile.

Patient Experience We believe in combining top-notch clinical expertise with a compassionate approach. You’ll enjoy a relaxed, judgment-free space where your questions are welcomed, and your comfort is our top priority. Whether it’s your first appointment or a follow-up, you can count on precise care delivered with respect and kindness.

What to Bring For your first visit, please bring the following:

Your dental insurance card (if applicable)

Any recent dental records or X-rays, if available

A list of any questions or concerns you’d like us to address

Advanced technology for your comfort and care

Technology plays a key role in delivering the efficient, high-quality care you deserve. At Clayton Family Dental, we’ve invested in state-of-the-art equipment to ensure accurate diagnoses, effective treatments, and greater convenience during every visit.

Cerec CAD/CAM for same-day crowns

Forget about waiting weeks for a new crown. With CEREC CAD/CAM technology, we create custom crowns in just one visit, minimizing disruption to your schedule while providing excellent, long-lasting results.

CBCT for precision imaging

Our CBCT imaging system allows us to capture highly detailed 3D images for precise planning of procedures like dental implants or root canal therapy. This cutting-edge technology ensures optimal outcomes and boosts patient confidence in their care.

Giving back to the community

Partnering with Note in the Pocket for a stronger, cared-for community

We’re proud to support Note in the Pocket, a local nonprofit addressing clothing insecurity for children and families in need. Providing dignity through access to basic necessities like clothing helps empower individuals to achieve their goals and lead fulfilling lives. We believe contributing to the well-being of our community is an essential part of who we are. Together, we can build a brighter, healthier future for everyone in Clayton.
