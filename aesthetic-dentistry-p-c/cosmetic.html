Crowns

Serving as a protective overlay, dental crowns are the solution for teeth that have been weakened by extensive decay, multiple fillings, or general instability. Beyond providing structural reinforcement, crowns enhance dental aesthetics through their uniform color and shape. They excel in correcting size discrepancies and misshapen teeth, and are pivotal in supporting dental bridges or covering implants, merging function and form flawlessly.

Ceramic & Porcelain Veneers

Operating on the forefront of non-surgical cosmetic dental enhancements, veneers are slender ceramics or porcelain layers adeptly affixed to the teeth’s surface. Versatile in their application, veneers offer aesthetic and restorative remedies for a variety of concerns including discoloration, minor misalignments, shape irregularities, and damage. With a spectrum of material choices, your dentist will guide you towards the veneers best suited to your dental aspirations, ensuring a harmonious and enhanced smile.
