Sealants

Dental sealants offer an excellent protective shield for molars and premolars, areas often tricky to clean thoroughly. Applied as a thin coating, sealants prevent plaque and bacteria from eroding the tooth enamel. This preventative solution is particularly beneficial for young patients but can be applied at any age to safeguard teeth.

Tooth Extractions

Tooth removal, while seemingly drastic, can act as a preventative strategy under certain circumstances. For instance, extracting impacted or improperly growing wisdom teeth can prevent overcrowding and infections, averting more serious dental issues. When infections advance significantly, extraction might become the necessary course of action, followed by treatments such as implants, crowns, or bridges.

Treating Dental Anxiety

For those with severe dental anxiety, nitrous oxide sedation and minimal (oral) sedation are proven options that facilitate a stress-free dental visit without pain or anxiety. Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is administered as a colorless gas, it allows patients to remain conscious and communicative while comfortably undergoing dental procedures. Minimal oral sedation involves anti-anxiety pills prescribed by your primary doctor.

Mouth & Night Guards

We recognize the importance of protecting teeth from damage. Our custom-fitted mouth guards offer robust defense against sports-related injuries, ensuring durability with proper care. Similarly, night guards are vital for those who grind their teeth in their sleep, preventing wear and potential disorders like TMD by cushioning the teeth during the night.

Gum Treatments

Addressing gum disease early on is critical to preventing damage to gums and the bones supporting teeth, which could lead to tooth loss. Aesthetic Dentistry offers comprehensive gum disease assessment and treatment options, responding to various disease severities and helping patients maintain healthy gums.
