About Us

Dental Providers Caring For Your Smile with a Holistic Approach

Nestled in the heart of Cheyenne, WY, Aesthetic Dentistry, P.C. is renowned for its patient-focused approach to dental care. Patients consistently praise the practice for transforming routine dental appointments into extraordinary experiences of comfort and personalized attention.

From the moment you step into the clinic, the professional yet warm atmosphere sets the stage for exemplary dentistry, whether it’s for a simple check-up or a comprehensive smile restoration. By prioritizing patient education, comfort, and state-of-the-art care, this dentist and their team have rightfully earned a reputation for excellence in oral health within the community.

Dr. Krivy and Dr. Dziardziel graduated from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 1993 and took over an office from a dentist that was leaving the state. As their practice grew, they built a new office building in 2006 and have been there ever since. Their daughter, Dr. Harlow, joined Aesthetic Dentistry in 2024.

Get To Know Our Dentists

Dr. Marykay Krivy

Dr. Mary Kay Krivy grew up in Flint, Michigan and moved to Wyoming at age 17. Her undergraduate studies included two years at Sheridan College and two years at the University of Wyoming. During that time, she decided the western lifestyle was captivating and took up horseback riding, which would lead to her losing one of her top front teeth.

She was mentored by Dr. Ray Braten, a general dentist in Buffalo, Wyoming. In 1988, she started at Creighton University School of Dentistry. She married her classmate, Dr. Eric Dziardziel, in 1989, and they both graduated in 1993 shortly after having their first child. They moved to Cheyenne, Wyoming, and have been in practice together for 30 years. They now have three children and one grandchild (and another on the way).

She loves spending time with her cats and staying active. Over the years, she has been into rollerblading, racquetball, running, biking, hiking, and weightlifting (no more horses). She also is a “forever student” and enjoys taking courses and reading publications to keep up to date in the field of dentistry.

Dr. Krivy is the current president-elect of the Wyoming Dental Association and is working hard to improve dentistry for both providers and patients. She has a fellowship through the Dawson Academy and is a past president of the Southeast District Dental Society . She volunteers her skills at Give Kids a Smile.

Dr. Eric A. Dziardziel

Dr. Eric Dziardziel grew up in Douglas, Wyoming on his family’s cattle ranch. He still drives up on weekends to help out with the cattle, horses, and machinery (he even worked as a mechanic in dental school). He has a soft spot for all animals and loves playing with his grandchild.

He graduated from the University of Wyoming with a Bachelors degree in Zoology in 1988. He attended Creighton University’s School of Dentistry, where he met his wife, Dr. Krivy, and had the first of three children. The doctors both graduated in 1993 and moved to Cheyenne, Wyoming to start their dental practice that started on Dell Range Blvd before outgrowing the space and moving into the building that they are in today.

Dr. Dziardziel is trilingual (English, French, and Spanish). He has a fellowship through the Dawson Academy and is a past president of the Southeast District Dental Society . He volunteers his skills at Give Kids a Smile and Donated Dental Services .

Dr. Drew Harlow

Dr. Drew Ann Harlow grew up in Cheyenne, Wyoming, and is proud to serve the community she calls home. She attended the University of Wyoming, where she earned bachelor’s degrees in Physiology and Psychology in 2019. She went on to graduate from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 2024.

Dr. Harlow is passionate about providing compassionate, high-quality dental care with a focus on patient comfort, education, and overall well-being. She believes in a holistic approach to dentistry and enjoys helping patients feel informed and confident about their oral health. Dr. Harlow works especially well with patients who experience dental anxiety, utilizing Anxiety Free Dentistry (AFD) methods to create a comfortable and positive experience.

Committed to giving back to the community, Dr. Harlow volunteers her skills through programs such as Give Kids a Smile and Donated Dental Services , helping expand access to dental care for underserved individuals and families.

Dr. Harlow is the daughter of Dr. Dziardziel and Dr. Krivy. She married Joseph Harlow in 2019, and together they have a toddler and an infant. Outside the office, she is a proud cat person and enjoys Harry Potter (Ravenclaw), Star Wars, Disney, and Percy Jackson.
