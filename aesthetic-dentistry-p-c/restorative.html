Dentures

The absence of natural teeth significantly impacts one’s appearance, speech, eating habits, and overall comfort. At Aesthetic Dentistry, we dedicate ourselves to crafting custom dentures that are carefully tailored in size, color, and material to match each individual’s needs, ensuring a natural look and comfortable fit.

Root Canals

Despite the daunting reputation of root canal treatments, they serve as a crucial intervention to save an infected tooth from extraction. A root canal preserves the tooth’s structure.

Implant Restoration

Recognized as a superior method for replacing missing teeth, implant restoration involves the placement of a titanium post into the jawbone, acting as a new root for the artificial tooth. This procedure not only restores the aesthetics of a smile with a custom-made crown but also supports bone preservation and can replace one or several teeth.

Bridges

Dental bridges effectively fill the gap left by missing teeth with a fixed, non-removable appliance, providing a stable and aesthetically pleasing solution. They not only restore the smile but also help maintain the position of adjacent teeth and the overall shape of the face, offering patients confidence and comfort.
