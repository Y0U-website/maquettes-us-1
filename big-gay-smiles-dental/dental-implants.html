What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone beneath your gums. Once in place, they allow your dentist to mount replacement teeth onto them. Because implants fuse to your jawbone, they provide stable support for artificial teeth, dentures , and bridges that won&#x27;t slip or shift in your mouth—an important benefit for eating and speaking.

Advantages of Choosing Dental Implants

Longevity and Strength

Unlike other restorative options, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. They are made from titanium, which integrates with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This fusion helps the implants act as a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth, providing a durability that mimics natural teeth.

Enhanced Oral Health and Comfort

Dental implants help preserve natural tooth tissue by avoiding the need to cut down adjacent teeth for conventional bridgework. They also help maintain jawbone structure, preventing bone deterioration that occurs when teeth are missing. The result is improved long-term oral health and enhanced comfort.

The Dental Implant Procedure at Big Gay Smiles

Initial Consultation and Imaging

Your journey to a restored smile begins with a detailed assessment using state-of-the-art digital imaging technology. This allows us to assess your oral health thoroughly and plan the implant procedure with high precision.

Implant Placement Surgery

The implant placement is a minor surgical procedure performed under local anesthesia or sedation. At Big Gay Smiles, we ensure your comfort throughout the procedure, using gentle techniques to minimize discomfort and promote a quick recovery.

Healing and Osseointegration

After the implant is placed, a period of healing is required for osseointegration to occur. During this time, the implant will securely fuse with the jawbone, creating a strong and durable foundation for your new tooth.

Crown Attachment

Once your implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, a custom-made crown that matches your natural teeth in color and shape will be attached. This final step restores full functionality and aesthetics to your smile.
