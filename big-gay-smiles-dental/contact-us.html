Our Commitment to LGBTQ+ Health Equity (H2)

At Big Gay Smiles, our dedication to the LGBTQ+ community is deeply rooted in personal experience and a commitment to inclusivity. Dr. Rob McKernan, our founder, knows firsthand the challenges of navigating the dental industry as an LGBTQ+ individual. His experience with discrimination in a Utah dental office during his undergraduate years was a pivotal moment that fueled his passion for creating a welcoming and affirming space for all.

Dr. Rob is proud to be a part of the Outcare Health Community, the most comprehensive resource for LGBTQ+ health equity. This affiliation reflects our ongoing commitment to providing high-quality, affirming care that meets the unique needs of our diverse community. In our practice, we focus on comfort, quality, and community, striving to redefine what it means to offer queer-focused dental care.

A cornerstone of our practice is our “10 % Promise,” where we donate 10 % of our annual revenue to local and national HIV/AIDS non-profits. This initiative underscores our belief in giving back and supporting the causes that matter most to our community.

Learn more about our journey and Dr. Rob’s commitment to ending LGBTQ2IA+ discrimination in the dental industry by visiting his profile on Outcare Health.
