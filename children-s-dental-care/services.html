Our Services

Preventative Dental Care

Children’s Dental Care emphasizes comprehensive preventative dental care to safeguard the oral health of young smiles from ages 1 through the teen years. Nestled in Batesville, IN, our dedicated team offers services, from regular dental hygiene check-ups to sealants and fluoride treatments, designed to protect your child’s teeth from cavities and promote good home oral health routines.

Leveraging advanced techniques and technologies, we ensure that each visit contributes to the long-term health and cleanliness of your child’s teeth. Our practice is committed to educating both parents and children on avoiding common dental issues, such as bruxism, baby bottle tooth decay, and thumb sucking, through personalized dental care plans. Contact us if you have any questions or want to know more regarding our services.

Our Services

Preventative Dental Care

Preventive dental care emphasizes regular check-ups and cleanings, patient education on proper brushing and flossing, and the application of sealants and fluoride treatments. These measures aim to prevent cavities and gum disease, promoting overall oral health.

Digital X-Rays

Our Digital X-ray service uses advanced technology to minimize radiation exposure, making it safe for kids and eco-friendly. It helps us detect early signs of decay and other dental issues, ensuring proactive care for your child’s oral health.

Nitrous Oxide

Our Nitrous Oxide, or laughing gas, service offers a pain-reducing and calming effect for children during dental visits. It’s safe, with minimal side effects, and wears off quickly. We provide all necessary pre- and post-treatment instructions for a stress-free experience.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry enhances the look of your gums, teeth, or bite with treatments like veneers, whitening, crowns, bridges, implants, and tooth-colored fillings.
