About Our Practice

Meet The Professionals Behind Your Beautiful Smile At Children’s Dental Care

Children’s Dental Care in Batesville, IN, under the expert care of Dr. Erica Bayoneto and Dr. Carol McKown, is the only pediatric dentist within a 60-mile radius dedicated to treating children from age 1 through their teenage years. Aligning with the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry’s recommendation, our practice urges the initiation of dental visits by age one. Our commitment is to provide a warm, welcoming environment where young patients can embrace dental health with confidence, building a lasting foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

About Children’s Dental Care

Meet Dr. Carol McKown

After a previous tenure at Children’s Dental Care two decades ago, Dr. Carol has joyfully rejoined the team, committing her vibrant personality and empathetic care to our community every Tuesday. When asked to return, she was so excited because she “loves the people of this community.” Known for her boisterous laughter and singing lullabies to little patients, she adds a touch of warmth to appointments.

When she’s not providing excellent dental care, Dr. Carol enjoys life with her family, including her partner Rex, her three adult children—Katie, Annie, and David—and her beloved grandchildren. Her interests outside of work include traveling, ballroom dancing, and pursuing new languages.

Meet Dr. Erica Bayoneto

Dr. Erica Bayoneto is a distinguished graduate of Michigan State University. She furthered her dental education by obtaining her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Michigan.

Dr Erica enjoys traveling and spending time with her family.
