The practice

Brandon K. Farrell, DDS has over fourteen years experience as a dentist. After graduating with a Bachelors of Science in Biology from Indiana University in Bloomington, Indiana, Dr. Farrell obtained his DDS from Indiana University School of Dentistry in Indianapolis, Indiana. After graduation, Dr. Farrell served in the U.S. Navy Dental Corps as a Dental Officer.

Lyndaray has been a dental assistant in offices throughout Wilmington and the surrounding counties for over 10 years. She received her Dental Assistant’s certificate through the University of North Carolina Board in 1997. Lyndaray is a long time resident of Rocky Point with her husband, son, and daughter.

Giovina is a Dental Hygienist at Brush Dental Care. She graduated from Cape Fear Community College’s Dental Hygiene program in 2004.
