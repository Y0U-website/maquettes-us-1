The practice

Braces Traditional braces are an excellent way to straighten teeth and create room for optimal growth. A time tested solution for enhancing the overall function and look of the smile, they’re effective for patients of all ages and stages of life!

Phase 1 Ortho Also known as early or interceptive orthodontics, Phase 1 is for children, typically between the ages of 6 and 10, when they still have some of their baby teeth. This phase helps encourage proper growth and development, with the goal of addressing a pertinent issue before it becomes a major problem.
