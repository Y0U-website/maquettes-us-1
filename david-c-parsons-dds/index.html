The practice

Rooted in small-town values and “treating people right” – we’re confident once you visit our dental office, you’ll quickly realize what makes us so unique.

This dental office was established by my father, Dr. Ray, when he returned from the Korean War. After he retired in 1990 and I was finished with my dental training, I took it over. I was born and raised in Winfield, making it important to me as a young dentist to come back and provide the community I love with top-tier, trustworthy dental care with a small-town feel that’s authentic to the area.…

Dr. Ray established a legacy of caring in 1954, and today, Dr. David Parsons continues that tradition. Even when patients don’t feel as though there’s a solution to the issue they face, with the latest technology and dental techniques in our pockets, we can solve virtually any oral health problem.

Our phenomenal, experienced dental team is what truly makes our dental office stand out. Most of our staff has been here for a decade or longer, and over that time, they’ve gotten to know every patient who visits us. Not only are they kind, but they also know exactly what they’re doing!

We believe great dentistry should be easy and accessible to everyone. Join us.

We understand how much of an investment dental insurance is, which is why we always help our patients maximize their benefits and are in-network with several PPO plans.

We also offer flexible additional financing through CareCredit. That way, you can split the cost of any treatment up into easily manageable monthly payments at little to no interest.

The focus at our dental office is to keep oral health problems from ever having the opportunity to wreak havoc on your smile. With routine checkups and cleanings, fluoride treatments, teeth grinding nightguards, and other preventive services, we can help you save money and keep your mouth healthy.
