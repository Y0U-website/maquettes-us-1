Meet the Doctor

Dr. Lawson Batten

After high school, Dr. Batten attended North Carolina State University, earning his bachelor’s degree in Human Biology with a minor in Psychology. He then went on to earn his Dental degree from East Carolina University School of Dental Medicine. Committed to serving the community that shaped him, Dr. Batten chose to stay and practice in eastern North Carolina.

Dr. Batten’s philosophy is simple: treat every patient the way he would want to be treated. He takes the time to get to know his patients personally, ensuring that each individual receives compassionate, personalized care. Dedicated to continual growth, Dr. Batten pursues advanced training in implants, root canals, and cosmetic dentistry to offer the highest quality care and help his patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

He is an active member of the American Dental Association , the Academy of General Dentistry , and the North Carolina Dental Society . Outside the office, Dr. Batten is an avid sports fan—especially when it comes to soccer, football, and anything NC State. He also enjoys traveling, reading, and hiking in his free time.

Dr. Batten looks forward to welcoming you to Batten Family Dental. Schedule your appointment today and take the first step toward a smile you’re proud to share.
