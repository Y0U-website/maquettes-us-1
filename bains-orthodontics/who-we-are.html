0 Who We Are Bains Orthodontics is proudly spreading smiles in the Lansing and Okemos communities as the leading provider for family orthodontics.

After many years of perfecting smiles and serving as a staple in the Lansing community, Dr. Ward is passing his orthodontic torch to Dr. Bains.

Dr. Ward and Dr. Bains have been working side-by-side for the past six months to prepare for this transition and Dr. Bains is ready to continue Dr. Ward’s remarkable legacy of changing smiles and transforming lives.

Join Dr. Bains and his team as they boost confidence, improve oral health and impact the lives of every patient who walks through their doors.

Meet Dr. Bains Dr. Balraj Bains brings orthodontic knowledge from all over the world to serve both the Lansing and Okemos communities as your trusted family orthodontist. After completing his dental education at Semmelweis University in Europe, and gaining three years of experience as a dentist in London, England, he furthered his studies in orthodontics at the University of Alabama.

During this time, he had the privilege of studying under one of the world’s foremost orthodontists. Dr. Bains is ABO certified and is a member of the AJO and MDA. Dr. Bains, his wife, and their one-year-old son are thrilled to call Lansing home, and Dr. Bains is ready to spread smiles all across Ingham County.
