​ Insurance Information

If you have dental insurance, we’re happy to help you navigate your benefits. We can submit claims on your behalf and bill most PPO plans directly.

Even if we’re out-of-network with your insurance plan, you may still be able to use your benefits at our office. We can submit claims and help you understand your coverage and estimated out-of-pocket costs.

Because your insurance policy is a contract between you and your insurance company, we are a third party and may not have access to every plan’s fee schedules, limitations, or coverage rules. While we’ll do our best to help you understand your benefits, your insurance provider ultimately determines coverage and payment.

If you’d like an estimate of your insurance coverage before treatment, we can request a pre-authorization from your insurance company for treatment recommended by Dr. Poh. This can provide a more accurate estimate of your out-of-pocket costs before scheduling treatment. Pre-authorizations are available upon request and with your consent.

Have questions about your insurance, treatment costs, or our financial policy? Call or text us at (707) 963-4611. We’re happy to help make navigating your dental care as simple as possible.

DENTAL INSURANCE HAS SEVERAL LIMITATIONS:

Most dental insurance plans have an annual maximum the most your insurance will pay toward your care in a calendar year. This is typically between $500 and $2,500.

The way your insurance pays out this maximum depends on your coverage percentages .

Plans typically cover different types of treatment at different coverage percentages (typically ranging from 40 %-100 %) for different categories of service:

- Basic Restorative (fillings/deep cleanings)

- Major Restorative (crowns/extractions)

The dollar amount your insurance actually pays is also based on the insurance company’s own f ee schedule which is always different from our office fees.

A fee schedule is a complete list of predetermined maximum prices or allowed amounts that an insurance company will pay a doctor for specific services. Because of this, you can typically expect to receive a balance after insurance processes your claim, even if you have already paid your estimated portion at the time of treatment.

We do not have access to every insurance plan’s fee schedule, so we can provide estimates, but not guaranteed final costs. If you’d like a more accurate estimate before treatment, we can submit a pre-authorization to your insurance company upon request. While pre-authorizations are still estimates and not guarantees of payment, they can give you a better idea of what your insurance may cover and what you may owe.

YOUR DENTAL OFFICE WORKS WITH YOU, NOT YOUR INSURANCE

We’re here to work with you not your insurance company. While our team isn’t made up of insurance specialists, we’re happy to bill your dental insurance as a courtesy. We’ll submit claims and help with the process so you don’t have to manage everything on your own.

Your insurance benefits are determined by your individual plan and your insurance company. Regardless of what your insurance pays, you are responsible for our office fees for the care you receive.

Our doctors’ recommendations are based on your individual dental needs and the health of your teeth and gums not on what your insurance covers. We will never recommend treatment simply because it’s covered, nor limit your treatment options because it isn’t. When there are multiple ways to address a dental concern, we’ll discuss your options, including alternatives that may better fit your needs and budget.

We understand that dental care can be a significant expense. If needed, we’re happy to discuss payment options to help make your care more manageable.
