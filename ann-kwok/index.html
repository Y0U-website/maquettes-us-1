The practice

Dr. Ann treats every patient with the "mom" test: if she wouldn’t recommend a treatment to her mother, she won’t recommend it to you. You’ll always leave with clear answers, even when that means telling you to save your money for the long-term results you actually want.

Step into a welcoming atmosphere that feels more like your favorite coffee shop than a clinic. We pick up right where we left off in your last visit to continue providing you with care that makes you feel comfortable and safe.

We leverage advanced dental technology to give you accurate and gentle care. While Dr. Ann takes the necessary time to ensure every detail is perfect, her meticulous use of modern tools means your results are predictable, and your recovery is smoother.

I knew I wanted to be a dentist when I realized I could marry the artistry of design with the privilege of taking care of people.

Dr. Ann is the kind of dentist who explains things until you truly understand, not with rushed medical jargon, but by taking the time to draw it out or walk through it on the screen. Beyond this, her commitment to learning never stops. Most weekends, she’s traveling for training. Not because she has to, but because she believes you deserve a dentist who’s always getting better.

You deserve an advocate who values your health as much as you do. Dr. Ann only recommends solutions that are truly in your best interest. She’ll guide you toward a predictable, permanent smile that feels so natural you’ll forget you ever had missing teeth.

The dentist isn’t exactly on your "top five" list of places to be. That’s why Dr. Ann focuses on being your advocate first and your clinician second. She provides thorough, judgment-free checkups to protect your whole-body health for the long term.

Most people expect a dental visit to feel clinical and impersonal. Ours doesn’t start that way. You’ll begin in a room that feels more like a friend’s living room than a medical office. We start by talking - really talking - before you ever see the dental chair. About what’s been bothering you. What you’re hoping to fix. What questions have been keeping you up at night?
