Crowns & Cerec Crowns

Same Day Crowns in Shreveport Bossier

A crown is a cap that covers the tooth surface restoring the tooth to normal function. There are numerous reasons a patient may need a crown, including but not limited to:

  • Decayed teeth.
  • Decayed large fillings.
  • Broken or fractured teeth.
  • Root canal.
  • Cosmetic enhancement.
  • More youthful looking smile.
  • Straighten teeth.

Cerec Crowns (Same Day All Porcelain Crowns)

Our dentists have found in many circumstances patients think they don’t have time for cosmetic dentistry because of lengthy appointments, but with Cerec crowns and bridges, dreams and beautiful smiles do come true. This state of the art equipment actually takes a 3D impression of your tooth allowing our dentist to create your new smile. The Cerec crowns can fix a broken tooth, a discolored tooth, an out of align tooth, a root canaled tooth that needs protection, and more. The possibilities with Cerec crowns and Cerec bridges are just about limitless.
