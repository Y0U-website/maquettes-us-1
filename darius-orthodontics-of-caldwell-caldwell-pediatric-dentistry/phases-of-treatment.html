Bonding

Bonding is the process of placing your orthodontic devices. This is your first official appointment, a very exciting time, and your first step to a beautiful smile. The process takes approximately and hour.

Adjustments

The following appointments, where the team will make adjustments to your devices, occur approximately once a month, though there are exceptions when will they be closer or father apart.
