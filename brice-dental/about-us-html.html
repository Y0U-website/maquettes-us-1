About Our Dental Office San Antonio, TX

Your Smile, Our Passion, Friends for Life

The moment you walk into our dental office, you’ll quickly realize that we do things a bit differently here. The dental office should feel more like a home, not just because it’s cozy and accommodating, but because we care deeply about establishing relationships with our patients that last a lifetime. By working as a team and combining our talents, we can provide care that is truly comprehensive from top to bottom. We can’t wait to meet you, so don’t wait to give Brice Dental a call today !

Locally-Owned & Operated Dental Office

Dr. Zachary Brice is incredibly proud to call his dental office locally-owned and not controlled by large corporations. Not only does this give him more freedom to provide exceptional dentistry to the community, but his expertise means he doesn’t have to send patients out to specialists. Furthermore, you can expect a dentist and team who provides care based on trust, not on how much money we can make. We care deeply about your lives; after all, it’s part of making you feel comfortable and at-home.

Home-Like Dental Atmosphere

Most dental offices have a tendency to feel cold or clinical in nature, but not Brice Dental. Instead, we feel like a home on the inside. When you visit to have your dental treatments completed, we take the time to hear how you’re doing, just like if you were visiting extended family. Our many services are meant to help people of all ages, and we never try to force treatment on those who may not want it. Our policy of asking patients what they want to do, rather than tell them, truly makes us stand out.

Our Dental Team Goes Above & Beyond

The team at Brice Dental has been working together for over 20 years, and it shows. Dr. Brice has a personal love for all of his team members and greatly appreciates their passion for serving others. He believes it demonstrates true character that they go above and beyond to bring joy to others by helping them achieve a beautiful and healthy smile. No matter what your needs are, our team is prepared to overdeliver and go above and beyond your expectations.

Committed to Community

Dr. Brice and his team are no strangers to giving back to the community in a variety of ways. For one, Brice Dental has raised over $100,000 for children’s charities over the past several years. We’ve also done work with Smiles for Life in partnership with the Crown Council. If there is an event or cause that you believe our dental office should be involved with, please don’t hesitate to tell us about it. We love finding new ways to give back to the community that has already given so much to us!
