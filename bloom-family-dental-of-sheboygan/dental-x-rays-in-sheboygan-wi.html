What are Dental X-Rays?

Dental X-rays are an important diagnostic tool that helps us detect dental problems that may not be visible during a regular exam. They provide detailed images of your teeth, gums, jawbone, and surrounding structures to support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. At Bloom Family Dental, we use digital dental X-rays to identify cavities, infections, bone loss, impacted teeth, and other oral health concerns early while ensuring a safe and comfortable experience for every patient.

Why Would You Need Dental X-Rays?

Dental X-rays help detect oral health problems that may not be visible during a regular dental exam. They provide detailed images of the teeth, roots, jawbone, and surrounding areas to help identify issues early. X-rays can reveal cavities between teeth, infections, bone loss, impacted teeth, and other hidden dental concerns. They are also useful for monitoring children’s dental development and planning future treatments.

At Bloom Family Dental, we use safe and modern digital X-ray technology to support accurate diagnosis and personalized dental care.

What Makes Someone a Candidate for Dental X-Rays?

Most patients may need dental X-rays as part of their routine dental care. X-rays are safe, quick, and helpful for identifying problems that cannot be seen during a regular exam.

They may be recommended to check for cavities, infections, bone loss, impacted teeth, or changes in oral health over time. Before taking X-rays, our team will review your dental and medical history to ensure safe and appropriate care.
