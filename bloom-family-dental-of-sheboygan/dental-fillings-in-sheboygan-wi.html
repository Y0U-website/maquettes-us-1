Dental Fillings in Sheboygan, WI

What is a Dental Filling?

Tooth fillings in Sheboygan, WI, are a common and effective way to treat cavities and restore damaged teeth. They help repair areas where decay has weakened the tooth, preventing further damage.

When a cavity forms, it can gradually break down the tooth structure and reach deeper layers if left untreated. Through dental filling treatment, the decay is removed and replaced with a durable material. Most commonly, we use composite fillings that people prefer because they blend seamlessly with natural teeth, providing natural-looking dental fillings while supporting tooth preservation and enamel repair.

Why might a Dental Filling be needed?

If a cavity is left untreated, it can grow and lead to more complex procedures like a root canal. A cavity filling is recommended early to remove decay and protect the tooth from further damage.

Choosing tooth fillings helps restore strength and function while maintaining the natural appearance of your smile. This simple treatment plays a key role in tooth preservation and prevents the need for more extensive dental work in the future.

Who is a candidate for Dental Fillings?

Many patients are unaware they have a cavity until they visit us for a routine exam. During your visit, we check for early signs of decay using specialized tools and X-rays.

If decay is detected, dental filling treatment is usually recommended right away. Patients with minor to moderate cavities are ideal candidates for composite fillings, which provide both durability and a natural look.

What to Expect During a Dental Filling Procedure?

The procedure begins by gently numbing the area to ensure your comfort. The decayed portion of the tooth is carefully removed to stop the spread of damage.

Next, the tooth is prepared for the filling using a conditioning gel. Once ready, the tooth-colored material is placed and shaped to match your natural tooth. The filling is then hardened using a special light.

This process not only restores the tooth but also supports enamel repair and long-term tooth preservation. Multiple fillings can be completed in one visit if needed, making tooth fillings a convenient and effective solution.
