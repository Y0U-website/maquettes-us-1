ABOUT Us

Hello

Welcome to Bloom Family Dental, where we provide compassionate, personalized dental care for every family member using modern treatment approaches in a comfortable, welcoming environment.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Nathan Bloom

Dr. Bloom is dedicated to providing compassionate, personalized dental care for every patient. He focuses on building lasting relationships and takes the time to understand each patient’s individual needs, concerns, and goals. Alongside his caring team, Dr. Bloom is committed to helping patients feel comfortable while achieving and maintaining optimal oral health.

“My favorite part about being a dentist is getting to know my patients individually and helping dental visits become a more comfortable part of their lives. I enjoy seeing families grow, hearing about patients’ experiences, and building connections within the Sheboygan community.” – Dr. Bloom

Dr. Bloom graduated from UW–Eau Claire with degrees in Chemistry and Business before earning his dental degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2011. Family plays an important role in his life. He and his wife, Valerie, have five children: Lydia, Eli, Benjamin, and twins Noah and Judah.

Outside the office, Dr. Bloom enjoys hiking and biking adventures with his family, listening to music, spending time with friends and extended family, and enjoying good coffee with his wife. They are also active members of their church and enjoy serving the community together.

Dr. Bloom is passionate about continuing education and stays up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry. He completes more than 100 hours of continuing education annually to provide patients with the highest quality care and modern treatment options.

Continuing Education: Kois Center Continuum Clinical Mastery Continuum DOCS - Oral Conscious Sedation The Art of Endodontics with Stephen Buchanon Lakeshore Study Club

What Guides Our Care

Integrity

Care begins with trust, and we believe patients deserve honesty, transparency, and support at every step.

  • Putting your needs and oral health first at every visit.
  • Listening carefully and working together to support your dental goals.
  • Providing honest, respectful, and clear communication you can trust.
  • Maintaining a dependable and caring team focused on delivering the best patient experience.

Compassion

We know dental visits can feel personal and sometimes overwhelming, which is why we lead with kindness and understanding.

  • Treating every patient with kindness, dignity, and respect.
  • Providing gentle, effective care tailored to your individual needs and goals.
  • Creating a comfortable environment where patients feel valued and supported.
  • Taking time to understand your concerns, comfort, and overall well-being, not just your dental health.

Excellence

Great dental care should feel thoughtful, precise, and built to support your smile for years to come.

  • Helping patients achieve and maintain long-term oral health.
  • Creating healthy, confident smiles patients can feel proud of.
  • Delivering precise, detail-focused treatment with trusted results.
  • Providing the high-quality patient care and service every patient deserves.
