ORAL CANCER SCREENINGS

Examination of the mouth and throat to detect any abnormalities.

WHITE FILLINGS

Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore and mimic natural tooth structure.

PORCELAIN & GOLD CROWNS

Lab-made restorations that replace missing or damaged tooth structure. The main difference between the two is the porcelain is tooth colored and gold makes you look fancy.

INLAYS & ONLAYS

Lab-made restorations that are placed on teeth when the cavity or lost tooth structure is too large to be restored by a filling. The process is very similar to a crown. After the tooth is prepared, it is cemented or bonded to the tooth.

IMPLANT PLACEMENT & RESTORATION

This is a great option for missing teeth. We place a synthetic tooth root (a titanium post) in the jaw and place the replacement tooth on the post. They are also a great option to increase the stability of partials and dentures.

ROOT CANAL THERAPY

Definitely the one everyone hears the most about, but don’t worry, we’ve got you covered if you’ve heard one too many horror stories. It’s a treatment of infected or severely inflamed root tissue. The infected or dead tissue is removed and the canal is cleaned, medicated and filled with a special material.

EXTRACTIONS

Exactly what it sounds like. Unfortunately, sometimes you have to kick out a house guest.

CONNECTIVE TISSUE GRAFTING

One of the specialized treatments we offer. Used to treat root exposure and maintain healthy gums and bone.

DENTURES & PARTIALS

Natural looking replacement teeth that are removable.

COSMETIC DENTISTRY

  • Porcelain veneers - thin pieces of porcelain used to recreate the natural look of teeth. It can be a great choice for those looking to make position alterations, or to change tooth shape, size, and/or color.
  • Teeth whitening
  • Replacement of old fillings
