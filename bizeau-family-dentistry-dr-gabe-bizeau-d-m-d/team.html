TEAM Bizeau Family Dentistry

The Team

Being small has its benefits. We are a little family under this roof. When you come here, you’ll get to know all of us, and all of us will get to know you. You’ll find we’re all here for the same reason, to provide quality care to each of our visitors in a comfortable and safe atmosphere. We love our team and can’t wait for you to meet everyone who makes Bizeau Family Dentistry a place we love to work!

Dr. Gabe

I grew up just a couple of hours away in Newberg, a small town just south of Portland, and majored in biology at the University of Idaho. I came back home as a graduate student and attended Oregon Health and Science University in Portland and received my Doctorate in 2007.

I am always studying and working to improve this practice for my patients.* I don’t believe in scary, overbearing care or treatment measures. Here, we aim for preventative, gentle, and comprehensive care for everyone who visits us. Our goal is to not only provide treatment but also knowledge to promote a healthy mouth for life.

On top of typical care, we are able to provide many services that are typically outsourced, enabling our patients to be cared for by a team they already know and trust.

I love hiking, surfing, taking care of the family farm animals, and especially spending time with my wife and four daughters.

*I’ve participated in study clubs including oral surgery, dental implants, sedation, esthetics, early intervention orthodontics, prevention, and connective tissue grafting. I am lucky to be an active participant in the Tooth taxi here in Tillamook county and also the Medical Teams International dental van. I’ve served on the board for the Oregon Academy of General Dentistry, the primary provider for continuing education in Oregon.
