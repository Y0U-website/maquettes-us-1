Dental Implants & Full-Mouth…

If you’re looking for bargain dentistry or quick cosmetic fixes, this page probably isn’t for you.

Full-mouth rehabilitation is a comprehensive approach to rebuilding your entire smile — not just fixing individual teeth as they fail. Whether through full-arch implants, implant-supported bridges, or a combination of advanced techniques, the goal is long-term stability, function, and confidence.

Most patients we see are tired of “fixing one tooth at a time.”

Dr. Jonathan Austin focuses on advanced implant and full-mouth reconstruction cases, combining surgical precision with restorative expertise. His work includes full-arch implant solutions and complex rehabilitations that require careful planning and execution.

This Is Not a Sales Appointment. Your evaluation is designed to give you clarity — not pressure.

4. Long-Term Stability Designed for durability, function, and confidence

This visit is designed to give you clarity about your options — whether that includes full-arch implants, implant-supported bridges, or alternative solutions.

Am I too old for dental implants? Age alone does not disqualify you. Overall health, bone quality, and goals matter more.

What if I’ve been told I don’t have enough bone? Advanced planning and modern techniques often allow solutions even when bone loss is present.

Can I be comfortable or asleep for treatment? Yes. Comfort and anxiety management options are available and discussed at your evaluation.

Do you offer financing? Yes. Financing options are available and reviewed during your consultation.

What if implants aren’t right for me? You’ll be told honestly. Our goal is the right solution — not the most expensive one.

If my teeth are savable, how long will it take? Each case is different but if we are just using the existing teeth, then 4-6 months. If we have to use an interdisciplinary approach, (Braces, Implants, Restorative, Periodontal) then it could take up to 18 months start to finish.
