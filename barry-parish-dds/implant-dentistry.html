Dental Implants

If dental anxiety is a concern, we offer conscious oral sedation to help you relax during the implant procedure, minimizing discomfort and anxiety.

At Benicia Family Dentistry, we also provide advanced laser and gum procedures for patients requiring extensive treatment. Additionally, we offer solutions for treatment for sleep apnea,ensuring comprehensive care for your overall oral health and well-being.

  • Prevention of Teeth Shifting:Implants help prevent neighboring teeth from shifting into the gap left by a missing tooth, maintaining proper alignment.
  • Enhanced Chewing:With the stability of dental implants, you can enjoy improved chewing function, allowing you to eat your favorite foods with confidence.
  • Preservation of Facial Structure:Implants support the jawbone, preventing bone loss and preserving your facial structure.
  • Prevention of Bone Damage:By replacing the missing tooth root, implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing deterioration over time.
