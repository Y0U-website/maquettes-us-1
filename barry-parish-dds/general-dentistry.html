General dentistry forms the cornerstone of a healthy smile. Our skilled hygienists, assistants, and doctors collaborate with you to gently address existing cavities and decay while preventing future issues, utilizing the latest in high-tech dentistry.

Dental Hygiene & Routine Care

Regular dental cleanings are vital for maintaining healthy gums and preventing gum disease. At Benicia Family Dentistry, we recommend adults receive routine cleanings twice a year. During these appointments, our skilled hygienists will thoroughly clean your teeth, removing plaque and tartar buildup. They will also carefully inspect your teeth for any signs of cavities or decay.

In addition to the cleaning, our hygienists will take the time to educate you on the best practices for at-home dental care. We believe in empowering our patients to be proactive about their oral health. By following these personalized recommendations, you can enhance the results of your professional cleanings and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Pediatric Dentistry

Early dental care is crucial. Children should start seeing a dental hygienist for cleanings by their first birthday. At Benicia Family Dentistry, we understand the anxiety children may feel about visiting the dentist. Our team works patiently and kindly with your child to ensure they feel comfortable and safe. We also educate them on proper at-home dental care habits for lifelong oral health.

Periodontics

Our periodontists focus on detecting and treating inflammation and periodontitis, a condition affecting the areas around the teeth, including the gums. We often utilize the Lightwalker laser, which offers a more comfortable experience compared to traditional treatments.

Prosthodontics

Prosthodontics involves creating and implementing tooth replacements, such as implants or dentures, to restore functionality and aesthetics.

Sealants

Sealants provide a protective layer on the tooth’s surface, shielding it from decay by blocking out food particles, plaque, and bacteria.

Crowns

Crowns are used to cover broken or decayed teeth, protecting them from further harm. At Benicia Family Dentistry, our crowns are scanned using digital impressions. This is better for a dental crown because it replaces messy, gag-inducing putty molds with a fast, highly accurate 3D scan. This precision leads to a better-fitting crown, fewer uncomfortable seating adjustments, and faster turnaround times from the dental lab.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a brand of clear aligners that are used to straighten teeth. It is a popular alternative to traditional braces, which use metal brackets and wires. Invisalign aligners are made of a clear plastic material that is virtually invisible when worn.
