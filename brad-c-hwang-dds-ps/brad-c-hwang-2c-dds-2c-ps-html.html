Dr. Brad Hwang has been practicing dentistry since 1986. Dr Hwang was raised and received his dental education in Taiwan. He came to the states in 1986. He received his D.D.S. degree from Loma Linda University and his graduate Pediatric Dentistry training from Howard University. Dr. Hwang is a Diplomat of The American Pediatric Dental Board as well as a Certified Member. Dr. Hwang is also a Member of The American Dental Association, The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, The Washington State Dental Association, and The King County Dental Society.

Dr. Hwang was a Clinical Assistant Professor at Loma Linda University. In addition to teaching dentistry, he assisted the student dentists clinically from 1990 to 1994. During this time Dr. Hwang also practiced Pediatric Dentistry in a private office. He relocated to Washington State in 1994. He practiced dentistry part time at Rainier School, the state facility for mentally or physically challenged patients, for over a year. He opened his own pediatric dental office in Kent in 1995.
