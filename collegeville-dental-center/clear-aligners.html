Our Dental Aligner Process

Clear aligners aren’t a one-size-fits-all process: they’re a personalized solution tailored to help you reach your goals. That’s why our dental aligner process goes as follows:

You&#x27;ll visit our office periodically throughout your treatment to receive new sets of aligners and have your progress carefully monitored, keeping your smile on track every step of the way.

  • We begin with a thorough consultation , as well as digital X-rays if necessary, taking the time to understand your unique needs and smile goals.
  • During this appointment, we carefully evaluate your teeth and bite to develop a personalized treatment plan.
  • Next, we take precise impressions of your teeth, which are used to design and create custom aligner trays specifically tailored to your mouth.
  • Once your aligners are ready, you&#x27;ll return to our office for fitting and detailed instructions on how to wear and care for them.
  • Each set of aligner trays is typically worn for about two weeks, for at least 22 hours a day, and should only be removed when eating, drinking anything other than water, or during cleaning.

Will clear aligners affect my speech?

Some people experience a minor lisp or change in speech when they first start aligner treatment. However, most patients adapt quickly within a few days and notice little to no lasting effects.

How do I clean my aligners?

To clean your aligners, gently brush them daily with a soft toothbrush and rinse with lukewarm water to remove any bacteria and residue. You should avoid hot water, as it can cause the aligners to lose their shape and affect how they fit.

What if I forget to wear my aligners for a day?

If you forget to wear your aligners for a day, try to resume wearing them as soon as possible and wear them for the recommended hours going forward. Missing time can slow your progress, so consistency is important.

Can clear aligners be used for adults with previous orthodontic work?

Yes, adults who have had braces or other orthodontic treatments can use clear aligners to correct new or recurring alignment issues. Our dentist will assess your current alignment to tailor a treatment plan that suits your needs.

What should I do if my aligners feel uncomfortable or cause sores?

If your aligners cause discomfort or sores, you can rinse your mouth with warm salt water and use orthodontic wax to protect irritated areas. If the problem persists, contact our dentist to adjust your treatment.
