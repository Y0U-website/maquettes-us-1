In House Dental Plan

  • No waiting periods—coverage begins immediately
  • No deductibles
  • No claim forms or pre-authorizations
  • No insurance cards required

Included Benefits

  • Two routine dental exams per year
  • Two bitewing X-rays for children or four bitewing X-rays for adults
  • Two routine cleanings
  • Two topical fluoride applications for children up to age 16
  • One emergency exam and X-ray
  • 20 % discount on eligible dental treatment when paid in full at the time of service

Enrollment Fees

Coverage begins once enrollment fees are paid in full.

  • Single Member: $367
  • Additional Adult Household Member: $345 per person
  • Dependents (25 and under): $215 per person

How to Enroll

  • Complete the enrollment application
  • Submit payment in full to activate membership

Important Policies & Eligibility

Certain exclusions apply, including reduced discounts on veneers, Invisalign, and orthodontic treatment, as well as services requiring referral to a specialist. Treatment fees are guaranteed for three months from the date quoted.

  • Valid only at Collegeville Dental Center
  • Available to individuals 18 and older; dependents include spouses, domestic partners, and children through age 25
  • Cannot be combined with dental insurance or other offers
  • Discounts apply only when payment is made in full at the time of service
  • Membership term is 12 months from enrollment date
  • All payments are nonrefundable
