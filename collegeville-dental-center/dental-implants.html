What Does Getting Dental Implants Involve?

The process of getting implants involves several visits over several months. These are some common steps:

Because several fittings may be required, this step may take one to two months to complete. After a healing period, the artificial teeth are securely attached to the implant, providing the patient with excellent stability and comfort.

  • First, X-rays and impressions (molds) are taken of the jaw and teeth to determine the bone and gum tissue available for an implant, as well as the spacing available for an implant.
  • On the implant placement day, the area is numbed, and the implant will be surgically placed into the bone. Then, up to 6 months may pass before the titanium post integrates with your jawbone, a process known as osseointegration.
  • Depending on the type of implant, a second surgery may be required to place the “post” that will hold the artificial tooth. With other implants, the post and anchor are already attached and placed together.
  • After several weeks of healing, the artificial teeth are made and fitted to the post portion of the anchor.

How Do I Recover from Dental Implant Surgery?
