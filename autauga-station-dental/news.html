A Message from Dr. Brock Miller

I want to take a moment to express my heartfelt gratitude to each of you for allowing me the privilege of serving you over the years. It has been an incredible journey, and I am truly thankful for the trust and kindness you’ve shown me throughout my time as your dentist.

After much thought and reflection, the time has come for me to retire due to age and health reasons. While this decision was not easy, it is one I make with a sense of peace and optimism for what lies ahead-- not just for me but for this practice that has meant so much to me. As many of you know, I decided early on that I would not sell the practice through online listings or brokers, or to a regional or national corporation. Instead, I chose to rely on faith and trust for the right opportunity to present itself.

It is my sincere hope that you will extend the same warmth and trust you have given me to Dr. Eades. I have no doubt that he will bring new energy and fresh perspectives while continuing the legacy of care that we have built together.

Thank you again for being such a wonderful part of my life and for allowing me to service you. I leave this practice in good hands and with a full heart. May God bless each and every one of you.
