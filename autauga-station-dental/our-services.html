Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and rebuilding your teeth to restore function, health, and appearance. Whether it is fixing a cavity with a filling or replacing a missing tooth, restorative treatments help bring your smile back to its best. It is about more than just aesthetics. Restorative care supports your overall oral health and helps you chew, speak, and smile with confidence. Some of the procedures we offer include:

  • Fillings
  • Crowns and Bridge Work
  • Dentures and Partials
  • Root Canals
  • Extractions
  • Minor Oral Surgery

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on enhancing the appearance of your smile. Whether you are looking to brighten your teeth or have a complete smile makeover- cosmetic treatments are designed to boost your confidence and help you love your smile. Some of the procedures we offer include:

  • Bonding
  • Whitening
  • Veneers
  • Amalgam filling removal
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Dental implant restorations

Children’s Dentistry

We are dedicated to providing comprehensive, compassionate dental care for children of all ages. Our team will ensure that your child receives the highest standard of care in a welcoming and comfortable environment.
