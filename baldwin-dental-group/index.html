The practice

Our mission is to redefine the dental experience through compassionate, patient-centered care, delivered by a trusted team of seasoned professionals. With decades of combined expertise and a commitment to staying at the forefront of dental innovation, we ensure every visit is comfortable, efficient, and tailored to your needs. At the heart of our practice is a dedication to trust, excellence, and…

Ten years ago, Dr. Ben Baldwin introduced something truly meaningful— Dentistry From the Heart —a special day devoted to serving our community with compassion, kindness, and free dental care for those in need.

Since then, this annual event has touched the lives of more than 700 patients , providing cleanings, fillings, extractions, and more. Each treatment is delivered with dignity, care, and a genuine commitment to improving lives.

Dentistry From the Heart is more than just an event—it’s a celebration of neighbors helping neighbors, of service without expectation, and of making a difference one smile at a time.
