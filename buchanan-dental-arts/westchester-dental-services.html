Our Technology in Buchanan Dental Arts

Intraoral Scanner

Our 3Shape TRIOS® intraoral scanner replaces messy, uncomfortable traditional impressions with a fast, comfortable digital scan. A small camera gently captures your teeth in full color, creating a highly accurate 3D model on screen in seconds.

This lets Dr. Carlos — and you — clearly see your teeth, gums, and bite from every angle, improving diagnosis, treatment planning, and communication. The result is more precise crowns, veneers, bridges, and implants that fit perfectly from the start, with fewer adjustments and better outcomes.

Patients love the comfort (no gagging or goo), the speed, and the amazing “wow” moment of seeing their own digital smile instantly.

Dental Cone Beam CT

The advanced Dental Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) technology — a state-of-the-art 3D imaging system that takes your dental care to the next level.

  • Plan treatments with exceptional accuracy (such as dental implants, orthodontics, or complex surgeries)
  • Detect issues that might not show up on standard X-rays
  • Ensure safer, more predictable outcomes for your care
