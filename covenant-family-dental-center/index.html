The practice

Covenant Community Care Dental provides high-quality, affordable dental services in Detroit for individuals and families of all ages. Our dental clinics are open to everyone, regardless of insurance status or ability to pay , because access to oral health care should never depend on income.

Whether you’re searching for a dentist near Detroit, need routine preventive care, or are addressing more complex dental concerns, Covenant Community Care offers comprehensive dental services in a welcoming, community-centered environment.

For a low cost, patients can receive dental exams, digital X-rays, and professional cleanings, helping prevent small issues from becoming bigger problems. Our experienced dental team uses modern technology and evidence-based practices to provide safe, effective care for the whole family.

At Covenant Community Care, we believe everyone deserves access to a trusted Detroit dentist and affordable dental services close to home. Our integrated, community-focused model ensures patients receive respectful, high-quality care without financial barriers standing in the way.
