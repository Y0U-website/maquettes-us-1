Will my medications or costs change?

Your provider will continue prescribing the medications you need.

Your medication cost depends on your insurance coverage and the pharmacy you choose. If you notice higher out-of-pocket costs or need help finding the most affordable option, please call your Covenant clinic. One of our nurses will be happy to help.

Will there be any delays?

While we complete the transition, there may be occasional temporary service changes. Our team is working hard to minimize disruptions and help ensure you receive your medications safely and as quickly as possible.

Why is Covenant opening its own pharmacy?

Having an on-site pharmacy will make your care more convenient by providing:

  • A Covenant pharmacy team that knowsyou and your healthcare needs
  • Improved communication between yourprovider and pharmacist
  • Enhanced coordinated care
  • Extended hours
  • Delivery services

Questions? We’re here to help!

Please speak with any member of your care team or call your Covenant clinic.
