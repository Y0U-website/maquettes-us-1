Dental Offices

Our dental providers focus on both preventive care and restorative treatment, helping patients achieve healthy smiles while addressing pain, infection, or tooth damage early.

  • Fillings and sealants
  • Root canals
  • Tooth extractions
  • Crowns
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Scaling and root planing (deep cleanings)
  • Dentures and partial dentures
  • Nitrous (laughing gas) – offered at select clinics (Joy Rd and Michigan Ave)

Children’s Dental

Gentle dental care for our youngest patients in a comfortable and safe atmosphere. Covenant Community Care’s dental staff is skilled with caring for children of all ages.

We are proud to offer pediatric dental care in Detroit designed to support children’s oral health from an early age. For a low cost, children can receive dental exams, X-rays, and cleanings in a setting that is calm, supportive, and family-friendly.

Our team works with parents and caregivers to develop healthy dental habits that can last a lifetime!

  • Exams, cleanings, and preventive care
  • Fillings and sealants
  • Pulpectomy and pulpotomy procedures
  • Extractions
  • Crowns
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Nitrous (laughing gas) – offered at select clinics (Joy Rd and Michigan Ave)

Oral Surgery

Covenant Community Care offers oral surgery services in partnership with our Oral Surgery Residency Program.

Covenant Community Care routinely hosts Oral Surgery Residents, allowing us to offer select oral surgery services on a limited basis. This program expands access to specialized dental care while supporting the training of future oral health professionals.
