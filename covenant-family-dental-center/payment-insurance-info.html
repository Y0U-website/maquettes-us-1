Payment & Insurance Info

Removing the barriers to getting you the care you need.

Our promise is to provide quality health care to all, regardless of a patient’s insurance coverage or ability to pay. Our goal is to make it easier for you to make appointments, get answers to your health care questions, and take charge of your personal health.

Discount Policy

We offer sliding fee discounts to low-income patients with out insurance or inadequate insurance. Please ask to see our Benefits Coordinator for determining your sliding fee discount. Sliding fee discounts are determined based on your family size and income.

Patients will not be denied services based on their inability to pay.

Sliding Fee Tables

Our sliding fee discounts are based on the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) guidelines as updated annually by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. To determine your sliding discount eligibility, please review the guidelines by clicking on the link below.
