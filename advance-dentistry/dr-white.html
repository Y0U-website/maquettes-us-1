Dr. Sara White, DDS

Cincinnati

“I’ve always enjoyed getting to know people and working with my hands. I knew at an early age that I wanted to be a dentist.

During college at DePauw University, I ran cross country, cycled, and majored in biochemistry. I also became an EMT and worked part-time for an orthodontist, both of which reinforced my passion to help others by becoming a dentist .

After DePauw, I spent four years at Indiana University School of Dentistry.

Upon graduating from dental school, I joined a practice in western Indiana that specialized in treating children with severe dental decay. Many of the young children came to our practice feeling very anxious, and I loved that I could alleviate the children’s fears by getting to know them and helping them feel comfortable with me and our office.

We moved to Cincinnati in 2010 and I practiced at an office in Milford for several years, providing general dentistry to patients of all ages.

I joined Advance Dentistry because the entire team goes above and beyond, striving to provide their patients with positive, no-fear dental care . I look forward to meeting you and providing you with a comfortable dental experience!”

DDS: Indiana University School of Dentistry Indianapolis, IN

Member: American Association of Women Dentists, New Dentist Study Club of Cincinnati

Misc: Dr. White enjoys gardening, reading, and traveling to new places.
