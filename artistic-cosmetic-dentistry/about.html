About Artistic Digital Dentistry

Blending Artistry, Innovation & Compassion in Every Smile

We specialize in crafting beautiful, healthy smiles by combining the latest advancements in dental technology with an artistic eye and a compassionate approach. Whether you need routine cleanings, a full smile makeover, or advanced restorative treatments, we’re here to support your oral health every step of the way.

Our team is made up of skilled, dedicated professionals who truly care about your experience. From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll feel the difference—warm smiles, clear communication, and a gentle, patient-first philosophy.

We proudly serve patients of all ages, from children to seniors, and are passionate about making every visit a positive one. With a culturally diverse team fluent in Bulgarian, Polish, Russian, and English, we ensure that every patient feels understood, respected, and empowered in their care decisions.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Angelova has completed numerous advanced training programs in both general and cosmetic dentistry. Her continuing education includes specialties such as all-ceramic crowns and veneers, implant restorations, Botox cosmetic applications, and the latest in preventive techniques, including SDF and ICON Resin therapy.

Known for her gentle approach and attention to detail, Dr. Angelova is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable and confident in the dental chair. She places a strong focus on prevention and education, helping individuals overcome dental anxiety and maintain long-term oral health.

A proud resident of Glenview, Dr. Angelova lives with her husband and their twin boys. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, dancing, and biking—always with a smile.
