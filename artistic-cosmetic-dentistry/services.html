Our Services

Oral Health Consultation

Your initial visit is set to establish your path to success in achieving your dental goals.

Your journey to a healthier smile starts here. Our Oral Health Consultation is a thorough and personalized evaluation of your current dental condition. Dr. Angelova and her team take the time to understand your goals, assess your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw, and identify early signs of potential issues. Using digital X-rays and advanced diagnostics, we provide clarity and direction, helping you make confident decisions about your dental care.

Questions? You’re covered.

How long does a consultation take?

Most consultations take between 30 to 45 minutes, depending on your needs.

Will I get a treatment plan during the consultation?

Yes, we’ll discuss our findings and outline a personalized treatment plan based on your goals.

Do I need X-rays at my first visit?

In most cases, yes—X-rays help us get a full picture of your oral health and detect issues not visible to the eye.

Smile Makeover

Our main interest over the years has been focused on cosmetic dental procedures.

A Smile Makeover is more than just a cosmetic upgrade—it’s a transformation that brings confidence, harmony, and personality to your appearance. Whether it’s whitening, veneers, reshaping, or alignment, we create a customized plan that enhances your smile while preserving what makes it uniquely yours. Dr. Angelova combines an artistic eye with technical precision to deliver stunning results that feel natural and authentic.

Questions? You’re covered.

How do I know which treatments I need for a Smile Makeover?

We’ll guide you through a detailed consultation and create a custom plan tailored to your goals.

Are Smile Makeovers permanent?

Some elements, like veneers, are long-lasting, while others (like whitening) may require maintenance.

Is a Smile Makeover painful?

Most procedures are minimally invasive and performed with comfort in mind. We offer numbing and sedation options if needed.

Comprehensive Dental Care

Though we focus on cosmetic dentistry, we are here to provide comprehensive dental treatment.

Our comprehensive dental care covers everything from preventive check-ups and cleanings to restorative treatments like fillings, crowns, bridges, and root canals. We take a proactive approach to dental wellness, focusing on early detection, long-term solutions, and gentle care. Whether you visit us for regular maintenance or need to restore function and comfort, you’ll receive the same high-quality, compassionate care.

Questions? You’re covered.

What’s included in comprehensive care?

It includes exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, gum care, and more—basically, everything needed to maintain or restore oral health.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a visit every six months, or more often if you have specific dental concerns.

Are all treatments done in-house?

Yes, we provide nearly all services under one roof for your convenience and continuity of care.

Hygiene & Periodontoloy Care

The major goal of our hygiene department is to help you prevent and fight gum disease.

Healthy gums are key to a healthy smile—and a healthy body. Our hygiene services include regular cleanings, deep cleanings (scaling and root planing), and advanced periodontal therapies to treat and prevent gum disease. We educate patients on effective at-home care, offer gentle techniques, and use modern tools to ensure your gums are strong, clean, and free from inflammation.

Questions? You’re covered.

What are the signs of gum disease?

Common signs include bleeding gums, bad breath, gum recession, and loose teeth.

What is scaling and root planing?

It’s a deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque and tartar from below the gumline to treat gum disease.

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most people benefit from a cleaning every 6 months, but patients with gum disease may need them more often.

Our Services

Teeth Whitening

Get a brighter, whiter smile with professional teeth whitening.

Brighten your smile with safe, effective professional whitening. Our in-office whitening provides dramatic results in just one visit, while our custom take-home kits let you whiten at your own pace. We use clinically proven methods to lift stains caused by coffee, wine, aging, and smoking—without harming your enamel or causing sensitivity.

Questions? You’re covered.

How long do the results last?

Results can last from several months to over a year, depending on your habits and touch-up maintenance.

Is it safe for sensitive teeth?

Yes, we offer gentle options for patients with sensitivity and customize the treatment to your comfort.

What’s the difference between in-office and take-home whitening?

In-office offers faster, stronger results; take-home kits allow more flexibility and gradual whitening.

Invisible Braces

A modern solution to strighten the irregular position of your teeth utilizing clear aligners.

Say goodbye to metal brackets and hello to discreet alignment. Our invisible braces (such as clear aligner systems) gently shift your teeth into the desired position with minimal impact on your daily life. Custom-fit and removable, they’re ideal for adults and teens who want to straighten their teeth comfortably and confidently.

Questions? You’re covered.

How long does treatment with invisible braces take?

Treatment typically lasts 6 to 18 months, depending on the complexity of the case.

Can I eat and drink normally with them?

Yes! Just remove the aligners while eating or drinking anything other than water.

How often do I need to wear them?

For best results, wear your aligners 20–22 hours a day, only removing them to eat, drink, brush, or floss.
