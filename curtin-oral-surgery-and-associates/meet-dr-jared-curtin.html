Education & Training

Dr. Curtin earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the New York University College of Dentistry, where he served on the Peer Review Board and conducted research on oral cancer and pain management. He completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, receiving advanced training in dentoalveolar surgery, dental implants and bone grafting, maxillofacial trauma, pathology, reconstruction, and anesthesia.

He also holds undergraduate degrees in Biochemistry and Chemistry from Worcester Polytechnic Institute.
