Explore Our Dental Implant Services

We offer several implant solutions depending on your needs and goals:

Mini Implants

A smaller, less invasive alternative to traditional implants—often used to stabilize dentures or replace smaller teeth without bone grafting.

Single Tooth Implant

Ideal for patients missing just one tooth, this option replaces the tooth root with a titanium implant and finishes with a lifelike crown.

Implant Supported Dentures

A secure, removable (or fixed) alternative to traditional dentures, offering improved stability, comfort, and chewing power.

All-on-4® Implants

A revolutionary solution to replace a full arch of teeth using just four implants per jaw. Ideal for patients seeking a fixed, non-removable option.

Not sure which option is right for you? Dr. Curtin will walk you through a personalized treatment plan after your comprehensive consultation.
