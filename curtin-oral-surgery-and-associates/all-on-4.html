The All-on-4® technique replaces a full set of missing teeth using just four dental implants per arch. These implants are strategically angled and positioned to support a custom, fixed full-arch prosthesis. Key Benefits of All-on-4® Include: Fewer implants needed, even in areas of low bone density
  • Often avoids the need for bone grafting
  • Immediate load options may allow for a temporary prosthesis on the day of surgery
  • Permanent, stable full-arch restoration
  • Improved speech, function, and quality of life
