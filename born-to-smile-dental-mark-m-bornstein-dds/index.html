The practice

Dental implants are a revolutionary way to replace missing teeth or secure slipping dentures. At Born to Smile Dental , we restore dental implants to provide you with comprehensive care.

We offer a range of services specifically formulated for your child including digital xrays, prophlaxis (cleaning), flouride treatments, sealants and BPA-free white fillings.

The trios intra-oral scanner replaces traditional gooey impression material for making crowns, bridges, and invisible braces.
