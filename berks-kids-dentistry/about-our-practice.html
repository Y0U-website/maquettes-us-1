Berks Kids Dentistry

Compassionate, Caring Dentistry for Children, Adolescents, and Special Needs Patients

Berks Kids Dentistry offers dental services to the children of Berks, Lancaster, Lebanon, and Schuylkill Counties of PA, including the metropolitan Reading area. With 25 years of combined experience, Dr. Kristin Gordos and Dr. Laura Snyder are fortunate to participate in the lives of children and their families.

Friendly Services

We look forward to creating a long-term, satisfying, professional, and friendly relationship with you and your child. Our practice is one of the only offices in the area that is limited to children, adolescents, and special needs. We are honored to give children a road to a lifetime of smiles.
