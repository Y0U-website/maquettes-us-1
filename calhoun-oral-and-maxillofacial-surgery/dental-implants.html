Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that provide a permanent foundation for replacement teeth, offering a natural look and function.

Most people with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density are good candidates. A consultation with Dr. Chung can determine if implants are right for you.

With proper care and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement after 10-15 years.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure comfort. Some mild soreness and swelling are expected afterward, but they can be managed with medication.

The entire process can take several months, as it involves healing time for the implant to integrate with the jawbone before placing the final restoration.

Care for your implants just like natural teeth—regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings will help ensure their longevity.

Coverage varies by provider. Some dental insurance plans may cover part of the cost, while others may not. Our office can assist with verifying your benefits.

Dental implants have a high success rate, typically over 95 %. Factors such as good oral hygiene and overall health contribute to long-term success.
