What is Airway Orthodontics?

Airway orthodontics is a specialized form of orthodontics that focuses on improving a patient&#x27;s airway function by optimizing the alignment of the teeth and jaw. This approach not only promotes better breathing but also improves overall sleep quality and can help reduce the risk of developing certain health conditions, such as sleep apnea and snoring.

How Does Airway Orthodontics Work?

At Bloom Holistic Dentistry, we take a personalized approach to airway orthodontics. First, we conduct a thorough evaluation of the patient&#x27;s airway, which may include a sleep study or other diagnostic tests. Based on the results of these tests, we develop a customized treatment plan that may involve orthodontic appliances, such as braces or clear aligners, to gently shift the teeth and jaw into proper alignment.

Benefits of Airway Orthodontics

The benefits of airway orthodontics are numerous. By improving the alignment of the teeth and jaw, patients can experience better breathing, reduced snoring, and improved sleep quality. In addition, airway orthodontics can help reduce the risk of developing sleep apnea and other health conditions associated with poor airway function. Furthermore, properly aligned teeth and jaw can lead to a more aesthetically pleasing smile, which can boost self-confidence and improve overall quality of life.
