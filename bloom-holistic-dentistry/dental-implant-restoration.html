Holistic Approach to Implant Restorations

Not all dental restorations are created equal. Traditional practices may use materials that trigger sensitivities or inflammation. At Bloom Holistic Dentistry, we take a more thoughtful, patient-centered approach:

Implant restorations are often completed using CEREC same-day crowns , allowing us to design and place your custom crown in one visit.

  • Biocompatible materials – We choose safe, non-metal, BPA-free ceramic crowns that blend beautifully with your natural teeth.
  • Custom-crafted restorations – Your crown or bridge is designed digitally to match your bite, shape, and facial symmetry.
  • Whole-body wellness – We use advanced diagnostics like saliva testing and biocompatibility testing to ensure the materials won’t negatively impact your immune system.
  • Minimally invasive dentistry – When preparing surrounding teeth or replacing old restorations, we preserve as much natural structure as possible and use ozone treatment for sterilization and healing support.

FAQs About Implant Restorations

  • What materials are used for implant crowns?: We use ceramic or porcelain materials that are biocompatible and metal-free, offering both durability and a natural appearance.
  • Can I get a crown on an implant placed by another dentist?: Yes. We frequently restore implants placed by oral surgeons or other providers and ensure a seamless aesthetic and functional outcome.
  • Is the restoration done the same day as the implant placement?: No. Healing from implant placement typically takes 3–6 months. Once integrated, the final restoration is attached — sometimes in a single visit using our CEREC system.
  • Does insurance cover implant restorations?: We are out-of-network but submit all claims on your behalf. Many patients receive partial reimbursement for implant crowns and related procedures.
  • Do implant restorations require special care?: They require the same hygiene as natural teeth: brushing, flossing, and regular professional cleanings. We also monitor them at your routine dental cleaning visits.
