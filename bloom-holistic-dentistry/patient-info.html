Please allow 1.5-2 hours for your first comprehensive visit. During this visit, we will use a variety of diagnostic tools including radiographs, CBCT, 3D scanning techniques, direct visualization with intraoral cameras, and even take a few clicks of your bite and smile. We use low radiation digital x-rays and CT scans when absolutely necessary in order to limit any exposures. Our main goal is to reach beyond the superficial layer of the teeth and gums in order to obtain a comprehensive look at your oral health and establish a roadmap for your dental care.

We take a lot of time to get to know you in this patient experience. If for any reason you are running late, please call our office as soon as possible so we can make arrangements to accommodate you the same day or reschedule for another day.
