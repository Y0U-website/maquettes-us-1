Meet the Doctors

Dr. Zahra Punjani, DMD IAOMT

After the birth of her first daughter, she began to pursue a higher understanding of airway dentistry in order to understand her daughter&#x27;s own challenges with tongue tie and jaw development. In her training, she quickly realized the importance of practicing airway dentistry as a holistic dentist as it affects so many aspects of children&#x27;s growth and development, learning and behavior, and in adults plays a very important role in sleep apnea, energy and mood.

Patient care comes first. What she&#x27;s learned in her years of practice is that by creating a comfortable and safe space for her patients to discuss their dental and overall health concerns, and together create treatment plans that are individually tailored.

To achieve a holistic lifestyle, Dr. Punjani learns and attempts a new way to improve overall health, from cooking a new nutritious recipe with produce that’s currently in season, to incorporating traditional medicine techniques such as Ayurvedic medicine into her lifestyle. In her free time, she enjoys yoga, outdoor sports, travel and dance with her husband and wholesome daughter.

With her passion of practicing whole body dentistry and her purpose of serving her patients, rest assured that you have a partner on your journey to better health.

Dr. Mijung Myung

Dr. Mijung Myung is familiar with Holistic medical practice as part of everyday life. Growing up in the Republic of Korea, Dr. Myung was able to witness and experience the pairing and mutual respect between Western Medicine and Eastern Medicine. In Korea, the referral of patients between hospitals and scientific medical practices and Hanbang, the Traditional Korean Medicine, are common. Hence, the idea that Western medicine and Hanbang which looks at the human body from a holistic perspective are not exclusive to the other formed at an early age for Dr. Myung.

Dr. Myung is committed to providing the utmost treatment for each patient, building trust through her care and creating a comfortable environment for patients to feel at ease. When Dr. Myung is not in the office, she enjoys spending quality time with her husband, S.E., and son, Jacob. Her favorite pastime is to read and journal while tucked away in a cafe with an extra-hot chai tea latte.

Dr. Punjani Shruti Raju Dandu DDS Dr.Dandu is a detail-oriented holistic dentist dedicated to providing biologically compatible, patient-centered care. Her passion for dentistry lies in its precision, where every detail is measured down to the millimeter. As a member of IAOMT, she stays forefront of safe dental practices, including SMART protocol, minimally invasive treatment, and whole-body wellness integration.

“After my own personal journey and learning about heavy metal toxicity, I transitioned from conventional general dentistry to a more holistic and biologically informed approach. I believe patients deserve to understand how oral health influences energy, inflammation, immune function, and overall vitality”- Dr.Dandu.
