Most patients should visit the dentist every six months for a checkup and professional cleaning . Regular visits help catch cavities, gum disease, and other concerns early, often before they become more serious or expensive to treat. Some patients may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health needs.

Your first visit usually includes a review of your dental and medical history, a comprehensive exam, any needed X-rays, and a conversation about your goals or concerns. In many cases, a cleaning may also be completed during the same appointment. It’s a chance for your dentist to get to know you and create a care plan tailored to your needs.
