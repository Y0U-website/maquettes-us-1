Our Team

Paul V. Affrunti, DDS, MAGD

Dr. Paul V. Affrunti and his wife, Dr. Elizabeth Affrunti, have practiced as the third owners & operators of Seaford Dental, P.C. for almost 30 years. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at New York University College of Dentistry, but he strongly believes in continuing his education so that he can provide the best possible care to his patients. Since then, he has received his FAGD MAGD, one of the academy’s highest awards given. He is a proud father to 3 young men and enjoys spending his free time doing activities with his wife and them.

Anthony D. Affrunti, DDS, MPH

Dr. Anthony D. Affrunti is the son to Dr. Paul and Dr. Elizabeth. He grew up, literally and figuratively, in their office - some might say dentistry is in his blood! He received his dental degree from New York University College of Dentistry while in the highly competitive DDS, MPH dual degree program. He recently achieved his Master of Public Health Degree (MPH)at Adelphi University, and is working towards his Fellowship degree (FAGD) through the Academy of General Dentistry. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys going boating and fishing with family and friends, playing golf, and being a foodie
