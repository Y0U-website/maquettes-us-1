Our Services

Preventative Care

Emergency Care & Oral Surgery

All teeth are different and accidents happen. Whether your teeth are crooked, chipped, damaged or if you’ve had mouth trauma, our team of endodontists, periodontists, orthodontists and oral surgeons can treat you quickly using the latest techniques and the finest materials.

Pediatric Dentistry

We know the dentist can be a stressful situation for little ones. Our board certified pediatric dentists provide a safe, comfortable environment for kids of all ages. They take their time with each patient, explaining all of our tools and processes to alleviate any fear!

Cosmetic Dentistry

We’ll make sure your smile is healthy and beautiful, whether that’s with veneers, bleaching, or our other state-of-the-art offerings!

Orthodontic Treatment

We offer consultations and treatment for Invisalign therapy and clear aligners and work with our local specialists depending on your needs. We also offer initial and replacement retainers.

Endodontic Treatment

Through the latest technology and techniques, we can provide a safe and effective treatment for teeth that require root canal therapy, sometimes in a single visit!

Crowns, Veneers, Implant Reconstruction + Removable Dentures

Can all be fabricated without direct impressions in your mouth. No more slimy mess! Through our affiliation with top-of-the-line laboratories most of our restorations can be fabricated using CAD-CAM technology. Our doctors scan most of your dental needs and image them utilizing one of the most powerful intraoral scanners on the market, namely the Trios 3Shape Scanner. This virtually eliminates gagging and choking on impression materials. Spectacular results can be achieved leveraging our partnerships with top notch dental laboratories.

Oral Medicine

Although this branch of dentistry is often overlooked, it is not forgotten in our office. All our doctors perform routine oral cancer screenings and screenings for autoimmune disease as well as diabetic oral complications. Over the years, we have detected and managed a multitude of healthcare related oral complications.

TMJ

With a combined knowledge of over 80 years of dental practice, our doctors can diagnose and offer initial treatment for most temporomandibular joint disorders, parafunctional habits, as well as clenching and grinding patients. Through the use of habit breaking intraoral digitally designed appliances, our doctors have seen significant relief in pain and improvement in function.
