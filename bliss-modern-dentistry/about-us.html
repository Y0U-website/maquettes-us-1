Meet the Doctors

Dr. Morningstar

Dr. Griffin Park is a dedicated dentist with over 9 of experience in providing high-quality dental care. A graduate of the University of Nevada, Las Vegas, Dr. Park is passionate about helping patients achieve optimal oral health through personalized treatment plans and a gentle approach.

Specializing in implant dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, comprehensive complex care and family dentistry, Dr. Park stays current with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques to ensure the best outcomes for patients. Known for caring demeanor and commitment to patient education, Dr. Park believes that informed patients are empowered patients.

When not in the office, Dr. Park enjoys cooking and exploring international cuisines as well as playing hockey, boxing and spending quality time with friends and family. Dr. Park also enjoys taking time to travel and explore the world.

Dr. Park looks forward to welcoming new patients and providing them with exceptional dental care in a warm and friendly environment.
