What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, these posts support custom crowns, bridges, or dentures. Implants are uniquely designed to fuse with the bone, offering unmatched stability and longevity.

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last several decades—often a lifetime. Regular cleanings and checkups help maintain the health of the implant and surrounding gum tissue.

Does getting a dental implant hurt? The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is often less uncomfortable than patients expect. Mild soreness after surgery is normal and manageable with over-the-counter medication.

What if I’m missing multiple teeth? Implants can replace single teeth or support full arch prosthetics. Implant-supported bridges and dentures are excellent solutions for multiple missing teeth.

Are dental implants covered by insurance? Coverage varies depending on the provider and plan. We work closely with patients to maximize their insurance benefits and offer financing through third-party providers when needed.

What’s the difference between implants and dentures? Dentures rest on the gums and can shift over time. Implants, on the other hand, are anchored directly into the bone, providing superior stability and function.
