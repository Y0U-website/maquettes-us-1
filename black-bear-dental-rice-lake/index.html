The practice

Originally from Bloomington, Minnesota, Dr. Jason Isaacson comes from a family of dentists. Dr. Isaacson’s father, Dana, practiced dentistry for over forty years, and Jason was able to see his father build relationships with patients in his early childhood, which helped forge his desire to become a dentist.

Dr. Isaacson looks forward to being able to serve Rice Lake and the surrounding communities. He prides himself on attention to detail and dedication to continuing education, going above and beyond the minimum requirements of a licensed dentist.
