Meet the Team at Complete Dental Care

Welcome to our Sioux Falls dental practice! Dr. Vitaly Perets and our knowledgable dental staff are here to provide a warm and inviting dental experience. We want to help your family maintain healthy and beautiful smiles for a lifetime.

From the first minute you walk into our dental practice, you can be sure to expect genuine, compassionate, and respectful treatment. Even if it s been a while since your last dental cleaning, we are a no-judgment zone. We build our patient relationships on integrity and kindness.

We take pride in the dental staff that we have built, and we want you to get to know Dr. Perets and everyone on our team. It s easier to come in for dental emergencies or when something seems abnormal when a patient relationship exists. We want to empower our patients through oral health education, so please ask us questions. Finally, we invite you to read our bios and get to know your Sioux Falls dental family.
