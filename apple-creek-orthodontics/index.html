Dr. Eichholz attended Marquette University in Milwaukee, WI for both his undergraduate and dental education. He completed a General Practice Residency in Dentistry at the Hennepin County Medical Center in Minneapolis, MN, and then took a second-year position in the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at the Zablocki VA Medical Center in Milwaukee, WI.

After practicing for several years as a general dentist, Dr. Eichholz developed an interest in orthodontics and helping people achieve their dream smiles. He was accepted into the dual-degree program at the University of Missouri-Kansas City where he achieved his certificate in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics as well as a Master of Science in Oral and Craniofacial Sciences.

Dr. Eichholz is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Wisconsin Society of Orthodontists, the Midwestern Society of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Wisconsin Dental Association.
