How often should you visit the dentist? It’s recommended that most patients visit the dentist every six months for routine dental cleanings and examinations. Though bi-annual appointments are usually enough for healthy patients, it’s essential to visit your dentist more often if you’re prone to plaque buildup and periodontitis.

Bleeding gums can indicate a sign of infection or early-stage periodontitis. At this stage, it’s easily treatable, but make sure your dentist treats it right away to prevent a larger infection.

What can you do about bad breath? Brush and floss your teeth more often － at least twice a day. Brush softly and be sure to scrape your tongue. You can also freshen your breath and better protect your teeth by rinsing with mouthwash. You should also avoid foods that can cause bad breath, like onions, garlic, dairy, and coffee.
