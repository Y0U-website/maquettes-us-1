Transform Your Smile with Bourne Orthodontics

Braces

Our braces are designed to straighten your teeth and improve your bite. We offer traditional metal braces, clear braces, and Invisalign.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a clear alternative to braces that uses a series of customized, removable aligners to straighten teeth. Our team is certified to provide Invisalign treatment.

Orthodontic Appliances

We offer a range of orthodontic appliances, including retainers, expanders, and space maintainers. Our team will work with you to determine which appliance is right for your needs.

Orthodontic Surgery

In some cases, orthodontic surgery may be necessary to achieve optimal results. Our team works closely with oral surgeons to provide comprehensive care.

Pediatric Orthodontics

We specialize in treating children and adolescents, and offer a range of orthodontic treatments that are designed specifically for younger, growing patients.

Adult Orthodontics

It’s never too late to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. We offer a range of orthodontic treatments that are designed specifically for adults.
