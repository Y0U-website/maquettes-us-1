Meet the Team at Campus Family Dental

Getting to know you and your family is our favorite part of what we do! If you are a student in the area, we love hearing about your accomplishments as you earn your degree. We also enjoy learning more about your family and what s important to you.

Developing meaningful long-term relationships is a great way to give you the dental care you want and need. By getting to know your goals for your health and smile, we can give you highly personalized dentistry!

If you are curious about us, take a moment to read through our biographies below.

Dr. Eric Hein

Dr. Hein is a Bloomington native and a graduate of Bloomington South High School and the Indiana University School of Dentistry. For the last 20 years, he has lived in Valparaiso where he was a partner in a large practice. As Dr. Hein and his wife prepare for their empty nest with kids going to college, they have made Indianapolis their home for the next chapter of their lives. Dr. Hein is excited to be the new owner of Campus Family Dental. He is looking forward to meeting and treating both our existing and new patients.
