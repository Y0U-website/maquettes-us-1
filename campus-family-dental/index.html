Welcome to Our Practice

Welcome to Campus Family Dental, we look forward to welcoming you to our dental office and providing you and your family with all of your dentistry needs.

The practice

We welcome patients of all ages and welcome your children, as well! We know that families are busy and driving across town to get the care you need for your family is impossible. Patients from the age of one all the way up to senior citizens are invited to our practice, and we will customize the care you need at every life stage.

Your health will likely change over the course of your lifetime, and we will adjust your treatment based on your current health care needs . Our goal is to keep you healthy and looking and feeling your best.

We use the latest technology to give you advanced dental care in the most accurate and efficient way possible. We use digital x-rays to give you better dental care and great results.

We never let technology get in the way of providing you with highly personalized dental care. Our team is friendly and loves getting to know our patients. It’s one of our favorite parts of what we do! We enjoy hearing about your family’s celebrations and accomplishments.

We strive to give you exceptional care and hope you will enjoy your time with us, even if dentistry is not your favorite!

We offer most dental services here in our Bloomington, IN dental office without the need for a referral. Root canal therapy , extractions , orthodontics , and dental implants are all common procedures in our practice. We make it easy to get your necessary care right here in your home dental office where you feel comfortable.

We work with most major insurance companies to maximize your benefits and offer payment options so that fitting your care into your budget is convenient and easy.

Our office is located on Dunn Street between E. Smith Street and 2nd Avenue. We offer convenient appointment times before work and after school to make scheduling easier.
