About

At Aurora Dentistry, we believe that dentistry is more than cleaning and filling teeth. Our role in general health and appearance is an important one, and our goal is to enrich lives through a sense of wellbeing. Our team provides personalized and high-quality care for all ages. We hope to be your home for all your oral healthcare needs.

If you are looking to brighten and enhance your smile, please don’t hesitate to contact us today at (651) 739-0640 ! Our Team

Dr. Erik

Dentist/Owner

Dr. Erik Davis graduated from St. Olaf College with degrees in economics and chemistry. He graduated from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2013 and completed a General Practice Residency at the V.A. Medical Center in Minneapolis the following year. During his time there he trained in implants, dental sleep medicine, and minimally invasive dentistry. Dr. Erik is also Invisalign certified.

Dr. Erik is a member of ASAP Pathways (A course for advanced trained in Airway, Sleep and Pediatrics), American Dental Association, and the Minnesota Dental Association where he currently serves as Immediate past-President for the St. Paul District. A lifelong learner, Dr. Erik nerds out on new dental literature and frequently attends lectures at the top dental academies in the country.

When Dr. Erik is not at the office, you will find him with his wife Amy and their two daughters, cooking a great meal, or enjoying a Minnesota United game. The Davis Family lives in Woodbury where Erik and Amy love being an active part of the community!
