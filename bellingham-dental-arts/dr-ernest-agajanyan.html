Dr. Ernest Agajanyan

Having early exposure to medicine due to presence of physicians (trauma surgeon, gynecologist) and dentists in the family, as well as having a grandmother who was medical nurse, lead Dr. Ernest Agajanyan to care for people, specifically for people in need of medical attention. Born in former USSR, Dr. Agajanyan attended Glendale High School in Glendale, CA , where he was able to graduate early only to continue his goal in higher education.

Dr. Ernest Agajanyan obtained his undergraduate degree in biochemistry from the University of California Los Angeles (UCLA) , then continued his studies and later achieved his Doctorate degree in dental medicine from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2008. Since completion of his studies Dr. Agajanyan has devoted himself to restoring beauty in patients’ smile as well as serving patients in community.

Currently, Dr. Agajanyan resides in Belmont, MA and while away from work, he enjoys spending time with family and friends.
