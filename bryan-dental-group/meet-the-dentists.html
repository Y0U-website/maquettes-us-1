Meet the Dentists

Kelly J. Bratsberg, D.D.S.

Kelly J. (Krill) Bratsberg grew up in Edgerton, Ohio. She attended the Ohio State University for her undergraduate degree (Human Nutrition) and doctorate degree in Dentistry. Following dental school, she completed a general practice residency at the University of Toledo Medical Center. She then worked in Cleveland while her husband was completing his master’s degree at Baldwin Wallace University. Dr. Bratsberg and her husband, Geoff, have 2 daughters, Lily, and Emma. They enjoy cooking, traveling, and spending time with family and friends.

Hannah E. Keller, D.D.S.

Dr. Hannah E. Keller brings a compassionate and comprehensive approach to dentistry. She is grounded in her early training as a dietician and her deep commitment to overall health and wellness. Dr. Keller is from Massillon, Ohio. She earned both her undergraduate and doctoral degrees from The Ohio State University.

Dr. Keller and her husband, Kevin, have three sons and spend most of their free time with their large extended families. The Keller’s enjoy long nature walks with their dogs, time by the water, and growing with their curious and energetic young boys.

Keri L. Slane, D.M.D.

Dr. Keri Slane is a West Unity native who is excited to return home and serve the community that helped shape her. Before becoming a dentist, Dr. Slane spent nearly a decade as a dental hygienist, an experience that deepened her commitment to patient education, preventive care, and a comprehensive approach to oral health. She earned her bachelor’s degree from Albion College before receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine–Arizona.

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys spending time with her fiancé, Matt, their two dachshunds, and traveling. Dr. Slane is honored to return to Northwest Ohio and looks forward to caring for patients and families throughout the community at Bryan Dental Group.
