The practice

We are specialists in caring for you and helping you to save your teeth. We spend the extra time diagnosing each patient and explaining their treatment recommendations and options. We want our patients to understand the “why” and “how” of their procedure, which helps to alleviate any anxiety they may have about the process.

Dr. Porras is passionate about creating a highly unique and tailored endodontic experience for his patients.

Big Waves Endodontics is focused on providing patient-centered care.

Dr. Benjamin Eric Porras is a second-generation board-certified Endodontist with over 9 years of specialty experience working in private practice. He received his undergraduate degree from the University of Florida (Go Gators!). He then received his dental degree from Temple University where he graduated Cum Laude. He was selected to complete a General Practice Residency at Virtua/West Jersey…

Dr. Porras completed Nova Southeastern University’s Endodontic Program where he served as Chief Endodontic Resident. Dr. Porras is a member of the American Association of Endodontists, the American Dental Association, and is board certified and a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics.

Dr. Porras enjoys spending time with his wife, son, daughter, and mini Aussie. In his spare time, he surfs and plays tennis. Dr. Porras is passionate about creating a highly unique and tailored endodontic experience for his patients.
