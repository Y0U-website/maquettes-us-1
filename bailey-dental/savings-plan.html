Program Exclusions and Limitations:

This program is a savings plan. This program is only honored at Bailey Dental in Palos Park, Illinois, and cannot be used at any other dental office. It is not a dental insurance plan, and cannot be used:

  • In conjunction with a separate dental plan
  • For services for injuries covered under workman‘s compensation
  • For treatment, which in the sole opinion of the treating dentist, lies outside the realm of their capability
  • For referrals to specialists
  • For hospitalization charges of any kind
  • For cost of dental care which is covered under automobile medical

Program Guidelines:
