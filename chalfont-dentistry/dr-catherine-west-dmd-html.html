Meet the Doctor

Catherine West, DMD

Dr. West received a Bachelor of Science degree in molecular biology from Auburn University and her DMD degree from Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry at Temple University. She inherited the “dental gene” from a long family line of dentists. Growing up, Dr. West spent a great deal of time in her father’s orthodontic practice in Monroe, Georgia (just outside Atlanta). After continuing the family dental tradition and graduating from dental school in 2008, she practiced throughout Philadelphia and surrounding communities.

As she is committed to providing the utmost care, Dr. West has tremendous dental instinct and a natural and easygoing way with patients. She employs technologically advanced diagnostic procedures and works closely with all patients to find their level of comfort while achieving outstanding aesthetic and healthy results.

In addition to the practice of dentistry, Dr. West has traveled to Haiti for a humanitarian effort providing dental care and outreach to Haitians in need. She is a member of the American Dental Association®, the Pennsylvania Dental Association and the American Association of Women Dentists.

Dr. West and her husband, Andrew (also a dentist), have two young daughters. Together, they enjoy listening to live music, weekends at the shore, big family meals and travelling. Dr. West feels very fortunate to be doing something she loves so much and looks forward to sharing her enthusiasm with you.
