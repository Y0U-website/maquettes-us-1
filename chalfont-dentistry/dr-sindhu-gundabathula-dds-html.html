Meet the Doctor

Dr. Sindhu Gundabathula

Dr. Sindhu graduated from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry in 2008. After graduating from college, Dr. Sindhu worked as an associate dentist until 2015, after which she started her own practice in Chalfont, PA. She is a member of the American Dental Association , Pennsylvania Dental Association , and Montgomery Bucks Dental Society .

Dr. Sindhu keeps up with the most recent advances in dentistry for her patients and actively takes part in continuing education from the very best instructors in the dental field. She focuses on the latest and most conservative techniques to provide quality dental care at affordable prices for her patients, and she appreciates the ability to build close relationships with them. Dr. Sindhu will take the time to discuss and design a customized treatment plan for each patient.

Though Dr. Sindhu enjoys all aspects of dentistry, she is really concerned with making patients feel comfortable and helping them get the treatment they need in order to maintain good oral health. Alleviating patient fears and providing them with quality treatment is very important to her, along with creating an atmosphere of trust and care that makes the patient want to continue coming to her office for many years. Dr. Sindhu has a special interest in family and cosmetic dentistry, and she enjoys transforming “average smiles” into beautiful ones.
