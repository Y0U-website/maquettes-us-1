What You Can Expect

Your First Visit

During your new patient exam at our practice, you can expect a thorough, gentle approach to dental care. Our team will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, including detailed examinations and diagnostics. We prioritize understanding your unique needs and concerns, ensuring a comfortable, informative, and supportive experience.

WHAT TO BRING

For your first appointment, please bring a valid ID, any relevant dental records, your dental insurance information, and a list of current medications. This helps us provide you with the most personalized and efficient care possible.
