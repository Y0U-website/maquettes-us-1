Dr. Ronald W. Britsch Jr., D.D.S.

Dr. Ron Britsch, Jr, was born and raised in New Orleans. After graduating from Jesuit High School, he attended the University of New Orleans and then earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2002 from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans. He is a third generation dentist, following in the footsteps of his grandfather, two uncles, and his father.

Dr. Ron Jr is an active member in good standing in the New Orleans Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association and American Dental Association.

Dr. Ron and his wife Carrey are the proud parents of two children, William and Aubrey.

Dr. Ron is an avid outdoors man and loves to golf, duck hunt, and fish.
