Welcome to Cedar Creek Dental Center in Sequim, Washington

We’re proud to call the Lavender Capital of North America home. Our clinic offers a relaxing atmosphere to take care of your dental needs.

Looking for a Dentist in Sequim?

Cedar Creek Dental Center is your clinic for all your dental needs—from routine dental care, restoration, and hygiene to cosmetic dentistry, including porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, and Invisalign®.

Regular visits to your dental hygienist for routine cleaning as well as periodontal maintenance also help us spot potential areas that need further evaluation and, if necessary, early treatment.
