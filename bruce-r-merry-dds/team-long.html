Bruce Merry DDS Team

And though each team member truly enjoys their respective dental careers, the following is a glimpse into what also brings them joy...............

As a General Dentist for over 38 years (a University of MN graduate), Dr. Bruce Merry has won thousands of patients over with his gentle and conservative approach. If it’s been a while since you’ve stepped inside a dental office, don’t worry, there won’t be a lecture. If you’re needle-phobic, you’ve come to the right place. He defines his approach to dentistry as commonsense solutions to restoring teeth that are appropriate to the patient’s circumstance .

Loves to tell his staff that he’s getting old, incapable and crotchety (with a smile on his face) but they continue to place patients on his schedule, regardless.

Free time is dominated by family (9 grandchildren), friends and a fascination with creating things; woodworking, painting, sculpting, and cocktail recipes, to name a few.

My one goal while maturing in life is to be grateful for all of it.

Dr. Lau s interest in oral healthcare began during her undergraduate years in her beloved hometown of Winnipeg, Canada. Following her graduation from the University of Minnesota Dental School (go Gophers!), she completed a General Practice Residency at Mercy Hospital in St. Louis, Missouri, before returning back to Minnesota to join private practice.

Dr. Lau strongly values supporting patients’ individual oral healthcare goals and finds joy in getting to know her patients and their families. She often launches her day with a daily quote for the staff. Throughout the office, she is often heard humming her favorite tunes during the day (no ear plugs required).

Outside the office, Dr. Lau enjoys spending time with family, golfing, biking and hiking. Her dog, Arnie, is named after the famous golfer, Arnold Palmer. Another fun fact, Dr. Lau plays electric guitar in a rock band, and cheers for anything Minnesota!

She has worked alongside Dr. Merry for over 20 years, and she could pretty much fill in for either doctor at any time (but since that’s not legal with her credentials, she’s happy to be their extra set of hands). When she’s not assisting, she’s our Radiation Safety Officer, Inventory Captain and affectionately, aka, the Sterilization Tyrant .

Outside the office, Jill values spending time with her family and friends. She keeps up with the rock music scene and participates in a volleyball league all year long. Her favorite movie is National Lampoon’s Christmas Vacation.

Meg joined our team in November, 2016, and couldn t be happier about it. When she isn t assisting the doctors with dental procedures, you can find her up front where she helps Meredith with administrative tasks, and makes sure lab cases are coming and going on time.

She and her husband moved to MN from the east coast to pursue careers in theater. Now 20 years later she assists the doctors by day and runs a small theater company by night. She s also a busy mom of two little girls. In her copious free time she enjoys spending time with her family, reading, and sleeping.

Robin joined the office in August, 2020. She graduated from Mankato State University and has been a hygienist for more years than she’d like to admit. Dentistry runs in her blood as her father and grandfather were both dentists.

She’s married and has two grown children. When not at work, she’s enjoying the outdoors, hiking, swimming, snowshoeing or simply walking in the woods up north.

She’ll let you know if you’ve been lax on your flossing, but don’t blame her, because that’s her job (and she’s good at it)! Just don’t slack on making an appointment with her. Kelly works on Wednesdays.

Amy, who hails from Wisconsin, has been with us since 2017. She is a graduate of the University of Minnesota, School of Dental Hygiene. Amy takes pride in her work and will call upon her own personal dental experiences when calming her patients fears or answering questions.
