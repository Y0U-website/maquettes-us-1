Troy NY Dentist

Dr. Santhosh Thomas is one of the leading cosmetic and family dentists in the Capital district area and has produced winning smiles for so many of his patients. Dr. Thomas graduated from New York University College of Dentistry in 2002, and has completed residencies at Lincoln Memorial hospital in Bronx and Interfaith Hospital Medical Center in Brooklyn.

Dr. Thomas specializes in Smile Makeovers and Smile Enhancements utilizing Laminates/ Veneers and Laser Teeth Whitening along with his Family-oriented practice. He is a member of American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, and the Third District Dental Society.

Dr. Thomas has been practicing in the area for more than 5 years. Since he began practicing dentistry, Dr. Thomas has kept up to date with many continuing educational classes, making certain that his patients receive the most comprehensive and advanced dental care.
