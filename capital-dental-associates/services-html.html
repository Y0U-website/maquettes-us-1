Our Services

Dental Bonding A dental procedure in which a dentist applies a tooth-colored resin material (a durable plastic material) and cures it with visible, blue light. This ultimately "bonds" the material to the tooth and improves the overall appearance of teeth.

Dental Bridges False tooth (or teeth) to replace a missing tooth, held in place by placing a crown on neighboring teeth.

Crowns A tooth-shaped cap placed over a tooth, covering the tooth to restore its shape and size.

Preventative Dental Care for Children and Adults Regular exams and cleanings performed at the determined appropriate interval. Includes updated bitewing and panoramic x-rays as needed, and an annual head and neck exam.

Dental Implants Artificial tooth root replacement that does not require a bridge to anchor it.

Partial and Full Dentures Removable replacement for missing teeth.

Extractions Our office performs simple extractions (removal of a tooth that is not impacted).

Invisalign (clear) Braces Clear removable aligners that are utilized to straighten teeth. For more information, call our office or visit the Invisalign website at www.invisalign.com .

Root Canals Treatment used to repair and save a tooth that is badly decayed or infected.

We focus on diagnosis of gum disease at its earliest point and you will be screened for signs and symptoms of gum disease during visits to our offices. Once diagnosed, treatment for gum disease can include non-surgical treatment in-office for earlier stages, and/or referral to a specialist when necessary.

Dental Sealants are a dental treatment consisting of applying a plastic material to one or more teeth, for the intended purpose of preventing dental cavities or other forms of tooth decay.

Fluoride treatments are applied to strengthen the enamel making it harder for acids to destroy your teeth. Our dentist can apply the fluoride treatment in the form of a gel, foam or varnish in minutes.

Veneers Thin porcelain shields that are bonded to the front of your teeth.

Smile Makeover is the process of improving ones smile through one or more cosmetic dentistry procedures such as teeth whitening, veneers, white fillings, Lumineers, crowns, dental caps, or bridges. A smile makeover will restore chipped, cracked, or discolored teeth, restore your bite, and return the muscles that control your jaw movement to their natural position.

Teeth Whitening Impressions are taken of the patient’s teeth, and mouth trays are made so the patient can perform the whitening at home, utilizing the trays and bleaching material. White and Brite refills are also available. There are 3 types of whitening options.

Venus! Take-Home Whitening System uses a revolutionary patent-pending gel to whiten your teeth an average of seven shades in seven nights.

Nite White bleaching is a much more effective and safer alternative to take-home bleaching systems. It comes in various strengths for people with different degrees of tooth sensitivity. Nite White also contains ingredients to strengthen your teeth and protect against cavities.

The state-of-the-art ZOOM! Teeth Whitening system can make your teeth whiter in just about an hour. ZOOM! Laser Activate Teeth Whitening is a long-lasting procedure and is very safe and comfortable.
