Meet Dr. Bates Board-Certified Orthodontist in Lynchburg, Virginia

At Bates Family Orthodontics, we pride ourselves on providing confident, beautiful smiles while building lasting relationships that allow us to love, serve, empower, and impact our patients and local Lynchburg community. At our office you will enjoy compassionate and high-quality care, state-of-the-art smile design, and a welcoming, family-friendly atmosphere.

Your treatment plan will be customized to meet your unique needs and concerns.

Dr. Bates wants each of his patients to be involved in their smile journey and to understand that THEY are valued.

Dr. Bates is a board-certified orthodontist with extensive training in all areas of orthodontics. He completed his training at some of the nation’s most prestigious programs.

We utilize the most advanced, research-proven technology to comfortably design your dream smile in way that best fits your lifestyle.

Investing in your smile is well worth it — we truly believe you can’t put a price on feeling good about yourself. We offer flexible payment options so finances don’t have to hold you back from your dream smile.

Our orthodontic practice is conveniently located in Lynchburg, Virginia. We welcome patients from Forest, Timberlake, Madison Heights, and other surrounding communities.
