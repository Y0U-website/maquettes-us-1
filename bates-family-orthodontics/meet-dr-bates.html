Meet Dr. Bates

Outside of the office, Dr. Bates maintains a strong commitment to ministry, mentorship, and giving back to the Lynchburg community. Dr. Bates and his family are members of Heritage Baptist Church. They are avid supporters of Young Life, Hope Smiles, and various other non-profit and ministry-focused organizations. Dr. Bates also periodically volunteers his time and dental expertise at the Free Clinic in downtown Lynchburg.

In addition to his faith, family, and orthodontics, Dr. Bates is passionate about all things outdoors. He loves fly fishing, shooting/hunting, backpacking, snow sports, ice hockey, golfing, exercising, traveling, and reading.

Dr. Bates has been the recipient of numerous awards for academic and research excellence, has authored two internationally published research articles, and is a reviewer for The Angle Orthodontist, one of the orthodontic speciality’s most renowned peer-reviewed journals. He is a part-time faculty member in the Department of Orthodontics at Virginia Commonwealth University and also a volunteer dentist at The Free Clinic of Central Virginia here in Lynchburg.
