Benavent Dental was started in 1945 by Dr. Arturo Benavent after he served in the war as a dentist in the United States Navy. The first location was in Mayaguez, Puerto Rico, where his son, Harry Herbert Benavent would eventually come to join the practice. In 2006, Benavent Dental moved to Clarksville, MD. Herby Benavent followed in his father and grandfather’s footsteps and joined the practice…

Dr. Arturo Benavent grew up in Puerto Rico and would travel by boat from Puerto Rico to New York for his undergraduate studies at College Park, MD in the 1930s. He continued his study of dentistry at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry.

After graduating, he became a dentist in the US Navy where he was stationed in South Carolina. After that he started his private practice in Puerto Rico and that was the start of Benavent Dental.

Dr. Arturo Benavent was a member of the Lions Club, where he served one term as President. He also served twice as President of the Colegio de Cirujanos Dentistas de Puerto Rico (College of Dental Surgeons of Puerto Rico). He was a proud member of the United States Coast Guard Auxiliary, United States Power Squadron, and the American Dental Association (ADA).

Dr. Arturo Benavent is the founder of Benavent Dental and the start of a legacy of dentists. His passion for dentistry and boating has continued on through his son and grandson.

Dr. H. Herbert Benavent grew up in Puerto Rico where he completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Puerto Rico. He then graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 1981.
