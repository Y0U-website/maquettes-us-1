Meet Dr. Michael Wierenga

Dr. Wierenga graduated from the University of Michigan for both his undergraduate and dental school. During his time in Ann Arbor, he was involved in clinical research, tutoring other students in subjects such as chemistry and biology, and also volunteered his time weekly at a local dental clinic that provided care to the underserved community.

He received his undergraduate degree in Cellular and Molecular Biology in 2008 and DDS degree four years later in 2012. Dr. Wierenga graduated in the top 2 % of his dental school class and was an active member of the Xi Psi Phi Dental Society and Christian Medical and Dental Association.

Currently, Dr. Wierenga is an active member in the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and West Michigan District Dental Society. He is passionate about continued learning and has sought extensive continuing education, including focused study and training in Invisalign® , soft tissue laser treatment, full-mouth reconstruction, complex implant restorations , and cosmetic dentistry.

His talents in areas of technology and digital dentistry have improved our ability to educate our patients and improve the quality of their care. Dr. Wierenga has completed the entire curriculum at the L.D. Pankey Institute, a world-renowned academic center for advancing dental education, clinical skills, and patient care. He is living the Pankey philosophy of personal and professional fulfillment.
