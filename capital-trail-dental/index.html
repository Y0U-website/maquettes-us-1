The practice

Capital Trail Dental offers comprehensive dental services to our residents and neighbors in and around Richmond, Varina, Rockett’s Landing, Church Hill, Manchester, Charles City, and surrounding neighborhoods.

We’re your neighbors and would love to be your neighborhood dentist.

Dr. Behnaz Movahed Dr. Behnaz and her dental team welcome you to Capital Trail Dental - located just around the corner from Virginia’s Capital Trail. Dr. Behnaz Movahed has been part of the Richmond community for over 25 years, working in private practice,

community health centers, and volunteering with Missions of Mercy Projects.
