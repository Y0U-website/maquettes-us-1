Does your office file for insurance?

As a courtesy to our patients, we are happy to file dental insurance claims for you. Whenever dental insurance coverage requires a predetermination of benefits prior to dental work, your dentist will submit a treatment plan with designated fees so that you and your insurance company are aware of fees involved. Please provide us with a current insurance card for each family member for each visit.

At what age should I start taking my child to see the dentist?

It is recommended that you take your children for their first dental appointment when they are around six months old. If you notice that your children have discolored teeth prior to this time, it is important to see a dentist at that time.

What should I use to clean my baby’s teeth?

Any soft-bristled toothbrush with a small head. Look for toothbrushes that are designed especially for infants and toddlers. An ideal time to brush is at bedtime.
