A Strong Commitment to Continuing Education

Education is a lifetime process, and continuing to learn is a vital part of a high-quality dental practice. We are committed to maintaining a current knowledge of the best practices and procedures in dentistry to assure the best result for our patients. Over the years we have attended countless courses and seminars on practice management, aesthetic dentistry, periodontics and implant prosthetics.

The Spear Institute of Advanced Dentistry provides participating dentists an opportunity to obtain the highest quality, most comprehensive, broad-based education for state-of-the-art dental skills and techniques. It is their goal to create a place that dentists and teams can trust for pragmatic, evidence-based, patient-centered knowledge delivered with truth and integrity. The Spear Institute is recognized internationally for its leadership in post-graduation education.

We attend education and training programs at the Spear Institute several times each year. We are Faculty Club members which provides us with a direct link to the best scientific and technical researchers and practitioners in the country. For more information about the Spear Institute of Advanced Dentistry, you can visit them at: www.speareducation.com/
