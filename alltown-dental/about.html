About Alltown Dental

Alltown Dental provides affordable dental services and is centrally located in the Buffalo area.

Alltown Dental has been serving the Buffalo area since 2013. It has been at its current location on Sweet Home Road since 2016.

Dr. Sergiu Stoica , DDS

Upon graduation in 2007, he was accepted into a comprehensive 1 year general practice residency program offered by Buffalo General Hospital. In 2013 he met Dr. Genau and his wife, Susan, from whom eventually purchased their long established dental practice in Amherst, NY.

Residing in Buffalo area for more than 10 years, Dr. Stoica considers himself a true Western New Yorker and loves living in this great community. He has been married to his soul mate Adina since 2001 and enjoys cooking, traveling and the Buffalo Sabres.

Gaye , Dental Hygienist

Gaye has been employed with Alltown Dental since 2013. With her years of experience in her profession, she is an invaluable asset to the practice. Gaye takes great pride in providing exceptional dental care and knowledge on behalf of the well-being of our patients and in maintaining the healthiest smiles. Gaye graduated with an Associate of Applied Science degree in Dental Hygiene in 1986 from Erie Community College in conjunction with the University at Buffalo.

Laurie , Office Manager

Attending John C. Calhoun College, Laurie graduated in 1982 with an Associates in Applied Science degree in Dental Assistance and Business Management. Her years of experience and commitment have been an asset to Alltown Dental since November 2016. Laurie is a proud member of the American Associate of Dental Office Managers along with our local chapter in here in WNY.

Denita , Dental Assistant

I enjoy getting to know my patients and helping them achieve the results they are looking for in their smile. I spend my free time with my family. We enjoy visiting Carolina beaches and spend time with our families.
