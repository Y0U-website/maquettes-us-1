Dr. Blake Sessions, DMD

"I am a third-generation dentist, and I remember spending time in my grandfather’s office when I was only five or six years old. My uncle became my dentist when my grandfather retired, and I got to pick his brain about the profession and really grew to love it.I studied hard for a lot of years, and now I’m so happy and excited that I did. For me, dentistry is the best job in the world. I love the people I work with, and I get to fix problems to make it easier for someone to smile. People often say they don’t like coming to the dentist, but my job is to make them happy that they did."
