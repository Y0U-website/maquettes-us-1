Dental cleaning is a critical part of dental care that takes place in a dentist’s office. What to Expect After a Dental Cleaning

After receiving a comprehensive dental cleaning Daytona Beach, it is not unusual to experience heightened sensitivity in your teeth and gums. This can occur due to the removal of plaque and tartar during the cleaning process. Sensitive teeth can react to cold or hot foods and drinks, a condition that should gradually wear off.

Professional dental cleanings are designed to target hard-to-reach areas that often host bacterial growth. After deep cleaning treatments and rigorous oral hygiene procedures to fight gum diseases, it is common to notice slight bleeding. Don’t be alarmed; it’s just a part of the healing process.

Fluoride treatments are used to strengthen tooth enamel and prevent decay as part of our dental treatment plan. After your dental cleaning session at our Daytona Beach office, you might experience a lingering after-taste from the fluoride. It’s advised to avoid eating, drinking, or rinsing for at least 30 minutes after the fluoride treatment to allow optimal absorption.

The sense of cleanliness after a dental cleaning should be your motivator to uphold good oral hygiene.
