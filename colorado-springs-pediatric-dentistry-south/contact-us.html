By virtue of living in Colorado, we are presented with extreme weather conditions from time to time. For the safety of our patients and our staff, we may be forced to close our office(s) when it has been determined that road conditions are not safe for travel. Please review our Inclement Weather Policy and please note that our policy is location-specific. Please also note, we reserve the right to close our office(s) early or shut down completely, regardless of this policy if pending and/or existing conditions pose a risk for safe travel.

*******Sedation Visits: If your child is scheduled for Sedation at either our North Office or South Office this will be handled on a case by case basis. Please be prepared to accept a call from our office. Both parties (patient & dentist) will determine if the appointment can safely be accommodated. If it is your preference to reschedule your appointment, we will understandably do so.

In the event of office closures, permitting we have a valid phone number, you will receive notification that our offices are closed due to weather conditions. If you are signed up to receive appointment notifications through Solutionreach, you will also receive notice that our Inclement Weather Policy is in effect.

Please remember that our policy is location-specific and that one of our offices may be open when another office is closed. Also be forewarned that you may still receive appointment notifications from Solutionreach reminding you of your appointment, although our offices are closed.

We apologize in advance for any inconvenience an office closure may cause, but the safety of our patients and our staff needs to take priority if weather conditions prevent safe travel. Thank you for your understanding.
