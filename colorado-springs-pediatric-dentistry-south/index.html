The practice

Our pediatric dentists welcome infants, toddlers, children, and adolescents from throughout Colorado Springs. We also have patients that visit us from Fountain, Widefield, Security, Monument, Manitou Springs, Falcon, Fort Carson, Peterson Air Force Base, Woodland Park, Florence, Salida, West Cliff, Penrose, and many other great cities inside and outside of Colorado.

We look forward to meeting your family and being a part of your child’s journey to their best smile.

Pediatric dental care presents unique challenges that our team is specialized and trained to handle. No matter your child’s needs, we’ll always take a gentle, proactive, and personalized approach to their dental care.
