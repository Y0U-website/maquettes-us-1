OUR PROMISE TO YOU

QUALITY, CUSTOMIZED TREATMENT FOR FAMILIES

WE SERVE ALL AGES

Our focus is on bringing families the care they deserve. That means we need to be able to treat all kinds of patients no matter the age . Our wide variety of services allows us to meet the needs of children whose teeth are still developing, as well as seniors who need to rely on prosthetics to bite and chew. We practice a team approach to dentistry where we can help develop a comprehensive plan while helping you obtain the best quality of care possible.

SPECIAL NEEDS WELCOME Working with all members of our community means being able to accommodate those that need special consideration. Whether it is Down Syndrome, developmental or physical delays, geriatric care or any number of conditions that could potentially complicate dental treatment, we are prepared to meet your family member’s special needs. Dr. Sharp and her team are extremely compassionate and will openly discuss your options with you in order to find a way to care for your family.

DENTAL INSURANCE ACCEPTED We know that it can be difficult to pay for dental care on your own, which is why we are happy to accept all kinds of dental insurance. If you call before scheduling an appointment and let us know what plan you currently have, we can help you figure out what kind of benefits you will be entitled to, should you choose to visit our dental office. Do not be afraid to ask any other questions that might be on your mind; helping you with the insurance process is all part of the job for us!

CONVENIENT DENTAL OFFICE HOURS Part of choosing the right dental office is looking for one with hours that fit well with your schedule. We know this, and we have carefully considered our schedule before choosing dental office hours that we feel work best for us and our patients. For those who do not want to pull their children out of school or take time off of work, we offer early morning appointments so that everyone can come in, have their teeth treated, then get on with the rest of their day.
