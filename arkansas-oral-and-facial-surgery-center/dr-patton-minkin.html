Dr. Patton Minkin, DDS, FACS

Procedures of special interest to Dr. Minkin include wisdom teeth extractions, dental implants, trauma procedures, biopsy, bonegrafting and maxillofacial pathology.

Dr. Minkin is a Board Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon and a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons.

Meet Dr. Patton Minkin:

Dr. Patton Minkin practices at our Springdale location where our team provides the placement of dental implants , facial trauma treatment , corrective jaw surgery , oral / maxillofacial pathology treatment , dental and surgical extractions , and anesthesia .

About Dr. Minkin

He began his educational training at Christian Brothers University in Memphis, where he played collegiate baseball and earned a chemistry degree. After college, Dr. Minkin began a career in pediatric cancer research within the pharmaceutical sciences department of St. Jude’s Children Research Hospital . During his time at St. Jude his research contributions in pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics were published in several peer reviewed journals.

He then earned his DDS from the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry . He continued his educational training, completing a General Practice Residency at Louisiana State University Hospital in New Orleans and an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery internship with the Oral Surgical Institute in Nashville. He then completed Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York City. He has provided wide scope Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery services to Northwest Arkansas since 2018.

He participates in regional facial trauma coverage maintaining privileges at Washington Regional Hospital , Arkansas Children’s Hospital and Mercy Hospital in Northwest Arkansas. His office based practice focuses on traditional Oral and Maxillofacial services including complex dental alveolar surgery, bone grafting / dental implant surgery, maxillofacial pathology, pre-prosthetic / orthodontic related surgery and pediatric oral surgical procedures.

Dr. Minkin is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (ABOMS), a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS), a Fellow of the American College of Surgeons (FACS), member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Arkansas State Dental Association (ASDA), and Northwest District Dental Society (NWDDS).

Dr. Minkin and his wife live in Fayetteville and are proud parents. They enjoy sports, hiking and exploring everything Northwest Arkansas has to offer. Dr. Minkin and his family are avid sports fans and enjoy anything related to professional and collegiate athletics.
