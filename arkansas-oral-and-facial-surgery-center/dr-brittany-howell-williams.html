Procedures of special interest to Dr. Williams include wisdom teeth extractions, dental implants, trauma procedures, biospy, bone grafting and maxillofacial pathology.

Dr. Williams is a Board Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon.

Meet Dr. Brittany Howell Williams:

Dr. Brittany Howell Williams practices at our Fayetteville location where our team provides the placement of dental implants , facial trauma treatment , corrective jaw surgery , oral / maxillofacial pathology treatment , dental and surgical extractions , and anesthesia .

About Dr. Williams

Dr. Williams is the first female oral surgeon in the state of Arkansas and we are proud to have her at our Arkansas Oral & Facial Surgery Center in Fayetteville.

Following graduation from the University of Arkansas , Dr. Williams worked as a surgical assistant for Arkansas Oral and Facial Surgery Center and soon after, was accepted to attend Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Illinois. She met her husband during her time in Fayetteville and together they spent the next 8 years in the Chicagoland area where she completed both her degree in Dentistry as well as a residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Loyola University Medical Center .

Along the way, Dr. Williams served as a Captain in the Army National Guard. During their free time, Dr. Williams and her husband enjoy cooking, traveling and spending time outdoors. They’ve now re-established their roots back home in Fayetteville, Arkansas with their three furry family members.
