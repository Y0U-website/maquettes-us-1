Les soins

Whether it’s something as simple as a minor dental extraction, or as complex as a reconstruction, we are here to help you improve your overall health.

Dr. Patton Minkin is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon providing tooth extractions , wisdom tooth extractions , ortho extractions (expose and bond or bond and bracket), dental anesthesia and dental sedation , bone grafting , dental implants and facial trauma…

He participates in regional facial trauma coverage maintaining privileges at Washington Regional Hospital , Arkansas Children’s Hospital and Mercy Hospital in Northwest Arkansas. His office based practice focuses on traditional Oral and Maxillofacial services…
