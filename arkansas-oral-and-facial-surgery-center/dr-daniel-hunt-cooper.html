Meet Dr. Daniel Hunt Cooper:

Dr. Daniel Hunt Cooper practices at our Fayetteville location as well as our Springdale location , where he provides sedation and anesthesia for the placement of dental implants , facial trauma treatment , corrective jaw surgery , oral / maxillofacial pathology treatment , dental and surgical extractions , and anesthesia.

Specialties:

Dr. Daniel Hunt Cooper is an anesthesiologist. He is a physician who use anesthesia to ensure you’re asleep or unconscious during surgery or other medical procedures. He also relieves pain before, during and after operations .

About Anesthesia:

At Arkansas Oral & Facial Surgery Center, we are dedicated to ensuring patient safety, comfort, and exceptional outcomes through expertly administered anesthesia tailored to each individual’s needs. For less invasive procedures, such as wisdom tooth extractions or minor oral surgeries, we utilize local anesthesia to numb the targeted surgical area, allowing patients to remain fully awake while experiencing no pain.

Our team employs state-of-the-art injection techniques and premium-grade anesthetics to achieve rapid onset and sustained pain relief, ensuring a comfortable experience throughout the procedure. For more complex surgeries or patients with heightened anxiety, we offer general anesthesia, expertly administered to induce deep sedation or complete unconsciousness. This approach is carefully customized based on the intricacy of the procedure, the patient’s medical background, and their unique preferences, guaranteeing the best possible surgical outcomes.

Safety is the cornerstone of our practice at Arkansas Oral & Facial Surgery Center. During every procedure, we utilize cutting-edge monitoring technology to continuously track vital signs, including heart rate, oxygen saturation, and blood pressure, ensuring patient stability and safety under anesthesia

Dr. Daniel Hunt Cooper has served as an anesthesiologist in medical facilities in Arkansas with more than 29 years of experience. He is a board-certified anesthesiologist in Arkansas as well as a Member of the American Board of Anesthesiology . Dr. Cooper graduated from University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) College of Medicine .
