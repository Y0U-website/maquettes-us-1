Oral Surgery Springdale, AR

Whether it’s something as simple as a minor dental extraction, or as complex as a reconstruction, we are here to help you improve your overall health.

Arkansas Oral & Facial Surgery Center in Springdale, AR is located northwest of Gutensohn Road and Huntsville Avenue, across the street from Central Junior Highschool .

At this clinic we proudly feature an Intraoral 3D Scanner , allowing us to provide Next Day Denture Delivery .

Dr. Patton Minkin is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon providing tooth extractions , wisdom tooth extractions , ortho extractions (expose and bond or bond and bracket), dental anesthesia and dental sedation , bone grafting , dental implants and facial trauma treatment .
