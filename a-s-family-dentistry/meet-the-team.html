Meet The Team

Nerissa D. Aquino, DDS- Lead Dentist

Gregorio J. Aquino, DDS - Associate Dentist

Dr. Gregorio Joseph Aquino, a local Port Saint Lucie resident, completed his Advanced Education in General Dentistry from Larkin Community Hospital in South Miami. In addition to his education at Larkin Community Hospital, Dr. Aquino also attended courses at the Misch Implant Institute to further his abilities to help patients with the need for dental implants. He is also trained & proficient in using Hissosen, MIS and Dentsply implants for patients who need it.

He shares his passion for Dentistry with his wife, Dr. Nerissa, and enjoys doing, in addition to providing reliable implants to patients, cosmetic veneers, crowns, root canals, and extractions.

Ashley

Ashley started working with the office when it opened in February of 2023. She loves helping out our patients. The best part is seeing the impact the team makes, and how patients come in so nervous and then leave happy. Seeing the looks on their faces when they get a new smile is something else. Ashley’s job is to verify all insurance and patient benefits as well as answering financial questions and finding a solution to help patients move forward with treatment. Ashley loves to be the smile you see when you first come in to our office and the last when you leave.

Ashley is married and has three children: two girls and one boy. She loves to spend her free time with her family, going to the beach, shopping, fishing and traveling.

Hormelle

Hormelle graduated from Fort Lauderdale High in 1982 before becoming a dental assistant with her certification in X-rays in 1990 after graduating from Nova University. She became a part of A’s Family Dentistry in February 2023. Her job is to keep the office organized, help the doctor as a chairside assistant, make supply orders and ensure those supplies are up to date. Hormelle loves the doctor; she loves her work as a DA and working with the team. Her patients are great, and they make her want to come to work every day.

Hormelle has been married since 1991, and she has three kids and five grandkids. She likes to cook, shop, dance, sing and create art in her free time.
