3. Preserved Jawbone and Facial Structure

Where you have a missing tooth, your jawbone will start breaking down in that area over time because it’s not being stimulated. You’re not chewing on it every day. It happens so slowly that you might not even notice it at first.

Then one day, you’ll see a photo of yourself and be shocked. That’s because when you have missing teeth, your face starts to sink in. Dental implants keep your jawbone stimulated so the bone stays healthy.

4. Long-Term Durability with Dental Implants for Missing Teeth Restoration

You have to replace bridges and dentures every 5-15 years. Not so with implants. They’ll last for decades. Even a lifetime sometimes. They’re a one-and-done investment in your quality of life and overall health.

We’ve all seen the video of the grandmother at Thanksgiving dinner who starts laughing and her dentures go flying across the room. You’ll never have to worry about that with dental implants. They’re fixed permanently in place. Just like your natural tooth used to be.
