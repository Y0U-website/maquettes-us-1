Wisdom Teeth Before Implant Planning

Wisdom teeth don’t always affect implants, but impacted wisdom teeth or chronic infection near wisdom teeth can influence bone health . If wisdom teeth removal is needed, we coordinate timing so you can heal and protect the jawbone often within a few months.

Serving the Bay Area

Patients come to our office from across the Bay Area, including San Francisco, South San Francisco, Daly City, and San Mateo, for coordinated planning in California in a welcoming environment with a knowledgeable clinician. Keeping evaluation, surgery, and restoration planning aligned in one practice supports modern dentistry and reduces surprises.

Planning, Bone, and Fit

Dental implants work best when planning is measured, not guessed. We assess bone volume, gum health, and bite forces, then place implants in positions that support the final restoration. Bone grafting is sometimes recommended to rebuild bone and improve long-term support and overall stability.

During the process, we check implant readiness at key checkpoints. That guidance helps us decide when we can restore the implant with a temporary tooth and when we should wait several months for full integration. This approach helps dental implants heal predictably.
