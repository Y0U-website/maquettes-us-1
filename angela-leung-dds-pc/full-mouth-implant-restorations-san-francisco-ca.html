Full Mouth Implant Restorations – Dental Implant

Who it’s for:

People missing most or all teeth, living with failing teeth, or struggling with unstable dentures who want a stable long-term replacement.

What we deliver:

A full-mouth implant restoration strategy that connects diagnostics, implant placement, and the final prosthesis so the end result supports comfort, function, and durability.

The treatment sequence (clear phases, clear checkpoints)

While each plan is customized, full-mouth implant restorations typically move through phases:

Phase 1: Evaluation + records

We gather CBCT + scans/photos, review health history, and map out the safest sequence.

Phase 2: Foundation steps (if needed)

This may include removing failing teeth, managing infection, or preparing bone/soft tissue.

Phase 3: Implant placement

Implants are placed according to the plan and stability targets.

Phase 4: Temporary teeth (when appropriate)

A transitional set of teeth helps maintain appearance and function while healing occurs.

Phase 5: Final restoration

The final prosthesis is designed for bite balance, comfort, and cleansability—then refined with follow-up adjustments.

You’ll always know where you are in the sequence and what the next checkpoint is.

Comfort, healing, and the “in-between” teeth

Many patients delay treatment because they’re worried about pain, time off work, or how they’ll look during healing.

Here’s how we keep the process manageable:

  • Clear expectations for healing, diet, speech changes, and adjustment visits
  • Follow-ups focused on bite balance and comfort (small changes make a big difference)
  • Long-term maintenance guidance to protect your investment
