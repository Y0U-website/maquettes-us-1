The practice

From your initial consultation through post-implant care, Dr. Dalton and the team guide you through every step of the process. Each treatment plan is customized to your dental health, bone structure, and goals, ensuring optimal results. With implants, patients can regain confidence in their smiles while maintaining the health and function of surrounding teeth.

Restores multiple missing teeth using strategically placed implants to support individual crowns or implant-supported bridges for optimal function and aesthetics.

Provides a complete set of replacement teeth for patients missing all or most teeth in an arch. This solution restores chewing, speaking, and facial structure with stability and comfort.

Comprehensive follow-up care and maintenance instructions to ensure your implants remain healthy, stable, and long-lasting.

Repairs and restores existing implants, crowns, or bridges to maintain function, aesthetics, and oral health.

We provide detailed guidance on caring for your implants, including hygiene routines and lifestyle tips, empowering you to protect your investment and maintain a healthy smile.

A consultation allows Dr. Dalton to create a tailored plan that addresses your specific needs, including the number of implants, placement strategy, and restoration type, ensuring the best possible long-term outcomes.

Through detailed examinations and imaging, the consultation identifies potential challenges such as bone density, alignment, or oral health issues, allowing for early interventions that improve implant success and minimize complications.
