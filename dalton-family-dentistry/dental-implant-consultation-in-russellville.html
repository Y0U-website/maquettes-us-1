A consultation allows Dr. Dalton to create a tailored plan that addresses your specific needs, including the number of implants, placement strategy, and restoration type, ensuring the best possible long-term outcomes.

Patients gain a full understanding of implant options, potential risks, and expected results, empowering them to make confident, educated decisions that support optimal function, appearance, and long-term oral health.
