Log into Student Commons Search CVTC.edu Search CVTC Submit Search Home // Digital Accessibility Chippewa Valley Technical College (CVTC) is committed to ensuring equal opportunity in its educational and administrative services, programs, and activities in accordance with federal and state law. The College is dedicated to providing accessibility in electronic and information technologies, including digital experiences for all users.

To that end, CVTC strives to adopt and conform to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 , Level AA standards. We work to continually modify our websites to ensure the information, features, and content are accessible to persons with disabilities.

Report a Digital Access Barrier

Individuals who encounter a disability-related barrier or access concern with digital materials, content, or technology may report using the Digital Access Barrier form: Report a Barrier
