0 Our Dental Services Explained Our range of dental services is designed to cover your whole family’s needs for expert dental care in Miami. Below is a breakdown of our services and the procedures we can perform for you.

A thorough dental exam is the starting point for many dental treatments. Regular dental exams can identify problems before they cause pain, discomfort, or tooth loss.

Routine dental cleaning can prevent the build-up of plaque and tartar – two key steps on the road to painful gum disease and tooth loss.

Whitening treatments (also known as tooth bleaching) could give your smile an instant upgrade. Whitening is among the most popular cosmetic dental treatments available.

Tooth damage doesn’t have to lead to tooth loss, and fillings are often the simplest form of treatment.

Dental bridges typically combine crowns with a false tooth to provide a secure replacement for a missing natural tooth.

In recent years, Invisalign clear aligners have changed the face of cosmetic dental services, becoming a very popular alternative to conventional braces.

The fitting of implants is among our most popular dental services. Implants offer an alternative to traditional dentures and behave more like natural teeth.

Dental veneers are made up of a shell-like layer that covers a damaged, undersized, or discolored tooth’s front surface.

Night guards are a simple form of protection from the wear caused by clenching or grinding the teeth while sleeping (bruxism).

A dental crown is a cap that sits over a damaged tooth, improving its strength while restoring its natural shape and function. Crowns may be used together with bridges.

Dentures remain a popular and affordable alternative to implants and can improve the appearance of your smile after tooth loss.

A root canal treatment removes an infected or inflamed area of dental pulp. The root canal can then be strengthened, allowing the tooth to be saved.

A dental extraction provides a fast and permanent solution for a badly damaged tooth. Dental extractions may also be needed in advance of other procedures.

Oral appliances can be effective in the treatment of sleep apnea and offer an alternative for patients who can’t (or don’t wish to) use a CPAP machine.
