Your First Visit: Getting Ready…

0 What to Expect Here’s what you can expect during your visit to Hernandez Family Dentist, a friendly, family-oriented dental office located in the heart of Miami.

First, a comprehensive examination is done, along with digital radiographs, to provide a diagnosis. If all is well, we can schedule your routine dental cleaning, which should be done every six months.

If further dental services are needed, we will discuss treatment plan options and schedule a follow-up appointment.
