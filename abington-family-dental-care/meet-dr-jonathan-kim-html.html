Meet Dr. Jonathan Kim

Dentist - Abington, MA

Dr. Jonathan Kim

Dr. Jonathan Kim was born on the West Coast and grew up in California and Arizona. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Arizona before moving to the East Coast to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Kim went on to complete advanced specialty training in Periodontics at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and gum recession, as well as dental implant placement and soft tissue procedures In order to help restore both the health and natural appearance of patients’ smiles.

Dr. Kim believes in treating every patient like family and strives to create a comfortable and supportive environment. He values patient education and takes the time to ensure each patient fully understands their treatment options, helping make dental care feel simple and approachable.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Kim regularly participates in continuing education to stay up to date with the latest advancements in periodontics and implant dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kim enjoys spending time with his wife, their son, and their two cats. In his free time, he enjoys outdoor activities such as hiking and golf.
