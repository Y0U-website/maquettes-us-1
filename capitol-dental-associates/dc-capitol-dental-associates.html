About DC

  • Operative Dentistry Operative Dentistry is what we do when we fix tooth decay through procedures like giving you a filling.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Many problems that plague us as adults can be headed off in our early years which is why pediatric dentistry is so important. We provide oral health care for young people the same as we do adults, but with particular attention paid to the issues of younger patients so that they can have a lifetime of pain-free smiles and eating.
  • Preventive & Community Dentistry Preventative care means helping people get the hang of regular brushing and flossing as well as regular trips to the dentist. We work in our schools and community to help get the word out so we can help prevent disease and decay.
  • Family Dentistry We treat patients from all stages of life from little kids to seniors. We are ready to serve you no matter what your dental needs are or how old you are. You will get great service and respect.
  • Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Whether we like it or not, sometimes we have problems that require a surgeon’s touch. Wisdom teeth get removed, grafts into the jaw bone are made, and implants are installed in order to improve function and aesthetics.
  • Orthodontics When most people think of orthodontics they think of braces. This is true, but did you know there are a lot of options in orthodontia? We try to correct the irregularities of your teeth, bite, and even your jaw with braces, Invisalign , or other options.
  • Periodontics Periodontics covers the treatment of problems in the gums and the pockets in your jaw where the tooth fits. Sometimes they do root canals, bone grafts, or dental implants.
  • Prosthodontics This is dental prosthetics like dentures, crowns, bridges, veneers, and implants.
