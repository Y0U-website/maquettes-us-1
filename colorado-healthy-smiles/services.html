Services

Colorado Healthy Smiles Services

We look forward to being your family’s dental care provided. Our service offerings include:

General Dentistry

Dental cleanings, gum health maintenance, and treatments for the dental needs of children, teens, and adults.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Improves the appearance of your smile. It includes veneers, teeth whitening, dental bonding, etc.

Restorative Dentistry

Restores and maintains the mouth’s function through treatments such as root canals, porcelain crowns, dental filling, inlays, etc.

Zoom Whitening

Philips Zoom! system is the easiest, safest and quickest way to whiten teeth and has been used by more than 10 million patients.

Invisalign

Using clear, removable trays to align teeth, Invisalign is an alternative to braces for teens and adults who want to discreetly straighten their teeth.

TMJ Treatment

Calms the muscles and ligaments around the temporomandibular joint (TMJ) to relieve the pain, “clicking” and pressure.

Conscious Sedation

Allows the fearful patient to enter a relaxed state while dental procedures are performed, effectively eliminating dental fears and anxieties.

Sleep and Breathing Disorders

Did you know a simple trip to the dentist could help with bed wetting and snoring issues?
