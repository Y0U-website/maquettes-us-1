Access Your Dental Care: Secure Patient Portal

Patient Portal

Our secure patient portal makes it easy to stay connected with your dental care anytime, anywhere. Through the portal, patients can conveniently access billing statements , review previous appointments , and view upcoming scheduled visits .

You can also keep track of your treatment history and stay informed about your dental care in one simple, secure location.

The portal is available 24/7, giving you the flexibility to manage your dental information, check appointment details, and review your account whenever it’s most convenient for you. It’s just one more way we make caring for your smile easier and more accessible.

First time users please select Forgot Password and follow prompts to access your portal
