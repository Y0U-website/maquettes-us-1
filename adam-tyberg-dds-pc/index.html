At Dental Care of Sherwood, we believe your smile is a vital part of your overall health, confidence, and quality of life. From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll experience a practice built on precision, trust, and long-term relationships. We take the time to listen, to understand your goals, and to create treatment plans that truly fit your life.

Dr. Tyberg is committed to continual growth and clinical mastery. As a graduate of the prestigious Kois Center and a participant in advanced implant and surgical training at the Pikos Institute, he has pursued some of the highest levels of continuing education in modern dentistry.

This dedication means you benefit from techniques grounded in research, precision, and proven results. Our focus is on creating stable, long-lasting oral health that supports your entire well-being.

One of the most important things patients should know about our practice is that we keep most dental work in-house.

With advanced tools such as scanned impressions, CAD/CAM milling, and digital printing, we combine technology and craftsmanship. This allows us to deliver beautiful, precise results without unnecessary delays.
