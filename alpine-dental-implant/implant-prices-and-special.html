How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

For a limited time, our single dental implant package starts at just $2600 and includes both the implant surgery and crown. Don’t miss out on our Dental Implant Special Prices!

The Cost of Dental Implant is determined by many factors and varies from patient to patient. Some patients will only require one implant, whereas others will require additional treatments such as bone grafting , tooth extraction, or multiple teeth replacements. Every case is unique so best way to get a quote is to request an appointment for free no obligation consultation.

On the surface, dental implants look incredibly expensive, especially when they are compared to other tooth replacement methods. However, Dental implants that are properly planned and placed are actually less expensive in the long run than other traditional dental treatments. A root canal, post, and crown can be as expensive as a single implant and may need to be replaced or repaired every 5-10 years. Dental implants are the best long-term solution for missing teeth, and we make them affordable without compromising quality.

There are no hidden costs once we provide you with a quote. You can be confident knowing your smile is protected with us.
