Dr. Haeman Noori, DDS

Credentials & Certifications

Dr. Haeman Noori is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon and a proud Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is also a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He currently holds hospital privileges at Presbyterian-St. Luke’s, Red Rocks and Clear Creek Surgery Centers, and is in the process of expanding his affiliations to Lutheran Hospital and the CU School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Noori is committed to clinical excellence and lifelong learning. He actively participates in continuing education, leads and attends multiple study clubs, and regularly speaks at national conferences. His special interests include dental implantology, bone and soft tissue grafting, and reconstructive surgery for facial trauma patients.

A natural teacher and the son of two university professors, Dr. Noori finds joy in educating his patients and guiding them through their treatment options with clarity and compassion. He’s also passionate about community service and is proud to provide care for underserved patients in need.

Personal

Dr. Noori and his wife—also a dentist and graduate of UNC School of Dentistry—are happily raising their two children, Aidan and Ava, in beautiful Colorado. Both kids are proud Denver natives, and the family loves spending time outdoors and exploring everything the state has to offer.

Originally from New England, Dr. Noori is a lifelong fan of the Patriots, Celtics, and Red Sox. He also cheers for his alma maters: Cornell Big Red, UNC Tar Heels, and Indiana Hoosiers. When he&#x27;s not in the clinic, he enjoys exercising, reading, and hunting down the best local coffee shops—feel free to send him your favorite spot!
