The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check-up by age 7. That doesn’t mean your child will need braces right away. An early visit gives the orthodontist a chance to evaluate how the teeth and jaws are developing and identify problems that may be easier to address while your child is still growing.

Many kids who need braces begin treatment between ages 9 and 14, once enough permanent teeth have come in.

Many dental and orthodontic plans cover at least part of the cost of braces. We accept most insurance plans and will handle the paperwork for you — and at your consultation, we&#x27;ll verify your benefits so you know exactly what&#x27;s covered.
