At Azalea Orthodontics, we believe in providing a personalized path to your perfect smile. Dr. Maddox and our team select the treatment that best fits your needs, lifestyle, and smile goals, from metal braces to clear aligners.

Built for efficiency and comfort, metal braces use the latest technology to shorten your treatment time and maximize comfort.

We design custom-made, clear aligners to fit your lifestyle, offering a virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth without disrupting your daily life..

Made from ceramic materials that blend with the color of your teeth, clear braces offer a less noticeable alternative to metal braces.
