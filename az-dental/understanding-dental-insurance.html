Understanding Dental Insurance

Many patients assume that dental insurance will cover all of their dental treatment. Unfortunately, that is one of the biggest misconceptions about dental insurance. At AZ Dental PC, your trusted White Plains dentist , we believe that understanding how dental insurance works helps our patients make informed decisions about their oral health.

Dental Insurance Is a Benefit, Not Full Coverage

Unlike medical insurance, most dental insurance plans were designed to help offset the cost of preventive and basic dental care—not to pay for every dental procedure.

These limitations mean that patients are often responsible for a portion of the treatment cost.

As your White Plains dentist , we often hear patients ask why their insurance does not pay the entire cost of treatment.

Please remember that insurance estimates are not guaranteed of payment. The final determination is always made by your insurance company after the claim is processed.

Sometimes delaying necessary dental treatment because of insurance limitations can result in more extensive—and more expensive—problems in the future.

Our priority as your White Plains dentist is to recommend the treatment that offers the best long-term outcome for your oral health. We will always discuss your treatment options, expected insurance benefits, and any out-of-pocket costs before treatment begins.

AZ Dental PC works with many major PPO dental insurance plans. Even if we are out of network with your insurance, many PPO plans still allow you to receive treatment from our office.
