What is the process of getting implant restorations?

Getting dental implants and implant restorations involves multiple visits to Cole Street Dental. After the process of planning your treatment, the first surgery involves placing the dental implant (or multiple dental implants) into your jawbone. The team makes an incision in your gums to access the bone and screws in the titanium implant.

The healing period between implant surgery and the placement of your restorations is three months. That gives your jawbone time to fuse to the implant in a process called osseointegration, essentially making the implant a permanent feature in your jaw.

During a later visit, the team exposes the implant, sanitizes it, and attaches your custom restoration. Some restorations are screw-retained while others are cement-retained. The team helps you decide which is best.
