Meet Dr. Patel

SKILLED SUGARCREEK TOWNSHIP, OH DENTIST

Get to Know Dr. Parthiv Patel

I like the fact that I can be the reason behind someone’s smile.

Dr. Patel says that dentistry allows him to combine all his passions into one fulfilling career. He loves interacting with people from different backgrounds, and he also loves working with his hands using the latest technology. He gets to do this every day as part of our team while making people’s lives better, and he couldn’t imagine doing anything else.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Patel’s journey toward becoming a dentist started when he was a child. He was playing hide and seek one day, and his cousin fell down and chipped his front tooth, so he was rushed to the dentist. They fixed his tooth right away and fully restored his smile, which was extremely fascinating to Dr. Patel. It was at that moment he decided he wanted to be a dentist.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

A graduate of the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Patel has completed advanced training courses to make him proficient in providing clear aligner orthodontics, dental implants, and Botox. He is also an active member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and on different committees within the Dayton Dental Society.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Patel grew up in India and has called the Dayton area home since 2016. He and his wife, Roshni, have two boys, Om and Ayaan. When he isn’t spending time with them, he likes to remodel his house, try different cuisines, and learn about new dental technologies.
