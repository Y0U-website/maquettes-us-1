Our Practice in Griffith, IN

Christopher Gall DDS in Griffith, IN

Our team at Christopher Gall DDS serves the community of Griffith, IN with quality dental care. Our location at 909 E. 45th Avenue is designed with the latest dental technology, ensuring quality treatments and top-notch comfort for every patient who walks through the door.

Our dental office was built in 1976 by our founder, Dr. John A. McAleer. In 1992, Christopher Gall DDS joined the practice and assumed full responsibility of the office in 2003 when Dr. McAleer retired.

Family-Friendly Dental Treatments

Our team understands the importance of good dental health in all stages of life. We offer quality treatments for people of all ages, giving you one location for the entire family. By focusing on the needs of each patient, we work hard to build life-long relationships that support the needs of your family.

Routine dentistry is available for young children and aging adults alike. Our goal is to preserve your natural teeth whenever possible through preventive care. Not only are our services focused on prevention, but we encourage routine visits and health at-home dental care so you can maintain a healthy smile. These preventive treatments are focused on common dental issues including both dental decay and gum disease.

Smile Restoration and Cosmetic Treatments

When minimally-invasive treatments aren’t sufficient for your dental concerns, then our team can assist with a variety of advanced restorations such as dental prosthetics and smile rehabilitation. Additionally, full cosmetic services are available if you want to boost your confidence with a new smile.

One of the benefits of choosing Christopher Gall DDS is the personalized services offered to every patient. We offer a thorough approach for diagnosing potential problems, and proactive solutions to preserve the integrity of your smile. When needed, a comprehensive interdisciplinary approach is available with specialists throughout Porter and Lake Counties, ensuring the best care for our patients.

Our experienced team is here to help you maintain a healthy, confident smile. Learn more by contacting us to schedule an appointment at your convenience. We offer Saturday services as well as after-hours support for dental emergencies as needed. Contact our office to book an appointment and learn about these quality services.

Do Things Right and Do the Right Thing!

At Christopher Gall DDS, our mission is to do things right and do the right thing. We are deeply committed to our community and show our support each year by sponsoring the Griffith Health Professions Scholarship. This scholarship is awarded to one graduating senior who plans to further their education in a healthcare related field.
