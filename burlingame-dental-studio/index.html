The practice

Dr. Zoya Galant believes in treating patients as people, not just teeth. “We recognize that there is a human being behind every tooth,” she says. Her practice is built on the foundation of trust, warmth, and a commitment to both quality care and patient comfort!

Her focus lies in her dedication to patient well-being. She fosters lasting relationships with her patients, creating a calm, welcoming environment where they feel genuinely cared for. Her philosophy is simple: “Our patients are an extension of our family!”

At Burlingame Dental Studio, Dr. Zoya Galant proudly offers CandidPro® Clear Aligners to help patients achieve a straighter, more confident smile. These nearly invisible aligners are custom-designed to gently and effectively move your teeth into proper alignment—all under the expert care of Dr. Galant.
