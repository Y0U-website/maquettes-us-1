What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a surgically installed titanium post placed in the jaw and designed to fuse with the surrounding bone tissue over time.

Dental implants function much like natural tooth roots. After the implant has healed, it can provide stable support for a customized replacement tooth or restoration.

Dental implants can also make speaking and eating easier. Because there is no bulky or unstable appliance getting in the way, you can eat, smile, and speak with greater confidence.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants can replace a single missing tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch of teeth.

If you have lost several teeth, a carefully planned number of implants placed along the upper or lower arch may provide the stability needed to support partial dentures, full dentures, or a bridge.

Living With Dental Implants

Proper care and professional support can help your implants remain comfortable, functional, and dependable.

Daily Implant Care

One of the benefits of dental implants is that they function similarly to natural teeth. They should be brushed, cleaned, and cared for as part of your regular oral hygiene routine.

Consistent home care helps protect your implant restoration, surrounding gums, and remaining natural teeth.

Routine Dental Visits

Dental implants also benefit from routine checkups and preventive care. Visiting our Blue Bell dental practice regularly allows our team to evaluate your implants, gums, and overall oral health.

The better you care for your implants and natural teeth, the longer and more reliably they can serve your smile.
