Dr. Ankit Karsalia, D.M.D.

Dr. Karsalia is a local to the area, he grew up right in Phoenixville, PA and continues to reside in Montgomery County. Dr. Karsalia attended Phoenixville area high school, middle school, and then went to Pennsylvania State University (GO LIONS!!) to receive his BS. After attending PSU, Dr. Karsalia received his DMD from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry.

He has experience with General Dentistry, Endodontics, Periodontics, and Oral Surgery. Dr. Karsalia Has also studied and received hands on training in Implant and Cosmetic Dentistry. He strives to help patients overcome their dental phobias; improve their comfort level in a dental environment, and to educate patients on oral health to improve their overall health.

During his free time he enjoys golfing, biking, running, hiking, paddle boarding, and yoga. Most importantly Dr. Karsalia enjoys spending time with his wife and family.

Dr. Aida Pasalic, D.M.D.

Dr. Pasalic has experience and knowledge in various specialties of dentistry such as Periodontics, Oral surgery, and Endodontics. She offers a wide array of cosmetic procedures including bonding, veneers, all ceramic (tooth-colored) crowns and bridges, and tooth-colored fillings. She is also skilled in providing dental implants.

Dr Pasalic obtained her degree in Dentistry from the UNIVERSITY OF PENNSYLVANIA SCHOOL OF DENTAL MEDICINE where she graduated with honors. She was selected to enter the post-doctoral training program at TEMPLE UNIVERSITY in Advanced Education in General Dentistry, which emphasized treating medically complex cases as well as implant dentistry.

Dr. Pasalic stays current with continuing education courses throughout the year to advance her knowledge and improve her skills. She is a member of THE ACADEMY OF GENERAL DENTISTRY and the AMERICAN DENTAL ASSOCIATION.

Dr. Aida Pasalic is committed to creating healthy and beautiful smiles for each of her patients in a calm and comforting atmosphere.

Dr. Padma Raman, D.M.D.

Dr. Padma Raman graduated with honors from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 2007. While at Penn, she received several awards including the Professor Modern Amsterdam award for excellence in treatment planning and patient care. Her personal highlight was when she got to meet Dr. Amsterdam after her presentation and received his vote. She was also inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

She feels a personal responsibility to ensure that no parent has to struggle as much as she did to get the right help. Dr. Raman views early airway management and dentofacial orthopedics as one of the greatest gifts dentistry can offer, helping prevent downstream health problems that patients may never even know they avoided. Today, she begins evaluating airway health as early as age three.

Outside the office, Dr. Raman enjoys spending time with her family, gardening, ’farm-to-table’ cooking, hiking, and birdwatching.
