About Us

Dr. Hansen is a compassionate and dedicated clinician. Her caring and gentle approach puts the most apprehensive of patients at ease. She graduated from the University of Bialystok Dental School in Poland and the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in New York and continues to advance her knowledge of dentistry through continuing education courses.

Following dental school, Dr. Hansen completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program at Stony Brook. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Society, Connecticut Dental Associate and the New Haven Dental Association.

Before moving to Connecticut Dr. Hansen practiced dentistry in Poland and New York. She resides in Orange, CT with her husband, Christopher, and son, Tristan. She enjoys skiing, ice skating, bicycling and spending time with her family. Additionally, Dr. Hansen, her husband and son are actively involved with the Polish School in Derby, CT and the surrounding Polish community.
