Quick Facts

A dental crown is essentially a custom-made cap that fits over a damaged tooth, protecting what&#x27;s left of the natural root while restoring the tooth&#x27;s shape, strength, and appearance. It&#x27;s one of the most common restorative procedures in dentistry — and once it&#x27;s in place, you care for it exactly like a natural tooth.

A dental bridge does something different: it fills the gap left by a missing tooth using crowns placed on the two teeth on either side, with an artificial tooth — called a pontic — suspended between them. If you&#x27;ve been avoiding smiling because of a missing tooth, or if you&#x27;ve noticed that neighboring teeth are starting to shift, a bridge can correct both problems at once.

Dr. Deshpande takes the time to examine your full mouth and explain exactly which option makes sense for your situation — not just the tooth in question, but how the surrounding teeth and your bite factor into the decision. You&#x27;ll leave your first visit with a clear treatment plan, a temporary restoration in place, and a realistic sense of what to expect at your second appointment.

Common Questions About Crowns & Bridges Will getting a crown or bridge hurt? Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the process is. Dr. Deshpande uses local anesthesia to keep you numb and at ease throughout the procedure, and any soreness afterward is typically mild and short-lived. If dental anxiety has kept you away in the past, just let us know — we&#x27;ll take extra care to make sure you feel comfortable every step of the way.

Does insurance cover crowns and bridges?

Many dental insurance plans cover a portion of crown and bridge treatment, typically classifying them as major restorative services. At Brilliant Smiles, we&#x27;ll review your specific benefits before treatment so you understand exactly what your plan covers and what your out-of-pocket responsibility will be — no surprise bills. For any remaining balance, we offer flexible payment plans and 0 % financing options to help make your care affordable.

How many visits does it take to get a crown or bridge?

Most crown and bridge cases are completed in two visits. During the first appointment, Dr. Deshpande prepares the tooth, takes precise impressions, and places a temporary restoration. Once your custom crown or bridge is ready from the lab, you&#x27;ll return for a final fitting and placement. We work to schedule those visits at times that fit around your work and family commitments.

How long will my crown or bridge actually last?

With proper care, dental crowns and bridges can last 10 to 15 years or longer — and many last even longer than that. Brushing, flossing (especially under a bridge), and keeping up with your twice-yearly checkups at our North Plainfield office all play a big role in extending the life of your restoration. Dr. Deshpande will give you specific at-home care guidance tailored to your situation.

Is a crown always necessary, or are there other options?

A crown is typically recommended when a tooth is too damaged or weakened for a filling to be a reliable long-term solution — such as after a large cavity, a crack, or a root canal. In some cases, a less extensive restoration like a dental bonding treatment or inlay may be an option, and Dr. Deshpande will always walk you through alternatives before recommending a specific course of treatment. Our goal is to help you make an informed decision, not to push unnecessary procedures.
