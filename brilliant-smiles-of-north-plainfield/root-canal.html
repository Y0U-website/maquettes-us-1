Quick Facts

The truth is, modern root canal treatment is designed to relieve pain, not cause it. Dr. Sonal Deshpande begins every procedure by fully numbing the area, so the treatment itself is typically no more uncomfortable than getting a routine filling. The discomfort you feel before calling us — the throbbing, the sensitivity to hot and cold, the ache that wakes you up at night — is what root canal therapy is designed to stop.

The procedure works by removing the infected or damaged pulp inside your tooth (the soft inner tissue that houses nerves and blood vessels), then carefully cleaning, disinfecting, and sealing the canals to prevent reinfection. Your natural tooth stays in place, intact and functional. For most patients, the relief begins within a day or two of treatment.

Dr. Deshpande takes time to explain every step before she begins, so you always know what to expect. There are no surprises, no pressure, and no judgment — no matter how long you&#x27;ve been putting this off.

What happens if I just ignore the infected tooth instead of getting a root canal?

An infected tooth doesn&#x27;t resolve on its own — the infection can spread to surrounding teeth, your jawbone, and in serious cases, beyond your mouth. Addressing it early with root canal therapy allows Dr. Deshpande to save your natural tooth and prevent a situation that would require more extensive and costly treatment down the road. Keeping your natural tooth is almost always the best outcome for your long-term oral health.

How long does a root canal take, and will I need more than one visit?

Many root canals can be completed in a single appointment lasting roughly 60 to 90 minutes, depending on the complexity of the case. Some teeth — particularly molars with multiple canals — may require a second visit to complete. We&#x27;ll give you a clear timeline at your appointment so you can plan accordingly, and we&#x27;ll work around your schedule to minimize disruption to your day.

Does insurance cover root canal treatment?

Root canal therapy is generally covered under most dental insurance plans as a major restorative service, though the exact percentage varies by plan. Before your treatment begins, our team will check your specific benefits so you have a clear picture of your coverage and any expected out-of-pocket costs. If you have a balance remaining, flexible payment plans and 0 % financing are available to help spread the cost.

What&#x27;s the recovery like after a root canal?

Most patients return to their normal routine the same day or the day after treatment. You may experience some mild tenderness or sensitivity for a few days, which over-the-counter pain relievers typically manage well. Dr. Deshpande will walk you through aftercare instructions and what to expect before you leave the office, and we&#x27;re always available if you have questions during your recovery.
