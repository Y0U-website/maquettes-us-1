Extra Support for Your Smile Journey

Some patients need extra support to get the best possible outcome. Our expanded treatment options and services are designed to address unique challenges, enhance results, and keep your smile looking its best for years to come.

Modified Tandem Appliance

For children with crossbites, we use the Modified Tandem Appliance to guide jaw development in a way that feels more comfortable and approachable than traditional options. It’s designed to be easier for kids to adjust to, creating a smoother, more positive experience for the whole family.

Invisalign Expanders

As a modern alternative to traditional expanders, Invisalign Expanders use a refined, low-profile design to support jaw development. This approach improves comfort while effectively creating the space needed for balanced, well-aligned results.

Advanced Technology

Your treatment starts with a clear, detailed plan built using advanced digital tools. By mapping out your smile in advance and using modern techniques throughout care, we’re able to create more precise results while making the entire process smoother and more comfortable.

Teeth Whitening

If you’re looking to brighten your smile after treatment, we offer an at-home whitening solution designed for comfort and results. Using custom-fit trays and prescription gel, you can safely reduce tooth discoloration on your schedule, with visible changes starting within days and continuing to improve over 10–14 days.

Invisalign First

Invisalign First is designed for kids ages 6–10, guiding early smile development with a gentle, comfortable approach. These custom clear aligners create space for incoming teeth and support phase 1 treatment, helping to prevent bigger issues down the road while keeping your child’s routine easy to manage.

Growth Modification Appliances

Class II growth modification appliances guide jaw development while your child is still growing, helping correct bite issues early. Appliances we may use include the: Invisalign Mandibular Advancement, Herbst Appliance, and PowerBar™. These appliances are carefully selected by Dr. Giselle to fit your child’s needs, making early treatment more effective and efficient.
