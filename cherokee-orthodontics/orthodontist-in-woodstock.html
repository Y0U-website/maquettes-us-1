Advanced Support Beyond Traditional Orthodontics

Some smiles require a more customized approach. That’s why we offer additional treatments and supportive services to manage complex cases, refine results, and ensure long-term stability throughout and after your orthodontic journey.

Modified Tandem Appliance

For children with crossbites, we use a Modified Tandem Appliance to help guide proper jaw growth in a more comfortable, kid-friendly way. This approach is designed to feel less overwhelming than traditional methods, making it easier for young patients to adapt while supporting effective results.

Invisalign Expanders

Instead of bulky traditional expanders, Invisalign expanders provide a more subtle, comfortable way to support jaw development. Their low-profile design helps create necessary space while keeping treatment easier to manage day to day.

Advanced Technology

We begin every treatment with a fully customized plan developed through advanced digital technology. By visualizing your smile from the start and applying precise, modern techniques along the way, we’re able to deliver accurate results with a more comfortable, streamlined experience.

Teeth Whitening

After orthodontic treatment, we offer a convenient take-home whitening option to enhance your final results. Using custom-made trays and professional-grade gel, this approach allows you to safely lift stains at your own pace, with noticeable improvements often appearing within just a few days.
