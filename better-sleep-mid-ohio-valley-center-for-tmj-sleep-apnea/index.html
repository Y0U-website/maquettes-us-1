TMJ pain is miserable. Often combined with limited mouth opening and the inability to eat without flaring the pain, we’re here to get you relief as soon as possible!

At Better Sleep MOV, we offer quiet, comfortable, and effective treatment for sleep apnea and snoring, all covered by Medicare and medical insurance.

As the main alternative to CPAP therapy, our oral appliance therapy utilizes custom-fitted appliances that resemble mouthguards. These appliances are designed to reposition a patient’s jaws and tongue, effectively opening up their airways.

To ensure optimal results, we customize both the appliances and treatment plans for each individual patient, taking into consideration the severity and type of sleep apnea conditions they experience.
