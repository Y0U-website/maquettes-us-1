What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. They are surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a solid foundation for a crown, bridge, or even full dentures. Over time, the implant integrates with your bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a secure and permanent bond.

At Carlisle Dental Care, we offer both single and multiple dental implants in Carlisle, PA. Whether you need to replace one tooth or an entire arch, implants are a safe, durable, and natural-looking option. They function like your own teeth and help prevent bone loss, shifting teeth, and changes to your facial structure.
