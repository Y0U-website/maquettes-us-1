What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a clear aligner system designed to gradually straighten your teeth using a series of custom-made, removable trays. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are nearly invisible and fit snugly over your teeth. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set, gently shifting your teeth into proper alignment.

This treatment works well for mild to moderate orthodontic issues, such as crowding, gaps, and bite misalignment. And because the aligners are removable, you can continue to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain your normal oral hygiene routine without any hassle.

Is Invisalign Right for You?

Wondering if Invisalign is a good fit for your smile? Consider these points:

  • You have crooked or misaligned teeth
  • You’re dealing with mild to moderate crowding or spacing
  • You want a straighter smile without wearing traditional metal braces
  • You’re committed to wearing aligners 20–22 hours per day
  • You’re looking for a comfortable and discreet orthodontic option
