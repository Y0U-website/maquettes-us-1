Dr. Demian Woyciehowsky

Dr. Demian Woyciehowsky is a 2012 UNLV School of Dental Medicine graduate. He first attended Arizona State University to pursue engineering, then transferred to the University of Nevada, Reno, to complete his undergraduate degrees. At the University of Nevada, Reno, Dr. Woyciehowsky received both a Bachelor of Arts in History degree and a Bachelor of Science in Biology.

Dr. Woyciehowsky was an Assistant Professor of Restorative Dentistry at the Oregon Health and Science University of Dentistry from 2016 to 2022. He is a proud member of the Washington Academy of General Dentistry . Dr. Woyciehowsky is also a proud member of National Academy of General Dentistry.

He places great value in investing in the development of his skills in dentistry through completing hundreds of continuing education hours. This gives Dr. Woyciehowsky the leading-edge knowledge and expertise to provide our community with top-notch dentistry. He feels that proper communication is of the utmost importance. As such, he prioritizes clear and respectful communication to help you through the decision-making process concerning your oral health.

You can expect to receive effective, safe, patient-centered, timely, efficient, and equitable care with Dr. Woyciehowsky attending to all your dental needs.

Marcela Arroyave

A graduate of the University of Nevada, Reno, Marcela takes pride in her mission to create an uplifting workplace for our staff so they can provide the most caring and comfortable dental care.

Having started dentistry as a file clerk 23 years ago, Marcela s diverse journey took her through various roles before leading her back to the dental field. Her extensive experience ranges from designing multi-million-dollar homes in the Sierra Nevadas to non-profit and employee management roles.

In 2021, Marcela enthusiastically partnered with her husband, Dr. Demian, to provide top-tier dental care to the Camas community. She completed the Dental Assisting Program at the Washington Academy of General Dentistry and, in 2022, seamlessly transitioned to her role as our operations manager.

Her love for building relationships with patients is at the heart of Marcela’s work. She takes great pride in helping them understand their dental benefits and ensuring they understand their treatment. Hearing about their life milestones and ensuring they have the best dental experience makes every day rewarding for Marcela.

Outside work, she enjoys exploring the Pacific Northwest with hiking, beach strolls with her dogs, and indulging in the region s fine wines and cuisines. Marcela is dedicated to maintaining wellness by regularly attending yoga and Pilates. One interesting fact patients might not know is that she was born in Guatemala, and it makes her day to converse with patients in her native Spanish.

Jordyn Witmer

Jordyn started her dental career after graduating from the Clark College Dental Hygiene Program. Her fond memories of childhood dental visits sparked a deep-rooted passion for the profession, which grew stronger as she shadowed various dental professionals.

Today, in her role as hygienist, Jordyn skillfully handles tasks like teeth cleanings, taking x-rays, charting periodontal health and assessing overall oral health. One of the most fulfilling aspects of her role is educating patients about oral hygiene and enabling them to smile confidently. Being a part of the Camas Dentistry family since 2022, she cherishes the close-knit, collaborative atmosphere of the team.

Jordyn loves spending time outdoors, unwinding with her favorite movies or TV shows, spending time with her two cats, and joining board games with her friends on weekends. A fun fact about Jordyn? She loves wearing fun novelty socks and has an extensive collection!
