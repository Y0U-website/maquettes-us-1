Damon Armstrong, DDS v

DENTIST

About

Dr. Armstrong grew up in Blackfoot and graduated from BHS. He earned his BS degree from BYU and his DDS degree from Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland Ohio. Dr. Armstrong has taken post graduate education courses in endodontics, implantology and sedation dentistry. He is a member of the American Dental Association as well as the Idaho Dental Association. Dr. Armstrong enjoys the people he works with in the office and the good people of Blackfoot. When he is not at work he enjoys cycling, water and snow skiing and playing outside with his family.
