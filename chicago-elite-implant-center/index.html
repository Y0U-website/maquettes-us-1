Dr. Naser began his dental journey at Tripoli University, where he completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. In 2010, he relocated to Boston, Massachusetts, to pursue advanced postgraduate training at Boston University (BU), one of the nation’s leading centers for dental education. There, he earned his Postgraduate Certificate in Prosthodontics and a Master’s Degree in Material Science…

After completing his training, Dr. Naser began practicing in 2015, quickly earning recognition for his meticulous approach to full-mouth rehabilitation and aesthetic restorative work. Over the past several years, his professional focus has evolved almost exclusively toward dental implantology an area where his precision, planning, and artistry converge to deliver life-changing results.

During the last four years, Dr. Naser has devoted his practice exclusively to dental implants, completing numerous advanced courses and hands-on training programs in both surgical and prosthetic implant techniques. His continued commitment to education ensures that his patients benefit from the latest innovations and evidence-based approaches in implant dentistry.

With a philosophy grounded in integrity, compassion, and clinical excellence, Dr. Ashraf Naser is passionate about restoring not just smiles, but confidence and quality of life. His calm, patient-centered manner and perfectionist attention to detail make every visit a reassuring experience for those seeking the highest standard of dental care.
