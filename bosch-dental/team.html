0 Our Team Riley Bosch Doctor of Dental Surgery & Owner

Riley has been in private practice serving the Twin Cities metro area, since 2011. He hopes to change the way people experience dentistry with his thoughtful, attentive, and caring approach. Dr. Bosch has helped countless patients move beyond their fears to actually look forward to their visits (seriously)!

Riley is a Minnesota native. He graduated cum laude, with a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry, from the Univeristy of Saint Thomas. While in college, Riley was a captain and academic All-American baseball player. He continued his studies at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry where he graduated with honors and received multiple awards for patient care and dental implants.

Stacy Shearen

Stacy has been providing dental care in Roseville since 2007. She enjoys seeing patients of all ages, and values patient-centered care and honest dentistry with a gentle touch. Dr. Shearen will go above and beyond to make your dental experience positive—she wants you to be comfortable, confident, and healthy!

Stacy is a lifelong Minnesotan. She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of St. Thomas and knew early on that she wanted to pursue a career in dentistry. Stacy was accepted into the early admissions program at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.
