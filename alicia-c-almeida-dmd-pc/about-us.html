Meet the Team at Alicia C. Almeida, DMD PC

Dr. Almeida, along with our entire team, have a simple commitment to provide you with outstanding dentistry in a caring and comfortable environment where you feel welcome and respected. We look forward to getting to know you. Take a moment to learn more about our team by reading about us below.

Alicia Almeida, DMD, PC

Dr. Almeida holds memberships in the American Dental Association, the Connecticut State Dental Association and the Greater Waterbury Dental Society. Dr. Almeida is committed to a lifetime of clinical learning and professional development. She is currently completing the core curriculum of the Dawson Academy. The Dawson Academy is a group of dentists committed to continued learning, professional growth, and providing the best care to their patients.

When she is not at the practice you can find her spending time with her husband & three little boys, exercising, and cooking.

Joy Salvatore

Joy has been with our practice for 25 years. She is a former dental assistant who transitioned into the role of Office Manager five years ago. Joy loves getting to know our patients and feels proud of the quality care that our team provides.

Originally from Watertown, Joy currently resides in Thomaston. Overall wellness is an important aspect of Joy s life. In her free time, Joy enjoys practicing alternative therapy. She is an avid learner, always searching to expand her knowledge. Joy enjoys cooking and trying out new recipes. You might catch her after hours working out at the gym!

Sylwia Carlson

Sylwia has been a dental hygienist since graduating from Fones School of Dental Hygiene in 2014 where she was a devoted member of the Honor Society. She has been a valued member of Dr. Almeida s team for nearly five years. Her favorite part of working as dental hygienist is taking care of her patients and making them feel comfortable!

Sylwia resides in Beacon Falls with her husband, Rob, and her precious cat, Roxy. In her free time, Sylwia enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, bicycling, and taking trips to a family home in Vermont. On the weekends you may see her out on the Farmington Canal Trail or at the nearby Larkin Bridle Trail!

My Hygienist, Sylwia, is gentle and skilled. Highly recommend her and this practice. Dr. Almeida has created an atmosphere that is warm and provides excellent dental care.
