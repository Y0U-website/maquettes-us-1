Slide Slide Slide Slide Single Implants Implants are an exciting advancement in dentistry because they allow for the replacement of lost teeth. Losing a tooth, especially in the front of the mouth, can be a traumatic experience. Implants are the ideal option for a single tooth replacement. Patients have the ability to restore the toothless area with a highly esthetic and extremely functional implant, allowing the patient to look and feel as if the tooth was never lost.
