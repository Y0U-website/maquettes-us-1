The practice

To ensure your child grows up with a healthy smile, we offer comprehensive dental care that ranges from checkups and cleanings to fillings, crowns and advanced pediatric dental procedures, including in-office sedation and general anesthesia .

We welcome parents in the treatment room with an open-door policy . Our jungle-themed office encourages children to experience dentistry in a fun, exciting environment, while parents can be sure their children are treated with the most state-of-the-art techniques, materials, and technologies.
