Meet Dr. Brasher

Dr. Austin Brasher

Dr. Brasher is a native Oklahoman, born and raised in Edmond, OK. He attended Brigham Young University where he walked onto the football team as a kicker before attending The University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Brasher chose to pursue an advanced surgical training residency and was accepted by his first choice, The Foundry Dental Implant Center, outside Birmingham, Alabama.

Dr. Brasher stays at the forefront of technology and procedural techniques to provide his patients with the best possible care. He is involved in a number of communities in which he shares his knowledge and experience with colleagues around the world. Dr. Brasher has also been involved with teaching dental implantology through the Alabama Implant Education program and is a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and is on track to be a diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology; the highest award that can be achieved in implant dentistry.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Brasher loves attending sporting events, playing golf, and spending time with family and friends. He has been married to his wife Baily for nearly 7 years and they have a one-year-old son, named Brooks. Dr. Brasher is also an active member of his church and is fluent in Spanish.
