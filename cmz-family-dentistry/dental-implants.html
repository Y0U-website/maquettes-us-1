Dental Implants

Dental Implants are used to replace a missing tooth or teeth. Dental implants can also be used to help stabilize a partial or complete denture. A dental implant is a high-tech replacement tooth that mimics the whole tooth structure. A titanium “root” is snugly inserted into the bone and is used to support a crown, bridge or denture. It looks, feels and acts like a real tooth and can last a lifetime if properly cared for.
