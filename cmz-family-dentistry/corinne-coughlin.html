Dr. Corinne Coughlin DMD

Dental Surgery, General

Born and raised in Middleboro Dr. Coughlin attended Coyle Cassidy High school before attending Fairfield University. She graduated with a bachelor’s degree in English Writing with minors in Classical Studies and Communications. She was a member of the Swimming and Diving team competing in one and three meter Springboard.

She continued her studies at Tufts University completing a Post Baccalaureate program in pre-medical studies. She then went on to graduate from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2009.

She joined the practice with her father, Dr. Zamaitis, in the summer of 2009. In her spare time she enjoys spending time with her family boating, skiing, hiking and golfing.
