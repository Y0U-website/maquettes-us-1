Meet Dr. Bhumesh Hirapara, DDS

Dr. Bhumesh Hirapara, DDS

("Dr. Boom")

Transforming Smiles with Advanced Implants & Personalized Care

Dr. Bhumesh Hirapara—also known as Dr. Boom—is the founder of BOOM Smiles and a proud local dentist who grew up in Jersey City, New Jersey. His passion for dentistry began early, following a high school internship where he discovered the unique blend of clinical care and business innovation that defines the field.

Today, Dr. Boom provides comprehensive, high-level care with a focus on cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and advanced All-on-X full-arch implant surgeries. As an Invisalign® Silver Provider, he has extensive experience creating confident, beautifully aligned smiles through clear aligner therapy.

Known for his charismatic personality and genuine passion for helping people, Dr. Boom takes a personalized approach to care—tailoring each treatment plan to meet the unique needs and goals of every patient. As a local dentist, he is deeply committed to giving back to the community, building lasting relationships through trust, transparency, and exceptional care.

Outside the office, he enjoys hiking, traveling, spending time with family, and exploring new restaurants throughout New York City.
