Our Dentists

Dr. Cortney Archbold

Dr. Cortney joined our team in 2016 and is a member of the American Dental Association , the Pennsylvania Dental Association and the Erie County Dental Association . She’s committed to continuing her education through lifelong learning and attending dental educational courses. Dr. Cortney strives to make patients comfortable and at ease throughout their dental visits and emphasizes providing them with the highest quality of personalized care.

Dr. Cortney has been in the dental profession for more than a decade and is one of only a handful of dentists providing oral screenings for Head Start programs. She has been a finalist for the Erie’s Choice Awards multiple times for multiple dental categories.
