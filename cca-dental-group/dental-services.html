Other Services

Nitrous Oxide Sedation

For patients with dental anxiety or discomfort during procedures, we offer nitrous oxide sedation to help you relax. This mild sedative is administered as a mixture of oxygen and nitrous oxide, providing a calming effect while keeping you conscious throughout your treatment. Effects wear off quickly, and side effects are rare, ensuring a safe and smooth experience.

Telescopic Loupes

Telescopic loupes are advanced dental microscopes that allow us to diagnose and treat dental issues with precision. This tool helps detect small problems, such as hairline cracks, areas of decay, or early signs of other oral health concerns, before they become serious. It’s a valuable tool for preventive care, minimizing the need for extensive dental procedures in the future.

Intraoral Camera

Our intraoral camera is a small, pen-sized tool that captures digital images of your oral structures. These images allow both you and your dentist to see a detailed view of your teeth and gums, making it easier to understand your treatment needs. It’s a powerful diagnostic and educational tool that ensures transparency and helps improve your oral care routine.

At-Home Whitening Kits

Want a brighter smile in the comfort of your own home? Our professional at-home whitening kits are tailored to meet your specific needs. Unlike over-the-counter products, these dentist-supervised whitening systems use safe and effective formulas to achieve noticeable results. With easy-to-follow instructions and custom-fit trays, you can gradually whiten your teeth at your own pace, restoring confidence in your smile.
