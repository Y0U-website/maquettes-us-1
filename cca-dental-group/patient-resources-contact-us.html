Are over-the-counter teeth whitening products effective?

Bleaching solutions contain peroxide (typically carbamide peroxide) which actually bleaches the tooth enamel. These are usually available in different concentrations which range from 10 % to 22 %.

How often should I have a dental exam and cleaning?

We recommend that most patients schedule a dental exam and cleaning every six months. Regular visits allow us to monitor your oral health, remove plaque and tartar buildup, and detect any potential issues early on. However, some patients may require more frequent visits based on their individual needs.

Do you offer teeth whitening services?

Yes, we provide professional teeth whitening treatments to help brighten your smile. Our team will assess your oral health and discuss the best whitening options for you during a consultation. Teeth whitening can effectively reduce discoloration caused by factors such as smoking, coffee, tea, and aging.

What are dental sealants, and who should get them?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars to prevent tooth decay. They are especially beneficial for children and teenagers, but adults without decay or fillings in their molars can also benefit from sealants. Sealants can last up to 10 years with proper care.

Can you help with teeth grinding or clenching?

Absolutely. We offer custom-made night guards to protect your teeth from the effects of bruxism (teeth grinding) and clenching during sleep. These appliances help prevent tooth wear, jaw pain, and other related issues.

What restorative dental services do you provide?

Our restorative services include dental bonding, crowns, bridges, and implant restorations. Whether you have a chipped tooth, missing teeth, or require a full-mouth restoration, our team is equipped to restore your smile’s function and appearance.
