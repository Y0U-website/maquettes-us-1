Frequently Asked Questions

What types of orthodontic treatments do you offer?

We offer a variety of options, including traditional braces, clear braces, Invisalign, and early orthodontic intervention for children.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

Invisalign is an excellent option for many patients and can be just as effective as braces for mild to moderate alignment issues.

How do I know if I or my child needs orthodontic care?

Common signs include overcrowded teeth, difficulty chewing, or noticeable bite misalignment. A consultation can help determine the best course of action.

Does Arch Orthodontics work with insurance?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans and can help you understand your benefits and out-of-pocket costs.

Is the first consultation at Arch Orthodontics free?
