Meet The Doctor

Carla H. Roher, DMD, FAGD, D. ABDSM

Dr. Roher is passionate about dentistry, excellence in care, and lifelong learning. A Diplomate of the American Board of Sleep Medicine, Dr. Roher now brings even more to her patients as a certified Durable Medical Equipment (DME) Supplier for oral appliance therapy. What she loves most about dentistry is combining the science of dental medicine with artistic precision to help patients achieve optimal oral health.
