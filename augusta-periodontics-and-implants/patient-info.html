Patient Info

While our objective is generally to complete the examination and consultation in one visit, we may schedule a follow-up consultation at a later date for patients with more complex treatment needs.

We are committed to providing comprehensive patient education and adopt a no surprises approach to patient care. We will always communicate with your general dentist through written correspondence and, if necessary, personal phone calls, ensuring that the entire healthcare team remains well-informed about each other’s treatment recommendations.

Please be aware that there is a fee associated with the initial examination and consultation. To streamline your initial visit, kindly assist us by providing the following information:

Please provide the referral slip and x-rays when you come to your appointment.

Please bring a list of your current medications.

If you have dental insurance, please bring any necessary forms or insurance cards to the appointment so we can assist you with any claims.

If you have a medical condition such as an artificial heart valve, a history of infective endocarditis, or a joint replacement that requires antibiotic premedication before dental appointments, please inform our office. Ensure you follow your physician’s instructions regarding premedication before your first visit.
