Meet Our Staff

Fred C. Heros, DDS

Dr. Heros is a member and former president of the Memphis Dental Society and the Tennessee Dental Association. He volunteers for the Church Health Center, and the Bellevue mobile unit. He has been the recipient of many awards including Outstanding Alumni at UT (2012), the Martin Luther King, Jr. Community Service Award (2013), and has been inducted into the International College of Dentists and the Pierre Fauchard Academy. He attends the annual TDA convention and the annual Mid South Dental Congress and has over 1000 continuing education credits in all aspects of dentistry.

In his spare time, he enjoys playing racquetball, spending time with his family at the lake and the beach, and playing with his new puppy Lolita.

Christie serves as office manager for Covington Pike Dental. She has been with us since 1995. She worked in Labor and Delivery as a registered nurse at Baptist Memorial Hospital for Women from 1999-2008. She graduated from Saint Agnes Academy and Baptist College of Health Science with a Bachelor of Science in Nursing and an Associates in Healthcare Administration. She enjoys spending time with her husband Kelly and their two children Steven and Maddie and vacationing at the beach.

Haley joined our team in 2004 as a Registered Dental Assistant. She is now serving as one of our dental hygienists after completing her Associate of Allied Health Science in Dental Hygiene at Concorde in 2013. Her and her husband, Drew, enjoy spending their spare time at the lake with their two fur babies.

Alexis is a registered dental assistant with certifications in dental radiology, coronal polishing, sealant application, and nitrous oxide monitoring. She joined our team in February 2018 after graduating from Concorde Career College in October of 2017. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her fiancé, Trey, on the lake and playing with her "pack" of fur babies.
