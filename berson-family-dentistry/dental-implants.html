Dental Implants in Monticello, NY

Getting dental implants is a step-by-step process, and we’re with you every step of the way. First, we place the implant post into your jawbone. Over the next few months, your bone naturally fuses with it creating a secure anchor. Once everything has healed, we attach a small connector (called an abutment) and then top it off with a beautiful, custom-made crown.

Everyone’s treatment plan is unique, so we’ll tailor the process to fit your specific needs.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many people are! We’ll evaluate your overall health, oral hygiene, and bone density to determine if implants are the best fit for you. In some cases, patients may need a bone graft beforehand to ensure the implant has a strong foundation. We’ll review everything in detail and answer any questions you have.
