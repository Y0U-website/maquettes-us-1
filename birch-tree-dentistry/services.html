Ask About Our Dental Prime Membership Program

Quality dental care should be simple and accessible. Our Dental Prime Membership is an in-house savings plan designed for patients without dental insurance. For one annual fee, members receive routine preventive care at no additional cost—including exams, cleanings, and X-rays—plus exclusive discounts on restorative treatments. No deductibles, no waiting periods, and no insurance restrictions—just straightforward care focused on keeping your smile healthy for life.
