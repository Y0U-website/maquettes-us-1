The practice

“A smile is infectious”. Each day I am utterly grateful for the opportunity I have to help people improve their health and confidence. The best part about my job is to make people happier and strengthen relationships with them in the process. I am excited that you are considering treatment and looking forward to meeting you!

We offer traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and self-ligating braces. We can also provide same-day braces.

Many orthodontic conditions respond well to treatment in early childhood, making orthodontic treatment later, more efficient. We offer two-phase orthodontics, airway orthodontics, habit-breaking appliances, and palatal expanders.

The Herbst appliance helps correct jaw growth discrepancies, preparing patients for further orthodontic treatment.

Patients of all ages benefit from orthodontic treatment. We can help adults who never received treatment as children or have developed new issues.

Also called orthognathic surgery, these procedures correct jaw issues affecting the ability to speak, chew, and bite.

We offer high-tech dental imaging, from detailed digital X-rays to the iTero intraoral scanner. The iTero scanner takes digital impressions, creating an accurate model of the patient’s oral structures. We 3-D print all appliances and models in-house.

These appliances are a must for every orthodontic patient. They maintain tooth alignment after treatment and help to ensure success.
