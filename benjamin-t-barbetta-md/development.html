Research, discovery and innovation

Together, we will go above and beyond to relentlessly innovate the healthcare of tomorrow

  • Build a shared Health Sciences research center with Michigan State University.
  • Create new research funds and endowed positions that will attract and retain top physicians, scientists and educators.
  • Fuel new investigations and treatment innovations addressing multiple sclerosis, breast cancer, heart disease and more.
  • Blend the science of research with the practicalities of implementation, so that our patients are the first to benefit.

Leading-edge clinical education

Together, we will go above and beyond to train - and retain - tomorrow’s top clinicians

  • Grow the doctoral and post-doctoral fellowships and endowed faculty positions that are crucial in recruiting top talent.
  • Enhance educational environment and infrastructure so we can teach students in modern spaces instead of trying to fit high-tech academics into pre-computer-age rooms.
  • In line with our commitment to healthcare equity, prioritize the development of new health professionals from underrepresented communities. Our pathway programs smooth the way to health careers for diverse local candidates who bring new ideas, important viewpoints and heartfelt dedication to our region.

Community health equity

Together, we will go above and beyond to bring better health to all

This campaign will accelerate our commitment to:

  • Building facilities to improve patient care, and ensuring that care will be financially accessible and culturally welcoming.
  • Elevating research efforts that emphasize improving health for diverse communities that are often left behind by medical science.
  • Expanding the education of new clinicians, and developing career pathways for health professionals from underrepresented communities.
