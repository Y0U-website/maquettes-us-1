The practice

We are honored that you are considering us for your care, and we don’t take that responsibility lightly. Our goal is to provide thoughtful, conservative dentistry in a setting where you feel heard, respected, and never rushed.

We check your teeth, gums, oral health, and x-rays when needed with careful attention to detail.

We allow one hour for checkups and cleanings, so we can sit down, listen, and do things the right way.

Drs. Todd and Jennifer Blincoe began their practice in Fort Mitchell over years ago, joining Dr. David Kramer, Jennifer’s father, who began the practice in Fort Mitchell in 1962. They are assisted in their work by an experienced staff of dental hygienists, assistants and office staff members, 90 % of which have been with the Blincoe office more than years. Dr. Todd Blincoe is originally from Louisville, KY, where his father was a general dentist for over 40 years. He graduated from the University Of Louisville School Of Dentistry in 1990 and began practice in Fort Mitchell that summer.

Dr. Todd is an avid history buff, enjoys any type of auto racing and loves to travel. He is an active member of the Northern KY Dental Society, KY Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Jennifer Blincoe, born and raised in Fort Mitchell, graduated from Xavier University in 1986 and the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 1990. She joined her husband Todd, and father, Dr. David Kramer, in the practice upon graduation.

She is an active member of the Northern Ky Dental Society, the Kentucky Dental Society, and the American Dental Association. In her free time, she enjoys being outdoors, reading and traveling.
