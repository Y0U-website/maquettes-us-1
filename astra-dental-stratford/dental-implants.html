If you have missing teeth, Dr. Sran at Astra Dental in Stratford, CT can restore both function and appearance with dental implants. These implants offer a permanent and natural solution, enhancing your smile and improving your ability to chew and speak.

Unlike traditional crowns, bridges, and dentures, dental implants are supported by titanium posts embedded in the jawbone, making them a lasting part of your mouth that mimics the look and feel of natural teeth.

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Dental Implants FAQ

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth can be evaluated for implants. Bone levels, gum health, medical history, and smile goals all matter, which is why the consultation is the right first step.

Do dental implants look natural?

Implant restorations are designed to match your smile and support comfortable chewing. The final result depends on planning, materials, bite balance, and healthy surrounding tissue.

Why do patients choose Dr. Sran for dental implants?

Dr. Harkirat Sran is a graduate of the Simply Implant Institute with advanced surgical training through Colorado Surgical Institute. At Astra Dental, he connects CBCT 3D imaging, computer-guided planning, implant surgery, temporary teeth, and final restoration design with an in-house digital lab.

Does Astra Dental use 3D imaging for implants?

Yes. Astra Dental uses CBCT 3D imaging to evaluate bone, roots, nerves, sinuses, infection, and surgical anatomy. Digital scans, exocad design, and SprintRay printing can also support surgical guides, temporary teeth, and final implant restorations when appropriate.

Will I leave with a tooth while the implant heals?

Many cases can be planned with a temporary tooth or temporary appliance, but the right choice depends on the tooth location, bite, bone, esthetics, and whether immediate loading is healthy.

Are implants always better than saving a tooth?

No. A healthy natural tooth is valuable. Dr. Sran first looks at whether a tooth can be predictably saved before recommending replacement.
