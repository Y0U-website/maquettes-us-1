If you are missing many teeth or are tired of dentures that shift and move, All-on-X treatment is the fixed implant denture conversation. The goal is to replace an entire upper or lower arch with teeth that attach to dental implants instead of resting loosely on the gums.

All-on-X Implants FAQ

Is All-on-X a type of implant denture?

Yes, patients often describe All-on-X as fixed implant dentures or full-arch implant teeth. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-X teeth are attached to dental implants and are designed to stay in place.

Is All-on-X different from snap-on dentures?

Yes. Snap-on dentures are removable and attach to implants for better stability. All-on-X is fixed full-arch treatment. The right choice depends on bone, bite, hygiene, esthetics, cost, and goals.

How do I know if I need full-arch treatment?

A consultation helps determine whether individual implants, an implant bridge, snap-on dentures, traditional dentures, or full-arch fixed implants make the most sense for your mouth and goals.

Is All-on-X completed in one day?

Some patients can receive fixed temporary teeth the day implants are placed, while others are healthier with staged treatment. The consultation determines what is realistic.

Do full-arch implants still need cleaning?

Yes. Implant-supported teeth require home care, professional maintenance, and periodic checks of the implants, bite, and prosthesis.
