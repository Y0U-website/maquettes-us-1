Life Outside The Practice

When she is not helping patients achieve beautiful smiles, Dr. Carlson and her husband, Hal, embrace every adventure they can find together. You will often spot them around town, enjoying the charm of Stillwater with their two beloved standard poodles.

They love to cook, boat, ski, and hike together in the great outdoors. Currently, they are taking on the exciting challenge of extensively remodeling their historic home by doing much of the work themselves.
