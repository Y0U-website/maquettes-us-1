Single Tooth Implants

We can replace a single missing tooth with a custom dental implant and a beautiful porcelain crown. This approach allows us to restore the gap in your smile without relying on or altering the neighboring healthy teeth.

Implant Bridges

If you are missing several teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge provides a secure and highly durable solution. This treatment anchors the restoration directly to dental implants rather than to your natural teeth for superior stability.

Implant Dentures

We provide implant-supported dentures for patients who need a full arch of teeth replaced. This lifelike solution snaps securely onto implants to eliminate slipping and restore your ability to eat and speak with total confidence.

Bone Grafting And Extractions

We offer comprehensive in-house oral surgery services, including gentle extractions and bone grafting. Our team will ensure your jawbone is healthy and strong enough to support a dental implant for long-term success.

Dental Implants FAQs

We understand that choosing dental implants is a big decision, and we want you to feel fully informed every step of the way. At our Stillwater, MN dental office, we’re here to help you feel confident in your options. Here are a few common questions we receive about dental implants and tooth replacement.

Are Dental Implants Right For Me?

Most adults who are missing teeth and have good overall health are excellent candidates for this procedure. We will evaluate your jawbone density using our modern 3D imaging to determine if implants are the right step for your smile.

How Long Does The Implant Process Take?

The timeline for dental implants varies depending on your individual healing process and whether you need foundational treatments like bone grafting. We will provide a clear, step-by-step timeline during your consultation so you know exactly what to expect from start to finish.

Will Dental Implants Feel Like Natural Teeth?

Yes, dental implants are designed to feel very similar to natural teeth once they have fully healed and been restored. Because they are anchored into the jawbone, they offer a secure, stable fit that helps you chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Is Bone Grafting Always Needed Before Dental Implants?

Not always. Some patients already have enough healthy bone to support implants, while others may need bone grafting to strengthen the area before placement. Your 3D imaging consultation will help determine the best approach for your smile.

How Do I Care For Dental Implants?

Dental implants should be cared for much like natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, and keeping up with regular dental visits are essential for protecting your gums, maintaining your restoration, and supporting the long-term success of your implant treatment.
