Founder Dr. Brandon Linn

Award-Winning Orthodontist

Dr. Linn attended Vanderbilt University, where he played soccer and graduated with a B.A. in English. After graduation, Dr. Linn lived in Vail, where he met his beautiful wife, Lisa. He then attended the University of Illinois, where he received his D.D.S. with honors and was selected for membership into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU) National Dental Honor Society. Dr. Linn completed his orthodontic residency and obtained his Certificate in Orthodontics and M.S. degree from Marquette University, whereupon he and Lisa immediately moved back to Colorado.

Awards

Dr. Linn has received many local and national awards in his career as a Castle Pines, CO, orthodontist, and Highlands Ranch, CO Orthodontist.

Dr. Linn has also had several scientific papers published in respected orthodontic journals. He is passionate about children’s causes and the local community and has volunteered as a member of the Kiwanis Club of Castle Pines, where he served on the Board of Directors. Dr. Linn is also the community representative on American Academy’s School Accountability Committee (SAC).

Dr. Linn and his wife, Lisa, have two girls, a boy, and three dogs - two golden doodles and a shih tzu. He is a big sports fan and likes all the Chicago (where he grew up) and Denver sports teams. In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his family, telemark skiing, running, and mountain biking.
