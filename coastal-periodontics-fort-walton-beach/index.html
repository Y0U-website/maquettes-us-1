The practice

After visiting our site, we hope that you will make the decision to visit Dr. Bartruff’s office for any issue that you may have regarding dental implants, cosmetic procedures, gum disease treatment, or any other periodontal concerns. Any questions that arise can be happily answered by contacting our office.

Dr Brent Bartruff is a proud graduate of the University of Florida. He graduated in 1994 with honors with his Bachelor of Science in Zoology. He then completed his DMD in 1998 from the University of Florida College of Dentistry and continued to specialize in Periodontology, graduating in 2001 from Louisiana State University.

Dr. Bartruff is an active member of the ADA (American Dental Society), the AAP (American Academy of Periodontlogy), the FDA (Florida Dental Association), the OWDS (Okaloosa Walton Dental Society) which he has previously held the position of President, and the Fort Walton Beach All Sports Association.
