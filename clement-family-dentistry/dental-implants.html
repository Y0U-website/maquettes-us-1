Dental Implants The Dental Implant Process 1. Initial Consultation Your dental implant journey starts with an initial consultation at Clement Family Dentistry. During this appointment, one of our skilled dentists—Dr. Drew Clement, Dr. Mike Luminais, or Dr. Matthew Clement—will assess your oral health using advanced technology such as intraoral cameras and digital X-rays. We’ll discuss whether dental implants are the best option for you and build a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

2. Implant Placement

The next step involves placing the titanium implant into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort, and our practice utilizes cutting-edge dental technology, including laser dentistry, to enhance precision and healing. After placement, the implant fuses with the jawbone in osseointegration, which usually takes a few months but ensures a stable, secure base for your new tooth.

3. Abutment and Restoration

Once the implant has fully integrated with your jawbone, we’ll attach an abutment—a small connector piece—to it. This abutment will hold the custom-made dental crown that completes your restoration. Crafted to blend seamlessly with natural teeth, the crown restores your smile’s appearance and function.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Proper care is essential to ensure the longevity of your dental implants. The good news is that implants require no special maintenance beyond regular oral hygiene practices. Brush and floss daily, and visit Clement Family Dentistry for regular checkups and cleanings. Avoid habits damaging your implants, such as chewing on hard objects or grinding your teeth. Taking good care of your dental implants can help them last for many years—possibly even a lifetime!
