Meet The Doctors

OUR DOCTOR

Dr. Drew Clement

Dr. Drew Clement has been delivering outstanding dental care for over 32 years. He attended Thibodaux High School and completed his undergraduate studies at Nicholls State University. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from LSU Dental School in 1981 and has additional specialty training in implant dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, endodontics, conscious oral sedation, and orthodontics.

OUR DOCTOR

Dr. Mike Luminais

Dr. Mike Luminais joined Dr. Clement and his staff in May of 2006. Dr. Luminais attended Riverside Academy and obtained a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of New Orleans. He received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from LSU Dental School in 2006.

OUR DOCTOR

Dr. Matthew Clement

Dr. Matthew, Dr. Clement’s son, is a graduate of E.D. White High School. He has obtained degrees from both the University of Louisiana–Monroe and Nicholls State University. Dr. Matthew completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at LSU School of Dentistry in 2019 and continued on to complete a residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the Foundry Dental Center in Bessemer, Alabama. Dr. Matthew is trained in all aspects of general dentistry, specifically dental implant placement and restoration, bone grafting, and dental sedation, including IV sedation.
