Kid Friendly Care

Your Child s First Dental Visit We ve been providing dental care for kids for decades. Did you know your child should visit the dentist by their first birthday? During your child s first dental visit, our team will ensure the appointment is efficient, stress-free, and fun! We take comfortable x-rays and images to assess your child s oral health and provide simple, easy-to-understand explanations to help them form positive associations with dental visits from the start.

Checkups and Preventive Care Clinton Dental Group follows the oral health guidelines established by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentists (AAPD), recommending that children have checkups every six months. If we identify multiple cavities or early orthodontic concerns, we may suggest more frequent visits.

In some cases, we may recommend a gentle tooth extraction for children suffering from decay or crowding. If your teen is experiencing pain in their back molars or at the back of their mouth, they may need to be referred to an oral surgeon for their wisdom teeth removed.

Tooth-Colored Fillings & Dental Crowns Our focus is on prevention, but cavities can happen for various reasons, including decay, injury, or orthodontic issues. For small cavities, we use tooth-colored composite dental fillings. If a large cavity or injury is discovered, we may recommend a full-coverage d ental crown . These tooth-colored restorations are designed to save the tooth and protect it from potential bacteria.

Mouth Guards For Athletics Do you or your child play sports regularly? Whether it’s football or soccer, it s crucial to protect your teeth during physical activities. We create custom mouth guar ds that are not only comfortable but also designed to safeguard your teeth from damage and reduce the risk of concussions. At Clinton Dental, we use digital scanners to efficiently take impressions of your teeth, without the mess of traditional impression materials.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pediatric Dentistry in Clinton, NJ

Q: When should my child have their first dental visit?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends children visit a dentist by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth coming in. Starting early helps establish healthy habits and allows us to catch any concerns before they become problems.

Q: How do you make dental visits comfortable for young children?

Our exam rooms have built-in televisions so kids can watch their favorite shows or movies during treatment. Our whole team takes a calm, patient approach- no rushing, no pressure. Most kids leave looking forward to their next visit.

Q: What dental services do you offer for children?

We provide a full range of children s dental services including routine cleanings, fluoride treatments, dental sealants , tooth-colored fillings, crowns, tooth extractions, and custom sports mouth guards.
