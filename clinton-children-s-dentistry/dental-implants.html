What Our Clinton, NJ Patients Ask About Dental Implants

Q: How much do dental implants cost in Clinton, NJ?

- The cost of dental implants varies depending on how many teeth need to be replaced, whether bone grafting is required beforehand, and what your dental insurance covers. Q: How long do dental implants last?

​- Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution. The titanium post that integrates with your jawbone can last a lifetime with proper care. The crown placed on top of the implant typically lasts 15 to 25 years before it may need to be replaced, depending on wear and oral hygiene habits. No other tooth replacement option comes close to the longevity of a well-maintained dental implant.

Q: Does getting a dental implant hurt?

- The implant placement procedure is performed by a trusted local oral surgeon or periodontist using local anesthesia, so you should feel pressure but not pain during the surgery. Most patients report that the recovery is more comfortable than they expected - similar to a tooth extraction. Once healed, the crown placement at our Clinton, NJ office is completely painless. If you have anxiety about dental procedures, let us know and we will do everything we can to make your experience comfortable.

Q: Why does Clinton Dental Group use digital scanning instead of traditional impressions for implant crowns?

- Traditional putty impressions are uncomfortable, prone to distortion, and can result in crowns that need multiple adjustments before they fit correctly. At Clinton Dental Group, we use digital intraoral scanning technology to capture a highly accurate three-dimensional model of your bite in just a few minutes. The digital file is sent directly to our dental lab, where your implant crown is fabricated to an exact fit reducing the chance of adjustments and meaning your final crown seats naturally and comfortably the first time.

Q: How long does the full dental implant process take?

- From your initial consultation to your final crown placement, the full implant process typically takes six to twelve months. The majority of that time is the healing period after the implant post is placed, during which the titanium fuses with your jawbone- a process called osseointegration. The consultations, digital scanning, and crown placement appointments themselves are relatively quick. We know that is a significant time commitment, and we will keep you informed and comfortable at every stage.

Q: Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

- Most adults in good general health are candidates for dental implants. The key requirements are sufficient jawbone density to support the implant post and healthy gums free from active periodontal disease. Certain health conditions and habits, such as uncontrolled diabetes or smoking, can affect healing and will be discussed during your consultation. If bone loss has occurred, a bone graft may be recommended first. The best way to find out if implants are right for you is to schedule a consultation at our Clinton, NJ office and let us take a look.

Q: What happens if I do nothing about a missing tooth?

- Leaving a gap where a tooth is missing causes more problems than most patients realize. The surrounding teeth will gradually drift into the empty space, changing your bite and potentially leading to jaw pain or difficulty chewing. The jawbone beneath the missing tooth also begins to shrink over time without the stimulation that a tooth root provides - a process called bone resorption. A dental implant is the only tooth replacement option that stops bone loss because the titanium post mimics the function of a natural tooth root.

Q: Do you offer implant-supported dentures at Clinton Dental Group?

- Yes . For patients who are missing multiple teeth or who currently wear traditional removable dentures, implant-retained dentures are an excellent option. Rather than relying on adhesive or suction, these dentures snap securely onto implant posts, eliminating slipping and restoring a much more natural chewing experience. They also help preserve the jawbone that removable dentures allow to deteriorate over time. Ask us about this option during your consultation.
