What Is Surgically Assisted Orthodontics?

Benefits of Surgically Assisted Orthodontics

Procedures Offered

Premolar Extractions

This involves the efficient removal of premolars, often necessary to create space for orthodontic movement. By extracting these teeth, dental professionals can significantly improve dental alignment, ensuring a more balanced and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Extraction of Baby Teeth for Guided Eruption

Strategic removal of primary teeth is crucial for guiding permanent teeth into proper positioning. By carefully timing these extractions, dentists can prevent overcrowding and ensure that adult teeth emerge in an orderly fashion, promoting optimal oral health and function.

Impacted Tooth Exposure

This procedure involves the surgical exposure and alignment of impacted teeth, such as canines, which have not erupted correctly due to obstruction or misalignment. By addressing impacted teeth, complications like cysts or misaligned bites can be avoided, helping to maintain overall oral health and prevent future dental issues.

Achieving a healthy, balanced smile often relies on close collaboration between your orthodontist and a skilled oral and maxillofacial surgeon. When standard braces alone cannot fully resolve severe jaw misalignment, surgical orthodontics—also known as orthognathic surgery or corrective jaw surgery—offers an effective correction for issues affecting the upper jaw, lower jaw, or chin.

During your initial appointment at our office in Port Washington or Mineola, our surgeons will thoroughly evaluate your jaws and create a personalized treatment plan to address functional difficulties with chewing or speaking, as well as improve facial symmetry. We take the time to answer all your questions and outline the entire process, including potential risks such as minor bleeding, temporary numbness, or infection. While procedure times typically last several hours (or just a few hours in certain cases), taking this step allows us to fine tune your bite for optimal results.

Once your treatment is complete, you can look forward to improved function, reduced symptoms, and renewed confidence in your smile. Contact us today to learn how our team can help!

Frequently Asked Questions About Surgically Assisted Orthodontics

What condition can be treated with Surgically Assisted Orthodontics?

Surgically Assisted Orthodontics is an advanced approach that addresses complex dental issues such as severe malocclusions and misalignments. It is particularly useful in cases where traditional orthodontics might be insufficient or when patients desire accelerated orthodontic results. Through this combination of surgical intervention and orthodontic treatment, a wider range of dental conditions can be effectively managed.

Is the procedure painful?

While undergoing any surgical procedure can involve some level of discomfort, we prioritize patient comfort using advanced anesthetic techniques. Post-operative care is designed to minimize pain and facilitate a smooth recovery. Our team is dedicated to ensuring that any discomfort is kept to a minimum and that the healing process is as swift as possible.

How long does the recovery take?

Recovery times can vary based on the individual and the specific procedure performed. Generally, patients can expect a few days of downtime. However, most individuals find they can resume their normal activities relatively quickly, often within a week. We provide detailed aftercare instructions to support a speedy recovery and ensure optimal healing.

Will insurance cover the procedure?

Insurance coverage for Surgically Assisted Orthodontics varies widely, depending on the specifics of your insurance plan. Our experienced team is well-versed in navigating the intricacies of different insurance policies and can assist you in determining your eligibility. We gladly help in processing claims to facilitate financial planning for this treatment.

Can adults benefit from this type of treatment?

Yes, adults can significantly benefit from Surgically Assisted Orthodontics, especially those who require effective and expedited solutions for their orthodontic needs. This treatment is tailored to address the specific challenges faced by adult patients, providing an opportunity for results that might not be achievable through conventional orthodontic methods alone.
