What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials such as titanium. They are surgically inserted into the jawbone and serve as a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth, crowns, or dentures. Dental implants mimic the function and appearance of natural teeth, offering a long-term solution for tooth loss.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants provide numerous advantages, including improved oral functionality, allowing patients to eat and speak with confidence. They also enhance aesthetic appeal, restoring the natural symmetry of your smile. Furthermore, implants contribute to long-term oral health by preserving jawbone density and preventing further tooth loss, making them a reliable solution for those seeking comprehensive dental restoration.

Procedures Offered

Single Tooth Implants:

These implants are perfect for replacing individual missing teeth. A single implant is placed in the jawbone, and a crown is attached, closely mimicking the appearance and function of a natural tooth.

Immediate Dental Implants:

With immediate dental implants, you can experience minimal waiting time. The implants are placed directly after tooth extraction, reducing the lengthy process of traditional implantation.

Full Arch Solutions using 4 or More Implants

This innovative solution supports an entire arch of teeth using only four to six strategically placed implants. It offers a stable and secure alternative to traditional dentures, giving patients the confidence of a full set of teeth with enhanced chewing ability and comfort.

Implant-Supported Dentures:

These dentures are designed to provide more stability and comfort compared to traditional dentures. By being anchored on implants, they reduce slippage and allow you to speak and eat with greater ease, enhancing your overall denture experience.

Zygoma & Pterygoid Implants:

Particularly beneficial for those with minimal bone structure, these implants serve as an alternative to bone grafting. Positioned in the cheekbone (zygomatic) or pterygoid area, they offer a reliable solution for patients who lack sufficient jawbone for conventional implants.

Are You A Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants should have healthy gums and enough bone to support the implant. Non-smokers with good oral hygiene habits are also preferred candidates. If bone loss is an issue, we offer advanced solutions like zygoma and pterygoid implants.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants boast a high success rate, reaching up to 98 % with diligent care and maintenance. Ensuring good oral hygiene and regular dental visits significantly contribute to this success.

How long does the implant process take?

The entire dental implant process can span several months, from initial consultation to final placement, including necessary healing time after surgery. Each stage is crucial for the durability and integration of the implant.

Are dental implants removable like dentures?

No, unlike dentures, dental implants are fixed in place and designed to function like natural teeth. They provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth, eliminating concerns about slipping.

Will my insurance cover dental implants?

Insurance coverage for dental implants varies widely. Our finance team is dedicated to helping you navigate your insurance coverage, ensuring you have a clear understanding of your costs well in advance of your procedure.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the dental implant procedure. Various anesthesia and sedation options are available to enhance comfort and reduce any potential pain during and after the surgery.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants involves practicing routine dental hygiene, such as daily brushing and flossing. Regular dental check-ups are also essential to ensure the health and longevity of the implants.

Can anyone get dental implants?

While most adults are suitable candidates for dental implants, a comprehensive evaluation by our dental team is necessary to determine individual suitability. Factors like bone density and oral health are carefully assessed.
