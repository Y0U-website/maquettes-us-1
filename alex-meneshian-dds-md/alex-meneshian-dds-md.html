ALEX MENESHIAN, DDS, MD

Dr. Alex Meneshian is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon specializing in dental implants, bone reconstruction, corrective jaw surgery (orthognathic surgery), trauma reconstructive surgery, and facial cosmetic surgery. He holds a DDS from McGill University and an MD from SUNY Stony Brook, with extensive training in General and Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery.

Dr. Meneshian’s dedication to his field is reflected in his leadership and active involvement in professional organizations. He is an attending surgeon at several prestigious Long Island hospitals and is the current Chief of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at NYU Langone - Long Island Hospital in Mineola, NY.

Dr. Alex Meneshian practices the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery with an emphasis on dental implants , bone reconstruction , orthognathic surgery , TMJ surgery , and treatment of sleep apnea patients.
