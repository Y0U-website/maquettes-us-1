How Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Work?

Am I a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Any adult that has lost all teeth in one or both arches is potentially a good candidate for the All-on-4 procedure. Since All-on-4 can often eliminate the need for a bone graft, it can be an excellent choice for people who have suffered bone loss in the jaw. We will need to confirm that you’re in good oral health before performing the surgery. If you have gum disease or another condition that could potentially lead to implant failure, it will need to be treated first.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 dentures can be permanently fixed in the mouth so that you can brush and floss them as if they were natural teeth.
  • The implant posts will help your new teeth feel more natural in your mouth.
  • Sturdy implants give your dentures more chewing power so that you can eat whatever you want.
  • The stimulation from implants prevents your jaw from losing bone density.

All-on-4 vs Traditional Dental Implants

Traditional implant dentures may require up to eight posts. In comparison, the All-on-4 procedure is more conservative and less costly. Also, All-on-4 can often be performed without bone grafting, and the implants are often stable enough to support a temporary denture right away; with traditional techniques, you may have to wait a long time after the initial surgery to get new teeth.

How Much Do All-On-4 Dental Implants Cost?

Every smile is unique, so the cost varies. The cost of your treatment depends on numerous different factors. This includes which preliminary procedures you require, like bone grafts or gum disease therapy, and the number of arches you are getting. During your consultation with us, we will discuss all fees associated with your treatment so you know exactly what you can expect. While All-On-4 is an investment, patients find that the price is worth it for their improved quality of life.

Is the All-On-4 Dental Implant Surgery Painful?

The procedure itself is not painful. Your mouth will be numbed with a local anesthetic after the procedure begins, and there are sedation options available to help you feel perfectly comfortable. For a few days after your surgery, you will likely experience some discomfort, but this can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers or prescribed pain medication. Cold compresses can also help. By closely following your aftercare instructions, you will be as good as new in no time!

How Long Does It Take to Get All-On-4 Dental Implants?

It can take anywhere from four to six months or longer to complete the All-On-4 dental implant process from start to finish. This will vary depending on if you need additional dental work before you can get implants. For the procedure itself, it will usually take a few hours to complete an arch of All-On-4 dental implants. However, the healing process will take several months to complete depending on your body’s healing rate.

How Do You Clean All-On-4 Dental Implants?

You should choose a soft-bristled toothbrush and brush your replacement teeth the same way you would brush your natural teeth. Do this twice a day. Don’t forget to brush the soft tissues around your prosthetic to prevent additional buildup. You may want to consider using a water pick to help remove food debris. An antibacterial mouthwash can also help to keep your smile extra fresh!
