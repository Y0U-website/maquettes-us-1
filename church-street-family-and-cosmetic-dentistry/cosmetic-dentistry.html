Bonding & White Filling

Bonding is a popular method to enhance the aesthetics of your smile. Bonding can be used to correct cracks or gaps in teeth, as a filling after a cavity has been removed, or to cover up stains or discolored teeth.

Bonding is an obvious improvement over unsightly silver amalgam fillings. With the advancements in dental technology, bonding usually lasts for over 10 years. It is a safe, affordable, and attractive solution for many dental problems.

First, we carefully select a composite resin shade that mirrors your natural tooth color. Next, we apply a gentle conditioning gel to lightly etch the surface, securing a strong bond.

The tooth-colored resin is then applied directly to the tooth, where our team hand-sculpts the material to match the shape and contour of your surrounding teeth. Once the ideal shape is achieved, a curing light hardens the resin instantly. The tooth is then polished to a natural sheen, completely correcting minor flaws through conservative tooth bonding in a single visit using the same advanced dental technology in Mount Laurel that we rely on for our restorative work.

A composite resin is molded and sculpted over an adhesive gel placed on the tooth. An ultraviolet light then hardens the resin, which is polished to give you a fresh, new smile.

Invisalign®

Invisalign’s® invisible, removable, and comfortable aligners will give you the beautiful straight teeth you’ve always wanted. And best of all, no one can tell you’re wearing them.

Invisalign® is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, custom-made, removable aligners to gradually straighten your teeth. Unlike traditional braces, there are no wires or brackets — just smooth, comfortable plastic that fits snugly over your teeth.

You’ll wear each set of aligners for about two weeks, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. As you replace each aligner with the next in the series, your teeth will move little by little — week by week — until they reach the final prescribed position.

We take a digital scan or impression of your teeth which is used to create a precise 3D model. From this model, your series of custom aligners are manufactured using smooth, BPA-free plastic and shipped directly to our office for your treatment to begin.

Choosing the Right Cosmetic Treatment

When dealing with a chipped front tooth, a stubborn gap, or a single discolored tooth, many patients assume the only solution involves porcelain veneers and multiple appointments.

  • Dental Bonding: Best for isolated issues like a single chip or a small gap. It can be completed in just one session without permanently altering the natural tooth underneath.
  • Veneers: Better suited for a dramatic, full-smile overhaul where maximum stain resistance and long-term durability are the primary goals.
  • Invisalign®: Reserved for patients looking to correct structural alignment issues, crowding, or bite gaps over time rather than hiding minor aesthetic flaws with immediate surface modifications.
  • Whitening : Our teeth whitening in Mount Laurel can remove tobacco stains, yellowing and more discoloration for a glistening smile.
