Post-Treatment Care for Acceleration Patients

After completing the accelerated orthodontics treatment, ensuring the longevity and stability of your new smile is crucial. Key aspects of post-treatment care include:

  • Retainers: It’s important to follow Dr. Santana’s instructions when wearing your retainers. This will prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original positions. Initially, we may instruct you to wear them full-time, transitioning to night-only wear after a certain period.
  • Regular Check-ups: It’s important to visit our skilled orthodontist regularly. This will help ensure that your teeth are staying in place. This will also help us check for any dental issues such as tooth decay, crooked teeth, overbite, and underbite.
  • Oral Hygiene: Maintaining impeccable oral hygiene is crucial. Brushing twice daily and flossing daily can help avoid potential oral health issues for your teeth and jaws.
  • Diet and Lifestyle: Continue to be mindful of consuming hard or sticky foods that could potentially damage your teeth or retainers. Also, a healthy diet contributes to your orthodontic results and overall well-being and longevity.
  • Immediate Address of Concerns: If you have any discomfort or notice issues with your retainers or teeth alignment, please let Dr. Santana know right away. This will enable timely interventions to occur.
