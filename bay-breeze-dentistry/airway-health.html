Sleep Apnea & Snoring

Getting a good night’s sleep is essential to your physical and mental health! If you are waking up tired or have trouble sleeping, you could be suffering from sleep apnea.

In simple terms, sleep apnea is a condition where you stop breathing due to your throat collapsing during the night. It is a serious problem that restricts the flow of oxygen through your nose or mouth, and it could lead to other serious health problems.
