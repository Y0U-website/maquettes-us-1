The practice

At Brooklyn Heights Pediatric Dentistry, we’re committed to helping children build healthy, happy smiles that last a lifetime. Our practice, located in Brooklyn Heights, focuses on preventive care, using modern technology to provide gentle, comprehensive treatments in a clean, calm, and efficient environment. With a focus on preventive care, we offer a complete range of pediatric dental services, including:

Preventive Cleanings & Exams: Encouraging proactive care to prevent decay and maintain healthy teeth.

Airway Focused Dentistry: Supporting healthy breathing and improving sleep quality.

Gentle Extractions & Fillings: Using the latest technology to ensure efficient and comfortable care for even the most sensitive patients.

Orthodontics & Braces: Setting the foundation for beautifully aligned teeth and strong oral health.

Our practice is designed to be a warm and welcoming space where kids can feel comfortable and even excited about caring for their teeth. Every member of our team, from Dr. Janice Lau to our friendly staff, is dedicated to creating an enjoyable experience that helps kids feel confident about their dental visits.
