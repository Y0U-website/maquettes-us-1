Biography

Dr. Noopur Shah understands that procedures like root canals, extractions, and implants can be as daunting as they sound. She’s dedicated to collaborating with you to develop the most appropriate treatment plan, ensuring your comfort, and keeping you well-informed at every stage. With a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from NPDCH Institute of Dental Sciences in India and a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Alabama at Birmingham, Dr. Noopur Shah brings both expertise and a gentle touch to help make your dental experience as smooth as possible.

Beyond her professional commitments, Dr. Noopur Shah enjoys spending quality time with her family and friends. Travel enthusiast, she’s always on the lookout for new cultures to explore and new cuisines to try and consider research for her next culinary adventure!
