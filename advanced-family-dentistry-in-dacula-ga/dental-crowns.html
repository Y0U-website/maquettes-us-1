What Are Dental Crowns?

At Advanced+ Family Dentistry, we understand how important a healthy, beautiful smile is to you. Dental crowns are a versatile restorative solution designed to protect damaged teeth, improve their functionality, and enhance your overall appearance. Whether you’re dealing with a chipped tooth, decay, or simply seeking to strengthen your smile, our expert team is here to help.

Dental crowns, often referred to as “caps,” are custom-made coverings that encase damaged or weakened teeth. Made from a variety of materials including porcelain, metal, or a combination of both, crowns are designed to restore the shape, size, strength, and appearance of your tooth.

What to Expect from Our Dental Crown Services

Your journey begins with a thorough examination and consultation. Our skilled dentists will evaluate your needs and discuss the best options for you.

If a crown is necessary, we will take precise impressions of your teeth to create a custom crown tailored to your unique smile.

While your permanent crown is being fabricated, we will place a temporary crown to protect your tooth and maintain your smile.

Once your custom crown is ready, we will securely bond it to your tooth, ensuring a perfect fit and comfortable feel.
