What Are Dental Implants?

At Advanced+ Family Dentistry, we understand that a beautiful smile can transform lives. Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent solution that looks, feels, and functions like your natural teeth.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to serve as a strong foundation for replacement teeth. They provide a reliable and durable option for individuals seeking to restore their smile and improve their oral health.
