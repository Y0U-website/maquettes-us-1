Orthodontics for Children

If your child needs early intervention to improve dental problems, beginning orthodontic treatment in childhood can be beneficial. Early intervention can correct dental issues before they worsen, reducing the need for more extensive treatments later. It can also guide the growth of the jaw and permanent teeth, ensuring proper alignment and spacing. Additionally, addressing dental issues early can improve oral hygiene, boost self-esteem, and prevent potential speech or eating difficulties. Early treatment paves the way for a healthier, more confident smile as your child grows.

Young people today have more options for smile and bite correction than children of the past. Technological advances have reduced treatment times and made wearing braces more comfortable and discreet.

Taking Care of Your Teeth While Wearing Braces

Regardless of the type of orthodontic method used, you must take good care of your teeth throughout the course of treatment. Food debris and plaque can easily build up around braces. At the same time, brushing and flossing are more difficult because of the wires, brackets, and small spaces between them.

To maintain your oral health while wearing braces, follow these guidelines:

  • After every meal or snack, brush your teeth with fluoride toothpaste
  • Brush with fluoride toothpaste before bedtime
  • Floss at least once daily
  • Follow your dentist’s guidelines for flossing around your braces
  • Continue seeing the dentist for regular cleanings and checkups
  • Use a prescription mouth rinse to prevent decalcification
