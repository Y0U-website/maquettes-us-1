Dr. Brown is originally from the Roswell area, so after he completed dental school, he knew he wanted to go back home to practice.

Dr. Brown love helping his patients smile! Especially since those are the smiles he’ll be seeing around town in the grocery store, on the ball field, and as fellow community volunteers.

It was his dream to create a different kind of dental office , one that felt more like an extension of home than a cold medical office that tends to make even the most collected person break into a sweat.

Both doctors have wide-ranging degrees and specialized trainings , but what they’re most proud of are the hundreds of positive customer reviews they’ve collected over the years. They’re eager to change the way you feel about going to the dentist, too.
