Dental Health and Nutrition

  • Phosphorus : Found in eggs, fish, lean meat, dairy, nuts, and beans, phosphorus is key in teaming up with calcium to help build strong teeth and bones. ‍
  • Vitamin C : This vitamin is crucial for gum health, helping to heal mouth wounds and ward off other ailments. Citrus fruits, tomatoes, peppers, broccoli, and spinach are excellent sources of vitamin C.

Beneficial Food for Dental Health

01 Fortified Plant Milk For those who cannot or choose not to consume dairy, fortified plant milk such as soy milk, almond milk, and coconut milk can also provide a good amount of calcium and vitamin D.

Dark leafy greens like spinach, kale, and collard greens are rich in both calcium and phosphorus. They also contain other vitamins and minerals that contribute to overall oral health.

Certain types of fish like sardines and canned salmon with bones are great sources of calcium.
