The practice

Cleanings & Exams Regular cleanings and check-ups are key to good dental care. During these visits, we monitor your child&#x27;s oral health, catching any issues early and keeping their smile healthy.

Special Needs Patients At Buda Pediatric Dentistry, we establish a nurturing atmosphere tailored to each child&#x27;s specific needs, guaranteeing they receive essential care in a comfortable environment.

Fluoride Treatments Fluoride is a mineral that strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent tooth decay! Our fluoride treatments are a simple and effective way to provide your child&#x27;s teeth with an extra shield, keeping cavities away.

Frenectomies Our office is proud to release tethered oral tissues (e.g. tongue ties and lip ties) with a state of the art CO2 laser that is exceptionally precise, minimizes discomfort, and reduces recovery time.
