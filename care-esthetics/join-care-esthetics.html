Meet the Team

CARE Esthetics providers come from a wide range of medical backgrounds by design. Our team includes experienced clinical providers, clinical researchers based at various universities who have helped develop technologies used in practice, plastic surgeons, and technology centers that treat patients using new techniques in research-based facilities (with informed consent, of course).

Drawing on this combined clinical experience, we collaborate to advance the field of facial esthetics with a consistent focus on patient safety and personalized, natural-looking results. We remain committed to minimally invasive, physician-directed approaches and to educating fellow medical professionals so we can continue to advance facial esthetics together.

EXPERIENCED CLINICIANS IN ESTHETIC MEDICINE

The Center for Advanced Rejuvenation and Esthetics® (CARE) focuses on personalized, minimally invasive approaches to facial esthetics. Guided by experienced international clinicians and researchers in platelet concentrates (PRP/PRF) and laser therapy, CARE Esthetics® providers deliver individualized treatment plans designed to support natural-looking results.

OUR PHILOSOPHY: NATURAL BEAUTY

We take a personalized approach to the appearance of facial aging, using minimally invasive laser and PRP/PRF-based treatments as part of customized plans designed to support a refreshed, natural-looking appearance.
