What Is the Difference between PRF and PRP Treatment? Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) is the first-generation platelet concentrate, sometimes called the “vampire facial.” It is prepared by processing a blood sample in a centrifuge and typically uses anti-clotting additives. Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is a more concentrated successor to PRP, prepared without chemical additives; because no additives are used, it forms a firmer clot. Ask your provider to explain the differences during your consultation.

What to Expect from a PRF Treatment Here’s what to expect when you visit a CARE Esthetics clinic for PRF Treatment:

  • Blood Extraction First, we will draw a small amount of your blood through a simple, generally well-tolerated procedure.
  • Blood Centrifugation Your blood will be processed in a centrifuge and spun slowly to produce PRF, ensuring that the blood will not separate as much as PRP.
  • PRF Collection & Injection The PRF will be collected from the vial through a syringe and injected into the treatment areas.
