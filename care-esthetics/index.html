The practice

We listen to your individual goals and fully address your esthetic questions. You will personally meet with our doctor to review and establish a customized plan for your skin care and facial esthetics.

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood and used during facial esthetic treatments. PRF is a more concentrated successor to Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP), the first-generation platelet concentrate sometimes called the vampire facial.

Ask your provider whether PRF is appropriate for your goals during a consultation.

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) is the first-generation platelet concentrate, sometimes called the vampire facial. It is prepared by processing a blood sample in a centrifuge and typically uses anti-clotting additives.

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is a more concentrated successor to PRP, prepared without chemical additives; because no additives are used, it forms a firmer clot. Ask your provider to explain the differences during your consultation.

CARE Esthetics offers minimally invasive facial esthetic treatments including DEKA laser therapy, microneedling, PRF and PRP, BOTOX® Cosmetic, and dermal fillers, each delivered through a personalized, physician-directed plan. Individual results vary.
