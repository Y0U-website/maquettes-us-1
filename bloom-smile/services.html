Frequently Asked Questions

Bloom Smile is not insurance. It offers straightforward membership savings without deductibles, waiting periods, or traditional insurance claims.

Bloom Smile may help fill gaps for those without routine dental coverage, including many Medicare beneficiaries. It cannot be combined with insurance for the same service unless permitted by the participating provider.

You can enroll online in minutes and begin using your membership with participating dental providers that accept the Bloom Smile plan.

Bloom Smile benefits are available through participating providers. Availability may vary by location, so please contact Bloom Smile for current provider information.

A dental membership plan is an alternative to traditional insurance. Members pay a recurring fee to receive included preventive care and discounts on eligible dental services.

Members can enjoy simpler pricing, preventive care benefits, and discounts on eligible services without the complexity of deductibles, annual maximums, or insurance claim approvals.

Membership can be paid securely online using an accepted payment method. Monthly and annual payment options may be available depending on the selected plan.

Your membership typically becomes active after enrollment and successful payment, unless otherwise stated in your selected plan terms.

After enrollment, you may schedule care with a participating dental provider and use your membership benefits according to your plan terms.

Your membership discount may apply to eligible services that are not included as preventive benefits. The final treatment cost is determined by the participating provider.
