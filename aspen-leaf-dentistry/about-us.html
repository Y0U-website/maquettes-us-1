Meet Us

Dr. Steven Frederick is thankful to the residents of Windsor and the surrounding communities for supporting his vision to provide quality oral health care since July 2000. He was educated at St. Olaf College in Northfield, Minnesota where he graduated with a B.A. in 1995.

His dental school education was completed at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine earning his D.M.D. in 1999. From there, his pursuits took him to Wisconsin before finally establishing Aspen Leaf Dentistry, P.C. in July 2000.

Passionate about short-term mission trips, Dr. Frederick has served communities in the Dominican Republic, Honduras, Peru, Equatorial Guinea, and Malawi. Outside of the office, Dr. Frederick loves spending time with his wife and two sons being active in the Colorado beauty. He also plays an active role in his church community.

Rosie has been a part of Aspen Leaf Dentistry, P.C. since 2012, serving as both a chairside assistant and patient coordinator. Outside of work, she loves spending time with her husband and two kids. Rosie’s warm personality and commitment to our patients truly make her an essential member of our team!
