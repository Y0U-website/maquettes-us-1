The Lasting Benefits of Full Mouth Dental Implants

Considered the “gold standard” solution for missing teeth, dental implants can make your life easier and your smile bigger, whether you’re missing one tooth or all of them! These permanent tooth replacements are made of three parts: a series of four titanium or zirconium posts that are implanted directly into your jaw, abutments that attach to the posts and provide a surface for the restorations to attach to, and the actual toothlike restorations, which are customized based on your existing teeth.

The Process of Getting Full Mouth Dental Implants

Getting dental implants always begins with a consultation. At this appointment, we’ll take X-rays and a CT scan of your mouth and jaw. You’ll have the chance to talk with Dr. Basar about your goals and desires. This helps him tailor your treatment to your needs.

On the day of your surgery, Dr. Basar will extract any remaining teeth you have, as well as performing any necessary jaw grafts. You’ll walk away that day with a temporary set of teeth. Four to six months later, you’ll return to the dental office for your final restorations, which will attach securely to your mouth. With proper care, these restorations can last the rest of your life!

What Dental Implant Options Do I Have?

Even if you decide that full mouth dental implants aren’t for you, you may qualify for implant-supported dentures. These fully removable dentures attach to a series of posts that are implanted in your jaw, much like with traditional implants. This allows you the benefits of removable dentures (such as ease of cleaning) with improved biting power and less risk of jawbone recession. In nearly all cases, implant supported dentures are superior to traditional dentures, so you should definitely consider them as an option for teeth replacement.
