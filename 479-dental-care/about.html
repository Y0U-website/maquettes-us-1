ABOUT DR. GRANT

Dr. Grant Oserowsky grew up in Joplin, Missouri. He attended college at the University of Arkansas, where he was on the Dean’s List, and he and graduated cum laude from Missouri Southern State University with a Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration while majoring in human resources and business management. He then attended the University of Missouri-Kansas City where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. While attending dental school, Dr. Grant co-founded the UMKC Dental Fishing Team and enjoyed volunteering at the Missouri Mission of Mercy dental clinics.

Dr. Grant is married to his high school sweetheart Sara, and they have one daughter and two dogs. They decided to move back to growing Northwest Arkansas to work, play, and raise their family. Dr. Grant previously worked at Community Clinic of Northwest Arkansas where he loved being able to help the underserved population in the area. He especially enjoyed getting to spend half of his time there treating children. Opening his own practice has always been a dream of Dr. Grant’s, and he cannot wait to welcome you and your family to 479 Dental Care!

When he’s not working, you can find Dr. Grant spending time with his family and friends. He enjoys spending time in nature, hiking, boating, swimming, exploring our beautiful area, skiing, and volunteering in the community.

479 Dental Care is 100 % owned and operated by our amazing dentist Dr. Grant.
