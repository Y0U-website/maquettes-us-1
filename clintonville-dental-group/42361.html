Dr. Kevin Albert

Dentist

Dr. Kevin Albert, a native Ohioan from Toledo, has called Columbus home since 2014, when he took ownership of Clintonville Dental Group. After earning a Bachelor of Science in Food Science and Human Nutrition from the University of Florida, Dr. Albert completed his dental training at Nova Southeastern University in 2011. Returning to Ohio felt like coming home, and he has been dedicated to serving the Clintonville community ever since.

Dr. Albert understands the importance of a confident smile and works closely with patients to develop personalized plans for achieving their best oral health. He encourages open communication, ensuring all questions are answered, and every patient feels fully comfortable with their treatment options. With a commitment to continuing education, Dr. Albert stays current on advancements in dentistry to provide the best possible care.

Outside the office, Dr. Albert enjoys live music, exploring new cuisines, and cheering on his favorite football teams. As a devoted husband and father, he treasures time with his wife, Dara, and their two children, Judah and Mina.
