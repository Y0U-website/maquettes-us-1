I consider it a true honor and privilege to provide orthodontic care to the Somerset and Columbia communities. I am very passionate about what I do and really enjoy seeing the positive effect that I have on the lives of those I serve. I love being able to provide this service and having seen so many individuals who are embarrassed of their smile gain confidence after treatment. I have no doubts about the incredible difference we can make on someone’s life just by helping them achieve the smile they deserve. Dr. Ryan N. Brown

From the moment you meet Dr. Brown, you will see and feel the passion he has for modern orthodontics, for people, and for creating and maintaining beautiful smiles.

Dr. Brown earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Harding University, from which he graduated magna cum laude . He then proceeded to Indiana University School of Dentistry, where he graduated valedictorian with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. After practicing dentistry for a while, he then went on to specialize in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, earning his Master of Science in dentistry and orthodontic specialty certificate. Dr. Brown also went on to complete the prestigious Richard P. McLaughlin 2 year Postgraduate Orthodontic Course.

Knowing the importance of steadily extending his knowledge and understanding of emerging orthodontic technologies and processes, Dr. Brown seeks out continuing education offerings throughout the year through the American Association of Orthodontists, Invisalign , and the Iowa Surgical Orthodontic Conference. This enables him to stay current on updates to techniques, materials, and diagnostic tools in order to provide his patients with the best, most effective orthodontic treatment possible.
