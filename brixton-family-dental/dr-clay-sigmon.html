The Professional Side

Dr. Clay Sigmon, DDS, is a highly skilled dental professional dedicated to providing top-quality care. He earned his bachelor’s degree from the University of Oklahoma before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry . With 16 years of experience, Dr. Sigmon continuously seeks innovative ways to enhance his patients’ dental health and overall experience. His commitment to excellence and patient care makes him a trusted provider in the community.

Outside the Dental Office

Beyond his role as a dentist, Dr. Clay Sigmon, DDS, values family time and personal interests. He enjoys creating lasting memories with his two daughters, playing golf, and spending quality time with friends and loved ones. His passion for dentistry extends beyond the office, as he believes in fostering strong patient relationships built on trust and comfort.
