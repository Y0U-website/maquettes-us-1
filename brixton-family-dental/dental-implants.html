Dental Implants

  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure, thanks to anesthesia and sedation options. Post-surgical soreness is manageable with prescribed or over-the-counter pain relief.
  • Do dental implants require special cleaning? Dental implants should be cared for like natural teeth, with regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups. Maintaining good oral hygiene ensures the longevity of your implants.
