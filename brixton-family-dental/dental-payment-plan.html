Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment do you accept? We accept cash, checks, major credit cards, ApplePay, and GooglePay.
  • When can I start using the dental savings plan? Your plan is effective as soon as you sign up.
  • How do I schedule my preventative care. Use our online scheduling tool or call us to schedule an appointment. Schedule an Appointment
  • How can I use my membership discount for treatment? It’s easy! Just schedule an appointment with us, and let us know you’re a member. We’ll make sure your membership is current so you get instant savings.
  • Can I add a member to my account? Yes. Your whole family can join.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Our dental savings plan is for Brixton Family Dental only, so you cannot transfer it to another dentist.
