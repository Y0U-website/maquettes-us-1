Complete Dental Care, All in One Place

At Acme Dental Health Care, we offer a full range of general, restorative, surgical, cosmetic, and dental implant services to help you maintain a healthy, confident smile. Whether you’re here for routine care or a more advanced procedure, our experienced team is here to make sure you feel informed, comfortable, and cared for every step of the way.

How We Help You Smile

Dental Cleanings

Routine cleanings help keep your teeth and gums healthy and allow us to catch issues like cavities early.

White / Composite Fillings

Tooth-colored restorations used to repair cavities or minor damage, blending naturally with your existing teeth. Made from a durable resin material, they bond directly to the tooth structure and help restore strength, function, and appearance.

Crown & Bridge Restorations

Crowns and bridges are used to restore or replace damaged and missing teeth. A crown is a custom-made cap that covers a broken or weakened tooth, while a bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth using neighboring teeth for support. Both help restore your bite, protect your teeth, and improve your smile’s appearance.

Gum Disease Treatment

We offer treatment for gum disease to help prevent tooth loss, infection, and long-term complications.

Tooth Extractions / Bone Grafting

The removal of problematic teeth can be done here in-office surgically. A bone graft may be placed in the socket afterwards to preserve bone volume and support future dental work, such as implants.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for missing teeth, offering a natural look and feel. Our in-office implant procedures performed by Dr. Erik Burke are precise, efficient, and designed to restore both function and confidence in your smile.

Root Canal Therapy

When infection causes pain, a root canal can relieve discomfort and preserve your natural tooth.

Dentures

Whether you need partial or full dentures, we provide comfortable, functional solutions tailored to your needs.

IV Sedation

IV sedation provides a deeper level of relaxation for patients undergoing more complex procedures or dealing with dental anxiety. This safe and controlled option allows for a more comfortable experience during treatment.

Kid-Friendly Dentistry

We’re here to help children feel safe, comfortable, and even have fun at the dentist. Gentle cleanings, cavity prevention, and education to help kids build healthy habits for life. The goal is to keep little smiles happy, healthy, and growing strong!

Veneers

Custom veneers improve the appearance of chipped, uneven, or spaced teeth, giving you a more confident smile.

SureSmile / Invisalign

Clear aligner therapy, such as Invisalign and SureSmile, uses a series of custom-made, removable trays to gradually straighten teeth without metal brackets or wires. Straighter teeth are easier to clean, which helps reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease. A more confident, attractive smile is a great bonus.

We’re happy to help you explore your options. Schedule a consultation to learn more about what’s right for your smile.
