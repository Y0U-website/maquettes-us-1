Appointments and Cancellations

Our services are by appointment only. We strive to see all patients on time we ask that you make every effort not to change a scheduled appointment. Cancellations must be received 24 hours prior to an appointment. Appointments canceled with less than this notice and any missed appointments will be subject to a $100 fee.

Billing and Payments

Full payment is due at the time of treatment. Cash, checks, and credit cards are accepted. An estimate of fees and estimated insurance co-payments are available when scheduling. Please call if you have questions or if you need assistance or clarification of our policies.
