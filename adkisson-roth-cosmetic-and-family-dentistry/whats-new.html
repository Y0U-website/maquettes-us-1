Introducing Dr. Roth

We’ve started to announce the start of an exciting new chapter! Dr. Natalie Simpson Roth — daughter of Dr. Joe Simpson — has returned home to Lenoir City and joined our practice to care for the community that raised her.

After nearly five decades of dedicated service, Dr. Simpson has stepped into a well-earned retirement. He’s passed the torch to Dr. Roth to work alongside his longtime partner Dr. Scott Adkisson.

Coming Home

Born and raised in Lenoir City, Dr. Roth earned her dental degree from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center and brings over 12 years of clinical experience home with her. She grew up around this practice — and is honored to continue her work here.
