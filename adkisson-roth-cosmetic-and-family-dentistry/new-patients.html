Everything you need for your first visit

Welcome to Adkisson & Roth. We’re glad you’re here, and our aim is to make getting started as easy as possible.

Before Your Visit

When you schedule your first appointment with our Lenoir City office, we’ll send everything you need to complete your new patient paperwork — quickly and conveniently from your phone or computer. Have questions before you arrive? Call us anytime at (865) 986-6566.

What to Expect

At your first appointment, we’ll review your dental and medical history, perform a comprehensive exam and take any necessary digital X-rays. We’ll discuss your goals, answer your questions and work with you to create a care plan that fits your needs and budget. Most importantly, we’ll make sure you feel comfortable and informed every step of the way.
