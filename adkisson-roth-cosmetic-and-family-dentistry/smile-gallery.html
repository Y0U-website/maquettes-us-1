Veneers

This patient was born with teeth that were small and unevenly shaped. After an orthodontist corrected the spacing, we placed four custom veneers — sized, shaped and color-matched to fit her perfectly. No shot required, and a beautiful, natural smile to show for it.

Drag the handle to compare Implants Can you spot the implant? A dental implant replaces a missing or unrestorable tooth with one that looks, feels and functions like the real thing. Uneven Smiles

Sometimes a smile only looks uneven. This patient — a hygienist referred by her own dentist — had teeth that were more even than they appeared; the way her lips moved created the illusion. We reshaped her smile to match her natural movement, giving her an even, confident result she loves.

Drag the handle to compare Restorations Years earlier, this patient had veneers and crowns placed elsewhere to repair damage to her front teeth — but she was never happy with how they looked. We replaced them with lifelike restorations for a more natural, beautiful smile.
