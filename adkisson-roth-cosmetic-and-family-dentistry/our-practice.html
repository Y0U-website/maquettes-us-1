Our Legacy of Care

Adkisson & Roth, formerly Simpson & Adkisson, was founded by Dr. Joe Simpson in 1979. He recently stepped into a well-earned retirement, passing the torch to his daughter, Dr. Natalie Simpson Roth, in July 2026.

Born and raised in Lenoir City, Dr. Roth graduated from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center and brings more than 12 years of clinical experience and a passion for patient-first care.

Dr. Scott Adkisson joined the practice in 2001. A Coalfield native who paid his way through school as a fly-fishing guide in the East Tennessee mountains, Dr. Adkisson graduated with honors from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center. For 25 years and counting, he has practiced with a philosophy of personalized, relationship-based care.
