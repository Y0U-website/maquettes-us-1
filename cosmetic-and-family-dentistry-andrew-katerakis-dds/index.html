The practice

Welcome to the dental office of Andrew Katerakis DDS! We are serious about exceptional dentistry, and it shows with every treatment we provide. But we can t hide the fact that we genuinely love what we do and enjoy every moment we get to spend with our valued patients.

Our dental office is an established part of the Troy, OH, community and has proudly served friends and neighbors here since 1955! We continue to serve generations of the same families, which we think says everything about who we are and what our patients have come to expect from us. We also love meeting and welcoming new patients!

Some of our team members have been with us for decades and know our patients by name. We work cohesively as a team behind the scenes to ensure you have a comfortable, seamless experience from the moment you arrive until you leave with a healthy, happy smile.

We put the "family" in dentistry because our patients really do become like family to us. We promise to provide the same level of care and attention for your family that we would expect for our own.

Our mission statement sums up our expectations for your visits:

We are pleased to offer general dentistry for families and individuals of all ages.

As your children grow into teens and adults, we ll provide services like preventive cleanings and exams, so they enjoy healthy teeth and gums for a lifetime.

We will continue to provide age-appropriate care at every stage of life to keep your family s smiles strong, healthy, and functioning comfortably through the years.
