Better breathing should lead to better living. My team and I measure outcomes to ensure it does.

In children, our airway focused treatments can produce 40 to 46 percent increases in airway size, with some cases doubling the narrowest part of the airway.

In adults, our non surgical expansion protocols can create 3 to 8 cubic centimeters of additional nasal space, often improving sleep quality while reducing snoring and fatigue.

Les soins

When the upper jaw is narrow, the airway is narrow. When the nasal cavity lacks space, breathing becomes unstable. Over time, that instability can affect sleep quality, oxygen levels, cardiovascular strain, and overall health.
