How We Give Back

Dr. Carron participates in the Dental Lifeline Network, which provides comprehensive dental treatment at no cost to select medically compromised patients who are veterans, disabled, or elderly and cannot afford to pay for dental treatment. Since 2020, Dr. Carron has delivered over $20,000 worth of dental services to in-need patients through the Dental Lifeline Network. We are honored to be a part of this initiative and look forward to continuing to make a positive impact in our community, one smile at a time.
