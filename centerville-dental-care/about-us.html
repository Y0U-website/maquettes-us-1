About Us

We Are Here For You

Centerville Dental Care provides a warm and friendly environment with modern technology and personal care for each individual patient. We specialize in cosmetic dentistry to improve our patients’ smile and instantly boost their confidence. Our ultimate goal is for every person to leave Centerville Dental Care with both a personal, comfortable experience and a great smile!

Our family is your family. Your comfort is our comfort.

Dr. Arsen Donesa earned his degree from the University of the East College of Dentistry in the Philippines and has been practicing in California for the past 10 years.

Dentistry has always been a part of his life. He was born to a family of dentists who greatly influenced his vision, passion and tremendous work ethics.

He has dedicated his professional career to providing the highest quality and most personalized care for his patients. Dr. Donesa stays up to date on the latest technologies and approaches in order to provide his patients with advanced, state of the art, quality dental care.

In his personal time, Dr. Donesa focuses on quality time with his family, physical fitness and singing in the church choir.
