Get to Know Our Annadale Dentists

Dr. Steven Johnson Dr. Johnson has been taking care of the local residents in Northern Virginia for over 30 years at Annandale Smiles Click to go Annandale Smiles home page . He graduated from the Medical College of Virginia, completed his general dental practice residency at West Virginia University, and completed the neuromuscular program at the World-Renowned Las Vegas Institute of Advanced Dental Studies.

Our Annandale, VA dentist is certified in Lumineers, Invisalign® Learn more about Invisalign® , and endodontic dentistry. He’s proud to work with a dedicated team that performs exceptional dental services of all kinds to meet your needs (from general to cosmetic and restorative care). Dr. Johnson invites you to our modern family dental office, where we combine patient-first comforts with exceptional dentistry that works!

Dr. Deepak Wararh (Dr. Dee)

Dr. Dee earned her undergraduate degree in Punjab, India, and moved to the United States to earn her doctor of dental surgery degree from the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry . To date, she continues to expand her expertise through continuing dental education and courses, plus her involvement in the Academy of General Dentistry as an active member.

Our Annandale Smiles dentist strives to give all her patients excellent dentistry with individualized care. She looks forward to meeting you and your family and helping you smile! In her free time, Dr. Dee loves to spend time with her loved ones, and some of her hobbies include reading and cooking for family and friends.

Dr. Michael Dong Dr. Michael is a Northern Virginia native and graduated magna cum laude from Virginia Tech with a BS in biology. After college, he spent time with his dentist and became inspired to pursue a career in dentistry. Dr. Michael earned his doctor of dental surgery degree from the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, plus completed an advanced education general dentistry residency at the University of California in Los Angeles.

He’s an active member of the Pankey Institute and the Academy of General Dentistry and participates in continuing education for dental implant, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Our Annandale, VA dentist is also an active member of the Spear Faculty Study Club, and his goal is for patients to gain the confidence that comes from a beautiful, healthy smile. Dr. Michael’s favorite pastimes revolve around basketball, running, and golf.

Dr. Ji Woung Choi

Dr. Choi grew up in South Korea and moved to the US with his family when he was 17. His mom was a dental hygienist, and Dr. Choi was exposed to the field early on, hoping to one day help others as well. He earned his BS in biology/chemistry from Southern Illinois University Edwardsville and his doctor of dental surgery degree from the New York University College of Dentistry.

His accomplishments include the Dr. Edward G. Kaufman Award for academic excellence and Dr. Harry Langa scholarship. Our dentist is Invisalign® certified, a member of the American Dental Association Learn more about ADA , and a fellow of the International Dental Implant Association Learn more about International Dental Implant Association . In his free time, Dr. Choi enjoys watching movies, listening to music, and playing tennis. A fun fact? His parents are his heroes!

Dr. Christine Do Dr. Christine Do is a general dentist who is passionate about providing comprehensive, high-quality dental care. Born and raised in Northern Virginia, she is excited to return to the area and serve the community she grew up in. Dr. Do believes in creating a welcoming dental home where every patient feels comfortable, valued, and cared for.

Dr. Do completed her undergraduate degree, doctorate, and residency at Virginia Commonwealth University. During her advanced general dentistry residency, she provided emergency and multidisciplinary oral health care while gaining additional training in complex prosthodontics, surgery, and digital dentistry. Dr. Do remains committed to continuing education and regularly seeks opportunities to expand her knowledge and stay current with the latest techniques and technology.

Outside of the office, Dr. Do enjoys staying active through Pilates, F45 workouts, running, and has even completed a full marathon. She also loves spending quality time with family and friends, exploring new cuisines, and cheering on the VCU Rams!

Dr. Justin Do

Justin Do, DDS, is a general dentist born and raised in Gaithersburg, Maryland. Dr Do completed his undergraduate education at the University of Maryland, where he studied Neurobiology and Physiology. He also received his doctorate training at the University of Maryland’s School of Dentistry. During that time, Dr. Do was accepted into the very competitive Oral Surgery clerkship.

After graduating from Dental School, Dr. Do pursued further education in a hospital-based residency at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York City to advance his knowledge of restorative, surgical, and cosmetic dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Do enjoys being very active. His hobbies include playing soccer and tennis. When he wants to relax, he enjoys going to musical concerts. Dr. Do is an avid volunteer and works with the Hope for Tomorrow organization, which provides free dental care to impoverished communities locally and throughout the world.

Dr. Juhee Park Juhee Park, DDS, loves helping patients feel comfortable and confident about their smiles. Born in Seoul, South Korea, she moved to the United States at the age of five and grew up in Lynchburg, Virginia. Dr. Park attended the University of California, Irvine, where she double majored in Business Economics and Biological Sciences, and went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. While there, she graduated with Distinction and served as a student ambassador, reflecting her passion for connecting with people.
