Your New Smile, Step by Step

Implants are placed in the jaw, where they are anchored firmly in the surrounding bone. Attachments are placed in the underside of the denture that connect to the implants. When placed in the mouth, the denture snaps onto the implants, keeping it stable and secure.

Speak, Eat, and Smile Freely

Confidence in Social Situations

Patients who opt for implant-supported arches are more confident when they talk, laugh, and eat in public. You’ll never have to worry about your false teeth slipping or falling out while you eat or are engaged in conversation.

Superior Comfort and Gum Health

Implant-supported arches are far more comfortable because they are secure and feel like natural teeth. There is also less irritation of gum tissue; patients who have conventional dentures often suffer pain and canker sores.

Natural Appearance and Bone Preservation

Implant-supported arches look more natural than conventional dentures because less plastic is needed for retention. Wearing implant-supported arches can also help your appearance in the long term by preventing jaw shrinkage and bone loss.

Clear and Easy Speech

Patients with conventional dentures have difficulty with speaking clearly. Slurred speech and clicking noises are common problems. Implant-supported arches can help you relax and talk easily with a more clear and natural voice.

No Adhesives or Removal Needed

Best of all, with implant-supported arches, there is no need to use denture adhesives or take them out to sleep.
