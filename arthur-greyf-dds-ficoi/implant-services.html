Implant Services

Sinus lifts and bone grafts restore lost bone before implants. Advanced 3D imaging ensures precise treatment and implant care.

Sinus Lift Augmentation

When upper posterior teeth are lost, the sinuses may enlarge. A sinus lift augmentation relocates the sinus cavity and replaces lost bone, creating the proper foundation for dental implants. This graft typically heals in four to six months before implants can be placed.

  • Repositions the sinus cavity to its original location.
  • Replaces lost bone with a graft to support implants.
  • Typically requires four to six months for complete healing.

Bone Grafting and Implant Site Development

In cases of significant bone loss, synthetic bone may be used to fill the area, allowing implants to be placed more securely. This bone will fuse with the natural bone in about four to six months, ensuring better implant success.

  • Uses synthetic bone to fill areas of bone loss.
  • Fuses with natural bone in four to six months.
  • Ensures a stable base for implant placement.

Computer-Enhanced Treatment Planning

We use KaVo OP 3D cone beam X-ray and 3D digital imaging to evaluate bone quality and plan precise implant placement. This technology ensures accurate results and successful dental implants.

  • Utilizes 3D digital imaging for detailed bone analysis.
  • Allows accurate implant placement planning.
  • Ensures a higher rate of implant success.

Implant Maintenance

After implant restoration, regular professional cleanings are essential to prevent plaque buildup and keep implants healthy. The frequency of cleanings is based on your individual needs.

  • Professional cleanings to prevent plaque and tartar buildup.
  • Ensures the health and longevity of your implants.
  • Frequency of cleanings tailored to your needs.
