The Advantages of Modern Dental Implants

Dental implants in Springboro are the gold standard for tooth replacement because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Anchored directly into the jawbone, implants help prevent bone loss and maintain facial structure, benefits that dentures and bridges alone cannot provide.

Implants also allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Unlike removable dentures, they don’t shift or require adhesives. With proper care, implants can last decades, making them a long-term investment in oral health.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a dental implant procedure take?

The full process typically spans several months. After implant placement, a healing period of 3–6 months allows the implant to fuse with bone before attaching the final crown or bridge.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Our team uses local anesthesia and offers sedation options to ensure comfort. Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure, similar to a simple tooth extraction.

Are dental implants right for me?

Dental implants are ideal for most adults with healthy gums and sufficient bone. We evaluate your oral health, bone density, and medical history to determine the best treatment plan.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last decades, often a lifetime, making them one of the most durable tooth replacement options.
