The practice

We specialize in tooth extractions so we can do them faster, better, and more affordably than other offices.

Impacted, erupted, or surgically complex wisdom teeth extracted same-day. We handle most cases in-office.

Swelling and infection can’t wait for a Monday appointment. We get the source of the infection out safely and quickly.

Cracked below the gum line or shattered from an accident — we love taking out broken teeth that others can’t!
