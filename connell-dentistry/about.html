About Connell Dental

Connell Dental has been offering comprehensive family and cosmetic dental care in the Shoals area since 1998. We have enjoyed getting to know the Shoals, what it has to offer, and the wonderful people who live here. We are dedicated to giving you and your family the most exceptional dental care to create a beautiful and lasting smile for years to come. Committed to more than cavities and brushing; we take pride in caring for your family and strive to provide a friendly, comfortable environment and experience. Payment information

We pride ourselves on offering some of the best prices in the Shoals area. Our focus is our patients, and we strive to give you and your family the absolute best care at a minimal cost. Meet our Dental Team

Dr. Keith Connell, DMD

Dr. Nolin Connell, DMD

Dr. Nolin is a native of Muscle Shoals, AL. He completed his undergraduate degree in Biomedical Sciences at Auburn University with a minor in Spanish. He then went on to pursue dentistry at UAB where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. He is a member of the American Dental Association, Alabama Dental Association, and American Academy of General dentistry. Dr. Nolin also works with pediatric patients (ages 1+) and enjoys treating our youngest patients with enthusiasm and fun! Outside of the office, Dr. Nolin enjoys being on the river and is an avid traveler.
