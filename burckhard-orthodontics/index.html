At Burckhard Orthodontics, we pride ourselves on being the premier provider of traditional metal braces. We understand that braces aren’t just about straight teeth—they’re about setting your smile and oral health up for a lifetime.

Clear braces offer the strength and reliability of traditional metal braces with a more subtle appearance. We use high-quality Clarity™ ceramic brackets that blend in with your smile—making them a popular choice for teens and adults seeking a less noticeable treatment option.

Retainers are essential after orthodontic treatment to keep your teeth in their new, aligned position—whether you choose a removable or bonded option. Over time, teeth can shift whether or not you’ve had braces, which is why Dr. Burckhard will recommend a personalized retainer plan to preserve your smile. In the event your retainer is lost or broken, please contact us right away for a replacement.

With over 15 years of orthodontic experience, Dr. Burckhard specializes in delivering personalized, effective care. His journey started in the Twin Cities metro and Children’s Hospital Minneapolis, but his passion always brought him back home to serve Minot as your trusted Minot ND orthodontist .

Outside the office, Dr. Burckhard is deeply committed to the Minot community—as a volunteer coach for youth sports, serving on the Minot State University Board of Regents, and formerly leading the Minot Dental Society. He’s also a husband, proud father of three, avid gardener and landscaper, Green Bay Packers fan, and active volunteer.
