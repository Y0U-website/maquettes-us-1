JOHN F. LANDIS , D.D.S.

Dr. Landis has strong ties to the Jacksonville area. Here, he met his wife Lisa in 2000 while stationed as a nurse corps officer at Naval Hospital Jacksonville. Although his military service carried them to many different duty stations along the East Coast, he and his family are excited about now calling Jacksonville their permanent home.

Dr. Landis attended dental School in Houston, TX, and graduated in 2007. He was then accepted into the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Residency at Naval Medical Center Portsmouth. After completion of his residency, Dr. Landis was assigned to Naval Hospital Pensacola. He obtained Board Certification in 2013. He is currently serving in the Navy Reserves.

The military enables Dr. Landis to pursue his education and provide for his family, making him feel he owes everything to the military. Thus, he has a passion to treat military service members and their families, as well as retirees and veterans. His focus is dentoalveolar procedures, extractions, and dental implant surgery. He offers a wide array of other oral and maxillofacial surgery services.

NATHAN M. HARPER , D.D.S.

Dr. Harper is very proud to serve as a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon to the residents of the Beaches and surrounding Jacksonville. He was born and raised in Loudoun County, VA, and attended Stetson University in DeLand, FL, for his undergraduate education, where he was a part of the Men’s Division 1 Basketball Team. After spending time completing research at the National Institute of Health in Bethesda, MD, Dr. Harper went on to attend the University of Southern California Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry under the United States Army Health Professional’s Scholarship Program.

Dr. Harper is trained in broad scope oral and maxillofacial surgery and has expertise in dental implants, simple and complex bone grafting, exodontia/third molar surgery, pre-prosthetic surgery, orthognathic surgery, and complex facial trauma. He also takes pride in providing safe and comfortable in-office sedation techniques. Dr. Harper strives to be a caring, trusted healthcare provider for his patients and their loved ones, with comfort and patient satisfaction as his ultimate goal.

He and his wife, Krista, and three young children live in Atlantic Beach, enjoying everything life at the beaches has to offer.
