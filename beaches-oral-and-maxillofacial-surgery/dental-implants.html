Dental Implants

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They act as sturdy foundations for artificial teeth, a permanent solution that looks and feels natural. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are fused with the bone over time, creating a secure and long-lasting restoration.

With proper care, dental implants in Jacksonville Beach, FL, can last a lifetime — making them a worthwhile investment in your oral health and confidence.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant procedure is a multi-step process that involves restoring missing teeth with artificial tooth roots.

First, our military-friendly oral surgeon in Jacksonville Beach, FL, will assess your jawbone to determine if it can support an implant. If needed, bone grafting may be required to strengthen the area. Once your jaw is ready, the titanium implant is surgically placed into the bone where it will serve as a sturdy foundation for the replacement tooth.

After the implant has integrated with the bone, typically over a few months, an abutment is attached to connect the implant to the crown. A custom-made crown is affixed to the abutment, completing your new tooth that looks and functions just like a natural one.

Throughout each step of this process, our oral surgeon in Jacksonville Beach, FL, will ensure you are comfortable and informed about what to expect during and after treatment. Contact us to learn more!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a popular and effective solution for replacing missing or damaged teeth, providing numerous benefits over traditional methods like dentures or bridges. Here are the key advantages of choosing dental implants in Jacksonville Beach, FL:

By offering a permanent solution to tooth loss, dental implants in Jacksonville Beach, FL, improve not only the functionality and aesthetics of your smile but also contribute significantly to a healthier lifestyle and psychological well-being.

  • Natural look and feel: Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth. They are crafted to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, providing a natural appearance and comfortable fit.
  • Long-lasting solution: With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. They are made from durable materials, such as titanium, which are well-tolerated by the body and can be fused with bone over time through a process called osseointegration.
  • Preservation of bone structure: One of the most significant benefits of dental implants is their ability to prevent bone loss. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in the area can begin to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Implants replace the tooth root, providing the necessary stimulation to maintain bone density and structure.
  • Improved oral health: Unlike bridges that require reducing adjacent teeth to support the restoration, dental implants in Jacksonville Beach, FL, do not require altering surrounding teeth. This preservation of the natural tooth structure contributes to better overall oral health.
  • Enhanced comfort and confidence: Implants are securely anchored in the jaw, which means they don’t slip or move. This stability eliminates the discomfort and insecurity that often come with dentures.
  • Improved eating and speaking: Because they function like natural teeth, dental implants allow for better chewing efficiency and clearer speech. This can enhance your ability to eat a variety of foods and communicate effectively, improving your quality of life.
  • Psychological benefits: Missing teeth can lead to self-consciousness about one’s appearance. Implants provide a cosmetic benefit that can greatly improve self-esteem and lead to a more active, engaging social life.

Aftercare and Maintenance for Dental Implants

Aftercare for implant dentistry in Jacksonville Beach, FL, is crucial to ensure their success and longevity.

By following these aftercare tips diligently, you can enjoy a beautiful smile with strong and healthy dental implants for years to come!

  • It’s essential to maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly. This helps prevent gum disease, which can jeopardize the stability of your implants.
  • Visiting our oral surgeon near me for regular checkups is also important. They can monitor the health of your implants and address any issues promptly.
  • Following a balanced diet and avoiding excessive sugar intake will benefit not only your overall health but also the condition of your implants.
  • In case you engage in activities that pose a risk to your dental implants, like contact sports or teeth grinding, consider using a mouthguard for protection.
  • Remember to quit smoking, as it can negatively impact the healing process after implant surgery.
