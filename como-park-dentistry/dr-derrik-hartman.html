Dr. Derrik Hartman

Dentist

Dr. Hartman was raised near Rochester, Minnesota and went to Dover-Eyota High School. He went to Luther College for undergrad where he received a bachelor of arts in biology. Dr. Hartman attended the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and graduated in 2013 with a doctorate of dental surgery.

Dr. Hartman lives in St. Paul and loves everything that the city has to offer. Outside of the office, Dr. Hartman is an avid sports fan, especially college football and remains a huge fan of the University of Minnesota Golden Gophers. He stays active by playing in local softball, curling, and basketball leagues. He enjoys going to craft breweries, and will always use a vacation as a chance to try a new brewery.
