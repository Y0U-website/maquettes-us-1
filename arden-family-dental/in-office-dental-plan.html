Your In-Office Dental Plan Benefits

Our in-office plan was designed to ensure patients across Arden receive routine dental care without the burden of insurance premiums. When you sign up for an in-office plan at Arden Family Dental, you can protect your smile from cavities, tooth decay, and more. This plan includes:

  • Child Cleanings: 2 per year
  • Adult Cleanings: 2 per year
  • Periodontal Maintenance: 3 per year
  • Periodic Exam: 2 per year
  • Bitewing X-Rays: 1 per year
  • Child Fluoride Treatment: 1 per year
  • Additional Cleanings & Fluoride Treatments
  • Fillings
  • Crowns & Bridges
  • Dentures & Partial Dentures
  • Dental Implants

Program Exclusions & Limitations

Before signing up for our in-office dental plan, there are a few important exclusions and limitations. This is a discount plan, not an insurance plan, and you can only enroll after receiving a comprehensive exam from one of our doctors. Keep in mind that this plan cannot be combined with other dental plans, dental insurance, discount offers, CareCredit, or Lending Club.

Our plan also does not cover injuries or services related to workman’s compensation, automobile, or medical insurance, nor does it cover specialist services. Any products or medications you need will also be at your own cost. If you have questions about whether this plan is the right fit for you, we recommend speaking to one of our staff members in the office.
