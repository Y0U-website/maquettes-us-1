Restoring Your Oral Function

There are many reasons why dental implants are the best investment you can make in your smile. While dentures are a great way to improve oral function, they may not be a good fit for your oral health condition. Some of the most significant benefits of investing in implants include the following:

Although conventional dentures can help you restore tooth loss, they can slip and slide throughout the day without proper adhesive. You don’t have to worry about this sliding with dental implants, as they are held in place by strong implant posts in your jawbone.

When you lose teeth, you may also deal with a deteriorating jawbone. This can lead to a sunken appearance, which may increase smile insecurity. An implant in your jaw stimulates bone growth, avoiding this sunken appearance.

Wearing dentures can cause bone loss, which can lead to ill-fitting dentures. With implants, you don’t have to worry about bone loss, meaning you can comfortably speak and eat without worrying about the prosthetic fitting poorly.

Because this prosthetic is attached to your jawbone, you can trust that it is more natural-looking than other options. Our team works hard to ensure your new implant matches its neighboring teeth, creating a seamless smile.

Who is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Not every dental patient is a good candidate for dental implants. When you turn to Arden Family Dental, you can trust that we’ll help you determine whether this fits your smile well. Good dental implant candidates must be in top health before scheduling surgery. If you aren’t in good health, you may struggle through recovery or experience implant rejection.

Additionally, those who frequently use tobacco products or are struggling with chronic illness may experience much slower healing throughout the process. Once our team clears you for dental implants, it is important that you maintain great oral hygiene. Your oral hygiene plays a major role in the success of these implants.
