All-on-X Dental Implants – A Permanent Solution for Full Arch Restoration

If you’re missing all or most of your teeth, All-on-X dental implants offer a transformative solution that restores the full function and appearance of your smile. Using just four to six strategically placed implants, the All-on-X technique secures a full arch of natural-looking replacement teeth—eliminating the need for traditional dentures. Unlike removable options, All-on-X implants are fixed in place, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence. This innovative procedure provides immediate results and is often referred to as “teeth in a day.”

At BAK Implant Studio in Los Angeles , Dr. Bak uses advanced 3D imaging and digital treatment planning to deliver precise, comfortable, and long-lasting outcomes for patients seeking full mouth dental implant restoration . Whether you’re transitioning from dentures or facing extensive tooth loss, All-on-X may be the life-changing solution you’ve been looking for. We also offer IV sedation and general anesthesia to ensure your comfort during surgery.
