Our Story

Dr. Bak is a board-certified periodontist and a Fellow of the Academy of Osseointegration , a distinction awarded to dental professionals who demonstrate exceptional commitment to advancing the science and clinical practice of implant dentistry. He is also a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology and Implant Dentistry , a credential earned by specialists who meet the highest standards of expertise and patient care.

He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) in 2005 , completed a PhD in Bone Biology in 2011 , and finished his specialty training in Periodontology and Implant Dentistry at the University of Southern California (USC) in 2014 .

With over 20 years of clinical experience in dental implants , bone grafting , and tissue regeneration , Dr. Bak currently serves as Director of the Implant Master Program at USC and remains an active faculty member. He has published extensively in peer-reviewed journals and is frequently invited to speak at national and international dental conferences. Patients throughout Los Angeles and Southern California seek Dr. Bak for his expertise in delivering evidence-based, minimally invasive implant and periodontal care.
