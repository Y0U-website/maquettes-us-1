Web Accessibility

Please note that the availability and effectiveness of these features can depend on the website’s configuration and ongoing maintenance. While we strive to ensure accessibility, we cannot guarantee that every part of our website will be fully accessible at all times. Some content may be provided by third parties or affected by technical constraints beyond our immediate control.

  • Contrast and color adjustments, including dark, light, high-contrast, and monochrome modes.
  • Highlighting of links, titles, and content for better visibility.
  • A text magnifier, reading mask, and reading line for easier reading.
  • Full keyboard navigation of the site and the toolbar interface.
  • Text-to-speech (read page) support.
  • The option to stop animations, hide images, or mute sounds.
  • Ready-to-use profiles, including Vision Impaired, Seizure Safe, ADHD Friendly, Blindness, and Epilepsy Safe modes.
  • The toolbar can be opened at any time using the accessibility icon on the site.
