A Great Way to Preserve Affected Teeth

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap that can be fitted over your tooth. Crowns are effective treatments for a variety of conditions, including broken, cracked, and painful teeth. A crown allows you to preserve your bite.

How long do crowns last?

The lifespan of a crown depends on many factors so there is no “specific” answer. Everyone is different so your situation is unique to you.

How do I know if I need a crown?

If you have previously had a tooth filled and are experiencing pain again, you may need a crown. The most common reasons patients need crowns are cracks and previous deep cavities. Call us today for an appointment if you think you may need a crown!
