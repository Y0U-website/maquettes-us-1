About Us

  • Skip to content Augusta Children’s Dental Center’s Kid-Friendly Staff ABOUT US Our staff has had extensive training in Pediatric Dentistry. As a children’s dental clinic, we have developed a relaxed environment to make your child’s appointment comfortable and enjoyable. Cultivating relationships with our patients helps to turn a visit with the doctors and staff into a visit between friends.
  • TAKING GOOD NOTES We take special notes on each child during the visit so we can maintain relevant communication from visit to visit. This helps foster relationships between staff, doctors and your child to last for years.
  • MAKING THE DENTIST VISIT FUN We care about what techniques work the best for your child. The goal is to make dentist visits fun, which helps to establish a foundation of life-long dental care.
  • PARENTAL COLLABORATION By creating an atmosphere of collaboration with parents we make sure you have a clear understanding of the treatment and follow-up that make up the solutions to your children’s dental health plan.

Our New Dentists

Welcome Dr. Pittman!

Dear Valued Patients and Families, We are thrilled to share some exciting news about a wonderful addition to the Augusta Children’s Dental Center team. Please join us in welcoming Dr. Bobby Pittman, a highly skilled and compassionate pediatric dentist, to our practice.

Dr. Pittman brings with him over 20 years of exceptional experience in pediatric dentistry. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and has spent decades honing his craft in treating children with the highest levels of care and attention. His extensive background includes a strong focus on IV sedation dentistry, where he has helped countless young patients feel comfortable and at ease during dental procedures

Welcome Dr. Truong!

Dear Parents, We are thrilled to announce an exciting addition to our team at Augusta Children’s Dental Center! Please join us in welcoming Dr. Khiem Truong as one of our new pediatric dentists.

Dr. Truong brings over a decade of experience as a pediatric dentist, specializing in providing compassionate care to children, including those with autism and special needs. He is also highly skilled in IV sedation and other advanced pediatric dental techniques, ensuring our patients receive the best possible care in a safe and comfortable environment
