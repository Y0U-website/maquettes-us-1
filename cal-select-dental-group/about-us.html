Cal Select Dental Group: Where Board-Certified Dentists and Skilled Teams Provide Tailored Oral Health Care

At Cal Select Dental Group, our mission is simple: to provide exceptional dental care tailored to your unique needs and goals. From the moment you reach out to us, our board-certified dentists and skilled team are dedicated to giving you individual attention and personalized oral health care.

With our practice expanding, Cal Select Dental Group proudly serves patients across California, including San Bernardino, Hesperia, and Riverside. We offer a comprehensive range of preventive services for patients of all ages, encompassing general, pediatric, emergency, and cosmetic dentistry. Whether you need routine check-ups or specialized treatments like Invisalign , custom-made bridges, or crowns, we re here to meet your needs.

That s why we accept state-funded insurance plans such as Denti-Cal & IEHP, and we offer flexible payment plans through partnerships with Care Credit. Our goal is to ensure that every patient can access the care they need within their budget.

Our dedicated staff works tirelessly to help you and your family achieve brighter, straighter smiles. We re here to support you on your journey to optimal oral health. Contact us today to schedule an appointment and experience the difference at Cal Select Dental Group.
