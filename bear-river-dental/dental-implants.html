What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process?

The dental implant process requires multiple appointments in our office. We start with an individual consultation to discuss your goals and determine the best treatment plan for your health and your budget.

For the implant to remain sturdy and provide the required support, you must have enough bone in the jaw. If necessary, bone grafts can provide the additional support that is needed as a first step.

If bone graft isn’t necessary, we are ready to begin the implant process by placing the implant into the jawbone. After this step is complete, a healing process must occur.

The healing period allows the implant time to fuse to the jawbone through osseointegration. This allows the implant to function like a natural tooth root in the mouth. This generally takes four to six months.

Once the healing process is complete, the patient returns to our office to complete the implant process. We place an abutment or connector on top of the implant, securely holding the crown. We craft the crowns to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. Temporary crowns may be necessary until patients return for the permanent crown.

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Evanston restore full chewing power and are as strong as natural teeth. Dental implants function like natural teeth, allowing patients to speak and eat normally. They are comfortable in the mouth and do not cause gum or mouth irritation like other restorative solutions. Implants provide long-lasting results; if cared for properly, they can last a lifetime.

They preserve the natural bone in the mouth and help to stimulate new bone growth. There are fewer food restrictions compared to other treatments. Implants are durable and provide long-term value financially compared to other restorative solutions. These are permanent and do not slip or click like other solutions.
