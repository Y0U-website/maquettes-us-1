ANNOUNCEMENT: We are now at our new location 1600 Camellia Blvd, Building D, Lafayette, LA 70508

Your initial appointment will consist of a consultation explaining your diagnosis and treatment options. In many cases, root canal treatment can be done during the initial appointment. However, surgeries and/or a complex medical history or treatment plan will usually require an evaluation and a second appointment for treatment.

Please assist us by providing the following information at the time of your consultation:

IMPORTANT: All patients under the age of 18 years of age must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for each visit to our office.

Please alert the office if you have a medical condition that may be of concern prior to treatment (i.e. diabetes, high blood pressure, hyperthyroidism, artificial heart valves and joints, rheumatic fever, history of osteoporosis or other bone abnormalities, etc.) or if you are on any medications (i.e. heart medications, aspirin, anticoagulant therapy, etc.).

As part of your treatment, we will take all necessary radiographs (x-rays).
