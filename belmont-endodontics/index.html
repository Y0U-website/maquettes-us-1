Root canal therapy is essential for saving natural teeth and eliminating pain caused by deep decay or infection. At Belmont Endodontics, we ensure this process is as comfortable and efficient as possible, preserving your smile and well-being.

Retreatment Endodontic retreatment is performed when a previous root canal does not heal properly. Our approach corrects complications and restores the tooth, focusing on long-term health and pain relief.

Microsurgery Endodontic microsurgery, known as an apicoectomy, offers a precise solution for persistent infections at the root tip. We use advanced microsurgical techniques to improve outcomes and ensure a faster recovery.

Emergencies Dental emergencies require prompt, expert attention. Our team is ready to alleviate severe pain and address urgent dental issues quickly, ensuring you receive immediate and effective care.

Seamless Experience At Belmont Endodontics, we streamline every step of your journey. From the initial consultation to the final follow-up, our process is designed to ensure a smooth and hassle-free experience for both patients and referring dentists.

Digital Office We’re proud to be a fully digital office. This means you can complete all necessary paperwork online before your appointment, ensuring a stress-free start to your visit.
