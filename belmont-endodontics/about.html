Get To Know

Dr. Tanjit Taggar

Dr. Tanjit Taggar’s journey into dentistry began in his hometown in central New Jersey, where he grew up in a family of dentists. This early exposure instilled in him a profound interest in dental care, leading him to pursue a career in the field from a young age. Dr. Taggar embarked on an accelerated path that combined his undergraduate studies with his dental training, allowing him to earn both his Bachelor of Science and his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) within seven years — a feat that required him to interview for dental school as a high school senior.

It wasn&#x27;t until his third year of dental school at the University of Pennsylvania that Dr. Taggar discovered his passion for endodontics. Following a rotation in the Endodontics Department, he was inspired to specialize further, leading him to the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. There, he completed his Certificate and Master of Science in Endodontics, equipping him with the specialized skills not just to treat but truly understand the complexities of root canal therapy and related procedures.

In 2019, Dr. Tanjit Taggar became board certified with the American Board of Endodontics. Less than 20 % of all endodontists achieve this status. The purpose of the American Board of Endodontics is to assure the public that the endodontists it certifies have demonstrated exceptional knowledge, skill, and expertise in the specialty of endodontics and to progressively raise the quality of patient care.

For the last eight years, Dr. Taggar has practiced in various locations across the Northeast. Now, he and his wife have settled in Charlotte, North Carolina, and plan to raise their family there.

Outside of the office, Dr. Taggar is passionate about travel, fitness, and food. He particularly enjoys exploring new cafes and can spend hours discussing the complexity of coffee.

Get To Know

Dr. Jared Sobo

Dr. Jared Sobo was born and raised in the North Charlotte and Lake Norman area, where he developed a strong appreciation for community and serving others. As the son of a general dentist, he grew up witnessing the meaningful impact dentistry can have on improving patients&#x27; health, comfort, and quality of life. That early exposure inspired his interest in the profession and laid the foundation for his career.

After earning his bachelor&#x27;s degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Dr. Sobo continued at the UNC Adams School of Dentistry, where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. After graduation, he practiced as a general dentist, where his passion for endodontics continued to grow, ultimately leading him to pursue specialty training.

Dr. Sobo completed his Certificate in Endodontics and Master of Dental Sciences degree at the University of Florida College of Dentistry, where he served as Chief Resident.

After completing his specialty training, Dr. Sobo was excited to return home to the Charlotte area and serve the community where he grew up.

Dr. Sobo is committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care while utilizing the latest evidence-based techniques and advanced technology to deliver precise, comfortable, and predictable outcomes. He is committed to making every patient feel comfortable, informed, and cared for throughout treatment.

Outside of the office, Dr. Sobo enjoys fitness, cover bands, discovering newfood spots, and cheering on his North Carolina sports teams and the Florida Gators.
