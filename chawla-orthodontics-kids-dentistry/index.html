The practice

We combine expert orthodontics and gentle pediatric care in a fun, welcoming space.

With locations in Westmont and Villa Park, we make healthy smiles easy for kids, teens, and adults.

Dr. Sumit Chawla brings energy, precision, and a deep sense of purpose to every patient interaction. He is passionate about helping people of all ages achieve healthier, more confident smiles. He sees each smile as more than just an outcome — it’s a transformation that can change the way a person feels about themselves.

Dr. Chawla is also passionate about using technology to enhance the patient experience. He integrates the latest advancements in orthodontics to make treatment more comfortable, efficient, and effective.

Gentle Pediatric Dentistry Checkups, cleanings, cavity care, and more — all delivered with a gentle touch in a kid-friendly space.

Smile Support That Lasts We stand behind every smile with long-term care programs like our Retainer Assurance Plan and Guarantee My Smile, so your results stay strong.

Behind every great smile is a dedicated team. From our doctors to our clinical and front office staff, everyone works together to provide personalized, compassionate care for your family.
