Experienced. Trusted. Committed to Your Smile.

Advanced training, and a passion for helping patients feel confident in their smiles. Meet the specialists leading your care.

“I think orthodontics is one of the most interesting professions in the world.

We are not just looking at teeth, but the person’s face as a whole, and we have an ability to change the face and make it look so much better. I really feel as though it is a privilege to be an orthodontist.”

Dr. Sumit Chawla

Dr. Sumit Chawla brings energy, precision, and a deep sense of purpose to every patient interaction. He is passionate about helping people of all ages achieve healthier, more confident smiles. He sees each smile as more than just an outcome — it’s a transformation that can change the way a person feels about themselves.

Dr. Chawla is also passionate about using technology to enhance the patient experience. He integrates the latest advancements in orthodontics to make treatment more comfortable, efficient, and effective.

Education & Credentials

  • Bachelor of Business Administration – Loyola University of Chicago
  • Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) – Tufts University
  • Certificate in Orthodontics – Roseman University of Health Sciences
  • Master of Business Administration (MBA) – Roseman University
  • Board-Certified Diplomate – American Board of Orthodontics

Professional Memberships
