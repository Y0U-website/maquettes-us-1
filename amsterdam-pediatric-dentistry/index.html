The practice

Services We provide a variety of services for patients ages 0-19, including routine examinations and cleanings, BPA free restorative dentistry, and more. Learn more about pediatric dental services.

Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) sedation dentistry is available. And as a credentialed staff member at several hospitals in the area, Dr. Bhatti is qualified to provide hospital dentistry for those that require it. Learn more about pediatric specialty services.
