General Dentist in Washington MI…

Dr. Lesinski grew up nearby in between Memphis and Armada and attended the University of Detroit Mercy for his undergraduate studies. There, he earned a biochemistry degree and worked as a dental assistant before attending the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry.

The continued pursuit of education is important to Dr. Lesinski. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the local Macomb Dental Association. Dr. Lesinski has taken educational courses on dental implants including all-on-4 style prosthetics and enjoys being able to replace missing teeth for patients.

Dr. Lesinski enjoys visiting campgrounds and riding on the Orchard trail with his family during the summer. In the winter, he spends time volunteering with the Romeo High School wrestling team. He and his wife Rebecca have four children, with the last two being a set of fraternal twins.
