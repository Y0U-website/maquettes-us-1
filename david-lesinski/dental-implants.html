What are Dental Implants?

Who is a Good Candidate for Tooth Implants?

Dental implants are a versatile and effective solution for replacing missing teeth, but certain criteria can influence candidacy. Dr. Lesinski will evaluate your mouth and take time to discuss the implantation procedure with you if it is the most effective solution.

Missing One, Multiple, or All Teeth in an Arch : Candidates for dental implants include individuals who are missing one, multiple, or even all teeth in an arch. Whether you need to replace a single tooth or an entire set, implants provide an effective solution.

Adequate Bone Density : For successful implant placement, you need sufficient bone in your jaw to support the implant post. If bone density is lacking, it may be necessary to undergo bone grafting to create a stable foundation for the implant.

Good Overall Oral Health : Healthy gums and teeth are crucial for the success of dental implants. Patients should be free from active periodontal disease or other oral health issues that could compromise the implant’s integration and long-term stability.

What to Expect During The Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation

The process begins with a thorough examination of your oral health, including X-rays or 3D imaging to assess the condition of your jawbone and surrounding structures. This helps ensure that there is adequate bone density and identifies any potential issues that need to be addressed. Based on the examination results, our team will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

The Implant Placement Procedure

The implant placement is typically performed in a few stages. Initially, a small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over time, the implant will integrate with the bone in a process known as osseointegration, providing a stable base for the final restoration. To ensure your comfort during the procedure, local anesthesia will be administered to numb the area where the implant is placed. This minimizes any discomfort and allows the procedure to be performed with minimal pain.

Follow-Up Care

After the implant placement, you will receive detailed instructions on how to care for the surgical site. This includes recommendations for oral hygiene, diet, and pain management to ensure a smooth recovery. Regular follow-up visits are important to monitor the healing process and ensure that the implant is integrating properly. These visits allow your dentist to address any concerns and make necessary adjustments to your treatment plan. Once healing is complete, the final restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) will be placed to complete the implant process.
