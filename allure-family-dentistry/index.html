The practice

Dr. Kristina Yakubov grew up in Queens, NY. She decided there is no better place to start her practice than in her hometown. She takes tremendous pride in the healthy and beautiful smiles of her patients. At Allure Dental it is our mission to ensure ease, efficiency and patient comfort every step of the way.

Dr. Kristina Yakubov earned her dental degree from Touro College of Dental Medicine and continued on to residency at BronxCare Hospital.

Dr. Kristina Yakubov is board certified, and is member of the American Dental Association and the New York State Dental Association.
