The practice

The good news is that most problems are a lot more manageable than you’re imagining. And the first step is almost always easier than what you’ve been building up in your head.

We promise we’re more fun than you’re expecting. Our patients will tell you - some of them actually look forward to coming in. We know, we know. We were surprised too.

Dr. Lake has been taking care of Farmington families since 2019. He always dreamed of having his own practice in a smaller community — a place where he could actually know his patients, not just their charts. When he and his wife Melissa visited Farmington, they knew it was the one.

What his patients notice most is how they feel during an appointment. He explains every step as he goes — not a rushed overview at the start, but a running conversation the whole way through. He checks in, he remembers your name and your kids’ names, and he will never suggest treatment you do not need.

A lot of people come to Dr. Lake after years of avoiding the dentist. Most of them leave wondering why they waited so long.

"I promise to always do what is best for you, my patient. I will always lay out all of the available options, explain the pros and cons, and ensure you are comfortable enough to make an informed decision. I will always respect your decision, and I would never suggest something I would not suggest for my own family."

Routine cleanings, exams, and x-rays for the whole family. The best dental work is the work you never need. We catch small problems before they become expensive ones and thorougly clean areas that may be hard to reach at home.

From fillings and root canals to implants, crowns, and bridges, we restore your teeth to full health and function. No tooth gets left behind if we can help it.
