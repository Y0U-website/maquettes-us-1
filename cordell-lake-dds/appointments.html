Appointment Requests

Dr. Lake’s office offers convenient hours for you and your family. We value your time and know how important it is to you. You will be pleased to find that we generally are able to see you right at the appointed time. We make every effort to complete your treatment in as few visits as possible, so you’re out the door and back living your life.

In order for us to be efficient with the time that we have scheduled for you, we would ask that you also be prompt and always give 24 hours notice if you are unable to keep an appointment; this helps ensure that our other patients on our waiting list may be served.

Please give us a call at 573-756-4344 to schedule; we look forward to hearing from you!

If you are a new patient, save time by downloading, completing and bringing the following forms to your first appointment. If you cannot fill out these forms ahead of time, we ask that you arrive 15 minutes early to your first appointment.
