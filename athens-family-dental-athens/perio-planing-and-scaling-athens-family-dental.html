Perio Planing and Scaling

After a thorough evaluation, your dentist will determine if dental scaling and root planing is the appropriate treatment for your specific condition and overall oral health needs.

  • Bone Loss: Periodontal disease can lead to the loss of bone supporting the teeth. Scaling and root planing can help prevent further bone loss and promote bone regeneration.
  • Bleeding Gums: Persistent bleeding or inflammation of the gums may indicate the need for scaling and root planing to remove the underlying cause of the infection.

Perio Planing and Scaling Process

The process of perio scaling and root planing is typically carried out over multiple visits, depending on the extent of the treatment area. During the procedure, your dentist or dental hygienist will use specialized instruments to meticulously remove plaque, tartar, and bacterial deposits from the tooth surfaces and root areas.

First, the scaling phase involves the careful removal of hardened plaque (tartar) and calculus from above and below the gum line. This process is essential for eliminating the breeding ground for harmful bacteria.

Next, the root planing phase focuses on smoothing the exposed root surfaces of the teeth. By creating a clean, smooth surface, it becomes more difficult for plaque and bacteria to accumulate, allowing the gums to reattach properly.

Throughout the procedure, your dentist or hygienist will exercise great care and precision to ensure thorough cleaning without causing unnecessary discomfort. After the completion of perio scaling and root planing , your dentist may recommend regular follow-up visits to monitor your gum health and address any potential areas of concern.
