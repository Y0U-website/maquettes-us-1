Meet the Total Dental Care Team

We are led by Dr. Chris Harper, Dr. Aaron George, Dr. Austin Baker and our Office Manager Carol Harper. Each doctor works with two Hygienists, two Assistants and one Scheduling Coordinator. In addition, our business team includes two Treatment Coordinators, a Hygiene Coordinator, a New Patient Coordinator and an Insurance Coordinator. We are also supported by two part-time staff: our Public Relations Director Mollie George, and a part-time Records Clerk. All of us work together to provide the best possible care for our patients.

Carol G. Harper, JD

Financial Manager

Carol is responsible for the financial aspects of the practice, including accounts payable, payroll, health plan administration, retirement plan administration, and facility management. Carol is a frequent guest speaker on leadership topics, and an active member of LifePoint Church. Carol enjoys reading, cooking, hiking and traveling to new and exciting places.
