Six Month Smiles® Cosmetic Braces

For teens and adults, we offer nearly invisible cosmetic braces that work in as little as six months! Six Month Smiles is designed for patients who want minor changes to the teeth that show when you smile. These tooth-colored brackets and wires are barely noticeable — they blend very well with the color of your teeth. These definitely aren’t metal braces! This system uses new and specific orthodontic technology that provides safe, comfortable and efficient tooth movement. To get started, call us at 582-CARE to schedule a consultation.
