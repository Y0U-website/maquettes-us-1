Our Team

Exceptional Care, Outstanding Service We strive to provide the highest quality dental care available and to provide superior customer service. We have established a non-compromising attitude when it comes to treatment and patient care. Every person working in our office is dedicated to these principles.

Get Treated Like a Friend

From your first visit, you will be treated like a friend, not just a patient. Our staff is here to help you with everything, from responding to your questions and concerns to scheduling your next appointment around your busy schedule.

We treat people the way we would like to be treated. We respect your time and strive for on-time appointments. We appreciate your business and we are grateful to you for allowing us to help your mouth and body stay healthy.

Happy to Help

Our Business team is amazing! We are here to make your experience as easy as possible. Let us help you to schedule appointments, answer your questions, and file your insurance for you. We will work to help you to find a way to fit the dentistry you need into your budget and into your busy schedule.

We can help you to understand the cost of treatment, and give you an estimate before your visit. Multiple financing options are available

Our Dental Hygiene Team

We are dedicated to providing you with a comfortable experience, whether you are visiting for preventative care or advanced periodontal (gum) therapy. Our dental hygienists are highly educated and trained to ensure that you receive exceptional service and that your individual needs are given top priority. You can trust in our team to provide you with customized and comprehensive care tailored to meet your specific needs.

We won’t lecture you or make you feel bad, that is not our style. Our team genuinely wants the best for you and your oral health, so we will help you to get the healthiest mouth possible. We have a gentle hand and a kind heart. Let us show you.

Dr. Amanda M. Levinsohn Dr. Amanda Levinsohn graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2001. She practices general dentistry for adults & children. She received advanced training in Invisalign Orthodontics (clear braces).

Dr. Amanda enjoys providing care for the entire family with her comprehensive care approach, including preventive care, fillings, crowns and removable prosthetics. She is also the Practice Manager for Anderson Ferry Dental, supervising our team of seventeen wonderful employees. Dr. Amanda enjoys reading, attending concerts with her husband Dr. Steve, and being a mom to three wonderful kids.

Dr. Steven A. Levinsohn Dr. Steven Levinsohn graduated from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2002. He received a perfect score on all clinical sections of his licensing board examination and graduated in the top of his class with special recognition for his research in Implant Dentistry.

He is a member of the American Dental Association , Ohio Dental Association , and Cincinnati Dental Society . Dr. Steve is passionate about music, fishing, and photography, and loves his job and his family. He has been happily married to Dr. Amanda for over 20 years.
