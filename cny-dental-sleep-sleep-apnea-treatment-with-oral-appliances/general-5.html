Insurance

top of page Insurances We Work With: At CNY Dental Sleep, we’re committed to honest, patient-first care with no surprise bills.

We accept Medicare and are in-network with many major insurance plans. If we’re not contracted with your insurance, we’ll still work with them to help you get the most out of your benefits.

If we’re in-network, we take care of all the paperwork and claims. If we’re out-of-network, we’ll still handle the claims & paperwork for you and do everything we can to make the process smooth and affordable. In fact, for about 90 % of our patients, we’re able to secure in-network rates even when we re not contracted with their insurance (Commercial Insurance Plans Only.)

Our goal is to make care accessible and stress-free. Even if your plan isn’t one we usually work with, just ask we’ll review it together and do everything we can to make it work for you.
