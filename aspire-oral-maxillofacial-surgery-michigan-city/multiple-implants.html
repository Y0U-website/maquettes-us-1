Multiple Dental Implants

WHAT TO EXPECT

Multiple titanium implants are placed into the jaw in exact positions for the teeth you need. Our doctors plan these cases with CT and digital scanning technology to ensure accurate placement for your new teeth. The crowns or bridges will be attached to the implants after the healing period is complete, leaving you with a secure and natural-looking smile and restored function. Implants can be used to restore one to two teeth up to the entire arch of teeth!

WHAT ARE THE BENEFITS OF GETTING MULTIPLE DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Better preserves natural bone health and density. Doesn’t require separate maintenance from regular dental check-ups and cleanings. Multiple dental implants replace teeth without support from adjacent natural teeth. Natural-looking, functional, decay-resistant, and comfortable. Most patients forget they aren’t their real teeth!

ARE MULTIPLE IMPLANTS RIGHT FOR ME?

Since the implants anchor into the jaw bone, it’s important that you have adequate bone density and volume for the procedure. I f your bone levels aren’t high enough, a bone grafting procedure may be recommended before or during your implant placement. Oral surgeons are the most skilled at all procedures involved with implant therapy. The doctors and team at Aspire encourage you to schedule a consultation to discuss your teeth replacement!
