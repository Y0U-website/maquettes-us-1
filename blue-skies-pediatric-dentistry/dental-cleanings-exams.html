Why Should I Get Pediatric Dental Cleanings & Exams?

Regular pediatric dental cleanings and exams are crucial for maintaining your child&#x27;s oral health and detecting any potential dental issues early. These visits allow our team to thoroughly clean your child&#x27;s teeth, removing any plaque or tartar that may have built up and could lead to cavities if left untreated.

Additionally, regular exams give Dr. Prieve the chance to monitor your child&#x27;s dental development and catch any signs of dental problems early when they&#x27;re easier and less invasive to treat. Regular visits also help your child become comfortable with the dentist, reducing dental anxiety and promoting positive dental experiences.

The Benefits Of Pediatric Dental Cleanings & Exams

Preventive Care

Regular pediatric dental cleanings and exams are a key part of preventive care. By removing plaque and tartar, we can help prevent cavities and gum disease. Regular exams also allow us to monitor your child&#x27;s dental development and catch any potential issues early.

Education and Habits

During your child&#x27;s dental cleaning and exam, our team also takes the time to educate both you and your child about proper oral hygiene practices at home. We show kids the right way to brush and floss, discuss the importance of a healthy diet, and answer any questions you or your child may have. This education and the habits formed now can benefit your child&#x27;s oral health for a lifetime.

Comfort and Trust

Regular visits to our office help your child become comfortable in the dental environment and build trust with our team. This familiarity can greatly reduce dental anxiety, making future visits and treatments much easier and more positive.

The Pediatric Dental Cleaning & Exam Treatment Process

Dental Cleaning

The first step in a pediatric dental cleaning and exam is the cleaning itself. Our dental hygienist will gently clean your child&#x27;s teeth, removing any plaque or tartar. We use child-friendly techniques and equipment to ensure your child is comfortable throughout the process.

Dental Exam

Next, Dr. Prieve will perform a comprehensive dental exam. He&#x27;ll check your child&#x27;s teeth, gums, and jaw for any signs of dental issues. This may include taking X-rays if necessary. Dr. Prieve will also monitor your child&#x27;s dental development, ensuring everything is progressing as it should.

Discussion and Education

Finally, Dr. Prieve will discuss the results of the exam with you, explaining any findings and recommending any necessary treatments. He&#x27;ll also answer any questions you may have and provide education on proper oral hygiene habits at home. This step is crucial for ensuring you&#x27;re informed and your child is set up for a lifetime of healthy smiles.
