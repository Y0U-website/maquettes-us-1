The practice

If you are looking for an experienced, skilled, gentle dentist in Queens, we are here to help. Dr. Teodora Constantinescu has been treating families in Queens for over 30 years. She is a member of the New York State and American Dental Associations.

At Constantine Dental we offer a full range of general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, endodonics (root canals) and orthodontics. Good oral health includes regular professional dental checkups for adults and children, so don’t forget to schedule your checkup and cleaning.

We have a dental laboratory on the premises, so we can quickly do crowns, bridges and dentures. Because of this we are one of the few dental offices that can do emergency denture repair while you wait.

We accept all major credit cards, including Amex and Discover. We accept all PPO insurance plans, such as Delta, Principal, Connection Dental, Empire Blue Cross/Blue Shield, Met Life, and Guardian. Financing is available for qualified customers with CareCredit.

We also offer a Senior Citizen discount. We speak English, Italian, and Romanian.

We are conveniently located near the center of Queens, close to Forest Hills, Rego Park, Woodhaven, Ridgewood, Glendale, Kew Gardens and Maspeth.

By bus, take the Q54 bus, which runs along Metropolitan Avenue, to Metropolitan Avenue and 78th Street. By car, there is parking on the side streets, and meter parking along Metropolitan Avenue. By subway, take the M train to the last stop, Middle Village-Metropolitan Avenue, and from there take the Q54 bus West to 78th Street.
