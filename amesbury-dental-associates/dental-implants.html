Dental Implant Procedure

Amesbury Dental Associates follows a detailed, step-by-step dental implant process to deliver superior results.

  • Consultation and Evaluation: The first step involves a complete assessment, using X-rays and impressions to evaluate bone density, gum health, and space for the implant.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium implant is securely placed into your jawbone, allowing it to merge with the bone and heal for up to six months.
  • Abutment and Restoration: Once the implant has fused with the bone, a connector (abutment) is placed to hold the personalized replacement tooth securely.
  • Final Fitting: After several weeks of healing, we place and adjust the final restoration for a perfect fit and natural look.

Post-Dental Implant Tips

Proper care of your custom dental implants is necessary to extend their lifespan.

  • Brush and floss daily It keeps the implant area clean and plaque-free.
  • Prevent strain on your implant by avoiding hard or sticky foods.
  • Schedule regular dental check-ups This will help you maintain implant health and address any concerns.
  • Dentist’s instructions You must stick to it.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes, dental implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth. Custom dental implants are tailored to match your existing teeth, ensuring a seamless appearance and restoring both function and confidence in your smile.

What materials are used in dental implants in Amesbury, MA?

Dental implants are typically made from medical-grade titanium or zirconia. These biocompatible materials allow implants to integrate with the jawbone through osseointegration, ensuring long-term stability, strength, and compatibility with surrounding tissues.

How do you evaluate the bone density before implants in Amesbury, MA?

Before placing implants, we use digital X-rays or 3D CBCT scans to assess bone density and structure. This evaluation ensures that dental implants are placed safely and have sufficient support for long-term success.

What complications can occur with dental implants?

Although rare, complications with dental implants may include infection, implant failure, or nerve irritation. Proper planning, hygiene, and follow-up care significantly reduce risks and improve outcomes for custom dental implants.
