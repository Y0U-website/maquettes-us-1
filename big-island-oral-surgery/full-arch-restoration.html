Full Arch Restoration Dental Implants

Dealing with extensive tooth loss can be incredibly challenging, impacting not only your smile but also your oral health and overall well-being. At our dental office in Hilo, Hawaii, we understand the impact of missing teeth and offer full arch restoration solutions using dental implants, a revolutionary approach to restore your smile and improve your quality of life.

We will thoroughly explain the process and provide you with a personalized treatment plan. Our full arch restoration process begins with a comprehensive assessment of your oral health, including digital imaging and 3D scans, to create a detailed picture of your dental structure. This information allows us to determine the best approach for your specific needs, including considerations for your jaw health and comfort.

Once we have a clear understanding, we will discuss the various options available, such as All-on-4® implants or traditional implant-supported dentures. All-on-4® is particularly beneficial for patients with significant tooth loss, as it utilizes just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of prosthetic teeth. This technique not only minimizes the need for bone grafting but also offers a quicker recovery time.

During the procedure, our skilled team will place the implants into your jawbone, providing a sturdy foundation for your new teeth and enhancing your ability to chew. Depending on your situation, we may be able to attach a temporary set of teeth on the same day, allowing you to leave our office with a functional and beautiful smile right away.

Post-procedure, we will provide you with detailed care instructions and follow-up appointments to monitor your healing process and prevent any complications from gum disease. Our goal is to ensure that you feel comfortable and confidently adjust to your new smile.

Understanding Dental Implants

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a remarkable tooth replacement option offered in modern dentistry. They are essentially artificial tooth roots, typically made of biocompatible materials like titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support implant-supported crowns. These dental implants provide a stable and permanent foundation for prosthetic teeth, such as a crown, bridge, or even an implant-supported denture designed to replace an entire arch of teeth. Dental implants are a cost-effective solution and improve the patient’s oral health.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting tooth replacement option. With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last for many years or even a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene practices, and avoiding habits like smoking can help extend the lifespan of your dental implants. Implants are a cost-effective solution, given their potential to last a lifetime. It is important to take care of your dental health to prevent decay and gum disease, which can impact your overall well-being.
