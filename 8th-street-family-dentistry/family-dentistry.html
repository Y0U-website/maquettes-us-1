Regular Cleanings

At 8th Street Dentistry in Traverse City, MI, regular dental cleanings are designed to be both thorough and comfortable, helping patients maintain a healthy, radiant smile year-round. During each visit, the friendly hygiene team begins with a gentle yet detailed cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing can’t reach. They carefully polish the teeth for a smooth, refreshed feel and may take digital X-rays or perform a quick oral health screening. Every step is explained clearly, so patients always know what to expect and can feel at ease throughout their appointment.

A Healthy Smile

Beyond just cleaning teeth, the team at 8th Street Dentistry focuses on prevention and education. Hygienists take time to discuss personalized home care tips—like brushing techniques, flossing methods, and product recommendations—to help patients keep their smiles in top shape between visits. The atmosphere is friendly, relaxed, and supportive, making it easy for patients of all ages to look forward to their regular checkups. With each visit, the 8th Street Dentistry team helps ensure that healthy habits and professional care work hand in hand to protect and enhance every smile.
