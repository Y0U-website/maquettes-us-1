The practice

Beautiful teeth do more than just enhance your smile, they restore your confidence. Discover the smile of your dreams.

We are a locally owned dental practice, caring for the families of Salem, NH, and the surrounding Rockingham County communities.

We provide comprehensive family dentistry at an affordable price. From dental hygiene cleanings and professional exams to root canals and reliable restoration treatments, we have all the tools to help you maintain optimal oral health and a beautiful smile.

Our dental team goes the extra mile to ensure each patient has a positive dental experience. Our office creates a soothing atmosphere and is equipped with advanced dental technology. Dr. Arsenault and his team perform each treatment with your comfort in mind and approach each treatment with a caring, gentle touch.

Dr. Peter Arsenault is a graduate of The University of Massachusetts where he received his Bachelor of Science and Master of Science degrees in plastics engineering along with a minor in chemistry. Dr. Arsenault worked in industry as an engineer before entering dental school. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine (one of the best dental schools in…

Upon graduation, Dr. Arsenault completed a General Practice Residency at Tufts New England Medical Center and then went on to private practice. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of Sports Dentistry, and the Society of Plastics Engineers along with the Society of Materials Engineers.

Dr. Arsenault has shown great interest in product development and intellectual property law. He holds several United States Patents and patent-pending products relating to the dental field.

Since 2005, Dr. Arsenault has taught at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and is currently a professor and the Division Head of Operative Dentistry in the Department of Comprehensive Care.
