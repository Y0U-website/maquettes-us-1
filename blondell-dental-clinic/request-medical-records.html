Special Circumstances:

  • If the patient is incapacitated, or unable to sign for him/herself: the patient representative must enter their relationship to the patient so that we can determine authority to request the records.
  • If the patient is deceased: the court-appointed administrator or executer of the state can request the medical records. A copy of the death certificate will be required to verify death if the patient did not expire in our facilities.
  • Other circumstances additional documentation may be required depending on the situation. For questions, please contact 718-920-4921 .

Delivery Expectations

In accordance with New York State law, requests are processed within ten days after the authorization is received.

Amendments

HIPAA provides you the right to amend your medical record. If you believe the information in your record is incorrect, you can request an amendment. A healthcare provider will review and respond to your request. A request to amend records can be filed in person by visiting one of our Montefiore Health Information Management (HIM) locations, where a member of our team can coordinate the amendment process.

If you have questions about your request or need assistance, contact the Health Information Management Department at 718-920-4921 .
