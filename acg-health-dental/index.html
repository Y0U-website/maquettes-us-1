The practice

At ACG Health , we provide high-quality dental care in our state-of-the-art, five-chair dental facility located in Sharon Hill, PA . Our clinic proudly serves individuals across Southeastern Pennsylvania , offering affordable and accessible dental services to adults aged 18 and above regardless of dental insurance.

Whether you’re coming in for a routine exam or more complex treatment, our experienced dental team is here to ensure you receive personalized, compassionate care in a comfortable setting.

We believe that everyone deserves access to great dental care, which is why we welcome patients regardless of insurance status . We also offer a sliding fee scale based on household income , helping make essential dental services more affordable for our community.

Appointments are available Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM . To schedule your visit, simply call our Sharon Hill dental office directly . Our friendly staff is here to assist you every step of the way.
