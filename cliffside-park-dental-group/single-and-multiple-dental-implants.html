New Teeth in Three Phases

Phase 1: Consultation

You’ll start your dental implant treatment with a consultation. During this appointment, we’ll discuss your smile goals and how dental implants can help. We’ll also complete an evaluation and any diagnostics we’ll need to plan your surgery. Generally, we will be able to attach a temporary dental crown or bridge the same day our periodontist places your dental implant(s).

Phase 2: Surgery

Our partnering periodontist will come to our office on the day of your surgery to place your dental implant(s). Because of the level of training and specialization, this procedure is efficient and provides the ideal position for optimizing the final function and aesthetics of your tooth. This surgical appointment may also include tooth extractions and/or bone or soft tissue grafts to ensure the long-term success of your new smile. Our periodontist specializes in these procedures as well.

Phase 3: Restoration

You’ll need to heal for a few months after your surgery. During this time, your dental implant will fuse to your natural bone and your gum tissue will heal over the post or around the temporary. When this phase is complete, you’ll return to our office to have your final dental crown or bridge attached. This restoration is fabricated from porcelain material and color-matched to look completely natural. You’ll be able to use this tooth like normal and won’t be able to distinguish it from the rest of your smile.
