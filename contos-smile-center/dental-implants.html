Aftercare for Dental Implants

Maintain optimal oral hygiene practices to preserve the health of your dental crown and contribute to overall oral well-being. Regular brushing, flossing, and rinsing are essential components of a robust oral care routine.

Schedule biannual appointments at our Chicago office for routine cleanings and exams . Frequently Asked Questions

Does dental implant surgery hurt?

How long does dental implant placement take?

The duration of dental implant placement varies but can typically be completed in two appointments. The initial visit involves affixing the implant fixture to the jawbone, followed by a healing period of three to six months. The second visit includes attaching the customized dental crown and new teeth, resulting in a fully restored smile.

How many teeth can you have on one implant?

Dental implants can support individual crowns, meaning one implant for each missing tooth. However, implants can also anchor bridges or dentures, allowing for a full set of securely attached, functional teeth with as few as four strategically placed implants.

How much does most dental insurance pay for implants?

The coverage for dental implants varies among insurance plans. While some plans may cover a portion of the costs, others may not provide coverage for cosmetic dental implant procedures. During your consultation with your dentist or oral surgeon, you’ll receive a detailed breakdown of the costs associated with your treatment plan.
