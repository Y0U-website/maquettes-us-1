The practice

Dr. Cenami brings her welcoming personality to help make the patient experience an easy and enjoyable time. She enjoys all aspects of general dental care , but her favorite part of dentistry has long been the time spent with patients making them feel comfortable and well cared for.

Dr. Cenami spends her free time exploring her new home in Boston or traveling both near and far for new adventures and experiences.

Dr. Nahata brings to the practice a genuine warmth and caring toward the people around him as well as a commitment to excellence. He provides all aspects of dental treatment , including prosthetic , endodontic and restorative procedures . Adults and children alike appreciate his cheerful demeanor and warm sense of humor.

Dr. Nahata earned his undergraduate degree at the University of Florida and his doctorate in dentistry at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He has practiced on the North Shore and in the Merrimack Valley and is a member of the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, and the American Academy for General Dentistry.

Dr. Nahata has taken numerous continuing education courses. He has extensive knowledge of dental materials and has published articles in national magazines regarding dental aesthetics and cosmetic dentistry.

In addition to being an avid sports fan, Dr. Nahata enjoys tennis, travel and spending time with his wife and three children.
