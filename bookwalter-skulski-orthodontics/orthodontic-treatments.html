Clear aligners.

Free initial consultation Smile Confidently. Clear aligners have become an essential part of orthodontic treatment, offering a less conspicuous and more comfortable alternative to traditional braces. According to the American Association of Orthodontists, clear aligners work best for straightening teeth and fixing mild to moderate dental conditions, such as crossbites. Like traditional braces, aligners are designed to move teeth a little at a time, providing patients with a healthy and beautiful smile.

However, clear aligners may not be for everyone, especially those with more severe cases which may require more complex treatment. Overall, clear aligners have revolutionized orthodontic treatment, providing patients with a more aesthetically pleasing and convenient option for achieving a perfect smile.

Permanent Retainers.

Made to last.

Free initial consultation Permanent retainers play a crucial role in maintaining your smile after orthodontic treatment. A permanent retainer consists of a thin, custom-fitted wire that is bonded to the tongue side of upper or lower teeth.

These retainers are essential in preventing teeth from shifting back to their original positions after braces or aligners have been removed, ensuring long-lasting results.
