About Us

What Sets Our Eau Claire Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Meet Your Dentists

A Group of Skilled and Compassionate Professionals in One Dental Office.

Dr. Julia Machgan

Background

Dr. Julia Machgan, born and raised in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Wisconsin Madison, earning a Bachelor of Science degree in Human Development and Family Studies, as well as Psychology. She then pursued her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree at the University of New England College of Dental Medicine in Portland, Maine, where she developed a strong foundation in comprehensive dental care.

Dental Style

Dr. Julia practices patient-centered dentistry, ensuring each individual receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs and preferences.

Fun Facts

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Julia enjoys traveling, attending concerts, skiing, and spending time with her friends and family.

Dr. Kyle Ronquist

Background

Dr. Ronquist grew up in Petoskey, Michigan before attending the University of Michigan College of Engineering for his undergraduate studies. After working as an engineer for a year at Stryker Instruments, he decided to go back to school to study dentistry. He returned to Ann Arbor where he graduated from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2017. After graduation, he moved to Eau Claire, WI to start his work for All Family Dental.

Dental Style

Dr. Kyle is a firm believer in evidence-based dentistry, which means using research and data to drive his decision making. Additionally, he believes in patient-centered care, where the patient’s oral health and desired outcomes are the primary focuses.

Fun Facts

Other than his love for teeth, Dr. Kyle also enjoys hiking, skiing, and mountain biking. He is married to his wife Kelly, and together they have two children.
