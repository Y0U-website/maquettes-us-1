Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots, placed securely into your jawbone. Once healed, they support beautiful, custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures—restoring the look, feel, and function of your natural teeth.   • Restores bite strength and chewing ability
  • No slipping or adhesives like traditional dentures
  • Looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth
  • Supports better oral and overall health
