Appliances Dr. Lowe May Recommend

Every child’s treatment plan is built around what their teeth and jaws actually need. Here are the tools we use most often at 540 Orthodontics.

  • Braces Brackets and a wire guide teeth into place with steady, gentle pressure. Kids pick their own band colors at every visit.
  • Clear Aligners Removable clear trays for kids and teens who want a low profile option they can take out to eat, brush, and play sports.
  • Expander Gently widens a narrow upper jaw to create room for adult teeth and correct crossbites while your child is still growing.
  • Bite Correction A fixed appliance that guides the upper and lower jaws into a healthier relationship, often without headgear or extractions.
  • Face Mask Worn at home and overnight to encourage forward growth of an underdeveloped upper jaw, most effective in younger children.
  • Orthognathic Surgery For significant jaw differences, Dr. Lowe coordinates with an oral surgeon once growth is complete. Rare, and always the last option we consider.
