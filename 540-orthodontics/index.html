The practice

Dr. Lowe founded 540 Orthodontics so he could practice the way he thinks orthodontics should work: one doctor who knows your case, enough time in the chair to answer real questions, and no corporate script to follow.

He is board-certified, which means he went through the voluntary examination process beyond residency that only a portion of orthodontists complete. He cares for families across Fredericksburg, Spotsylvania, and Stafford from the office on Courthouse Road.
