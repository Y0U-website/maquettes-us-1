  • About 540 Orthodontics | Dr. Lowe | Fredericksburg About Us Dr. Ronald Lowe D.D.S., M.S., ABO-Board Certified About Dr. Lowe Dr. Lowe earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, where he graduated with honors at the top of his class. Following dental school, he continued his advanced training at the University at Buffalo, earning his Master’s degree in Orthodontics in 2020.
  • During his postgraduate training, Dr. Lowe served as Chief Resident, published multiple peer-reviewed articles, and presented his research internationally, contributing to the advancement of the field of orthodontics.
  • Dr. Lowe is deeply passionate about dentistry and orthodontics and is committed to lifelong learning. He regularly collaborates with local dental study clubs alongside general dentists, attends national orthodontic meetings to stay at the forefront of innovation, and dedicates time to serving families affected by pediatric cancer through his nonprofit organization, Saving Smiles.

Outside The Office

Community & Interests

Dr. Lowe is passionate about supporting the local community and especially enjoys cheering on patients at sporting events and theater performances whenever he can. When he’s not in the office or on the sidelines, he enjoys hiking and has completed 20 of the 46 Adirondack High Peaks a goal he continues to work toward.

Sundays with the Bills

Patients also quickly learn that Dr. Lowe is a devoted Buffalo Bills fan. On Sundays, he can often be found enthusiastically cheering (and occasionally negotiating with the television) in hopes of a Josh Allen touchdown.
