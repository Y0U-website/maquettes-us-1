Your Teen’s Treatment Options

By the teen years, most adult teeth are in and Dr. Lowe can correct alignment and bite in a single phase. Here is what that usually looks like.

  • Braces The most predictable way to handle rotation, crowding, and spacing. Nothing to remember to wear, and teens choose fresh band colors at every visit.
  • Clear Aligners Nearly invisible trays that come out for meals, brushing, sports, and photos. Results depend on wearing them 20 to 22 hours a day, so they suit teens who stay consistent.
  • Bite Correction Straight teeth are only half the job. A fixed appliance worn alongside braces or aligners brings an overbite or underbite into a healthier position while there is still growth to work with.
