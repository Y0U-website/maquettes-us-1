The practice

The practice recently underwent significant renovations to now offer the latest in low dose digital x-rays, dental software, intra-oral camera s, and the worlds quietest electric dental drills for a more relaxing dental experience.

Parking is available at our practice, and we are also located adjacent to Concord Commuter Rail Station.

We welcome new patients, and can offer a second opinion if needed.

We are unique in that each treatment room has its own completely independent isolated air treatment system. This technology kills airborne viruses, bacteria, mold, dust and pollen. Each room will be all yours, not shared with any other patients or their air!

Enjoy routine dental examinations with the latest digital xrays with 40 % less radiation, dental software, and intra-oral photography allowing you to see, what we see!

We explain to you the pro s and con s of each material and can provide other options also.

We can provide many options for missing, discolored, crowded, spaced, and unesthetic teeth. We have many options for crowns, and we believe in explaining the pro s and con s to reach the best decision for your smile.

We have a local certified Master Technician nearby, we never send our work overseas.

Les soins

We can provide implant secured dentures for a more confident bite and smile. We have many options when it comes to dentures from simple to high end dentures. We also believe in only using local labs here in the USA.
