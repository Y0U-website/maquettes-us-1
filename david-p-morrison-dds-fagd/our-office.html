Payment Options

We accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover credit cards as well as cash and personal checks. We accept Delta Dental, and Cigna insurance plans. Find Out If You Or a Loved One Qualifies for Our Veteran Program

Morrison Dental Care is proud to offer our “Make a Veteran Smile Again” program, founded to rebuild smiles lost while serving in the United States military. This program is designed to help veterans boost their confidence. We welcome veterans who we serve to establish as patients of the practice; however, the program is designed for a one-time restoration. Call or request an appointment today to see if you qualify.
