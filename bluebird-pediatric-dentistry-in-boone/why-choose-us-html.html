Why Choose Us?

At our office, we don’t just fix teeth; we are building a home for your smile!

Keeping your child’s comfort in mind, we also offer services in private treatment rooms with a TV on the ceiling and the option for dimmed lighting for an optimal patient experience. We encourage active participation by parents and love that they can see how gentle and caring we are with their child. Parents can be present during their children’s visits so they can offer support and comfort to their child and benefit from the education we provide. Keeping children’s health in mind, our office is mercury free and latex free.

Our compassionate team provides high-quality dental care to kids of all ages that is personalized. At Bluebird, we strive to create memorable and fun dental experiences where you and your child are treated as part of our own family. Whatever your dental needs, come visit us for the kind of quality, convenient care that means healthy teeth and gums and beautiful smiles!

Your children are so much more than just patients in our office, they are part of our Bluebird family. It is our goal to do more than just fix teeth, and we invite you to join us on that journey! We want to make a difference in the world and create a whole new generation who likes coming to the dentist and understand that value. We strive to make a positive impact on your lives. We feel it is our responsibility to make the place where we live and work better than we found it. We hope you will join us in spreading smiles throughout the community.
