The practice

Every treatment at AB Orthodontics is crafted with precision, using advanced technology and meticulous attention to detail to achieve beautiful smiles.

Dr. Darrick A. Brown and the AB Orthodontics team love straightening teeth to change smiles and transform lives, one person at a time.

Ours is a family-oriented and welcoming practice, where we treat everyone with respect and compassion. Beyond your orthodontic needs, we care about you, and feel privileged to help you achieve the beautifully confident smile you deserve!

Dr. Brown uses advanced orthodontic techniques to gently shift teeth, improving smiles and oral health for children, teens, and adults. Your unique treatment experience may include traditional metal braces, clear (ceramic) brackets, or clear aligners.

We welcome the opportunity to meet and speak with you. Please contact our Oakmont or Pittsburgh office and schedule a complimentary consultation with Dr. Brown and our team. No pressure or sales pitch, we promise! Instead, an honest conversation and personalized treatment plan to get you to the radiant, one-of-a-kind smile you desire.

After our first chat about what you’re hoping for, I’ll come up with a plan that fits you perfectly. We’ll go over everything - what I recommend, why I think it’s a great fit, and any other options you have. We’ll make sure you’re in the loop, answer all your questions, and decide on a plan together - one that works for you and your life, not just your teeth!

The best part of my job is watching you go from your first visit to that moment when you’re rocking your new smile. You’ll come in as a patient, but you’ll leave as part of the family."

Dr. Brown completed his undergraduate degree at Indiana University of Pennsylvania. He then attended the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine.
