Drs. Kellen and Ramona Bosma…

Drs. Kellen and Ramona have been together for 19 years and married for 17. They met in their last year of undergraduate study at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. They had been living in the St. Louis area for about 11 years while going to dental school and later practicing as associates at separate practices. They hoped to one day live near family and practice dentistry together. After much searching their hope became a reality when they found Drs. Chulick and Currey’s practice.

We look forward to celebrating your smile with you!
