Orthodontics in Waynesboro, VA

Part of having a beautiful smile is having a smile that is properly aligned. Not only does alignment affect your appearance, it also affects how your teeth function. Where orthodontics is concerned, comfort and beauty must go hand-in-hand for treatment to be a success!

Dr. Steve Browning is a general dentist with extensive orthodontic experience. He worked for six years in an orthodontic practice before relocating to the valley and joining Browning Family Dentistry. His skills and expertise bring convenient orthodontic treatment options to our Waynesboro, VA dental office.

ClearCorrect Clear Aligners

For older teens and adults wishing to improve the appearance of their smiles or who suffer from minor misalignment issues such as overlapping teeth, gaps, or teeth that appear out of place, ClearCorrect provides you with discreet and effective treatment options.

With ClearCorrect, you will wear clear plastic aligner trays and change them out approximately every two weeks. Each tray will bring you closer to your ideal smile without the hassle of brackets and wires.

Because you can remove your aligner trays to eat, brush, and floss your teeth, you will experience unsurpassed convenience during your treatment. They are also smooth and comfortable, so you will not need orthodontic wax to protect your delicate tissue.

The cost and length of treatment will depend on your orthodontic needs. An evaluation with Dr. Browning can determine if clear aligners are right for you.

Post-Treatment Orthodontic Retainers

No matter which route you choose for your treatment, you will need to wear an orthodontic retainer to prevent your teeth from relapsing to their former positions. We create quality, custom orthodontic retainers so that your new, straighter smile will last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I too old for braces?

Many people associate orthodontics with childhood, so it’s natural to wonder if you are too old for orthodontics once you reach adulthood. For some people, orthodontic treatment was not an option in their youth, and others may have experienced orthodontic relapse because they did not wear their retainers after treatment.

At Browning Family Dentistry, we offer full orthodontic services for patients of all ages. Dr. Steve Browning received advanced training in orthodontics and spent six years in an orthodontic dental practice. He offers traditional orthodontics, Invisalign, ClearCorrect, and Powerprox Six Month Braces to meet the needs of all of our patients.

If you are an adult looking to improve the appearance of your smile, you may wish to explore options such as Invisalign and Six Month Braces because they are extremely convenient.

Invisalign and ClearCorrect Both systems use clear, plastic aligner trays to gradually move your teeth into an ideal position. Your clear aligners are removable so that you can eat and clean your teeth normally. Treatment times and costs vary depending on the treatment you require.

Six Month Braces Powerprox Six Month Braces uses a clear memory wire and orthodontic brackets to apply low force to your teeth and move your teeth quickly. Treatment time is six months, on average, and is often more cost-effective than traditional orthodontics.
