Dr. Marissa Spruell

Dr. Spruell is a dedicated family dentist who loves caring for patients of all ages, from toddlers visiting for their first check-up to adults seeking advanced dental treatments. With a warm and friendly approach, Dr. Spruell ensures every visit is a positive experience, creating healthy, beautiful smiles for the entire family.

After earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Howard University College of Dentistry,

Dr. Spruell has gained extensive experience in both pediatric and adult dentistry. She specializes in a wide range of services, including preventive care and restorative treatments, using the latest technology to deliver exceptional results. Understanding that every patient is unique, Dr.Spruell tailors treatment plans to meet individual needs and emphasizes patient education, helping families develop lifelong habits for optimal oral health. Outside the office, Dr. Spruell enjoys spending time outdoors, traveling, reading, and trying new food and restaurants.

Dr. Spruell and the team at Confident Smiles are committed to creating a welcoming environment where patients feel at ease, making dental care simple, convenient, and stress-free for children and adults alike
