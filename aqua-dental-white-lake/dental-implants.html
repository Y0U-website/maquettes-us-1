The Dental Implant Process at Aqua Dental

Step 1 – Consultation & Planning: We evaluate your teeth, gums, and bone structure and create a personalized implant treatment plan.

Step 2 – Implant Placement: The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia for your comfort.

Step 3 – Healing & Integration: Over the following weeks, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.

Step 4 – Crown Placement: Once healed, a custom-crafted dental crown is attached to complete your new tooth — perfectly matched to the color and shape of your natural teeth.
