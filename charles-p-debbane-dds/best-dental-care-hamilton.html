Family Friendly Dentists

Dr. Debbane and their professional dental staff provide comprehensive dental services for the whole family. We are family-oriented and enjoy seeing patients of all ages. Our office strives to serve as your dental home to help you maintain excellent oral health throughout your lifespan. We are invested in the future of your smile.

Personalized Oral Care

Charles P. Debbane, D.D.S. believe in providing personalized care to all of their patients. We use digital X-rays and an intraoral camera to help develop a personalized treatment plan just for you. We carefully listen to your wants and needs and explain solutions to help you achieve your goal. We’re here to provide you with the smile you’ve always wanted.

Pain-Free Experience

You don’t have to fear going to the dentist anymore! From our calming and comfortable atmosphere to our friendly and competent dental staff, you will feel relaxed and at ease for the duration of your appointment. Our dental staff is understanding and patient. Once you’re one of our patients, you’ll love going to the dentist!

Updated Facilities

We have a newly renovated waiting room, private operatories, and flat-screen televisions in each operatory, which can be used for entertainment, to help diagnose patients, and to discuss treatment plans.

We Accept Many Insurance Plans

Our office accepts many different dental insurance plans. Please call our office to see if your plan is in our network.
