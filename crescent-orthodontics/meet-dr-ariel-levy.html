Meet Dr. Ariel Levy

Get to Know Dr. Ariel Levy

Dr. Ariel Levy has been practicing orthodontics for more than a decade. Dr. Levy specializes in advanced clear aligner cases, conventional metal and ceramic braces for adults and children, and the use of mini implants for difficult tooth movement (TADs).

Free Time and Hobbies

Dr. Levy is supported and loved by a great family, including his wife and two dogs (yes, they’re corgis).

Together, they enjoy traveling and learning languages. Dr. Levy is fluent in English, Spanish, and Hebrew, and is also studying Russian, Portuguese, Mandarin, and Arabic.

Education

Dr. Levy earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

He then completed his residency specializing in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at Saint Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, New York City.

Chester also completed his education. He successfully graduated from agility training and is now a certified Good Boy.
