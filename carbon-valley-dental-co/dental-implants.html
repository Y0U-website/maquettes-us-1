The Ideal Candidate for Dental Implants

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. You may be a strong match if you:

During your consultation, we’ll walk through your health history, lifestyle, and goals to determine the best plan for you — with complete transparency and support.

  • Have enough healthy bone to support an implant (or qualify for bone grafting)
  • Are in generally good health and free from chronic gum infections
  • Don&#x27;t smoke, or are willing to pause during healing
  • Want a durable, long-lasting alternative to bridges or dentures
  • Are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene and regular dental visits

What to Expect During Your Implant Procedure

All procedures are performed in-house, from surgery to final restoration — so no referrals are needed. Dr. Walker is a locally trusted expert and hospital-trained dentist who’s part of your community, so you can trust that your smile is in the best hands.
