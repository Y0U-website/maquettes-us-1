The practice

Dr. Emmerich, a Marquette University School of Dentistry alumna and Dawson Scholar, brings her expertise back to her hometown, where she delivers comfortable, state-of-the-art dental care. A member of the American Dental Association and the American Dental Political Action Committee, she is dedicated to staying at the forefront of dental advancements.

Dr. Jacomet, a graduate of the Marquette School of Dentistry and a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, offers expert dental care backed by extensive training in complex dental issues. With his rich experience and numerous accolades, patients can trust they are in capable hands for all their dental needs.

Dr. Dwan, a Marquette University School of Dentistry graduate, has significantly enhanced his expertise with extensive post-graduate training, earning accolades such as a Fellowship in the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and a Dawson Academy scholarship. He offers customized treatments ranging from general dentistry to cosmetics, root canals, and implants.
