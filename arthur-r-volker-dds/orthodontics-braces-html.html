Invisalign Clear Braces

Are you looking for an orthodontic treatment that nobody else will notice? We can create a series of custom-made Invisalign trays to discreetly treat certain alignment issues. They consist of clear, smooth plastic that closely hugs the teeth, making them comfortable as well as practically invisible. Invisalign doesn’t get in the way of meals or oral hygiene; just take the trays out and continue your daily routine as normal!

Six Month Smiles

Traditional orthodontics can take up to three years, but adults can make certain corrections in much less time thanks to Six Month Smiles. This system uses special clear braces that gently move only the most visible teeth in as the name suggests a mere six months! Like Invisalign, it blends in well with the rest of the smile, and the treatment is often far more comfortable compared to regular braces. Get in touch with us today to see if Six Month Smiles is the right solution for you.
