The practice

Our approach is to treat the whole patient. That is why we explain to you what you can expect during your visit and what to expect afterwards.

We treat patients of all ages, including infants. We also treat handicapped children and adults, always giving them reassurance and gentle care.

Our oral surgery services include wisdom tooth and other tooth extractions, dental implants, oral biopsies and any issues involving the mouth and jaw.

We are located in western Suffolk County, we serve all of Long Island, including Commack, Kings Park, East Northport, Hauppauge, Smithtown and Deer Park. Contact us if you are looking for an oral surgeon.

COVID-19 We have increased our infection control following Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and American Dental Association protocols. Our surgery rooms have state-of-the-art ultraviolet light sterilization equipment, our waiting room has a HEPA air purifier.
