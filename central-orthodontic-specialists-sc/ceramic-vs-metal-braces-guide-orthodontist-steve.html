FAQ

Q: Are ceramic braces as effective as metal braces for straightening teeth? A: Yes, ceramic braces can be highly effective, especially when planned by a specialist, and they use the same core mechanics as traditional systems.

Q: Do ceramic braces stain easily? A: Ceramic brackets resist discoloration, but ligatures and edges can stain if you frequently drink coffee, tea, or soda, so good rinsing and brushing habits help a lot.

Q: Who is a great candidate for ceramic braces in Stevens Point? A: Adults and teens who want a lower visibility look and who are ready to follow good hygiene routines often love ceramic braces.

Q: How do I choose between ceramic braces and aligners? A: It comes down to your bite goals, compliance, and lifestyle, and our team will show you side by side options during your exam so you can pick a clear choice with confidence.
