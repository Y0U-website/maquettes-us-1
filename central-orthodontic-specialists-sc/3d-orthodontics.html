Benefits of 3D Orthodontics

Choosing 3D orthodontics at Central Orthodontic Specialists offers a range of benefits that traditional methods can’t match:

  • Greater Accuracy: Intraoral scanning provides a highly accurate digital model of your teeth, improving the precision of your treatment plan.
  • More Comfortable: With 3D imaging, there’s no need for messy, uncomfortable dental impressions. The scan is quick, painless, and more comfortable for patients of all ages.
  • Faster Treatment: The accuracy of 3D orthodontics often leads to shorter treatment times, as fewer adjustments are needed along the way.
  • Enhanced Visualization: With 3D models, you’ll be able to see a detailed image of your teeth and understand the expected outcomes of your treatment, giving you greater confidence in the process.

3D Orthodontics for Braces and Clear Aligners

At COS, we integrate 3D orthodontics into both braces and clear aligner treatments, ensuring that you receive the most precise care possible.

  • For Braces : 3D imaging helps us design customized treatment plans that predict how your teeth will move over time, allowing us to make precise adjustments throughout your treatment.
  • For Clear Aligners : When you choose clear aligners, 3D scans are used to create your custom aligners. The digital model ensures that each aligner fits perfectly, moving your teeth step-by-step toward the ideal result.
