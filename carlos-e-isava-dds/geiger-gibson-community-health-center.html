Geiger Gibson Community Health Center

Our pharmacy is open to YOU! You do not have to be a patient at Geiger Gibson to fill and refill prescriptions.

Our pharmacists take the time to help you find ways to make your medications more affordable. Plus you can pick up at our location or we can mail prescriptions to you for free!

Talk to your provider about filling your prescriptions at our pharmacy! Our pharmacy is listed as Geiger HC Pharmacy in the EPIC electronic medical record system.

You can also call our pharmacy for assistance at (617) 533-2295 .

Learn more about the benefits of using our pharmacy

Parking is free and the health center is located within walking distance from the JFK/UMASS Red Line Station.

Need help with transportation to an appointment? Please let us know when you call to make an appointment if you will need help with a ride.

Need help when we are closed? Call the health center after hours or on holidays at (617) 288-1140 . We have a nurse or a doctor available to talk to you when the health center is closed.
