The practice

Geiger Gibson Community Health Center is located at 250 Mount Vernon Street in Dorchester, Massachusetts . You can find kind and convenient care for your whole family at our community health center.

MassHealth for all ages, Health Safety Net, and most insurance plans accepted. For patients without insurance, we can help you apply and offer a sliding scale of fees. Discounts are available based on family size and income.

We have a Quest Lab and a community pharmacy at Geiger Gibson! You can get your labs and pick up medications all in one location.

Medical services are now open at Geiger Gibson! New primary care patients are welcome. Same day appointments are available.

Our pharmacy is open to YOU! You do not have to be a patient at Geiger Gibson to fill and refill prescriptions.

Our pharmacists take the time to help you find ways to make your medications more affordable. Plus you can pick up at our location or we can mail prescriptions to you for free!

Talk to your provider about filling your prescriptions at our pharmacy! Our pharmacy is listed as Geiger HC Pharmacy in the EPIC electronic medical record system.

Parking is free and the health center is located within walking distance from the JFK/UMASS Red Line Station.
