Frequently Asked Questions

A pediatric dentist completes 2–3 additional years of specialty training after dental school, focused exclusively on the oral health and development of infants, children, and teens.

Board-certified pediatric dentists have completed advanced written and oral examinations through the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, demonstrating a high level of expertise and commitment to ongoing education.

We recommend scheduling the first visit when the first tooth appears or by your child’s first birthday.

Most children benefit from a dental visit every six months to support preventive care and early detection of concerns.

Staying positive helps! Use simple, reassuring language like “The dentist helps keep your teeth healthy.” Avoid scary words — our team uses gentle, child-friendly explanations during visits.
