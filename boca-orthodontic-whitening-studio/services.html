WHAT TO EXPECT ON YOUR FIRST VISIT

The Ultimate Orthodontic, Whitening + Aesthetic Consultation

Welcome to BOCA. You made it to our studio for your Smile Consultation appointment! Check in with our BOCA Concierge and enjoy a beverage while you relax in our resort-inspired Lounge. You likely won’t wait long, if at all, but you may not want to leave.

Our Concierge Assistant will escort you from the Lounge into our Imaging Studio to obtain photographs and radiographs. Your imaging is a tool for Dr. Erin to diagnose and educate. These images also allow us to monitor progress through treatment.

Dr. Erin will review your imaging, check out your smile and chat one-on-one to get to know you and your smile goals. Once she understands your desires and lifestyle, she will design and present possible customized treatment options available to you.

We absolutely love questions. It’s very important that you ask and we listen to your concerns and questions. It will make you feel more comfortable and confident in the process. And it will help us get to know you, your smile and your desires better!

Wherever you choose to go, feel out the vibe of the squad and the space. Similar to a first date, your consultation will allow you to see if you want to continue the relationship. We are here to ensure you have the best smile journey possible!
