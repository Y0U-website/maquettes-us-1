when to schedule your child’s first visit

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that every child have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. By age 7, your child’s smile gives us a clear view of how everything is developing.

At this stage, Dr. Erin can identify developing concerns early, monitor growth, and determine the ideal timing for treatment—often helping make future orthodontic care simpler, healthier, and more predictable. Even if no treatment is needed right away, you’ll leave with peace of mind and a personalized plan for your child’s growing smile.

Sometimes it’s simply a check-in to ensure everything is on track, while helping your child get comfortable and excited about future care. Other times, it’s a gentle, proactive treatment to prevent bigger issues down the road.

At BOCA, we focus on early insight, observation, and intentional care. It’s not about rushing treatment—it’s about guiding growth, preventing issues, and creating space for a confident, healthy smile.

THE BOCA APPROACH

We believe orthodontics should be just as much about the experience as it is about the outcome. That’s why we always put your child first—creating a calm, encouraging, fun environment where they feel safe, seen, heard, and involved every step of the way.

Through Dr. Erin’s signature hands-on Little Smile Artists philosophy, children become active participants in designing their own smile. They don’t just leave with a smile they love—they enjoy every step of creating it.

Every Little Smile Artist Has Their Own Style

Every child’s smile deserves a treatment plan that’s as unique as they are.

Invisalign® offers a comfortable, modern approach to orthodontic treatment using a series of custom-made clear aligners designed to gently guide growing smiles as children develop and help reveal their most confident smiles.

At BOCA, we combine advanced technology with a highly personalized approach to create a treatment plan customized to your child’s unique smile, growth, and lifestyle.

As a national lecturer for Invisalign®, Dr. Erin is recognized for her enthusiasm for modern orthodontic treatment, innovative technology, and experience-driven care that she brings to every patient. By incorporating the latest advancements in clear aligner therapy, she creates an orthodontic experience that is as seamless and enjoyable as it is effective.

Using 3D digital scans and advanced treatment planning software, Dr. Erin carefully maps each stage of tooth movement to create predictable, healthy results. Invisalign® for children can address crowding, spacing, narrow arches, developing bite concerns, and create room for incoming permanent teeth—all while allowing kids to eat their favorite foods, brush and floss with ease, and continue enjoying the activities they love.

Nervous about your little one wearing Invisalign clear aligners? Ask us about our Invisalign Kid & Teen Guarantee!

Every growing smile is unique, which is why we offer multiple expander options tailored to your child’s needs, anatomy, and lifestyle. Whether your child is best suited for a removable appliance, a traditional fixed expander, or the innovative Invisalign® Palatal Expander, Dr. Erin will discuss the options and provide a recommendation on the one that will provide the healthiest, most predictable results for your child.
