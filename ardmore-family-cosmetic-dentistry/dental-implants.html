Dental Implants Ardmore, PA

A dental implant is a small, titanium screw surgically placed in the jawbone below the gums. Dental implants are restorative dentistry treatments that can be used to replace one or more missing teeth. They can anchor a tooth crown , dental bridge , or denture and offer long-lasting dental restorations. Since they are permanently secured to your jawbone, they help restorations function similarly to natural teeth, allowing patients full chewing capacity. They also do not come with any dietary restrictions.

Dr. Jeffrey Bellisario , Dr. Evan Berson, and Dr. David Robbins restore dental implants and implant-secured dental restorations in Ardmore, PA. They work with oral surgeons to place the implants. Then, our team constructs custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures that the implants will permanently secure. Implants offer a restoration that looks and functions close to your natural teeth.

The Benefits of Dental Implants in Ardmore, PA

Compared to traditional restorations, dental implants offer multiple advantages to our patients. We recommend dental implants above all other treatment options to replace missing teeth.

Enhanced Aesthetics

When you lose permanent teeth, the jawbone begins to break down at the site where you lost the tooth root. Without replacing the tooth root, the deterioration continues. In that area, the face sags and appears sunken. It can also impact your speech patterns, depending on where the missing tooth is in the mouth. All of these things can greatly impact your self-esteem. Implants eliminate the sagging appearance and give you a natural smile and appearance.

One of the reasons implants are ideal is that they closely match the look, color, and shape of your natural teeth. They’re the most natural-looking and feeling tooth replacement option. When you replace missing teeth with dental implants, they will blend into your smile seamlessly, so you can feel confident smiling and laughing in public again.

Oral Health and Functionality

Implants feel the most like your natural teeth. They’re the only solution that replaces the tooth root. This stops jawbone deterioration and gives you a stable option. You don’t have to worry about dental implants slipping or falling out when you eat or speak. Implants give you back close to 100 % of your bite function, as opposed to around 40 % for traditional dentures. You don’t have to remove any foods from your diet because you can process all of them.

They don’t require any extra maintenance either. You don’t have to remove them; they can always stay in your mouth. All you have to do is brush and floss as you would normally. Solutions like dentures require special cleaning devices and soaks for peak performance. You don’t have that extra time and cost issues with dental implants.

Likewise, traditional tooth replacement methods can incur further future costs because they don’t restore jaw function or halt deterioration. Your jawbone continues deteriorating and changing with solutions like dental bridges or traditional dentures. You’ll have to spend money to reline or replace these solutions so that your restoration works as well as possible.

Treatment with Dental Implants

Before dental implant treatment in our Ardmore, PA office, we ensure that patients have enough bone tissue in their jaw to support their implants. If they do not have enough bone tissue to support implants, bone grafting is an option. Our office works with a local oral surgeon to place the implants. The surgeon inserts the titanium dental implants into your jawbone beneath the gums. During a process called osseointegration, the implants fuse with the jawbone. This may take anywhere from three to six months.

Once we determine that your implants have healed, we will place an abutment and temporary crown. The abutment attaches to the implant and connects it to the restoration. We will fit you for a temporary crown until your permanent restoration is created.

To create your permanent restoration, we will take dental impressions of your mouth that can be used to make your crown, bridge, or denture. The restoration will match your natural tooth’s exact color, shape, and size. Once your permanent restoration is complete, you can enjoy your new, aesthetically pleasing, and functional smile.

Same-Day Dental Implants

We offer same-day dental implants for individuals who need to replace an entire arch of missing teeth. Also known as All-On-Four, this treatment uses four or more implants to secure a denture. We place the implants strategically along the arch at specific angles to ensure we maximize support for the restoration.

Patients can enjoy their new teeth immediately, as we can temporarily restore them on the same day the implants are placed. We will provide you with your permanent prosthetic when the dental implants are healed. This type of implant typically requires less bone density than traditional ones, so All-on-4 may be a good option if you do not have enough bone density to support traditional implants. Call our office or schedule a consultation today to determine if All-On-4 could benefit your smile.

Dental Implant Aftercare

After we place your implants, you may experience tenderness and bleeding in your gums. This is completely normal and nothing to be concerned about. An OTC pain reliever can help alleviate pain. If your gums are actively bleeding, place gauze on them to help clotting form.

We recommend that patients refrain from smoking, drinking alcoholic beverages, and drinking anything through a straw for at least 24 hours. We also recommend getting ample rest the day following your implant placement and laying your head above your heart. Rinsing with saltwater can help reduce pain and swelling. It is also important to be careful and gentle while brushing your teeth around the procedure site.

Implants are pretty easy to take care of in your daily routine. Usually, implants involve a fixed restoration like a crown or denture. You need to brush, floss, and visit the dentist regularly as you would with natural teeth. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to ensure you don’t scratch or damage the restoration. If you need to floss or clean around a fixed bridge, we’ll teach you techniques and recommend any necessary tools.

Dental implants are designed to be permanent restorations. You’ll rarely encounter complications that require changes. However, if the crown restoration that completes the implant becomes damaged or worn down over time, you may need to replace it.

Dental Implant FAQs

Dental implants are the best choice to replace missing teeth. Learn more with answers to common questions about dental implants in Ardmore, PA:

Can smokers get dental implants?

While smokers can get dental implants, smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure. Smoking affects blood circulation and slows the healing process, making it harder for the implant to integrate with the bone. If you’re a smoker, your dentist may advise quitting before and after implant surgery to improve success rates. Even reducing smoking can ensure your implants last longer.

How long does it take to get dental implants?

Getting dental implants takes several months because your mouth needs time to heal between steps. Pretreatments like tooth extractions or bone grafts can also add time to treatment. Once your mouth is ready, we’ll place the implant. You need to wait usually 3 to 6 months while your bone heals and grows around the implant. You also need several weeks after we place the connecter piece.

In total, the whole process can take 5 to 9 months or more. This timeline will depend on your specific needs and how well your mouth heals. Your dentist will explain your timeline and help you at every step.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post that integrates with the jawbone is highly durable and resistant to decay. But due to normal wear and tear, the crown or prosthetic attached to the implant may need replacement after 10 to 15 years. Regular dental visits help ensure that the implant and surrounding tissues remain healthy.

Will I be able to eat normally with dental implants?

Dental implants function like natural teeth so you can eat normally without restrictions. Once fully healed, you can enjoy all types of food, including crunchy and chewy foods that may be difficult with traditional dentures. During the healing process, your dentist may recommend a soft diet to avoid excessive pressure on the implant. After full integration, implants provide excellent chewing efficiency.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

You may be a good candidate for dental implants in Ardmore, PA, if you:

  • Are in good oral health: Dental implants need healthy gums and surrounding teeth to support them. If you have gum disease or other serious oral health problems, your dentist may recommend treating those first. Taking care of your teeth and gums is important before, during, and after implant treatment.
  • Have one or more missing teeth: Dental implants are used to replace missing teeth. Whether you’ve lost a single tooth or several teeth, implants can help fill the gaps in your smile. They can also prevent other teeth from shifting out of place over time.
  • Have enough jaw bone to support the implant: Dental implants are placed into the jawbone, so there needs to be enough bone to hold them in place. If you’ve been missing teeth for a while, your jawbone might have started to shrink. Sometimes we recommend a bone graft can rebuild the area so we can successfully place an implant.

Do dental implants ever fail?

While dental implants have a high success rate of over 95 %, failures can occur due to infection, poor healing, or insufficient bone support. Factors like smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and poor oral hygiene can increase the risk of failure. If an implant fails, we can remove it and place a new implant as you heal. Regular checkups help detect any issues early and improve implant success.

Can dental implants improve my speech?

Dental implants can help you speak more clearly, especially if you used to wear dentures that didn’t fit well. Traditional dentures can move around in your mouth and make it hard to say some words. But dental implants stay firmly in place, which helps you talk more naturally and clearly. Many people feel more confident when speaking after they get dental implants.

How do dental implants prevent bone loss?

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Dental implants act like natural tooth roots to stimulate the bone and prevent bone resorption. This helps maintain facial structure and prevents the sunken appearance that can happen after you lose teeth.
