Types of Implant Restorations We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth with a dental implant topped by a custom crown for a seamless, functional result.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Ideal for multiple missing teeth, this restoration uses implants as anchors for a fixed bridge without affecting neighboring teeth.

Implant-Retained Dentures

These dentures snap into place over implants for improved stability, making them more comfortable and secure than traditional dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Fully fixed dentures that attach permanently to dental implants, providing maximum strength and a natural feel without adhesives.
