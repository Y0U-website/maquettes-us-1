Dr. Loan Le Truong

Providing Compassionate Dental Care for Over 18 Years

Dr Truong is a skilled and experienced dentist specializing in general dentistry and implants. Her passion is to create beautiful smiles for patients and to provide individualized care to each patient, tailoring treatments to their specific needs.

Dr Truong graduated from University of California at Berkeley and earned her doctor degree from Tufts School of Dental Medicine in 2006.

With a wide range of services offered including crowns and bridges, clear aligners, bone grafting and implants, Dr Truong has helped many patients to attain a healthy and beautiful smile. She has stayed up-to-date with the latest advancements including lasers, CTBT, intra oral scanner to ensure her patients receive the highest quality of dental care. Dr Truong is known for her expertise, compassion, and a gentle touch to create a comfortable dental experience.
