The American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA)

The American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA) is a group of dental professionals in North America who focus on clear aligner treatments like Invisalign . Their goal is to improve patient results and keep dentists up to date with the latest tools and techniques. AACA members receive special training through webinars and workshops led by experts like Dr. David Galler.

Dentists who earn Fellowship status have treated hundreds of Invisalign cases and passed advanced exams, showing a high level of skill. Members also stay current by joining study groups, reading dental journals, and attending events where they learn about the newest technology in clear aligner care.
