Frequently Asked Questions

How does dental insurance work? Dental insurance works by providing coverage for specific dental services. Typically, you’ll pay a monthly premium and have a deductible, which is the amount you’ll need to pay out-of-pocket before your insurance kicks in. Your plan may cover preventive services like cleanings and checkups and restorative services like fillings and crowns.

What does dental insurance usually cover? Dental insurance plans vary, but most cover preventive services like cleanings and checkups and restorative services like fillings and crowns. Some plans may also cover orthodontic treatment, oral surgery, and other specialty services.

What kind of dental insurance do you accept? We accept PPO plans, both in-network and out-of-network.

Can you find out what my plan covers before my appointment? Yes! We offer a complimentary insurance check to help you understand your coverage and benefits before your appointment.

Do you offer a discount plan if I don’t have insurance? Yes, we offer an in-house discount plan for patients without insurance. You can find more information about this plan on our in-house plan page .
