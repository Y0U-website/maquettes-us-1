Emergency Treatment

emergency treatment

Unexpected dental emergencies happen, and we want to be there for you when they do. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, call the office at any time to schedule an appointment. We will try to accommodate you the same day! If you are a patient of record, there is an emergency line available for you.

The most important thing is your safety. If you experiencing severe pain, facial swelling or have had trauma to the face, seek help immediately at your nearest urgent care facility or emergency room!

Emergency visits usually consist of a limited exam focused on the area of concern. We may take an x-ray. Once we have determined the problem we will schedule your treatment as soon as possible. If the schedule permits, we will gladly perform any treatment needed the same day.
