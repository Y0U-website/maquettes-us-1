Brandon H. Barton Jr.

D.D.S.

Dr. Brandon H Barton graduated from the University of Cincinnati with a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering and earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery from Meharry Medical College, School of Dentistry. He is married to Lauren Barton, MD and has 3 adult children and 3 grandchildren.

He opened a private dental practice located in Northeast Detroit near City Airport and continues to provide service to the community in the same location for over 40 years.

Dr. Barton believes patient education and preventive treatment are the keys to a healthy smile and good oral health. Patient education involves teaching patients the proper way to maintain their dental health through proper oral hygiene, good nutrition, understanding the causes of dental diseases and how they can be prevented.

Dr. Barton also offers preventive counseling at his clinic as a part of the patient educational experience. Many of the health problems and dental diseases are directly related to nutritional and life style habits. Pay close attention to our blog and Community Resource pages for updates on our patient educational experience.
