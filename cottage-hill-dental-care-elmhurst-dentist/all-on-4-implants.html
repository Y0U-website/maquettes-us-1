All-on-4 Implants vs. Dentures While dentures have been the go-to for missing teeth for eons, All-on-4 is giving them a run for their money. Why?

  • Durability : They’re designed to last, which means less fuss in the long run.
  • A Natural Feel : Say goodbye to slipping and sliding — they’re as sturdy as your original teeth.
  • Bone Loss Prevention : Protect that jawbone and keep your facial structure in check.
  • Planning : Advanced tech and imaging guide us in the perfect implant placement.
  • Placement : Those four implants? They’re put in places where the bone is densest.
  • Setting the Bridge : Once the implants are in, a temporary bridge is set, giving you an instant smile boost.
  • Healing : Over time, the implants fuse with your jawbone, setting the stage for your permanent bridge.
  • Permanent Set : Once the healing completes, we’ll pop on your permanent bridge and you can go about your day, smiling, eating, and talking with confidence.
  • Stability : No need for adhesives; these implants aren’t budging.
  • Comfort : Forget about those gum irritations.
  • Ease : Simplified oral care and no more “denture nights” in a glass by your bed.

Pricing, Insurance, and Making It All Possible

Initially, All-on-4 might seem like it’s leaning on the pricier side compared to other options. But remember: this is a lifelong investment. The benefits of stability, comfort, and a boost in confidence can outweigh the cost in the long run. Besides, depending on your situation, the permanence of the All-on-4 implant can be cheaper than other options in the long run.
