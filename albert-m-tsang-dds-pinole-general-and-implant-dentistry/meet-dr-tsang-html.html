Meet the doctor

Albert M. Tsang, DDS

Dr. Tsang was born and raised in Fairfield. He is a 2003 graduate of University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco. Dr. Tsang is well versed in all phases of dentistry and recieved advanced training and fellowship in Dental Implants.

He is a warm, caring individual who continually seeks to improve himself on the newest and latest technology to provide the most comfortable dental treatment. Enjoys living nearby Pinole and his favorite pastimes are swimming and exploring the outdoors.

He is active with educating the importance of dental health in maintaining a healthy lifestyle in Pinole, Hercules, El Sobrante, Richmond, San Pablo Communities in the East Bay. He is also involved with the Contra Costa Dental Society.
