Dr. Kinghorn’s Career and Personal Life

Dr. Kinghorn hails from Lewisville, Idaho. He has been married since July 2004 and is a father of two lovely daughters and a son. He and his family love living in the Gallatin Valley.

Dr. Kinghorn is very friendly and conscientious. He chose his profession because he loves meeting and interacting with people and providing exceptional care for them. Being in the field of dentistry allows him to get the best of both worlds.

As a dental professional, he lives by his four core values: respect, integrity, attention to detail, and hard work. He pledges to work endlessly to provide his clients with the best dental experience possible. He also speaks fluent Spanish to cater to the needs of his Hispanic patients.

When he isn’t working as a family dentist, Dr. Kinghorn enjoys spending time with his family and making new memories. He enjoys recreational hobbies like golf, water skiing and just about anything that takes him outdoors.

Kinghorn and his family love to spend time visiting their families in Southwestern Idaho, where he and his wife both grew up.

Dr Heap’s Career and Personal Life

After being out of the valley for five years, Dr. Heap and his family are blessed to be back where their loved ones and close friends live. He and his wife are proud parents of three children: two boys and a girl.

Heap and his wife both grew up in the Bozeman-Belgrade area, making fond memories and establishing strong friendships. Over the years, life has taught Chase valuable lessons, including making and maintaining good relationships with those he comes in contact with.

He has learned that good relationships take mutual respect and sacrifice, which he wants to incorporate into his practice as a family dentist. Having had many experiences working for other offices, Dr. Heap can attest that the professional and caring nature of Belgrade Dental’s staff is unparalleled.

Every patient will have a wonderful experience here. He looks forward to meeting you and helping you address your dental health needs.

If you don’t see Dr. Heap in the office, you can find him enjoying T-ball or a game of racquetball at The Ridge or on the river fishing.

Sue Williams

A registered dental hygienist (RDH) is a professional that practices preventative dentistry. While they work closely with dentists, they’re mainly responsible for prophylaxis and oral health assessments. Aside from those, they are also in charge of giving proper patient education, including proper brushing and flossing.

At Belgrade Dental, we’re fortunate and grateful to have our highly skilled resident RDH. Sue was born and raised in Big Timber, MT. She had a basketball scholarship at Caroll College, which paved the way for her to finish a Bachelor’s Degree in Dental Hygiene.

She has been practicing for more than 30 years and has done 5 international missions—once in Africa, Columbia, and Moldova and twice in Ecuador.
