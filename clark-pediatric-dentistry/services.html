Your First Visit

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends every child to see a dentist for routine care when the first tooth is visible and no later than 1 year of age. At the first dental visit, our team will allow your child to become acclimated to their surroundings. The dentist will complete a comprehensive examination and discuss recommended preventative care and treatment. The dentist will review diet/habits, brushing and flossing- preparing you and your child for a lifetime of healthy mouths
