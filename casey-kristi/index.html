We offer the best of preventive dentistry in the Elizabethton, TN area. Our in-depth exams, high-quality patient education, and quality preventive products will help you keep your mouth healthy for life. Our sealants, fluoride varnishes, and nutritional counseling services are all designed with you in mind. For our patients we recommend a cleaning (or prophylaxis) at least once every 6 months.

Our composite fillings are strong, long-lasting, and natural looking. Old amalgam fillings, the ugly silver fillings of the past, can fail and need to be replaced after some time. Our tooth-colored composite fillings are more effective at protecting the cavity from further decay. We will even remove your old amalgam filling, if necessary, to better protect your tooth.

Whether you need a full or a partial denture, our practice can give you the highest functioning dentures possible today. Full dentures replace all the teeth while partials replace only some missing teeth, using the remaining teeth for retention and support.

Are you in pain? At Appalachian Dentistry, we work hard to help our patients in whatever way we can. If you are in pain, we want to help eliminate your discomfort.

A root canal doesn’t have to be something you dread. At our dental office, we make the experience comfortable, efficient, and stress-free. Using the latest technology and gentle techniques, our skilled team removes infection, relieves pain, and restores your tooth’s health. If you’re experiencing tooth sensitivity or discomfort, don’t wait saving your natural tooth is always the best option.
