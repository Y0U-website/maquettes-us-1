The practice

At Bogacki & Bogacki DDS PC, we specialize in general dentistry, helping families and individuals with gum and tooth care as well as education on general oral health.

Want the most convenient way to get a new crown? With our single-visit dentistry services, you can get a new smile in just one appointment.

Alongside general dentistry, we have the facilities and expertise to help treat periodontal disease and bruxism (teeth grinding).
