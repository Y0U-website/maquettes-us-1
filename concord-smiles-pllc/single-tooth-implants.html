Are You a Candidate for a Single-Tooth Implant?

  • Adequate bone density to support the implant.
  • Healthy gums in the implant area.

Benefits of Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Single-tooth implants are an excellent way to restore your smile and offer several advantages:

  • Prevent shifting of adjacent teeth.
  • Maintain jawbone health, reducing the risk of bone loss.
  • Support facial structure.
  • Mimic the look of a natural tooth, creating a seamless appearance.

The Single-Tooth Implant Procedure

The cost of a single-tooth implant can vary depending on the tooth and work required, but generally falls between $3,500 and $7,000.

  • Preparation: Necessary prep work, such as periodontal treatment, bone or gum grafting, or tooth extraction, may be done beforehand.
  • Step 1: Implant Post Placement: Under local anesthesia, we will insert a titanium post into the jawbone. Afterward, a healing period of several weeks allows the post to integrate with the bone, potentially using a temporary crown in the meantime.
  • Step 2: Crown Placement: Once the area has healed, an abutment will connect the permanent porcelain crown to the implant, completing the procedure. The entire process generally takes a few months.
