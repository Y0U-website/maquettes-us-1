Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

The cost of dental implants can vary depending on the type and number of implants needed, as well as the procedure’s complexity. During your consultation, we’ll conduct an oral exam, take x-rays, and create a customized plan, allowing us to provide an accurate estimate for your specific needs.

We offer flexible financing options and accept most major insurance plans, which may cover a portion of the cost. Our team will work with you to find an affordable solution that fits your budget.

Additional Services for Optimal Implant Success

For dental implants to succeed, adequate jawbone structure is essential. If necessary, we can perform bone grafting to build up the jawbone. For patients with existing oral health concerns, such as gum disease or tooth decay, we may also recommend additional treatments before proceeding with implant placement.

Proper implant care is essential. Regular dental checkups and a good at-home oral hygiene routine will help protect your investment and keep your implants in top condition.
