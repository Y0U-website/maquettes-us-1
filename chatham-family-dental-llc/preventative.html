Preventative

Helping Keep Your Teeth Clean and Well Maintained

Chatham Family Dental LLC in Chatham, New Jersey offers reliable dental solutions for our patients. Check out the exceptional preventative dental services we provide in order to negate long-term damages and rot.

Thorough Dental Evaluations

Our skilled professionals will meticulously check your teeth for potential signs of cavities like weakened enamel and dentin. We can also conduct a thorough assessment for symptoms of gum disease like inflammation, redness, or sores.

Expert Dental Cleanings

With a routine six-month appointment at our clinic, you can rest assured that our expertise and equipment will be used to effectively remove plaque buildup and stubborn tartar from the surfaces of your teeth above your gums.

Effective Fluoride Treatments

The fluoride treatments we provide are quick and effective. The fluoride may be applied in the form of a gel, foam, or varnish. We may recommend fluoride treatments at regular intervals of 6 to 12 months, depending on the condition of your oral health.

Dental Sealants for Decay Prevention

To protect against tooth decay, a slim plastic coating is applied to the surface of your teeth, focusing on premolars and molars. This sealant quickly adheres to the grooves of the teeth, forming a protective layer over the enamel.

In-Depth Oral Screenings

Our exams are comprehensive during your full oral examination, we do not leave any details out, always ensuring that your oral health is in top condition.

If needed, our team can perform a thorough examination of your throat and neck for any abnormalities.
