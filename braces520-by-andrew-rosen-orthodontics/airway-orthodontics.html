Understanding Airway Orthodontics

Parents are often surprised when we ask about their child’s breathing during their first visit. However, scientists are learning more and more the effect that breathing issues can have on your child’s dental and facial development. This is a growing area of research, and unfortunately often goes overlooked and unnoticed by other orthodontists, pediatricians, pediatric dentists and other healthcare providers.

Early Intervention for Lasting Benefits

Early detection and treatment are crucial. We always recommend getting children in for their first orthodontic exam at age 7. Every year that the child grows further, the underlying issues become more difficult to correct. By addressing airway issues during childhood, we can:

  • Prevent the development of more severe problems
  • Guide proper facial and dental growth
  • Enhance overall quality of life from a young age
