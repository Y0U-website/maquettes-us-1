Digital Dental Technology

No modern dental office would be complete without digital dental technology. Digital X-rays create easy-to-read images that we can use to educate you about the state of your oral health. Because they create digital images, digital X-rays create less waste and save time during your visits. These images can be viewed chairside for effective treatment planning, and they can be saved in paperless records for more efficient record keeping. Also, patients appreciate the reduction in exposure to radiation. Digital X-rays emit up to 90 % less radiation than traditional radiographs!

Intraoral cameras can magnify images of your teeth, gums, and other oral structures several times, giving us an up-close look at your oral health. We can show you these images to point out various trouble spots we may detect, helping to strengthen your oral health routines and guide you toward the right treatments. We believe that education is the foundation of any healthy smile, and intraoral cameras help us teach you about your mouth.

We also provide warm blankets to help keep you comfortable during your time at our office. Our hope is that all of these amenities will help to take your mind off the dentistry, allowing you to relax during visits.
