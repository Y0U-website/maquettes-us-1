Financial Policy

You can pay for your office visits and treatment by cash, check, credit or debit card at the time of treatment. We accept Mastercard, Visa & Discover. We also accept most major dental insurances. To discuss payment options or insurance coverage, please call us at 508-832-0919.

We make every attempt to verify your insurance eligibility, however an estimate of benefits is not a guarantee of payment. Final determination of payment is ultimately determined by your insurance carrier. If you have questions regarding your account, please contact us.
