Meet Our Team

Our doctors and staff fulfill all national requirements for continuing education and relicensing qualifications, in order to stay up to date with breakthroughs in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and technology.

Our staff strives to exceed your expectations and provide the beautiful smiles our patients deserve in a friendly and compassionate atmosphere. ​

Dr. Laith Azzouni

Dr. Azzouni received his DMD from the University of Pennsylvania (UPENN), School of Dental Medicine in 2004 where he graduated with honors. He is a member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU) the national dental honor society.

Dr Ahmed received his D.M.D. from Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. He was awarded the best Dental Implant Student award during his dental school education by the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (AAOMS). Dr Ahmed completed his Oral & Maxillofacial training from Boston University.

Dr. Ahmed presented in multiple conferences, including Yankee Dental Congress, American Association for Dental Research (IADR), Annual International American Association for Achievements in Science (AAAS) and American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (AAOMS).

Dr. Ahmed is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery.

​Dr Ahmed practices all aspects of Oral & maxillofacial surgery;In addition to providing patients with specialty consultation, Dr. Ahmed provides services including Dento-alveolary surgery, Implant surgery, Bone grafting, Soft tissue grafting, Exposure and bracketing of impacted teeth, Office-Based anesthesia, Maxillofacial trauma, Oral Pathology.

Mary Salerno

Surgical Assistant

She has been a surgical assistant for 18 years and enjoys working in this field very much. She is CPR and radiology certified.
