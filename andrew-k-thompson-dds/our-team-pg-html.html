Our Team

A top priority at our office is to work as a team and to maintain a family feel. We love our job and enjoy providing you with the highest quality dental care. We know that the way we work together enables you to have a comfortable experience and will build your trust in us as you are treated like family!

Dr. Andrew Thompson

Dr. Thompson has been practicing dentistry in Eldersburg for over 23 years. He grew up here and went to Freedom Elementary, Sykesville Middle and graduated from Liberty High School in 1994. From there he earned a BS in Biology from Houghton College, NY. Dr. Thompson earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from the University of Maryland Dental School in 2002.

He lives in Eldersburg with his lovely wife and 5 incredible children. His hobbies include golf, mountain biking, surfing, reading, building projects and cheering for the Ravens! He serves as an elder at LifePoint church and leads the Medical Missions team there. For updates on his overseas work, please see the Dental Missions tab.
