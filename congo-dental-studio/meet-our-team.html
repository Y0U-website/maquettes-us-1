Schroepfer Dental Team at Congo Dental Studio

Dr. Tracy Schroepfer Dr. Tracy Schroepfer is the owner of Congo Dental Studio. Raised in New Berlin, Wisconsin, as the sixth of eight children, she discovered her passion for dentistry at age 13 and began her career as a periodontal assistant. She completed her education in dental hygiene and biological sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee before earning her Dental Medicine Doctorate and a Certificate in Public Health with a dental emphasis from A.T. Still University–Missouri School of Dentistry and Oral Health.

Dr. Schroepfer earned the Horace Wells Senior Student Award from the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology as well as the Missouri Primary Care Association Award for dedication to community service. She provides honest, high-quality dental care at Congo Dental Studio for individuals and families in the Benton community.
