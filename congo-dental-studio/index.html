Welcome to Congo Dental Studio!

Congo Dental Studio provides a personalized, patient-centered dentistry for all ages in a calm, welcoming environment, making us one of the top choices among Benton dentists.

The practice

Dental care should feel personal, not rushed or intimidating. Our approach focuses on understanding your concerns, clearly explaining your options, and creating treatment plans that support your long-term oral health and comfort.

At Congo Dental Studio, we take the time to listen, answer your questions, and ensure you feel confident in every decision, whether it’s about general dental care or exploring options like Botox or PDO threading .

Congo Dental Studio uses advanced tools to provide precise and efficient treatment while preserving natural tooth structure whenever possible.

Our office is designed to feel calm and welcoming, especially for those who feel nervous about visiting a dentist. Congo strives to create a comfortable environment for everyone!

We offer everything from routine cleanings to advanced procedures, ensuring continuity of care for individuals and families.

We’re proud to serve Benton and surrounding communities with honest recommendations and compassionate care, prioritizing your dental needs, and ensuring you receive the best treatment possible.

We accept most major dental insurance plans and are in-network with many providers, including Delta Dental, Blue Cross & Blue Shield, Humana Dental, MetLife, United Healthcare, and United Concordia (Military). Our team is here to assist you with any questions about your coverage.

We are honored to provide dental services to patients of all ages at Congo Dental Studio, including Active Duty and Reserve Service Members through the Reserve Health Readiness Program (RHRP).
