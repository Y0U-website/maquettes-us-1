Emergency Dental Care & Oral Surgery

Early treatment can often prevent the need for more complex procedures later.

  • A chipped, cracked, or broken tooth
  • A knocked-out or loose tooth
  • Facial swelling or signs of infection
  • Bleeding that does not stop
  • Dental trauma from an accident or injury

Oral Surgery Services

Oral surgery may be recommended when a dental issue cannot be treated with more conservative methods. Our office provides oral surgery services using modern techniques and advanced technology to ensure precision, safety, and comfort.

Tooth extractions, including impacted or severely damaged teeth Surgical treatment for oral infections Management of dental trauma affecting the teeth or surrounding structures

Each procedure is carefully planned with your comfort and long-term oral health in mind.

Emergency Dental Care

Emergency dental care focuses on immediate relief and stabilization. During your visit, we will evaluate the issue, address pain or infection, and determine the most appropriate treatment plan.

Same-day evaluations for urgent dental concerns Treatment for tooth fractures or damage Management of dental infections or abscesses Temporary or permanent restorations as needed

Our goal is to help you feel comfortable and protected while restoring function and health.

Gentle, Modern Care

We understand that dental emergencies can be stressful. Our team is committed to providing compassionate, efficient care using the latest techniques, high-quality materials, and a gentle approach. We take time to explain your options and ensure you feel confident in your treatment.

If you are experiencing a dental emergency or believe you may need oral surgery, don’t wait . Prompt care can make a significant difference in both comfort and outcomes.
