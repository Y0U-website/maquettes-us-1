About Us

Confidental Studio Inc.

For close to twenty years Confidental Studio has provided dental restorations for dentists in the greater DMV area. Whether it’s a simple crown or bridge case to a hybrid denture case, we’ve got you covered with exceptional quality and unbeatable support!

About Us

With more than 20 years of experience, Confidental Studio Inc. has provided dental restorative options to practices and practitioners within the DMV area. Whether the treatment calls for an analog or digital approach, we apply the natural wax technique with the occlusal compass theory in mind to our restorations. Your practice can rest assured that our restorations follow anatomical function with attention to esthetic detail.

Our Purpose

Throughout the many hurdles and obstacles we’ve overcome since opening our doors, our aim has remained the same since day one. Our intention was and still is to provide dental products and services that restore a patient’s confidence, and ultimately a client’s confidence in us. We continue striving to achieve this with each of our clients through the personal attention and care we bring to each and every case.

Our Mission

Our mission is to grow our organization, provide opportunities for our employees within a great work environment, and become a provider of high-quality restorations for dental practices across the U.S.

Confidence

We have a wide variety of restorative options and services to select and fit the needs of your patients. Every restoration is fabricated with FDA-compliant materials and we guarantee that no corners have been cut from intake to delivery.

High Quality Restorations

Because of the pricing and fast turnaround times offered by overseas labs, we’ve found that the only way to compete is through the quality of our work. Overseas labs can offer such rates and turnaround times because of their efficient assembly line workflow. One case may go through the hands of five to ten different employees. At Confidental Studio, we dedicate only one to two dental technicians to work on the case. This ensures a workflow conducive to consistency, efficiency, and ultimately the quality of your restoration.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

We believe that our lab’s success is dependent on our clients’ success. In order to foster mutual growth, we are always striving to do more than just our due diligence. We set a high standard of service and our support staff is trained accordingly. From treatment planning to case consultation, our dedicated team of support specialists will exceed your expectations!
