Customer Experience

Dental practices continue to face their own pressures with rising customer expectations (in value, quality, and speed) coupled with the challenging economy. Rather than concentrating on dentistry, clinicians are pushed to fill chairside time, accept treatments, and new patient acquisitions. Clinicians face many challenges when trying to meet the unique needs and desires of each and every patient. Confidental Studio understands this plight and we offer a variety of products and services to tackle these challenges and improve the overall patient experience.

Our restorations, products and services include a wide range including all-ceramic crowns and bridges comprised of both E.Max and Zirconia, PFMs, dentures, partials, implants, rebases, and much much more. We believe in providing you and your patients with a wide range of options for the most advanced restorative options.

Treatment Planning by Confidental Studio’s dedicated department managers and CDTs simplifies the flow of each and every case.

If a consultation is requested, rest easy knowing that trained and experienced technicians provide their expertise and knowledge, offering a collaborative path to a successful smile design in addition to material selection.

Additional services such as custom shades and stains can be provided upon request if additional assistance is necessary for shade matching.
