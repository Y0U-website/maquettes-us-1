Medical Clinic

Crescent Community Health Center is certified as a Patient-Centered Medical Home by NCQA, offering a full range of primary medical care services. Our medical practice uses the care team approach and is made up of physicians, nurse practitioners, nursing staff, pharmacists, care coordinators, behavioral health specialists, and health educators. We provide:

Comprehensive Care Coordination Comprehensive care coordination is the deliberate organization of patient care activities across multiple providers and settings to ensure safe, effective, and personalized healthcare delivery. It involves:

  • Physicals/Wellness Exams & Vaccinations
  • Women’s Health and Family Planning
  • School and Sports Physicals
  • Treatment for Acute Illnesses
  • Minor Procedures (skin biopsies, wound care, joint injections, cryotherapy)
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Lab Services
  • Sharing timely and accurate information among all participants in a patient’s care—clinicians, nurses, social workers, pharmacists, and community support teams
  • Developing and maintaining care plans that reflect the patient’s medical history, current conditions, medications, and social needs.
  • Facilitating transitions between care settings (e.g., hospital to home, pediatric to adult care) to avoid gaps in treatment.
  • Coordinating referrals and follow-ups , including transportation and insurance authorizations when needed.
  • Engaging patients in self-management , education, and goal-setting for chronic disease management
