Dental Clinic

Your smile matters to us. Crescent Community Health Center provides comprehensive dental care to support your overall health and well-being. Our team includes board-certified dentists and specialists in pediatric dentistry, oral and maxillofacial surgery, and orofacial pain and temporomandibular disorders (TMD/TMJ). From preventive care to advanced treatment, you can count on our expertise to help you achieve and maintain a healthy smile.

Services We Provide

  • Comprehensive Exams – thorough check-ups to monitor and protect your oral health.
  • Digital X-Rays – safe, low-radiation imaging for accurate diagnosis.
  • Cleanings – routine cleanings to keep teeth healthy and prevent decay.
  • Fillings – repair cavities with durable, natural-looking materials.
  • Periodontal Treatment – care for gum health to prevent and treat disease.
  • Crowns and Bridges – restore damaged teeth and replace missing ones.
  • Complete and Partial Dentures – customized solutions for restoring function and smiles.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Cleanings & Exams: Preventive care to keep kids’ teeth healthy.
  • Cavity Prevention & Treatment: Fluoride, sealants, and fillings made for children.
  • Dental Education: Teaching kids (and parents) how to brush, floss, and care for teeth.
  • Emergency Care: Quick solutions for unexpected dental needs.
  • Tooth Extractions: Gentle removal when teeth are damaged, decayed, or crowded.
  • Crowns: Strong, protective crowns to restore function and keep smiles healthy.
  • Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Routine and Surgical Extractions
  • Wisdom and Impacted Teeth Extractions and Exposure
  • Dental Implants
  • Sinus Lift / Bone Grafting
  • Ridge Augmentation
  • Socket Preservation
  • Soft and Hard Tissue Biopsies
  • Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology
  • Sleep Apnea
  • TMJ Related Disorders
  • Orthognathic Surgery
  • Cleft Lip and Palate Repair (Primary and Secondary)

  • Post Cancer Reconstruction
