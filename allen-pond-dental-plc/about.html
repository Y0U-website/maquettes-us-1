About Us

At Allen Pond Dental, we strive to provide exceptional, caring dentistry services to the community we live in, while educating our patients on the importance of a healthy smile as it relates to the overall health and wellbeing of each individual.

Our professional mission is simple and pure: To remember everyday to treat our patients, ourselves and our environment with dignity, thoughtfulness and joy.

We are an eco-friendly dental practice, which means we care about what is going on outside our doors just as much as we care about our patients inside. Dr. Michael Holbert takes a unique approach to combine dentistry with environmentally responsible business practices, using non toxic products, reducing waste and conserving energy.

Your comfort and care is our first priority. We want to make your dental visit one that is comfortable and friendly. Our beautiful, state-of-the-art practice is not only cutting edge in technology, but also provides our patients with the calming ambience of home.
