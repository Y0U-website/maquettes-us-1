The practice

Welcome to Cherry Bay Dental, the office of Dr. Heather Rhoads Pfefferle, where our primary goal is your optimal oral health. Our comfortable, quaint, and relaxed office provides the perfect atmosphere for our patients and their families to receive individual and personalized treatment. Let us help you keep your teeth for a lifetime!

We promise to treat you like a person, not a procedure. Learn more about our services and how they can help maintain or restore your smile.

Since 2010, Dr. Pfefferle has Provided the community with outstanding, personalized, and friendly dental care.

She and her dental team give every patient the attention and focus they deserve in a comfortable and quaint setting. She particularly enjoys finding solutions for her patients who are experiencing muscle pain and headaches. Dr. Pfefferle’s patients are always impressed with her ability to make each person feel like they are important and that their needs are her first priority.
