Our Team

Dr. Heather Rhoads Pfefferle

Dr. Heather Rhoads Pfefferle is a Leelanau County native and graduate of Traverse City West High School. Dr. Pfefferle attended Hillsdale College where she graduated summa cum laude with a B.S. in Music Performance (violin). Following her undergraduate studies, she earned her Doctorate of Dental Sciences from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2008.

Since returning to Traverse City in 2010, Dr. Pfefferle has been providing the community with outstanding, personalized, and friendly dental care. She and her dental team give every patient the attention and focus they deserve in a comfortable and quaint setting. She particularly enjoys finding solutions for her patients who are experiencing muscle pain and headaches. Dr. Pfefferle’s patients are always impressed with her ability to make each person feel like they are important and that their needs are her first priority.

When not creating beautiful smiles for her patients, Dr. Pfefferle enjoys spending time with her husband, Ryan, and their three daughters—Elsa, Ainsley, and Adele. As a family, they like to camp in the Upper Peninsula, and enjoy hiking and fishing. Dr. Pfefferle also likes to cook and spends much of the summertime gardening.

Meet our team

Kristi

Jenni

A graduate of Northwest Michigan College’s Registered Dental Assistant program, Jenni joined the Cherry Bay Dental team in the summer of 2015. Her hard work ethic and attention to detail make her a great chairside assistant to Dr. Pfefferle. She especially appreciates the intimate and family-friendly environment.

Jenni grew up in Traverse City on a small family farm raising dairy and beef cattle. In her youth, she was very active in 4H and continues to participate in the program. She spent two years in California with her husband (who served in the Marines for five years) and son before returning to the Traverse City area. In her free time, Jenni enjoys hiking, fishing, and spending time with her family and her Australian cattle dogs.

Mandy

Mandy graduated from Kellogg Community College in 2017 with her degree in Dental Hygiene. She is a Traverse City native and joined Cherry Bay Dental in 2023.

She enjoys working one on one with our patients to better their oral hygiene routines along with their overall health.

She is a mom to 2 busy boys and loves spending time being outdoors with her husband and kids.

Carolyn

Carolyn graduated from the University of Detroit Mercy Dental School. She has lived in the Traverse area for over 20 years and loves all it has to offer. Carolyn loves spending time with her family, gardening, hiking with her dog Mocha, wine, dining out, and traveling.

Ryan

Ryan and Heather married in September 2009, and he has served an invaluable role since Cherry Bay Dental’s inception. Shortly after his graduation from Defiance College, Ryan enlisted in the Army where he served with the 5th Special Forces Group out of Fort Campbell, KY, and experienced three tours in Iraq. After his discharge, he earned a Master of Business Administration from Keller Graduate School of Management and took a number of horticulture classes from Michigan State University.

When he’s not busy helping with the financial and administrative aspects of Cherry Bay Dental, Ryan is often working diligently on the family cherry farm or taking care of their three daughters. He is also a volunteer coach for the Traverse City West Varsity Football program. He enjoys woodworking, fishing, hiking, and camping with his family.
