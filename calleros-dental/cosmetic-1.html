Tongue-Tie Release Procedures for Infants, Children & Adults A tongue-tie release can make a world of difference—for infants, children, and adults alike. By gently releasing the tight tissue beneath the tongue, this simple procedure can improve speech, eating, swallowing, oral hygiene, and even reduce tension in the jaw and mouth. It’s a small change that can have a big impact on everyday comfort and function.

While some babies with tongue- or lip-ties may latch and nurse well, many face real challenges with breastfeeding. That’s where we come in.

Dr. Calleros has helped hundreds of families navigate this journey with care and confidence. Known for her gentle touch and expertise, she’s a trusted resource for patients near and far—many of whom travel specifically to see her for this specialized care.
