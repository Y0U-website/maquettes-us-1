Meet your dentist

Dr. Jude Jocham

Dedicated to providing comprehensive dental care in a relaxed and welcoming environment, Dr. Jude Jocham is passionate about removing the stigma of anxiety-provoking dentistry. His goal is to ensure every patient feels comfortable, confident, and empowered in maintaining a healthy smile.

Dr. Jocham began his education at Penn State University, earning a Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Sciences before pursuing his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at New York University College of Dentistry. Following graduation, he completed a General Practice Residency at Jersey City Medical Center, where he gained advanced experience in emergency care and oral surgery.

In 2017, Dr. Jocham moved west to Denver, Colorado, where he practiced for three years and completed an intensive implant residency, further advancing his skills in implant dentistry and surgical care. In 2020, he and his family relocated to Maine, where he now combines advanced training with a patient-first philosophy to deliver care that is comfortable, approachable, and anxiety-free.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Jocham is an active member of the Maine Dental Association and the Casco Bay Study Club, where he collaborates with colleagues to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Jocham is an avid golfer, skier, and runner, and enjoys spending time with his wife and two young sons exploring all that Maine has to offer.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Cait Kauffman

Dr. Kauffman graduated with honors from Cornell University with a B.S. in Plant Biology. As a Fulbright Scholar, she conducted biochemistry research in India, where she lived for one year. She went on to earn her D.M.D. from the University of Pennsylvania, graduating with honors. After dental school, she completed a general practice residency at Albert Einstein Medical Center, with a focus on restorative dentistry, crown and bridge work, implant placement, surgical extractions, and removable dentures.

Dr. Kauffman began her dental career as the Dental Director of a community health center in Seattle, Washington. She later transitioned to private practice in Seattle, where she honed her expertise in full-mouth restorative and esthetic dentistry, implant prosthetics, Invisalign, and removable prosthodontics. She works closely with her patients to achieve outcomes tailored to their individual needs and goals.

Passionate about dentistry and the opportunity it offers to serve others, Dr. Kauffman is honored to continue her work here in Maine.

Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with her twin daughters, hiking, and gardening.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Jonathan Bernstein

Dr. Jonathan Bernstein is a highly experienced dentist who proudly provides sedation, implant, and restorative dentistry to patients across Central and Southern Maine. A graduate of Northwestern University Dental School with honors, he combines advanced training with a compassionate approach to help patients feel comfortable and cared for. Certified in both oral and IV conscious sedation, Dr. Bernstein offers safe and personalized options for even the most anxious patients.

Committed to lifelong learning, he has trained extensively in sedation, implantology , surgery, and esthetic dentistry at leading institutions including the Pikos Institute, Garg Implant Seminars, Duquesne University, and Spear Education. His patient-first philosophy centers on listening carefully to each individual’s goals and creating treatment plans that support both oral and overall health.

Outside the office, Dr. Bernstein enjoys cycling, hiking, skiing, and road racing, with tennis as his latest passion. He also loves classic cars, live music (especially Phish), and exploring Maine’s restaurants with family and friends.

Comfort and innovation at every visit

We combine modern dental technology with creature comforts to elevate your experience. From advanced diagnostic tools to small touches that make you feel more at home, our goal is to create a dental visit that feels a little less like a chore—and a lot more like self-care.

IV sedation We’re proud to be one of the few practices in the area offering moderate IV sedation in-house. Ideal for patients with dental anxiety or complex treatment plans, IV sedation creates a deeply relaxed experience while allowing you to remain responsive during the procedure.

TRIOS scanner Our advanced TRIOS scanner replaces messy dental impressions with quick, comfortable digital scans. This technology ensures more precise planning for restorations, implants, and cosmetic cases—and a better fit for your final results.

Nitrous oxide Also known as laughing gas, nitrous oxide is a safe and gentle sedation option that helps patients feel calm and anxiety-free during treatment. It’s one of the many options we offer to help even the most nervous patient feel confident in their care.

Headphones and comfort options To help you relax during your visit, we offer headphones so you can tune out and unwind. Whether you enjoy music or a podcast, we want your dental experience to feel as calm and personalized as possible.

Coffee bar Start your visit with a fresh brewed cup at our coffee bar. It’s one of the small ways we let you know your comfort matters to us—from the moment you walk in.

Digital forms We offer convenient paperless forms to make your check-in process seamless and efficient. Fill them out at your own pace before your visit so you can walk into your appointment with confidence and ease.
