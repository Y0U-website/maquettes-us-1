Dental Fillings

Cavities are one of the most common oral diseases, so it&#x27;s imperative we tackle them as soon as they begin to compromise the health and wellness of your teeth. When left untreated, they can cause serious discomfort and may require more invasive procedures to alleviate. Thanks to fillings, your dentist can halt the progression of tooth decay in a single visit. By stopping by for your regular 6-month cleaning and checkup, you can prevent new cavities from forming in the first place.

IV Sedation

Your treatment doesn’t have to feel overwhelming. If you’re feeling anxious, have a sensitive gag reflex, or are preparing for a complex procedure, IV sedation can help you relax deeply and confidently. This advanced sedation method creates a calm, dream-like state while keeping you conscious and responsive. Most commonly used for oral surgery and other advanced treatments, IV sedation is carefully tailored to your needs to ensure comfort, safety, and peace of mind every step of the way.

Dental Crowns

If you’re dealing with a damaged, decayed, or discolored tooth, dental crowns might be the perfect solution for restoring function and aesthetics. A dental crown, often also called a “cap,” is designed to fully cover the visible portion of a tooth, protecting it and restoring its shape, size, and strength.

Root Canal Therapy

Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, your dentist may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement.

Dental Implants

Dental Bridges

If you’re dealing with missing teeth and longing to restore your smile, dental bridges could be the solution you’ve been searching for. Dental bridges are custom-made restorations designed to fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth, helping you regain a natural appearance and full functionality.

Emergencies

Dentures

Dentures are a removable, affordable solution that can complete your smile after missing one or all of your natural teeth. To ensure a natural look and feel, most dentures are made of a combination of acrylic and metal and are custom-crafted for a comfortable fit. Looking to see if dentures are right for you? Schedule a consultation to find out if a set of full or partial dentures can restore your bite. It&#x27;s about time you got back to living your life with a smile.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Tired of your dentures slipping and sliding around in your mouth? Implant-retained dentures, also called overdentures or snap-on dentures, can replace all of your teeth, just like traditional dentures. However, instead of relying on suction or adhesive, they are secured in place with several dental implants. These removable appliances make it easy to keep up with your oral hygiene, and are made from a combination of acrylic and metal materials for enhanced durability.

Implant Crowns

After your dental implant has been affixed, your dentist will make the finishing touches by adding an implant crown! Dental crowns act as tooth "caps" that are fitted onto the implant. Your dentist will ensure the size and color of your implant crown matches with the rest of your other teeth, so you can have a seamless new smile.
