Who Can Benefit from Guided Implants?

Our fully guided implant procedure is ideal for patients who:

We use digital impressions (iTero) and 3D imaging to plan your procedure precisely and ensure a comfortable, efficient experience.

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Prefer a fixed alternative to dentures
  • Want a minimally invasive procedure
  • Are healthy enough for minor oral surgery
  • May need supportive care like bone grafting

The Treatment Process

Here’s what to expect during the guided implant process:

Our goal is to give you a restoration that feels, functions, and looks just like a natural tooth.

  • Consultation – Dr. Gaskill will evaluate your oral health and take digital scans.
  • Planning & Guide Design – Using your 3D scans, we create a custom guide to map the exact angle, depth, and location for your implant.
  • Surgical Placement – The implant is inserted using the guide to ensure maximum precision.
  • Healing – Over several months, the implant integrates with your jawbone.
  • Restoration – Once healed, the implant is completed with a crown , bridge , or full or partial denture .

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does guided implant placement take from start to finish?: The full process takes several months, including healing and final restoration, but the planning and surgery are completed in fewer appointments due to the precision of the guides.
  • Is guided implant surgery more accurate than traditional methods?: Yes. Guided surgery allows for exact placement, minimizing complications and optimizing aesthetics and longevity.
  • Is the procedure painful?: Most patients report minimal discomfort. We offer oral sedation for a relaxing experience.
  • How long do dental implants last?: With good hygiene and regular dental cleanings , implants can last 20 years or more.
  • Can I get an implant if I’ve lost bone?: Yes, but you may need bone grafting first to support the implant properly.
