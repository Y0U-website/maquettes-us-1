Is 6-Month Smiles Right for You?

This treatment is best for adults (16+) who:

If you’re unsure whether this option is right for you, our team will also review alternatives like Full Invisalign treatment or ceramic braces during your consultation.

  • Have mild to moderate crowding or gaps
  • Want a cosmetic improvement without long-term braces
  • Are not seeking major bite correction
  • Are looking for faster results for events like weddings, photoshoots, or career milestones
  • For orthodontic relapse cases

What to Expect During Treatment

  • Initial Consultation We’ll evaluate your smile, take digital scans, and determine if you’re a good candidate for 6-Month Smiles.
  • Custom Treatment Planning Your aligners are customized and wires are customized based on your specific alignment goals and tooth positioning.
  • ‍ Smile Reveal & Retention When treatment is complete, you’ll receive a custom retainer to keep your new smile in place. Our retainer services include repairs and replacements as needed.
