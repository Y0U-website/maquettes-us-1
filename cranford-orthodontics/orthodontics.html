Early Orthodontic Treatment (Phase I)

Early orthodontic treatment helps guide proper jaw growth and address developing bite issues while a child is still growing. At Cranford Orthodontics, our board-certified orthodontists evaluate children around age 7 to determine if early intervention can prevent more complex problems later.

Phase I treatment may include palatal expansion to widen the upper jaw, create space for incoming teeth, and improve bite alignment. We also offer modern solutions like the Invisalign Palatal Expander, which provides a more comfortable, removable alternative to traditional expanders.

Learn more about Early Orthodontic Treatment and how early intervention can support healthy smile development.
