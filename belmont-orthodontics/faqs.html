General Questions

What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. Orthodontists use braces, aligners, and other appliances to straighten teeth, correct bite issues, and improve overall oral health and aesthetics.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. At this age, early detection of potential issues allows for timely intervention if necessary. However, not all children will need early treatment—many will simply be monitored until the appropriate time for braces or aligners.

Am I too old for orthodontic treatment?

Absolutely not! Orthodontic treatment can be successful at any age. In fact, about 1 in 4 orthodontic patients is an adult. With options like Invisalign clear aligners, many adults can achieve their dream smile discreetly and comfortably.

What s the difference between an orthodontist and a dentist?

While all orthodontists are dentists, only about 6 % of dentists are orthodontists. After completing dental school, orthodontists complete an additional 2-3 years of specialized training in tooth movement, jaw alignment, and facial development. This advanced training makes orthodontists the experts in creating beautiful, functional smiles.

Treatment Questions

What treatment options are available?

We offer a variety of treatment options including traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, and Invisalign clear aligners. During your consultation, Dr. Melita will evaluate your specific needs and recommend the best treatment option for your lifestyle, budget, and goals.

Will braces hurt?

You may experience some mild discomfort for a few days after braces are placed or adjusted. This is completely normal and indicates that your teeth are moving. Over-the-counter pain relievers and a soft diet can help during this brief adjustment period. Most patients adapt quickly and find braces quite comfortable.

Can I still play sports with braces?

Yes! We recommend wearing a protective mouthguard during contact sports and activities. We can provide a custom mouthguard designed to fit over your braces for optimal protection.

How do I care for my braces?

Good oral hygiene is essential during orthodontic treatment. Brush after every meal, floss daily using a floss threader or orthodontic flosser, and avoid sticky or hard foods that could damage your braces. We’ll provide detailed care instructions and are always here to answer questions.

Timing & Duration

How long will treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the severity of your case and the type of treatment chosen. On average, treatment takes 12-24 months. Some minor corrections may take as little as 6 months, while complex cases may require longer. Dr. Melita will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

How often will I need appointments?

Most patients visit us every 6-10 weeks for adjustments and progress checks. These appointments typically last 15-30 minutes. We’ll work with your schedule to find convenient appointment times.

What happens after my braces come off?

After your braces are removed, you’ll wear a retainer to keep your teeth in their new positions. Initially, you’ll wear it full-time, then transition to nighttime wear. Following your retainer instructions is crucial for maintaining your beautiful new smile!
