What to Expect at Your First Visit

  • See our state-of-the-art facility

Comprehensive Examination

We’ll Evaluate:

  • Tooth alignment and spacing
  • Jaw alignment and function
  • Oral health and gum condition
  • Bite relationship (how teeth fit together)
  • Facial symmetry and profile
  • Airway and breathing patterns

Digital Records & Imaging

Digital Photos

We’ll take photos of your face and teeth from multiple angles to document your starting point.

Digital X-Rays

Panoramic and cephalometric X-rays show us what’s happening beneath the surface.

Good news: All of our imaging is digital, which means faster results, less radiation, and more comfort for you!

Personalized Consultation with Dr. Melita

This is where the magic happens! Dr. Melita will sit down with you to discuss her findings and recommendations. You’ll see your X-rays and photos on our screen as she explains everything in easy-to-understand terms.

We’ll Discuss:

  • Your concerns and goals – What do you want to achieve with treatment?
  • Diagnosis – What orthodontic issues need to be addressed?
  • Treatment options – Invisalign, braces, or other solutions tailored to you
  • Timeline – How long will treatment take?
  • Expected results – What will your new smile look like?

Financial Consultation

Our financial coordinator will meet with you to discuss treatment costs, insurance coverage, and payment options. We’ll create a payment plan that fits your budget—with zero-interest financing available!

  • Detailed cost breakdown
  • Flexible payment plans
  • Insurance benefits verification
  • Zero-interest financing

Schedule Treatment & Next Steps

If you’re ready to get started, we can often begin treatment the same day! If you need time to think it over, that’s perfectly fine too—we’ll schedule your next appointment whenever you’re ready.

Same-Day Start Options:

  • Get braces placed today (if time permits)
  • Receive your first set of Invisalign aligners
  • Schedule your treatment start date
