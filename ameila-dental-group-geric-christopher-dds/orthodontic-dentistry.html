  • Will Invisalign® aligners straighten my child’s teeth? Because they still have baby teeth that need room to come out, dentists don’t recommend Invisalign for young children. As well, most kids don’t have the discipline needed to wear, care for, and keep up with the Invisalign aligners.
  • How long does treatment last? Although the actual length of time depends on the individual case, usually Invisalign will straighten your smile in 6 to 18 months, often less time than with traditional orthodontic therapy. Your dentist will see you every four to six weeks to monitor your progress.
  • Since the aligners are removable, can I take them out whenever I want? Most patients love the fact that they can take their aligners out for eating, brushing, or on special occasions. For optimal results, you need to wear the aligners about 22 hours a day on a consistent basis.

Retainers

The "retention phase" is perhaps the most critical part of any orthodontic journey. Once your braces or Invisalign treatment is complete, your teeth require support to settle into their new positions and prevent "relapse," or shifting back to their original alignment. We offer both removable and fixed retainers to ensure your smile stays perfectly straight. Removable retainers are made of clear, BPA-free plastic for a discreet look and are easily taken out for eating and cleaning.

For patients who prefer a "set it and forget it" approach, fixed (permanent) retainers consist of a thin, custom-shaped wire bonded to the back of the teeth. This provides constant, 24/7 support without the need to remember to wear an appliance. Regardless of the type you choose, a custom-fitted retainer is the only way to guarantee that the time and financial investment you put into your orthodontic treatment lasts a lifetime.

Custom Mouthguards

Custom-made mouthguards are an essential defense against tooth damage, whether you are on the field or fast asleep. For athletes, a professionally fitted sports guard is a vital piece of equipment that far outperforms store-bought "boil-and-bite" options. Because it is molded to the unique contours of your teeth, it provides superior shock absorption against impacts, significantly reducing the risk of broken teeth, soft tissue lacerations, and jaw injuries.

Beyond the athletic arena, mouthguards (often called night guards) are a primary solution for bruxism, or chronic teeth grinding. Nighttime grinding can lead to worn enamel, fractured dental work, and painful TMJ disorders. Our custom night guards provide a durable, comfortable barrier that cushions the jaw muscles and preserves your tooth structure. By investing in a tailored fit, you ensure maximum comfort, which makes it much easier to stay compliant with wearing your guard every night.
