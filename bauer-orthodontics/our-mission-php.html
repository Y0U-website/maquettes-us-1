Our Mission

Bauer Orthodontics mission is to provide comprehensive orthodontic care that goes beyond straightening teeth—we are committed to creating healthy, stable, and functional smiles that last a lifetime. Through a focus on airway health, facial development, and orthopedic guidance, we take a whole-patient approach to treatment at every stage of life.

As a family-owned practice, we prioritize personalized care, trust, and long-term relationships with the families we serve. We provide braces for children, adolescence and adults. Specializing in early treatment, airway dentistry and TMJ splint therapy and orthognathic surgery preparation, we deliver thoughtful, evidence.
