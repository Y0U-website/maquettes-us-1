Welcome

Bauer Orthodontics isn’t your ordinary orthodontic practice. There is a sense of teamwork and a professionalism you won’t find anywhere else. This is a one-of-a-kind office where we pride ourselves in not only providing clinical excellence, but also in creating lasting personal relationships with all our patients.

Dr. Matthew Bauer is dedicated to the highest level of orthodontic care, and welcomes you and your family to his office. Dr. Bauer is proud to serve the communities of St. Charles, St. Peters, O’Fallon, Weldon Spring, and Cottleville Missouri. Please call us today or fill out the Bauer Orthodontics New Patient Form to schedule your complimentary orthodontic consultation.

What Makes Our Office So Unique?

  • Warm and caring team
  • Patients will see Dr. Bauer at every visit
  • Dr. Bauer and his entire staff take the time to thoroughly explain treatment
  • We are not just straightening teeth, but restoring function to the teeth and jaws
  • Average treatment times are 12-18 months for full orthodontic treatment
