The practice

Welcome to Breckenridge Dental Care Friendly, Caring Dentistry in Hikes Point Drs. Molly Braun and Michelle Moure lead a family practice on Hikes Lane that cares for patients at every stage of life — a child’s first cleaning, a crown that has finally given out, a smile you have been meaning to do something about for years. Whatever brings you in, you will be treated like a person rather than a…

We are accepting new patients of every age. And if something is hurting, say so when you call: urgent cases are worked in ahead of routine visits, and easing the pain comes before any conversation about treatment plans.

We also try to remove the usual friction around dental care. Our office files your insurance claims for you, works with a long list of carriers, and offers Cherry payment plans when treatment needs to be spread out. You will hear about costs before treatment starts, not after.

A cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, a swollen gum, an abscess — none of these improve on their own, and none of them need a diagnosis from you first. Describe what happened when you ring the office and we will take it from there.

You do not have to be an existing patient for us to see you. We will find out what is wrong, ease the pain, and tell you what any further treatment will cost before you agree to it.

Healthy gums are the foundation everything else depends on. We look for gum disease early and treat it thoroughly, because addressing it now is what protects the teeth you still have.

Contact Us Whether you need a routine cleaning and exam, a crown, treatment for gum disease, a root canal, dentures, or prompt care for a tooth that is hurting right now, we can help.
