Patient-Centered Medical Home

Aaron E. Henry Community Health Services Center, Inc.(AEH) is a patient-centered medical home. A PCMH is a primary care model that focuses on the patient and coordinates care across the health system. The aim is to improve the patient experience, reduce costs, and boost population health. They are often referred to as medical homes for short.

PCMHs use a team-based approach that’s patient centered and culturally appropriate. They facilitate partnerships between patients, their healthcare providers, and sometimes, the patient’s family. They offer comprehensive, coordinated primary care that’s personal and focused on meeting the patient’s needs. PCMHs have been associated with improved quality of care, increased preventive care, cost savings, and increased patient and provider satisfaction.
