Dental Services

Comprehensive Dental Care

Chester Creek Dental is pleased to offer a wide array of dental care services spanning all areas of dentistry. Our passion is working closely with patients to understand their needs and desires. Dental care cannot be comprehensive if it does not provide a patient with a complete sense of dental well-being. Whether you have dental pain, functional concerns, cosmetic concerns or you just want someone to talk to, we are here for you.

Cosmetic Dentistry +

Dental well-being is not only about being free of oral disease, it is also about having confidence and pride in your smile. If you have cosmetic dental concerns we would love to work with you to come up with a customized solution.

Dental Hygiene +

We are blessed with a fantastic staff of trained and experienced hygienists. They will get to know you and your specific hygiene needs while making your experience comfortable and rewarding.

Dental Implants +

A dental implant is a screw-shaped titanium post that is placed into the bone where a tooth is missing. It mimics the function of a natural tooth root and provides an incredibly stable foundation for a dental prosthesis. At Chester Creek Dental, we expect our dental implants to last a long time and provide many years of reliable function with proper maintenance.

Oral Surgery +

Chester Creek Dental has been caring for patients for 40 years. Our experience with oral surgery procedures means we’ve refined our approach over many years and countless successful treatments. Whether you need a simple extraction or something more complex, we’ll make sure you understand exactly what to expect and feel supported throughout the process.

Endodontics +

Root canal treatments can be used effectively to save deeply infected teeth.

Orthodontic Services +

At our practice, orthodontics is not a separate service. It’s an extension of the relationship we’ve built with you and your family. If you’re wondering whether orthodontic care is right for you or your child, we’d be happy to talk through it.

Pediatric Dentistry +

As a family dental practice, we’ve built our reputation on creating positive experiences for children and peace of mind for parents. We communicate with kids at their level, whether we’re explaining a procedure to a curious five-year-old or discussing oral hygiene with a self-conscious teenager. Every child is different, and we adapt our approach to meet each one where they are.

Periodontal Disease Treatment +

For some patients, regular cleanings are not sufficient to control periodontal disease. There are many treatment options available for control of periodontal disease, including scaling and root planing as well as periodontal surgery.

Prosthodontics +

Prosthodontic treatments include complete dentures, partial dentures, implant-supported dentures, crowns and bridges.

Restorative Dentistry +

Restorative dentistry is about restoring your smile. That doesn’t just mean repairing or replacing teeth with the best treatment for you. It means giving you more reasons to smile, from the positive experience you’ll have to our team’s ability to ease dental pain.

State-of-the-Art Radiography +

Digital radiography means less radiation and less waiting time for you.

For more information about a particular dental procedure, please visit our What To Expect page.

Referrals to Dental Specialists

We provide a great deal of care in our own office. However, in some cases we prefer to utilize the vast knowledge and skill sets of dental specialists. Duluth has excellent dental specialists in all of the following areas:

  • Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
  • Endodontics
  • Prosthodontics
  • Periodontics
  • Orthodontics
  • Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology
  • Pediatric Dentistry
  • Temporomandibular Disorder (TMD)
