Orthodontic Services

A straighter smile is about more than looks. It’s about health, comfort, and confidence. Orthodontic services guide teeth into proper alignment, making it easier to clean your teeth, reduce wear and tear, improve bite function, and support overall oral health. Whether you’re seeking treatment for yourself or a loved one, we offer options that are as thoughtful and personalized as the care we give to every patient.

Are orthodontic services right for you?

Orthodontic care can be helpful at many stages of life. Children, teens, and adults may all benefit from treatment, depending on their needs. If you or a family member experiences crowding, gaps, bite issues, or simply wants a more aligned smile, we’d be happy to explore your options together. Depending on your situation, we will either refer you to a local orthodontist or discuss options for care right in our office.

Benefits of orthodontic treatment

We offer orthodontic treatment with a focus on comfort, convenience, and results. Our team takes time to explain every step, answer your questions, and create a plan that fits your life. With consistent follow-ups and gentle adjustments, we support steady progress at a pace that feels manageable.

Our in-house orthodontic solutions also mean fewer referrals, fewer appointments, and care that stays right here with the team you already know.

The difference at Chester Creek Dental

At our practice, orthodontics is not a separate service. It’s an extension of the relationship we’ve built with you and your family. We understand that life is busy and convenience is important. We also know that even adults can feel nervous about starting orthodontics. That’s why we offer a calm, judgment-free environment with modern tools and flexible options to help you feel at ease.

We’re here to listen, assess carefully, and guide you toward the option that fits best, both medically and personally.

SureSmile® Clear Aligners

For patients looking for a nearly invisible way to straighten their teeth, we offer SureSmile® clear aligners : a precise, customized, and removable alternative to traditional braces. These aligners are designed using advanced digital imaging for a tailored fit and efficient results.

Let’s talk teeth

If you’re wondering whether orthodontic care is right for you or your child, we’d be happy to talk through it. Schedule a gentle, no-pressure consultation with us to discuss your goals and concerns. We’ll walk through options, answer your questions, and help you feel informed every step of the way.

We look forward to helping your family smile with confidence.
