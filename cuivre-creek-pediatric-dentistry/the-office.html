About Our Office Cuivre Creek Pediatric Dentistry

0 About Our Office: Cuivre Creek Pediatric Dentistry (pronounced “quiver”) is a specialized pediatric oral health care office in Troy, Missouri. Dr. Casey named the practice after the Cuivre Creek, a branch of the Cuivre River that flows through his grandparent’s family farm in nearby Bowling Green, Missouri.

Cuivre Creek Pediatric Dentistry is located on the second floor of the Troy Professional Building in Troy, Missouri. The office was optimally designed to provide a non-intimating place for children to establish a dental home. The rural, farm-themed atmosphere is welcoming and familiar to infants and children. They will love the large canvas photographs of farm animals that decorate the walls, along with scoring some awesome toys at the end of each appointment!

Enjoy a movie in our rustic waiting room while your children play on our magnet boards and iPads in the play area. Our dental check-up and cleaning bay uses simple bench-style chairs that avoid intimidating lights or unnecessary tilting back and forth. Overhead TVs in the operatories allow our patients to watch cartoons while having restorative work completed. Please tour our office with the photographs below, and don’t hesitate to call us if you have any questions or special requests while at the office. Dr. Casey can’t wait to meet your family!

If you have a child that loves to hunt or fish, please bring in a picture of that big buck or first fish to put on the “bragging board!” It will make Dr. Casey’s day!
