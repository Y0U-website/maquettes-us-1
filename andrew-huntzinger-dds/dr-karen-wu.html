Dr. Karen Wu

MEET DR. KAREN WU

Dr. Karen Wu is a graduate of the University of Michigan and Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine. She went on to complete a general practice residency at the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston. Dr. Wu is a member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, Toledo Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Wu regularly attends CE courses to stay up to date with new technologies, materials, and techniques. She is Solea Laser certified and trained with the American Academy of Facial Esthetics on the management of TMJ and facial pain with botox. She enjoys all aspects of dentistry with special interest in cosmetic procedures, crown and bridge, pediatric dentistry, and management of dental anxiety.

Dr. Wu resides in Sylvania with her husband, Isaac, and their children Olivia and Julian.
