Meet the Team

Dr. Ben

Dr. Ben Nemeth is a native of St. Cloud. He completed his undergraduate studies at St. Olaf College. He then went on to receive his D.D.S. from the University of Minnesota and his orthodontic specialty training and Master of Science degree from the University of Manitoba.

Dr. Ben Nemeth spends a lot of time in his continuing education effort. He stays abreast of the latest advances in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics through memberships in the following professional organizations:

University of Minnesota Orthodontic Study Club

Executive Committee member of the MN Association of Orthodontists

Dr. Ben Nemeth was a past president of the St. Cloud Dental Society and of the West Central District of the Minnesota Dental Association.

Dr. Ben Nemeth has published his research in The American Journal of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics. The journal is the official publication of the American Association of Orthodontists and its constituent societies.

Dr. Ben Nemeth and his wife, Kari, have four sons. When he is away from the office, he enjoys spending time with his family, running and water and snow sports.
