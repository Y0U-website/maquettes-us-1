How Invisalign Works

Invisalign consists of customized clear aligners that are tailor-made to move with your smile as it progresses throughout treatment. When worn 20-22 hours each day, the continuous, gentle pressure that is applied by the aligners helps to move teeth in the right direction until the desired results are achieved. In the meantime, Invisalign aligners are to be worn for 1-2 weeks at a time before switching out each set for a new one.

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Invisalign is not designed for everyone, as some cases are too complex and require traditional braces to complete a successful process. With that said, if you are dealing with any of the following, you may be considered a good candidate for treatment. So, don’t hesitate to get in touch with us to schedule a consultation!

Crowded Teeth

Do your teeth crowd together? Are they tilted out of alignment? If so, Invisalign can move your teeth apart to create a healthy space. Not only will this improve the appearance of your smile, but it will also make it easier to thoroughly clean between teeth, reducing your risk of cavities and tooth decay. Plus, the entire process will be metal-free, making your teeth-straightening journey more discreet and comfortable than ever before.

Gaps Between Teeth

If you have gapped teeth, then you know all too well how easily food can get trapped between your teeth. Of course, there are aesthetic drawbacks too, which is why many teens and adults are interested in exploring their orthodontic treatment options. If you’re a candidate for Invisalign, then we can make sure that your teeth move closer together to close the space and generate a healthier and decay-free smile.

Overbite

An overbite is when there is considerable space between your upper and lower teeth. This can lead to several problems, including difficulty enunciating clearly, which is why it’s so important that you schedule a consultation with us to see if you’re a candidate for Invisalign. If you are, then we can create a custom series of clear aligners that place gentle pressure on your teeth, moving them and your bite into proper alignment.

Underbite

Unfortunately, even mild cases of underbites can lead to chronic jaw pain, TMJ dysfunction, and worn-down tooth enamel. But there is good news: Invisalign can realign your bite in such a way that it minimizes any discomfort caused by exhausted jaw muscles and joints. Furthermore, it prevents tooth enamel from wearing away over time while also giving you a more pleasing aesthetic.

Crossbite

Essentially, a crossbite is a mix of an overbite and an underbite. One of our biggest concerns is premature wear and tear on certain teeth, which can negatively impact the look, health, and function of your smile over time. To prevent that from happening, we can use clear aligners, attachments, and other orthodontic accessories to gently and meticulously move your teeth and bite into proper alignment.

Open Bite

If your front teeth or back teeth don’t come together evenly when you bite down, then you might have an open bite. The best thing to do is schedule a consultation with us so we can determine if that’s the root of the problem and, if it is, if Invisalign is the answer. That way, we can align your bite with clear, metal-free, and comfortable braces.

Benefits of Invisalign

Compared to metal braces, Invisalign comes with more (and varied) perks. It’s for this reason that the trays are pretty popular lately, especially among young adults and teens. These and other patients love that Invisalign can straighten smiles smoothly, easily, and quickly! As for the exact upsides of the treatment, Charm Smile Dental will gladly share them with you. Simply keep reading to learn the benefits of Invisalign in more detail.

Discreet Trays

While traditional braces would give you a metal mouth, that isn’t true with Invisalign. The latter’s trays are very discreet and blend seamlessly with your smile.

Don’t forget that Invisalign trays use transparent, near-invisible plastic. Thanks to this material, the aligners don’t stand out or draw unwanted attention from outsiders. They’re clear enough that you can wear them without friends, family, or peers noticing a thing. In fact, folks will just see that your smile is slowly (but surely) coming into proper alignment.

Improved Comfort

Invisalign doesn’t make you deal with brackets or wires like braces do. On the contrary, it offers a much comfier smile-aligning experience for patients.

It again comes down to Invisalign’s clear and smooth plastic material. This substance isn’t sharp and doesn’t poke around, so it shouldn’t upset your gums, lips, or the insides of your cheeks. The most you’ll feel from your trays is a slight soreness as you switch them. (Even then, the aches should fade within a day.)

Non-Invasive Care

Today’s Invisalign trays are far less invasive than metal braces. To that extent, you can trust your aligners won’t cause issues with your everyday life.

The lack of invasiveness stems from Invisalign’s removability. Since the trays can be taken off as needed, they won’t affect how you brush, floss, eat, or smile. These aligners also have shorter check-in visits than metal braces (due to their flexible wear time), so patients with hectic schedules can maintain treatment progress.

Better Dental Hygiene

Oral care can get tricky with metal braces, but Invisalign doesn’t have that issue. Its clear aligners don’t get in the way of your dental hygiene.

Remember that Invisalign trays are completely removable. Because they aren’t cemented to your teeth like braces, you can take them out of your mouth before brushing or flossing. That means they’ll let you clean your smile without disruptive elements like brackets or wires. You’ll then have a lower risk of cavities, gum disease, and other oral issues than you would while wearing braces.

Boosted Confidence

If you get Invisalign, it won’t make you feel awkward like metal braces could. Its trays should leave you assured and confident during and after treatment.

Invisalign’s main material is clear plastic, meaning its aligners are nearly invisible. You can trust that your trays won’t affect how your smile looks to others. This fact is part of what makes Invisalign appealing to teens and young adults who like to socialize.

Quick Follow-Ups

Invisalign treatment has swift follow-up visits. Indeed, these sessions don’t take as long as those for braces and involve less discomfort.

The average Invisalign check-in visit is done every 6-8 weeks. Each one only has a few steps, lasts about 15-30 minutes, and doesn’t require complicated setups. As a result, a dentist doesn’t need to do much to confirm your treatment is on track and moving your smile correctly.

Are There Any Dietary Restrictions with Invisalign?

Fortunately, the answer is no! As long as you remove your aligners, store them properly, and brush your teeth afterward, you can eat whatever you’d like throughout your orthodontic treatment. Of course, your dentist may make a few recommendations if you have a lot of attachments, like exercising caution when eating crunchy, hard, and sticky foods.

Can I Drink Coffee with Invisalign?

Yes, you can drink coffee during your Invisalign treatment! However, you shouldn’t drink any dark-colored beverages with your aligners in. That’s why we recommend enjoying this caffeinated beverage with your breakfast instead of separately. This will cut down on the amount of time you’re not wearing your aligners. Remember, they only work to move your teeth while you’re wearing them, so meeting the 20-22 hour-a-day wear time is essential.

How Long Does Invisalign Take?

Invisalign treatments average between 12 and 18 months. With that said, that doesn’t necessarily mean that’s how long your treatment will be. The length of yours will depend on the complexity of your case, how many aligners you need, and other important factors. As a result, you could end up wearing your aligners for as little as six months or more than 18 months it depends entirely on your specific dental needs.

What Happens After Invisalign?

You know that you need to work through your entire series of aligners, but what happens after that? Well, once you’re happy with your results, it’s time to preserve them with a custom retainer. Back in the day, patients were told they only had to wear theirs for a year or two. However, the rates of orthodontic relapse were also upwards of 50 %. That’s why dentists now encourage their patients to make wearing theirs a lifelong habit.
