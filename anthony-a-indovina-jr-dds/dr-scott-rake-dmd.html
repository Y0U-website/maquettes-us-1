Dr. Scott Rake, DMD at Dakota Valley Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Dr. Rake is a board-certified member of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons. He is a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, Minnesota Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, American Dental Association, and the St. Paul District Dental Society.

Dr. Scott Rake lives in the south metro on a working farm with his wife, oral surgeon, Dr. Angela Rake . He has one adult daughter who is a student and artist in New York.
