The practice

Specialized pediatric dental care rooted in the Coastal Carolina community where every child is treated like one of our own.

Coastal Pediatric Dentistry is committed to providing our patients with the best possible care in a comfortable and safe environment. We want our practice to be a place that kids and families look forward to visiting. Dr. Bailey’s experience in dentistry is coupled with genuine concern for her patients. All of our staff is dedicated to providing a fun, safe, and compassionate environment. Here we…

We offer a full range of pediatric dentistry services including preventative care, restorative care, special needs dentistry, and pediatric sedation dentistry.

Dr. Bailey is a board-eligible pediatric dentist and a North Carolina native. She completed her undergraduate studies at NC State University, where she was a diver on the varsity team for four years all while graduating with a degree in Materials Science and Engineering. The discipline, energy, and team spirit she built in the pool are the same qualities she now brings into the dental chair every…

After earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, Dr. Bailey completed her pediatric dental residency at Geisinger Medical Center in Pennsylvania, where she served as Chief Resident and gained extensive experience in preventive, minimally invasive, and restorative care for kids of all ages. Along the way, she’s always made it a priority to care for…

Dr. Bailey believes that a trip to the dentist should feel safe, fun, and even a little exciting. Whether she’s walking a nervous first-timer through their very first cleaning or guiding a family through more involved treatment, her goal is always the same: to help kids feel confident, comfortable, and proud of their smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Bailey is settling into life in New Bern with her husband, Zack, and their baby girl, Blake - who already has personality twice her size. She loves spending time with her family, reading, crafting, doing yoga, and all things Disney. She is thrilled to be putting down roots in the coastal community and can’t wait to be part of your child’s dental journey for years to come.
