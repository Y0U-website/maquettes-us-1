About Us

Now serving as the State Dental Officer in the Virginia National Guard, Dr. Paukert brings a deep commitment to patient-centered care at Charlottesville Dental Health Partners. A board-certified dentist and Master in the Academy of General Dentistry—an honor held by less than 1 % of dentists—he is also a published author and lecturer dedicated to lifelong learning.

Dr. Peter Fredrickson

Dr. Peter Fredrickson, a UVA alumnus originally from Virginia Beach, has returned to Charlottesville to continue his career in dentistry. After earning his D.D.S. with Honors in Esthetic Dentistry from NYU, he practiced in California, focusing on restorative, general, and cosmetic dentistry.

Now at Charlottesville Dental, he is dedicated to providing individualized, high-quality care rooted in the latest techniques and technologies. A lifelong learner and passionate practitioner, Dr. Fredrickson is trained in macro photography and advanced dental education through the Kois Center, and is an active member of organizations like the AACD, ADA, and SPEAR Education.
