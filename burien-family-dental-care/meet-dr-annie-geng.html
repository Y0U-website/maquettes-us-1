Annie Geng, DMD

Dr. Annie Geng is an exemplary dentist who caters to the oral needs of the population of Burien, WA. She completed her bachelor’s degree from the University of Toronto , Canada and her dental degree from Boston University . Currently, she performs her expertise at Burien Family Dental Care.

Dr. Geng holds active memberships in the American Dental Association and the Massachusetts Dental Society . Apart from her professional work, she has volunteered as an oral health screener in the Goldman School of Dental Medicine. She is also certified in Basic Life Support (BLS) by the American Heart Association.

Patients visit Dr. Geng for a wide spectrum of common and specialized dental needs, including dentures, tooth extractions, fillings, tooth decay, and toothaches. She understands the importance of quality care and leaves no stone unturned when it comes to providing such care.
