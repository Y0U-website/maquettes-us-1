Am I A Good Candidate For Invisalign?

Primarily, this depends on your orthodontic needs. Invisalign can usually be used to correct many orthodontic issues, including crooked teeth, gaps in your smile , or misaligned teeth.

However, Invisalign is not usually appropriate if you have major orthodontic problems like a malpositioned jaw or severely rotated, crooked teeth. Traditional braces are more effective for correcting these kinds of conditions. To find out if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign, we recommend scheduling a consultation with Dr. Blacher .

Caring For Your Aligners Our Top Tips

Wondering how you can care for your Invisalign aligners, and ensure your treatment progresses properly? Here are a few tips:

  • Rinse after removal Whenever you take your aligners out, rinse them to avoid the buildup of dried-out saliva, bacteria, and plaque.
  • Do not eat or drink while wearing your aligners Eating while wearing your aligners will damage them, and drinking any beverage except water could stain your aligners. Sugary beverages will also encourage bacterial growth. Do not eat or drink anything (except water) when wearing your aligners.
  • Keep your case at-hand Whenever you take your aligners out, you should put them into their carrying case to ensure they don’t get lost or broken. Losing or breaking an aligner can set your treatment back several weeks while a replacement is manufactured.
  • Brush after eating Whenever you eat, you should brush your teeth afterward to ensure bacteria does not collect around your aligners. If you are unable to do so, we recommend at least rinsing with water or mouthwash.
  • Clean your aligners every night Don’t use toothpaste, as this can erode the plastic material. Use an antibacterial soap that washes away cleanly, and use warm water. Do not use excessively hot water, which could cause the aligner to warp.
