About Dr. Chandler Reed

Dr. Chandler Reed was born and raised in Salt Lake City, UT. He received his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from Southern Utah University with honors. Dr. Reed went on to receive his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Virginia Commonwealth University.

Dr. Reed takes pride in providing patient focused care while keeping the big picture in mind. He enjoys getting to know each of his patients and strives to make every experience a positive one. The thing Dr. Reed loves most about dentistry is the opportunity to make a positive impact in his community on a daily basis.

When he’s not at work, Dr. Reed is likely at home with his high school sweetheart Haley and their two rescue dogs Finley and Taffy (yes he will always show you pictures if you ask). He enjoys baseball, is a huge Red Sox fan, playing board games, catching up with friends and most of all relaxing and enjoying life.

Dr. Reed would love to meet you and learn how he can make a difference for you!
