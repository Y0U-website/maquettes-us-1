Payment options:

⦁ Dental visits are an investment for your well-being. At Brookings Dental Clinic, we strive to provide you with high quality dentistry at an affordable price.

⦁ Payment is due at the time service is provided. If you have a copayment with your dental plan, we request it be paid at the time of your appointment.

⦁ We proudly accept CareCredit, which is an outside billing service that specializes in financing dental procedures with affordable interest-free payments up to 12 months. We also accept, cash, check, Visa, MasterCard, or Discover.

Dental Insurance:

⦁ As a courtesy, we are happy to assist you in filing your insurance claims. Please bring your insurance card to your appointment, as well as any necessary information if you are not the carrier of the plan.
