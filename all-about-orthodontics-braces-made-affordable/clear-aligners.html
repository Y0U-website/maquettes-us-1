Clear Aligners & 3M Clarity

Straighten discreetly with clear aligners.

A nearly invisible, removable way to straighten your smile — for teens and adults alike, with the same specialist care and transparent pricing.

A discreet & comfortable way to straighten your smile.

Looking for a virtually invisible way to get a straight smile? Clear aligners are a comfortable, hassle-free alternative to traditional braces — a popular choice for teens and adults alike. We treat with 3M Clarity Aligners .

Nearly invisible

A discreet solution with no metal brackets or wires — most people will never notice you are wearing them.

Custom-fit for effectiveness

Each aligner is precision-made to gradually shift your teeth into alignment, one tray at a time.

Removable for convenience

Eat your favorite foods, brush and floss without restrictions — just pop them out and back in.

Fewer office visits

Spend less time at the orthodontist with a treatment that fits around a busy schedule.

Your path to a perfect smile.

Advanced digital technology, a plan built around your teeth, and check-ins that fit your schedule.

Digital scan & custom plan

State-of-the-art 3D imaging maps your teeth, then we design a personalized aligner series.

Wear consistently

Aligners are worn 20–22 hours a day, switching to a new set every 1–2 weeks as teeth shift.

Expert monitoring

Our team checks in regularly to keep results on track and adjust the plan if needed.

Maintain your smile

After your series, a custom retainer keeps your teeth in their new position for good.

The benefits of 3M Clarity aligners.

3M Clarity isn’t a different treatment — it’s the aligner system we use, chosen for its precision, durability and comfort.

Advanced material for durability

High-quality, stain-resistant material keeps your aligners looking fresh throughout treatment.

Customized for your smile

Designed with cutting-edge digital technology for a precise, tailored plan and optimal results.

Gentle yet effective movement

Controlled pressure delivers efficient alignment — noticeable improvements in just a few months.

Virtually invisible & comfortable

A discreet alternative to traditional braces, so you can smile confidently while in treatment.
