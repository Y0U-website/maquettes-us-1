Quality orthodontics, made affordable for everyone.

At All About Orthodontics® , we offer comprehensive orthodontic services for all ages — regardless of financial barriers — because we believe that everyone deserves quality orthodontic treatment at an affordable price that fits within your budget.

From children and teens to adults, we tailor every treatment plan to your unique smile, with a focus on comfort, honesty, and results that last across our Warren and Southgate offices.

Known warmly as “Dr. P,” Dr. Putrus is an orthodontic specialist with advanced training and a 3-year post-dental-school residency. Over 8 years he’s built a practice around one belief: exceptional orthodontic care should be accessible to everyone.
