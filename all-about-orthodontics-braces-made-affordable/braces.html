Metal braces

The gold standard for decades. High-quality stainless steel brackets, now smaller and more comfortable — and customizable with colored elastics.

Clear braces

Translucent, tooth-colored ceramic brackets that blend with your enamel. The same effectiveness as metal, in a far subtler look.

Gold braces

The strength and performance of metal braces in an elegant gold-plated finish — a bold, stylish statement that lasts the whole treatment.
