Nitrous Oxide vs Oral Sedation

Choosing between nitrous oxide and oral sedation depends on several factors, including your anxiety level, the complexity of your procedure, and your personal preferences.

Oral Sedation Benefits:

  • Quick onset—you’ll feel relaxed within minutes
  • Effects wear off rapidly, allowing you to drive yourself home
  • Adjustable levels throughout your procedure for optimal comfort
  • No needles or pills required
  • Ideal for mild to moderate anxiety
  • Safe for both children and adults

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas) Benefits:

For oral sedation, yes. You must arrange for a responsible adult to drive you to and from your appointment and stay with you until the sedation fully wears off.
