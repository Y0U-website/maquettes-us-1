Are Implants Worth it?

​Proven to be safe and effective for over half a century, dental implants serve as a strong and sturdy foundation for supporting replacement teeth that don’t require lifestyle modifications. With proper oral care and follow-up dental visits with Dr. Kostrzewski and Dr. Spennato, your new dental implants should last 25 years or more, making them a cost-effective alternative to other tooth-restoration procedures.
