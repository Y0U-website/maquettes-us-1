What Is the Process of Getting Full Mouth Dental Implants?

All-on-X implants can be a life-changing solution that restores your ability to enjoy simple things like your favorite meal. We understand that many people feel unsure about getting dental implants because of the longer process involved compared to quick fixes like dentures, but the results are well worth the time commitment! We work closely with trusted implant surgeons near you in Sonoma County to ensure a seamless, successful process.

  • Consultation & Exam – We conduct a thorough evaluation of your oral health and discuss your goals to create a customized treatment plan. Your lifestyle, budget, and overall needs will guide our recommendations.
  • Implant Placement – If necessary, we’ll perform preparatory procedures like tooth extractions or bone grafting. Then, your implant surgeon will use advanced techniques and effective pain management to place your implant posts safely and efficiently.
  • Temporary Restorations – Whenever possible, we provide a temporary restoration so you can continue daily activities with ease while your implants heal.
  • Healing & Integration – Over the next few months, your implants will bond with your jawbone, creating a secure foundation for your permanent teeth.
  • Final Smile Transformation – Once your implants have fully integrated, we’ll attach your custom-designed, durable teeth. These restorations are crafted to mimic the beauty and strength of natural enamel, giving you a long-lasting, radiant smile.
