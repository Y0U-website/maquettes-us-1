Delivering Modern Dentistry

Learn About Our Technology

At Clemson Family Dentistry, we are dedicated to delivering comfortable, modern dental treatments. As such, we use only the latest tools, technology, and equipment including cone beam computed tomography (CBCT), intraoral scanners, and CEREC dental crowns. This helps us keep you comfortable and ensures our treatments are as effective and precise as possible.

We Are the Official Dentist of Clemson University Athletics!

Clemson Family Dentistry is proud to be recognized as the official dentist of Clemson University Athletics. We are honored that this prestigious organization puts their trust in our team. For care that is trusted by the roughest and toughest athletes out there, we have you covered.

Going Beyond Dentistry

Our Outreach

We pride ourselves on being connected with our community and feel strongly that it is our duty and privilege to give back whenever we can. That is why we support many community efforts and organizations. This includes sponsoring sports teams, school booster clubs, school fundraisers, the scholarship efforts of Clemson University, local Rotary clubs, and the Oconee Memorial Foundation. If you’d like us to consider supporting your local organization or nonprofit, don’t hesitate to reach out !
