Preventive Dentistry in Decatur GA

Preventive dentistry is a specialized field of dentistry focused on caring for the teeth and gums to keep them healthy, strong, and free from decay or disease. Decatur GA preventive dentistry treatments and procedures help detect problems early on when they are easier to treat and prevent serious dental problems from occurring in the future.

By practicing good oral hygiene habits at home and seeing the dentist regularly, you can reduce your risk of developing cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues. Patients of all ages should brush twice daily, floss daily, and visit the dentist at least every six months for checkups and cleanings. These practices help remove plaque buildup from the surfaces of teeth and prevent cavities from forming.

Preventative Dentistry Treatments and Procedures

Regular dental cleanings and exams are essential to maintaining good oral health. Our dentist, Dr. Dahlia Levine, will check for signs of decay, infection, or other problems during your exam. We will also remove plaque and tartar from your teeth, which can help prevent cavities and gum disease.

Fluoride is a mineral that helps strengthen tooth enamel and prevent tooth decay. We offer fluoride treatments at our office to protect your teeth against cavities.

A sealant is a thin, protective coating placed on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth to help prevent cavities. Sealants are often used on children’s teeth but can also be used on adults’ teeth.

We will perform an oral cancer screening during your routine appointment to look for any abnormalities in your mouth that could be a sign of cancer. Early detection is critical to successful treatment of this severe condition.

The Benefits of Preventative Dentistry

Regular visits to the dentist can prevent tooth decay and other dental issues and are an essential part of preventative dentistry. During your visit to our dental practice, our dentist or hygienist will clean your teeth to remove plaque and tartar that can cause cavities. The dentist will also check for signs of decay or other oral health issues. If a problem is found, it can be treated with a simple procedure before it progresses and becomes more serious.

Preventive dentistry in Decatur, GA, can also prevent gum disease. Bacteria in plaque and tartar buildup cause gum disease. This can cause your gums to become inflamed, red, swollen, and tender. Not treated, these issues can lead to tooth loss and other health problems.

Finally, preventive dentistry can help to keep your teeth looking their best. A professional cleaning removes surface stains and keeps your teeth looking bright and white. You can also discuss cosmetic treatments with our dentist to improve the appearance of your smile.

Emphasis Of Preventative Dentistry in Decatur GA
