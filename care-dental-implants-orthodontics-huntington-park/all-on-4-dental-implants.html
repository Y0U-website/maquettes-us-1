What is All-on-X ?

All-on-X — also called All-on-4 — is a full-arch tooth replacement that uses just a few strategically placed implants to support a complete set of upper or lower teeth. It’s made for people with several missing or failing teeth, or a denture they’re tired of fighting with, and it gives you a fixed, natural-looking smile — often in a single day. Because the teeth are anchored to the implants instead of resting on your gums, they stay secure and comfortable when you eat and talk.

Why people choose fixed teeth.

Fixed, not removable

Your teeth stay in your mouth — anchored to implants, not resting on your gums. Nothing to take out or soak.

Often in one day

Many patients leave with a full set of temporary teeth the same day, then return for the final, custom set.

Works even with bone loss

All-on-X is designed to use the bone you have. Even long-time denture wearers are often candidates — we’ll tell you honestly.

Is All-on-X right for you?

Multiple missing or failing teeth

You have several missing teeth — or teeth that are failing and need to come out — and you want a complete restoration rather than piece-by-piece repairs.

A denture that won’t stay put

You’re unhappy with a denture that slips, shifts, or feels uncomfortable, and you want teeth that stay in place on their own.

Bone loss in the jaw

You’ve experienced significant bone loss. All-on-X is designed to work with the bone you have, so many people who’ve been told they aren’t candidates still are.

You want fixed, permanent teeth

You’re looking for a fixed, permanent solution that looks and feels natural — not something you take out at night.

Common questions about All-on-X

All-on-4 replaces a full arch — every tooth on the top or the bottom — with a complete, fixed set of teeth anchored on about four implants. Some cases use a few more implants for extra support, which is why it’s also called All-on-X. If you need both the upper and lower replaced, that’s two arches. We confirm the exact plan with a 3-D scan.

In many cases, yes. Most patients leave surgery with a full set of fixed temporary teeth the same day, so you’re never without a smile. Your permanent set is crafted and fitted after your implants heal to the bone, usually over the following few months. We’ll tell you at your consultation whether same-day teeth are right for your case.

With good care, All-on-X implants are built to last for many years. The titanium implants fuse with your jawbone and can serve you long-term, while the attached set of teeth may eventually need refreshing from normal wear. Daily cleaning, regular checkups, and avoiding tobacco all help your new smile stay strong.

The procedure is more comfortable than most people expect. It’s done under local anesthesia, with sedation options available, so you won’t feel pain while the implants are placed. Some mild swelling or soreness for a few days afterward is normal and is easily managed with pain relievers. Most patients are back to daily life quickly.

Caring for All-on-X is much like caring for natural teeth. Brush at least twice a day, clean beneath the fixed teeth (the prosthesis) the way we show you, and use any tools we recommend to keep the area free of plaque. Keep up with regular checkups and cleanings, and we’ll monitor your implants to keep your smile healthy for years.
