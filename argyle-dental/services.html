BOOK AN APPOINTMENT Our Dental Services in Syracuse, UT OUR SERVICES We Offer the Following Services Through these services, our goal is to help you achieve and maintain optimum dental health by regular hygiene visits, dental exams, and healthy home oral health habits.

TMJ and Jaw Pain Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMJ) is a condition affecting the jaw joint and muscles that control jaw movement. It can cause pain, discomfort, clicking or popping sounds, difficulty chewing, and even headaches. TMJ can significantly impact daily life and overall well-being.

Sleep Related Issues Sleep issues can encompass a range of problems, including disrupted sleep patterns, snoring, and sleep apnea. These issues can lead to daytime fatigue, difficulty concentrating, irritability, and overall decreased quality of life. Snoring, often a symptom of underlying sleep disorders, not only affects the individual but can also disrupt the sleep of their partner, leading to strain on relationships.

If you or someone you know is struggling with sleep issues, it’s essential to seek professional help. Argyle Dental specializes in diagnosing and treating various sleep disorders, including snoring and sleep apnea. Their experienced team can provide comprehensive evaluations and personalized treatment plans to help you achieve better sleep and improve your overall well-being.

Same Day Crowns (Cerec) Crowns are a permanent covering over a tooth that has decayed or cracked. The decayed or damaged part is removed and built back up to provide for a strong foundation. All of our crowns are done on the same day eliminating the need to wear a temporary crown or come back for a second visit to get the permanent crown. We also do all porcelain crowns that look much better than traditional PFM or gold crowns. Patients seldom can tell which tooth has a crown because they fit and feel so natural.

Implants Dental implants are a wonderful replacement for missing teeth. Whether you are only missing one tooth, a couple, or all of them, we can replace missing teeth with implants allowing you to smile and eat comfortably again.

Bonding / Veneers Bonding and Veneers can fix chipped or cracked teeth, reduce gaps, and overall improve the appearance of your teeth. It is a quick process and bonds to an existing tooth.

Inlays/Onlays (Cerec) Often only a part of a tooth is broken down and needs to be replaced. Inlays and onlays are perfect solutions to help preserve healthy, natural parts of the tooth while still providing a strong structure that will last for years. These procedures can also be perform in a single visit in our office as opposed the traditional two visits. Inlays and Onlays are typically made out of a porcelain material.

General Maintenence and Prevention Regular 6 month appointments help prevent decay and other problems. We also provide more frequent appointments to patients who have a history of periodontal disease and need to be seen more often.

Periodontal Therapy If you have been diagnosed with periodontal disease or have gums that bleed often, we can perform periodontal scaling and root planing bring your gums back to health. Many patients are aware that they may have this disease and it is often underdiagnosed.

Teeth Whitening Improve your appearance with a bright smile. This service is growing in popularity and we provide several fast, safe and effective options to whiten your teeth.

Fillings The concept of a “filling” is replacing and restoring your tooth structure that is damaged due to decay or fracture with a material. We will replace old, broken-down amalgam/metal fillings with white fillings (composites) to restore your smile and teeth to a more natural look and feel.

Bridges A bridge can prevent shifting of the teeth and jaw problems by filling a space that a previous tooth occupied. There are a few different types and we will help determine which one is best for you.

Dentures/Partials We provide several different artificial tooth replacements and strive to help you feel confident and natural.

Extractions Sometimes the best thing or the only thing to do is to remove a tooth. This happens when a tooth has an excessive amount of decay that would not allow the dentist to have adequate tooth structure to place a crown. A severely broken tooth could also require extraction. Extreme cases of periodontal disease would also necessitate removing a tooth. There are multiple options to replace removed or missing teeth.

Digital Impressions No longer do we need to get impressions of teeth with a bunch of goop! Our office has the technology that allows us to digitally scan your teeth and recreate 3D representations in order to make crowns and perform various other procedures.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas) Sedation Laughing gas provides anxious patients an opportunity to relax while they have their dental treatment performed. No escorts are needed because of the immediate removal of any effects once we take you off of this relaxing gas.

Microneedling Microneedling is a minimally invasive procedure using thin needles. The procedure can help reduce the appearance of skin concerns such as acne scars, stretch marks or wrinkles.

BTA & Jeuveau BOTOX, more correctly called botulinum toxin A (BTA), has been used safely in esthetics for years. There are several trade names for botulinum toxin A marketed by various companies (e.g., BOTOX, Dysport, Jeuveau, Xeomin, Daxxify).

Bio Dermal Fillers Dermal fillers can directly address signs of skin aging and noticeable hollowness through a quick non-surgical procedure. Dermal fillers are soft tissue injectables administered into the skin to fill in areas that lack volume.
