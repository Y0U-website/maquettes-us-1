BENEFITS

More Benefits of BTA

Temporary, but long-lasting wrinkle solution

How long does it last?

Results may vary, depending on the particular BTA used, but expect to see results 3-7 days after your procedure but maximum results typically are present two weeks after treatment. So be sure to give us notice well before the day of that big event! Your skin will be smoother and

wrinkle-free after your BTA treatment! These results will begin to fade typically around 3-4 months, however, you can safely repeat BTA treatment three to four times a year to maintain the look you desire. BTA also prevents new lines from occurring and with continued use, your face will begin to “forget” to wrinkle and often require fewer treatments over time as the muscles become weaker from not functioning.

Does it hurt?

Pain from the injections is minimal. The needle used to inject is very little.

How much does BTA cost?

Some doctors charge by the area, and some charge by the unit. At our office, we charge by the unit to ensure you get exactly what you need and only pay for what you need. Also, different BTA products cost different amounts and require more or less units dependent on the type of BTA injected. The cost is $9-12 per unit depending on the BTA product used.

Aftercare for BTA

There is virtually no aftercare required and you can drive yourself to and from the appointment. Should any minor, temporary bruising occur, you may cover it with makeup and continue with your daily schedule.

What not to do after a BTA? Don’t get a facial or head massage for a full 24 hours after fillers. You should also avoid rubbing your face in the area that was treated. Don’t lie down for at least

1 hour after receiving fillers in your face. Don’t go into any saunas, hot tubs, or tanning booths for at least 4 hours.
