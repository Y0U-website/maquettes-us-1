The practice

At Harris Dental Clinic, we understand that every patient’s dental needs are unique. That’s why we offer a wide range of dental services to ensure that we can provide the care that you need. Whether you’re looking for routine dental exams and cleanings or more advanced treatments such as dental implants or root canals, we have everything you need to keep your smile healthy and bright.

State-of-the-Art Technology Our Laurel practice uses the most advanced tools and technology to care for our patients. With digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and laser dentistry, our treatments are safer and more comfortable than ever before.

Comprehensive Dental Care Our dentists provide a comprehensive selection of procedures to address all your dental care needs. Since we gladly treat patients of all ages, our office is a convenient one-stop shop for the entire family.

Affordable Treatment We believe that everyone deserves high-quality dental work regardless of their budget. Our practice provides several solutions to make treatment more affordable, including financing with little or no interest.
