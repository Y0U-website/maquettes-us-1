What to Expect: The Braces Treatment Process

1. Free Consultation & Treatment Planning

Your first visit includes a free orthodontic assessment in Sheffield Village OH, during which Dr. Herberger examines tooth alignment, jaw position, and reviews your dental history. We’ll take photos, X‑rays, and digital scans to craft a personalized treatment plan.

2. Getting Braces Applied

On your next visit, we bond brackets and attach wires customized to your smile. You’ll get detailed instructions on caring for your braces, including oral hygiene best practices and dietary adjustments.

3. Periodic Follow‑Up Appointments

Every 4–6 weeks, you’ll return for adjustments. These office visits ensure your treatment is progressing as planned and keep everything comfortably moving forward.

4. Completion & Retainers

Once your teeth reach their ideal positions, braces are removed and you’ll receive a retainer. Retainers are vital for Sheffield Village OH patients to maintain your new smile and prevent relapse.

Caring for Your Braces

Brushing & Flossing

Brush after every meal using fluoride toothpaste and a soft-bristled brush to avoid plaque buildup. Flossing with a threader or specialized tool helps clean between brackets and wires.

Foods to Avoid

To prevent damage to your braces, steer clear of:

  • Sticky treats like caramel or taffy
  • Hard items such as popcorn kernels, nuts, and hard candies
  • Excessively chewy or crunchy snacks

Managing Discomfort

After adjustments, mild soreness is common. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help. Applying orthodontic wax over protruding wires adds comfort until your mouth adjusts.
