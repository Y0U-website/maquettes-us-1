Frequently Asked Questions

How do implant-supported dentures differ from regular dentures?

Traditional dentures sit directly on top of your gums and rely on natural suction or dental glues to stay in place, meaning they can still slip and slide. Implant-supported dentures snap directly onto stationary titanium posts embedded firmly in your jawbone, eliminating shifting and friction.

Can I sleep with implant-supported dentures in?

No. While they are stable and stay locked in place all day, implant-supported dentures are designed to be removed at night. Treating them this way allows your gum tissues to rest and breathe, preventing irritation, and makes it easy to thoroughly clean your prosthetic and the underlying attachments.

How many dental implants do I need for a snap-in denture?

Generally, a snap-in overdenture requires between two and four dental implants per arch, though the exact number depends on your specific bone density, whether we are treating the upper or lower jaw, and your functional needs. Dr. Morgan will determine the ideal number during your initial 3D digital imaging evaluation.

Am I a good candidate for implant-supported dentures?

Most adults who are missing an entire arch of teeth, or who currently wear traditional dentures, are excellent candidates. The primary requirement is having adequate jawbone density to safely support the titanium posts. Even if you have experienced bone loss, Dr. Morgan can evaluate your 3D scans to determine if you are a candidate or if a minor preliminary procedure can help.

Does getting implant-supported dentures hurt?

We prioritize your physical and emotional comfort. We offer dental sedation options to ensure you remain relaxed and stress-free. Most patients report only mild, temporary tenderness during the days following the procedure, which is easily managed with standard aftercare instructions.
