Precision Planning and Flawless Execution

We prioritize your relaxation from the moment you walk through our doors and make your treatment as predictable and comfortable as possible.

Step 1: Planning Your Care

Before treatment begins, Dr. Morgan carefully evaluates your oral health, bone structure, and aesthetic goals. Advanced imaging and careful planning help place your implants precisely, leading to predictable results that will look natural and feel secure.

Step 2: Placing the Foundation

A titanium implant is carefully placed into the jawbone to replace the missing tooth root. This creates a strong base designed to support long-term stability and function.

Step 3: Giving Your Smile Time to Heal

As healing occurs, the implant naturally bonds with the jawbone. This process creates the secure connection needed for your restoration to feel comfortable and dependable.

Step 4: Preparing for Your New Tooth

After healing, a small connector is placed on the implant. This allows the final tooth to sit securely while maintaining proper fit and comfort.

Step 5: Completing Your Smile

A custom-made crown is designed to match your natural teeth in shape, color, and proportion. Once placed, it restores both the look and function of your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant process typically takes several months from start to finish, allowing time for proper healing. The exact timeline depends on your treatment plan, and Dr. Morgan will provide a clear schedule during your consultation.

Does getting dental implants hurt?

Most patients experience little to no pain during dental implant placement. Any post-procedure discomfort is usually mild and temporary and can be managed with medication and simple aftercare instructions.

Are dental implants as strong as natural teeth?

Yes, dental implants are designed to function like natural teeth. By replacing the tooth root, they provide strong, stable support for chewing, speaking, and everyday use.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many adults with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Dr. Morgan evaluates factors such as oral health, bone structure, and overall health to determine the best treatment option for you.
