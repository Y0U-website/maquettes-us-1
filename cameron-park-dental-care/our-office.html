Insurance Plans

We accept Indemnity and Private insurance plans. Please call us with your insurance information prior to your visit and we will be happy to explain your benefits to you.

Payment Options

We are considered a "contracted provider" with Delta Dental Premier Plan. This means we charge you the discounted fee’s that we are contracted to charge. For your convenience, we accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover credit cards as well as cash and personal checks. In addition, financing options such as CareCredit are available for major dental work done in our office. Our billing coordinator will be happy to discuss any insurance or billing questions you may have.

Convenient Location

Our office is located at 3470 Palmer Drive, Suite 2, Cameron Park, CA 95682 with parking available in our lot.

We are located near the Hwy 50 - Cameron Park Drive exit, in the Golderado Shopping Center next to Jiffy Lube. Those using Hwy 50 should take the Cameron Park Drive exit, turn north and then turn right between the gas station and Kentucky Fried Chicken. Upon entering the shopping center, turn right and you will see the office on your right.

Driving Directions

Coming from Placerville: Take Highway 50 towards Sacramento (west) and exit the freeway at Cameron Park Drive and turn right. Go past the Gas station and turn right into the BelAir shopping center. We are located on your immediate right, directly between Jiffy Lube and The Kayoto Japenese Restraunt.

Coming from Sacramento: Take Highway 50 towards Placerville (east), exit the freeway at Cameron Park Drive and turn left. Proceed under the freeway overpass and turn right directly after the gas station into the BelAir shopping center. We are located on your immediate right directly in between Jiffy Lube and Kayoto Japanese Restaurant.
