Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most patients benefit from routine dental exams and cleanings every six months, although some individuals may require more frequent visits.

Do you offer same-day dental services?

Yes. We provide same-day emergency care, same-day crowns using CEREC technology, and same-day denture repairs for eligible patients.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Contact our office as soon as possible. We offer same-day emergency appointments whenever possible to address urgent dental concerns.

Can you replace missing teeth?

Yes. We offer several tooth replacement options, including dental implants, bridges, and dentures, depending on your needs.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely. Our pediatric dental services focus on preventive care, education, and creating positive dental experiences for children of all ages.
