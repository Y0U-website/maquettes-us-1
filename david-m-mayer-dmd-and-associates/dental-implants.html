Treatment Options

Every patient’s smile is unique, which is why we provide personalized treatment recommendations based on your needs and goals.

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

A dental implant and custom crown can replace one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Patients missing multiple teeth in a row may benefit from a bridge supported by dental implants.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Dental implants can provide additional support and stability for full or partial dentures, improving comfort and confidence.

Tooth Replacement Following Extraction

If a tooth requires extraction, implants may be discussed as part of your long-term restoration plan.

What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are completed in several stages to ensure long-term success.

We’ll evaluate your oral health, review imaging, and determine whether you’re a candidate for dental implants.

A titanium implant is carefully placed into the jawbone to serve as the foundation for your future restoration.

Over the following months, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration.

Once healing is complete, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, completing your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

You got the questions, and we got the answers; please feel free to get in touch.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots and support crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone support are candidates for implant treatment. A consultation will determine if implants are right for you.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and routine dental care, dental implants can last for many years and often decades.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes. Custom restorations are designed to match the appearance of your surrounding teeth, creating a seamless result.

Are dental implants comfortable?

Once fully healed, dental implants typically feel and function much like natural teeth.

How long does the implant process take?

Treatment timelines vary depending on individual needs, healing time, and whether additional procedures are required.

Can implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants can support single crowns, bridges, or even full-arch restorations depending on your situation.

Will dental implants help prevent bone loss?

Yes. Because implants stimulate the jawbone similarly to natural tooth roots, they help reduce the bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.
