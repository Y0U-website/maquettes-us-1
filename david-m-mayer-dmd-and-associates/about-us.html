Caring for Smiles, Changing Lives

At David M. Mayer, DMD, we are more than just a dental practice – we’re a trusted partner in your journey to optimal oral health and confidence. With over 40 years of experience serving the Paterson, NJ community, we’ve built a reputation for compassionate care, advanced technology, and personalized treatments tailored to each patient’s unique needs.

We understand that visiting the dentist can feel overwhelming, especially for our elderly patients, which is why we’ve created a welcoming and relaxed environment. Our team is here to listen, answer your questions, and provide care that makes you feel valued and understood.

Whether it’s same-day emergency dental care, repairing dentures, or restoring your smile with cutting-edge dental implants, our mission is to deliver exceptional care that keeps you smiling for years to come.

Our Philosophy

We believe every smile is as unique as the person it belongs to. That’s why we take the time to get to know you, understand your concerns, and develop a treatment plan that works for your lifestyle and goals. At our practice, you’re never just a patient – you’re family.

Meet Dr. David M. Mayer

Known for his gentle approach and meticulous care, Dr. Mayer combines his expertise with a genuine passion for improving lives.
