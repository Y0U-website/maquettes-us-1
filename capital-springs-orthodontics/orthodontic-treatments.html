Personalized Orthodontic Treatments We Offer at Our Practice

After performing a thorough evaluation of your orthodontic needs, Dr. Brad will develop a customized treatment plan to help you achieve a straighter smile. Our treatment options include:

Braces

Conventional braces are one of the most reliable ways to address the misalignment of teeth and jaws. At our practice, we use advanced metal braces with smaller brackets and specialized archwires that offer greater comfort. Once we attach them to your teeth, they apply gentle, consistent pressure to gradually shift teeth into proper alignment. With this versatile treatment, we can treat a wide range of misalignment or bite issues.

Invisalign®

If you are looking for a more discreet way to straighten your teeth, Invisalign® may be the ideal option. This advanced system uses a series of clear, removable aligners that are custom-made to fit your teeth and guide them into a straight alignment. Since the aligners are virtually invisible, you can go about your day without feeling self-conscious about your smile. You can also remove them easily before eating or drinking anything, meaning no dietary restrictions during treatment!

Retainers

Once your orthodontic treatment is complete, wearing retainers is essential to preserve your newly aligned smile. Our dental practice offers different types of retainers based on your specific orthodontic needs. They help keep your teeth in place and prevent them from shifting back over time. We will guide you through the proper use of retainers to ensure long-lasting results.
