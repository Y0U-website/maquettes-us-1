Braces

Braces remain one of the most effective ways to correct misaligned teeth, bite issues, and jaw concerns. With options like metal, ceramic, and clear braces, you can get a straighter, healthier smile comfortably and efficiently. Whether you’re a teen or an adult, our team will guide you through every step of your journey.

Invisalign®

Invisalign® offers a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth using clear, removable aligners. These aligners make it easy to eat, brush, and floss without the restrictions of traditional braces. Perfect for busy teens and adults, Invisalign® combines convenience with predictable, beautiful results.

Airway / Sleep Apnea

Orthodontic treatment can play an important role in addressing airway issues and sleep apnea. By guiding jaw development and improving alignment, we can help open the airway for better breathing and improved sleep quality. Our customized approach focuses on both your oral health and your overall well-being.

Surgical Orthodontics

For patients with severe jaw misalignment or facial imbalances, surgical orthodontics offers a path to improved function, appearance, and comfort. We combine orthodontic treatment and corrective jaw surgery to achieve lasting, transformative results that enhance both your smile and quality of life.

Orthodontic Retainers

Retainers are essential for keeping your smile straight and healthy after orthodontic treatment. We design retainers to fit comfortably and maintain your results. With proper use and care, your retainer helps protect the time and effort you’ve invested in your smile.

Zoom Whitening

Zoom Whitening offers a safe and efficient way to improve your smile. This professional treatment helps reduce stains and discoloration, providing noticeable improvement in tooth brightness with care tailored to your needs. Ask us if Zoom Whitening is right for your smile goals.

When is the best time to start orthodontic treatment?

We recommend an orthodontic screening by age 7. Early evaluation lets us identify issues early and guide your child for the most effective treatment when permanent teeth arrive.

Can adults get braces?

Absolutely! Many adults achieve straighter smiles with options like Invisalign® or clear braces, making treatment comfortable, discreet, and tailored to busy lifestyles.

What is Phase I treatment?

Phase I is early orthodontic care for children, addressing developing issues to prevent complications later. It often reduces future treatment time and may eliminate more invasive procedures.

My dentist offers braces; why should I see an orthodontist?

Orthodontists have specialized training to correct bite and alignment issues. At Capital Springs, we ensure precise, effective treatment for both simple and complex cases.
