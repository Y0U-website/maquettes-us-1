Types of Braces Available in Sherman Oaks

At Clove Dental, we offer a range of orthodontic treatments to suit different preferences and lifestyles:

1. Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional braces use high-quality stainless steel brackets and wires to gradually align your teeth, making them one of the most effective options for orthodontic treatment.

  • Suitable for complex orthodontic cases.
  • Budget-friendly option.
  • Add colorful elastics for a personalized touch.

2. Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces use clear or tooth-colored brackets that blend in with your natural teeth, offering a more discreet orthodontic solution.

  • Less noticeable than traditional metal braces.
  • Suitable for addressing various orthodontic issues.
  • Ideal for image-conscious teens and adults.

3. Lingual Braces

For those who prefer an invisible treatment, lingual braces are attached to the back of your teeth, making them completely hidden from view.

  • Invisible from the front.
  • Custom-fitted for each patient’s teeth for maximum comfort.
  • Great for professionals who need a discreet orthodontic option.

The Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Investing in orthodontic treatment offers more than just a beautiful smile. Here are some key benefits:

  • Improved Oral Hygiene : Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Correct Bite Alignment : Braces can fix bite issues, making chewing and speaking easier.
  • Long-Lasting Results : Properly aligned teeth are less likely to wear down unevenly, protecting your oral health in the long run.
  • Boosted Confidence : A straight smile can significantly enhance your self-esteem.

Do’s and Don’ts During Orthodontic Treatment

Do’s:

  • Brush and floss regularly to maintain clean and healthy teeth.
  • Use orthodontic wax to reduce discomfort caused by brackets or wires.
  • Visit us for regular adjustments to ensure your treatment stays on track.

Don’ts:

  • Avoid hard and sticky foods that can damage your braces.
  • Don’t skip appointments; regular visits are essential for optimal progress.
  • Do not use your teeth to open packages or bite hard objects.
