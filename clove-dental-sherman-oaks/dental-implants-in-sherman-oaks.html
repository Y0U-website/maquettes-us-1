Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are suitable for most individuals with one or more missing teeth. However, several factors must be assessed to determine candidacy:

  • You have missing teeth and are seeking a permanent replacement.
  • You possess sufficient bone density in your jaw to support the implant.
  • You maintain good oral hygiene and overall health.
  • You are free from gum disease or other conditions that could hinder healing.
  • You are prepared for a treatment process that typically requires several months, including healing and follow-up visits. If you are uncertain about your suitability for dental implants, our implant dentist in Sherman Oaks will conduct a thorough evaluation and create a personalized treatment plan.

The Dental Implant Process

At Clove Dental in Sherman Oaks, we follow a structured approach to ensure the success of your dental implants. Here’s what you can expect during your treatment:

  • Initial Consultation: In your first visit, our implant dentist will perform a comprehensive oral examination and take X-rays or 3D scans to assess the health of your jawbone. This evaluation helps determine whether your jaw can support an implant. We will also discuss your medical history and develop a tailored treatment plan based on your unique needs.
  • Implant Placement: The next step is surgically placing the titanium implant post into your jawbone. This post will serve as the foundation for your new tooth. Over the following months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, creating a solid base for the final restoration.
  • Healing and Recovery: After the implant is placed, your jawbone will require time to heal and fully integrate with the post. This healing phase typically lasts several months.
  • Abutment and Crown Placement: Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the post, and a custom-made dental crown is placed on top, completing the restoration and giving you a fully functional, aesthetically pleasing tooth.

Types of Dental Implants

At Clove Dental in Sherman Oaks, we offer various types of dental implant solutions to meet the specific needs of our patients:

  • Single Tooth Implants: Ideal for replacing a single missing tooth, this option involves placing one implant and attaching a custom-made crown for a seamless restoration.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: For patients who are missing multiple teeth, an implant-supported bridge can replace several teeth using fewer implant posts, providing a stable and cost-effective solution.
  • All-on-4 Implants: If you are missing most or all of your teeth, the All-on-4 treatment uses four strategically positioned implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, ensuring full functionality and aesthetics with fewer implants.
