The Scaling & Root Planing Process

At Clove Dental, we focus on making your experience as comfortable as possible. Here’s what to expect:

  • Thorough Examination: Your dentist will carefully examine your gums and measure the depth of the pockets around each tooth to assess the severity of gum disease.
  • Scaling: Using specialized instruments, our team will meticulously remove plaque and tartar from the surfaces of your teeth and beneath the gums.
  • Root Planing: After scaling, the dentist will smooth the roots of your teeth, promoting healing and preventing bacteria from accumulating.
  • Aftercare Instructions: You’ll receive personalized aftercare tips, including how to manage any post-procedure sensitivity and maintain optimal gum health moving forward.

Why Early Treatment Matters

Addressing gum disease early with scaling and root planing offers many long-term benefits:

  • Prevention of Tooth Loss: Early intervention reduces the risk of tooth loss, keeping your smile intact.
  • Improved Gum Health: Removing harmful bacteria decreases inflammation and encourages healing.
  • Enhanced Systemic Health: Healthy gums can lower the risk of systemic issues like heart disease and diabetes.
  • Fresher Breath: Reducing bacterial buildup leads to noticeably fresher breath and increased confidence.

Tips for Aftercare and Prevention

Maintaining good oral hygiene after your scaling and root planing treatment is essential for long-term success. Follow these tips to keep your gums healthy:

  • Brush Twice Daily: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste to brush for at least two minutes each morning and night.
  • Floss Regularly: Daily flossing helps remove plaque between teeth and along the gum line.
  • Rinse with Antimicrobial Mouthwash: Use an antimicrobial rinse to control bacteria and reduce the risk of infection.
  • Attend Regular Checkups: Schedule dental checkups every six months to monitor your gum health and catch potential issues early.
  • Avoid Smoking: Quitting tobacco products will significantly improve gum health and lower the risk of disease.
