About Melissa

Melissa Timberlake is the owner, and Medical Director of Allure Med Spa. She is a double board-certified Nurse Practitioner in Acute Care and Family. Melissa spent the first 10 years as an RN working in the intensive care unit, emergency department and bone marrow transplant. She spent the last 11 years as an Acute Care and Family Nurse Practitioner in bone marrow transplant, oncology, medical aesthetics and wellness.

Melissa has completed training in anti-aging therapies including, Neuromodulators, dermal fillers, laser services, platelet rich plasma/fibrin, weight management and bio-identical hormone replacement therapy. She continues to grow her knowledge by traveling the country for many educational opportunities such as cadaver anatomy and master laser courses.

About Jamie

As a dedicated Nurse Practitioner specializing in medical aesthetics, I am passionate about helping individuals enhance their natural beauty and boost their confidence. With over 16 years of experience in the healthcare field, I have honed my skills in a variety of aesthetic treatments, including injectables, neurotoxin and laser therapies.

I earned my Master’s degree in Nursing from Nova Southeastern University and have completed specialized training in medical aesthetics. My approach to care combines a deep understanding of the anatomy and physiology with the latest techniques and technologies in the aesthetic industry.

I believe in a holistic approach to beauty, taking the time to listen to my clients’ goals and concerns. My priority is to create personalized treatment plans that achieve natural-looking results while ensuring the utmost safety and comfort.

I look forward to partnering with you on your path to confidence and self-expression.

About Summer

Summer Bevans-Mowry is a board-certified Acute Care Nurse Practitioner with over 20 years of experience in the healthcare industry, including 15 years in the role of Nurse Practitioner. Summer has accumulated extensive expertise in various settings, such as hospital environments, outpatient oncology, geriatrics, and hospice care.

In 2022, she transitioned her passion for enhancing well-being into the field of medical aesthetics. Summer is dedicated to providing high-quality, client-centered aesthetic procedures that empower individuals to look and feel their best. Her commitment to professional and informative care ensures that each client receives the attention and expertise they deserve.
