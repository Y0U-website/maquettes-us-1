The practice

We are a family-owned and operated small business established in 1987 by founder, Paul Horn, CPO.

It is our passion to provide the best quality care for amputees and those requiring either long-term or short-term stability needs as well as other pain-relieving supportive devices.

With nearly 40 years of experience, we understand the challenges people face in finding a dependable and knowledgeable healthcare provider. Here at Able Orthopedics, we work to ensure our patients receive the care they deserve by working closely with the patient s other healthcare providers and by working with insurance providers to get the best quality outcomes.
