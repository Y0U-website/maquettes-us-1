Dr. Konstantinos Vazouras

Dr. Vazouras studied at the University of Athens in Greece for his first Doctor of Dental Surgery degree before going to England, UK, where he earned his Master of Science degree in Dental Biomaterials & Dental Technology from the University of Manchester, UK. He later completed a three-year specialty training in Prosthodontics at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, earning his certificate in Prosthodontics and in Dental Implant Surgery along with his Master of Science.
