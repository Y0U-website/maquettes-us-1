Silver Diamine Fluoride

Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) is a clear liquid dental compound containing silver, ammonia, fluoride, and water, renowned for its exceptional ability to combat tooth decay and alleviate dental discomfort. This innovative solution boasts a dual-action mechanism, utilizing silver ions to eradicate harmful cavity-causing bacteria and releasing fluoride ions that facilitate tooth enamel remineralization, making teeth more resistant to future decay.

SDF is an effective solution we use at Ceres Pediatric Dentistry for cavity arrest, tooth sensitivity relief, and preventive dental care. It offers a non-invasive treatment option, particularly for young children or individuals with special needs.

Multiple applications may sometimes be required to achieve the desired results. Patients are typically advised not to eat or drink briefly after SDF application to allow the solution to set appropriately. The dentist will also provide instructions on maintaining good oral hygiene to maximize the treatment’s effectiveness.

The Benefits of Silver Diamine Fluoride

  • SDF treatment in Ceres, CA, is non-invasive and typically painless, making it an excellent option for patients anxious about dental procedures.
  • The application process is relatively quick, making it a convenient choice for patients and dental professionals.
  • SDF can effectively halt the progression of cavities, reducing the need for more invasive treatments like fillings or crowns.
  • SDF can provide rapid relief from tooth sensitivity, allowing patients to enjoy hot or cold foods and beverages without discomfort.
  • SDF can be used preventively to reduce the risk of new cavities developing in high-risk patients.
