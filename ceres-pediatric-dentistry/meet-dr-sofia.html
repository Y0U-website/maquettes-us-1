Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Sofia obtained both her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree and Pediatric Dental certificate from Tufts School of Dental Medicine in Boston. She is a board-certified pediatric dentist.

While she enjoyed the snow on the East Coast for a few years, she missed sunny California and decided to move back to join her now husband in Northern California.

Dr. Sofia brings a gentle and compassionate approach to caring for children, making her a soothing presence in the dental office. She is enthusiastic about playing a role in cultivating positive oral care habits among the next generation and is committed to creating the most favorable experiences for children during their visits.

Beyond the dental office, Dr. Sofia balances her passion for pediatric oral health with her love for family. When she’s not treating patients or expanding her expertise in pediatric sleep, breathing, speech, and orofacial development, you’ll find her savoring precious moments with her husband and daughter.

Why choose a pediatric Dentist?

When it comes to your child’s smile, a pediatric dentist like our very own Dr. Sofia is your best ally! These dental specialists have unique qualities that set them apart. While all dentists receive education in dental school, pediatric dentists undergo additional years of postgraduate training in clinical and hospital settings. This specialized education equips them with expertise in handling a wide range of oral health conditions specific to children.

Dr. Sofia is an expert at making dental visits fun and stress-free for children of all ages. She’s skilled in communicating with kids and ensuring every visit is a positive experience. Whether it’s a baby’s first tooth or a teenager’s growing grin, Dr. Sofia understands the specific oral health needs at every stage of childhood

Dr. Sofia Is A Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Sofia is a board-certified dentist, and her decision to become certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry (ABPD) demonstrates her unwavering commitment to excellence in dentistry. This certification serves as a symbol of Dr. Sofia’s expertise and competence, and she has proven herself to be a highly skilled professional who goes above and beyond for her patients. Driven by passion, Dr. Sofia sets herself apart from others in her field through continuous growth and improvement while delivering patient-centered care with an extensive understanding of pediatric dentistry.
