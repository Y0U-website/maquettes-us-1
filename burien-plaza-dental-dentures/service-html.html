Provided Services

General Dental Services / Endodontic Treatment / Periodontic Care

Our Doctors and staff are committed to treating you with the compassion and respect they deserve. By providing a wide range of treatment and dental services, we hope we address both your short and long-term dental needs needs in which, it all comes down to great care.

Examination / Cleaning

For healthy oral hygiene, it is recommended to do checkups at least twice a year. A examination will usually have a dentist or hygienist clean your teeth and then evaluate if there were any oral health risks.

Extraction

Teeth are extracted because they are severely decayed; others may have advanced periodontal disease, or have broken in a way that cannot be repaired.

Crown

A crown is a tooth-shaped “cap” that is placed over the tooth. It restores the tooth’s shape and size, strength, and appearance.

Bridge

A dental bridge holds your teeth in place and helps preserve your teeth as a lost tooth which can cause the remaining teeth to loosen, which may also lead to additional tooth loss.

Restoration / Fillings

Restorations and Tooth-Colored Onlays create fillings that are not only beautiful, but also add strength to weakened teeth.

Dentures

A denture is an appliance that is inserted in the mouth, replaces natural teeth and provides support for the cheeks and lips.

Endodontics

When you have a diseased nerve, there is a chance that you can lose that tooth. This is when root canal comes in to save the day. A root canal is generally a simple procedure, removing the nerve inside the tooth canal, seal it with bio-compatible material called Gutta Percha. This procedure usually results in one appointment. People often view this process as painful, but the truth of the matter is that the feeling of getting root canal procedure is similar to cavity filling. Here are some reasons one may need a root canal procedure done:

  • Inflamed/infected tooth pulp
  • Severe sensitivity to hot and cold elements
  • Deep tooth decay
  • Chipped or broken tooth
  • Blow to the tooth
  • Swelling or tenderness near the infected tooth
  • Repeated dental procedures on a tooth
