The practice

At Banning Endodontics, we employ dental operating microscopes, digital radiographs (X-rays), 3D imaging with our advanced CBCT unit (check out the video on our site about CBCT imaging), and other specialized equipment to deliver top-notch endodontic care.

Since its inception, community involvement and giving back have been integral to Banning Endodontics.

A native of Moreno Valley, Dr. Lynn Diaz earned her Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology from the University of California at Riverside in 1991. After completing her dental degree at UCLA in 1995, she attended a one year general practice residency at Rancho Los Amigos Medical Center. In 1999, she completed the Graduate Endodontics Program at Loma Linda University School of Dentistry.
