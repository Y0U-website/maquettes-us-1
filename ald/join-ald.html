ALD Member Benefits

From Your Member Dashboard you will have open access:

  • Introduction to Lasers Online Course - Complimentary to Members ($75 non-members)
  • Foundations in Laser Dentistry Online Course (6 CE Credits, Members save $50!)
  • Continuing Education - The ALD is an ADA CERP Recognized Provider
  • Eligibility to apply for the Seidner Student Scholarship and Research Grant programs
  • Laser Help Network - Connect with experienced and passionate dental laser users from around the world, who can answer your clinical and procedural questions.
  • Do-It-Yourself Laser Safety Guide - Every dental office needs this!
  • Laser Buying Guide - Be sure you’re getting the best laser to suit your specific needs
  • Patient Education Brochures - Professionally prepared brochures that help educate patients on laser therapy
  • Proud Member Logo - Download logo and display on your website
  • Find-A-Dentist - Upload your practice details in the ALD Member Directory for patients and dental professionals
  • Advocacy - Regulatory Affairs, Position Papers , Statements on Laser Use
  • Photobiomodulation, Photomedicine, and Laser Surgery Journal - Discount to ALD Members
  • Certification Program Certificate - With the successful completion of certificate programs
  • Annual Conference Discounts - Special Member Pricing
  • Discounts with our Corporate Members
