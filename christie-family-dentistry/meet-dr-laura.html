About Dr. Laura Marszalek

Dr. Marszalek is a member of the American Dental Association, Indiana Dental Association and Eastern Indiana Dental Society. Dr. Laura enjoys treating a variety of patients with different needs. She has attended numerous continuing education courses and places mini-implants to help stabilize lower dentures. Dr. Laura is an active member of the community and is involved with numerous local groups such as 4-H and Cub Scouts. Dr. Laura and her husband, Joshua, have a son, Levi, and a daughter, Kathryn.
