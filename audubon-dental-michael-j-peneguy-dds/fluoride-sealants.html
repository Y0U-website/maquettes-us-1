Enamel Protection and Decay Prevention Mechanisms

Fluoride and sealants serve distinct but complementary roles in protecting teeth from decay at Audubon Dental in Thibodaux, LA.

Fluoride and Remineralization Support

Fluoride enhances the natural remineralization process by attracting calcium and phosphate ions to weakened areas of enamel. This helps restore mineral content in early stages of enamel breakdown.

Sealants as Physical Barriers

Sealants function as a physical shield over the chewing surfaces of teeth, preventing food particles and bacteria from entering deep fissures where brushing bristles may not reach effectively.

Combined Preventive Effect

When used together, fluoride strengthens the enamel structure while sealants reduce exposure to decay-causing agents in high-risk areas. This dual approach is part of preventive dental care planning.
