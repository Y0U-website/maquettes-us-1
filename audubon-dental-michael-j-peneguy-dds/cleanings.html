Cleanings

This phase of dental cleaning is essential for maintaining periodontal stability and preventing progression of gum-related conditions.

  • Subgingival Scaling Subgingival scaling involves cleaning beneath the gumline where bacteria can accumulate in periodontal pockets. This area requires careful instrumentation due to the sensitivity of surrounding gum tissue.
  • Instrumentation Techniques Both ultrasonic devices and hand scalers may be used depending on the location and severity of buildup. Ultrasonic instruments use vibration and water irrigation to loosen deposits, while hand scalers provide controlled manual removal.

Periodontal Assessment and Gum Health Evaluation

Each dental cleaning at Audubon Dental includes a periodontal evaluation to assess the condition of the gums and supporting structures of the teeth.

Gum Measurement Assessment

A periodontal probe is used to measure the depth of the spaces between the teeth and gums. These measurements help identify areas where gum attachment may be reduced.

Tissue Condition Review

The gum tissue is evaluated for signs of inflammation, bleeding, or recession. These observations help determine whether standard cleaning procedures are sufficient or if additional periodontal care is necessary.

Bone and Support Structure Consideration

While dental cleanings primarily address soft tissue and surface deposits, the evaluation process also considers the long-term stability of the bone structures supporting the teeth.

This assessment is a standard part of dental cleaning visits in Thibodaux, LA and helps maintain accurate clinical records over time.

Recommended Frequency and Maintenance Schedule

The frequency of dental cleanings at Audubon Dental in Thibodaux, LA varies depending on oral health status and the rate of plaque accumulation observed during previous visits.

  • Routine Cleaning Intervals For patients with stable oral conditions, cleanings are often scheduled at regular intervals to manage ongoing plaque formation and maintain oral hygiene consistency.
  • Periodontal Maintenance Considerations Patients with a history of gum disease may require more frequent cleanings to manage bacterial buildup and monitor periodontal stability.
  • Monitoring Changes Over Time Each cleaning appointment contributes to a longitudinal record of oral health, allowing for comparison of gum condition, plaque levels, and enamel integrity across multiple visits.

Post-Cleaning Care and Oral Hygiene Guidance

After a dental cleaning at Audubon Dental , patients in Thibodaux, LA may experience temporary sensitivity in the gums or teeth due to the removal of plaque and tartar deposits.

Immediate Post-Cleaning Sensations

It is common for teeth to feel smoother and for gums to be slightly sensitive following scaling and polishing procedures. These sensations typically subside within a short period.

At-Home Oral Hygiene Practices

Maintaining cleaned tooth surfaces involves routine brushing and flossing techniques that help reduce plaque reformation. These practices are especially important immediately following a professional cleaning.

Monitoring Gum Response

Following the procedure, the condition of the gums may continue to be observed during future visits to ensure stability and proper healing of any previously inflamed areas.
