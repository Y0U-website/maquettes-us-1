Implants

This surgical stage is a foundational step in implant treatment in Thibodaux, LA and determines the stability of future restoration.

  • Implant Insertion A titanium post is carefully placed into the prepared site within the bone. The position and depth are selected based on pre-treatment planning and anatomical mapping.
  • Surgical Closure Once the implant is placed, the surrounding tissue is closed over or around the implant site to allow the initial healing process to begin.

Osseointegration and Healing Process

After placement, the implant undergoes a biological process known as osseointegration, where the surrounding bone gradually fuses with the titanium surface.

Bone Integration Development

Over time, bone cells attach to the implant surface, creating a stable structural connection that anchors the implant within the jaw.

Healing Timeline Monitoring

The healing process is monitored to ensure proper integration. This phase can vary depending on bone density, implant location, and individual healing response.

Tissue Stability Evaluation

The surrounding gum tissue is also evaluated during the healing phase to ensure proper adaptation around the implant site.

Osseointegration is essential for the long-term stability of implants in Thibodaux, LA.

Abutment Placement and Restoration Design

Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone, the next stage involves attaching the abutment and designing the final restoration.

This restorative phase completes the structural assembly of implants in Thibodaux, LA.

  • Abutment Attachment An abutment is connected to the implant post, serving as the interface between the implant and the visible tooth restoration.
  • Impression or Digital Scanning A detailed impression or digital scan is taken to create a custom restoration that fits precisely within the patient’s bite and surrounding teeth.
  • Restoration Fabrication A crown, bridge, or denture component is fabricated to match the shape, size, and alignment of natural teeth.

Final Crown Placement and Functional Integration

After fabrication, the final restoration is attached to the implant system and adjusted for proper function.

Restoration Placement

The custom-made crown or prosthetic tooth is secured to the abutment, completing the visible portion of the implant.

Bite Alignment Adjustment

The restoration is evaluated to ensure it aligns properly with opposing teeth during biting and chewing movements.

Functional Testing

Chewing function and comfort are assessed to confirm that the implant integrates smoothly into normal oral activity.

This stage finalizes implant restoration in Thibodaux, LA and restores full tooth function.

Maintenance and Long-Term Implant Monitoring

Dental implants at Audubon Dental require ongoing evaluation to ensure continued stability and oral health integration.

Implants in Thibodaux, LA are maintained through structured clinical monitoring to support long-term function and stability within the oral environment.

  • Gum Tissue Evaluation The surrounding gum tissue is regularly examined to ensure it remains healthy and free from inflammation around the implant site.
  • Bone Stability Monitoring Radiographic imaging may be used periodically to assess bone levels and confirm that integration remains stable over time.
  • Restoration Condition Assessment The crown or prosthetic component is checked for wear, fit, and structural integrity during routine dental visits.
