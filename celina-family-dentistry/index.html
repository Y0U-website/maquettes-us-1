When a tooth becomes severely damaged or infected, removing and replacing it may be the best way to protect your overall oral health. Our oral surgery services are designed to eliminate these problems and restore your smile—often in as little as one day. With natural-looking tooth replacements, including dental implants, we not only improve your smile’s appearance but also restore its strength…

Healthy gums are essential for a healthy smile. If gum disease develops, it can weaken the support structure of your teeth and lead to more serious problems. Our periodontal treatments focus on stopping the infection and protecting your smile. Services such as deep cleanings (scaling and root planning) and gum grafting help restore gum health, reduce inflammation, and prevent future damage.
