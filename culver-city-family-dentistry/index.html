Dental implants are titanium posts that are inserted into your bone and act as the roots of your teeth. They help maintain your bone structure and are the best solution for missing teeth. Dental implants can help you get your smile and confidence back.

For most patients, the placement of dental implants involves two procedures. The first involves placing the implant into the bone. The second part begins months after the first, and once the implant has bonded to the bone. The entire procedure usually takes six to eight months but can vary depending on the patient’s needs.
