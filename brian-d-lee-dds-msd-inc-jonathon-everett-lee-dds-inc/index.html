The practice

The Pediatric Dentist is a specialist who is dedicated to the oral health of children from infancy through the teenage years. Every day we work with amazing families to provide personalized care in a fun and engaging environment!

The Orthodontist is a specialist in aligning the teeth and fixing the bite. In addition to taking care of your child’s teeth, we help create Happy Healthy Smiles!

We invite you to hop over to our blog so you can remain up to speed on the latest advancements related to dental and orthodontic treatments, as well as interesting news happening in our office.
