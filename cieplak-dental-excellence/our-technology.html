Innovative Dental Technologies For Optimal Results

CUTTING-EDGE TECHNOLOGIES FOR GENERAL DENTISTRY AND ADVANCED TREATMENTS

Turn to our advanced family dentistry practice for modern treatments and a high level of care. Dr. Patrick Cieplak and our team make use of several digital technologies and innovative systems to ensure high success rates with our procedures. We can perform an accurate diagnosis and deliver a high standard of care to our patients with some of the most advanced dental technologies in the industry.

Operating Microscope

A dental microscope allows us to magnify the oral cavity and other treatment areas with ease during a procedure. We use an operating microscope when performing some of our more complex services, such as dental implant placement, root canals, and tooth extractions, in some cases.

Operating Microscope

Digital X-Rays

The latest digital x-ray technology allows us to capture high-quality images of the teeth, bone, and jaw within minutes. We use the digital x-ray system before performing many of our general dentistry procedures and to update our patient records. We keep electronic copies of your x-rays in your file for review.

Intraoral Camera

We can view images of your mouth in real-time with the help of an intraoral camera. This camera allows us to move around the mouth and identify key areas of concern while you relax in the dental chair. It is very useful for performing a co-diagnosis, as you see the images on a computer screen in front of you.

Intraoral Camera

Cetacaine

We use cetacaine, a topical anesthetic for advanced procedures. This topical numbing gel works quickly to desensitize soft tissues. It also wears off very rapidly. If you find needles uncomfortable, you will appreciate the power and reliability of cetacaine!
