The practice

​Currently, Dr. Milio maintains her private practice in Bronxville. In recent years, she held a faculty appointment at the NYU Advanced Education Program of Prosthodontics. She hopes to return to teaching in the near future.

During her free time, Dr. Milio enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughers. Her pastimes include travelling, the arts and interior design. She is an active volunteer with the Junior League of Bronxville and works to Stamp out hunger in Lower Westchester.
