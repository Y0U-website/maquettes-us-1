Meet The Staff

At Cooper Family Dentistry we strive to give you the best possible treatment and experience to help you achieve your dental goals!

Dr. Cooper

Lanell Shetley

Lanell has been working in dentistry for 30 years and has been working with Dr. Cooper since 1996. She loves her patients and feels rewarded when she can make them feel at ease and comfortable during their dental visits. Lanell is married, has 3 children and four grandchildren. When Lanell is not at work, she enjoys spending time with her family, especially her grandchildren.

Rachel

Rachel graduated with her Bachelors of Science in Dental Hygiene from Wesleyan University and has worked in the field since 2017. Training patients on the importance of oral hygiene and its positive, long term preventative properties are very important to Rachel. Her gentle spirit contributes to a comfortable work environment and overall patient experience. Rachel enjoys spending weekends with her Husband, pets, Family and Friends.

Katie Woods

Attending Roane State Hygiene School, Katie graduated at the top of her class and brings that expertise and knowledge to the practice. Characterized as a true people person , Katie’s influence on her patients is one of care and training to achieve optimal oral hygiene. When she is not at the office, you will find Katie enjoying music, dancing and spending time with her Husband, three dogs, Family and Friends.
