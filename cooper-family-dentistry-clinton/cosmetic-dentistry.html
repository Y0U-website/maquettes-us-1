What is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry places a core focus on the looks of the teeth, rather than the function.

Examples of cosmetic dentistry include:

● Teeth Whitening : Professional teeth whitening can help successfully lift stubborn stains, yellowing, and discoloration to reveal a bright and white smile.

● Dental Veneers : Dental veneers are thin layers of porcelain or composite which are bonded directly to the surface of the teeth. The patient has the ability to customize the shape, size, and color of the teeth.

● Dental Crowns : Crowns are protective caps that are placed over the natural tooth structure. Patients have the ability to completely customize the look.

● Dental Implants : Dental implants are placed directly into the jaw bone to replace a missing tooth. A customized crown is placed over the top of the implant to complete the smile line.

● Invisalign : Invisalign is a discreet form of orthodontics that allows the patient to straighten their teeth without the use of traditional metal braces.

Benefits of Cosmetic Dentistry

For many people, feeling self-conscious about the look of the teeth or gums can have a large negative impact on confidence and self-esteem as a whole. In turn, this can make social or work events quite uncomfortable and unenjoyable.

Cosmetic dentistry can be incredibly beneficial for individuals who are looking to feel more confident about the look of their smile. Other benefits of cosmetic dentistry include improved oral health and improved chewing and speaking abilities.
