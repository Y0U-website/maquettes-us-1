Bone Grafting

Some patients may not have enough jawbone to support dental implants right away. In these cases, we may recommend a bone graft. This procedure restores bone density by adding grafting material, which encourages your body to rebuild natural bone. With bone grafting, we create a strong foundation so your implant can fuse securely, ensuring long-term success.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & Evaluation – We begin with a thorough consultation where Dr. Irby evaluates your oral health, medical history, and imaging results. During this step, we take the time to answer your questions and explain your options so you feel confident moving forward. We have carefully designed a comfortable, collaborative process for placing dental implants near Roanoke, VA.
  • Implant Placement – Once we’ve created a treatment plan, we surgically place the titanium implant into your jawbone. This step is performed with advanced precision and local anesthesia to ensure you remain comfortable. The implant acts as a secure artificial root for your future restoration.
  • Healing Period – After placement, your jawbone needs time to heal and fuse with the implant in a process called osseointegration. This usually takes several months. During this time, we monitor your healing and provide guidance on diet, hygiene, and care to ensure the best outcome.
  • Restoration Attachment – Once your implant is fully integrated, we place the final restoration — whether that’s a crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture. This step restores both the appearance and function of your teeth, giving you a natural-looking smile you can use with confidence.

Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Implants in Roanoke VA

What is the average cost of dental implants in Virginia?

The cost of dental implants in Virginia can vary widely depending on the number of teeth replaced and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed. On average, a single implant may cost between $3,000 and $4,500.

What is the average cost of a one-tooth implant?

A single dental implant with a crown typically costs between $3,000 and $4,500 in Virginia. The exact cost depends on factors such as bone density, type of crown used, and overall oral health.

How long does a dental implant treatment take?

From start to finish, dental implant treatment usually takes 3–6 months. This includes the healing period after implant placement, during which the implant fuses securely with your jawbone before the final restoration is attached.

How many teeth can I replace?

Dental implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or even a full arch. Whether you need one implant or a full set of implant-supported dentures, we provide customized solutions to fit your needs.

What is the average cost of a full denture implant?

The average cost of a full denture implant is typically $15,000–$30,000 per arch in the U.S., depending on the number of implants and materials used. The final price may vary if additional procedures like extractions or bone grafting are needed.

What factors affect the cost of dental implants?

Several factors influence implant cost, including the number of implants needed, the type of restoration, whether bone grafting is required, and the technology used in planning and placement.

Will insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by insurance provider. Some plans may cover parts of the procedure, such as extractions or crowns, but not the implant itself.
