Les soins

Preventive dental care is used to find problems early and lower the risk of future disease. During a routine visit, your recent health history may be reviewed. Then, the teeth, gums, bite, and surrounding tissues can be examined.

Oral surgery may be recommended when a tooth cannot be repaired or when the supporting bone or gum tissue must be treated.

A dental implant can be used to support a replacement tooth or larger dental prosthesis. The implant is placed within the jawbone. Later, a crown, bridge, or denture may be attached.

Cosmetic dentistry can improve the color, shape, spacing, alignment, and balance of a smile. Still, appearance should not be considered apart from oral health.
