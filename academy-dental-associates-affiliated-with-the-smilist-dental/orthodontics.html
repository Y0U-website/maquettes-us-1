What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized field within dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and jaws. It is dedicated to straightening teeth and ensuring proper alignment, ultimately contributing to better oral health and a more confident smile.

Thanks to advances in dental technology, orthodontic treatments have become more efficient, offering faster results than ever before. Read on to learn about its benefits and our professional services.

Orthodontic Services for Kids, Teens, and Adults

At The Smilist, our team of experienced orthodontists prioritizes the dental health of adults, teens, & kids alike, and works diligently to create customized treatment plans tailored to their needs.

Traditional Braces

For those seeking a time-tested solution, traditional braces remain an effective option for correcting teeth misalignment.

With regular adjustments, these braces gradually guide teeth into their proper positions, resulting in a straighter smile and improved oral function.

Invisalign® Clear Braces

For a more discreet alternative, consider Invisalign® clear braces. These virtually invisible aligners offer the same benefits as traditional braces without the noticeable wires.

Invisalign® allows you to straighten your teeth discreetly, making it an ideal choice for those seeking a more aesthetically pleasing orthodontic solution.

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

  • Improved Dental Health : Orthodontic treatment helps align teeth properly, reducing the risk of dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, and abnormal wear on tooth surfaces.
  • Enhanced Aesthetic Appearance : Straighter teeth not only contribute to a more attractive smile but also boost self-confidence and overall facial aesthetics.
  • Better Bite Functionality : Correcting misaligned bites through orthodontic treatment improves chewing efficiency, speech clarity, and overall oral function, leading to better overall health and quality of life.

FAQs

Insurance coverage for orthodontic treatment can vary depending on the specific insurance plan. Many dental insurance plans offer some coverage for orthodontic procedures, including traditional braces. However, the extent of coverage, including any limitations or conditions, can differ significantly between plans. It’s essential to review your insurance policy or contact your insurance provider directly to understand what orthodontic services are covered and what costs you may be responsible for.

Additionally, some insurance plans may have waiting periods or require pre-authorization for orthodontic treatment, so it’s crucial to be aware of these details when considering orthodontic care.
