All-On-4 ® Dental Implants

Replace all the teeth on the top or bottom of your mouth with just four dental implants.

What Is An All-On-4 ® Dental Implant?

Thanks to the advances in modern dental technology, you can now benefit from these full mouth dental implant restorations to restore your smile. Unlike a single dental implant that is used to replace a single missing tooth, an All-On-4® dental implant only requires four dental implants to replace the whole top or bottom arch of your mouth.

These full mouth dental implants are a great choice for individuals who have multiple missing teeth or are tired of living with the discomfort and hassles of wearing and caring for dentures.

What Is A Dental Implant?

Dental implants are a small titanium screw that is placed in your jawbone, replacing the the root-part of a missing tooth. In order to accomplish this, minor oral surgery is required to insert the dental implant.

Once the dental implant is securely attached, a crown is then placed on top to give you a highly realistic-looking and functional prosthetic tooth. These permanent teeth will stay in place like natural teeth, so you don’t have to worry about caring for them like you would dentures.

Why Replace Your Missing Teeth With All-On-4 ® Dental Implants?

You deserve to smile confidently, and we would like to help you achieve this goal. With only four dental implants on the top or bottom of your mouth, we’re able to replace multiple teeth at once rather than having multiple single dental implants to restore your full smile.

This is the greatest benefit of having a full arch dental implant placed at once. Because dental implants are made out of titanium, they have the unique ability to fuse to living bone and function as part of your mouth.

This causes the dental implant to become strong and long-lasting and serves as the foundation to your new smile. Another added benefit is that dental implants can prevent future bone loss in the jaw, which promotes better oral health.
