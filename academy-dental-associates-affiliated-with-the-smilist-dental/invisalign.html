What is Invisalign®?

Invisalign is an effective orthodontic treatment that uses a thin, clear aligner made from a flexible thermoplastic material named SmartTrack. This product is designed to make the treatment more subtle and less noticeable on the teeth. And though they are not completely invisible, these clear coverings fit tightly over your dental pieces and are much more discreet than the brackets and wires of traditional braces .

Benefits of Invisalign®

Beyond aesthetics, Invisalign offers numerous benefits, making it an appealing choice for individuals seeking to improve their dental health and overall well-being. Here are a few benefits of Invisalign:

  • Fewer Consultations Required. With this new method, much of the orthodontist’s work happens before you wear your aligners, so there’s no need to visit your dentist for adjustments. This way, appointments are kept to the bare minimum to check on progress. This translates, basically, as less time spent in the Invisalign dentist’s office.
  • Invisalign® looks better. The reality is that, at the end of the day, Invisalign just looks better. The biggest benefit of this amazing treatment is that it’s virtually invisible. This can be a very appreciated advantage for adults who don’t want people to know about their undergoing orthodontic treatment.
  • Invisalign® is comfortable to wear. If you’ve been wondering if Invisalign is more painful or uncomfortable than braces, the answer is a loud no. They are just like regular orthodontics, and as can be expected, there might be some discomfort on the very first days of their collocation, but this is just a normal annoying feeling that will fade as days pass.

FAQs

Invisalign provides many benefits to those who choose to wear them. Those range from their incredible seamlessness on the teeth to their simplicity when it comes to putting them on and taking them out. And more importantly, the before-and-after results have shown incredibly positive changes.

Braces, in contrast to Invisalign, are traditionally made from metal, and attached to the teeth using rubber bands that manipulate them with the goal of correcting orthodontic problems. They can’t achieve the seamless look on the teeth that Invisalign provides to the patient.

The duration of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the individual’s specific dental alignment issues. On average, treatment with Invisalign typically takes about 12 to 18 months, but it can be shorter or longer depending on factors such as the severity of misalignment, the patient’s adherence to wearing the aligners, and the treatment plan outlined by the orthodontist or dentist.

Some minor cases might be resolved in as little as six months, while more complex cases may take up to two years or more. It’s important for patients to follow their orthodontist’s instructions closely and wear the aligners as directed to ensure optimal results within the estimated timeframe.
