About Traditional Braces

Traditional braces are one of the most common types of braces that people use. They consist of stainless-steel brackets that are glued to your teeth, wires that connect the brackets, and elastic ligatures that keep the wires firmly attached.

This orthodontic treatment is applied by a dentist or orthodontist to correct a wide range of issues and help you obtain a beautiful, healthy smile.

What Are Traditional Braces?

Traditional braces are orthodontic devices used to straighten teeth and correct alignment issues such as crowding, gaps, and malocclusion. They work by applying consistent pressure to move teeth into their desired positions gradually.

Although they are visible when worn, traditional braces are highly effective and can be personalized with colored ligatures for a fun touch. Even though they are not exactly cheap, traditional braces are often a bit less expensive than other options.

Traditional Braces vs Damon Braces: What Are The Differences?

Traditional braces and Damon braces are both orthodontic treatments aimed at straightening teeth and correcting some other dental issues, but they differ in their design and mechanics. Traditional braces use metal brackets, wires, and ligatures to adjust teeth gradually.

In contrast, Damon braces employ a self-ligating system that eliminates the need for ligatures, reducing friction and discomfort. This system allows for smoother tooth movement and fewer adjustments, potentially shortening treatment time.

While traditional braces are more noticeable due to their metal components, Damon braces offer a more discreet option with clear brackets available. In the end, the choice is yours.
