Am I a good candidate for cosmetic dentistry?

If you&#x27;re looking to enhance the aesthetics of your smile and boost your self-confidence, and you have no underlying oral health issues, like gum disease, you&#x27;re likely an excellent candidate for cosmetic dentistry. If you do suffer from certain oral conditions, they may require treatment before completing any cosmetic procedures.

Are veneers permanent?

While veneers are durable, they are not entirely permanent and may require replacement over time to maintain their optimal appearance. However, because they may require the removal of some enamel in certain cases, they will need to be maintained and replaced to protect the tooth underneath.
