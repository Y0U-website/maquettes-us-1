The practice

Doctor Jim Snodgrass and Doctor Valerie Ryan are specialists in orthodontics, trained and equipped to provide the latest in state of the art care for patients of all ages. New, digital diagnostic records improve treatment planning and analysis. Less intrusive techniques and appliances are easier on patients as they speed the treatment process.

With three office locations in LaVale , McCoole , and Oakland , MD, we are able to work with patients throughout the surrounding areas, offering customized orthodontic solutions to help achieve their goals.

Orthodontic needs vary throughout every stage of life. We offer treatment for children, adolescents, and adults. Our team will work with you to determine the treatment method that will work best for your orthodontic needs. Our goal is to help you achieve and maintain a straight, healthy and functional smile that you will love to show off!
