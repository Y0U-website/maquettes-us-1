What to Expect During the Implant Process

Initial Consultation

  • We’ll evaluate your oral health and jawbone density using advanced imaging to determine if an implant is right for you.

Treatment Planning

  • A customized treatment plan is created to ensure optimal results, tailored to your unique needs.

Implant Placement

  • A titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a sturdy foundation for your new tooth.
  • The area is allowed to heal over a few months as the implant integrates with the bone (osseointegration).

Abutment and Crown Placement

  • Once healed, an abutment is attached to the implant, followed by a custom-made crown that matches the color and shape of your natural teeth.

Common Questions

Is the implant placement painful?

No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort during and after placement. How long does the implant process take? The timeline varies, but it typically takes 3-6 months to allow for proper healing and integration before the final crown is placed. Am I a candidate for an implant? Most patients with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density are candidates. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your suitability and discuss options if bone grafting is needed.
