What to Expect During the Full Mouth Restoration Process

Comprehensive Evaluation

  • We’ll conduct a detailed assessment, including advanced imaging, to evaluate your oral health and jawbone structure.

Customized Treatment Plan

  • A personalized plan is created to address your unique needs, combining treatments like implants, crowns, or bridges.

Implant Placement

  • Titanium posts are strategically placed to anchor restorations securely.
  • Healing and integration (osseointegration) take several months to ensure stability.

Temporary Restorations

  • While healing, temporary restorations may be provided for functionality and appearance.

Final Restorations

  • Custom-designed crowns, bridges, or dentures are placed to complete your full mouth restoration.

When Is a Full Mouth Restoration Right for You?

  • Have lost most or all of your teeth.
  • Experience significant tooth damage or decay.
  • Struggle with ill-fitting dentures or partials.
  • Want a long-lasting, stable solution to restore oral functionality and aesthetics.
