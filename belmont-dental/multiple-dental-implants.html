What to Expect During the Implant Process

Implant Placement

  • Titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone at strategic locations to support multiple teeth.
  • Healing and integration (osseointegration) typically take several months to ensure stability.

Abutment and Restoration Placement

  • Once the implants have fully integrated, abutments are attached, followed by custom crowns or a bridge to complete your smile.

When Are Multiple Implants the Right Choice?

  • You have several missing teeth in a row or scattered throughout your mouth.
  • You’re looking for a permanent solution instead of removable dentures or partials.
  • You want to maintain the health and alignment of your remaining natural teeth.

How are multiple implants different from dentures?

Multiple implants are a permanent solution that doesn’t require adhesives or removal for cleaning. They provide greater stability, functionality, and a more natural feel.
