State-of-the-Art Technology

We pride ourselves on staying current with the latest (proven) technology, including:

  • Modern Digital Scanning: Digital dentistry is convenient to the patient with proven accuracy. No more gagging or choking on gooey impressions. A digital scanner improves the fit and longevity of crowns, bridges and mouthguards. New Video
  • Waterlase Dental Laser: This technology enables excellent healing when used for gum tissue treatments including frenectomies (lip or tongue ties), gingivectomies (excessive gum tissue removal), and cold sore abrasion to expedite healing.
  • Zirconia Crowns: Developed in recent modern history, these crowns are known as white steel. They provide great strength without irritating the gum tissue and look very natural.
  • e.Max Crowns: When an esthetic, natural crown is critical an e.Max crown combines an excellent appearance with adequate strength.
  • Digital X-Rays: These modern x-rays minimize radiation and provide immediate feedback with no waiting.
