Dr. Daniel Osorio

Dr. Daniel Osorio joins the Carewell Dental team after over five years of private practice experience outside of Albany, NY. Dr. Dan is a graduate of Cornell University, New York University College of Dentistry (D.D.S. 2017). Following dental school, he went on to complete two years of post-graduate General Practice Residency training at Woodhull Medical Center where he served as Chief Resident and had a focus in restorative dental services , including endodontics, dental implants , and periodontal surgical procedures.

Dr. Osorio is a member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, an associate member of the American Academy of Periodontology, and Massachusetts Dental Society. When not in the office caring for patients, Dr. Osorio enjoys spending time cooking, baking, gardening, and spending time with his wife.
