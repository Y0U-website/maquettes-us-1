Educating Children on Oral Hygiene During Cleanings

Education is a key part of our dental cleanings. We take the time to teach children about the importance of regular brushing and flossing, and how to properly care for their teeth at home. Our team provides tips and techniques in a way that is both fun and easy for children to understand.
