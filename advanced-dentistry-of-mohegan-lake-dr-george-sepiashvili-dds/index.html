The practice

With our extensive qualifications, advanced technology, and patient-centered approach, we’re redefining what it means to be a dentist in Mohegan Lake, NY.

Missing one or more teeth, or about to lose some? We can replace them with durable, beautiful, long-lasting dental implants. We can even restore your complete smile!

From minor wear and discoloration to major flaws, we can rejuvenate your smile with a variety of proven treatment options—up to and including a full smile makeover.

Advanced Dentistry of Mohegan Lake is dedicated to providing an exceptional experience to patients of all ages and stages of dental health. Decades in practice and focused expertise in cosmetic dentistry, neuromuscular dentistry, dental implants, and TMJ treatment are just part of that.

We use many techniques and technologies not found in most dental offices because we believe that you deserve the very latest treatment available—and because one solution doesn’t work for everyone.

Our multilingual doctors work with you to provide care that meets your goals and satisfies your needs. You won’t find another comparable dentist in Mohegan Lake, NY.

A native of the country of Georgia, Dr. Sepiashvili earned degrees in dentistry from the University of St. Petersburg (Russia) and New York University. He followed this with a two-year residency focused on dental implants.

Like Dr. Sepiashvili, Dr. Mejia completed two dental degrees—one at Dental University in Santo Domingo in her home country of the Dominican Republic and the other at New York University. She also completed a two-year residency focused on dental implants after dental school.
