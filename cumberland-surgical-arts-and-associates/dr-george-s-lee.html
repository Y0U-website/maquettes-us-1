Meet George S. Lee, MD, DDS

Certifications and Memberships

Dr. Lee is board-certified in oral and maxillofacial surgery. He is an active member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Surgery , American Academy of Facial Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery , Tennessee Dental Association , and a former president of the Eighth District Dental Society.

Fellowships

Dr. Lee is also a Fellow of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons , a Fellow of the American College of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, a Fellow of the American College of Dentists, and a Fellow of the International College of Dentist. He served on the board of directors of the Good Samaritan Medical and Dental Ministry. Currently, he is on the Clarksville Academy Board of Trustees. Dr. Lee is the medical director for Cumberland Surgical Arts and Associates, PLLC .

Dr. Lee proudly resides with his wife and three children in Clarksville, where he is active in numerous civic and charity programs.
