The Coleman Experience

At Coleman Orthodontics, new patients quickly discover that orthodontic care here is about more than straightening teeth - it is about feeling welcomed, understood, and supported from day one. Led by board-certified orthodontists, our team combines genuine hospitality with personalized care to create a fun, rewarding, and life-changing experience for patients of all ages. From warm greetings and friendly faces to thoughtful communication at every step, we focus on building lasting relationships while helping you feel confident, comfortable, and excited about your smile journey.

On Your First Visit

Financing Coleman Orthodontics believes finances should never stand in the way of a healthy, confident smile. We offer flexible, interest-free in-house payment plans and work with many insurance providers, including flex-spending and HSA accounts, to make orthodontic care affordable and accessible. Before treatment begins, our team clearly reviews costs, insurance benefits, and payment options so there are no surprises. Our goal is to help every patient and family find a financial arrangement that fits comfortably within their budget while receiving exceptional orthodontic care.
