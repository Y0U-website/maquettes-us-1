Accessibility

If you require alternatives to a mouse or keyboard, speech recognition software such as Dragon Naturally Speaking may assist with navigating web pages and online content.

  • Keyboard navigation to move through pages without a mouse
  • Browser or device settings to increase text size
  • Screen magnification tools
  • Adjustments to background and text color contrast
  • Enhanced mouse pointer visibility (Windows only)

Multimedia Accessibility

Where available, video content on our website includes closed captioning , which provides synchronized text for spoken audio. Captions support users who are deaf or hard of hearing and also benefit users in sound-limited environments. Caption settings can typically be adjusted within the video player, such as YouTube’s caption controls.

Audio Controls

Most devices, including computers, tablets, and mobile phones, offer built-in volume controls. Media players may also include independent volume settings. Adjusting both device-level and media-level controls may help optimize audio accessibility.

Ongoing Efforts

Coleman Orthodontics continues to evaluate our website and digital content to identify and address accessibility barriers. We strive to improve compliance with WCAG 2.0 and WCAG 2.1 standards and welcome feedback to help us enhance accessibility for all users.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website or require assistance, please contact us directly. We are happy to help and will make reasonable efforts to provide information in an accessible format.
