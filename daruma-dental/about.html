My Story

After graduating from Roseman College of Dental Medicine, UT in 2020, I had the blessing of serving as an NHSC Scholar in Ellensburg, WA until 2023. After completion of my scholarship, I founded Daruma Dental as a place to explore clinical and artistic mastery in dentistry. My maternal grandfather is a third generation fusumaya - a type of woodworker that specializes in Japanese sliding doors. I always knew that whatever profession I chose, I wanted to embody the craftsman spirit of my grandfather by working with my hands and pursuing artistic perfection.

After learning of Dr. Marshall Hanson in 2021 and then being blessed in 2023 to receive him as a mentor, I have relentlessly pursued mastership of his technique and treatment philosophy. His work is defined by excellence in clinical knowledge, hand skill, and the preservation of natural dentition. Through his guidance, I am proud to offer esthetic dental answers to my client s biggest frustrations in a non-invasive, long lasting way.
