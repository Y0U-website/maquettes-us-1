Terry Griffin, DMD

Dr. Griffin has been serving Oakmont and the surrounding area since 1986.

Dr. Griffin is currently a member of the American Dental Association , The Pennsylvania Dental Association , and the Western Pennsylvania Dental Society . In the past, Dr. Griffin has served as the President for the Alli-Kiski Dental Society and on the dental alumni board for the University of Pittsburgh . He has been a member of the Seattle Study Club for the past 20 years.

Some of the activities Dr. Griffin enjoys are time with family, golfing, traveling, and woodworking. His woodworking can be attributed to his degree in Industrial Arts Education. Prior to becoming a dentist, he previously taught woodworking, printing and photography at Riverview High School.

He is a Past President of the Oakmont Carnegie Library and is currently involved in a nonprofit, The Blessing Board.

Dr. Griffin has always been committed to providing the highest standard of care with modern techniques. He enjoys all aspects of dentistry and remains committed to the Oakmont community.
