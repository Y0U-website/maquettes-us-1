Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are thin porcelain shells cemented to the front of a tooth that could use a little improvement. With this, teeth can be given a more attractive shape or length, gaps between teeth can be filled, and a discolored tooth can be covered.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Crowns are an effective way to strengthen damaged teeth. It is a clean white cap that fits over a prepared tooth. Meanwhile, bridges enable us to replace one or two missing teeth in a row. It is made up of two crowns that will fit over teeth on either side of the gap created by missing teeth. Between the crowns are one or two replacement teeth to fill in the gap.

Root Canal Treatment

Sometimes, a tooth suffers deep decay that reaches the soft, live inner tissues of a tooth. Other times, it may be fractured in an accident. These teeth may be saved with root canal treatment to clean out the infected or damaged soft tissue. We recommend a crown for a tooth that has experienced a root canal, so we can strengthen and protect it.

Dental Implants

The state of the art for tooth replacement is the dental implant. It is made of a titanium post that is placed in the bone of the jaw. The implant will then be allowed to heal and bind firmly with the bone in the jaw. Finally, a clean white crown will be attached to the implant. This new tooth will look, feel, and function just like the rest of your teeth and will need no special care.

Gum Disease Therapy

Treatment for gum disease is called periodontal care. Healthy gums are essential if you are to keep your teeth healthy. So, if we see any puffy, bleeding areas in your gums, we will advise you how you should change your at-home care to eliminate these signs of disease. If the problem is advanced, we will recommend deep cleaning below the gums to reverse it.
