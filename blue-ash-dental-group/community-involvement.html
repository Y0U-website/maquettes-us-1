Community Involvement

Here at Blue Ash Dental Group, we are dedicated to helping our neighbors in our community. In an effort to better the lives of our neighbors, here are charities we have donated to and will continue to donate to. We encourage you to get involved as well, as every little bit helps our neighbors live healthier and happier lives. Call us today if you have any questions about donating to these charities! 513-891-8555

Salvation Army

Blue Ash Dental Group had the pleasure of donating to the Salvation Army in efforts to help them distribute coats. If you’d like to help us get more coats to those in need, please click on the logo and donate! Thank you so much for helping!

Matthew 25: Ministries

The donation we have provided to Matthew 25: Ministries benefits them and their work with the poorest of the poor and disaster victims. As we all know, those in Texas have been hit especially hard with extreme weather conditions. Matthew 25: Ministries is doing great work to help those in need there and our donation will certainly help their mission.

STAF: Save the Animals Foundation

STAF is funded entirely by donations and is operated by volunteers. They are a no-kill shelter that dedicates themselves to helping dogs and cats find homes. Many of these animals have been abandoned, abused, or literally tossed away. Please consider donating to them and help these wonderful people care and find homes for those amazing animals!

Operation Give Back

Blue Ash Dental Group donated to Operation Give Back in March. Their mission is to strive for educational equity and provide community outreach to students and families in need, inspiring all to realize their full potential and GIVE BACK to society. Please click on their logo to find out how you can help them too!
