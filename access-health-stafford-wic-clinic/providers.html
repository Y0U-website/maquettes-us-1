About Christina Berg

Christina Berg, MSN, RN, FNP-C, brings her passion for learning, educating, and helping others to her practice at AccessHealth. Born in Illinois, Christina moved to Texas as a young child and grew up in San Antonio’s Alamo Heights, surrounded by her large family of eight siblings and a close-knit extended family. She earned both her Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) and Master of Science in Nursing (MSN) degrees from Prairie View A&M University.

As a Family Nurse Practitioner certified by the American Association of Nurse Practitioners (AANP), Christina focuses on family practice, primarily caring for adults. Her commitment to autonomy in practice and her joy in improving the lives of her patients are at the core of her career. She is also DEA-certified and fluent in English, Spanish, and French, allowing her to connect with diverse patients in a meaningful way.

Beyond her professional life, Christina enjoys exploring her creative and active side. She loves cooking and baking foods from different cultures, weight training daily, brewing kombucha, knitting, and spending time outdoors. Christina’s vibrant personality and dedication to her craft make her an invaluable asset to the AccessHealth team and the patients she serves.

Yaqueline Paredes, LCSW

Yaqueline Paredes, LCSW, is a bilingual Licensed Clinical Social Worker dedicated to serving the Houston community. Born and raised in Houston, Texas, with roots in Guatemala, Yaqueline brings a unique cultural perspective to her practice. She holds a Bachelor of Psychology and a Master of Social Work from the University of Houston, Main Campus.

With a decade of experience, Yaqueline has worked extensively with children, families, and adults, addressing a variety of life issues and mental health conditions. Growing up in an immigrant household with limited knowledge about mental health sparked her curiosity and drive to learn more. Her personal experiences and interactions with mental health professionals during her formative years inspired her to pursue a career in Social Work, with a commitment to helping others as she was helped.

Beyond her professional life, Yaqueline enjoys the outdoors, biking, walking her dog Flan, and participating in CrossFit. She loves challenging her physical abilities through hikes while also appreciating relaxing days by the pool and exploring new foods and restaurants.

About Charissa Davis

Charissa Davis, LPC-S, LCDC-Intern, is a dedicated mental health professional with deep roots in Houston, Texas. She earned her undergraduate degree from Dillard University in New Orleans, Louisiana, and completed her Master’s degree at the University of Houston. Charissa holds a Licensed Professional Counselor certification and a Counseling Supervisor certificate from the Texas Licensing Board of Professional Counselors.

Since 2004, Charissa has contributed significantly to the mental health and social service fields in various roles. She is an active member of the Texas Counseling Association, ACU JEDI Committee, and proudly pledged with the first African-American sorority in the U.S. Her professional interests include therapeutic interventions and education focusing on trauma, depression, empowerment, and women’s issues. Charissa enjoys a fulfilling personal life as a married mother, finding joy in the adventures of parenthood. She loves decorating for events, savoring good food, and listening to live music.
