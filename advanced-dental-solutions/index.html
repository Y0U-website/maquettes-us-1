An implant supported prosthesis is a dental bridge, a partial, or a denture that is connected and supported by multiple implants; it can be fixed which is non-removable, all-on-6 dental implants or it can be removable to easy access for hygiene and home-care.

The entire implant process is performed over the course of a few months. The treatment starts with CBCT 3D scan. A custom implant guide is made, which use to insert the implant with precision. Multiple implants can be placed at once.
