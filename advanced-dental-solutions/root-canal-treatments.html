Root Canal Treatments

We use advanced rotary endodontic technology , featuring an electric handpiece designed for greater efficiency, improved patient safety, and more predictable treatment outcomes.

Root canal therapy allows you to preserve your natural tooth while eliminating pain and infection.

  • Root Canal Treatments - Advanced Dental Solutions Skip to content ENDODONTICS (ROOT CANAL TREATMENT) ENDODONTICS (ROOT CANAL TREATMENT) Endodontics, more commonly known as root canal treatment, is a procedure used to remove infected or inflamed nerve tissue from inside the tooth’s root canals. During the procedure: The infected pulp (nerve tissue) is carefully removed
  • The canals are thoroughly cleaned and disinfected
  • The canals are then filled with a permanent material called Gutta-Percha to seal and protect the tooth

Root Canal Retreatment

In some cases, a tooth that has previously received root canal treatment may fail to heal properly, or a new infection may develop. When this occurs, root canal retreatment may be necessary.

Retreatment helps save the natural tooth and prevent the need for extraction whenever possible.

  • Reopen the tooth
  • Remove the previous filling material from the root canals
  • Carefully examine the tooth for additional canals or hidden infection
  • Thoroughly clean and disinfect the canals again
  • Refill and reseal the canals

Why Choose Root Canal Treatment?

With modern techniques and technology, root canal treatment is a safe, comfortable, and highly effective procedure.

If you are experiencing tooth pain or sensitivity, contact our office for an evaluation. Early treatment can prevent further complications and protect your smile.

  • Relieves tooth pain
  • Eliminates infection
  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Prevents the need for extraction
  • Restores normal chewing function
