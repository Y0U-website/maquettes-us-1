The practice

Born and raised in Ogden, Utah, Dr. David Wayment grew up on a dairy farm where he developed a strong work ethic and a deep love for the outdoors. He earned his undergraduate degree from Weber State University before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the Medical College of Virginia at Virginia Commonwealth University (MCU/VCU) in Richmond, Virginia.

After dental school, Dr. Wayment pursued advanced training through a General Practice Residency at the Veterans Hospital in Salt Lake City. Since then, he has been practicing dentistry for over 28 years, developing a wealth of experience and a passion for providing high quality care. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Wayment treasures spending time with his wife, three children and grandson. Summers are spent basking in the sunshine at Lake Powell and fishing rivers; winters are filled with skiing Utah’s breathtaking mountains. His love for the outdoors reflects the energy and enthusiasm he brings to both his personal life and professional practice.

With nearly three decades of experience, Dr. Wayment takes pride in the relationships he has built with his patients and the positive impact his care has had on their lives. He remains dedicated to continuous learning and providing exceptional dentistry that helps each patient feel confident in their smile.
