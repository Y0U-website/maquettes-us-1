SEDATION OPTIONS To Offer You Comfortable, Pain-free Dental Care

Do you often put off your dental work because of dental fear? You don&#x27;t have to do that anymore! For patients who experience stress or anxiety about visiting the dentist, sedation dentistry is ideal. We offer sedation options to help keep you calm and comfortable throughout your office visit. Let us make your dental care relaxing and comfortable!

SELECT YOUR COMFORT LEVEL PREFERENCE

Local Anesthesia

I just want a localized numbing in my treatment area. We achieve this with a local injection or topical treatment.

Mild Sedation

I have dental fear and prefer to be in a relaxed but awake state. We achieve this level of relaxation using with the use of nitrous oxide.

Medium Sedation

I wish to stay in a near-sleep state but still responsive. We achieve this level of sedation using an IV drip.

Full Sedation

I prefer to be completely unconscious. This level is attained using deep sedation and general anesthesia.
