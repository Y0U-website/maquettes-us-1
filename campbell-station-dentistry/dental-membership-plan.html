Dental Exclusions & Limitations:

This program is a discount plan, not dental insurance plan. It cannot be used:

  • * In conjunction with a dental insurance plan.
  • * For procedures performed outside the office.
  • * For procedures that are referred to a specialist.
  • * For cost of dental care under workers compensation.
  • * For any dental products such as Sonicare brushes, teeth whitening, Botox, etc.
