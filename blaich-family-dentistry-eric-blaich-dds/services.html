Highlighted Services

Of all the services we provide, here are some we are asked about the most.

Implants

Dr. Eric has received advanced training on planning and placement of dental implants. He has invested in the best technology available, and is passionate about sharing how the process works with his patients.

Through the use of Conebeam CT, digital scanning, and 3D printing he can show you how your implant can be placed precisely where it needs to go. If you think you may need implants, give us a call for a consultation.

Invisalign

While there are a lot of other options on the market, Dr. Eric only recommends Invisalign. Why? Invisalign has the best aligner material and has proprietary software what allows the doctor to direct the treatment.

Dr. Eric has extensive training in Invisalign, including completing the MOLIS program, ran by the top Invisalign doctor in the US.

We use digital scanning to screen our patients. If you are interested in using clear aligners to improve your smile, give us a call.

Oral Health

Even the best brushers will build tartar over time. Patients who receive routine cleanings are far less likely to develop cavities and gum disease. Whether it s fillings, crowns, or implants, even the best dental work cannot stand up to gum disease and bone loss.

Whether you re age 2 or 102, make consistent cleanings a part of your dental schedule. Give us a call to book a cleaning today!
