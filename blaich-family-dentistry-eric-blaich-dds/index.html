The practice

Our office was built as a dental office in 1985 by Dr. William Jackson. His vision for the building focused on developing relationships and patient privacy. He and his son, Dr. Dan Jackson, practiced together until his retirement in 1987. When Dr. Eric purchased the practice in 2019, the interior underwent a remodel while maintaining the original vision of the practice.

Our mission is to partner with our patients to reach your dental needs as a team. We want to provide you with the information you need to make treatment decisions that fit your lifestyle and goals.

Our driving focus is on building relationships with our patients. Going to the dentist shouldn t be something to dread. We use the latest technology to make your experience as positive as possible.
