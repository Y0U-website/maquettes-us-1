The Benefits of Braces

Affordable Treatment Braces are typically the most cost-effective way to straighten teeth and improve bite function. They&#x27;re also covered by some dental insurance plans, especially for children under 18.

Extremely Versatile Braces offer unmatched versatility compared to alternatives. With the right treatment plan, an experienced orthodontist can correct nearly any issue with your teeth and bite, from simple straightening to complex alignment problems.

Prevents Further Oral Health Problems Misaligned teeth create challenges for proper cleaning, increasing risks of gum disease and decay. They may also wear down prematurely and become more susceptible to damage. Additionally, misalignment can contribute to problems like teeth grinding and TMJ disorders. Braces effectively address and prevent these issues, supporting long-term oral health.

The Braces Treatment Process

Orthodontic Consultation & Braces Placement

Orthodontic Consultation & Braces Placement

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we&#x27;ll discuss all your treatment options. Once we decide braces are right for you, you&#x27;ll return for the placement appointment. If you schedule your consultation in the morning, and don&#x27;t have have any pending dental work, we may be able to place your braces on same day! This completely non-invasive procedure typically takes 60-90 minutes and sets the foundation for your new smile.

Adjustments & Checkups

Adjustments & Checkups

After your braces are attached, you&#x27;ll visit us every 6-8 weeks for adjustments. These brief 20-minute appointments allow us to check your progress and make necessary adjustments to ensure your teeth continue moving into proper alignment. Each adjustment brings you one step closer to your goal smile.

Braces Removal & Retainers

Braces Removal & Retainers

Most patients wear braces for approximately 24 months. Once your treatment is complete, we&#x27;ll remove your braces and create custom retainers. These retainers help maintain your newly aligned teeth by preventing them from shifting back to their original positions. You&#x27;ll typically wear them at night to preserve your beautiful results for years to come.

FAQs

How do braces work?

Braces work by applying gentle, consistent pressure to gradually move teeth into their desired positions. As this pressure continues, your jaw bone naturally adapts, allowing teeth and their roots to shift. The brackets attached to your teeth hold the archwire, which guides this movement over time. While each case is unique, treatment typically takes anywhere from six months to two years, depending on your specific needs.

What are the benefits of traditional metal braces?

Traditional metal braces remain one of the most popular and effective methods for correcting a wide range of orthodontic issues. They&#x27;re incredibly durable, withstanding daily activities like eating and brushing throughout your treatment. Unlike removable options, metal braces work continuously since they&#x27;re permanently attached until treatment is complete. This consistent pressure often results in more predictable outcomes, especially for complex alignment issues.

How do you care for your braces?

Caring for braces involves a few simple but important steps. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush to clean around brackets and wires, removing food and plaque. We may provide a special V-shaped brush to help reach difficult areas. Flossing is essential—floss threaders or water flossers can make this easier with braces. Regular dental checkups, alongside your orthodontic appointments, help ensure your teeth stay healthy throughout treatment. With proper care, your braces will efficiently do their job without complications.

Am I a good candidate for braces?

Braces can effectively address numerous orthodontic issues. If you have crowded, overlapped, gapped, or crooked teeth, braces can help restore proper alignment. They&#x27;re also excellent for correcting bite problems such as overbites, underbites, crossbites, or open bites. During your consultation, we&#x27;ll evaluate your specific situation and determine if braces are the right solution for your smile goals.

How much do braces cost?

The cost of braces varies for each patient based on several factors, including the complexity of your case, treatment duration, appointment frequency, and insurance coverage. To get an accurate estimate for your specific situation, we recommend scheduling a consultation. During this visit, we&#x27;ll assess your needs and discuss all available payment options, including insurance, financing plans, and possible discounts.
