FAQs

How do clear aligners work?

Clear aligners use a series of custom-made trays that gradually shift your teeth toward their ideal position. Each set is worn for about a week, gently guiding your teeth little by little. Most patients complete treatment in about 1-2 years, though this varies depending on your specific needs. The aligners work on multiple teeth simultaneously, which often results in faster treatment compared to traditional methods.

Are clear aligners safe?

Absolutely! Clear aligners are crafted from BPA-free, medical-grade plastic that&#x27;s both safe and comfortable for daily wear. The materials have been thoroughly tested and are designed to work with your body&#x27;s natural processes to gently move teeth without compromising your health.

Are clear aligners faster than traditional braces?

Clear aligner therapy often produces quicker results compared to traditional braces. Unlike braces, which adjust each tooth individually, clear aligners work on all your teeth simultaneously, which can speed up the process. However, the exact timeline depends on your specific dental needs. Following your dentist&#x27;s instructions about wearing your aligners correctly is crucial for achieving the best results in the shortest possible time.
