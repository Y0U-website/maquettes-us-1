Get to know your orthodontic specialist

Meet Dr. Kimberly Baptist Dr. Baptist is a certified orthodontic specialist, best known for her exceptional chairside manner, attention to detail, and dedication to helping patients achieve their individual treatment goals.

Originally from Mt. Prospect, IL, she graduated with honors in Biology from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where she spent summers as a camp leader for children with special needs. She earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Southern Illinois University, graduating among the top of her class and being inducted three times into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Baptist went on to complete her Master’s degree and Orthodontic Specialty Certification at Saint Louis University. While there, she also trained with the Cleft Lip and Palate Team at Cardinal Glennon Hospital.

When she’s not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Baptist enjoys spending time with her rescue dogs and cats, gardening, working on home renovation projects, and going out to the many local restaurants and breweries around Champaign.
