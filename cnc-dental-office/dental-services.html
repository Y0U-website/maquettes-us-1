Our Services

General Dentistry

At CNC Dental, we offer a wide array of family dentistry services in Pomona to suit the needs of you and your loved ones. You can trust our office to offer preventative dental services including: dental examinations and cleanings, digital x-rays, fluorides and sealants, mouthguards, and nightguards.

Invisalign

Clear aligners can be a great option for anyone who wants to improve their smile and oral health. If you are experiencing crowded or crooked teeth, gaps between teeth, having an overbite or underbite, exhibiting a crossbite (where the top and bottom teeth don’t align properly when closed), and or experiencing difficulty cleaning teeth properly, consider clear aligners.

Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are prosthetic structures that act as tooth roots to replace missing teeth. They are mostly made of Titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone beneath the gums where the tooth is missing.

The nature of your implant surgery would depend on the condition of your jawbone and the type of implant being placed. Patients can benefit from implants as they would provide a stable base for your teeth.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry involves any dental process which focuses on repairing or restoring damaged oral structures. This can include procedures such as fillings, bridges, and implants among many other services.

Whitening

Dental whitening can be used to correct tooth discoloration. Discolorations can be caused by staining, aging, or chemical damage to teeth. Using the latest in whitening technology, we can offer a safe method for creating a beautiful “brilliant” smile. In cases of extreme tooth discoloration, crowns or veneers may be the only choice, but because of the low cost of whitening treatments, teeth whitening is nearly always worth a solid try.

Prosthetic Dentistry

A happy, healthy smile can make all the difference in your confidence levels. Replacing your missing teeth with dentures can make that positive difference possible and improve your quality of life. Dentures are a simple, removable replacement for extracted or missing teeth. We know that cost-effective, natural-looking dentures are a great way to deal with tooth loss and help you get back to your smiling, confident self.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Correct issues such as discoloration, chips, gaps, and irregularities. A few popular cosmetic dental methods include teeth whitening, crowns, tooth-colored fillings, and more.

Gum Disease / Periodontics

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, is a bacterial infection of the gums and tissues that support your teeth. It is caused most often by the build-up of plaque and tartar when teeth are not routinely brushed and flossed.

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical and deep cleaning procedure that removes plaque below the gum line, where regular brushing and flossing can’t reach. Unlike a routine dental cleaning, this procedure is recommended when gum disease progresses from gingivitis to periodontitis, which is damage to the soft tissue around the teeth.

Emergency Dentistry

Emergency dental situations like swelling, bleeding gums or teeth injuries, infection, and extreme pain require immediate medical attention. Our team offers same-day emergency dental care whenever possible.
