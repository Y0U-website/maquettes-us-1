Our Commitment

At Altera Orthodontics Dr. Hashemi and his team are committed to delivering the highest quality orthodontic treatment and experience to every patient that becomes part of our family. The Altera team is dedicated to creating a welcoming and relaxed atmosphere, as well as providing the personalized, patient-centered orthodontic care you and your family deserve.

Our highly skilled and passionate team is committed to understanding your needs in order to create a custom treatment plan that will help you reach your smile goals and optimize your oral health. We will work with you throughout each step of the treatment process to ensure your orthodontic experience is a rewarding one. Our goal is to create healthy, beautiful, lifetime smiles for patients of all ages. This is your journey, but we are in it together!
