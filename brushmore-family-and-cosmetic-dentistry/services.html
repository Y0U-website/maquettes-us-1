Nitrous Oxide

Nitrous Oxide, commonly known as laughing gas,’ is a safe and effective sedative used to help patients relax during dental procedures. Administered through a small mask placed over the nose, it induces a state of calm and reduces anxiety without putting you to sleep. The effects of nitrous oxide are felt quickly, providing a sense of euphoria and relaxation, making dental treatments more comfortable.

Orthodontics

Clear aligners that predicably move the teeth to their ideal position.

Orthodontics focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and jaws, enhancing both function and appearance. We offer clear aligners, a discreet and effective way to gradually move teeth into their ideal positions. Suitable for teens and adults, clear aligners provide a comfortable and removable alternative to traditional braces. Achieve a straighter, healthier smile with our personalized orthodontic treatments designed to fit your lifestyle and dental needs.

Minimal Sedation

Nitrous Oxide IE. Relaxing gas. May combine one muscle relaxant for level 1 sedation.

Minimal sedation, often using Nitrous Oxide ( laughing gas’) combined with an oral sedative, helps patients remain calm and comfortable during dental procedures. This level of sedation allows you to stay awake and responsive while feeling relaxed and at ease. It’s ideal for patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing longer treatments. The sedative effects wear off quickly, enabling a swift return to normal activities post-appointment.

Endodontics

Sometimes a cavity is so deep that the nerve of the tooth is involved and must be removed and disinfected before it is restored.

Endodontics focuses on treating infections within the tooth. When a cavity or trauma affects the tooth’s nerve, a root canal procedure is often necessary. This involves removing the infected pulp, thoroughly cleaning and disinfecting the canal, and then sealing it to prevent further infection. Root canals save natural teeth, alleviate pain, and restore functionality, helping patients maintain their oral health and avoid tooth extractions.
