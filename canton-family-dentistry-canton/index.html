No matter your age, a straighter smile not only looks great, but can also help with oral health and hygiene. Clear aligners can transform your smile comfortably and conveniently with a treatment plan designed for your unique needs.

Come in for a consultation and we’ll develop a plan to get you a straighter, healthier smile.

We’ll assess your teeth and develop a custom treatment plan to meet your needs. We’ll send it off to the lab to develop your custom aligners. Once we receive your kit, you’ll come back, go over instructions, and get started. Regular check-ins will be scheduled during your treatment, and we’ll discuss how to maintain your new, straighter smile!

Very discreet. Clear aligners are made from transparent plastic, making them nearly invisible. They’re a great choice for adults and those in a professional setting.

Easy oral hygiene. Clear aligners are removable, so you can brush and floss as usual to maintain your oral health.

Eat what you want. Unlike traditional braces, aligners are removed for eating so you’re able to eat crunchy or sticky foods (within reason, of course).

Comfortable to wear. Your aligners are custom designed for your teeth. They’re made of smooth material so there’s less irritation on your cheeks and gums.
