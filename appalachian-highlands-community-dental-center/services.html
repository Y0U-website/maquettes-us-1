Our Treatments

Professional Teeth Cleanings Regular teeth cleanings maintain good oral health.

Tooth Sealants Sealants are applied to help prevent tooth decay.

Bondings Bondings to repair small chips or cracks.

Dental Crowns + Bridgework Crowns and bridgework to replace large amounts of lost toothe structure and/or missing teeth.

Porcelain Veneers Veneer treatment is for repairing larger chips and cracks, and reshaping teeth.

Dental Implants Dental implants for the longest-lasting tooth replacement available today.

Fillings Fillings to make your teeth strong and healthy again

Oral Cancer Screenings Screenings to detect disease at a curable stage.

Periodontal (GUM) Disease Therapy Periodontal, or gum disease, therapy helps to prevent tooth loss.

Removable Dentures After major tooth loss and removal, to help you smile again.

Root Canal Treatment Root canals to save an infected tooth.

Teeth Whitening Teeth whitening service to brighten a faded or discolored smile.

Tooth Extractions Extractions for when a tooth is hopelessly damaged or decayed.

Blood Pressure Screenings To help identify health issue markers.
