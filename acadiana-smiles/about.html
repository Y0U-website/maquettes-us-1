Meet the Doctors

Dr. Mark Broussard began practicing dentistry in 1997, after graduating from LSU School of Dentistry and completing a one-year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at LSU. He has completed many continuing education courses such as the LSU Cosmetic Continuum, The Dawson Academy, Invisalign certification, and Botox administration certification.

Dr. Broussard feels very fortunate to be able to use his education and experience to bring smiles to the people of his hometown. He enjoys providing a high level of quality dentistry to his family and friends, as well as to new patients who are meeting him for the first time. Providing excellent dentistry in a warm, caring environment is his top priority.

He is married to Diana Cramer Broussard, and they are the parents of four children. Outside of his dental practice, Dr. Broussard is a parishioner of St. Michael Catholic Church in Crowley. He also enjoys exercising, spending time with his family, attending concerts and sporting events, and visiting with friends.

Meet the Doctors .

Dr. Stuart Taylor, a native of Crowley, is proud to serve the community he calls home. Dr. Stuart graduated from Notre Dame High School in 1995. From there he moved to LSU in Baton Rouge, where he received his bachelor’s degree in Biology in 1999. He then attended LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans, where he earned his DDS in 2003. He worked in the New Orleans area for 2 years before returning to his hometown of Crowley.

Dr. Stuart is a strong advocate of continuing education. He attends classes regularly and is a member of the Seattle Study club, which is an international organization of dentists committed to the highest level of excellence. He is also a member of the Louisiana Dental Association as well as the American Dental Association.

Dr. Stuart believes that having a healthy dentition is one of the keys to leading a healthy lifestyle. He believes in conservative and preventive dentistry.

Dr. Stuart is married to Dr. Kristy Meaux Taylor of Iota. Together they are the proud parents of 4 children; Matthew, Lillie, Cate and Ella. In his free time, he enjoys travelling, dining, spending time with family and friends, anything related to LSU, and watching his children in their sporting events.
