Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Restoration

How long do dental implant restorations last?

The prosthetic portion — such as a crown or bridge — typically lasts 10–15 years or more, depending on the material, oral hygiene, and bite habits. Implant posts themselves can last a lifetime with good care.

Can you fix a cracked crown on an implant?

Yes. In many cases, we can replace the crown without affecting the implant post. If the damage is minor, a repair may be possible.

What if my implant feels loose?

A loose restoration may be the crown or the abutment connecting the crown to the implant. In rare cases, the implant itself may be compromised. We&#x27;ll perform a detailed evaluation to determine the cause and the best solution.

Do I need to return to the original implant provider?

Not at all. We regularly restore and maintain implants placed by other offices. Our team is experienced with multiple implant systems and components.

Are implant restorations covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Many plans cover part of the cost of the prosthetic. Our team will provide a detailed breakdown and help maximize your insurance benefits.
