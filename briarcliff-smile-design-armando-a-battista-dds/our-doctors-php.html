Dr. Myong Mike Lee

Dr. Lee completed his undergraduate education at Brigham Young University. He then attended Loma Linda University Dental School in Southern California and graduated in 2001.

Dr. Lee spent several years practicing general dentistry with a focus on endodontic treatment. During that time, he performed more than 4,000 nonsurgical root canal treatments and completed more than 300 cosmetic dental implant cases.

After eight years in general dentistry practice, he pursued an endodontics residency in July 2013 at New York University Lutheran Medical Center in Puerto Rico.

Dr. Lee provides specialized care when a patient’s restorative dental needs require treatment of the soft tissue, or pulp, of the tooth. He commonly provides root canal therapy and is also skilled in advanced endodontic microsurgery, including apicoectomy or root-end surgery.

In addition to his broad dental experience, Dr. Lee speaks Spanish, Korean and English. We are proud of the experience and passion he brings to his specialty.

Dr. Daniel Royzman

Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology, Assistant Clinical Professor in the Department of Periodontology at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, and a graduate of Harvard School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Royzman grew up in New York, earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University and completed his postgraduate training at Harvard University School of Dental Medicine. His master’s thesis was published in the Journal of Periodontology in June 2004, and he has been published in several periodontal journals.

In addition to actively practicing periodontics, Dr. Royzman serves as an Assistant Clinical Professor of Periodontics at Columbia University. He pursues advanced continuing education annually to provide current periodontal care and dental implant treatment options.

Catherine D’Avanzo, RDH

With more than 25 years of experience as a registered dental hygienist, Catherine D’Avanzo has built her career around one of the parts of dentistry she values most: caring for people. She enjoys getting to know her patients, listening to their concerns and helping each person feel comfortable and confident throughout their visit.

Catherine brings a warm, gentle and highly personal approach to dental hygiene. She understands that every patient has different needs, comfort levels and oral-health goals, and she takes the time to tailor her care accordingly. Her focus is not only on providing a positive hygiene experience, but also on helping patients understand the everyday habits that can support a healthy smile between visits.

For Catherine, lasting oral health begins with trust, consistency and compassionate care. Her goal is to make each appointment a positive experience while helping patients protect their smiles for many years to come.

Nina Melendez, RDH
