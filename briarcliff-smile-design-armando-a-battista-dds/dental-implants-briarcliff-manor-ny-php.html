Replacing the Tooth and Its Root

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone where a tooth is missing. Through osseointegration the implant fuses with the surrounding bone, creating a secure foundation for a custom-made crown.

Unlike dentures or traditional bridges, implants replace both the visible tooth and its root, helping preserve jawbone health while preventing neighboring teeth from shifting.

Ideal Candidates

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums
  • Adequate jawbone
  • Good overall health
  • Commitment to oral hygiene

Preparing for Long-Term Success

Patients with bone loss may benefit from bone grafting before implant placement. Proper home care—including brushing, flossing, regular cleanings and routine examinations—helps implants remain healthy and functional for decades.

Our office uses advanced imaging, precise treatment planning and high-quality restorative materials to deliver predictable, long-lasting results.

Daily Care

  • Brush twice daily
  • Floss around implants
  • Routine professional cleanings
  • Avoid tobacco
  • Wear a nightguard if needed
  • Keep regular checkups
