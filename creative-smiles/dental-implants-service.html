Dental Implants Rebuild Your Smile with Confidence Implant Restoration Dental implants are one of the most exciting areas of dentistry today. Losing a tooth to trauma or disease used to mean that replacement would never quite match the look and feel of the natural tooth. Not so with implants, which are a titanium screw that is embedded in the jawbone. The implant is placed by an oral surgeon or periodontist and is allowed to heal. Then our team steps in.

We custom shape, color, and craft a crown that rests on top of the implant. It will function just as your natural tooth did; you can brush, floss and care for it like a natural tooth. If you’d like to replace a tooth, or you know you’ll have one to replace in the near future, please see us for a periodontist recommendation and a beautiful final restoration.
