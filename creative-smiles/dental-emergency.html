Tips for Handling a Dental Emergency

While waiting for professional care, taking the right steps can reduce pain and prevent further damage:

These steps help stabilize the situation until our team can provide expert care.

  • Severe Toothache: Rinse your mouth, use a cold compress, and avoid chewing on the affected side.
  • Broken or Chipped Tooth: Save the tooth fragment if possible and rinse your mouth gently.
  • Knocked-Out Tooth: Handle by the crown, rinse gently, and store in milk or saline if possible.
  • Lost Filling or Crown: May use over-the-counter temporary filling material or cement until your appointment.
  • Soft Tissue Injury: Apply gentle pressure to stop bleeding and rinse with salt water.
