Dr. Chris and Dr. Brian Burseth share a passion for outreach dentistry and an interest in restoring smiles to change lives.

Dr. Brian volunteering in the Kuna Yala

Dr. Brian is a welcomed site on the small island of Achutupu, where the 620+ Kuna children lack access to dental care. Following preventative care for all of the children and triage examinations, the Mission Save a Smile team provided over 150 extractions, restored 107 tooth surfaces, provided root canal therapy, preventative care and sealants.

The doctors at Burseth Family Dentistry showed their support at Mission Save a Smile’s 5th annual Paddle for Smiles Fundraiser. Funds raised at the event will purchase dental equipment and supplies for the dental care of the over 600 Guna children on Achutupu, Panama.
