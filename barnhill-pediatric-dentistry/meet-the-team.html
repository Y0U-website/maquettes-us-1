Meet The Team

The entire staff at Barnhill Pediatric Dentistry is committed to maintaining the highest levels of accreditation every day. Our dentists work to pursue ongoing education and innovation to stay on top of the latest trends in both dental technologies and the entire medical field. Our practice is excited to treat infants, toddlers, and small children every day within the Tri-County community. We truly are a family, and pride ourselves in creating a relaxed, happy and safe dental environment!

Meet Linsey Cornelius

Dr. Linsey Cornelius completed her undergraduate degree in Biology at Eastern Kentucky University in 2010. She then went on to obtain her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Kentucky in 2014. It has always been her passion to work with children so she went on to specialize in pediatric dentistry. She completed her specialty degree at the University of Kentucky in 2016. Dr. Linsey strives to make every child’s dental experience one that will lead to a lifetime of great oral health. Dr. Linsey now resides in London, Kentucky with her husband and 3 children.
