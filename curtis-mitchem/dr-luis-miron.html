About Dr. Luis Miron

Dr. Luis Miron Mitchem Dental Get to Know Our Dentists: Dr. Luis Miron He has over 18 years of experience in general dentistry with an expertise in Dental Implants and Restorative Dentistry as well as being dedicated to staying at the forefront of the latest medical advancements.

Dr. Miron holds a Doctor of Dental Science degree from Francisco Marroquin University and a Master’s in Osseointegrated Oral Implants from Andres Bello University. Additionally, he has completed Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Miron is excited to join our team and looks forward to meeting and working with our wonderful patients. His commitment to excellence aligns perfectly with our practice’s values, and we are confident that he will be a great asset to our team.

He is fluent in both English and Spanish. His expertise and language skills will undoubtedly enhance our ability to serve our diverse community effectively.

Beyond his professional expertise, Dr. Miron is an adventure enthusiast who loves mountain biking, gravel cycling, and playing tennis. He’s also an avid reader. He treasures quality time with his wife and two young kids, especially enjoying thrilling soccer matches with his children. His adventurous spirit and dedication to family life perfectly complement our practice’s values.
