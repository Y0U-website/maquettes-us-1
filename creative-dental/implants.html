Common Questions About Dental Implants

What should I know before deciding on a dental implant?

We recommend understanding the full scope of the process — from surgical placement to healing and restoration. We walk patients through each stage so they know what to expect and can make informed choices about their care.

How do I know if I have enough bone for a dental implant?

We use digital imaging to assess bone density and structure. If additional support is needed, we can explore treatment options that help create the foundation needed for implant success.

What can I expect during the healing phase after implant surgery?

The healing phase typically involves mild soreness that improves within a few days. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and schedule check-ins to monitor healing as the implant integrates with the bone.

How will I take care of my dental implant after the procedure?

We guide our patients in maintaining daily hygiene habits like brushing and flossing, and recommend regular checkups to ensure the implant and surrounding tissue stay healthy over time.

What are the long-term benefits of choosing a dental implant?

Dental implants support bone health, restore natural chewing ability, and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting. Over time, this contributes to stronger, more balanced oral function.

How long should I expect my dental implant to last?

With consistent care and healthy habits, dental implants can remain in place for decades. We help our patients understand what’s needed to maintain their implants for the long term.

What types of dental restorations can be attached to an implant?

Depending on your needs, we can place a crown for a single tooth, a bridge for multiple missing teeth, or even a full denture supported by implants. We work with each patient to determine the most suitable option.
