The practice

We understand that every patient’s needs and goals are unique, and we take the time to listen and understand your individual concerns. We work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that addresses your specific needs and helps you achieve the smile of your dreams.

At Gatehouse Dental, we are committed to providing our patients with the highest quality modern dental care possible. We use only the best materials and equipment, and we adhere to strict sterilization and safety protocols to ensure the health and well-being of our patients.

We are conveniently located on Route 1 in Clinton, Connecticut, making it easy for you to access the care you need. Our state-of-the-art facility is designed to be comfortable and welcoming, and our friendly staff is dedicated to making your visit as pleasant as possible.

Achieve optimal oral health by maintaining regular cleanings with our skilled and dedicated hygienists. Our professional teeth cleanings include removal of plaque, tartar, and stain, as well as personalized oral hygiene instruction.
