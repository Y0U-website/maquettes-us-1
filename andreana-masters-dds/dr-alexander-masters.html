Dr. Alexander Masters, DDS

Dr. Alexander Masters has spent over four decades doing what he loves: building relationships with his patients and delivering care that truly changes lives. A 1980 graduate of the University of Detroit School of Dentistry, Dr. Masters has never stopped learning. He has completed advanced training at the Seattle Institute for Advanced Dental Study, the Dawson Center for Advanced Dentistry, and the Kois Center, and is honored as a Fellow of the Misch International Implant Institute.

His patients know him as someone who listens first, explains clearly, and takes the time to get things right. That approach, combined with decades of experience in comprehensive and implant dentistry, is what keeps families coming back generation after generation.

When he is not in the office, Dr. Masters can usually be found on the golf course, out on his bike, or planning his next trip with his family and grandchildren.
