Gum Health, Comfort, And How Often

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. If you have bleeding when brushing , persistent bad breath , tenderness , or buildup that returns quickly , we will explain what it can mean and what to do next.

If you have sensitivity or dental anxiety , tell us. We can adjust the pace, use comfort measures, and keep the appointment calm and clear . You are always in control.

Most patients do well with cleanings every 6 months , but some need visits more often, especially with a history of gum disease , inflammation , smoking , diabetes , or heavy buildup . We will recommend the interval that best protects your health.
