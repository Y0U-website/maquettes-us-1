Dental Implant FAQs What can dental implants replace? Dental implants can replace one missing tooth, several teeth, or help stabilize a denture. We commonly provide single implant crowns, implant bridges, and implant-retained dentures. What’s the difference between a single implant crown, an implant bridge, and an implant-retained denture? Single implant crown: replaces one tooth without involving neighboring teeth.

Implant bridge: replaces several teeth in a row using implants for support.

Implant-retained denture: uses implants to improve denture stability and comfort.

We’ll recommend the option that best fits your goals, health, and budget.
