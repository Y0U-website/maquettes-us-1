Preventive services are just what they sound like. We help prevent future dental problems caused by tooth wear, decay or gum disease (periodontitis). These services include cleanings, sealants, fluoride application, night guards and sports mouth guards.

These services include whitening, re-contouring, crowns, veneers, implants and bridges. Whether it is a limited improvement or overall cosmetic improvement, the patient’s desires help dictate the treatment plan.

This is what is most commonly referred to as fillings . These are restorations that are placed into teeth after decay is removed. We restore teeth with tooth colored filling material called composite.

This is an overall rebuilding process to regain lost tooth structure from wear or from actual loss of teeth. Some of these types of cases require a team approach by working with some of our local dental specialists, i.e. Periodontist, Orthodontist, Oral Surgeon or Endodontist.
