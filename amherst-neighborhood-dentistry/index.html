The practice

Our chairside milling unit carves your crown from a solid ceramic block in under 15 minutes.

Matched to your tooth shade for a result that blends seamlessly with your smile.

We take a precise 3D scan of your tooth — no messy impressions.

We design your custom crown on screen, dialing in the exact fit and shape.

Our milling unit carves the crown from solid ceramic while you relax.

We check the fit, bond it in place, and you’re done — same day.
