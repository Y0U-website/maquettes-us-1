Patient Information

All patients under the age of 18 need to be accompanied by their parent or legal guardian.

We consider it an honor to care for your endodontic needs and greatly appreciate the trust you place in us to do so. We will do everything we can to make your visit as comfortable as possible. Due to advances in the field of endodontics and related technology, a visit to the endodontist is not very different from having a tooth filled or being treated for other common dental procedures. Every patient is unique, and we are committed to providing you with high quality endodontic care based on your individual needs. Above all else, we want to make your visit to our office a pleasant experience.

DENTAL INSURANCE

While we are not considered "in network" by most dental insurance companies, we will work with insurers to maximize your benefits. Patients are responsible for all treatment fees at their first visit. Our office staff will submit claims to your insurance company for you. Your dental insurance company will reimburse you directly for covered services.

WHAT DOES YOUR PAYMENT COVER?

Most patients will present for an evaluation in our office following referral for endodontic treatment. Payment of the evaluation fee is expected at the conclusion of this visit.

Our treatment fees cover the cost of a comprehensive endodontic diagnostic exam, high quality state of the art materials and treatment time. The cost of treatment also includes a future checkup appointment if necessary, allowing our doctors to monitor ongoing healing and ensure successful treatment outcomes.

CANCELLATION POLICY

We understand that unanticipated events happen in everyone’s life. In our desire to be effective and fair to all patients, we have created a clear policy for cancellations, no-shows, and late arrivals.

CANCELLATIONS

NO SHOWS

Anyone who either forgets or consciously chooses to forgo their appointment for whatever reason will be considered a no-show" and will be charged a $50 fee to offset the unavoidable costs of that missed appointment. This amount must be paid prior to rescheduling your next appointment. Patients who repeatedly miss appointments will not be rescheduled.

LATE ARRIVALS

If you arrive late, your appointment may need to be shortened in order to accommodate others whose appointments follow yours. Depending upon how late you arrive, we will then determine if there is enough time remaining to start a treatment. If you are simply too late for us to see you, it will be treated as a no-show (see above). Out of respect and consideration for our staff, other patients, and especially yourself (you deserve care!) please plan accordingly and be on time. We suggest you arrive 15 minutes early to your appointment to ensure that you have time to check in.

ILLNESS

In this case, sharing is not caring! If you are sick or contagious, please reschedule your appointment for another time. For the wellness of all of our patients, we must decline treatment to anyone who arrives to an appointment sick.

WEATHER RELATED CANCELLATIONS

Winter in New England is unpredictable and if conditions are hazardous, we may reschedule your appointments. If you cannot make it for the same reason, please let us know in advance so we may plan our schedules accordingly.
