Our Mission

Dr. Anitha AbdulRahiman and her experienced assistants and office personnel have the goal of patient centered care. Her mission is to provide evidence based endodontic care in a comfortable, relaxed atmosphere, and we’ll be there for you every step of the way!

DMD: Harvard School Of Dental Medicine,2015

Specialty training in Endodontics: Harvard School of Dental Medicine 2018

Dr. Anitha AbdulRahiman was born in India, and did her initial dental training there following which she practiced general dentistry for two years. She then spent the following six years at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, where she attained her DMD degree and then went on to receive her Masters in Advanced Graduate Studies in Endodontics from Harvard in 2018. While at Harvard, she was recipient of the prestigious and coveted Krakow award for her research thesis.

Dr. AbdulRahiman is currently a member of the American Association of Endodontists (since 2015), American Dental Association( since 2013)and the New Hampshire Dental Society (since 2018).

In her free time, Dr. AbdulRahiman enjoys spending time with her husband and two cats, cooking/experimenting with fusion cuisine and traveling. Music has always been an important part of her life (she was lead singer in a band before she became a dentist!) and you may hear her sing during treatment.
