Meet Dr. Bobb

Dr. Heather Hodson Bobb is a highly experienced general dentist with a heart for people and a deep commitment to comprehensive, relationship-based care.

Her journey began in Oklahoma, where she graduated valedictorian from Tahlequah High School before earning her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Lyon College, graduating magna cum laude. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio Dental School, graduating cum laude with honors in clinical dentistry.

After dental school, Dr. Bobb practiced in southeast Ohio before being offered a partnership in a dental practice in Tahlequah, Oklahoma. During that season, she was also honored to serve patients within the Cherokee Nation.

In 2008, she opened her own dental practice in Dripping Springs, Texas, where she proudly cared for the community for more than 14 years. After relocating to the Atlanta area in late 2022, Dr. Bobb continued her passion for patient care and is now honored to lead Bobb Dental at Northgate.
