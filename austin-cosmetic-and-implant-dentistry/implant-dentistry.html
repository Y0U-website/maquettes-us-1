Implant dentistry

What Are the Types of Implant Restorations? Here’s a look at some of the state-of-the-art implant restoration options offered at Austin Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry:

  • Implants are the only restorations that mimic the function of natural tooth roots by stimulating bone in the area, which helps prevent bone loss.
  • Implants are remarkably natural-looking and comfortable. They provide the confidence to smile, eat, and speak without worrying about dentures slipping or shifting.
  • Implants are incredibly convenient. You won’t have to deal with removing dentures or messing with denture adhesives.
  • Implants don’t require the reshaping of any surrounding teeth, as traditional dental bridges do. That leaves more of your natural tooth structure intact, improving your long-term oral health.
  • Dental implants support your facial structure. That helps maintain your smile and natural, youthful appearance.
  • Implants are the next best thing to your natural teeth. They help improve your oral health and restore your natural, healthy, and functional smile.
  • Single Tooth Implants: A single implant provides a secure foundation to anchor a dental crown to restore a missing tooth. Once in place, the replacement tooth will look, feel, and function just like a natural tooth!
  • Full-Arch Dental Implants: Full-arch implants utilize a series of four to six dental implants to restore a complete arch of missing teeth. Once the implants heal and fuse to the bone, we will create your permanent or removable implant-supported dentures to restore your smile’s function and aesthetics.
  • All-On-4 Dental Implants: All-on-4 implants rely on just four dental implants strategically placed where needed to support a complete denture. They are ideal for patients with bone density concerns that would interfere with dental implant placement.
  • All-On-X Dental Implants: All-on-X dental implants, also known as “teeth in a day” implants, are permanent tooth restoration solutions without the wait of traditional dental implants. Even if some natural teeth need removal, you can still get the implants placed, and in most cases, have the implant restorations positioned on the same day!
  • Pterygoid and Zygomatic Implants: They offer an excellent tooth replacement solution when there is inadequate quantity or quality of bone to anchor regular dental implants, without the need for bone grafts. They are anchored to bones that never lose their hardness or density, the pterygoid bone in the case of pterygoid implants and the zygomatic bone in the case of zygomatic implants.
  • Zirconia Implants: Zirconia, or zirconium dioxide implants, are innovative non-metal alternatives to traditional titanium implants. The revolutionary material offers exceptional strength and durability. It is bioinert, meaning it will never corrode, trigger chemical reactions, or migrate to other sites in the body.
