How Does It Work?

To properly understand how it works, we first need to explain what dental implants are. Dental implants are small titanium screws that we place in your jawbone to substitute for a missing tooth root. Inserting the implants requires minor surgery, and once that is done, we will attach crowns so you will have a functional and realistic-looking prosthetic tooth. You will not need to get an implant for each tooth that you are missing. You will only need four strategically placed implants on the bottom of your mouth and another four at the top to get your smile back.

Since the implant is made out of titanium, it is capable of fusing with your jawbone and then functioning together with it. Thus, your dental implant will eventually be part of your jawbone and be a strong, durable base for your teeth. There is also another benefit to this, aside from keeping your implants permanently in place: It prevents jawbone loss. This will help you keep better oral health and also make your facial structure look more youthful. You will see how easily and quickly the All-on-X / Teeth in a Day can change your life.

The Recovery Timeline

Our team at Austin Cosmetic & Implant Dentistry meticulously plans and executes the All-on-X procedure. It begins with a comprehensive consultation to assess your oral health and understand your specific needs. This is followed by a tailor-made treatment plan, which might include addressing any existing dental issues.

The surgical placement of implants is a critical stage, carried out with utmost care and precision, often under anesthesia for your comfort. Temporary teeth are then fitted, enabling normal function while your jawbone integrates with the implants.

The final step is the fitting of permanent teeth, crafted to match your natural teeth in shape, size, and color. Throughout this journey, our priority is to ensure your comfort and to provide a solution that not only restores your smile but also maintains the integrity of your facial structure.
