Clear aligners straighten your smile comfortably and discreetly. Enjoy the flexibility to eat, brush, and floss without the hassle of brackets and wires.

How Do Clear Aligners Work?

Clear aligners use gentle, consistent pressure to gradually move your teeth into their ideal positions, giving you a healthier, more confident smile. Each set of custom-made aligners fits snugly over your teeth, guiding them into alignment over time. Dr. Christensen designs your treatment plan to ensure precise, effective, and lasting results.

What to Expect During Aligner Treatment

We start by creating a personalized treatment plan designed specifically for your smile. Using advanced digital scans, we capture a precise, detailed view of your dental structure—no messy putty impressions required.

Once your plan is ready, you’ll receive a series of custom-made aligners that gently move your teeth into alignment. You’ll switch to a new set of aligners every few weeks as your smile progresses. Regular check-ins help us monitor your progress and ensure your treatment is on track.

After completing your aligner journey, you’ll wear a custom retainer to keep your new smile looking great.
