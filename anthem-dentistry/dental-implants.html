What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post that replaces a missing tooth root and supports a custom restoration. Once the implant integrates with the jawbone, a crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore your smile.

Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, dental implants help preserve jawbone health while providing exceptional stability, comfort, and function.
