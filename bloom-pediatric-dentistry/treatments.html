Dental Check Ups

During your child’s dental checkup, we examine multiple areas of oral health. This includes things like hygiene, proper tooth alignment oral habits (thumb sucking, pacifiers, etc.), cavities, and diet.

Restorative Dentistry

Every child deserves a smile that is both functional and aesthetically pleasing. Our restorative procedures can accomplish both. We offer treatments that include crowns, bridges, fillings, cavity repair, and much more.

Sedation Dentistry

Dr. Kemper and his staff are proud to offer in-office sedation to help nervous and anxious kids. We utilize oral conscious sedation, as well as nitrous oxide, to help put kids at ease to keep them safe and calm.

Emergency Dentist

If your child has a broken or loose tooth or has lost a tooth completely due to an injury, call our office immediately. Dr. Kemper will assess the severity of the damage and determine the best course of action.

Oral Surgery

Dr. Kemper is experienced in Pediatric Oral Surgery, specializing in tooth extractions and tongue clips. If one of these treatments is necessary, he will thoroughly explain the procedure so you stay informed.

Orthodontics

Did you know that we offer braces? Yep! Once your child reaches adolescence, we can straighten their teeth with traditional braces. Why have someone else treat your kid’s smile when you already trust Dr. Kemper!

Crowns & Bridges

Sometimes a kid’s smile needs a little extra TLC. We offer dental crowns and bridges to help improve the look and function of your child’s smile. Call our office to find out if a crown or bridge is the best option for your child.

First Dental Visit

Your baby is growing up and getting them use to the dentist and oral health is critical to the success of their growing teeth. Learn what to expect during your first visit and how to get them on the right path to good oral habits.
