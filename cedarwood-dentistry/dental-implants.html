What Exactly Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a metal post, typically Titanium, that is surgically fused to the jaw under your gum to hold a replacement tooth, crown, or bridge. After the post is in position, it serves as a stable support for the artificial tooth, or teeth, that your dentist will secure to it.

Why Would I Need Dental Implants?

If you are missing a single adult tooth, you are likely a candidate. Unlike a dental bridge, an implant doesn’t need other teeth nearby to support the new teeth.
