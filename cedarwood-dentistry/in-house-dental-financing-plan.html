What’s Included in Our In House Dental Financing Plan

With our financing plan, we wanted to focus on providing Midlothian-area patients with a simple, cost-effective solution for receiving dental care when you don’t have insurance . We make it easy for patients to come in for dental visits twice a year and not have to think about the financial burden. Here’s what’s included with our in-house dental financing plan:

Not only does our financing plan benefit our patients, but it also helps us provide better care. For example, some patients who pay cash don’t want x-rays taken due to the additional charge. By including x-rays in our financing plan, we can find and treat early signs of tooth decay before it becomes a larger problem.

Some patients may need extra cleanings each year. For example, patients with periodontal disease or patients who smoke may need more frequent cleanings. While two free cleanings are included in the plan, additional cleanings are not. However, any extra cleanings are available at a 20 % discount.

  • Two dental cleanings with exams and x-rays (when needed)
  • One emergency exam with x-ray
  • 20 % discount on all services, including cosmetic dental work

Affordably Priced for Accessibility

Cedarwood’s in-house dental financing plan makes dental care more affordable and accessible to those without insurance. Patients who get the in-house dental plan can potentially save hundreds on the included services. Our In-House Dental Financing Plan vs. Insurance

Dental insurance is great for costly restorative work and emergency care. But our in-house discount plan is a more affordable option for cosmetic procedures, even for someone with insurance. Although some cosmetic work is sometimes covered by insurance like veneers deemed restorative treatments other cosmetic procedures aren’t covered by insurance. This means that supplementing your dental insurance with our in house dental financing plan can reduce the cost of cosmetic procedures by 20 %.
