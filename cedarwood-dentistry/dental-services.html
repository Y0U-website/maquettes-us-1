Dental Services at Cedarwood Dentistry

Welcome to Cedarwood Dentistry! We offer a full range of services at our dental office in Midlothian, designed to help patients of all ages achieve and maintain beautiful, healthy smiles. From preventive exams to emergency care and cosmetic enhancements, we provide expert dental support in a friendly, stress-free atmosphere.

Dental Exams & Cleaning

Our comprehensive dental exams & cleanings include diagnostic x-rays, a screening for oral cancer, a check for gum disease, an evaluation of current dental restorations, and a thorough dental cleaning to rid the teeth of stains or plaque.

Family Dentistry

We help patients of all ages to form good dental hygiene habits through personalized care and education.

Dental services for the whole family include routine cleanings, emergency and cosmetic procedures, and more, with a positive approach to care.

Cosmetic Dentistry

In addition to traditional services, we offer cosmetic dentistry services such as Invisalign , veneers, teeth whitening, and more.

These services are tailored to your specific age and needs so all Cedarwood Dentistry patients can experience the smile they’ve always wanted.

Extractions

For extractions, the area around the tooth will be numbed to ensure little to no pain and discomfort. Once you have received the anesthetic, the tooth will carefully be extracted from the socket.

Prior to the procedure, your options will be explained in-depth to ensure you’re informed and at ease.

Dentures

Restore your smile with dentures! We offer full, partial and hybrid dentures to fill in the gaps when necessary, making it easier to smile, eat, and live life on a day to day basis.

Our dentures are customized to your needs to ensure you’re comfortable and confident with your new smile.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers revive your smile to its natural brilliance. They effectively remedy tooth gaps, chips, and cracks, as well as correct misshapen teeth and crooked smiles.

We custom-make each set of veneers to match your natural teeth color and fit seamlessly into your mouth. Learn more about getting dental veneers in Midlothian on our blog .

Implant Restoration

We offer implant restoration in the event that you lose a single tooth.

We’ll help you understand the process, explain the timeline and steps for proper healing, and create a crown to blend in perfectly with the rest of your teeth.

Root Canals

Patients who receive root canal treatment will be fully recovered within one to two visits at Cedarwood Dentistry.

Emergency Dentistry

Whether from facial trauma, tooth pain, swelling, or tooth sensitivity, our trained and experienced staff can treat your unplanned issue.

If you have a dental emergency, please contact our emergency dentist at (804) 482-0757.

TMJ Treatment

If you’re experiencing symptoms of TMJ , which include pain in the jaw, popping or clicking when opening your mouth, and hearing problems, among many others, visit Cedarwood Family Dentistry. We will provide you with a consultation and treatment methods to mitigate and treat your pain in a way that is catered to your specific needs.

Preventative Dentistry

Oral health can impact more than just your smile. In fact, dental health is linked to other health issues such as cardiovascular disease. Through preventative dentistry, you can avoid common dental problems that can affect your health in other ways. Prevention focuses on maintaining dental hygiene and minimizing the risk for disease, decay, or damage. Stay on top of your health with dental services from Cedarwood Dentistry.
