About Our Team of Dental Experts

Dr. Darin Bach earned his dental degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and has been in dentistry since 2000. His postgraduate education includes training the Kois Center, and he’s met the rigorous requirements to achieve Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Darin has achieved Diplomate status with the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine and is one of only a few Diplomates in the State of South Dakota.

He is proud to be a member of the American Dental Association, the South Dakota Dental Association, and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine.

Dr. Adam Erickson, DDS

Dr. Adam Erickson grew up in Fargo, North Dakota, and attended Fargo South High School. Dr. Jenna VanSickle, DDS

Originally from New England, ND, Dr. Jenna Vansickle graduated from the University of Mary before obtaining her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Creighton University in 2024. She is overjoyed to have the opportunity to serve in Watertown, a community close to her heart, as it’s where her husband grew up and has deep roots.

She is a proud member of the South Dakota Dental Association and the American Dental Association.

Providing each patient with soothing, compassionate, and informed care is Dr. VanSickle’s passion. She enjoys getting to know new people in the community and does all she can to help her patients achieve their oral health goals through restorative, cosmetic, and preventative care.

Dr. Vansickle is deeply involved in the Watertown community as a parishioner at the Holy Name Catholic Church and is a proud supporter of local small businesses. She loves spending time with her husband, Bailey, their newborn son, Baylor, and their dog, Trigger. She also finds great pleasure in tackling various home improvement projects!
