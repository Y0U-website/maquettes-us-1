Featured restorative options

ZirFit Prime

Material Indications Milled from an Ivoclar ZirCAD puck featuring Gradient Technology (GT). GT pucks have two raw oxide-ceramic materials combined to produce exceptional properties. Most zirconia pucks on the market are layered from 5-7 shades stacked and pressed together forming the puck. GT is blended and not layered, which gives you the best aesthetics from the body transitioning to incisal. - Posterior Crowns - Multi-unit bridges - Implant crowns

ZirFit HT Prime

High-translucency full-contour zirconia crown finished with MiYO liquid ceramic.

Material Indications With higher translucency than ZirFit Prime, ZirFit Prime HT is characterized by a seamless progression of shade and translucency, which can only be achieved due to the use of Gradient Technology (GT). - Posterior Crowns - Anterior crowns - Multi-unit bridges - Implant crowns
