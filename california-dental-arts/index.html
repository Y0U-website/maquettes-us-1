We offer chair-side technical assistance for any patient appointment and same-day service for repairs and relines. With established protocols for higher success rates and Ivocap® injected dentures for better fits, we help you achieve the most consistent outcomes.

With an entire department dedicated to the treatment of hybrid denture cases, our dental technicians have the proficiency in treatment planning and chairside procedures to support dentists through even the most complicated prosthetic cases.

Carestream Dental brings the power of CBCT imaging directly into the hands of restorative dentists. The CS 8200 3D is a compact, high-resolution imaging system that bridges chairside diagnostics with lab precision—empowering smarter, faster, and more collaborative treatment planning.

The MiYO esthetic system is a new technique that replaces traditional stain and glaze with a thin liquid ceramic while retaining the same in-lab process. This means better esthetics, higher translucency, and more accurate shade matching. MiYO works with all zirconia and lithium disilicate materials, and is the new standard in our crown and bridge department.
