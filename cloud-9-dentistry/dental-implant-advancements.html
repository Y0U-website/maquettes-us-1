FAQs

The process usually includes implant placement, a healing period, and final restoration. First, we capture digital images and plan the placement. Then, during dental implant surgery, the implant is placed under local or sedation anesthesia. After healing, the final crown, bridge, or denture is attached. In certain cases, a temporary crown can be placed immediately.

Most patients are surprised at how comfortable the procedure feels. With local anesthesia, sedation options, and minimally invasive techniques, discomfort is typically mild. Some swelling or soreness may occur, similar to a tooth extraction, but recovery is generally quicker than many expect.

With healthy gums, good oral hygiene, and routine dental visits, implants can last decades. Advances in materials and design support longevity, but ongoing maintenance is essential.
