Meet the Dr. and his Staff at Broward Dental Group Broward Dental…

Dr. Ramer has been practicing in his specialty field since 1992 and has been an active member of the American College of Prosthodontists, The American Dental Association and many other local, state and national organizations.

Dr. Ramer is a former faculty member of The Broward County Dental Research Clinic where he served a Co-Chairman of their denture clinic for 10 years. He has also served as an esteemed lecturer and faculty member at Mount Sinai Medical Center and both the Dade County and the Palm Beach County Dental Research Clinics. Dr. Ramer also spent two years as a Clinical Prosthetic Instructor for the undergraduate clinic for the University of Maryland Dental School. Dr. Ramer has been published in the Journal of Prosthodontics and quoted in numerous other dental and business publications.

Raised in Nutley, New Jersey as the son of a dentist, Dr. Ramer now lives with his wife and 4 children in the city of Parkland, FL. Dr. Ramer has attended hundreds of hours of continuing education in all areas of Prosthodontics and Implant Dentistry and has been on the forefront of his field as both a practitioner and educator to his patients and the public. In his spare time, Dr. Ramer enjoys spending time with his family, playing tennis and exercising.

Caio- Lab Technician

Originally a natural born citizen of Brazil, Caio graduated from the State University of Dentistry and practiced as a general dentist in Brazil for 20 years before moving to South Florida. He moved to Boca Raton and decided to attend McFatter Technical School where he graduated as a Dental Lab Technician with a specialization in dentures and partials.

Caio have worked as Dr. Ramer’s private in-house denture technician for the past ten years and always look forward to helping our patients with the newest techniques and hands on approach like Dr. Ramer.

Caio is married with 3 beautiful children and loves to play tennis, surf and spend time with his family.

The remainder of Dr. Ramer’s office staff has been with the Broward Dental Group a combined 32 years. Office manager, Alicia and Dental Assistant Aline are like family to the patients in the practice. With warm, smiling faces and a desire to ensure the greatest quality of care, patients know they are in good hands from the moment they enter the office.
