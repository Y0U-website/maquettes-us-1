Dental Treatment For the Whole Family

Orthodontic treatment is not only for children and teenagers, but also for adults who want to improve their smile and oral health. It can correct problems such as crooked teeth, overbite, underbite, crossbite, and gaps between teeth. By aligning the teeth and jaws, orthodontic treatment can prevent issues such as tooth decay, gum disease, jaw pain, and headaches.

Orthodontic treatment can also boost your confidence and self-esteem by enhancing your appearance. Whether you need braces, clear aligners, or other appliances, our experienced orthodontists can provide you with personalized and effective care for the whole family.

Contact the staff at Brockman Orthodontics for an appointment!

Early Treatment (Phase I)

Early orthodontic exams are crucial for children’s dental and facial development. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child’s first orthodontic exam by age 7. Early detection of sleeping disorders, tongue and swallowing problems, and adverse jaw growth issues can lead to better treatment.

Brockman Orthodontics offers early orthodontic treatment for facial structures and crowding, focusing on young, active patients for optimal results, using appliances and treatments to increase jaw width and growth patterns. The initial exam evaluates concerns like facial development, crowding, crossbites, open bites, airway, and tongue position. Phase I treatment plans are individualized.
