The practice

Our team of friendly, experienced staff relies on the most modern technology along with caring, highly professional service. We strive to give you the best and most comprehensive dental care possible. Visit our office for dental services from general cleaning and routine examinations to improving your smile with teeth whitening, bonding, crowns and veneers.

Dr. Camp is a warmhearted, experienced dentist known for her exceptionally gentle touch and sincere commitment to patient comfort. She understands that visiting the dentist can be stressful, and she takes pride in creating a calm, supportive environment where patients feel heard, respected, and genuinely cared for.

Originally from Amarillo, Texas, Dr. Camp grew up inspired by her father, Dr. Bentley, who is also a dentist and remains her greatest role model both personally and professionally. She went on to graduate from Texas A&M University and has been proudly practicing dentistry for 16 years, providing high-quality, patient-centered care to families in the Bryan-College Station/Franklin communities.

Dr. Camp is married to her high school sweetheart, and together they are raising four children—three boys and one girl. Outside of the office, she cherishes time spent with her family and is actively involved in her church.
