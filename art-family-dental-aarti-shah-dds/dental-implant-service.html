What are Dental Implants?

Types of Dental Implants

At Art Family Dental, we understand that every patient is different. This is why we have available a variety of dental implant options to suit various conditions.

Single Tooth Implant

It’s a great procedure for those patients missing a single tooth; an implant takes the place of the missing tooth’s root, and a crown is placed over the top—restoring your smile and function.

Multiple Teeth Implants

For patients with multiple missing teeth. This consists of putting several posts to support a bridge, leaving the patient with a fixed and natural look.

Full Arch Implants

Ideal for patients whose missing all teeth in an arch. Implants that support a full denture can provide retention for better stability and comfort than complete dentures alone.

Implant-Supported Dentures

They are used to add increased stability to dentures. These dental implants will hold the dentures in position and prevent slipping, as well as enhancing comfort.

All-on-4® Implants

It is a revolutionized restoration in full arch for just four implants. This offers a quicker and less invasive full-mouth restoration alternative.

Benefits of Dental Implants

There are so many advantages to having dental implants that go far beyond the ability to replace missing teeth. Here are some of the best reasons:

Natural Look & Feel

Implants are custom-designed to be in harmony with your own teeth, thus providing for a perfect look and comfortable fit.

Preserves Jawbone

Implants stimulate the growth of bone. This stops the loss of bone that generally happens in conjunction with missing teeth and helps to retain the structure of your face.

No Diet Restrictions

Munch into all your favorite foods without worry. Implants give you the ability to bite and chew with the same force as natural teeth.

Enhanced Oral Health

Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering adjacent teeth. This preserves more of your natural teeth, benefiting your overall oral health..

Durability & Longevity

Dental implants can last a lifetime with appropriate care. They prove to be a very cost-effective, long-term solution.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Getting dental implants involves several steps, but the results are well worth the journey. Here’s what you can expect:

Initial Consultation

Our dentists will evaluate your oral health, take necessary x-rays, and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants in Artesia.

Bone Grafting (if needed)

(if necessary): If you have insufficient bone density, a bone graft may be performed to ensure there’s enough bone to support the implant.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This requires a healing period to allow the implant to integrate with the bone.

Abutment Placement

A small connector, called an abutment, is placed on top of the implant post to hold the custom crown.

Crown Placement

The custom-made crown, matching the color and shape of your natural teeth, is attached to the abutment, completing the restoration.

Aftercare

Following the procedure, proper care and regular dental check-ups are crucial to ensure the success and longevity of your implants.
