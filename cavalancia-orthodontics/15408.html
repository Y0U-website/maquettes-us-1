The following is a list of appliances and a brief description to give you a better understanding of what we have to offer.

Invisible Aligners

Invisible aligners are clear, removable trays that are custom-made to fit your teeth. They gently move your teeth into the proper position over time. Because they are clear, nobody will even know you re wearing them.

Invisible (Lingual) Appliances

Lingual appliances are placed on the tongue side of your teeth and are completely invisible. They work just like regular brace appliances, but because they are hidden, they are not easily detected by your peers.

Ceramic (Clear) Appliances

Ceramic or clear appliances are made of clear materials and are much less visible than metal braces. They are ideal for patients who are looking for a more aesthetic option.

Metal Appliances

Metal appliances are the most common type of appliances. They are made of high-grade stainless steel and are very strong and durable. Most kids will have this type of brace appliance at a young age to straighten their teeth or address any orthodontic concerns.

Retainers

Retainers are custom-made, removable appliances that are worn to hold your teeth in their new, straight position after braces. They are usually made of clear plastic and metal wires and fit snugly over your teeth.
