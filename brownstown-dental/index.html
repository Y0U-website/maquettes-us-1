Dr. Anthony Hoang "Dr. Anthony Hoang, who is often known to his patients as Dr. Tony, grew up in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. He received his bachelor s degree in Chemistry from Washington & Jefferson College in 2018 before earning his doctorate degree from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2024.

Following dental school, Dr. Tony began practicing as an associate at Merit Dental in Sinking Spring, Pennsylvania, where he spent two years developing his clinical skills in restorative and surgical dentistry. His primary clinical interests include implant dentistry, restorative treatment, and oral surgery.

Dr. Tony believes that excellent dentistry begins with patient education and comfort. He takes the time to ensure his patients understand their treatment plans, the reasoning behind recommended procedures, and all available options so they can feel confident in their care. His goal is to provide high-quality dentistry while creating a comfortable and positive experience for every patient.
