Dental Membership Savings Plan Details

Our savings plan is designed with your oral health in mind, covering exams, annual oral cancer screenings, and many other important dental services. The plan includes:

  • Necessary X-rays with exam
  • Annual oral cancer screening
  • $99 basic cleaning or periodontal maintenance
  • $175 SRP per quadrant

Membership FAQs

  • What types of payment are accepted? For your convenience, we accept cash, checks, major credit cards, Google Pay, CareCredit ® , and Sunbit .
  • When can I start using my membership? Once you sign up, your membership becomes active that day, so you can start saving immediately.
  • How do I schedule an appointment for preventative care? It’s quick and easy to schedule an appointment. Request an appointment online or give us a call, and we’ll find a time that works best for you. Schedule Online
  • How can I use my savings plan discount for treatment? Please let us know if you’re a plan member when scheduling an appointment. We’ll confirm your membership is current and ensure your discounts are applied automatically to your treatment.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Our savings plan is exclusive to Crossroads Dental and Implants and cannot be transferred to another dental provider.
