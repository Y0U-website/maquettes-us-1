Meet Dr. Roah Kelly

The Professional Side

Dr. Kelly grew up in Payson, Utah, and earned his bachelor’s degree in biology from Utah Valley University . He later completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Roseman University of Health Sciences in Utah.

After graduation, Dr. Kelly moved to Oklahoma and has been serving patients in the Tulsa area ever since. He has dreamed of becoming a dentist since childhood and is passionate about helping his community achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Kelly believes quality dental care can transform lives and is dedicated to providing compassionate, personalized treatment for every patient. He takes pride in helping each person feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their smile.

The Personal Side

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kelly enjoys spending time with his wife and two children. He also loves watching sports, hunting, and staying active through exercise.
