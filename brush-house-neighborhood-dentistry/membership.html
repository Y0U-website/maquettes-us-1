Family Pricing

The Brush House dental membership plan provides affordable coverage for families of various sizes. The pricing structure encourages multiple family members to sign up by offering progressively greater savings per person as additional members are added.

Membership Terms Discounts cannot be combined with insurance benefits or other promotional offers.

Membership plans are valid for 12 months from the date of enrollment and are non‑refundable .

Discounts apply only to services performed in your office and do not apply to procedures referred to specialists.

Discounts are applied to your practice’s standard fees at the time of service.
