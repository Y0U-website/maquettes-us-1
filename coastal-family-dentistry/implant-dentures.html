What to Expect During the Process

At Coastal Family Dentistry, we make the process of receiving implant dentures straightforward and comfortable.

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

Your dentist performs a thorough exam, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to determine if you are a candidate for implants. A treatment plan is customized to meet your needs.

Step 2: Implant Placement

A small number of implants, usually two to four, are surgically placed into the jawbone. These act as secure anchors for the dentures.

Step 3: Healing and Integration

The implants bond with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation. Temporary dentures may be used during this phase.

Step 4: Attachment of Dentures

Once the implants are fully integrated, special attachments are added that allow the dentures to snap securely into place. You can easily remove them for cleaning and maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are implant dentures permanent?

Implant dentures snap onto implants but can be removed for cleaning. This makes them secure yet convenient.

How many implants are needed?

Most patients require two to four implants per arch. The exact number depends on your individual needs and bone health.

Do implant dentures hurt?

The procedure is performed with anesthesia, and sedation is available if needed. Most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.

How do implant dentures compare to All-on-4® implants?

All-on-4® is a fixed solution that stays in place permanently, while snap-in dentures are removable. Both options provide stability, but the best choice depends on your lifestyle and goals.

Will insurance cover implant dentures?

Coverage varies by plan. Our office team will review your benefits and provide a clear estimate before treatment begins.
