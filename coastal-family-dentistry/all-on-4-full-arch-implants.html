What to Expect During the All-on-4® Process

At Coastal Family Dentistry, we provide a step-by-step approach to ensure your comfort and success.

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

We begin with a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, and 3D imaging. This helps us evaluate your bone structure and design a personalized treatment plan.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Four titanium implants are strategically placed into the jawbone. Two are positioned at the front for stability, while two are angled at the back to maximize support and minimize the need for bone grafting.

Step 3: Temporary Teeth

In many cases, a temporary prosthetic arch is attached to the implants the same day. This means you walk out with a complete smile right away.

Step 4: Healing and Integration

Over the next few months, the implants fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration. During this time, your temporary teeth allow you to eat, speak, and smile normally.

Step 5: Final Prosthetic

Once healing is complete, a custom-designed permanent arch is attached. This final restoration is crafted for comfort, function, and natural appearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the All-on-4® procedure take?

The implant placement and temporary restoration can often be completed in one day. Full healing and final prosthetics typically take a few months.

Are All-on-4® implants painful?

No. The procedure is performed with local anesthesia, and sedation is available for comfort. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward, similar to other dental surgeries.

Who is a good candidate?

Most patients missing several or all teeth are candidates, even those with reduced bone density. A consultation with digital imaging will confirm eligibility.

How long do All-on-4® implants last?

With proper care, implants can last decades or longer. Prosthetic teeth may need replacement over time due to normal wear.

Do All-on-4® implants look natural?

Yes. Prosthetic arches are custom-designed to match your facial structure and look like natural teeth.
