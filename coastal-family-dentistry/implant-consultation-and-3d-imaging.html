What to Expect During Your Implant Consultation

When you visit Coastal Family Dentistry for an implant consultation, you can expect a thorough and informative experience.

Step 1: Discussion of Goals

We begin by listening to your concerns and goals. Whether you are missing a single tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire arch, we discuss your options and answer your questions.

Step 2: Oral Examination

Your dentist will evaluate your teeth, gums, and bite to identify any issues that may affect treatment.

Step 3: 3D Imaging and Digital Records

Step 4: Treatment Planning

Using the 3D images, we design a customized plan. This includes determining implant placement, evaluating the need for bone grafting or sinus lifts, and planning the restoration.

Step 5: Reviewing Options

We explain your treatment choices clearly, including costs, timelines, and expected outcomes. This ensures you feel informed and confident before beginning.
