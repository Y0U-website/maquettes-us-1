What to Expect During the Procedure

The process of receiving an implant-supported bridge involves several steps, each carefully designed for your comfort and success.

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

Your dentist will perform a detailed examination, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to evaluate your bone health and determine the best placement for implants. A personalized treatment plan is then created.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone at specific points to anchor the bridge. These posts act as artificial tooth roots.

Step 3: Healing and Integration

Over several months, the implants fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong foundation for your new bridge.

Step 4: Abutments and Bridge Placement

Once healing is complete, abutments are attached to the implants, and your custom bridge is secured in place. The result is a restoration that looks and functions like your natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many teeth can an implant-supported bridge replace?

Bridges can replace two or more teeth in a row. The number of implants needed depends on the size and location of the gap.

Are implant-supported bridges painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and sedation is available for patients who need it. Most patients report little to no pain during recovery.

How long does the process take?

From implant placement to final bridge attachment, the process can take several months to allow for healing and osseointegration. Temporary restorations may be provided during this time.

Do implant-supported bridges look natural?

Yes. Bridges are custom-made to match the size, shape, and color of your surrounding teeth, making them virtually indistinguishable.

Will insurance cover this treatment?

Coverage varies depending on your plan. Our team will review your benefits and provide a clear estimate before beginning treatment.
