Our Team

Dr. Barry E. Yelk DMD, PC - Pottstown Dentist

Dr. Barry Yelk is a graduate of Temple University Dental School. He has been practicing since 2000. Dr. Yelk believes it is important to remain current in all facets of Dentistry and continues to take post graduate training. He feels patients must be informed and takes time to educate them on the treatment they require and explains the treatment options available.

He provides comprehensive care; Hygiene, Restorative, Prosthodontics, Implants, Endodontics, Oral Surgery, Cosmetics and minor Othrodontics . His approach to your treatment starts with patiently listening. After a complete and thorough examination, he reviews his findings and then discusses the diagnosis and the treatment options.

Dr. Yelk is a member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, and the Montgomery/Bucks Dental Association.

"I am proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options and patient comfort. It is my pleasure to address all your questions and concerns."

Our Staff

Dental Assistant

Jamie Gresko has been with Dr Yelk since 2001. She is a very caring individual and has a warm chair side manner. Jamie helps anxious patients feel at ease; Moreover, working together with Dr. Yelk, she has helped countless patients get through dental treatment they originally thought would not be possible. Office Manager

Kevin also has significant experience helping patients secure financing for their dental needs and helps simplify this process.
