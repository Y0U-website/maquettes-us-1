The practice

Atlas Dental Group is a leading dental corporation that embodies Washington’s innovative spirit. Our aim is simple: We sustain the values of private practice while maximizing the advantages of collaboration. Our deeply held values allow us to balance excellence in patient care with an abiding commitment to the growth and development of our people.

The tools, technology, and clinical support are unparalleled, all of which combine to help us strengthen the extraordinary culture that shines through our service .

We strive for a standard of service that exceeds patient expectations.

We honor ourselves and others by upholding our word and holding true to the standards we set for ourselves.

We treat others with respect and seek to alleviate their suffering.

We work collectively towards common goals and understand that we are more powerful as a group than as individuals. We want each other to be successful.

Our primary purpose is to provide the highest quality dental care possible to all our patients. We strive to provide quality, comfortable dentistry to all adults and children in a friendly, relaxed professional atmosphere. Each patient deserves our focused attention and best efforts to make his/her experience one that stands far above average. Our practice depends on the success of these efforts.
