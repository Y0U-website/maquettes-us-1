Meet the Doctors

Dr. Sidharth Bansal

Dr. Sidharth Bansal is dedicated to providing you and your family with quality dental care for a lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles! He places great emphasis on being an honest, gentle, caring dentist who treats his patients, not just their teeth.

Dr. Bansal received his initial degree in Europe over 26 years ago. After moving to the U.S., he earned an additional dental degree from New York University. Dr Bansal then practiced dentistry for five years in Philadelphia and North Carolina before opening his own dental practice in Pittsboro in 2008.

A technology enthusiast, Dr. Bansal believes on keeping up-to-date on the latest advancements in modem dentistry. Advanced training in cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry, as well as certification in invisalign and Lumineers, allows Dr. Bansal to work with patients on a wide variety of dental concerns, forming a partnership in achieving your optimum oral health.

Originally from India, Dr. Bansal is married to Dr. Shipra Patel, a pediatric endocrinologist at UNC. They are proud parents of a young daughter. His outside interests include soccer, field hockey, and martial arts. He also enjoys cooking and gardening. He recently has taken time off to be a part of the National Field Hockey team that traveled to South Africa for the world Championships!

Dr. Scott Hardin

Dr. Scott Hardin grew up in Gastonia, North Carolina as the middle of three boys. He studied engineering at North Carolina State University prior to attending dental school at UNC, graduating in 2004. He practiced in Richmond, Virginia until 2006, when he and his wife, Annelise, moved back to Chapel Hill. Dr. Hardin joined Pittsboro Dental as an associate in September 2021.

Dr. Hardin enjoys partnering with his patients to help them achieve the best oral health they can, whether that means educating them about home care and diet, or reconstructing their mouth to give them a new smile. He has studied at the Pankey institute and has been a member of multiple local study clubs.

In his spare time, Dr. Hardin loves spending time with his wife, Annelise Hardin, a pediatric dentist, and his two children, Will and Charlotte. Dr. Hardin enjoys doing anything outdoors, from hiking and mountain biking to surfing and rock climbing. When he’s not outside he enjoys cooking and reading.
