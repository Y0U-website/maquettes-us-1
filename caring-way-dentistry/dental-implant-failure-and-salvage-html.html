Why Do Dental Implants Fail?

As you can probably guess, there are multiple ways that a dental implant can fail. The most common culprit is peri-implantitis, which is when the tissues around the implant post become infected and deteriorate over time. This can ultimately result in the implant posts coming loose. Other possible causes of dental implant failure include certain medical conditions, physical trauma involving the mouth, and implant posts that never manage to fuse with the surrounding bone.

Symptoms of Failed Dental Implants

Dental implants can fail a short time after the initial placement surgery, or they might fail years later. Regardless of the timing, you may notice the following symptoms:

  • Your implant posts might feel loose. A successful dental implant should stay firmly in place at all times.
  • There’s unusual pain around the implant post. Note that this does not include the discomfort that normally occurs after dental implant surgery, which is completely normal and will go away over time.
  • There’s evidence of an infection around your dental implants. Some telltale signs of an infection include bleeding, swelling, and the presence of pus.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

Step one of the dental implant salvage process is to take a look at your dental implant posts as well as the rest of your mouth. Once the underlying problem has been identified, appropriate treatment measures can be taken. An infection can often be dealt with through conservative means such as deep cleaning. Meanwhile, severe damage to the tissues in your mouth may warrant removing the implant posts altogether. Don’t worry, though; once your oral health is back on track, it will often be possible to place new implant posts.
