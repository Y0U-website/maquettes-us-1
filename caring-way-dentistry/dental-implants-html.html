What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

While every dental implant treatment that we create here at Caring Way Dentistry of Port Charlotte is custom-tailored to meet our unique patients’ needs, there are four primary steps that will always happen. These include the consultation, dental implant placement, healing period, and attachment of the final replacement teeth. At your consultation, Dr. Villescas will outline what your journey will look like based on his examination. From here, our team can help you plan each aspect of your procedure so it’s seamless and as efficient as possible.

The Initial Consultation

To begin your journey, you’ll need to schedule your initial consultation. This visit is meant to help you determine whether dental implants are the right tooth replacement treatment for you. In order to do this, Dr. Villescas will examine your dental X-rays and conduct a thorough examination of your teeth and gums. Based on his findings, he will outline what your treatment journey will look like, including how long it will take and the cost estimate.

Some patients may require preparatory treatments before they can move on to getting dental implant surgery. In these cases, Dr. Villescas may walk you through what a sinus lift or tissue and bone grafting procedure will look like during this visit, too.

Dental Implant Surgery

Dr. Villescas has completed over 400 hours of surgical training at the Advanced Dental Implant Institute in San Juan, Puerto Rico that focused on the surgical planning and placement of dental implants, sinus augmentation, and tissue and bone grafting. As a result of his expanded skillset, patients are able to have their dental implants placed in-house. This streamlines the entire process and minimizes any need for referrals.

Dental implant placement is a minimally invasive surgical procedure that involves creating a small window in the gums. This will allow him to place the implants into the jawbone. Afterward, the gums will be closed and you will be ready to begin the healing process.

Healing & Osseointegration

Following your surgery, you’ll begin your healing process. Because the procedure isn’t very invasive, many patients feel ready to go back to work within a day or two. However, it will take between three and six months for the implant posts to completely fuse with your jawbone, which is a process called osseointegration. Your gums will completely heal within two to three weeks.

Attaching the Final Restorations

After osseointegration is successful, our team will call you back in to place small metal connector pieces atop your implant posts, called abutments. Your gums will undergo an additional healing period of about two weeks before we can attach your final, permanent restoration and complete your smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Every year, over 500,000 people choose dental implants. Thanks to their unique surgical placement in the jawbone, dental implants give you a smile that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural one. Unlike traditional bridges or dentures, implants provide a completely comprehensive tooth replacement, allowing for maximum comfort and durability. Read on to learn some of the most important benefits of dental implants in Port Charlotte.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Diet: Are you tired of avoiding your favorite foods due to missing teeth or slipping dentures? Dental implants allow you to eat virtually anything, giving you the chance to enjoy a more nutritious and delicious diet.
  • Maintenance: Traditional dentures require cleaning daily by removing your smile overnight. However, implants can be cared for just like your natural teeth. Simply brush twice a day, floss once a day, and visit our implant dentist in Port Charlotte regularly to care for your smile.
  • Appearance: Dental implants allow your restorations to mimic the way that your natural teeth emerge from the gums, giving you the most beautiful and natural-looking appearance.

Health Benefits

  • Bone health: Even losing one tooth can cause the jawbone to start shrinking because it is no longer being stimulated with the same number of teeth. Thankfully, dental implants act as natural tooth roots to stimulate the jawbone and prevent it from deteriorating. This also allows you to enjoy a fuller, more youthful facial appearance.
  • Dental health: Your teeth rely on each other to stay in place one missing tooth can cause misalignment throughout your smile. Implants fill in gaps to maintain your smile’s straight appearance. Plus, they lower your risk of developing common problems like decay and infection.
  • Mental health: Have you been hiding your smile due to missing teeth or old dentures? Dental implants allow you to smile, speak, and eat with total confidence in any social or professional setting. This alone will improve your self-esteem and overall outlook.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Predictability: Dental implants are unique because they require surgery, which can make patients anxious. However, you can enjoy total peace of mind knowing that they have a success rate of 95 % even after 10 years. This is dependability that you can’t get anywhere else.
  • Durability: Dental implants are made of biocompatible materials that fuse to the jawbone, becoming a permanent part of the body. This allows them to last for decades up to the rest of your life with proper care. Just be sure to practice good at-home dental care and visit our office routinely.
  • Cost: While dental implants tend to cost more than other tooth replacement options, they’re actually the most affordable option in the long run. Because they last for decades, dental implants help you avoid extra replacements along with the cost associated. Plus, they keep you healthier, lowering the price you’d pay for additional dental treatments in the future.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

To qualify for dental implants, you need to meet the following criteria:

We can discuss certain treatments such as bone grafts or periodontal therapy that can help you meet these requirements. Then we can figure out what type of implant treatment best matches your situation.

  • You are missing at least one tooth.

Missing One Tooth

When a single tooth is missing, an implant can be placed into the gums, and a crown can be attached to it to fill in the gap in your grin. An implant crown can be placed without making any changes to the surrounding teeth, making it a better option for those that want to preserve their natural dental structure.

Missing Multiple Teeth

When multiple teeth are missing, it’s often possible to replace them all at once with just a few implants. For example, with two dental implants and a bridge, we can fill in a gap left by three or more consecutive teeth. Implant bridges are strong and reliable without requiring alterations to natural teeth.

Missing All of Your Teeth

An entire arch of missing teeth can be attached to your dental implants. Implant dentures closely resemble traditional dentures, but they are designed to click securely onto the implant posts so that they don’t slip. Many implant dentures always stay in the mouth, but others are designed to be removable.

Dental Implants Post-Op Instructions

After your dental implant surgery, you’ll be given a number of post-op instructions. Following these instructions to the letter is the key to ensuring a successful recovery process, keeping your discomfort under control, and avoiding unnecessary complications. Below is a brief overview of the steps you should take after receiving dental implants. Remember that you can always call our office if you have questions or concerns.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Your main focus after your dental implant surgery should be taking care of your mouth. In particular, you will want to make sure that the blood clots that have formed at the implant sites stay where they are; if they’re disturbed in any way, the healing process could be delayed. Protect your blood clots by avoiding anything that could dislodge them. This includes spitting, drinking through a straw, smoking, or directly touching the implant site.

Common Side Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

Following dental implant placement, you may notice the following symptoms:

Remember, these symptoms should normally go away by themselves. If they persist or seem to grow more severe, a problem might have occurred, so call our office immediately.

  • Intermittent bleeding, which can be controlled by holding a piece of gauze over the area.
  • Swelling that lasts around 3 to 5 days and can be reduced with the use of a cold compress.
  • Discomfort that can be managed with a prescribed or over-the-counter pain reliever.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

When your mouth is recovering from dental implant placement or any other type of oral surgery, it is generally advisable to maintain a diet of soft or liquid foods. Make sure your meals include mashed potatoes, scrambled eggs, pasta, soups that aren’t too hot, and other foods that require little or no chewing.

After a few days, you should be able to eat solid foods again. That said, you will still need to avoid chewing directly on your implant posts.

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

When you brush your teeth in the days following your dental implant surgery, you should be very gentle when cleaning around the implant posts. Also, to protect your mouth from harmful bacteria that could cause an infection, you should rinse with salt water after every meal. Do not spit the salt water out when you’re done with it; let it pour out instead. If you choose to use mouthwash, make sure that it does not contain any alcohol, which could irritate the tissues around the implant posts while they’re trying to heal.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

After your mouth has fully healed, you’ll eventually be ready to have a crown, bridge, or denture attached to your implant posts. This can sometimes cause mild sensitivity in your gums, so take an over-the-counter pain reliever if you need to. Aside from that, there shouldn’t be any noticeable side effects. If any bleeding or swelling occurs at this point, reach out to our team right away.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term investment in your smile, and taking care of them is essential for lasting success. At Caring Way Dentistry of Port Charlotte , Dr. Villescas provides expert guidance on keeping your implants healthy and strong.

With a little effort and the right daily habits, your dental implants can look, feel, and function like natural teeth for decades. Read more below to find the tips you need to get the most out of your implant care routine.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Even though dental implants can’t get cavities, the surrounding gum tissue is still vulnerable to infection. Brushing twice a day and flossing daily are non-negotiables. Be sure to use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to avoid scratching the surface of your implants.

To get the plaque that brushing can’t reach between your teeth, a water flosser or interdental brush can do wonders. Dr. Villescas recommends establishing a routine and sticking to it to ensure that your teeth and implants are well-maintained and healthy.

Eat a Healthy Diet

A balanced diet supports implant health from the inside out. Choose foods that strengthen bones and fight inflammation:

Healthy nutrition helps your implants stay strong and stable, not to mention the benefits your body will see!

  • Leafy greens (like spinach and kale) offer calcium and vitamin K.
  • Fatty fish (such as salmon) is rich in omega-3s for gum health.
  • Eggs and dairy provide vitamin D and protein to support healing.
  • Finely processed nut butters and seeds are packed with magnesium and healthy fats.

Break Bad Habits

Certain habits can shorten the life of your dental implants. Some should be limited while others should be slowly phased out entirely to ensure the health of your implants. For example:

At our Port Charlotte dental office, we encourage mindful choices to protect your smile. Ditching these habits for good protects your smile investment and ensures you can smile brightly for years to come.

  • Severely limit or quit smoking or vaping, which slows healing and increases infection risk.
  • Avoid chewing ice or hard objects, which can crack implant crowns.
  • Never use your teeth as tools for tasks like opening packages or biting your nails.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Physical trauma is one of the top reasons dental implants fail. If you grind your teeth at night or play contact sports, protection is a must. We offer custom nightguards to help you keep your smile safe as you sleep.

If you’re concerned about shielding your teeth from physical contact, even a basic boil-and-bite guard from a sporting goods store is much better than nothing. Wearing one consistently can prevent cracks, chips, and pressure-related damage to your implants and natural teeth alike.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Routine dental visits are a cornerstone of long-term implant success. At our Port Charlotte dental office, our team will check your implants during every cleaning to ensure they re secure and your gums are healthy.

These visits allow us to detect potential issues like bone loss or inflammation as soon as possible, allowing us to take action. Aim for a checkup every six months for the best results.

Although not always necessary, you may be required to undergo preliminary treatments before receiving your dental implants. If you need one or more of these services, you can expect the price to be incorporated into the total estimate.

Whether you need preliminary treatment or not, another aspect of your treatment cost is the actual surgical procedure. Fortunately, our team provides start-to-finish dental implant placement in-house, so you won’t need to seek an outside specialist and pay a separate fee.

The Parts of Your Dental Implant

You may not realize it, but the parts of your dental implant are also factored into your cost estimate.

  • How many dental implants do you require Do you need one or six? Replacing a single tooth will cost much less than receiving a full denture to replace an entire arch of teeth.
  • The materials used The most common materials used to construct dental implants are titanium and zirconia. Depending on your needs, our dental team will decide which one is right for you.
  • The type of restoration you need Crowns, bridges, and dentures are all possible restorations when receiving dental implants. The kind you will receive is ultimately dependent on how many teeth you need to have replaced. Each restoration is custom-made to match a patient’s existing smile.
  • The brand There are brands that are less expensive; however, they often don’t hold up as well over time. When it comes to quality dental implants, cheaper is not always better. We’ll explain during your initial consultation which brand we use and why.

How Dental Implants Can Save You Money

Dental implants in Port Charlotte are far superior to other tooth replacement solutions. When comparing their benefits and cost-savings with other treatment options (i.e., dental bridges, dentures), you’ll find that you can save in tremendous ways:

  • You won’t require specialized supplies or products to keep your new teeth clean. Although you’ll need to purchase non-abrasive toothpaste, you do not need to worry about denture adhesives or soaking cleaners because your teeth are permanently in place.
  • Unlike traditional dentures and dental bridges that must be replaced every few years, dental implants can last 30 years or more with regular care and maintenance.
  • You’ll avoid many common systemic issues that are often linked to tooth loss. Diabetes, osteoporosis, and even heart disease require much time and money to treat. By replacing your teeth with dental implants, you can reduce your risk and keep more money in your pocket.
