Dr. Kim is a board-certified prosthodontist specializing in implant dentistry, full mouth reconstruction, and interdisciplinary care.

His extensive educational background in mechanical engineering prior to becoming a dental specialist has helped him to better design and create ideal occlusions utilizing the latest digital CAD/CAM technologies.

Dr. Kim’s treatment philosophy is to restore a healthy and natural smile with a comprehensive yet minimal approach that is patient-centered. He always takes the time to partner with you, review all of your options, and create a plan that meets your needs and expectations.
