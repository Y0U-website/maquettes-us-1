What is General Dentistry?

General dentistry focuses on maintaining the health of your teeth, gums, and entire mouth through preventive care, early diagnosis, and restorative treatment when needed. Think of it as the foundation of lifelong oral health.

Regular visits allow Dr. Abbo to monitor changes over time, identify concerns before they become more serious, and help you maintain a healthy, functional smile.

Why Are Routine Dental Visits Important?

Scheduling routine checkups with Dr. Abbo at Davie Advanced Dentistry offers vital benefits for your health and your wallet:

  • Early Detection of Hidden Issues: Minor enamel weak spots can be identified and treated long before they transform into painful infections requiring deep root canals or extractions.
  • Professional Tartar Removal: Even meticulous brushing cannot remove tartar once it hardens on your teeth. Only a trained hygienist using specialized instruments can clear this buildup, protecting you from chronic bad breath and gum inflammation.
  • Systemic Health Monitoring: Clinical research consistently links oral infections to wider systemic health issues, including cardiovascular problems and blood sugar imbalances. Keeping your mouth clean supports your entire body’s well-being.
  • Long-Term Financial Savings: Investing in affordable preventive cleanings twice a year drastically reduces the likelihood that you will face complex, expensive emergency surgeries down the road.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my family schedule general checkups?

For the majority of children and adults, a routine dental visit is recommended every six months. These biannual cleanings allow Dr. Abbo to monitor changes in your mouth, remove hard plaque, and catch structural decay before it causes physical pain or requires intensive care.

How can I protect my teeth from gum irritation at home?

In addition to keeping your six-month appointments for general dentistry, you should brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss every evening. Cleaning between your teeth removes hidden food debris that standard toothbrushes cannot reach, protecting your gums from swelling and bleeding.

What can I expect during my first general dentistry visit at Davie Advanced Dentistry?

We will discuss your medical and dental history, listen to any specific concerns you have, and perform an oral cancer screening. A professional cleaning will also be completed, leaving your teeth fresh and smooth.
