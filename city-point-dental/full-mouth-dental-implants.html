Full-Mouth Dental Implants in Hopewell, VA

Full-mouth dental implants replace all missing or failing teeth in the upper jaw, lower jaw, or both arches. We use detailed imaging to strategically place implants that support a customized, full-arch restoration that remains securely in place. This treatment restores dependable chewing function while helping preserve the jawbone. There are two options for implant-supported dentures.

Implant snap-on dentures are the most popular option and offer the best value. They snap in and out. You remove them nightly.

Not sure which option is right for you? We’ll walk through every option together–no pressure, no judgment. Dr. Daniel Baker has advanced training in implant dentistry and surgery, enabling him to carefully plan treatment tailored to your oral health and goals.

What’s next?

Follow all instructions for managing swelling and protecting the surgical sites. Choose soft foods while your mouth heals and gradually resume your regular diet as directed. Clean around your implants and temporary restoration using the recommended techniques. Attend follow-up appointments so Dr. Baker can monitor healing and implant integration. Avoid smoking or tobacco use, which can interfere with healing and implant success. Continue routine dental checkups and professional cleanings after receiving your final restoration.

FAQ

  • Am I a candidate for full-mouth dental implants? You may be a candidate if most or all of your teeth are missing, failing, or beyond repair. Your gums, jawbone, overall health, and treatment goals also matter. We’ll use a thorough exam and 3D imaging to evaluate your options, then explain what makes sense for you without pressure or judgment.
  • How many implants are needed for a full arch of teeth? A full arch is commonly supported by four to six implants, although the right number depends on your jawbone, bite, and restoration. More implants don’t automatically mean better treatment. We’ll plan their number and placement carefully to create the stability your new teeth need.
  • Are full-mouth dental implants removable? They can be fixed or removable, depending on the restoration you choose. An implant screw-in denture stays in place and is removed only by a dental professional. Snap-on dentures attach securely to implants but can be removed at home for cleaning. We’ll explain the everyday advantages and tradeoffs of both options.
