Experience Exceptional Dental Care at Acclaim Dental Group

Our mission is to help you achieve your dream smile while maintaining optimum dental health for a lifetime. We emphasize in preventive dentistry as well as restorative dentistry. We keep up to date with modern dental treatment and techniques and maintain all OSHA and CDC standards.

Our team is committed to excellence and quality of care in all our dental services. Our goal is to help our patients gain the highest level of oral and dental health in an atmosphere of trust and compassion.

Meet Dr. Flora Tajalli

Dr. Tajalli, has earned her dental degree and completed her residency in Advanced General Dentistry from Baltimore College of Dental Surgery at the University of Maryland Dental School. She continues to advance her dental education by attending numerous continuing education classes. She utilizes the most current technologies and protocols in the field of dentistry. She provides a wide range of preventive and general dental treatments including Invisalign, Implants, cosmetic dentistry.

Her reputation is well-known for her level of integrity and honesty with a gentle touch and caring demeanor. She takes time to listen and learn about the individual needs of her patients. She then discusses all available treatment options to help them make an informed decision and builds a customized treatment plan that supports each patient’s unique oral care goals. She also has a preventive approach by educating her patients about ways to improve their dental health.

She is a member of American Dental Association (ADA), Virginia Dental Association (VDA), Northern Virginia Dental Association (NVDS).

Our experienced team of dental professionals are dedicated to delivering high-quality, personalized care to patients of all ages. From routine check-ups and cleanings to advanced restorative and cosmetic treatments, we offer a wide range of services to meet your oral health needs. Visit us today and experience the difference that Acclaim Dental Group can make in your smile.
