New Patients with Insurance

Our office is able to bill most dental insurance plans, even if we are not in-network with your plan. When calling to schedule, please have your dental insurance information available and our insurance specialist would be more than happy to check into your dental coverage prior to your child’s appointment. Most insurance plans cover the initial appointment at 100 %, as long as it has been 6 months since your child’s last appointment.

Our office DOES NOT accept OHP.
