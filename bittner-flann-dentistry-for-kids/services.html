Features & Amenities

At our office, we not only pride ourselves on providing the highest quality dental care, but also on delivering exceptional customer service in a fun, child-friendly environment.

While your children are having their teeth cleaned, they can sit back, relax, and enjoy a movie or favorite sports program on the overhead flat-screen televisions. If siblings are waiting, they can play video games in the reception area, have fun playing on a Nintendo Switch, or explore our beautiful saltwater fish tank.

For added convenience, we also have the ability to see up to four children at the same time, if desired. This allows parents to relax with a fresh cup of coffee, tea, hot cocoa, or water while enjoying a book—or simply taking a few moments for themselves. Credentials/Board Certified What is Board Certified? A board certified pediatric dentist is a pediatric dentist that demonstrates an exceptional knowledge and expertise at a standard not possessed by all dentists.

The American Board of Pediatric Dentistry verifies credentials and a voluntary oral board examination must be successfully completed. A dental practitioner with this certification exhibits a drive and determination for excellence in the field of pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Bittner and Dr. Flann have achieved this certification with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry , which represents the highest possible credentials within the field of Pediatric Dentistry. Fewer than 38 % of all pediatric specialists within the United States have attained this accomplishment.

Contests and Winners

No Cavity Club

All children who have no cavities at their check up appointments will become members of the Bittner & Flann Dentistry for Kids No Cavity Club. At the end of each week, one lucky child will have their names randomly drawn out of the no cavity club bowl. If your child is a winner, you will be notified by phone and/or email. Your child will get to come by the office and pick any prize off of the No Cavity Club Wall.
