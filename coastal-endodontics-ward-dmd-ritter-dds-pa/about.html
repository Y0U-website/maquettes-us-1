Your First Visit Guide

Patient Experience Overview

When you visit Coastal Endodontics for your new patient exam, expect a thorough consultation and diagnostic process. Our team will guide you through each step, from initial X-rays to discussing your treatment options, ensuring you feel informed and at ease throughout your visit.

Essential Visit Tips

Please complete necessary registration forms that have been sent to you from our office and arrive at your scheduled appointment time. For treatment appointments, we recommend eating a light meal prior to your visit as we do not want you to be chewing until after the local anesthetic wears off. Consider bringing headphones to listen to your own music and a light sweater for your comfort.
