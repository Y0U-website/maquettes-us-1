Dr. Justin Ponquinette

We are pleased to announce that as of July 27, 2015, Dr. Justin Ponquinette has joined the Atlantic Oral Surgery and Dental Implant Center. Dr. Ponquinette is an Aurora, IL native who specializes in oral and maxillofacial surgery.

His undergraduate was at Hampton University in Virginia. His professional degree he earned at University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia. He earned his doctorate in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Montefiore Hospital in the Bronx, NY where he gained knowledge and experience in wisdom tooth extraction, facial trauma , TMJ disorders and implants among other maxillofacial issues. He is board certified as of 2010.

He is a devoted father of two and enjoys playing basketball and golf.

The specialized skills and experience that Dr. Ponquinette brings provides you with security and satisfaction. Our practice has a strong body of patients and it is important that we continue to provide you with the service and attention that you require.
