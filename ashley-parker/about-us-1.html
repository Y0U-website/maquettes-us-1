Meet Our Doctors

Michael P. George, D.D.S.

Dr. Mike graduated in 1976 from a Uniontown Area High School. He attended West Virginia University, and graduated Cum Laude in 1980 with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology. He received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 1984 from the West Virginia University School of Dentistry. In 1984, Dr. Mike joined his brother, Mallard, at George Dental Associates . Upon Dr. Mallard’s retirement in 2011, Dr. Mike assumed the practice at George Dental Associates.

Dr. Mike is a member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Dental Society of Western Pennsylvania, and is a former President of the Fayette County Dental Society.

Dr. Mike plays a large part in the community and has been a part of several committees and boards along the way, including the following:

Served as a Chairman: Children’s Dental Health Month of Fayette County ; Children’s Dental Health Month for the Dental Society of Western Pennsylvania ; American Heart Association of Fayette Division ; Uniontown YMCA

Served as a Campaign Chairman: Phase IV Capital Campaign for YMCA expansion in 1998 ; Capital Campaign to raise $1.1 Million for St. George Maronite Church renovation in 2014

Served on the Board or Council: Southwestern Pennsylvania Dental Society; Uniontown Hospital for over 15 Years; Uniontown YMCA for over 25 Years; West Virginia University Board of Governors for the Alumni Association of the School of Dentistry, and is a former President; St. George Maronite Church Parish Council for 25 Years, and has served as President for several terms; Pennsylvania Dental Association Delegate

Dr. Mike is passionate about restorative prosthetics focusing on crown and bridge work and esthetic dentistry.

Dr. Mike lives in Uniontown with his lovely wife, Paula, and their two beautiful daughters, Kendall and Erin.
