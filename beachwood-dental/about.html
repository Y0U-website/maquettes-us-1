Calming Comforts, Modern Dentistry

At Beachwood Dental, your comfort and well-being are our top priority. In order to better serve you and your smile, we’ve hand-selected the best amenities and technology modern dentistry has to offer.

TV’s in Every Room

Catch up on your favorite shows and movies while we take care of your smile.

Adjustable Lighting & Music

Bright lights and loud music are recipes for anxiety, especially at the dentist. At Beachwood Dental, you can sit back and relax with soft light and calming music

Candles & Diffusers

Using natural scents from candles and diffusers, we’ve cultivated a spa-like environment to promote your well-being.

iPads

We provide iPads for easy check-in and paperless forms. They also make treatment planning a breeze!

Digital Intraoral Scanners

With intraoral scanner technology, we’re able to capture highly detailed digital impressions for better precision and treatment planning.

3D CBCT Scanner

Our CBCT scanner allows us to produce high-quality 3D digital images of your smile, soft tissue, and more with a single scan.

3D Printer

We utilize 3D printing technology to create your custom dental pieces in-office while you wait!
