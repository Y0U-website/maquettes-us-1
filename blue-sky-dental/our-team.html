Our Team

Our Blue Sky Dental team in Rochester, MN is made up of professionals who are generous, positive and kind. Our family dentists are good listeners who are highly skilled and compassionate. Together, we create a relaxing environment where you will be welcomed and well cared for.

Dr. SonjaMae Langton-Yanowitz

Giving back to my community is important to me, and in both Minnesota and California, I volunteered my services to Project Homeless Connect, Union Mission Gospel, Give Kids a Smile, and the Good Samaritan Dental Clinic. I have also supervised dental students providing care to children in mobile settings.

In my free time I enjoy playing the Irish fiddle and the organ, gardening, traveling, hiking, biking, sailing, cross country skiing, hunting for giant puffball mushrooms, and spending time with my family. My guilty pleasure is mini donuts and cheese curds.

In the office, my desire is to get to know my patients so that I can help guide them towards treatment tailored to their needs and goals. I’m so glad that you are considering Blue Sky Dental for your dental home. : )

Dr. Stephen Sylwestrak

Dedicated to actively helping those in need, I have gone on medical missions to Zambia and service trips to New Orleans, Nicaragua, and Mexico. I worked at non-profit dental clinics throughout Minnesota (Willmar, Cook, Hibbing, Minneapolis, and Rochester). I also volunteer with the Good Samaritan dental clinic in Rochester.

Born and raised outside Chicago, I am now happy to call Rochester my home. In my free time, I like to travel, hike, cook, bake pies in our outdoor grill, see live theater, play the cello, do crossword puzzles and spend time with my family. My favorite fruit is a tie between coconuts and pineapples.

At the office, my goal is to educate patients so that they can make decisions in their own best interest. Welcome to the Blue Sky family!
