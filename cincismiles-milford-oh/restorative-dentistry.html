Questions About Our Restorative Dentistry Services Why is it important to replace missing teeth? If you don’t replace missing teeth, the teeth situated on either side of the gap can shift out of place. This can result in misalignment issues and problems with your bite, which can place excess pressure on the remaining teeth, hinder oral function, and raise your risk of gum disease.

What is the difference between a dental crown and a filling?

Fillings are used to restore teeth affected by small to medium-size cavities. Dental crowns are used to restore teeth with cracks, deep decay affecting one or more cusps, or other issues that require more reinforcement than a filling can provide.
