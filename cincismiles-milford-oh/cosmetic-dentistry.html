Brighten and whiten a discolored smile.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Over the counter whitening products rarely deliver the same level of change you get from a dental professional. If you want noticeable and reliable results in Cincinnati Ohio, professional teeth whitening at CinciSmiles gives you options that fit your lifestyle and goals.

You can choose an in office treatment or a take home option. Our in office whitening uses KöR and Zoom. These systems brighten teeth in a single visit and are popular with people who want fast results. If you want to whiten at home, you receive prescription strength gel and custom trays that give you controlled even whitening.

Many patients choose to start in office and then maintain their results at home. This approach supports a brighter smile for a longer time and gives you a process that feels manageable and predictable.

If you want a whiter smile in Cincinnati and care about real results, professional whitening at CinciSmiles gives you the tools and guidance to reach your goal.

Revitalize your smile. Dental Bonding Dental bonding works well for small flaws like chips, cracks, small gaps, and areas of discoloration. If you want a conservative cosmetic option in Cincinnati Ohio, this treatment can improve the look of your teeth without major changes to your natural enamel.

Your dentist applies a tooth colored composite resin, shapes it with precision, and bonds it securely to your tooth. After the material is contoured and smoothed, it is polished so it blends with the texture and shine of your natural enamel. The result looks natural and feels comfortable when you bite and speak.

Many people in Cincinnati choose dental bonding because it offers a fast way to refine their smile, and most treatments can be completed in a single visit. If you want a subtle change that still delivers a noticeable improvement, dental bonding at CinciSmiles can be a good fit.

Brighten your teeth.

Restore Confidence

A smile that looks healthy and balanced can change how you carry yourself in social and professional settings. When you feel good about your teeth, you tend to speak up more, smile more, and approach interactions with a calmer mindset. Many patients also notice improvements in everyday habits, like being more consistent with oral care because they want to maintain their results.

Redefine your gumline for a more balanced smile. Gum Contouring Excess gum tissue, sometimes called a gummy smile, can make your teeth look shorter than they are. Laser gum contouring removes that extra tissue with precision so more of your natural tooth surface shows. The result is a more balanced and even smile.

The laser allows your dentist to reshape the gumline with accuracy while limiting bleeding and swelling. Many people appreciate the short recovery time and the comfort of this method compared to traditional approaches. Most patients feel ready to smile soon after treatment.

If you are considering gum contouring in Cincinnati Ohio, think about what you want your smile to look like once more tooth surface is visible. Do you prefer a subtle change or a more noticeable shift in your gumline?

Porcelain Veneers

If you want to address chips, cracks, discoloration, small gaps, or misshapen teeth, veneers give you a reliable path toward a more polished smile. Preserve your teeth and enhance your smile.

Dental Crowns

At CinciSmiles, dental crowns can serve as both a restorative and cosmetic solution for teeth that have damage or noticeable flaws. A crown covers the entire tooth, which works well for concerns like deep discoloration that whitening cannot address. It also lets you refresh older dental work that no longer looks or feels right.

Each crown is designed specifically for your tooth to match your bite and your natural enamel. You get strength and durability along with a natural appearance that blends with the rest of your smile. This approach supports both function and aesthetics, helping you feel confident every time you speak or smile.

Straighten your smile discreetly using clear aligners.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a brand with a strong reputation for producing excellent results. This innovative system of clear aligners gently moves the teeth over time into their ideal positions. Invisalign is ideal for patients who are a little self-conscious and want to avoid traditional braces. They are removable and have less of an impact on your lifestyle, making them a great choice for discreet orthodontic treatment.
