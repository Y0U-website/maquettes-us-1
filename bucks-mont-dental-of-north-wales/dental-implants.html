Aftercare techniques for your smile post-procedure

  • Consume Only Soft Foods: While implants are durable, avoiding chewing on hard or sticky foods that could damage the restoration or surrounding gums is ideal, especially during the initial healing period.
  • Quit Smoking: Smoking can increase the risk of implant failure and affect the treatment and healing process. If you smoke, consider quitting to improve the longevity of your implants.
  • Wear a Nightguard if Necessary: If you grind or clench your teeth at night, ask our dentist about nightguards to protect your smile!

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. The titanium post that acts as the root of the implant fuses with the jawbone, creating a secure foundation. The dental crown, which sits on top of the implant, may need to be replaced after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear, but the implant itself is incredibly durable.
