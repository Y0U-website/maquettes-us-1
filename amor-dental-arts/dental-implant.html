A dental implant is a long-lasting, natural-feeling solution for replacing missing teeth. It involves placing a small titanium post in your jawbone that supports a new tooth or set of teeth. This helps restore your chewing ability, speech, and appearance while also supporting bone health. Types of Dental Implant Solutions

At Amor Dental Arts, we offer a range of implant treatments to meet different needs. Every plan is customized to ensure the best possible outcome for you:

Single Tooth Implants Ideal for replacing one missing tooth, this solution helps restore function and appearance without affecting nearby teeth.

Multiple Tooth Replacement For those missing several teeth, implants can support bridges that provide strength and stability while giving you a natural look.

All-on-4 or Full-Arch Restoration A complete solution for those without teeth or with severely damaged ones. Using four implants, we securely anchor an entire arch of replacement teeth for improved function and aesthetics.

Implant-Supported Dentures A more stable option than traditional dentures, implant-supported dentures give you the confidence to eat, speak, and smile without worrying about slipping.

Every treatment plan is carefully designed to fit your needs, ensuring long-lasting results and a smile you can trust.
