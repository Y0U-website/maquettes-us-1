When Should Your Child See a Pediatric Dentist?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) recommends that children see a pediatric dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth appears. Regular checkups every six months help detect issues early and keep teeth strong.

Signs your child may need to see a children’s dentist sooner include:

If you have concerns about your child’s oral health, schedule a visit with our kid-friendly dentists today.

  • Tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Bleeding gums
  • White or brown spots on teeth (possible early cavities)

FAQs About Pediatric Dentistry

At what age should my child start seeing a pediatric dentist?

Children should visit the dentist by age one or within six months of their first tooth coming in .

How often should my child see the dentist?

Routine checkups every six months help prevent cavities and detect issues early.

What if my child is afraid of the dentist?

Our gentle approach and sedation options help ease dental anxiety. Learn more about sedation dentistry.

Do baby teeth really matter?

Yes. Healthy baby teeth are essential for speech development, chewing, and guiding permanent teeth into place.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most insurance plans, including Medicaid . Our team will help maximize your benefits.
