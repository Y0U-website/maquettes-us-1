Meet The Founder

Dr. Raphael R. Putrus, DMD, MPH, MHA

In addition to a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree, he holds two master’s degrees: in public health (MPH) and health administration (MHA). His degree in public health gives him the skills to work with communities and groups to help them meet their orthodontic needs. His degree in health administration enables him to operate well-managed orthodontic offices to provide affordable braces for everyone in the community.

His vision to give every child greater access to care is why he has started Bubble Gum Kids Dentistry! He wants every infant, child, adolescent & parent to have an amazing dental experience! This starts by providing a super cool vibe, fun scented offices, terrific like-minded team members and an overall experience to remember! This dental home extends coordinated appointments, radiograph sharing and less time away from family, friends & school!
