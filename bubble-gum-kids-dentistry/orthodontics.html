FAQs About Orthodontics for Kids

When should my child see an orthodontist for the first time? We recommend an orthodontic evaluation around age 7. This early assessment helps identify issues with bite development or jaw growth before they become more complex.

Does my child need braces or clear aligners? Every child’s needs are different. After an exam, we’ll discuss the best option—whether it’s traditional braces, clear aligners, or Invisalign® First—based on your child’s bite, lifestyle, and preferences.

How long will treatment take? Treatment time depends on the complexity of the case. Most children and teens complete orthodontic treatment in 12 to 24 months, though some cases may vary.

Is orthodontic care painful? There may be mild discomfort as teeth shift, especially after adjustments. However, advances in technology—like smooth aligners and low-profile brackets—minimize irritation and soreness.

Does insurance cover orthodontics? Many dental insurance plans—including Medicaid—cover a portion of orthodontic care for children. We’ll work with you to maximize your benefits and provide flexible payment options.
