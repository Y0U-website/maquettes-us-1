Welcome to Arch Dental Care

Formerly known as Birch Street Dental, our practice has grown with much love and support from our patients these past few years. Through fortunate events, we were able to work on a merge to benefit our need for a larger facility.

Arch Dental Care&#x27;s mission is to provide personalized, high-quality care on an as-needed or preventative basis. We have created a practice that we believe in and choose for our own family members.

We are a full-service family practice of dedicated, experienced dentists who believe in working with our patients to maintain and improve their health. We work together to serve your entire family for all of your dental needs in all stages of life. Our dentists believe in providing comprehensive dental care services to our patients in a friendly, relaxed atmosphere. We also believe in educating our patients as we work with them to achieve the best physical state possible.

Here at Arch Dental Care our team of doctors and specialists work together delivering excellent treatment to our patients. We believe that when the doctor speaks directly with the specialists, our team is able to deliver a better overall patient experience. Our dedicated team of professionals focusing on dental care is a key strength of Arch Dental Care.

Our office believes in Go Green. All our forms and documents are digital and filled out online or iPad in effort to minimize paper waste and our x-rays are all digital as well.

We are conveniently located close to route 101, just a few blocks from route 82 (El Camino Real) near the heart of Redwood City.

Meet Our Doctors

Our team of experienced general dentists and board-certified specialists work together to deliver exceptional care for your entire family.

General Dentists

Dr. James H. Kim, DDS

Dr. James H. Kim is a founding dentist at Arch Dental Care and is dedicated to providing comprehensive, personalized dental care to patients of all ages. He believes in building lasting relationships with his patients and their families, and is committed to staying current with the latest advances in dentistry to deliver the highest quality of care.

Dr. Michelle Y. Kim, DDS

Dr. Michelle Kim spent her youth in Atlanta, Georgia, completed her Bachelor of Science degree in cell molecular biology from the University of Michigan, and received her dental degree from the University of California San Francisco. Upon graduating, she practiced general dentistry in Massachusetts, but relocated back to the Bay area in 2013 with her husband, James.

Dr. Michelle is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the San Mateo Dental Society. She is dedicated to provide her patients with caring and comprehensive treatments that are based on conservative and preventive dentistry. She truly loves practicing dentistry and always aspires to learn new techniques and practices to enhance patient care.

When she can find enough time off out of her busy schedule, Michelle enjoys traveling to various parts of the world, immersing herself in and being inspired by these new cultures. She also enjoys painting, yoga, spending time with her sons and the occasional baking.

Dr. Rachel Choi, DDS

Dr. Rachel Choi is a dedicated general dentist committed to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care to patients of all ages. She brings a gentle approach and a thorough understanding of preventive and restorative dentistry to every patient visit.

Specialists

Dr. Neha Tamhane, DDS

Dr. Neha Tamhane is a board-certified Periodontist specializing in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease, as well as the placement of dental implants. She brings extensive training and a patient-centered approach to every procedure, from pocket reduction surgery and soft tissue grafting to implant placement and sinus lifts.

Dr. Tamhane is dedicated to helping patients achieve and maintain optimal gum health in a comfortable, supportive environment.

Dr. Tanya Zaghi, DDS

Dr. Zaghi provides specialty Endodontic care to patients requiring root canal treatment and Oral Microsurgery. She is skilled at all root canal procedures including basic root canal treatment to the most difficult root canal re-treatment cases for adults and children. Additionally, Dr. Zaghi is skilled at the most delicate microsurgery treatments of the root. She completed her Dental and Endodontic studies at UCSF where she researched studies investigating pain management and response of teeth to different stimuli.

Dr. Zaghi has a long list of awards and affiliations. She is dedicated to providing the highest standard of care for her patients.

Dr. Zaghi has lived in the Bay Area for more than 40 years and practicing dentistry since 2006. She is married and has 2 children. On her free time she enjoys reading root canal journals, snowboarding, swimming, and watching her kids&#x27; sporting events.

Dr. Katherine Kieu, DDS, MS

Dr. Kieu was born and raised in Orange County. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from the University of California, Los Angeles, where she graduated Magna Cum Laude. She then moved to Northern California to complete her dental training at the University of California, San Francisco. Nominated by faculty, Dr. Kieu was awarded the Craig Memorial Scholarship by the Rho Rho Chapter of Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU), National Dental Honor Society, for outstanding scholarship and ethical character.

After receiving her dental degree, Dr. Kieu worked as a general dentist in San Anselmo before starting her orthodontic residency at the University of the Pacific. Recognizing the importance of providing research-based treatment, she maintains her position as an Instructor of Orthodontics and works closely with the orthodontic Department Chair at UOP.

Dr. Kieu is a board-certified orthodontist and an active member of the following professional associations: American Association of Orthodontists, California Society of Orthodontists, and Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists. She also serves on the alumni committee for the Pacific Orthodontic Chapter of the Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry Alumni Association.

Dedicated to excellence and patient-centered care, Dr. Kieu is a compassionate orthodontist who strives to ensure that patients enjoy high-quality results. She looks forward to working with our dental team to help patients achieve healthy and beautiful smiles.
