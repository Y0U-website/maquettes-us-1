About Soft Touch Dental

0 About Us Dental Office Tour Your initial visit to Bee Cave Soft Touch Dental starts at the reception desk, where our friendly staff will check you in and address any questions or concerns you may have. They can also assist with explaining insurance coverage and payment options.

The next stage involves undergoing a simple oral checkup, where we’ll capture any needed x-rays and offer guidance on enhancing your oral hygiene routines at home.

Our team is also available to demonstrate proper brushing and flossing techniques for your benefit.

Dr. Patel will review your x-rays, perform a thorough oral examination, and address any questions you may have. Following this, she will discuss your diagnosis with you and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to her assessment and your specific needs.

We welcome new patients to our dental practice and appreciate referrals to family and friends from current patients. You can be assured that everyone will receive the great service they deserve.

Our goal is to ensure that your experience with us is both positive and as satisfying as possible, whether it is your first visit or you are a returning patient. We pride ourselves on the expertise of our dentist, our knowledgeable staff and our strong relationship with our patients.
