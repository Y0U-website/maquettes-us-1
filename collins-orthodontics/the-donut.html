THE STORY BEHIND OUR WINGS

The wings painted at our clinic right outside of The Donut—by nationally renowned Nashville Wings artist, Kelsey Montague—are inspired by child cancer survivors. They symbolize the inspiring hope and courage these little heroes have and our shared goal of lifting them up—together. We encourage you to share your photos and snap selfies with the hashtag, #whatliftsyou, to get more eyes on our cause.
