Meet Dr. Max Barsky

Max Barsky, D.D.S. graduated from the University of Minnesota with a double major in Chemistry and Human Physiology. He continued on and graduated from the University of Minnesota School or Dentistry in 2006. Dr. Barsky joined Brook West Family Dentistry in 2018.

In 2023 Dr Barsky graduated from the Kois Center in Seattle, Wa. The Kois Dental Center is the most comprehensive post graduate prosthodontic continuum attended by dentists from around the globe. He became the 15th dentist to be get this prestigious award.

Dr. Barsky also regularly volunteers his time and effort working with Donated Dental Services – a program that helps ensure that all individuals have access to lifechanging dental care.

Dr. Barsky’s focus is on both family and cosmetic dentistry. He resides in Maple Grove with his wife, Dina, and 2 daughters, Gabriella and Adriana.
