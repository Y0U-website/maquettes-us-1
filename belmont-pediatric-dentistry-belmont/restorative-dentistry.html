Restorative Dentistry If your child has a chipped or damaged tooth, we’re here to assist! Whether the tooth needs a filling, a crown, or another customized treatment, our doctors can assess your child’s specific needs and, through restorative dentistry we restore his or her teeth to health and beauty. We consider the overall health care needs of each patient, including medical, dental, psychological, and social health, as we carefully create individualized treatment plans.

Advantages of Composite Fillings:

If your child has old fillings that are cracked, and you’re in Belmont, NC, our team at Belmont Pediatric Dentistry can replace them with composite fillings to prevent future problems. Contact us today to book an appointment!

  • They outperform in terms of practicality and aesthetics.
  • They seamlessly blend with the tooth and the surrounding teeth.
  • They involve a less intrusive procedure.
  • They possess exceptional strength.
  • They adhere more effectively to existing teeth.
  • They can repair cracks or chips in teeth.
  • They quickly harden within seconds, making the process fast.
