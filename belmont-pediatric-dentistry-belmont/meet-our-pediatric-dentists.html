Dr. Ashley Goss, DDS

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Ashley Goss is passionate about working with children, shaping positive oral habits in the youth and building long trusting relationships with patient parents. Dr. Goss pursued her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University Of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and she proudly holds her Board Certification with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Her professional affiliations include the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Diplomat, Southeastern Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, North Carolina Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the National Dental Association.

Dr. Kaitlin Gotschalk

Board Eligible Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Kaitlin Gotschalk looks forward to providing the best oral healthcare for your children. She cares deeply about the connection between oral health and overall health, and the importance of developing long-lasting relationships with her patients.

Dr. Kaitlin is happy to be back in North Carolina and exploring the Charlotte area! Outside of the office, Dr. Kaitlin enjoys going to the beach, playing pickleball, and spending time with friends and family.

Dr. Somto Akamnonu

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Somto Akamnonu received her DMD degree from Boston University, School of Dental Medicine and graduated with Honors. She completed her Pediatric Dental Residency at NYU Langone Hospitals.

Dr. Somto was drawn to the career of pediatric dentistry because of her desire to make a change in the life of children. To her, knowing that she can make a difference in the care of her patients leaves her with a very gratifying feeling. Dr. Somto enjoys working with kids because with kids, there is never a dull moment, no single day is the same. Seeing a child have that breakthrough and leaving a smile on the faces of these little angels warms the cockles of her heart.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Somto is a family woman, mother to two lovely kids and loves being a mommy . In her spare time, Dr. Somto enjoys traveling, cooking, exercising, volunteering at the local community and coaching at neighborhood kids’ events.

Dr. Cecilia (Sun) Hwang, DDS

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Cecilia (Sun) Hwang grew up in Charlotte and graduated from Myers Park High School. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with distinction from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2003, after first earning a Bachelor of Science in chemistry at UNC. During dental school, Dr. Hwang participated in rotations at the Children’s National Medical Center in Washington, DC and the pediatric dental clinic at Gaston Health Services in Gastonia.

Dr. Hwang is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is also a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, NC Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry, NC Dental Society and Charlotte Dental Society.

Dr. Hwang chose her career in pediatric dentistry due to her own fear of dentists as a child. She strives to make a difference in each of her patient’s dental and overall health by providing individualized and compassionate care. Outside the office, Dr. Hwang enjoys reading, cooking and volunteering at her sons’ schools. She and her husband also love to spend family time together exploring NC mountains, camping and trying new restaurants.

Dr. Emily Willett

Board Certified Orthodontist

Dr. Willett is dedicated to creating beautiful smiles while fostering a cheerful and supportive atmosphere for her patients. Committed to delivering top-tier orthodontic services to both you and your family, her ultimate goal is to guide and support patients throughout their smile journey, ensuring they attain a result that brings them genuine satisfaction.

Originally from Ormond Beach, FL, Dr. Willett was inspired to pursue a career in dentistry after undergoing her own orthodontic treatment. During her residency at the University of Washington, Dr. Willett gained experience in treating patients of all ages with various dental issues, including early interceptive treatment, orthognathic surgery, and interdisciplinary care.

Dr. Willett is passionate about advancing the field as she actively participates in continuing education courses to explore new orthodontic technologies and conducts innovative research.

Dr. Angelica Saclayan

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

Originally from the Jersey Shore, Dr. Angel earned her dental degree from Temple University in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania before pursuing a pediatric dental specialty at One Brooklyn Health in Brooklyn, New York. During residency, where she served as Chief Resident, she developed a passion for treating children of all backgrounds, especially those with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Angel is committed to creating positive dental experiences and helping every child feel cared for in the dental chair. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband and dog, group fitness classes, and exploring her new home city.
