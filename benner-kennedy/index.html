Benner and Townsend Dentistry is a popular hometown dental clinic located in Winder GA. Led by our exceptional Doctors, our practice offers a wide range of services to help patients achieve optimal oral health.

Dental implants are the superior choice for restoring the beauty and functionality of your smile.

Ceramic crowns are special high-quality crowns used for cosmetic enhancement.

Invisalign ® treatment is the clear alternative to metal braces for kids, teens, and adults.

Dentures, either removable or fixed, are a tooth replacement option for patients missing one or more teeth.

Our doctors and staff are dedicated to providing you with a pleasant visit and results that you’re proud to show off. Our administrative staff is ready to help you with questions about scheduling, financial policy and insurance, to make that part of the process as simple as possible.
