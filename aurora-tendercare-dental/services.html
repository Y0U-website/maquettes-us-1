Dental Services in Aurora, CO

Our services help support healthy growing smiles

Aurora TenderCare Dental provides gentle, compassionate dental care for children, teens, and young adults. Our services are designed to help families feel comfortable, informed, and confident at every visit.

From preventive care to urgent dental concerns, our team is here to create positive experiences while helping every patient build a healthier smile.

Preventive Dental Care Routine exams, cleanings, fluoride, sealants, and guidance to help kids, teens, and young adults build strong, healthy smiles.

Pediatric Dental Care Gentle dental care for infants, children, teens, and young adults in a calm, family-friendly environment.

Emergency Dental Visits Gentle care for tooth pain, swelling, dental injuries, broken teeth, and urgent dental concerns for kids, teens, and young adults.

Dental Sealants Protective sealants help shield back teeth from cavities and support long-term oral health for growing smiles.

Fluoride Treatment Fluoride treatments help strengthen enamel and protect children’s teeth from decay between visits.

Pulpotomy Gentle treatment for deep decay or tooth pain that helps protect a baby tooth until it is ready to come out naturally.

Space Maintainer Custom space maintainers help hold room for adult teeth when a baby tooth is lost early, supporting healthier smile development.

Tooth Extractions Gentle tooth extraction care for kids, teens, and young adults when a tooth is damaged, painful, or affecting oral health.
