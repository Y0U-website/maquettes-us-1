Dr. Brummer and his team are dedicated to great dental experiences.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends you bring your child in by his or her first birthday.

Your initial exam will last approximately 30 minutes. Oral hygiene instructions will also be given with suggestions to help your child care for his or her own teeth. In most cases, we will also clean their teeth on this visit, and provide an evaluation that will outline any existing dental problems and proposed treatment.

Please bring your child’s dental insurance card with you to your first appointment. Thank You.
