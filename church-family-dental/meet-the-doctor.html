MEet the Doctor

Dr. Brent Church

Dr. Church is originally from Pleasant Grove, AL and he is a graduate of The Jefferson County International Baccalaureate School in Irondale, AL. He attended The University of Alabama (Tuscaloosa) on academic and music scholarships and earned a B.A. in Music and Biology in 2012. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from The University of Alabama (Birmingham) School of Dentistry in 2016.

Dr. Church strives to provide the best dental services around for every patient. As soon as you walk into our office, his goal is to give you the absolute best experience from top to bottom. Our advanced dental practice is equipped with all the tools necessary to give you a beautiful smile and transform your oral health for the better.

In his free time, Dr. Church enjoys traveling and spending time with his family and friends. He is also an active member of Grace Fellowship Church.
