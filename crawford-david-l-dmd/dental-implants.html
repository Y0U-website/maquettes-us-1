What Does Getting Dental Implants Involve?

The process of getting implants requires several visits to our Indiana, PA, office over several months. We take X-rays and impressions of the jaw and teeth to determine bone, gum tissue, and spacing for an implant. While the area is numb, the implant will be surgically placed into the bone. The implant will take up to six months to heal.

The artificial teeth created could take several fittings, making this step take one to two months to complete. After a healing period, the artificial teeth are securely attached to the implant, providing excellent stability and comfort.

Good oral hygiene, eating habits, and regular dental visits, will aid in the life of your new implant. If you have questions about dental implants or would like to schedule a consultation, please contact our office .
