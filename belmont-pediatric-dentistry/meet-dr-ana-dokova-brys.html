Meet Dr. Ana Dokova Brys

A Passion for Pediatric Dentistry Dr. Dokova gravitated towards pediatric dentistry because she loved being able to inspire and empower children to be advocates for their own oral health. She believes deeply in the connection between oral health and overall health, and loves to educate patients on the importance of having a healthy smile and building positive habits to last a lifetime.

Beyond the Dental Chair Outside of dentistry, Dr. Dokova enjoys exploring the outdoors all season round and spending time with her husband, kids and big goofy Standard Poodle.

Our Commitment to Your Child
