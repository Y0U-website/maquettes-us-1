What Sets Us Apart There are a lot of dentists who see kids. Here’s why Belmont Pediatric Dentistry is different.

Specialty Care Isn’t Just a Marketing Term

Any dentist can put "we see kids" on their website. Pediatric dentists are different. We complete dental school and then dedicate an additional two to three years exclusively to the oral health, development, behavior, and special needs of children — from infancy through adolescence.

Dr. Dokova didn’t just check that box. She earned a Master of Science along the way, conducts research, stays deeply involved in academia, and built a practice where every detail exists for one reason: to give your child the best possible start in dental health.

Six Reasons Families Choose Us

From our training to our team to our environment — every aspect of this practice is intentional.

Board-Certified Specialist Training Dr. Dokova completed an additional three years of specialty training in pediatric dentistry and orthodontics at UNC Chapel Hill — on top of dental school at the University of Michigan. She is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, a distinction held by fewer than half of all pediatric dentists. That credential isn’t just a title — it represents a rigorous, ongoing commitment to clinical excellence.

Dr. Dokova is trained in evidence-based behavior guidance techniques that help even the most nervous kids feel safe. We never rush, we never force, and we never shame. We meet each child where they are.
