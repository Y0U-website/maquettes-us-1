Our Dental Services

General Dentistry – We can help you maintain a healthy smile and reduce the risk of gum disease with our general and preventative dentistry services.

Oral Surgery – We perform several oral surgery procedures to eliminate your pain and help you feel like yourself again. We offer wisdom teeth removal, tooth extraction, and dental implants.

We also offer sedation dentistry for all oral surgeries as well as other dental services. Sedation dentistry reduces anxiety, discomfort and pain through medications administered before or during a dental procedure.

Cosmetic Dentistry – We offer several cosmetic dentistry procedures to improve the appearance and overall health of your smile. Porcelain veneers, cosmetic bonding, and teeth whitening are all available at our practice.

Orthodontics – Our orthodontic options include traditional braces, cosmetic braces with Six Month Smiles, and clear aligners with Invisalign. Our orthodontic consultations allow us to customize treatment for each patient.

Pediatric Dentistry – We provide children with the dental care they need in a positive and safe environment. Your child receives comfortable oral health services from a team of fun and friendly dental professionals who have the child’s best interest at heart.
