The practice

Whether you need a dental checkup, want to whiten your teeth, or are interested in dental implants or invisible braces, Align Dentistry is equipped with the expertise and state-of-the-art equipment to provide you an enjoyable experience. Our exceptional and experienced staff can provide you the most beautiful smile dentistry can offer.

Contact us today at our Schiller Park or Batavia dental office if you have any questions or to schedule an appointment.

Meet Dr. Tiffany Leung Meet Dr. Christopher Chen Meet the Team What Sets Us Apart

Dr. Leung completed her undergraduate studies from the University of Illinois at Chicago where she graduated Magna Cum Laude. She also received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry, where she graduated with honors.

Dr. Leung practices all phases of dentistry with a primary focus on dental care for children and cosmetic dentistry for adults. She is a member of the Chicago Dental Society, the Illinois State Dental Society, the American Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

In her spare time, she enjoys traveling, watching movies, and spending time with family and friends.
