Christopher Chen, DDS

Dr. Chen received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry. He also holds a Bachelor of Science degree in dentistry. While in dental school, Dr. Chen was honored to receive the Dr. Albany Vlazny Restorative Award for excellence in dental restoration and clinical skills. He also received the Dr. Gould Memorial Scholarship Award for recognition in academic excellence.

Dr. Chen is a member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, and American Academy of Implant Dentistry. To remain at the forefront of modern dentistry, he consistently pursues continuing education. Dr. Chen practices all phases of dentistry with a primary focus on dental implant placement and restoration.

In his spare time, Dr. Chen enjoys biking, flying his remote control helicopter, and spending time with his family.
