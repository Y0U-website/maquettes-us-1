ORTHODONTICS

We are pleased to be able to offer you multidisciplinary care at our office. Rest assured that you will receive the best possible orthodontic advice from our certified specialist Dr. Tina Manh.

Because this is the standard of care when you want to align teeth, our doctors will always do an extensive study of your jaws, your growth, and your teeth before explaining to the patient the right treatment. So feel confident to be treated with the right informations when you consult our doctors. It’s not just about straight teeth, it’s about a healthy straight smile.

Treatment cost is determined according to the complexity of the treatment and its duration. We do offer interest-free payment plans. Do not hesitate to ask our team about this option.

Many insurances will offer some coverage to align your teeth. Contact your insurance directly to find out if this applies to you. Our office will then gladly complete required paperwork for submission.

ORTHODONTIC CONSULTATION

Only interested in a consultation with Dr. Tina Manh, Specialist in Orthodontics?

Dr. Tina will be more than happy to give you options on how to fix your smile.

Patient of all ages are welcome to request a consultation. You do not have to be an established patient at Cloud Dental to request a consultation with the orthodontist. The orthodontist specialist will work with your current dentist if necessary. If you are eligible, we do bill insurances (including Medicaid) for your orthodontic care.
