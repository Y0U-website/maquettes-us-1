Candid Clear Aligners

Candid Clear Aligners utilizes a system of clear, biocompatible aligners that are personalized for your unique smile and gently push your teeth into their ideal positions. Not only will you be able to enjoy all the foods you love during your treatment with these removable aligners, but many patients find that Candid Clear Aligners are less expensive than Invisalign for them! We’ll be happy to review your estimated price and eligibility for this treatment during your consultation.

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Invisalign treatment involves wearing a series of customized aligners that are made from clear plastic. Each set of aligners exerts a small amount of pressure on a specific area of the smile, gradually shifting the teeth until they’re in perfect alignment. You’ll wear these aligners for about 22 hours a day, but you can take them out at any time for easy eating and oral hygiene.
