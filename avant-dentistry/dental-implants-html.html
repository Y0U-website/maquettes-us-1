What Are Dental Implants?

Essentially an artificial tooth root, a dental implant is a small post that is placed into the jawbone to replace the structure of the tooth that was lost. The post is usually made from titanium or other biocompatible substances, meaning that the jawbone bonds with it as if it were a natural tooth. Then, one or more replacement teeth are fixed on top of the implant to bring back the visual portion of your smile.

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are the best tooth replacement option out there. Thanks to their ability to replace the roots of the missing teeth, they have a wide range of benefits that you can’t get with traditional dentures or dental bridges. To achieve this, getting dental implants requires a multi-step process that takes place over the course of several months. Everyone’s treatment looks a little bit different, but here are the four steps that everyone can expect.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The first step in getting dental implants is to meet with Dr. Yu for an initial consultation. Her examination will help her determine whether you’re a good candidate, and she can also answer any questions you may have. Some patients require preliminary treatments, like gum disease treatment and bone grafting, before they can get dental implants. If this applies to you, she will help you to get those scheduled. Once your gums and jaw are in good shape, you can move on to the next step of the process.

Dental Implant Surgery

Once she has given you the green light, Dr. Yu will refer you to a local specialist to have the implants surgically positioned in your jawbone. After numbing the area with a local anesthetic and providing sedation, and incision is made into the gum tissue so the dental implants can be strategically placed in the bone. Then, the gums are closed and protective caps are placed over the ends of the implants so the healing process can commence.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Next comes osseointegration, the process of your jawbone fusing to the implants, which can take up to six months. Then, you’ll undergo another surgical procedure to have abutments placed, which are tiny metal connectors that will secure your replacement teeth. Impressions are taken of your mouth and sent to the dental lab so your customized restoration(s) can be crafted.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

Once your final restoration has been received, you will return to our practice. Finally, Dr. Yu can attach your customized dental crown, bridge, or denture atop your abutments to complete your brand-new pearly whites. If everything looks and feels great, you will be free to show off your new and improved smile to the world!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Since more than half a million dental implants are placed annually, they have become one of the most popular ways to restore lost teeth. Because of the specialized process by which they are inserted into the jawbone, these devices can deliver a range of impressive benefits that traditional restorations like dentures and dental bridges cannot match. Here’s a brief guide to how dental implants from Avant Dentistry can improve your health, confidence, and quality of life.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Dental implants can allow you to enjoy several remarkable benefits that you may notice during your daily activities such as:

  • Giving you the power to thoroughly chew all your favorite foods such as steak, popcorn, corn-on-the-cob, and raw fruits and veggies.
  • Being easy to brush and floss so you can keep your smile clean and your breath odor-free without elaborate cleaning rituals like those required by traditional restorations.
  • Allowing you to speak and eat confidently without worrying that your restorations slipping out of place.
  • Improving your self-image so you can smile proudly at friends, family, and strangers, which can make engaging socially much easier.

Health Benefits

Dental implants can also make it easier to stay in good health in ways like:

  • Powerful chewing ability can help you avoid issues like malnutrition and indigestion.
  • Stimulating your jawbone to prevent or even reverse the process of bone atrophy that begins after tooth loss.
  • Protecting your gum tissue from injury by food debris.
  • Supporting the neighboring teeth so they don’t drift out of place.
  • Not sliding around on the gums, which can lead to frustrating soft tissue irritation and enamel damage.
  • Simple cleaning requirements make it easy to avoid nasty oral infections like tooth decay and gum disease.

Long-Term Benefits

Dental implants have also allowed many patients to enjoy long-term benefits like:

  • An incredible success rate of over 95 % even ten years after the implants are placed.
  • Lasting for decades or even a lifetime if they receive excellent care, which can spare a patient the expense of periodically replacing traditional restorations like dental bridges and dentures.
  • Giving you the confidence you need to smile frequently, which can make you appear friendlier, more trustworthy, and more confident to the people around you.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

Almost any adult patient with missing teeth can be a good dental implant candidate. All that you need is a generally healthy mouth and body as well as a jawbone that is strong enough to support the implants. If that’s not currently the case, we can help change that! During your consultation, Dr. Yu can outline your treatment based on the number of teeth you’re replacing.

Missing One Tooth

Rather than shaving enamel off of two perfectly healthy teeth to place a dental bridge, a single implant post can be vertically positioned into the space where your tooth used to be. Then, Dr. Yu can top it with a crown that is personalized to mimic the surrounding teeth.

Missing Multiple Teeth

A small number of strategically placed implants can hold a bridge or partial denture in place without the need to alter your natural teeth. These prosthetics are much stronger and more reliable than the traditional versions.

Missing All Teeth

If you’re frustrated with a constantly slipping denture, you’ll be pleased to hear that just four to eight implants are often enough to hold a full denture firmly in place. Implant dentures fit more comfortably and allow you to chew more easily than regular dentures ever could.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Before you commit to getting dental implants, it’s important to make sure that you fully understand what you’ll need to do to take care of them. Proper maintenance is essential for ensuring that your implant posts are able to last as long as possible. Luckily, caring for dental implants is relatively simple and not unlike caring for real teeth. Here are some tips from our team that can help you maintain your new smile.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

You’re probably already in the habit of brushing twice a day and flossing once a day. When doing so, make sure that you give your dental implants just as much attention as you give your natural teeth. Dental implants cannot get cavities, but if an infection occurs in the gum tissue around them, they could ultimately fail. As such, good oral hygiene practices are essential for making sure that your entire smile dental implants included remains in good condition.

Eat a Healthy Diet

If you want your mouth to remain the ideal environment for dental implants, you need to put careful thought into the foods you’re eating on a regular basis. Avoid overindulging in candy, chips, and other sugary or starchy foods that can increase your risk for oral health issues. Instead, try eating more foods that help promote the health of your gums. This includes fatty fish, nuts, seeds, milk, cheese, and a variety of fruits and vegetables.

Break Bad Habits

If you put too much pressure on your dental implants, it could eventually lead to serious damage. Thus, it’s worth identifying any poor oral habits you might have, such as chewing your fingernails, crunching ice, or tearing open packages with your teeth, so that you can break them long before they can result in dental implant failure. Remember, bad habits can often be replaced with good ones. As just one example, if you frequently feel the urge to chew on something, you can start carrying sugar-free gum with you.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Plenty of sports, particularly contact sports like football, carry a high risk of getting hit in the mouth, which can end up damaging your dental implants or the tissues holding them in place. As such, you should never engage in any sort of sport without first making sure that you’re wearing a mouthguard.

Additionally, if you have bruxism, you should take steps to make sure that your dental implants are protected from any grinding or clenching that might occur during the night. To this end, a nightguard can be a wise investment.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

You should already be in the habit of visiting your dentist every six months (or more often, depending on the state of your oral health). During these routine appointments, your dentist can examine your dental implant posts as well as the prosthesis they support in order to make sure that there aren’t any issues. Finding potential problems as early as possible can make all the difference for your dental implants in the long run.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

The lifespan of dental implants typically depends on the health and lifestyle choices of the patient. To ensure that dental implants last, it’s crucial that you brush twice and floss once daily. Rinsing with mouthwash every day is another great step to keep your smile clean. You should attend regular cleanings and checkups and avoid chewing on anything particularly hard or sticky. With proper care, dental implants near Friendship Village can last upwards of three decades! This is several times longer than traditional bridges and dentures.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

When placed by a skilled professional, dental implants have an incredible success rate of over 95 %! However, the success of your implants will depend on how well you maintain them with proper oral hygiene, checkups, and lifestyle choices. Success can also vary based on the location of the implant in your mouth. For instance, molars receive more strain from chewing than front teeth, so they are slightly more likely to fail.

Am I Too Young to Get Dental Implants?

Most dental professionals will not place implants in patients younger than 18. If implants are placed before the jaw is finished growing, it can interfere with further bone development and result in complications. Most people’s jaws aren’t fully developed until young adulthood some not until their mid-20s. During your consultation with us, we can discuss whether dental implants are the best option for you at this time.

Will People Be Able to Tell That I Have Dental Implants?

Impressions will be taken on your mouth that are used to design your personalized restorations. Once they have been placed, no one should be able to differentiate them from your natural teeth. Dental implants are placed in the jaw like tooth roots, so you don’t need to worry able your replacement teeth remaining stable like you would with ill-fitting dentures.
