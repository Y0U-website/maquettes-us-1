Implant Dentistry

  • Retention: Patients who have experienced removable full or partial dentures know that keeping their dentures in place is always a challenge. Dental implants offer a great improvement to denture retention for all patients. In some cases the denture can be secured to a group of implants with special screws that stabilize the denture completely.
  • Preserving natural tooth structure: Often, the preferred method of replacing a single missing tooth is a bridge. Bridges require extra preparation for the surrounding teeth to ultimately connect 3 or more teeth. This negatively impacts your health by making the task of retaining your teeth more difficult and by often requiring the destruction of existing tooth structure to create room for the new bridge. An implant is mostly an independent unit and does not negatively affect the adjacent teeth.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

What is the typical healing time after dental implant surgery?

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most healthy adults with sufficient bone structure and good oral hygiene habits are suitable candidates for dental implants. A dental evaluation is necessary to confirm eligibility.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to last many years. With proper care and maintenance, they can remain functional and secure for decades.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

Local anesthesia and sedation are used during the procedure, making it relatively comfortable. Mild soreness is expected afterward but can be managed with prescribed medications.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Implants generally offer greater stability, comfort, and durability compared to dentures. They provide a fixed solution that does not rely on adhesives or removal for cleaning.
