Cherry Payment Plans

At Advanced Dental Arts, we believe quality dental care should be accessible when you need it. That’s why we offer flexible financing through Cherry , allowing eligible patients to split the cost of treatment into manageable monthly payments. Whether you’re planning restorative treatment, cosmetic dentistry, or another recommended procedure, Cherry can help make it easier to move forward without delaying your care.

How Cherry Financing Works

  • Apply online – Complete a quick application in about 60 seconds.
  • View your payment options – Eligible applicants can review available monthly payment plans before accepting an offer.
  • Begin treatment – Once approved, you can use your financing toward your dental treatment.
  • Pay over time – Make predictable monthly payments according to your selected plan.

Benefits of Cherry Financing

Financing terms, approval, and available payment plans are determined by Cherry based on eligibility.

  • Fast application process
  • No hard credit check to see if you qualify
  • Flexible monthly payment options
  • Easy online account management

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cherry?

Cherry is a healthcare financing company that allows qualified patients to pay for dental treatment over time through convenient monthly payment plans.

Does applying affect my credit score?

Cherry allows patients to check their eligibility using a soft credit inquiry , which does not impact your credit score.

How long does the application take?

Most patients can complete the application in about 60 seconds and immediately review any available financing offers.

Can I use Cherry for any dental treatment?

Cherry financing may be used for many dental procedures, including preventative, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. Our team can help determine whether your planned treatment qualifies.

Are 0 % financing options available?

Qualified applicants may be eligible for promotional financing, including 0 % APR payment plans. Available terms vary based on individual eligibility and Cherry’s approval process.

Why does Advanced Dental Arts offer Cherry?

We understand that unexpected dental treatment can place financial stress on families. Offering Cherry gives our patients another payment option so they can receive recommended treatment without unnecessary delays.

If you have questions about Cherry financing or would like help determining whether it’s the right payment option for you, our team is happy to help. Contact Advanced Dental Arts today to discuss your treatment plan and available financing options.
