Meet The Advanced Dental Arts Dentists

As a family-owned and operated dental practice in Manhattan, KS, we have been committed to providing the highest quality of general and cosmetic dentistry since 2006.

We understand that every mouth is different and requires individualized attention and care. That’s why we offer a wide range of dental services to meet all of your needs, including routine cleanings, advanced orthodontics, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, and much more.

Dr. Ellen Remsing, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Ellen Remsing graduated with a B.A. in Human Biology from the University of Kansas, where she was on athletic scholarship for the KU Women’s Rowing team. She attended Creighton University Dental School and graduated in 2004. She was an associate for a few years in Wisconsin before moving back to Kansas to open Advanced Dental Arts with her father (Dr. Errol Remsing, retired) in 2006.

Dr. Ellen is a member of American Dental Association (ADA) and the Kansas Dental Association (KDA). She has an avid interest in continuing education and regularly attends classes to help further her knowledge and give patients the best care possible.

Dr. Ellen is originally from North Pole, AK, and her family moved to Wamego, KS in 1994. She is thrilled to call Manhattan home and loves that she is close to family. She enjoys spending her free time traveling the world and seeking out new adventures with her husband James. When not on some adventure, Dr. Ellen loves cheering on her beloved Jayhawks and spending time with friends.

Dr. Ellen takes pride in making sure the patient experience is a positive one. Her motto is, I see YOU first and then I see your teeth. She is committed to getting to know her patients on a personal level and finds joy in changing their lives for the better!

Dr. Travis Wymer, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Travis Wymer graduated with a B.S. in Chemistry and a B.S. in Biology from Kansas State University. He went on to complete his DDS at the University of Missouri-Kansas City graduating in 2008. After completing additional studies through the American Academy of Sleep Medicine Dr. Wymer is recognized as a qualified dentist of sleep medicine.

Dr. Wymer is a member of the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine (AADSM) and the American Dental Association (ADA) as well as the Kansas Dental Association (KDA). Dr. Travis Wymer is originally from Wichita, KS, but after attending KSU he fell in love with Manhattan and decided to make it his new home. Dr. Wymer and his wife Amanda met in college, and they have a son, Kaiser and two daughters Lyla and Nora. Dr. Wymer is an avid sports fan. He enjoys being outside and spending time with his family.

Dr. Wymer is a compassionate dentist committed to the well-being of his patients and looks forward to getting to know you too. Our experienced dentists and dedicated staff work together to cater to the unique dental needs of each of our patients.
