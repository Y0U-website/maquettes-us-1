Welcome

At Dr. Ashby + Dr. Tran’s office, we believe the best greeting is with a smile.

You ll find a dental team that welcomes you into our family with opens arms

and only wants the best for your oral health.

Our goal is to provide you with the highest quality dentistry in a personable, relaxed environment. Our dental team strives to go above and beyond to ensure that you feel comfortable and your visit goes as smoothly as possible.

Dr. Ashby is a local dentist in La Crescenta who takes great pride in providing honest, caring, and professional dentistry including geriatric services. Having practiced for almost 38 years, Dr. Ashby and his staff are dedicated to improving the dental health of all of our patients as well as preventing dental problems through patient education, home care instructions and early detection.
