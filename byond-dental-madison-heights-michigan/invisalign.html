How much does Invisalign cost?

The price of Invisalign aligners will vary depending on your specific needs. The cost will also vary according to your treatment goals, the time treatment will take and the difficulty of your case. In general, the price ranges anywhere from $3,500 to $5,500.

Does Invisalign affect speech?

When you start wearing your new aligners, one of the first things you may notice is a lisp that was not there before. You do not need to worry, as you get used to wearing your aligners, you will also get used to talking with them and that lisp will dissapear.

How long will it be until I start seeing results?

This is a great question because we get it so often. Each aligner is moving the tooth no more than a quarter of a millimeter per week. Therefore, it usually takes about three to four weeks for people to start noticing changes, but things are changing from Day 1!

What s the best age for teeth straightening treatment?

Invisalign treatment can be used on a wide array of ages including young children, teens, adults, and even seniors. It is important 6-10 year olds are evaluated for preventative orthodontics, also called Phase 1. Teens who have most of their permanent teeth are eligible for Invisalign, as well as adults in their 20’s up to any age.

Does Invisalign hurt?

This is the most common question we get. Whenever moving teeth, inflammation is normal and part of the process. You may experience some soreness in the beginning of treatment. However, Invisalign is much more gentle and controlled than traditional wire and bracket braces, and our patient’s all say they are more comfortable, and less painful!
