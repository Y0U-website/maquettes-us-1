Meet Claremont CA Dentist Dr. Marcelo Durski

Dr. Marcelo Durski

Restorative Dentistry / Prosthodontics

Dr. Marcelo Durski received his D.D.S. degree in 2006. He is certified in Restorative Dentistry and Prosthodontics, a Masters, PhD Degree and Postdoctoral Fellowship in Dental Materials.

Dr. Durski is a member of the American College of Prosthodontics (ACP), the International Team for Implantology (ITI), the Brazilian Group of Restorative Dentistry Professors (GBPD), the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD), the American Dental Education Association (ADEA), and the International Association for Dental Research (IADR).

Dr. Durski has published many scientific articles in various Dental Journals and he has served as reviewer for: Journal of Operative and Esthetic Dentistry and Journal of Operative Dentistry.

Dr. enjoys spending time with his wife & two kids and experiencing new adventures traveling together when possible.
