Meet Your Claremont, CA, Oral Surgeon: Dr. Nadia Hassan

Meet Your Oral Surgeon: Dr. Nadia Hassan

Dr. Nadia Hassan is a Southern California native who graduated from the UCLA Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery program. Her career began with a degree in biological science/neuroscience from the University of Southern California, where she graduated at the top of her class. She later decided to further her education by obtaining a dental degree from UCLA.

Dr. Hassan truly enjoys what she does and is committed to enhancing patient care by integrating her biological education, dental, and surgical skills. She hopes to pursue research in the future, but is dedicated to patient care as her first priority.

When not working, Dr. Hassan enjoys traveling, skiing, reading, or just hanging out with her family and friends.
