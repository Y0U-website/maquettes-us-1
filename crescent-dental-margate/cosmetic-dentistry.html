Professional Teeth Whitening

Your teeth and enamel are exposed to a variety of substances that can cause yellowing and discoloration, including coffee, wine, acidic foods, and more. Despite your daily brushing routine, you may still be noticing that your teeth aren’t as bright as you would like them to be. Our office offers professional whitening solutions that deliver faster, safer, and more dramatic results than store-bought whitening kits.

Our Custom Tray Whitening includes custom trays created from impressions taken here in our office. These trays will allow precise delivery of the whitening gel, ensuring that those tough to reach spots and crevices still receive the whitening agent, resulting in a brighter, all-over white smile.

For at home whitening, we carry Professional Strength Crest Whitestrips Supreme, a professionally dispensed whitening strip that is four times stronger than those sold in stores. With only two 30-minute applications a day, you can be on your way to a whiter smile, all in the comfort of your home.

Crowns, Bridges, and Veneers

When it comes to correcting chipped, uneven, or missing teeth, advances in dental technology now allow for more affordable and natural procedures, with some able to be accomplished in a single office visit. Our team will conduct a thorough exam to determine the best correction to give you the smile you want.

Dental crowns , also called a cap, are permanent coverings used to cover and protect broken or damaged teeth. Designed to fit over the base of the tooth, crowns will blend in with the natural shape and color of your tooth, resulting in a seamless look and feel. Placement of a cap not only results in improved appearance, but added strength.

Bridges can be used to fill gaps or spaces caused by missing teeth. These gaps in the mouth can cause other teeth to shift and lead to additional dental problems. Also created to look as natural as possible and blend in with your existing teeth, bridges are affixed to the teeth on either side of the gap to provide a stable and strong foundation.

Veneers are a popular choice for patients that are not happy with the cosmetic appearance of their existing teeth. They are a thin, porcelain shell that are placed over your teeth, correcting discoloration, shape, and the overall appearance. Custom made to provide the most natural look, fit, and feel, veneers are bonded to your original teeth to create a long-lasting smile enhancement.
