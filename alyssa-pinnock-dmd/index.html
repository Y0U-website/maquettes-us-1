Gingivitis is the earliest stage of gum disease and it’s more common in kids than many parents realize. The good news: with consistent habits and timely care, it’s highly preventable and reversible. Here’s what causes gingivitis in children, how to recognize it, and the steps that keep growing smiles healthy.

Gingivitis is inflammation of the gums caused by plaque, a sticky film of bacteria that collects along the gumline. Unlike periodontal disease, which affects the bone and deeper tissues supporting the teeth, gingivitis involves only the gums and does not include bone loss.
