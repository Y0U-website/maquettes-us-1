Facial Collapse

On the right we have two photographs of actual patients illustrating facial collapse. Notice the shortened distance between the nose and chin, the shriveled appearance of their faces, and see how the upper lip appears to be sunken in each case. If their mouths were properly restored, they would not only be able to eat properly, but they would look much younger.
