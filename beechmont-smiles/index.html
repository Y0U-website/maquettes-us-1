Proper oral hygiene and regular teeth cleanings help maintain your dental health for a lifetime. Dental cleanings remove tartar and plaque and help prevent pain and unnecessary future healthcare costs. The American Dental Association recommends that you see your dentist at least twice yearly for a checkup and cleaning.

Restorative dentistry involves repairing damaged or decayed teeth. Even with proper preventative measures, some patients may still need restorative care. Any tooth can fracture, chip, or develop cavities, but restorative treatment helps improve oral health and appearance.

CROWNS When a substantial portion of a tooth fractures or decays, a crown reinforces and protects the remaining tooth. A crown fits over the tooth and looks and chews like nature intended. A crown can also enhance a tooth s appearance or strengthen a tooth after root canal treatment. Crowns are also used to hold dental implants in place too.
