The Porcelain Veneer Procedure

The porcelain veneer process at Fiori Dental typically requires three appointments:

1. Consultation and Treatment Planning

We evaluate your smile, discuss your goals, and design a customized treatment plan.

2. Tooth Preparation

During your preparation appointment, a small amount of enamel (approximately 0.5 mm) is gently reshaped to accommodate the veneer. Impressions are taken and sent to a dental lab for precise fabrication. Temporary veneers may be placed to protect your teeth.

3. Veneer Placement and Bonding

Two to three weeks later, your custom veneers are ready. Drs. Ferrera will carefully check fit and shade before permanently bonding them using advanced adhesive techniques. A specialized curing light hardens the bonding material for maximum durability.

Most veneer appointments take one to two hours, and patients leave with a dramatically enhanced smile.
