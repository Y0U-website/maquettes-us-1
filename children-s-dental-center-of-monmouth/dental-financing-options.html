Our Mission: Providing Exceptional Dental Care for Children

At the Children’s Dental Center of Monmouth, our mission is to provide exceptional dental care for children in a warm and welcoming environment. We are dedicated to promoting healthy smiles and instilling positive oral hygiene habits that will last a lifetime. Our experienced pediatric dentists and friendly team are committed to delivering gentle and personalized care to each child we serve. Our staff also speaks Spanish for your convenience!

Appointments

At the Children’s Dental Center of Monmouth, we try our best to accommodate your appointment needs. Through experience, we have found that younger children in need of extensive dental work are better to be seen earlier in the day when they are fresher and have a more positive attitude. We understand that some appointments may require your child to miss some school; however, these are considered excused absences. With regular checkups and continued care, appointments during school can be kept to a minimum.

We request 24-hours notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment. We understand that unforeseen events sometimes lead to missing an appointment, but we ask for your help as a courtesy to all of our patients. Continued broken confirmed appointments may result in an additional charge.
