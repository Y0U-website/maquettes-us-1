Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we’re always welcoming new patients from Wallingford, CT and surrounding communities . Whether you need a routine checkup or advanced treatment, we’re here to help.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Absolutely. If you’re experiencing tooth pain, a broken tooth, or another dental emergency, call our office right away. We do our best to provide same-day appointments.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Yes, clear aligners can effectively straighten teeth for many patients. They are discreet, removable, and comfortable, making them a popular alternative to traditional braces.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients report little to no discomfort. With modern techniques and anesthesia, dental implant procedures are safe, effective, and typically easier than expected.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend scheduling a checkup and cleaning every six months to maintain healthy teeth and gums and to catch potential problems early.

Do you offer cosmetic dentistry options?

Yes, we provide services like teeth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers to enhance your natural beauty and boost your confidence.
