About Us

Patient-Centered Dental Philosophy

Arte Dental represents our belief that the most beautiful art is nature itself. Inspired by the way all artists look to nature for guidance, we focus on preserving natural teeth and enhancing smiles in the most conservative and respectful way possible.

Our approach emphasizes minimally invasive dentistry, prioritizing the protection of your natural teeth while supporting long-term oral health.

Under the leadership of Dr. Heejin Chang, we offer personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s individual needs. Using modern technology alongside proven techniques, we provide safe, effective, and patient-centered dental care.

Our goal is to treat every patient like family and deliver an exceptional dental experience in a comfortable and trustworthy environment.

Dr. Heejin Chang

Dual-Licensed in the U.S. & Korea., Protecting What Nature Perfected.

Dr. Heejin Chang is a distinguished dentist whose career exemplifies dedication and expertise. Graduating early from Daegu Science High School, she earned her bachelor’s degree from KAIST, followed by advanced degrees in dentistry from Kyungpook National University and Seoul National University.

Dr. Chang holds dental licensure in both Korea and the United States and completed the USC Advanced Standing Program for International Dentists. She has served as an associate dentist at Gugu Plant Dental Clinic and 365 Yeollin Dental Clinic. She previously ran E-UM Dental Clinic and now brings her endless passion to HUE Dental Group, where she delivers top-tier, patient-focused care.

A member of prestigious organizations such as the ADA, CDA, and KDA, Dr. Chang stays at the forefront of dental advancements. Her commitment to excellence is matched by her personal interests in reading, swimming, Pilates, playing the cello, and art.

At HUE Dental Group, Dr. Chang combines her extensive knowledge with compassionate care, ensuring every patient receives the highest quality treatment. Her relentless passion for dentistry drives her to continually improve and innovate, making HUE Dental Group a leader in patient care.
