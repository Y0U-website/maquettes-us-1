When Should Your Child Consult an Orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists suggests that children see an orthodontist at age seven. The jaw also continues to grow at this age, and treatment at an early age can prevent more serious dental problems in the future. Visiting an orthodontist near you at the right time allows early detection and proper guidance for your child’s developing smile.

If you notice any of these indicators, then an orthodontist for children in Culver City, CA, will advise you on the next steps.

  • Spaces between the teeth Difficulty in chewing or biting
  • Thumb-sucking
  • Premature or delayed loss of primary teeth Clicking or pain in the jaw

Advantages of Early Orthodontic Treatment

Early orthodontics for children in Culver City has a number of benefits:

  • Avert severe problems: Fixing alignment issues early may minimize the requirement for advanced treatment down the line.
  • Enhances oral health: Straight teeth are simple to clean, decreasing the chances of cavities and gum disease.
  • Facilitates facial growth: Correctly aligned jaw assists in balanced facial beauty.
  • Increases confidence : A perfectly aligned smile makes kids more confident and comfortable.
