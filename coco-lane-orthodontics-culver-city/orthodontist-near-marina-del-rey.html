Our Services

We offer many different ways to help your teeth become straight and healthy. Here’s a list of some of the things we can do for you:

  • Orthodontics for Children: It’s great to start checking teeth when kids are young. We can help guide their teeth as they grow to make sure there’s enough space for all their adult teeth.
  • Orthodontics for Teens: Many teenagers get braces. We offer different options to help them get a straight smile they’ll love.
  • Orthodontics for Adults: It’s never too late to get a straight smile! We help adults too, so they can feel more confident.
  • Soft Tissue Laser Treatment: Sometimes, we use a special light, called a laser, to help with the gums. It’s very gentle.
  • Lip and Tongue Tie Releases: For some people, their lip or tongue is a little tied down, which can make it hard to eat or talk. We can fix this with a simple procedure.
  • Gum Procedures: We also help make sure your gums are healthy, because healthy gums are important for healthy teeth.
  • Night Guards & Sports Guards: If you grind your teeth at night or play sports, we can offer a special guard to protect your teeth.
  • Teeth Whitening: Want a brighter smile? We can help make your teeth whiter.
  • Invisalign: These are clear, removable aligners that help straighten your teeth without traditional braces. Most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them!
  • Traditional Braces : These are the regular braces that use small brackets and wires to move your teeth into the right place. They work very well!
