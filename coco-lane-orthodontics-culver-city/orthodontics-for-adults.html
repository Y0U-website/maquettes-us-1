Why Adults Choose Orthodontic Treatment

There are numerous reasons why adults choose orthodontic treatment. Some have had braces as kids but have developed shifting teeth as adults. Others just never had an opportunity to straighten their teeth when they were young. Orthodontic care can:

  • Straighten crooked, overlapping teeth
  • Restore normal bites to teeth that bite the wrong way, such as overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Make it easier to brush teeth with reduced tooth surfaces to clean
  • Give confidence a boost with the formation of a more attractive smile

Orthodontic Treatment Options for Adults

At Coco Lane Orthodontics, our orthodontist in Culver City , CA, offers multiple treatment options to fit different preferences and lifestyles.

Traditional Braces

Traditional Braces remain the best approach for aligning teeth. Made from metal or ceramic, metal and ceramic braces with wires carefully nudge teeth to their proper place. Ceramic is a sought-after option by adults who prefer the less noticeable model. At Coco Lane Orthodontics, we offer expert orthodontics for adults near you with reliable traditional braces to maintain your smile.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

For the more subtle option, Invisalign provides clear, removable aligners that move teeth without wires or metal brackets. The aligners are virtually invisible and comfortable, and enable easy eating and brushing. They are a great option for adults who desire successful treatment with minimal disruption to their lifestyle.

Retainers and Post-Treatment Care

After orthodontic treatment is complete, retainers maintain the results, preventing teeth from moving back to their pre-treatment position. If you are looking for orthodontics for adults in Culver City, at Coco Lane Orthodontics, we provide personalized retainers for long-lasting results.

Benefits of Orthodontics for Adults

Selecting orthodontics for adults near you has numerous advantages beyond a more aligned smile.

  • Improved Oral Health: A proper alignment of teeth makes dental hygiene easier which minimizes the risk of gum disease and cavities.
  • Boosted Confidence: A lovely smile has the potential to enhance self-confidence and make social and professional interactions more enjoyable.
  • Enhanced Function: Bite problems can be alleviated through correction, minimizing jaw pain, teeth grinding, and uneven tooth wear.
  • Increased Options for Treatment: Thanks to innovations in orthodontic treatment, adults have multiple options available to suit their preferences and comfort levels.
