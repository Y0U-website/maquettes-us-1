FAQs

Answers to your frequently asked questions.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last 20 years or more—often a lifetime. The key is maintaining healthy gums and surrounding bone.

Are dental implants painful?

No. Implant placement is done under local anesthesia, and most patients report only mild soreness afterward, similar to a routine extraction. Any discomfort can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good oral health and sufficient jawbone density are candidates. If bone loss has occurred, bone grafting may be recommended to strengthen the area for implant placement.

How do dental implants compare to dentures or bridges?

Unlike dentures, implants are permanent, stable, and don’t require adhesives. Unlike bridges, they don’t affect surrounding teeth. They’re the most natural, long-term replacement for missing teeth.
