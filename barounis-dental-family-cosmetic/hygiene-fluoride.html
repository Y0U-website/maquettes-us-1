FAQ

FAQs

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Most patients benefit from cleanings every six months, though some may need more frequent visits based on their oral health.

Is fluoride treatment safe?

Yes, fluoride is safe and effective when applied in-office and used as directed.

Can adults benefit from fluoride?

Absolutely. Fluoride isn’t just for kids—it strengthens enamel and helps prevent cavities at any age.

What should I do at home between visits?

Brush twice a day, floss daily, and avoid sugary snacks. Our team will offer personalized tips to keep your smile healthy.
