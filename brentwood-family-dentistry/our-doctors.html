About Our Practice

We are dedicated to your smile and are pleased to offer a complete array of general and specialty dental services of the highest standard to exceed your dental aspirations. Whether you are coming in for a dental checkup, cosmetic dentistry such as veneers & teeth whitening, or requiring dentures or dental implants, our excellent group of dentists and hygienists are here for you.

Brentwood Family Dentistry is a premier family dental practice in Brentwood, MO, serving the Kirkwood, Ladue, Webster Groves and greater St. Louis area with an outstanding reputation.

Our family trio of Dr. Michael J. Doherty, Dr. Michael R. Doherty & Dr. Daniel T. Doherty provide top-notch, friendly and comfortable patient care in all aspects of advanced and general dental care. The Doherty family has been taking care of dental patients in the area since 1993.

We care about our patients, that’s why at Brentwood Family Dentistry, we ensure that we use the latest dental technologies when it comes to dental care. Our staff is friendly and competent to provide gentle, professional dental care.

Our main objective is to be keen listeners to our patient’s dental concerns. We will take time to explain and inform our clients on new dental procedures that are available to patients to ensure they are better informed and that they make the best decisions when it comes to their customized treatment options for maintaining a healthy natural smile.

Meet The Doctors

Dr. Michael J. Doherty, D.D.S.

Mick graduated from UMKC Dental School in 1985 and completed a general practice residency at Barnes Jewish Hospital. He has been at Brentwood Family Dentistry since 1999. Mick lives in Kirkwood and has three children, 6 grandchildren, and loves to golf and ride his bike in his spare time.

Dr. Michael R. Doherty, D.D.S.

Michael grew up in Kirkwood and attended St Louis University High School and Truman State University before graduating cum laude from UMKC School of Dentistry in 2014. He then completed additional training in a General Practice Residency at Mercy Hospital-St. Louis . Following completion of his residency, Michael joined the residency program as an attending doctor while also practicing as an associate in a private practice at Mercy for 5 years. Michael currently lives in Brentwood with his wife Kristen, and three children. He loves to golf and enjoy his family in his spare time.

Dr. Daniel T. Doherty, D.D.S.

Daniel grew up in Kirkwood and attended St Louis University High School and University of Missouri-Columbia. He graduated summa cum laude from UMKC School of Dentistry in 2018 and went on to complete a residency at Mercy Hospital where he further developed his skills in all aspects of general dentistry. Following completion of his residency, Dan joined the Brentwood Family Dentistry team. Dan currently lives in Richmond Heights with his wife, Lucy and their two sons. In his free time, Dan loves to spend time with friends and family and support St. Louis sports.
