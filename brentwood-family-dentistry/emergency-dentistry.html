Emergency Dental Care

It’s important to seek emergency dental care in the case of any of the above, or any other condition that is causing you pain or discomfort. Ignoring a dental emergency can lead to further damage and potentially, the loss of a tooth.

  • Knocked-out tooth
  • Chipped or broken tooth
  • Lost filling or crown
  • Abscess or infection
  • Soft tissue injury (e.g. bitten tongue or lip)

How Our Emergency Dentistry Services Can Help

At our dental practice, we are committed to providing high-quality emergency dental care to our patients. Our team of experienced dentists is equipped with the latest technology and techniques to diagnose and treat a wide range of dental emergencies.

Whether you’re suffering from a severe toothache, a broken tooth, or any other type of dental emergency, we can provide you with the necessary care to relieve your pain and prevent further complications. Our goal is to get you back to feeling comfortable and confident in your smile as quickly as possible.

Here are some of the emergency dentistry services that we offer:

  • Pain management: We will provide you with the necessary pain management techniques and medication to alleviate your discomfort and make you feel more at ease.
  • Tooth extraction: If a tooth is beyond repair and needs to be extracted, our dentists can perform a safe and gentle extraction, minimizing the risk of complications.
  • Repair of broken or chipped teeth: If you have a broken or chipped tooth, our dentists can repair it with a filling, crown, or other restorative treatment, to restore the strength and appearance of your tooth.
  • Lost filling or crown replacement: If you’ve lost a filling or crown, we can replace it as soon as possible to prevent further damage to the tooth.
  • Treatment of abscesses or infections: If you have an abscess or infection, our dentists can drain the abscess, prescribe antibiotics, and provide other treatments to help you recover quickly.
  • Soft tissue injury treatment: If you’ve bitten your tongue or lip, our dentists can provide you with the necessary treatment to prevent infection and help you recover quickly.

Don’t Let a Dental Emergency Ruin Your Day

If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, don’t hesitate to reach out to us. We are available 24/7 to provide you with the necessary care and support to relieve your pain and prevent further complications.

At our dental practice, we believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care, no matter what time it is. That’s why we are committed to providing emergency dental services to our patients, and ensuring that they receive the care they need, when they need it.
