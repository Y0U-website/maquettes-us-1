Why Join our Wellness Plan ?

Our membership plan makes it easy to stay on top of your dental health — without the stress of insurance paperwork, deductibles, or yearly maximums. You’ll enjoy predictable costs, priority scheduling, and exceptional care from our team.

This is not an insurance plan but rather a dental care membership program that guarantees you a specific amount of preventive services as well as a discount on most dental procedures. Excluded services include Invisalign orthodontic cases. THis plan is available at our Eagle Rd. location only and does not pertain to referred services outside our office. This plan cannot be used for the following:

  • In conjunction with another dental plan.
  • For services or injuries covered under workman’s compensation.
  • For referrals or lab costs.
  • For hospitalization of any kind.
