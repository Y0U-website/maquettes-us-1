Dentures Frequently Asked Questions

Scheduled dental cleanings at Boise Dentistry Co. go beyond the motions. We provide a lasting relationship with our team while offering these services at your routine visit:

How long do dentures typically last?

With proper care, dentures can last several years. However, changes in your gums and jawbone over time may require adjustments, relines, or replacement to maintain comfort and function. Implant-supported options often last longer due to their added stability.

Will dentures feel natural?

Modern dentures are designed to look natural and feel comfortable. While there is an adjustment period, most patients adapt within a few weeks. Implant-supported dentures often feel the most natural because they are anchored securely in place.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Yes, dentures are designed to restore your ability to eat comfortably. It is best to start with softer foods and gradually return to a normal diet. Implant fixed dentures typically allow for stronger chewing and fewer food restrictions.

Do dentures require special care?

Dentures should be cleaned daily to remove plaque and food debris. Removable dentures should be taken out at night unless otherwise directed. Our team will provide clear instructions to help you care for your dentures properly.

Are dental implants required for dentures?

Not always. Conventional dentures do not require implants, while overdentures and implant fixed dentures do. During your consultation, we will review all options and help you decide which solution best fits your needs and goals.
