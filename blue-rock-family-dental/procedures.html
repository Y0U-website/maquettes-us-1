Oral Cancer Screening

Oral Cancer Screenings with our new Identafi® light can detect questionable or cancerous tissue early.

Signs and symptoms of oral cancer are often overlooked with the naked eye and early detection under this light leads to early treatment. Early treatment of oral cancer gives a cure rate of nearly 90 %. This new medical light uses mult-spectral technology to identify biochemical and morphological changes in the cells of your mouth, tongue, and throat. Speak with us today about an oral cancer screening with the Identafi® light.
