Financing Options

No Money? No Insurance? No Problem! Advanced Dentistry of Plymouth Meeting’s easy financing will make you smile.

We accept Cash, Checks, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, Sunbit and Care Credit .

When extensive dental treatment is needed or when dental insurance does not cover everything, financial arrangements can be made through or CareCredit for low or no interest financing.

We accept most PPO and Discount dental insurance plans. If you have dental insurance coverage, we will file your claim electronically as a courtesy. There are thousands of insurance companies and it is impossible for us to be familiar with all of the individual plans. We will do our best to estimate your portion or co-pay.

IT IS YOUR RESPONSIBILITY TO INFORM US OF YOUR CURRENT DENTAL POLICY AND ANY CHANGES IN COVERAGE.

YOUR Insurance Policy is a contract between YOU and YOUR insurance company. We Advanced Dentistry of Plymouth Meeting is NOT a party to this contract and as such have no control over reimbursements or coverage.

If you have dental insurance, please understand that all dental services are charged directly to you and therefore you are personally responsible for the payment of all dental services. This dental office CANNOT render services on the assumption that our charges will be paid by YOUR dental insurance company. If you cannot provide proof of insurance, you will be expected to pay in full at the time services are rendered unless other financial arrangements are made PRIOR to treatment.
