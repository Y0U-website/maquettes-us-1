About Us

Our Business

ABC Dental was opened in September 2008 as a safety net office for the under-served children of Western Ohio. However, within days of opening our practice, numerous parents saw and liked how we treated their children and asked if we would see them too. We said, "Yes," and as of 2012, 65 % of our patients are adults. We love being part of the growing, vibrant community of Huber Heights. The staff and dentists have enjoyed forming thousands of strong relationships with our wonderful patients.
