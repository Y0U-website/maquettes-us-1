Get to Know

Dr. Trevor Murray

Dr. Trevor Murray has been practicing family, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry for 25+ years. After graduating from State University and the Indiana University School of Dentistry, he continued his education and achieved Graduate status at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. His advanced training in dental implants, porcelain veneers, and TMJ and migraine treatment allows him to offer comprehensive solutions for even the most complex dental needs.

As a Diamond Invisalign® provider, Dr. Murray combines technical skill with an artistic eye to create beautiful, functional smiles that boost confidence and enhance quality of life. He loves seeing how the right dental care can transform not just a person&#x27;s appearance, but their overall happiness and wellbeing. When he&#x27;s not changing lives through dentistry, Dr. Murray enjoys golf, running, and basketball.

Get to Know

Dr. Christian Mazur

Dr. Christian Mazur brings 25+ years of advanced dental experience to every patient he treats. After graduating from Lafayette Jefferson High School, he earned his BS in Biology from Indiana University Bloomington and a Masters Degree in Biology from Purdue University before completing his DDS from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2000. Today, he holds Fellowship status at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies, with extensive post-graduate training in neuromuscular and cosmetic dentistry.

As a Diamond Invisalign® provider with advanced expertise in CEREC technology, 3-D implant placement, and neuromuscular orthodontics, Dr. Mazur blends technical precision with natural artistic talent. This combination allows him to create dental solutions that look beautiful while functioning perfectly. Outside the office, Dr. Mazur enjoys fly fishing, golf, playing the guitar, and woodworking—putting his artistic abilities to use creating furniture and gifts for others. He&#x27;s also spent many seasons coaching baseball, football, and softball for his children.

Your First Visit

Expect Excellence

We’ve reimagined what it means to go to the dentist. From the moment you step through our doors, you’ll notice the difference—a calm, modern space and a team who truly listens. Whether you’re here for a routine exam or cosmetic consultation, your visit is designed to be personal, seamless, and centered around you.

It All Starts Here Your first appointment typically includes a comprehensive exam, a professional cleaning, and time to talk through your goals with one of our doctors. We’ll listen to your concerns, walk through your options, and create a tailored treatment plan that supports both your immediate needs and your long-term dental health.

Begin With Confidence To make your first visit as smooth as possible, please bring:

Your ID and dental insurance card (if applicable)

A list of any medications you&#x27;re currently taking

Completed new patient forms emailed to you ahead of your appointment—or arrive 15 minutes early to complete them in-office

Any recent dental X-rays or records from previous dental providers (if available)

Modern Technology, Exceptional Comfort

At Allure Dental, we&#x27;ve invested in the latest dental technology to make your treatment more comfortable, efficient, and effective. Combined with thoughtful amenities, we&#x27;ve created a dental experience that feels nothing like traditional dentistry.

iTero Digital Scanners

Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable dental impressions. Our iTero digital scanners create precise 3D models of your teeth quickly and comfortably. This technology allows for more accurate treatment planning, especially for Invisalign treatment, and lets you see a preview of potential results before treatment begins.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

No more temporary crowns or multiple appointments. Our CEREC technology allows us to design, create, and place your custom crown in just one visit. These precision-crafted restorations match your natural tooth color and are made from durable ceramic materials for long-lasting results.

Comfortable Amenities

From TVs in each treatment room to a welcoming beverage station and calming design elements throughout, we’ve created an environment that feels more like a retreat than a routine appointment. Thoughtfully curated and intentionally designed, our office helps redefine what it means to feel truly at ease in the dental chair.
