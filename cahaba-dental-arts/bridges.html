Bridges

Missing a tooth? A bridge is a good way to replace it. With a bridge, a crown is placed on the tooth in front and behind the missing tooth — these are called abutments. The bridge spans across the missing space — this section is called a pontic. Bridges are made and placed very similarly to crowns.
