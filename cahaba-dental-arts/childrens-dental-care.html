Children s Dental Care Cahaba Dental Arts

  • Children’s Dental Care Early check-ups are key to initiating your child’s good dental habits. At Cahaba Dental Arts, we strive to make your child’s dental experience a pleasant one. At these early check-ups we will examine your child’s teeth, give you updates on their oral health and growth, and show you proper home care. There are a few things you can say and do that will help ease your child’s anxiety about their visit: Tell your child that the hygienist is going to polish their teeth and then the dentist will come in and count them
  • Set a good example by brushing and flossing in front of your child and by visiting your dentist regularly
  • Don’t use words such as “hurt, drill, shot, needle, or pull”
  • Don’t let your child know that you may have anxiety about going to the dentist
  • Don’t let siblings or friends tell your child scary stories about going to the dentist
  • Try to make dental visits fun for your child

Baby Teeth are Important

Baby teeth are important to your child’s overall health. Children need strong baby teeth to eat well, and a healthy smile builds self-esteem. Baby teeth also maintain space for permanent teeth. Early loss of baby teeth can lead to an unhealthy diet and crowding problems. We provide complete preventative and restorative services for your child.
