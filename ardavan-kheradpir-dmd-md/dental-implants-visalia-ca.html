Healthy Replacement Teeth That Look and Feel Natural

Dental implants are inserted into the jaw bone and act just like natural teeth roots. By restoring a missing tooth with a dental implant, you help prevent teeth from shifting out of place and preserve facial structure. To care for your implants, simply brush and floss them like natural teeth and they have the potential to last a lifetime.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Prevent bone loss.

Dental implants are the only type of tooth restoration that fuses to the jaw bone, which prevents bone deterioration caused by tooth loss.

Beautiful results.

Every implant crown is custom-made to blend in with your other teeth for natural-looking results.

Eat comfortably.

Your implants stay in your mouth at all times and because of their sturdy foundation, you can enjoy your favorite foods comfortably.

Love your smile.

Having a complete smile that you feel proud of will boost your self-esteem and help you feel more confident about eating, smiling, and laughing around others.

Types of Dental Implants in Visalia, CA

Our oral surgeons offer a variety of implant-based treatments. During the consultation, we will take 3D scans of your mouth, examine your oral health, discuss your restoration goals, and determine the right type of implant treatment for you.

Immediate implants can replace broken, failing, or missing teeth in a single surgical visit. The number of appointments is reduced, and you enjoy your new teeth immediately. Watch the video to learn more!

  • Single Tooth Replacement. We can replace a missing or damaged tooth anywhere in the mouth as long as there is adequate jaw bone to support the implant.
  • Immediate Implants. Immediate or same-day dental implants are inserted immediately following tooth extraction. This reduces the overall treatment time by eliminating the need for a separate surgical visit, saving you time and money.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge. If multiple teeth are missing, we can place an implant-supported bridge. Typically, one implant post can support up to three replacement teeth.
  • Full-Arch Restoration . As few as four implants are needed to support a fixed denture in one or both arches. The results are a beautiful new smile that stays in place and functions like natural teeth.

Dental Implant Procedure

At the consultation, your oral surgeon will explain your treatment options and walk you through every step of your oral surgery journey. We will also discuss your anesthesia options and help you choose the right type for your comfort. The details of your procedure will vary depending on your treatment plan and if you require additional procedures, such as bone grafting or tooth extraction.

On the day of your procedure, we will administer the selected form of anesthesia. After it takes effect, your surgeon will make an incision in the gum tissue and insert the dental implant. The site will be closed with dissolvable sutures, and you will rest until the anesthesia wears off and we feel you are ready to go home.

Sometimes we attach the abutment during the initial procedure. If not, you will return for a second surgical visit where we will uncover the implant post and attach the abutment. In some cases, we will attach a temporary crown to the implant, which will provide you with a temporary restoration to wear while your implant heals. After the implants have healed, you will visit your restorative dentist to receive your final crown.

Denture Replacement With Dental Implants

Removable dentures or bridges are another option for restoring teeth, but they do not prevent bone loss or restore full functionality. Many patients who wear dentures dislike having to take their teeth out during meals and brushing. Although dentures may seem cheaper upfront, the maintenance costs can accumulate over time. Dentures should be replaced every few years, and without a tooth root or implant post present to prevent bone loss, costly bone repair treatments may be necessary.

An implant-supported denture prevents bone loss, preserves facial structure, and stays in place at all times. Whether you are looking to restore one or both arches of teeth, full-arch restoration can help you achieve the beautiful and fully functional smile you deserve. Contact us in Visalia, CA, to see how full-arch restoration can provide you with a comfortable, convenient, and healthy alternative to traditional dentures.
