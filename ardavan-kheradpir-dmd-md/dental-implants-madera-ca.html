Restore Your Smile With Healthy Tooth Replacement Options

Dental implants are a versatile solution to your oral health needs. They can replace a single tooth or even an entire arch of teeth. Having a missing tooth or several missing teeth can not only make you feel self-conscious about your smile, but can also lead to other oral health complications such as bone loss and dental shifting. You may find your speech is also affected and you are unable to eat the foods you love comfortably.

A huge advantage to dental implants is that they allow you to eat the foods you love, speak clearly and without difficulty, and smile naturally. In addition, dental implants can prevent bone loss and dental shifting while preserving facial aesthetics. Our expert oral surgeons are highly trained in placing dental implants from our office in Madera, CA. For more information and to schedule a consultation, contact our office.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Enjoy Food Again.

By fusing to the jaw bone, your implant has a solid foundation, allowing you to eat your favorite foods without any moving parts or discomfort.

Prevent Bone Loss.

Missing teeth cause the jaw bone to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Dental implants are designed to replicate a natural tooth, providing stimulation to the jaw and preventing bone loss.

Easy Maintenance.

Your implant will remain in place during eating and brushing. Simply brush and floss regularly to maintain good oral hygiene and your implant can last a lifetime.

Protect Oral Health.

Dental implants prevent nearby teeth from shifting out of place and by keeping the jaw healthy, they prevent facial sagging associated with bone loss.

Types of Dental Implants in Madera, CA

During your first visit, we will discuss the goals for your smile and oral health, then determine the right type of implants for your treatment.

  • Single Tooth Replacement. Wherever a tooth is missing or needs replaced, a dental implant can be inserted into its place, provided there is enough jaw bone.
  • Immediate Implants. Following a tooth extraction, your surgeon can insert an implant post into the empty tooth socket. This reduces the number of appointments necessary to restore the tooth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges. Dental implants can support a bridge, which restores the appearance of several teeth missing next to each other. This protects your healthy teeth from needing to be ground down to fit the bridge.
  • Full-Arch Restoration. Several dental implants can support a fixed prosthesis in one or both dental arches. This treatment completely restores your smile with natural-looking and fully functioning results.

Dental Implant Surgery

We will take 3D scans of your mouth during your first visit to assist with treatment planning and creating surgical guides. We will also discuss your anesthesia options to ensure you are as comfortable as possible during surgery. If you need bone grafting or tooth extractions , you may have more or fewer appointments.

Your next appointment will be the day of the surgery. After administering the anesthesia, your oral surgeon will place the dental implant post into the jaw bone. Next, the surgeon closes the surgical site, and you will rest until the anesthesia wears off. If you have undergone IV sedation, a responsible adult must drive you home after your procedure and remain with you for 24 hours. Our office provides detailed instructions to follow before and after surgery. Doing so will ensure a smooth and speedy recovery.

Cost and Value of Dental Implants

The cost of your dental implant procedure depends on how many and what types of implants you need, your anesthesia choice, your insurance, and if you require additional procedures. We discuss all of these items with you during the consultation and provide you with an accurate price estimate.

Removable dentures and bridges are an alternative to dental implants. However, these cheaper options come with many downsides. For example, dentures must be removed during eating and brushing, then cleaned using special rinses each day. Ill-fitting dentures can slide around and cause sores, and many patients find them inconvenient. Because there is nothing fused to the jaw bone where the teeth are missing, the jaw breaks down over time. Severe bone loss results in dental shifting, sagging facial features, and costly surgical repair down the line.

With dental implants, you avoid these issues. Your implant remains in your mouth at all times and because it looks and feels natural, you’ll likely forget you even have a dental implant.
