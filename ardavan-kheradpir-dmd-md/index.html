The practice

After receiving his undergraduate degree in biochemistry at the University of Ottawa , Dr. Kheradpir went to dental school at Tufts University , where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2000. He then completed his oral and maxillofacial surgery residency program here in Fresno before going to medical school. He earned his medical degree from Louisiana State University in Shreveport…

Dr. Kheradpir is certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery ( ABOMS ). To achieve this certification, he had to pass a test and demonstrate his knowledge of the treatments and conditions of the oral and maxillofacial region. This rigorous testing process further prepared him to treat patients at the highest level of care.

Dr. Kheradpir is Stemodontics ® -certified and offers stem cell services at this practice. Stem cells can be harvested from extracted wisdom teeth and stored for future uses in combating illness or disease.

When he is not at the practice, Dr. Kheradpir cherishes his time with his family. He also enjoys tennis, skiing, and traveling.
