Healthy and Secure Replacement Teeth

If you are missing a single tooth or many teeth, dental implants may be the right option for you. Dental implants are a secure tooth replacement solution that offers the same look, feel, and function of healthy natural teeth. You can eat the foods you want and smile with confidence again. Dental implants are a versatile option for tooth replacement and can replace a single tooth or an entire arch of teeth.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

A significant advantage of dental implants is that they function just like a natural tooth. The implant, which is a post composed of medical-grade titanium, is surgically placed beneath the gum tissue and into your jaw bone. The dental implant fuses with your natural bone over time, which creates a sturdy foundation for your replacement teeth. The abutment piece affixes to the implant post and supports the custom-designed crown. The results are beautiful, natural, secure, and best of all, permanent.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Permanence.

The implant post fuses to the jaw bone, creating a sturdy foundation that allows you to eat the foods you love without any moving parts. With proper care, your dental implant can last a lifetime.

Prevent Bone Loss.

Dental implants are the only restoration solution that protects the jaw bone by providing stimulation, similar to a natural tooth’s root. This prevents bone loss and keeps your jaw healthy.

Natural Results.

The crown is custom-made to blend in with your other teeth because the post is embedded into the jaw, your implant will look, feel, and function just like a natural, healthy tooth.

Improve Health.

Having a complete smile prevents teeth from shifting out of position and prevents bite problems.

Dental Implant Surgery

Based on your individual needs and circumstances, the dental implant process can vary. However, it usually requires three or more office visits: one consultation appointment, one surgery appointment, and a couple of follow-up appointments. If you need bone grafting, tooth extractions, or want immediate implants, you may have more or fewer appointments.

At your consultation, your oral surgeon will evaluate your condition and examine your medical and dental history before determining your treatment plan. 3D scans will be taken at this time to review your specific facial anatomy and develop your custom treatment plan. These scans can also assist in the creation of custom 3D surgical guides. If you have any questions about your dental implant procedure, your consultation is a perfect time to ask. Your oral surgeon will address all of your questions and will help you to feel comfortable and informed about your upcoming procedure.

Your next appointment will be the day of the surgery, which is when you will receive your dental implants. After administering the agreed-upon anesthesia , your oral surgeon will place the dental implant post into the bone. Dissolvable sutures will be placed, as needed and you will rest until the anesthesia wears off. Then, you are ready to go home. We may provide you with a temporary crown to wear. Once your surgical sites have healed, your restorative dentist will place the new crown, which is custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Dental Implants: Their Cost and Value

Your dental implant cost will vary depending on a number of factors, including how many teeth you need replaced, the type of implant and anesthesia, your insurance, and if you require additional procedures. Our staff will provide you with a cost estimate during your first visit so you know exactly what to expect.

Some patients choose removable bridges or dentures because they are cheaper in the beginning. These options do not prevent the jaw bone from deteriorating, and removable oral appliances should be replaced every few years. Without support, the jaw bone can break down so much that your facial features begin to sag, teeth shift out of place, and you require more costly bone repair surgery.

Dental implants have a high success rate and require the same care as natural teeth. Simply brush and floss regularly, and your implant can last a lifetime. There are no moving parts that cause sores or need to be removed during meals.
