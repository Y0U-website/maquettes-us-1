Address Your Common Teeth Conditions

At Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry, we understand that dental issues can affect more than just your smile they can impact your overall health and confidence. Our office provides comprehensive care for a wide range of dental conditions, helping you restore your oral health quickly and comfortably.

Patients from Ashland, Framingham, Hopkinton, Holliston, Southborough, Natick, Milford, and across MetroWest visit our office for prompt diagnosis, advanced technology, and comprehensive treatment options

Toothache

At Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry, under the care of Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI, we understand that a toothache can be both painful and disruptive. We provide a thorough examination using digital X-rays and diagnostic tools to pinpoint the cause, whether it’s decay, infection, or trauma.

Once the source is identified, Dr. Almaawi develops a personalized treatment plan, which may include fillings, root canal therapy, or other restorative procedures to relieve pain and restore oral health. Our goal is prompt, effective relief while preserving as much of your natural tooth as possible.

Broken or Chipped Tooth

A broken or chipped tooth can affect both function and appearance. At Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry, Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI evaluates the damage using advanced imaging and determines the best solution for restoration.

Depending on the severity, we offer treatments such as dental bonding, veneers, or crowns to restore the tooth’s strength and appearance. Our approach ensures that your smile looks natural while protecting the tooth from further damage.

Missing Teeth

Missing teeth can impact chewing, speech, and confidence. Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI and our team at Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry provide customized solutions to replace missing teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics.

Treatment options may include single or multiple dental implants, implant-supported bridges, or dentures. Explore our full range of missing teeth treatment options on our implant dentistry page. Because our office provides in-house implant placement and restoration, many missing-tooth cases can be planned and restored in one familiar Ashland dental office.

Each plan is tailored to your oral health, bone structure, and lifestyle, ensuring long-lasting, natural-looking results.

Tooth Sensitivity

Tooth sensitivity can make eating and drinking uncomfortable. At Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry, Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI identifies the underlying cause, whether it’s enamel erosion, gum recession, or tooth decay.

Treatment may include desensitizing agents, fluoride applications, enamel restoration, or targeted restorative procedures. Our office focuses on relieving sensitivity while protecting and strengthening your teeth for long-term comfort.

Bite or Alignment Problems

Problems with your bite or tooth alignment can lead to discomfort, uneven wear, or jaw issues. Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI carefully evaluates your bite at Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry using advanced diagnostics to create a precise treatment plan.

Treatment options may include Invisalign® clear aligners, occlusal adjustments, or restorative solutions to balance your bite. Our approach is designed to improve function, comfort, and the overall appearance of your smile.

Gum Disease

Gum disease can compromise oral health if left untreated. At Ashland Family and Implant Dentistry, Dr. Ali Almaawi, DDS, FICOI provides personalized periodontal care to treat and manage gum disease at every stage.

Our treatments may include deep cleanings (scaling and root planing), antimicrobial therapy, and ongoing maintenance to restore gum health and prevent progression. Our office focuses on both effective treatment and patient education to maintain long-term oral wellness.
