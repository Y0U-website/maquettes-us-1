The practice

Families from Framingham, Natick, Hopkinton, Holliston, Southborough, Milford, and communities across the broader MetroWest area choose our practice for this in-house capability. Whether you need a single tooth replaced or a full-arch implant reconstruction, every step is handled by the same clinical team, in one location.

Our technology includes 3D cone beam CT imaging, digital intraoral scanning, and in-office CEREC crown milling tools that improve diagnostic accuracy, allow precise treatment planning, and reduce the number of visits needed to complete care.

We take time to explain findings, walk through your options, and give you a clear picture of what to expect before any treatment begins.

We want every patient in Ashland, Framingham, Natick, and the MetroWest area to have access to the care they need. We accept Cherry Financing and Proceed Finance, with flexible monthly payment plans available. Ask about our In-House Membership Plan for patients without dental insurance.
