The practice

Welcome to blue sky dental Quality care matters We believe that whatever your dental needs may be, you will be treated with the highest quality dentistry available and with the most compassionate, respectful care you deserve.

We provide quality dental care, conveniently in your neighborhood and cater to your schedule. Because your comfort is important to us, we offer many of the modern amenities you’ve come to expect in a dental office, like DirectTV in all of our rooms, noise-canceling headphones, and leather massage chairs.

At Blue Sky Dental, we are committed to providing the highest level of care by utilizing the latest advancements in dental technology. Our state-of-the-art equipment enhances precision, improves patient comfort, and ensures the best possible outcomes for your dental health.

Gone are the days of uncomfortable, goopy impressions. Our ITero Scanner creates highly accurate 3D digital impressions in minutes, offering a faster, more comfortable experience for treatments like crowns, bridges, and Invisalign. This cutting-edge technology allows us to visualize your treatment plan in real-time, ensuring the most precise fit and best results.

For complex cases, our Cone Beam CT technology provides detailed, 3D images of your teeth, jaw, nerves, and soft tissues. This advanced imaging system allows us to diagnose and plan treatments with unparalleled accuracy, making procedures such as dental implants, root canals, and extractions safer and more efficient.
