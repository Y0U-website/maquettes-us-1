Dental implants

A dental implant is a common tooth restoration option because they are easy to care for, indistinguishable from natural teeth, and are permanently secured into your jaw bone. The post of a dental implant improves the stability of the jaw because it stimulates the bone, which is crucial to maintaining the structural integrity and strength of the jaw bone. Dental implants are versatile and can be used to support a permanent denture, which is known as a full-arch restoration.

Wisdom teeth

Wisdom teeth are the last teeth to develop in the mouth and rarely grow without causing problems. These teeth, also known as third molars, can become trapped beneath the gum and can cause tumors, cysts, infection, pain, and can even threaten the health of other teeth. Dental professionals often recommend having wisdom teeth extracted during the mid to late teen years to prevent these oral health problems from having the chance to arise.
