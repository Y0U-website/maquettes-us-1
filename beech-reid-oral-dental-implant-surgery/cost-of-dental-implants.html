An Investment Worth Making

By investing in replacing missing or failing teeth with dental implants now, you prevent numerous procedures from being necessary in the future. When a tooth is absent, the gums around it and bone beneath it start to deteriorate. If the tooth is not replaced with a dental implant, which has a post designed to act like a tooth’s root, severe jaw bone atrophy can occur. This can lead to extensive bone repair treatment, which often requires multiple surgeries. Patients with gum disease may suffer from tooth loss as a result of teeth loosening over time.

Missing teeth can also lead to dental shifting, which may require braces. The cost of orthodontic care can stretch over several years, as it takes time for teeth to become properly realigned. Patients missing multiple teeth can benefit from a full mouth of dental implants, or a completely new smile using full-arch restoration. This treatment uses implants to support a fixed prosthesis, providing long-term functional and cosmetic benefits that traditional dentures cannot.

Not having a complete smile leads many people to want to hide their smiles. With dental implants, our surgeons help patients restore their smiles with confidence and convenience.
