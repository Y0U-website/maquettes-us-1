The practice

We look forward to welcoming you and your family to our dental practice and helping each of you achieve a comfortable and healthy smile.

Dr. Craig Weinstein has valuable experience in all aspects of family and general dentistry. He also provides expanded services such as extractions , clear braces , and root canal treatment so you can get the quality care you need in the comfort of our office.

It is important to us that we make dental care convenient for the families in our community. Racing all over town from one dentist to another is tough! We welcome your children to our dental practice and can often make treatment easier by providing simultaneous appointments for members of your family.

This practice started with Dr. Weinstein’s father-in-law 35 years ago, Dr. Hugh Habas. Because of our continuous community presence, we have had the unique opportunity to watch the families here grow and provide them dental care throughout their lifetimes.

This long-term patient relationship is important to us because we can provide the best care based on your personal goals for your health and smile.

One of the greatest joys in what we do is getting to know each of you, learning your name, meeting your family, and seeing you come back again and again for your dental needs. You are never just patients to us you are also friends!

We work with a number of insurance companies to make getting the care you need easy and affordable. We also offer patient financing when you need to pay off your dentistry over time. We will work with you to create a treatment plan that fits your dental needs and help you fit your care into your budget.

We welcome patients from Fords, Metuchen, Perth Amboy, Woodbridge, Edison, Keasbey, and the surrounding communities. We look forward to meeting you and can’t wait to see your smile!
