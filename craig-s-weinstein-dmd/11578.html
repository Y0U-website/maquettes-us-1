Dental Implants

Dental implants look so much like your natural teeth, that no one will notice you have a dental implant when you talk, smile, or eat. The only visible part of your dental implant will be the custom implant crown.

  • Unrestricted diet
  • Long-term solution for missing teeth
  • Convenient care
  • Stability
  • Excellent esthetics
  • Support for bridges and dentures

Dental Implant Treatment

Your dental implant treatment occurs in several steps:

Evaluation First, Dr. Weinstein will evaluate your oral health and determine if you are healthy enough for dental implants. Healthy teeth and gums are essential!

Treatment planning He will also take x-rays to determine your bone levels and may even refer you to have more detailed x-rays taken. This part of the treatment allows the surgeon to accurately place your dental implants in an area with sufficient bone.

Surgery After your surgery, you will need time to heal. Dental implant surgery causes minimal discomfort, and patients recover quickly. The healing time we recommend will allow your dental implants to integrate with your natural bone.

Restoration Dr. Weinstein will design your custom dental implant crown so it matches your natural teeth. This provides a lifelike look for our patients.

Maintenance Caring for your dentistry is essential to its longevity. You should brush and floss normally, come to our office for dental cleanings and exams, and report any changes in your oral health. We will monitor your dental implant at your regular dental exams.
