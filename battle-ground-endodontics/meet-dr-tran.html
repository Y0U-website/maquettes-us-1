Meet Dr. Edward Tran

Edward Tran, DDS

A proud graduate of Vancouver’s Skyview High School, Dr. Tran is honored to provide specialized care to the community he calls home. At Battle Ground Endodontics, he brings a unique perspective that blends advanced specialty training with a strong foundation in comprehensive dental care.

Dr. Tran earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry. He then spent seven years serving the Southwest Washington community as a general dentist. This experience allows him to collaborate closely with referring providers and deliver endodontic care that supports each patient’s long-term restorative goals. He later pursued his passion for specialty care by completing his certificate in Endodontics at Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU) School of Dentistry.

Committed to clinical excellence, Dr. Tran integrates the latest advancements in dental technology—including high-resolution CBCT imaging and state-of-the-art surgical microscopy—to ensure precision, efficiency, and patient comfort at every step.

Dr. Tran is also dedicated to giving back to his community. He volunteers at the Free Clinics at Battle Ground HealthCare and actively participates in the Clark County Dental Society and the Marshall Baumgartner Study Group, where he continues to collaborate with peers and stay at the forefront of the field.

Outside the office, Dr. Tran enjoys exploring the Pacific Northwest with his wife and two daughters. Whether it’s a quick trip to the coast, visiting loved ones in Seattle or Spokane, or discovering a new local restaurant, he values time spent with family and creating lasting memories together.
