Do you accept Medicare/ Medicaid, or any supplemental plans?

We are not contracted with Medicare or Medicaid, and therefore can not file to those entities. We do not file supplemental insurance plans, but we would be glad to provide you with all the information you would need to self file.

We DO accept care credit. We do not apply for patients in office, but if you are a pre-existing Care Credit cardholder we are more than happy to accept that form of payment. We also take checks, cash, Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, and Discover.
