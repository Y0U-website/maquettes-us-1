Restorative & Cosmetic Dentistry

Cantrell West Dental’s full range of services includes restorative dentistry, such as fillings, crowns, implants, and cosmetic dentistry, such as teeth whitening, gum re-shaping, and more.

Highlights

  • Tooth-colored fillings, and all-porcelain crowns; veneers, onlays, and crowns for decayed, cracked, or broken teeth.
  • Dental implant placement and restoration
  • Tooth replacement procedures including dental implants, fixed bridges, and removable partial and custom full dentures.
  • Highly effective treatment for tooth sensitivity problems, and the methods and materials to never let tooth sensitivity prevent you from eating the things you enjoy most.
  • Cosmetic procedures such as tooth whitening/bleaching, bonding, porcelain veneers and crowns designed to enhance your smile. Or you can get a total makeover! Take a look at some of our “makeovers” when you’re in our office
  • Contact-lens-thin veneers for the most conservative treatment cosmetic change (when possible)
  • Gum re-shaping to properly “frame” the teeth and correct smile issues
  • Tooth re-shaping when small changes are warranted without the need for veneers or crowns.
  • Replacement or repair of previous cosmetic dentistry procedures or where undesirable results were obtained.

TMJ Treatment

Cantrell West Dental’s full range of services includes treatment of TMJ dysfunction.

Highlights

  • Treatment for jaw/jaw joint pain and headache pain, including the NTI-tss appliance—the only appliance approved by the FDA for prevention and treatment of migraine headaches (which is also extremely helpful for those with tooth clenching and grinding problems).
  • Bite and jaw re-alignment procedures when imbalances are causing pain and discomfort through use of computerized occlusal analysis—very accurate!
  • Dental splints and other appliances to aid in bite imbalances and jaw, TMJ, and ear pain.

LASER Technology

Cantrell West Dental is one of the 5 % of all dental practices nationally to utilize LASER technology for easier, less painful, and more effective periodontal (gum) treatments that are faster healing, last longer, and require fewer antibiotics.
