Practice Information

Office Hours

Cantrell West Dental is open Monday through Thursday (with some selected Fridays). Appointments are available from 7:10 am to 12:00 pm and from 1:00 pm to 4:00 pm. To make an appointment, call us at (501) 664-3279 .

Emergency Dental Care

Any problem you consider a dental emergency is a dental emergency. Please give us a call at (501) 664-3279 . We will do everything possible to care of your problem as soon as we can. Cantrell West Dental provides emergency care whenever possible to patients of record only. If it’s after-hours, call our office and follow the instructions. If it is a serious problem, call 911 or get to the nearest emergency room.

Payment Options

Our financial options are flexible and friendly. Dr. Siria and Dr. Deems are Delta Dental and Blue Cross/Blue Shield providers, as well as Aetna, Always Care, Ameritas, Careington, Cigna DDPO, Connection, Guardian, Principal, Sunlife/DHA, United Concordia (UCCI), and United Health Care (UHC). We accept most major credit cards and other financial services: MasterCard, Visa, American Express, Discover Card, Dental Fee Plan, Care Credit, and others.
