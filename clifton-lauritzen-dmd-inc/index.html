The practice

ClearCorrect is a modern orthodontic solution that uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gradually straighten teeth without the need for traditional braces. Made from a smooth, transparent plastic, ClearCorrect aligners are nearly invisible and removable, allowing patients to eat, brush, and floss comfortably.

Temporary anchorage devices, or TADs, are small titanium anchors used in certain orthodontic cases to help achieve quicker tooth movement with more efficiency and comfort. TADs may be used in addition to braces or as an alternative to headgear.

Orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics is a specialty that manages tooth movement and guides facial growth, mainly during childhood, using braces, headgear, and other specialized appliances to address facial abnormalities.

Surgical orthodontics, also called orthognathic surgery, corrects severe cases of bad bites, jaw bone abnormalities, and malocclusion. It involves collaboration between an orthodontist and an oral and maxillofacial surgeon to provide specialized care for complex craniofacial issues.

Craniofacial orthodontics is a sub-specialty of orthodontics that focuses on treating patients with birth defects like cleft lip and palate. The orthodontist works with a team including speech pathologists, oral surgeons, and craniofacial plastic surgeons to plan treatment.
