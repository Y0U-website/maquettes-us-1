About Us

Your dental appointment, smarter .

We’ve all been there. You wait an hour in an exam room for a doctor to pop in for 30 seconds, offering a quick diagnosis that leaves you with more questions than answers. In a world focused on things that “spark joy,” going to the dentist isn’t usually one of them. It’s different here.

At Smart Dental Care, you get advanced care from highly-trained professionals using the most state-of-the-art technology at an affordable cost. But – more than that – you get a visit focused on you. Your visit starts with a friendly “hello” from our compassionate team who is eager to learn about what brings you in. After completing only the treatment you actually need, we’ll get you on your way with confidence about your health and the best version of your smile.

Could going to the dentist become the best part of your day?

We’re up for the challenge. At Smart Dental Care, you get:

Modern Dentistry

You’ll find we use the most up-to-date techniques and technology, including digital x-rays that greatly reduce radiation, intraoral cameras that show you what we see, computerized electronic claims for faster insurance payments, and treatments to get the results you want, faster.

Plus, since we know how frustrating it can feel to be referred somewhere else, we offer most any treatment you need in our office.

Our Golden Rule Promise

We promise to treat you the way we’d like to be treated. Your time is valuable and we have built in measures to stay on time, so we can avoid wasting yours. This means that you’ll be seen on time for your appointment ( really ).

Any treatment we recommend will come with a thorough discussion and ample time to ask questions. Plus, we’ll always offer options, so you can choose the treatment right for you.

Comfortable Care for You (& Your Wallet)

We’ve gone to great lengths to make our office as warm and comfortable as possible when you visit us. Amenities include an internet bar in the reception room, a children’s area with video games, televisions with headphones in each treatment room services available upon request.

Plus, we believe that financial concerns should never stand between you and the dental care you deserve. So, we’ll work with you and your insurance (if applicable) to reach a financial agreement that’s comfortable.
