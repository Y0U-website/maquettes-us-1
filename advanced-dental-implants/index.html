The practice

The Yomi Robotic System is the most advanced, guided implant surgery equipment available today, taking precise dental implant placement, particularly for complex cases, to an unparalleled level of accuracy.

ADI is one of the only oral surgery practices in the region with a fully-equipped, outpatient surgery center. Precise Smile surgical procedures are performed in the surgery center with a highly trained, skilled surgical staff and an anesthesiologist.

Emergency Same Day Implants Patients who experience unanticipated tooth loss and want their smile restored as quickly as possible can usually have an emergency, or immediate implant and temporary crown placed the same day as the exam and consultation.
