Insurance & Financing

Affordable care, no surprises

We accept most major dental insurance plans and offer flexible financing options so that cost is never a barrier to the care you deserve.

We work with your plan

Chilton Dental Group accepts most major dental insurance carriers. Please call our office at (908) 352-4438 or email us to verify that your specific plan is accepted before your visit.

PPO Plans

We are in-network with most major PPO dental insurance plans. In-network patients receive the highest level of benefits and lowest out-of-pocket costs.

Out-of-Network

If we are not in-network with your plan, we will still file claims on your behalf and help you maximize your available benefits.

Verify Your Coverage

Not sure if your plan is accepted? Call us at (908) 352-4438 and our team will check your benefits before your appointment.

Flexible payment options

We believe everyone deserves quality dental care. If you do not have insurance or need help covering your portion of treatment, we offer payment plan options to spread costs over time.

Payment Plans

We can work with you to set up an in-office payment arrangement for larger treatment plans. Ask our front desk team for details.

Major Credit Cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Payment is due at the time of service unless prior arrangements have been made.

Third-Party Financing

We can refer patients to third-party financing providers for extended payment terms on larger treatment costs. Ask us for a referral.

CareCredit Apply Today

CareCredit is a health care credit card that helps you pay for treatments and procedures for the entire family. It lets you make convenient monthly payments and, with special financing options, you can pay over time with low monthly payments.

CareCredit is accepted at Chilton Dental Group and can be used for virtually any dental service from routine cleanings to Invisalign, implants, and full-mouth restorations.

6, 12, 18 & 24 month options with approved credit
