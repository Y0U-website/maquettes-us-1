Services

Everything your smile could ever need

From routine cleanings to same-day implants, our Elizabeth office handles every stage of dental care all under one roof.

Keeping your smile healthy

Fillings

All fillings are tooth-colored composite, blending seamlessly with your natural teeth to restore strength and appearance after cavities or damage.

Hygiene & Cleanings

Our hygienists provide routine cleanings and advanced periodontal procedures. We strive to deliver minimally invasive, comfortable care at every visit.

Oral Surgery & Extractions

Surgical extractions including wisdom tooth removal are performed in our office with gentle technique and detailed aftercare guidance.

Endodontics (Root Canals)

Modern techniques allow us to complete root canal therapy rapidly and painlessly, saving infected teeth and relieving pain in a single visit.

Straightening your smile

Invisalign

Clear, removable aligners that are virtually undetectable. Whether correcting a first-time issue or retreat after not wearing a retainer, we have fast, discreet solutions.

Orthodontic Treatment

We offer many quick solutions to correct shifting or crowding using modern orthodontic techniques tailored to your specific bite and smile goals.

A smile you are proud of

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening products break stains into smaller pieces, making the color less concentrated and your teeth noticeably brighter results you can see in one visit.

Bonding

A quick, single-visit solution to close small gaps, fix chips or cracks, and remove discolorations using tooth-colored resin applied directly to the tooth.

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells placed over the front of your teeth to create a natural-looking, well-aligned smile. Ideal for staining, chips, gaps, or uneven sizing.

Replacing & rebuilding

Conventional Implants

We place conventional implants to permanently replace missing teeth and stabilize existing dentures, giving you a natural-looking and fully functional result.

Mini-Implants

Mini-implant placement is performed in our office to stabilize loose dentures. This procedure can dramatically improve the fit, comfort, and confidence of your dentures.

Crowns & Bridges

All-porcelain crowns and bridges look and feel natural. An excellent option if you have unsightly black lines around older crowns or bridges.

Dentures & Partials

Full dentures, partial dentures, and immediate dentures tailored to your needs. Many patients benefit from implant-supported dentures for greater stability.

Immediate Full-Mouth Restoration

We offer permanent full-mouth restoration in a single day using cutting-edge implant technology an entire mouth predictably restored in one visit.
